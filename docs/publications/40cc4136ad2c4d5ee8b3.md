# JAXtari: High\-Throughput and Easy\-to\-Modify Arcade Learning Environment

**Autores:** Quentin Delfosse; Raban Emunds; Paul Seitz; Sebastian Wette; Jannis Blüml; Daniel Kirn; Dominik Mandok; Kristian Kersting

**Data:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** preprint

## Resumo

The Arcade Learning Environment \(ALE\) has been a cornerstone of Deep Reinforcement Learning research, providing a standardized benchmark for evaluating general agents\. However, its lack of native support for object\-extraction and environment modification restricts research into evaluating whether agents learn robust causal models or rely on brittle visual heuristics\. Furthermore, the ALE&\#x27;s dependence on CPU\-based emulation creates a significant bottleneck in modern GPU\-accelerated training pipelines, limiting both experimental scale and iteration frequency\. In this work, we introduce JAXtari, an open\-source, high\-performance reimplementation of Atari 2600 environments\. By leveraging massive GPU parallelization, JAXtari allows PPO agents to finish training on 100M environment steps on a single A100\-GPU in under an hour\. Beyond speed, it provides object\-centric observations and a flexible modification system, allowing researchers to, e\.g\. develop neuro\-symbolic methods or discover novel shortcuts and subsequently train and adapt their agents rapidly, sometimes in only minutes\.
