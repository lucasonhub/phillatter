# The Role of Digital Leadership and Artificial Intelligence in Sustainable Tourism Strategies: Evidence from Turkey

**Autores:** Elif Savaşkan

**Data:** 2026-10-15 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

This study investigates how digital leadership and artificial intelligence \(AI\) contribute to the development of sustainable tourism strategies, focusing on the context of the Turkish hospitality industry\. The central research question examines how digital leadership practices guide the strategic use of AI technologies toward sustainability\-oriented outcomes\. Grounded in digital leadership theory and the triple bottom line sustainability framework, the study adopts a qualitative multiple case study design\. Data were collected through document analysis of sustainability reports, strategic plans, and publicly available digital transformation materials from four leading hotel groups in Turkey: Rixos Hotels, Titanic Hotels, Maxx Royal Hotels, and The Marmara Hotels\. The data were analyzed using thematic analysis to identify recurring patterns and strategic practices\. The findings reveal that AI\-enabled applications support sustainability by enhancing operational efficiency, enabling personalized service delivery, and improving environmental management practices, particularly in areas such as energy efficiency, resource optimization, and carbon footprint reduction\. Furthermore, the results indicate that the alignment of digital leadership with ethical responsibility, innovation orientation, and employee digital capability development strengthens organizational competitiveness and long\-term sustainability\. The study contributes to the literature by offering an applied strategic framework that conceptualizes digital leadership as a governance mechanism directing AI technologies toward sustainability objectives\. Beyond academic contributions, the findings provide practical insights for managers and policymakers seeking to integrate digital transformation initiatives with sustainable tourism strategies, especially in emerging tourism economies\.
