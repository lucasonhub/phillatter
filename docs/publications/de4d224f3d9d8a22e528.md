# Understanding English News Literacy and Digital Communication Through the Study of Headlines and Reports with Machine Learning and Data Visualisation

**Autores:** Dipika Gautam; Subodh Kumar Yadav

**Data:** 2026-10-10 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

In today’s digital world, most people get their news from online sources\. But they often only read the headline and skip the full report\. This makes the language of headlines very important\. If a headline is too difficult, readers may not understand it and may not click on it\. This study looks at how English news headlines use language and how that affects news literacy\. We collected 40 English news headlines from popular sources like BBC, CNN, The Guardian, The Hindu and The Times of India\. These headlines cover five areas: Education, Technology, Environment, Social Issues and Business\. We used readability scores, word count and sentiment analysis to study them\. We also used a machine learning model to classify headlines as simple or complex\. The model gave 87\.5% accuracy\. We made six charts to show our results\. The charts include readability distribution, category\-wise comparison, word count distribution, sentiment pie chart, scatter plot and confusion matrix\. Our results show that 45% of headlines are difficult to read\. Environment news is the hardest\. We also found a strong negative correlation between headline length and readability\. This means longer headlines are harder to understand\. This study shows how machine learning and data visualisation can help us understand news language better\. It can help journalists write headlines that more people can understand\.
