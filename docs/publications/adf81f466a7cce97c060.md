# Marques, Teresa: The Aptness of Anger&\#x27;s Expression? The Outbreak of Angry Citizens

## Descrição do feed

In Thomas Sturm &amp; Sabine Döring, Nationalism and Populism: Emotions, Reason, and the Crisis of Contemporary Democracy\. London and NY: Routledge\. pp\. 153\-178\. 2026In what contexts is the public expression of anger permissible? Although philosophers have classically objected to expressing anger in rational political deliberation, there have been recent arguments put forward in favour of anger’s public expression\. These arguments rely crucially on the idea that anger has aptness conditions, as an appraisal of wrongs or injustice, and has “emotivational” goals that are not essentially retributive\. If anger is apt when it appraises moral wrongs, then its expression should likewise be permissible and apt, or so the argument goes \(Srinivasan 2018, Lepoutre 2018, Shoemaker 2017, Silva 2021a, 2021b\)\. In the European context, citizens would be right to express anger when faced with injustices\. In this paper, I show that it is far from obvious that anger constitutively appraises injustice\. Moreover, we cannot ignore its counterproductive effects, which provide all things considered reasons against its role in deliberation\. I conclude with some considerations about the current political context in Europe and the dangers of not addressing the underlying causes of citizens’ anger\.
