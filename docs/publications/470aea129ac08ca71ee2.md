# Role of the digital specialist community public health nurse: reimagining community nursing in the AI\-enabled public health landscape

**Autores:** Michelle Lorraine Vizard

**Data:** 2026-10-15 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Digital transformation and artificial intelligence are redefining how people access and navigate community healthcare\. Concurrently, community nursing services are facing a sustained workforce decline\. While digital nurse leadership has matured in many acute organisations, community nurses are increasingly expected to deliver technology\-enabled care without equivalent leadership structures, support or career pathways\. In this article, the author details a proposal for a digital specialist community public health nurse \(digital SCPHN\) role framework\. The author describes the digital SCPHN as first and foremost a clinician, with digital capability as an essential enhancement rather than a replacement of core nursing objectives\. Positioned as a digital translator and champion within community teams, the digital SCPHN helps people to navigate complex digital healthcare systems while supporting their colleagues through technology\-enabled care prioritisation, secure safeguarding communication and community risk insight\. The author also outlines six actionable recommendations for front\-line nurses, team leads and system commissioners to strengthen community nursing impact, improve digital literacy and ensure equitable, person\-centred care in digitally enabled neighbourhood healthcare systems\.
