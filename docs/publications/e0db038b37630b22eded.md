# IMPACT OF QUANTUM LEADERSHIP ON ORGANIZATIONAL PERFORMANCE THROUGH HUMAN RESOURCE AUTOMATION AND DATA\-DRIVEN DECISION SUPPORT SYSTEM

**Autores:** Mughis Ur\-Rehman Hashmi; Aneeqa Zia

**Data:** 2026-10-12 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Nowadays, in the business world that is hard and technological, companies have to embrace the culture of change through adaptive leadership and high\-tech digital systems to maintain organizational performance\. Conventional leadership is usually ineffective in dealing with uncertainty, rapid change, and interdependence\. This research analyzes the relationship between the concepts of quantum leadership and organizational performance, with human resource automation used as a mediating factor and data\-driven decision support system used as a moderator\. This research is framed on the foundation of Resource\-Based View \(RBV\) theory, which provides an integrative research framework between leadership, human resource automation, and data\-driven decision\-making\. A quantitative research design is adopted, and data were collected through structured questionnaires from 360 employees of FrieslandCampina Engro Pakistan Limited\. The collected data were analyzed using SPSS and SmartPLS to test the hypotheses and structural relationships\. The findings provide empirical evidence that quantum leadership supports organizational performance through effective human resource automation, and this relationship is reinforced by data\-driven decision support system\. The study adds theoretical value to the literature on digitalization and leadership and provides practical implications for organizational leaders and human resource practitioners interested in enhancing performance in dynamic organizational settings\. Keywords: Quantum Leadership, Organizational Performance, Human Resource Automation, Data\-Driven Decision Support System\.
