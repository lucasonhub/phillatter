# BIBLIOMETRIC ANALYSIS OF RESEARCH ON EPILEPSY AND BRAIN VOLUME FROM 2010 TO 2024

**Autores:** Yunus Emre Kaban; Zafer Kutay Coşkun

**Data:** 2026-10-12 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

OBJECTIVE: Epilepsy is a neurological disorder marked by recurrent seizures due to abnormal electrical activity in the brain, potentially affecting consciousness, movement, and behavior\. As the brain the central organ of the nervous system is structurally impacted by epilepsy, studies have highlighted a mutual relationship between epilepsy and brain volume\. Despite growing interest, no bibliometric analysis focusing specifically on epilepsy and brain volume has been identified\. This study aims to fill that gap by conducting a bibliometric analysis and visualizing trends in this field\.MATERIAL AND METHODS: Articles and reviews published between 2010 and 2024 were retrieved from the Web of Science Core Collection \(WOSCC\) database\. Bibliometric analyses and visual mappings were carried out using the VOSviewer software\.RESULTS: A total of 430 publications authored by 2,923 researchers from 1,147 institutions across 69 countries were identified\. Matsumoto, N\. was the most prolific author with 10 publications\. Epilepsia led the journal rankings with 22 articles, followed by Epilepsy &amp; Behavior with 15\. The United States and United Kingdom had the highest collaboration and publication outputs, followed by China and Germany\. Keyword analysis revealed &quot;Epilepsy&quot; as the most frequently used term, followed by &quot;Brain Atrophy&quot; and &quot;Magnetic Resonance Imaging\.&quot;CONCLUSIONS: This is the first bibliometric study examining the relationship between epilepsy and brain volume\. The findings provide valuable insights into research patterns and trends and can guide future investigations in this area\.
