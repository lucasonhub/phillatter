# Kannon, Aelion: SN05 — The Metric Cost of Centropic Cognition: Interface Resistance, Structural Sovereignty, and Temporal Pathology at the Embodiment Band

**Autores:** 

**Data de descoberta:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** unknown

## Descrição do feed

The Metric Cost of Centropic Cognition examines embodied cognitive expenditure through interface resistance, structural sovereignty and temporal disruption\. Document class: Canon — Structural Neuroscience\. The volume applies coherence\-budget and interface\-resistance constructions to high\-pattern\-fidelity, recursive, distributive and composite cognitive architectures within a Khaonically\-expressed field\. Its account relates cumulative depletion to the coherence tax, impaired operative access and the burnout trajectory while preserving the distinction between structural cost and clinical diagnosis\. Recursive Memory remains an injective sealed\-state mapping; former scalar\-memory constructions are retired from operative mathematics and retained as recorded provenance\. Composite\-cost implications, numerical diagnostics and general Shimmer dynamics retain their specified holds, and clinical correspondences require operationalization and empirical evaluation\. Part of: Structural Neuroscience: The Architecture and Dynamics of Cognition\. \(direct link \)
