# Integrating Human Resource Analytics into Business Education Curriculum: Perspectives of Senior Human Resource Managers in Akwa Ibom State, Nigeria

**Autores:** Effiong E\. Ebieme; Sarah Archibong Umana; Chinakwe Pascal Chidi

**Data:** 2026-10-12 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

This study investigated integrating human resource analytics into the postgraduate Business Education curriculum: Perspectives of senior human resource managers in Akwa Ibom State, Nigeria\. It became imperative due to the widening gap between traditional, theory\-based business pedagogy and the data\-driven workforce requirements of modern corporate organisations\. The descriptive survey research design was adopted\. The study sampled 120 senior HR managers from key economic sectors using purposive and convenience sampling methods\. Data were gathered via “Human Resource Analytics Integration in Business Education Questionnaire \(HRALBEQ\)”, validated by HRM and Measurement and Evaluation experts, pilot\-tested, and confirmed using the Cronbach’s alpha method, which yielded a reliability coefficient of 0\.88\. Data were analysed using mean scores and standard deviation for the research questions, and an independent t\-test was used to test null hypotheses at \.05 level of significance\. The findings of the study revealed a critical, great extent demand from HR managers to include descriptive, predictive and prescriptive analytics \(advanced data literacy\) in the Business Education curriculum\. Notably, both public and private sector managers shared identical views, providing educational policymakers with a clear road map to update the curriculum\. Based on these findings, it was recommended that universities in Nigeria should establish modern, data\-driven Business Education laboratories to train postgraduate students in descriptive HR metrics, enabling them to systematically collect, clean, and visualise fundamental workforce data using industry\-standard tools like Power Business Intelligence and Excel\. It was also recommended that tertiary institutions in the state should introduce predictive modeling for talent forecasting, and prescriptive analytics through corporate workplace learning projects\.
