# Soilless Agriculture: Advantages Over Traditional Agriculture, Challenges, Vertical Farming Potential, and Artificial Intelligence

**Autores:** Abdullah Havan

**Data:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

The rapid growth of the global population and the increasing scarcity of agricultural land and water resources necessitate the development of innovative production approaches to ensure food security\. Soilless agriculture techniques present a significant alternative for sustainable agriculture due to their capacity to enhance productivity while reducing water, land, and fertilizer usage\. In this review study, the fundamental methods of soilless agriculture are examined, and the advantages these methods provide in terms of water and energy use, land requirements, and environmental impacts are evaluated in comparison with traditional soil\-based agriculture\. Furthermore, the technical and economic limitations encountered in soilless farming applications are addressed, and the effect of lighting management on production efficiency and energy consumption in vertical farming systems is discussed in line with current literature findings\. In addition, the distinction between fresh weight and dry matter/calorie\-based approaches in yield assessments, along with the variability of the environmental impact of soilless agriculture depending on the energy source utilized, are explored in light of the current literature\. Key words: Sustainable cultivation, Food security, Nutrient solution, Water use efficiency, Dry matter accumulation, Plant factories\.
