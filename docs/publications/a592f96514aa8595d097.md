# AI literacy: Gen Z expectations vs\. organisational practices in India&\#x27;s dynamic job market

**Autores:** Kumkum Tripathi; Aastha Patidar; Shine David

**Data:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

The mismatch between Generation Z&\#x27;s \(Gen Z\) high expectations of artificial intelligence \(AI\) and organisational AI practices has profound implications for vocational well\-being in India&\#x27;s job market\. This research explores the structural path between Gen Z’s AI Expectations, Organisational AI Practices, and AI Literacy\-Driven Vocational Well\-being, with Skills Velocity as a key contextual variable in a rapidly changing, technology\-driven landscape\.This research used a mixed\-methods approach\. A structured questionnaire was administered to 203 Gen Z professionals and HR managers in AI\-heavy industries \(IT, BFSI and manufacturing\) in Tier\-1 and Tier\-2 cities in India\. Structural Equation Modelling \(SEM\) using the lavaan package in R was used to test the path model\. We triangulated our findings with 20 semi\-structured interviews\. The sample was skewed towards males \(70:30 male\-to\-female\) and non\-normal\.Based on the findings, the model fit was satisfactory\. Gen Z&\#x27;s AI Expectations positively impacted Organisational AI Practices \(β = 0\.608, p &lt; \.001\) and AI Literacy\-driven Vocational Well\-being \(β = 0\.449, p &lt; \.001\)\. Organisational AI Practices also significantly and positively influenced Vocational Well\-being \(β = 0\.418, p &lt; \.001\)\. The variance in Organisational AI Practices and Vocational Well\-being explained by the model was 36\.9% and 60\.4%, respectively\.The research suggests that matching organisational AI practices to Gen Z expectations is crucial to improving AI literacy\-based vocational well\-being in India&\#x27;s ever\-evolving labour market\. The findings reveal the important role of Gen Z as a driver for change in organisations and the need for forward\-looking, skills\-velocity\-sensitive human resource management strategies such as AI upskilling and training, ethical AI policies and lifelong learning opportunities to enhance retention and workforce preparedness\.
