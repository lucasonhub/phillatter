# ACCURACY AND RELIABILITY OF AI CHATBOTS IN CBCT\-BASED IMPLANT SITE ASSESSMENT AND PROSTHODONTIC TREATMENT PLANNING: A COMPARATIVE STUDY

**Autores:** 

**Data:** 2026-10-14 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

BackgroundArtificial intelligence \(AI\) chatbots are increasingly being explored as clinical decision\-support tools in dentistry\.Cone beam computed tomography \(CBCT\) plays a vital role in implant site assessment and prosthodontic treatment planning; however, interpretation of CBCT data requires clinical expertise and may vary among clinicians\.The reliability and accuracy of AI chatbots in CBCT\-based implant planning remain insufficiently explored\.Aim To evaluate and compare the accuracy and reliability of an AI chatbot in CBCT\-based implant site assessment and prosthodontic treatment planning with expert prosthodontist evaluations\. Materials and MethodsTwenty CBCT cases indicated for single\-tooth implant placement were selected from departmental archives\.Each case was independently evaluated by an AI chatbot \(ChatGPT\) and expert prosthodontists with a minimum of five years of clinical experience\.Standardized clinical scenarios and a predefined set of ten structured questions related to implant site assessment and treatment planning were used for all evaluations\.Expert consensus responses served as the reference standard\.AI\-generated responses were scored using a three\-point ordinal scale: 0 = incorrect, 1 = partially correct/incomplete, and 2 = completely correct\.Descriptive statistics and Chi\-square analysis were performed, with statistical significance set at p &lt; 0\.05\. ResultsThe AI chatbot demonstrated higher accuracy in implant site suitability assessment and identification of critical anatomical structures, with accuracy percentages of 94\.7% and 92\.5%, respectively\.Moderate accuracy was observed for bone assessment, implant angulation, implant positioning, and prosthodontic treatment planning\.The lowest accuracy was observed for implant length and diameter selection \(60\.0%\)\.The overall weighted accuracy of the AI chatbot was 79\.6%\.Chi\-square analysis demonstrated a statistically significant difference in response distribution among evaluated parameters \(χ² = 51\.62,df = 18, p &lt; 0\.001\)\.Conclusion AI chatbots demonstrated acceptable overall accuracy in CBCT\-based implant site assessment and prosthodontic treatment planning, particularly in basic diagnostic and anatomical evaluation tasks\.However, reduced accuracy was observed in complex treatment planning decisions requiring advanced clinical judgment\.AI chatbots may serve as useful adjunctive tools in implant dentistry and prosthodontics but cannot currently replace expert clinician evaluation\.Further studies with larger sample sizes and multiple AI platforms are recommended\.
