# Challenges to Biodiversity Rights and Intellectual Property Rights under the Biological Diversity Act, 2002

**Autores:** Nidhi Shubhanand

**Data:** 2026-10-14 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

The Biodiversity Act, 2002, was enacted to conserve biological diversity, ensure sustainable use of biological resources, and facilitate equitable sharing of benefits arising from their utilization\. This paper examines the interface between the Biodiversity Act and Intellectual Property Rights \(IPR\), focusing on the challenges of balancing innovation with biodiversity conservation\. It analyses the legal safeguards against biopiracy, the role of prior approval mechanisms, and benefit\-sharing provisions in patent applications involving biological resources and traditional knowledge\. The study highlights implementation challenges and suggests policy measures to strengthen biodiversity governance while promoting sustainable development and responsible innovation\.
