# A unified RT\-DETR–Mask2Former framework for segmentation\-guided plant leaf disease detection and classification in real\-world agricultural environments

**Autores:** C H\. Mohan Sai Kumar; Golthu Anusha; V\. Dhilip Kumar; Oana Geman; Roxana Toderean; Mihaela E\. Hnatiuc

**Data:** 2026-10-15 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Plant leaf disease image analysis under real\-world field conditions remains challenging due to cluttered backgrounds, illumination variation, overlapping leaves, scale changes, and irregular lesion patterns\. This study presents a unified RT\-DETR–Mask2Former framework for segmentation\-guided plant leaf disease detection and classification\. In the proposed pipeline, RT\-DETR first localizes leaf regions from complex agricultural images, after which Mask2Former generates fine\-grained lesion masks within the detected regions\. The refined lesion masks are used to isolate disease\-affected regions, enabling EfficientNet\-B4 to extract disease\-focused feature representations for classification\. An EMA\-normalized adaptive task\-weighting procedure was applied with AdamW optimization to modulate the relative gradient scales of the separately supervised modules\. Model development and internal evaluation were conducted using leakage\-controlled, group\-aware PlantVillage \(PV\) partitions, whereas 4,200 field\-acquired images were reserved exclusively for independent external evaluation\. On the complete 38\-category PV internal test, accuracy and macro\-F1 were 98\.34% and 97\.97%\. On the nine\-category source\-matched subset, accuracy and macro\-F1 were 97\.43% and 96\.77%, versus 84\.93% and 80\.80% in the field\. PV\-to\-field performance declined from 95\.63% to 89\.28% for leaf\-localization AP@0\.5 and from 92\.47%/86\.00% to 77\.90%/63\.80% for lesion\-positive Dice/IoU\. Lesion guidance improved field macro\-F1 from 73\.97% to 80\.80%\. The complete pipeline required approximately 285 GFLOPs and 63 ms per image \(16 FPS\), demonstrating a practical accuracy–efficiency trade\-off while retaining a measurable field\-domain gap\.
