# Evaluation of breach and rheological parameters for self\-rescue zone \(ZAS\) definition of mining dams

**Autores:** Pedro de Carvalho Thá; André Luiz Tonso Fabiani

**Data:** 2026-10-15 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Recent tailings dam failure in Brazil resulted in significant social, environmental and economic impact\. Following the failures, Brazilian regulation changed and demanded tailings dam breach analysis to consider Non\-Newtonian fluid behavior, where applicable\. However, in addition to rheology, other variables such as failure mode, breach hydrograph and Manning coefficient also influence tailings runout\. The objective of the present study is to evaluate the influence of rheological and breach parameters \(including failure mode\) on tailings runout and, especially, on self\-rescue zone \(ZAS\) definition\. The study evaluated tailings breach runout for four iron ore tailings dams located in Brazil\. Runout simulations considered different peak flow hydrographs for overtopping and piping, through Monte Carlo analysis\. For liquefaction a single hydrograph was considered\. Different combinations of hydrographs and rheology were evaluated, as well as Newtonian behavior \(water parameters\)\. Rheological parameters were taken specifically for one dam and from a database of 1162 vane rheometer tests in iron ore tailings and sediments for other three dams\. Manning coefficient variation was also evaluated\. Results were evaluated for four hydraulic variables along downstream path: frontal wave arrival time, time to peak, maximum wave velocity and height\. Comparison of results through Kruskall\-Walis test indicated that for most of the cases rheological parameters and Manning coefficient variation produced similar results\. Peak flow hydrographs influenced results significantly\. Self\-rescue zone downstream extent was only influenced by liquefaction failure mode\. Lateral extent of self\-rescue zone was only slightly influenced by rheology variation but was influenced by peak flow hydrographs\.
