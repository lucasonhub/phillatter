# Teng, Lu: Conscious Schematic Imagery in Aphantasia

## Descrição do feed

\_Mind and Language\_\. forthcomingAphantasics report lacking voluntary visual imagery but perform successfully on diverse imagery\-related tasks\. This disparity raises a puzzle\. I argue for two theses\. First, I distinguish between “painterly” and “schematic” forms of visual imagery and argue that, while aphantasics lack the former, they typically retain the latter\. Second, I draw on findings concerning metacognitive confidence to suggest that aphantasics’ retained schematic imagery is conscious\. My account is distinct from views that primarily appeal to alternative strategies, such as spatial imagery, to make sense of aphantasics’ performance\. It also offers a unified explanation\.
