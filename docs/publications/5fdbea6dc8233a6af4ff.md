# Legal Discipline of Data Procurement and Corporate Governance in Generative AI Development: A Comparative Analysis of U\.S\.\-Japan Copyright Law and Responsibility Allocation for Infrastructure Providers and Users

**Autores:** Yoko Hasebe

**Data:** 2026-10-12 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Abstract:This research monograph provides a rigorous comparative legal and corporate governance analysis of data procurement in frontier generative artificial intelligence development\. Focusing on the normative divergence between Article 30\-4 of the Japanese Copyright Act and the Fair Use doctrine under 17 U\.S\.C\. §107, we examine cross\-border conflicts of law arising when data extraction, compute training, and model serving occur in separate jurisdictions\. Furthermore, the paper analyzes the liability allocation between AI developers and compute/cloud infrastructure providers, distinguishing passive IaaS/PaaS conduits from integrated strategic partnerships\. Grounded in Delaware corporate governance standards \(the Caremark doctrine\) and ESG supply\-chain due diligence, we establish a practical compliance and contractual risk\-allocation architecture to ensure sustainable, transparent knowledge ecosystems\.
