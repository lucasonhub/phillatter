# The Impact of Problem\-Based Learning \(PBL\) on Intellectual Indicators: A Comparative Analysis of STEM and Humanities Disciplines

**Autores:** Алишер Эрназаров

**Data:** 2026-10-15 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

This study investigates the influence of problem\-based learning \(PBL\) on students&\#x27; intellectual development, comparing its effects within STEM and humanities disciplines\. The paper reviews contemporary experimental, quasi\-experimental, and mixed\-methods approaches to measuring cognitive and metacognitive change, describes the adaptation of PBL to the specific demands of STEM and humanities coursework, and outlines a prospective research design—including growth\-curve and hierarchical Bayesian modeling—for tracking long\-term cognitive outcomes\. The findings indicate that PBL meaningfully strengthens critical thinking, analytical reasoning, and independent problem\-solving in both fields, with STEM disciplines showing a stronger effect on logical and systemic reasoning and humanities disciplines showing a stronger effect on critical interpretation and reflective ability\. Implementation challenges, limitations, and directions for future research are discussed, and recommendations are offered for institutions seeking to adopt PBL in a discipline\-sensitive manner\.
