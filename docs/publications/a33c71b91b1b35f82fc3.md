# Weisbrod, Victor: Philosophical Foundations of AI Certification

## Descrição do feed

\_Certified Ai Project\_\. 2026This white paper conducts a systematic inquiry into the philosophical foundations of AI certification, that is, into the basic assumptions of the institutionalised endeavour to assess AI applications as ethically good or bad\. It identifies and examines two paradigms of AI governance: the dominant principles paradigm and the alternative systems paradigm\. By clarifying and problematising the foundational assumptions of both paradigms, the white paper elucidates their respective strengths and weakness\. The principles paradigm provides an administratively legible, legally predictable, and standardised framework for product\-level auditing\. However, it encounters significant challenges regarding underdetermination, value arbitration, narrow artefact boundaries, and ethics washing\. The systems paradigm approaches AI applications in a manner that aims to capture these as embedded in complex ecological, social, and economic systems with inherent standards of good functioning\. In turn, it faces significant hurdles concerning system unboundedness, data opacity, normative projection, and temporal bottlenecks\. Ultimately, by explicating the implicit assumptions and limitations of both paradigms, the white paper aims to deepen our understanding of AI certification as a complex governance practice, and to provide foundational insights for future research, standard\-setting, and policymaking in this field\.
