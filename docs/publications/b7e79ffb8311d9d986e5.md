# BO‘LAJAK MUSIQA O‘QITUVCHILARIDA KREATIV KOMPETENTLIKNI RIVOJLANTIRISHNING PSIXOLOGIK XUSUSIYATLARI

**Autores:** KOMIL TOXIROVICH ABDIRIMOV

**Data:** 2026-10-13 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

This article examines motivational, cognitive, emotional\-volitional, and reflective psychological factors involved in developing creative competence in future music teachers\. Drawing on a conceptual analysis of international research in music education, it proposes a component\-based model of creative competence and a structured set of learning activities\. Musical improvisation, divergent thinking, creative self\-efficacy, and pedagogical reflection are considered interconnected but independently developable dimensions of professional preparation\. The proposed framework highlights observable learning outcomes and formative assessment strategies and offers practical guidance for improving preservice music teacher education\. The article does not claim to report newly collected empirical data\.
