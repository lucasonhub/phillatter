# Research Trends in Attention Deficit Hyperactivity Disorder and Allergic Diseases: A Bibliometric Analysis \(1994–2025\)

**Autores:** Fatma Çiftçi; Oğuzhan Koyuncu

**Data:** 2026-10-17 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Aim: Attention\-deficit/hyperactivity disorder \(ADHD\) and allergic diseases research has expanded rapidly, yet its global structure and hotspots remain fragmented\. This bibliometric analysis maps ADHD, allergic diseases, and asthma/atopy research to identify publication growth, influential sources, collaboration patterns, and emerging themes\.Method: A Web of Science Core Collection search was conducted on 9 January 2026\. English\-language articles and reviews published between 1994 and 2025 were included, yielding a total of 244 records\. Bibliometrix/Biblioshiny was used for descriptive indicators and thematic mapping, and VOSviewer for co\-authorship, co\-citation, and keyword co\-occurrence networks\. Keywords and thematic evolution analyses were applied to detect shifts in ADHD and allergic diseases topics over time\.Results: The dataset comprised 244 publications across 168 sources and 1,306 authors \(mean 6\.32 authors/document\), with 20\.81% international co\-authorship\. Annual production increased markedly after 2015, peaking in 2021–2023\. Source analyses showed concentration in a limited set of core journals, while co\-citation patterns confirmed an interdisciplinary base spanning pediatrics, psychiatry, and allergy/immunology\. Keyword mapping highlighted persistent links between ADHD and asthma, atopic dermatitis, and allergic rhinitis, alongside emerging interest in inflammation\-related and life\-course topics\.Conclusion: ADHD–allergic diseases research is growing and increasingly interdisciplinary, with rising emphasis on mechanistic and inflammation\-oriented themes\. Future studies should prioritize longitudinal and causal designs and broaden international collaboration to strengthen clinical relevance\.
