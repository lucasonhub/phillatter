# Lalescu, Cristian Constantin: The Turing test is the only objective criterion of consciousness

**Autores:** 

**Data de descoberta:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** unknown

## Descrição do feed

Many humans are now regularly interacting with non\-human information\-processing entities \(AI\), with specialists warning of profound long\-term consequences\. It seems important for such predictions to establish the complexity of future AI\-humanity interactions\. One aspect already being brought up by experts is the issue of AI becoming conscious\. This work uses known results to constrain the notion of consciousness and related concepts, with the purpose of informing the public conversation\.
