# EL\-RAKHAWI NEURO\-PHOTONIC SILICON ARCHITECTURE: EMULATING BIOLOGICAL DENDRITIC COMPUTATION VIA NON\-EQUILIBRIUM THERMODYNAMICS AND CAUSAL SPIKING NEURAL NETWORKS

**Autores:** mohamed kamal arafa el\-rakhawi

**Data:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** book

## Resumo

The El\-Rakhawi Neuro\-Photonic Silicon Architecture \(NPSA\) revolutionizes neuromorphic computing by emulating biological dendritic computation to overcome the Von Neumann bottleneck and extreme energy consumption of modern AI\. It integrates four core modules: silicon\-photonic memristive synapses for low\-power Spike\-Timing\-Dependent Plasticity; dendritic optical interferometers combining linear Mach\-Zehnder interferometers with non\-linear phase\-change elements for localized signal integration; Causal Spiking Neural Networks utilizing a Temporal Asymmetry Constraint to learn true cause\-and\-effect rather than statistical correlations; and non\-equilibrium thermodynamic control to minimize Joule heating\. Performance is quantified by the El\-Rakhawi Neuro\-Photonic Efficiency Metric \(ENPEM\), measuring causal information throughput per Joule\. By bridging solid\-state photonics, thermodynamics, and causal neuroscience, NPSA enables scalable, ultra\-low\-power edge AI for real\-time applications, achieving energy efficiencies orders of magnitude beyond traditional digital processors while strictly adhering to physical limits\.
