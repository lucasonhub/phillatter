# How populisms are established: political organizations and leadership in Latin America

**Autores:** Mario Poblete; Camila Contreras

**Data:** 2026-10-13 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

At the time of writing, studies of populism have been dominated by discursive approaches, which undermine research concerning populism’s causes\. Although there are existing studies that investigate causes, systematic studies of specific connections between causes and outcomes are largely absent\. This article attempts to fill this void by relying on process\-tracing methods that provide the necessary tools for highlighting the ways in which populism is established\. By assessing the causal mechanisms examined in three recent studies that, rather implicitly, look at what is in between, a new and more general mechanism is abstracted\. This is preliminarily tested in three paradigmatic cases of Latin American populist leadership\. Doing this improves the mechanism inductively and allows for the provision of an alternative way of explaining populism in this region\.
