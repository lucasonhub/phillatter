# CORAL\-IDS: A Calibrated Risk Layer for Explainable, Open\-world Intrusion Detection

**Autores:** Sarah Hikmat Khaled; Russl Hussein Hasan; Ali Talib Mohan; Ali saif aldeen Aubaid Khioon

**Data:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Deep\-learning intrusion detection systems \(IDS\) tend to accumulate architectural components faster than evidence that those components help\.This paper takes the opposite route: starting from a rich candidate pipeline, additional retain only what survives paired statistical testing across five seeds, and report every rejected addition alongside its measured effect\.The result, CORAL\-IDS, is a plain 73\.518K\-parameter temporal Transformer wrapped in a Calibrated Operational Risk\-Assessment Layer: a class\-mean prototype memory that signals embedding\-space novelty, Monte Carlo Dropout predictive uncertainty, and a three\-term auditable risk score, with escalation thresholds calibrated as percentiles of verified\-benign validation traffic; SHapley Additive explanations \(SHAP\) accompany every alert as explanation only, without entering the decision path\.Six candidate additions \(isolation\-forest noise filtering, adaptive feature refinement, joint feature\-and\-temporal attention on raw features, prototype\-contrastive training, extreme\-value recalibration, and tenfold rare\-class oversampling\) were each fully implemented and evaluated: none improved closed\-set performance; joint feature\-and\-temporal attention consistently degraded it across all five paired seeds, and rare\-class oversampling lowered R2L recall\.On NSL\-KDD, the minimal detector reaches competitive accuracy and ranking quality under two disclosed evaluation protocols, at sub\-millisecond CPU inference; more importantly, the operational layer whose gains are deterministic for a fixed classifier recovers more than half of the attacks the classifier silently misses, at a precision where the majority of resulting escalations are genuine attacks\.External validation on UNSW\-NB15 shows the learned representation transfers well by ranking quality even as classification accuracy degrades under measured distribution shift, which this paper converts into a concrete deployment recalibration protocol\.
