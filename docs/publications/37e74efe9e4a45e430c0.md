# A Comprehensive Study of Logistics 5\.0 Adaptation &amp; Challenges in the Indian Supply Chain Industry

**Autores:** Pritha Chaturvedi; Soumyajyoti Bhattacharjee

**Data:** 2026-10-17 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Logistics 5\.0 indicates the combination of Industry 5\.0’s important principles such as human\-centricity, resiliency, and sustainability with the advanced digital capabilities of Logistics 4\.0\. While a growing number of developed countries are starting to adopt AI, digital twins, collaborative robots, and human\-machine decision\-making systems, there is still only limited evidence available regarding Logistics 5\.0 implementation in India\. The purpose of this study is to find out the main technological, financial, human, infrastructural, regulatory, organizational, cultural, and sustainability hurdles on the way to Logistics 5\.0 implementation in Indian logistics\. We used a qualitative analysis of secondary data to conduct the research, using logistics cost assessments data, e\-commerce and last mile markets data, previously conducted published studies in the field of Industry 4\.0/5\.0, and some examples from Delhivery Ltd\. The results indicate that following improvements in the logistical cost efficiency of India and rapid growth in the e\-commerce logistics, the utilization of technology is still inconsistent within the industry\. Factors affecting its implementation include high costs of implementation, shortage of skilled workers, cybersecurity threats, poor lastmile infrastructure, lack of digital capabilities, and resistance within organizations to cooperate with machines\. EXPERTS NOTE that most of the large firms in private and public sectors have made significant strides in implementing Logistics 5\.0 while small and medium sized enterprises face numerous obstacles\. The above article discusses readiness issues of the sector and proposes readiness roadmaps as well as specific steps in accordance with the National Logistics Policy of India and PM GatiShakti framework\.
