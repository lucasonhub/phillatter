## Descrição do feed

\_Philosophy and Technology\_ 39\. 2026Contemporary AI systems are becoming increasingly integrated into scientific practices, from designing experiments to assisting with peer review\. This paper argues that AI integration poses a threat to scientific objectivity\. Feminist philosophers of science argue that diversity plays an important role in creating and maintaining scientific objectivity because diverse perspectives are better able to identify and evaluate background assumptions and values, meaning that any one individual perspective is less likely to have an outsized influence on scientific research\. We argue that the integration of AI will reduce the number of opportunities for diverse perspectives to meaningfully contribute to scientific practices, and in doing so, make it more likely that background values will remain transparent and unquestioned\. To show this, we consider a thought experiment where scientists increasingly offload their decisions onto a superhuman scientist who is both infinitely productive and generous with her time\. We suggest that such a case would lead to the superhuman scientist’s idiosyncratic subjective preferences having an outsized influence on the field, even if she is doing perfectly good science\. We then argue that the integration of AI into science is epistemically analogous by showing that AI systems have a perspective in at least a thin sense, meaning that their perspective can have an outsized influence on scientific research\. Finally, we argue that empirical work on algorithmic monocultures and outcome homogenization shows that increasing the number of AI models involved in scientific practices does not solve the problem\.
