# Pothoscape: Survey of Automated Pothole Detection and Hazard Warning

**Autores:** Uttkarsh Hiray, Master\. Suhas Satdive, Ms\. Mansvi Sonje; Mrs\. Pramila S\. Gaidhani Ms\. Tanmayee Patil

**Data:** 2026-10-12 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Potholes and other road\-surface defects are persistent transportation\-safety and infrastructure\-maintenance problems\. Conventional inspection methods can be labour\-intensive, periodic and expensive, while isolated sensing approaches may detect a road anomaly without providing sufficient visual context, accurate location or timely warning to road users\. This literature survey reviews research on automated pothole and road\-damage detection using computer vision, deep learning, smartphone sensors, GPS, crowdsensing and integrated real\-time systems\. The reviewed studies show a progression from vibration\-based and sensor\-based detection toward image\-based object detection and, more recently, systems that combine detection with geographic mapping and driver\-oriented warning\. The literature also indicates that model accuracy depends on dataset diversity, environmental conditions, camera quality and deployment constraints\. Smartphone\-based approaches offer low\-cost sensing, whereas deep\-learning approaches provide stronger visual identification but can require greater computational resources and representative training data\. Based on the comparison, a practical gap remains in integrating low\-cost automatic detection, location tagging, continuous road\-condition mapping and timely hazard warning within one deployable workflow\. The survey therefore establishes the research basis for Pothoscape, a proposed road intelligence system intended to detect potholes automatically, warn approaching users and maintain a continuously updated road\-condition database for future maintenance support\.
