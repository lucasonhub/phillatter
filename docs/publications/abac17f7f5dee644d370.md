# Environmental Vector Theory

**Autores:** Tejass Thakre

**Data:** 2026-10-12 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

ENVIRONMENT VECTOR presents an E\-first theory of how environmental forces drive the connected physical history of Earth—from chemical organization and the emergence of life to evolution, consciousness, human civilization, technology, and artificial intelligence\. It develops a mathematical account of these transitions through physical laws, coupled equations, and feedback mechanisms, connecting successive levels of organization within one continuous environmental\-to\-AI trajectory\.
