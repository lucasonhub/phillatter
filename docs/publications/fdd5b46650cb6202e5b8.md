# Amine\-terminated zirconium metal\-organic framework as an efficient adsorbent for lanthanum removal

**Autores:** Saranya Jagadeesan; Sivalingam Ramesh; R\. T\. Rajendra Kumar; Yuvaraj Haldorai

**Data:** 2026-10-12 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

The current study developed an amine\-terminated zirconium metal\-organic framework \(UiO\-66\-NH2\) to effectively adsorb lanthanum \(La3\+\) from water\. The UiO\-66\-NH2 was confirmed by various analytical techniques\. The optimization of La3\+ removal was achieved through response surface methodology\. For the adsorption of La3\+, the best conditions were determined to be 10 mg of adsorbent, pH 9, and 150 min of contact time\. The adsorbent UiO\-66\-NH2 exhibited a high surface area of 682 m 2 /g with a removal efficiency of 99\.86% and an adsorption capacity of 312\.5 mg/g for La3\+\. The solution pH and the adsorbent quantity had a great effect on the rate of La3\+ adsorption\. The Freundlich model was well suited the adsorption isotherm with an R2 value of 0\.9986\. The La3\+ adsorption on the UiO\-66\-NH2 followed the pseudo second\-order kinetic model\. The Weber\-Morris model suggested that the adsorption process was primarily governed by a single mass\-transfer mechanism throughout the examined time interval\. Therefore, it is possible to use this type of MOF as an adsorbent to remove heavy metals and rate earth metals from sea water\.
