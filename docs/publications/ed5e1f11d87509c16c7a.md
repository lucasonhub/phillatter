# GENERATIVE AI AND EFL WRITING: ITS IMPACT ON ACCURACY, COMPLEXITY AND FLUENCY

**Autores:** Bobur Suyunov

**Data:** 2026-10-15 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

The rapid development of Generative Artificial Intelligence \(GenAI\) has significantly influenced second and foreign language education, particularly the teaching and learning of writing\. Generative AI tools such as ChatGPT can provide immediate feedback, generate ideas, suggest vocabulary, revise grammatical structures, and support learners throughout different stages of the writing process\. However, the extent to which AI\-assisted writing contributes to learners’ actual linguistic development remains an important research question\. This conceptual paper examines the potential impact of GenAI on three central dimensions of English as a Foreign Language \(EFL\) writing performance: accuracy, complexity, and fluency \(CAF\)\. Drawing on recent research in second language writing and AI\-assisted language learning, the paper analyzes the benefits and limitations of GenAI for each dimension\. The literature suggests that AI\-assisted writing can contribute particularly to grammatical accuracy and lexical sophistication by providing immediate corrective feedback and exposure to alternative linguistic forms\. Evidence concerning syntactic complexity and writing fluency, however, is less consistent\. Although learners may produce more polished and fluent\-looking texts with AI assistance, this does not necessarily indicate corresponding development in their independent writing ability\. The paper therefore argues that the pedagogical value of GenAI depends largely on how learners interact with the technology\. When used as a supplementary writing assistant rather than a replacement for independent production, GenAI can support language development, learner autonomy, and reflective writing practices\. The paper concludes by proposing pedagogical principles for responsible AI integration into EFL writing instruction and identifying directions for future empirical research\.
