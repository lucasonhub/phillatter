# Modern human δ¹³C isoscapes in Brazil reveal spatial structure in a C₄\-dominated diet landscape

**Autores:** Maria Gabriella da Silva Araújo; Juliana Soares Emenes; Murillo Q\. R\. Bastos; Rachel Tinoco; Ricardo O\. Mascarenhas; Anelize M\. Bahniuk; Gabriela Bielefeld Nardoto; Deoclecio J\. Amorim; Luiz Antônio Martinelli

**Data:** 2026-10-11 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

This study presents δ¹³C isoscapes for modern human tissues in Brazil, based on paired analyses of dentine collagen and fingernail keratin\. Using a random forest modeling approach combined with climatic and socio\-economic covariates, we reveal spatial patterns in dietary carbon source assimilation across the Northeast and Southeast regions\. While δ¹³C values in dentine correlated mainly with environmental variables, those in fingernails were more closely tied to socio\-economic indicators such as municipal GDP and income inequality\. Partial dependence plots illustrated contrasting relationships between δ¹³C and predictor variables, suggesting that environmental factors may act as spatial proxies, while socio\-economic variables more directly influence dietary intake\. The difference in δ¹³C values between tissues allowed for an exploratory assessment of changes in C₄ food consumption across the life course\. Despite modest model performance, the findings highlight the potential of stable isotope isoscapes as tools for dietary reconstruction and forensic applications in complex socio\-geographical settings\. We emphasize the need for temporally resolved data and expanded sampling to improve model robustness and support interpretations of regional and life\-stage dietary dynamics\.
