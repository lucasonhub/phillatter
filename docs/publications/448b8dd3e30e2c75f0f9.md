# Smart Vaccination Tracking System: A Digital Solution for Efficient Immunization

**Autores:** Mr\. Pratik Anil Palve; Harshad Wagatkar; Monali More; Yash Pawar

**Data:** 2026-10-12 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

The Smart Vaccination Tracking System aims to simplify the records management process for vaccination data while using a digital database to store the data\. Additionally, the Smart Vaccination Tracking System allows health care personnel and end users to register, modify, and manage vaccination records via an application\. In comparison to the traditional methods of track vaccination records using paper, binder, or spreadsheet, the proposed Smart Vaccination Tracking System offers a more accurate and faster method of storing, retrieving, and protecting data\. The system offers a digital process to manage the record of an individual&\#x27;s vaccination history and Automatic notifications of pending vaccination activities, calendar records and reports for active vaccinations and reports for inactive statuses\. This research shows that digital processes for tracking activities increase healthcare system trust and efficiency\. Future advancements of the system will include cloud computing, and artificial intelligence \(AI\) to provide data tracking and system recommendations\. This system is the starting point for developing an integrated health care system\.
