# Controlled sol\-gel nanoassembly of Au25\(SR\)18 nanoclusters for structural and functional integration

**Autores:** Mahdi Hesari; Marysa Colvin

**Data:** 2026-10-12 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Structural assembly of nanomaterials imparts new and enhanced properties to individual components\. The “Sol\-gel” approach has been a traditional technique to connect diverse types of materials\. Here, 3\-mercaptopropyl \(trimethoxysilane\) \(MPTS\), a dual\-functional ligand, has been employed to assemble one of the popular members of atomically precise nanoclusters–Au 25 − and connect individual nanoclusters via the formation of the Si\(OR\) 4 “sol\-gel” network during the purification process\. Depending on the purification procedure, a particle or film of Au 25 \-SiO 2 was achieved and characterized using different techniques\. The obtained superstructures of nanomaterials were utilized as a catalyst for the nitrophenol hydrogenation reaction, revealing a 2\.5 times faster reaction rate of Au 25 \-SiO 2 \-film relative to the particle\.
