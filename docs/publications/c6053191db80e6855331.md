# HUMAN TRANSLATION AND AI IN MODERN EDUCATION: WHY WE NEED BOTH

**Autores:** Sitora Yarasheva

**Data:** 2026-10-15 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

This thesis examines the intersection of Artificial Intelligence \(AI\) and human expertise in modern higher education&\#x27;s translation processes\. While neural machine translation offers unprecedented speed and cost\-efficiency, it frequently compromises conceptual depth, cultural nuances, and factual accuracy\. This study argues against a binary substitution model, advocating instead for a hybrid paradigm\. By integrating the scalability of AI with the critical evaluation of human translators, global universities can optimize educational content delivery without sacrificing academic rigor\.
