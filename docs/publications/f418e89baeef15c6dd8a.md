# Predicting Indie Game Success: A Diverse Stacking Ensemble Approach for Consumer Demand Forecasting

**Autores:** Tongyuan Zhang; Bin Hu

**Data:** 2026-10-10 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

As consumer expectations escalate across the video game market, accurately understanding player preferences is crucial for maximizing profitability and long\-term retention\. This is particularly challenging for independent \(indie\) developers who lack the resources of AAA studios\. However, forecasting game ownership using the vast, heavy\-tailed data from digital distribution platforms remains a significant hurdle\. To address this gap, we propose a specialized, robust machine learning framework exclusively tailored for the indie market segment\. Our approach utilizes a Diverse Stacking Regressor ensemble—integrating Random Forest, XGBoost, LightGBM, and CatBoost algorithms alongside a Huber meta\-learner—to predict game ownership based on comprehensive SteamSpy data\. Compared to standalone baselines, such as a traditional Random Forest which achieved an $R^2$ score of 0\.69 and a Median Absolute Error \(MedAE\) of nearly 100,000, our stacking approach demonstrates superior accuracy\. It yields an $R^2$ score of 0\.72, lowers the Mean Absolute Percentage Error \(MAPE\) to approximately 20\\%, and reduces the MedAE from approximately 100,000 to under 5,500\. Ultimately, this predictive framework empowers indie developers to systematically interpret large\-scale consumer data, facilitating optimized resource allocation and data\-driven game design\.
