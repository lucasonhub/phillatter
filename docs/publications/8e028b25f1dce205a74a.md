# Fujie, Naoto: Ziran System: A Distributed Architecture for Operational Observation, Re\-Entry, and Reconfiguration

**Autores:** 

**Data de descoberta:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** unknown

## Descrição do feed

\_Zenodo\_ 1\. 2026This paper presents the canonical formulation of the Ziran System: a distributed research architecture for operational observation, history re\-entry, intervention, feedback, and reconfiguration\. Ziran System begins from the premise that scientific inquiry should not permanently fix its own conditions of access\. Sensors, variables, boundaries, scales, temporal windows, coupling structures, external memory, inferential interfaces, resources, and intervention pathways can themselves become objects of comparison and revision\. The architecture therefore extends scientific autonomy beyond automated action or model updating toward the revision of observability itself\. Its central structure connects configuration\-indexed observation, retention of non\-redundant differences, Address Domains, history re\-entry, real effects, reality penetration, downstream reachability, feedback, and renewed reconfiguration\. No particular module, observer, human subject, machine system, or representational scheme is treated as an invariant component of the architecture\. The paper also integrates preserved empirical evidence from an accessible Ziran/Xeno Simulator study\. The experiments show configuration\-sensitive effects of sensing and actuation, increasing damage as coordinated revision is delayed, measurable false triggers in self\-triggered reconfiguration, and an unseen regime in which revision itself becomes maladaptive\. These results support specific components of the architecture while also establishing explicit limits on the current empirical claims\. Ziran System is presented neither as a final ontology nor as a universal autonomous\-science algorithm\. It is a revisable architecture for preserving, reintroducing, and experimentally testing consequential differences across changing configurations of inquiry\. \(direct link \)
