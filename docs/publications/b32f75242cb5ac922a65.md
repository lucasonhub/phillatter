# Fujie, Naoto: Before the Concept: Operational Shape, Operational Modal Intervals, Portable Relation\-Generation, Inheritance, Reality Penetration, and the Conditions Under Which Concept\-Equivalent Operation Emerges

## Descrição do feed

\_Zenodo\_ 1\. 2026This paper asks what must occur before an operation can count as conceptual\. Rather than beginning with concepts as already constituted units of thought, representation, or language, it analyzes the operational conditions under which concept\-equivalent functions emerge\. The paper distinguishes operational shape from conceptual classification and introduces operational modal intervals: configuration\-dependent ranges within which relations of possibility, necessity, incompatibility, exclusion, and continuation become practically consequential before they are explicitly represented as modal concepts\. It further examines portable relation\-generation, the capacity of a relation\-producing operation to remain effective across changes of medium, scale, address, or implementation, and inheritance, the preservation or transformation of such operations across successive configurations\. Reality penetration names the process by which these locally generated relations alter subsequent physical, informational, institutional, or inferential possibilities rather than remaining merely internal representations\. On this basis, the paper proposes criteria for concept\-equivalent operation without presupposing language, explicit judgment, human cognition, or a prior conceptual scheme\. A concept\-like operation emerges when distinctions can be retained, reapplied, generalized, inherited, and used to constrain subsequent possibilities across sufficiently varied configurations\. The broader claim is that conceptuality should be treated as an achieved operational interval rather than as the universal starting condition of thought, inference, or world\-directed discrimination\. \(direct link \)
