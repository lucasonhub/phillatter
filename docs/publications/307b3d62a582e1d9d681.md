# Does the Higgs Have a Family? A Search for Charged Scalar Production via Vector Boson Fusion With the ATLAS Experiment

**Autores:** T\. C\. Gosart

**Data:** 2026-10-15 · **Tipo:** dissertation

## Resumo

This dissertation presents a combined search for the production of singly\-charged \($H^\{\\pm\}$\) and doubly\-charged scalars \($H^\{\\pm\\pm\}$\) with the ATLAS experiment at the Large Hadron Collider \(LHC\)\. The charged scalars are produced via vector\-boson fusion processes and decay into vector bosons, producing a final state with two jets and two vector\-bosons \($VVjj$\)\. The search is performed with 140 fb$^\{\-1\}$ of data collected at $\\sqrt\{s\} = 13$ TeV and 164 fb$^\{\-1\}$ of data collected at $\\sqrt\{s\} = 13\.6$ TeV with the ATLAS detector, corresponding to the 2015 \- 2018 and 2022 \- 2024 data taking periods at the LHC, respectively\. Results of this combined search are interpreted in the context of the Georgi\-Machacek \(GM\) model\. Three search strategies are presented\. The first reproduces cut\-based strategies from two prior ATLAS results, each of which observed small excesses of data\. The second attempts to improve upon the selection criteria used in the published $H^\{\\pm\\pm\}$ search\. The third, and most sensitive, strategy utilizes machine\-learning techniques, specifically gradient boosted decision trees \(BDTs\)\. Individual BDTs are trained for each search channel, and the models are further divided within each channel to provide sensitivity for specific ranges of masses of the charged scalars\. A total of 18 models are trained across the two channels\. For each strategy, limits are set on the primary GM model parameter and on the production cross\-section of each charged scalar\.
