# Design and evaluation of a 55\-kHz clamping\-type ultrasonic scalpel utilizing Mn:PIN\-PMN\-PT single crystals

**Autores:** 石文杰; Shiyang Li; Qian Xue

**Data:** 2026-10-14 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Abstract Piezoelectric materials are crucial for the performance of the ultrasonic scalpels\. However, the relatively low piezoelectric properties of PZT ceramics limited electromechanical coupling coefficient and power density, thereby restricting the overall performance of these devices\. To address these limitations, a novel 55\-kHz ultrasonic scalpel design utilizing Mn:PIN\-PMN\-PT single crystals was proposed and fabricated in this study\. Experimental results demonstrate that the effective electromechanical coupling factor of the Mn:PIN\-PMN\-PT scalpel reaches 0\.155, which is more than double that of conventional PZT systems\. Under the same excitation voltages, the single\-crystal scalpel achieves a higher no\-load distal amplitude than the conventional PZT scalpel\. Moreover, under load conditions, the power density of the single\-crystal scalpel is 34\.66 times that of the PZT counterpart\. Finally, pork tissue cutting experiments confirm that the scalpel exhibits superior cutting performance\. In summary, the present study verified the effectiveness of Mn:PIN\-PMN\-PT single crystals in 55\-kHz clamping\-type ultrasonic scalpels\. It can provide a new perspective for the improvement of the ultrasonic scalpels\.
