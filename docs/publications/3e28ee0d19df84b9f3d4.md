In education, simulation platforms, adaptive learning systems, and virtual patient support competency development and personalized instruction\. In research, AI enhances large\-scale data analysis, predictive modeling, and evidence synthesis\. However, implementation raises concerns regarding data privacy, algorithmic bias, automation complacency, and inequitable access to technology\.ConclusionsAI is reshaping allied health education, research, and clinical practice\. Responsible integration requires discipline\-specific validation, ethical governance, and interprofessional collaboration to ensure AI strengthens\- rather than replaces\-professional expertise and patient\-centered care\.
