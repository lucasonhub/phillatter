# Wolof\-Adapted Industry\-Ready Open\-Weight Language Models

**Autores:** Gwénolé Lecorvé; Delphine Charlet; Pierre Adam; Floriane Behanzin; Lionel Delphin\-Poulat; Johannes Heinecke; Géraldine Damnati; Pelin Kaytmaz; Nourredine Khaous; Charbel Kindji; Meriam Maadi; Abdoulaye Mbaye; Claire Perroux; Ismaël Rousseau; Papa Séga Wadé; Gilles Xiberras; Olivier Zhang; Malek Zitouni

**Data:** 2026-10-14 · **Tipo:** preprint

## Resumo

Wolof is spoken by 23M people, mostly in Senegal, but remains weakly supported by open\-weight language models, while API models raise cost, governance, and sovereignty constraints for local deployments\. In this paper, we present 0\.6B\-14B open\-weight models adapted to Wolof through instruction tuning, preference alignment, and model merging\. After presenting the pipeline and data, extensive experiments on 63\~public and in\-house tasks show good performance on Wolof understanding and generation while remaining effective on other languages and cross\-lingual skills, with the 8B and 14B models being particularly competitive with substantially larger commercial systems\. The models are currently being evaluated in internal customer\-care pilots and are publicly available at https://huggingface\.co/collections/Orange/wolof\-lm \.
