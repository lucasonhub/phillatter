# A Comprehensive Use Case of Decision\-Theoretic AI: Fraud Review Escalation under Capacity Constraints

**Autores:** Syed Muntasir Mamun

**Data:** 2026-10-11 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

This paper develops a comprehensive, self\-contained use case that operationalises the decision\-theoretic framework of Noguer i Alonso \(2026\) as reviewed and summarised in the preceding thread\. Building on the anatomical review of output contracts, acquisition contracts, capacity gates, and the discrete/continuous routing\-geometry dichotomy, and on the subsequent extraction of key lessons and the illustrative fraud\-review example, the paper specifies a complete bank fraud\-escalation system\. It defines the action set and reward matrix, identifies the minimal output contract, constructs the net acquisition advantage, implements the capacity\-constrained optimal gate, and contrasts boundary\-focused versus strongest\-signal routing under discrete versus continuous exposure decisions\. A numerical illustration demonstrates the calculations\. The use case translates the theoretical results into implementable design principles while remaining faithful to the scope and non\-claims of the original framework\.
