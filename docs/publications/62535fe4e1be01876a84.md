# CALM\-MER: Calibrated Alignment and Latent Modality\-conflict Modelling for Multimodal Emotion Recognition with a Released Audio\-visual Congruence Benchmark

**Autores:** Mamta Goyal; Vivek Srivastava

**Data:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Audio\-visual emotion recognition systems are increasingly deployed in affective\-computing and clinicalcommunication settings, yet their reliability collapses precisely when the face and the voice disagree, and that failure is rarely quantified against an independent ground truth\.Existing conflict\-aware fusion methods estimate disagreement from their own unimodal probes, which is circular, and report point estimates without calibration, statistical testing or a reproducible artefact\.This paper introduces CALM\-MER, a three\-stage framework that self\-supervisedly aligns frozen contrastive predictive coding speech tokens and ConvNeXt\-V2 facial tokens through an InfoNCE objective, estimates modality conflict with a dual\-source prior fusing probe disagreement with a newly collected human\-rated audio\-visual congruence signal, and routes fusion through a Beta\-distributed uncertainty gate calibrated by temperature scaling\.It releases IEMOCAP\-AVCongruence, an independent congaruence annotation over 5,531 utterances rated by three annotators at an average\-measures ICC\(2,k\) = 0\.75\.Under one immutable evaluation script and five seeds it attains 80\.9% weighted and 81\.4% unweighted accuracy on six\-class IEMOCAP and 67\.2% macro\-F1 on six\-label CMU\-MOSEI, at an expected calibration error of 0\.029 and a Brier score of 0\.071, exceeding eight external audiovisual\-only baselines \-a naive\-concatenation reference, three systems reimplemented from their published descriptions and four adapted to this protocol \-together with an internal fixed\-gate ablation of CALM\-MER, giving nine comparators evaluated under one identical protocol and one scoring script\.Under McNemar&\#x27;s exact test with Holm\-Bonferroni correction over the nine comparators, seven of the eight external baseline comparisons reach a corrected p &lt; 0\.01 and the comparison with the strongest baseline, SADGR\-AV, reaches a corrected p &lt; 0\.05; the internal fixed\-gate ablation also reaches a corrected p &lt; 0\.01\.Conflict supervision is out\-of\-fold and speakerindependent, one mandatory inference configuration never reads a human rating, and the full artefact is released at https://github\.com/mamtagp/Calmmer\_code\.
