# An interpretable machine learning framework for SDG 7 electricity access forecasting and policy scenario analysis in sub\-Saharan Africa

**Autores:** Sena Gemechis File

**Data:** 2026-10-18 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Abstract Universal electricity access by 2030 \(Sustainable Development Goal 7, SDG 7\) remains far off track in Sub\-Saharan Africa \(SSA\), where approximately 565 million people lacked access in 2023\. This study proposes an interpretable framework that integrates forecasting, explainability, and policy scenario analysis into a reproducible workflow, demonstrated on a World Development Indicators panel of 45 SSA countries \(2001–2023; 1035 observations\) with Ethiopia as the case study\. Linear Regression \(LR\), Random Forest, XGBoost, and LightGBM are compared under leave\-one\-country\-out and temporal\-split validation\. With path dependence represented through a lagged access feature, LR achieves the lowest error: a mean absolute error \(MAE\) of 1\.93 percentage points across the 45 held\-out countries \(2\.04 for Ethiopia\) and 1\.33 for Ethiopia in the temporal split\. LR improves on a persistence baseline in 33 of 45 countries \(median MAE improvement 15\.9%\)\. SHapley Additive exPlanations \(SHAP\) show that prior\-year access dominates predictions, as expected under the autoregressive specification and consistent with infrastructure path dependence\. SHAP informs the diagnostic assessment of the predictive structure, while multicollinearity screening, coefficient\-sign verification, and scenario\-sensitivity checks determine the selection of LR as the scenario model\. Under Slow, Business\-as\-Usual, and Accelerated scenarios, Ethiopia is projected to reach 67\.5–69\.9% access by 2030, leaving a gap of 30–32 percentage points\. Within the socioeconomic scenarios represented, macroeconomic improvements alone are unlikely to close this gap; structural interventions such as grid extension, lower connection costs, and off\-grid solar deployment are needed\. The framework is open and potentially transferable, pending validation elsewhere\.
