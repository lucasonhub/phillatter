# Open Science in an Age of Nationalised Scientific Purpose

**Autores:** Frontiers Policy Labs

**Data:** 2026-10-15 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

As AI and other digital technologies become strategic assets, governments are recasting the purpose of science around national competitiveness and security\. Nanditha Mathew and Luc Soete argue that this “nationalisation of scientific purpose”, visible in both the US and Europe, erodes the institutional autonomy, openness and international collaboration on which scientific leadership depends\. The challenge is not to insulate science from national priorities, but to stop the pursuit of strategic advantage from undermining the openness that makes scientific progress possible\.
