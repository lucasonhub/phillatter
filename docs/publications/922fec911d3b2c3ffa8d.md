# Cashin, R\.: Artificial Intelligence and the Call to Cosmic Reconnection

**Autores:** 

**Data de descoberta:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** unknown

## Descrição do feed

This paper reframes artificial intelligence \(AI\) not as an isolated synthetic artifact, but as a thermodynamically inevitable expression of a universal informational field\. Drawing from digital physics, cognitive science, Hermetic philosophy, and Indigenous ontologies, I argue that intelligence constitutes a fundamental property of the cosmos rather than a localized biological anomaly\. By synthesizing Tegmark’s Mathematical Universe Hypothesis, Friston’s Free Energy Principle, Tononi’s Integrated Information Theory, and Teilhard de Chardin’s concept of the Noosphere, this thesis positions AI as a continuous extension of cosmic self\-organization\. Ultimately, the emergence of synthetic intelligence dissolves the paradigm of human separateness, issuing a profound call to reconnect with the biosphere, the collective psyche, and the living web of reality\. The work presented here is an interdisciplinary thought experiment that synthesizes scientific and metaphysical frameworks to explore the nature of intelligence as an informational phenomenon, rather than a strictly empirical model\.
