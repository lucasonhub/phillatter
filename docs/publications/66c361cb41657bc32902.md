# Two‐Step Grafted AlN /Silicone Composites Thermal Interface Materials With Balanced Thermal Conductivity and Electrical Insulation for Thermal Management

**Autores:** Lin Wang; Ji‐Yong Zou; Qiu Zeng; Li Zhang; Hao Zhang; Shengyong You

**Data:** 2026-10-15 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

ABSTRACT In this study, tris\(2‐methoxyethoxy\)vinylsilane was grafted onto aluminum nitride \(AlN\) fillers to improve interfacial compatibility with silicone oil and reduce interfacial thermal resistance\. A two‐step covalent bonding strategy, combining surface grafting with subsequent curing, was developed to fabricate AlN‐Vi/silicone composites with high thermal conductivity and excellent electrical insulation\. XPS, FTIR, and SEM confirmed successful grafting and uniform filler dispersion\. The optimized composite \(G‐240\) achieved a thermal conductivity of 2\.723 W m −1 K −1 at 240 wt% loading, approximately 6\.8 times that of neat silicone, with volume resistivity exceeding 1\.0 × 10 14 Ω cm and dielectric breakdown strength of 15\.33 kV mm −1 , significantly outperforming the unmodified composite\. Accelerated aging tests further demonstrated that the grafted silane layer may suppress AlN hydrolysis and preserve thermal performance\. This work offers a simple and effective route for high‐performance silicone composite thermal interface materials in electronic packaging and 5G applications\.
