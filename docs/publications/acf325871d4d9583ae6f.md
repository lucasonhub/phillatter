# Research on Performance Evaluation of Innovation and Entrepreneurship Ecosystem in Universities Under the Background of Internet Education

**Autores:** Qinjue Kang; Fenglin Yan

**Data:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Some universities in the world have successfully implemented innovation and entrepreneurship education, but there is still a lack of exploration on the innovation and entrepreneurship education system in higher education institutions\. This article uses the factor grouping method, theoretical analysis method, and Delphi method to construct a comprehensive evaluation index system\. Then, using the analytic hierarchy process, it calculates the weight coefficients of each indicator in the comprehensive evaluation index system\. By analyzing the advantages, heterogeneity, and rational allocation ratio of original ecological resources in the ecosystem, the authors gradually promote the application of innovation and the entrepreneurship education ecosystem\. This study indicates that the ecological environment for college students&\#x27; innovation and entrepreneurship urgently needs to establish a model for college students&\#x27; innovation and entrepreneurship and provide effective guidance for college students&\#x27; innovation and entrepreneurship in multiple aspects\.
