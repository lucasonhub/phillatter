# Regenerable, closed\-loop, nonenzymatic continuous glucose monitoring enabled by in situ electrochemical interfacial alkalinization

**Autores:** Qiang Zeng; Liangjun Xia; Junyao Gong; Jieyao Qin; Sijie Zhou; FU Zhuan; Jiajing Zhang; Ke Lin; Chunhua Zhang; Wei Zhang; Weilin Xu

**Data:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Continuous glucose monitoring in interstitial fluid \(ISF\) is important for diabetes management, but enzyme\-based sensors can lose activity, while many nonenzymatic alternatives show optimal activity in alkaline media\. Here we present a regenerable wrist\-worn hollow microneedle platform for closed\-loop nonenzymatic ISF glucose monitoring\. Negative pressure extracts ISF into a detachable sensor cartridge that separates the tissue interface from the electrochemical sensing chamber and enables rinsing and reuse\. A brief cathodic step triggers the hydrogen evolution reaction \(HER\) at an Au–Pt/Ni foam electrode, electrochemically generating a local alkaline interface for the subsequent glucose oxidation reaction \(GOR\)\. Integrated pH feedback regulates the HER–GOR sequence\. In vitro and in vivo studies demonstrate rapid ISF extraction and quasi\-continuous glucose monitoring in rats, with preliminary feasibility in one human participant\. This work illustrates local microenvironment regulation as a strategy for enabling alkaline\-dependent glucose electrocatalysis in near\-neutral ISF\. Continuous glucose monitoring is central to diabetes management, yet conventional testing provides only intermittent snapshots and may miss clinically important glycaemic fluctuations\. Here, the authors present a wrist\-worn hollow microneedle platform that combines active interstitial fluid extraction with nonenzymatic electrochemical glucose sensing in a wearable format\. Its detachable microneedle cartridge enables independent cleaning or replacement, supporting quasi\-continuous monitoring while improving practicality and long\-term usability for wearable glucose tracking\.
