# Histopathological Alterations and DNA Content Changes in Brain, Liver, and Kidney Tissues Induced by Tomtom Candy\-Lacasera Drink Mixture in Wistar Rats

**Autores:** Friday  Titus Emmanuel; Iliyasu Omoyine Musa; Ameh Omede; Oladele Joshua Alabi; Shadrack Egbunu Akor; Mathew Abubakar Akpa; Abubakar Al\-Hassan; Nicholas Aruwa Udeh; Alexander Lawrence

**Data:** 2026-10-10 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

This study investigated the potential histological effects of a mixture of Tomtom candy and Lacasera drink \(LTT\) and its individual components on brain, liver, and kidney tissues of Wistar rats\. Four groups of rats \(n=4 per group\) were used: a control group \(Group A\) received distilled water; Group B received LTT; Group C received Lacasera drink \(LC\); and Group D received Tomtom candy in water \(TTW\)\. The treatments were administered orally for 28 days\. Results revealed that all treatment groups showed histological alterations in the brain, liver, and kidney\. The most pronounced effects were observed in the TTW group \(Group D\), which showed moderate axon clumping and dendrite loss in the brain, suggesting significant neuronal impairment\. In the liver, all treatment groups exhibited some degree of hepatic necrosis \(HN\)\. The kidney tissue of the TTW group \(Group D\) showed tubular adhesion \(TA\) and slight tubular necrosis \(TN\), indicating the most severe renal damage among the groups\. The LTT mixture \(Group B\) also induced notable effects, including moderate Kupffer cell hyperplasia \(KH\) in the liver and slight glomerular and tubular necrosis in the kidneys\. Feulgen staining of brain tissue showed a paradoxical increase in DNA content in the TTW group, despite the structural abnormalities\. This may indicate a cellular stress response, such as DNA repair mechanisms or the proliferation of support cells, to counter the toxic insult\. The findings suggest that both the LTT mixture and its individual components can induce histological alterations in the vital organs of Wistar rats\. The TTW mixture appeared to cause more pronounced effects in the brain and kidneys than the LTT or LC drink alone\. Further studies with larger sample sizes and long\-term exposure are needed to fully elucidate the underlying mechanisms and potential long\-term consequences of these effects\.
