# Lightweight Hybrid CNN\-Transformer Network for Robust Maize Leaf Disease Detection and Edge Deployment

**Autores:** Awwab Mohammad; Punith kumar; Bhavani K; Sailee N\. Panchbhai; Sagarkumar S\. Badhiye; Manoj Kumar S B; Dharamvir; Sumit Kumar Sar; Rani Chhagan Dalvi; Yogesh Harishchandra Bhosale

**Data:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Early and accurate detection of maize leaf diseases is essential for improving crop productivity and supporting precision agriculture\.However, deep learning models trained on controlled leaf\-image datasets often experience performance degradation under real\-world agricultural conditions because of illumination variation, background clutter, occlusion, leaf orientation, and imaging noise\.To address these challenges, this study develops a lightweight deep learning framework for robust maize leaf disease classification and edge\-oriented deployment\.The framework employs MobileNetV3\-Small for efficient extraction of disease\-discriminative visual features and investigates lightweight feature\-learning strategies for improving classification under challenging field conditions\.Experiments were conducted using PlantVillage\-Maize and Field\-Maize datasets comprising 6,188 images from four categories: Maize Leaf Blight \(MLB\), Common Rust, Healthy, and Leaf Spot\.The image\-only MobileNetV3\-Small model achieved 97\.8% accuracy and a 97\.4% F1\-score on the controlled PlantVillage\-Maize dataset, while its performance decreased to 86\.1% accuracy and an 83\.9% F1\-score on Field\-Maize, highlighting the domain gap between controlled and real\-field imagery\.Robustness was further evaluated under Gaussian noise, illumination variation, and partial occlusion, together with computational\-efficiency and edge\-deployment analyses\.The findings demonstrate the importance of lightweight and robust visual feature learning for practical maize disease diagnosis under real\-world agricultural imaging conditions\.
