# Anti\-Narcotics Law Enforcement and Border Management in Northeast India: A Delphi Study on Socio\-Economic Impacts and Policy Priorities

**Autores:** Rohan Pramod Botrea; Anil S\. Maheshwari

**Data:** 2026-10-17 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Purpose \- The Northeast border regions of India encounter several complex challenges related to border vulnerability, networks, narco\-terrorism, enforcement, governance arising from insurgent activities, transnational crimes, porous borders, and ever\-evolving geopolitical dynamics\. This study aims to systematically identify, prioritize, and analyze key border vulnerability indicators and develop a consensusbased knowledge about their socio\-economic impacts and policy priorities in border regions of Northeast India\. Methodology – The study employed the Delphi research design to draw expert\-consensus on key border and anti\-narcotic law enforcement and governance issues\. Professionals and subject\-matter experts \(total n=18\) were drawn from three categories \(operational enforcement; policy, legal and strategic; and community or socio\-economic impact experts\), participating in three Delphi rounds \(screening, agreement, consensus\-building\) for structured consultation\. Mixed\-methods approach was used to refine expert insights, opinion, and establish consensus\. Findings – Study outcomes revealed real\-world experiences and insights on NE India border region vulnerabilities as multi\-dimensional constructs\. Experts identified and reached final consensus on 26 unique indicators out of total 69, further categorizing into five key dimensions \(border vulnerability, organized networks, narco\-economy, enforcement and governance, and socio\-economic impact\)\. The study also finds governance deficits, limited institutional inter\-agency coordination, criminal\-insurgent convergence, inadequate infrastructure, and socio\-economic marginalization as critical issues surrounding border vulnerabilities\. There is also an urgency for an integrated border management approach\. Implications – Theoretical implications include contribution to all four theories addressed in the study, demonstrating inter\-connectedness and proposing a holistic understanding by integrating all the five key dimensions\. Practical implications are provided based on evidence\-based insights for security agencies, policy\-makers, and border\-governance institutions\. Originality/value – The Delphi panel experts’ insights are original and valuable based on their long\-term real\-world experiences in such border regions\. Further, a systematic approach through Delphi iterative rounds for refinement and consensus\-building are highly scientific and valuable, contributing to the richness of study outcomes thus contributing to existing body of knowledge\. The study proposed a B\-O\-NE\-S conceptual framework which is novel and supported with evidence\-based study\. However, this needs further empirical testing using large\-scale quantitative data\.
