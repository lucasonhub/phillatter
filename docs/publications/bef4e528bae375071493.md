# Achieving Operational Excellence Through Strategic Asset Maintenance: A Business Perspective in China

**Autores:** Li Xiao Qing; Lorenzo C\. Lorenzo

**Data:** 2026-10-12 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

This study assessed the relationship between Strategic Asset Maintenance and Operational Excellence among 188 employee\-respondents\. Specifically, it examined the respondents’ demographic profile, their assessment of Strategic Asset Maintenance and Operational Excellence, the significant differences in their assessments when grouped according to age, sex, and position, and the relationship between the two major constructs\. The study used a quantitative descriptive\-correlational design with a structured survey questionnaire as the primary data\-gathering instrument\. Data were analyzed using frequency and percentage distribution, weighted mean, standard deviation, t\-test, one\-way analysis of variance, and Pearson correlation\. Findings showed that most respondents were aged 25\-34, male, and employed as technicians, production staff, or engineers\. Strategic Asset Maintenance was assessed as practiced, with an overall mean of 2\.67, while Operational Excellence was also assessed as practiced, with an overall mean of 2\.74\. Among the Strategic Asset Maintenance dimensions, Governance and Strategic Alignment and Transformation Readiness obtained the highest ratings, while Digital Capability and Data Quality had the lowest rating\. For Operational Excellence, People Capabilities and Culture received the highest assessment, while Measurement and Governance obtained the lowest\. The results further revealed no significant differences in the assessments of Strategic Asset Maintenance and Operational Excellence when respondents were grouped according to age, sex, and position\. However, a significant positive relationship was found between Strategic Asset Maintenance and Operational Excellence, with an overall computed r of 0\.611 and a significance value of less than 0\.001\. These findings suggest that stronger asset maintenance practices are meaningfully associated with stronger operational performance\. Based on the results, an Integrated Strategic Asset Maintenance Plan was proposed to strengthen digital capability, data quality, risk\-based planning, resource utilization, and operational excellence integration\.
