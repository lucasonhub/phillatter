# Predictive Machine Learning Models for Process Parameters in Additively Manufactured CoCrMo Alloys for Dental Applications

**Autores:** Carlos Peaceful Kakuwa; Martin Ruthandi Maina; Joshua Ngoret; Tshenolo Phinah Leso

**Data:** 2026-10-14 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Dental disease prevalence has increased the demand for effective dental implants\. Laser Engineered Net Shaping \(LENS\) can produce complex geometries through controlled process parameters\. Machine learning \(ML\), on the other hand, enables data driven parametric prediction in Additive Manufacturing \(AM\), though reliability depends on data quality\. This study explores a framework for integration of AM and Latin Hypercube Sampling \(LHS\) based data synthesis with ML trained on 108 synthetic samples to enhance parametric prediction and surface quality control\. Model validation presented robust performance, with ANN achieving R2 values of 0\.981 for surface roughness \(governing osseointegration\), 0\.955 for microhardness \(governing wear resistance\), and 0\.973 for porosity \(governing structural integrity\), while the best RF prediction performance achieved an R2 of 0\.967 for surface roughness, with both models yielding &lt;1\.5% prediction error for microhardness\. These findings highlight the potential applicability of synthetically generated data to improve scalability and reproducibility of data for ML training in ML based multi\-objective optimization of LENS produced CoCrMo alloys for dental applications\.
