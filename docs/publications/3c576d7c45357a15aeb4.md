## Resumo

The use of artificial intelligence in the investigation and detection of crime, as well as the legal assessment of the evidentiary value of data obtained through its application, is of significant importance\. The paper examines the specific features of the formation, analysis, and use of digital traces processed through intelligent systems in criminal proceedings\.
