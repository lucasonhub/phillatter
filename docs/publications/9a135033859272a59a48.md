# Measuring destination image dissonance: A compositional data analysis of cross\-media representations in Sanya’s Tourism

**Autores:** Xueli Li; Jianlan Ding; Yina Zhang; Tao Hu

**Data:** 2026-10-15 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

This study examines Sanya&\#x27;s tourism image disparities across cognitive, spatial, and affective dimensions in Press Generated Content \(PGC\), Tourist Generated Content \(TGC\), and Official Generated Content \(OGC\) \(2013\-2022\), comparing pre\-pandemic \(2013\-2019\) and COVID\-19 \(2020\-2022\) periods\. Results show: cognitive consistency in &quot;tourism/hotels&quot; but TGC focusing on &quot;coastal \+ food&quot; vs\. PGC/OGC emphasizing policy elements; spatial misalignment between policy\-driven spaces and tourists&\#x27; preferred spaces, revealing a systematic dissonance between institutional promotion and on\-the\-ground practices; affective dominance of &quot;enjoyment/romance&quot; but TGC expressing unmet needs and OGC suppressing negatives\. Systematic cognitive divergence exists between official and tourist narratives\. The Aitchison distance measuring this divergence jumps from 19\.574 in the pre\-pandemic phase \(2013\-2019\) to 40\.648 during the pandemic\-impacted phase \(2020\-2022\), demonstrating that structural gaps between the two narrative systems become more pronounced amid the public health crisis\. Targeted strategies are accordingly proposed to narrow such narrative discrepancies\.
