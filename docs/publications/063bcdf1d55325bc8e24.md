# INSTRUCTIONAL DESIGN OF A WORKPLACE\-ORIENTED GENERAL AIGC COURSE IN THE ERA OF DIGITAL INTELLIGENCE

**Autores:** Yishen Liao; Lei Shu; 赵爱华; Yun Zhang; Hejie Yu

**Data:** 2026-10-17 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

In the era of digital intelligence, artificial intelligence generated content \(AIGC\) has become deeply integrated into workplace workflows\. However, a growing gap has emerged between corporate demand for AIGC\-driven productivity and the lag in talent development\. Current introductory AIGC courses suffer from fragmented content, superficial assessment methods, and limited adaptability\. To address these challenges, this study proposes a holistic instructional design framework based on the &quot;structural reconstruction, capability building, and closed\-loop evaluation&quot; model, featuring an 8\-hour modular curriculum: prompt engineering \(2 hours\) to establish foundational concepts for cross\-domain transfer, core tool mastery \(4 hours\) covering four mainstream AI platforms \(ERNIE Bot, DeepSeek, WPS AI, and Doubao\), and role\-specific applications \(2 hours\) offering tailored practical exercises across five key professions \(finance, education, software development, copywriting, and design\)\. The framework employs a hybrid assessment model combining self\-evaluations, instructor feedback, and AI\-driven evaluations to target real\-world problem\-solving skills and adaptability to new tools, while a data\-driven feedback loop enables continuous optimization of both course content and personalized learning interventions\. Ultimately, this study provides a scalable, iterative system framework for empowering today&\#x27;s AI\-driven workforce\.
