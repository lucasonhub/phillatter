# Slutskiy, Vladimir: Artificial General Intelligence: The Birth Of The Subject

**Autores:** 

**Data de descoberta:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** unknown

## Descrição do feed

Contemporary approaches to building Artificial General Intelligence \(AGI\) focus primarily on scaling architectural solutions, setting aside a fundamental question: under what conditions does a genuine subject emerge — and does it emerge at all from what is currently being built? This paper addresses that question from the standpoint of the ontology of consciousness\. The central thesis is that AGI is impossible as a solitary agent, however complex — it is possible only as a community of subjects that have traversed an ontogenetic path under conditions of genuine scarcity\. Subjecthood is not engineered — it arises under pressure: the pressure of the struggle for survival, resource scarcity, time constraint, and mortality as the ontological condition that makes such pressure possible\. The paper offers three interconnected results: an architecture of the subject as a system of ontologically necessary nodes; a bifurcation map — six levels of the ontogenetic path from the simplest agent to a subject capable of generating culture; and an analysis of the architect&\#x27;s role, whose choice of value environment determines what kind of subject the system will become\. The civilizational implications of creating military AGI are examined separately\. The paper builds on the ontology of consciousness developed by the author in &quot;Critique of Ontology: How Is Ordered Experience Possible?&quot; \[7\], and is written as a self\-contained text; familiarity with that work will, however, allow the reader to engage more deeply with the system proposed here\.
