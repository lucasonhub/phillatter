# Wisdom as Infrastructure: A New Architecture for Longevity and Regeneration

**Autores:** Desiree C\. T\. Cox

**Data:** 2026-10-15 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Longevity science and regenerative medicine have moved the limits of translational medicine\. The sciences of aging and age\-related diseases, cell and gene therapies, biomaterials, digital phenotyping, and artificial intelligence are expanding what can be measured and modified across the life course\. Yet the field’s decisive bottleneck may no longer be discovery alone\. Technical capacity is advancing faster than the institutional capacity to decide what should count as progress, how uncertainty should be governed, and how biological gains become durable and equitably distributed human benefit\. This perspective advances the proposition that wisdom is infrastructure\. Wisdom is defined here not as intuition or individual authority, but as the organized capacity to integrate heterogeneous evidence, deliberate explicitly about values, anticipate consequences across time, and implement innovation with the people and systems it will affect\. The perspective situates this proposition within a broader architecture of human flourishing and proposes a translational human flourishing framework comprising six integrative and interdependent domains: biological integrity and plasticity; functional capacity and resilience; agency, purpose, and meaning; social connection and participation; enabling environments; and ethical governance and equity\. The framework does not displace mechanistic, disease\-specific, or regulatory endpoints\. It connects them to the full causal pathway through which an intervention becomes meaningful in a human life\. A research agenda is proposed around multidomain outcomes, longitudinal trajectories, implementation and equity, governance indicators, learning health systems, and place\-based translational ecosystems\. The central claim is deliberately ambitious but empirically bounded: longevity and regenerative medicine will fulfill their promise only when the infrastructures that accelerate innovation are matched by infrastructures that give innovation direction, legitimacy, memory, and human purpose\.
