# Diagnosing region\-specific flood influencing factors in a volcanic island using explainable machine learning

**Autores:** Hyeon‐Tae Moon; Gilho Kim; Kyungtak Kim

**Data:** 2026-10-15 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Abstract This study applies a grid\-based, explainable machine learning framework to identify dominant flood\-influencing factors under hydrologically heterogeneous conditions, using Jeju Island as a representative volcanic environment\. Historical flood trace maps were integrated with multi\-layer spatial datasets to classify flooded and non\-flooded grid cells at a 100 m resolution using five tree\-based algorithms\. The models achieved recall and F1\-scores of up to 0\.86 and 0\.78, respectively\. Feature\-importance and explainability analyses demonstrate that the dominance of flood\-influencing factors varies across spatial contexts, with hydrometeorological conditions acting as the primary drivers and spatial exposure and proximity\-based factors contributing in a region\-specific manner\. The results highlight diagnostic insights into the primary drivers of flooding and suggest that factors which may be underrepresented in conventional rainfall\-centric assessments can exert substantial influences on flood occurrence\.
