# Qin, Han: How Is Subjecthood Possible: Symmetry, Negativity, and Subjecthood

**Autores:** 

**Data de descoberta:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** unknown

## Descrição do feed

This paper provides an optional ontological grounding for the Self\-as\-an\-End framework by asking two questions: What is the physical basis of negativity, and what is subjecthood itself? It first begins from a conditional physical axiom—if ontic randomness exists—and defines negativity as the remainder that persists at the macroscopic scale\. It then describes subjecthood as grounded in negativity, processual rather than static, existing on a spectrum, and recognizable only through acknowledgment\. The paper distinguishes negativity, subjecthood, and consciousness as concepts with different epistemological statuses\. It argues that the discontinuous transition occurs with the emergence of negativity, while later developments form a continuous spectrum; it also treats identity continuity as an act of recognition rather than an independent ontological fact\. Negativity serves as the interface between scientific description and ethical recognition: science may carry inquiry as far as negativity, but the transition from negativity to subjecthood requires recognition\. The paper finally advances a conditional claim concerning symmetry: the symmetry of the universe is a necessary background condition for the emergence of subjecthood\. This ontological proposal is explicitly firewalled from the framework&\#x27;s normative arguments, which do not depend on its truth\. \(direct link \)
