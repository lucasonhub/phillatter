# Audino, Andrea: The Situated Love Object

**Autores:** 

**Data de descoberta:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** unknown

## Descrição do feed

Falling in love is usually explained by the other person’s traits, the subject’s preferences, compatibility, reciprocity and idealization\. This article asks a prior question: how does a real person come to be constituted as a love object for a given subject at a given moment? It develops the Self of the Moment \(SOM\), a thesis from earlier work on the situated first\-person standpoint, into a testable account of romantic evaluation\. The SOM is a set of momentary motivational states \(minimally security, novelty and status\) that modulate the threshold beyond which a person becomes a love object and, more tentatively, the weights given to the dimensions on which they are evaluated\. The love object is neither the real person nor the representation of them, but a structure combining the person’s salient dimensions and the position they occupy in the subject’s motivational hierarchy; the intensity of infatuation is a distinct dimension\. The article specifies the model formally, derives predictions, proposes a within\-person experimental design and a longitudinal one, states falsification conditions, and positions the proposal against multi\-attribute models, the fundamental motives framework and self\-expansion theory\. No prediction has yet been tested; incremental validity over existing constructs remains an open empirical question\.
