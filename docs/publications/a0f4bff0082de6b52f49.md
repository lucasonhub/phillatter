# Integrating Artificial Intelligence in Journalism Education: Curricular Gaps and Educator Perspectives From Bangladesh

**Autores:** Md\. Uzzal Talukder; Catherine A\. Luther

**Data:** 2026-10-12 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Artificial intelligence \(AI\) is transforming journalism and creating challenges for journalism education\. Guided by Diffusion of Innovations theory, this study examines AI integration in journalism education in Bangladesh\. Using a qualitative multi\-method design, the study analyzed 21 curricula from 17 universities and interviewed 18 journalism educators, including 12 faculty members and 6 department heads\. Findings reveal limited formal AI integration despite educators’ recognition of its professional importance, reflecting a gap between individual recognition and institutional adoption shaped by infrastructure, expertise, funding, and policy constraints\. The study highlights the need for context\-sensitive curriculum reform, sustained faculty development, and practical AI training\.
