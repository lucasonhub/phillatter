# Maritime security and risk assessments—2nd edition

**Autores:** Marko Perkovič; Ivica Pavić; Maciej Gucma; Sebastian Feuerstack; Andrej Androjna

**Data:** 2026-10-12 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Maritime safety and security are increasingly shaped by dense traffic, digitised navigation, automation, cyber\-physical dependencies, decarbonisation and geopolitical conflict\. Deliberate attacks on civilian shipping compound the difficulty of protecting crews and assisting disabled vessels carrying hazardous cargoes in contested waters\. Building on the first edition’s focus on AIS\-enabled surveillance, anomaly detection, collision\-risk assessment and maritime domain awareness, this second edition examines the progression from risk identification to risk\-informed decision\-making, validation, and system resilience\. Its twelve contributions represent three research streams: \(i\) regional collision\-risk sensing, route planning, COLREG\-compliant decision\-making and autonomous\-system validation
