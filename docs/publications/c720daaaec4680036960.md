# Agreement is not enough: Making tumor\-infiltrating lymphocyte scoring reproducible and biologically faithful across whole sections and microarrays

**Autores:** Mulavagili Vijayasimha; Mulavagili Srikanth

**Data:** 2026-10-13 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

We read with great interest the CytoJournal article by Sughayer et al\. evaluating interobserver concordance of stromal tumor\-infiltrating lymphocyte \(sTIL\) assessment in triple\-negative breast carcinoma using whole sections \(WSs\) and tissue microarrays \(TMAs\), \[1\] as well as the authors&\#x27; subsequent response clarifying the intent and interpretation of their findings\.\[2\] The study convincingly demonstrates higher interobserver agreement for TMA\-based assessment compared with WS\.As clarified by the authors, the objective was not to replace WS for clinical decision\-making but rather to examine concordance patterns across observers and specimen formats\.\[2\] We fully agree with this position and acknowledge that WS remains superior for capturing tumor immune contexture and spatial heterogeneity\.
