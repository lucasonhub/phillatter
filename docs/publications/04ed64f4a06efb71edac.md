# Machine learning\-assisted ultrasonic assessment of tendon duct grouting in post\-tensioned concrete structures

**Autores:** Ali Mirzazade; Cosmin Popescu; Mats Holmqvist; Björn Täljsten

**Data:** 2026-10-10 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Voids left by incomplete grouting make post\-tensioning strands vulnerable to corrosion, yet the grouting condition is still judged largely by visual interpretation of ultrasonic tomograms, which is operator\-dependent\. This paper presents a machine learning\-assisted framework for grouting assessment based on ultrasonic pulse\-echo data from the MIRA A1040 tomograph\. Seventeen physics\-based features are extracted, nine from raw in\-phase/quadrature waveforms and eight from images reconstructed with the Synthetic Aperture Focusing Technique, each linked to a specific mechanism of shear\-wave interaction with grouted and ungrouted ducts\. The dataset comprises 331 B\-scans: 15 from a laboratory calibration specimen and 316 from the Gisund Bridge, an in\-service post\-tensioned bridge affected by alkali–silica reaction, labelled from endoscopy\-confirmed grouting conditions\. Over 100 repeated stratified train–test splits, six classifiers reached balanced accuracies of 0\.66–0\.70 against a 52\.3% majority\-class baseline; XGBoost, selected for the most stable generalisation, achieved a balanced accuracy of 0\.70 and a Matthews correlation coefficient of 0\.40\. Grouped leave\-one\-section\-out testing showed weaker cross\-section generalisation, and the model agreed with five of six core\-drilled verification openings\. Physics\-based features therefore carry transparent discriminative information, and the framework is best used as decision support for inspectors\.
