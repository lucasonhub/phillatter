# Harmonization of the Legal Framework for Hajj and Umrah Administration under Law Number 8 of 2019 and Law Number 14 of 2025

**Autores:** Eka Farah Khoirun Nisa’; Suhadi; Fauzan Adim

**Data:** 2026-10-18 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

This doctrinal article compares Indonesia’s Law Number 8 of 2019 on Hajj and Umrah Administration with its third amendment, Law Number 14 of 2025\. Through statutory and conceptual interpretation, it identifies amendments governing ministerial responsibility, allocation of Hajj quotas, adjustment of pilgrimage costs, supervision of special Hajj and Umrah operators, and the Ministry Information System\. The amendment does not repeal the 2019 law in its entirety: provisions not amended remain in force\. The article distinguishes statutory duties supported by specific provisions from proposed indicators concerning service standards, archiving, integrated digital governance, and pilgrim complaints\. It argues for article\-based implementation guidance, explicit coordination with the Hajj Financial Management Agency, traceable oversight decisions, and stronger accessible remedies for vulnerable pilgrims\. Its contribution lies in a verifiable mapping of institutional responsibilities and legal consequences rather than an unsupported claim of wholesale replacement\.
