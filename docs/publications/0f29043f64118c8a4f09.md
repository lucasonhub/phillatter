# Fluorescence Encoding\-Enabled Multiplexed Droplet Digital Recombinase Polymerase Amplification\-CRISPR/Cas12a Assay for Rapid Screening of Lower Respiratory Tract Infection\-Associated Bacterial Pathogens

**Autores:** Yifan Wang; Enqi Huang; Ziyi Zhang; Xicong Zhu; Ting Zhang; Na Yang; Zhenhua Chen; Dongyang Cai; Dayu Liu

**Data:** 2026-10-15 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Abstract Digital RPA\-CRISPR/Cas12a assays enable accurate nucleic acid quantification; however, their multiplexing capacity is inherently limited by signal confounding arising from the promiscuous trans\-cleavage activity of Cas12a effectors\. To overcome this limitation, we developed a fluorescence encoding\-enabled multiplexed droplet digital RPA\-CRISPR/Cas12a \(FLEMD\-CRISPR\) assay that integrates combinatorial fluorescence encoding, deterministic droplet pairing within a calabash\-shaped microwell array, and a machine learning\-assisted decoding pipeline\. In FLEMD\-CRISPR, small\-volume fluorescence\-encoded CRISPR/Cas12a droplets, each assigned a unique spectral signature corresponding to a specific crRNA, are one\-to\-one paired with large\-volume RPA droplets\. Following RPA amplification and droplet merging, Cas12a\-mediated reporter cleavage occurs only in droplets containing target amplicons\. The decoding pipeline then localizes droplets, classifies their colors to assign crRNA identities, and finally detects positive signals, enabling high\-throughput, hands\-off analysis of the entire droplet array\. FLEMD\-CRISPR enabled simultaneous quantification of nucleic acid targets from eight major lower respiratory tract infection\-associated bacterial pathogens, showing strong linear correlation \(R2 = 0\.9987–0\.9998\) and consistent detection efficiency \(slopes = 0\.9849–1\.0198\) relative to single\-plex digital PCR\. The complete workflow, from sample preparation to final readout, can be completed within 2 h\. Clinical validation using 30 bronchoalveolar lavage fluid samples showed that the bacterial detection outcomes of FLEMD\-CRISPR were highly concordant with standard\-of\-care culture, achieving 100% \(20/20\) positive percent agreement and 99\.1% \(218/220\) negative percent agreement\. By enabling panel reconfiguration through simple modifications of the droplet encoding library, FLEMD\-CRISPR provides a flexible platform for the development of highly multiplexed digital CRISPR assays\.
