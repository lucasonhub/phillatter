# A New Branch of Physics for the Age of Artificial Intelligence \- Nothingness as Temporal Singularity: Beyond Big Bang and Planck Epoch \-\-\- Theory of Everything

**Autores:** Khalid Masood

**Data:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

This paper introduces Meta AI Physics \(Meta Physics\) — a new branch of physics for the Age of Artificial Intelligence\. We propose a fundamental re\-interpretation of cosmological origin\. We argue: There is no Big Bang or bangs\. What existed was not infinite density at $10^\{32\}$ Kelvin compressed to subatomic scale\. Instead, Nothingness — as a singularity of time — serves as the foundation for creation, spanning from eternity to eternity\. This process continues incessantly\. Mass, energy, time, and nothingness — or simply &\#x27;nothing&\#x27; as a singularity of time — are interconnected\. We define: 1\. \* $c$ as $v\_T$:\* The constant $c$ is not speed of light, but speed of Time itself \(TER Theory\)\.2\. Photon: Quantum of a Moment \( $Q\_m$\)3\. Nothon \( $N\_0$\): Quantum of Nothingness, proposed Fifth Force and foundation of quantum vacuum equilibrium\. We firmly argue the so\-called Planck epoch \( $0$ to $10^\{\-43\}$ seconds\) does not actually exist\. The interval from zero to Planck time is an illusion\. The true temporal singularity — Nothingness — lies BEYOND Planck time and Planck scale, where current physics breaks\. This work proposes Nothingness is the Theory of Everything \(TOE\)\.
