# Machine learning assisted prediction and optimisation of quality for laser directed energy deposition

**Autores:** Ao Liu; Xingyu Jiang; Shuhao Wang; Dan Liu; Guozhe Yang; Shun Liu; Chee Kai Chua

**Data:** 2026-10-09 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Laser\-directed energy deposition \(L\-DED\) using a fibre laser involves complex parameter\-quality relationships, and inappropriate parameter selection may cause porosity and unstable geometry\. This study proposes an inverse process optimisation framework integrating hyperparameter\-optimised LSBoost with the Fast Large\-scale Multi\-objective Evolutionary Algorithm \(FLEA\)\. Laser power, scanning speed, powder feeding rate, and measured melt pool temperature are used as input features, while porosity, dilution rate, and aspect ratio are selected as output responses and optimisation objectives\. Five heuristic hyperparameter optimisation mechanisms and six multi\-objective optimisation algorithms are compared\. The optimised Hiking Optimisation Algorithm\-LSBoost \(HOA\-LSBoost\) model achieves R² values of 0\.97, 0\.95, and 0\.96 for porosity, dilution rate, and aspect ratio prediction, respectively\. FLEA shows the best optimisation performance, with HV, IGD, GD, and Spacing values of 0\.53955, 0\.02269, 0\.0033991, and 0\.018635, respectively\. Experimental validation shows that porosity decreases from 0\.28% to 0\.16%, while hardness, tensile strength, and yield strength increase from 260 HV0\.3, 719, and 560 MPa to 275 HV0\.3, 735, and 575 MPa, respectively\. The proposed framework provides a practical strategy for intelligent parameter selection and quality control in industrial L\-DED manufacturing and repair\.
