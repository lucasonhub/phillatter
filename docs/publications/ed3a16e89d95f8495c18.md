# Role of trap position variation on electrical parameters in hetero\-dielectric double gate TFET

**Autores:** Riddhi Dutta; Aryan Bose; Shanidul Hoque; Rajesh Saha

**Data:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

This work is a statistical reliability study of the Heterojunction Dielectric Double Gate \(HDDG\) tunnel field\-effect\-transistor \(TFET\) with respect to the randomized location of interface traps\. The device configuration consists of a Ge source, and a p \+ pocket to increase the tunneling probability\. Using TCAD Sentaurus, the impact of various interface trap localizations arising from the random nature of the interface is examined through a comprehensive variability analysis\. A comprehensive variability study is conducted to evaluate the sensitivity of the device to interface trap localization\. Of particular interest is how the device parameters change due to the incorporation of Gaussian\-distributed traps in 5 nm segments of the oxide semiconductor interface\. The statistical analysis of 100 devices is performed over four unique cases; \(i\) no traps, \(ii\) traps localized in the oxide/n\+ channel region, \(iii\) traps localized in the oxide/p\+ pocket region, and \(iv\) traps distributed across both regions\. The typical behavior of the devices is examined and the statistical variation of the following parameters is discussed; On\-Current \(I ON \), Off\-Current \(I OFF \), Threshold Voltage \(V th \), Band\-to\-Band Tunneling \(BTBT\) generation rate, electron velocity and subthreshold swing \(SS\)\.
