# AI\-Driven Cyber Resilience Framework for Critical Digital Infrastructure Using Reinforcement Learning

**Autores:**  Dr\. T\. Samraj Lawrence; Rupal Snehkunj; S\. Ramya

**Data:** 2026-10-11 · **Tipo:** book

## Resumo

AI\-Driven Cyber Resilience Framework for Critical Digital Infrastructure Using Reinforcement Learning Modern digital infrastructure faces increasingly complex cybersecurity threats\. From healthcare networks and banking systems to power grids, telecommunications, cloud platforms, and industrial control systems, protecting essential digital services requires more than traditional security mechanisms\. AI\-Driven Cyber Resilience Framework for Critical Digital Infrastructure Using Reinforcement Learning explores how Artificial Intelligence \(AI\), Machine Learning \(ML\), and Reinforcement Learning \(RL\) can support adaptive cyber defense, intelligent threat detection, and resilient infrastructure management\. This book introduces the fundamental principles of cybersecurity and cyber resilience before examining the challenges associated with securing critical digital infrastructure\. It explains how intelligent security mechanisms can analyze system behavior, identify suspicious activities, support defensive decisions, and help organizations respond to changing cyber threats\. What You Will Learn Cybersecurity Fundamentals: Understand security principles, the CIA triad, common cyberattacks, vulnerabilities, and defense mechanisms\. Critical Digital Infrastructure Protection: Explore cybersecurity challenges affecting healthcare, energy, banking, telecommunications, industrial systems, and interconnected digital environments\. Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning: Learn how AI\-based analytics can support anomaly detection, threat classification, risk assessment, and predictive security\. Reinforcement Learning for Cyber Defense: Understand the relationship between security states, defensive actions, rewards, and adaptive decision\-making\. Adaptive Cyber Defense: Examine approaches for dynamically adjusting security policies, isolating compromised components, and responding to emerging threats\. Cyber Resilience and Recovery: Explore preparation, protection, detection, response, recovery, and adaptation as essential elements of resilient infrastructure\. Human\-AI Collaboration: Understand why human oversight, validation, and operational safeguards remain important when deploying intelligent security systems\. Who Should Read This Book? This book is intended for computer science and information technology students, cybersecurity researchers, AI and machine learning practitioners, research scholars, faculty members, security engineers, and professionals interested in intelligent cyber defense\. It can also serve as a learning resource for readers exploring the intersection of reinforcement learning, cybersecurity, critical infrastructure protection, and resilient computing\. Building the Next Generation of Cyber Defense Cybersecurity is no longer only about preventing attacks\. Modern organizations must also be prepared to withstand disruptions, maintain essential services, recover affected systems, and adapt to future threats\. By bringing together cybersecurity fundamentals, AI\-driven analytics, reinforcement learning concepts, and cyber resilience principles, this book provides a foundation for understanding intelligent and adaptive approaches to protecting critical digital infrastructure\. Discover how AI and reinforcement learning can contribute to a more adaptive, resilient, and security\-aware digital future\.
