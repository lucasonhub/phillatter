# Deep learning assisted screening of light\-responsive piezoelectric crystals for next\-generation photoacoustic and photothermal spectroscopy

**Autores:** Peng Chen; Gaiying Meng; Yongming Fu; Jie Ma

**Data:** 2026-10-14 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Introduction High piezoelectric materials are promising alternative resonator materials for quartz\-enhanced photoacoustic spectroscopy \(QEPAS\) and laser\-induced thermoelastic spectroscopy \(LITES\), as enhanced piezoelectric response directly improves acoustic detection sensitivity\. However, the discovery of materials combining high piezoelectricity with visible\-light responsiveness remains a significant challenge\. Methods We developed an improved crystal graph convolutional neural network \(I\-CGCNN\) model for high\-throughput screening of visible\-light\-responsive piezoelectric crystals\. The model incorporates a specialized algorithm for learning bond feature vectors and synchronizes the updates of atomic and bond feature vectors during multilayer convolution\. Using this model, we re\-calculated the bandgap and piezoelectric moduli of all piezoelectric crystals in the Materials Project database\. Results The I\-CGCNN model significantly outperforms existing graph neural networks in predictive accuracy for both bandgap and piezoelectric modulus\. The high\-throughput screening identified several promising candidate crystals that exhibit both strong piezoelectric response and visible\-light responsiveness\. Discussion The screened candidates show considerable potential for piezo\-phototronic applications, and the superior predictive performance of I\-CGCNN suggests that it provides a powerful tool for accelerating the discovery of novel piezoelectric semiconductors for next\-generation photoacoustic and photothermal spectroscopy\.
