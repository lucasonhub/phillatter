# RAQAMLI TA’LIM RESURSLARI VOSITASIDA YUQORI SINF O‘QUVCHILARIDA MUSTAQIL FIKRLASH KOMPETENSIYASINI SHAKLLANTIRISH METODIKASI

**Autores:** Muxlisa Abdulla qizi Ergasheva

**Data:** 2026-10-10 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

This article examines the scientific and pedagogical foundations and methodology for forming the independent thinking competence of high school students through modern digital educational resources\. It also highlights practical approaches aimed at developing students&\#x27; analytical, critical, and creative thinking abilities by introducing interactive and digital technologies into the educational process\. The author outlines effective forms and methods of using digital resources and analyzes the results of experimental work\.
