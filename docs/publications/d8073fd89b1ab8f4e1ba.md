# Interdisciplinary consensus of classification oncoorthopedic complications\.

**Autores:** Е\. А\. Сушенцов; I\. M\. Mikailov; V\. V\. Teplyakov; Artem V\. Bukharov; Петриченко Анна Викторовна; Andrey N\. Nikolaenko; Тихилов Рашид Муртузалиевич

**Data:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

The article presents an interdisciplinary consensus of the Russian\-language version of the classification of oncoorthopedic complications developing in patients with musculoskeletal tumors who have undergone limb\-salvage surgery\. The classification developed by the International Society of Limb Salvage \(ISOLS\) committee in 2014, being the most widespread and frequently cited in the literature, was used as the basis\.
