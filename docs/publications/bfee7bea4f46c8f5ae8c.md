# Poska, Mindaugas: Natural Possibility 35 Meditation, Is a Prayer

**Autores:** 

**Data de descoberta:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** unknown

## Descrição do feed

This paper 35, enters Ring Number Four of the Natural Possibility Framework, moving the investigation into a deeper exploration of the human system, consciousness, spirit, agency, information and the conditions through which new possibilities may become visible\. It explores meditation through my own experience and understanding, and considers how meditation may create different conditions within the human system\. The paper looks at the relationship between body, mind, spirit, agency, consciousness and information, while continuing the investigation developed through earlier Natural Possibility papers\. Meditation is presented not only as a spiritual practice, but also as a possible way of changing the condition of the system and allowing different connections, understanding and possibilities to emerge\. The paper also considers the personal meditation room within my mind, the relationship between meditation and my internal library, and the possibility of reaching different levels of consciousness\. These ideas are presented as part of my continuing investigation rather than as final conclusions\. Ring Number Four now opens a new stage of the Natural Possibility investigation\.
