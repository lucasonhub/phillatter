# Fake QR Code Detection System

**Autores:** Mrs\. V\. M\. Khanapure, Supesh Jaware, Aryan Pande; Aryan Bankar Rohit Shahapure

**Data:** 2026-10-12 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

QR codes have become an essential part of modern digital services, particularly for online payments, website access, information sharing, authentication, and contactless transactions\. However, the increasing use of QR codes has also created opportunities for cybercriminals to distribute fake or tampered QR codes that redirect users to phishing websites, fraudulent payment pages, malicious links, or unauthorized payment requests\. To address this security concern, this paper presents a Fake QR Code Detection \(FQRD\) system designed to assess the security of scanned QR codes before users interact with the associated content\. The proposed framework performs multiple security checks, including URL analysis, blacklist verification, domain\-age analysis, suspicious keyword detection, brand impersonation detection, and payment\-risk assessment\. Instead of depending only on previously reported malicious URLs, the system combines multiple indicators to identify suspicious QR codes that may not yet be present in existing blacklists\. The results obtained from these checks are combined to classify each scanned QR code into three categories: Safe, Suspicious, or Dangerous\. This multi\-signal approach improves the ability to detect both known and potentially emerging QR\-based threats while providing users with a clear and understandable security result\. The system is intended to provide an additional layer of protection against QR\-based phishing, payment fraud, and website impersonation\. By performing security analysis before the user proceeds to the detected URL or payment request, the proposed FQRD framework can help reduce the risk of financial loss, credential theft, and other forms of cyber fraud\. The proposed approach demonstrates how automated QR analysis and multiple security indicators can be integrated into a practical security solution for safer and more informed use of QR codes in everyday digital transactions\.\.
