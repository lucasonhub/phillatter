# ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE DISCOURSE IN MODERN LINGUISTICS: THE LINGUISTIC STATUS OF PROMPTS AS TEXT AND COMMUNICATIVE ACT

**Autores:** Azimova Guzal Bakhtiyarovna; Worldly Knowledge Publishing Centre

**Data:** 2026-10-12 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

This article examines the phenomenon of artificial intelligence discourse within the paradigm of modern computational and cognitive\-pragmatic linguistics\. It focuses on identifying the linguistic and communicative status of prompts as new natural language units functioning in human\-large language model interaction\. The pragmatic parameters of prompts through the prism of speech act theory, textual criteria, cohesion, and coherence, as well as their structural\-syntactic features, are analyzed\.
