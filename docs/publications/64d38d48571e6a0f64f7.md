# OpenTyreF1: A Data\-Driven Mobile Platform for Formula 1 Telemetry Analysis, Tyre\-Degradation Modelling and Counterfactual Race\-Strategy Simulation

**Autores:** Atharva Joshi; Prachi Jain; Ankit Sawalakhe; Araju Yelekar; Devshree Khodke; Kripa Patil

**Data:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Formula 1 publishes one of the richest open telemetry streams in professional sport, yet most public tools built on it are descriptive: they show what happened and stop there\. This paper presents OpenTyreF1, a full\-stack system that turns that data into decision support on a mobile phone\. The system integrates three public sources \(Jolpica\-F1/Ergast, OpenF1 and FastF1\) into one normalised 15\-table relational corpus of 237,308 records covering 492 races from 2002 to 2026\. On top of this corpusit builds a shared clean\-lap filter, a gradient\-boosted \(XGBoost\) lap\-time model, a counterfactual stint\-strategy simulator, a 1,500\-run Monte Carlo outcome estimator, seven pit\-wall decision modules and a grounded question\-answering assistant, all served by a FastAPI backend to an Expo/React Native app\. Evaluated on 12 completely unseen races, the model achieves a mean absolute error of 0\.893 s, RMSE of 1\.205 s and R 2 of 0\.978, beating the strongest naive baseline by 0\.270 s\. An ablation shows that predicting a lap’s offset from its race median, rather than the absolute lap time, cuts error four\-fold, a far larger effect than adding features\. The results show that, for motorsport data of this size, careful lap admission, target design and leakage\-free evaluation matter more than the choice of learner\.
