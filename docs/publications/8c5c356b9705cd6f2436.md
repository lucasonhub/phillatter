# AR\-LRX: Robustness of Adaptive Residual Linear Regression\-XGBoost Forecasting Across Pharmaceutical and Retail Demand Regimes

**Autores:** Wahyu Ramadhan; Edi Noersasongko; Abdul Syukur; Moch Arief Soeleman

**Data:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Residual hybrids that combine a linear model with gradient boosting are widely reported to improve demand forecasting, yet published comparisons mix different splits, preprocessing pipelines and target scales\.This study asks whether such a hybrid is robust and what its robustness depends on\.A single experimental contract is applied to every model, and eight baselines from recent forecasting work are retrained under it instead of quoted\.We propose AR\-LRX, which aligns the first stage with the structure of the data, trains the residual learner on cross\-fitted first\-stage predictions, and weights its correction by a gate selected on the validation fold\.On a 1,115\-outlet drugstore panel AR\-LRX attained a test RMSE of 954\.90, 5\.45% below the strongest retrained baseline, and led the tree baselines at four forecast origins and five seeds\.On pharmaceutical series the gate closed in 17 of 32 configurations, reducing mean degradation from 1\.77 to 0\.22 percent\.
