# Comparing change goals and feasibility beliefs about changing social, emotional, and behavioral skills vs\. personality traits among adolescents, parents, and teachers

**Autores:** Tommaso Feraco; Hayley K\. Jach; Lisa Bardach; Myriel Kopatz; Kou Murayama; Madison Nicole Sewell; Christopher M\. Napolitano; Christopher J\. Soto

**Data:** 2026-10-10 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

This study compared adolescents&\#x27;, parents&\#x27;, and teachers&\#x27; change goals and feasibility beliefs for Big Five traits versus social, emotional, and behavioral \(SEB\) skills\. Participants were 676 Italian high school students who reported about themselves, 461 parents who reported about their participating child, and 704 international teachers reporting about students in general\. We found that all groups viewed SEB skills as more changeable than traits, with the strongest skill preference among teachers\. Student differences were clearest for Social Engagement versus Extraversion\. Teachers, but not parents, also reported greater interest in skill\-based than trait\-based interventions\. These findings suggest that skill framing may increase perceived malleability and support for developmental interventions in educational contexts\.
