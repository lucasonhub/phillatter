# Neurological complications in patients receiving CAR T\-cell therapies

**Autores:** Justin Jiang; Virginia Battistin; Marcos J\. G\. De Lima; Francesca Cottini

**Data:** 2026-10-14 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Chimeric antigen receptor \(CAR\) T\-cell therapy targeting CD19 or B cell maturation antigen has revolutionized the treatment of patients with relapsed lymphoma, acute lymphoblastic leukemia, and multiple myeloma\. However, this therapy is associated with both short\- and long\-term immune\-mediated toxicities\. Among them, neurological complications represent a distinct and clinically significant category, with a wide range of presentations\. In this paper, we describe the different types of immune effector cell–associated neurotoxicity syndrome \(ICANS\) and non\-ICANS neurological toxicities, including their clinical features, recommended diagnostic evaluations, underlying mechanisms, and available treatment approaches\.
