# Broaching Intersectionality Across Disciplines: Enhancing Collaboration for Reproductive and Perinatal Health

**Autores:** Nouna Jalilzadeh; Ashley J\. D\. Ford‐Graham

**Data:** 2026-10-11 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

ABSTRACT Integrating broaching and intersectionality enhances interdisciplinary collaboration in perinatal healthcare, particularly for Black, Indigenous, and People of Color \(BIPOC\) individuals facing systemic disparities\. Counselors bridge mental health and medical fields to address barriers in education, supervision, and policy\. By fostering culturally responsive care, interdisciplinary teamwork can improve maternal health outcomes\. Practical strategies for counselor educators, supervisors, and systemic change are provided to promote inclusive, client‐centered collaboration in perinatal and reproductive healthcare\.
