# LEGAL ISSUES OF ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE APPLICATIONS

**Autores:** Mahmoud Hayat; Abderrazak Louafi

**Data:** 2026-10-13 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Artificial intelligence is the challenge of the new era that has imposed its presence and intervened in various aspects of life, especially the science of law\. Will the machine improve the art of drafting legal texts to control human behavior, now that its intelligence has surpassed that of humans? Have legal texts addressed such dangerous interference that has included several fields, starting from copyright and e\-commerce to the point of the existence of predictive judiciary as an alternative to traditional judiciary governed by machines? Is the law really going through the digitization crisis imposed on it by reality? We are facing a new challenge, and a new legal arsenal is required to regulate its provisions and benefit from its advantages\.
