# Karlan, Brett &amp; Westra, Evan: On folk\-psychological innovation

**Autores:** 

**Data de descoberta:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** unknown

## Descrição do feed

\_Philosophers&\#x27; Imprint\_\. forthcomingHuman folk psychology is strikingly innovative: across time, cultures, and domains of social life, we display a seemingly unbounded capacity to carve up the mental realm in new and creative ways\. We argue that, when properly understood, this feature of human folk psychology has new and surprising upshots for debates in cognitive ontology\. After exploring the cognitive underpinnings of folk\-psychological innovation, we show its challenging but useful implications for classic debates between realists and eliminativists\. Specifically, we argue that the innovative character of folk psychology motivates both a new form of eliminativism \(what we call bottom\-up eliminativism\) and a new form of realism \(what we call arm&\#x27;s\-length realism\)\.
