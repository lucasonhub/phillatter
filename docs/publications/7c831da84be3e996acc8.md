# An Agricultural Skid\-Steering Robot for Tomato Leaf Disease Detection and Targeted Pesticide Spraying: An Updated Prototype and Current Technology

**Autores:** P\. Durgashini V\. Pavithra; Dr\. R Senthamil Selvi Dr\. S Mohana

**Data:** 2026-10-13 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Agricultural workers may be exposed to pesticides during conventional crop\-protection operations, while timely identification of leaf diseases remains important for effective crop management\. This paper presents an updated discussion of an agricultural skid\-steering robot originally developed for tomato\-leaf disease identification and targeted pesticide spraying\. The prototype combines a four\-wheel skid\-steering platform, ultrasonic sensing, an MPU6050 inertial sensor, Arduino Nano control, ZigBee communication, MATLAB\-based image processing, K\-means clustering and an artificial neural network \(ANN\) classifier\. Tomato\-leaf images representing healthy leaves, bacterial spot, early blight, late blight and leaf mold are processed through image acquisition, preprocessing, colour\-space transformation, clustering, segmentation, feature extraction and classification\. The detected disease condition is then communicated to the robot controller, which activates the pump for the corresponding programmed spraying interval\. The original prototype demonstrated the feasibility of integrating image\-based disease recognition with a mobile agricultural platform\. The present manuscript retains the original prototype observations while adding a current technology perspective\. Recent studies show a transition toward YOLO\-family detectors, lightweight deep\-learning models, edge AI, autonomous navigation, multi\-sensor perception and variable\-rate robotic spraying\. These developments identify practical pathways for extending the prototype toward real\-time field deployment without claiming that such newer technologies were implemented in the original system\.
