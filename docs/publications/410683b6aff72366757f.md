# Lee, Shun\-Ching: Physical Dynamics of the Universal Field of Intelligence \(UFI\): A Synthesis of Maria Strømme’s Foundational Field Model and Non\-dual Ontology

**Autores:** 

**Data de descoberta:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** unknown

## Descrição do feed

This paper provides a critical expansion of the foundational field framework proposed by Maria Strømme \(2025\)\. By introducing the Universal Field of Intelligence \(UFI\), the author bridges quantum physics and non\-dual philosophy, offering a non\-reductionist model for the emergence of structure and experience\. Key contributions include the formulation of the $n^2$ Coherence Model and the definition of the Biological Antenna within the pre\-spatiotemporal order\. \(direct link \)
