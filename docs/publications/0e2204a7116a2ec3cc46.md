# Transmission Path of Chinese Traditional Music in College and University Under Big Data Based on the Analysis of User Portraits and a Platform Case

**Autores:** Yunran Wang; Cheng Shi

**Data:** 2026-10-14 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

With the development of new media technology, the important mission of inheriting China&\#x27;s traditional music culture is facing great challenges, including the influence of multiculturalism and the insufficient utilization of technical resources\. In this context, it is very important to explore effective communication strategies in the new media environment for the preservation and innovation of traditional music culture\. This paper discusses the opportunities and challenges of social media and big data in spreading traditional music culture\. The proposed strategies include building an online education platform, optimizing the teaching system, enriching the cultural database and establishing a technology\-integrated museum\. In addition, improving students&\#x27; media literacy and expanding multimedia platforms can significantly enhance cultural communication\. By exploring concept innovation, resource mining, field expansion and integration with market economy, this study provides implementation strategies for promoting traditional music culture in the university environment\.
