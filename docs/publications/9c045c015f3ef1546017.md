# AI in Digital Marketing: From Consumer Analytics to Intelligent Decision\-Making

**Autores:** Emad Tariq

**Data:** 2026-10-13 · **Tipo:** book

## Resumo

The book provides a comprehensive and contemporary examination of the role of Artificial Intelligence \(AI\) in modern marketing and its growing influence on digital business transformation, consumer engagement, and intelligent decision\-making\. The book explores key topics including Introduction to AI Marketing, AI marketing technologies and analytics, AI in consumer behavior, AI\-driven marketing strategy, Integrated Marketing Communications \(IMC\), and the implementation of AI systems within organizations\. In addition, the book critically discusses emerging issues related to ethics, transparency, privacy, and responsible AI practices in increasingly data\-driven marketing environments\. To strengthen the practical relevance of the subject, the book incorporates contemporary case\-based studies and essay questions that encourage analytical thinking and applied learning\.
