# Artificial intelligence policy, digital industry agglomeration, and industrial land intensive use: evidence from a quasi\-natural experiment in China

**Autores:** Ning Wang; Yunpeng Fu; Zhihong Liu

**Data:** 2026-10-15 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Against the backdrop of the deep integration of the digital economy and artificial intelligence, whether and how AI policies can enhance the intensification of industrial land use is a critical issue concerning the efficiency of land resource allocation and urban sustainable development\. Using the development of China’s National new Generation Artificial Intelligence Innovation and development pilot zones as a quasi\-natural experiment, this study employs an overlapping difference\-in\-differences model based on panel data from 271 prefecture\-level and higher cities in China from 2010 to 2023 to systematically evaluate the impact, transmission mechanisms, and policy synergies of artificial intelligence policies on the intensification of industrial land use\. The study found that: first, AI policies significantly improved the intensification of industrial land use in pilot cities, a conclusion that withstood a series of robustness tests; second, the clustering of digital industries played a significant partial mediating role, revealing the transmission mechanism of “AI policies → digital industry clustering → intensification of industrial land use\.” third, there is a significant positive synergy between AI policies and public data openness policies; the combined implementation of these two policies yields greater promotional effects on both the intensification of industrial land use and the clustering of digital industries than either policy alone, indicating that the combination of digital and AI policies can produce a “1 \+ 1 &gt; 2” amplification effect\. This study provides a theoretical basis and policy guidance for optimizing the allocation of industrial land, promoting the coordination of digital and intelligent policies, and achieving the efficient use of land resources\.
