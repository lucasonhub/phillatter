# Uller Avelar Ribeiro, Rafael: A razão cabe em um algoritmo? Pensamento e técnica a partir de Bento Prado Júnior

**Autores:** 

**Data de descoberta:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** unknown

## Descrição do feed

\_Coluna Anpof\_\. 2026This essay revisits Bento Prado Júnior’s critique of naturalistic reductions of thought in order to examine contemporary claims about artificial intelligence\. Taking as its starting point the distinction between the causal conditions of cognition and the normative space of reasons, it argues that the successful computational reproduction of certain inferential and linguistic operations does not by itself establish that thought is reducible to algorithmic processes\. The essay does not deny the philosophical significance of artificial intelligence, nor does it claim that machines cannot think\. Rather, it questions the inference from computational performance to a complete definition of intelligence and reason\. Drawing also on Hegel’s critique of the immediate identification of spirit with material properties, it suggests that the central philosophical risk lies in redefining thought according to what machines are currently able to reproduce\. The problem, therefore, is not whether technical systems can perform increasingly complex cognitive tasks, but whether technical efficacy should become the measure of reason itself\. \(direct link \)
