# PEDAGOGICAL FOUNDATIONS FOR DEVELOPING STUDENTS&\#x27; ACADEMIC WRITING COMPETENCE THROUGH ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE

**Autores:** Mokhlaroyim Khamdamova Oybek qizi

**Data:** 2026-10-14 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

This paper examines the pedagogical foundations for integrating artificial intelligence into the development of students&\#x27; academic writing competence\. It adopts a conceptual approach informed by writing pedagogy, self\-regulated learning, and human\-centred principles of AI use in education\. Academic writing competence is understood as the ability to construct coherent arguments, evaluate and integrate evidence, follow disciplinary conventions, and revise texts independently\. The paper proposes an instructional framework combining explicit teaching, guided AI interaction, critical feedback evaluation, and reflective revision\.
