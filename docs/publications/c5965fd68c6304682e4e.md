# Assessing the sustainability performance of large food retail companies: a generic framework and pilot application to the environmental dimension in Germany

**Autores:** Christian Schader; Olivia Keller; Moritz Teriete; Marlene Sander; Carola Schafhausen; Christian Herzig; Hyewon Seo

**Data:** 2026-10-09 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Introduction Food systems are central to Sustainable Development Goal 12, and large food retailers occupy a strategic position between producers and consumers\. Comparative scientific assessments of retailer sustainability performance and retailer\-specific conceptual frameworks remain limited\. Methods We developed a generic four\-step framework covering objectives and scope, evaluation methodology, data collection, and evaluation\. We applied it in a pilot assessment of the environmental sustainability performance of the eight largest food retailers in Germany, using 2020 as the reference year and operationalising 22 fields of action, 43 indicators, and 112 sub\-indicators across supply chains, own sites, and consumption\. Results Companies used only part of their available scope for action\. Performance was stronger in strategy and communication than in implementation, while fields in which all companies performed poorly suggested constraints that cannot be addressed by individual firms alone\. Discussion The framework provides a transparent structure for comparative company\-level sustainability assessment and the pilot demonstrates its practical applicability\. Results are specific to the German market, the 2020 reference period, and the environmental dimension; application elsewhere requires context\-specific adaptation of indicators, benchmarks, and data sources\.
