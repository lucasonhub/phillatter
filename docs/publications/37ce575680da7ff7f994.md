## Descrição do feed

\_Zenodo\_\. 2026The central thesis of this paper is methodological: the quality of a philosophical definition is measured by its generativity — the capacity to yield subsequent concepts without introducing new primitives — and by the number of additional constructions required after its adoption\. A system of seven non\-recursive definitions resting on a single primitive yields nineteen derived concepts without introducing new foundations\. This result is compared with four prior approaches, each of which exhibits predictable accumulation of apparatus when the foundation is weak\. \(direct link \)
