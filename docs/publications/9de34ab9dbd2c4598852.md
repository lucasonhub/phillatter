# CONTAMINATED AND POTENTIALLY HAZARDOUS AGRICULTURAL LAND IN THE SYSTEM OF UKRAINE’S FOOD SECURITY FACTORS

**Autores:** Dmytro Kurinskyi

**Data:** 2026-10-09 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

The article develops a model that distinguishes the procedural status of a land area from the verifiability of reported data when estimating the agricultural potential of land affected by mine action\. The model prevents operationally surveyed, formally released and actually cultivated areas from being treated as one measure of restored productive capacity\. Only land with a reported production status enters the physical\-output scenarios; certified land without evidence of cultivation remains outside them\. The model also specifies the site\-level data needed for NPV and benefit\-cost analysis: release and restoration costs, time to first harvest, crop, yield and product use, without deriving those indicators from disconnected aggregates\. Contaminated land is interpreted as a constraint on absorptive capacity, agronomic recovery as an adaptive function, and registry integration as a transformative function\.
