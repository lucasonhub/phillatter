# False Friends and Lexical Interference in Multilingual EFL Production

**Autores:** Khatira Mammadova

**Data:** 2026-10-10 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

False friends can make familiar\-looking English vocabulary difficult to use accurately, particularly when learners draw on several previously acquired languages\. This article examines lexical interference in multilingual English as a foreign language production, with attention to Azerbaijani\-speaking learners and possible influences from Turkish and Russian\. It combines a critical discussion of relevant scholarship with an exploratory interpretation of a reported classroom inventory containing 126 error instances\. The original inventory assigns 58 instances to semantic errors, 34 to collocational errors, 21 to pragmatic misunderstandings, and 13 to lexical production errors\. These figures describe the recorded classifications; incomplete sampling information and overlapping categories prevent estimates of prevalence or causal attribution\. Analysis of selected lexical contrasts distinguishes semantic mismatch, phraseological compatibility, communicative consequences, and evidence of transfer\. It also shows why grammatical sentences and similar\-looking English words should not automatically be classified as false\-friend errors\. The article proposes a more explicit diagnostic procedure and a teaching sequence integrating contextual comparison, learner explanation, guided production, and delayed retrieval\. Its contribution is a production\-oriented framework for interpreting and teaching false friends while separating documented observations from plausible explanations and pedagogical proposals\.
