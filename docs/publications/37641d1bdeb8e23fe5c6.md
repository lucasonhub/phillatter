# Artificial Intelligence Readiness and Educational Management Effectiveness: The Role of Digital Leadership

**Autores:** Setiawan; Ali Kuswadi; Dwi Sugianikl; Feri Riski Dinata; Muslih Qomarudin

**Data:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

This quantitative explanatory study examines relationships among artificial intelligence readiness \(AIR\), digital leadership \(DL\), and educational management effectiveness \(EME\) in questionnaire responses from 78 people involved in educational management or administration\. In the supplied results, AIR was positively associated with DL \(standardized coefficient = 0\.292, t = 2\.663, p = \.009; R² = \.085\)\. AIR was strongly associated with EME after adjustment for DL \(β = 0\.951, t = 26\.460, p &lt; \.001\), whereas the adjusted DL–EME coefficient was 0\.012 \(t = 0\.328, p = \.744\); EME model R² was \.911\. The source reports an indirect estimate near 0\.0033, calculated from unstandardized paths, but no bootstrapped confidence interval or direct test of the indirect effect\. A non\-significant DL–EME path alone does not establish the significance of mediation\. Furthermore, EME had Cronbach’s alpha of \.665, and the measurement model, institutional sampling frame, and item wording are not provided\. These cross\-sectional self\-reports support associations within this sample, not a causal effect or a verified absence of mediation\. The measurement evidence and indirect\-effect interval should be supplied before submission\.
