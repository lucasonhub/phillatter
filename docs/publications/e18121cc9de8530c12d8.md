# System analysis approaches for the development of advanced architectures

**Autores:** Г\. В\. Горячев

**Data:** 2026-10-13 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Artificial Intelligence \(AI\) has established a new technological and organizational landscape across all industries, yet the process of creating AI architecture remains disjointed, intuitive, and domain\-oriented\. The proposed study introduces a System\-Analytical Framework of Artificial Intelligence Architecture Design \(SA\-AI Framework\), a collection of principles from systems analysis and multi\-criteria decision\-making intended to harmonize architectural design processes across heterogeneous AI domains\. The paper is based on twenty\-five current studies in explainable AI, neural\-symbolic architectures, human\-centered robotics, agentic systems, and domain\-specific applications, such as healthcare, finance, energy, and education, that inform a comparative synthesis of architectural design principles\. A Research Design Science Research \(DSR\) approach is used to model and test the SA\-AI Framework conceptually across four stages: requirements analysis, identification of architectural alternatives, multi\-criteria evaluation, and model validation\. Results show that the framework offers a systematic, defensible method for constructing AI architectures, enabling greater exposure, flexibility, and decision\-making effectiveness\. It has cohesively unified the different models of AI, including the agentic AI, Explainable AI \(XAI\), Human\-Centered AI \(HCAI\), and safety\-driven architectures, providing both theory and practice to AI engineering and policy design\. This paper concludes that system analysis helps design AI systems that integrate both technical and socio\-technical systems to enhance the governance, reliability, and sustainability of intelligent architecture in the next generation\.
