# Using Temporal Convolutional Autoencoders for Sound\-Based Fault Detection in Industrial Machinery

**Autores:** Igor Assunção; Rodrigo de Paula Monteiro; Andrea Maria Nogueira Cavalcanti Ribeiro; Diego Pinheiro

**Data:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

We propose using two deep autoencoder architectures, i\.e\. the Temporal Convolutional Network Autoencoder \(TCN\-AE\) and Wasserstein Autoencoder with Generative Adversarial Network \(WAE\-GAN\), to perform anomaly detection in the industrial context\. In this work, we detect anomalies by introducing a feature engineering pipeline that structures univariate acoustic signals into sequential multivariate representations via Mel Frequency Cepstral Coefficients \(MFCCs\)\. The trained models were evaluated using 10\-fold cross\-validation, and their performances were assessed through an empirical analysis of hyperparameter sensitivity, mapping prominent structural configurations for each neural architecture\. The optimized TCN\-AE showed superior performance, achieving an average AUC\-ROC of 0\.8716 for pump devices and 0\.7834 for fan devices, thereby surpassing state\-of\-the\-art literature benchmarks, while also demonstrating robust performance for the slider device with an average AUC\-ROC of 0\.9646\. Both architectures presented limitations with the valve device due to its highly transient behavior\. The results achieved confirm the effectiveness of sequential reconstruction\-based models for continuous machine operations while highlighting specific open challenges in acoustic industrial monitoring\.
