# AutoCare AI: An AI\-Assisted Vehicle Service Management System

**Autores:** Om Singh; Perseus Bhavnagri

**Data:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Independent garages commonly manage repairs with paper job cards, verbal estimates and memory, so the accuracy and efficiency of their service cannot be measured\. This paper presents AutoCare AI, a zero\-cost web application built with Flutter and Firebase that digitises the garage workflow from walk\-in to invoice, estimates a job&\#x27;s fault category, labour time and cost from the customer&\#x27;s free\-text complaint using Google Gemini \(with a keyword\-rule engine for offline demonstration\), suggests the least\-busy suitable mechanic, predicts each vehicle&\#x27;s next periodic service and records manual, AI and actual outcomes on every job\. The study follows a design\-science approach with secondary, quantitative data: 31,236 owner complaints from the public NHTSA complaints database, labelled into seven of the application&\#x27;s fault categories and split into 24,988 training and 6,248 test records\. A TF\-IDF and Logistic Regression classifier reached 90\.6 % accuracy \(macro\-F1 0\.907\), significantly above an 80 % target \(exact binomial p ≈ 1\.09 × 10⁻¹¹⁵\), whereas the application&\#x27;s keyword rules reached only 57\.6 %\. The learned classifier was significantly more accurate than the rules \(exact McNemar, 2,199 against 136 discordant pairs, p &lt; 0\.001\)\. In a 250\-day simulation, greedy assignment lowered the mean daily spread of mechanic workload to 1\.49 h against 4\.03 h for random assignment \(Wilcoxon p ≈ 3\.85 × 10⁻⁴²\)\. Gemini was not evaluated at scale because AI was kept switched off to guarantee zero cost; its few live estimates are reported only as anecdotal evidence\.
