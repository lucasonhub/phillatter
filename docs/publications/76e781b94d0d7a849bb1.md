# Inteligencia artificial en la investigación académica de los estudiantes de Comunicación de una universidad pública de Ica\-2026

**Autores:** José Julio Bendezú Huaroto; Mag\. Julio Cesar Ortiz Rojas; Navale Quiroz Cano; Rosario Huayanca Zapata; Fabrizio Antonio Jurado Montenegro

**Data:** 2026-10-14 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Context: The integration of artificial intelligence into higher education has prompted interest in its relationship with academic research\. Objective: To determine the relationship between artificial intelligence and academic research among Communication Sciences students at a public university in Ica in 2026\. Methods: A quantitative correlational study used a nonexperimental, cross\-sectional design\. The sample comprised 153 students selected through simple random sampling\. Results: A positive association was identified between the self\-reported scores for both variables \(ρs = 0\.640; p &lt; 0\.001\), pending verification against the dataset and the valid sample size\. Descriptive percentages still require reconciliation with the sample of 153 students\. Conclusions: The reported results indicate a positive, statistically significant relationship between artificial intelligence and academic research, subject to the pending verification of the data\.
