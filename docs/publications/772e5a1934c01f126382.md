# How to Break Through the Ceiling of Scientific Progress?—Innovation Challenges and Pathways Based on the Fitness Landscape Framework

**Autores:** Kunjie Zhu; Li Sun; Peng Du

**Data:** 2026-10-13 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Science has not hit an absolute ceiling; it is stranded on a local peak\. Viewed through Wright&\#x27;s fitness landscape, today&\#x27;s evaluation and funding systems over\-select, refining work within established paradigms while suppressing the drift and recombination needed to cross fitness valleys\. Restoring room for non\-consensus, curiosity\-driven and interdisciplinary research is an institutional task as much as an intellectual one\.
