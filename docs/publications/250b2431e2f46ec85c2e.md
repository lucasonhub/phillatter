# Advanced drug delivery and PBPK modelling as translational tools for precision therapies in juvenile idiopathic arthritis

**Autores:** Marina Cuquerella\-Gilabert; Cristina Martín‐Sabroso; Javier Zarzoso\-Foj; Juan Aparicio\-Blanco; Alejandro Balsa; Ana Isabel Torres‐Suárez; Victor Mangas Sanjuan; Carlos Rodríguez‐Nogales

**Data:** 2026-10-14 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Patients with persistent forms of juvenile idiopathic arthritis \(JIA\) often report limited therapeutic benefit from currently available treatments\. Despite substantial advances in the management of rheumatoid arthritis \(RA\) and related disorders over recent decades, treatment strategies for JIA remain insufficiently defined\. The application of advanced drug delivery systems holds promise in addressing these limitations by enabling controlled and sustained drug release over extended periods\. Other integrative approaches such as novel physiologically based pharmacokinetic \(PBPK\) modelling have demonstrated to be a critical tool in optimizing pharmacotherapy in pediatric populations, particularly by improving dose selection and predicting drug disposition\. This review summarizes current disease\-modifying antirheumatic drugs \(DMARDs\) for JIA management and critically examines the current, still largely prospective, integration of emerging drug delivery systems with PBPK modelling, highlighting existing translational examples alongside key opportunities and challenges for precision therapies in this field\.
