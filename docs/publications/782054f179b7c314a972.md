# SHARQ MUTAFAKKIRLARI MEROSIDA USTOZ\-SHOGIRD AN’ANASI VA UNI OLIY TA’LIMDA TARIX FANINI O‘QITISHDA QO‘LLASH \(FOROBIY, BERUNIY, IBN SINO, KAYKOVUS MEROSI MISOLIDA\)

**Autores:** Фотима Насировна Ширинова

**Data:** 2026-10-12 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

This article analyzes the content and pedagogical principles of the ustoz–shogird \(mentor–disciple\) tradition in the works of Eastern thinkers Abu Nasr al\-Farabi, Abu Rayhan al\- Biruni, Abu Ali ibn Sina \(Avicenna\), and Kaykavus\. The thinkers’ ideas concerning the unity of education and upbringing, a critical approach to sources, collaborative and step\-by\-step learning, and the ethics of knowledge are examined comparatively\. The article explores the possibilities of applying these ideas to teaching history in higher education, proposes a three\- way “mentor–student–historical source” model, and identifies criteria for evaluating its effectiveness\.
