# Bradley, Adam &amp; Saad, Bradford: Is computational functionalism crucial to AI consciousness?

**Autores:** 

**Data de descoberta:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** unknown

## Descrição do feed

Whether we can build a \(phenomenally\) conscious AI system is often thought to turn on computational functionalism\. We argue against this thought by identifying three ways of precisifying it and showing that they do not survive scrutiny\. In particular, we consider the suggestions that computational functionalism is sufficient for the possibility of AI consciousness, that computational functionalism is necessary for the possibility of AI consciousness, and that computational functionalism is a methodological presupposition of the scientific study of AI consciousness\. After explaining why these suggestions are tempting and why they are wrong, we consider candidates for more defensible claims in the vicinity\. However, problems with the necessity and sufficiency claims extend to significantly weaker theses that are couched in terms of functionalism proper rather than computational functionalism\. One moral we draw is that these suggestions for how the possibility of AI consciousness depends on computational functionalism are not even close to correct\. But we identify a version of the methodology claim that is defensible and which eschews both computational functionalism and functionalism in favor of a much weaker presupposition\. The upshot is that the investigation of AI consciousness should abandon the presumption of \(computational\) functionalism and instead work under the much less tendentious assumption that consciousness has functional markers\.
