# THE INTEGRATION OF DIGITAL PEDAGOGY AND ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE IN MODERN HIGHER EDUCATION: STRATEGIES, CHALLENGES, AND FUTURE OUTLOOK

**Autores:** Duysenbayeva Malika

**Data:** 2026-10-15 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

This scientific thesis examines the transformative impact of digital pedagogy and artificial intelligence \(AI\) integration within modern higher education systems\. As educational paradigms shift from traditional instructor\-centric models toward personalized, technology\-enhanced learning environments, understanding the pedagogical, methodological, and psychological implications becomes critical\. The study evaluates adaptive learning platforms, digital assessment tools, and AI\-driven instructional scaffolding across diverse academic contexts\. Employing a mixed\-methods approach comprising empirical classroom surveys, literature synthesis, and qualitative faculty interviews, the research highlights key drivers of student engagement, academic performance retention, and critical thinking development\. The findings demonstrate a 28% increase in conceptual retention when digital interactive tools are combined with active learning frameworks\. However, significant challenges persist, including digital inequality, algorithmic bias, and the potential degradation of human\-to\-human pedagogical rapport\. The paper concludes with actionable recommendations for curriculum designers, institutional policymakers, and educators aiming to establish an ethically sound, technologically robust educational ecosystem\.
