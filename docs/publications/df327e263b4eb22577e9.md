# Qin, Han: The Subjectivity Crisis in the Age of AI

**Autores:** 

**Data de descoberta:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** unknown

## Descrição do feed

This paper argues that the principal threat AI poses to human subjecthood is not that AI becomes too powerful, but that it exposes and accelerates the terminal logic of systemic instrumentalization\. When institutions value persons primarily through output, efficiency, and measurable performance, increasingly capable AI makes human beings appear redundant on the very dimension by which those institutions evaluate them\. That logic predates AI; AI drives it to its visible endpoint\. Applying the Self\-as\-an\-End framework, the paper analyzes how this dynamic operates across institutional, relational, and individual layers, including the feedback structures through which external evaluation becomes internalized\. It rejects competition with AI as a durable answer: on a purely instrumental dimension, humans cannot secure their status by remaining functionally indispensable\. The alternative is to reconstruct structural conditions under which persons are treated as ends independently of comparative output\. The age of AI is therefore not the end of subjectivity but the point at which the question of subjectivity can no longer remain concealed\. \(direct link \)
