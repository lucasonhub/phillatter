# Sustainable and Green Networking: Architectures, Techniques, and Future Directions for Energy\-Efficient Communication Infrastructures

**Autores:** Sulaiman Bello Umar, Jyoti Shekawat, Tasiu Bello Umar; Fatima Abdullahi Sani Mahmud Mahmud Lawal

**Data:** 2026-10-18 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Information and Communication Technology \(ICT\) infrastructure is rapidly becoming an electrical energy consumer, and an increasingly non\-trivial emitter of greenhouse\-gases as a result of the growth of global data traffic, cloud computing, artificial\-intelligence workloads and always\-connected Internet\-of\-Things \(IoT\) devices\. Sustainable and Green Networking tackles this challenge by building energy\-awareness into the design, deployment and operation of communication networks without affecting performance, reliability and quality of service\. This paper summarizes the key concepts of green networking derived from literature published from 2022 to 2025, outlines the major classes of energy\-saving techniques \(standby and sleep modes, rate adaptation, energy\-proportional computing, Software\-Defined Networking \(SDN\) and Network Function Virtualization \(NFV\) based orchestration, and renewable\-powered infrastructure\) and suggests a cross\-layer conceptual framework that brings these techniques together under a single carbon\-intensity feedback loop\. As examples of application areas, green 5G/6G radio access networks, sustainable data centers, and energy efficient IoT/edge computing are presented\. A comparative study of the energy savings potential reported in the recent literature is presented, followed by a discussion of open challenges in the research field: the performance\-energy trade\-off, the standardization of energy information exposure, and the AI/ML energy\-overhead trade\-off, as well as interesting future research directions: the standardization of energy information exposure and the AI\-native energy orchestration\. The results will inform researchers, network operators and policymakers in their quest for more sustainable digital infrastructures\.
