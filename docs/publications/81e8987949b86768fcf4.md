# PEDAGOGLIK KASBINING SATHLARINI TASHKIL ETUVCHI KOMPETENSIYALARNING NAZARIY VA AMALIY ASOSLARI

**Autores:** Mirshoxid Imomaliyev

**Data:** 2026-10-10 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

The article analyzes the content, structure, and interrelationships of the competencies that constitute different levels of the teaching profession\. Competencies demonstrated at various stages of teachers’ professional activity are examined in terms of professional knowledge, practical skills, communication, reflective practice, and innovative approaches\. The article also explores ways to improve pedagogical competencies according to levels of professional development\. Scientific and practical recommendations are proposed to support teachers’ professional growth\.
