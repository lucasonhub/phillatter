# The diagnostic blind spot of spirometric staging in biomass\-related non\-smoking chronic obstructive pulmonary disease: a cross\-sectional study of spirometry\-diffusion discordance in a rural South Indian population

**Autores:** Chittin N Swamy; Sandeepa H\. S\.; Deepu C\. C\.; Chirag K\. C\.; Nandakishore Bommeri

**Data:** 2026-10-12 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Chronic obstructive pulmonary disease \(COPD\) is heterogeneous, yet severity is conventionally graded by spirometry \[forced expiratory volume in 1 second \(FEV1\)\] on the assumption that airflow limitation reflects parenchymal damage\. Biomass\-related non\-smoking COPD \(NS\-COPD\) is common in low\- and middle\-income countries\. In this hospital\-based cross\-sectional study, we compared the physiological phenotypes of smoking COPD \(S\-COPD\) and biomass\-related NS\-COPD and tested the concordance between Global Initiative for Chronic Obstructive Lung Disease \(GOLD\) spirometric severity and diffusion\-based gas\-exchange impairment\. A total of 91 stable COPD patients \(S\-COPD, n=54; NS\-COPD, n=37\) underwent post\-bronchodilator spirometry, single\-breath diffusing capacity of the lung for carbon monoxide \(DLCO\) with transfer coefficient \(KCO\), residual volume to total lung capacity ratio, and the 6\-minute walk test; cohorts were stratified by exposure \(&gt;10 pack\-years vs\. &gt;60 hour\-years of biomass\) and compared using the Student t\-test and Fisher&\#x27;s exact test\. Compared with smokers, non\-smokers had higher DLCO \(71\.4±12\.4 vs\. 57\.7±17\.5% predicted; p&lt;0\.001\) and KCO \(0\.95±0\.19 vs\. 0\.71±0\.22 mmol/min/kPa/L; p&lt;0\.001\)\. All non\-smokers with severe \(GOLD 3\) airflow limitation \(8/8, 100%\) retained normal or only mildly impaired diffusion \[7/8 \(87\.5%\) strictly normal\], whereas 14/28 \(50\.0%\) GOLD 3 smokers had impaired gas exchange \[4/28 \(14\.3%\) severe\]\. Non\-smokers more often retained a normal DLCO \[31/37 \(83\.8%\) vs\. 26/54 \(48\.1%\); odds ratio 5\.6, 95% confidence interval 2\.0\-15\.5; p&lt;0\.001\]\. This difference persisted after multivariable adjustment for age, sex and body mass index \(odds ratio 30\.0, 95% confidence interval 2\.5\-358\.5; p=0\.007\) and after further adjustment for FEV1 percent predicted \(odds ratio 21\.1, 95% confidence interval 1\.6\-282\.7; p=0\.021\), indicating that the dissociation was not explained by these covariates or by the degree of airflow limitation itself\. GOLD staging tended to overestimate parenchymal severity in biomass\-related NS\-COPD, in which airflow obstruction was largely uncoupled from gas\-exchange loss, whereas in smokers the two were more tightly coupled\. These cross\-sectional, single\-center findings, limited by a sex imbalance and the absence of imaging confirmation, support routine DLCO/KCO measurement to phenotype these patients and, where imaging is scarce, as a low\-cost structural surrogate\.
