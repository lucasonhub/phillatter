# Reconstructing The Concept Of Self Actualisation In Artificial Intelligence Based Learning : An Integrative Literature Review

**Autores:** Ahmad Mujahidin; Ahsan Hakim

**Data:** 2026-10-13 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

The development of Generative Artificial Intelligence \(GenAI\) has transformed the learning ecology by positioning AI as a cognitive partner that supports personalisation, feedback, problem\-solving, and creativity\. This transformation necessitates a reconstruction of the concept of self\-actualisation, as some human cognitive activities can be delegated to AI\. This study aims to analyse the transformation of self actualisation, identify the conditions that strengthen or hinder it, and formulate a conceptual framework for self\-actualisation in AI\-based learning\. This study employs a qualitative library research approach using an integrative literature review design\. The findings indicate that GenAI is paradoxical in nature: it can serve as a cognitive scaffold that strengthens autonomy, competence, self\-regulation, creativity, metacognition, and epistemic agency, while also potentially encouraging cognitive offloading, dependence, and the weakening of cognitive autonomy\. This study proposes the Reflective\-Relational Self Actualisation Framework in AI Learning Ecologies \(RRSA\-AILE\), which encompasses autonomy, competence, relatedness, epistemic agency, metacognitive reflexivity, generative creativity, and ethical responsibility\. Theoretically, RRSA\-AILE integrates humanism, Self\-Determination Theory, Self\-Regulated Learning, and learner agency\. Practically, the framework provides a foundation for AI\-enhanced learning that is augmentative, reflective, and centred on human agency\.
