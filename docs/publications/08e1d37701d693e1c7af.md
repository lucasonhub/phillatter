In Petra Grimm, Kai Erik Trost &amp; Oliver Zöllner, Mensch – Maschine revisited\. Roboter und KI\-Systeme im öffentlichen Raum\. Stuttgart: Franz Steiner Verlag\. pp\. 87\-114\. 2026This qualitative study \(&quot;When Chatbots Give Advice&quot;\) investigates the acceptance of artificial systems, specifically ChatGPT outputs, as personal life advisors in everyday dilemmas\. Drawing on six in\-depth interviews conducted in Stuttgart, the case study explores whether individuals are able to distinguish human from artificially generated advice, and how they evalutate each form of advice\. The study employs a comparative experimental design, presenting participants with two responses to a real\-life ethical dilemma: should a terminally ill person acquire a pet dog? While one response has been written by a journalist and published in a magazine, a second one has been generated by prompting that same dilemma to ChatGPT\. Both advices were read out to the interviewees by a text\-to\-speech synthetic voice\. Reactions reveal a nuanced acceptance: while participants generally reject AI as a substitute for human friendship or emotional support, they acknowledge its potential as a supplementary tool for routine decisions\. Participants readily identified the AI\-generated response as such, noting its overly rational, structured, and option\-rich approach, and contrasted it with the human response&\#x27;s empathetic, personal, and emotionally resonant tone\. Despite skepticism about AI&\#x27;s reliability, transparency, and potential for manipulation, many participants expressed openness to using ChatGPT for simple, low\-stakes decisions, viewing it as a source of neutral, fact\-based perspectives\. However, they emphasized the need for critical evaluation and human verification\. The study concludes that AI is not seen as a replacement for human wisdom or emotional support, but rather as a potentially useful instrument in decision\-making processes that should be used cautiously, reflecting a deep\-seated humanist value system that prioritizes authentic human connection and moral agency\. \(direct link \)
