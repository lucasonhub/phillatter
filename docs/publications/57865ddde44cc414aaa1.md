# Safety evaluation of new energy three\-electric systems using enhanced physics\-informed neural networks

**Autores:** Wei Luo; Yifang Gao; Wenqiang Xu; Pengfei Yan; Yedong He; Shunshun Zhang; Chuanchuan Wang; Chongchong Shen; Jiahao Wang

**Data:** 2026-10-12 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Accurate safety\-state measurement of the three\-electric system in new energy vehicles is essential for ensuring reliable and secure operation\. This paper proposes an artificial\-intelligence\-assisted safety measurement framework that integrates a searchable encryption mechanism with Enhanced Physics\-Informed Neural Networks \(EPINNs\)\. In the proposed framework, the searchable encryption module supports privacy\-preserving storage and retrieval of multi\-source measurement data, while EPINNs serve as the core Artificial Intelligence \(AI\) model for multi\-parameter state estimation and fault identification\. A lightweight searchable encryption scheme based on block mapping is first designed to achieve feature\-level obfuscation and protect sensitive measurement data\. Then, an EPINNs\-based state estimation model is developed by incorporating data\-driven learning, subsystem physical constraints, and energy\-consistency terms to enhance robustness under noisy sensor data\. Experimental results show that the proposed method achieves improved performance compared with conventional Multi\-Layer Perceptron \(MLP\) and Convolutional Neural Network \(CNN\) models in battery state\-of\-health estimation, motor fault detection under injected fault conditions, and microgrid load measurement, with the motor fault detection accuracy exceeding 95% under the tested conditions\. These results indicate that the proposed framework provides an effective and secure technical solution for intelligent safety monitoring of new energy vehicle three\-electric systems\.
