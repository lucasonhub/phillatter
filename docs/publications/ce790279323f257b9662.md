# PENGARUH AI SEBAGAI TUTOR ONLINE TERHADAP PEMAHAMAN MATERI DI MEDIASI OLEH LITERASI DIGITAL

**Autores:** Guntur Gunawan

**Data:** 2026-10-13 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

The use of artificial intelligence \(AI\) in learning is expanding rapidly; however, empirical evidence regarding the role of AI as an online tutor in supporting subject matter understanding through digital literacy remains limited\. This study aims to analyze the relationships between AI as an Online Tutor, Digital Literacy, and Subject Matter Understanding, as well as the mediating role of Digital Literacy\. An explanatory research design with a quantitative approach was employed\. Data were collected from 110 participants selected via random sampling from a population of 245 individuals\. The instrument comprised 11 indicators, and the data were analyzed using PLS\-SEM\. The results indicate that AI as an Online Tutor is positively associated with Digital Literacy \(β = 0\.725; p = 0\.000\) and Subject Matter Understanding \(β = 0\.426; p = 0\.000\)\. Digital Literacy is also positively associated with Subject Matter Understanding \(β = 0\.472; p = 0\.000\)\. Furthermore, Digital Literacy mediates the relationship between AI as an Online Tutor and Subject Matter Understanding \(β = 0\.343; p = 0\.000\)\. These findings demonstrate that AI as an online tutor supports Subject Matter Understanding both directly and through the enhancement of Digital Literacy\. The study underscores the importance of integrating AI usage with Digital Literacy development in learning, enabling learners to effectively leverage AI support to comprehend the material\.
