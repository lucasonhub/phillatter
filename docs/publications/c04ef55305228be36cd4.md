# USE OF GENERATIVE ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE FOR DESIGNING ROBOTIC MODELS USING ENGINEERING GRAPHICS

**Autores:** Olena Solona; Олена Джеджула

**Data:** 2026-10-12 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

The article examines the possibilities of using generative artificial intelligence in teaching the discipline “Engineering and Computer Graphics” in the design of robotic models for the processing industry\. The feasibility of integrating generative artificial intelligence models into the design process of robotic systems as a means of developing spatial thinking, graphic competence, design skills, and digital skills of agricultural university students is substantiated\. It is established that modern generative systems \(ChatGPT, Microsoft Copilot, Gemini, Claude, DALL·E, etc\.\) support all stages of educational design, from developing technical specifications and generating product concepts to creating drawings and three\-dimensional models, visualizing designs, preparing technical documentation, and analyzing possible design solutions\. A methodology for using generative artificial intelligence in the educational project “Design of a Robotic Manipulator” is proposed\. The methodology is based on the principles of project\-based learning, STEM education, the competency\-based approach, and the use of CAD systems\. A structural model for integrating generative artificial intelligence into the process of graphic training has been developed\. It includes the stages of problem formulation, concept generation, sketch creation, three\-dimensional modeling, preparation of design documentation, and expert evaluation of the finished product\. It is demonstrated that the use of generative AI contributes to increasing students’ motivation, developing engineering thinking, fostering digital literacy, and reducing the time required for routine operations in the design of robotic models\.
