# Circulating cell\-death–associated biomarkers and inflammatory severity in Hymenoptera sting envenomation: a single\-center retrospective study

**Autores:** Pan Bian; Yuhua Wang; Shaohui Zhang; Yang Gui; Zhenya Shen

**Data:** 2026-10-14 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Background Severe Hymenoptera sting envenomation can cause systemic inflammation, shock, and multi\-organ injury\. Gasdermin D cleavage and RIPK3 phosphorylation are associated with inflammatory cell\-death pathways, but circulating measurements cannot by themselves establish a specific cellular death mechanism\. We therefore evaluated the clinical associations of serum GSDMD\-p30, p\-RIPK3, IL\-1β, and IL\-18 with illness severity in patients with Hymenoptera sting envenomation\. Methods We conducted a single\-center retrospective study of 142 adults admitted between January 2023 and December 2025\. Clinical severity was categorized at admission using a four\-level institution\-specific scheme: Grade I, local/minimal systemic manifestations without organ dysfunction; Grade II, systemic allergic manifestations without organ dysfunction; Grade III, single\-organ failure; and Grade IV, multi\-organ failure and/or anaphylactic shock\. Archived serum was available for 98 patients \(Grade I/II/III/IV: 30/33/22/13\)\. Serum GSDMD\-p30, p\-RIPK3, IL\-1β, and IL\-18 were measured by ELISA\-based assays\. Associations with severity grade and SOFA score and exploratory same\-cohort ROC performance for Grade III\-IV disease were assessed\. Results The cohort included 42 Grade I, 48 Grade II, 34 Grade III, and 18 Grade IV patients\. Sting count, time to hospital presentation, inflammatory indices, organ\-injury markers, SOFA score, ICU admission, and length of stay increased with clinical severity\. In the serum subset, mean GSDMD\-p30 increased from 0\.82 ± 0\.21 ng/mL in Grade I to 9\.43 ± 2\.18 ng/mL in Grade IV, and p\-RIPK3 increased from 0\.64 ± 0\.18 to 7\.84 ± 1\.87 ng/mL \(global p &lt; 0\.001 for both\)\. IL\-1β and IL\-18 showed similar severity\-associated increases\. Correlations with SOFA score were ρ = 0\.814 for GSDMD\-p30, ρ = 0\.783 for p\-RIPK3, ρ = 0\.791 for IL\-18, and ρ = 0\.762 for IL\-1β \(all p &lt; 0\.001\)\. Apparent AUCs for Grade III\-IV disease were 0\.861, 0\.843, 0\.834, and 0\.812, respectively\. Conclusion Circulating GSDMD\-p30, p\-RIPK3, IL\-1β, and IL\-18 were associated with clinical severity and organ\-failure burden in this retrospective cohort\. These findings support further evaluation of these proteins as candidate severity\-associated biomarkers, but they do not demonstrate PANoptosis, establish cell\-specific origin, or validate a clinical triage tool\. Prospective multicenter studies with standardized assays and adjusted analyses are required\.
