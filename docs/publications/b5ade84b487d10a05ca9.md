\_AI and Society\_:1\-15\. forthcomingThis study empirically examines the impact of Artificial Intelligence \(AI\) on the subjective well\-being \(SWB\) of 29 Asia–Pacific economies over the period 2019–2024\. The study considers the measures of both the cognitive and affective components of SWB as the dependent variable\. The Government AI Readiness Index was used to measure AI\. We employ the econometric techniques of instrumental\-variable two\-stage least squares \(IV\-2SLS\) regression to investigate the relationship between AI and SWB\. The study finds a positive and significant impact of AI preparedness on the SWB of Asian economies over 2019–2024 in a multivariate model with several macroeconomic variables as additional control variables\. The study also identifies economic growth, unemployment, health expenditures, and climate change as mediating variables in affecting the nexus between AI and SWB\. The results of the study clearly recommend more preparedness of nations for AI, as it is expected to be beneficial for overall well\-being of individuals in these economies\. \(direct link \)
