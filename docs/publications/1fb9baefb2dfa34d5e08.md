# MANAGEMENT OF ORGANIZATIONAL MARKETING COMMUNICATIONS IN THE CONTEXT OF DIGITALIZATION: AN INTEGRATED APPROACH

**Autores:** Ірина Іванівна Білик; Наталія Косар; Ю\. Т\. Савченко

**Data:** 2026-10-15 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

The article examines an integrated approach to managing organizational marketing communications under digital transformation\. It substantiates that digitalization changes traditional communication models, expands stakeholder interaction channels, and increases the need to combine analytical and generative tools\. The purpose of the study is to analyze the theoretical foundations and practical aspects of integrated marketing communication management in public administration bodies and nonprofit organizations, including sports organizations\. The study analyzes Big Data, CRM systems, social media, AI\-generated content, and chatbots in communication activities\. The study compares objectives, audiences, channels, digital tools, challenges, and ethical risks\. The components of an integrated approach are proposed: consistency of information messages, multichannel communication, orientation toward target audience needs, and continuous feedback\. It is established that CRM systems support stakeholder relationship management, personalized interaction, and communication performance analysis; Big Data expands opportunities for audience segmentation and behavioral analysis; and generative artificial intelligence facilitates adaptive content creation and communication automation\. Digital integration increases audience trust and engagement, optimizes expenditures, and enables faster responses to environmental changes\. The effectiveness of integrated communications depends on the consistency of messages across different channels and their adaptation to the specific needs of stakeholder groups\. The systematic combination of digital technologies with traditional communication tools is substantiated as a basis for organizational resilience in a dynamic environment\. Ethical, reputational, institutional, and resource\-related challenges associated with disinformation, non\-transparent use of AI, personal data protection, unequal digital skills, and limited resources are systematized\. The scientific novelty lies in conceptualizing an integrated approach as a universal yet sector\-differentiated model for managing organizational marketing communications\. The practical value lies in applying the model to improve communication strategies\.
