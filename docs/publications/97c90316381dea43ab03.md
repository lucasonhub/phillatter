# Creating Student\-Generated Infographics to Foster Civic Engagement Research in a First\-Year Seminar

**Autores:** Carlton Anthony Usher

**Data:** 2026-10-15 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Infographics, visual tools like charts or diagrams, simplify complex data into accessible formats\. This study explores how student\-generated infographics foster civic engagement in a first\-year seminar\. Survey data from first\-year students \(N=29\) revealed 85% rated the project highly valuable, and 87% reported improved retention of societal issues\. While research was the least favored aspect, students praised the project’s creativity and peer\-sharing potential, with 40% emphasizing its value for visual learning\. Most expressed confidence in sharing their work publicly\. These findings support the use of infographics as a pedagogical tool for enhancing civic engagement among first\-year students navigating academic transitions\. Future studies should investigate the long\-term effects on retention and activism\.
