# BO‘LAJAK HARBIY XIZMATCHILARDA OILAVIY QADRIYATLARGA MUNOSABATNING SHAKLLANISHI VA UNING INDIVIDUAL\-PSIXOLOGIK XUSUSIYATLARI

**Autores:** JAHONGIR BAXTIYOROVICH ATADJANOV

**Data:** 2026-10-15 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

This article examines the formation of attitudes toward family values among future military personnel, their individual psychological characteristics, and theoretical issues related to personal responsibility, emotional maturity, reflective activity, social adaptability, and psychological readiness for family life\. It also explores approaches to studying the relationships between cadets&\#x27; understanding of family values, personal attitudes, and psychological qualities, and proposes recommendations for developing family responsibility, mutual trust, emotional stability, and constructive communication skills\.
