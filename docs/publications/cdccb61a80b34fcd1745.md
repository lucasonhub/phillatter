# Fake News Detection Using BERT\-Based Language Model\-With Explainable AI

**Autores:** Sandeep Kumar Yadav; Saud Haseb; Shiv Shankar Yadav; Vaishali Singh

**Data:** 2026-10-14 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

social media has become one of the fastest means of spreading false information, thus there is an urgent need to develop automatic fact\-checking mechanisms\.The prior fake news detection systems were largely based upon manually engineered lexical features and classical machine learning classification techniques\.Recent studies in deep learning have shown that deep learning models are capable of developing representations\.However, deep learning model are limited in their ability to understand context and provide meaningful explanations\.Many of the early methods for detecting false information used manual extraction of linguistic attributes in conjunction with conventional statistical classifications\.We are developing an end\-to\-end system for detecting fake news\.This system combines a finetuned version of BERT \(Bidirectional Encoder Representations from Transformers\), with an Explainable AI Module\.The proposed system will be applied to the task of binary classification on social media posts and/or online news articles written in English that range from short\-tomedium length\.We have used WELFake as our main dataset for this study, and we intend to validate our results by comparing them with ISOT\.We will also compare our results with those from other fake news datasets such as LIAR\.Our methodology involves the following steps: cleaning and normalizing the data; tokenizing the data with BERT WordPiece tokenizer; creating sequences; fine\-tuning BERT\-base\-uncased; performing binary prediction; providing post\-hoc explanations of the predictions using LIME and SHAP; and visually presenting token\-level importance\.As this is a planning document for a future research study, we do not present actual accuracy, precision, recall, F1 score, latency, or statistically significant differences\.Instead, we describe the methodology, evaluation protocol, system architecture, and expected outcomes that will guide the empirical evaluation of our proposed framework\.
