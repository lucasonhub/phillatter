# Leveraging Artificial Intelligence for Emotional Intelligence Management in Public Universities: Antidote for Improving Lecturers&\#x27; Well\-being in Lagos State, Nigeria

**Autores:** Taofeek Olawale Shittu; Oluwakemi Chidiebere Okere; M\. O\. B\. Prof\. Mohammed

**Data:** 2026-10-18 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

This study explored the role of Artificial Intelligence \(AI\) as an antidote for improving lecturers&\#x27; well\-being through effective emotional intelligence management in public universities in Lagos State, Nigeria\. The emotional well\-being of lecturers has become a critical concern in public universities due to increasing workloads, overcrowded classroom, technological disruptions, and the pressure to achieve excellence in teaching, research, and community service\. The study adopted a conceptual approach and explores how AI\-powered tools such as intelligent counselling systems, emotion recognition software, virtual assistants, and predictive analytics can assist lecturers in monitoring emotions, managing stress, enhancing interpersonal relationships, and maintaining psychological balance\. The study further argues that integrating Artificial Intelligence \(AI\) with emotional intelligence competencies can foster resilience, job satisfaction, motivation, and a positive work environment among academic staff\. In addition, issues relating to digital literacy, ethical considerations, institutional support, and data privacy are discussed as essential factors for successful implementation\. The study concludes that the strategic deployment of Artificial Intelligence \(AI\) for emotional intelligence management offers a sustainable solution for enhancing lecturers&\#x27; well\-being and overall institutional effectiveness\. It is therefore recommended that university administrators should invest in AI\-driven emotional support systems and develop policies that encourage responsible and inclusive AI adoption in Nigerian public universities\. Keywords: Artificial Intelligence, Emotional Intelligence Management, Lecturers&\#x27; Well\-being, and Public Universities\.
