# Bulgaria, Jamel: Unification of Esoteric and Biological Topography: The Tri\-Centric Psychophysiological Architecture of the Core Emotion Framework

## Descrição do feed

This paper develops a unified psychophysiological and metaphysical architecture for understanding human affect by integrating the Core Emotion Framework \(CEF\) with enactive affective science, developmental neurobiology, esoteric somatic systems, and the Quantum Tathagatagarbha Manifestation Model \(QTMM\)\. The CEF reconceptualizes emotion as a structured matrix of ten functional operators distributed across three somatic centers—Head, Heart, and Gut—constituting a hardware‑independent “Human Operating System\.” Drawing on Giovanna Colombetti’s enactive theory of affectivity and the life–mind continuity thesis, the paper argues that emotional appraisal is not a representational computation but an organismic, embodied regulation process\. This enactive grounding is then mapped onto QTMM’s tri‑layer ontology \(Essence–Form–Utility\), yielding a holographic boundary–bulk model in which somatic centers function as conformal boundary states and subjective emotional episodes emerge as bulk projections\. The analysis further integrates developmental reflex theory and classical yogic physiology, demonstrating that the Brahma, Vishnu, and Rudra Granthis correspond to retained primitive reflexes that generate chronic autonomic dysregulation and somatic bracing\. Detailed neurobiological exposition of the cardiac and enteric nervous systems supports this mapping, while somatic‑mechanical principles \(e\.g\., Alexander Technique, vagal decompression, vestibular modulation\) provide a physical account of emotional stability and breakdown\. Finally, the paper formalizes operator dynamics using quantum‑cognitive state‑space geometry and non‑commuting projections, offering a mathematically tractable model of emotional transitions, fusions, and rigidity\. Together, these integrations establish a comprehensive philosophical framework that unifies embodied cognition, comparative metaphysics, affective neuroscience, and quantum‑informational ontology\. \(direct link \)
