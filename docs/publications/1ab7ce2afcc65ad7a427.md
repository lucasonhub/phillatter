# A deterministic resolvent filter approach for strongly correlated two\-electron atoms: boundary\-agnostic solutions in hyperspherical coordinates

**Autores:** Muhammad Nurhuda; Abdurrouf; Mauludi Ariesto Pamungkas; Agus Naba; Sugeng Rianto; Gancang Saroja; Setyawan P\. Sakti

**Data:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Abstract We present a deterministic, grid\-based framework for solving the stationary Schrödinger equation for strongly correlated few\-body systems\. This formulation leverages a resolvent filter operator \( H − E \) − 1 operating directly in spatial representations, offering an operationally boundary\-agnostic approach where edge points act as free dynamical unknowns and physical asymptotic decay emerges spontaneously from internal Hamiltonian dynamics without manual boundary matching\. We apply this framework to the helium atom in hyperspherical coordinates as a prototypical benchmark for strong electron correlation, successfully extracting both the ground state and a diverse manifold of low\-lying excited states\. By systematically analyzing grid configurations, we show that discretization errors follow a strict O \( Δ ρ 2 \) behavior, which is efficiently eliminated via Richardson extrapolation\. The resulting grid\-extrapolated energies exhibit exceptional agreement with high\-precision variational benchmarks, demonstrating that deterministic spectral filtering provides a robust and powerful alternative for complex quantum systems, achieving rapid convergence within only a few iterations\.
