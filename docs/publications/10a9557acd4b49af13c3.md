# TALABALARNING MANTIQIY FIKRLASH KOMPETENSIYASINI RIVOJLANTIRISHNING DIDAKTIK ASOSLARI

**Autores:** Rasulov Jasur Saparovich

**Data:** 2026-10-12 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

This article examines the didactic foundations for developing students’ logical thinking competence and explores its conceptual essence, key characteristics, and significance in Zamonaviy jamiyat va innovatsiyalar ilmiy nazariy jurnali 2026\-yil, 9\-Son, 2\-Qism\. ISSN 3060\-544X higher education\. It addresses the theoretical and practical aspects of organizing instruction that promotes logical reasoning, including the systematic acquisition of learning materials, identification of cause\-and\-effect relationships, evaluation of arguments, resolution of problem\- based situations, and development of independent reasoning skills\. Particular attention is given to the educational potential of problem\-based learning, interactive teaching methods, logical reasoning tasks, and reflective approaches\. The article substantiates the importance of considering students’ individual cognitive characteristics, connecting educational content with practical tasks, and strengthening pedagogical interaction as essential didactic conditions for developing logical thinking competence\. The presented ideas may provide a theoretical basis for designing methodological recommendations aimed at enhancing students’ intellectual engagement, analytical abilities, and capacity to make independent and well\-grounded decisions\. The proposed approach emphasizes the integration of cognitive activity, instructional strategies, and reflective learning to support the systematic development of logical thinking in university students\.
