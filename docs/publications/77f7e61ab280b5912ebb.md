# Generative AI in Higher Education: Adoption, Risks, and Opportunities in the Nordic Region

**Autores:** Jerónimo Paiva

**Data:** 2026-10-17 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

This study investigates how students and academic staff in Nordic higher education engage with generative artificial intelligence\. It asks what drives students to continue using these tools after initial adoption, and how academic staff perceive the opportunities and risks they bring\. The study is grounded in the Technology Continuance Theory, which holds that continued use is shaped by perceived usefulness, satisfaction, and confirmation, with trust and perceived security added as contextual factors relevant to the Nordic setting\.The research was conducted across five Nordic countries: Sweden, Finland, Norway, Denmark, and Iceland\. A mixed\-methods sequential design was used\. The quantitative phase involved an online questionnaire administered to higher education students across the region, analysed using Generalized Structured Component Analysis to test the hypothesised relationships between the constructs\. The qualitative phase involved semi\-structured interviews with professors, analysed thematically to capture the perspectives of academic staff on the use of generative AI in teaching and learning, with particular attention to linguistic representation and institutional practice\. The two phases were integrated to provide a fuller picture of adoption than either method could offer alone\.
