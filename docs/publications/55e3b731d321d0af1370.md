# Through the Eyes of Women Writers Women in the Context of Family Problems

**Autores:** Ebru Sancar; Mehmet TÖRENEK

**Data:** 2026-10-09 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

In recent Turkish short stories, female authors’ awareness of women’s issues is particularly noteworthy\. Authors who examine women through the lens of social life, family life, and professional life address the challenges women face in these areas and explore women’s attitudes toward these challenges\. The visibility of women’s experiences in social life within literary works fosters significant awareness regarding women’s issues\. The challenges women face, particularly within the family, are a recurring theme in the works of nearly every female writer\. A woman’s position within the family, the responsibilities she assumes, the conflicts she experiences with other family members, and her approach to overcoming these challenges shape the focus of works where she takes center stage\. Writers who draw inspiration from women hold a mirror to her journey from the confines of the home to the wider world\. This study examines the issues women face within the family through the works of Turkish writers such as Adalet Ağaoğlu, Cihan Aktaş, Fatma Barbarosoğlu, Sibel Eraslan, Naime Erkovan, Ayfer Tunç, and Yıldız Ramazanoğlu writers who have revitalized the short story genre in Turkish literature and and aims to reveal how female writers approach women’s issues within the family framework within the boundaries of the short story genre\.
