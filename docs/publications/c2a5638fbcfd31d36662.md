# Integrating Conversational AI and Interactive Projection Mapping for Cultural Heritage Interpretation: A Framework and Pilot Evaluation

**Autores:** Ponsan Siridechanont; Patiwet Saokong; Teerapong Jatuma; Noppanut Onenaphi; Kannikar Intawong; Kitti Puritat

**Data:** 2026-10-10 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Cultural heritage interpretation requires communicating relationships among physical locations, historical narratives, and symbolic meanings while accommodating diverse visitor information needs\. Conversational artificial intelligence \(AI\) enables flexible, visitor\-initiated access to cultural knowledge, but conversational interfaces alone provide limited spatial grounding\. This study proposes an AI\-supported interactive framework that combines conversational AI and projection mapping as complementary mechanisms within a unified heritage experience\. Conversational AI supports semantic inquiry, while projection mapping spatially connects curated narratives with corresponding locations on a physical heritage model\. The framework was implemented through the Seven Sacred Weeks Interactive Projection Mapping System at Wat Ched Yod, Thailand, with four modes: Tour, AI Conversation, Game, and Exploration\. A mixed\-methods pilot evaluation was conducted with 90 university students using pre\- and post\-test knowledge assessments, the System Usability Scale \(SUS\), and open\-ended questions\. Immediate post\-test knowledge scores were significantly higher than pre\-test scores following exposure to the complete system \(p &lt; 0\.001; Cohen’s d = 1\.416\), and the system achieved a SUS score of 78\. Qualitative findings highlighted spatial visualization, interactive activities, game\-based interaction, and AI\-supported inquiry, while limitations concerned waiting time, participation capacity, and AI response speed\. The findings provide preliminary evidence of the feasibility and usability of coordinating technically distinct but complementary AI and spatial media mechanisms within a visitor\-centered cultural heritage experience\.
