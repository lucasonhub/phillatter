# Protocol for genome\-wide differential methylation using long\-read DNA sequencing in the cortex of a mouse model of Alzheimer’s disease

**Autores:** Ümran Yaman; Emil Karl Gustavsson; Jasmaine Lee; Hermione Ugolini; Hannah L\. Macpherson; Paige Mumford; Frances K\. Wiseman; John Hardy; Dervis A\. M\. Salih

**Data:** 2026-10-09 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Third\-generation sequencing enables direct detection of genome\-wide 5\-methylcytosine without bisulfite conversion\. Here, we present a protocol for generating long\-read DNA sequencing from mouse cortex and performing genome\-wide differential methylation analysis\. We describe steps for high\-molecular\-weight DNA extraction, data generation, and downstream analysis\. We demonstrate the workflow in the App NL\-G\-F mouse model of amyloid pathology and provide a reproducible pipeline that links modified\-base BAM \(modBAM\) files to annotated differentially methylated regions and functional enrichment results\.
