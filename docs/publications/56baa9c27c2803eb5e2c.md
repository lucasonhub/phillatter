# Recent advances in dentistry

**Autores:** Kumar Tathagat Singh; Ahemer Arif Shaikh; Sumit Bhatt; Venkatesh Kulkarni; Vivek Chaudhary

**Data:** 2026-10-18 · **Tipo:** book

## Resumo

Dentistry has entered a transformative era, driven by rapid technological innovation, interdisciplinary research, and a growing emphasis on patient\-centered care\. The past decade has witnessed remarkable progress across diagnostic, therapeutic, and preventive domains, reshaping both clinical practice and academic inquiry\. This book, Recent Advances in Dentistry, seeks to capture these developments in a structured and comprehensive manner, offering readers a panoramic view of the evolving landscape of dental science\. The chapters assembled herein highlight the convergence of digital technologies, biomaterials, nanoscience, and regenerative medicine with traditional dental practice\. From the integration of artificial intelligence and machine learning into diagnostic workflows, to the revolution brought by CAD/CAM systems and 3D printing, dentistry is increasingly defined by precision, personalization, and efficiency\. Advances in smart biomaterials and nanotechnology promise enhanced durability and biocompatibility, while innovations in orthodontics, pediatric dentistry, and caries management continue to refine patient outcomes across age groups\. Equally compelling are the strides made in endodontics and periodontology, where regenerative approaches and novel instrumentation are expanding therapeutic possibilities\. The domain of implantology and esthetic dentistry has been reshaped by digital planning, guided surgery, and minimally invasive techniques, ensuring both functional and aesthetic excellence\. Emerging modalities such as laser therapy, photobiomodulation, robotics, and augmented reality further underscore the dynamic future of dental practice\.
