# Limited incremental value and cross\-cohort transferability of CT radiomics for survival prediction after TACE in hepatocellular carcinoma

**Autores:** Krzysztof Bartnik; Belgutei Tsevegmed

**Data:** 2026-10-13 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

PURPOSE: To evaluate whether pretreatment CT radiomics adds information for overall survival \(OS\) prediction among patients with hepatocellular carcinoma \(HCC\) treated with transarterial chemoembolization \(TACE\), and assess frozen imaging\-model performance in an independent cohort\. METHODS: This retrospective study included 221 WAW\-TACE and 98 HCC\-TACE\-SEG patients with pretreatment arterial and portal venous CT\. Incremental value of a tumor\-radiomics Random Survival Forest score \(R1\) beyond cohort\-specific clinical Cox models \(C0\) was evaluated within each cohort using 5\-fold cross\-validation repeated 10 times\. External validation was restricted to frozen WAW\-trained R1 models \(Top\-K = 5, 10, 15\) applied to HCC\-TACE\-SEG without refitting; the combined C0 \+ R1 model was not externally validated\. RESULTS: WAW\-TACE included 162 deaths and HCC\-TACE\-SEG 86 deaths\. In WAW\-TACE, C0, R1 Top\-K = 15, and C0 \+ R1 achieved C\-indices of 0\.646 ± 0\.043, 0\.593 ± 0\.049, and 0\.639 ± 0\.045, respectively\. Corresponding HCC\-TACE\-SEG estimates were 0\.496 ± 0\.087, 0\.530 ± 0\.072, and 0\.495 ± 0\.094\. Thus, R1 did not consistently improve cohort\-specific clinical reference models\. In frozen external application, Top\-K = 5, 10, and 15 achieved C\-indices of 0\.463 \(95% CI 0\.397\-0\.532\), 0\.535 \(0\.468\-0\.608\), and 0\.528 \(0\.460\-0\.598\), respectively\. CONCLUSION: The evaluated models showed no consistent incremental value for OS prediction within these TACE cohorts and limited frozen\-model discrimination in the clinically distinct HCC\-TACE\-SEG cohort\. These findings do not support clinical use of these models or establish a general limitation of CT radiomics\.
