# Blockchain Based Secured Data Shearing in Healthcare

**Autores:** Mahajan Aarti Karbhari, Prof\. L\.S\. Bhadke, Dr\. H\. B\. Jadhav

**Data:** 2026-10-12 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Healthcare organizations generate a large amount of sensitive patient information such as medical records, test reports, prescriptions, and personal details\. Sharing this information between hospitals, doctors, laboratories, and patients is important, but the existing centralized systems can face problems such as unauthorized access, data modification, privacy loss, and lack of proper tracking\. This research focuses on using blockchain technology to provide a more secure way of sharing healthcare data\. The proposed approach uses blockchain to maintain a secure record of data\-sharing activities and to provide better control over who can access patient information\. The actual medical data is stored outside the blockchain in encrypted form, while important information related to the data and access transactions is recorded on the blockchain\. Access to the data is provided only to authorized users through suitable authentication and access\-control methods\. Smart contracts can be used to check access requests and record the activities performed by different users\. The system also supports removing access when a user&\#x27;s permission is no longer valid\. This helps to reduce unauthorized use of healthcare information and provides a clear record of data access\. The proposed approach is discussed in terms of data security, privacy, access control, integrity, and transparency\. The main aim of this research is to develop a secure and practical healthcare data\-sharing model that gives patients and healthcare organizations better control over sensitive medical information while maintaining reliable and traceable data\-sharing activities\.\.
