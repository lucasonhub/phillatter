# Structural and Optical Properties of Pb1\-x\(Mn,Eu\)xTe Epitaxial Films

**Autores:** 

**Data:** 2026-10-12 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

This study investigates the growth, structural, and optical properties of P b1\-xM nxT e and P b1\-xEuxT e epitaxial films grown on freshly cleaved BaF2 \(111\) substrates by molecular beam epitaxy under a vacuum of 10 \-4 P a with an additional T e vapor source\.The optimal growth conditions were determined as substrate temperature T sub = 573\-640K, source temperature Tsour = 1123\-1173K and condensation rate ϑc = 8 ÷ 12 Å/sec\.Optical absorption spectroscopy and X\-ray diffraction analyses confirmed the formation of single\-phase epitaxial films with high crystalline quality\.For x = 0\.05, the lattice constants were approximately 6\.43 Å for P b1\-xM nxT e and 6\.476 Å for P b1\-xEuxT e\. Optical measurements showed that increasing M n 2\+ and Eu 2\+ concentrations leads to band\-gap widening\.Specifically, the optical band gap at 77K increases from about 0\.21eV for pure P bT e to approximately 0\.44eV for P b1\-xM nxT e and 0\.53eV for P b1\-xEuxT e, exceeding 0\.5eV at europium contents x &gt; 0\.09\.This effect is associated with modifications of the electronic band structure caused by M n \(Eu\) substitution in the P b sublattice, exchange interactions, lattice effects, and the interaction of localized M n 3d and Eu 4f states with band\-edge states\.Additionally, the influence of γ\-irradiation on the structural properties of the films was investigated\.These findings show that compositional engineering of P b1\-xM nxT e and P b1\-xEuxT e epitaxial films enables controllable tuning of the band gap in the mid\-infrared range, making them promising candidates for infrared optoelectronic, thermoelectric, and spintronic device applications\.
