## Descrição do feed

In Real Time: A Reinvention in British Metaphysics 1883\-1928\. Oxford University Press\. 2026French philosopher Henri Bergson had been writing for years when his work exploded into British philosophy\. From 1909 onwards, his popularity surged: Bergsonian thought appears in many later thinkers throughout this book\. This chapter explores some of Bergson&\#x27;s key views on time: he holds that time is non\-linear; he arguably offers a growing block theory; he holds that time brings novelty; and evolutionary novelty is powered by a nisus, the élan vital\. This chapter goes on to chart the spread of Bergson’s work through Britain, via the likes of Victoria Welby, H\. Wildon Carr, and none other than a past Prime Minister—Arthur Balfour\.
