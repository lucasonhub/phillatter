# COMPARATIVE ANALYSIS OF ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE\-BASED APPROACHES TO FORECASTING AND OPTIMIZATION OF COMPLEX SYSTEMS

**Autores:** Dilfuzakhon Abdullayeva

**Data:** 2026-10-13 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Forecasting and optimization form an integrated foundation for the intelligent management of complex systems in industry, logistics, energy, transportation, and service operations\. The interaction of multiple components, changing operating conditions, and interconnected decisions creates valuable opportunities for data\-driven modeling and adaptive resource allocation\. This study presents a comparative literature\-based analysis of statistical forecasting, machine learning, deep learning, mathematical optimization, and reinforcement learning approaches\. Their suitability is examined in relation to temporal patterns, explanatory variables, uncertainty representation, computational requirements, and decision objectives\. Particular attention is given to hybrid architectures that connect probabilistic forecasts with optimization models and continuously updated decisions\. The synthesis establishes a conceptual framework for evaluating forecasting quality alongside operational performance and identifies promising directions for intelligent management of dynamic complex systems\.
