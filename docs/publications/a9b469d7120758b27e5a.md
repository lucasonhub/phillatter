# Hasan, Ali: Virtually Yours: The Cost of AI Companions

**Autores:** 

**Data de descoberta:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** unknown

## Descrição do feed

AI companions can provide enjoyment, comfort, emotional stability, and even personal growth\. This paper argues that “relationships” with AI companions nevertheless risk incurring an ethical cost even when users flourish by ordinary measures and suffer no physical or psychological harm\. The cost in question includes not just the absence of the intrinsic \(non\-instrumental\) good of genuinely interpersonal \(healthy\) relationships, but also the presence of something intrinsically bad, a kind of misfit between one’s affective orientation and the world\. AI companions can encourage ill\-fitting interpersonal attitudes even among users who deny that their companions are real persons; and while sustained engagement with an AI companion might, in principle, be stably maintained as a harmless form of purely fictional or fiction\-like emotional or affective engagement, a number of features of AI companions, I argue, systematically destabilize the fictional frame\.
