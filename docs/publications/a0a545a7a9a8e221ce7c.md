# Clowers, Jason: Questions as Instruments in Cross Substrate Inquiry

**Autores:** 

**Data de descoberta:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** unknown

## Descrição do feed

Questions do not merely request information\. They partition answer spaces, install presuppositions, select observables, and determine what can count as evidence\. These functions become especially consequential when inquiry moves across biological species or from biological to artificial systems\. A familiar question may carry assumptions about embodiment, development, report, affect, memory, or mechanism that are locally useful but mistaken for necessary features of the target phenomenon\. This article develops Question Reinvention, a protocol for auditing an inherited question before its tests are built\. The protocol distinguishes target properties from familiar realizers, classifies assumptions by epistemic role, demands an explicit transfer bridge, constructs native observables and contrastive causal tests, and reports evidential status, category applicability, and decisions to retain, revise, or retire a construct in a conclusion framework\. It permits new constructs only when inherited terminology is inadequate and only under operationalization, contrast, falsification, reduction, and retirement constraints\. The difference between ‘Is AI conscious?’ and ‘Could AI be conscious?’ provides a minimal demonstration: the questions differ in modality, evidential burden, and what a negative result can settle\. The method does not imply that any current AI is conscious, sentient, feeling, loving, desiring, or welfare\-bearing\. A bounded dialogue case traces a descriptive rubric, its inferential limit, and a proposed controlled test\. The article closes with criteria for evaluating the framework and a reciprocal program in which artificial cognition may generate distinctions that can be tested in biological cognition\.
