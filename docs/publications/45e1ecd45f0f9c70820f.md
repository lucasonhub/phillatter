# Liver transplantation for hepatocellular carcinoma: Mechanisms, risk stratification, and evolving treatment landscape

**Autores:** Xue\-Lin Xiang; Yi\-Chi Wu; Zijie Zhang; Xiang\-Kun Zhan; Ying Tong; Junekong Yong; Yi Zhou; Hao Feng; Qiang Xia

**Data:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Hepatocellular carcinoma \(HCC\), remains a leading cause of mortality\.This article addresses the mechanisms underlying post\-transplant HCC recurrence and evaluates current evidence\-based strategies for diagnosis and management\.Based on a targeted narrative review of high\-impact literature published between 2010 and 2025, we synthesize evidence on the interplay between immunosuppression, tumor biology, and host\-related factors driving recurrence\.Using HCC recurrence after liver transplantation as the principal model, we critically examine existing risk stratification systems and surveillance protocols\.We further summarize multimodal therapeutic approaches, ranging from curativeintent local therapies for oligo\-recurrence to systemic treatments for advanced stages, where prognosis remains poor\.Additionally, we discuss the emerging and controversial role of adjuvant therapies in high\-risk populations\.The review highlights areas of consensus, ongoing controversies, and key gaps in knowledge that require further investigation to improve long\-term post\-transplant outcomes\.Particular attention is given to the role of immunosuppressive regimen modulation and emerging biomarker\-driven surveillance strategies aimed at earlier detection and individualized risk prediction in post\-transplant patients to ultimately improve survival and clinical decision\-making\.
