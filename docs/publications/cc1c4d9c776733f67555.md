# University faculty competence deficits in the safe application of artificial intelligence

**Autores:** Mikhail Zamyslov; M\. D\. Kitaygorodskiy

**Data:** 2026-10-14 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

The purpose of the study is to identify the state of university faculty competence in the field of safe application of artificial intelligence technologies \(AI competence\) across its various components and, on this basis, to substantiate the content of a professional development program\. The article clarifies the concept of a teacher’s AI competence as a constituent part of their digital competence, identifies its general user, general pedagogical, and subject\-pedagogical components, and differentiates between the technological limitations of AI systems and the ethical and legal norms governing their use\. The results of a survey involving 44 university teachers are presented\. The scientific novelty of the study lies in clarifying the concept and component structure of university teachers’ AI competence and in obtaining empirical data on its state, including the nature of teachers’ understanding of the ethical risks associated with the use of artificial intelligence\. As a result, it was established that approximately half of the teachers have practical experience in applying AI, while their understanding of AI technologies is limited to the interface level\. Ethical risks are not differentiated by almost half of the respondents; the remainder conceptualize them primarily as data transfer risks, whereas the risks of unreliability and opacity of generated results are underestimated – notably, active users rate the risk of unreliability lower than others\. Based on the identified deficits and a content analysis of existing courses, a modular structure for a professional development program with a risk\-oriented approach to content design is substantiated\.
