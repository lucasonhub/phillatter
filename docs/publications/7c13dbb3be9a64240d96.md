# Cognitive Engagement, Learning Strategies, Adjustment to College and Academic Motivation: A Structural Equation Model on Academic Success Among College Students

**Autores:** Jason D\. Dacutin; Eugenio S\. Guhao

**Data:** 2026-10-17 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

This study examined the relationships among cognitive engagement, learning strategies, adjustment to college, academic motivation, and academic success to determine the best\-fit structural equation model among Bachelor of Elementary Education students in Region XIII \(Caraga\), Philippines\. Anchored in Self\-Regulated Learning Theory and Academic Adjustment Theory, the study employed a quantitative, non\-experimental descriptive\-correlational design involving 470 second\-, third\-, and fourth\-year students from three state universities, selected through proportional stratified random sampling\. Data were collected using five adapted, validated, and reliability\-tested questionnaires and analyzed through descriptive statistics, Pearson correlation, and structural equation modeling\. Results revealed very high levels of cognitive engagement \(M = 4\.27\), learning strategies \(M = 4\.32\), college adjustment \(M = 4\.34\), academic motivation \(M = 4\.32\), and academic success \(M = 4\.31\)\. All four predictors demonstrated significant positive correlations with academic success, with academic motivation exhibiting the strongest relationship \(r = \.519\)\. The final structural model demonstrated excellent fit \(CMIN/DF = \.845, CFI = 1\.000, RMSEA = \.000\) and explained 32\.5% of the variance in academic success\. Academic motivation \(β = \.270, p = \.020\) and learning strategies \(β = \.262, p = \.017\) emerged as significant direct predictors, whereas college adjustment and cognitive engagement showed nonsignificant direct effects\. The findings emphasize the importance of strengthening academic motivation and effective learning strategies through learner\-centered instruction and institutional support programs\. The study supports Sustainable Development Goal 4 \(Quality Education\) by providing evidence to inform educational practices and student success initiatives\. Its sustainability contribution lies in supporting evidence\-based academic planning, student retention, and continuous improvement in higher education\.
