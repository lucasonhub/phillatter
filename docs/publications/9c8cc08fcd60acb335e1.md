# Redefining Audit Efficiency: A Model for Streamlined Financial Reporting in Nigerian Chartered Accounting Practices

**Autores:** Ifeoma E\. Okoli

**Data:** 2026-10-15 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

This critical review examines how Nigerian chartered accounting practices can redefine audit efficiency and streamline financial reporting\. It applies a structured thematic synthesis of 250 DOI\-verified, peer\-reviewed and institutional sources published through 2022, coding the evidence across four dimensions: conceptual definition, causal mechanism, empirical support, and implementation constraint\. Grounded in agency theory, audit\-quality theory, institutional \(isomorphism\) theory, and lean process\-efficiency theory, the synthesis shows that efficiency in Nigerian audit practice follows from standardized workflows, risk\-focused procedures, independent audit\-committee oversight, staff capability, and timely review, while professional judgment continues to protect audit quality where formal controls are weak\. Unlike prior single\-determinant studies of Nigerian audit quality, which typically isolate one governance variable at a time, this review&\#x27;s contribution is an integrative operating model that connects audit inputs, workflow design, review discipline, reporting accuracy, and engagement performance into a single framework, together with four testable propositions and a Nigeria\-specific research agenda for stronger validation, transparency, and institutional comparison\. The review also proposes a repositioned role for chartered accounting firms as active drivers, rather than passive recipients, of digital transformation, continuous auditing, and regulatory reform in Nigeria&\#x27;s financial reporting ecosystem\.
