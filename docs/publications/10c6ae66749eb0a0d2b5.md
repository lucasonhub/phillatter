# Bulgaria, Jamel: The Structural\-Constructivist Synthesis of Affect: Analyzing the Methodology, Empirical Validation, and Functionalist Foundations of the Core Emotion Framework

**Autores:** 

**Data de descoberta:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** unknown

## Descrição do feed

The Core Emotion Framework \(CEF\) advances a structural constructivist synthesis of affect that resolves the long standing divide between discrete and dimensional models of emotion by grounding emotional phenomena in ten universal functional operators distributed across the Head, Heart, and Gut centers\. As described in the manuscript, emotions are conceptualized not as static categories or subjective qualia, but as active regulatory operators that transform interoceptive signals to coordinate adaptive behavior—an approach that aligns with Adolphs and Andler’s methodological functionalism, which treats emotions as latent variables inferred from system level evidence \(“emotions are system\-level phenomena that depend on dynamic network interactions in the brain” and “functionalism about emotions must be empirical and discovery\-based”\)\. The framework integrates etiological and causal role functionalism, mapping evolutionary functions onto algorithmic mechanisms such as Herbert Simon’s interrupt architecture \(“emotions are engaged specifically when normal, ongoing cognitive processes must be interrupted”\)\. The Decalogue of Operators provides a formal somatic architecture, enabling precise modeling of emotional rigidity, flexibility, and clinical dysregulation, including operator level explanations of overthinking loops \(“Sensing and Calculating… become pathologically fused, or ‘stuck together like Velcro’”\)\. Computational implementation is achieved through the TS 6 hybrid schema and the EL 1 v1\.0 lexicon, which represent emotional states as 10 dimensional operator vectors, enabling drift free semantic geometry and similarity space analysis\.17 Empirical validation is underway through an OSF registered, split sample psychometric program employing 100 scenario based items to test the factor structure of the ten operators \(“Total Items = 10 Vignettes × 10 Operators = 100 Items”\)\. Together, these theoretical, computational, and empirical components establish CEF as a unified, multi level blueprint for affective science, clinical intervention, and synthetic emotional systems\.
