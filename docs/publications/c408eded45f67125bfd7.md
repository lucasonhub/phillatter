## Descrição do feed

\_Ancient Philosophy\_ 46 \(2\):461\-489\. 2026Aristotle is not known as a theorist of erotic love, his apparent disinterest contrasting both with Plato’s extensive treatment as well as the intensity of his own interest in friendship\. Yet there is an original account to be uncovered in his scattered remarks on the subject\. Aristotelian eros is rooted in the sexual norms operative in Aristotle’s time—a ‘paiderastic paradigm’—yet is not merely the restatement of classical Greek commonplaces\. This article reconstructs a finely tuned account of Aristotelian eros using the Rhetoric’s framework for understanding emotions\. This descriptive, phenomenological approach stands apart from Plato’s prescriptions to turn our erotic attentions away from the sexual domain, as well as from contemporary philosophers who treat love as an unproblematic and self\-evidently desirable phenomenon\. \(direct link \)
