# Sleep Characteristics in Adolescents with Major Depressive Disorder: Associations with Non\-Suicidal Self\-Injury and Suicidality

**Autores:** Tugce Turan Kaya; Sevay Alşen Güney

**Data:** 2026-10-10 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Background: Sleep disturbances impact outcomes in major depressive disorder \(MDD\)\. This cross\-sectional study evaluated subjective sleep quality and habits in adolescents with MDD and their associations with suicide attempt \(SA\) and non\-suicidal self\-injury \(NSSI\)\.Methods: Sixty\-eight adolescents aged 13–18 years with MDD were classified as NSSI\-only, SA\-only, and combined\. Clinical severity and depressive symptoms were measured with Clinical Global Impression– Severity scale and Children’s Depression Rating Scale–Revised \(CDRS\-R\)\. Self\-harm and suicidality were evaluated using Inventory of Statements About Self\-Injury and Columbia\-Suicide Severity Rating Scale\. Pittsburgh Sleep Quality Index \(PSQI\) and Children’s Sleep Habits Questionnaire \(CSHQ\) assessed past\-month sleep quality and past\-week sleep habits\.Results: Participants had poor sleep quality \(89\.7%\) and disturbed sleep habits \(83\.8%\), without group differences\. CSHQ scores correlated moderately with lifetime NSSI frequency \(r = \.36, P = \.003\), whereas PSQI scores correlated weakly \(r = \.28, P = \.023\)\. PSQI scores were modestly related to past\-month suicidal ideation severity \(r = \.28, P = \.022\)\. In hierarchical regressions controlling for sedative medications and CDRS\-R scores, CSHQ \(β = 0\.299, P = \.022\) and PSQI \(β = 0\.248, P = \.047\) accounted for incremental variance in lifetime NSSI frequency\.Conclusion: Sleep problems are common among adolescents with MDD, suggesting a clinical feature rather than a subgroup\-specific trait\. Sleep disturbance was associated with lifetime NSSI burden beyond depression severity, indicating a clinically relevant assessment domain\. Prospective longitudinal studies should clarify temporal and bidirectional relationships with self\-harm outcomes\.
