# TALABALARDA O‘ZINI\-O‘ZI BOSHQARISH KOMPETENTLIGINING PSIXOLOGIK MAZMUNI VA TARKIBIY KOMPONENTLARI

**Autores:** ERKIN G&\#x27;AYRAT O&\#x27;G&\#x27;LI ISKANDAROV

**Data:** 2026-10-10 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

This article examines the psychological content of students’ self\-management competence, its role in organizing academic activity, and its major structural components\. Self\- management competence is viewed as a complex psychological system that enables students to set meaningful goals, plan their activities, monitor their behavior, evaluate achieved results, and modify their strategies when necessary\. The study considers the motivational\-goal, cognitive, emotional\-regulatory, volitional, reflective, and behavioral components of self\-management competence as interconnected elements of a unified psychological system\. Particular attention is given to the developmental characteristics of the student period, including the formation of independence, responsibility, self\-control, planning skills, and decision\-making abilities\. The article substantiates the idea that a well\-developed self\-management competence contributes to better organization of academic activities, personal maturity, and readiness for independent professional functioning\. Self\-management competence is interpreted as an integrative personal capacity that combines motivational, cognitive, emotional, volitional, and reflective mechanisms in the regulation of behavior and activity\.
