# THE ROLE OF SYNTACTIC ANALYSIS IN NATURAL LANGUAGE PROCESSING AND COMPUTER LINGUISTICS

**Autores:** Nurmuxammedova Nurjaxon

**Data:** 2026-10-14 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

The rapid development of artificial intelligence, machine learning, and digital communication has significantly increased the importance of technologies capable of processing human language\. Every day, large volumes of textual information are generated through websites, scientific publications, social networks, digital libraries, and electronic communication platforms\. Analyzing this information efficiently requires computational methods that can identify not only individual words but also the grammatical relationships that connect them\. Natural language processing \(NLP\) has emerged as an interdisciplinary field that combines computer science, linguistics, statistics, and artificial intelligence to address these challenges\.
