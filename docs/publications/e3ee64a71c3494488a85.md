# The Relationship Between ICT Utilisation and the Effective Management of Teacher Welfare in Junior Schools in Wajir County, Kenya

**Autores:** Issa Kurash Abdille; Fredrick Ngala; Stephen Kipkorir Rotich

**Data:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

ICT integration in education is increasingly widespread, yet its influence on teacher management remains under\-researched, particularly within the Kenyan context\. The purpose of this study was to examine the relationship between ICT utilisation practices and four constructs of effective teacher management in junior schools in Wajir County, Kenya: classroom teaching management, teacher welfare, teacher discipline, and teacher professional development, as well as to determine prevailing ICT utilisation practices in junior schools\. A cross\-sectional convergent mixed\-methods research design was adopted\. 116 junior schoolteachers from Wajir East and Wajir West completed a structured questionnaire aimed at determining the relationship between ICT utilisation and the four components of teacher management\. Fourteen education leaders comprising twelve headteachers and two sub\-county education officials from the two sub\-counties participated in semi\-structured interviews\. Quantitative data were analysed using descriptive statistics, Pearson correlation, and multiple linear regression analysis, while qualitative data were analysed thematically\. The research instrument demonstrated good reliability \(Cronbach’s α = \.857\)\. The findings established a statistically significant positive relationship between ICT utilisation and teacher welfare \(β = 0\.736, p &lt; \.001\)\. Qualitative findings indicated that ICT supports administrative communication, record management, instructional preparation, professional collaboration, and continuous professional development\. However, inadequate infrastructure, unreliable internet connectivity, and inconsistent electricity supply, particularly in remote ASAL schools, constrained integration\. The study concludes that ICT utilisation is critical for improving administrative efficiency, accountability, and welfare service delivery\. It recommends strengthening ICT infrastructure, internet connectivity, teacher digital literacy, and institutional support\.
