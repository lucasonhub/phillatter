# Prevalence of renal impairment in urban adults: A community\-based study from Kerala, India

**Autores:** Geethadevi Madhavikutty; Anju C Mathew;  Radha T\.R\.;  Sreevidhya K\.R\.; Unnikrishnan Ramachandran V; Reji Manjunathan; Netto George Mundadan; Chitra James; Archana Sobha; Kripa Soman

**Data:** 2026-10-12 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Background and objectives Community\-based studies on detecting renal impairment are minimal from India, though early detection could prevent progression to end\-stage renal disease \(ESRD\)\. This study aims to estimate the prevalence of renal impairment among adults aged 30 y or above, and to find out the socio\-demographic, lifestyle, and comorbid factors responsible for low estimated glomerular filtration rate \(eGFR\)\. Methods Renal impairment is a reduction in kidney function, often defined by a low eGFR, an increase in serum creatinine, or proteinuria\. A cross\-sectional, population\-based study was done by the cluster\-sampling method from March 2024 to February 2025, among 1031 participants in Kerala\. The eGFR and albuminuria were estimated using facilities at a multidisciplinary research unit\. Associations of sociodemographic, lifestyle, and comorbid factors with renal impairment were studied\. Results Among the 1031 participants, 24\.9 \- 44\.4% had low eGFR\. The older age group had a higher prevalence of renal impairment \(mean age 62\.2 y\)\. Mean serum creatinine was 1\.03±0\.24 mg/dL \(1\-5 mg/dL\)\. Main comorbidities were diabetes \(40\.5%\) and hypertension \(41\.5%\)\. Regression analysis showed that advanced age \[62\.2 y vs \. 53\.9 y, Odds ratio \(OR\) 1\.92 \(1\.28\-2\.9\)\], being unmarried/widower/divorcee \[2\.7% vs \. 1\.7%, OR 4\.18 \(1\.29\-13\.51\)\], ever detected as a hypertensive \[55\.4% vs\. 34\.1%, OR 2\.97 \(1\.42\-6\.17\)\], and the presence of abdominal obesity \[84\.2% vs\. 55\.2% in males, OR 8\.18 \(2\.76\-24\.21\)\] were significantly associated with the development of renal impairment\. Interpretation and conclusions The prevalence of renal impairment ranged between 24\.9 to 44\.4%\. Advanced age, being unmarried/widower/widow/divorcee, ever detected as a hypertensive, and the presence of abdominal obesity contributed to the development of renal impairment\.
