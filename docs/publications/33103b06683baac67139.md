# 1,2,4‐Benzotriazine‐Based Asymmetric Acceptors with Dual π‐Conjugation Extension Achieve 20\.74% Efficiency in Organic Solar Cells

**Autores:** Wendi Shi; Qiansai Han; Wenkai Zhao; Jie Wang; Ruohan Wang; Guankui Long; Zhaoyang Yao; Chenxi Li; Xiangjian Wan; Yongsheng Chen

**Data:** 2026-10-13 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Core\-asymmetric acceptors, which combine the advantages of electron\-deficient cores and molecular asymmetry, represent a promising yet underexplored design strategy for organic solar cells\. Herein, we designed and synthesized two asymmetric 1,2,4\-benzotriazine\-based acceptors, H\-Th and H\-Th\-FN, both featuring a chlorinated thiophene unit side\-grafted onto the central core\. The electron\-rich thiophene modulates the strong electron deficiency of the triazine core, while effective orbital overlap enhances molecular rigidity\. Notably, H\-Th\-FN incorporates a fluoronaphthalene terminal to achieve dual π\-conjugation extension in the central core and end group, thereby affording a narrower bandgap and weaker electron\-phonon coupling\. This structural strategy optimizes intermolecular packing, suppresses aggregation\-caused quenching, and reduces non\-radiative recombination loss to as low as 0\.213 eV\. As a result, the PM6:H\-Th\-FN binary device achieves a power conversion efficiency of 19\.67%, while the corresponding ternary device achieves an impressive efficiency of 20\.74%\. This work develops a family of 1,2,4\-benzotriazine\-based acceptors for high\-performance organic solar cells, underscoring the advantages of central\-core asymmetry and its broad potential\.
