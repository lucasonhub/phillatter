# BO‘LAJAK O‘QITUVCHILARNING KASBIY\-PEDAGOGIK KOMPETENSIYALARINI RIVOJLANTIRISHDA MANTIQIY FIKRLASHNING ROLI

**Autores:** Rasulov Jasur Saparovich

**Data:** 2026-10-12 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

This article examines the role of logical thinking in developing the professional and pedagogical competencies of future teachers, highlighting its theoretical and practical significance and its contribution to effective teaching\. Particular attention is given to the relationship between professional preparation and the main components of logical thinking, including analysis, comparison, generalization, identification of cause\-and\-effect relationships, evidence\-based reasoning, and the formulation of rational solutions to pedagogical problems\. The article also explores approaches to fostering independent thinking, problem\-solving skills, systematic lesson planning, and informed pedagogical decision\-making among prospective teachers\. The methodological potential of problem\-based learning, pedagogical case analysis, classroom discussions, reflective activities, and practice\-oriented tasks is considered in relation to the development of students’ logical reasoning abilities\. The topic is particularly relevant to improving the intellectual capacity of future teachers, strengthening their professional and pedagogical competencies, and preparing specialists who can respond effectively to the demands of contemporary education\. Developing logical thinking is considered an important component of teacher education because it supports the integration of theoretical knowledge, practical skills, and reflective professional judgment\.
