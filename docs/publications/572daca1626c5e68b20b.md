# School Librarians as Academic Migrants: Information Poverty and Digital Resilience in Cross\-Cultural Information

**Autores:** Nancy Everhart

**Data:** 2026-10-13 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

This study explores school librarians as academic migrants who must adapt to unfamiliar cultural and technological environments\. Drawing on an autoethnographic study conducted during a six\-month Fulbright appointment in China, 2,181 information\-seeking incidents are analyzed through Chatman&\#x27;s Theory of Information Poverty\. Findings reveal how information barriers were addressed through mobile technologies, risk\-taking, and adaptive information practices\. Interpreted through a school librarianship lens, the results demonstrate that digital resilience and intercultural competence are critical professional capacities for school librarians working in international schools, global partnerships, study\-abroad programs, and culturally diverse educational communities\.
