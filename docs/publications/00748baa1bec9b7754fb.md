# Data\-Based Exploration of Gold in Chartered Lands: Combining Remote Sensing and Artificial Intelligence in Hajjah Province, Yemen\.

**Autores:** ebrahim adeb

**Data:** 2026-10-11 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

This study analyzes and collects data to assess gold exploration in uncharted sites of Hajjah province, Yemen, is predominantly caused by the time constraints, dangers, and limits of the exploration\. The province is located in the Arabian\-Nubian shield, which is an under explored site because of the unending logistical constraints and geopolitical issues\. This study is the first to develop a framework for artificial intelligence\-based and context\-driven mineral prospect mapping for regional frontiers\. Remote sensing data from ASTER and Sentinel\-2 were used to establish zone maps for hydrothermal alteration \(areas of historical geological change\) and for various rock type indicators\.This model used density and intersection analyses based on lineament density and digital elevation model \(DEM\) analyses\. A mineral\-system framework was modeled to provide various datasets with the random forest and data\-efficient deep learning mechanisms, with special reference to the sparse deep forest architecture and the imbalanced dataset\. The data and local conditions were adapted through transfer learning and data augmentation within physically defined limits\. The findings support gold prospecting within the alteration\-related and structurally controlled prospecting, which is consistent with the organic models\. Prospecting maps based on probabilistic prospecting and metrics of uncertainty support the prospecting of target areas, which is consistent with the defined risks, and demonstrate for a smart exploration model for under explored terrains\.
