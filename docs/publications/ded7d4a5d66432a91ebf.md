## Resumo

Chagas cardiomyopathy \(ChCM\) affects up to 30% of patients with chronic Chagas disease and lacks reliable biomarkers for severity and conduction abnormalities\. We profiled cardiac autoantibodies using 2\-dimensional gel electrophoresis and mass spectrometry, followed by enzyme\-linked immunosorbent assay validation and machine learning analysis\. Six autoantibodies linked to glycolysis, adenosine triphosphate metabolism, and sarcomere structure distinguished ChCM stages \(area under the curve: 0\.87; 95% CI: 0\.777\-0\.977\) and a mean classification accuracy \(0\.84; 95% CI: 0\.705\-0\.935\)\. These findings suggest that an antibody\-based panel may enable cost\-effective risk stratification and prognostication in ChCM, with prospective validation before clinical application\.
