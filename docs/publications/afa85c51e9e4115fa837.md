# Trends in the Implementation of Forensic Auditing in Colombia

**Autores:** Danna Alexandra Rosales Cañizares; Sara Elizabeth Yela Eraso

**Data:** 2026-10-13 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Forensic auditing represents a relevant tool for the prevention and detection of financial fraud and the strengthening of control processes within organizations; however, in Colombia there are still conceptual, training, technological and methodological challenges that limited its implementation and development\. In this context, the research aimed to analyze the trends related to the implementation of forensic auditing in Colombia, considering its evolution, main methodologies, tools and contributions to the prevention and detection of fraud\. Methodologically, the study was developed under the positivist paradigm, with a quantitative approach, empirical\-analytical method and descriptive type of research, through a systematic review of literature based on the Kitchenham protocol\. The population was constituted by scientific production related to forensic auditing, after applying criteria of inclusion, exclusion and quality studies retrieved through Redalyc, Google Scholar, DOAJ and ScienceDirect, 50 articles published between 2020 and 2025 were selected; for the collection and organization of the information, a data extraction matrix was used\. The results showed trends towards a preventive approach, integration with internal control and risk management, and the incorporation of emerging technologies such as artificial intelligence\. It was concluded that forensic auditing advanced towards preventive, technological, and interdisciplinary approaches to strengthen fraud detection and organizational control\.
