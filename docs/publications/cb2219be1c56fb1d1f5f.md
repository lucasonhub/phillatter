# Влияние искусственного интеллекта на результаты деятельности фирмы: систематический обзор литературы

**Autores:** Дарья Павловна Степанюк; Олеся Олеговна Ярыга

**Data:** 2026-10-15 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

This article presents a systematic review of contemporary empirical studies dedicated to examining the impact of artificial intelligence on firm performance\. The review findings demonstrate that the influence of artificial intelligence on firm activities is not a direct cause\-and\-effect relationship but rather constitutes a complex system mediated by organizational capabilities, dynamic capabilities, and contextual factors\. Based on the conducted analysis, a conceptual model is proposed, illustrating the interrelationship between formative factors, such as technological, organizational, and external ones, artificial intelligence technologies, and firm performance outcomes, including operational, financial, strategic, and sustainability indicators\. The article emphasizes the necessity of transitioning from a narrow technical approach to implementing artificial intelligence technologies to a comprehensive approach that views AI adoption as a complex organizational transformation process, and it identifies promising directions for future research, in particular, exploring the specifics of AI implementation within the Russian business context among small and medium\-sized enterprises \(SMEs\)\.
