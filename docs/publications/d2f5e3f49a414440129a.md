# Machine learning enhanced electrohydrodynamic printing of heteroatom\-doped composite electrodes for high\-performance flexible supercapacitors

**Autores:** Hailong Guo; Kai Li; Zhongxian Wu; Xuchen Yang; Yuzuo Wang; Zhijun Qiao; 阮殿波; Dazhi Wang; Yang Lu

**Data:** 2026-10-10 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Flexible supercapacitors are promising power sources for wearable electronics, but single\-component electrodes often provide insufficient capacitance or poor rate performance\. Integrating materials with different charge\-storage mechanisms while controlling electrode structure remains challenging\. Here, a machine\-learning\-enhanced electrohydrodynamic printing \(MLEP\) strategy was developed to fabricate multi\-material composite electrodes with controlled resolution and thickness\. Petroleum\-coke\-derived activated porous carbon \(APC\) served as the electric double\-layer capacitive framework\. Egg yolk–K 3 \[Fe\(CN\) 6 \]\-derived carbon material \(YFC\) provided additional pseudocapacitive active sites, while PEDOT:PSS improved interfacial contact and electron transport\. A multi\-objective model related the main printing parameters to electrode resolution and thickness, allowing more reliable selection of printing conditions\. The APC/YFC electrode delivered 561\.91 F g −1 at 0\.5 A g −1 \. PEDOT:PSS increased the capacitance of APC from 345\.93 to 377\.11 F g −1 at 1 A g −1 \. The assembled flexible device achieved 214\.65 F g −1 at 0\.5 A g −1 , retained more than 88% of its initial capacitance after 10,000 cycles, and remained stable at bending angles from 0° to 180°\. This work offers a practical route for preparing multi\-component electrodes for flexible energy\-storage devices\.
