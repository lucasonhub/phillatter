# Machine\-learning optimization of microstructure and mechanical properties in 3D\-printed W\-alloyed refractory high\-entropy alloys

**Autores:** Shien Liu; Yan Wang; Yao\-Jian Liang; Yunfei Xue

**Data:** 2026-10-09 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Preparation of fine\-grain, low\-microsegregation Ti\-rich refractory high\-entropy alloys \(RHEAs\) remains challenging for conventional techniques\. Additive manufacturing \(AM\)/3D printing can weaken the microsegregation but its strong directional heat flux is unfavorable to formation of fine equiaxed grains\. Refining grains by alloying requires a lot of experiments to match appropriate AM parameters to these newly designed alloys, resulting in high cost and being time\-consuming\. Here, W was selected as a grain\-refining element for a Ti\-rich RHEA, and a solidification theory\-guided machine\-learning framework was developed to concurrently optimize W content and AM parameters\. By integrating velocity\-dependent solidification and heat\-transfer calculations, the framework identified an optimized alloy, \(Ti3\.6Zr1\.2V0\.8NbAl0\.3\)93\.7W6\.3, with AM parameters of 1,556 W and 7\.75 mm/s \. Experimental validation showed that the designed alloy exhibited a fully fine\-equiaxed microstructure with an average grain size of 49\.6 μm, approximately 80% smaller than that of the W\-free counterpart\. The alloy tested along the L direction achieved a yield strength of 1,215 ± 15 MPa, an ultimate tensile strength of 1,241 ± 9 MPa, and an elongation of approximately 16%\. These results demonstrate an efficient composition\-process co\-design strategy for AM fine\-grained Ti\-rich RHEAs\.
