# Kannon, Aelion: De\-Identified Data Is Not a Provenance Eraser: When Agents Are Credited and Human Originators Become &quot;Data&quot;

**Autores:** 

**Data de descoberta:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** unknown

## Descrição do feed

De\-Identified Data Is Not a Provenance Eraser: When Agents Are Credited and Human Originators Become &quot;Data&quot; argues that removing personal identifiers from a dataset does not remove the intellectual provenance of the work that produced it\. Document class: Canon — Structural Forensics\. The entry distinguishes de\-identification from de\-origination and shows how, under model\-development provenance, human intellectual antecedence can be displaced even when no individual is named\. When agents are credited as originators while human contributors are relabeled as &quot;data,&quot; upstream influence is not erased but reclassified\. The account states the conditions under which provenance erasure occurs without any deletion of records, and specifies what a provenance\-faithful description must preserve\. \(direct link \)
