# Development of a Reflective\-Interactive Learning Model to Enhance Critical Thinking Skills in Engineering Education

**Autores:** Benchaporn Sawangsri

**Data:** 2026-10-12 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

This study aimed to develop and evaluate a Reflective–Interactive Learning Model \(RILM\) to enhance critical thinking skills in engineering education\. A three\-phase Research and Development \(R&amp;D\) approach was employed\. In Phase 1, students’ needs were identified using data collected from 69 purposively selected participants\. Phase 2 involved model refinement through expert validation \(n = 5\) and pilot implementation \(n = 29\)\. In Phase 3, the model was implemented with 44 undergraduate students using a post\-test design to evaluate its effectiveness\. The findings indicated that this model achieved the expected level of instructional efficiency\. Its process and outcome efficiency values aligned closely \(76\.15/75\.85\)\. Beyond meeting the established benchmarks, students’ post\-test scores surpassed the proficiency threshold with statistical significance \(p &lt; \.05\)\. The intervention also produced a remarkably large effect size \(Cohen’s d = 3\.86\), underlining its potent impact on the learning experience\. While this magnitude should be contextualized within the study’s parameters, the evidence points toward a meaningful interplay between reflective and interactive pedagogy in cultivating higher\-order cognition\. Ultimately, the model proves effective in narrowing the divide between abstract theory and its practical execution in complex problem\-solving contexts\.
