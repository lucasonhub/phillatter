# End of Instrumentalism through the lens of a teacher\.

**Autores:** Farangiz Izzatullayeva; Worldly Knowledge Publishing Centre

**Data:** 2026-10-12 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Humans consider themselves being &quot;the most intelligent&quot; among other species\. This is what they think makes them special in the entire universe\. Intelligence and reasoning are what gives them a sense of power, a sense of uniqueness\. However, the arrival of Artificial Intelligence \(AI\) threatened humans to take away the only distinctive power humanity has today\.
