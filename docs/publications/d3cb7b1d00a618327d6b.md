# Color Transfer Methods in Mixed Reality Systems: A Review

**Autores:** Alexander Buchatsky; Daria Kuzmina

**Data:** 2026-10-12 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Background\. One of the unsolved problems in mixed reality systems is the color consistency of embedded virtual objects with their real surroundings\. Existing methods either apply a single global transformation without accounting for lighting geometry, or demand computational resources unavailable in real time\. Aim\. To systematize and critically analyze existing color transfer, illumination estimation and image harmonization methods for mixed reality, including the latest approaches of 2024–2026\. Methods\. Analytical review of scientific publications; comparative classification by operating principle, computational requirements and scope of applicability\. Results\. A nine\-group classification is proposed; an approach to the color consistency of virtual objects based on semantic segmentation and separate correction of lightness \(L\*\) and chromatic \(a\*, b\*\) components is proposed, intended for subsequent software implementation; a comparative analysis of color difference and image quality metrics is conducted, including CIEDE2000, SSIM, PSNR, fMSE and LPIPS\. Novelty\. For the first time, color transfer and image harmonization methods for video\-based mixed reality systems are systematized in a unified review spanning 2001 to 2026\. Theoretical and practical significance\. The proposed classification supports justified selection of color correction methods in mixed reality system design, balancing quality and computational constraints\.
