# Zöllner, Oliver \(ed\.\): Große Sprachmodelle – neue Kommuni­kation? Der Einsatz von KI für Sprach­erwerb und inter­kulturellen Austausch

**Autores:**

## Descrição do feed

This edited volume presents seven empirical studies conducted by students in the summer semester of 2025 at Stuttgart Media University, Germany, as part of the seminar &quot;Empirical Media Research: Digitality and Society\.&quot; The studies explore the application of chatbots in language acquisition, intercultural exchange, and everyday settings\. By compiling and publishing these course\-based findings, the volume aims to enhance transparency in teaching and facilitate the broader dissemination of insights\. This aligns with the principles of the Scholarship of Teaching and Learning \(SoTL\) as outlined by Felten \(2013\), which have recently been revisited in higher education pedagogy debates\. The individual contributions examine the following topics: \(1\) Artificial intelligence as a possible substitute model for foreign language learning; \(2\) The impact of AI on language\-learning motivation among students on exchange programs; \(3\) Learning and using languages with the support of artificial systems; \(4\) The role of chatbots in foreign language acquisition and the labor market integration of refugees; \(5\) The use of language models in intercultural communication; \(6\) The effects of chatbot deployment in public authorities on individuals with limited or no proficiency in the German language; \(7\) Linguistic anthropomorphism in chatbots and the question of their trustworthiness\. \(direct link \)
