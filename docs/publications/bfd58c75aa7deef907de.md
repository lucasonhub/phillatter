# Visualization of Solitary Waves Using a Multistep Predictor\-Corrector Algorithm

**Autores:** Özlem Ersoy Hepson; Bülent Saka; İdris Dağ

**Data:** 2026-10-12 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

In this paper, we combine the cubic B\-spline Galerkin method with the predictor\-corrector method to get the numerical method for solving the RLW equation\(RLWE\)\. Thus fully\-integration of the RLWE is managed by employing the time integrator of the four step explicit Adams\-Bashforth as predictor and the four step implicit Adams\-Moulton as corrector and the cubic B\-spline Galerkin method for spatial discretization\. The performance of this new method is carried out studying the single solitary wave, wave undulation and wave generation and findings of error norm L∞ and numerical conservation laws are compared with exact solutions and those already published\.
