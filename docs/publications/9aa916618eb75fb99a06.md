# Electrocoagulation and electro\-oxidation treatment of industrial wastewater from the Nigerian bottling company: Optimisation and performance evaluation

**Autores:** Sunny Chukwuemeka Eke; Henry Chijioke Uzoma; Utoh Chukwudi Promise; Okoli Chineye Mary\-Cynthia; Micheal Abimbola Oladosu; Moses Adondua Abah; Olaide Ayokunmi Oladosu; Kelechi Asogwa; Timilehin Elijah Ayoola

**Data:** 2026-10-09 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

InformationIndustrial wastewater from beverage manufacturing contains diverse pollutants that frequently exceed regulatory discharge limits, necessitating advanced and cost\-effective treatment technologies\.This study investigated the effectiveness of electrocoagulation \(EC\), electro\-oxidation \(EO\), and a hybrid EC\-EO process for treating wastewater collected from the Nigerian Bottling Company \(NBC\), Owerri, Nigeria\.Aluminium electrodes were used for EC \(optimum time: 20 min; current density range: 4\.0\-10\.4mA/cm²\) and iron electrodes for EO \(optimum time: 30 min; current density range: 4\.75\-12\.36mA/cm²\)\.At optimal conditions, EC achieved removal efficiencies of 100% for lead, 97\.02% for copper, 97\.35% for nickel, 96\.54% for turbidity, 91\.45% for total dissolved solids \(TDS\), and 100% for chemical oxygen demand \(COD\)\.EO demonstrated comparable performance: 100% lead, 97\.35% copper, 84\.71% nickel, 96\.79% turbidity, 89\.24% TDS, and 100% COD removal\.The hybrid EC\-EO process effectively removed heavy metals and physical pollutants but consistently elevated COD by 170\-1100%, attributed principally to iron\-electrode fouling\.Individual EC and EO processes reduced contaminant concentrations to within Nigerian Environmental Standards and Regulations Enforcement Agency \(NESREA\) discharge limits, confirming their viability as sustainable and scalable treatment technologies for beverage industry effluents\.
