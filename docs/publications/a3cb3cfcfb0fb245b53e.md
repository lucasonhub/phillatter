# THE EL\-RAKHAWI COMPREHENSIVE BIOLOGICAL ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE FRAMEWORK: UNIFIED MULTI\-OMIC FOUNDATION MODELS FOR CAUSAL DISEASE MODELING AND PERSONALIZED THERAPEUTIC DISCOVERY

**Autores:** mohamed kamal arafa el\-rakhawi

**Data:** 2026-10-15 · **Tipo:** book

## Resumo

The El\-Rakhawi Comprehensive Biological AI \(ECB\-AI\) Framework revolutionizes medical research by transcending fragmented, correlational models\. It introduces a Unified Multi\-Omic Foundation Model mapping genomics, proteomics, and clinical data into a topologically protected Riemannian manifold\. Its core innovation, the Causal Biological Dynamics Engine, utilizes Neural ODEs to model disease as continuous trajectories, enabling accurate counterfactual therapeutic simulations\. Coupled with an In\-Silico Personalized Therapeutic Optimizer, it evaluates treatments on patient\-specific digital twins to steer disease states toward health\. To overcome data silos, it employs Privacy\-Preserving Federated Biological Learning with homomorphic encryption, ensuring global collaboration without compromising patient privacy\. Performance is quantified by the El\-Rakhawi Biological Predictive Fidelity Metric \(EBPFM\), yielding bits per Joule to link causal predictive power directly to thermodynamic efficiency\. ECB\-AI accelerates personalized drug discovery for intractable diseases while upholding strict ethical and privacy standards\.
