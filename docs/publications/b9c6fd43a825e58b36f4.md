# Howell, Robert J\.: Assessing Subjective Physicalism

**Autores:** 

**Data de descoberta:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** unknown

## Descrição do feed

In Consciousness and the Limits of Objectivity: The Case for Subjective Physicalism\. Oxford: Oxford University Press\. pp\. 152\-173\. 2013Two different versions of Subjective Physicalism are considered\. The preferred form maintains that a completed scientific picture of the world would mention all the properties there are but they would not convey a full grasp of them\. Important “aspects” of physical properties will be left out\. The distinction between aspects and properties is discussed and defended\. Subjective Physicalism is then compared to other views, such as phenomenal concept theories, indexical views, and type\-B physicalist views in general\. Several objections are then considered, leading to a description of the way in which an acquaintance theory can account for standing phenomenal beliefs\. It is argued that subjective physicalism cannot claim some of the benefits of more immodest physicalisms, but that it ultimately should be preferred over dualism because of concerns about mental causation\.
