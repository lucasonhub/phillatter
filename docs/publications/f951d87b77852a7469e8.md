# BOSHLANG‘ICH SINF O‘QUVCHILARIDA SUN’IY INTELLEKT VOSITALARI ASOSIDA INDIVIDUAL TA’LIMNI SHAKLLANTIRISHNING PEDAGOGIK IMKONIYATLARI

**Autores:** Alisher Yuldashev

**Data:** 2026-10-12 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

This article explores the pedagogical potential of using artificial intelligence tools to develop individual learning pathways for primary school pupils, taking into account their educational needs, cognitive abilities, and individual learning pace\. Particular attention is given to adaptive learning, the personalization of learning tasks, the analysis of learning outcomes, and the timely provision of pedagogical support\. The study emphasizes the importance of maintaining the teacher’s leading role in integrating artificial intelligence into primary education, considering children’s developmental and psychological characteristics, ensuring digital safety, and supporting independent thinking\. It also examines how the purposeful and pedagogically grounded use of artificial intelligence can improve learning effectiveness, strengthen pupils’ motivation, and support the educational development of every child\.
