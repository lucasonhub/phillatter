# STRUKTUR DAN KAIDAH KEBAHASAAN TEKS LAPORAN HASIL OBSERVASI TULISAN SISWA KELAS X SMA NEGERI 3 MUARO JAMBI

**Autores:** Tiara Amanta Wisesa; Vonny Dahayu Amanda; Afnita

**Data:** 2026-10-14 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

This study describes the structure and linguistic features of observation report texts written by tenth\-grade students of SMA Negeri 3 Muaro Jambi\. The problem addressed is that students&\#x27; observation reports often exhibit incomplete structure, inconsistent labeling, and non\-standard language use\. The purpose is to analyze the completeness of text structure, the use of linguistic features, and spelling and standard language errors\. A descriptive qualitative approach with content analysis was employed\. Data consisted of 28 handwritten observation report texts from an online assignment dated 9 September 2026\. A coding sheet was used to assess structure, labels, linguistic features, and spelling\. Results show that all texts \(100%\) functionally contain the three main structural parts: general statement, description of parts, and closing\. However, only 26 texts \(92\.9%\) include an explicit benefit description\. Only 18 texts \(64\.3%\) write structural labels completely and accurately\. Linguistic features such as definition sentences, relational verbs, technical terms, and conjunctions are present, but non\-standard abbreviations \(e\.g\., yg, dn, tmpt\), mislabeled sections, and first\-person perspective are still found\. Structural completeness does not automatically indicate text quality\. Therefore, learning should emphasize content detail, objectivity, and standard spelling\.
