# Full Factorial Experiment: From Modeling Motor Learning and Motor Ability Development to Model\-Oriented Management of the Pedagogical Process

**Autores:** О\. В\. Іващенко; О\. М\. Худолій; Mykola Khudolii

**Data:** 2026-10-12 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Background\. Full factorial experiments \(FFE, 2ᵏ\) are widely used to estimate main effects and interactions and to construct mathematical models of multifactorial processes\. However, the methodological function of FFE extends beyond statistical analysis when experimental models are incorporated into pedagogical control, programming, and management of the teaching and training process\. The mechanisms underlying this transition require conceptual and methodological clarification\. Objectives\. To reconstruct the methodological system for using full factorial experiments \(2ᵏ\) in modeling motor learning and motor ability development and to identify the mechanisms of transition from an experimental model to pedagogical control, programming, and system\-level management of the teaching and training process\. Materials and Methods\. The study was conducted as a conceptual\-methodological narrative review with a retrospective reconstruction of a research program\. Dissertation studies were analyzed as integrated methodological constructions, while related publications were used to specify individual experimental and analytical components\. The reconstruction focused on the sequence from controlled multifactorial experimentation and mathematical modeling to state recognition, pedagogical decision\-making, programming, and system\-level organization of the teaching and training process\. Results\. The reconstruction showed that FFE 2ᵏ functions as the experimental core of a model\-oriented methodology rather than as a complete management system\. Regression models describe the dependence of outcomes on controlled factors and therefore perform the function of models of influence, whereas discriminant and classification models identify the state formed after pedagogical or training interventions\. Their integration provides the informational basis for pedagogical decision\-making\. The transition from local optimization to programming occurs when an effective regime is incorporated into a sequence of interventions that takes into account the current state of the system, the target subsequent state, and the temporal structure of immediate, delayed, and cumulative effects\. At the system level, these elements form a closed functional circuit linking experiment, modeling, state identification, decision\-making, programming, control, and adjustment\. Conclusions\. The methodological role of FFE 2ᵏ is determined not by the factorial design itself but by the way the resulting mathematical model is incorporated into subsequent pedagogical decision\-making\. The reconstructed methodological system can be represented as the sequence: controlled factorial space → FFE 2ᵏ → mathematical model of influence → state identification → pedagogical decision → programming → control → system\-level management\. This framework clarifies the transition from local experimental optimization to model\-oriented organization of motor learning and motor ability development\.
