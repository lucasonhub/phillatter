# Applications of Neutrosophic Topology in the Indian Context: A Review and Emerging Research Directions

**Autores:** N\. Raksha Ben; G\. Helen Rajapushpam

**Data:** 2026-10-12 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

AbstractNeutrosophic topology is an extension of classical topology designed to represent informationcharacterized by truth, indeterminacy, and falsity simultaneously\. Unlike classical mathematicalmodels, which generally assume that an element either belongs or does not belong to a set,neutrosophic approaches permit the coexistence of partial truth, uncertainty, and contradiction\. Thisfeature makes neutrosophic topology potentially useful for modelling complex systems in whichinformation is incomplete, inconsistent, imprecise, or dynamically changing\. India, with its largeand heterogeneous population, geographically diverse environment, rapidly expanding digitalinfrastructure, and complex decision\-making systems, provides several important contexts in whichsuch mathematical frameworks may be investigated\. This article reviews the development ofneutrosophic topology and examines its potential and emerging applications in the Indian context\.Particular attention is given to geographical information systems, medical diagnosis, data mining,decision\-making, smart\-city management, environmental monitoring, agricultural informationsystems, transportation networks, and information systems\. Existing Indian contributionsdemonstrate that neutrosophic topological structures are increasingly being studied in connectionwith data analysis and decision problems\. The article also identifies limitations in current research,particularly the gap between theoretical topological constructions and validated real\-world datasets\.Finally, several future research directions are proposed, including integration with geographicinformation systems, artificial intelligence, Internet\-of\-Things data, remote sensing, healthcareanalytics, and multi\-criteria decision\-making\. The study concludes that neutrosophic topology offersa promising mathematical framework for representing uncertainty and inconsistency in Indian socio\-technical and environmental systems, provided that future research emphasizes empirical validation and computational implementation\. Keywords: Neutrosophic topology; neutrosophic sets; uncertainty; indeterminacy; fuzzy topology; GIS; decision making; data mining; India; mathematical modelling\.
