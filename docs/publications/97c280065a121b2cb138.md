# Evaluation of Antioxidant, Anticholinesterase, and Antiproliferative Activities of Sparganium erectum Extracts

**Autores:** Nuh Korkmaz; Mustafa Tamer Uzun; Мustafa Sevindik

**Data:** 2026-10-12 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

In the present study, the antioxidant capacity, cholinesterase\-inhibitory activity, and antiproliferative effects of Sparganium erectum extracts were investigated\. Antioxidant analyses showed that the ethanol extract had a total antioxidant status \(TAS\) of 2\.925±0\.023 mmol/L, a total oxidant status \(TOS\) of 10\.116±0\.042 µmol/L, and an oxidative stress index \(OSI\) of 0\.346±0\.003\. For the methanol extract, these values were recorded as 2\.491±0\.039 mmol/L, 12\.889±0\.056 µmol/L, and 0\.517±0\.006, respectively\. The cholinesterase inhibition assays revealed that the ethanol extract inhibited acetylcholinesterase \(AChE\) and butyrylcholinesterase \(BChE\) with IC₅₀ values of 79\.33±3\.36 µg/mL and 148\.56±3\.86 µg/mL, respectively\. The corresponding IC₅₀ values for the methanol extract were 93\.70±2\.32 µg/mL for AChE and 181\.03±1\.86 µg/mL for BChE\. Galantamine, used as the reference inhibitor, exhibited IC₅₀ values of 7\.65±0\.35 µg/mL against AChE and 17\.89±0\.22 µg/mL against BChE\. In the antiproliferative assay, both extracts decreased the viability of A549 lung cancer cells in a concentration\-dependent manner\. At the highest tested concentration, the ethanol extract produced a greater reduction in optical density than the methanol extract\. These findings indicate that the ethanol extract exhibited stronger antioxidant, cholinesterase\-inhibitory, and antiproliferative activities than the methanol extract\. Overall, S\. erectum extracts exhibited measurable antioxidant, cholinesterase\-inhibitory, and antiproliferative activities, supporting further investigation of their bioactive constituents\.
