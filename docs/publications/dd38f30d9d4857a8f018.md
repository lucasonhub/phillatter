# Association between digital agricultural technology promotion and farmers’ adoption of extreme climate adaptation technologies: evidence from chili pepper farmers in Guizhou Province, China

**Autores:** Hongjuan Gao; Han Huiqing

**Data:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Adaptation technologies are important for reducing the risks associated with extreme climatic events, particularly in mountainous areas where complex topography can increase agricultural vulnerability\. Digital Agricultural Technology Promotion \(DATP\) provides an increasingly important channel through which farmers access information related to climate adaptation technologies\. However, empirical evidence on the association between DATP and farmers’ adoption of such technologies in mountainous areas remains limited\. Using survey data from 846 chili pepper farmers in Guizhou Province, China, this study employs baseline regression, mediation analysis, moderation analysis, and heterogeneity analysis to examine the association between DATP and farmers’ adoption of extreme climate adaptation technologies\. The results show that DATP is significantly and positively associated with both adoption degree and sustained adoption willingness\. Mediation analysis identifies three potential pathways consistent with this association: broader information access channels, greater information trust, and stronger information application ability\. Moderation analysis further shows that benefit and risk expectation confirmation significantly condition the association between DATP and adaptation behavior\. Heterogeneity analysis indicates that the positive association is strongest for water resource management and biological control technologies and is relatively stronger among pure farmers, farmers with higher digital literacy, and large\-scale chili farmers\. These findings highlight the relevance of information access, information credibility, digital capabilities, and farmer heterogeneity in understanding digital approaches to climate adaptation in mountainous agricultural areas\. The findings provide empirical evidence for developing differentiated digital strategies to support farmers’ climate adaptation\.
