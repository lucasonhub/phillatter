# Development And Validation of Questionnaire to Assess the Knowledge Attitude and Practice About Unilateral Spatial Neglect Post Stroke Among Physiotherapist

**Autores:** 

**Data:** 2026-10-09 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Background: Unilateral spatial neglect \(USN\) is a disabling post\-stroke condition which is described as an impairment of perception, attention and behaviour that affects the contralateral side of the cerebral lesion leading to impaired functional outcomes and quality of life, hence early identification and appropriate rehabilitation are crucial\.Despite of the increased incidence of USN in post stroke, limited evidence exists regarding Indian physiotherapists&\#x27; knowledge, attitudes, and practices \(KAP\) towards USN\.Objective: This study aimed to develop and validate a structured questionnaire assessing the KAP of Indian physiotherapists in post\-stroke USN\.Methods: The study was conducted in 2 phases, in phase I questionnaire development and content validation by 20 expert physiotherapists across India was done\.In phase II, validation of the tool was done through pilot study of 26 physiotherapists using convenient sampling method\.The statistical analysis was performed using SPSS 23 and reliability of questionnaire was obtained using Cronbach&\#x27;s Alpha\.Results: The questionnaire demonstrated excellent reliability: knowledge \(a = 0\.958\), attitude \(a = 0\.913\), and practice \(a = 0\.958\)\.EFA supported construct validity with KMO values &gt; 0\.8 \.Conclusion: This study presents a statistically valid and reliable questionnaire to assess USN related knowledge, attitude and practice among physiotherapist\.\.
