# ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE IN RECOMBINANT BIOMANUFACTURING: INTEGRATING BIOINFORMATICS AND INDUSTRIAL BIOTECHNOLOGY FOR NEXT\-GENERATION THERAPEUTICS

**Autores:** Yogesh R\.; Senthilrajan D\.; Sivasankari Madheswaran; Srividhya R\.

**Data:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Artificial intelligence \(AI\) is emerging in recombinant biomanufacturing, where data\-driven approaches are used to optimize complex biological systems\. This paper reviews the integration of artificial intelligence, bioinformatics and industrial biotechnology in the recombinant production process from genetic design to large\-scale processing\. These approaches enhance protein engineering through structure prediction, codon optimization and functional analysis\. In upstream processing, machine learning is used to support strain development, media optimization and adaptive control strategies to improve yield and product quality\. For downstream operations, efficient purification is achieved through AI\-based modelling of chromatographic processes and monitoring critical quality attributes in real time\. This is further supported by integration with Quality by Design \(QbD\), Process Analytical Technology \(PAT\) and digital twins, enabling continuous manufacturing and real\-time release testing \(RTRT\)\. Despite these gains, challenges remain, including data limitations, model interpretability, and regulatory constraints\. Overall, AI\-driven bioprocessing represents a transformative approach with the potential for efficient, scalable and next\-generation biopharmaceutical production\.
