# Context\-Aware Edge Computing for Service Response and Affective Interaction Design in Smart Home IoT Systems

**Autores:** Xiang Li; Yue Wang; Yaxin Chen

**Data:** 2026-10-14 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Smart home Internet of Things \(IoT\) systems require service\-response decisions that account for uncertainty, device availability, service risk, and edge latency rather than relying solely on activity recognition\. To address this, we propose an edge\-constrained context\-aware service\-response framework\. It integrates activity prediction, recognition confidence, device state, service risk, and context into a service\-oriented context state\. An uncertainty\-aware policy then selects auto, ask, or none\. Experiments on Center for Advanced Studies in Adaptive Systems \(CASAS\)\-Aruba and Activity Recognition with Ambient Sensing \(ARAS\) compare the method with traditional Human Activity Recognition \(HAR\), deep HAR, lightweight edge HAR, rule\-based, context\-aware reasoning, and uncertainty\-removal baselines\. Macro\-F1, Service Response Accuracy \(SRA\), and Edge Response Latency \(ERL\) are primary metrics, with Ask\-F1 and Auto Error as supplementary indicators\. Results show improved response action consistency under rule\-mapped labels while maintaining context recognition and edge\-side decision\-making within latency constraints\. Response labels are rule\-constructed and do not reflect real user preferences\. Further validation with real service logs and long\-term user feedback is needed\.
