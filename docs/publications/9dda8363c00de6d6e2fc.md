# Proceedings of the 20th International Scientific and Practical Conference «Science and Practice: Implementation to Modern Society»

**Autores:** 

**Data:** 2026-10-09 · **Tipo:** book

## Resumo

The agricultural sector must meet increasing food demand using fewer natural resources and increase its resilience to climate\-related shocks\.In this transformation, green agriculture refers to resource efficiency and ecosystem integrity, while digital commerce means transporting agricultural products to international markets in a traceable, fast, and reliable manner\.This study examines the Industry 5\.0 approach through the relationship between green agriculture and digital commerce\.Based on a conceptual assessment of highly cited literature and policy documents, it is argued that digital infrastructure, farmer competence, precision manufacturing, electronic certification, and environmental verification should be designed together\.The study&\#x27;s central argument is that Turkey&\#x27;s ability to transform its green agriculture capacity into a sustainable export advantage through digital trade depends less on the speed of technology acquisition and more on human\-centered governance and measurable environmental outcomes\.
