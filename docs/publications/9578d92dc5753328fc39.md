# MONITORAMENTO DE INTEGRIDADE ESTRUTURAL EM UM EDIFÍCIO UTILIZANDO MACHINE LEARNING

**Autores:** Carlos Daniel Oliveira Vitório; Simone Silva Frutuoso de Souza

**Data:** 2026-10-14 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

This work presents the development of an intelligent system for structural health monitoring in buildings using machine learning\. An artificial neural network Multi\-Layer Perceptron \(MLP\) was implemented with the backpropagation algorithm, employing simulated vibration signals as input data\. The system was trained on a dataset of 1,400 signals divided into seven classes, including the normal condition and six types of faults\. After training, the network achieved 98,43% accuracy in classifying the test samples, demonstrating high efficiency and generalization capability\. The study highlights the potential of artificial neural networks in structural fault diagnosis, contributing to the development of intelligent monitoring systems that promote safety and durability in civil structures\.
