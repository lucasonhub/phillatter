# Coherence\-Driven Intelligence, Book II: Coherence Geometry and Modern Machine Learning\. A Source Theory of Learning Systems

**Autores:** B\. Petersen

**Data:** 2026-10-17 · **Tipo:** book

## Resumo

Coherence\-Driven Intelligence, Book II: Coherence Geometry and Modern Machine Learning\. A Source Theory of Learning Systems develops the source\-to\-endpoint bridge of the Coherence\-Driven Intelligence \(CDI\) framework\. The book presents a finite coherence\-memory Source description of learning systems and shows how familiar machine\-learning objects and architectures may arise as endpoint projections of retained Source structure\. It develops Source presentations, local coherence views, closure maps, target geometry, basin readout, projection equivalence, and projection loss, and uses these structures to distinguish the organization of memory from its downstream endpoint representation\. The framework is applied across a broad range of contemporary learning systems, including deep and recurrent networks, convolution, attention, transformers, mixture\-of\-experts architectures, associative and energy\-based models, autoencoders, diffusion models, self\-supervised and contrastive learning, graph neural networks, retrieval\-augmented systems, state\-space models, reinforcement learning, continual learning, meta\-learning, and composite AI systems\. The volume also develops source\-transparent memory operations including replay, editing, seam and margin diagnostics, growth, pruning, repair, and reclosure\. The later part of the book moves beyond architecture\-by\-architecture catalogue coverage and develops a finite structural grammar for learning operations\. It culminates in the Finite Machine\-Learning Projection Theorem, which establishes finite representational universality for the stated class of finite typed machine\-learning systems: their operational structure can be realized within the finite CDI framework and their declared endpoint observables recovered through projection\. The result does not, by itself, assert complete\-Source representability, complete\-Source dynamical descent, persistent admissible Source lifting, or equal efficiency or utility across architectures\. This volume is Book II of the Coherence\-Driven Intelligence series\. Book I develops the mathematical foundations of CDI, while Book III constructs source\-explicit intelligent systems directly through the WaveModules reference realization\.
