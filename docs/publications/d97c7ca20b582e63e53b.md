# Du, Nuoheng: When AI Appears to Make Moral Claims: Personality Attribution and the Ethics of Design

## Descrição do feed

\_Journal of Ethics and Emerging Technologies\_ 37 \(1\)\. 2027Conversational AI may elicit a sense of moral obligation without establishing that the system has the standing to demand anything\. Building on a phenomenological account of initial personality attribution, this letter distinguishes the subject’s attribution, its apparent bearer, and the locus of moral accountability\. Simulated distress and AI apologies illustrate opposite directions of the same possible mismatch\. The resulting design responsibilities depend on foreseeable effects and the availability of meaningful accountability\.
