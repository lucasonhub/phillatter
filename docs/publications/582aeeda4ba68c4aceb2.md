# IMPLEMENTASI GAMIFIKASI DIGITAL DALAM PEMBELAJARAN PENDIDIKAN AGAMA ISLAM UNTUK PENGUATAN KETERLIBATAN MURID DI SD NEGERI 2 TUNGKAL ILIR

**Autores:** Meli Puspita; Cukup Islamiarso; Mardiah Astuti; Yuniar

**Data:** 2026-10-13 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

The implementation of digital gamification in Islamic Religious Education \(IRE\) learning represents an instructional approach that can provide a more interactive learning experience while supporting student engagement at SD Negeri 2 Tungkal Ilir\. The study employed a qualitative approach with a case study design\. Data were collected through observations of learning activities, interviews with the IRE teacher and students, and documentation of learning activities involving digital gamification\. Data were analyzed interactively through data condensation, data display, and conclusion drawing\. Data validity was established through source and technique triangulation\. The findings indicate that digital gamification was implemented by integrating game elements into IRE learning, including points, interactive quizzes, challenges, rewards, and feedback\. These elements provided a more interactive learning experience and created opportunities for students to participate actively in learning activities\. Student engagement was demonstrated through active participation, attention to learning materials, willingness to answer questions, involvement in completing challenges, and enthusiasm during learning activities\. The implementation of digital gamification also encouraged interaction between teachers and students as well as among students throughout the learning process\. However, its implementation faced several challenges, particularly limited access to digital devices, unstable internet connectivity, differences in students’ digital literacy skills, and learning time management\. The findings suggest that digital gamification can serve as an instructional strategy in Islamic Religious Education learning to support student engagement when it is designed in alignment with learning objectives, students’ characteristics, technological availability, and the school context\.
