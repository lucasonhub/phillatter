Description: Víctor Ezequiel Eginini Rodríguez is an independent researcher and author developing three interconnected theoretical frameworks: Intrinsic Deterministic Monism \(MDI\), the Intrinsic Symmetric Theory of Information \(TSII\), and the Theory of Unification of Intrinsic Information Fields \(TUCII\)\.
