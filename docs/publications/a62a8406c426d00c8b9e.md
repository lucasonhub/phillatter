# NuXLApp : Interactive data analysis and visualization for protein‐nucleic acid crosslinking mass spectrometry

**Autores:** Arslan Siraj; Sergei A\. Moshkovskii; Tom David Müller; Henning Urlaub; Oliver Kohlbacher; Timo T\. Sachsenberg

**Data:** 2026-10-15 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Protein\-nucleic acid \(protein\-Nu\) interactions are crucial for many cellular processes and biological functions\. Crosslinking mass spectrometry \(XL\-MS\) has emerged as a powerful method for identifying these interactions\. However, the analysis of protein\-Nu XL\-MS data requires an integrated workflow from raw data processing to results inspection and validation\. Existing workflows for protein\-Nu XL\-MS often require switching between tools and considerable manual processing\. Here, we introduce NuXLApp, an interactive web\-based application for data processing and results visualization in protein\-Nu XL\-MS studies\. NuXLApp integrates the protein\-Nu crosslink search engine &quot;NuXL,&quot; machine\-learning models for peptide property prediction, and interactive result visualization within a reproducible workflow\. It enables rapid validation of false discovery rate\-controlled crosslink spectrum match\-level identifications and facilitates collaboration and intuitive exploration of experimental results\. In addition, NuXLApp generates a spectral library from identified protein\-Nu crosslinks to enable quantitative analysis of crosslinks using data\-independent acquisition \(DIA\)\. NuXLApp supports discovery\-driven identification of protein\-nucleic acid crosslinks and enables DIA\-based follow\-up experiments for their quantitative analysis\. NuXLApp is open\-source and available via the OpenMS WebApps platform at https://nuxl\.webapps\.openms\.de/\.
