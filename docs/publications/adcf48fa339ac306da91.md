# Organised instruction as a fragile space of minority\-language contact: Slovene in Croatia during the COVID\-19 pandemic

**Autores:** Barbara Riman; Mojca Medvešek

**Data:** 2026-10-14 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Introduction This article examines organised Slovene language instruction in Croatia as an institutionally mediated space of minority\-language contact in a context of uneven intergenerational transmission and limited use of Slovene within the family and community\. It focuses on the conditions for continuing organised instruction and maintaining learners&\#x27; regular contact with Slovene during COVID\-19\-related restrictions\. Methods The empirical analysis draws on an online survey conducted among teachers of Slovene in Croatia in 2021 \( N = 18\), complemented by a follow\-up questionnaire completed by eight teachers in 2026\. The follow\-up was used as a retrospective qualitative complement, rather than as a second measurement point, to contextualise the 2021 findings through teachers&\#x27; accounts of pandemic\-related disruptions and adaptations\. Results The findings indicate that the formal continuation of instruction during the pandemic did not necessarily ensure learners&\#x27; regular contact with Slovene or sustained opportunities to use the language\. Such contact depended on interrelated institutional, organisational, pedagogical, social, and communicative conditions\. Discussion The article conceptualises this dependence as a form of structural fragility: instruction may continue formally even as opportunities for regular language contact are disrupted\. The case of Slovene in Croatia illustrates the important but contingent role of organised instruction where intergenerational language transmission is limited or uneven\.
