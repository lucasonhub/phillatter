# Singh, Mayank: Reality = Existence = Being: Without an Ultimate Reality

**Autores:** 

**Data de descoberta:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** unknown

## Descrição do feed

Reality, existence, and being are commonly treated as separate philosophical questions\. Reality is asked about as what is real, existence as the fact of being, and being as the fundamental condition of what is\. This separation can be useful for conceptual investigation, but it can also create an ontological division that may not exist in what is itself\. This paper proposes the formulation Reality = Existence = Being\. The equation does not establish a single substance, a final foundation, or an ultimate reality\. It expresses a simpler recognition: reality, existence, and being can be understood as different conceptual recognitions of what is\. At the same time, this formulation does not lead to the conclusion that there is an ultimate reality behind existence\. There is no final reality waiting to be discovered beyond what exists\. Reality does not stand behind existence, existence does not point toward a final metaphysical layer, and being does not become a hidden substance underlying everything\. What exists can be investigated without assuming that investigation must eventually arrive at an ultimate ground\. Reality has no final conceptual endpoint\. It has depth, expression, relation, movement, and further possibility of recognition\. Thus, Reality = Existence = Being is not a theory of an ultimate reality\. It is an attempt to remove unnecessary separation while keeping existence open\. \(direct link \)
