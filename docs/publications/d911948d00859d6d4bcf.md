# Inteligência artificial na educação e neuroplasticidade: contribuições, limites e desafios para a aprendizagem

**Autores:** Daniela Hayoshi

**Data:** 2026-10-17 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

The literature on artificial intelligence \(AI\) in education focuses almost exclusively on performance, perception, and engagement; brain changes associated with neuroplasticity have rarely been measured\. This narrative review, using explicit search and classification criteria, distinguishes three types of evidence: \(a\) learning outcomes \(grades, retention, and transfer\), \(b\) behavioral measures of cognitive effort, and \(c\) direct neurophysiological measures \(EEG and fNIRS\)\. The most robust performance studies indicate that the effects of AI depend on task design: a randomized trial involving approximately one thousand high school students found gains ranging from 48% to 127% during AI\-assisted practice, while performance declined on an exam taken without AI among students with unrestricted access to the model; this decline was eliminated when the system was configured to provide hints rather than answers\. The most frequently cited meta\-analysis supporting AI \(g = 0\.867\) was retracted in 2026 due to analytical errors and the inclusion of invalid studies\. The few studies using brain\-based measures are small \(n ranging from 24 to 54\), short in duration, unpublished in peer\-reviewed journals or reporting null findings, and assess momentary engagement rather than lasting structural or functional plasticity\. It is therefore concluded that there is no basis for claiming that AI, by itself, either enhances or impairs neuroplasticity\. The evidence supports a narrower conclusion: activities that replace retrieval and elaborative effort tend to reduce measured learning, whereas activities that preserve such effort tend to maintain or enhance learning gains\. A research agenda is proposed incorporating longitudinal designs, transfer measures, and pre\- and post\-intervention neuroimaging with active control conditions\.
