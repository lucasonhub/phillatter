# O‘SMIR XULQ\-ATVORIDAGI SALBIY O‘ZGARISHLARNI TADQIQ QILISHNING PSIXOLOGIK IMKONIYATLARI

**Autores:** Axmatova Guli Xurram qizi

**Data:** 2026-10-12 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

This article analyzes the psychological possibilities of studying negative changes in adolescent behavior \(aggressiveness, conflict proneness, deviant behavior, emotional distress, declining interest in learning\)\. The possibilities of observation, conversation, interview, questionnaire and activity performance analysis, as well as K\.N\. Thomas’s “Study of conflict situations” technique, Kondash’s “Method for studying the level of emotional distress” and A\.N\. Orel’s “Determining the propensity for deviant behavior” technique are described\. The article draws on the works of L\.S\. Vygotsky, E\. Erikson, A\. Bandura, U\. Bronfenbrenner, D\. Elkind, T\. Moffitt, L\. Steinberg, as well as Uzbek scholars\. As a result, a step\-by\-step complex psychodiagnostic model for studying negative changes in adolescent behavior is proposed\.
