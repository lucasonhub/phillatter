# AI\-Powered Social Media&\#x27;s Effects On Democracy

**Autores:** Smt\. Seema Mishra

**Data:** 2026-10-15 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Abstract By enabling sentiment analysis, targeted advertising, automated moderation, tailored content recommendations, and generative content production, artificial intelligence \(AI\) has radically changed social media platforms\. AI\-powered social media has improved civic engagement, political communication, and public awareness, strengthening democratic participation\. However, it has also brought about serious problems, such as deep fakes, algorithmic bias, political polarization, electoral manipulation, misinformation, and privacy threats\. These trends have significant ramifications for electoral integrity, public trust, and democratic governance\. This study looks at the advantages and disadvantages of AI\-powered social media for democracy and makes policy suggestions for maintaining moral AI governance while defending democratic principles\.
