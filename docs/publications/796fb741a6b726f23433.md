# BO&\#x27;LAJAK O&\#x27;QITUVCHILARNING REFLEKSIV\-PEDAGOGIK KOMPETENTLIGINI RIVOJLANTIRISHNING STRUKTURAVIY\-FUNKSIONAL MODELI: LOYIHALASH VA NAZARIY VALIDATSIYA

**Autores:** Utkirbek Ilhomjon o&\#x27;g&\#x27;li Xakimov

**Data:** 2026-10-09 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

This study conceptualizes and theoretically validates a structural\-functional model to foster the reflective\-pedagogical competence of prospective teachers within an AI\-driven adaptive environment\. Utilizing the Design and Development Research methodology \(R\. Richey, J\. Klein\) and a systematic review of 15 key sources, a four\-pillar framework \(target\- methodological, content\-conceptual, technological\-procedural, outcome\-evaluative\) was Zamonaviy jamiyat va innovatsiyalar ilmiy nazariy jurnali 2026\-yil, 9\-Son, 2\-Qism\. ISSN 3060\-544X developed\. The model synthesizes four cognitive\-psychological theories with global pedagogical modeling traditions\. Content, construct, and face validity were critically substantiated\. Distinct from conventional models \(e\.g\., ADDIE\), this framework unifies educational modeling, AI architectures, and reflection theories into an integrated feedback\-driven system\.
