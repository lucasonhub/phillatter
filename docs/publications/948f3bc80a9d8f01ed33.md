# SmartTwin AI: An Explainable Predictive\-Maintenance Decision\-Support Framework for Smart Manufacturing

**Autores:** Khaled Taha Fouly

**Data:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

This research presents SmartTwin AI, a software\-based framework for predictive maintenance in smart manufacturing\. The study explores how machine\-learning models can use industrial sensor measurements to identify potential machine failures and support maintenance decisions\. Experiments were conducted using the AI4I 2020 Predictive Maintenance dataset, which contains 10,000 records of machine operating conditions\. Three machine\-learning models were evaluated: Logistic Regression, Random Forest, and Gradient Boosting\. The evaluation included precision, recall, F1\-score, ROC\-AUC, and confusion matrices\. A threshold\-tuning experiment was also performed to improve the detection of potential failures\. The results show that the Random Forest model achieved a ROC\-AUC of 0\.9653\. After threshold tuning, the model achieved a recall of 79\.41% on the test set, demonstrating a potential approach for identifying machines that may require maintenance\. This work combines concepts from Artificial Intelligence, Data Science, and Engineering and Technology\. It provides a reproducible software\-based research workflow, experimental results, visualizations, and a proposed system architecture\. Important limitation: The AI4I 2020 dataset is a synthetic benchmark dataset\. The experiments were conducted on this dataset and do not represent a live deployment or a physical factory experiment\. The proposed framework is a research prototype and requires further validation using real industrial data\. The accompanying research paper and reproducibility materials document the methodology, results, limitations, and potential future work\.
