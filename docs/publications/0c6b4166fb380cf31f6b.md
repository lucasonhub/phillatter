# Multi\-strategy Engineering of a Glycosyltransferase for More Sustainable and Efficient Biosynthesis of Mogroside IE

**Autores:** Zhiyi Lu; Yameng Zhou; Tao Xu; Tian‐Qiong Shi; He Liu; Feng Xue

**Data:** 2026-10-10 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Abstract Mogroside IE is a valuable natural sweetener precursor, but its industrial production is hampered by inefficient plant extraction and nongreen chemical synthesis\. Herein, we report a computational\-experimental engineering strategy that transformed a putative glycosyltransferase, UGTX1, with negligible initial activity \(2\.4% conversion\) into a highly efficient biocatalyst\. This was achieved by synergistically combining machine learning \(ESM\-2\) for zero\-shot prediction, dynamic cross\-correlation network analysis \(DCCNA\) for allosteric control, and structure\-guided tunnel engineering to rationally design a focused mutant library\. Screening identified beneficial single\-point mutations, which were mapped onto the enzyme structure to define three functional zones \(core, tunnel, and shell\)\. Guided by this spatial insight, a focused rational iterative site\-specific mutagenesis \(FRISM\)\-based combinatorial assembly yielded the quintuple mutant UGTX1\-M5, which exhibits a 54\-fold increase in catalytic efficiency \(kcat/Km = 1\.13 × 104 M–1 s–1\)\. The engineered enzyme enabled the scalable synthesis of mogroside IE in a simple, green aqueous\-phase system, achieving &gt;98\.5% conversion of 1\.5 mM mogrol within 14 h at 35 °C without exogenous metal ions\. Molecular dynamics simulations revealed that the mutations collectively stabilize a catalytically competent conformation, enlarge the substrate\-binding pocket, and optimize interaction networks\. This work establishes an efficient and sustainable route to mogroside IE and provides a generalizable multi\-strategy engineering paradigm for unlocking the potential of unexplored enzymatic resources\.
