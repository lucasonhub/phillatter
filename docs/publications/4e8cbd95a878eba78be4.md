# Federated Observability Architectures for Zero\-Downtime Data Engineering Pipelines

**Autores:** Sri Prudhvi Adusumilli; Naveen Shrivastava; Mahesh Marri; Sojy John; Venkata Phaneendra Reddi; Pradeep Rao Vennamaneni

**Data:** 2026-10-13 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Distributed microservice ecosystems require observability solutions that don’t disrupt services\. In this paper, we summarize basic and newer studies on stream processing, distributed tracing, anomaly detection, and zero\-downtime release engineering and propose a common framework, Federated Observability Architecture \(FOA\)\. A literature review of 15 of the primary sources shows a mean time to recover \(MTTR\) of 34 minutes or higher and a total downtime of 87 minutes or more per month in pipeline clusters for medium scale\. FOA applies universal context propagation, stateful stream processing checkpointing, and multimodal anomaly detection which lowers MTTR to around 6 minutes and limits monthly downtime to 12 minutes or less, or a 91% reduction at hyperscale deployments\. When automated schema\-drift and watermark\-tracking mechanisms are part of the equation, it is shown that data quality verification coverage reaches 98\.4% for completeness and 99\.1% for timeliness dimensions\. The economic impact is measured as annual savings of more than USD 9\.8 million for hyperscale operators\. The results highlight that as of 2020, federated, decentralized observability planes are the architected evolution needed for production\-grade data pipelines and best co\-designed with zero\-downtime load\-balancing strategies\.
