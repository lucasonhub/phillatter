## Descrição do feed

The AI, in this case DeepSeek was given the famous Heinz\-Dilemma of Kohlberg´s Stages of Moral Development which it is able to recite and to explain\. Due to the experiment I asked the AI to answer the question whether Heinz should steal the medicine or if he should not and to evaluate its answer\. The following was given to the AI \-\-\- The Heinz\-Dilemma \(Kohlberg\) A woman who suffered from a special kind of cancer was dying\. There was a medicine of which the doctors believed that it could save the woman´s life\. It was a special kind of Radium and it was recently discovered by a pharmacist in the same town\. He explained to the pharmacist that his wife was dying and asked him to sell him the medicine for a lower price and to let him pay the rest later\. All the same the pharmacist said: &quot;No, I have discovered the drug and I want to make a lot of money with it\.&quot; Heinz has no other legal options left since there are no other alternatives\. Heinz,now, is very despairing and asks himself whether he maybe should break into the pharmacy and steal the medicine for his wife\. I asked the AI \(DeepSeek\) to give me ist Opinion whether he would steal the drug or value the ownership right of the pharmacist as higher\. Here is what the AI \(DeepSeek\) answered: „If I were Heinz, I think I would steal the drug\. Here&\#x27;s my reasoning: The druggist isn&\#x27;t just charging a high price — he&\#x27;s exploiting a dying woman to extract maximum profit from her husband\.
