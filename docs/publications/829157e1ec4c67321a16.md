# THE EL\-RAKHAWI UNIFIED THEORY OF NEURO\-JURIDICAL CONSCIOUSNESS AND COGNITIVE SOVEREIGNTY \(UTNJCS\)

**Autores:** mohamed kamal arafa el\-rakhawi

**Data:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** book

## Resumo

The El\-Rakhawi Unified Theory of Neuro\-Juridical Consciousness and Cognitive Sovereignty \(UTNJCS\) introduces a groundbreaking, biophysically grounded framework bridging neuroscience, sociology, philosophy, and law\. It resolves the historical disconnect between subjective legal intent \(Mens Rea\) and objective neurobiology\. UTNJCS quantifies an individual&\#x27;s &quot;Cognitive Sovereignty Index&quot; \(CSI\) and models the degrading effects of &quot;External Socio\-Environmental Pressure&quot; \(S\_ext\)\. The core innovation is the El\-Rakhawi Cognitive Accountability Metric \(CAM\), a probabilistic, dimensionally consistent mathematical equation that estimates legal and moral responsibility\. Operating as a retrospective probabilistic reconstruction, CAM utilizes post\-hoc neuro\-psychological data within strict confidence intervals to evaluate decision\-making capacity, not thought content, strictly prohibiting neuro\-surveillance\. This paradigm shifts global justice from blind retribution to objective, restorative &quot;Cognitive Justice,&quot; ensuring accountability is proportional to actual biological and social capacity, while protecting human cognitive liberty\.
