# Neural Networks and Deep Learning

**Autores:** Prof\. P\. A\. Waghule Landge Samruddhi; Dr\. H\. B\. Jadhav Prof\. L\. S\. Bhadake

**Data:** 2026-10-14 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Neural networks have moved from relatively simple computational models to a central technology for modern artificial intelligence\. This paper presents a structured review of how neural\-network methods have developed, how different architectures address different forms of data, and where deep learning is being applied in practice\. Rather than treating all neural networks as interchangeable, the study relates architectural choices to the characteristics of the Problem, including spatial patterns, sequential information, graph relationships, and generative tasks\. The discussion covers feedforward networks, convolutional models, recurrent and gated networks, autoencoders, generative adversarial networks transformers, capsule networks, graph neural networks, and attention\-based methods\. The paper also considers practical concerns such as data requirements, computational expense, generalization, model transparency, and deployment efficiency\. The review indicates that progress in deep learning increasingly depends not only on larger models, but also on better representation learning, efficient training, responsible deployment, and architectures matched carefully to the structure of the available data
