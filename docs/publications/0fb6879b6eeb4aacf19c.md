# Zhalnin, Alexander E\.: The Origin of Human Consciousness from the Perspective of the Philosophy of Reality

**Autores:** 

**Data de descoberta:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** unknown

## Descrição do feed

The book proposes an original philosophical hypothesis of the origin of consciousness, based on Alexander Zhalnin’s Philosophy of Reality and its Fundamental Method of Philosophy\. The author examines the development of human thinking as a historical process of differentiation and mutual transition between the objective and subjective aspects of mental activity\. Particular attention is paid to the transformation of animals’ physiological signals into object‑based designations, the emergence of concepts, and the formation of two interrelated phases of consciousness — the discrete and the continuous\. The book offers a new philosophical perspective on the evolution of thinking and the relationship between nature, language, and consciousness\.
