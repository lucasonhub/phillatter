# ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE IN DENTISTRY: CURRENT APPLICATIONS, ADVANCEMENTS, CHALLENGES, AND FUTURE PERSPECTIVES

**Autores:** 

**Data:** 2026-10-13 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Artificial Intelligence \(AI\) has emerged as one of the most transformative technologies in modern healthcare and is increasingly influencing contemporary dental practice\.Recent advancements in machine learning, deep learning, computer vision, and generative AI have enabled the development of intelligent systems capable of analyzing complex clinical, radiographic, and histopathological datasets with remarkable precision\.AI applications have demonstrated substantial benefits across multiple dental specialties, including oral radiology, oral pathology, orthodontics, periodontology, prosthodontics, conservative dentistry and endodontics, oral and maxillofacial surgery, pediatric dentistry, and public health dentistry\.These technologies facilitate early disease detection, automated image interpretation, treatment planning, outcome prediction, and workflow optimization\.The integration of AI with digital dentistry technologies such as cone\-beam computed tomography, intraoral scanners, computer\-aided design/computer\-aided manufacturing systems, and electronic health records has accelerated its adoption in clinical settings\.Despite these advantages, challenges such as data privacy concerns, algorithmic bias, transparency issues, implementation costs, and regulatory uncertainties continue to limit widespread clinical implementation\.Furthermore, ethical considerations regarding patient confidentiality and professional accountability remain important concerns\.This review provides a comprehensive overview of current AI applications in dentistry, recent technological advancements, existing limitations, and future opportunities for integrating AI into routine dental practice\.
