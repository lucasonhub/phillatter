# Bridging the Gap Between Practice and Science: Reflections on the Journey Towards Becoming a Scholar\-Practitioner

**Autores:** Willem Jan H Roestenburg

**Data:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

The scholar\-practitioner paradigm has become increasingly important in social work as the profession continues to strengthen its commitment to evidence\-based practice \(EBP\) and accountability\. This article reflects on the role of the social work practitioner as both a consumer and producer of knowledge, emphasizing the importance of practice\-based research in narrowing the gap between theory and practice\. Drawing on the author’s three decades of experience as a social work educator, doctoral program coordinator, and mentor to undergraduate and postgraduate students, the article reflects on how practitioners transition toward becoming scholar\-practitioners\. Four developmental phases are proposed: \(1\) the beginning phase, in which practitioners learn to connect theory and practice; \(2\) the discovery phase, characterized by the use of theory and evidence to guide practice decisions; \(3\) the scholar realization phase, where practitioners begin generating and analyzing practice\-based evidence; and \(4\) the practicing scholar phase, in which findings are disseminated through presentations, publications, and broader scholarly engagement\. The article highlights common barriers to practitioner\-led research, including time constraints, organizational limitations, insufficient research training, and uncertainty regarding the implementation of EBP\. It further identifies key characteristics of successful scholar\-practitioners, including methodological competence, data literacy, technological adaptability, writing proficiency, and engagement in mentorship relationships\. The article concludes that strengthening the scholar\-practitioner identity is essential for advancing social work knowledge, improving practice outcomes, and enhancing professional accountability\. Recommendations are offered to promote practitioner\-scholarship through supportive training environments, mentorship opportunities, and dedicated publication platforms that encourage the dissemination of practice\-generated evidence\.
