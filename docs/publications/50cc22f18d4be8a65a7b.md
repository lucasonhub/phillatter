# FedHEAL\-IoMT: A Federated Hierarchical Evidential Adaptive Learning Framework for Zero\-day DDoS Detection in Internet of Medical Things Networks

**Autores:** Tejinder Sharma; Bharti Sharma

**Data:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Distributed denial\-of\-service \(DDoS\) traffic dominates the attack taxonomy of current Internet of Medical Things \(IoMT\) testbeds, and federated learning \(FL\) has become the accepted way of training detectors without moving patient\-adjacent traffic off hospital premises\.Existing federated IoMT detectors nevertheless combine conventional averaging with an off\-the\-shelf classifier and report results on independent and identically distributed partitions, so their published accuracy reveals little about the conditions a deployed hospital federation meets: attack variants absent from the training distribution, gradual drift in traffic composition, compromised gateways that poison the aggregate, and devices too constrained to host the full model\.This paper presents FedHEAL\-IoMT, a three\-tier framework treating those conditions as one coupled problem\.An evidential head emits Dirichlet concentration parameters whose total uncertainty acts as an open\-set alarm and, through an exponentially weighted shift statistic, as a label\-free drift trigger; a trust\-weighted rule gates outlying updates by geometric\-median deviation, evaluated inside a two\-server secure\-computation protocol that reveals no individual update, distance or trust score, before a differentially private secure sum; and a normalized\-cost rule places the split\-learning cut layer per device class\.Validation spans CICIoMT2024, WUSTL\-EHMS\-2020, ECU\-IoHT and CICIoT2023\.On CICIoMT2024 FedHEAL\-IoMT attains 99\.41% accuracy under homogeneous partitions against 99\.35% for the strongest baseline, a difference of 0\.06 points lying inside the seed spread, while raising macro\-F1 by 4\.57 points at Dirichlet α = 0\.1, open\-set area under the receiver operating characteristic curve by 4\.31 points over the strongest zero\-day baseline, and accuracy under 30% non\-adaptive malicious gateways by 5\.34 points over the strongest robust comparator, FedMADE, and 8\.01 over Krum, at 71\.9% lower peak resident memory measured on the Raspberry Pi 4B gateway platform\.Code, configurations, seeds and per\-seed results are released publicly\.
