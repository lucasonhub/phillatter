# CONCEPT AND CHARACTERISTICS OF A NON\-ORIGINAL OBJECT GENERATED BY A COMPUTER PROGRAM

**Autores:** O\. R\. Vyshnevska

**Data:** 2026-10-15 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

The article provides a comprehensive analysis of the concept and features of non\-original objects generated by computer programs, while also distinguishing them from works created by humans using computer technologies\. The study examines three main features of a non\-original object generated by a computer program derived from its legal definition under the Law of Ukraine “On Copyright and Related Rights”: objective novelty, origin as a result of the functioning of a computer program, and the absence of a direct creative contribution by a natural person\. Based on the analysis of Ukrainian legislation and international law enforcement practice, it is proven that the line between a work and a non\-original object lies in the presence of real and substantial subjective creative human control over the final result\.
