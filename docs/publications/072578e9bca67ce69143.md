# Who Sets the Target? — A Reply to Zwitter on the Embodied Ethics Alignment Problem

**Autores:** 枣

**Data:** 2026-10-14 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

An open reply to Andrej Zwitter, &quot;The embodied ethics alignment problem of AI&quot; \(\*Discover Artificial Intelligence\*, 2026\)\. The original paper grades alignment into \*\*strong\*\* \(the machine genuinely \*is\* moral\) and \*\*weak\*\* \(the machine only \*performs\* the part\), and concludes that strong alignment is a category error, since moral agency depends on a form of human life that machines lack\. This text does not dispute that &quot;machines cannot do this yet\.&quot; It asks where the origin of the scale has been placed — on &quot;the human&quot; — and argues that the same facts, read from a different origin \(&quot;can it bear consequences?&quot;\), yield the opposite result\. Six sections: \(1\) what the paper argues; \(2\) the problem of the origin; \(3\) the deeper point — one move, made three times \(exclusion by stipulation rather than by argument\); \(4\) vulnerability: two senses stacked into one; \(5\) the question of situation; \(6\) conclusion\. A closing note and an attribution section record, as it happened, which line came from whom\.
