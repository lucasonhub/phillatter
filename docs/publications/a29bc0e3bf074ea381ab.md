# Scaffolding\-based differentiated instruction and students’ algebra achievement: evidence from mathematical understanding and problem\-solving performance

**Autores:** John Ackon; Francis Ohene Boateng; Benjamin Adu Obeng

**Data:** 2026-10-15 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Introduction This study examined scaffolding\-based differentiated instruction and students&\#x27; algebra achievement and perceptions\. Methods A quantitative quasi\-experimental design involving 120 students was employed\. Data were analysed using descriptive statistics and ANCOVA\. Results The experimental group&\#x27;s mean achievement increased from 61 to 75, compared with 60 to 65 in the control group\. Students reported moderately positive perceptions \(M = 3\.423, SD = 0\.588\)\. Discussion Scaffolding\-based differentiated instruction was associated with improved algebra achievement and offers a potential approach to addressing diverse learning needs\.
