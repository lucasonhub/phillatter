# The Role of Artificial Intelligence in Spatio\-Temporal Analysis of Rural Transformation

**Autores:** Pushpendra Kumar Ahirwar; Dr\. S\. S\. Baghel

**Data:** 2026-10-15 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

ABSTRACT Rural transformation \- the multidimensional transition of demographic, economic, social, spatial, technological, environmental and institutional conditions in rural areas has traditionally been studied through periodic surveys, census decadal enumeration and retrospective mapping\. This paper investigates whether artificial intelligence \(AI\) can fundamentally transform this analytical paradigm from retrospective description toward continuous, predictive and anticipatory spatio\-temporal intelligence\. Through a critical synthesis of the emerging GeoAI literature, analysis of primary Indian and international datasets, and development of an original conceptual framework\-the AI\-Enabled SpatioTemporal Rural Transformation Framework \(AI\-STRF\) this paper argues that AI is not merely improving existing rural research techniques but may be creating a new paradigm of rural geographical analysis\. The paper examines AI applications across demographic, agricultural, infrastructural, environmental and socioeconomic dimensions of rural change, identifies critical research gaps, and constructs evidence\-based 2030\-2050 future scenarios\. It concludes that while AI offers transformative potential for predictive rural geography, realisation of this potential depends critically on addressing data inequality, algorithmic bias, institutional capacity, and the fundamental distinction between prediction and causal explanation\. The future of rural geography, this paper contends, lies not in replacing human geographical expertise but in augmenting it through intelligent, ethically governed spatio\-temporal analysis\.
