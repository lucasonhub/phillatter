# An Assessment of Green Management and Artificial Intelligence in Sustainable Business Management

**Autores:** Ayla AVCI

**Data:** 2026-10-18 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

The aim of this study is to examine academic publications on green management published in 2024, identifying key research themes, findings, and relationships with sustainability, environmental performance, and green human resource management\. A qualitative research design based on document analysis was adopted for the investigation\. Data were gathered from 23 academic publications retrieved via Google Scholar using the keyword “green management”\. Selected publications underwent rigorous content analysis, and findings were synthesized thematically alongside geographical and sectoral distributions\. China represented the largest share, accounting for 39\.1% of all reviewed publications\. Research predominantly focused on the corporate sector, followed by higher education, manufacturing, and small and medium\-sized enterprises \(SMEs\)\. Notably, none of the examined studies directly addressed green artificial intelligence\. The findings indicate that green management is strongly associated with green innovation, environmental performance, resource efficiency, employee environmentally responsible behaviors, and overall organizational sustainability\. Green technology adoption, organizational culture, and environmental regulations also emerged as critical drivers\. Ultimately, the results underscore the necessity of integrating environmental objectives into core organizational strategies\. Future research should explore green management across diverse national and sectoral contexts\.
