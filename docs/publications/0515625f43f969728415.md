# On physical implementability of reinforcement\-learning\-driven reasoning

**Autores:** Eiji Konishi

**Data:** 2026-10-09 · **Tipo:** preprint

## Resumo

In the machine learning community, the reinforcement\-learning\-driven reasoning process has been intensively studied since around 2025\. In this article, we present a negative result on the hardware implementation of this reinforcement\-learning\-driven reasoning process in a physical neural network model in the presence of a reward external potential energy for a given reward\. In this hardware implementation, the existence of a chain of thought in dynamics contradicts the Lyapunov stability of the system\.
