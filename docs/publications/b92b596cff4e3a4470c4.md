# Impact of revised antral follicle count threshold on PCOS diagnosis: A comparative clinical study

**Autores:** Saloni Kamboj; Neena Malhotra; Reeta Mahey; Taruna Katyal Arora; Aditi Goyal; Snigdha Soni; Anshu Jyoti; Spondita Banerjee; Rintu Kutum

**Data:** 2026-10-15 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

OBJECTIVE: To evaluate the clinical impact of the revised antral follicle count \(AFC\) threshold on polycystic ovary syndrome \(PCOS\) diagnosis\. METHODS: A retrospective cross\-sectional observational study including women diagnosed using the Rotterdam criteria, stratified by AFC: 12\-19 \(low AFC\) and ≥20 \(high AFC\)\. Clinical, metabolic, and hormonal parameters were compared against controls and amongst low and high AFC group\. RESULTS: Of 336 PCOS women, 208 \(62%\) had an AFC between 12 and 19 and would not meet the revised ≥20 follicle threshold for the diagnosis of polycystic morphology\. These women had worse metabolic and androgenic profiles than controls, and were similar to the high AFC group\. CONCLUSION: Excluding low AFC women may misclassify patients who require clinical care\.
