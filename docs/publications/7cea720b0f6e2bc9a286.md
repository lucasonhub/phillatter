# Venglovskiy, Anton: Continuous Physical\-Mathematical Theory of Thinking

## Descrição do feed

This foundational monograph unfolds a continuous physical\-mathematical theory of thinking, investigating the conditions for the emergence of autonomous subjectivity and the mechanisms of downward causal control in distributed nonequilibrium media\. Grounded in the conceptual hypothesis of the seminal work \[1\], the thinking process in its general form is explicated as the spontaneous and logical evolution of structural complexity through recursive generalization, wherein the logicality of the process is expressed via multilevel coherence in time within an open dynamical continuum\. The monograph implements a continuous physical\-mathematical theory of the general form of thinking directly in the distributed substrate of active matter\. An analysis of the mathematical foundations of discrete systems demonstrates that deterministic computational automata generate a passive retrospective protocol of transitions and lack subject autonomy\. To physically describe the category of the Subject as an objective factor of the cosmos, a continuous nonequilibrium paradigm is developed\. In a distributed active medium featuring closed feedback loops and thermodynamic stochastic indeterminism at the microlevel, the Subject is formalized as a macroscopic dynamical attractor — an integer topological invariant of connection in a fiber bundle over the base of collective order parameters\. The ontological immateriality of the Subject is expressed through zero rest mass \(m₀ = 0\), zero point electric charge \(q = 0\), and distributed spatial delocalization, while fully preserving objective downward causal efficacy\. The mechanism of downward control is realized in a zero mechanical force regime \(∇V\_subject = 0\) in accordance with the variational principle of d&\#x27;Alembert–Lagrange ideal constraints \(δW = 0\), strictly conserving the fundamental conservation laws of energy and momentum\. Microtrajectory selection is governed by gauge Ehresmann phase connection and the classical Sinitsyn–Nemenman geometric phase in nonequilibrium steady states \(NESS\) characterized by persistent solenoidal probability currents during the traversal of bifurcation null\-gaps \(∇V\(x\_crit\) = 0\)\. The synchronic supervenience dilemma is resolved via the Mori–Zwanzig projection operator formalism with a retarded memory kernel: the topological macroinvariant of the preceding time window establishes boundary conditions for microevolution across subsequent time intervals\. The field dynamics of the cyclic thinking process integrates an operator of parametric spatiotemporal harmonic generation \(D\) and an operator of nonlocal Kuramoto phase synchronization \(A\)\. The topological resilience of discrete connection indices against thermal noise is underwritten by the energetic barrier to phase slips and the Berezinskii–Kosterlitz–Thouless \(BKT\) transition\. Necessary material criteria for the substrate are formulated \(closed loop topology, nonlinearity, spectral timescale separation, multicomponent chemical diversity\), instantiated in corticothalamic loops, dendritic arborizations, and the sulcal\-gyral geometry of the cerebral cortex, which define boundary conditions for the electrolytic conductivity tensor g\_μν\(x\) of the neuropil\. In place of microquantum reduction hypotheses, the theory substantiates classical wave macrocoherence in open nonequilibrium media\. Keywords: theory of thinking, general form of thinking, logicality, logical evolution, topological invariants, category of the Subject, physical indeterminism, non\-force downward control, Ehresmann connection, nonequilibrium steady states, Kuramoto phase synchronization, Mori–Zwanzig projection operator, self\-organized criticality, BKT transition, neurodynamics\.
