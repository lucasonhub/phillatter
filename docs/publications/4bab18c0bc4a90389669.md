# Self\-calibrating multi\-fidelity active learning for microstrip patch optimization across substrates and bands

**Autores:** David  Jin Tao Leong; Doreen  Ying Ying Sim

**Data:** 2026-10-13 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Physics\-aware active learning can reduce the full\-wave electromagnetic \(EM\) cost of microstrip\-patch design, but its sampling priors are commonly tuned to one substrate and band\. We present an integrated self\-calibrating, matching\-first, physics\-aware active\-learning workflow for probe\-fed rectangular patches, together with coarse\-mesh screening and full\-resolution confirmation\. The online calibration estimates sampling bands from each run’s observations instead of prescribing case\-specific probe locations\. Under an equal budget of 37 openEMS simulations, 4 optimizers were compared on 4 substrate/frequency cases using 10 replicate seeds per case\. The integrated workflow produced the lowest mean and median final objective in every case, returned a matched design in all forty runs, and was lower than the three baselines in nine of twelve exact directional Mann–Whitney comparisons after within\-case Holm adjustment; six contrasts remained significant in a two\-sided unpaired analysis and seven in a paired\-by\-seed sensitivity analysis\. Multi\-fidelity screening reduced mean equivalent\-high\-fidelity cost by 19%–46% across cases \(36\.9% overall\) and returned a matched full\-resolution design in 39 of 40 runs, although endpoint objective quality was not uniformly preserved\. Nominal near\-to\-far\-field calculations for four selected designs gave broadside directivities of 6\.51–7\.00 dBi and realized gains of 3\.59–5\.77 dBi\. All 12 finer\-mesh, larger\-domain, and perfectly matched layer \(PML\) stability checks remained matched; maximum absolute directivity and realized\-gain changes were 0\.180 and 0\.121 dB\. A ten\-variable E\-shaped\-patch case study matched all ten integrated\-workflow runs\. Its advantage over random search was robust to unpaired and paired\-by\-seed analyses; inference against unstructured radial\-basis\-function \(RBF\) surrogate search depended on the treatment of seed blocks, and the genetic comparison remained unresolved\. A five\-seed in\-loop impedance–gain pilot produced a directionally favorable but non\-significant hypervolume trend\. The evidence is numerical and openEMS\-based; no commercial\-solver or fabricated prototype validation is claimed\.
