# Krause, Décio &amp; Jorge, Juan Pablo: Connecting Lombardi’s quantum property ontology to Dalla Chiara and Toraldo di Francia’s intensional quantum metaphysics

**Autores:** 

**Data de descoberta:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** unknown

## Descrição do feed

We present a brief sketch of both ontological views about quantum objects: those of O\. Lombardi and that of M\.L\.Dalla Chiara and G\. Toraldo di Francia\. We articulate a parallel in these lines, which view quantum objects either as bundles of properties or as nomological objects defined by physical law\. The aim of the paper is to bring the discussion about quantum ontology, specifically regarding the question of whether there is a necessity to postulate the existence of something \(in this case, the basic entities representing matter and interactions\) underlying these two views\. Our opinion is that yes, these ghost entities cannot, or should not, be disregarded in any interpretation\.
