# A Novel Red\-Eared Slider Optimisation Algorithm Based on A Two\-Stage Basking–Foraging Strategy

**Autores:** H\. Hammod Helal Al\-Badrei; N\. Ali Majeed Alhammadi; A\. Rajab Kadhim; Z\. Ali Abdulabbas

**Data:** 2026-10-15 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Purpose: This study aims to develop a novel nature\-inspired metaheuristic optimisation algorithm based on the semi\-aquatic behaviour of the red\-eared slider \(Trachemys scripta elegans\)\. Design/Methodology/Approach: An experimental research design was adopted\. The proposed algorithm models two behavioural stages of the red\-eared slider: “foraging in water” as an exploration mechanism and “sunbathing on a platform” as an exploitation mechanism\. The agent population adaptively switches between these two behavioural states\. RESO is evaluated using 23 single\-objective benchmark functions with different dimensions and compared with Particle Swarm Optimisation \(PSO\) and Genetic Algorithm \(GA\)\. Performance was analysed descriptively through convergence\-curve comparison in terms of solution quality, convergence speed, and robustness against local optima\. Research Limitation: The evaluation is limited to 23 standard single\-objective benchmark functions and is based on convergence curves without inferential statistical testing; further validation with statistical tests and on real\-world optimisation problems is required\. Findings: The experimental results indicate that RESO achieves faster convergence and better final solutions than PSO and GA on many benchmark functions, with competitive results on most others\. The combination of adaptive switching and spatial memory also supports effective exploration of the search space and reduces the tendency to become trapped in local optima\. Practical Implication: The proposed framework provides a basis for applying the algorithm to engineering, industrial, planning, control, and path optimisation problems\. Social Implication: The development of effective optimisation techniques may indirectly support more efficient computational solutions for complex practical problems\. Originality/Value: The study introduces a two\-stage optimisation framework inspired by the behavioural cycle of the red\-eared slider and integrates adaptive behavioural switching with a spatial memory of promising platforms, offering a behaviour\-driven way to balance exploration and exploitation in metaheuristic search\.
