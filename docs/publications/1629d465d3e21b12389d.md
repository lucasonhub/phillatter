# Crafting Resilient AI policies for Education

**Autores:** Delight Ejiaka; Miranda Hannasch; Branden Cunningham

**Data:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

The rapid integration of generative artificial intelligence into academic spaces requires a shift from passive restriction to resilient, active pedagogical frameworks\. This virtual panel explores actionable strategies to protect academic integrity, promote deep critical thinking, and manage AI use, particularly in foundational courses\. Panelists will discuss the implementation of high\-engagement classroom interventions\. The will offer frameworks for transparent student disclosure practices\. Attendees will leave with practical templates for syllabus policies, assignment rubrics, and disclosure benchmarks designed to foster a culture of transparency and process\-oriented learning in an AI\-permeated landscape\.
