# INCLUSION STRATEGY REVIEW AGENT \(ISRA\) v8\.1\.5 HYBRID

**Autores:** Niall Devlin

**Data:** 2026-10-12 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Inclusion Strategy Review Agent \(ISRA\): A Deterministic AI\-Supported Framework for Reviewing School Inclusion Strategies School inclusion strategies are increasingly expected to demonstrate not only compliance with policy requirements but also a coherent theory of change linking identified barriers, strategic priorities, implementation activities, resources, outcomes and monitoring arrangements\. Traditional document reviews can be subject to significant variation due to differing reviewer interpretations, inconsistent application of criteria and subjective assessments of quality\. This paper presents the Inclusion Strategy Review Agent \(ISRA\) v8\.2 Hybrid, a deterministic review framework designed to improve the consistency, transparency and auditability of inclusion strategy evaluations\. The framework combines evidence\-based assessment, structured compliance testing, alignment analysis, proposition checking and implementation\-science principles within a single review methodology\. ISRA introduces a multi\-level evidence hierarchy, explicit domain scoring, contradiction mapping, confidence calibration and judgement thresholds to reduce reviewer drift and strengthen reproducibility\. A key innovation is the integration of targeted proposition checks for activities, outcomes, monitoring and governance\. These checks assess the completeness of strategic statements by testing for the presence of essential components such as ownership, timescales, baselines, targets, accountability arrangements and monitoring mechanisms\. This approach improves objectivity while preserving professional judgement and maintaining an improvement\-focused orientation\. The framework is specifically designed to support schools, local authorities and education partnerships in evaluating the quality, coherence, compliance and implementation readiness of inclusion strategies\. By combining proportional scoring with transparent evidence requirements, ISRA aims to provide a practical bridge between policy expectations, implementation science and AI\-assisted document review\.
