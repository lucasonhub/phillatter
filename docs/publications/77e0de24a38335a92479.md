# del Campo Velasco, José Carlos: Habitar lo que cambia

**Autores:** 

**Data de descoberta:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** unknown

## Descrição do feed

This monograph examines the conditions that allow a life or an organization to be sustained as its demands change\. It argues that evaluating outcomes requires attending not only to what is achieved, but also to the capacities that ongoing activity preserves, depletes, or reconstructs, and to the distribution of costs across persons and temporal horizons\. Through conceptual analysis and reflection on experience, the book distinguishes persistence, viability, metaviability, habitability, and legitimacy\. It engages with viability theory, resilience, the capability approach, enaction, the ethics of care, and debates on epistemic injustice and structural responsibility\. A fictional organizational example accompanies the argument, while two documented cases—the 2003 Columbia accident and the 1995 Chicago heat wave—provide material for examining information, authority, vulnerability, and effective access to support\. The book offers a philosophical articulation and an agenda for further inquiry; the historical cases are not presented as empirical validation of the framework\.
