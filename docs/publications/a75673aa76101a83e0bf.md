# Stacking\-factor\-integrated production planning for 3D\-Stacked high\-bandwidth memory manufacturing: an optimization approach

**Autores:** Jeong Woo Kim

**Data:** 2026-10-13 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

As AI\-driven computing accelerates, demand for High\-Bandwidth Memory \(HBM\) manufactured through 3D DRAM stacking and Through\-Silicon\-Via \(TSV\) technology has surged, yet existing production\-planning models risk overestimating package\-level capacity by treating multi\-layer stacking conversion linearly\. We develop a mixed\-integer optimization framework that explicitly incorporates a stacking factor \( S j \) for 4\-, 8\-, 12\-, and 16\-layer HBM structures and refines turn\-around\-time logic to preserve physical flow consistency across extended lead times\. Applied to a 6\-month, three\-line, fourteen\-configuration case study solved via CPLEX, the baseline model produced 554,163 chips over the planning horizon while accurately converting die\-level output into package\-level demand fulfillment; a demand\-stabilizing extension substantially reduced production disruption for high\-volume configurations relative to low\-volume configurations under simulated demand fluctuation\. These results indicate that explicitly modeling commercial stacking constraints prevents capacity overestimation at the die\-to\-package conversion stage and supports more reliable production planning for high\-performance, multi\-layer semiconductor manufacturing\.
