# Nova: A Real\-Time AI Sales Call Assistant with Live Speech Transcription, Knowledge\-Grounded Coaching and Simulated Sales Training

**Autores:** Gokul Raj R; D\. Mohanapriya; Nithyanandh Selvam

**Data:** 2026-10-13 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Sales representatives must respond to customers in real time while recalling product details, recognizing customer needs and buying signals, and deciding on suitable follow\-up actions\. This paper presents Nova, a full\-stack Real\-Time AI Sales Call Assistant that supports representatives during customer conversations\. The browser captures live speech through the Web Speech API and converts it into a running transcript, and the React frontend sends the relevant conversation data to a Flask backend through REST APIs\. An AI coaching service analyzes the conversation context and returns live suggestions, namely a recommended response, a follow\-up question, objection\-handling guidance, detected buying signals and a next action\. Lead management, call history, call notes, follow\-ups, a dashboard and follow\-up email workflows are supported using SQLite and SQLAlchemy with JWT\-based authentication\. A training module simulates customer scenarios and scores the practice session\. Completed calls store the transcript, call duration, speaker information, sentiment, summary and follow\-up recommendations\. The implemented system supports live microphone capture, speech transcription, real\-time coaching suggestions, lead management and call\-history storage\. The current coaching logic is built around rules, knowledge and application logic, and no formal predictive\-model accuracy is reported\. The system integrates live transcription, coaching, record keeping and training within a single web application\.
