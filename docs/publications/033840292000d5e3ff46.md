# Impatiens lianchengensis \(Balsaminaceae\), a new species from southeastern China

**Autores:** Jiedong Lin; Junlong Li; Boheng Li; Xinjie Jin; Meizhen Wang; Pan Li

**Data:** 2026-10-15 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

A new species, Impatiens lianchengensis J\. D\. Lin &amp; P\. Li, sp\. nov \., from a moist valley on Mt\. Guanzhai, Liancheng County, Fujian Province, southeastern China, is described and illustrated\. It resembles I\. huangyanensis X\. F\. Jin &amp; B\. Y\. Ding and I\. tainingensis J\.\-D\. Lin &amp; P\. Li in gross morphology but differs in numerous features\. The molecular phylogeny of Impatiens indicated that I\. lianchengensis is closely related to I\. kuocangshanica , I\. wuyiensis , I\. platysepala , and I\. tainingensis based on plastome and ITS sequences\.
