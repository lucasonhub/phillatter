# The Importance of Effective Management of Population Welfare in Poverty Reduction: The Case of Bukhara Region

**Autores:** Negmatillayeva Zarina Baxodirovna

**Data:** 2026-10-11 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

This article examines the role of effective management of population welfare in reducing poverty, using Bukhara region of Uzbekistan as a case study\. Drawing on a regional time series for 2019–2025 and cross\-sectional data for 13 districts and cities, the study assesses the regional welfare management system, estimates the association between wages and poverty with a regression model \(R² = 0\.862\), and describes poverty dynamics with an exponential deceleration model \(R² = 0\.974\)\. The main weaknesses of the system are found in mahalla\-level competencies, interagency coordination, monitoring, and territorial disparities\. The article proposes an Integrated Poverty Reduction Management Model \(IPRMM\) of five components together with an eight\-dimension KPI dashboard\. Scenario modeling suggests that the regional poverty rate could fall from 5\.7 percent in 2025 to 3\.23 percent by 2030 under current trends and to 1\.77 percent if the model is implemented\.
