# Cascaded deep learning for synthesis of 18F\-FDG PET from 18F\-octreotide: model parameter optimization and performance evaluation

**Autores:** Ruxi Chang; Liping Yang; Jungang Gao; Xiang Liu; Bo Wang; Yan Li; Xiaoyi Duan

**Data:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Objective To investigate a cascaded conditional generative adversarial network \(cGAN\)–based method for synthesizing 18 F\-FDG PET images from 18 F\-octreotide, optimize model parameter configurations, and evaluate the quality of the synthesized images\. Methods Imaging data from 71 tumor patients who underwent dual\-tracer PET/CT examinations with 18 F\-FDG and 18 F\-octreotide between May 2022 and November 2025 were retrospectively collected\. A cascaded deep learning architecture \(first stage: 3D U\-Net; second stage: self\-attention 3D V\-Net\) was employed to evaluate the effects of image preprocessing, resampling size, and patch size on synthesis quality\. Three preprocessing strategies \(log transformation, normalization, and a combined approach\) were compared\. Image quality was assessed using five objective metrics \(SSIM, PSNR, MSE, MAE, and NMI\) and Likert 5\-point ratings from three nuclear medicine physicians; liver and lesion\-level SUV agreement were further evaluated\. Results In the held\-out test cohort \( n = 14\), the combined preprocessing method \(log transformation \+ normalization\) significantly outperformed the individual methods \(SSIM: 0\.8454 ± 0\.0328 vs\. 0\.7831 ± 0\.0337 vs\. 0\.7438 ± 0\.0362, P &lt; 0\.001\)\. On the identical held\-out test cohort \( n = 14\), the Group 2 parameter configuration \(resampling size: 256 × 256 × 512; patch size: 128 × 128 × 128\) outperformed Group 1, with an SSIM of 0\.8854 ± 0\.0455 and a PSNR of 28\.15 ± 3\.35 dB, representing relative improvements of 19\.1% and 83\.5%, respectively, over Group 1 \(paired\-samples t\-test, both P &lt; 0\.001\)\. The mean Likert score was 4\.53 ± 0\.19\. Quantitative analysis of liver SUVmean showed no significant difference between synthesized and real images \( P = 0\.967, paired t\-test\)\. Lesion\-level quantitative analysis demonstrated high agreement between synthetic and real 18 F\-FDG images, with Pearson correlation coefficients of 1\.0000, 0\.9995, and 0\.9991 for SUVmax and SUVmean \( n = 14\) and SUVpeak \( n = 13\), respectively \(all P &lt; 0\.001\), no significant differences on paired t\-tests \(all P &gt; 0\.05\), and all Bland–Altman differences within the 95% limits of agreement\. Conclusion The cascaded deep learning model enabled cross\-tracer synthesis from 18 F\-octreotide to 18 F\-FDG PET images\. In this exploratory, single\-center pilot cohort, the optimized configuration demonstrated promising quantitative and visual performance, supporting image\-synthesis feasibility and potential clinical utility of a single\-injection, dual\-parameter molecular imaging approach for neuroendocrine tumors\. Prospective multicenter validation is warranted before any clinical application\.
