# A new delta\-headed branchiosaurid temnospondyl from the early Permian Meisenheim Formation of Germany

**Autores:** Rainer R\. Schoch

**Data:** 2026-10-09 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Two temnospondyl specimens from the Ruthweiler deposit \(M8\) in the early Permian Meisenheim Formation of the Saar\-Nahe Basin are identified as pertaining to a new branchiosaurid taxon, Schoenfelderpeton brevifrons \. They are distinct from all other temnospondyls in the region by the delta\-shaped skull, small orbits, and short frontals\. The maxilla fails to contact the cheek and has tiny, pin\-shaped teeth, a feature that persists in the mature specimen\. Schoenfelderpeton brevifrons is found to nest with Leptorophus and “ Melanerpeton ” humbergense and forms the sister taxon to Schoenfelderpeton prescheri , with which it shares the short gape, open cheek, and an emarginated squamosal embayment\.
