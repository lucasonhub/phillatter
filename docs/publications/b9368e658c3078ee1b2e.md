# Data Science for the Next Millennium: A Ubiquitous Review

**Autores:** Miss\. S\. B\. Avhad Miss\. Warale Tejasvi Narsinha; Prof\. J\. R\. Mahajan

**Data:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Data science has become a practical foundation for understanding complex information and supporting decisions across modern society\. Digital services, connected devices, scientific instruments, business systems and public platforms continuously generate structured and unstructured data\. This review examines data science as an interdisciplinary process that combines statistics, computing, domain knowledge, data engineering, machine learning and communication\. It discusses the typical lifecycle of a data science project, major analytical techniques, commonly used tools, and applications in education, healthcare, business, environmental monitoring, transportation and public services\. The discussion is informed by a review of literature published mainly between 2018 and 2023, including the review by Anwar and colleagues, while also drawing on established books and methodological publications\. Rather than presenting a new experiment, the paper synthesizes recurring themes and practical considerations reported in the literature\. The review highlights that useful outcomes depend not only on advanced algorithms but also on data quality, appropriate problem formulation, reproducibility, privacy, fairness, and communication of uncertainty\. It concludes that the future of data science will be shaped by responsible automation, accessible analytical platforms, stronger interdisciplinary collaboration and governance that keeps human needs at the centre of data\-driven decisions\.
