## Descrição do feed

This paper presents a comprehensive philosophical and scientific analysis of the Core Emotion Framework \(CEF\), a structural constructivist model that reconceptualizes human affect as a system of ten functional operators distributed across three somatic evaluative hubs: the cephalic, cardiac, and enteric centers\. Integrating enactive affectivity, developmental neurobiology, embodied cognition, and quantum cognitive modeling, the paper argues that emotional episodes arise from coordinated operator dynamics instantiated through multimodal neural architectures\. The analysis formalizes these operators using Bloch sphere qubit geometry, non commuting projection operators, and Kuramoto phase synchronization, demonstrating how emotional flexibility, fusion, and pathological rigidity can be mathematically characterized as transitions in a high dimensional quantum semantic state space\. The paper further synthesizes the CEF with Shun Ching Lee’s Quantum Tathagatagarbha Manifestation Model \(QTMM\), establishing a boundary–bulk ontology in which somatic centers function as conformal boundary states and subjective emotional experience emerges as a holographically projected bulk phenomenon\. Developmental reflex retentions \(FPR, Moro, ATNR\) are shown to constitute the physiological basis of the classical yogic Granthis, linking esoteric metaphysics with contemporary neurophysiology\. The resulting framework provides a unified account of affective processing, somatic embodiment, and cognitive behavioral regulation, offering a philosophically rigorous foundation for future research in embodied cognition, comparative metaphysics, affective science, and synthetic emotional architectures\.
