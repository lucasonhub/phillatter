# PENGARUH COGNITIVE STYLE TERHADAP KEMAMPUAN COMPUTATIONAL THINKING SISWA SMP DALAM PEMBELAJARAN INFORMATIKA

**Autores:** Husnul Hafizin; Dyah Susilowaty; Heri Sopian Hadi

**Data:** 2026-10-10 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

This study aims to analyze the influence of cognitive style on students&\#x27; computational thinking abilities during Informatics instruction in Grade IX at SMPN 1 Kediri\. Research Method: The study employed a quantitative approach using an associative, ex post facto research design\. Data were analyzed using statistical tests to determine whether there were relationships or differences in computational thinking abilities based on FI\-FD \(Field\-Independent/Field\-Dependent\) cognitive styles\. Key Findings: The results indicate no significant relationship or difference between cognitive style \(FI\-FD\) and students&\#x27; computational thinking abilities\. This is evidenced by significance values ​​of 0\.293 \(&gt; 0\.05\) for both the t\-test and the F\-test\. Consequently, the alternative hypothesis \(Hₐ\) positing a relationship or difference between cognitive style \(FI\-FD\) and computational thinking abilities was rejected, while the null hypothesis \(H₀\) was accepted\. Novelty: These findings demonstrate that differences in FI\-FD cognitive styles do not constitute a factor that significantly differentiates students&\#x27; computational thinking abilities within the context of Informatics instruction in this study\.
