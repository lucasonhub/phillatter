# Trustworthy AI for Sustainable Cities: A Multimodal Orchestration Agent\-Based Framework for Civic Compliance

**Autores:** Hasmawaty Ahmad Rahman; Zaid Amin; Nazlena Mohamad Ali; Rahma Santhi Zinaida

**Data:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Sustainable municipal resource stewardship requires a harmonized artificial intelligence \(AI\) architecture that aligns technical performance with human\-centered behavioral dynamics\. Traditional smart city enforcement frameworks frequently trigger psychological reactance and perceptual failure in noisy, crowded urban spaces\. This study presents HARMONY, a socio\-technical edge\-computing and agent\-based framework that integrates localized computer vision \(YOLOv8\) with Mesa 3\.0 agent modeling to promote non\-coercive civic compliance in waste disposal\. We formally calibrate behavioral constants, including the Politeness Modifier \(Lp = 0\.98\), Gratitude Loop increment \(δ = 0\.025\), and irreducible human defiance floor \(ε = 0\.12\), grounded in self\-determination theory, politeness theory, and empirical habit formation models\. We also mathematically resolve sensory asymmetry in Hierarchical Orchestration Logic \(HOL\) by enforcing strictly normalized, bi\-directional modality allocation \(wv \+ wa = 1\.0\) conditioned on acoustic noise \(N\) and optical reliability \(Qv\)\. Across 100\-run Monte Carlo simulations, HARMONY achieves a terminal Correct Disposal Rate \(CDR\) of 87\.9% ± 3\.3% \(95% CI: \[87\.3%, 88\.6%\]\), outperforming the coercive baseline \(57\.0% ± 4\.9%\)\. A comprehensive six\-arm factorial ablation study isolates the distinct contributions of interactional politeness \(\+30\.5% compliance gain\), HOL sensory resilience \(\+1\.6% to \+21\.4% under severe noise\), and gratitude habituation \(\+0\.5% to \+30\.3% sustained retention\)\. Furthermore, population heterogeneity evaluations across diverse civic cohorts confirm robust performance from civic champions to highly resistant populations\. Localized edge prototyping demonstrates 18\.4 ms inference latency with 94\.2% mAP, operating under an Edge\-Only Ephemeral Vector Processing \(E\-EVP\) paradigm that guarantees privacy\-by\-design\. These findings demonstrate that cooperative, polite AI stewardship offers a scalable, legally robust, and ethically aligned alternative to punitive urban surveillance\.
