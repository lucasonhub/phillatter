# Serum Interleukin\-1β and High\-Sensitivity C\-Reactive Protein Across Schizas Morphological Grades in Degenerative Lumbar Spinal Stenosis: An Exploratory Cross\-Sectional Study

**Autores:** Arazy Gifta Prima; Rendra Leonas; Theodorus

**Data:** 2026-10-18 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Background: In degenerative lumbar spinal stenosis \(LSS\), symptom severity often bears little relation to the narrowing seen on imaging, and inflammation may contribute\. Objective: To determine whether the Schizas grade of the dural sac is associated with serum interleukin\-1β \(IL\-1β\) and high\-sensitivity C\-reactive protein \(hs\-CRP\) after confounder control\. Methods: Thirty non\-obese patients with LSS \(body mass index \[BMI\] &lt;30 kg/m²\) at Dr\. Mohammad Hoesin General Hospital, Palembang, were studied after a verified 7\-day anti\-inflammatory washout\. Two blinded radiologists graded axial T2\-weighted magnetic resonance imaging \(MRI\): Grade A, n = 10; B, n = 11; C/D, n = 9 \(κ = 0\.84\)\. Morning IL\-1β and hs\-CRP were compared with Kruskal–Wallis tests and bootstrapped regression\. Results: BMI \(p = 0\.312\) and sex \(p = 0\.927\) were similar across grades, but age and symptom duration rose with grade \(both p &lt; 0\.001\)\. IL\-1β medians climbed from 5\.60 \[4\.90–6\.40\] pg/mL in Grade A to 8\.45 \[7\.20–9\.50\] pg/mL in Grade B and 11\.20 \[9\.10–13\.80\] pg/mL in Grade C/D \(p &lt; 0\.001\); hs\-CRP medians were 2\.15 \[1\.80–2\.60\], 3\.40 \[2\.90–4\.10\] and 4\.90 \[3\.80–6\.20\] mg/L \(p = 0\.003\)\. With age and BMI in the model, grade remained associated with IL\-1β \(B = 2\.05, β = 0\.46, p = 0\.012, R² = 0\.41\) and hs\-CRP \(B = 1\.12, β = 0\.49, p = 0\.009, R² = 0\.44\); no variance inflation factor reached 2\.5\. Conclusion: Greater dural sac compression went with higher serum IL\-1β and hs\-CRP, independent of age and BMI\. Larger longitudinal cohorts are needed before these markers are used alongside MRI\.
