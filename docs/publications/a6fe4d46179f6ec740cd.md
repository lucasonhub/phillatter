# Kablukov, Vitalii: Distinction and Reflection: A Unified Cycle from Ontology to Ethics and Society

**Autores:** 

**Data de descoberta:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** unknown

## Descrição do feed

\_Zenodo\_\. 2026This work proposes a unified system of definitions derived from two fundamental operators — distinction and similarity — without introducing additional terminal primitives\. From these operators the following are sequentially derived: being, consciousness, observer, information, thinking, arbitration, norm, responsibility, justice, society, power, and propaganda\. The system contains 70 definitions and 12 principles\. It is shown that ethics follows from the ontology of the observer as a structural consequence of fixation, not as an independent domain\. The gap between ‘is’ and ‘ought’ is resolved through the obligation of coherence: an observer who has fixed a state of affairs is structurally obligated to maintain coherence with subsequent fixations\. Social concepts \(institution, power, propaganda\) are derived from the same apparatus\. A number of established concepts \(legitimacy, popular will, social contract\) are decomposed into already\-defined components and shown to function as containers masking the absence of arbitration\. The system is self\-unpacking: when loaded into a language model, it allows derivation of definitions not contained in the text\. This property has been verified on several independent models\.
