# Data\-Driven Estimation of the Permissible Running Speed of a Self\-Propelled Railcar from Frame Strain\-Gauge Measurements

**Autores:** Izzatillo Rakhmiddinov

**Data:** 2026-10-14 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Speed limits for special self\-propelled rolling stock are usually set from design documents, while the actual stress state of an aged frame may differ\. This thesis shows how a strength\-based speed limit can be obtained directly from running\-test data\. Maximum stresses measured by 16 strain\-gauge channels on the frame of an ADM\-1 railcar at 10, 30, 55 and 60 km/h were fitted by linear regression\. The models explain 79–99% of the variance \(median R² = 0\.92\)\. The most loaded zone, under the diesel\-engine beam, reaches the permissible stress of 152\.75 MPa at about 54 km/h\. The same regression structure can serve as a first surrogate model for machine\-learning\-based monitoring of electric rolling stock\.
