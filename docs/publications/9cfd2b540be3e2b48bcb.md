# Integrating Ion Mobility\-Resolved Tandem Mass Spectrometry and Microcrystal Electron Diffraction for Structural Elucidation

**Autores:** Aleyna Lumsden; Amalia‐Sofia Piticari; Amar Rai; Artemy Novokhrost; Timea Palmai‐Pallag; Yufeng Li; Zhiyuan Ding; Gloria Gao; Stefan Lohmann; Siyu Liu; Zoltán Takáts; Adam S\. Nelson; Angus I\. Kirkland; Marcus Gallagher\-Jones; Béla Paizs

**Data:** 2026-10-12 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Abstract Determining the three\-dimensional structures of small molecules in complex mixtures remains challenging as current approaches typically require physical isolation and quantities of material that are excessive for many components\. Here we present a reciprocal structure\-determination strategy that integrates ion mobility\-resolved high\-resolution tandem mass spectrometry \(IMS\-HRMS\-MS/MS\) with microcrystal electron diffraction \(MicroED/3DED\), enabling chemically consistent three\-dimensional structure elucidation from sub\-microgram quantities of material\. Analysis of synthetic reaction mixtures revealed multiple stereoisomeric products with distinct ion\-mobility and fragmentation behaviour\. Mechanistically grounded modelling of fragmentation pathways and collision cross sections constrained viable three\-dimensional molecular geometries, while MicroED independently resolved the structure of a crystallographically accessible isomer\. Crucially, the gas\-phase\-derived geometries served directly as molecular replacement search models for MicroED, including for a second reaction product whose diffraction data could not be solved by direct methods alone, thereby demonstrating direct transfer of structural information between mass spectrometry and electron diffraction\. Together, IMS\-HRMS\-MS/MS and MicroED provide complementary structural projections whose integrated interpretation enables structure elucidation of small molecules directly from complex heterogeneous mixtures using minimal amounts of material\.
