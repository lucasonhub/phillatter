# BOSHLANG‘ICH SINF O‘QUVCHILARINING TADQIQOTCHILIK KO‘NIKMALARINI STEAM YONDASHUVI ASOSIDA RIVOJLANTIRISH METODIKASINI TAKOMILLASHTIRISH

**Autores:** JAMOLOVA SHOYIRA UYG&\#x27;UN QIZI

**Data:** 2026-10-13 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

This article examines the pedagogical and methodological foundations for improving the development of primary school pupils’ inquiry skills through the STEAM approach\. In contemporary education, learners need not only to acquire knowledge but also to identify problems, formulate research questions, conduct observations and experiments, analyse evidence, and justify Ta’limda istiqbolli izlanishlar \- xalqaro ilmiy\-metodik jurnal № 9/2026 ISSN: 3060\-527X conclusions\. The article explores ways of integrating Science, Technology, Engineering, Arts, and Mathematics into the stages of inquiry\-based learning\. A proposed methodological model includes problem identification, question formulation, hypothesis generation, experimental design, data collection and analysis, solution improvement, and presentation of findings\. Criteria for assessing inquiry skills, strategies for teacher scaffolding, and principles for organising practical learning activities are discussed\. Recommendations are provided for piloting the proposed model in primary education and evaluating its effectiveness through empirical evidence\.
