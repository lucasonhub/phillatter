# Effects of a gamified intervention on gratitude in a Colombian population

**Autores:** Sebastián Pinzón\-Salazar; Camila Salazar\-Ramírez; Sara Querubín Gaviria; Maria Alejandra Parra Ural; Jaime Arenas; Consolación Gil; Eliana Quiroz\-González

**Data:** 2026-10-14 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Gamification promotes the development of competencies in continuing education contexts\. This study analyzed the effects of a gamified intervention on gratitude in a Colombian population\. A four\-condition quasi\-experimental design was used \(experimental, training, infographic, and no\-treatment\), with pretest–posttest measurements obtained from 160 participants\. The G\-20 Gratitude Questionnaire was administered\. Analyses using linear mixed\-effects models revealed a significant Condition × Time interaction for total gratitude, F\(3, 156\.0\) = 4\.83, p = \.003, with significantly greater increases in the experimental condition than in the other conditions and effect sizes ranging from medium to large\. Significant effects were observed for recognition of gifts \( p = \.005\) and interpersonal gratitude \( p = \.018\)\. The findings provide evidence of the potential of gamification as a pedagogical strategy in continuing education programs, with implications for instructional designers and human resource management professionals\.
