# Interaction Patterns and Embodied Design Processes Using an AI Robotics Kit for K–12 Education

**Autores:** Anssi Lin; Henriikka Vartiainen; Anssi Salonen; Matti Tedre

**Data:** 2026-10-15 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

This multidisciplinary study explores how children interact with a low\-cost robotics educational tool designed to teach machine learning \(ML\) concepts through hands\-on making\. Artificial intelligence \(AI\) and ML are increasingly integrated into K\-12 education, yet tangible approaches are not extensively studied\. We conducted three researcher\-led interventions with children aged 10, 11 and 13, each tasked to build an AI\-enhanced robot using the robotics kit\. Interventions were analyzed both qualitatively and with process mining methods to gain an in\-depth view of the interactions\. Findings indicate that designing and building were very central throughout the interventions, with the affordances guiding the design process forward\. Children exhibited emerging data\-driven thinking and ownership of their design process, while still requiring tailored scaffolding throughout the design process\. We identify a general structure of the design process from all the interventions and emphasize the importance of building and designing activities\. This study provides a new methodological lens to analyzing the interactions between children and embodied AI\-educational tools while also contributing to the current body of knowledge on designing and effectively leveraging embodied learning tools\.
