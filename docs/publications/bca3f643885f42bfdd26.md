# The Correctness Gap: Why AI Billing and Prior Authorization Agents Need Enforcement, Not Just Automation

**Autores:** Anurag Chatterjee

**Data:** 2026-10-14 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Although artificial intelligence \(AI\) is being used in the management of healthcare billing, coding, utilization control, and prior authorization, the efficiency of the administrative work does not mean that the administrative actions taken by AI are more accurate or compliant\. This paper addresses the correctness gap, that is, the gap between the AI recommendation and the verification needed before that recommendation is implemented\. According to previous research in healthcare administration, AI\-supported utilization review, and administration governance, proper automation requires controls that ensure the automation is not just capable of performing the recommended task, but also whether the recommended action should be taken\. To address this issue, the paper proposes a compliance gate which includes deterministic checks for verifiable requirements, controlled AI judgment on objective criteria in clinical records, and human review for unresolved cases, conflicting evidence, exceptions, and decisions that require human judgment\. It also proposes a scoring system of compliance using metric of blocked, missed violation rate, false block rate, escalation rate, time to take the expected action, and estimated cost avoided\. This is conceptual and has not yet been evaluated against existing administrative controls\. Its purpose is to determine an architecture and an approach to be evaluated by future research rather than claim benefits in compliance\.
