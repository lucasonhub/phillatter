# PERSONALIZED DIAGNOSTICS AND TREATMENT OF ACUTE SURGICAL AND UROLOGICAL PATHOLOGY \(BASED ON THE RESULTS OF PLANNED RESEARCH\)

**Autores:** V\. Polovyi; Ruslan I\. Sydorchuk; V\. S\. Zaitsev; O\. Rotar; О\. С\. Федорук; K\. A\. Vladychenko; P\. M\. Volyanyuk; І\. І\. Ілюк; Р\. П\. Кнут; A\. S\. Palianytsia; Петрюк Богдан Васильевич; O\. M\. Plehutsa; S\. I\. Railianu; Yu\.M\. Myshkovskyi; Yurii М\. Solovei

**Data:** 2026-10-14 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Urgent surgical and urological diseases possess a leading position in the structure of pathologies\. At the same time, the existing healthcare system, which is based on the use of therapeutic approaches that have proven effective in other patients, is insufficiently effective, prompting the development of personalized treatment tactics\. Aim of the work – to summarize the results of the planned scientific research of the Department of General Surgery and Urology for 2022\-2027, scientifically substantiate and improve existing and develop new methods of personalized diagnosis and treatment of acute surgical and urological pathology\. Materials and methods\. The study is combined \(clinical\-experimental, prospective, cohort\)\. The experimental part was conducted on 280 white nonlinear rats using the method of polarization tomography of birefringence of the polycrystalline component of histological sections and blood films during modeling of peritonitis and abdominal sepsis of varying severity\. The clinical block covers 1039 patients: 696 \(66\.99%\) with acute abdominal pathology, 198 \(19\.06%\) with non\-abdominal surgical pathology \(including 123 patients with diabetic foot syndrome – DFS\), 145 \(13\.95%\) patients with acute purulent\-inflammatory diseases of the urinary system\. For analysis and formation of personalized algorithms, methods of variation statistics, special software, and auxiliary artificial intelligence tools were used\. Results\. The polyetiological nature of urinary system lesions was confirmed with dominance of E\. coli and S\. aureus, but the pathogen was not identified in 13\.48% of cases\. In surgical treatment of DFS, the high technical effectiveness of balloon angioplasty \(94\.3%\) was proven and the expansion of individual indications for revascularization was substantiated\. According to polarization tomographic mapping of blood films of experimental animals, a scenario of necrotic destruction of optical anisotropy was revealed corresponding to the severity of peritonitis\. The use of developed personalized approaches to determine the timing and stages of interventions allowed reducing the frequency of postoperative complications by 28\.1% and shortening the length of hospital stay by 14\.08%\. Conclusions\. The conducted scientific research allowed a comprehensive assessment of the main parameters of acute surgical and urological diseases, to develop new and improve existing methods of diagnosis and treatment\. It was shown that acute surgical and urological diseases dominate in the structure of pathologies treated in the clinic of general surgery and urology\. Integration of clinical, laboratory, and instrumental parameters into a single prognostic algorithm using AI tools can be the basis for optimizing treatment\. The obtained data allow asserting that personalized diagnostic and therapeutic approaches have development potential and require active implementation\.
