# Pneumatic retinopexy identification in electronic medical records using an on\-premises large language model

**Autores:** James Tsimiklis; Shrirajh Satheakeerthy; Jai Ethan Paris; Weng Onn Chan

**Data:** 2026-10-09 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Reliable procedure identification in electronic medical records \(EMRs\) is important for clinical registries, trial recruitment, quality assurance, and operative log validation\. Administrative coding may not accurately reflect clinical diagnoses or procedures\. In an Australian two\-hospital study, only 53\.9% of ophthalmology procedures were correctly coded \[ 1 \]\.
