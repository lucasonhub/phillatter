# Design and Chemical Fabrication of MoxMn1\-xFe2O4/g\-C3N4 Nanocomposite for Photocatalytic Degradation of Direct Green 6 Dye: Environmental Cleaner Approach

**Autores:** Azqa Amjad; Shumaila Kiran; Muhammad Bilal Qadir; Shazia Abrar; Muhammad Naveed Anjum

**Data:** 2026-10-15 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

MoₓMn₁₋ₓFe₂O₄/g C₃N₄ nanocomposites were successfully synthesized via the hydrothermal method by incorporating mixed spinel ferrite \(MoₓMn₁₋ₓFe₂O₄\) nanoparticles onto graphitic carbon nitride \(g C₃N₄\) sheets to enhance visible light\-driven photocatalytic performance\. XRD, SEM, TEM, FT\-IR, and UV\-Vis were used to characterize the synthesized nanocomposites, and the successful formation of the heterostructure with an enhanced surface area and modified bandgap was observed\. The MoₓMn₁₋ₓFe₂O₄/g C₃N₄ catalysts effectively showed improved photocatalytic degradation of Direct Green 6 \(DG 6\) dye under visible light irradiation, which is superior to g C₃N₄ and ferrite\. The performance of the composite is enhanced due to the synergistic effect between MoₓMn₁₋ₓFe₂O₄ and g\-C₃N₄ parts, where efficient separation of electron hole pairs and long light absorption take place\. The degradation reaction of DG 6 was found to be of pseudo\-first order, and the radical trapping experiments suggested that the major reactive oxygen species in the photocatalytic reaction were the superoxide radicals \(•O₂⁻\)\. The results show the potential of MoₓMn₁₋ₓFe₂O₄/g\-C₃N₄ nanocomposites as an efficient visible light photocatalyst working for wastewater treatment applications\.
