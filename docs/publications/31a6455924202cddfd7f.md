# Crowdsourced Intelligence vs\. AI Propaganda: The Challenges of Global Governance in the Age of Digital Warfare

**Autores:** Pragati Khanna; Harsh Dixit; Jannet Emma Tonateni Fransisco; Varsha Dhio Dora; Trisha Dev

**Data:** 2026-10-15 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Abstract Artificial Intelligence has fundamentally changed the modern digital operation, whereby creating a constant struggle between what is perceived to be true and the human reality\. This study uses a qualitative, exploratory\-descriptive study using secondary data from academic journals, policy documents and institutional reports to examine the interaction between crowdsourced Open\-Sourced Intelligence \(OSINT\) and Artificial Intelligence \(AI\) driven propaganda in modern warfare, alongside the global governance challenges that arise from their interaction\. Findings reveal that there is a serious tension within the digital information ecosystem: whilst generative Artificial Intelligence promotes public opinions to spread disinformation and misinformation that is persuasive and may manipulate political processes, crowdsourced intelligence offers real\-time verification and transparency\. There are Regulatory Frameworks such as the European Union’s AI Act and Digital Services Act that serve as blueprints to counter AI\-driven manipulation from technical solutions to the adoption of human\-centered governance models\. Ultimately, the study demonstrates that it is important to maintain digital information integrity by balancing computational tools with collaborative human verification to foster resilience against any revolving threats in digital security and global governance\. Keywords: Artificial Intelligence, Open\-Source Intelligence \(OSINT\), Crowdsourced Verification, AI\-Driven Propaganda, Digital Warfare, Global Governance, Disinformation, Epistemic Sovereignty
