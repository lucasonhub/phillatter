# Transforming Financial Management: The Impact of Artificial Intelligence

**Autores:** Ankit Shah

**Data:** 2026-10-14 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Society is evolving and so too \(finally\) are the plans of modern financial institutions\. This is because corporate sustainability has moved away from business as usual and is instead utilized to grow competitive advantage by increasing choice for employees and power\-with stakeholders to pursue non\-traditional routes\. This makes robust financial management in the financial industry imperative to uphold sustainable practices and advance\. Successful financial management now combines its primary duties with artificial intelligence \(AI\) to monitor, evaluate, and execute projects within the time constraints set\. A few important credit decisions made during the growth or expansion of credit options were significantly improved using an array of AI techniques in this case\. This method is so well used by finance managers that they have the ability to watch over all of their offices or a single transaction within any part of them\.
