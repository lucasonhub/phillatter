# A lightweight residual multilayer perceptron method for geomagnetic matching navigation

**Autores:** Songnan Yang; Haoxuan Tang; Yu Yuan; Wenqi Bai; Yan Yan; Yong Huang; Shiliang Zhang

**Data:** 2026-10-15 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Abstract To address the inherent challenges of localization in regions with weak or ambiguous magnetic gradients, this paper proposes a lightweight residual multilayer perceptron \(MLRP\) method for geomagnetic matching navigation\. By establishing an accurate nonlinear mapping between geomagnetic vectors and spatial coordinates, the proposed method prevents gradient degradation and the consequent decline in accuracy and stability observed in existing geomagnetic matching methods\. Furthermore, the MLRP method employs an efficient network architecture, achieving a balance between efficiency and accuracy on resource\-constrained mobile platforms, making it suitable for long\-distance navigation missions\. Simulation results demonstrate that, under varying noise conditions, MLRP achieves the lowest mean route\-level error among the evaluated methods\. In one measured route in Xi&\#x27;an, MLRP reduced J\_MAD by 13\.94% relative to ICCP and achieved a batch\-one latency of 1\.7706 ms on an NVIDIA Jetson TX2, confirming the practical feasibility of achieving high\-precision autonomous navigation in GNSS\-denied environments using the MLRP method\.
