# TARIX TA’LIMIDA RAQAMLI STORYTELLING TEXNOLOGIYASINI QO‘LLASHNING PEDAGOGIK MODELI

**Autores:** NARGIZA RAXIMOVNA QORABOYEVA

**Data:** 2026-10-10 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

This article examines the pedagogical potential of digital storytelling technology in history education and proposes a pedagogical model for its effective integration into the learning process\. The main purpose of the study is to identify pedagogical conditions for applying digital storytelling in history lessons and to develop a model aimed at developing students’ historical thinking, independent inquiry, source analysis and digital competencies\. The study employs systemic, competency\-based, activity\-oriented and pedagogical modelling approaches\. As a result, a pedagogical model consisting of six components—target, content, process, technological, assessment and outcome components—is proposed\.
