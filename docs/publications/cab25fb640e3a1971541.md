## Descrição do feed

\_Zenodo\_ 1\. 2026Understanding and disagreement emerge, persist, diverge, and become locally identifiable through heterogeneous operational configurations\. This paper develops a philosophical reconstruction of these processes by examining observation, measurement, communication, historical re\-entry, explanation, intervention, correction, and the reorganization of subsequent possibilities\. The analysis traces material interactions that precede shared conditions of comparison and investigates how records, observational arrangements, temporal scales, and experimental interventions modify the conditions under which understanding and disagreement can arise\. Predictive performance, explanatory relations, epistemic justification, communicative success, and real effects are treated as distinct but potentially interacting operational processes\. The argument engages contemporary theories of scientific understanding and disagreement alongside Sellars, Brandom, Wolfendale, Brassier, and Sacilotto\. It connects these investigations to operational\-shape realism, the Ziran System, and Xenoscience\. Two synthetic PyTorch studies comprising 500 and 300 runs examine restricted observability, distribution shifts, historical reuse, predictive divergence, and simulated interventions\. Their results establish configuration\-dependent differences in prediction and action under specified experimental conditions\. They do not establish machine understanding, philosophical disagreement, or autonomous scientific discovery\. The existing Xeno–Ziran Simulator serves as an architectural reference and was not directly connected to these experiments\. The paper develops a configuration\-dependent account of how heterogeneous operations can alter the conditions under which understanding and disagreement become possible, persist, disappear, and re\-emerge\. \(direct link \)
