\_Philosophical Studies\_ 182 \(1\):229\-255\. 2025How should we design AI systems that make moral decisions that affect us? When there is disagreement about which moral decisions should be made and which methods would produce them, we should avoid arbitrary design choices\. However, I show that this leads to a regress problem similar to the one metanormativists face involving higher orders of uncertainty\. I argue that existing strategies for handling this parallel problem give verdicts about where to stop in the regress that are either too arbitrary or too difficult to implement\. I propose a new strategy for AI designers that is better than these alternatives\. \(direct link \)
