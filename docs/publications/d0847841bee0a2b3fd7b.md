# Molecular Docking, Network Pharmacology, and Antioxidant Assays Suggesting Therapeutic Potential of L\-Theanine in the Treatment of Mood Disorders

**Autores:** 

**Data:** 2026-10-12 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Aim: In the present study, the authors carried out the molecular docking, network pharmacology, and antioxidant efficacy studies on L\-Theanine\.Results: The findings suggested the interaction between L\-Theanine and MAO, highlighting the ligand&\#x27;s potential as an MAO\-A and MAO\-B inhibitor\.The defined grid box parameters and detailed mapping of interactions lend credibility to the docking results, supporting the hypothesis that L\-Theanine may inhibit MAO\-A activity\.Network pharmacology analysis suggested the possible interaction with interactions with MgATP, MgADP, phosphate, and TAS1R1, further suggesting the possible modulation of hedonic and anhedonic behavior\.The antioxidant assays suggested that L\-Theanine demonstrates radical\-scavenging ability in the DPPH assay, reduces ferric ions \(Fe3\+\) to ferrous ions \(Fe2\+\) and minimizes metal\-induced oxidative reactions in the FRAP assay, and general antioxidant effectiveness in the ABTS assay\.Conclusion: The present study suggested the possible interaction of L\-theanine with MAO\-A and B, modulates taste and exerts antioxidant activity, further providing a mechanistic basis for its therapeutic potential\.
