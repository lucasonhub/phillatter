# An opsonic peptide promotes radiation\-induced macrophage extracellular trap clearance to alleviate inflammation

**Autores:** Yongchan Lee; Gaifeng Ma; Dilara Aylar; Monowar Aziz; Ping Wang

**Data:** 2026-10-12 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Ionizing radiation exposure leads to inflammation and tissue damage, in which macrophage extracellular traps \(METs\) may play a role\. Effective strategies to mitigate inflammation caused by radiation\-induced METs are needed\. We have recently developed a novel opsonic peptide, OP18, that targets multiple damage\-associated molecular patterns \(DAMPs\) and enhances their clearance by macrophages\. Because METs contain DAMPs, including histone H3 and high\-mobility group box 1 protein \(HMGB1\), we hypothesize that OP18 promotes the clearance of radiation\-induced METs and thereby reduces inflammation\. In vitro studies were conducted using macrophages exposed to radiation and METs, with or without OP18 treatment\. METs uptake, endo\-lysosomal trafficking, integrin involvement, mitochondrial function, NF\-κB activation, and cytokine release were assessed\. OP18 significantly enhances MET uptake by macrophages, an effect that is mediated by α v β 3 \-integrin and involves the endo\-lysosomal trafficking\. This enhanced clearance by OP18 alleviates METs\-induced mitochondrial stress and restores metabolic function \(ATP production, basal/maximal respiration\) in macrophages\. Furthermore, OP18 treatment exhibits a trend towards decreasing METs\-induced NF\-κB activation while increasing the release of anti\-inflammatory cytokine IL\-10\. OP18 represents a novel therapeutic approach to mitigate radiation\-induced inflammation by promoting the efficient clearance of pro\-inflammatory METs, thereby restoring cellular homeostasis and dampening inflammatory signaling\.
