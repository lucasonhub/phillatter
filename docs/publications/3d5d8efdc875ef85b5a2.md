# Reformulating the General Theory of the State and Constitution in the Age of Radical Transformations Challenges of Digitalization, Artificial Intelligence, and Third\-Generation Rights

**Autores:** mohamed kamal arafa el\-rakhawi

**Data:** 2026-10-11 · **Tipo:** book

## Resumo

EL\-RAKHAWI CONSTITUTIONAL THEORY REFORMULATION: SUMMARY This doctrine updates classical state and constitutional theory for the digital age, AI, and climate crises through five pillars: \(1\) Digital State and Cyber Sovereignty; \(2\) Constitutional Supremacy vs\. Algorithmic Legislation; \(3\) Reengineering Separation of Powers against the Hidden Fourth Power \(Big Tech\); \(4\) 3rd/4th Generation Rights including Eco\-Digital Constitutionalism; \(5\) Constitutional Review of Algorithmic Impact with Reversal of the Burden of Proof\. Key innovations include Transnational Digital Constitutional Treaties and the Digital Constitutional Resilience Index \(DCRI\), employing a Weighted Geometric Mean with an Operational Floor to mathematically evaluate constitutional adaptability\.
