# Naoto, Fujie: How Do Operational Shapes Penetrate Reality? Xeno\-Time, Address Domain, Ziran System, Xenoscience, Embodiment, Institutions, Xenocapital, and Off\-Planet Operation

**Autores:** 

**Data de descoberta:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** unknown

## Descrição do feed

\_Zenodo\_ 1\. 2026This paper develops a general account of how operational shapes penetrate reality: how locally generated differences propagate beyond their initial configuration and alter subsequent physical, informational, biological, technological, institutional, or extraterrestrial possibilities\. Penetration is distinguished from representation, correlation, and local causal effect by requiring a difference to survive or transform across changes of medium, scale, temporal interval, address, or organizational boundary while continuing to modify downstream reachability\. Xeno\-Time analyzes the heterogeneous temporal rates through which such effects persist, decay, synchronize, or become irreversible\. Address Domain identifies where an operational difference can be retained, reactivated, or coupled to later consequences without presupposing a single enduring subject or carrier\. The Ziran System provides a distributed architecture for tracking observation, intervention, memory, and feedback across heterogeneous configurations, while Xenoscience extends the analysis to scientific systems whose variables, boundaries, and observational conditions remain revisable\. Embodiment and institutions provide contrasting cases in which penetration reorganizes bodily coordination, social constraints, authority, and resource access\. Xenocapital tracks the conversion of such differences into differential future reachability through infrastructure and resource concentration\. Off\-planet operation tests whether these relations remain identifiable when terrestrial bodies, institutions, temporal rhythms, and infrastructures no longer provide the default configuration\. The broader claim is that reality penetration should be identified by configuration\-crossing changes in subsequent possibilities, not merely by the persistence of a representation or the occurrence of an isolated effect\. \(direct link \)
