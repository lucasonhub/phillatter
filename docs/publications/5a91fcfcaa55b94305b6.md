# HR 5\.0 Practices for Bridging the Skill Gap and Enhancing Youth Employability: Evidence from Corporate\-Oriented Training in Ludhiana, Punjab

**Autores:** Jahangeer Ahmad; Divya Bhatia; Satinderbir Kaur

**Data:** 2026-10-09 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

The rapid transformation of the workplace driven by Industry 5\.0, digitalization, and emerging technologies has significantlyinfluenced the nature of skills required by organizations\. In this evolving business environment, bridging the skill gap amongyouth has become a critical challenge for enhancing employability and ensuring sustainable workforce development\. The present study examines the role of corporate\-oriented training initiatives in developing employability skills among youth withinthe framework of Human Resource 5\.0 \(HR 5\.0\)\. The study focuses on understanding how industry\-driven training approaches,technological integration, and human\-centric HR practices contribute to improving workforce readiness and career opportunities\. The studies is performed in Ludhiana, Punjab, thinking about its importance as an business hub with numerous employment opportunities\. An empirical studies layout has been followed to research the perceptions of children concerning company schooling effectiveness, talent improvement practices, and employability enhancement\. Primary facts become accumulated from respondents thru a established questionnaire, and suitable statistical strategies have been carried out to observe the connection among company\-orientated schooling and children employability\. The findings of the study highlight that industry\-aligned training programs play a significant role in reducing skill mismatches, enhancing technical and soft skills, and improving employment prospects among young individuals\. The study emphasizes the importance of HR 5\.0 practices that integrate technology, continuous learning, and human\-centric approaches to develop a future\-ready workforce\. The research contributes to the existing literature by providing empirical insights into how organizations, educational institutions, and policymakers can collaborate to design effective training frameworks for youth employability enhancement\.
