# Network Traffic Feature Selection for Intrusion Detection Using Statistical and Correlation Analysis

**Autores:** Su Mon Tint Soe; Cho Me Me Maung

**Data:** 2026-10-13 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

In the contemporary landscape of increasingly sophisticated network attacks, effectively selecting salient features from network traffic data has become a critical step in developing robust Intrusion Detection Systems \(IDS\)\. However, the efficacy of Machine Learning \(ML\) and Deep Learning \(DL\)\-based IDS is often compromised by the high dimensionality of network traffic features, disparate data distributions, and the presence of irrelevant features\.
