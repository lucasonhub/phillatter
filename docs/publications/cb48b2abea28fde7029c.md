# Digital twin\-driven transformation of hydropower and dam structures: toward smart structural health monitoring

**Autores:** Amal Aboulhassane; Abdelilah Jerrari; Fouzia Kassou; Om El Khaiat Moustachi

**Data:** 2026-10-13 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

ABSTRACT In the digital transformation era, civil engineering infrastructure is undergoing profound life\-cycle evolution, enhancing efficiency, safety, sustainability, and resilience\. Given their unique nature and the complexity of their design, construction, and management, as well as their direct impact on water security and public safety, Hydropower and Dam Structures \(HDS\) are a high\-priority domain for the implementation of digital technologies, particularly Building Information Modeling \(BIM\), the Internet of Things, and artificial intelligence\. This paper aims to provide an in\-depth state\-of\-the\-art review of the implementation of digital technologies, particularly BIM and Digital Twin, in HDS, with a focus on real\-time Structural Health Monitoring \(SHM\)\. This review builds on a systematic and critical analysis of more than 120 carefully selected, highly relevant references\. It investigates the main digital technologies supporting HDS transformation \(information modeling, data acquisition and pipelines, and data processing\) and highlights adoption drivers and barriers in developed and developing countries\. It clarifies the concepts of BIM and Digital Twin, along with the supporting tools and technologies\. It also summarizes the most recent life\-cycle HDS applications and discusses interoperability challenges and potential solutions\. In particular, the review provides further insights into real\-time SHM of HDS by analyzing the most advanced BIM\-driven case studies integrating the Internet of Things, artificial intelligence, numerical simulations, and remote sensing\. This review establishes a comprehensive foundation and provides practical recommendations to guide researchers and practitioners seeking to design, assess, and operationalize digital solutions for large civil infrastructure, thereby accelerating digital transformation while strengthening safety, intelligence, and resilience\.
