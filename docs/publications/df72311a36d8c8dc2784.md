# PENGARUH PENGGUNAAN E\-COMMERCE DAN PROMOSI ONLINE TERHADAP PERILAKU BELANJA MAHASISWA FAKULTAS EKONOMI UNIVERSITAS MAHAPUTRA MUHAMMAD YAMIN

**Autores:** Syarifah Mutia; Dwi Annisa Rahmanillahi; Meyriska Eka Putri; Karin Septiani

**Data:** 2026-10-14 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Abstract: The growth of e\-commerce has transformed consumer shopping patterns, particularly among university students, who constitute a group of active internet users\. Beyond the convenience offered by e\-commerce, online promotions also serve as a factor influencing students&\#x27; shopping behavior\. This study aims to determine the impact of e\-commerce usage and online promotions on students&\#x27; shopping behavior\. A quantitative method with an associative approach was employed\. The study population consisted of 145 students, with a sample of 107 respondents determined using Slovin&\#x27;s formula\. Data collection was conducted via questionnaires, while data analysis utilized SPSS Version 25 software, covering validity tests, reliability tests, classical assumption tests, multiple linear regression analysis, t\-tests, F\-tests, and the coefficient of determination\. The results indicate that e\-commerce usage has a positive and significant effect on students&\#x27; shopping behavior, with a calculated t\-value of 2\.728 \(exceeding the t\-table value of 1\.659\) and a significance value of 0\.007 \(below 0\.05\)\. Online promotions also have a positive and significant effect on students&\#x27; shopping behavior, with a calculated t\-value of 4\.609 \(exceeding the t\-table value of 1\.659\) and a significance value of 0\.000 \(below 0\.05\)\. Simultaneously, e\-commerce usage and online promotions significantly influence students&\#x27; shopping behavior, with a calculated F\-value of 45\.155 \(exceeding the F\-table value of 3\.08\)\. The variable exerting the most dominant influence on students&\#x27; shopping behavior is online promotion\.\.Keywords: E\-Commerce, Online Promotion, and Shopping Behavior
