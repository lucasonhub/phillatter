# A low\-cost UAV\-edge\-cloud system for agricultural straw burning monitoring: multi\-target detection and event\-based alerting

**Autores:** Wannan Wang; Ying Zhang; Zeying Han; Tizhou Qiao; 江铁鹰

**Data:** 2026-10-09 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Crop residue burning damages air quality and affects agricultural environments\. Small and short\-duration events are difficult to detect through satellite observations or manual patrols\. This study develops a low\-cost UAV\-Edge\-Cloud system for real\-time straw burning monitoring that combines local inference with event\-based alert reporting\. The system detects flame, smoke, and burned residue\. These targets represent active combustion, early or smoldering combustion, and post\-event evidence\. A CBAM\-enhanced YOLOv8s model improves detection of small flames\. INT8\-quantized CBAM\-YOLOv8s is deployed on the MaixCAM\-Pro camera, a low\-cost edge platform capable of capturing RGB imagery and performing model inference locally\. The deployed model achieves 0\.864 mAP@0\.5 and runs at 24\.5 frames per second\. In field tests, the system attained 91% image\-level classification accuracy, a local response around 100 ms and a cloud reporting around 500 ms\. This work demonstrates that practical deep learning\-based agricultural fire monitoring is achievable on low\-cost edge hardware, offering a basis for cost\-effective environmental monitoring\.
