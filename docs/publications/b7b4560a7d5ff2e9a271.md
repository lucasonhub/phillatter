# Deep Q‐Network‐Guided TiO 2 /NOA61 Microstructures for Light Outcoupling and Angular Emission Redistribution in Blue Top‐Emitting OLEDs

**Autores:** Zi‐Run Zhao; Kun‐Peng Song; Heng Liu; Yu Zhang; Na Jiang; Dandan Song; Bo Qiao; Suling Zhao

**Data:** 2026-10-09 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

ABSTRACT Enhancing light outcoupling in blue top‐emitting organic light‐emitting diodes \(blue T‐OLEDs\) requires optical architectures capable of redirecting internally confined energy into extractable radiative channels\. However, the coupled geometric‐compositional parameter space makes empirical optimization inefficient\. Here, a deep Q‐network \(DQN\)‐guided strategy is developed to optimize TiO 2 /NOA61 microcylinder arrays for mode‐redistribution‐enhanced outcoupling in blue T‐OLEDs\. The cylinder radius, array period, and TiO 2 filling fraction φ are optimized as coupled variables, and the selected structures are nanoimprinted onto the Ag top electrode\. Compared with the reference device, the optimal device with φ = 9\.1% achieves a 52\.6% increase in maximum luminance at 45\.5°, where the emission peak is located at 464 nm\. EQE rises from 18\.66% to 21\.75% at φ = 0 and 23\.21% at φ = 9\.1%, showing geometry‐ and composition‐associated gains\. Finite‐difference time‐domain \(FDTD\) and temporal coupled‐mode theory \(TCMT\) analyses reveal that the enhancement originates from power‐flow redistribution, loss‐channel repartitioning, and strengthened oblique‐angle radiative coupling, yielding a simulated angle‐integrated outcoupling enhancement factor of 1\.82\. Beyond this blue device, the DQN framework provides a basis for future wavelength‐ and angle‐specific outcoupling designs targeting directional luminance delivery and reduced off‐cone optical waste in near‐eye AR/VR displays\.
