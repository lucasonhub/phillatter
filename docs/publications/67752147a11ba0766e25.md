# Developing smart learning environments in general education schools: A conceptual framework for digital transformation

**Autores:** Đinh Thị Hải

**Data:** 2026-10-14 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Digital transformation has created new opportunities for improving teaching, learning, and educational management through the development of smart learning environments\. However, the effective integration of digital technologies into general education requires a comprehensive approach that connects technological infrastructure, pedagogical innovation, data driven management, and institutional capacity\. This study aims to examine the theoretical foundations of smart learning environments and propose a conceptual framework for their development in general education schools\. Using a qualitative research approach based on literature review and conceptual analysis, the study synthesizes international perspectives on smart education, digital transformation, and technology enhanced learning\. The findings identify five interconnected components of smart learning environments: digital infrastructure and learning resources, smart pedagogical practices, learner centered support, educational data and assessment, and institutional governance and teacher capacity\. Based on these components, an integrated conceptual framework is proposed to guide the development and implementation of smart learning environments in primary and secondary education\. The study further discusses implementation strategies, institutional requirements, and implications for Vietnamese general education schools\. The proposed framework provides a theoretical foundation for aligning digital transformation initiatives with educational objectives, emphasizing that technological innovation should support meaningful learning experiences, educational equity, and sustainable school development\.
