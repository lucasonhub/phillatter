# YOUTHIRON: Design and Evaluation of a Mobile Health–Based Adolescent Anemia Screening Information System Using Design Science Research

**Autores:** Ni Wayan Priscila Yuni Praditya; Vieronica Varbi Sununianti; Heribertus Andi Widagdo

**Data:** 2026-10-09 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Adolescent anemia remains a significant public health problem in Indonesia due to limited early detection systems and low integration of digital health data management\. This study aims to design and evaluate YOUTHIRON, a mobile\-based anemia screening information system that integrates digital hematology parameters and a rule\-based decision\-support mechanism\. The research applies the Design Science Research \(DSR\) approach, comprising problem identification, artifact design, development, and evaluation\. System evaluation was conducted using black\-box testing, the System Usability Scale \(SUS\) involving 30 adolescent respondents, and system performance testing\. The results indicate that all functional modules achieved 100% validation, with a classification accuracy of 90% based on confusion matrix analysis, and a SUS score of 82\.3 \(SD = 4\.5\), categorized as excellent\. The average system response time was 1\.8 seconds\. The findings demonstrate that YOUTHIRON improves screening efficiency by 66\.7% and supports early detection and monitoring of adolescent anemia\. However, this study is limited by a relatively small sample size and controlled testing conditions\. This study contributes to the information systems field by providing a validated integration of mobile health architecture, rule\-based clinical decision support, usability evaluation, and system performance measurement within a unified digital screening platform\.
