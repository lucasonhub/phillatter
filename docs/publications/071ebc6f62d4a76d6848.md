# Ma, Hanlin: The Limit of Artificial Intelligence Moral Enhancement

**Autores:** 

**Data de descoberta:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** unknown

## Descrição do feed

\_Journal of Dialectics of Nature\_ 42\. 2020AI moral enhancement is a sub\-topic within the broader current of “human moral enhancement\.” Its proponents hope that an “artificial moral advisor” \(AMA\), capable of “objective” moral representation and powerful information acquisition, can help human beings raise the level of their moral cognition\. However, since an AMA cannot, after all, meet the standard of the “ideal observer” \(IO\), it inevitably falls into the trap of expertism\. Moreover, researchers who seek to build “moral machines” also presuppose some form of IO perspective when they use learning algorithms to construct systems for automatic moral classification and decision\-making\. Through an examination of the experimental study of Guarini’s Moral Case Classifier \(MCC\), we find that a hypothesis function without pre\-classification cannot be fitted, which means that a generalist AMA consistent with the IO cannot be built\. In view of this, we propose a different approach to AI moral enhancement—an “AMA as the Other”—and hope that artificial general intelligence \(AGI\) will provide the basis for its realization\. \(direct link \)
