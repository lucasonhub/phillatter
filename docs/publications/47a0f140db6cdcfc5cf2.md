# Asociación de multimorbilidad, fragilidad y discapacidad funcional con la autopercepción de la salud en adultos mayores colombianos: un análisis de la encuesta SABE Colombia

**Autores:** Janeth Jurado\-Delgado; José Mauricio Ocampo\-Chaparro; Daniel A\. NIEVA\-POSSO; Carlos A\. Reyes\-Ortiz

**Data:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Introduction\. Self\-rated health is a general marker of morbidity and mortality that may also be correlated with geriatric conditions\.Objective\. To assess the association of multimorbidity, frailty, and functional disability with self\-rated health in older adults\.Materials and methods\. We used data from the SABE \(Salud, Bienestar y Envejecimiento\) Colombia 2015 survey, which included adults aged 60 years or more\. We analyzed a subsample of 4474 participants and evaluated self\-rated health \(poor versus good\), frailty, multimorbidity, and functional disability\. We used multivariate logistic regression models to assess the association of the dependent variable \(poor self\-rated health\) with frailty, multimorbidity, and functional disability combined as the main independent variables\.Results\. In the first model, poor self\-rated health was significantly associated with frailty/functional disability \(OR = 4\.12; 95% CI: 3\.00\-5\.66; p &lt; 0\.0001\); in the second, with functional disability/multimorbidity \(OR = 3\.54; 95% CI: 2\.73\-4\.58; p &lt; 0\.0001\); in the third, with multimorbidity/frailty \(OR = 3\.15; 95% CI: 2\.49\-3\.98; p &lt; 0\.0001\), and in the fourth, with frailty/functional disability/multimorbidity \(OR = 5\.22; 95% CI: 3\.41\-7\.99; p &lt; 0\.0001\)\. This last model showed the highest association with poor self\-rated health\. Conclusion\. Self\-rated health, a subjective measure for older adults’ health status, was correlated with geriatric conditions such as multimorbidity, frailty, and functional disability\.
