# CAROTID ARTERY STENTING WITHOUT ANTI\-EMBOLIC DEVICE: A SINGLE CENTER EXPERIENCE

**Autores:** Murat Kocaoğlu; Özkan Çeli̇ker; Duygu Aras Seyit

**Data:** 2026-10-10 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Background Carotid artery stenting \(CAS\) is a frequently used method in carotid artery stenosis\. There are many publications in the literature regarding whether or not anti\-embolic devices should be used during carotid stenting\. In this study, we reported the short\-term results of our patients in whom we did not use anti\-embolic devices during carotid stenting procedure\. Methods We retrospectively analyzed patients who underwent carotid artery stenting between June 2022 and June 2025 in our clinic\. The patient&\#x27;s clinical characteristics and procedural datas were obtained by scanning patient files\. Results Sixty patients were included in the study\. The average age of the patients was 70±9\. Fifty five patients underwent stenting under local anesthesia and 5 under general anesthesia\. Right carotid artery in 12 patients, left carotid artery in 40 patients, and bilateral carotid artery stenting was performed in 8 patients\. Self\-expandable stents were used in all patients\. But, no anti\-embolic devices were used in any of the procedures\. Patients were followed up for 11± 5 \(minimum 1\-maximum 24\) months for recurrent stroke and death\. No recurrent stroke or transient ischemic attack \(TIA\) was detected in any patient during the follow\-up period\. Conclusion In experienced centers, CAS is successfully performed without the use of anti\-embolic devices and the risk of complications is quite low\.
