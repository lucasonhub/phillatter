# AEGIS: an operational infrastructure for post\-market governance of adaptive medical AI under US and EU regulatory frameworks

**Autores:** Fardin Afdideh; Mehdi Astaraki; Fernando Seoane; Farhad Abtahi

**Data:** 2026-10-13 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Medical AI requires governance preserving safety during continuous improvement\. FDA Predetermined Change Control Plans \(PCCP\) and EU post\-market surveillance address iterative updates but lack operational tooling\. We present AEGIS, an internal governance and post\-market\-monitoring infrastructure designed to support the documentation of predetermined governance pathways for PCCP and Medical Devices Regulation alignment\. AEGIS comprises dataset assimilation and retraining, model monitoring, and conditional decision\-making modules\. It utilizes a four\-category taxonomy \(APPROVE, CONDITIONAL APPROVAL, CLINICAL REVIEW, REJECT\) and an independent ALARM signal\. Instantiating AEGIS for sepsis prediction \(PhysioNet\) and brain\-tumor segmentation \(BraTS\) exercised all decision categories, successfully routing failures to the critical REJECT \+ ALARM state \(no deployable model; released model at risk\)\. On real cross\-site data, drift detection ROC\-AUC rose from 0\.82 to 1\.00, reliably triggering near 500\-patient batches with zero false alarms\. This proof\-of\-concept utilizes illustrative clinical models; drift scoring quantifies covariate shift only, leaving concept drift for future work\.
