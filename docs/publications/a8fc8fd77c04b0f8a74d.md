# Structure‐Property Engineering in Ag 23 Nanoclusters for Electrocatalytic Nitrate Reduction

**Autores:** Jianglu Yuan; Demiao Ma; Xingmiao Huang; Hua Sheng; Zhixun Luo

**Data:** 2026-10-12 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

ABSTRACT Electrocatalytic nitrate reduction reaction \(NO 3 RR\) provides a sustainable pathway for ambient ammonia synthesis; however, achieving concurrent high activity, high selectivity and appropriate stability of catalysts remains a substantial challenge\. Here we report the synthesis and structure‐property engineering of a class of atomically precise silver nanoclusters via geometric tuning and copper substitution\. Compared with the parent nanocluster Ag 23 \(PET\) 18 \(PPh 3 \) 8 \(abbreviated as Ag 23 ‐1\), a Cu‐substituted Ag 19 Cu 4 \(SCH 2 PhCl\) 18 \(PPh 3 \) 4 nanocluster \(i\.e\., Ag 19 Cu 4 \) modulates the electronic structure and catalytic active sites, giving rise to an improved Faradaic efficiency \(FE ∼71%\) upon NO 3 RR electrocatalysis but with irreversible alteration of the metal oxidation states\. Intriguingly, a geometry‐tuned \[Ag 23 \(SAdm\) 12 \] 3\+ cluster \(Ag 23 ‐2\) exhibits enhanced stability due to its superatomic eight‐electron shell structure \(1S 2 \|1P 6 \|\|\) and delivers an NH 3 yield of ∼6\.5 mg NH3 ·h −1 ·mg −1 cat at −0\.9 V, outperforming the parent Ag 23 ‐1 and comparable with the Ag 19 Cu 4 NCs\. Density functional theory \(DFT\) calculations indicate that the reaction proceeds via a stepwise hydrogenation pathway, with the \*NO 2 ‐to‐\*NO conversion serving as the rate‐determining step, where the energy barriers can be efficiently lowered by the Ag 19 Cu 4 NCs\. This work illustrates that structural engineering constitutes an effective strategy for tailoring activity, selectivity, and stability in atomically precise nanocluster catalysts\.
