# Performance of ChatGPT\-5\.5, Gemini 3, and copilot 2026 in answering chemistry course questions: a cross\-sectional study

**Autores:** Hadigheh Dorri; Morteza Zahedy Gol; Karim Naraki; Mohsen Khosravi

**Data:** 2026-10-10 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Chemistry, environmental health, and occupational health have historically developed in close collaboration\. This study aimed to evaluate the performance of LLMs, in responding to a chemistry course questionnaire used in environmental and occupational health department for educational purposes\. This was a cross\-sectional study conducted in 2026\. The study used a 50\-item multiple\-choice questionnaire, primarily in Persian with limited English terms and formulas, which was administered to LLMs \(ChatGPT\-5\.5, Gemini 3, and Copilot 2026\)\. Performance was evaluated using confusion matrix analysis\. Cochran\`s Q test, McNemar\`s test for pair\-wise comparisons, and Point\-biserial correlation were also conducted to further evaluate the study data\. The LLMs presented mean sensitivity, specificity, and accuracy of 0\.81, 0\.29, and 0\.68, respectively\. Gemini 3 presented a high level of performance, achieving the highest sensitivity \(0\.85\), specificity \(0\.56\), and accuracy \(0\.80\)\. ChatGPT\-5\.5 had the lowest sensitivity \(0\.76\), while Copilot showed the lowest specificity \(0\.07\) and an accuracy of 0\.62\. No significant correlations were found between performance and questionnaire characteristics; however, all models performed poorly on negatively framed items requiring identification of incorrect options\. The study presented some preliminary insights on the performance of LLMs in chemistry\-related questions\. Further research is warranted\.
