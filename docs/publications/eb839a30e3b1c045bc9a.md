# STRUCTURAL\-SEMANTIC AND PRAGMATIC FEATURES OF NEOLOGISMS RELATED TO ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE IN THE ENGLISH LANGUAGE

**Autores:** Rizayeva Xurshida Ravshan qizi

**Data:** 2026-10-12 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

This study examines how English\-language expressions associated with artificial intelligence \(AI\) are formed, how their meanings become specialized, and how speakers deploy them for explanation, persuasion, evaluation, and risk communication\. A purposive, illustrative set of twenty widely recognizable AI\-related expressions is analysed through morphological classification, semantic component analysis, and pragmatic interpretation\. The study distinguishes established lexical items undergoing semantic extension from recently popularized compounds, blends, derivatives, and initialisms\. In the selected set, compounding accounts for 11 of 20 items \(55%\); these proportions describe the analytical selection only and do not estimate the frequency of neologisms in English\. The findings suggest that AI vocabulary performs dual functions: it provides operational labels for technical workflows and frames public judgments about agency, reliability, creativity, and responsibility\. Particular attention is paid to metaphorical expressions such as hallucination and to socially evaluative forms such as AI slop\. The article proposes a replicable coding scheme for future corpus\-based inquiry and discusses implications for terminology management, translation, and critical AI literacy\.
