# Early Dual\-path Detail Preservation for Thin Retinal Vessel Segmentation with U\-Net

**Autores:** Lukman Hakim; Mochammad Lutfi; Cahya Bagus Sanjaya; Setiawan Cahyono Putro; Muthu Subash Kavitha; Qien Yu

**Data:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Thin retinal vessels can be attenuated during early encoder downsampling while contributing little to conventional overlap scores\.We propose Dual\-Path Detail Preservation \(DPD\), a low\-overhead U\-Net modification that retains max\-pooling and adds zero\-initialised, phase\-resolved residuals beside the first two encoder reductions\.Standard U\-Net and DPD were compared over three training seeds on the official DRIVE test set and by datasetspecific out\-of\-fold \(OOF\) evaluation on STARE and subject\-grouped CHASE\_DB1\.On DRIVE, DPD increased sensitivity from 0\.8251 to 0\.8377 and reduced thin\-vessel false negatives by 5\.52%\.A two\-way seed\-and\-case bootstrap retained confidence intervals excluding zero for sensitivity and thin\-vessel false\-negative reduction, whereas missed\-branch and coverage effects became seed\-sensitive\.The primary recovery direction also persisted across 11 prespecified structural\-diagnostic settings and both DRIVE manual annotations\.STARE and CHASE\_DB1 showed smaller, mixed, seed\-dependent effects, with all two\-way OOF intervals including zero\.DPD adds 0\.95% trainable parameters and approximately 2\.70% convolutional GMACs\.The evidence supports a targeted, dataset\-conditional improvement in thin\-vessel recovery rather than universal pixel\-level superiority\.
