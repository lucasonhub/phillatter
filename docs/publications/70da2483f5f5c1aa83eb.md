# Overdimension Vehicle Violation Detection and Monitoring System with Multi\-camera Sensors Using Deep Learning

**Autores:** Arief Rianto Kurniawan; Diah Puspito Wulandari; Rudzidatul Akmam Dziyauddin; Javier Janeti Suprantiyo; Eko Mulyanto Yuniarno

**Data:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Overdimension vehicles, defined as vehicles exceeding legally permitted dimensional limits, remain a challenge in traffic enforcement due to limited automated monitoring\.This study proposes an edge\-based multi\-camera system integrating YOLOv8n\-seg, DeepSORT, and SIFT\-based cross\-camera association\.The selected model achieved an mIoU of 0\.66, a Dice coefficient of 0\.75, and 14\.87 FPS\.On 800 held\-out cross\-camera pairs, SIFT achieved 0\.863 accuracy and a 0\.856 F1\-score, compared with 0\.861 accuracy and a 0\.869 F1\-score for a recent ReIDbased baseline\.In 57 labeled trajectories, overdimension classification achieved 91\.23% accuracy and a 73\.68%F1score\.Ablation on 35 vehicles increased coverage from 94\.29%/88\.57%for the single cameras to 100% and consolidated 64 local trajectories into 35 unique records\.For seven physically measured overdimension vehicles, height estimation achieved an MAE of 0\.061 m, RMSE of 0\.071 m, and MAPE of 2\.46%\.A web\-based platform supports monitoring and violation management\.
