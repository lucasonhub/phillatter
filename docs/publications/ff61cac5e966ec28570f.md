# Design of a questionnaire to assess digital competence for employability training

**Autores:** Yolanda Deocano\-Ruiz; Laura Alonso\-Díaz

**Data:** 2026-10-13 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Introduction Information and Communication Technology \(ICT\) is a fundamental element in personal and professional development, essential for addressing job instability and reducing the risk of unemployment and social exclusion\. Methods This article describes the design and validation process of a questionnaire to assess the digital competence of the population actively searching for work \( Cuestionario de Capacitación Digital de la Población en Búsqueda activa de Empleo \- Digital Training Questionnaire for Active Job Seekers\- , CDPBAE\)\. The methodology combined qualitative and quantitative techniques, including content validation by nine experts, construct validation via Exploratory Factor Analysis using Principal Components Analysis \(PCA\), and reliability analysis through Cronbach&\#x27;s alpha on a sample of 572 unemployed individuals\. Results The final instrument measures three core dimensions: digital ability, digital attitude, and digital perception\. Results demonstrated satisfactory psychometric properties and a good overall internal consistency, with a Cronbach&\#x27;s alpha coefficient of \.810\. Discussion The CDPBAE is a reliable and suitable instrument for determining the level of digital competence in the unemployed population, providing a solid foundation for designing targeted employability and training procedures\.
