## Resumo

AbstractPurpose: This study aimed to evaluate the awareness, knowledge, and attitudes of physicians working in the field of Child and Adolescent Psychiatry in Türkiye toward telepsychiatry\.Materials and methods: This cross\-sectional descriptive study was conducted using an online questionnaire\. Of the 121 physicians reached across Türkiye, 105 completed the survey\. Data were collected using a sociodemographic information form and a 27\-item telepsychiatry attitude form developed by the researchers\. The overall telepsychiatry attitude score was calculated from relevant items between questions 8 and 38 and scored on a 0\-1 scale\. Multiple linear regression analysis was conducted to determine the predictors of the general attitude score toward telepsychiatry\.Results: The mean age of participants was 32\.86±6\.07 years, and 78\.1% were female\. The mean attitude score was 0\.74±0\.13; 37\.1% of participants had a high, 52\.4% a moderate, and 10\.5% a low level of positive attitude\.
