# Maier, Markus: On the Preservation of Explanatory Structure: Understanding Deep Neural Networks via Analogical Mappings Between Models of Complex Systems

**Autores:** 

**Data de descoberta:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** unknown

## Descrição do feed

Deep neural networks \(DNNs\) are remarkably successful, yet their inner workings remain poorly understood\. This thesis addresses this lack of understanding from a philosophy\-of\-science perspective and asks whether ideas from the study of complex systems can help\. It identifies the transfer of knowledge between models of different complex systems as a core method of complex system sciences and reconstructs it as analogical mappings between scientific models\. Drawing on the literature on scientific models and explanation, and on effective theories and renormalization in physics, it develops a framework for analyzing which effective explanations \(higher\-level explanations and renormalization group explanations\) survive such a transfer\. The framework is applied to a case study: the effective theory approach to neural networks proposed by Roberts, Yaida, and Hanin in &quot;The Principles of Deep Learning Theory&quot; \(2022\), situated within the broader &quot;physics of AI&quot;\. The analysis finds no simple one\-to\-one relation between source and target domain\. Instead, it identifies multiple generalized source domains and a partial preservation of explanatory structure, which supports some of the explanations offered by the approach\. The thesis closes by discussing the limits of the formal framework and the tension between formalism and the transdisciplinary promise of complex system sciences\. Revised version of a dissertation accepted by the Munich School of Philosophy \(Hochschule für Philosophie München\) on April 27, 2026\. \(direct link \)
