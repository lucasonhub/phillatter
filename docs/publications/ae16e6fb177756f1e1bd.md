# A Multimodal\-Experimental Approach to Data Visualization: Embodying Information

**Autores:** Murray Dick

**Data:** 2026-10-15 · **Tipo:** book

## Resumo

Data visualization is an increasingly present feature of contemporary life\. It is argued that this popularity is due to their communicative efficiency, and indeed, simplicity in design is embraced as a virtue in many guides to best practice\. And yet, not everyone finds them easy to interpret; during the UK’s 2023 COVID Inquiry, former Prime Minister Boris Johnson was said, by his Chief Scientific Advisor, Sir Patrick Vallance, to have often been ‘bamboozled’ by the daily graphs produced to track the crisis\. Ideas about how data visualizations &\#x27;communicate&\#x27; \(or, how they are understood\) are often based on a computational theory of mind, with an emphasis on cognitive mechanisms, and an underlying assumption that people are essentially rational actors\. But while such an approach may be helpful in terms of helping us understand comprehension, it provides little explanation in terms of what initially attracts people to data visualizations, nor in terms of what in these designs people find engaging\.Alternatively, this book will take an embodied approach to cognition, foregrounding audience affect in theory and in design\. This monograph will introduce an experimental ‘ecology’ of design principles for data visualization, a multimodal\-experimental approach incorporating cognitive metaphor theory and visual grammar, as well as storytelling and dramaturgical techniques\. It will offer a new approach to best practice guidance, based on a conception of data visualization as multisemiotic text\. These ideas will be developed iteratively via a mix of methods and project work; including visitor studies, interviews, and pedagogic research\.
