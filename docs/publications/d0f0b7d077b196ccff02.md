# Principiul bunei administrări în era inteligenței artificiale

**Autores:** Camelia\-Raluca Voinea

**Data:** 2026-10-13 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

This study examines the impact of integrating artificial intelligence tools on the principle of good administration, enshrined in Article 41 of the Charter of Fundamental Rights of the European Union, focusing on the guarantee of a reasonable time for resolving administrative requests\. The paper begins with the legal and case\-law foundation of the reasonable time standard, both at European level \(ECtHR and CJEU case law\) and at national level \(Romanian court practice and the express regulation of time limits in Romanian legislation\), before analysing, in a second stage, the positive contributions that digitalisation may bring to compliance with this standard: faster processing of standardised requests, reduction of human error, increased transparency and accountability, easier access to public information, and optimisation of complex procedures\. The study also identifies the structural limits of this positive impact and the autonomous risks generated by the use of AI – technological dependency, algorithmic errors, excessive standardisation, algorithmic opacity, diluted accountability and unequal access to performant digital resources\. The central conclusion is that, in the age of artificial intelligence, the reasonable time standard becomes a dynamic standard, whose observance depends on a set of institutional and technological safeguards\.
