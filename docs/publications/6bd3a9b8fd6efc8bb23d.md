# Yandangshan Dendrobium officinale flower flavonoids alleviate dextran sulfate sodium \(DSS\)\-induced colitis in association with local intestinal ecological remodeling

**Autores:** Xiang\-Dong Wang; Yujiao Zhang; Jiahao Pan; Rong Huang; Jinou Lin; Xiaodan Zhang; Bolu Sun; Xiaojie Zheng

**Data:** 2026-10-13 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Abstract Objectives Ulcerative colitis arises from epithelial barrier disruption, gut microbial dysbiosis, and aberrant mucosal immune activation\. Given that many food\-derived flavonoids achieve limited systemic exposure after oral administration, how local intestinal activity translates into host tissue protection remains a central question\. Resolving this process is challenging because microbial communities, metabolite outputs, and mucosal responses operate across distinct biological levels and often yield disconnected evidence\. Materials and Methods : Here, we studied chemically consistent Dendrobium officinale flower flavonoids \(DOFF\), validated across six independent batches by liquid chromatography\-mass spectrometry \(LC\-MS\), and integrated absorption, distribution, metabolism, and excretion \(ADME\) prediction, conventional and antibiotic\-perturbed dextran sulfate sodium \(DSS\) colitis models, 16S rRNA sequencing, targeted short\-chain fatty acid quantification, untargeted fecal metabolomics, host\-response network analysis, single\-cell virtual knockout, and whole\-slide artificial intelligence \(AI\)\-assisted spatial pathology\. Results DOFF reduced dextran sulfate sodium\-induced disease activity and colonic injury, restored ZO\-1\- and Occludin\-associated barrier integrity, reconfigured gut microbial structure and fecal metabolic profiles, and suppressed TLR4/MYD88/NF\-κB signaling; computational integration and single\-cell virtual knockout further prioritized FFAR2/HCAR2, TLR4/RELA, and EGFR/LGR5 as candidate host targets associated with metabolite sensing, inflammatory regulation, and epithelial repair, respectively, while DOFF retained partial tissue protection after broad\-spectrum antibiotic perturbation, accompanied by limited shifts in residual microbiota and AI\-predicted spatial pathology features\. Conclusions These findings support a local ecology\-first working model in which DOFF\-associated microbiota\-metabolite remodeling coincides with epithelial barrier restoration and mucosal inflammatory suppression without necessarily requiring high systemic exposure or action through a single host target, although direct causal roles for the microbiota, key metabolites, and host receptors require further validation\.
