# Fraser, Patrick &amp; Miller, Michael E\.: The Kochen\-Specker theorem with determinables and determinates

**Autores:** 

**Data de descoberta:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** unknown

## Descrição do feed

We use the framework of determinable and determinate properties to provide a novel account of the constraints imposed by the Kochen\-Specker theorem\. Specifically, we show that the result may be understood as a prohibition on the possibility of providing an account of quantum properties, as they are usually represented using Hilbert space operators, according to which quantum systems are fully determinate\. That is, the Kochen\-Specker theorem entails that any account of quantum properties compatible with the predictions of quantum mechanics must exhibit determinable\-based metaphysical indeterminacy\. \(direct link \)
