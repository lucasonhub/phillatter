# Fujie, Naoto: Xenoscience

**Autores:** 

**Data de descoberta:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** unknown

## Descrição do feed

Xenoscience develops a philosophical framework for scientific inquiry under conditions in which the objects, variables, boundaries, scales, temporal windows, observational devices, and inferential interfaces of science are not assumed to be fixed in advance\. Rather than defining science by a privileged human subject, a stable ontology, or a predetermined set of categories, the book analyzes how scientific operations emerge, persist, transform, and cease across heterogeneous configurations\. Its central claim is that scientific intelligibility depends on configuration\-specific ignition conditions: what becomes observable, measurable, distinguishable, inferentially available, and experimentally actionable depends on the coupling of sensors, boundaries, scales, temporal horizons, resources, external memory, institutions, and feedback structures\. The book therefore distinguishes reality\-directed scientific constraint from any particular human conceptual scheme while rejecting the assumption that scientific progress converges on a final, exhaustive representation of reality as a whole\. Xenoscience extends scientific realism by treating observability, variable formation, theory construction, and even the operational identity of science itself as revisable achievements within evolving configurations\. It also examines autonomous science, machine\-generated variables, heterogeneous scientific civilizations, nonhuman inquiry, and the possibility that future scientific systems may generate forms of intelligibility not reducible to present human epistemic structures\.
