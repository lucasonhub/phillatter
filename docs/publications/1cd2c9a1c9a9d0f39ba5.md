# Chinese High\-Level Comprehensive Universities’ Participation in Science Teacher Education in the AI Era: Policy Design, Implementation Status, and Sustainable Mechanisms

**Autores:** Tao Deng; Yilong Zhang; Boyang Deng

**Data:** 2026-10-18 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Artificial intelligence is reshaping science education, intensifying demands for high\-quality science teachers\. In 2023, China launched the National Excellence Program to promote the institutionalized participation of high\-level comprehensive universities in teacher education\. Drawing on policy analysis, fieldwork across 12 pilot universities, and the case of Northeast Normal University, this study examines the program’s policy design, implementation status, and sustainable mechanisms in the AI era\. The findings show that while the initiative drives a transition toward an open, multiactor collaborative model, disciplinary and technological resources do not automatically translate into teacher education capacity\. Pilot universities still confront unstable participation incentives, fragmented collaboration, fragile institutional guarantees, and unsystematic evaluation\. Moving from project\-based participation to institutionalized implementation therefore requires sustained organizational translation\. The study proposes a four\-dimensional integrated framework comprising Composite Driving, Collaborative Implementation, Multi\-Dimensional Guarantee, and Effect Evaluation mechanisms to support the transformation of disciplinary and technological resources into stable teacher education capacity and cultivate scholar\-like science teachers with academic literacy, educational literacy, and digital\-intelligence mastery\.
