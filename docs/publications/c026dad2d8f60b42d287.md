# Care and control under dual transformation pressures: a three\-wave multi\-source study of paternalistic leadership, ambivalent psychological safety, surface acting, and employee voice in Malaysia’s service industry

**Autores:** Yuanyuan Ping; Sharmila A\. P\. Jayasingam; Raida Abu Bakar

**Data:** 2026-10-14 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Malaysia’s service industry is undergoing simultaneous artificial\-intelligence transformation and statutory minimum\-wage pressure, creating a context in which service leaders are expected to show individualized care while also enforcing stronger productivity discipline\. Drawing on conservation of resources theory and employee voice research, this three\-wave, multi\-source study examined whether the benevolent\-authoritarian duality of paternalistic leadership was associated with promotive and prohibitive voice through ambivalent psychological safety and emotional resource depletion, and whether surface acting conditioned this pattern\. The analytic sample comprised 360 service employee\-supervisor dyads nested within 72 supervisors and 18 organizations/branches\. Employees reported paternalistic leadership and surface acting at Time 1 and ambivalent psychological safety and emotional resource depletion at Time 2; supervisors rated promotive and prohibitive voice at Time 3\. The benevolence x authoritarianism interaction was positively associated with ambivalent psychological safety \(b = 0\.400, p &lt; 0\.001\), which was positively associated with emotional resource depletion \(b = 0\.390, p &lt; 0\.001\)\. Surface acting strengthened the ambivalent\-safety\-to\-depletion association \(b = 0\.151, p = 0\.001\)\. Resource depletion was negatively associated with promotive voice \(b = −0\.442, p &lt; 0\.001\) and prohibitive voice \(b = −0\.247, p &lt; 0\.001\), with a stronger association for promotive voice\. Conditional indirect associations were stronger under high surface acting\. These observational findings are consistent with the proposition that care\-control leadership can reassure employees while being associated with lower emotional resources for constructive voice in AI\- and wage\-pressure service contexts\.
