# Tourism, Hospitality and Digital Transformation in India: Economic Contributions, Employment, and Pathways to Sustainable Growth

**Autores:** Parul Gupta and  Partho Pratim Seal

**Data:** 2026-10-18 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Tourism and hospitality contribute significantly to India&\#x27;s economic development through employment, foreign exchange earnings, investment, entrepreneurship, and demand in other industries\. Other factors also influence the sector&\#x27;s contribution to development\. Digital transformation, enabled by online travel agencies, mobile money, artificial intelligence \(AI\), analytics, CRM, and smart destination technologies, offers opportunities to reduce transaction costs, expand market reach, improve productivity, and enhance visitor experiences\. However, challenges such as limited digital skills among small businesses, platform dependence, cyber threats, privacy concerns, algorithmic biases, and job losses must be addressed\. This paper analyzes the tourism and hospitality sector&\#x27;s impact on the Indian economy and how technology affects the sector&\#x27;s growth and development\. Using a qualitative, exploratory approach with secondary data from government publications, policies, and academic literature, the paper discusses the significance of productivity, employment, inclusiveness, technology, and resilience\.
