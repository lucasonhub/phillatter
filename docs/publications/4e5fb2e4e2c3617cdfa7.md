# Inteligencia Artificial Explicable en programación competitiva: análisis comparativo de métodos de Machine Learning bajo los principios FATE

**Autores:** Claudia Morales Herrera; Hubert Viltres Sala; Aneyty Martín García; Yeleny Zulueta Véliz; Yaniselis Sánchez Hormigó; Leonides Rodríguez Martínez

**Data:** 2026-10-15 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

This review article analyzes the application of Explainable Artificial Intelligence \(XAI\) in the context of programming education, structuring the analysis around the FATE principles \(Fairness, Accountability, Transparency, Explainability\)\. A systematic review was conducted of the scientific literature published primarily between 2020 and 2026 in databases such as Scopus, IEEE Xplore, and Scielo\. Based on the findings, the review also incorporates an empirical analysis of seven comparable systems that apply ML in educational and competitive programming contexts\. The analysis shows that most ML models applied to programming education prioritize predictive accuracy over transparency\. A structural gap is identified: the most accurate models exhibit the lowest levels of explainability, compromising the FATE principles\. The combination of Decision Trees as an intrinsically interpretable model with TreeSHAP as a post\-hoc explainability technique offers the optimal balance between performance and pedagogical transparency\. The integration of XAI into learning analytics is not a marginal technical improvement, but an ethical imperative to transform automated assessment systems into pedagogical allies that guarantee equity and causal feedback to students\. Keywords: accuracy, decision tree, explainability, FATE, method, transparency, TreeSHAP, XAI\.
