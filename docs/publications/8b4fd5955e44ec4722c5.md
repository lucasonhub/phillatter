# Research on the Innovation of School\-Local Collaborative Education Mode for Rural Smart Culture and Tourism Vocational Education in Hubei Ethnic Minority Areas

**Autores:** Shihao Ye; Shihao Ye; Shihao Ye

**Data:** 2026-10-15 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Driven by the dual strategies of in\-depth integration of culture and tourism and digitalization of vocational education, rural smart culture and tourism has become a core industrial driver for rural revitalization in ethnic minority areas of Hubei Province, while the insufficient supply of localized skilled talents has become a key bottleneck restricting the quality improvement and upgrading of the industry\. Taking Enshi Tujia and Miao Autonomous Prefecture, Changyang Tujia Autonomous County and Wufeng Tujia Autonomous County of Yichang City as the core research fields, this study systematically sorts out the research context and theoretical basis of school\-local collaborative education for rural smart culture and tourism vocational education by comprehensively adopting methods such as literature research, field investigation and case analysis\. It further diagnoses the practical dilemmas existing in the current school\-local collaborative education in Hubei ethnic minority areas, including insufficient subject collaboration, disconnection between talent training and industrial demand, low integration of ethnic culture and smart technology, and lack of long\-term operation mechanism\. On this basis, this study constructs an innovative school\-local collaborative education mode for rural smart culture and tourism vocational education featuring &quot;four\-dimensional linkage, three\-stage progression, and two\-way empowerment&quot;, and clarifies the rights\-responsibility boundaries and collaborative logic of four main subjects, namely the government, vocational colleges, rural communities, and cultural and tourism enterprises\. Furthermore, it designs specific implementation paths from five dimensions: joint major construction, integrated curriculum development, co\-construction of training bases, joint cultivation of teaching staff, and sharing of research outcomes, and puts forward a four\-in\-one guarantee mechanism covering policy, organization, resources and system\. The research results can provide theoretical references and practical schemes for cultivating localized rural smart culture and tourism talents and promoting the long\-term implementation of school\-local collaborative education in Hubei ethnic minority areas, and also offer reference for the reform of culture and tourism vocational education in similar ethnic minority areas in central China\.
