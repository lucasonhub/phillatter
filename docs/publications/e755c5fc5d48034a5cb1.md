# Boosting Vocabulary Mastery: ChatGPT’s Role in Advancing EFL Learners’ Language Skills

**Autores:** Ali Al Ghaithi; Behnam Behforouz

**Data:** 2026-10-18 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

This study attempted to implement artificial intelligence in language learning using the well\-known ChatGPT\. This study focused on measuring the effects of ChatGPT on vocabulary size and depth of knowledge\. To collect the required data, 60 Omani intermediate EFL learners were divided equally into control and experimental groups\. An updated vocabulary level test \(UVLT\), developed by Webb et al\. \(2017\), and a Word Associate Test \(WAT\) developed by Read \(1998\) were used as the pretests to ensure the homogeneity of students’ vocabulary knowledge\. Although both groups continued the traditional face\-to\-face training, the experimental group had the opportunity to use ChatGPT as a facilitator of vocabulary learning with the chance of tailored and personalized activities\. The UVLT and WAT were administered again as posttests to compare learners’ performance after the treatment\. The findings revealed that both groups showed increases in the posttest regarding vocabulary size, but the experimental group outperformed the control group remarkably\. Although no progress was made in the depth of vocabulary by the control group, the experimental group performed significantly better\. The findings are insightful for teachers, curriculum designers, students, researchers, institutions, and policymakers\.
