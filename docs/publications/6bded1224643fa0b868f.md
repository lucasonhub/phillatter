# Mueller, Bernhard: Agreement and Surprise: Global Equilibrium from Local Repair

**Autores:** 

**Data de descoberta:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** unknown

## Descrição do feed

Local correction supplies a common description of distributed computation, neural settlement and some physical models, but its breadth creates an identification problem: which observable claim does the description exclude? We specify compatibility independently of an update rule and separate equilibrium, legitimate continuing operation and complete\-history consistency\. Three obstructions organise the comparison\. Compatible states do not determine a dynamical law; a reduced state can omit information needed for intervention responses; and eliminating a local mediator can destroy a pairwise representation\. An elementary reduction criterion gives an unavoidable prediction\-error bound when two histories collapse to one proposed state, and a standard recurrence separates reduction error from numerical accuracy\. The programme&\#x27;s two running realizations, a continuing brain and an executable finite observer, add four results to the theory: repair is gated by a declared disagreement, records are part of the state and decide the response under intervention, a change of schedule that preserves every equilibrium changes what a two\-state learner is taught by an exactly computable amount, and the certificate a law admits follows the sign structure of its interactions\. Exact controls and measured examples carry each: a memory designed from the reduction criterion confirmed on unseen seeds with the gain and the loss the criterion predicts, a solver comparison with all work charged that retained the existing mechanism, a positive certificate on a whole\-body wiring beside the uncertified fly, and record\-controlled feedback in the finite observer\. A concise physical application states the stronger hypothesis of computation executed by internally constructed hardware in an emergent observer\-relative time, without presenting it as a demonstrated closure\. The contribution is an operational test of proposed shared mechanisms and bounded model evidence, with no claim of a new universal convergence law\.
