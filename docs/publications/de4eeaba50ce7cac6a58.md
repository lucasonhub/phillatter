# Community pharmacists’ awareness, attitudes, and confidence regarding opioid abuse and naloxone use in Kosova: A cross\-sectional survey

**Autores:** Zana Ibraimi; Andi Haxhiu; Driton Shabani; Vjosana Qeriqi; Adhurim Bresa; Dion Haliti

**Data:** 2026-10-09 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Background : Evidence on community pharmacy professionals’ involvement and preparedness to address opioid misuse and related harm in Kosova remains limited\. This study assessed their awareness, attitudes, and self\-reported confidence regarding opioid abuse and naloxone use\. Methods : A cross\-sectional online survey was conducted among 460 community pharmacy professionals across Kosova using a structured online questionnaire\. Results : Participants reported high levels of self\-reported awareness and generally positive attitudes toward addressing opioid\-related problems\. Most respondents \(93\.5%\) reported understanding the difference between opioid abuse and misuse, while 75\.1% were willing to provide counseling\. Familiarity with naloxone was reported by 81\.5%, although 43\.8% did not feel confident administering it\. Confidence in naloxone administration was significantly associated with work experience \( p = 0\.017\)\. Conclusion : Despite positive attitudes and reported awareness, confidence in naloxone administration was lower, indicating a gap in self\-reported preparedness\. Practical training and clearer professional and regulatory guidance may help address this gap\.
