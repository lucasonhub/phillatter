# The analytical study of strategic alliances, digital transformation, and relationship marketing in the pharmaceutical sector

**Autores:** І\. В\. Пестун; Z\. M\. Mnushko; Н\. А\. Сліпцова; Юлія Валеріївна Парфьонова

**Data:** 2026-10-13 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

The relevance of the study is to substantiate the significance and development directions of strategic pharmaceutical alliances in the context of a combination of biological sciences, digital technologies, and new geopolitical conditions, as well as the European integration processes of domestic pharmaceutical enterprises and the need for foreign investment\. Aim\. To study, analyze, and generalize the features of strategic pharmaceutical alliances and their use of digital technologies and relationship marketing as innovative tools for developing and improving the competitiveness of pharmaceutical companies\. Materials and methods\. Scientific literature data, information websites of online publications, and pharmaceutical companies were used\. The methods applied included the content analysis, system\-analytical, structural\-logical, grouping, and generalization methods\. Results and discussion\. The importance of strategic relationships at the international level has been shown; the features, advantages, possible problems and risks, as well as methods for mitigating them, and the future development prospects of pharmaceutical alliances have been identified\. Attention is focused on the fact that the main goals of international strategic relationships in pharmacy are to solve problems of drug shortages and stabilize supply chains through harmonized regulatory frameworks and investment attraction\. The role of digitalization and partnership relationships between pharmaceutical companies and digital platforms in the drug development and market promotion has been demonstrated\. The advantages and forms of relationship marketing with examples from the pharmaceutical sector have been generalized\. Conclusions\. Strategic pharmaceutical alliances are one of the most effective and widespread forms of partnership that aligns with current trends in international cooperation and offers a significant potential for national pharmaceutical enterprises\.
