# Machine Learning Quantifies Within\-Subject Facial Change in Acromegaly

**Autores:** Ali Ammar; Hana Hadley; Saif Rjaibi; Adam Z\. Levitan; Chris Brogly; Alireza Sadeghian; Michael D\. Cusimano; BEAGLE International Study Group; Mihaly Kis; JEANNETTE GOGUEN; Shereen Ezzat; Michael Chicoine; Domenico Solari; Stephanie Cheok; Garni Barkhoudarian; Ian McCutcheon; Joao Paulo Almeida; Donato Pacione; Gabriel Zada; Hani Marcus; Adam Mamelak; James Liu; Michael Catalino; Tomas Garzon Muvdi; Nelson Oyesiku; Jill Sisco; Pat McBride; Muriël Marks; Miranda Day; Kesava Reddy; Karan Singh; Olli Saarela; Ashirbani Saha; Fahad Alkherayf; Euihyun Kimx

**Data:** 2026-10-15 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

OBJECTIVE: Machine\-learning models can distinguish acromegaly from controls in single photographs, but whether gradual facial change can be measured within the same person from ordinary photographs is unknown\. We tested whether a person&\#x27;s face shifts measurably toward an acromegaly\-associated pattern over time\. DESIGN: Multicenter, international, cross\-sectional cohort study with within\-subject longitudinal analysis\. METHODS: People with acromegaly and other pituitary tumors contributed retrospective photographs\. Two timepoints per person were paired by self\-reported disease stage when available, with chronological pairing otherwise\. A face\-recognition model and a general\-image model described each image, and leave\-one\-patient\-out scoring quantified within\-person movement toward the acromegaly\-associated pattern\. RESULTS: Of 582 participants, 110 contributed usable frontal photographs and 69 had two timepoints \(50 acromegaly, 19 tumor controls\)\. The facial score separated held\-out advanced\-disease from control faces \(AUC 0\.858\)\. In acromegaly, the median within\-person shift was 0\.59 and 76\.0% moved in the predicted direction \(p=0\.0003\); 81\.5% of clean untreated pairs moved as predicted \(p=0\.0008\)\. The three contrast strata ordered directionally, although the trend was not significant \(p=0\.15\)\. The 15 non\-Cushing aging controls showed no significant shift \(p=0\.72\), and the between\-group difference remained after adjustment for photograph interval \(p=0\.029\)\. Pre\-diagnosis untreated baselines showed modest separation from all control baselines \(AUC 0\.65, one\-sided p=0\.033\)\. The general\-image model showed no within\-person change \(p=0\.32\)\. CONCLUSIONS: Acromegaly\-associated facial change is measurable within individuals from ordinary photographs, supporting further evaluation of longitudinal photograph\-based monitoring and earlier recognition\.
