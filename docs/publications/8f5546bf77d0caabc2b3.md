# Tra prossimità e distanza: relazioni di cura, affettività e sessualità nella disabilità adulta

**Autores:** Valéria Friso; Giulia De Rocco; Sara Marchesani

**Data:** 2026-10-11 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Adults with disabilities are frequently subject to medicalising and infantilising representations that produce desexualisation and an educational void around affectivity and adult bodily experience\. Professionals working in care services thus find themselves managing physical proximity without shared tools or collective reflection within their teams\. This contribution sits at the intersection of embodiment theories and special education, proceeding from the assumption that the care relationship is constitutively embodied: gaze, rhythm, and proxemics are not incidental but substantive dimensions of the educational encounter\. Within this framework, self\-determination is understood as agency developed within networks of support, and consent as a relational process negotiated through the micro\-gestures of the body – one that requires educators to cultivate an awareness of their own corporeality\. The contribution presents the preliminary findings of a training programme involving approximately 200 coordinators, educators, and care assistants from a cooperative based in Modena, combining theoretical inquiry, experiential activities, and ethnographic observation\. The results indicate that participants&\#x27; representations of their own corporeality shape the conditions under which consent is understood and negotiated in care settings, repositioning bodily self\-awareness as a foundational pedagogical dimension\.
