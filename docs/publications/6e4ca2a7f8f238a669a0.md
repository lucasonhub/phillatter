# CRDTs tolerantes a faltas bizantinas, verificação de invariantes de domínio e arquiteturas local\-first para interoperabilidade na Administração Pública: uma revisão sistemática da literatura

**Autores:** Isaac Hossi Manuel Eduardo

**Data:** 2026-10-12 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

This systematic literature review examines the state of the art in Byzantine fault\-tolerant Conflict\-Free Replicated Data Types \(CRDTs\), domain invariant verification, and local\-first architectures, with the aim of providing a theoretical foundation for the design of the RSTB model \(Byzantine Fault\-Tolerant Semantic Reconciliation\) for data interoperability in the Angolan Public Administration\. The review followed the PRISMA 2020 guidelines\. Searches were conducted in Scopus, the Web of Science Core Collection, and the ACM Digital Library, complemented by forward and backward citation searching\. Peer\-reviewed publications in English relevant to the five predefined research questions were included\. Given the heterogeneity of the studies, a narrative synthesis approach was adopted\. The database searches identified 1,992 records, of which 487 met the eligibility criteria\. An additional 61 studies were identified through citation searching, resulting in a total of 548 included studies\. Of these, 110 formed the prioritized synthesis corpus, comprising the 61 studies identified through citation searching and 49 studies selected using a relevance score stratified by research question\. These studies underwent detailed data extraction and methodological quality assessment\. The remaining eligible studies were characterized using their bibliographic metadata\. The evidence indicates that no existing solution yet integrates strong eventual consistency under Byzantine adversaries, semantic verification of domain invariants, and local\-first operation on resource\-constrained hardware\. Nevertheless, advances in BFT\-CRDTs, Proof\-Carrying CRDTs, and DL\-Lite provide a solid foundation for integrated solutions\. This identified research gap supports the relevance of the RSTB model, which, at the time of this review, had not yet been designed\. The review therefore precedes and informs its design\.
