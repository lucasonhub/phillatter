# Разработка интеллектуальной системы управления энергопотреблением жилого дома: интеграция современных технологий и возобновляемых источников энергии

**Autores:** Али Исмаил; Эдик Койрунович Аракелян

**Data:** 2026-10-12 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

The aim of the study is to develop an intelligent energy consumption management system for residential buildings \(RBEMS\) under the climatic conditions of the Syrian Arab Republic\. The focus is placed on elaborating optimization strategies for storage battery operation to minimize dependence on the centralized grid, achieve more efficient use of renewable energy, and reduce CO₂ emissions\. In carrying out the study, machine learning techniques and artificial neural networks \(ANN\) were employed\. The proposed RBEMS integrates solar generation forecasting, load\-priority management, and reinforcement learning algorithms\. As a result of the study, optimization strategies for storage battery systems have been developed using ANNs applied to predict solar power generation and evaluate the factors influencing load switching between conventional power grids and local energy sources\. The proposed management system demonstrates high efficiency in distributing electrical loads based on priority without restricting energy consumption, ensuring seamless switching between grid\-connected and renewable energy sources\. The study highlights the versatility of the proposed approach and its contribution to improving the resilience and reliability of power supply in residential sectors\.
