# Professional future expectations and mental health in university students: a psychosocial and environmental approach

**Autores:** Tânia Gaspar; Ana Cerqueira; Fábio Botelho Guedes; Elisa Aires; Catarina N\. Matias; Cátia Oliveira; André Tavares Rodrigues; Inês Jongenelen

**Data:** 2026-10-13 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Introduction University students face heightened risks regarding mental health, with depression, anxiety, and stress being the most prevalent issues\. Students’ mental health is associated with their academic performance and psychosocial well\-being, as well as with their development and future professional trajectories\. Methods This study is part of the Iberian Observatory of Healthy Learning and Youth Participation \(OIASP\-J\) and uses the Healthy Learning Environments Ecosystems instrument\. The sample consisted of included 2,315 university students, of whom 65\.5% \( n = 1,516\) were female, with a mean age of 22\.9 years \( SD = 5\.18\)\. Results Older male students who value institutional ethics and values, demonstrate greater academic engagement, report higher levels of well\-being, more effective stress management, and greater happiness, exhibit fewer psychosocial risks related to well\-being and mental health\. Social isolation emerged as a significant factor associated with greater vulnerability in well\-being and mental health\. Regarding professional future expectation, students with more positive expectations exhibited better individual, psychosocial, and organizational indicators than those with more negative expectations\. Conclusion We conclude that the results provide evidence for higher education institutions, health professionals, and policymakers to invest in more skills programs and mental health interventions\. These programs may help students thrive academically, maintain good mental health throughout their studies, and successfully complete their training\. In turn, this facilitates a smooth transition into professional life and enables students to contribute to society\.
