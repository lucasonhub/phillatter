## Descrição do feed

\_American Journal of Bioethics Neuroscience\_ 14 \(2\):148\-157\. 2023Consciousness\-based approaches to non\-human moral status maintain that consciousness is necessary for \(some degree or level of\) moral status\. While these approaches are intuitive to many, in this paper I argue that the judgment that consciousness is necessary for moral status is not secure enough to guide policy regarding non\-humans, that policies responsive to the moral status of non\-humans should take seriously the possibility that psychological features independent of consciousness are sufficient for moral status\. Further, I illustrate some practical consequences of calling consciousness\-based views into question\. \(direct link \)
