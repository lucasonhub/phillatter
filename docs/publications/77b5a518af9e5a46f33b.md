# Maibom, Marc: When is a Physical System the Same System? \- Identity, Dynamics, and Representative\-Independent Evolution

**Autores:** 

**Data de descoberta:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** unknown

## Descrição do feed

Physical theories determine how states transform, but a same\-system claim asks a different question: whether the transformed description concerns the same physical subject\. La Profilée \(LP\) proposes a universal structural law of nature governing identity under transformation; here “universal” denotes a functional necessity claim about determinate same\-subject judgments, not a derivation of domain\-specific dynamics, constants, masses, or couplings\. In LP, persistence is not an independent primitive alongside identity: it is diachronic identity, the positive consequence of preserving the relevant sameness relation through admissible transformation\. This paper gives the physical identity formulation of that law and isolates a necessary dynamical condition: No Coreference Without Congruence\. If two admissible representatives are identified as the same physical subject through the transformation under analysis, they must induce the same retained future; otherwise no representative\-independent reduced evolution exists on the proposed identity quotient\. Q1 certifies whether a determinate integrated bearer continues at all\. Q2 is the identity verdict on that continuing bearer and, where the physical realization requires distinct carriers, is typed into Q2a, identity\-class continuity, and Q2b, continuation\-stable profile/history identity\. A constrained bridge π\_p:H\_ℛ ⇀ Ā\_ℛ relates those carriers, and Q2b ⇒ Q2a ⇒ Q1\. The congruence requirement is stated for deterministic maps, linear quotients, CPTP channels, and quantum instruments\. Greatest\-fixed\-point, bisimulation\-like, and quotient\-congruence techniques are established mathematical tools; the contribution is their typed integration into the physical identity law supplied by LP\. Persistence is then a regime consequence of successful identity preservation, not a primitive used to define identity\. \(direct link \)
