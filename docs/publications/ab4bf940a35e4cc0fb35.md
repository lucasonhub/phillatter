# Reconstructing the Fiqh of Time

**Autores:** Rohendi Rohendi; Neng Sarah; Abdul Manaf Lubis

**Data:** 2026-10-13 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Time is an important dimension of Islamic law because certain legal rulings depend on specific temporal conditions\. However, existing studies generally examine time within specific fields of Islamic jurisprudence or as a factor in legal change, without an integrated framework that explains its role in Islamic legal reasoning\. This study reconstructs Fiqh of Time as a conceptual framework for temporal legal analysis, rather than as a new branch of Islamic jurisprudence\. Employing doctrinal legal research with conceptual and comparative approaches, the study analyzes normative sources, classical juristic works, and contemporary scholarship through qualitative content analysis, doctrinal interpretation, and conceptual synthesis\. The study develops three analytical functions: Temporal Activation, in which temporal conditions activate legal applicability through sabab or sharṭ; Temporal Limitation, in which temporal boundaries determine the scope or termination of legal consequences; and Temporal Adaptation, in which changes in temporal conditions, as part of changing realities, are evaluated through ‘illah, maṣlaḥah, and maqāṣid al\-sharī‘ah before legal application is retained or adjusted through ijtihad\. The model connects textual norms, temporal facts, ḥukm waḍ‘ī, changing realities, and maqāṣid within a single analytical process\.
