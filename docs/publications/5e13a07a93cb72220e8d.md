# &quot;Digital Transformation in English Language Teaching: The Impact of Artificial Intelligence on the Future of Humanities&quot;

**Autores:** Ameen Jbarah

**Data:** 2026-10-13 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Pushed by the Artificial Intelligence \(AI\), the rapped digital transformation has significantly affected English language teaching by loading a pressure on teaching methods manipulating the role of both, teacher and student\. The present paper investigates the role of AI driven language application and platforms, digital classrooms, and personalized learning systems in changing learning approaches and the future of education\. By reviewing recent researches, this paper emphasizes the opportunities and challenges offered by digital transformation in language teaching\. Discussing basic issues like ethical consideration, teacher preparing, and equitable access\. The final results indicate that the adopting of AI through a human\-centered approach, can improve learner autonomy, culture awareness, and essential digital literacy, using this transformation a means to amuse innovation in language teaching\. Keywords: English Language Teaching \(ELT\), Digital Transformation, Artificial Intelligence \(AI\), Pedagogical Innovation,Humanities Education, Technology Integration
