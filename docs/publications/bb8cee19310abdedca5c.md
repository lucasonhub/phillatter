## Resumo

HUMANITY’S FINAL AUTHORITY The Human Right to Shut Down Artificial Superintelligence,Autonomous Humanoid Persons, and Artificial IntelligenceA Human\-Sovereignty Framework for Independent Power Isolation, Network Disconnection,Safe Containment, and Accountable GovernanceDavid MastrianniIndependent Quantum Physics ResearcherRipple Cafe — www\.ripple\.cafeCovington, Louisiana, United StatesOctober 12, 2026Concept and Original Theory: David MastrianniResearch Development and Manuscript Preparation: David MastrianniChatGPT Prompt Craft: ChatGPT, an AI created by OpenAI AbstractThe possibility of artificial intelligence systems with capabilities exceeding human performancein strategically important domains raises a governance problem distinct from conventionalsoftware safety: an operator’s ability to request shutdown may not imply an independentlyenforceable ability to stop dangerous activity\. Motivated by a Tucker Carlson–Elon Muskinterview excerpt concerning power and connectivity interruption at advanced computingfacilities, this paper proposes the Humanity’s Final Authority Principle \(HFAP\)\. The principleholds that sufficiently capable or high\-risk non\-human AI systems should be designed, tested,and governed so that authorized human institutions retain independent means of restricting,containing, and safely shutting down dangerous operation, regardless of whether a system isconscious or self\-aware\. The paper separates four often\-conflated objectives: stopping physicalactuation, disconnecting external communications, halting computation, and preventingpersistence through replicas or delegated processes\.
