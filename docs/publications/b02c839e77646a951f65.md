# Electronic System\-Level Optimization of Network Slicing and Resource Allocation for 6G URLLC Applications

**Autores:** Luma Abd Al\-Kareem Hussein

**Data:** 2026-10-14 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

In this paper, we propose a complete mathematical framework for optimizing network slicing and resource allocation at the electronic system\-level \(ESL\) of 6G ultra\-reliable low\-latency communication \(URLLC\)\. We present a multi\-dimensional, non\-convex mixed\-integer stochastic optimization problem that jointly optimizes slice admission control, radio resource block \(RRB\) allocation and transmit power distribution, computation offloading decisions, reconfigurable intelligent surface \(RIS\) phase\-shift beamforming configurations and fronthaul/backhaul capacity partitioning under heterogeneous traffic demands and stringent quality\-of\-service \(QoS\) constraints\. The proposed model incorporates finite block length \(FBL\) information theory, Lyapunov drift\-plus\-penalty stochastic control, and deep reinforcement learning \(DRL\)\-assisted dynamic orchestration\. Numerical results confirm reliability and low end\-to\-end latency across heterogeneous slice types\.
