# Boulahia, Aaziz: The Existential Bridge: A Structural Theory of Consciousness \(Academic Synopsis\)

**Autores:** 

**Data de descoberta:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** unknown

## Descrição do feed

This paper presents the academic synopsis of The Existential Bridge: A Structural Theory of Consciousness\. It advances a structural theory of consciousness based on five jointly necessary conditions: coherence, closure, irreducibility, differentiation, and structural accessibility\. Its central claim is that when these conditions converge, purely external description becomes structurally non\-exhaustive, and minimal presence becomes a stronger interpretation than neutral remainder\. The synopsis presents the main thesis, conceptual framework, and theoretical ambition of the project in concise form\. \(direct link \)
