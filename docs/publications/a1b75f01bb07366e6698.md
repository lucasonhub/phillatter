# Preliminary analysis of the proportions and characteristics of dual TCR T cells and dual BCR B cells in echinococcus granulosus\-infected mice using scRNA\+TCR\+BCR\-seq

**Autores:** Su Peng; Kai Quan; hongxia yang; Yadong Gong; Xinsheng Yao

**Data:** 2026-10-13 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Introduction Cystic echinococcosis \(CE\) is a zoonotic parasitic disease caused by Echinococcus granulosus, and the T and B cell responses and regulatory mechanisms in CE remain unclear\. Methods In this study, we analyzed the data of peripheral blood and spleen of mice infected with Echinococcus granulosus\(EG\) by scRNA \+TCR\+BCR\-seq\. Results We made the following novel findings: \(1\) Peripheral blood of Echinococcus granulosus \(EG\)\-infected mice contained a higher proportion of dual TCR\(T cell receptor\) T cells with higher clonality than uninfected mice; \(2\) There was a bias in V gene usage between single TCR T and dual TCR T cells, as well as between single BCR\(B cell receptor\) B cells and dual BCR B cells in EG\-infected mice; \(3\) Dual TCR Tregs, dual TCR effector CD8 T cells, dual BCR Bregs, and dual BCR memory/dividing B cells exhibited relatively high clonal expansion in EG\-infected mice; \(4\) Shared CDR3 sequences and consistently highly expressed mRNA molecules exist between single receptor T/B and dual receptor T/B cells in EG\-infected mice\. KEGG enrichment analysis revealed that dual BCR B cells are closely related to the expression of factors associated with pathways such as &quot;Th1/ Th2 differentiation&quot; and &quot;antigen processing and presentation&quot;\. Discussion These results suggest that dual TCR T and dual BCR B cells may be involved in the specific responses and regulation of EG\-infected mice, providing a novel perspective for the adaptive immune response mechanisms and targeted therapy of parasitic infections\.
