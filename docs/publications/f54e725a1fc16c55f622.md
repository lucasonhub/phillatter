# Pace, Joshua Craig: The Prioritization Regress: Why the Pull to Act Cannot Be Read, and Why It May Be Felt

**Autores:** 

**Data de descoberta:** 2026-10-19 · **Tipo:** unknown

## Descrição do feed

Accounts of consciousness often conflate value, how good or bad something is taken to be by the organism, and urgency, the pull to act\. Prioritization is a matter of urgency\. Any account on which prioritization produces a score that something separate reads faces a regress: the reader&\#x27;s rule is either fixed, so the scores&\#x27; significance for action was settled elsewhere, or adaptive, so it needs a further evaluator\. Systems with no separate reader avoid it, but where tension resolves directly into action they cannot deliberate\. Standard planners, active inference included, deliberate but fall back in\. What both deliberates and ends the regress is a held pull: urgency that persists undischarged, whose change under each rehearsed strategy is that strategy&\#x27;s evaluation\. I propose, as an identity thesis, not a deduction, that the system\-wide state of the field holding such pulls is identical with consciousness, and state how each claim could fail\. \(direct link \)
