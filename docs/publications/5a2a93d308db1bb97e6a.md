# Promoting sustainable travel intentions: the effect of information nudges

**Autores:** Martina Shakya; Franziska Drescher

**Data:** 2026-10-14 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Introduction Tourism contributes substantially to global greenhouse gas emissions\. To reduce travel\-related carbon emissions, a shift toward more sustainable travel choices is urgently needed\. Research has consistently shown a significant gap between environmental attitudes and sustainable travel behaviors\. Yet, it has proven difficult to promote sustainable travel behavior without understanding and narrowing the gap between environmental attitudes and sustainable travel intentions\. This study examines the effect of information nudges to support the shift toward more climate\-friendly travel intentions\. Methods We conducted randomized choice experiments among a large sample of German holidaymakers \( n = 2,186\), covering the three main segments of German vacation demand: domestic/proximity tourism, mid\-range Mediterranean travel, and long\-haul travel\. Participants first self\-selected their preferred travel segment, allowing for realistic decision\-making scenarios, and were then randomly assigned to one of three carbon\-related information nudges or a baseline condition without additional information\. We examined the effects of the nudges on transport and accommodation choices\. Results The results indicate that carbon\-related information can influence specific travel choices toward more climate\-friendly alternatives, although effects were not consistently observed across all contexts and nudges\. Significant effects were found particularly for transport choices in Mediterranean travel and for accommodation choices in this segment\. Discussion The findings suggest that communicating carbon emissions can contribute to narrowing the gap between environmental attitudes and sustainable travel intentions, particularly among travelers who value ecological responsibility\. However, the heterogeneous effects across travel contexts indicate that this effectiveness depends on the specific travel decision and choice context\. Disclosing carbon\-related information for various travel offers therefore appears as an effective approach to fostering more climate\-friendly travel intentions, although its effectiveness varies across different market segments\.
