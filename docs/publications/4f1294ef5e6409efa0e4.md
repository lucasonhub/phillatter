# Assessment of myocardial perfusion using dual\-source computed tomography myocardial perfusion imaging in a goat model of reperfusion injury induced by distal microembolization

**Autores:** Hui Jin; Chaowei Duan; Xiaolin Chen; Yaojun Lu; Xiaolin Liu; Meng Li

**Data:** 2026-10-14 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Background Myocardial ischemia–reperfusion injury \(MIRI\) contributes to adverse outcomes after acute myocardial infarction\. Microvascular obstruction \(MVO\), a key component of MIRI, impairs myocardial perfusion despite successful epicardial revascularization\. This study aimed to establish a reproducible large\-animal model of embolization\-induced MVO and evaluate myocardial perfusion heterogeneity using dual\-source CT myocardial perfusion imaging \(DSCT\-MPI\)\. Methods Eight male Saanen goats underwent 30 min of left circumflex artery occlusion followed by distal microcatheter injection of 20\- μ m polystyrene microspheres to induce reperfusion\-associated MVO\. DSCT\-MPI was performed immediately after model establishment, with myocardial perfusion assessed by iodine distribution\. Pathology was evaluated using triphenyltetrazolium chloride and hematoxylin–eosin staining, and Bcl\-2, Bax, and caspase\-3 were quantified\. Receiver operating characteristic \(ROC\) analysis was used to determine the iodine concentration threshold for infarcted myocardium\. Results Seven of eight goats survived and underwent DSCT\-MPI\. Of 119 myocardial segments, 39 were infarcted, 14 were non\-infarcted hypoperfused segments consistent with distal MVO, and 66 were normal\. Binary ROC and diagnostic\-performance analyses included the 39 infarcted and 66 normal segments, excluding the 14 MVO\-consistent segments\. At an iodine concentration cutoff of 1,582 μg/cm 3 , DSCT\-MPI achieved 89\.7% sensitivity, 90\.9% specificity, and an AUC of 0\.848 \(95% CI, 0\.736–0\.959\), with substantial histopathological agreement \( κ = 0\.798\)\. Infarcted myocardium showed reduced Bcl\-2 and increased Bax and caspase\-3 expression\. The 14 hypoperfused segments without TTC\-confirmed necrosis were classified as MVO\-consistent rather than CMD\. Conclusions A minimally invasive, reproducible goat model of embolization\-induced myocardial reperfusion injury was established\. DSCT\-MPI identified infarcted myocardium and non\-infarcted hypoperfused regions consistent with distal MVO, demonstrating post\-reperfusion perfusion heterogeneity without supporting CMD\. Because no direct CMR comparison was performed, these findings should be considered exploratory pathology\-based validation\.
