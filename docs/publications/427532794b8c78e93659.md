# Fuzzy Logic\-Based Risk Assessment for Foreign Economic Activity of Agro\-Industrial Complex Entities

**Autores:** 

**Data:** 2026-10-09 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

HistoryRisk assessment of foreign economic activity in the agro\-industrial complex has become increasingly complex amid geopolitical instability, sanctions pressure, and limited access to reliable statistical data\.Traditional probabilistic and econometric approaches often adapt poorly to rapidly changing environments characterized by uncertainty and incomplete information\.This article aims to develop a fuzzy logic\-based decision support system to assess foreign economic activity risks for agro\-industrial complex enterprises subject to export control regulations under data\-deficit conditions\.The proposed methodology extends the classical risk formulation by incorporating an accessibility constraints factor that reflects the availability of export markets, logistics corridors, and financial infrastructure under geopolitical restrictions\.The model uses a Mamdani fuzzy inference system, integrating economic, geopolitical, customs, environmental, logistics, and information risk groups into a single hierarchical structure\.Evaluation was conducted through scenario\-based simulation and sensitivity analysis rather than empirical\.Scenario modeling yielded integrated risk index values ranging from 0\.298 \(stable export environment\) to 0\.745 \(trade corridor blockade\), demonstrating the framework&\#x27;s capacity to capture nonlinear risk escalation patterns\.Accessibility constraints exerted a disproportionately strong influence on the final risk estimate compared with probability and impact factors alone\.The proposed conceptual framework provides a basis for developing an interpretable, adaptable instrument for exporters and regional policymakers operating under global uncertainty\.It can support export vulnerability assessment, logistics diversification planning, and adaptive foreign trade decision\-making\.
