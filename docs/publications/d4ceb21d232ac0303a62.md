# THE EL\-RAKHAWI ADMINISTRATIVE LAW OF THE ALGORITHMIC AGE From Paper Bureaucracy to Transparent Digital Administration A Comprehensive Theoretical Framework for Modernizing Public Law in the Era of Artificial Intelligence, Automated Decision\-Making, and Digital Governance

**Autores:** mohamed kamal arafa el\-rakhawi

**Data:** 2026-10-11 · **Tipo:** book

## Resumo

The El\-Rakhawi Administrative Law of the Algorithmic Age \(DOI: 10\.5281/zenodo\.23293659\) presents a comprehensive theoretical framework across six volumes addressing the governance of algorithmic administration: \(1\) General Theory covering the evolution of public service, administrative discretion, legality, and the right to explanation; \(2\) The Algorithmic Administrative Decision defining automated acts, the right against solely automated decisions, transparency, and bias; \(3\) The Civil Servant as Algorithmic Supervisor with mandatory oversight training; \(4\) Digital Administrative Contracts mandating public ownership of publicly\-funded algorithms; \(5\) Administrative Liability establishing strict, non\-delegable state liability with vendor recourse; \(6\) Digital Administrative Justice empowering courts to pierce the black box\. Four surgical refinements ensure doctrinal defensibility: \(1\) Article 9\.1 explicitly includes indirect discrimination via proxy variables \(zip codes, device types, browsing history\); \(2\) Article 6\.1 mandates human reviewers possess actual authority to override, preventing &quot;rubber\-stamping&quot;; \(3\) Article 26\.1 establishes judicial confidentiality orders or secure technical enclaves for source code disclosure, subordinating trade secret claims to fair trial rights; \(4\) AAJI mathematical structure where Bias \(B\) and Opacity \(C\) act as penalizers driving the score toward zero\. The Algorithmic Administrative Justice Index: AAJI=\(T\*E\*A\*R\)/\(\(T\*E\*A\*R\)\+B\*C\+0\.01\), where T=Transparency, E=Equality, A=Accountability, R=Right to Human Review\. Under the Weakest\-Link Axiom, AAJI equals 0 if any positive factor is absent, approaching max \~0\.9901 \(Asymptotic Perfection Principle\)\. Protected by a Sovereign Legal Framework with AI\-training bans, Defensive Publication, and Digital Justice Ethics Clause, released under the Ferial Zayed Humanitarian License—establishing a blueprint for protecting citizens from algorithmic tyranny in the digital age\.
