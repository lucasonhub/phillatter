# Inteligência artificial na gestão em enfermagem: scoping review

**Autores:** Sofia Pinto Bernardino; Palmira Martins Gonçalves Azevedo; Carla Pinto Bernardino; Elisabete De Fátima Lavrador Gonçalves; Paula Maria Carvalho Xavier; Sara Emanuel Da Silva Brandão Ribeiro

**Data:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

The growing integration of Artificial Intelligence \(AI\) in organizations has been profoundly transforming leadership processes, decision\-making, and team management\. Between 2019 and 2026, the literature shifted from a predominantly technological approach to a view focused on human\-AI interaction, emphasizing digital leadership skills, ethics, algorithmic governance, and organizational transformation\. AI has been energizing healthcare systems, with increasing applications in nursing, not only in direct care but also in workforce management, care organization, decision support, document automation, and performance monitoring\. A scoping review was conducted to map the evidence published between 2021 and 2026 on the use of AI in nursing management, identifying applications, benefits, barriers, ethical implications, and knowledge gaps\. Eleven articles were included in the final review\. The most recent studies suggest that AI can improve operational efficiency, support resource management, enhance patient safety, and assist nursing leaders in decision\-making\. However, challenges remain related to AI literacy, interoperability, data privacy, and the lack of involvement of nurse managers in implementing solutions\. AI emerges as a strategic opportunity for nursing management, but its real value will depend on governance quality, the profession itself, process transparency, data protection, and the active involvement of nursing leadership\. It&\#x27;s essential to turn this innovation into sustainable improvement focused on people and ethics\.
