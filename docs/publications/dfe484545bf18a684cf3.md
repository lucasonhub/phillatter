# Assessing Functional Variability and Risk in Tugboat Design Using an Integrated FRAM\-Fuzzy FMEA Framework

**Autores:** Adem VİRAN

**Data:** 2026-10-15 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Tugboats are essential auxiliary vessels that play a vital role in port and offshore operations\.The life cycle of tugboat involve complex couplings among hydrodynamic performance, propulsion systems, stability requirements, operational demands, production activities, regulatory compliance, and organizational coordination\.Such socio\-technical systems, variabilities may propagate significant technical and operational consequences\.In this study, the functional resonance analysis method was applied to identify functional couplings, performance variability, and potential variability propagation throughout the tugboat design and construction lifecycle\.The identified variability sources were subsequently transformed into failure modes and assessed using fuzzy failure mode and effects analysis\.Expert judgments obtained from tugboat designers, shipyard managers, captains, and operators were represented through fuzzy numbers to address uncertainty in risk evaluation\.fuzzy risk priority numbers were calculated based on occurrence, severity, and detection criteria\.The results revealed that hull propulsion\-incompatibility, propulsion system alignment deficiencies, accumulated functional resonance at ship delivery, verification shortcomings, classification compliance deficiencies, engineering team limitations, and coordination failures constitute the most critical risk areas\.The findings demonstrate that risks emerge not only from isolated technical failures but also from interacting functional variabilities\.The proposed framework supports proactive risk identification, prioritization, and more reliable tugboat design and construction processes\.
