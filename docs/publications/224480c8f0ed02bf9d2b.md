# ALGORITHMIC PERSONALIZATION AND CONSUMER TRUST IN EMERGING DIGITAL MARKETS: A CONCEPTUAL FRAMEWORK FOR MARKETING MANAGEMENT IN UZBEKISTAN

**Autores:** Yusufjonov Diyorbek

**Data:** 2026-10-12 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Artificial intelligence \(AI\) has become a central instrument of contemporary marketing, particularly in the form of algorithmic personalization of offers, messages and recommendations\. While the literature in advanced economies documents both the commercial value of personalization and the consumer resistance it can provoke, emerging digital markets remain comparatively underexplored\. This conceptual paper develops an integrative framework that explains how the quality of AI\-driven personalization translates into purchase intention and loyalty in an emerging\-market setting, using Uzbekistan as the reference context\. Drawing on research on AI in marketing, algorithm aversion, organizational trust and information privacy, the paper positions consumer trust in the AI system as the key mediating mechanism and identifies algorithmic transparency and perceived human oversight as its principal antecedents\. Perceived privacy risk and decision involvement are proposed as boundary conditions, and linguistic and cultural fit is introduced as a context\-specific component of personalization quality\. Five testable propositions are formulated, a four\-phase research design is outlined, and managerial implications are presented for firms operating in mobile\-first, multilingual markets with fast\-growing e\-commerce and fintech sectors\. The paper concludes with a research agenda for empirical validation\.
