# Constructing t\-way test suites: a systematic survey and qualitative synthesis of the One\-Parameter\-at\-a\-Time approach

**Autores:** Muhammad Sabo Yahaya; Ahmad Sobri Hashim; Hussaini Mamman; Shamsu Abdullahi; Umama Umer; Dahiru Adamu Aliyu; Idrissa Mohammed Godowoli

**Data:** 2026-10-15 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Parameter interactions are a major source of software failures, yet the exponential growth of possible configurations makes exhaustive testing impractical\. Combinatorial Interaction Testing \(CIT\) is a highly effective sampling technique for identifying software defects caused by parameter interactions\. Over the past two decades, the One\-Parameter\-at\-a\-Time \(OPAT\) constructions paradigm has emerged as a cornerstone of CIT test suite generation\. Although numerous OPAT strategies have been proposed to optimize test suite size and execution speed, the literature remains fragmented, and direct side\-by\-side quantitative benchmarking has been impeded by a lack of open\-source tooling and standardized platforms\. To address this, this study presents a systematic survey and qualitative synthesis of 40 primary OPAT studies published between 2000 and 2026, retrieved from four major digital libraries\. Rather than attempting a direct quantitative benchmark comparison of execution times, which is shown to be mathematically invalid due to highly heterogeneous self\-reported hardware environments and the closed\-source nature of most academic tools, we establish a unified taxonomy that classifies OPAT research into five major categories: deterministic refinements, scaling and distribution strategies, advanced feature\-enhanced strategies, metaheuristic and hybrid strategies, and specialized OPAT adaptations\. Our analysis synthesizes qualitative trends, structural characteristics, parameter\-order growth mechanisms, and feature\-integration gaps across the literature\. We conclude with a critical assessment of the tooling and reproducibility challenges in the field, outlining vital future pathways, including diversified metaheuristic and hyper\-heuristic techniques, deterministic parallel algorithms, and AI\-driven adaptive test generation, to transition OPAT research into industrial\-grade, open\-source software engineering pipelines\.
