# Wang, Kaining: Conceptual Relation and Creative Yield

**Autores:** 

**Data de descoberta:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** unknown

## Descrição do feed

Human–AI creative difference is often attributed to reasoning capacity\. This paper proposes a different mechanistic hypothesis: part of the difference may arise from how conceptual relations are organized and evaluated\. We distinguish two evaluation capacities—evaluation optimized for an existing conceptual space, and evaluation capable of recognizing the potential value of cross\-domain candidates\. We develop this hypothesis through four propositions\. Proposition One is the exploration–evaluation mismatch hypothesis: evaluation optimized within a current space is not equivalent to evaluation capable of recognizing value outside that space\. Proposition Two hypothesizes that an optimal relational distance may depend on evaluation capacity\. Proposition Three hypothesizes that differences between framework repair and framework revision may constitute a source of human–AI creative difference\. Proposition Four extends the analysis institutionally: evaluation appropriation, evaluation self\-reproduction, and procedural deficit\. We define relational distance as a computable quantity relative to a fixed corpus, and state falsification conditions for each proposition\. The central claim is that research on human–AI creative difference should shift from “who is stronger” to the mechanism chain of conceptual relation, exploration, evaluation, and creative yield\.
