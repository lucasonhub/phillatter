# Case Report: Electroencephalography\-informed low\-frequency repetitive transcranial magnetic stimulation targeting the supplementary motor area in severe functional movement disorder

**Autores:** Chiaki Takahashi; Takashi Asahi; 基生 神田; Yoshihiko Ohta; Isao Matsushita

**Data:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Background Functional movement disorder \(FMD\) is increasingly being recognized as a condition associated with abnormal brain network connectivity; however, effective treatment strategies remain limited\. Methods We report the case of a patient with severe FMD that developed following an occupational injury and was characterized by functional tremors accompanied by migraines and prominent autonomic symptoms\. Based on electroencephalography \(EEG\) network analysis, low\-frequency repetitive transcranial magnetic stimulation targeting the supplementary motor area was administered, followed by staged rehabilitation incorporating visual feedback\. Results The combined intervention reduced tremors and improved gait speed, activities of daily living, and EEG network connectivity\. Conclusion This case highlights the potential utility of combining EEG\-guided neuromodulation with staged rehabilitation for the treatment of FMD and supports further investigation of this approach\.
