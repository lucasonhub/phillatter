# QANDLI DIABETGA CHALINGAN SHAXSLARGA PSIXOLOGIK XIZMAT KO‘RSATISH MEXANIZMLARINI TAKOMILLASHTIRISHNING ILMIY\-NAZARIY VA AMALIY ASOSLARI

**Autores:** Nafosat Akmalova

**Data:** 2026-10-15 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

This article examines the scientific, theoretical, methodological, and organizational foundations for improving psychological support mechanisms for people living with diabetes mellitus\. Drawing on scientific literature, it systematizes factors influencing emotional well\-being, diabetes\-specific distress, treatment motivation, cognitive appraisal of Ta’limda istiqbolli izlanishlar \- xalqaro ilmiy\-metodik jurnal № 9/2026 ISSN: 3060\-527X illness, and social adaptation\. A comparative analysis is conducted using data from the International Diabetes Federation, the American Diabetes Association&\#x27;s 2026 Standards of Care, studies on diabetes distress, and the relevant legislation of the Republic of Uzbekistan\. Based on the theoretical synthesis, a five\-stage integrative psychological support model is proposed, incorporating psychological screening, assessment of individual needs, psychological interventions, social collaboration, and dynamic monitoring\. The proposed framework seeks to support psychological well\-being among people with diabetes and improve the continuity of integrated medical and psychological care\.
