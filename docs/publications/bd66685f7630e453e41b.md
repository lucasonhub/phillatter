# Pengembangan Bahan Ajar Digital Interaktif Menggunakan Flip PDF Professional untuk Pembelajaran Geometri SMP

**Autores:** Fatqurhohman Fatqurhohman; Maulidina Chintia Maharani; Nike Novianti

**Data:** 2026-10-15 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

The limitation of digital learning materials that remain predominantly PDF\-based is one of the challenges in providing interactive geometry learning\. This study aimed to develop an interactive digital learning material based on Flip PDF Professional for curved\-surface solids for eighth\-grade students at SMPN 4 Jember\. The study employed a Research and Development approach using the ADDIE model, comprising Analysis, Design, Development, Implementation, and Evaluation\. Data were collected through a literature review, interviews, expert validation sheets, teacher and student response questionnaires, and a learning achievement test\. Data were analyzed descriptively using percentages\. The validation results showed scores of 80\.0% for language, 83\.3% for media, and 66\.7% for material, categorized as very valid, very valid, and valid, respectively\. The practicality assessment yielded responses of 84\.4% from the teacher and 95\.8% from students, both categorized as very practical\. In the field test involving 31 students, 23 students \(74\.2%\) achieved the mastery criterion\. The developed product was valid and practical and showed potential effects in supporting students’ mathematics learning achievement\.
