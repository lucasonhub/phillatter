# Development of a conceptual integrated approach combining cluster analysis and machine learning for credit risk assessment

**Autores:** N\. S\. Lyoshin; Д\.А\. МЕЛЬНИКОВА; Liliya Valerievna ERMOLINA

**Data:** 2026-10-15 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

In the context of the rapid growth in volume and complexity of financial data, classical credit scoring models face limitations in forecast accuracy and sustainability\. At the same time, clustering and machine learning methods are evolving, enabling the identification of latent borrower segments and the integration of this additional information directly into predictive models\. This paper addresses the methodological gap between data segmentation and machine learning in credit risk assessment\. The goal of the study is to develop a concept of a hybrid model that combines clustering analysis and gradient boosting algorithms by incorporating cluster meta\-features into the feature space of a scoring model\. The methodological framework includes an analysis for existing approaches to credit scoring, the design of a sequential data\-processing pipeline, and the construction of a feature set that reflects the borrower’s structural position in the data space\. In addition, the study evaluates the stability of clustering results with respect to the choice of metric, the number of clusters, and sample characteristics, as well as compares different ways of encoding cluster information\. The proposed approach is aimed at improving model discriminatory power by accounting for latent customer segmentation\. The expected deliverable is enhanced default prediction quality and expanded model interpretability, which increases their practical value for credit risk management and decision support in financial institutions\.
