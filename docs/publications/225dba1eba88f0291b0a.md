# OTA\-ONA VA FARZANDLAR O‘RTASIDAGI “KECHIRIMLILIK” KONSEPTI

**Autores:** Мўминов Шерозбек Сиддиқжонович

**Data:** 2026-10-12 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

This article analyzes the psychological, linguo\-cognitive, and communicative characteristics of the concept of &quot;forgiveness&quot; as it manifests in the relationship between parents and children\. Forgiveness is viewed as a significant conceptual phenomenon that serves to mitigate hurt, resentment, misunderstandings, and conflicts arising within family relationships\. The study identifies the emotional, cognitive, motivational, and communicative components of the &quot;forgiveness&quot; concept\. It highlights the importance of empathy, mutual understanding, sincere apology, acceptance of responsibility, and open communication in the process of forgiveness between parents and children\. Furthermore, the role of forgiveness in fostering psychological stability, trust, and positive relationships within the family environment is examined\. An analysis of scientific literature demonstrates a close link between the quality of the parent\-child relationship, empathic abilities, and forgiveness\.
