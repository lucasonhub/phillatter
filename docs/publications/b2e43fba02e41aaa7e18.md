# DEVELOPING SOCIOLINGUISTIC THINKING IN THE AGE OF ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE: PSYCHOLOGICAL AND PEDAGOGICAL PERSPECTIVES

**Autores:** Alisher Safaraliyevich Otaqulov

**Data:** 2026-10-13 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Artificial intelligence \(AI\) has become a new participant in everyday and educational communication\. Learners now talk to chatbots, use machine translation and receive automated feedback on their writing\. This conceptual paper examines how these tools affect the development of sociolinguistic thinking, understood as the ability to select, interpret and adapt language forms according to social context, participants and communicative goals\. Drawing on sociocultural theory, social learning theory, sociolinguistics and intercultural pedagogy, the paper outlines the cognitive, social and emotional mechanisms involved and analyzes the opportunities and risks of AI as a language model and interlocutor\. It then proposes five pedagogical conditions for developing sociolinguistic thinking in AI\-supported learning environments: critical AI literacy, contextualized task design, teacher mediation, comparison of human and machine language, and a supportive psychological climate\.
