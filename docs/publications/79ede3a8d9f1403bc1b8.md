# Arriagada, Leonardo: Human\-Artificial Intelligence \(AI\) Collaboration and Aesthetic Abstraction in Music: A Transdisciplinary Analysis of I AM AI \(2018\) and PROTO \(2019\)

**Autores:** 

**Data de descoberta:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** unknown

## Descrição do feed

\_Escena Revista de Las Artes\_ 85 \(3\):1\-29\. 2026Introduction: This paper examines computer\-generated art \(CG\-art\) in the musical domain, diverging from the predominant focus of research on artificial intelligence \(AI\) generated images\. Objective: To analyse two albums, I AM AI \(2018\) and PROTO \(2019\), produced with Artificial Intelligence \(AI\) under contrasting collaboration models\. The former employs AI to generate instrumental stems complemented by human vocals, while the latter features AI\-generated synthetic vocals layered over human\-composed arrangements\. Methods: Using a transdisciplinary framework \(Cognitive Science, Art Theory and Computer Science\), this article assesses the status, the human\-machine aesthetic evaluation, the AI’s creative potential, and the inorganic abstraction in the creative process of Taryn Southern’s I AM AI and Holly Herndon’s PROTO\. Results: Both albums integrate AI significantly; however, neither qualifies as AI\-generated artworks\. The Recurrent Neural Networks \(RNNs\) used lack creative autonomy, functioning solely as instrumental tools without intrinsic algorithmic self\-evaluation\. Conclusions: These long\-play records \(LPs\) are collaborative creations where AI serves as a tool with passive and hybrid aesthetic capabilities\. Ultimately, the aesthetic realization of these works relies on human emotional projection, contrasting with the non\-emotional and inorganic abstraction characteristic of the algorithms involved\. \(direct link \)
