# Bulgaria, Jamel: The Structural\-Constructivist Architecture of the Core Emotion Framework: The CEF Mirror as a Heuristic Interface for Psychological Regulation

**Autores:** 

**Data de descoberta:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** unknown

## Descrição do feed

The Core Emotion Framework \(CEF\) proposes a structural constructivist architecture that reconciles the longstanding divide between biological essentialism and social constructivism in affective science\. By organizing human experience into ten irreducible functional operators and three systemic centers, the framework provides a high resolution map of psychological regulation that remains independent of speculative biological claims\. The CEF Mirror—the practitioner level interface—functions as a heuristic Human Interface that retains full utility even if the Human OS hypothesis is empirically falsified\. Its effectiveness derives from its ability to reorganize verified self improvement mechanisms into a unified operator lexicon, enabling precise identification of cognitive, relational, and conative blockages\. The framework integrates major psychological models, including Self Determination Theory, ACT, DBT, and CBT, and operationalizes them through practitioner protocols such as the Mirror Technique, the 60 Second Routine, and the 7 Step Detangling Protocol\. Preliminary empirical work, including Pilot Study 3, demonstrates operator accessibility and reflexive–deliberate divergence, supporting the emerging factor structure of the CEF Scale\. The result is a meta framework that offers a rigorous, intuitive, and empirically tractable interface for emotional regulation and personal optimization\.
