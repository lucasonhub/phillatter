# From Literary Narrative to Immersive Experience: A Multisensory Design Proposal Based on La vorágine

**Autores:** Sheerley Mujica Vildosola

**Data:** 2026-10-15 · **Tipo:** dissertation

## Resumo

This thesis investigates how a multisensory immersive experience can be developed from José Eustasio Rivera’s La vorágine to engage contemporary audiences with the novel’s spatial, sensory, and historical dimensions\. Combining theoretical inquiry, qualitative corpus analysis, and research\-creation, the study examines 393 literary fragments through six analytical categories\. The analysis identified 42 narrative\-spatial events and eight narrative\-spatial clusters, revealing patterns of transformation across the narrative and establishing a progression from textual relationships to experiential conditions\. These findings informed translation matrices, experiential maps, and design diagrams that connect literary interpretation with spatial organization, atmosphere, multisensory strategies, bodily relationships, and digital interaction\. The research culminated in a sequential multisensory immersive proposal and an audiovisual prototype\. The thesis contributes a traceable methodological framework linking literary analysis with immersive experience design and provides a basis for future full\-scale prototyping, participant evaluation, and exploration of the framework’s applicability to other literary works across diverse narrative contexts\.
