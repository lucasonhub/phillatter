# DEVELOPING THE PROFESSIONAL\-METHODOLOGICAL COMPETENCE OF FUTURE ENGLISH LANGUAGE TEACHERS THROUGH ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE AND ADAPTIVE LEARNING TECHNOLOGIES IN THE DIGITAL AGE

**Autores:** Sharifa Jo&\#x27;rayevna Qarshiyeva

**Data:** 2026-10-14 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

This thesis examines the potential of artificial intelligence \(AI\) and adaptive learning technologies in developing the professional\-methodological competence of future English language teachers in the context of digital transformation\. The integration of intelligent educational tools into teacher training creates opportunities to personalize learning, improve methodological decision\-making, enhance digital literacy, and develop the practical skills required for effective English language instruction\. Particular attention is given to the role of AI\-powered applications, adaptive learning platforms, and digital assessment systems in preparing prospective teachers to respond to learners&\#x27; diverse educational needs\. The study highlights the importance of combining technological innovation with pedagogical principles, critical thinking, and professional reflection\. It argues that the purposeful integration of AI and adaptive technologies can contribute to a more flexible, learner\-centered, and practice\-oriented model of teacher education\.
