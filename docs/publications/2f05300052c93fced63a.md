# Global Research Trends and Emerging Themes in Poultry Coccidiosis: A Comprehensive Bibliometric Analysis of PubMed Literature

**Autores:** 

**Data:** 2026-10-15 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Coccidiosis is still among the most cost\-efficient parasitic diseases in the poultry sector in the world, and it has caused a significant loss in production and rise in the costs of management\.Over the past few years, the increasing alarm about the problem of anticoccidial drug resistance and the necessity of environmentally friendly systems of poultry production have enhanced academic activities in the given context\.The current paper is a bibliometric review of the current trends in poultry coccidiosis research around the world with the help of the data retrieved in PubMed\.One thousand and five hundred nine articles from 2019 to 2026 were searched using the Bibliometrix package via the Biblioshiny interface\.The outcomes showed that there was a very strong and steady growth of scientific production, with the growth rate of 49\.04% per annum, which demonstrates the fast developing research interest\.It was seen that the research environment was very collaborative, with an average of 7\.17 co\-authors per document and 29\.65% international collaboration\.Poultry Science and Veterinary Parasitology were the few specialized journals in the publication scene, which is predictable according to the law of Bradford\.There was a law of productivity of authors that emphasized the role of a few active researchers\.Themes and key word analysis revealed key research areas such as Eimeria biology, anticoccidial drugs, vaccine development, and gut health\.It is interesting to note that the development of new themes, including microbiota control and phytogenic feed additives, can be viewed as the evolution of a new paradigm of sustainable and integrative disease control\.The analysis of collaboration networks also showed that there were well\-developed clusters of research with the focus on the necessity of multidisciplinary approaches\.Although the study has certain restrictions associated with the utilization of one database and decreased citation indicators, this research acts as a valuable resource of information about the intellectual framework and development of poultry coccidiosis studies\.The results show that the field is dynamic and provides a direction of future studies to conduct especially on how to achieve sustainable and innovative control measures in order to enhance poultry health and productivity\.
