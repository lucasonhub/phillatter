# Qatrani, Osama S\.: Illusion and Consciousness: Disentangling Human Existence from Artificial Intelligence

**Autores:** 

**Data de descoberta:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** unknown

## Descrição do feed

This paper examines the persistent confusion between artificial intelligence \(AI\) and human consciousness\. Drawing on philosophy of mind, theology, and ethics, it argues that conflating computational artifacts with conscious experience constitutes a category mistake\. The paper introduces the Qatrani Epistemic Distinction Model \(Q\-EDM\), which establishes three propositions: \(1\) ontological non\-equivalence between AI and human beings, \(2\) the epistemic scope constraint that AI simulations lack semantics or qualia, and \(3\) an ethical\-policy imperative to focus on governance, bias, and accountability rather than attributing metaphysical agency to machines\. By clarifying these distinctions, the paper provides a conceptual framework to guide researchers and policymakers, ensuring that AI remains a powerful tool but never a substitute for human existence\.
