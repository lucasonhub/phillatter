# Agro\-food waste valorization for sustainable biosurfactant production: Integrating fed\-batch fermentation with machine learning

**Autores:** Nisha Kumari Pandit; Sumer Singh Meena

**Data:** 2026-10-14 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Biosurfactants are promising eco\-friendly alternatives to synthetic surfactants; however, their large\-scale application is constrained by high production costs\. A novel biosurfactant\-producing bacterium, Pseudomonas sp\. BSP1 was isolated from oil\-contaminated soil and characterized as a glycolipid\-producing strain\. To enhance sustainability, peanut residue \(PR\) and pineapple peel hydrolysate \(PPH\) were used as agro\-food waste substrates in fed\-batch fermentation, with distributed feeding improving biosurfactant production compared to pulse feeding\. In the distributed strategy, 3% PR was supplied initially, followed by 1% PPH per day for 3 days, whereas pulse feeding received 3% PPH as a single addition on day 1\. Distributed feeding increased biosurfactant production from 2\.021 ± 0\.019 to 2\.174 ± 0\.035 g L −1 and EI 24 % from 56\.85 ± 0\.18 to 60\.44 ± 0\.51%\. A modified Latin Hypercube Sampling \(LHS\) strategy combined with Extreme Gradient Boosting \(XGBoost\) was employed to optimize six process variables using 100 experimental runs\. Gain\-based feature importance and SHAP analyses indicated that pH contributed most to model predictions\. The optimized conditions yielded a predicted biosurfactant production of 2\.903 g L −1 and an emulsification index \(EI 24 %\) of 63\.53%\. Experimental validation closely matched the predictions, achieving 2\.948 ± 0\.071 g L −1 and 63\.24 ± 0\.99%, corresponding to a ∼35\.6% increase in production compared to pre\-optimized conditions\. Time\-course analysis identified day 6 as the appropriate harvesting point, with biosurfactant production and EI 24 % continuing to increase after biomass accumulation peaked on day 5\. These findings demonstrate the potential of integrating agro\-food waste valorization with machine learning\-assisted bioprocess optimization for sustainable biosurfactant production\.
