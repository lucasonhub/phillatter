# Deep learning\-assisted graphene\-based THz metasurface for label\-free and high\-performance MCF\-7 breast cancer cell sensing

**Autores:** Hassan Zakeri; Gholamreza Moradi; Ahad Tavakoli

**Data:** 2026-10-13 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

A triple\-band graphene\-based terahertz metasurface, integrated with a DL framework, is proposed for high\-efficiency polarization conversion and label\-free MCF\-7 breast cancer cell detection\. The unit cell, comprising concentric graphene elements and silver strips on an \\\(\\mathrm \{Al\_2O\_3\}\\\) substrate with a silver ground plane, facilitates reflective\-mode operation with polarization conversion ratios \(PCR\) exceeding 90% across three distinct operational bands\. The finite\-element method \(FEM\), finite\-difference time\-domain \(FDTD\) technique, and multiple\-interference theory rigorously validate the physical mechanisms and electromagnetic responses, showing strong agreement\. For biosensing applications, the metasurface leverages the distinct dielectric signatures of MCF\-7 cells, inducing measurable resonance shifts at 1\.48, 2\.85, and 4\.12 THz\. The platform achieves a peak sensitivity of 0\.866 THz/RIU and a figure of merit of 4\.04 RIU \\\(^\{\-1\}\\\) \. To improve spectral analysis, we develop a one\-dimensional convolutional neural network to predict PCR responses directly from THz spectral data\. This model shows exceptional robustness, achieving an \\\(R^2\\\) of 0\.972 even under 5% additive white Gaussian noise\. By accurately resolving minute refractive\-index variations associated with early\-stage cellular\-level oncogenic alterations, the proposed deep\-learning\-assisted biosensor offers a highly sensitive, non\-contact, and non\-destructive platform with significant potential for early\-stage breast cancer screening\.
