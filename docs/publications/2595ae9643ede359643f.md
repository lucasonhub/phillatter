# Robust Spam Email Classification Using an Ensemble of Traditional ML and BERT\-Based Deep Learning Models

**Autores:** Mushtaq Talib Mahdi

**Data:** 2026-10-14 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Phishing and spam emails have become enormous cybersecurity threats that call for accurate and efficient identification processes\. A comparative study of classical machine\-learning and deep\-learning approaches is developed in this paper, where we also present a BiLSTM\-based model\. The textual data is preprocessed \(lowercased, punctuation, non\-alphanumeric characters and stop words removed\) followed by TF\-IDF feature extraction\. The obtained features are passed to MNB and SVM classifiers, whose probabilistic predictions are combined using soft voting\. Along these lines, fine\-tuned BERT and BiLSTM models are used to exploit the sequential and contextual dependencies in the text data\. Performance of the model is evaluated using Accuracy, Precision, Recall and F1 score\. All models are trained and evaluated on a single, publicly available spam\-classification dataset \(spam\.csv, 5,573 labeled messages categorized as spam or ham\) using a held\-out test split\. The proposed BiLSTM\-based model proved its competitive classification performance with moderate computational complexity: the accuracy of 98\.92% and F1\-score of 96\.03%\. These results suggest that BiLSTM can accommodate contextual dependencies in spam and phishing\-related text, while providing a trade\-off to handle predictive performance and computational resources effectively\. Hence, the proposed method provide a good foundation for text\-wise spam and phishing detection that is both effective and scalable\.
