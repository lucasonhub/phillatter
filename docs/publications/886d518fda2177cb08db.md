# New Challenges and Directions for Technology Innovation in China: A Review of the 14th Five\-Year Plan and an Outlook on the 15th Five\-Year Plan

**Autores:** Jinzhi Li

**Data:** 2026-10-14 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

This paper reviews the main achievements of China’s science and technology \(S&amp;T\) development during the 14th Five\-Year Plan \(2021–2025\) and connects them to the priorities set out in the 15th Five\-Year Plan \(2026–2030\)\. Drawing on official Chinese statistics, reports from the World Intellectual Property Organization \(WIPO\), and peer\-reviewed research, the paper traces a steady rise in research\-and\-development \(R&amp;D\) intensity, a first\-time entry into the global top ten on innovation rankings, and substantial progress in artificial intelligence, semiconductors, quantum technologies, green energy, biotechnology, aerospace, nuclear fusion, and sixth\-generation \(6G\) telecommunications\. It then examines how the 15th Plan translates these gains into a new agenda organized around self\-reliance, original innovation, and what Chinese policymakers describe as “new quality productive forces”\. The paper also identifies persistent challenges, especially in advanced\-node chip manufacturing, demographic and fiscal pressure on R&amp;D, and mounting technology\-related trade friction, that will shape the trajectory of innovation in China during the coming five years\.
