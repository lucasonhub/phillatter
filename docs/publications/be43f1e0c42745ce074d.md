# The SINKRONISASI DESAIN KAWASAN KONSERVASI BUDAYA DAN KETERLIBATAN STAKEHOLDER TERHADAP NILAI EKONOMI WISATA: STUDI KASUS KAWASAN HERITAGE KOTA BANDUNG: SINKRONISASI DESAIN KAWASAN KONSERVASI BUDAYA DAN KETERLIBATAN STAKEHOLDER TERHADAP NILAI EKONOMI WISATA: STUDI KASUS KAWASAN HERITAGE KOTA BANDUNG

**Autores:** Inggi Lestari

**Data:** 2026-10-10 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

The dynamics of global spatial transformation currently indicate a massive paradigm shift, where urban design is required not only to focus on physical aesthetics but to evolve into a strategic instrument for achieving economic resilience and social inclusivity\. This research aims to analyze the simultaneous and partial influence of the Dynamics of Urban Space Design Processes \(Variable X1\) on the Effectiveness of Sustainable Tourism Infrastructure Development \(Variable X2\) in Bandung City&\#x27;s tourism nodes \(Braga, Cihapit, Dago, and Cilaki\)\. Using a Mixed Methods approach with an Explanatory Sequential design, this study synthesizes quantitative survey data from 60 MSME actors and tourists, supported by in\-depth qualitative interviews\. Parametric statistical tests reveal an R Square value of 0\.082 with regression significance \(Sig\. 0\.087\), indicating that visual and physical spatial interventions alone are not significantly enough to boost economic turnover without inclusive governance\. These findings highlight that public space revitalization focusing solely on beautification is prone to triggering gentrification and alienating local citizens\. This study recommends the need for a more participatory Pentahelix governance framework, integrating equitable spatial distribution for the informal sector to maintain economic resilience and the area&\#x27;s heritage identity in the future\.
