\_Zenodo\_ 1\. 2026This paper develops an integrated philosophical architecture connecting Ziran System, Xenoscience, and the Realist Operational Shapes System \(ROSS\)\. Its central concern is the formation, reconstruction, retention, transformation, and possible extinction of heterogeneous realist operations across configurations, temporal scales, and material conditions\. Through engagements with Plato, Leibniz, Wilfrid Sellars, and Ray Brassier, the paper reconstructs distinct operational relations without reducing them to a universal realist mechanism\. ROSS tracks the conditions under which historically situated operations can be reconstructed and re\-entered into subsequent inferential, experimental, or institutional configurations\. Such re\-entry may alter subsequent possibilities, remain causally ineffective, or become redundant\. The integration with Xenoscience examines scientific operations that may proceed independently of human operation, human\-system energy provision, human\-readable representations, or human conceptual frameworks\. These independence conditions are distinguished from the reproduction of scientific evolutionary cycles\. Supplementary PyTorch experiments investigate adaptive feature selection, correction latency, cumulative predictive loss, and the limits of fixed candidate sets\. Their results illustrate restricted computational operations without establishing the implementation of ROSS or the full integrated architecture\. The paper develops a conditional account of realist operational reconstruction, reality penetration, feedback, and self\-extinction while preserving the possibility that scientific and philosophical operations continue without ROSS or human philosophical subjects\. \(direct link \)
