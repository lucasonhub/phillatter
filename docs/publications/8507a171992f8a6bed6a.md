# A novel serum four\-biomarker biosignature and field\-deployable colloidal gold strip for accurate identification of subclinical and active tuberculosis

**Autores:** Jingyuan Feng; Duan Hongfei

**Data:** 2026-10-09 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Background Current TB diagnostics fail to detect subclinical and paucibacillary cases, hindering End TB Strategy goals\. We validated a novel serum four\-biomarker biosignature and developed a colloidal gold strip for accurate subclinical and active TB identification\.Methods 280 participants \(HC, PN, STB, ATB; n = 70 each\) were enrolled\. Serum PDGFC, CXCL9, CXCL11, and CCL1 were measured by ELISA in training \(n = 200\) and external validation \(n = 80\) cohorts\. Six machine learning algorithms built diagnostic models\. A four\-plex colloidal gold strip with simplified visual criteria was developed and compared against smear, TB DNA, and IGRA\.Results All biomarkers were significantly elevated in STB/ATB versus HC/PN \(p &lt; 0\.001\), confirming Mtb\-specific responses\. Logistic regression achieved 97\.5% validation sensitivity\. The strip using a ‘≥2 positive lines’ criterion yielded 96\.0% sensitivity, 95\.0% specificity, 95\.5% accuracy, and outperformed conventional tests, effectively identifying smear\-negative subclinical TB\.Conclusions This biosignature is a robust TB diagnostic tool\. The companion strip offers a rapid, sputum\-free, instrument\-minimal solution for decentralized screening, bridging biomarker discovery and field application in resource\-limited settings\.
