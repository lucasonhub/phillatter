# Smart farms, virtual mirrors: AI\-powered digital twins reshaping modern agriculture

**Autores:** A\. R\. Neha; B\. Bindu; S\. Simi; P\. R\. Manju; Saklain Mulani; B\. Suchitra

**Data:** 2026-10-10 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Sustainable agriculture is increasingly threatened by a combination of factors, including inadequate food security, deteriorating land quality, shifting climate patterns, and a rapidly expanding population\.Digital twin \(DT\) technology has emerged as a transformative tool in modern agriculture, allowing the development of real\-time virtual representations of physical farming systems to support data\-informed decision\-making and sustainable resource use\.The present study examines the concepts, frameworks and practical applications of digital twins in agriculture, emphasising their integration with the Internet of Things, artificial intelligence, machine learning and remote sensing techniques\.Key application areas of DT in agriculture include land cultivation, digital twin\-empowered drones, controlled environment agriculture, disease detection, hydroponics, smart irrigation, livestock farming, and beekeeping\.Reports from multiple studies show that DTs significantly enhance the prediction accuracy of crop yield, the detection of pests and diseases, irrigation efficiency, and livestock health monitoring, while reducing labour, resource waste, and environmental impact\.Despite the advantages, challenges related to data quality, implementation and integration with systems remain\.This study explains the critical role of DT in advancing precision agriculture and building resilient, sustainable food production systems that address global challenges including a changing climate, growing global population and the need to ensure reliable access to food\.
