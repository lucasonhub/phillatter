# Bulgaria, Jamel: Validation and Empirical Overlaps of the Core Emotion Framework: A Multi\-Disciplinary Translational Analysis

**Autores:** 

**Data de descoberta:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** unknown

## Descrição do feed

The Core Emotion Framework \(CEF\) presents an ambitious structural\-constructivist paradigm that systematizes colloquial psychological heuristics into a mathematically explicit, neurobiologically grounded, and mechanically actuated architecture\. By translating the intuitive, division\-based &quot;Head, Heart, and Gut&quot; hubs into three autonomous, localized neural networks hosting ten discrete functional operators, the framework seeks to unify classical theories of innate affect with contemporary constructionist models\. To confirm the scientific validity of the framework, this report maps its core structural, mathematical, biological, and somatic assertions against established empirical evidence in quantum cognition, neurocardiology, neurogastroenterology, clinical psychology, and embodied sensorimotor feedback\. \(direct link \)
