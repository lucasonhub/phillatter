# AI\-Native Product Management: A Vision

**Autores:** Ricardo Britto

**Data:** 2026-10-10 · **Tipo:** preprint

## Resumo

Artificial intelligence is increasingly used to support product management tasks\. At the same time, AI\-native research and development \(R&amp;D\) may increase software realization capacity\. Organizations may realize software faster than they can identify, qualify, and reconsider what should be realized\. This paper asks how product management should be re\-envisioned under these conditions\. We synthesize research on software product management, product learning, human\-AI collaboration, and the Product Management and R&amp;D boundary\. We propose a vision in which AI becomes a persistent participant in product work\. The vision covers sensing, exploration, decision\-making, realization, and learning\. We propose six principles and make the vision tangible through an operating model grounded in human professionals, AI personas, agents, skills, and a shared product context\. We further argue that AI\-native Product Management and AI\-native R&amp;D can form a continuous product\-learning process\. Finally, we identify four AI\-specific research directions that require empirical investigation\.
