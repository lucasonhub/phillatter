# Digital Identity in the Age of Generative AI: A Conceptual Comparative Analysis of the UK, Italy and Poland

**Autores:** Malgorzata Sulimierska; Ashwinth Sankar; Agata Kocia

**Data:** 2026-10-14 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Purpose: Generative Artificial Intelligence \(GenAI\) weakens the assumed link between authenticated identity and reliable organisational evidence\. This conceptual paper reconceptualises digital organisational identity as governance infrastructure that links actors, organisational authority, economic substance, and AI\-mediated evidence in business, accounting and assurance\. Design/Methodology/Approach: The paper adopts a conceptual methodology that combines critical literature synthesis, comparative analysis, and model construction\. Guided by Power’s Audit Society framework, it integrates digital identity, accounting, and AI\-governance literature and compares the UK Trust Framework, Italy’s SPID and Poland’s mObywatel to identify key constructs and map their relationships within an identity–evidence trust\-chain model\. Findings: The model shows that authentication does not establish organisational authority or economic substance; GenAI adds provenance risk by enabling credible\-looking evidence to become detached from its underlying event\. Original/Value of the Paper: The paper connects previously separate literatures and integrates four evidentiary construct’s identity, organisational authority, economic substance and AI provenance with second\-order auditability as a mechanism for preserving accountable professional judgement in business\. Research Limitations/Implications: The framework is conceptual and requires empirical validation\.
