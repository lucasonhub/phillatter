# ENHANCING INTEGRATED ASSET AND LIABILITY MANAGEMENT IN COMMERCIAL BANKS USING MACHINE LEARNING AND OPTIMIZATION METHODS

**Autores:** Musaev Mansur Mirzayarovich

**Data:** 2026-10-12 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

This article examines the improvement of integrated asset and liability management in commercial banks through the application of machine learning and optimization methods\. It explores the role of data\-driven forecasting, interest rate risk assessment, liquidity management, credit portfolio analysis, and capital allocation in strengthening banking efficiency and financial stability\. Particular attention is given to the integration of predictive models with mathematical optimization techniques to support decision\-making under changing economic conditions\. The article also identifies practical approaches to implementing intelligent analytical systems in commercial banks, emphasizing data quality, model reliability, regulatory compliance, and human oversight\. The proposed recommendations aim to improve the coordination of assets and liabilities, reduce financial risks, and enhance the sustainable performance of commercial banks\.
