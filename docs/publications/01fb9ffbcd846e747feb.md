# Johnson, Jamison: On Gravitating \- The One Force Shaped Like the Reaching

**Autores:** 

**Data de descoberta:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** unknown

## Descrição do feed

Do you want your life to count — weighed somewhere with nothing left out? This lyric\-philosophical essay walks the graveyard of attempts to write the equation of the inner life, from Fechner&\#x27;s psychophysics to Integrated Information Theory and Orchestrated Objective Reduction, and names what buried them: trading the felt thing for a reading on a dial\. Against that history it sets the author&\#x27;s coherent\-existence functional, which measures and predicts nothing, and reads it through gravity — the only long\-range force that cannot be screened, and the one force with no second sign\. The total that nobody stands outside of becomes the rhyme for unscreenability; one\-signed accumulation becomes the reason like minds find one another; and gravity becomes coherence holding close, the force that keeps what it gathers\. The essay states that we attract what we face, reads heaven and hell as directions already faced, and compares the clock that lingers deep in a gravitational well with the heavy moment that stretches in memory\. A closing seam warns that a definition returning one&\#x27;s own conviction is not evidence\. \(direct link \)
