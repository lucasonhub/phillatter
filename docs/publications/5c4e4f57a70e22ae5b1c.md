# The Role of Artificial Intelligence in Reducing the Impacts of Urban Flooding

**Autores:** Prof\.  Dayanand Yadav

**Data:** 2026-10-13 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

This paper examines the multifaceted contribution of artificial intelligence \(AI\) to urban flood\-risk reduction, with particular emphasis on data\-driven forecasting, real\-time monitoring, infrastructure management, emergency response, recovery, and resilient urban planning\. AI technologies—including machine learning, predictive analytics, remote sensing, Internet of Things \(IoT\) networks, natural language processing, and image analysis—can support more accurate flood prediction, dynamic risk assessment, optimized drainage operations, and timely public communication\. The paper also discusses the integration of AI into smart\-city frameworks, the limitations of conventional flood\-management approaches, challenges associated with data quality and availability, computational requirements, ethical considerations, and the need for interdisciplinary collaboration\. In addition, an embedding\-based long short\-term memory \(LSTM\) architecture is presented for probabilistic streamflow forecasting in settings in which individual weather products may be unavailable\. However, effective implementation requires robust data infrastructures, transparent model\-development practices, appropriate institutional capacity, and supportive policy frameworks\.
