# AI for Grading from the Teaching Perspective \(with an EssayGrader Case Comparison\)

**Autores:** Ian J\. Shepherd

**Data:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

AI has taken the academic world by storm, dramatically affecting written assignments and student performance evaluations\. This paper examines the use of Artificial Intelligence \(AI\) from the teaching perspective, with emphasis on daily grading workflows\. We analyze benefits such as fair and consistent application of rubrics, the ability to incorporate articles and course materials as inputs to grading, and the delivery of individualized, extensive feedback\. We compare these capabilities against a representative product—EssayGrader—situate our claims within current scholarship on Automated Essay Scoring \(AES\) and Large Language Models \(LLMs\) as graders, and provide policy guidance\.
