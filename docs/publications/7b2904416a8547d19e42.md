# Mapping Digital Transformation, Innovation and Sustainability in European Union Countries

**Autores:** Dilek VEYSİKARANİ

**Data:** 2026-10-15 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Digital transformation, innovation and sustainability are important factors for the competitiveness and long\-term development performance of a country\. This study aims to compare the digital transformation, innovation and sustainability performance of the European Union countries and to reveal their similarities and differences\. The Knowledge, Technology and Future Readiness dimensions of the IMD World Digital Competitiveness Index were used to represent digital transformation\. For innovation, the Global Innovation Index was used and for sustainability, respectively, the SDG Index, Spillover indicator and Environmental Performance Index\. The first analysis was made by the Multidimensional Scaling method and the structure obtained was tested for accuracy by the hierarchical clustering and the K\-Means analysis\. The findings reveal that the European Union countries are divided into three clusters for digital transformation, innovation and sustainability\. The analysis results show a strong relationship between digital competitiveness and innovation performance while sustainability indicators show more limited differences between countries\. It was also found that Northern and Western European countries have a similar and high performance in terms of digital transformation and innovation, and Eastern and Southern European countries have a relatively low performance\.
