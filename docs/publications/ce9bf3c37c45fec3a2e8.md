# Biopolymer\-based coatings for egg preservation: recent advances, current challenges, and future prospects

**Autores:** Dariush Khademi Shurmasti; Clara Mariana Gonçalves Lima; Renata Ferreira Santana; Nishant Kumar; Michelle Carlota Gonçalves; Renata Cristina Ferreira Bonomo; Henrique Douglas Melo Coutinho; Charles Odilichukwu R\. Okpala

**Data:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Eggs, despite being globally consumed given their nutritional value, remain highly susceptible to storage quality challenges, from physicochemical changes, through microbial contamination, to oxidative deterioration\. Moreover, if sustainable preservation outcomes extending egg shelf life were to be steadily achieved, it would require continued literature syntheses about how internal quality and safety \(of egg shelf life\) are maintained\. To supplement existing information, this current review synthesizes biopolymer\-based coatings for egg preservation, from recent advances, through current challenges, to future research prospects\. Polysaccharide\- and protein\-based biopolymer matrices are established as important coating platforms for improving egg quality and shelf life\. Nanocomposite coatings incorporating nanofillers and natural bioactive compounds provide multifunctional barrier, mechanical, antimicrobial, and antioxidant properties\. Such multifunctional coating systems can enhance preservation performance through combined mechanisms involving moisture regulation, controlled gas transfer, oxidative stabilization, microbial inhibition, and retention and gradual availability of active compounds\. Unlike previous reviews that largely dwelt on individual coating materials, the current review synergizes biopolymer matrices, nanocomposite reinforcement, bioactive functionalization, alongside emerging intelligent coating strategies\. Besides, consumer acceptance, manufacturing scalability, production cost, and regulatory compliance remain important considerations and potential barriers to commercialization\. However, if multifunctional, intelligent, and commercially viable coating technologies are to successfully enhance sustainable egg preservation, more efforts are required\. Overall, coating technology has progressed beyond simple protective barriers toward edible multifunctional preservation systems with promising potential to improve egg quality, safety, shelf life, and sustainability\.
