# Sergent, Brandon M\.: Closed for Business: Why ∃E Is the Only Coherent Totality

**Autores:** 

**Data de descoberta:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** unknown

## Descrição do feed

Philosophy routinely evaluates claims about ultimate reality as though they were ordinary hypotheses situated inside a larger neutral arena\. That treatment is structurally mistaken\. A genuine totality is a special logical case\. If a candidate is truly exhaustive, nothing real can stand outside it to explain, cause, validate, ground, compare, complete, or judge it\. Any proposed exterior either belongs to reality, in which case it was never exterior, or does not belong to reality, in which case it cannot perform real explanatory work\. Paul Edwards identified one form of this closure in 1967 when he argued that if “the universe” means “the totality of things,” explanation cannot appeal beyond it\. Agrippa exposed the corresponding regress in justification\. F\. H\. Bradley recognized that ultimate reality must be internally coherent and identified reality with sentient experience, yet retained temporal and relational commitments that generated contradictions he then repaired with the Absolute\. Experiential Empiricism unifies these partial discoveries under ∃E: experience is\. This paper distinguishes totality closure from candidate identification, derives a general admission rule and checklist for totality claims, and tests matter, God, mathematical structure, information, neutral substance, Bradley’s Absolute, and experience against exhaustiveness, closure, self\-sufficiency, self\-application, coherence, regress containment, semantic population, and operational conservation\. The tests are not designed around ∃E\. They follow from what it means for anything to be genuinely ultimate\. ∃E alone survives the full procedure without silently reserving an exterior from which it is known, grounded, validated, or completed\. The act of asking, doubting, modeling, denying, grounding, or meta\-evaluating experience is itself another experiential occurrence\. Reality is closed for business because anything capable of doing business with it is already part of the inventory\. \(direct link \)
