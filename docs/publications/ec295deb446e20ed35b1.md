# Exponential Laws for Mixed\-Order Ultradifferentiable Mappings

**Autores:** Hamza Alzaareer

**Data:** 2026-10-13 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

We introduce the mixed\-order classes C \[ M \] r , s by combining mixed differentiability with the weight\-matrix framework, in both the Beurling and Roumieu cases\. At finite order we prove a chain rule under the Faà\-di\-Bruno property\. The currying map is a linear injection\. If r and s are finite, the class coincides with C r , s and the exponential law reduces to the mixed\-order law without weights\. Continuity of the inverse on the image holds when a lower weight bound is available at a single index\.
