# Fujie, Naoto: The End of the Scientific Paper, the Beginning of Xenoscience: AI Generation, Machine Verification, Autonomous Falsification, and Homogeneous\-Research Compression

## Descrição do feed

\_Zenodo\_ 1\. 2026This paper examines whether the scientific paper remains a necessary unit of scientific knowledge production under conditions of increasingly autonomous artificial intelligence\. Rather than treating AI\-generated text as merely a new authorship tool, it analyzes a broader transformation in which hypothesis generation, model construction, verification, falsification, replication, literature synthesis, and result compression can be performed continuously by machine systems\. The paper argues that the scientific paper historically compresses heterogeneous research operations into a human\-readable and institutionally stabilized format, but that this format need not remain the primary operational unit of science\. AI generation, machine verification, and autonomous falsification can instead produce recursive scientific workflows in which claims are generated, tested, revised, rejected, and re\-integrated without waiting for conventional publication cycles\. Homogeneous\-research compression refers to the reduction of large volumes of structurally similar research into non\-redundant operational differences, thereby shifting scientific value from document production toward the detection of differences that alter prediction, intervention, explanation, or future experimental possibilities\. Xenoscience is introduced as a framework for analyzing scientific systems in which the paper, author, journal, and fixed research sequence become contingent interfaces rather than necessary components\. The broader claim is that the end of the scientific paper would not mark the end of science, but the reorganization of scientific communication, verification, and self\-correction around machine\-operational rather than document\-centered forms\. \(direct link \)
