# Inteligencia artificial y rúbricas para la generación de instrumentos de evaluación

**Autores:** Azahara Casanova Pistón; Patricia Sáenz\-Valiente

**Data:** 2026-10-15 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

The use of rubrics in Higher Education has become established as a key strategy for improving learning assessment by providing clear criteria, promoting transparency, and facilitating student self\-regulation\. The incorporation of ICT has enabled their evolution expanding their possibilities for use and analysis, although it has also raised concerns related to privacy and data security when online environments are used\. In this context, the design of automated assessment instruments capable of functioning offline is proposed in order to guarantee the protection of students’ information\. Based on a literature review, a proposal is developed using spreadsheets, for the creation of two types of rubrics: one focused on grading and another aimed at generating feedback\. Automation is achieved through the use of formulas, macros, and Visual Basic programming, making it possible to streamline the assessment process and reduce teachers’ workload\. Furthermore, the integration of artificial intelligence through prompting techniques facilitates code generation, the expansion of comments, and the improvement of feedback writing, increasing its personalization and coherence\. These tools make it possible to build more efficient, adaptive, and secure assessment systems, although they require an adequate level of teachers’ digital competence\. The possibility of integrating both rubrics into a single system and exploring solutions that combine the advantages of offline environments with guarantees for data management in digital settings are proposed as future lines of development\.
