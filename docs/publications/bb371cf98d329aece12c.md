  report. Based on these intervals, the system can categorize a value as being within
  the supplied range, below the supplied range, or above the supplied range. If the
  reference interval or measurement unit is missing, ambiguous, or incompatible, the
  result should be flagged for manual review. This approach aims to make the analysis
  process more transparent by showing the extracted values and the reference information
  used for comparison. A user-friendly dashboard is proposed to display the organized
  laboratory information in a clear and accessible manner. By presenting relevant
  parameters and highlighting potentially out-of-range values, the dashboard can help
  users locate important information more easily. The system also emphasizes traceability,
  allowing extracted information to be checked against its original source wherever
  practical. These features are intended to reduce the effort involved in locating
  and organizing relevant details within laboratory reports. An important aspect of
  this research is the responsible use of AI in healthcare-related applications. A
  result outside a laboratory's reference interval does not necessarily indicate a
  disease, and a result within that interval does not guarantee that a person is healthy.
  Therefore, the proposed system is intended to support the review of medical information
  rather than provide autonomous diagnoses, prescribe treatments, or replace the professional
  judgment of qualified healthcare practitioners. Human verification, clear communication
  of uncertainty, and appropriate interpretation of laboratory findings remain essential.
  The research also considers privacy, data security, and the reliability of extracted
  information. Medical reports may contain sensitive personal data, making it important
  to use appropriate access controls, secure data handling practices, file validation,
  and suitable data-retention policies. For development and evaluation, synthetic,
  de-identified, or appropriately authorized datasets can be used to reduce privacy
  risks. The proposed evaluation framework includes measures for extraction accuracy,
  classification performance, processing time, robustness across report formats, and
  usability. Actual performance results must be obtained through systematic testing
  before the effectiveness of the implemented system can be established. The proposed
  system follows a modular architecture consisting of a user interface, document-processing
  or OCR component, preprocessing and validation layer, data-normalization module,
  reference-range comparison mechanism, and dashboard for presenting the results.
  This modular structure provides a foundation for future improvements, including
  multilingual laboratory report processing, better handling of complex tables, visualization
  of changes across multiple reports, explainable AI techniques, and enhanced tools
  for professional review. The main contribution of this research is the proposed
  integration of report extraction, data validation, normalization, reference-range
  comparison, and visualization into a traceable workflow. Rather than presenting
  automated output as a definitive medical conclusion, the approach emphasizes transparency,
  verification, and human oversight. Its practical value will depend on implementation
  quality, dataset coverage, systematic evaluation, and the ability to handle different
  report layouts reliably.

# Design and Development of an AI\-Assisted Medical Laboratory Report Analysis and Decision\-Support System

**Autores:** Vivek Kumar; Nitish Pal; Prince Munna Yadav; Akash Verma

**Data:** 2026-10-13 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Artificial Intelligence \(AI\) is becoming an important part of modern healthcare by helping organize medical information, improve data accessibility, and support more efficient workflows\. Medical laboratory reports contain valuable information about a person&\#x27;s health, including test names, measured values, units, and reference ranges\. However, understanding these reports can be challenging for individuals who are unfamiliar with medical terminology\. Manually reviewing and organizing laboratory data can also be time\-consuming, particularly when reports are available in different formats, such as digital PDFs, scanned documents, and images\. This research paper presents the design of a proposed AI\-assisted Medical Laboratory Report Analysis and Decision\-Support System that aims to make laboratory information easier to process, organize, and review\. The system focuses on combining document processing, Optical Character Recognition \(OCR\), data extraction, validation, normalization, and visualization within a structured workflow\. Its primary goal is to improve the accessibility and consistency of laboratory report information while supporting the identification of results that may require further attention\. The proposed system begins by accepting laboratory reports in supported digital or scanned formats\. When a report contains image\-based text, OCR technology can be used to convert the visible information into machine\-readable text\. For digital PDF files containing an accessible text layer, document\-parsing techniques can be used to extract relevant information directly\. The extracted content is then processed to identify important laboratory parameters, numerical values, measurement units, and reference intervals\. After extraction, the system applies preprocessing and validation techniques to organize the collected information\. This stage is important because laboratory reports may contain inconsistent formatting, different parameter names, missing values, unclear units, or variations in table layouts\. The system is designed to preserve the original information wherever possible and identify uncertain or incomplete fields for manual verification rather than making unsupported assumptions\. Data normalization helps present the extracted information in a more consistent and structured format\. The next stage focuses on comparing laboratory results with the reference intervals provided in the original report\. Based on these intervals, the system can categorize a value as being within the supplied range, below the supplied range, or above the supplied range\. If the reference interval or measurement unit is missing, ambiguous, or incompatible, the result should be flagged for manual review\. This approach aims to make the analysis process more transparent by showing the extracted values and the reference information used for comparison\. A user\-friendly dashboard is proposed to display the organized laboratory information in a clear and accessible manner\. By presenting relevant parameters and highlighting potentially out\-of\-range values, the dashboard can help users locate important information more easily\. The system also emphasizes traceability, allowing extracted information to be checked against its original source wherever practical\. These features are intended to reduce the effort involved in locating and organizing relevant details within laboratory reports\. An important aspect of this research is the responsible use of AI in healthcare\-related applications\. A result outside a laboratory&\#x27;s reference interval does not necessarily indicate a disease, and a result within that interval does not guarantee that a person is healthy\. Therefore, the proposed system is intended to support the review of medical information rather than provide autonomous diagnoses, prescribe treatments, or replace the professional judgment of qualified healthcare practitioners\. Human verification, clear communication of uncertainty, and appropriate interpretation of laboratory findings remain essential\. The research also considers privacy, data security, and the reliability of extracted information\. Medical reports may contain sensitive personal data, making it important to use appropriate access controls, secure data handling practices, file validation, and suitable data\-retention policies\. For development and evaluation, synthetic, de\-identified, or appropriately authorized datasets can be used to reduce privacy risks\. The proposed evaluation framework includes measures for extraction accuracy, classification performance, processing time, robustness across report formats, and usability\. Actual performance results must be obtained through systematic testing before the effectiveness of the implemented system can be established\. The proposed system follows a modular architecture consisting of a user interface, document\-processing or OCR component, preprocessing and validation layer, data\-normalization module, reference\-range comparison mechanism, and dashboard for presenting the results\. This modular structure provides a foundation for future improvements, including multilingual laboratory report processing, better handling of complex tables, visualization of changes across multiple reports, explainable AI techniques, and enhanced tools for professional review\. The main contribution of this research is the proposed integration of report extraction, data validation, normalization, reference\-range comparison, and visualization into a traceable workflow\. Rather than presenting automated output as a definitive medical conclusion, the approach emphasizes transparency, verification, and human oversight\. Its practical value will depend on implementation quality, dataset coverage, systematic evaluation, and the ability to handle different report layouts reliably\.
