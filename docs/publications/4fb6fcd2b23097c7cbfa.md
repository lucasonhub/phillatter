# Deep Learning\-driven ECG Signal Interpretation for Predictive Cardiac Health Monitoring in Personalized Medical Care Systems

**Autores:** Mahmood Hameed Majeed; Mohammed H\. Dosh; Safa L\. Kailan; Ali HamzahNajim; Maizatul Alice Meor Said

**Data:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Cardiovascular diseases \(CVDs\) remain a major global health burden, making accurate ECG\-based classification important for automated cardiac assessment\.Existing ECG interpretation methods often employ computationally complex hybrid or Transformer\-based architectures and provide limited cross\-dataset evaluation\.To address these limitations, this study proposes a TCN\-MLP framework that uses a Temporal Convolutional Network \(TCN\) with causal dilated convolutions and residual connections to extract temporal ECG representations, followed by a Multi\-Layer Perceptron \(MLP\) for CVD classification\.Age, gender, and their missingness indicators are incorporated as demographic metadata to examine their contribution to classification\.The framework was evaluated on PTB\-XL, MIT\-BIH, and PhysioNet/CinC 2021 using a patient\-independent 70% training, 10% validation, and 20% testing protocol, with additional five\-fold cross\-validation\.The proposed framework achieved 98\.7% accuracy, 98\.5% precision, and 98\.7% AUC\-ROC, outperforming the evaluated baseline models\.The results demonstrate effective demographic\-assisted ECG classification and cross\-dataset evaluation under retrospective patient\-independent testing\.
