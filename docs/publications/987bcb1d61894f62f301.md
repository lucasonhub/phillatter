# THE BIO\-LEGAL INTELLIGENCE FRAMEWORK FROM NEURONS TO CONSTITUTIONS: A Unified Theory of Neurobiological, Sociological, and Philosophical Foundations of Legal Systems

**Autores:** mohamed kamal arafa el\-rakhawi

**Data:** 2026-10-18 · **Tipo:** book

## Resumo

The Bio\-Legal Intelligence Framework: From Neurons to Constitutions by Dr\. Mohamed Kamal Arafa El\-Rakhawi \(DOI: 10\.5281/zenodo\.23458534\) establishes Bio\-Legal Intelligence as a groundbreaking academic field integrating neuroscience, evolutionary biology, sociology, philosophy, and legal theory\. The central thesis posits that law functions as the collective nervous system of society—constitutions as central processors, laws as neural pathways, justice as homeostatic balance, and legal evolution as cultural biological evolution\. The framework unfolds through four pillars: \(1\) Biological Foundations of Legal Consciousness, exploring the moral brain, evolutionary justice origins, and social neuroscience of law; \(2\) Law as Bio\-Social Living System, analyzing legal systems as collective neural networks with homeostasis, plasticity, and immune functions; \(3\) Bio\-Philosophy of Law, grounding natural rights in evolved biological needs and examining evolutionary justice; \(4\) Integration and Applications, proposing bio\-constitutional design principles, bio\-legal education reform, and future civilization vision\. The book introduces novel concepts including legal homeostasis, legal immunity, legal plasticity, memetic propagation of norms, and biological foundations of rights\. It provides transformative applications for constitutional design, legal education, judicial decision\-making, and addressing 21st\-century challenges including climate change, artificial intelligence, biotechnology, and global inequality\. This foundational treatise offers a unified theoretical framework connecting individual neural processes to collective legal institutions, providing both rigorous scholarship and practical guidance for creating legal systems that serve human flourishing based on evolved needs and capacities\. The work is protected under international copyright law and available for academic citation across multiple formats \(APA, Chicago, MLA, Harvard, IEEE, Vancouver\)\.
