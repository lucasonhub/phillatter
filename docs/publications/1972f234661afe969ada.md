# MathemAfrica: A Multilingual Explainable Artificial Intelligence Ecosystem for Adaptive Mathematics Education in Resource\-Constrained African Learning Environments

**Autores:** Jamilu Auwalu Adamu; Muhammad Bello Sadiq; Professor Promise Mebine

**Data:** 2026-10-12 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Secondary mathematics education in developing and resource\-constrained contexts confronts systemic bottlenecks, including overcrowded classrooms, acute teacher shortages, frequent electrical blackouts, prohibitively expensive cellular data, and linguistic dissonance between official English instruction and students&\#x27; indigenous home languages\.To address these interconnected challenges, this study developed and evaluated MathemAfrica, an innovative digital learning platform integrating Bayesian Knowledge Tracing, Multidimensional Item Response Theory \(M\-3PL IRT\), offline optical handwriting verification, and multilingual scaffolding in Hausa, Yoruba, Igbo, and Swahili\.Employing an ADDIE\-based design science methodology and a 16\-week quasi\-experimental non\-equivalent pretest\-posttest control group design, the intervention was evaluated across six public secondary schools in Kano State, Nigeria, with 1,428 Grade 10 students\.Expert reviews confirmed high content validity \(97\.06%\) and media quality \(94\.56%\), while students reported exceptional practicality \(96\.13%\)\.Analysis of Covariance revealed statistically significant and substantial gains in mathematical proficiency, with adjusted posttest scores of 73\.84% in the intervention group versus 57\.22% in conventional instruction \(F \(1, 1425\) = 148\.92,p &lt; \.0001,Cohen&\#x27;s d = 0\.94\)\.Furthermore, the platform closed 80\.4% of the indigenous language achievement penalty, while maintaining real\-time adaptive sequencing at merely 38\.4 kB per session\.The results provide empirical evidence that combining edge\-native computation with culturally responsive multilingual scaffolding strengthens secondary mathematical achievement under severe resource constraints\.
