# Poska, Mindaugas: Natural Possibility 36 Does God Exist?

**Autores:** 

**Data de descoberta:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** unknown

## Descrição do feed

Natural Possibility 36: Does God Exist? explores the concept of divinity by moving away from classical, single\-entity monotheism toward an emergent, systems\-based framework\. Reconceptualizing &quot;God&quot; as &quot;Nature,&quot; the text examines the cosmos as an interconnected network of autonomous fields, structures, and developmental boundaries\. It establishes a multi\-tiered hierarchy of the human system, extending from physical body to mind, spirit, agency, and observer, to distinguish the capacity to perceive from the operational capacity to act\. Power is framed not as absolute centralized control, but as distributed coordination across nested systems\. Incorporating concepts like the Universal Library, dynamic feedback loops, and evolutionary guard barriers, the paper proposes that reality self\-protects through developmental boundaries, permitting higher systemic participation only through alignment with the whole \(&quot;We&quot;\)\. Ultimately, it grounds cosmic theology in ecological ethics, framing environmental action as the foundational form of prayer\.
