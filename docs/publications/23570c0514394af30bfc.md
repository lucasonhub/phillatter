# XAI and its Applications in Decision Support System

**Autores:** Gitin Garg; Kiya Jain; Vishal Shrivastava; Akhil Pandey

**Data:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Artificial Intelligence \(AI\) has transformed decision\-making across various industries by enabling systems to analyse large volumes of data and provide intelligent recommendations\. However, many modern AI models, particularly deep learning algorithms, function as &quot;black boxes,&quot; making it difficult for users to understand how decisions are generated\. This lack of transparency limits the adoption of AI in critical domains such as healthcare, finance, law, and business\. Explainable Artificial Intelligence \(XAI\) has emerged as a promising solution that improves transparency, interpretability, and trust in AI systems\. Decision Support Systems \(DSS\) assist decision\-makers by combining data, analytical models, and domain knowledge\. Integrating XAI into DSS enables users to understand AI\-generated recommendations, thereby improving confidence and accountability\. This review paper explores the concepts of XAI, different explanation techniques, and their applications in DSS\. The paper also discusses recent research, challenges, limitations, and future research directions
