## Resumo

This Reprint focuses on recent advances in collaborative learning and optimization theory and their applications in intelligent systems\. It presents emerging methodologies that integrate collaborative mechanisms with optimization strategies to address complex problems in artificial intelligence and computational intelligence\. The contributions cover topics including machine learning, deep learning, evolutionary computation, reinforcement learning, fuzzy systems, collaborative optimization algorithms and multi\-agent intelligent systems\. By highlighting theoretical developments, algorithmic innovations and practical applications, this Reprint provides valuable insights into the design of efficient, adaptive and intelligent computational frameworks for solving challenging real\-world problems\.
