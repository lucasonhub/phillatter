# Wu, Longjian: \[CVES01\] The Unified Essence of Emotion: An Emotional Generation Theory Based on Core Value Flux

**Autores:** 

**Data de descoberta:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** unknown

## Descrição do feed

The diversity and complexity of human emotional experiences have long placed emotion research in a state of theoretical fragmentation\. Existing paradigms—including basic emotion theory, dimensional models, and cultural constructionism—have each contributed to description and measurement, yet none has provided a unified generative mechanism explanation for the full spectrum of emotions\. Moreover, these theories remain confined to inductive classification of already\-named emotions, unable to encompass complex emotional experiences that language cannot precisely describe\. This study proposes that all conscious\-level human emotions are, in essence, subjective presentations of dynamic changes in an individual&\#x27;s core values; emotions are not discrete psychological entities, but continuous experiences formed by the concurrent activation and integration of a finite set of emotional monomials\. This paper constructs an emotion generation framework parameterized by value change direction, object relation, temporal closure, and controllability judgment\. Core mechanisms proposed include: complex emotions as cognitive naming after polynomial concurrency, past and future emotions as essentially unclosed psychological simulations, expectation as a value advance operation, and empathy as value weight mapping\. Based on this framework, this paper provides unified mechanistic explanations for more than thirty typical emotions, and clarifies that &quot;ineffable emotions&quot; are natural products of rare combinations in parameter space and transitional superposition states\. This study offers a new unified perspective for emotion research, logically possessing the potential to cover all nameable and unnameable emotional experiences, and lays a theoretical foundation for subsequent quantitative modeling of emotions and the construction of general artificial intelligence emotion systems\.
