# Tan, Kwan Hong: Embodied Authenticity: Being True to Oneself in a Simulated World

**Autores:** 

**Data de descoberta:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** unknown

## Descrição do feed

This thesis explores the concept of authentic human existence within the context of a simulated reality\. Drawing upon existential phenomenology, simulation theory, and empirical studies of virtual environments, it develops a novel theoretical framework of Embodied Authenticity\. This framework posits that authenticity is not contingent upon the metaphysical nature of reality, but rather on the quality of embodied experience within a given world\. The thesis argues that an individual can live authentically in a simulated world if their experience is characterized by embodied presence, existential coherence, and perceived realism\. Through a critical analysis of these concepts, this work aims to provide a new perspective on what it means to be true to oneself in an age of increasing technological mediation and virtualization\. This extensive analysis, spanning over 10,000 words, offers a PhD\-level contribution to the fields of philosophy, media studies, and psychology, providing a robust and novel theoretical lens through which to understand the future of human experience\. \(direct link \)
