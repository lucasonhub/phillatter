# T cell Cyp27b1 promotes granzyme B expression and antiviral host defense in a mouse model of H1N1 influenza A virus infection

**Autores:** Nicole E\. Froelich; Mengzhu Tang; Nicholas J\. Hanna; Troy C\. Sutton; Mark B\. Meyer; Margherita T\. Cantorna

**Data:** 2026-10-14 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Introduction Immune cells express vitamin D receptors \(VDR\) and the CYP27B1 enzyme needed to produce the VDR ligand, 1,25\(OH\) 2 D; suggesting that perhaps the vitamin D response is regulated locally at the site of infection\. Here, we examined the role of immune cell\-derived 1,25\(OH\) 2 D in host resistance to influenza infection\. Methods Mice were infected with mouse\-adapted H1N1 influenza A virus, to determine the role of CYP27B1 on host resistance to influenza infection\. The mouse strains infected include; wild type \(WT\) littermates, whole\-body Cyp27b1 knockout \(Cyp27b1 KO\) mice, mice lacking renal 1,25\(OH\) 2 D production \(double intronic \(DI\)KO\), mice with T cell\-specific deletion of Cyp27b1 \(T\-Cyp27b1 KO\), and mice with T cell\-specific deletion of Vdr \(T\-Vdr KO\)\. Results Whole body Cyp27b1 KO mice developed severe influenza A virus disease, including infection induced mortality, compared to WT littermates\. Oral treatments of the Cyp27b1 KO mice with 1,25\(OH\) 2 D failed to completely protect the Cyp27b1 KO mice from influenza A virus disease\. Mice without renal 1,25\(OH\) 2 D had similar influenza disease as WT littermates\. Influenza infection of T cell\-specific Cyp27b1 KO resulted in increased respiratory distress and lethargy compared to WT littermates\. WT and DIKO mice had more CD8\+ T cells expressing higher amounts of granzyme B\+ \(GrzB\) and disruptions in PD\-1 than either whole\-body Cyp27b1 KO or T\-Cyp27b1 KO mice\. Discussion The work suggests that renal 1,25\(OH\) 2 D is not required for host resistance to this influenza A virus infection\. The work further demonstrates that immune cells and in particular T cells may produce 1,25\(OH\) 2 D to locally support CD8\+ T cell responses to influenza A virus infection\. Defining the mechanisms that regulate local 1,25\(OH\) 2 D production in T cells or other immune cell types is needed\.
