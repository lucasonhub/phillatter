# Process\-driven glycerol catalytic conversion to lactic acid over nickel\-doped hydroxyapatite: Effects of temperature and CO₂ pressure

**Autores:** Leily Nurul Komariah; Susila Arita Rachman; Neza Rahayu Palapa; Nino Rinaldi; Cindi Ramayanti; Willy  Arya Kasuma; Desi Erisna; Tsabita Nayaka Paramasyifa

**Data:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

This study investigates the roles of CO 2 and reaction temperature in the catalytic conversion of glycerol to lactic acid \(LA\)\. The hydrothermal reaction between glycerol and KOH is conducted over a nickel\-doped hydroxyapatite catalyst in a high\-pressure autoclave reactor\. CO 2 pressure was varied at 10, 26, and 42 bar and compared to the process without CO 2 \. The results indicate that the presence of CO₂ has a significant influence on glycerol conversion\. At a CO 2 of 26 bar, the reaction yielded the highest glycerol conversion and concentration\. It facilitates effective coupling between Ni\-catalyzed dehydrogenation and base\-driven rearrangement to LA\. Furthermore, over a range of 150 to 250°C, temperature is the dominant driving force for LA formation under CO₂\-rich conditions\. LA yield increases linearly with temperature\. The optimum condition is considered at 225°C and 26 bar CO 2 , where the LA yield and glycerol conversion 39% and 46\.4%, respectively\. The synergistic effects observed in the glycerol conversion indicate that the reaction mechanism is primarily governed by glycerol dehydrogenation, with in situ\-generated hydrogen serving as the reducing agent\. Keywords: CO 2 ; dehydrogenation; glycerol conversion; hydrothermal; lactic acid; Ni\-HAp
