# Johnson, Jamison: On Magnetizing \- The Force Shaped Like The Aligning

**Autores:** 

**Data de descoberta:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** unknown

## Descrição do feed

If your perception were a magnet for reality, what would you attract? This lyric\-philosophical essay reads magnetization as alignment rather than addition: a bar of iron becomes a magnet when its domains stop pointing every which way and start pointing together, and it keeps part of that alignment after the field is gone\. From remanence and coercivity the essay draws a picture of character as a facing that outlives the field that set it, and that a strong enough counter\-field can still turn\. Setting this beside the author&\#x27;s coherent\-existence functional, it argues that the one term in what a person lives that is theirs alone is their facing, since intensity buys only a share of the shared average; it states the principle that we attract what we face, not what we deserve, and reads karma in its Upanishadic sense as action worn into a groove\. The unhalvable dipole becomes an image of the claim that no facing has content in isolation\. A historical section follows Ørsted, Faraday, and Maxwell, and a closing discipline marks every resemblance as structural, never as physics of mind\. \(direct link \)
