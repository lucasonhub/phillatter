# AI\-Based Interactive Media and Reading Literacy as Determinants of IPAS Learning Outcomes: A Study of Students at SDN Lesanpuro 3 Malang

**Autores:** Meiarini Priastuti; Sudi Dul Aji; Roni Alim Ba’diya Kusufa

**Data:** 2026-10-14 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

This study was motivated by low learning outcomes in Integrated Natural and Social Sciences \(IPAS\) among fifth\-grade students at SDN Lesanpuro 3 Malang, which is driven by reliance on static, conventional media and students&\#x27; limited reading literacy skills\. The objective of this research was to analyze the main effects of AI\-assisted interactive learning media and reading literacy levels, as well as their interaction, on IPAS learning outcomes\. A quantitative quasi\-experimental approach featuring a 2 x 2 factorial design was used with a sample of 56 fifth\-grade students\. Data were analyzed using Two\-Way ANOVA\. The findings revealed a statistically significant difference in learning outcomes between the AI\-assisted media group and the conventional media group \(p = 0\.005\) \(2\)\. Reading literacy exerted a highly significant influence on academic achievement \(p = 0\.000\), and A significant interaction was identified between AI\-based media usage and reading literacy levels \(p = 0\.002\)\. It is concluded that integrating AI\-driven interactive media while strengthening foundational reading literacy creates an effective instructional synergy, demonstrating that educational technology is most effective when supported by adequate student literacy capacity\.
