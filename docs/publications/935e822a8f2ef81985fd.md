# Fujie, Naoto: What Is Respectful Operation Before It Becomes Normative? Operational Respect, Trust, Active Inference, Machine Agency, and Nihilism

## Descrição do feed

\_Zenodo\_ 1\. 2026Respect is usually introduced as an already normative relation involving recognition, reasons, autonomy, or moral standing\. This paper asks an analytically prior question: what operational differences become non\-redundant when interactive respect is introduced? It proposes operational respect not as respect simpliciter, but as a candidate operational constituent of interactive respect: the maintained non\-closure of pathways through which differences arriving from another configuration can alter subsequent possibilities of operation and, under relevant conditions, the terms of future revisability itself\. The paper distinguishes respect from trust, agreement, obedience, politeness, agency, reward, and generic responsiveness\. It connects this analysis to Active Inference, machine learning, corrigibility, Sellarsian and Brandomian normativity, and Ray Brassier’s nihilism without reducing nihilism to passivity\. A sequence of computational experiments examines constructive separability, learned revision, architecture\-dependent intervention, closed\-loop return and reconfiguration, and an Active\-Inference\-inspired probabilistic epistemic baseline\. The central result is not a scalar measure of machine respect but configuration\-dependent separability: candidate operational constituents of interactive respect can become coupled, separable, revisable, or non\-identifiable depending on configuration, history, temporal window, boundary, and intervention topology\. The experiments establish computational distinctions under synthetic conditions; they do not establish moral respect in machines\. \(direct link \)
