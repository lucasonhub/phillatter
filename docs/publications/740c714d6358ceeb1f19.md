# Auditable schema\-conditional reuse of differentially private synopses for constrained and fair clustering

**Autores:** Xinjie Tan

**Data:** 2026-10-12 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Differentially private clustering can separate a private data release from subsequent optimization, but reuse depends on whether the released statistics support each requested objective\. This study specifies and audits a synopsis\-based workflow for plain, balanced, and group\-fair clustering\. A trusted curator releases noisy cell statistics; subsequent computations use only that synopsis and public inputs\. A restricted interface executes three objectives on one archived group\-resolved synopsis and passes all 12 predefined validation events\. Historical benchmark suites provide separate performance evidence rather than demonstrating one shared release across every experiment\. At a privacy budget of one, the fair variant reduces median mass\-weighted parity violation relative to three controls in five protected\-attribute settings, while increasing median normalized intra\-cluster variance in every setting\. A matched six\-optimizer audit finds no Holm\-corrected difference between the quantum\-inspired artificial bee colony configuration and its direct\-space, best\-guided control; this does not establish equivalence\. The contribution is an inspectable specification of the release boundary and its empirical limitations, rather than a new privacy theorem or a uniformly superior optimizer\. Practical evaluation must distinguish schema compatibility, byte\-identical reuse, clustering utility, and local group representation\.
