# Peer Pressure in Green Innovation: Green Innovation or Greenwashing?

**Autores:** Ruoying Chen; Haiyan Jiang; Le Luo

**Data:** 2026-10-12 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

ABSTRACT This study examines whether a firm engages in green innovation or greenwashing in response to pressure from its business rivals&\#x27; engagement in green innovation\. Using a sample of 2958 firm‐year observations from 720 US publicly listed firms from 2010 to 2023, we find that a gap in green innovation between a focal firm and its top business rivals is significantly and positively associated with the focal firm&\#x27;s greater greenwashing but not green innovation\. This effect is more pronounced when the firm is financially constrained, operates in a non‐carbon‐intensive industry, or faces stronger product market competition\. These findings provide new insights for investors, policymakers, and scholars into the externalities, showing that rival firms&\#x27; positive environmental initiatives may generate negative spillovers when focal firms respond opportunistically\.
