# The EU&\#x27;s Indo‐Pacific Strategy and South Korea: Convergence, Perceptions, and Prospects for Strategic Cooperation

**Autores:** Sung‐Won Yoon; Sae Won Chung; Sung\-Hoon Park; Michael Reiterer

**Data:** 2026-10-13 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

ABSTRACT This article examines the evolving European Union–South Korea relationship through the framework of the EU&\#x27;s Indo‐Pacific Strategy \(IPS\)\. Drawing on interviews with policymakers, officials, and influencers, it traces six decades of bilateral ties, highlighting Korea&\#x27;s 2022 IPS and its convergence with EU priorities in digital governance, green transition, and sustainability\. Although Korean perceptions acknowledge the EU&\#x27;s normative and regulatory roles, limitations arise from the EU&\#x27;s lack of military presence and Korea&\#x27;s shifting domestic politics\. The Ukraine conflict underscores global interdependence and reshapes priorities\. The article concludes with policy recommendations to enhance European Union–Korea cooperation as pragmatic and normative middle powers\.
