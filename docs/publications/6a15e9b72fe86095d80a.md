# Development and cost\-utility analysis of a predictive model to reduce central venous catheterization in patients with shock: study design and analysis plan, amendment 1 \(Alberta 2012 to 2021, external validation in MIMIC\-IV\)

**Autores:** Jasper C Ho; Nasim Zamir; Elizabeth Munroe; Tiago V Pereira; Federico Angriman; Naheed Jivraj; Akash Goel; Nick Bosch; H\. Tom Stelfox; Hannah Wunsch; Bijan Teja

**Data:** 2026-10-17 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

This version adds the external validation in MIMIC\-IV version 3\.1: the performance of the equations deposited in the previous version, saved with their checksums before any model was fitted to MIMIC\-IV outcomes, then the recalibrated equations\. This version adds the development results, deposited before the external validation is run \(Section VII of the plan\): the availability table and the frozen list of candidate predictors \(made before any outcome was derived\), the development equations of every model with their bootstrap settings and optimism\-corrected performance, the final model and its points score, and the temporal validation within Alberta\. The equations in this version are the ones applied, unchanged, in MIMIC\-IV\. This amends the analysis plan registered on the Open Science Framework on June 23, 2025 \(https://doi\.org/10\.17605/OSF\.IO/G93JA\)\. The attached version of October 17, 2026 lists the changes and the reasons for them in its Section XII\. In summary: Data\. The model is developed in Alberta ICU data from July 2012 to December 2021: the eCritical Alberta extract of the registered plan \(2012 to 2018, 31 ICUs\) and a later extract of the same clinical information system \(2019 to 2021\)\. It is validated externally in MIMIC\-IV version 3\.1 \(Beth Israel Deaconess Medical Center, 2008 to 2022\)\. A 2012 to 2025 data cut considered in an unregistered revision \(October 1, 2026\) is not used\. Validation\. Instead of a random split, development with bootstrap internal validation in the Alberta patients; the fixed equations are deposited \(as a new version of this record\) and then validated once in MIMIC\-IV, the primary result; recalibration if needed; a temporal validation within Alberta \(2012 to 2018 to 2019 to 2021\)\. Time of prediction and population\. Every eligibility criterion is assessed 3 h after vasopressor initiation \(t3\): an infusion running at t3; no central venous catheter at vasopressor start; no ECLS; no comfort or medical\-care goals\-of\-care designation in effect; dose not already above 0\.3 mcg/kg/min; one stay per patient\. Outcome\. Acceptable peripheral delivery = maximum norepinephrine\-equivalent dose \(sustained 30 minutes\) of 0\.3 mcg/kg/min or less from t3 to 72 h; the registered outcome \(which also required 72 h or less of cumulative vasopressor infusion\) is analysed and reported beside it\. Predictors and missing data\. Predictor sets A \(dose trajectory in the first 3 h\), a middle set, B and C; availability rules fixed before the outcome is derived; complete\-case analysis with a multiple\-imputation sensitivity analysis\. Respiratory rate, pulse oximetry and serum bicarbonate are not used \(not recorded in the extracts\); invasive and non\-invasive ventilation are separate candidates\. Modelling\. Lasso logistic regression with the one\-standard\-error rule and an unpenalized refit; an unpenalized logistic comparator; one flexible lasso comparator instead of the three machine\-learning models; a numerical rule for the final model; minimum sample sizes by Riley et al\.; AUROC from continuous probabilities, calibration, net benefit\. Simulation\. Model\-informed conversion to a central catheter at 3 h when the predicted probability of an event is at or above the risk threshold \(0\.25, 0\.5 and 0\.75; the registered wording was reversed\), simulated in the base case and the highly resourced vascular access scenario of the decision model, each with and without a delay between the decision to convert and catheter placement, and compared with its three strategies, with a two\-way threshold analysis of the sensitivity and specificity a model would need\. Work done before this amendment: models using the dose trajectory only were fitted in the 2012 to 2018 extract in 2025 with a split\-sample design \(results seen; superseded\); descriptive checks were run in the 2012 to 2025 data cut, which is not used\. For this study nothing has been done in the 2019 to 2021 extract or in MIMIC\-IV; aggregate descriptions of both Alberta extracts made for other studies \(inventories, file coverage, record counts\) were seen, with no outcome of this study\. Ethics: University of Calgary Conjoint Health Research Ethics Board, REB19\-0959 \(Alberta extracts\); MIMIC\-IV under the PhysioNet Credentialed Health Data Use Agreement\.
