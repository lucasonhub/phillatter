# Stage Migration, Treatment Patterns, and Machine Learning\-Based Prediction of 12\-Month Mortality in Lung Adenocarcinoma Across the COVID\-19 Era: A SEER Population\-Based Study

**Autores:** Omer Emin Cinar; Yasemin Adalı

**Data:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Background/Objectives: The COVID\-19 pandemic coincided with widespread disruption of cancer care\. We examined changes in lung adenocarcinoma presentation and outcomes across the COVID\-19 era and whether diagnosis\-time information identifies patients at risk of early death\. Methods: Adults with microscopically confirmed lung adenocarcinoma diagnosed during 2018–2023 were identified from the Surveillance, Epidemiology, and End Results 17 Registries database and grouped into three diagnostic periods\. Logistic regression, time\-varying Cox, and Fine\-Gray models evaluated stage, metastasis, surgery, delay, and mortality\. Four machine\-learning algorithms using only diagnosis\-time variables were developed \(2018–2021; n = 87,281\) and temporally validated \(2022; n = 22,012\)\. Results: Among 133,306 patients, COVID\-19 versus pre\-COVID\-19 differences were significant but small in absolute terms: stage IV disease 47\.9% versus 47\.4% \(adjusted odds ratio \[OR\], 1\.03; 95% CI, 1\.00–1\.06\), organ\-specific metastasis 45\.8% versus 44\.4% \(OR, 1\.06; 1\.04–1\.09\), and treatment delay exceeding 90 days 11\.1% versus 10\.2% \(OR, 1\.11; 1\.06–1\.17\)\. Post\-COVID\-19 \(2022–2023\) diagnosis showed less advanced disease but substantially longer delay \(14\.5% versus 10\.2%; OR, 1\.45; 1\.39–1\.52\)\. Gradient boosting achieved a 2022 ROC AUC of 0\.840 \(0\.834–0\.845\); it flagged 46\.8% of patients and correctly flagged 81\.6% of those who died, against 44\.0% and 75\.4% for stage IV classification\. Conclusions: COVID\-era changes in stage and metastatic presentation were small in absolute terms, whereas the increase in treatment delay was larger and persisted\. A diagnosis\-time model modestly improved identification of early death beyond stage alone, flagging 28 more patients per 1000, of whom 21 died within the year\. These findings support further evaluation; external validation is required before clinical use\.
