# Independent correlates of vancomycin steady\-state trough concentration among patients undergoing combined CABG and valve surgery: observational findings for intra\-aortic balloon pump support

**Autores:** Bei\-bei Shao; Tao Feng; Ji\-cheng Jiang; Wen\-Lu Xing; 倪明; Lu\-lu Li; Chao Liu; Shu\-ping Meng; Yong\-cheng Ma; Ai\-feng Wang

**Data:** 2026-10-12 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Background Patients undergoing coronary artery bypass grafting \(CABG\) combined with valve surgery represent a high\-risk surgical population with substantial perioperative hemodynamic disturbances and highly variable organ function, leading to high variability in vancomycin pharmacokinetics\. Conventional vancomycin dosing solely on renal function frequently fails to achieve target trough concentrations in this complex subgroup\. This study aimed to identify independent correlates c of vancomycin steady\-state trough concentrations \(C ss\-min \), and to report observational findings for intra\-aortic balloon pump \(IABP\) support, to inform individualized therapeutic drug monitoring\. Methods A single\-center retrospective observational study enrolled adult patients undergoing combined CABG and valve surgery from January 2018 to October 2024\. Clinical data and vancomycin TDM records were extracted from electronic medical records\. Vancomycin C ss\-min was detected by enzyme\-multiplied immunoassay technique\. Generalized Estimating Equations \(GEE\) accounting for within\-patient repeated TDM measurements were used to identify clinical correlates of C ss\-min \. IABP support was pre\-specified and force\-entered into the multivariable model based on clinical observations and pathophysiological reasoning, independent of univariate screening results\. We performed a sensitivity analysis for patients with at least one TDM within postoperative day 7, prespecified interaction tests for effect modification, and binary logistic\-GEE for the supratherapeutic endpoint \(C ss\-min &gt; 20 mg/L\)\. A two\-tailed P &lt; 0\.05 was set as statistical significance\. Results A total of 283 eligible patients with 473 valid C ss\-min measurements were included\. Multivariate GEE demonstrated that categorical vancomycin dosing regimen, age, ventilator dependence ≥96 h, continuous renal replacement therapy \(CRRT\), IABP support, serum creatinine \(Scr\), and serum urea nitrogen \(SUN\) were positively associated with higher geometric\-mean C ss\-min \. Associations remained largely consistent in the sensitivity\-analysis subgroup of patients with at least one early\-phase TDM within postoperative day 7\. A significant CRRT\-by\-postoperative\-time interaction was detected, indicating that the influence of CRRT on vancomycin exposure varied across different postoperative phases\. Of note, the association signal linking IABP to supratherapeutic trough concentrations \(C ss\-min &gt;20 mg/L\) was modest and requires cautious interpretation\. Conclusion Multiple clinical factors jointly affect vancomycin steady\-state trough concentrations in patients undergoing combined CABG and valve surgery, including dosing regimen, age, renal biomarkers, and critical\-care supportive interventions such as IABP, CRRT and prolonged mechanical ventilation\. IABP support represents an intriguing observational signal; this finding remains hypothesis\-generating and requires prospective external validation before it can guide formal dose\-adjustment strategies\. Individualized vancomycin management for this population should comprehensively integrate hemodynamic support measures and laboratory indices rather than depend merely on renal\-function markers\. This study focuses on clinically accessible trough concentration monitoring rather than constructing complex pharmacokinetic models, which is more in line with real\-world clinical practice\.
