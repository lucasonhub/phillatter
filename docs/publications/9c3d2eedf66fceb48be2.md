# G‘AZNACHILIK BIZNES\-JARAYONLARINI SUN’IY INTELLEKT VA MASHINAVIY O‘QITISH TEXNOLOGIYALARI ASOSIDA OPTIMALLASHTIRISH

**Autores:** Kurbanov A\.

**Data:** 2026-10-13 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

This article examines the theoretical and methodological aspects of optimizing treasury business processes through artificial intelligence and machine learning technologies\. It substantiates the need for treasury digitalization to move beyond converting processes into electronic form towards the intelligent use of data\. The article proposes a decision\-support mechanism based on analyzing digital process traces through Process Mining, identifying redundant and repetitive operations, intelligently automating standard low\-risk operations, and generating forecasts using machine learning\. The author develops G\-TOP, a mechanism for the intelligent optimization of treasury business processes, and TIOI, the Treasury Intelligent Optimization Index, to evaluate its effectiveness\. The research findings demonstrate that artificial intelligence should be used in treasury operations not as a substitute for human decision\-making, but as a technology that improves the quality of decisions
