# Linking Green Innovation to sustainable performance: moderation by AI and mediation by green human capital

**Autores:** Huong Dinh Thi; Ngoc Anh Nguyen; Hong Nhung Nguyen; Kim Thoa Bui Thi; Nguyen Hong Anh

**Data:** 2026-10-14 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

This research seeks to evaluate the influence of green innovation on the sustainable performance of enterprises, while also investigating the roles of artificial intelligence \(AI\) and green human capital within this context\. Data was gathered from 646 managers employed in manufacturing firms in Vietnam and analyzed through PLS\-SEM methodology\. The findings indicate that green innovation exerts a significant and positive effect on the sustainable performance of businesses\. Additionally, AI not only has a direct effect on sustainable performance but also serves a moderating function, enhancing the impact of green innovation on sustainable development outcomes\. Concurrently, green human capital is recognized as a crucial intermediary mechanism that facilitates the conversion of green innovation initiatives into sustainable economic, social, and environmental results\. These results contribute to enriching the theoretical framework surrounding the interplay between green innovation, AI, green human capital, and sustainable performance, particularly in the context of emerging economies\. Furthermore, the study broadens and integrates the Resource\-Based View \(RBV\) theory and sustainable development theory to provide a more comprehensive understanding of how technological and human resources can be transformed into beneficial economic, social, and environmental outcomes\. From a practical standpoint, the research offers significant governance recommendations aimed at assisting manufacturing enterprises in Vietnam to enhance AI integration, cultivate green human resources, and foster green innovation, thereby improving sustainable performance and long\-term competitiveness\.
