# Cochrane, Tom: Happiness for Mortals

**Autores:** 

**Data de descoberta:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** unknown

## Descrição do feed

This book presents an original theory of well\-being which aims to make sense of the everyday ways that humans seek and verify their own happiness\. The ‘amorist’ theory is that the happy life is one organized around a pattern of successful loves\. I argue that this theory is attractive for combining insights from virtue theory, hedonism, desire satisfaction, and objective list theory\. In the first half of the book, I build up to my theory by defending my methodological naturalism, the central importance of our biological drives, and the tension between the ideals of absorption and rational detachment\. After outlining the core principles of the amorist view, in the second half of the book I refine the view by investigating the major challenges to human happiness: competition with others, the problem of evil, permanent loss, and the fear of death\. \*uploaded file is a pre\-proof of the contents list and introduction only\*
