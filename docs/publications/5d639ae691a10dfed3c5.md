# 4D percolation modeling of flexible conductive composite materials\(FCCMs\) for smart sensor applications: a Monte Carlo simulation approach

**Autores:** SangUn Kim; YeonJu Lim; Seungah Yang; Jooyong Kim

**Data:** 2026-10-12 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Abstract This study proposes a four\-dimensional \(4D\) percolation modeling approach to investigate strain\-dependent filler\-network connectivity in flexible conductive composite materials \(FCCMs\) under compressive deformation\. In this study, “4D” denotes a sequence of three\-dimensional filler configurations indexed by compressive strain, rather than a fourth Euclidean spatial dimension or physical time\. Monte Carlo simulations are used to examine how filler shape \(sphere, rod, and plate\), filler loading, particle number, aspect ratio, and Poisson’s ratio affect the formation and evolution of a z\-spanning connected network\. The primary calculated quantity is the infinite cluster ratio P∞, defined as the fraction of fillers belonging to the z\-spanning cluster\. Accordingly, P∞ is treated as a geometric connectivity metric rather than as absolute electrical conductivity\. A static phenomenological formulation is compared with the Monte Carlo connectivity transitions for representative filler geometries, while the strain\-dependent simulations illustrate the competition between axial densification and transverse expansion\. A complementary XAI\-hybrid analysis is additionally used to examine nonlinear response behavior and model\-input sensitivity, with SHAP attribution interpreted as model sensitivity rather than direct physical causality\. The proposed framework provides a geometric basis for interpreting deformation\-dependent network evolution relevant to piezoresistive FCCM sensors; quantitative prediction of current or absolute conductivity would require an additional electrical\-transport model\.
