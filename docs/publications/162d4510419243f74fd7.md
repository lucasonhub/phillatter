# The Impact of Technology Dependence on Students&\#x27; Critical Thinking Skills in Digital Learning

**Autores:** Charles Samuel Albert; Muh\. Zuhdy Hamzah; Tedy Haryadi; Saepullah Saepullah; Achsanul Fiqri

**Data:** 2026-10-15 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

This study aims to analyze the impact of technology dependence on students’ critical thinking skills in digital learning\. The study employs a library research approach by examining books, journal articles, previous studies, scientific reports, and other relevant scholarly literature\. The collected data were analyzed using content analysis to identify patterns, relationships, and significant information concerning technology dependence, critical thinking, and digital learning\. The findings indicate that technology provides substantial benefits by facilitating information access, supporting academic tasks, and improving learning efficiency\. However, excessive dependence on technology may encourage cognitive offloading, in which students delegate certain cognitive processes to technological systems\. This condition may reduce students’ direct engagement in analyzing information, evaluating evidence, drawing conclusions, and developing independent reasoning\. Therefore, technology use in digital learning should function as a supporting instrument rather than a substitute for students’ cognitive processes\. Digital learning practices emphasizing verification, reflection, source evaluation, and evidence\-based argumentation can help maintain and strengthen students’ critical thinking skills while maximizing the educational benefits of technology\.
