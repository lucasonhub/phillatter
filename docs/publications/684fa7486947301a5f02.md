# Mitigating Algorithmic Bias in Information Systems: A Comprehensive Framework for Fair Artificial Intelligence

**Autores:** Muhammad Qasim, Laraib Tabish, Falak Ali

**Data:** 2026-10-12 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

As information systems \(IS\) transition from deterministic rule\-based engines to probabilistic machine learning architectures, the risk of systematic bias and disparate impact increases significantly\. While advanced predictive models optimize across high\-stakes domains—such as credit scoring, criminal justice, human resources, and biometric authentication, they frequently perpetuate historical inequities via proxy variables even when explicit sensitive attributes are omitted\. This paper presents a detailed investigation into the theoretical foundations, mathematical formalisms, and systemic pipelines of &quot;Fair AI\.&quot; We propose an integrated sociotechnical framework spanning human perception, technological intervention, and organizational governance, fully incorporating recent advances in intelligent automation and secure architecture\.
