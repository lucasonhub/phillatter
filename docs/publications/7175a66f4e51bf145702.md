# Wang, Daolin: Why You Are Not Your Parents&\#x27; Soul: A Fragment‑Aggregation Hypothesis of Consciousness

**Autores:** 

**Data de descoberta:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** unknown

## Descrição do feed

Why do identical twins, sharing nearly identical genes and prenatal environments, often exhibit radically different temperaments, talents, and fears? This paper proposes the Consciousness Fragment Aggregation \(CFA\) hypothesis: individual consciousness is not generated solely by the brain, nor is it inherited through reincarnation\. Instead, it is assembled at birth from countless dormant &quot;consciousness fragments&quot; scattered throughout nature\. These fragments—non‑narrative, sensory imprints released by all living organisms at death—are awakened by the biological &quot;information field&quot; of a newborn\. The hypothesis offers a three‑layer model \(genetic scaffold, fragment colouring, neural integration\), explains the absence of past‑life memories, and generates three empirically testable predictions\. If falsified by future neuroscience, the hypothesis should be abandoned\.
