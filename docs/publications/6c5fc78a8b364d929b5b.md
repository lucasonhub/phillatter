# Mindful Mind: Understanding Internal Dialogue, Managing Emotions, and Finding Calm Through Communication with the Self

**Autores:** Rahma Yurliani; Rahmi Putri Rangkuti; Indri Kemala Nasution; Eka Ervika

**Data:** 2026-10-13 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

This study examines how internal dialogue functions as intrapersonal communication, how mindful awareness supports emotional regulation, and how deliberate self\-communication can contribute to psychological calm\. A non\-empirical conceptual design was employed by synthesizing theoretical propositions and empirical findings concerning self\-talk, inner speech, mindfulness, self\-compassion, cognitive reframing, emotion regulation, and breathing\-based calming practices\. The analysis organized these materials into functional relationships linking internal interpretation, emotional activation, regulatory awareness, and self\-directed action\. The findings indicate that internal dialogue can operate through supportive or critical modes, while cognitive distortions such as all\-or\-nothing thinking, catastrophizing, and mind reading can intensify emotional difficulty\. Mindful self\-communication supports regulation through emotional labeling, psychological distancing, balanced reframing, and the Pause–Reflect–Choose sequence\. Self\-compassion and reality\-based affirmations further strengthen constructive self\-relating, while breathing practices support physiological calming\. These elements were integrated into the Mindful Mind framework consisting of Notice, Name, Pause, and Reframe, supported by daily intention setting, mindful breathing, non\-judgmental review, and journaling\. The study contributes an integrated communication\-based model for reflective learning, emotional literacy, and everyday self\-regulation while recognizing that severe psychological distress requires appropriate professional support\. The framework positions calm not as the absence of difficult emotion, but as regulated engagement through aware, constructive, and deliberate self\-response\.
