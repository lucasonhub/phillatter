# Safety and Efficacy of Lutein and Zeaxanthin in the AREDS2 Formulation for Age\-Related Macular Degeneration

**Autores:** Haryo Dimasto Kristiyanto; Amaze Grace Sira; Yosafat Reza Herlambang Wibisana; Haryadi Aristo Tamma; Made Anom Danuartha

**Data:** 2026-10-15 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Age\-related macular degeneration \(AMD\) is a progressive degenerative disease of the central retina and is a leading cause of vision loss among older adults worldwide\. The original Age\-Related Eye Disease Study \(AREDS\) supplement formula reduced the risk of progression to advanced AMD\. However, the beta\-carotene in this formula was associated with a higher risk of lung cancer in current and former smokers\. AREDS2 evaluated lutein and zeaxanthin as safer substitutes for beta\-carotene\. These macular carotenoids have antioxidant effects and a better safety profile\. Studies show that lutein and zeaxanthin help slow AMD progression through antioxidant effects and retinoprotective mechanisms\. Researchers have also studied genetic factors, including complement factor H \(CFH\) and age\-related maculopathy susceptibility 2 \(ARMS2\)\. However, current evidence is not strong enough to support individualized treatment based on these factors\. Overall, lutein and zeaxanthin appear to provide a favorable balance of efficacy and safety as replacements for beta\-carotene in the AREDS2 formulation\.
