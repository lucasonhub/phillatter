# Substantiation of a multi\-sensor automated system for the protection of categorized facilities under conditions of mass riots

**Autores:** F\.B\.Achilov; A\.A\.Jalolov

**Data:** 2026-10-10 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

The article presents the scientific and technical foundations of a multi\-sensor intelligent automated protection system designed to ensure the security of categorized facilities under conditions of mass riots\. An algorithm is proposed for early threat detection, threat level assessment, automatic control of protective mechanisms and prompt notification of responsible personnel through the integration of acoustic, vibration, infrared and video surveillance means\. The experimental studies substantiate that the automated system reduces response time compared with a conventional system and increases operational speed efficiency\. In addition, the reliability, stability and economic efficiency of the system are evaluated, and the possibilities of its application for the intelligent technical protection of categorized facilities are substantiated\.
