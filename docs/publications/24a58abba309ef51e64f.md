# The Main Issues of Choosing Effective Concrete Curing Methods and Providing Information on their Implementation

**Autores:** Armine Ghulyan; Robert Noramiryan; Khachik Kirakosyan; Artur Manukyan

**Data:** 2026-10-14 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Improving the concrete curing and reinforced concrete structures has an important meaning in ensuring their physical and mechanical properties by design\. Concrete curing measures aimed at controlling temperature and humidity ensure that sufficient water is available for concrete to achieve its desired properties\. The advantages and disadvantages of existing methods of concrete curing in the climatic conditions of the Republic of Armenia are presented\. Based on statistical analyses and scientific experiments, effective concrete curing schemes, technological process schedules, and technical means of automation are proposed
