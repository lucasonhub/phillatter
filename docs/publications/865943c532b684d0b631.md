# Constrained Multi\-Agent Reinforcement Learning in Imagination

**Autores:** Raz Shmueli; Shahaf S\. Shperberg

**Data:** 2026-10-15 · **Tipo:** preprint

## Resumo

Predicting cost in a multi\-agent world model moves most constrained policy updates into imagination: the stronger model\-free baseline needs $10$ to $20$ times CA\-MAMBA&\#x27;s interactions on the median SMAC configuration, and CA\-MAMBA earns $2\.8$ to $12$ times the baselines&\#x27; MAMuJoCo reward at equal budget\. In controlled tests on one task per benchmark, pricing the model&\#x27;s cost advantages at parity more than halves a cost\-blind learner&\#x27;s cost and meets a tight continuous\-control limit the learned multiplier misses, suggesting the model&\#x27;s cost estimates are not the bottleneck\.
