# Silin, Valerii: Embodied Biographical Alignment \(EBA\) v0\.2\.2: Integration ≠ Retrieval?

**Autores:** 

**Data de descoberta:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** unknown

## Descrição do feed

\_Ssrn\_\. 2026Embodied Biographical Alignment \(EBA\) investigates whether the integration of multiple, finite, developmentally structured biographies can be used as a source of alignment\-relevant learning for an artificial system\. This note treats existing studies not as tests of EBA, but as sources of experimental results, methods, and constructions that intersect with particular components of its research question\. Work on knowledge distillation, representation and policy transfer, path dependence, sequential reinforcement learning, continual learning, and training\-state transfer shows that artificial systems can transfer more than final outputs; that similar performance does not guarantee matching internal mechanisms or represented information; that training history can leave persistent internal differences and influence subsequent learning and generalization; and that some causally relevant consequences of history can be retained in identifiable components of training state and transferred between systems\. For EBA, these results form a map of existing experimental possibilities\. On this basis, the note introduces an operational question of developmental equivalence: is reproducing a system&\#x27;s current functional state sufficient, or does reproducing its subsequent learning, adaptation, and generalization require preserving additional causally relevant consequences of its developmental history? \(direct link \)
