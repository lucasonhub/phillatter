# The Interplay of Copyright and Personhood Rights in the Age of Digital Creation

**Autores:** Ms\. Shreya Goswami; Apoorva Dixit

**Data:** 2026-10-12 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

The rapid advancement of digital technologies has transformed the creation, distribution, and protection of intellectual property, raising important questions about the relationship between copyright law and personhood rights\. Traditionally, copyright has focused on safeguarding the economic interests of creators; however, in the digital environment, creative works are increasingly intertwined with personal identity, dignity, reputation, and self\-expression\. This paper examines the interplay between copyright and personhood rights in the age of digital creation, emphasizing the role of moral rights, particularly the rights of attribution and integrity, in protecting creators’ personal and professional identities\. It explores key challenges arising from unauthorized reproduction, digital remixing, data mining, privacy violations, cross\-jurisdictional enforcement, artificial intelligence \(AI\)\-generated content, and deepfakes\. The paper further examines the limitations of existing legal frameworks in addressing the complex relationship between creative ownership and individual identity in an increasingly interconnected digital landscape\.\.\.\.
