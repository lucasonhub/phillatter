# Machine learning uncovers previously undetectable genetic deletions in cancer panel sequencing

**Autores:** Irina A\. Kerle; Oleksandr Husak; Thomas Gross; Andreas Hartig; Rebecca Prause; Daniela Richter; Sascha Brückmann; Maximilian Werner; Anja Kögler; Dorothea Hanf; Lino Möhrmann; Doreen William; Karsten Wendt; Nicole Pfarr; Carolin Mogler; Daniela Ellen Aust; Evelin Schröck; Christoph Heining; Hanno Glimm

**Data:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Missed genetic deletions can prevent cancer patients from receiving effective targeted therapy\. Standard pipelines, such as TSO500, use bin count averaging for copy number variation detection but often overlook focal events\. To address this, we developed a machine learning \(ML\) pipeline for deletion detection and zygosity evaluation within the MASTERsg precision oncology program\. We focused on the tumor suppressor gene PTEN, whose loss confers sensitivity to mTOR/Akt inhibition\. An Extreme Gradient Boosting model trained on PTEN\-deleted and wild\-type tumor samples identified focal deletions missed by the TSO500 pipeline and CNVkit that were associated with concordant PTEN protein loss by immunohistochemistry\. Importantly, the model detected PTEN deletions in 33/204 profiled samples that were classified as PTEN wild\-type by TSO500; 75% \(15/20\) of stainable cases showed absent or reduced PTEN protein expression\. These findings demonstrate the potential of ML to enhance panel sequencing, uncovering therapeutically relevant alterations missed by established diagnostic methods\.
