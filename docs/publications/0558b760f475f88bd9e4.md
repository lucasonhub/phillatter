# Bannan, John: THE PHILOSOPHY OF SUPERDETERMINISM ON HUMAN CONSCIOUSNESS

**Autores:** 

**Data de descoberta:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** unknown

## Descrição do feed

The philosophy of superdeterminism is based on a single scientific fact about the universe, namely that cause and effect \(and probabilistic causation\) in physics are not real\. In 2020, accomplished Swedish theoretical physicist, Dr\. Johan Hansson proved by applying Albert Einstein’s Theory of Special Relativity to what has already been scientifically verified about spin measurement correlations observed in entangled particle pairs that cause and effect \(and probabilistic causation\) in physics are not real\. The lack of cause and effect in physics means that we live in a predetermined static block universe, where nothing actually moves\. Consequently, our conscious experience of movement must be a predetermined illusion created by our brain particles over the static dimension of time\. The foundation of the universe is logic as evidenced by the logic of the mathematical laws of physics that describe our universe\. Consciousness being an aspect of the physical block universe is also an exhibition of logic, but a logic of the possible as opposed to particle logic\. Consciousness is the reference identity from which things come into and go out of existence under the logic of the possible\. Qualia are likewise aspects of this logic of the possible\. While a particle and human consciousness appear distinct and incompatible, they both are natural realities exhibiting different forms of logic within the same superdeterministic universe\.
