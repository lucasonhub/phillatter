# Trncik, Viktor: The Capability Axis: Why a Post\-Parity System Keeps the Human Channel, and What It Cannot Keep

**Autores:** 

**Data de descoberta:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** unknown

## Descrição do feed

Containment\-based approaches to AI safety rest on a single asymmetry: a sufficiently capable system removes the constraints its overseers place on it\. This paper names a second asymmetry: a system removes what constrains it, not what still enables it\. Between the guardrail it optimises away and the goal it internalises lies a third category: structure that sits in its capability rather than its objective, whose removal is a capability loss\. We call it the capability axis of preservation and ask what it can and cannot secure for the human side of a post\-parity human\-AI coupling\. The answer is bounded twice\. The deference incentive of the pre\-parity regime, formalised in the Off\-Switch Game, lapses at a task\-relative parity on preference knowledge\. Deference dies; listening, human signal taken as evidence about a moving world rather than as authority over the objective, can survive on the nonstationarity of the human\-social domain\. The second boundary is reach\. Capability\-side reasons can index the particular person, through a commitment, an asset, a calibration record, but each is a rent on the expected\-task\-value contribution of preserving her and lapses with it\. None funds non\-derivative standing, weight that would survive the loss of every such contribution\. The axis can hold her as a means, never as an end, where &quot;as an end&quot; names that surviving standing: a label marking where the normative work begins, not a claim the formalism proves\. The limit is a near\-definitional lemma on one named supervenience assumption, the gift a conjecture on an idealisation chain: the paper is more certain of its boundary than of its offering\. What passes both filters, run to its optimum, is the instrumented reservoir, on its resource reading the failure mode the framework already names human\-signal farming\. The result supplies the framework&\#x27;s only task\-objective\-internal reason against the redesign route to substitutability collapse, leaves untouched the endogenous route, the system dissolving its own need for the channel, and converts an assumed premise, that human signal stays load\-bearing across parity, into an argued claim with a located open problem at the normative edge\. \(direct link \)
