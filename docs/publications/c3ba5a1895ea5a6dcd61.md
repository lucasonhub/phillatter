# Each Reward to Its Relevant Tokens: Reward\-Specific Token\-Level Credit Assignment for Multi\-Reward RL

**Autores:** Skylar Zhai; Haonan Wang; Zhining Liu; Linxin Song; Xuwei Ding; Jingcheng Liang; Jian Kang

**Data:** 2026-10-15 · **Tipo:** preprint

## Resumo

Language models are increasingly trained against several verifiable rewards at once, such as the correctness and the format of a tool call, or the individual constraints of a complex instruction\. Reward\-decoupled normalization \(GDPO\) normalizes each reward separately so that different reward combinations keep distinct advantages, but it still spreads every reward&\#x27;s advantage evenly over the whole response, although each verifier checks only a few positions\. We propose MRTPO, which assigns the advantage of each reward to the tokens relevant to it, using the information its verifier checks, without auxiliary models or token\-level annotations\. For each reward, the policy replays its own response with and without that reward&\#x27;s verifier information, and the per\-token Jensen\-Shannon distance between the two next\-token distributions measures each token&\#x27;s relevance and reassigns the advantage\. On text\-to\-SQL, multi\-constraint instruction following and tool calling, MRTPO converges faster and achieves better performance than the compared methods\.
