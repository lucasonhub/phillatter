# Politics as a Principal Instrument of Social Change

**Autores:** Anokwuru  PhD Rev Fr Christian Uche

**Data:** 2026-10-14 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

This paper examines politics as the primary instrument of social transformation and human advancement\. It argues that political participation is an essential aspect of human existence and collective development\. Drawing on the philosophical insights of Aristotle, John Locke, and Hannah Arendt, the study contends that politics is the framework through which societies translate ethical ideals into structures that sustain justice and order\. It further highlights how the deliberate engagement of citizens, especially young people, is central to the renewal of governance and the pursuit of social equity\. The discussion integrates classical thought with contemporary realities, showing how technology and civic awareness expand the possibilities of democratic participation\. By situating politics within the broader discourse of moral responsibility and social progress, the paper concludes that the true measure of any political system lies in its capacity to cultivate integrity, accountability, and community welfare\. Politics, therefore, remains the most vital means through which humanity organizes itself toward sustainable and inclusive development\.
