# A knowledge\-based fault diagnosis library for air handling units with diagnostic Bayesian networks in commercial buildings

**Autores:** Karzan Mohammed; Wei Luo; Zoltán Nagy; Rick P\. Kramer

**Data:** 2026-10-09 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Diagnostic Bayesian Networks \(DBNs\) offer a practical trade\-off for Air Handling Unit \(AHU\) fault diagnosis, but existing studies rely on limited expert input or single building experiments and rarely publish their model parameters\. This study developed a framework for building a Knowledge\-Based AHU Fault Diagnosis Library through semi\-structured interviews with 17 Heating, Ventilation, and Air Conditioning professionals from three companies\. The library provides the structure and parameters to develop a DBN for AHU fault diagnosis in commercial buildings\. In a case study, a Knowledge\-Based DBN \(KDBN\) derived from the library was compared against a Literature\-Based DBN \(LDBN\), parameterized from published literature, and a Data\-Driven DBN \(DDBN\) serving as an internal benchmark, parameterized from Building Management System data\. The KDBN identified reference faults within the top two ranked faults for six of seven symptoms without prior calibration, demonstrating acceptable diagnostic performance\. The LDBN identified reference faults for only three of seven symptoms, suggesting that direct parameter adoption from published literature is insufficient\. The DDBN achieved top two identification for all seven symptoms, though its parameters were derived from the same dataset used to define the Experimental Benchmark\. Sensitivity analysis confirmed that uniform prior probability scaling has a limited influence on diagnostic rankings, while variable prior probabilities have a greater influence\. These findings suggest that a KDBN derived from the library shows promising agreement with the Experimental Benchmark, and that the library may provide a useful starting point for DBN\-based AHU fault diagnosis when labeled, building\-specific fault data are unavailable\.
