# Effect of NAC and vitamin D on pulmonary hypoplasia in rats with nitrofen\-induced congenital diaphragmatic hernia

**Autores:** Nergül Çördük; Seniha Esin Söğüt; Ferda Bir; Gülçin Abban Mete

**Data:** 2026-10-10 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Purpose: This study investigated the effects of N\-acetyl\-cysteine \(NAC\) and vitamin D, separately and in combination, on pulmonary hypoplasia in rats with nitrofen\-induced congenital diaphragmatic hernia \(CDH\)\. Materials and methods: Pregnant Sprague\-Dawley rats were divided into 5 groups: control \(C\), nitrofen \(N\), N\+NAC, N\+VitD, and N\+NAC\+VitD\. Rats, except group C, received 200 mg nitrofen on day 9\.5 of gestation\. NAC and vitamin D were administered for four days starting on day 16\. The lungs of rats born on day 21\-22 were dissected and examined morphologically, histopathologically, and immunohistochemically \(transforming growth factor β1 \(TGF\-β1\) and surfactant protein A \(SP\-A\)\)\. Results: Lung/body weight was significantly lower in group N than in group C\. The pulmonary epithelial type was mostly mature in the treatment groups and immature in group N \(p&lt;0,05\)\. Alveolar thickness was significantly increased in group N compared to the others\. TGF\-β1 expression was significantly increased while SP\-A was decreased in group N compared to the others\. SP\-A expression was significantly increased in groups C and N\+NAC\+VitD compared to groups N\+NAC and N\+VitD\. Conclusion: Vitamin D and NAC reduced pulmonary hypoplasia in rats with CDH\. Although increased SP\-A expression suggests that their combined use is more effective, other results do not support this\.
