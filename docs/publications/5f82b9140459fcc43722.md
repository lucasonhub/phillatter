# Effect of discharge planning rooted in the integrated Care for Older People Concept on the maintenance of intrinsic capacity in older adults

**Autores:** Danni Zheng; Ren Xu; Shuhong Cai; Danfeng Pang; Xuemin Zhang; Yunxuan Zhu; 赵敏

**Data:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Objective To assess how integrated care for older people \(ICOPE\)\-based discharge planning \(DP\) affects older patients’ intrinsic capacity \(IC\)\. Methods Between December 2023 and June 2024, 112 geriatric inpatients were randomly assigned to control \(routine care\) or Experimental \(routine care \+ ICOPE discharge services\) groups\. Intrinsic capacity domains were evaluated using the Short Physical Performance Battery, Saint Louis University Mental Status, Geriatric Depression Scale\-Short Form and Mini Nutritional Assessment\-Short Form tools and self\-report at discharge and 3 months post\-discharge\. Analysis of covariance and binary logistic regression were used, with baseline scores, living status and gender as covariates\. The primary analysis was supplemented by a sensitivity analysis using continuous domain scores as separate outcomes to verify the robustness of the composite score findings\. Results 108 patients completed follow\-up\. Intrinsic capacity decline was lower in the experimental group \(1\.2 ± 0\.2\) than in the control group \(3\.1 ± 0\.3; mean difference: –1\.9, 95% CI: −2\.5 to −1\.3, p &lt; 0\.001\)\. Motor, cognitive, psychological and nutritional domains improved more in the Experimental group \(all p &lt; 0\.001\); sensory function showed no difference \( p = 0\.267\)\. Conclusion The ICOPE\-based DP approach slows IC decline and enhances multiple functional domains, offering a valuable reference for transitional care for older adults\.
