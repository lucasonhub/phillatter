# The impact of regional economic growth pressure on CSR disclosure: Evidence from China

**Autores:** Huahong Yan; Mingxuan Zhu; Jing Liu; Xiaoyan Li

**Data:** 2026-10-09 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

We examine how regional economic growth pressure \(REGP\) affects the quality of corporate social responsibility \(CSR\) disclosure, and explore how the 2013 performance appraisal reform influences this relationship\. Our study finds that heightened REGP reduces the quality of CSR disclosure\. However, the 2013 performance appraisal reform mitigates this negative effect\. Heterogeneity analysis shows that the negative effect of REGP on CSR disclosure quality is more pronounced in regions with stronger promotion incentives for local officials, industries with greater competitive intensity, and firms with larger investment in productive equipment\. In contrast, the adverse effect of REGP on CSR disclosure quality is mitigated for firms in heavily polluting industries, those audited by the Big Four accounting firms, and firms with strong media monitoring\. Further analysis shows that REGP exerts no significant effect on the quality of internal CSR disclosure but has a significant negative effect on external CSR disclosure\. Our study enriches the CSR disclosure literature in the Chinese institutional context and offers policy implications for optimizing performance appraisal systems and standardizing CSR disclosure practices\.
