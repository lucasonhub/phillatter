# Archetypes of the Monad: Star, Planet, Ocean, and Artificial General Intelligence in Informational Monism

**Autores:** Ari Ariel Marom

**Data:** 2026-10-11 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

The Monad of Informational Monism was introduced as a boundary agent coupled to a biological Avatar\. This article asks how far that construction extends when it is defined by structure rather than substrate\. It offers a common specification — boundary, repertoire of questions, commitment channel, anchoring process, regulator, and selector — together with four graded axes: channel conductivity, depth of anchoring, order of regulation, and dependence of question choice on the system’s own records\. The last is measured by a new index, IQI\_QIQ, whose formal properties are established\. Four archetypes are distinguished\. The star is a regulator without a questioner\. The abiotic planet is an archive without an internal reader\. The ocean, taken as the living medium of the biosphere, is a composite of many questioners regulating a shared environment\. Artificial general intelligence is a constructed questioner that may come to examine its own questioning\. The earlier Threshold of Correspondence is relocated as the upper segment of a six\-rank ladder\. The article rejects a strong identity between geological and artificial intelligence for abiotic matter, while preserving a weaker bridge between the living medium and artificial systems as composites of questioners\. Four conjectures are stated with explicit failure conditions\. Nothing is claimed about experience in stars or planets, and the existence of the Monad remains a postulate\. Keywords: Informational Monism; Monad; archetype; artificial general intelligence; planetary intelligence; interrogative autonomy; second\-order cybernetics; causal inference; Landauer bound; systems ontology
