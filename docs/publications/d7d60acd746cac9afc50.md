# A Graph\-Theoretic Encryption Framework: Laplacian Spectra And Chromatic SK Keys

**Autores:** R\. Rajambigai; J\. Ravi Sankar; T\. Praveen

**Data:** 2026-10-15 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Cryptography plays a critical role in securing digital communication by guarding data from unwanted access and cyber threats\. This demand drives the development of creative and mathematically sound encryption methods\. Various mathematical encryption techniques, especially using graph theory, have been introduced\. In this paper, chromatic topological SK indices have been used for encryption methods for an improved data transfer security\.
