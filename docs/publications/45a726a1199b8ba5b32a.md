# 二\.A Primitive Theory of Next\-Gen General Intelligence Based on Global Mathematical Verification Closed\-Loop

**Autores:** tiantai chen

**Data:** 2026-10-15 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Existing Transformer and large model theoretical systems uniformly adopt the ”prediction\-first”paradigm, defining intelligence as ”fitting the data distribution of the real world and next\-tokenprediction”\. This theory overturns conventional understanding and proposes the unique primitiveof intelligence: a continuous, multi\-layered, global closed\-loop for mathematical verification andchecking\.All artificial intelligence behaviors \(fitting, reasoning, association, generation, generalization, er\-ror correction, cognition\) are essentially mathematical comparison, constraint checking, differentialstability verification, and vector structure matching across different dimensions\.All low\-level operations of the traditional Transformer \(vector embedding, QKV dot\-product at\-tention, gradient backpropagation, distribution fitting\) are not ”predictive behaviors”, but nativechecking operations at the statistical level\. The root causes of large model hallucinations, logicalfailures and generalization defects lie in the sole possession of statistical distribution checking, lack\-ing rigorous mathematical checking of axiomatic structures, differential constraints and geometrictopology\.This theory constructs a ”dual\-layer unified checking intelligence system”, completing a funda\-mental paradigm revolution from traditional probability\-fitting intelligence to global mathematical\-verification intelligence\. It can directly guide the design of native intelligent models with no hallu\-cinations, strong logic, self\-correction capability and ontological cognition for the next generation\.
