# The Gendered Voice of AI: Literary Representations and Digital Patriarchy

**Autores:** Manisha

**Data:** 2026-10-13 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

It is unsettling that most of the world’s leading AI assistants speak in a female voice: Siri, Alexa, Cortana, three names with an easy, almost slick, effortless fluidity\. This paper asks why this is so and what it reveals about the many\-sided connection among gender, technology and power\. The argument is not that AI was “feminised” out of necessity or for commercial appeal, although both play a part\. It is rather that the gendered voice of AI displays the continuity of patriarchal logic in the digital space, a logic that literature and popular culture have rehearsed for more than a hundred years\. Through an analysis of literary writings, films and contemporary design philosophies for digital assistive devices, the paper traces the origins of machine femininity and questions the assumptions lurking within it\. The discussion spans four interrelated landscapes: gendered machines in the literary and cinematic imagination; the anthropomorphic impulse that leads designers to give their technologies a human face and a feminine voice; the repression of female voice and agency in these technologies; and the wider culture of mediating technologies in which these moves are normalised\. The goal is not to offer a technical solution but to open a space for critical thinking about how images of compliant women might be displaced from our most advanced technologies\.
