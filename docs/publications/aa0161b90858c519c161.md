# Development and application of a smartphone\-based thermal contrast amplification reader for photothermal lateral flow immunoassays

**Autores:** Jiajie Liang; Peijun Teng; Lei Wu; Wanli Liang; 宋其芳; Guanbo He; Yanqiang Hao; Haorong He; Yaqi Wang; Yong He; Xiaoli Zhang; Xiangming Peng; Wei Xiao; Bing Zhu; Yong Tang; Daxiang Cui; Jianfu Zhao

**Data:** 2026-10-10 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

The high cost and poor portability of external infrared thermal imagers have limited the widespread adoption of photothermal lateral flow immunoassays \(PT\-LFIAs\) in on\-site testing\. To address this, we developed a smartphone\-based handheld Thermal Contrast Amplification \(TCA\) Reader, eliminating the need for costly external hardware\. Compared with existing recent external thermal imager\-based PT\-LFIA readout, this reader integrates a selected thermal\-sensor\-equipped smartphone, a motorized laser module, Bluetooth\-enabled control, and a fixed 3D\-printed holder into a compact device\. The reader enables automated thermal signal acquisition with a same\-strip measurement CV below 1% and supports multiple PT\-LFIA formats\. Using Au@Ag core\-shell nanoparticles \(Au@Ag NPs\) as reliable photothermal labels, the handheld TCA reader\-based PT\-LFIAs \(hT\-PT\-LFIAs\) offers higher sensitivity compared to conventional LFIAs\. The hT\-PT\-LFIAs can be used for the multiplex detection of viral antigens \(Influenza A/B, SARS\-CoV\-2\), small molecule drugs \(morphine, methamphetamine, ketamine\) and nucleic acids \(HPV 16/18\), demonstrating versatility across various LFIA formats, good clinical performance in training cohort, and high repeatability for small molecule assay\. By seamlessly integrating micro\-engineering, nanotechnology, and smartphone\-based sensing, this work provides a low\-cost, portable, and broadly applicable PT\-LFIAs detection strategy that bridges a critical technological gap\.
