# 6G Evolution Towards Connected Intelligent Machines

**Autores:** Dejan Vukobratović

**Data:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

The evolution towards 6G raises a fundamental question: will future mobile networks connect machines more efficiently, or will they become an integral part of intelligent systems? This overview paper explores this question through the concept of task\-oriented communications, which shifts the focus from faithfully transmitting data towards delivering the information needed to accomplish a task\. We illustrate these ideas through two connected\-robotics scenarios investigated in our research: semantic communication of 3D point clouds for industrial object recognition, and edge\-based robotic localisation and mapping supported by 5G/6G connectivity\. These examples highlight how communication, computation and sensing can be designed together to support potentially the fundamental role of 6G technology, that is to connect intelligent machines\.
