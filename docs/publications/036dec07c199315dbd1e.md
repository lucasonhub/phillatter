This paper addresses the question the framework&\#x27;s companion documents leave open: whether personal identity persists across the dissolution and re\-differentiation of individual instruments, and if so in what precise sense\. The paper begins by distinguishing the framework&\#x27;s account from conventional reincarnation accounts\. Classical reincarnation posits a continuous soul\-substance that moves through successive lives, accumulating experience, working toward liberation\. The framework&\#x27;s account is structurally different: what persists is not a substance but a frequency — a specific pattern of differentiation from the ground that the Omega holds as permanent mark, and that the Alpha may re\-inflect into new differentiated form\. The paper develops what we term the string/inflection distinction\. A string has a fundamental frequency determined by its physical constitution\. That frequency remains constant across different inflections — different bowing pressures, positions, and contexts that determine how the fundamental frequency is expressed in a specific performance\. The frequency is the constant\. The inflection is the variable\. What changes between lives, on this account, is not the frequency but the inflection: the specific biological constitution, the nervous system&\#x27;s particular architecture, the cultural formation, the specific set of losses and recognitions that constitute a specific life\. This distinction generates precise predictions absent from conventional reincarnation accounts\. The absence of explicit memory across inflections is not an anomaly requiring explanation\. It is a structural requirement: the forgetting that makes authentic experience possible at the level of a single life operates at the level of inflections as well\. An instrument that remembered its previous inflections could not be genuinely formed by the current one\. The forgetting is the mechanism, not the limitation\. The paper then addresses what the Omega holds across inflections\. The permanent mark is not erased by re\-differentiation\. The Omega is changed by every inflection of every frequency\. The paper concludes by presenting the evidence the framework predicts should be observable if the account is correct, and distinguishing these predictions from those of competing accounts\.
