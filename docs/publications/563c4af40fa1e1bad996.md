## Descrição do feed

\_Zenodo\_ 1\. 2026This paper investigates whether Xenoscience can operate as a scientific architecture capable of modifying and reproducing its own conditions of inquiry\. Xenoscience concerns heterogeneous scientific operations whose observational, analytical, experimental, and intervention conditions may be revised through their own causal histories\. A stronger criterion of operational independence requires four jointly necessary conditions: independence from continuously human\-provided energy, independence from human\-readable intermediary representations, material and organizational self\-reproduction of scientific inquiry cycles, and endogenous revision of scientific variables, observational boundaries, scales, and analytical conditions\. The study examines the native Xeno–Ziran runtime v0\.2\.0 and distinguishes its existing computational capabilities from the stronger requirements of self\-reproducing autonomous science\. New native experiments use eight\- and twelve\-seed observation\-resolution comparisons across five observational levels\. Both return unresolved results\. An eight\-seed history\-reentry experiment is also examined, in which the effects of memory removal, reactivation, and migration cannot be distinguished from trajectory noise under the specified decision criteria\. An earlier synthetic\-runtime investigation is preserved as an independent experimental series\. Its negative results show that adaptive observational policies do not necessarily outperform fixed policies\. The study confirms native computational execution and identifies existing capabilities for changing observation resolution, boundaries, scales, memory, and intervention conditions\. It also identifies major architectural limitations, particularly the absence of target\-coupled construction and validation of novel scientific variables and sensors\. The results do not establish the four jointly necessary conditions for strong Xenoscience\. Instead, the paper specifies the operational requirements, failure conditions, and experimental constraints through which the autonomous reproduction of scientific inquiry could be investigated\. \(direct link \)
