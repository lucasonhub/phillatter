# Innovative Educational Technologies in Improving the System of Higher Education

**Autores:** Zh\.T\. Kadenova; K\. Kydyralikyzy

**Data:** 2026-10-15 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

The article examines modern trends in the development of higher education in the context of digital transformation of the educational environment\. Particular attention is paid to the implementation of innovative educational technologies as an important factor in improving the quality of education\. The possibilities of applying modern educational technologies in teaching foreign languages in inclusive education are analyzed\. The article also presents practical experience in the use of digital technologies in the educational process of the Kyrgyz Uzbek International University named after B\. Sydykov\. It is concluded that the integration of innovative pedagogical methods and digital technologies contributes to improving the efficiency of the educational process and developing students’ professional competencies\.
