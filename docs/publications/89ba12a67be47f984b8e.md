# The Impact of ESG Ratings on Corporate Value

**Autores:** Yunhan Hu

**Data:** 2026-10-14 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

A company’s environmental, social and governance performance serves as a key indicator of its sus\-tainability and plays a pivotal role in its development\. However, as ESG ratings vary across different rating agencies, this inconsistency diminishes the informational value of ESG data and consequently reduces corporate value\. This study examines the impact of ESG rating discrepancies on the corporate value of Chinese listed companies between 2015 and 2024, and analyses the mechanisms through which such discrepancies affect corporate value\. Empirical results indicate that differences in ESG ratings have a sig\-nificant suppressing effect on corporate value, with the mechanism being short\-sighted behaviour on the part of management\. Heterogeneity tests reveal that, in listed companies with poor governance capabilities among management, the negative impact of ESG rating discrepancies on corporate value is more pro\-nounced\. The findings of this study offer valuable insights for enterprises seeking to improve their ESG evaluation systems and enhance corporate value\.
