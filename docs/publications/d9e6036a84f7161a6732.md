# Marčeta, Momir: On Karma: A Hypothesis on Experiential Continuity, Microconsciousness, and Reincarnation

**Autores:** 

**Data de descoberta:** 2026-10-19 · **Tipo:** unknown

## Descrição do feed

On Karma presents a philosophical hypothesis concerning karma, experiential continuity, microconsciousness, and reincarnation\. It proposes that what continues beyond an individual life is experience rather than personal identity\. Karma is understood as an automatic, self\-regulating process through which experiential traces contribute to the formation of a new life configuration, rather than as a system of moral reward and punishment\. The book explores the relationship between microconsciousness and macroconsciousness, the emergence of ego and identification, and the possibility of liberation through the ending of identification\. It also examines responsibility without interpreting suffering as deserved punishment\. The model is offered as a philosophical framework for inquiry, rather than an established scientific theory\.
