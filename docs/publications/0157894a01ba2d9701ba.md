# BaniRostam, Touraj: Mind Concepts: Key Ideas and Connections in Mind, Cognition &amp; AI

**Autores:** 

**Data de descoberta:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** unknown

## Descrição do feed

Mind, cognition, intelligence, understanding, intentionality, agency, and consciousness are central concepts in philosophy of mind, cognitive science, and artificial intelligence\. Although closely related, they are not interchangeable\. Overlooking their differences can create conceptual confusion, particularly when assessing the capacities of artificial systems\. This paper examines 60 anchor concepts organized into 14 interconnected clusters using an educational approach termed analytical conceptual mapping\. For each cluster, it defines key concepts, provides illustrative examples, distinguishes related terms, and traces connections across philosophical and scientific perspectives\. The discussion focuses on several important relationships: learning, knowledge, and understanding; representation, meaning, and symbol grounding; attention, metacognition, and self\-awareness; and functional organization, computation, and mental realization\. It also distinguishes operational descriptions of artificial cognitive capacities from philosophical claims about mental states and subjective experience\. Behavioral or functional similarities alone do not establish equivalent mental capacities, but differences in physical structure do not necessarily rule out their possibility\. The paper does not propose a new theory of mind or attempt to settle debates about artificial consciousness\. Instead, it offers an educational framework, accompanied by a visual map of selected relationships among the clusters, to clarify conceptual distinctions and connections\. The aim is to support interdisciplinary learning and encourage more careful assessment of claims about cognition and artificial intelligence\.
