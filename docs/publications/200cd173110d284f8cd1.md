# Bridging the Gap: What Sports Media Hiring Managers Actually Want from Journalism School Graduates

**Autores:** Adam Pitluk; Brian A\. Petrotta; Jason Stamm

**Data:** 2026-10-10 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

The sports media industry has undergone seismic structural transformation over the past two decades, driven by the collapse of legacy newspaper models, the proliferation of digital platforms, and the emergence of artificial intelligence\. This qualitative study examines what sports media hiring managers and editors actually require from recent journalism school graduates as they enter a rapidly evolving workforce\. Drawing on semi\-structured, in\-depth interviews with 15 sports media professionals who self\-identified as editors and/or publishers and who are affiliated with the Football Writers Association of America \(FWAA\) and the U\.S\. Basketball Writers Association \(USBWA\), this study employs a human capital theory framework to analyze the alignment, and the persistent misalignment, between what journalism programs produce and what the industry demands\. Findings reveal four dominant themes: \(1\) passion and intrinsic motivation as non\-negotiable hiring criteria; \(2\) the depreciation of formal credentials relative to demonstrated, verifiable work; \(3\) a widening deficit in source cultivation, relationship building, and reporting fundamentals among recent graduates; and \(4\) a growing demand for multimedia versatility and platform fluency\. Implications for journalism education curricula, industry\-academy collaboration, and the professional preparation of the next generation of sports media workers are discussed\.
