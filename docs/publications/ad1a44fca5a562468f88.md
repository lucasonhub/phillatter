# Pyrolysis of Pinus roxburghii: process optimization, product characterization and scale\-up potential

**Autores:** Madhuka Roy; Sayon Chakravarty; Brij Kishore; Krishnendu Kundu

**Data:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

The growing accumulation of Pinus roxburghii needles in the Indian Himalayan region presents both a significant fire hazard and an underutilized biomass resource\. This study investigates the conversion of pine needles into bio\-oil and biochar through bench\-scale pyrolysis, with process optimization using Response Surface Methodology \(RSM\)\. In contrast to previous studies largely limited to laboratory\-scale investigations, this work demonstrates the scale\-up potential of pine needle pyrolysis and provides data relevant to its practical deployment\. Product characterization confirmed the successful conversion of pine needles into value\-added products, with biochar exhibiting enhanced carbon\-rich properties and bio\-oil containing diverse organic compounds\. The findings not only validate RSM as a robust optimization tool for scale\-up but also highlight the potential of pine needle pyrolysis products for renewable energy, carbon sequestration and environmental applications\. By converting a fire\-prone forest residue into valuable bioenergy products, this study contributes to sustainable biomass valorization and forest fire risk mitigation\.
