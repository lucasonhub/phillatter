\_Zenodo\_ 1\. 2026This paper investigates twenty\-four candidate autonomous representational operational shapes as provisional investigative distinctions rather than independently existing kinds of representation\. It shifts the focus of representational inquiry from human\-centered cognition toward autonomous machine operations and Xenoscience, without privileging machines as new transcendental subjects\. The study distinguishes twenty candidate axes of representational attribution and four reflexive candidates examining the explanatory necessity of representation and operational\-shape vocabulary itself\. Each candidate is investigated through situated conditions of ignition, interaction, transformation, efficacy, and withdrawal\. A central methodological contribution is the separation of operational function \(F\), representational attribution \(R\), and explanatory increment \(E\)\. Counterfactual comparisons examine how sensory, causal, computational, conceptual, inferential, and scientific descriptions overlap, diverge, or become redundant under changing experimental configurations\. Particular attention is given to the distinction between numerical error\-driven updating and practices involving challengeable commitments, evidential entitlement, and inferential incompatibility\. Engaging Sellars, Brandom, Brassier, Dretske, and Millikan, the paper recognizes existing naturalistic, informational, functional, and inferential accounts of representation while investigating the conditions under which representational descriptions provide additional explanatory discrimination\. The inquiry further examines whether concepts, the concept of a concept, and operational\-shape descriptions are necessary for autonomous scientific operations\. Through the nuance\-operational model and Non\-Closure Realism, it treats the emergence, transformation, and expiry of representational distinctions as situated and revisable\. The resulting framework offers conditional comparative judgments rather than empirical validation of twenty\-four representational kinds\. It develops a defeasible method for investigating representational attribution, conceptual necessity, and scientific operations beyond mandatory human philosophical authorization\. \(direct link \)
