# The stimulus of virtual brand activation: how businesses can leverage interactive advertising in the metaverse to make a positive social impact

**Autores:** Manzir Abbas; Rasha Harith Abbood; Khaled Tarik Mahmood Alkhasawneh; Uğur Tarık Özkut; Fidelis Ejeh Abba

**Data:** 2026-10-09 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

The emergence of the metaverse has transformed how companies interact with customers and cultivate brand loyalty while offering a new forum for online brand advocacy\. Corporate social responsibility and branding tactics are combined in virtual brand activism which has expanded to incorporate new interactive elements in online spaces such as the metaverse\. This study explores how businesses might use metaverse interactive advertising to make a significant social impact with an emphasis on immersive and interactive advertising strategies that increase customer participation\. It examines the tactics, systems and difficulties that companies have when participating in virtual brand activism\. The study also intends to provide insights into how virtual brand activism can advance social change and enhance brand equity at the same time by examining case studies and empirical data that have already been published\. This paper offers a series of useful recommendations for companies looking to use interactive advertising techniques in the metaverse that will guarantee both brand expansion and social effect\. In methodology we use both qualitative and quantitative techniques while taking our 550 participant and use PLS 4\.0 that have taken part through different medium\. It also looks at the moral issues of virtual brand activism and provides advice on how companies may stay true to themselves while negotiating the challenges of online activism\. In its conclusion, the review points out gaps in the existing body of literature and makes recommendations for future directions\.
