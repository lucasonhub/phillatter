# Nurse as patient: a qualitative study on nursing service quality gaps based on the SERVQUAL model

**Autores:** Jie Yu; Juan Li; 陈丽霞; Huafang Zhang

**Data:** 2026-10-09 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Abstract Aim To explore nursing service quality gaps in tertiary hospitals via nurses’ dual perspective as care providers and patients, guided by the SERVQUAL model\. Design: A single\-center qualitative phenomenological study\. Methods Ten clinical nurses were recruited using purposive and maximum variation sampling\. Data were collected through face\-to\-face semi\-structured interviews and analyzed thematically per Giorgi’s descriptive method\. Rigor was ensured via Lincoln and Guba’s trustworthiness criteria and the COREQ checklist\. Results 5 core themes and 13 sub\-themes were identified, mapping to the SERVQUAL model’s five gaps: \(1\) demand for medical service process optimization; \(2\) shortcomings in hospitalization environment and service details; \(3\) insufficient nurse\-patient communication and humanistic care; \(4\) deviations in nursing professional competence and execution; \(5\) limited awareness of the hospital’s specialized strengths\. Conclusion Nurses’ dual identity uncovers latent service gaps overlooked by traditional evaluations\. The findings provide context\-specific strategies to optimize patient\-centered care, advancing the WHO’s vision of integrated people\-centered health services\.
