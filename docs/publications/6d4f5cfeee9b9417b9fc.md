# From AI Consumers to AI Value Creators: Assessing Urban Indian University Students&\#x27; Readiness for Viksit Bharat 2047

**Autores:** Ms\. Ramya R\.; Sri Abirami Lakshmi H; R\. Maya Smriti

**Data:** 2026-10-14 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

This study aims to explore the technological sovereignty of urban Indian university students with respect to the use of AI towards Viksit Bharat 2047\. It demonstrates the current state of AI usage orientation among the younger generation as AI consumers or AI value creators and strives to assess the path that we are journeying in towards a developed nation\. With the current digital era, AI tools seek to help in research, improve problem\-solving skills, and encourage innovative thinking\. Generative AI and prompt templates help in comprehending the cognitive abilities of university students using digital learning platforms and enable the students to utilize artificial intelligence to design creative solutions and have an entrepreneurial mindset\. Ultimately, this research delves into the future scope regarding urban Indian university students&\#x27; readiness in terms of automotive technology towards Viksit Bharat 2047\.
