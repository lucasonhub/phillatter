# Evaluating the efficacy of sightconsec: A tripartite analysis of information retention, cognitive load reduction and optimal implementation parameters in consecutive interpreting

**Autores:** Laurent Roger Azambou Ndongmo; Grace Divine Esther Eya’an Mengue’e

**Data:** 2026-10-13 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

This study conducted a tripartite empirical evaluation of sight consecutive interpreting \(sightconsec\) – a hybrid modality integrating sight translation with consecutive interpreting – to assess its efficacy in enhancing information retention \(exhaustivity\) and reducing cognitive load in final\-year trainee interpreters\. Employing a mixed\-methods quasi\-experimental design, the research compared traditional consecutive interpreting with sightconsec assisted by automatic speech recognition \(Otter\.ai\), using controlled interpretation tasks and NASA\-TLX questionnaires\. The investigation was conducted under high\-density speech conditions and analysed omission patterns, cognitive effort redistribution and the influence of variables such as speech rate and text complexity\. The study is grounded in Gile’s effort model and cognitive load theory and aimed to identify optimal implementation parameters so as to contribute to both interpreter training methodologies and the theoretical understanding of multimodal processing in hybrid interpreting\. Results indicate that sightconsec improves exhaustivity \(information retention\) significantly but, simultaneously, increases perceived cognitive load, which is characterised by heightened coordination demands rather than memory effort\. The modality proved most effective for interpreters of intermediate proficiency and for managing dense, technical content\. The findings demonstrate that sightconsec redistributes cognitive effort rather than reducing it, which necessitates an expansion of theoretical processing models\. The study concludes with implications for integrating targeted sightconsec training into interpreter pedagogy to develop essential multimodal strategic competence\.
