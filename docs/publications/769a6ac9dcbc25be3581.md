# Selective cytotoxicity of Ferulic Acid\-Loaded ZIF\-8 nanoparticles and free Ferulic Acid on ovarian carcinoma and dental pulp stem cells

**Autores:** Elif Önder; Nazlı Çil; Hasan İlhan; Hande Şenol; Gülçin Abban Mete

**Data:** 2026-10-10 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Purpose: To overcome the rapid oxidation and low stability of Ferulic Acid \(FA\), this study designed and characterized FA\-loaded Zeolitic imidazol framework \-8 \(FA\-ZIF\-8\) nanoparticles, evaluating their selective cytotoxic, anti\-proliferative, anti\-migratory, and apoptotic effects on OVCAR\-3 and SKOV\-3 ovarian cancer cells alongside healthy primary dental pulp stem cells \(DPSCs\)\.Materials and methods: FA\-ZIF\-8 nanoparticles were synthesized and characterized\. OVCAR\-3, SKOV\-3, and DPSC cohorts were treated with varying concentrations of FA \(5 μM–1 mM\) and FA\-ZIF\-8 \(25–500 μg/mL\) for up to 72 h\. Cell viability and 48\-h half\-maximal inhibitory concentrations IC50 were quantified using the XTT assay\. Transcriptional and translational expressions of apoptotic markers were analyzed via qRT\-PCR and immunocytochemistry and TUNEL analysis\. Long\-term survival and lateral cell motility were monitored via coloni and scratch wound healing assays\.Results: FA exhibited a non\-toxic profile, actively stimulating the proliferation of both cancer lines and DPSCs\. FA\-ZIF\-8 inverted this response, yielding 48\-h IC50 values of 308\.2098 μg/mL for OVCAR\-3 and 336\.3022 μg/mL for SKOV\-3\. FA\-ZIF\-8 upregulated Bax expression and Caspase\-3 levels while suppressing Bcl\-2, driving a significant increase in the Bax/Bcl\-2 \(p=0\.0024\)\. These results were confirmed by an increase in TUNEL\-positive apoptotic cell\. Furthermore, FA\-ZIF\-8 inhibited colony formation and significantly reduced lateral migration indices, particularly in SKOV\-3 lines\.Conclusion: FA acts as a proliferative ligand, its strategic formulation into FA\-ZIF\-8 nanoparticles enables a, &quot;Trojan Horse&quot; effect, delivering selective cytotoxicity, anti\-metastatic suppression, pH\-responsive apoptotic induction to epithelial ovarian cancer cells\.
