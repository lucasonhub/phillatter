# Detection of Brain Tumors using Convolutional Neural Networks

**Autores:** Rohit Kumar Gupta; Nitesh Gupta

**Data:** 2026-10-18 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

The variation of appearance of brain tumours is a challenging detection task—the interpretation of cells due to abnormal growth in dimension decreases the accuracy of the detection algorithm\. Recently, several algorithms have been applied based on transform\-based and machine learning for detecting brain tumours\. The major limitation of these algorithms is the mapping of extracted features of a brain tumour in the classification algorithms\. The proposed algorithm was tested in MATLAB environments with BRATS datasets in 2013\. The proposed algorithm compares with existing algorithms for brain tumour detection, including CNN, SVM, and EL, among others\. The experimental analysis of algorithms improves detection accuracy by 2–3 percent with the existing algorithm\. The results suggest that the proposed algorithm is very efficient for brain tumor detection\.
