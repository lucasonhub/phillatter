# Efficacy of a single\-file reciprocating protocol vs\. a multi\-file rotary protocol on the removal of gutta\-percha and ceraSeal sealer from oval canals: a volumetric and active instrumentation time analysis

**Autores:** Ahmed Zorek; Abeer Hashem Mahran; Mohammed Nabeel; Tarek Medhat Elsewify

**Data:** 2026-10-14 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Objective To evaluate and compare the overall efficacy and time efficiency of two complete manufacturer\-recommended retreatment protocols, a single\-file reciprocating protocol, Reciproc \(RC\), vs\. a multi\-file continuous rotary protocol, ProTaper Universal Retreatment \(PR\), in removing gutta\-percha and CeraSeal sealer from oval\-shaped root canals\. Methodology Thirty\-six single\-rooted human mandibular premolars with single oval canals were selected, instrumented, and obturated with a matching cone gutta\-percha and CeraSeal sealer\. Initial filling volumes were evaluated via CBCT\. The samples were randomly divided into two experimental groups \( n = 18\) according to the file used for retreatment\. The net active instrumentation time was recorded\. Post\-retreatment CBCT scans were performed to measure the volume and percentage of remaining filling materials\. Data were statistically analyzed at a significance level of p ≤ 0\.05\. Results Both systems significantly reduced the filling volume \( p &lt; 0\.0001\)\. No significant difference was found in the initial filling volumes between groups \( p = 0\.616\)\. However, the RC group demonstrated a significantly lower mean volume \(0\.474 ± 0\.313 mm 3 \) and percentage \(5\.52 ± 1\.97%\) of remaining filling material compared to the PR group \(1\.735 ± 0\.753 mm 3 and 20\.72 ± 2\.93%, respectively; p &lt; 0\.0001\)\. Furthermore, the RC group required significantly less active instrumentation time \(48\.69 ± 4\.98 s\) than the PR group \(68\.49 ± 5\.43 s, p &lt; 0\.0001\)\. Conclusion Within the limitations of this in vitro study, neither mechanical retreatment protocol completely removed CeraSeal sealer and gutta\-percha from oval root canals\. However, the single\-file RC protocol was significantly more effective and time\-efficient than the multi\-file PR protocol\. The superior performance of the RC protocol reflects the combined effect of its matched apical size \(size 40 vs\. size 20\), single\-file sequence, cross\-sectional design, and reciprocating kinematics, rather than kinematic motion alone\.
