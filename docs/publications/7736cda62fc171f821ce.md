# Minamikata, Juza: From Phenomenal Facts to Primitive Qualia: Auditing Metaphysical Promotion

**Autores:** 

**Data de descoberta:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** unknown

## Descrição do feed

This paper asks what the existence and character of phenomenal experience establish about its metaphysical status\. It does not deny that experiences occur, differ qualitatively, appear unified or immediate, or sometimes resist exhaustive articulation\. The target is the further claim that phenomenal character is metaphysically primitive\. I distinguish two principal, potentially interacting routes to this stronger claim: phenomenological promotion, in which features of experience are used to support claims about constitution or primitive status, and theoretical positing, in which primitive phenomenality is introduced as a fundamental basis for explanatory or metaphysical reasons\. Rather than treating these arguments as instances of one formal fallacy, the paper identifies three non\-exclusive mechanisms of metaphysical promotion: register transfer, candidate selection, and warrant\-strength escalation\. It gives operational criteria for distinguishing these mechanisms and a typed account of what their justificatory bridges must accomplish\. Revelation provides the central register\-transfer case; grounding\-physicalist responses illustrate candidate selection; and the move from fundamentality to modal finality provides the clearest strength escalation\. The audit is source\-preserving: failure of a bridge does not by itself defeat the phenomenal, epistemic, explanatory, or comparative claim from which it begins\. The result is a framework for delimiting how far a given argument reaches without denying the phenomenal facts that motivate it\. \(direct link \)
