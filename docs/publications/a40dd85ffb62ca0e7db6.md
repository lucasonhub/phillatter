# New quality productive forces\-empowered neutrosophic group decision\-making approach for engineering cost risk assessment: evidence from farmland water conservancy projects

**Autores:** Wu XiaoRong; Xu Chen

**Data:** 2026-10-14 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Introduction Engineering projects are typically characterized by large investment scales, prolonged construction periods, and substantial uncertainty, making cost management inevitably exposed to various risks\. In the context of new quality productive forces, engineering cost risk evaluation involves multiple conflicting attributes and the judgments of multiple decision\-makers, and can therefore be formulated as a multi\-attribute group decision\-making \(MAGDM\) problem\. Methods Considering the inherent ambiguity and uncertainty involved in evaluating engineering cost risks under new quality productive forces, single\-valued neutrosophic sets \(SVNSs\) are employed to represent uncertain assessment information more comprehensively\. Meanwhile, the Logarithmic TODIM \(LogTODIM\) method is introduced to capture the bounded rationality and psychological behavioral characteristics of decision\-makers during the evaluation process\. On this basis, an integrated single\-valued neutrosophic number\-based LogTODIM \(SVNN\-LogTODIM\) method is developed to address MAGDM problems in a neutrosophic environment\. Results To demonstrate the feasibility and effectiveness of the proposed SVNN\-LogTODIM method, a detailed numerical example concerning engineering cost risk evaluation for farmland water conservancy construction projects supporting high\-standard agricultural production is conducted\. The obtained evaluation results demonstrate that the proposed approach can effectively distinguish the risk levels of different alternatives and provide a reasonable ranking of engineering projects under uncertain decision\-making conditions\. Discussion The proposed SVNN\-LogTODIM framework integrates the uncertainty\-handling capability of SVNSs with the behavioral decision\-making characteristics of the LogTODIM method, thereby providing a more comprehensive approach for engineering cost risk assessment\. The developed method can offer useful decision support for project managers and relevant stakeholders in identifying and prioritizing engineering cost risks under the development requirements of new quality productive forces\.
