# Cholbi, Michael: Grief and Voluntary Assisted Dying

**Autores:** 

**Data de descoberta:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** unknown

## Descrição do feed

In Mark Alfano, The Moral Psychology of Grief\. Bloomsbury\. forthcomingRecent research on the impact of voluntary assisted dying \(VAD\) on the grief of family and loved ones generally paints post\-VAD grief in a positive light — as less prolonged, difficult, or traumatic than normal\. This chapter provide a philosophical explanation of these findings by contrasting relatively benign post\-VAD grief with post\-suicide grief in particular and by situating these findings in relation to my practical identity account of the nature or significance of grief \(Grief: A Philosophical Guide, 2021\)\. Choosing to die within the kind of institutional context provided by lawful assisted dying alters the interpersonal dynamics between dying individuals so as to encourage healthy grieving\. The deaths of others compel us to alter our practical identities by altering our relationships with individuals who die, and the process of assisted dying lends itself to both healthy examination of those relationships and the satisfactory reconstruction of our practical identities\.
