# An interaction perspective on situation awareness in human–AI teaming: A scoping review

**Autores:** Mingyan Wang; Tianrong Chen; Calvin Kalun Or

**Data:** 2026-10-10 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Situation awareness \(SA\) is crucial for effective teaming and is becoming increasingly relevant in human\-AI teaming as AI systems take on more active roles\. AI systems rely on designed interactions to communicate information and coordinate with humans, which may influence how human teammates perceive, interpret, and anticipate task situations\. Therefore, this scoping review synthesizes how SA is framed and examined in human\-AI teaming from an interaction perspective, providing a coherent understanding of fragmented evidence across application domains\. The review followed the JBI framework and was reported according to the PRISMA\-ScR guidelines\. We searched five databases on January 13, 2026: Scopus, Web of Science, IEEE Xplore, ACM Digital Library, and PsycINFO\. Studies were eligible if they involved humans and AI jointly engaging in a shared task, addressed SA or related constructs, and described interaction processes involving situational information exchange\. Screening and data charting were independently conducted by two reviewers, with discrepancies resolved through discussion\. We included 52 studies published between 2005 and 2025, mapping application domains, SA framing, teaming configurations, situational information used by AI systems, and reported interaction processes involving situational information exchange\. The results showed that within the retrieved corpus, studies published in later years more frequently addressed non\-domain\-specific and everyday\-life contexts\. Interaction processes varied in information exchange direction, trigger, and functional role, with recurring patterns centered on AI\-to\-human information provision, modification of plans, and alignment of shared understanding\. The synthesized interaction\-centered framework offers a structured basis for future research on SA\-oriented interaction design in human\-AI teaming\.
