# Kowalski, Bob: The Volitional Index: A Probabilistic Framework for Decision\-making in Tripartite Systems

## Descrição do feed

\_SSRN\_ 1:8\. 2026This paper introduces the Volitional Index \(V\) as a formal, probabilistically grounded framework for decision\-making in tripartite architectures\. Unlike binary systems that merely select between A and B, we define a tripartite topology consisting of three fundamental operations: Act \(A\), Stop \(S\), and Counteract \(B\)\. The model introduces an internal distribution of preference weights \(πA, πS, πB\), which are not execution probabilities but measures of decisional evidence\. From these weights we derive the net orientation V = πB − πA and the commitment level C = 1 − πS\. The final decision is deterministic: the system stops \(S\) if commitment is low \(C ε\. Uncertainty \(entropy\) and metacognitive conflict act through a multiplicative damping factor δ that pulls the entire distribution toward the uniform point \(1/3, 1/3, 1/3\), not by necessarily increasing πS or decreasing C, but by reducing differentiation among all alternatives\. At the perfect equilibrium \(1/3, 1/3, 1/3\) we have V = 0, which inevitably leads to S for any ε &gt; 0, independent of θ\. The framework is implemented across AI\-2 \(predictive estimation\) and AI\-3 \(deliberative evaluation\), with fully symmetric variables for A and B\. It challenges reductionist models such as Yann LeCun&\#x27;s by making inhibition and suspension structurally explicit in the decision topology, rather than treating them as implicit side effects of a policy\. Philosophically, it implements a compatibilist functional view: the capacity to revise criteria and to suspend action constitutes the computational equivalent of freedom within a deterministic causal structure \(direct link \)
