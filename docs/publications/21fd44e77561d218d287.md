# Artificial Intelligence and Buyer–Supplier Relationships in Procurement

**Autores:** Dennis Ejike Onyishi; Conference Ekeutomhinye; Linda Chinelo Agbasianya; Chima Roland Obiora; Abdulquddus Olanrewaju Abdulyaqeen; Ayo David Adeyemi

**Data:** 2026-10-12 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

This study investigates the influence of Artificial Intelligence \(AI\) applications on buyer–supplier relationship quality in Nigeria&\#x27;s mobile manufacturing sector, with specific reference to TECNO Mobile Nigeria\. Drawing on the Commitment\-Trust Theory of Relationship Marketing \(Morgan &amp; Hunt, 1994\), the study operationalises AI through three proxies: Predictive Analytics \(PA\), Automation of Processes \(AP\), and AI Risk Management \(AIR\)\. A survey research design was adopted, and structured questionnaires were administered to 250 employees and supply chain partners purposively selected from TECNO Mobile&\#x27;s procurement, logistics, IT, and supplier management functions\. Data were analysed using descriptive statistics \(frequencies, means, standard deviations\) and one\-sample t\-tests benchmarked against the scale&\#x27;s neutral midpoint \(μ₀ = 3\.00\)\. All three null hypotheses were rejected at p &lt; 0\.001\. Predictive Analytics recorded an aggregate mean of 3\.88 \(SD = 0\.34; t\(249\) = 40\.53\), Automation of Processes 3\.91 \(SD = 0\.36; t\(249\) = 40\.05\), and AI Risk Management 3\.92 \(SD = 0\.36; t\(249\) = 40\.47\), all significantly above the neutral midpoint\. Findings confirm that respondents perceive each AI dimension as positively and meaningfully contributing to trust, efficiency, transparency, and long\-term relational stability with suppliers\. The study contributes empirical evidence from an under\-studied domain in the AI\-supply chain literature, Nigeria&\#x27;s mobile manufacturing sector, and offers practical guidance for practitioners and policymakers pursuing AI\-enabled supply chain transformation in emerging economies\.
