  membrane continuity, critical details, visible leakage symptoms, dampness, ponding
  and flood-test documentation, defect identification, photographic evidence, defect
  numbering, rectification tracking and reinspection closure. Particular emphasis
  is placed on defect pinpointing. Suspected defects are linked to controlled photo
