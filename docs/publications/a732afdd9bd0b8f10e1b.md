# AI\-Based Waste Classification and Smart Waste Management Systems: Techniques, Challenges, and Future Directions

**Autores:** Mohammed Zain\. I; Mohammed Hashir; Mohammad Zuhaib Wani; Md Yusuf Ali; Kavitha D D

**Data:** 2026-10-18 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Rapid urban growth and the corresponding rise in municipal solid waste have intensified the need for efficient and scalable waste management solutions\. Conventional segregation practices, which largely rely on manual intervention, are often time\-consuming, inconsistent, and unsuitable for handling large volumes of waste\. In recent years, advancements in Artificial Intelligence \(AI\), Machine Learning \(ML\), and Deep Learning \(DL\) have facilitated the development of intelligent systems capable of automating waste classification and monitoring processes\. This survey provides a detailed review of 13 research studies published between 2021 and 2025, with a focus on AI\-driven approaches for waste management\. It examines a range of techniques, including Convolutional Neural Networks \(CNNs\), object detection frameworks, and hybrid learning models, along with the datasets, evaluation metrics, and system designs employed in these works\. A comparative assessment is carried out to analyze the performance, advantages, and limitations of the existing methodologies\. The review further identifies critical research gaps, such as the absence of standardized benchmark datasets, limited real\-time implementation, lack of multimodal sensor integration, and insufficient emphasis on model interpretability\. Based on these findings, the paper outlines potential future directions, including the adoption of edge\-based AI systems, integration with IoT\-enabled smart infrastructure, and the use of advanced detection models to support scalable and efficient waste management in smart city environments\.
