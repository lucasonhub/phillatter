# STUDI FENOMENOLOGI PENGGUNAAN GADGET YANG MEMPENGARUHI KONSENTRASI BELAJAR DAN KECAKAPAN SOSIAL MURID KELAS XI SMA

**Autores:** Amalia Khoirunnisa; Devy Probowati; Zafifatuz Zuhriyah

**Data:** 2026-10-15 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

The prevalence of digital distractions has created numerous challenges, particularly regarding gadget usage\. Uncontrolled gadget use can impact students&\#x27; learning concentration and social skills\. This study aims to describe the experiences of Grade XI MCA students at SMAN 4 Malang regarding gadget usage and its effects on their learning concentration and social skills\. A qualitative approach utilizing the phenomenological method was employed\. The study participants consisted of five Grade XI MCA students \(as primary informants\) and one guidance counselor \(as a supporting informant\)\. Data collection involved interviews, observation, and documentation, while data analysis proceeded through data reduction, data presentation, and conclusion drawing\. The findings indicate that the factors driving gadget use during lessons include boredom, the habit of checking devices, peer influence, and low levels of student self\-control\. Such usage affects learning concentration, as students are easily distracted by digital activities\. Furthermore, the intensity of gadget use influences students&\#x27; social skills, evidenced by reduced social interaction when devices are used during social activities\. Consequently, the role of guidance counselors and the school is crucial in fostering wiser gadget usage habits through guidance services that strengthen self\-control and time management regarding device use\.
