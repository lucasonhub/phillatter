# Vierhout, Luuk: Hunting down Consciousness: A Radical New Approach

**Autores:** 

**Data de descoberta:** 2026-10-19 · **Tipo:** unknown

## Descrição do feed

This manifesto proposes a radical dissolution of Chalmers’ &quot;Hard Problem of Consciousness&quot; through a combined epistemological and evolutionary shift\. It establishes Darwin&\#x27;s mechanism of variation and selection not merely as an evolutionary engine for species, but as the primary, fundamental form of intelligence itself\. Rather than treating intelligence as an internal property or brain state, it reframes it as a dynamic, relational coordination operating across the boundary between organism and environment\. Spanning cellular phototaxis, LLMs, and human cognition, this broad Darwinian process shows that subject and object are merely stabilized outputs of a continuous trial and error process\. Epistemologically, we are not detached observers of a fixed reality, but active, adaptive &quot;scanners&quot; updating weights within survival limits\. Consciousness emerges when this primary Darwinian loop folds back on itself across time, forcing the organism to collapse a vast, distributed biological storm of data into an immediate, highly compressed, qualitative first\-person readout out of structural necessity\.
