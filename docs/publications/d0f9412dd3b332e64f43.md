# Existence and Stability Results for Boundary Value Problems of Two\-Point Tempered Fractional Difference Equations

**Autores:** Mustafa Aydın

**Data:** 2026-10-13 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

This paper mainly explores Green’s function to deduce the unique solution of boundary value problems in two\-point tempered fractional difference equations\. Discrete tempered fractional calculus has been slightly improved\. Using a weighted norm, sufficient and necessary conditions are determined to show the existence of a unique solution to two\-point tempered fractional difference equations\. The stability results of these equations are analyzed\.
