# Cost\-effectiveness of remote monitoring of dental patients: tariff formation and cost analysis based on real\-world data of a healthcare organization\.

**Autores:** Чернявская Татьяна Константиновна; Konstantin Eduardovich Sobolev; М\. А\. Амхадова; Z\. V\. Bazhanova

**Data:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Relevance\. The development of telemedicine and artificial intelligence has driven the wide adoption of remote monitoring of dental patients, whose clinical and organizational effectiveness has been demonstrated\. However, the economic justification of such technologies and approaches to tariff setting for the remote\-monitoring service remain undeveloped, which hinders their inclusion in healthcare financing\. Objective: to develop a method for calculating the cost \(tariff\) of the remote\-monitoring service for dental patients and to assess the cost\-effectiveness of the technology using real\-world data of a healthcare organization\. Material and methods\. The study comprised a prospective clinical\-organizational part and an economic evaluation\. Clinical and organizational effects of remote monitoring were obtained in a prospective single\-center non\-randomized controlled study \(528 patients enrolled; 516 analyzed: 262 remote monitoring, 254 conventional follow\-up\)\. Real\-world unit costs were derived from the 2025 service registry of the dental department of MONIKI \(13,200 services totalling RUB3,532,311\)\. The tariff was calculated using the normative method of direct and indirect cost calculation\. Cost\-effectiveness was assessed by a cost\-offset analysis with calculation of the net economic result, benefit–cost ratio, break\-even point, and budget impact; robustness was tested in a base\-case \(conservative\) scenario and a full\-visit\-cost scenario\. Results\. The estimated cost of remote monitoring was RUB333\.12 per patient per month \(labour 49\.6%, indirect costs 49\.0%, equipment depreciation 1\.4%\)\. With a mean postoperative follow\-up of 45 days \(≈1\.5 months\), the cost of remote support per patient was about RUB500 per episode\. A reduction of 4\.4 in\-person visits per patient generated savings of RUB1,091 \(conservative, perconsultation cost\) to RUB7,115 \(full\-visit cost\) per patient\. The net economic result ranged from \+RUB591 \(base\-case conservative scenario\) to \+RUB6,615 \(upper bound\) per patient per episode, with a benefit–cost ratio from 1:2\.2 to 1:14\.2\. The technology remained cost\-saving as long as the cost of one in\-person visit exceeded RUB114\. Conclusion\. Remote monitoring of dental patients is a cost\-saving technology with a robustly positive economic result that persists in the conservative scenario\. The developed tariff\-calculation method provides a basis for including the remote\-monitoring service in the financing of specialized dental care\.
