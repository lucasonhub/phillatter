# A digital leadership cycle for strengthening primary teachers’ lesson planning

**Autores:** Yopi Nadia; Y\. Yantoro; Ely Yuliawan

**Data:** 2026-10-14 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Background—Primary teachers need support to translate digital access into pedagogically coherent lesson plans and teaching materials\. Objectives—This study examined five connected aspects of principals’ support: diagnosing digital\-learning needs, enabling situated professional learning, orchestrating digital resources, evaluating practice and renewing support, and synthesizing a cross\-case leadership cycle\. Method—A qualitative multiple\-case study was conducted in four public primary schools in Muaro Jambi, Indonesia\. Four principals and eight experienced upper\-primary teachers participated, providing accounts complemented by non\-participant observations and school documents\. Within\-case summaries and cross\-case matrices were interpreted through source and method triangulation and member checking\. Results—The cases shared needs diagnosis, workplace learning, resource coordination, and formative supervision, but differed in the evidence used, learning arrangements, and access constraints\. Teaching materials considered in the study included teaching modules, worksheets, digital media, and assessments; no standardized improvement scores were available\. Education\-report analysis was prominent in Case A and Case B, peer\-supported learning in Case C, and phase\-based discussion in Case D\. The synthesis yielded a diagnose–enable–evaluate–renew cycle, with resource orchestration embedded in enablement\. Conclusions—The cycle offers a context\-sensitive explanation of leadership support for digital lesson planning\. It is an interpretive proposition for further testing, rather than evidence of causal effects on teacher competence or student outcomes\.
