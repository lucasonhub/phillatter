# DIGITAL HEALTH IN A GLOBAL PERSPECTIVE: A BIBLIOMETRIC ANALYSIS

**Autores:** Dayara de Nazaré Rosa de Carvalho; Marcos Rodrigo Pereira Eismann; Elcyana Viana Santos Mendonça; Brena Bentes da Silva Brasil; Gabrielly Cristine Lopes Pereira; Simone Aguiar da Silva Figueira; Viviane Ferraz Ferreira de Aguiar; Ilma Pastana Ferreira

**Data:** 2026-10-13 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

The COVID\-19 pandemic accelerated the digital transformation in healthcare, driving the use of technologies such as artificial intelligence, telehealth, and mobile devices\. This process has consolidated Digital Health as a strategic global field, reshaping healthcare delivery and management\. The present study aimed to analyze the scientific production on Digital Health between 2020 and 2025, identifying relevant trends and challenges for the sector\. This is a quantitative and descriptive bibliometric study, based on data extracted from the Scopus database \(open access\) in Portuguese, English, and Spanish\. The analysis was performed using VOSviewer software, utilizing similarity networks to identify thematic clusters, authorship networks, and trends in scientific production\. A total of 9,488 eligible publications were identified, with a higher concentration of recent production and leadership by the United States, the United Kingdom, and Australia\. Five main thematic axes emerged: Digital Technologies and AI, Mobility and Self\-Care, Clinical Conditions and Telehealth, Public Health and Primary Care, and Research and Implementation in Digital Health\. The study demonstrated significant advances but highlighted challenges regarding regulation, ethics, digital inclusion, and methodological evaluation\. This study reaffirms the importance of digital technologies in redefining healthcare delivery and management, emphasizing the need to align technological innovation with principles of equity, ethics, governance, and social inclusion to ensure a digital transformation that promotes justice and universal access to health\.
