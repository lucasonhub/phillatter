# ASSESSMENT OF QUALITY OF LIFE AND CLINICAL EFFECTIVENESS OF SURGICAL TREATMENT IN PATIENTS WITH VARICOSE VEIN DISEASE OF THE LOWER EXTREMITIES

**Autores:** Mykyta Druzhkin; A\. V\. Klymenko

**Data:** 2026-10-17 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Introduction\. Chronic venous disease \(CVD\) of the lower extremities in the stage of chronic venous insufficiency is characterized by a progressive course, a high rate of complications, and a significant decline in patients’ quality of life \(QoL\)\. Despite the widespread implementation of endovenous thermal treatment methods, isolated elimination of truncal reflux does not always ensure complete clinical and functional recovery in the postoperative period\.Aim\. To assess quality of life and clinical manifestations of the disease in patients with chronic venous disease of the lower extremities in the early and long\-term postoperative periods depending on the method of surgical treatment\.Materials and methods\. A total of 139 patients \(100%\) with CVD at the stage of chronic venous insufficiency \(C3\-C6, CEAP\) were included and divided into three groups\. Group 1 \(n=32; 23\.02%\) underwent endovenous radiofrequency ablation \(RFA\) of the great and/or small saphenous veins combined with miniphlebectomy and perforator vein ligation\. Group 2 \(n=64\) included subgroup 2A \(n=31; 22\.30%\) – RFA with miniphlebectomy without perforator ligation, and subgroup 2B \(n=33; 23\.74%\) – RFA with perforator ligation without miniphlebectomy\. Group 3 \(n=43; 30\.94%\) underwent isolated RFA\.The study methods included clinical assessment \(complaints, medical and life history, physical examination, laboratory tests\), duplex ultrasound of the lower limb veins, quality of life evaluation using CIVIQ\-20 \(invGIS\), VCSS scoring, and statistical analysis\.Results\. In all groups, improvement in QoL indicators and a reduction in clinical severity were observed after treatment\. The most pronounced and sustained positive changes were noted in patients who underwent multimodal combined treatment \(RFA combined with miniphlebectomy of tributary veins and ligation of perforator veins\)\. After 12 months, CIVIQ\-20 scores in the main group were significantly higher, while VCSS scores were lower compared to the groups of mono\-endovenous and partially combined treatment \(p&lt;0\.05\)\.Conclusions\. Multimodal treatment of CVD provides the most complete restoration of patients’ QoL and significantly reduces the clinical severity of the disease compared to monoablation and partially combined treatment\.
