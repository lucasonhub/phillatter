# Building Resilient Telecom Operators through Artificial Intelligence: A CTE\-Based Approach to Autonomous Network and Business Operations

**Autores:** Igor Jurčić; Marko Salai

**Data:** 2026-10-13 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Artificial Intelligence \(AI\) is changing where competitive advantage comes from in telecommunications\. It used to depend mainly on network assets \- spectrum, infrastructure, reach\. Increasingly, it depends on how intelligent, adaptable, and resilient the systems running those assets are\. This paper builds on the Comprehensive Techno\-Economic \(CTE\) Model and its AI\-augmented extension \(AI\-CTE\) to propose a resilience framework that covers both autonomous network operations and autonomous business operations\. Previous AI\-CTE work formalized AI maturity, augmentation, dependency, and governance constructs, but mostly at the Technical Level\. This paper extends that work in two directions\. First, it introduces a Network Resilience Composite, combining the existing AI Resilience Score with a new Shock Absorption Capacity metric to quantify how well autonomous networks withstand and recover from disruptive events\. Second, it develops three new AI\-augmented equations at the Business Level \- product and service resilience, customer and revenue resilience, and workforce resilience \- which together make up a Business Resilience Index\. These technical and business constructs, together with an Environmental/Governance Resilience Index, are combined into a single Telecom Operator Resilience Index \(TORI\)\. An illustrative application, extending a previously published case study, shows how the index can expose a gap between strong technical autonomy and weaker business\-level resilience, giving operators concrete guidance on where to prioritize AI investment\. The paper closes with an implementation roadmap and a discussion of limitations and future research directions\. KeyWords: Comprehensive Techno\-Economic Model, AI\-CTE, Organizational Resilience, Autonomous Networks, Telecom Operators, AI Maturity Indeks\.
