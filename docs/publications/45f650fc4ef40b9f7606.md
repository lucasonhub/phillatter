# Robust high\-fidelity three\-qubit gates design for superconducting quantum computers via model\-free reinforcement learning

**Autores:** Xinxin Zhu; Yangyang Fei; Liu FuDong; Zheng Shan

**Data:** 2026-10-14 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Abstract Multi\-qubit gates are crucial to realizing quantum algorithms\. Direct implementation of these gates will facilitate a reduction in circuit depth\. However, existing model\-based methods rely on precise characterization of the system Hamiltonian as well as execution of the control fields, which present challenges in obtaining high fidelity multi\-qubit gates\. In this paper, we exploit the bang\-bang control protocol and model\-free reinforcement learning to realize several kinds of three\-qubit gates for superconducting quantum computers with tunable/fixed frequency qubits, including the iToffoli gate, the parity check gate and the CCZ gate\. The simulation results show that the fidelity of each gate can achieve 99\.2% with the Adam Gradient Descent Optimizer\. Moreover, the proposed method is robust against random noise in the control pulse\. Gate fidelity can be maintained over 95% even in the case of Gaussian noise with variance of 0\.3\. Besides, the impacts of parameter iterative optimization strategies are also analyzed\.
