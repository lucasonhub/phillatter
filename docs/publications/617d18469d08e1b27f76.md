# The Role of Artificial Intelligence in Shaping Political Opinion Among Indian Youth

**Autores:** Prof\. Madhvi Shukla

**Data:** 2026-10-11 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

The political socialization of each generation is influenced by the media landscape that shapes its political maturation; for India&\#x27;s youth, that environment is now substantially algorithmic\. This paper examines how artificial intelligence through recommendation systems, micro\- targeting infrastructure, generative and synthetic media, and conversational AI interfaces has inserted itself into the formation of political opinion among young Indians, a cohort that constitutes over a fifth of the national electorate and whose habits of political attention were never disciplined by the broadcast\-era institutions that shaped their parents&\#x27; generation\. The analysis situates this shift within established frameworks of political communication agenda\-setting, the structural transformation of the public sphere, and theories of group polarisation while grounding the argument in documented evidence from India&\#x27;s 2024 and 2025 election cycles, including verified deepfake incidents, Election Commission advisories, and survey data on youth media consumption\. The primary assertion of the paper is that the impact of AI on democratic deliberation in India is not entirely harmful nor entirely beneficial: it has measurably expanded multilingual access to political information and strengthened fact\-checking capacity, even as it has narrowed incidental exposure to opposing views, enabled synthetic deception at low cost, and displaced editorial accountability with engagement\-optimised curation\. What is absent, the paper argues, is not awareness of these risks but institutional durability in addressing them India&\#x27;s response to date has been advisory, cyclical, and confined to election windows, rather than statutory and continuous\.
