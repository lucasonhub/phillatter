# KASBIY TA’LIM MUASSASALARIDA ISH BERUVCHI VAKILLARINING AMALIYOT JARAYONIDAGI TA’SIRI

**Autores:** Sodiqova Gulnora Ergashaliyevna

**Data:** 2026-10-17 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

This article provides a scientific analysis of the participation of employer representatives in the internship process within vocational education institutions\. It details the mechanisms of integrative collaboration between employers and educational institutions, the role in developing students’ professional skills and competencies, as well as the control and evaluation systems during internships\. The study examines the effectiveness of active employer participation in aligning curricula with real labor market requirements, enhancing vocational education quality, and ensuring students’ professional success\.
