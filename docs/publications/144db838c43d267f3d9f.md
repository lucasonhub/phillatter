# Patient‐Reported Outcome Measures in Musculoskeletal Oncology: Current Concepts, Barriers, and the Future

**Autores:** Shujaa T Khan; Rowan J Barker\-Clarke; Brian Benyamini; Zachary D\. C\. Burke; Nathan W\. Mesko; Lukas M\. Nystrom

**Data:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Patient\-reported outcome measures \(PROMs\) remain underutilized in musculoskeletal oncology, with only 4\.9% of tumor\-focused articles incorporating them\. This review synthesizes current PROM instruments\-including Toronto Extremity Salvage Score, PROMIS, and emerging tools such as the Sarcoma Assessment Measure\-and identifies barriers unique to this population, including psychological distress, workflow complexity, and disease heterogeneity\. Future directions include flexible baseline collection, EHR\-integrated dashboards, national registry linkage, wearable technology, and AI\-driven processing to increase PROMs utilization\.
