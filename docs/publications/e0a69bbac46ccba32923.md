# O papel da inteligência artificial na pipeline completo de efeitos visuais no cinema contemporâneo

**Autores:** Romualdo Amaral

**Data:** 2026-10-15 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

This article examines the role of artificial intelligence \(AI\) across the complete visual effects \(VFX\) production pipeline in contemporary cinema, emphasizing the practical integration of machine learning \(ML\) techniques into pre\-production, production, and post\-production\. It argues that AI has become a set of supporting tools integrated into established VFX workflows, including camera and object tracking, rotoscoping \(roto\), digital paint and object removal, simulation, rendering, compositing, and final delivery, reducing operational challenges and expanding opportunities for creative experimentation\. The analysis draws on technical sources \(SIGGRAPH/ACM\), official tool documentation, and institutional guidelines addressing professional credits, intellectual property, consent, and digital replicas\. When quantitative data on time and cost savings are not publicly available in sufficient detail, this limitation is explicitly acknowledged, with emphasis placed on qualitative evidence and documented technical mechanisms\.
