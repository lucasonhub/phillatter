# Amornbunchornvej, Chainarong: No Tragedy From Inside: The Charge, Persistence, and Release of Moral Residue

**Autores:** 

**Data de descoberta:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** unknown

## Descrição do feed

Some guilt survives everything the person could learn\. A mother forced to choose which of two children to save, a soldier who followed an order that killed civilians, a driver who could not have stopped in time: each knows there was no better option, and the guilt stays\. Philosophers have read this as evidence that the person really did wrong, or as a mistake that reflection should correct\. This paper offers a mechanism instead\. Each of a person&\#x27;s commitments keeps its own account of what is owed, and each judges a loss by one question only: could this debt have been paid? When two commitments demand things that cannot both be done, the commitment that loses finds that its debt could have been paid\-\-\-by giving up the other\-\-\-and charges the person\. It never asks whether both could have been paid, because it carries only one\. So the charge is correct on its own books and wrong about the person, and no fact can remove it, because the missing fact has no place in the account\. The charge persists because the mind keeps searching for what could have been done, and in an irreversible loss it keeps finding something\. The same search can find what the lost person would want now\-\-\-people run because she ran, keep the garden she kept\-\-\-and that quiets the charge\. What it does not find alone is what she would say about what happened\. That release is available, and someone usually has to ask for it\.
