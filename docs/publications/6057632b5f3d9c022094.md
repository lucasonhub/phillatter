# Temperature dependence and consistency of dark current measurement methods for infrared focal plane arrays

**Autores:** Rongjie Feng; Jiaping Long; Xinjie Li; Yongyong Cao; Zilong Zeng; Zhenyi Deng; Zixin Liang; Yu Liu; Bo Li; Sanjie Liu

**Data:** 2026-10-15 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Dark current is a critical parameter for infrared focal plane detectors, while practical measurement methods may exhibit temperature\-dependent discrepancies\. This study systematically evaluates the consistency between the blind cold shield \(BCS\) and theoretical coefficient \(TC\) methods using paired measurements of 24 mid\-wave infrared detector chips at 120 K and 150 K, with 140 K measurements included as an intermediate experimental condition for descriptive analysis\. Relative deviation and Bland–Altman analyses were employed to quantify inter\-method consistency\. At 120 K, 70\.8% of chips showed TC dark\-current values more than 20% below the BCS baseline, compared with 29\.2% at 150 K\. The mean absolute relative deviation decreased from 74\.9% to 42\.1%, while the geometric mean TC/BCS ratio increased from approximately 0\.40–0\.92\. Bland–Altman analysis showed persistent absolute chip\-to\-chip variability, while the geometric mean TC/BCS ratio moved closer to unity at 150 K\. These results indicate improved relative BCS–TC consistency over 120 K–150 K while emphasizing that the applicable measurement range is detector and configuration dependent\.
