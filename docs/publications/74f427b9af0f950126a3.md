# Three doors, three locks: a domain\-specific framework for the residual door\-to\-balloon gap in STEMI care

**Autores:** Sajid Kadir; Travis Murphy

**Data:** 2026-10-13 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Krishnan and colleagues report that 91% of STEMI patients at a large Indian tertiary centre received primary PCI within the 90\-minute door\-to\-balloon \(D2B\) target, a meaningful achievement in contemporary STEMI care\. Their analysis of the 9% who missed the target is equally valuable: delays distributed almost evenly across three categories, delayed informed consent, hemodynamic instability, and diagnostic ambiguity\. We argue these categories are not interchangeable quality\-improvement targets\. They are mechanistically distinct problems requiring different solutions\. We propose a conceptual, hypothesis\-generating framework rather than a validated taxonomy\. Future STEMI quality improvement efforts should categorize residual delays by domain rather than reporting them in aggregate, and should match interventions to the specific mechanism of delay\.
