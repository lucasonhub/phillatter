# Wang, Jiaping: From Three Axioms to the Hard Problem of Consciousness: A Constructive Response via the Information\-Level Unidirectional Constraint English Version

**Autores:** 

**Data de descoberta:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** unknown

## Descrição do feed

Mainstream theories of consciousness largely address what Chalmers calls the easy problems and do not explain, from first principles, why a complete third\-person description cannot recover first\-person experience\. Starting from three axioms \(finite resources, non\-equilibrium persistence, and discrete input\) and arguing by eliminative convergence, this paper forms an Information Capacity Frame and shows that a finite system maintaining itself across time must encode in levels: a higher\-level format can only append coordinates to a lower level and cannot be recovered from it without loss, an Information\-Level Unidirectional Constraint \(ILUC\)\. The hard problem is then split in two\. The format gap is closed by this constraint, its width being the dimension difference between encoding levels, a calibratable engineering quantity\. The existence question is dissolved by a structural definition: experience is the first\-person self\-access process itself, meeting four formal conditions of anchoring, access termination, whole\-package presentation, and mandatory weight, with no phenomenal entity beyond structure; the stubborn intuition of a phenomenal quality is explained as a predictable introspective misjudgment arising from access termination\. The framework thereby answers the explanatory gap, Mary&\#x27;s Room, and the zombie argument together, preserves and re\-describes qualia, presupposes no neural substrate\. \(direct link \)
