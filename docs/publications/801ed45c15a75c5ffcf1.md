# Posterior Frequencies and the Cost of Not Fitting: A Correction to a Memorization Lower Bound

**Autores:** Zhenisbek Assylbekov; Rustem Takhanov

**Data:** 2026-10-14 · **Tipo:** preprint

## Resumo

Feldman&\#x27;s memorization lower bound assigns a frequency coefficient based on how often a point appears in the training sample\. We give an exact three\-point counterexample to its conditional and averaged forms, using independent fair labels and a permutation\-equivariant learner\. A sequence of sampling priors makes the ratio of the proposed penalty to actual excess risk grow exponentially, ruling out a universal positive constant\-factor repair\. The priors vary with sample size, the offending profiles become rare, and both quantities vanish\. Retaining the original model, we obtain an exact replacement using full\-sample posterior probabilities\. Under exchangeability, these depend on multiplicity and the complete frequency profile\. We characterize the largest multiplicity\-only coefficients valid for unrestricted learners\. The original coefficient has the posterior\-mean form of Good&\#x27;s exact frequency formula and is an occupancy\-weighted average of the corrected coefficients\.
