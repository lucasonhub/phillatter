# Social Representations About Museums of Preservice Teachers Who Participate in a Museum Internship

**Autores:** Carlos Arturo Soto Lombana; Claudia Alejandra Mazzitelli; Erica Gabriela Zorrilla; Verónica Cardona Arango

**Data:** 2026-10-14 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

ABSTRACT This article presents the results of a research project aimed at identifying the Social Representations of a group of preservice teachers who participated in a museum internship and inferring the influence of these representations on educational practice in this context\. The research employs a mixed‐methods design with a qualitative approach\. The sample consisted of eight preservice teachers of natural sciences from a Colombian university\. Following a previous examination of the social representations associated with museums and the pedagogical practices developed in this context, data collection took place during a workshop that explored content related to museum education\. Findings suggest that the Social Representations perceived by the participants identify a museum as an educational space conducive to teacher training, where learning, mediation, and motivation are aspects that occur with various audiences\.
