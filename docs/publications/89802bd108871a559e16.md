# Physician Training for the AI Era

**Autores:** Jesse Burk‐Rafel; Robert M\. Wachter

**Data:** 2026-10-14 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

This Viewpoint discusses the use of artificial intelligence \(AI\) in medical education, and how physician training should change in parallel with AI use including emphasizing skills that AI makes more valuable, cultivating those it makes newly necessary, and carefully de\-emphasizing those it makes less important\.
