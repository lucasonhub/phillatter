## Descrição do feed

\_Zenodo\_\. 2026This paper stress\-tests A5 \- The Primal Pain of — the species\-stable standing condition of constitutive attachment, a continuously active background expression of the attachment and primary emotional systems, and this corpus&\#x27;s Hamilton–Bowlby–Panksepp–Frankfurt–Williams\-grounded foundational premise — against the widest set of falsification candidates the historical and clinical record makes available: family and colleague denunciation under China’s Cultural Revolution, the Soviet Union’s propaganda cult of Pavlik Morozov alongside the documented family betrayals of the Great Terror, East Germany’s Stasi informant network, Taiwan’s White Terror, Ethiopia’s Red Terror, the Rwandan genocide, the American HUAC blacklist, and the Nazi regime’s doctrine of loyalty transfer from family to Führer and Volk\. The first edition of this paper tested the naive\-override reading against three cases and found it wanting\. This edition tests the same reading against seven, spanning four continents, three ideological registers \(Marxist\-Leninist, anti\-communist authoritarian, and liberal\-democratic moral panic\), and one instance — Rwanda — organized around ethnicity rather than any stated ideology at all\. The naive reading fails uniformly\. In every documented case, the same two mechanisms this corpus’s companion paper A5’s Black Swan predicts recur: coercive suppression under self\-directed existential threat, which Hamilton’s Rule explains without requiring attachment to be overridden, evidenced by refusal cases, later guilt, and rescuer testimony recorded in every single system examined; and disorganized attachment engineering, in Alexandra Stein’s technical sense, which relocates rather than erases the bond by systematically reproducing the familial and ritual architecture attachment already requires\. No system examined achieved denunciation for free\. Every one of them built something to get it, and what they built is evidence for what they were working against\. \(direct link \)
