# De familias de acogida a familias de adopción: experiencias en Chile

**Autores:** Viviana Castro Blanco; María Moyano Yogovic; Maité Cereceda Martínez; Miguel Salazar\-Muñoz; Gina Osorio Carvajal; Viviana Zambrano Lizama

**Data:** 2026-10-09 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Introduction: Transitions from non\-kinship foster care to adoption among children in Chile have been scarcely studied\. This study aimed to describe the experiences of families who transitioned from non\-kinship foster care to adoption and to identify relevant psychosocial and legal criteria for assessing these processes\. Methodology: A qualitative cross\-sectional study was conducted\. Data were collected through in\-depth interviews with four members of families who adopted children previously placed in their care, seven psychosocial professionals, and five legal professionals with experience in foster care and adoption\. Data were analyzed using content analysis\. Results: Findings indicate that the decision to adopt primarily emerged from the bond developed during foster care and the impossibility of family reunification\. Conclusions: The findings highlight the need to strengthen interinstitutional coordination, establish clear regulatory frameworks, and promote decision\-making guided by the best interests of the child, thereby supporting stable and permanent family trajectories
