# Digital Accounting System to Improve Financial Transparency in An Employee Cooperative

**Autores:** Yunanda Eka Putra; Sri Liniarti; Rizky Surya Andhayani Nasution

**Data:** 2026-10-12 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Originality/novelty: This study demonstrates how combining an accessible, low\-cost digital tool \(Google Sheets\) with a member\-facing WhatsApp distribution routine can effectively bridge the financial transparency gap and mitigate information asymmetry in small cooperatives\. Objective: To evaluate the implementation of a low\-cost digital accounting information system designed to replace manual handwritten records and restore member trust in an employee cooperative&\#x27;s governance\. \. Methods/approach: Framed as participatory action research with asset\-based elements, the project spanned three phases: needs assessment, system design/implementation, and training/mentoring\. Analyzed through the lens of TAM, digital transformation, and cooperative governance theory, data were gathered from four informants, observations, document analysis, and post\-training questionnaires, evaluated using thematic analysis and triangulation\. Results: Within four weeks, monthly financial reports became readily available to all board members\. Post\-training evaluations showed that 92% of respondents understood how to operate the system, while 85% expressed increased confidence in the cooperative’s financial transparency, promoting active member participation during meetings\. Implications: The study highlights that small\-scale cooperatives can achieve meaningful digital transformation and stronger governance without high financial investment, provided design simplicity, practical capacity building, and regular disclosure routines are prioritized\.
