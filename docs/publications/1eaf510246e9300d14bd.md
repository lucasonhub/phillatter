# Tratamiento de datos personales mediante inteligencia artificial en plataformas digitales

**Autores:** Isabel María Caballero Ariza

**Data:** 2026-10-14 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

The emergence of generative artificial intelligence models and systems capable of acting semi\-autonomously has exacerbated issues relating to the processing of personal data, profiling and automated decision\-making\. The average user is unaware of the true extent to which their personal information is processed, what data is collected, how long it is retained, for what purpose it is used, or which third parties may access it\. This situation leads to a gradual loss of effective control over personal information and calls into question the adequacy of consent as the primary mechanism legitimising data processing\. This phenomenon is particularly significant on everyday digital platforms such as Spotify, where artificial intelligence plays an essential role in generating music recommendations, personalising content and developing behavioural profiles through the continuous analysis of activity carried out within the platform\.
