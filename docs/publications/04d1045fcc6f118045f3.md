# Direct multiscale modeling: Accounting for stress localization near grain boundaries

**Autores:** K A Kurmoiartseva; Iakov V\. Viatkin; Остапович Кирилл Вадимович

**Data:** 2026-10-11 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

This article describes a modified multiscale constitutive model for analyzing the stress\-strain behavior of polycrystalline materials, which explicitly accounts for the influence of grain boundary misorientation on the mechanical response\. The model was implemented as a user subroutine and added to the SIMULIA Abaqus FEA software suite\. A slip system compatibility factor was introduced into the hardening law via a relation for critical shear stresses, enabling the capture of interactions between dislocations and grain boundaries\. The model was used in a numerical study of a generated polycrystalline structure of 308LSi austenitic stainless steel\. The results of the study reveal the localization of stresses and plastic strains near high\-angle grain boundaries, demonstrating the significant influence of crystallo\-geometric factors on material hardening\.
