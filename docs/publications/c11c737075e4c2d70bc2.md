# КОНЦЕПТУАЛЬНІ ЗАСАДИ ФОРМУВАННЯ ЦИФРОВОЇ ЕКОСИСТЕМИ БЕЗПЕКИ ТУРИЗМУ ТА ІНДУСТРІЇ ГОСТИННОСТІ

**Autores:** К\.Г\. Антонюк

**Data:** 2026-10-12 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

The article substantiates the conceptual foundations for forming a digital security ecosystem in the tourism and hospitality industry\. It is established that the digital transformation of tourism and hospitality enterprises and the spread of online booking platforms, mobile services, artificial intelligence, geographic information systems, the Internet of Things and cloud technologies simultaneously expand safety management capabilities and create new cyber, information, operational and reputational risks\. The concept of a «digital security ecosystem in the tourism and hospitality industry» is clarified as an integrated human\-centred environment for interaction among tourists, enterprises, digital platforms, public authorities, insurance and financial institutions, and emergency services, within which continuous data exchange, monitoring, forecasting, prevention, localisation and risk minimisation are ensured at all stages of the tourist journey\. The main groups of threats are systematised, and the stakeholder, information, technological, functional and institutional components of the ecosystem are identified\. The author’s Digital Security Ecosystem for Tourism and Hospitality \(DSETH\-model\) is proposed, combining digital identification, data integration, risk monitoring, predictive analytics, communication, response and recovery\. The principles of its functioning are substantiated: integration, interoperability, prevention, continuity, adaptability, cyber resilience, confidentiality, reliability, inclusiveness and accountability\. The stages of practical ecosystem implementation and a set of indicators for assessing its effectiveness are determined\. Particular attention is paid to the integration of digital technologies into a unified security environment capable of supporting proactive risk management and improving coordination among all stakeholders\. The proposed conceptual framework may serve as a methodological basis for developing digital security strategies and implementing innovative management solutions in the tourism and hospitality industry\. It is proved that applying an ecosystem approach will help reduce threat response time, increase consumer trust, strengthen the resilience of enterprises and tourism destinations, improve service quality and enhance the competitiveness of the tourism and hospitality industry\.
