# Regime\-Dependent Systematic Risk of Sustainability\-Oriented Investments: Evidence from Borsa Istanbul

**Autores:** Tutku ÜNKARACALAR

**Data:** 2026-10-15 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

This study examines whether the systematic\-risk exposure of sustainability\-oriented investments varies across market states in Borsa Istanbul\. Rather than treating sustainability as an unconditional source of superior performance or financial resilience, the analysis focuses on the regime dependence of market sensitivity\. A two\-state Markov Regime\-Switching Model combined with a Regime\-Switching Capital Asset Pricing Model \(CAPM\) is applied to an equally weighted, dynamically updated sustainability\-oriented portfolio over January 2015–December 2025\. Portfolio and market returns are expressed as U\.S\. dollar\-denominated excess returns\. The results identify two persistent market states with markedly different volatility characteristics\. The sustainability\-oriented portfolio exhibits a beta of 0\.81 in the low\-volatility regime and 1\.18 in the high\-volatility regime, corresponding to a point\-estimate increase of 0\.37\. The high\-volatility state is also characterised by negative average excess returns and a return variance 8\.5 times that of the low\-volatility state\. These findings provide evidence that the systematic\-risk exposure of sustainability\-oriented investments is strongly regime\-dependent\. However, because portfolio composition and other firm characteristics cannot be fully separated from sustainability status, the findings are interpreted as regime\-dependent associations rather than as evidence of a causal sustainability\-induced resilience effect\. The study complements recent regime\-switching evidence from Borsa Istanbul by emphasising dynamic portfolio membership, portfolio\-construction sensitivity, and a more conservative interpretation of sustainability\-related risk dynamics\.
