# AdaC\-GRPO: Towards Mastering Long\-Horizon Visual Sudoku Puzzles via Adaptive Curriculum Learning

**Autores:** Sida Li; Yuanxin Liu; Linli Yao; Shicheng Li; Xu Sun; Haoli Bai; Xiaohui Li

**Data:** 2026-10-09 · **Tipo:** preprint

## Resumo

While reinforcement learning with verifiable reward \(RLVR\) enhances reasoning in large language models, its efficacy in long\-horizon multimodal tasks remains underexplored\. Visual Sudoku puzzles pose a representative challenge, requiring multi\-step deductive reasoning over both local constraints and global dependency chains\. To this end, we introduce FG\-Sudoku, a fine\-grained benchmark categorized by grid size and required deductive steps\. Evaluations reveal that frontier MLLMs fail on complex long\-range dependencies \(e\.g\., 9\*9 puzzles\)\. During post\-training, this near\-zero success rate causes severe reward sparsity, halting standard RLVR\. We propose Adaptive Curriculum Group Relative Policy Optimization \(AdaC\-GRPO\) to overcome this\. Unlike rigid or prior\-based schedules, AdaC\-GRPO dynamically constructs training batches by tracking the model&\#x27;s &quot;Zone of Proximal Development&quot; via online rollout accuracy\. Experiments demonstrate that AdaC\-GRPO enables Qwen2\.5\-VL\-7B to approach the performance of Gemini\-2\.5\-Flash while bridging 62\.2% of the overall accuracy gap to Gemini\-2\.5\-Pro, with its generalizability further validated across InternVL\-3\.5 and Qwen3\-VL\.
