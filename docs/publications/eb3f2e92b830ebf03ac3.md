# La educación y los saberes emergentes

**Autores:** Judeicy Lara

**Data:** 2026-10-13 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Grounded in the premise that the essence of education lies in presenting the world as a realm of possibility,this theoretical\-reflective essay critically examines the tension between the disruptive promis of digital technologies and the persistence of anach­ ronistic pedagogical paradigms\. This transformation rests upon three fundamental pillars: a pedagogy grounded in neurocognitive principles that respects neurodiversity and individual learning rhythms; a critical appropriation of techno\-educational models and artificial intelligence; and an overarching horizon oriented toward Education for Sustainable Development \(ESD\), which fosters citizens aware of their interdependence with the planet\. This study, which is focused on Latin America, reveals that technological adoption without such profound restructuring not only perpetuates existing divides, but may also exacerbate inequalities\.Received: 10/09/2026 \- Approved: 30/09/2026
