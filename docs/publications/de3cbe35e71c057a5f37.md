# Safety, Tolerability, Pharmacokinetics and Exploratory Pharmacodynamics of Melanin‐Concentrating Hormone Receptor‐1 Antagonist RGH‐706 in Healthy and Overweight or Obese Men

**Autores:** Thomas Brevig; Borbála Szepes; Z\.B\. Dombi; G\. Magyar; Zoltán Juhász; Margit Kapás; Éva Cseh; Zsuzsa Hegedűs; Tibor Farkás; Zsuzsanna Kiss; B\. Szatmári

**Data:** 2026-10-14 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

AIM: This Phase 1 program evaluated the safety, tolerability, pharmacokinetics and exploratory pharmacodynamics of the novel melanin\-concentrating hormone receptor\-1 antagonist RGH\-706\. METHODS: Study A was a first\-in\-man, randomized trial comprising a double\-blind, placebo\-controlled single\-ascending\-dose part \(0\.1\-162\.5 mg\) and a two\-way crossover food\-effect assessment at 62\.5 mg in healthy men\. Study B was a double\-blind, placebo\-controlled, single\- and multiple\-ascending\-dose study in overweight or obese \(otherwise healthy\) men receiving a single dose followed by 14 days of once\-daily dosing \(2\.5\-22\.5 mg/day\)\. Safety assessments included clinical evaluations, ECGs and mood and sleep scales\. RGH\-706 concentrations in plasma and urine samples were measured by validated methods\. Pharmacokinetics was characterized using non\-compartmental analysis\. Study B evaluated exploratory pharmacodynamic measures including appetite\-related outcomes, food preference, test\-meal food and macronutrient intake, body composition and metabolic biomarkers\. RESULTS: between 2\.5 and 3\.5 h and a bi\-exponential disposition with a 29\-35 h terminal half\-life\. Food ingestion had a negligible effect on exposure and renal clearance was also negligible\. In Study B \(n = 36\), RGH\-706 showed moderate accumulation with steady state reached within 8 days\. Exploratory pharmacodynamic measures showed a trend towards lower total energy intake, preference for fatty foods and weight gain, primarily with the lower doses of 2\.5 and 7\.5 mg/day\. RGH\-706 was well tolerated across all doses and conditions in both studies\. CONCLUSIONS: RGH\-706 was safe, well tolerated and exhibited well\-characterized pharmacokinetics\. The pharmacodynamic data support further exploration of RGH\-706 in weight management\.
