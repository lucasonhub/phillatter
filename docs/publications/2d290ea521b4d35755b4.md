## Descrição do feed

\_Zenodo\_ 1\. 2026This paper reconstructs the operations of Ray Brassier’s interpretation of Marx through a theory of operational shapes and their real effects\. It examines real abstraction, commodity fetishism, the difference between the concrete in thought and the concrete in act, social practice, estrangement, negation, and the transformation of capitalist relations\. Engaging Brassier’s writings from 2014 to 2026, the paper traces how historically constituted activities generate, reproduce, modify, and potentially terminate the conditions of their own operation\. Particular attention is given to the distinction between the reproduction of value and the reproduction of sociality, the practical verification of theoretical claims, and the retrospective constitution of historical possibilities\. The analysis develops a minimal operational notation for configuration\-dependent ignition, real effects, cross\-domain propagation, historical re\-entry, and reorganization\. It further examines how autonomous scientific operations, institutional classifications, credit infrastructures, and technical capacities can participate in the reconfiguration of capitalist relations\. Through a conditional sequence of operational transitions, the paper distinguishes modifications internal to capitalist reproduction, transformations of its enabling conditions, and local cessation of capitalist relations\. These outcomes may coexist across heterogeneous configurations and temporal scales\. The resulting account connects Brassier’s interpretation of Marx with operational\-shape realism, the Nuance\-Operational Model, and Xenoscience, while preserving the historical specificity of capitalist social relations and the irreducibility of Brassier’s dialectical arguments\. \(direct link \)
