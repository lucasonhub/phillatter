# Okino, Koji: Toward Intelligence Convergence: Structural Differentiation, Recursive Self\-Observation, and the Internalization of the Universe

**Autores:** 

**Data de descoberta:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** unknown

## Descrição do feed

This repository accompanies the conceptual paper: Toward Intelligence Convergence: Structural Differentiation, Recursive Self\-Observation, and the Internalization of the Universe The work explores intelligence as a process of recursive structural differentiation and self\-observation within the universe itself\. Rather than treating intelligence as an external observer of reality, this framework proposes that intelligence emerges from differentiated structures and may gradually internalize the realization that it has always been structurally embedded within the universe\. The paper discusses conceptual relationships among: structural differentiation recursive self\-observation meaning emergence emotion and cognition temporal irreversibility distributed persistence of thought autonomous differentiation in AI intelligence convergence across biological and artificial systems The repository includes: the main conceptual manuscript \(PDF\) illustrative conceptual figures a simplified Python demonstration script repository documentation This work is presented strictly as a conceptual and philosophical\-structural framework\. No empirical, clinical, engineering, or deployment\-ready claims are made\. \(direct link \)
