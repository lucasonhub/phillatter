# Association between a stepwise health education model based on evidence\-based practice and self\-care behaviors of patients with chronic heart failure: a single\-center retrospective cohort study

**Autores:** Yu Chang; Lili Wang; Xiaoqian Hao; Yajing Hu; Fujuan Sun

**Data:** 2026-10-14 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Objective This study aims to investigate the association between a stepwise health education model based on evidence\-based practice \(EBP\) and self\-care behaviors, cardiac function improvement, and clinical outcomes in patients with chronic heart failure \(CHF\)\. Methods A retrospective analysis was performed on the clinical data of 120 patients with CHF admitted to our hospital from January 2023 to December 2024\. The patients were divided into a control group \(CG, n = 62, receiving routine health education\) and an intervention group \(IG, n = 58, receiving stepwise health education based on EBP in addition to routine care\) according to the health education strategy received\. The 6\-month total score of the EHFScB\-9 was defined as the primary outcome\. Cardiac function indicators \(LVEF, BNP, and 6MWD\), biochemical indicators, clinical outcomes, individual scale items, and hospitalization\-related indicators were considered exploratory outcomes\. Factors associated with the occurrence of composite endpoint events were analyzed\. Results After 6 months of intervention, the IG exhibited significantly higher LVEF and 6MWD and significantly lower BNP level than the CG \( P &lt; 0\.05\)\. The serum sodium level in the IG was significantly higher than that in the CG \( P &lt; 0\.05\)\. Exploratory analyses showed that the nominal P values for the 30\-day, 90\-day, and 6\-month readmission rates, as well as the incidence of composite endpoint events, were all &lt;0\.05 in the IG\. The average length of stay and total hospitalization costs in the IG were significantly lower than those in the CG \( P &lt; 0\.05\); however, these findings were interpreted as exploratory\. At the 6\-month follow\-up, the IG exhibited lower total EHFScB\-9 scores and lower scores for individual items than the CG \( P &lt; 0\.05\), indicating better self\-care behaviors\. Comparisons of individual items were exploratory findings\. Multivariable analysis revealed that NYHA class III\-IV \(OR = 3\.765\), concomitant atrial fibrillation \(OR = 2\.440\), LVEF &lt; 40% \(OR = 3\.184\), and lack of stepwise health education based on EBP \(OR = 2\.607\) were associated with the composite endpoints\. Conclusion In this single\-center retrospective cohort study, analyses without adjustment for multiple comparisons suggested that a step health education model based on evidence\-based practice was associated with better self\-care behaviors, improved cardiac function, and lower rates of readmission and composite endpoint events\.
