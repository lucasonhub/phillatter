# Amlima, Mawuvi: EXECUTIVE SUMMARY: Proposal for the Nzila Spirit\-Tech Research Lab &amp; Institute

**Autores:** 

**Data de descoberta:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** unknown

## Descrição do feed

The Nzila Spirit\-Tech Research Lab &amp; Institute Executive Summary presents the founding vision for an African\-led research initiative dedicated to exploring the intersection of indigenous knowledge systems, consciousness studies, cosmology, ecological intelligence, and emerging technologies\. Grounded in the Nzila Spirit\-Tech Paradigm, the proposal outlines the Institute&\#x27;s mission, vision, institutional objectives, and long\-term research agenda\. It advocates an interdisciplinary framework where indigenous epistemologies and contemporary scientific inquiry can be investigated together with intellectual rigor, fostering innovative approaches to technological development while preserving Africa&\#x27;s rich knowledge heritage\. The document also introduces opportunities for strategic partnerships, research collaboration, and philanthropic support toward establishing a globally recognized research institution
