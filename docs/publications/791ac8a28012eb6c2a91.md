# Tracing the Cosmic Origins: Machine Learning Reconstruction of the Primordial Density Field from EoR Observations

**Autores:** Anchal Saxena; P\. Daniel Meerburg; Guochao Sun; Tzu‐Ching Chang; Lluís Mas\-Ribas

**Data:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Abstract Reconstructing the initial conditions of the Universe from late\-time tracers would unlock cosmological information buried by non\-linear structure formation and astrophysics\. We reconstruct the initial density field corresponding to z = 300, with its linear amplitude extrapolated to z = 0, from simulated 21\-cm and CO\(1–0\) line\-intensity maps at z = 8 generated with the semi\-numerical code, LIMFAST\. Using a three\-dimensional U\-Net, we reconstruct the initial conditions and evaluate their impact on cosmological parameter constraints\. The two tracers probe complementary environments: 21\-cm emission traces neutral, low\-density regions of the intergalactic medium, while CO traces overdense, star\-forming regions\. We additionally train and evaluate the reconstruction on maps incorporating SKA1\-Low– and COMAP\-ERA–like instrumental response and thermal noise\. In the noiseless case, combining both tracers delivers the most accurate reconstruction across ionisation states, with the cross\-correlation coefficient between the reconstructed and true initial density fields, \|C\(k\)\| ≳ 0\.90 for k ≲ 0\.75 Mpc−1\. With observational effects, small\-scale information is degraded, but the joint reconstruction retains substantial large\-scale coherence, with \|C\(k\)\| ≳ 0\.70 for k ≲ 0\.3 Mpc−1\. To quantify the cosmological information gain, we perform simulation\-based inference using power\-spectrum summaries pre\- and post\-reconstruction\. Across an ensemble of realisations, reconstruction yields the largest improvements for σ8 and ns, with a median aggregate marginal compression of 1\.17 bits in the noiseless case, corresponding to a 56% reduction in the factorised product of prior\-normalised 68% marginal widths\. With observational effects, the corresponding median compression is 1\.29 bits\. These results demonstrate that joint 21\-cm–CO reconstruction can recover cosmological information beyond that captured by the pre\-reconstruction summaries\.
