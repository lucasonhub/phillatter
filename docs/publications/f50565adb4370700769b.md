# Comparison of the Effectiveness of Ultrasound‐Guided Ozone and Platelet‐Rich Plasma Injections in Chronic Plantar Fasciitis

**Autores:** Burcu Özalp Horsanalı; Derya Güner

**Data:** 2026-10-12 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

BACKGROUND: Plantar fasciitis is a common cause of chronic heel pain, and ultrasound\-guided biological injection therapies are increasingly used in patients refractory to conservative management\. However, direct comparative data between platelet\-rich plasma \(PRP\) and ozone injections remain limited\. OBJECTIVE: To compare the short\-term clinical and functional outcomes of ultrasound\-guided PRP and ozone injections in patients with chronic plantar fasciitis\. METHODS: Patients over 18 years of age diagnosed with plantar fasciitis via physical examination and radiological assessment, experiencing plantar fascia pain for more than 6 months, and unresponsive to conservative treatments were included\. Visual Analog Scale scores and Foot Function Index scores, assessed at baseline and at 3\-month follow\-ups in the pain clinic, were retrospectively analysed and compared\. RESULTS: A total of 43 injections were evaluated, distributed as 21 in the ozone group and 22 in the PRP group\. Both groups demonstrated significant improvements in VAS and FFI scores at 3 months compared to baseline \(p &lt; 0\.001\)\. When between\-group differences were analysed, the magnitude of improvement in the FFI\-Activity subscale and total FFI score was significantly greater in the PRP group than in the ozone group \(p &lt; 0\.05\)\. No serious adverse events were observed\. CONCLUSIONS: Both PRP and ozone injections are effective in reducing pain in patients with chronic plantar fasciitis; however, greater functional improvement was observed in the PRP group, particularly in activity\-related domains\. LEVEL OF EVIDENCE: Level III, retrospective comparative study\.
