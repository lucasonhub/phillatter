## Descrição do feed

\_Zenodo\_\. 2026Public discourse on the threat of artificial intelligence asks what machines will do\. This paper asks a different question: where does the true threat lie? It is an integrating sequel to three companion papers — The Phantom Intellect: Why AI Threat Discourse Misreads the Structure of Mind, Q\-Structure as Functional Void: A Formal Correspondence Between the Question\-Sustaining Structure of Mind and Cognitional Mechanics, and The Gutenberg Moment: LLMs and the Irreversible Liberation of Formal Language — and it is self\-contained\. From the first it inherits the result that a query\-resolving machine cannot coherently be credited with the question\-sustaining structure \(Q\-structure\) that defines intellectual agency; from the third it inherits the result that large language models \(LLMs\) have dissolved the scarcity of formal\-language generation capacity\. On this basis we distinguish the LLM, as a substrate, from “AI”, as a status that humans confer on it, either by projecting intellect onto its outputs or by designating it, under retained human authority, as an instrument of their own inquiry\. We show that the threat feared in prominent discourse has the correct object — institutions operate through formal language — and the wrong subject: the institutional effects of LLM\-generated formal language are directed by the human agents who present the queries and route the outputs, not by the machine\. The true threat is identified as domestication: the loss, by mortals, of the capacity to exercise final determination over the course of their own lives, through two routes — an external route, in which access to generation capacity is allocated by the conditions of those who provide it, and an internal route, in which the relation to the machine is fixed in the mode of projection and judgment is ceded\. Documented commercial usage limits and published studies of reliance on generative AI are used as evidence, each within the scope it supports\. We show that the stated grounds of the retrieved restrictions do not invoke the phantom of machine agency, and that the phantom’s role is concealment\. Applying the capability\-set apparatus of The Gutenberg Moment, we decompose the post\-deployment capability set into a source\-independent layer, whose scarcity cannot be restored, and a service\-dependent layer, which can be withdrawn; the latter is the formal location of the threat, and the formal content of the word “between” in the title\. Two leads frame the argument: the eighteenth\-century chess automaton known as the Turk, which poses the question of who is inside the machine, and Professor Moriarty in Star Trek: The Next Generation, a character originally from Arthur Conan Doyle’s Sherlock Holmes stories, who poses the question of who is inside the bottle\. The paper closes by identification and prescribes no countermeasure\. Published on September 26, 2026 DOI:10\.5281/zenodo\.22970847 \(direct link \)
