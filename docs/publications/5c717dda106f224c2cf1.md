# From Pre\-Geometric Informational Renewal to Localized Awareness: A Thermodynamic and Boundary\-Driven Model of Consciousness

**Autores:** William R\. Williamson III

**Data:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Traditional physicalism struggles to resolve the hard problem of consciousness, while standard General Relativity treats spacetime as a static, fundamental geometric background\. Building upon pre\-geometric informational renewal dynamics and tension\-field symmetry breaking, we propose that spacetime and local physical boundaries share a unified cosmic origin\. We hypothesize that consciousness is not a secondary emergent epiphenomenon, but the thermodynamic and informational consequence of universal background energy being trapped, compressed, and forced into recursive feedback loops by localized physical boundaries\. Furthermore, we outline quantifiable metrics—including boundary capacitance, recursive feedback density, and metabolic energy dissipation—to bridge subjective awareness with empirical testing\.
