# PREZIDENT MAKTABLARIDA O‘QUVCHILARNING INDIVIDUAL TA’LIM TRAYEKTORIYALARINI BOSHQARISH MEXANIZMLARI

**Autores:** Allanazarova Dilnoza Saidovna

**Data:** 2026-10-09 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

The article analyses the theoretical, legal, and methodological foundations for managing students’ individual learning pathways in Presidential Schools\. An individual learning pathway is interpreted as a course of learning that evolves in accordance with a student’s preparedness, interests, educational needs, and long\-term goals\. A comparative analysis examines individual learning plans in Finland, flexible subject\-based learning practices in Singapore, and recommendations on metacognitive learning in England\. Drawing on national legislation, the article substantiates mechanisms for diagnostic assessment, consultation, resource coordination, and formative assessment\.
