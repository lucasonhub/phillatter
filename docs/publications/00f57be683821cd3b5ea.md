# Medication Safety in the Era of Digital Health: A Comprehensive Review

**Autores:** 

**Data:** 2026-10-14 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Medication safety remains a major priority in contemporary healthcare systems because medication\-related harm can occur across the entire medication\-use process, including prescribing, dispensing, administration, monitoring, and patient self\-management\.Digital health technologies have created new opportunities to reduce preventable medication errors and improve pharmaceutical care\.This comprehensive narrative review examines the role of electronic prescribing, computerized provider order entry, clinical decision support systems, artificial intelligence, generative artificial intelligence, digital pharmacovigilance, telepharmacy, mobile health applications, barcode medication administration, smart infusion pumps, automated dispensing systems, and closed\-loop medication management in improving medication safety\.The review also provides a critical appraisal of evidence, highlighting that many digital interventions reduce process\-related errors, whereas fewer studies demonstrate consistent reductions in severe patient\-centered outcomes\.Digital medication safety is best understood as a sociotechnical process requiring pharmacist expertise, workflow integration, high\-quality data, clinical governance, cybersecurity, patient engagement, and continuous evaluation\.Pharmacists have a central role in validating prescriptions, optimizing alerts, interpreting artificial intelligence outputs, maintaining smart pump libraries, supporting telepharmacy, improving pharmacovigilance, and educating patients\.Digital technologies can strengthen medication safety when they support, rather than replace, professional judgment and patient\-centered care\.
