# Human\-AI Collaboration and Organizational Adaptation: Insights from Knowledge Workers in Banten, Indonesia

**Autores:** Danish Akbar Firdausy Asbari

**Data:** 2026-10-14 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

This qualitative descriptive study explores how Human\-AI collaboration shapes organizational adaptation among knowledge workers in Banten, Indonesia\. Using multi\-case inquiry with 20 purposively selected participants comprising knowledge workers, middle managers, senior managers, and digital transformation leaders, this research employed semi\-structured interviews, organizational document analysis, and observation protocols\. Thematic analysis revealed four critical patterns: \(1\) AI integration reshapes collaborative workflows through augmentation rather than replacement; \(2\) knowledge work transformation emerges through new competency demands and skill reconfiguration; \(3\) organizational learning accelerates through human\-AI knowledge co\-creation; and \(4\) adaptive organizational practices evolve through dynamic capability reconfiguration\. The study contributes theoretically by extending dynamic capability theory, organizational adaptation theory, and knowledge\-based view through an emerging economy lens, demonstrating that Human\-AI collaboration functions as a mechanism for organizational adaptation when complemented by deliberate knowledge management systems, adaptive leadership, and human\-centric learning cultures\. Practically, findings offer strategic guidance for organizational leaders, human resource managers, and policymakers navigating AI\-driven transformation in knowledge\-intensive sectors\. This research uniquely addresses the gap between AI adoption studies and organizational adaptation processes, providing nuanced understanding of Human\-AI collaboration in an emerging economy context where institutional support and digital literacy remain evolving\. The novelty lies in positioning Human\-AI collaboration as central to organizational adaptation rather than treating it as a peripheral technological implementation concern, with particular relevance to organizations in Southeast Asian emerging markets\.
