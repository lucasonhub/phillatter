# RECURRENT BENIGN MELANOCYTIC NEVI AFTER REMOVAL: A RETROSPECTIVE SINGLE\-CENTER STUDY OF RISK FACTORS

**Autores:** Ye\.L\. Torbenko; Viktor I\. Litus; Юлия Валерьевна Щербакова

**Data:** 2026-10-17 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Aim\. To determine the incidence of recurrent nevus after removal of benign melanocytic nevi and to evaluate the influence of clinical, dermoscopic, morphological, and treatment\-related factors on recurrence risk\.Materials and methods\. A retrospective single\-center analytical study with passive follow\-up was conducted\. A total of 339 cases of benign melanocytic nevi removed between May 2020 and May 2025 were included\. The mean patient age was 36\.7±11\.9 years; 234 patients \(69\.0%\) were female and 105 \(31\.0%\) were male\. Sex, age, lesion localization, diameter, dermoscopic characteristics, histological subtype, treatment method, and recurrence rate were analyzed\. Statistical analysis was performed using Pearson’s χ² test and Student’s t\-test\.Results\. Recurrent nevus was identified in 45 of 339 cases \(13\.3%\)\. The mean time to recurrence was 5\.24±1\.05 months\. Patients with recurrence were significantly younger than those without recurrence \(29\.89±13\.46 years vs\. 37\.71±11\.27 years; p=0\.00005\)\. No statistically significant associations were found between recurrence and sex, lesion localization, lesion diameter, dermoscopic pattern, histological subtype, structural organization, or treatment method \(p&gt;0\.05\)\.Conclusions\. The incidence of recurrent nevus after removal of benign melanocytic nevi was 13\.3%\. Most recurrences developed within six months after treatment\. Younger age was the only statistically significant factor associated with recurrence\.
