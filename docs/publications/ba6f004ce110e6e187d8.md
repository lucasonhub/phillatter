# BOSHLANG‘ICH SINF O‘QUVCHILARIDA MUSTAQIL O‘QISH KO‘NIKMASINI SHAKLLANTIRISHNING BOSQICHLI METODIK MODELI

**Autores:** JO&\#x27;RABEK NORMENGOV

**Data:** 2026-10-13 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

This article examines independent reading instruction in primary school literacy lessons\. It aims to develop a staged model connecting task complexity, instructional support, and assessment criteria\. Theoretical approaches and instructional strategies were examined through qualitative analysis, comparison, and pedagogical modelling\. The resulting framework specifies progression across Grades One to Four, a text analysis sequence, and separate indicators for comprehension and independence\. It emphasises evidence, planning, and self\-checking\. The study is theoretical and methodological; effectiveness requires empirical testing\. Classroom tasks are presented as illustrative teaching materials\.
