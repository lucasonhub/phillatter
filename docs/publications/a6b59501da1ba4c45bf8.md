# Developing GenAI\-Based Feedback Through Iterative Prompt Design: An Exploratory Study of Online Students’ Cognitive, Affective, and Behavioral Perceptions

**Autores:** Meva Bayrak Karsli; Sinem Çilligöl Karabey; Muhammed Guler; Ömer Arpacık; Melike Aydemir Arslan; Engin Kurşun; Ebubekir Kaba

**Data:** 2026-10-15 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

This study reports the iterative prompt\-engineering development of Generative Artificial Intelligence \(GenAI\)\-based feedback in an online graduate course and examines students’ cognitive, affective, and behavioral perceptions\. Grounded in the Student–Feedback Interaction Model, a qualitative case study involved 14 graduate students in a 14\-week fully online course on open and distance learning\. Students completed forum\-based formative activities and received instructor\-reviewed GenAI feedback\. Informed by expert review, instructor observations, and student reflections, prompts evolved through three stages: checklist\-style criterion verification, criteria\-anchored synthesized explanations, and personalized diagnosis of strengths and weaknesses\. Semi\-structured interviews were analyzed through qualitative content analysis\. Students reported improved feedback comprehensibility, detail, tone, and timeliness, valuing personalized, explanatory, criterion\-based comments\. However, inconsistencies between AI comments and instructor scores, limited conceptual evaluation, and lack of emotional presence weakened trust\. GenAI therefore functions best as a draft\-generation layer requiring sustained, active instructor oversight rather than as a stand\-alone source of formative feedback\.
