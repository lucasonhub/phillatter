# Deepfake and Intellectual Property Rights: And Its Emergence Legal Challenges

**Autores:** Vandana Preyshi

**Data:** 2026-10-17 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

The developing nature of deepfake technology requires versatile response in the implementation of law uses in technological measures and cross\-boarder cooperation to uphold the rights of the particular in the era of internet\. These technologies have not only revolutionized the generation it also created a possible threat to the privacy and security of the people\. AI uses term LLM\(Large Language Models\) for the purpose to generate almost exact replicas of the original video and vocal, which is fun in the private provinces, when it is pernicious I become even more dangerous when used in common or public spheres\.The term deepfake is not defined in the Indian statutes or by the courts\. This technology involves two components that is a &quot;generator&quot; that creates fake \(images or videos\) &quot;discriminator&quot; that evaluates the authenticity of these Creations\. Courts uses the meaning that is &quot;Any type of AI\-generated audio, video, image content that influence or convincingly replicates the likeness, voice or performance of real person&quot;\. Deepfake technology is a form of synthetic media created using artificial intelligence \(AI\) it created for the purpose of significant legal, ethical, social concern and its emergence\. In India, where digital media and social platforms are widely used, deepfake technology has raised serious concerns, about a nonconsensual pornography content, where a person&\#x27;s face is superimposed onto sexually explicit material without their knowledge or consent, it is problematic for women\.
