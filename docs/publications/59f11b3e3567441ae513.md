# Wentzell, Alexander: What is the Format of Pre\-Attentive Vision?

**Autores:** 

**Data de descoberta:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** unknown

## Descrição do feed

\_The Philosophical Quarterly\_\. forthcomingA current debate in the philosophy of cognitive science centers around the representational format of visual processing prior to the deployment of focused visual attention\. Recent work has argued that pre\-attentive vision’s properties imply it represents in a map\-like format\. This argument is based on the empirical phenomena of conjunctive illusions and conjunction searches\. In this paper, I argue instead that we should adopt an iconic view of pre\-attentive vision, where it represents in a picture\-like format\. I’ll first argue that the map\-like view cannot accommodate a certain type of phenomenon in the pre\-attentive vision literature: gist perception\. I argue that gist perception implies a holistically bound format and that this favors the iconic view\. Second, I’ll argue that conjunctive illusions and conjunction search can be explained by means other than positing map\-like representations in early vision\.
