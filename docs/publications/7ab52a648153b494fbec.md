# Pāli Lexicon Nītattha and Neyyattha Sakaaya Nirutti Canon Interpreting Canon Sutta\-to\-Sutta Knowledge Graph Dhamma\-Aligned AI 5\. License \(බලපතරය\) License: Creative Commons Attribution 4\.0 International \(CC\-BY\-4\.0\)

**Data:** 2026-10-14 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Contemporary artificial intelligence models and computational linguistic tools systematically distort Pāli canonical texts due to training data bias, over\-reliance on Sanskritized etymologies, and a lack of hermeneutic awareness\. This paper introduces an Automated Canon\-Driven Exegesis Framework that resolves these distortions by embedding the Nītattha \(ultimate, direct truth\) versus Neyyattha \(conventional, metaphorical\) distinction directly into a filtering pipeline and a structured JSON Knowledge Base\. Grounded exclusively in the Pāli\-Sinhala Dictionary by Madiṭiyawela Sri Sumangala Thera \(1965\) and primary Pāli Suttas \(such as the Nītattha/Neyyattha Suttas, Cūḷasaccaka Sutta, Vasala Sutta, Valāhaka Sutta, and Bhūmicāla Sutta\), this architecture prevents secular and Brahmanical appropriations, ensuring that Pāli terms are interpreted strictly through Sakaaya Nirutti \(intrinsic meaning\)\.
