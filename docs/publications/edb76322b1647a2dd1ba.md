## Descrição do feed

In this wide\-ranging study, Levine explores both sides of the mind\-body dilemma, presenting the first book\-length treatment of his highly influential ideas on how one explains the physical nature of an experience\. This puzzle, the &\#x27;explanatory gap&\#x27; between mind and body, is the focus of this work by an influential scholar in the field\. \(direct link \)
