# Prediction of flow maldistribution in 3D\-printed water\-flow distributors for thermal\-fluid system design

**Autores:** Nibras M\. Mahdi; Jenan S\. Sherza; Zainab Talib Al\-Sharify; Amar S\. Abdul‐Zahra; Luttfi Ahmed Al\-Haddad; Ahmed Ali Farhan Ogaili

**Data:** 2026-10-09 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Flow distributors are important components in water\-flow and compact thermal\-fluid systems because non\-uniform flow division among parallel channels can disturb pressure balance and convective heat\-transfer stability\. However, evaluating distributor performance through repeated experimental trials and Computational Fluid Dynamics \(CFD\) simulations can be time\-consuming during early\-stage design\. This work develops an Artificial Intelligence \(AI\)\-assisted framework for predicting and classifying flow maldistribution in 3D\-printed water\-flow distributors using combined experimental and CFD\-supported data\. The dataset was obtained from water\-flow measurements of rectangular distributors, conical distributors with an obstacle, and conical distributors without obstacle, while the CFD\-supported dataset included Volume of Fluid \(VOF\) and single\-phase flow results for selected conical configurations\. Distributor family, height, flow rate, data source, and simulation method were used as input variables, while the non\-uniformity flow coefficient, Φ, was used as the prediction target\. An Extreme Gradient Boosting \(XGBoost\) regression model was developed to estimate Φ, and a classification layer was used to assign maldistribution severity levels\. The model achieved coefficient of determination \( R 2 \) = 0\.962, Mean Absolute Error \(MAE\) = 0\.126, Root Mean Square Error \(RMSE\) = 0\.161, Mean Absolute Percentage Error \(MAPE\) = 4\.87%, and Coefficient of Variation of Root Mean Square Error \(CVRMSE\) = 6\.12%\. The model achieved accuracy = 92\.22%, precision = 92\.90%, recall = 92\.22%, and F1\-score = 92\.36%\. The results showed that conical distributors generally produced lower maldistribution than rectangular distributors, while the AI model accurately captured both numerical Φ behavior and severity trends\. The framework provides a tool to screen distributor designs and support CFD\-guided water\-flow and thermal\-fluid system development\.
