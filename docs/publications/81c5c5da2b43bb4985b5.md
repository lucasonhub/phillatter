# Extracellular matrix remodeling and regulated cell\-death\-immune associations in adhesive capsulitis: a three\-cohort transcriptomic analysis

**Autores:** Lin Huang; Ling Cheng; Jin He; Rui Zhang; Xia Li; Qiurong Xia

**Data:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Background Adhesive capsulitis \(AC\) is a fibroinflammatory disorder characterized by painful shoulder restriction and progressive capsular fibrosis\. Whether regulated cell\-death \(RCD\) programs are consistently altered across independent transcriptomic cohorts remains uncertain\. Methods We reanalyzed three public bulk RNA\-seq cohorts\. GSE140731 was the discovery cohort \(22 AC and 26 shoulder\-instability surgical controls\), and GSE190023 and GSE238053 were independent validation cohorts\. Differential expression was estimated from filtered raw counts with PyDESeq2, with age/sex adjustment in GSE140731\. Curated pyroptosis, ferroptosis, and necroptosis gene sets; immune marker–gene scores; pathway enrichment; STRING network structure; cross\-cohort concordance; an exploratory three\-gene classifier; and qPCR validation of four selected genes in TGF\-β1\-stimulated SW982 cells were evaluated\. Results The adjusted discovery analysis identified 439 upregulated and 113 downregulated genes at FDR &lt; 0\.05\. ECM–receptor interaction was strongly positively enriched \(NES = 2\.299, q = 0\.001\), making extracellular\-matrix remodeling the clearest pathway\-level signal\. RCD findings were mixed: pyroptosis was not different \(delta = −0\.105, q = 0\.708\), ferroptosis was lower but borderline after FDR correction \(delta = −0\.426, q = 0\.056\), and necroptosis was lower \(delta = −0\.320, q = 0\.035\); the core necroptosis gene set was also negatively enriched \(NES = −1\.673, q = 0\.038\)\. Six immune marker scores were FDR\-significant in discovery, but none replicated at cohort\-wise FDR significance in the validation cohorts\. Cross\-cohort gene\-effect concordance was heterogeneous \(Spearman rho = −0\.047 for GSE190023 and 0\.383 for GSE238053\)\. The COL11A1–SPON2–PITPNC1 classifier achieved a pooled out\-of\-fold AUC of 0\.820 \(95% CI, 0\.684–0\.930\) in GSE140731; external\-cohort AUCs ranged from 0\.750 to 0\.812 with wide confidence intervals, and non\-nested feature selection precluded claims of clinical utility\. qPCR showed higher COL11A1 and SPON2 and lower PLA2G4A expression after TGF\-β1 stimulation, whereas PITPNC1 did not differ significantly, providing model\-specific support without establishing causal or clinical validity\. Conclusion Across the three cohorts, ECM remodeling was the most reproducible feature of AC, whereas RCD\- and immune\-related signals were cohort\-dependent and did not support uniform activation of pyroptosis, ferroptosis, or necroptosis\. The classifier and RCD signatures remain hypothesis\-generating\. The SW982 qPCR results provide model\-specific experimental support for selected candidates, but broader functional validation in primary AC tissues and cells is required\.
