# Design of an IOT\-Based Digital Rice Weighing System \(Smart Rice\-Ws\) to Improve Efficiency and Accuracy in Supporting Smart Village Economic Development

**Autores:** Yayak Kartika Sari; Joko Iskandar; Nailariza Umami

**Data:** 2026-10-10 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Rice weighing in village\-scale rice industries is generally still performed manually, resulting in dependence on operators, potential recording errors, and limited access to real\-time weighing information\. This study aims to formulate and develop the initial concept of SMART RICE\-WS \(Smart Rice Weighing System\), an Internet of Things \(IoT\)\-based digital rice weighing system designed to improve the efficiency, accuracy, and traceability of the weighing process\. The research employs a Design\-Based Research Methodology \(DBR\) through iterative stages of analysis, design, development, implementation, and evaluation\. The research activities include existing process analysis, literature review and state\-of\-the\-art analysis, user requirement identification, system architecture design, algorithm development, simulation module implementation, and initial testing\. The proposed system integrates a Load Cell, HX711, ESP32, actuator, LCD, IoT communication, database/cloud storage, and monitoring dashboard\. Initial testing was conducted through ten measurements using reference loads of 1 kg and 2 kg\. The results showed that the system was able to measure both loads, with an average absolute error of 1\.55%\. These initial findings indicate that the Load Cell–HX711–ESP32 module can serve as a foundation for further system development\. Subsequent development will focus on calibration, repeated testing, actuator integration, and IoT\-based monitoring\.
