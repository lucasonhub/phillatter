# Leveraging Zakat as a Multilayered Interest\-Free Financing Framework for Entrepreneurial Empowerment

**Autores:** Rashed Jahangır

**Data:** 2026-10-17 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Zakat, a core pillar of Islam, is a religious duty requiring the redistribution of wealth to eight eligible categories \(asnaf\) specified in the Qur&\#x27;an\. Beyond its redistributive role, Zakat has significant potential to alleviate poverty and promote broader socioeconomic empowerment when managed effectively\. This study introduces the Jellyfish Zakat Model, a layered, interest\-free fund system that combines structured fund pooling with Islamic social finance to direct Zakat toward immediate relief and sustainable nano\-entrepreneurship\. The model operates through cyclic, layered fund pools that transfer Zakat from local \(micro\) to national \(macro\) levels in a “jellyfish\-like&quot; pattern\. Each layer allocates a large share to urgent aid for disadvantaged groups and a smaller share to fund nano\-businesses for vulnerable individuals, helping them achieve self\-sufficiency\. This approach aligns with Shariah objectives of wealth sharing and social justice, fostering financial inclusion and sustainable development\. Methodologically, the paper uses conceptual models and an illustrative distribution analysis based on secondary macroeconomic data from Bangladesh\. Results reveal that, with full collection, the framework could support around 3\.54 million nano\-enterprises annually and deliver direct aid to about 53 million households\. Even with a conservative 20% collection rate, over 700,000 nano\-enterprises could benefit\. The study also explores conditions needed for success, including regulatory reform, Islamic financial literacy, and multi\-layered governance\. It discusses policy implications for Islamic finance, highlighting that a well\-structured, multi\-tier Zakat system could transform obligatory charity into a powerful tool for socioeconomic growth among vulnerable populations\.
