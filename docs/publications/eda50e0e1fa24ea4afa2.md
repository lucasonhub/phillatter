# O impacto do implante coclear na função cognitiva da pessoa idosa: uma revisão narrativa

**Autores:** Helena Gabriela Fiúsa Alves; Graça Cristina Cardoso De Oliveira

**Data:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Population ageing is associated with an increased prevalence of chronic conditions, including hearing loss and cognitive decline\. Age\-related hearing loss is one of the most common sensory impairments among older adults and significantly affects communication, autonomy, quality of life, and social participation\. In recent years, hearing loss has been recognized as an important modifiable risk factor for cognitive decline and dementia\. This narrative review aimed to analyse the role of cochlear implantation in preventing or mitigating cognitive decline in older adults with severe\-to\-profound hearing loss\. A literature review was conducted using major scientific databases, focusing on studies published between 2015 and 2025\. The findings suggest that cochlear implantation is associated with significant improvements in speech perception, communication, quality of life, and social participation\. Benefits have also been reported in several cognitive domains, including working memory, attention, processing speed, executive functions, and verbal fluency\. Potential mechanisms include reduced sensory deprivation, decreased cognitive load, and enhanced social engagement\. Although methodological limitations remain, current evidence supports the importance of early identification of hearing loss and timely auditory rehabilitation, including cochlear implantation, as a potential strategy to promote healthy ageing and preserve cognitive function\.
