# MAKTAB MASLAHATCHILARIDA KASBIY KUYISH SINDROMINING OLDINI OLISH VA STRESSGA BARDOSHLILIKNI RIVOJLANTIRISHNING PSIXOLOGIK MEXANIZMLARI

**Autores:** MARJONA TOLIB QIZI TOLIBOVA

**Data:** 2026-10-10 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

This article presents a scientific and theoretical analysis of the psychological factors contributing to professional burnout syndrome among school counselors, its characteristics associated with emotional exhaustion, reduced professional motivation, and depletion of personal resources, as well as the psychological mechanisms underlying the development of stress resilience\. Furthermore, the article examines psychological prevention approaches aimed at promoting school counselors&\#x27; psychological well\-being, reducing the negative consequences of emotional strain, strengthening emotional self\-regulation skills, and preventing professional burnout syndrome\.
