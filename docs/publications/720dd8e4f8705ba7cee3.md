# In silico Screening of Indonesian Coffee Bioactive Compounds as Potential CDK4\-Binding Ligands: An Integrated Molecular Docking and Drug\-Likeness Analysis

**Autores:** Fachrurrazie Fachrurrazie; Lilis Sulistiawaty; Joko Untung; Lutfi Anshory; Raden Haryo Bimo Setiarto

**Data:** 2026-10-15 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

The cyclin\-dependent kinase 4 \(CDK4\)/cyclin D1 signaling axis is a key regulator of G1\-to\-S phase cell\-cycle progression and an established therapeutic target in hormone receptor\-positive \(HR\+\)/human epidermal growth factor receptor 2\-negative \(HER2−\) breast cancer\. Although CDK4/6 inhibitors have improved clinical outcomes, acquired resistance and treatment\-associated adverse effects highlight the need to explore additional chemical scaffolds with potential CDK4\-targeting properties\. This study aimed to computationally screen Indonesian coffee\-derived bioactive compounds for their potential to interact with human CDK4\. A curated library of 133 coffee\-associated bioactive compounds was evaluated using molecular docking against the high\-resolution crystal structure of human CDK4 in an inactive conformation \(PDB ID: 2W9Z; 2\.3 Å resolution\)\. Among the screened compounds, 5\-O\-caffeoylquinic acid showed the most favorable docking score \(−6\.3 kcal/mol\)\. Two\-dimensional interaction analysis indicated that these compounds interacted with residues in the CDK4 binding region, including hydrogen\-bond interactions with Asp159, Asn24, and His163, as well as hydrophobic contacts with Val27 and neighboring residues\. Drug\-likeness assessment showed that the two top\-ranked compounds met several criteria of Lipinski’s Rule of Five\. However, their low lipophilicity \(logP = −0\.65\) and relatively high topological polar surface area \(TPSA = 164\.75 Å²\) may affect membrane permeability and pharmacokinetic behavior\. Ramachandran analysis indicated that the CDK4 structure retained acceptable stereochemical quality in the structural model used for the computational analysis\. Overall, 5\-O\-caffeoylquinic acid was prioritized as a potential CDK4\-binding scaffold from Indonesian coffee bioactives\. These computational findings provide a structural basis for further biochemical and cellular studies to determine their actual CDK4 inhibitory activity, binding affinity, selectivity, and anticancer potential\.
