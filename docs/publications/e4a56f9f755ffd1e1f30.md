# Lean Manufacturing and Industry 5\.0

**Autores:** S\. Vinodh

**Data:** 2026-10-12 · **Tipo:** book

## Resumo

In today’s rapidly evolving business landscape, organizations are facing numerous challenges when it comes to implementing Lean and Industry 5\.0 technologies\. This book aims to provide decision support for technology selection and assess organizational readiness for Industry 5\.0 implementation, making it an essential guide for managers seeking to navigate these complexities\. Lean Manufacturing and Industry 5\.0: Concepts, Technologies, and Methods includes a detailed examination of the principles and concepts of Lean and Industry 5\.0, insights into Industry 5\.0 technologies and their selection within the context of Lean practices, strategies for performance measurement, and an analysis of workforce attributes crucial for successful Lean and Industry 5\.0 implementation\. Through a combination of theoretical frameworks, practical examples, and case studies, this book explores the core concepts and advancements of Lean and Industry 5\.0, offering valuable insights for practitioners looking to enhance their understanding and application of these methodologies\. This book offers a comprehensive guide to Lean and Industry 5\.0 for professionals, students, and practitioners in vendor development, industrial automation, and production planning\. It provides management executives, CIOs, CEOs, IT professionals, and academics with essential insights to navigate modern business environments\.
