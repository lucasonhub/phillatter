# KEBIJAKAN PEMERINTAH KABUPATEN PIDIE TERHADAP EFEKTIVITAS BANTUAN SOSIAL \(Studi Pada Dinas Sosial Kabupaten Pidie\)

**Autores:** Riska Riska; Ricky Muliawan Hansyar; Zulkifli Zulkifli

**Data:** 2026-10-10 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

ABSTRACTSocial assistance is a government policy aimed at improving the welfare of poor and vulnerable communities by helping them meet their basic needs\. In its implementation, the Social Affairs Office of Pidie Regency continues to face several challenges, including inaccurate targeting of beneficiaries, limited data updating, and insufficient transparency, which affect the overall effectiveness of the program\. This study aims to analyze the effectiveness of the implementation of social assistance policies by the Social Affairs Office of Pidie Regency and to identify the factors influencing their effectiveness\. This research employed a descriptive qualitative approach using observation, interviews, and documentation as data collection techniques\. The findings indicate that the implementation of social assistance has generally been effective, as reflected in the distribution of assistance based on applicable regulations, the use of verified beneficiary data, and coordination among the Social Affairs Office, sub\-district administrations, and village governments\. Factors influencing effectiveness include the accuracy of beneficiary data, inter\-agency coordination, the quality of human resources, and administrative management\. Meanwhile, the main obstacles include changes in the socio\-economic conditions of the community, budget limitations, and the existence of eligible beneficiaries who have not yet been registered\. Therefore, improving data accuracy, transparency, monitoring, regular data updates, and institutional coordination is essential to ensure that social assistance is distributed more accurately, fairly, and effectively\.Keywords: Government Policy, Effectiveness, Social Assistance, Social Affairs Office of Pidie Regency\.
