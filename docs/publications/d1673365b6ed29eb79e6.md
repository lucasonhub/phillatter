# Climate policies and adaptive capacities: a cross\-analysis of sectoral, intersectoral, and international actions

**Autores:** Jean\-Marie Gankou Fowagap; Magali Duchel Mandio Abomo

**Data:** 2026-10-09 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Against intensifying climate risks, this study compares the associations of sectoral, cross\-sectoral, and international climate policies with annual changes in adaptation readiness\. Using an unbalanced panel of 59 countries from 2002 to 2022, it estimates OLS and SUR models with policy–income interactions and assesses robustness through Mundlak and dynamic GMM specifications\. Cross\-sectoral policies display the most consistent positive association with readiness improvements, although Wald tests show no statistically significant difference between their coefficients and those of sectoral policies\. The two approaches therefore appear complementary rather than substitutable\. International policies are mainly associated with improvements in lower\-income countries, and their contribution weakens as income rises\. The findings emphasize cross\-sectoral coordination, sector\-specific expertise, sustained implementation, and strategies adapted to national economic and institutional capacities\.
