# The Cost of Order: Entropy, Consciousness, and a Cosmic Meliorism\.

**Autores:** Henri Baskorvil

**Data:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** book

## Resumo

This book develops and revises arguments concerning cosmology, thermodynamics, artificial intelligence, and philosophy, defending a position termed &quot;cosmic meliorism\.&quot; It draws upon essays in the philosophy of cosmology, technology, and hope, exploring topics ranging from the limits of knowledge and cosmological fine\-tuning to the cost of order and the future of human societies\.
