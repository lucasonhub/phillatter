# Machine learning\-based prediction formulas for buckling resistance of welding\-strengthened circular steel tubes

**Autores:** Jiafeng Lyu; Su Yan; Shen Yan; Shen Yan; Shen Yan; Xianzhong Zhao

**Data:** 2026-10-10 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Welding curved cover plates is commonly used to strengthen circular hollow section \(CHS\) members under service loads, but welding heat input can induce lateral deflection and complex post\-strengthening stress states that reduce the buckling resistance\. This study develops machine\-learning \(ML\)\-based prediction models and a practical buckling\-resistance prediction formula for such members\. A database of 800 finite\-element \(FE\) samples, derived from 400 structural configurations, was generated using validated sequentially coupled thermo\-mechanical analyses covering service loading, welding, cooling, and post\-strengthening loading\. K\-nearest neighbours \(KNN\), adaptive boosting \(AdaBoost\), artificial neural networks \(ANN\), and deep and cross networks \(DCN\) were trained to predict the stability factor, welding\-induced deflection amplification factor, and post\-strengthening compressive stress level\. Among them, DCN showed the best overall performance, with test\-set R 2 values exceeding 0\.992 for all three targets\. SHapley Additive exPlanations \(SHAP\) identified post\-strengthening normalised slenderness \( λ ns \), service load level \( α p \), the diameter\-to\-cover\-plate\-thickness ratio \(2 γ s \), and original\-member yield strength \( f y0 \) as the governing features\. Higher α p and lower 2 γ s and f y0 increased deformation and compressive stress, thereby reducing buckling resistance\. Based on these relationships, an explicit resistance prediction formula was established by combining a service\-load\-induced resistance reduction factor with column\-curve selection criteria\. The proposed formula achieved a mean predicted\-to\-FE resistance ratio of 0\.952 and a MAPE of 6\.03%, outperforming an existing semi\-empirical model and code provisions\.
