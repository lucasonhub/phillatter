# When Does Muon Actually Win? A Paired Data\-, Time\-, and Tuning\-Budget Benchmark

**Autores:** Mushir Akhtar; Hazem N\. Nounou; Majdi Mansouri; Mohamed N\. Nounou

**Data:** 2026-10-15 · **Tipo:** preprint

## Resumo

Muon&\#x27;s gains in data efficiency motivate evaluating its performance under explicit data, time, and tuning budgets\. On a single A100 GPU, we compare a reference single\-device Muon implementation with AdamW across 17\.7M\- and 33\.6M\-parameter causal language models and 2\.70M\-parameter vision transformers\. A common 23\-candidate development budget selects configurations evaluated with five matched seeds, full validation trajectories, optimizer\-step timing, and a frozen one\-shot test\. Muon reduces validation\-loss area under the curve by 3\.25\-\-22\.44\\% at equal data and lowers test loss in all 20 primary pairs\. In this configuration, Muon improves time\-normalized loss area\} by 19\.68\-\-22\.31\\% on vision, while AdamW is better by 3\.38\-\-6\.96\\% on language\. The full Muon optimizer call, including auxiliary AdamW updates, consumes 56\.1\-\-69\.6\\% of training time, compared with 6\.1\-\-9\.1\\% for the AdamW baseline\. Muon also has higher expected regret under restricted access to these candidate pools\. These results characterize small models with reference kernels; their wall\-clock ordering does not establish a ranking for larger models or optimized implementations\.
