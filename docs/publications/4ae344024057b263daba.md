# Artificial Intelligence\-Supported Cultural Art Practices: A Bibliometric Analysis in the Field of Fine Arts

**Autores:** Sema Kara

**Data:** 2026-10-11 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

This study aims to evaluate the international literature on artificial intelligence\-supported cultural art practices through bibliometric analysis\. A total of 37 studies retrieved from the Web of Science and Scopus databases in accordance with the PRISMA flow diagram and meeting the inclusion criteria were included in the analysis\. VOSviewer software was used for data analysis, and the examined studies were also subjected to thematic classification based on content analysis\. The findings show that publication production in the field increased markedly, especially in 2024 and 2025, that a limited but growing impact network with a total of 109 citations has emerged, and that the highest citations were received by studies examining cross\-cultural audience perception\. The institutional and country\-based distribution displays a broad but low\-density structure, while production is concentrated in China\-based research groups\. The conceptual structure has evolved over time from technically oriented concepts toward cultural and interdisciplinary themes; the studies were grouped under three main themes: preservation of cultural heritage, artificial intelligence\-supported artistic production, and cultural experience/social perception\. It was determined that the literature predominantly focuses on production processes, whereas reception and social impact dimensions are relatively underrepresented\. It is recommended that future research be expanded to include different databases and languages, that studies focusing on audience perception and social acceptance be increased, that research on the preservation of cultural heritage be deepened around local aesthetic authenticity, that international collaboration networks be strengthened, and that multidimensional frameworks integrating the ethical, legal, and pedagogical dimensions of artificial intelligence\-supported art be developed\.
