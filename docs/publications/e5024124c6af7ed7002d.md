# Influência das condições de bem\-estar na performance reprodutiva da raça Nelore \(Bos indicus\)

**Autores:** Vanessa Tirado Quaglio; Melina Marie Yasuoka

**Data:** 2026-10-12 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

The Brazilian beef cattle industry relies heavily on Nellore cattle \(Bos indicus\), whose adaptability and hardiness are contrasted by behavioral reactivity to human handling, representing worse reproductive results for reproductive efficiency\. This literature review aimed to analyze the impact of animal welfare practices on pregnancy rates in Nellore females, detailing the neuroendocrine pathways of stress and mitigating animal science strategies\. Activation of the hypothalamus\-pituitary\-adrenal axis increases circulating cortisol, suppressing gonadotropin\-releasing hormone \(GnRH\) and luteinizing hormone \(LH\) secretion, compromising follicular dynamics and luteal progesterone \(\) output\. Calm Nellore females exhibit timed artificial insemination \(TAI\) conception rates 8% to 12% higher than excitable counterparts, whereas severe heat stress with high temperature\-humidity index induces up to 14% higher early embryonic losses\. In conclusion, adopting low\-stress handling, providing thermal comfort through shade, and investing in team training are mandatory biotechnical tools to increase pregnancy rates and enhance the bioeconomic sustainability of tropical beef production systems\.
