# Niami, Morteza: Ontological Divergence through Semantic Erosion: Wicker Park as a Laboratory for Testing and Extending the Ontology of Finite Continuity

**Autores:** 

**Data de descoberta:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** unknown

## Descrição do feed

This paper argues that the narrative structure of Paul McGuigan&\#x27;s Wicker Park \(2004\)—characterized by non\-linear temporality, chain misrecognitions, and temporal reconstructions—reveals the conditions of possibility for observing processes of semantic erosion and ontological divergence, and through this revelation enables the refinement and extension of the Ontology of Finite Continuity\. Rather than treating the film as an illustration of a pre\-existing philosophical theory, I position it as a conceptual laboratory that makes visible three phenomena: ontological divergence through semantic erosion, homeorhesis as the pattern of continuity under erosive conditions, and structural reconstruction of meaning as an active ontological process\. The analysis introduces the concept of ontological divergence through semantic erosion to explain how fragmented communication progressively generates alternative ontological worlds for each character without requiring substantial alteration in external events\. Small disruptions in communicative chains accumulate across time, activating three types of hermeneutic failure simultaneously—structural, institutional, and paradigmatic—and producing hermeneutic friction as their nonlinear emergent product\. The paper further demonstrates that the film exemplifies homeorhetic continuity rather than homeostatic return: characters do not recover prior equilibrium but maintain their developmental trajectory under erosive conditions\. Through integrating film\-philosophy with contemporary ontology, this study contributes to philosophical film studies and ontological theory by proposing a new conceptual model of ontological divergence, demonstrating how cinema can function as a laboratory for testing and extending ontological frameworks, and offering a methodological bridge between phenomenological description and ontological inference\. Keywords: Ontological Divergence, Semantic Erosion, Homeorhesis, Hermeneutic Friction, Finite Continuity, Film\-Philosophy, Wicker Park \(direct link \)
