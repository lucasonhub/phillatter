# Blockade of the calpain\-1/HSP90/TGF\-β1 axis by Astragaloside IV ameliorates bleomycin\-induced pulmonary fibrosis

**Autores:** silin wei; Yu Liu; Bailin Tang; Yang Yu; Nan Sun; Meili Lu; 孙莉莉; Guijun Wang

**Data:** 2026-10-14 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Background Previous studies have confirmed the therapeutic potential of Astragaloside IV \(AS\-IV\) in pulmonary fibrosis, yet the upstream regulatory mechanisms governing TGF\-β1 stability, particularly involving proteolytic enzymes, are not fully understood\. This study investigates the pivotal role of calpain\-1 in modulating the HSP90/TGF\-β1 signaling pathway, thereby elucidating the effects of AS\-IV on bleomycin \(BLM\)\-induced pulmonary fibrosis\. Methods Calpain\-1 knockout \(CAPN1 −/− \) and wild\-type \(WT\) mice were used to establish pulmonary fibrosis models via intratracheal instillation of BLM\. Both animal and cell models were administered varying doses of AS\-IV for intervention\. Inflammatory, oxidative stress, fibrotic, and epithelial\-mesenchymal transition \(EMT\) markers were subsequently evaluated\. Additionally, molecular docking, molecular dynamics simulations, and surface plasmon resonance \(SPR\) experiments were employed to assess the binding interactions between AS\-IV and the calpain\-1/HSP90/TGF\-β1 axis\. Results The calpain\-1/HSP90/TGF\-β1 axis was significantly activated in BLM\-induced pulmonary fibrosis mice\. AS\-IV ameliorated pulmonary fibrosis, EMT, oxidative stress, and inflammation while simultaneously suppressing this overactivated axis\. Calpain\-1 knockout inhibited HSP90 upregulation, attenuated TGF\-β1/Smad2/3 activation, and reduced pulmonary fibrosis\. In vivo experiments further confirmed the inhibitory effects of AS\-IV and calpain\-1 inhibition on lung epithelial cell injury and fibroblast proliferation\. Notably, calpain\-1 overexpression in MLE\-12 cells abolished the protective effects of AS\-IV against EMT, oxidative stress, and inflammation\. Molecular docking, molecular dynamics simulations, and SPR experiments confirmed the direct binding between AS\-IV and calpain\-1, supporting its upstream regulatory role in this signaling axis\. Conclusion AS\-IV attenuates pulmonary fibrosis by targeting calpain\-1 to disrupt the HSP90/TGF\-β1 signaling axis\. These findings identify calpain\-1 as a novel therapeutic target and support AS\-IV as a promising candidate for PF treatment\.
