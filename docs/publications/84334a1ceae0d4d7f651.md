# Magnetic‐Field‐Annealing‐Induced Short‐Range Order in High‐Entropy Alloys for Enhanced Electromagnetic Response

**Autores:** Lu‐Yang Li; Zhuang Ma; Ji‐Xing Bai; Wen‐Qiang Cao; Lihong Gao; Qi Cao; Miao Jiang; Mao‐Sheng Cao

**Data:** 2026-10-14 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

The growing demand for microwave absorbers in 5G/6G technologies motivates processing routes that balance matching thickness, attenuation, and temperature response\. Here, magnetic field annealing \(MFA\) is investigated in rGO\-supported FeCoNi, FeCoNiCr, and FeCoNiCrCu compositionally complex alloys at 0, 0\.5, and 0\.95 T\. Among the investigated FeCoNiCr samples, the 0\.5 T sample exhibits a favorable combination of dielectric and magnetic responses\. Reflection loss yields a minimum of \-72\.30 dB at 1\.59 mm, and a maximum effective absorption bandwidth of 4\.40 GHz\. At 85°C, the maximum bandwidth is 4\.08 GHz, corresponding to 92\.7% retention of temperature\-specific maxima\. Local microscopy, field\-associated atomistic modeling, and fixed\-composition electronic\-structure calculations support an interpretation of the electromagnetic response in terms of Co\-centered chemical correlations\. Finally, flexible antennas and strain sensors based on this composite demonstrate stable wearable responses, validating this SRO strategy from atomic design to device integration\.
