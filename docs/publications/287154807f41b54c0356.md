# Hybrid ConvNeXt–ViT feature extraction for rice blast disease severity classification

**Autores:** M K Shwetha; V Ravi Kumar

**Data:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Rice blast is one of the most destructive fungal diseases affecting rice cultivation, which causes large yield losses and creates a major risk to the world&\#x27;s food security\. In addition to early detection, accurate severity\-level assessment is crucial for effective disease management\. A hybrid deep learning method based on ConvNeXt and Vision Transformer \(ViT\) models is proposed for assessing rice blast disease severity in the present work\. A new well\-organised dataset comprising 1,440 rice leaf images annotated into seven severity levels ranging from 1–10% to 61–70% based on the lesion area coverage is utilised in the model\. The proposed hybrid ConvNeXt–ViT model has two parallel feature extraction branches, where ConvNeXt captures spatial and textural patterns, while ViT learns global contextual representations\. Complementary feature representations from both branches are fused through a unified classification head to enable fine\-grained severity prediction\. A series of tests was carried out to evaluate the effect of different fusion strategies, pre\-trained weights, patch sizes, and batch sizes on model performance\. The proposed approach demonstrates consistent training behaviour and generalisation across different configurations\. Comparative evaluation against state\-of\-the\-art CNN models such as ResNet, EfficientNet, VGG, MobileNet, and Xception, as well as a transformer\-based baseline shows that the proposed approach achieves superior performance\. The ConvNeXt–ViT model achieves a classification accuracy of 92\.63%, demonstrating its potential for automated rice blast severity assessment in agriculture applications\.
