# Contemporary Approaches to Archiving of Digital Originals: A Review of Concepts and Technologies

**Autores:** Arian Rajh; Hrvoje Stančić; Jelena Bolkovac

**Data:** 2026-10-14 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Digital materials are increasingly prevalent as a means of record creation\. This paper presents interim results from the research project “Long\-term preservation of the authenticity of digital originals using blockchain technology and artificial intelligence \(I\.Trust\.Original\)”, funded by the European Union’s NextGenerationEU fund, and focuses on concepts and tools for archiving digital materials\. The authors review the concepts of the digital original and the archival bond and examine their application within the Open Archival Information System \(OAIS\) framework\. Blockchain and distributed ledger technologies \(DLT\) are analysed as mechanisms for creating digital originals and for ensuring integrity, transparency, and trust\. The paper also explores the role of artificial intelligence \(AI\), including large language models \(LLMs\), in archival processes and in supporting blockchain\-based preservation\. Semantic web and linked data are regarded as tools for enhancing interoperability and discoverability, while smart contracts are recognised in the context of rights management\. The paper aims to map these concepts and technologies and assess their impact on the archiving of digital originals, highlighting key benefits and limitations\. It emphasises the interconnectedness of archival theory and technological innovation, and outlines directions for future research and practical implementation\.
