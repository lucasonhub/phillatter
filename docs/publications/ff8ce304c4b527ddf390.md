# TabReliBench: Evaluating Tabular Foundation Models Beyond Predictive Accuracy

**Autores:** Tianqi Zhao; Jia Guo; Yan Shuo Tan; Qiong Zhang

**Data:** 2026-10-09 · **Tipo:** preprint

## Resumo

Tabular foundation models \(TFMs\) are increasingly popular for their strong predictive performance\. Many also provide posterior predictive distributions \(PPDs\), whose reliability remains less studied\. We assess reliability through point prediction, distributional quality, marginal and subgroup calibration, and interval efficiency\. We then introduce TabReliBench, a large classification and regression benchmark for these comparisons and dataset\-level analyses\. We find that TFMs&\#x27; rankings change between TabArena and our benchmark and across reliability metrics\. Regression TFMs usually achieve near\-nominal marginal coverage and retain narrow intervals, but this favorable marginal behavior does not imply valid conditional coverage\. Dataset\-level analyses show that classification difficulty often accompanies poor calibration, whereas regression predictive performance is not a reliable indicator of calibration\. Ensembling improves predictive performance but does not consistently improve calibration of PPDs; TFMs also benefit little from conformal post\-processing, which does not reliably resolve subgroup gaps\. Reliable TFM selection therefore requires multidimensional evaluation beyond aggregate rankings\. Code is available at https://github\.com/Tianqi\-Zhao/TabReliBench\.
