# Heavy\-Duty Vehicle Electrification and Energy Resilience in Indonesia’s Mining Sector: A Strategic Assessment

**Autores:** Maximillian Kolbe Widhi Wijayanto; Suyono Thamrin; Imam Supriyadi; Subur Wahono

**Data:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Indonesia’s mining sector remains heavily dependent on diesel\-powered heavy\-duty vehicles, exposing mining operations to fuel price volatility and supply disruptions\. Electrification presents opportunities to reduce diesel consumption but also introduces new dependencies on electricity infrastructure and technology suppliers\. This study aims to assess the strategic implications of heavy\-duty vehicle electrification for energy resilience in Indonesia’s mining sector\. A qualitative strategic analysis was conducted using secondary data from a corporate presentation on the heavy\-duty truck market dated June 2026, triangulated with 15 Indonesian journal articles and supplementary corporate disclosures\. The analysis examined market drivers, energy substitution, potential resilience benefits, emerging vulnerabilities, and policy options\. The findings indicate that battery\-electric mining trucks can reduce diesel dependence and operating energy costs under favorable electricity pricing and vehicle utilization conditions\. Illustrative corporate comparisons suggest approximately 80% lower hourly energy costs than those of comparable diesel\-powered trucks; however, these estimates are based on case\-specific assumptions and should not be interpreted as nationally representative benchmarks\. Integrated supplier ecosystems can accelerate electrification deployment but may simultaneously increase dependence on battery systems, charging infrastructure, financing arrangements, spare parts, and specialized technical services\. These findings demonstrate that electrification redistributes energy\-related vulnerabilities rather than automatically eliminating them\. The study concludes that achieving meaningful improvements in energy resilience requires diversified electricity sources, interoperable charging infrastructure, domestic maintenance capabilities, and diversified supplier networks\. Coordinated policies and strategic investments can enhance the reliability and continuity of mining operations while strengthening national energy resilience\.
