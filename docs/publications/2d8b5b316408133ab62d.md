# Structural, Optical, and Magnetic Properties of Mn x Fe 3‐x O 4 Ferrofluids Derived From Iron Sand for Energy Harvesting Application

**Autores:** Sri Rahmawati; Babita Tripathi; Yap Wing Fen; Arif Hidayat; Nurul Hidayat; Anindya Bella Monica; ST\. Ulfawanti Intan Subadra; Munasir; Ahmad Taufiq

**Data:** 2026-10-17 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

ABSTRACT In this research, we report the synthesis of iron sand‐based Mn x Fe 3‐x O 4 ferrofluids along with the characterization of their structural, optical, and magnetic properties, as well as their energy harvesting performance\. The Mn x Fe 3‐x O 4 nanoparticles in the ferrofluids constructed an inverse spinel structure, with their diffraction peaks shifting to lower angles as the Mn 2\+ content increased\. FTIR spectra showed the Fe‐O and Mn‐O stretching detected at wavenumbers of 430–521 cm −1 and 411 cm −1 , respectively\. The magnetic nanoparticles tended to agglomerate with particle sizes ranging from 24\.0 nm to 39\.6 nm\. The bandgap energy increased as the Mn 2\+ content increased\. Furthermore, the energy harvesting performance was represented by voltage values in the range of 16\.17–96\.20 µV as a function of Mn 2\+ content\. Although the voltage was relatively low, the obtained results indicate promising potential for future energy harvesting applications\.
