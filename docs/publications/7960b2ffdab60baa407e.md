# กรอบเขมทศการศกษาไทยสอนาคต: ความกาวหนาในวชาชพคร

**Autores:** Panus Junsrithong; Anothai Teeratee; Pratakorn Kulsrithanaphat

**Data:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

This article aims to analyze the directions of educational development and professional advancement in the teaching profession by linking the concepts of Education 2030 and Education 2040 with the development of learner competencies, curriculum quality, and teacher professional development, based on a synthesis of relevant documents and the author’s perspectives\. Future education faces significant challenges arising from technological, economic, and social transformations, necessitating a shift in educational systems from traditional academic knowledge transmission toward the development of competencies and learner attributes aligned with the demands of the future world\. This approach emphasizes the cultivation of knowledge, skills, competencies, and values essential for enhancing learners’ quality of life\. However, limitations remain in practical implementation\. Consequently, emerging approaches increasingly highlight the pivotal role of teachers and instructional systems\. Learners must be developed as well\-rounded individuals equipped with essential competencies such as critical thinking, creativity, communication, and collaboration\. Learning in both real and virtual environments must be meaningfully integrated and balanced\. Curricula should therefore be designed to encompass self\-development, readiness for lifelong learning, and practical competencies\. Such developments directly influence teacher growth, which constitutes a crucial factor affecting student achievement\. Teachers are thus required to possess competencies in instructional management, technology integration, research, and innovation, as well as a deep understanding of individual learners\. Professional development for teachers should emphasize competency\-based career pathways, innovation creation, and collaborative learning to foster lifelong learning at their full potential\. Enhancing professional advancement in the teaching profession requires the establishment of competency\-based career progression systems that systematically link performance outcomes, student learning achievements, and educational innovation with transparent and sustainable evaluation and professional ranking systems\. Furthermore, promoting Professional Learning Communities \(PLCs\) is essential to enable teachers to engage in collaborative learning, knowledge exchange, and continuous self\-improvement, ultimately leading to career stability and advancement alongside the overall enhancement of educational quality\.
