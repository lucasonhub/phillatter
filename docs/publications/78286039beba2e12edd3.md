# Conference Report: International Congress on Health Sciences and Medical Technologies \(ICHSMT’25\)

**Autores:** Abdeldjalil Khelassi; Thierry Oscar Cojo Edoh

**Data:** 2026-10-10 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

The International Congress on Health Sciences and Medical Technologies \(ICHSMT\) is a periodical international conference established to foster interdisciplinary collaboration and technological innovation across clinical, preclinical, and technological domains\. Organized by Knowledge Kingdom Publishing, the 9th edition \(ICHSMT’25\) was held virtually from Tlemcen, Algeria, between December 9 and 11, 2025\. Gathering contributors from 17 countries, the congress comprised five dedicated sub\-conferences covering medical technologies, cancer research, preclinical sciences, genetics, and scientific ethics\. Out of 117 received submissions, 89 high\-quality works were accepted after double\-blind peer review \(76\.06% acceptance rate\)\. This report summarizes the scientific achievements, organization, statistical metrics, and publishing outcomes of ICHSMT’25, and announces the call for the 10th edition \(ICHSMT’26\)\.
