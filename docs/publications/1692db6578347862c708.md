# THE NATURE OF THE SOUL \(Volume 2\)

**Autores:** Majid Karimi Baghmaleki

**Data:** 2026-10-13 · **Tipo:** book

## Resumo

The second volume follows the transformation of the soul through Jewish, Christian, Islamic, medieval, Renaissance, and early modern philosophy\. The central transition examined here is the movement from the soul as metaphysical and theological principle toward the modern problem of consciousness and subjectivity\. Maimonides, Augustine, Aquinas, Avicenna, al\-Ghazali, Ibn Arabi, Mulla Sadra, Descartes, Spinoza, Leibniz, Hume, Kant, and other major thinkers contributed different solutions to the relationship between body, intellect, consciousness, identity, and transcendence\. The volume demonstrates that modern philosophy did not simply abandon the soul\. It transformed the question\. The ancient question “What is the soul?” increasingly became the modern questions “What is consciousness?”, “What is the self?”, and “What makes experience mine?” These transformations provide the bridge to contemporary philosophy of mind
