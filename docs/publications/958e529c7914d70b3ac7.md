# ORGANIZATIONAL PSYCHOLOGY, LEADERSHIP AND PERSONAL MANAGEMENT IN THE CONTEXT OF LEGAL SUPPORT OF MANAGEMENT ACTIVITIES

**Autores:** Олена Бабіна; Наталія Іченська; Роман Сторожeв

**Data:** 2026-10-13 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

The purpose of the article is to substantiate an integrated approach to combining organizational psychology, leadership, personal management, and legal support in contemporary managerial activity\. The relevance of the topic is determined by the growing importance of employee well\-being, psychological safety, responsible leadership, and protection of labour rights in conditions of organizational change, remote and hybrid employment, digital monitoring, and the increasing use of algorithmic tools in personnel management\. The methodological basis of the research includes analysis and synthesis, scientific generalization, comparison, system and structural\-functional approaches\. The formal legal method was applied to determine the regulatory boundaries of managerial influence, while logical modelling was used to develop a comprehensive framework for responsible decision\-making\. The research identifies the functional relationships between the psychological, leadership, personal, and legal dimensions of management\. Organizational psychology provides an understanding of employee motivation and behaviour; leadership transforms organizational goals into coordinated actions; personal management supports a manager’s self\-regulation, emotional stability, and professional responsibility; and legal support defines the permissible limits of managerial decisions\. The study establishes that psychological or leadership effectiveness cannot justify practices that violate employee dignity, privacy, equality, or labour rights\. An integrated framework for evaluating managerial decisions is proposed\. It includes organizational feasibility, psychological validity, leadership responsibility, and legal compliance\. Particular attention is paid to preventing discrimination, mobbing, excessive psychological pressure, biased personnel assessment, and improper processing of employee data\. The practical value of the results lies in their applicability to personnel selection, motivation, performance evaluation, conflict resolution, team development, and digital monitoring\. The proposed approach can help managers improve decision quality, strengthen organizational trust, reduce legal and reputational risks, and create a psychologically safe and legally compliant working environment\.
