# Secondary education teachers&\#x27; perspectives on the use of ChatGPT in teaching

**Autores:** MEGALOMYSTAKA CHRISTINA; TSAOUSIDOU IOANNA

**Data:** 2026-10-17 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

This study explores the attitudes and level of readiness of secondary education teachers regarding the integration of ChatGPT into teaching\. Adopting a mixed\-methods approach utilizing a questionnaire, the study examined a sample of teachers from two schools in Central Macedonia\. The findings reveal an attitude of cautious optimism\. Participants acknowledge the tool&quot;s significant utility in lesson preparation and material creation, which saves valuable time\. However, they express strong concerns regarding the reliability of information, ethical issues, the absence of a regulatory framework within schools, and the risk of undermining students&quot; critical thinking\. In conclusion, although artificial intelligence is envisioned as a future digital assistant, there is an urgent need for systematic and experiential institutional training for teachers to ensure its pedagogically safe utilization\.
