# Comparative evaluation of ChatGPT and Gemini in answering patient\-oriented questions about coronary artery bypass grafting: An expert\-based cross\-sectional study

**Autores:** Hakan Baltacı; Yusuf Taşpınar; Üzeyir Yılmaz

**Data:** 2026-10-14 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Objectives: Large language models \(LLMs\) are increasingly used by patients to obtain information about high\-risk procedures such as coronary artery bypass grafting \(CABG\)\.This study aimed to compare expert evaluations of responses generated by ChatGPT \(GPT\-5\.5 Instant, OpenAI\) and Gemini \(Gemini 3\.5 Flash, Google\) to frequently asked patient\-oriented questions about CABG\. Patients and methods:In this expert\-based cross\-sectional study, responses generated by ChatGPT and Gemini to 29 frequently asked CABG\-related questions developed by cardiovascular surgeons were evaluated\.The questions were submitted in English to both models through free, publicly available web interfaces using a standardized prompt\.Each question was entered in a separate new chat session, and only the first generated response was recorded\.Optional web search and Deep Research features were not manually enabled\.After model identities and model\-specific elements were removed, the responses were presented separately in randomized order\.Five cardiovascular surgeons independently rated the responses using a five\-point Likert scale that jointly assessed clinical accuracy and usefulness\.In the primary paired analysis, the sum of the five evaluators&\#x27; ratings for each question was used, yielding 29 paired questionlevel observations\.Results: Both models received high scores\.The mean question\-level total score was 23\.31±1\.54for ChatGPT and 23\.00±1\.31for Gemini\.The mean paired difference was 0\.31 points \(bootstrap 95% confidence interval \[CI\], \-0\.07 to 0\.69\) and was not statistically significant \(Wilcoxon signed\-rank test, W=60\.0; p=0\.129; matched\-pairs rank\-biserial correlation=0\.37\)\.At the individual\-rating level, 97\.2% of ratings were 4 or 5 for each model\.The average\-measures absolute\-agreement intraclass correlation coefficient was 0\.50 \(95% CI, 0\.13\-0\.74\)for ChatGPT and 0\.15 \(95% CI, \-0\.49\-0\.56\)for Gemini\.Conclusion: Under the reported evaluation conditions, responses generated by ChatGPT and Gemini received similarly high combined ratings for clinical accuracy and usefulness\.These findings reflect the expert\-rated clinical appropriateness of model responses generated at a specific time point; they do not establish guideline concordance, within\-model reproducibility, patient comprehension, or clinical benefit\.LLM\-generated CABG information may have a supportive role in patient education; however, this possibility requires validation in patient\-based studies\.These tools should not replace individualized physician\-led counseling\.
