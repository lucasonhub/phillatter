# Hallucination Is All You Need: When AI Dreams Meet the Hospitality Labor Crisis

**Autores:** Po\-Chen Chen

**Data:** 2026-10-17 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Hallucination Is All You Need: When AI Dreams Meet the Hospitality Labor Crisis Po\-Chen Chen THE AMNIS, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Kaohsiung, Taiwan Email:amnischenadrian@gmail\.com ——————————————————————————————————————— Abstract This paper examines the relationship between anthropomorphic narratives in artificial intelligence \(AI\), the technical capabilities of contemporary computational systems, and the material labor challenges facing the hospitality industry\. Through a fourfold conceptual analysis, the paper investigates how metaphorical representations of AI may influence public understanding of technological capabilities and expectations regarding the automation of physical service work\. First, it distinguishes the mathematical and statistical mechanisms underlying large language models from interpretations that attribute human\-like subjective experience to their outputs\. Second, it examines Dream\-RSI as a case study in the use of biological metaphors to describe computational processes, distinguishing replay\-based exploration\-policy improvement from human dreaming or subconscious reflection\. Third, it considers the physical and engineering constraints of AI infrastructure and discusses their implications for the deployment of robotic systems in hospitality environments\. Fourth, it explores how narratives of technological transformation may influence investment expectations and operational decision\-making in labor\-intensive industries\. The paper argues that technological capabilities should be evaluated according to demonstrated performance rather than anthropomorphic expectations\. It further identifies targeted mechanical automation, workforce development, and responsible international labor mobility as potential approaches to addressing hospitality labor shortages, while recognizing the need for context\-specific economic and empirical evaluation\. By connecting AI narratives with the material requirements of service delivery, this paper proposes a framework for examining the relationship between technological expectation, operational feasibility, and the future of human labor in hospitality\. Keywords: AI Anthropomorphism; Hallucination; Dream\-RSI; Hospitality Labor Crisis; Humanoid Robots; Migrant Workers; Capital Allocation\.
