# Two New Federal Efforts Designed to Accelerate Clinical Trials

**Autores:** 

**Data:** 2026-10-14 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Abstract: Both ARPA\-H and the FDA recently launched programs to help accelerate clinical trials\. ARPA\-H announced the SURPASS program, which is intended to create faster clinical trials that are more cost\-effective and have fewer participants than current studies\. It is seeking ideas from cross\-disciplinary teams in statistics, artificial intelligence, and clinical trial design, operations, and regulation\. The FDA has created a pilot program for accelerating phase I clinical trials that pairs drug sponsors with research organizations\.
