# EL\-RAKHAWI FAULT\-TOLERANT QUANTUM COMPUTING ARCHITECTURE: NEUROMORPHIC TOPOLOGICAL ERROR CORRECTION AND DYNAMIC ENVIRONMENTAL DECOUPLING FOR SCALABLE, COMMERCIAL\-GRADE QUANTUM SUPREMACY

**Autores:** mohamed kamal arafa el\-rakhawi

**Data:** 2026-10-15 · **Tipo:** book

## Resumo

The El\-Rakhawi Fault\-Tolerant Quantum Computing \(FTQC\) Architecture revolutionizes quantum computing by solving the critical decoherence and overhead bottleneck that has prevented commercial\-grade quantum supremacy\. Addressing the fragility of qubits and the prohibitive 1000:1 physical\-to\-logical qubit ratio of traditional Quantum Error Correction, FTQC integrates four novel modules: a Topologically Protected Logical Qubit Substrate encoding information non\-locally using non\-Abelian anyons for passive immunity against local noise; a Neuromorphic Quantum Error Correction \(N\-QEC\) Engine employing ultra\-low\-power analog neuromorphic hardware for real\-time syndrome decoding with sub\-microsecond latency, learning non\-Markovian noise profiles; a Dynamic Environmental Decoupling Matrix using predictive machine learning to proactively cancel environmental perturbations before decoherence occurs; and a Noise\-Aware Quantum Compiler optimizing circuit routing to minimize depth and error probability\. Performance is quantified by the El\-Rakhawi Quantum Fault\-Tolerance Efficiency Metric \(EQFTEM\), yielding Logical Operations per Joule, directly linking computational throughput to thermodynamic cost\. This architecture reduces the overhead ratio to &lt;100:1, making million\-qubit fault\-tolerant machines commercially viable, unlocking precise simulation of complex quantum systems, post\-quantum cryptography, and global optimization, while democratizing quantum access through reduced energy requirements\.
