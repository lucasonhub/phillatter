# Qureshi, Khalid: The Emergence of Homo Spiritualis: A Synthesized Model of Quranic Anthropology, Population Genetics, and Cognitive Evolution

**Autores:** 

**Data de descoberta:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** unknown

## Descrição do feed

This paper presents a rigorous theological\-scientific framework reconciling the Quranic account of human origins with modern evolutionary primatology, cognitive neuroscience, and population genetics\. We demonstrate that the Quranic narrative employs a dual paradigm: &quot;Adam&quot; functions both as a symbolic representation of an early, behaviorally modern human society and as a historical, divinely chosen individual who served as the genetic progenitor of modern Homo sapiens\. By integrating textual exegesis of critical Quranic passages \(including Surah 7:11, 7:24, 32:7–9, and 2:31–33\) with evolutionary theories of pair\-bonding, neocortical expansion, and genetic coalescent theory \(Y\-Chromosomal Adam and Mitochondrial Eve\), we propose that human ensoulment coincided with a heritable genomic transformation\. Crucially, this biological transition did not occur as an instantaneous physical miracle overnight, but rather unfolded across generational time as a rapid, punctuated evolutionary expansion\. This biological\-spiritual convergence marks the transition from pre\-human hominins to self\-aware, moral beings \(Homo spiritualis\)\.
