# Little, Sabrina: What Does It Mean to Be Whimsical?

**Autores:** 

**Data de descoberta:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** unknown

## Descrição do feed

\_Res Philosophica\_\. forthcomingWhimsy is sometimes described as an asset of persons, connected to playfulness and humor\. Other times, it is described as a vice akin to being fickle or noncommittal, involving a lack of foresight or premeditated thought\. Defined as a trait, whimsy would be unusual\. Generally, traits predictably dispose us to act in a given situation, yet the whimsical person’s extemporaneous choices defy prediction\. Also, whimsy resists habituation in certain ways, to preserve choice over well\-trodden patterns of acting\. This article offers a preliminary account of whimsy\. It explores whimsy’s metaphysical status—as a trait, cluster concept, or something else\. It investigates possibilities for whimsy in the virtuous or morally mature person, and it assesses ways in which whimsy might contribute to, or detract from, a flourishing life\.
