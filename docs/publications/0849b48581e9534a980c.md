# Sex and stage\-defined exploratory inflammatory and amyloid\-associated readout patterns in female APP/PS1 mice exposed to TAK\-242

**Autores:** Xiaolu Ren; Ting Li; Shan Liu; Ling Li; Fang Li

**Data:** 2026-10-14 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

TLR4\-related inflammatory signaling has been implicated in Alzheimer’s disease\-related immune responses, and TAK\-242 has been used as an experimental tool in studies examining this signaling context\. This exploratory study characterized inflammatory cytokine and amyloid\-associated histological readout patterns associated with TAK\-242 exposure in female early\-stage APP/PS1 mice\. Female APP/PS1 mice were assigned to saline, lipopolysaccharide, or TAK\-242 exposure conditions for 6 weeks, with age\-matched non\-transgenic mice serving as reference controls \( n = 8/group\)\. Brain TNF\-α, IL\-1β, and IL\-6 levels were measured by ELISA, and 6E10\-positive staining area was quantified by immunohistochemistry\. Directional differences in cytokine levels and 6E10\-positive staining area were observed between APP/PS1 mice exposed to TAK\-242 and untreated APP/PS1 mice\. However, because a vehicle\-matched APP/PS1 control group was not included, these differences cannot be attributed specifically to TAK\-242 and were interpreted as exploratory exposure\-associated patterns rather than pharmacological efficacy\. This study provides a sex and stage\-defined exploratory dataset of inflammatory and amyloid\-associated readout patterns in female early\-stage APP/PS1 mice to inform future vehicle\-controlled investigations\.
