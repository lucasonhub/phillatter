# Sulfur‐ and Selenium‐Decorated Coumarins: Synthetic Strategies Involving Cyclization and Late‐Stage Functionalization

**Autores:** Bruna R\. Schneider; Laura D\. de Avila; Maurício Carpe Diem Ferreira Xavier; Alex F\. C\. Flores; Liane K\. Soares; Gabriel Pereira da Costa; Angelita M\. Barcellos

**Data:** 2026-10-12 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Chalcogen\-containing coumarins have attracted considerable attention as a versatile class of heterocyclic compounds because sulfur\- and selenium\-substituted derivatives can significantly influence molecular reactivity, electronic characteristics, and biological activity\. Over the past decades, synthetic approaches toward these compounds have advanced through two main strategies: direct construction of the coumarin framework through cyclization processes and late\-stage functionalization of preformed coumarin scaffolds\. This review provides a comprehensive survey of the methodologies reported over the past two decades for the preparation of chalcogen\-substituted coumarins, with particular emphasis on derivatives functionalized at the C\-3 and C\-4 positions\. Special attention is given to substrate scope, regioselectivity, mechanistic insights, and the use of modern enabling technologies as well as a literature\-based quantitative analysis\.
