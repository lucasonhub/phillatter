# Seismic – Gravimetric analysis of the Algeciras fault system \(Colombia\): spatio\-temporal correlations from GRACE and GNSS\-InSAR

**Autores:** Edwin Mauricio Pinzon Monroy; Carlos A\. Vargas

**Data:** 2026-10-14 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Integrating seismological, gravimetric, and geodetic observations provides a complementary framework for investigating spatiotemporal relationships among seismic activity, mass redistribution, and surface deformation in active fault systems\. This study investigated these associations in the Algeciras Fault System \(AFS\) by integrating seismic records, monthly gravimetric observations from the Gravity Recovery and Climate Experiment \(GRACE\) and GRACE Follow\-On \(GRACE\-FO\) missions corrected for hydrological effects using the Global Land Data Assimilation System \(GLDAS\), and surface deformation observations derived from Global Navigation Satellite System \(GNSS\) and Interferometric Synthetic Aperture Radar \(InSAR\)\. Cross\-correlation analysis between temporal gravity variations and GNSS displacements identified positive and inverse associations with correlation coefficients ranging from 0\.0001 to 0\.61, time lags of up to 45 months, and heterogeneous spatial patterns along the AFS corridor\. In addition, the integrated analysis of gravimetric variations and InSAR\-derived vertical displacements identified areas where simultaneous decreases in gravity and elevation accounted for approximately 99% of the total seismic energy released, compared with areas corresponding to the other relationships evaluated between the two variables\. A fluctuation was also observed in the GRACE\-derived gravimetric time series during the period surrounding the 2019 Mesetas earthquake \(Mw 6\.0\)\. Overall, the proposed methodology provides an exploratory and replicable approach for analyzing the associations observed among the different sources of information and offers complementary analytical insights for future research on seismic hazard and surface dynamics in tectonically active regions\.
