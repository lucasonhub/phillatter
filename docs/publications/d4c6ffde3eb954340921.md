# Parkconnect: AI\-Powered Residential Parking Marketplace

**Autores:** Lakshana MK; Durairaj Mohanapriya; L\. Thara

**Data:** 2026-10-15 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Residential parking facilities are not utilized effectively since they remain vacant for many hours each day while visitors have trouble locating convenient and inexpensive options \[6\]\. Traditional parking facilities are seen as a result of infrastructure problems due to physical property issues rather than providing opportunities for private owners to generate revenue from idle spaces\. Recent advancements on the internet such as smart parking, parking prediction, shared parking/reservation and two\-way parking platform show how these systems are able to match supply demand effectively \[1\]\-\[10\]\. The study is about this topic of research direction which relates to ParkConnect that it is a platform based on artificial intelligence where people who own spaces can connect with those who need temporary parking services\.
