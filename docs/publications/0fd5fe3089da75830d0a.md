# Human\-Centric Talent di Era Digital

**Autores:** Ach Zaki Mubarok; Nabila Dzata Dini; Sevila Nurul Khusaini; Mochammad Isa Anshori

**Data:** 2026-10-14 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Digital transformation and the development of artificial intelligence \(AI\) have reshaped how organizations manage talent, while creating a growing need to ensure that technology continues to support human roles, competencies, and well\-being\. This study aims to analyze the concept of human\-centric talent in the context of the digital era, identify the key aspects that support its implementation, and examine the roles of AI, digital competencies, leadership, transparency, and human–technology collaboration in talent management\. This study employs a Systematic Literature Review \(SLR\) approach by analyzing relevant scientific literature through the stages of identification, screening, evaluation, and thematic synthesis of findings\. The results indicate that human\-centric talent is not merely concerned with technology adoption but places people at the center of digital transformation through the development of digital competencies, adaptive leadership, transparent and fair AI implementation, and balanced collaboration between humans and technology\. AI can improve the efficiency of various talent management functions but still requires human oversight, contextual judgment, and decision\-making\. The findings contribute conceptually by positioning human\-centric talent as an integrative approach that connects technology, people, organizations, and governance\. Practically, the study highlights the importance of competency development, human\-centric leadership, responsible AI governance, and work design that preserves human involvement in the face of digital transformation\.
