# Airway analysis in orthodontics: a comprehensive review of methods, relevance, and clinical implications

**Autores:** Anadha N Gujar

**Data:** 2026-10-12 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Abstract Learning about and assessing any airway\-related issues is crucial for each patient who comes to the orthodontic office\. An essential part of the decision\-making process should be assessing the impact of orthodontic treatment on the upper airway&\#x27;s dimensions\. There is strong evidence that various therapeutic techniques, including RME, functional jaw orthopaedics, and especially maxillomandibular advancement treatments, have positive associations and effects on airway\-related problems\. In this review article, we have tried to give a comprehensive description of the various methods for assessing the airway and its clinical consequences in orthodontics\. An essential part of the decision\-making process should be assessing the impact of orthodontic treatment on the upper airway&\#x27;s dimensions\.
