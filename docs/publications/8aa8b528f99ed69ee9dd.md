# Attentional landscaping

**Autores:** Sebastian Watzl

**Data:** 2026-10-12 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Abstract Our sensory environments are attentionally landscaped: they have been changed in order to affect our patterns of attention\. This paper analyzes attentional landscaping as a form of cognitive scaffolding and argues for its significance\. While not uniquely human, attentional landscaping is a pervasive aspect of our social life\. It is significant in at least three respects\. First, it is important for understanding attention and agency\. Attentional landscaping can yield a dramatic expansion of agency by collectivizing mechanisms of attention guidance and turning them into a partly communal project\. Second, attentional landscaping illuminates mindshaping and cognitive scaffolding more generally: since attention is both central and relatively easy to shape, attentional landscaping is a key ingredient in many forms of social interaction\. Third, our discussion sheds light on communication\. Much ostensive communication can be understood as a specific form of attentional landscaping, and ostensive practices together with the social norms that structure them can function as safety devices that help reap the advantages of friendly and pro\-social landscaping while constraining hostile forms of attentional manipulation\.
