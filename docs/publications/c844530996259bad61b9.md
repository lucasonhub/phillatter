# Export competitiveness and indicative financial performance of quinoa in Ecuador and Bolivia: a comparative analysis

**Autores:** Carolina Jaramillo; Ramiro Jaramillo; Lorena Muquis

**Data:** 2026-10-14 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Introduction This study compares the export performance and indicative financial performance of quinoa in Ecuador and Bolivia from 2016 to 2024\. Methods A country\-year dataset comprising 18 observations was compiled from official statistical and trade sources\. Export performance was assessed descriptively using export volume, FOB value, export unit value, growth, and continuity\. Financial performance was evaluated separately through an indicative 2026 benefit \-cost \(B/C\) scenario based on the implicit export unit value and reference production costs\. Results Bolivia maintained considerably higher and more continuous export volumes, while Ecuador showed lower export volumes but periods of higher unit export values\. The indicative financial scenario produced a B/C ratio of 2\.33 for Bolivia and 1\.22 for Ecuador\. Discussion These estimates are indicative because comparable annual observed production costs were not available\. Certifications are discussed as institutional mechanisms that may facilitate market access and product differentiation, but their economic effect is not quantified\. Overall, the evidence indicates substantial differences in export scale and continuity, while the indicative 2026 scenario highlights differences in reference cost conditions\.
