# Physics\-Informed AI for Defect Prediction in WAAM for Space Systems \(Academic Edition\)

**Autores:** Sangat Naik

**Data:** 2026-10-15 · **Tipo:** book

## Resumo

Physics\-Informed AI for Defect Prediction in WAAM for Space Systems \(Academic Edition\) This book explores the integration of physics\-informed artificial intelligence with Wire Arc Additive Manufacturing \(WAAM\), focusing on defect prediction and its potential applications in space manufacturing systems\. The work examines the fundamental physics of WAAM, defect formation mechanisms, computational modeling, machine learning, physics\-informed neural networks, and hybrid predictive architectures\. Particular attention is given to challenges associated with data scarcity, process uncertainty, microgravity, and autonomous manufacturing\. The book also introduces a proposed Physics\-Informed WAAM Intelligence Framework and identifies research opportunities for improving manufacturing reliability in demanding environments\. Intended for researchers, engineers, and graduate students, this academic edition provides a foundation for further investigation into intelligent additive manufacturing, materials engineering, and future space technologies\. This edition is made available for academic reference, research, and citation\.
