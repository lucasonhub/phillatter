# ZAMONAVIY YOSHLARNI OILAVIY HAYOTGA TAYYORLASHDA EMOTSIONAL INTELLEKT DINAMIKASINI TASHXIS ETISH MUAMMOLARI

**Autores:** Raximova Gulhayo Alisherovna

**Data:** 2026-10-13 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

This article analyzes the role of pre\-marital emotional competence in the psychological readiness of modern youth for family life\. The issue of developing emotional intelligence \(EQ\) components among young people is examined as a subject of scientific research, and the importance of this factor in ensuring the stability of future families is substantiated\. The article outlines the object and subject of the study, the methodological foundations, and practical approaches to fostering emotional stability among young people\.
