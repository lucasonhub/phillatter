# Spatiotemporal patterns of cultural ecosystem service supply, demand and service flows along the Jiangsu music–cultural corridor: a grid\-based framework across 13 prefecture\-level cities

**Autores:** Chenghan Zhang; Yi Fang; Yanxia Wang

**Data:** 2026-10-14 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

This study evaluates potential cultural ecosystem service \(CES\) supply, potential demand and modelled least\-cost accessibility pathways across Jiangsu Province on a common 1 km grid for 2014, 2019 and 2020\. Nine indicators are organised into a six\-indicator supply block and a three\-indicator demand block\. After direction adjustment and min\-max standardisation on the common valid\-cell mask, entropy\-derived within\-dimension weights are normalised to sum to one and the supply and demand blocks are each assigned an equal block weight of 0\.5 before the relative supply\-demand balance is calculated\. Spatial dependence is assessed with row\-standardised Queen contiguity, 999 permutations and false\-discovery\-rate\-adjusted local statistics\. Strong source and sink patches are identified from the 70th percentiles of positive surplus and deficit magnitude, and deterministic patches covering at least 90% of strong surplus/deficit are linked through an OSM\-derived travel\-time resistance surface by a source\-conserving, deficit\-weighted least\-cost allocation\. Supply clustering weakened slightly \(Moran’s I 0\.7598 to 0\.7488\), whereas demand clustering strengthened \(0\.8062–0\.8203\) and the spatial clustering of the balance surface increased \(0\.6985–0\.7356\)\. Mean relative balance shifted from −0\.000147 in 2014 to −0\.003555 in 2019 and \-0\.004462 in 2020; significant low\-low balance clusters expanded from 5,834 to 7,044 cells\. Under the main decay setting \(decay parameter β = 0\.02\), weighted mean least\-cost time increased from 97\.0 to 105\.7 min and the share of allocated flow within 60 min fell from 28\.75% to 26\.58%\. In contrast, the 90th\-percentile corridor skeleton did not show a monotonic loss of topological connectivity: the largest\-component share was 78\.32%, 79\.15% and 79\.14%\. Taken together, the three cross\-sections indicate a shift toward greater demand\-side imbalance and higher modelled accessibility cost, while allocated pair flow decreases slightly and the q90 corridor skeleton does not exhibit a monotonic decline in topological connectivity\. The pathway layer therefore represents modelled potential accessibility rather than observed cultural movement\.
