# CFP: Sata vuotta tekniikan historiaa Suomessa \-antologia

**Autores:** Nicklas Yli\-Kauppila

**Data:** 2026-10-12 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

The Finnish Society for History of Technology \(THS\) will celebrate its centenary in 2028 by publishing the anthology One Hundred Years of the History of Technology in Finland\. The peer\-reviewed volume invites previously unpublished articles examining the history of technology and industry from a broad range of perspectives\. Possible topics include innovations, industrial heritage, energy, security of supply, technological dependencies, the role of technology in society, sustainable development, and artificial intelligence\. The deadline for article proposals is 30 November 2026, and the anthology will be published in 2028\.
