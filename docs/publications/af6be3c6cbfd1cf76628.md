# Alfano, Mark \(ed\.\): The Moral Psychology of Grief

**Autores:** 

**Data de descoberta:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** unknown

## Descrição do feed

Is it good to be the sort of person who grieves the loss of valued others, or should we be wary of those who are deeply affected by grief? Grief seems to be a human universal, perhaps the continuation of love after loss, yet it is deeply painful and even debilitating\. This edited collection examines grief from a variety of perspectives in philosophy and the social sciences\. The contributors seek to explore such topics as the nature of grief, its connection to a range of other emotions, under what conditions it is appropriate to grieve, and what role grief might play in our intellectual, moral, and political lives\. Containing diverse voices and viewpoints who draw connections to many historical philosophers’ thoughts on grief, this book aims to illuminate the complexities of grief in our lives\.
