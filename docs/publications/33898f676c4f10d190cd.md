# Nyholm, Sven: Online Relationships

**Autores:** 

**Data de descoberta:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** unknown

## Descrição do feed

In Sarah Stroud &amp; Monika Betzler, The Oxford Handbook of the Philosophy of Personal Relationships\. Oxford University Press\. forthcomingThis chapter discusses online relationships and their value\. The chapter discusses whether online relationships can be as valuable – and realize as many important relationship goods – as offline relationships; and it also discusses whether online relationships with AI chatbots that can convincingly imitate human beings can be as valuable – and realize as many important relationship goods – as online relationships with fellow human beings\. The first wave of philosophical discussions about online relationships where typically very skeptical about such relationships, with several philosophers arguing that offline relationships are superior to online relationships in various different ways\. A second wave of philosophical papers about online relationships were much more optimistic, arguing that online relationships can be as good as offline relationships in many important ways, and that they can even be preferable to offline relationships in some ways \(e\.g\. by operating according to different norms, or by not involving certain risks related to offline relationships\)\. In response to those who argue that online relationships can be equal in value with offline relationships and sometimes even better in some respects, the chapter then asks whether there is any need to have a human being on the other side in an online relationship if there can be AI chatbots that can convincingly imitate human online friends\. Indeed, these days, there are many people who think their online relationships with AI chatbots are better than human relationships in certain respects\. The chapter ends with a critical assessment of that idea\.
