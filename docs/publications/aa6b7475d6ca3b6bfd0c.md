# Composite Indices in Anatomy Education Research: Reproducibility as a Minimum Requirement

**Autores:** L Gharbi

**Data:** 2026-10-10 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Reproducibility is a minimum requirement for composite indices in anatomy education\. Sharma et al\. recently introduced the Pedagogical Visualization index \(PVI\) in this journal, a composite of knowledge gain, engagement, and perceived clarity scaled to 0\-1 and used to compare chalkboard and teaching, 3D models, and a hybrid approach\. My concern is with the index itself and with what peer review currently asks of composite outcome measures\. I would suggest that three conditions should be satisfied before any composite enters the literature as a comparative metric\. The PVI is a useful worked example because the paper reports enough detail for a reader to check all three\.
