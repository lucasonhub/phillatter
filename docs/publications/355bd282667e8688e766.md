# University–Industry–Government Collaboration for Construction Materials Innovation: A Grant\-to\-Commercialization Model from Uzbekistan

**Autores:** Igamova Shakhinya Zikrilloyevn

**Data:** 2026-10-13 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

This study develops a regional grant\-to\-commercialization model for construction\-materials innovation in Uzbekistan\. It combines legal and policy analysis, official statistics for 2020–2026, recent university–industry collaboration research, and benchmarking of commercialization programmes in Finland, the United Kingdom, the United States and the European Union\. Uzbekistan already permits local authorities to finance innovation, technology transfer and commercialization, and recent reforms have strengthened sector–enterprise–university–research linkages\. Yet an institutional “last\-mile” gap remains: public research funding is not systematically organized around regional, sector\-specific joint applications in which firms host industrial pilots and commercialization expertise is funded from the outset\. The paper proposes Regional Construction Materials Innovation and Commercialization Funds \(RCMICFs\), accessible only to university–enterprise consortia\. Each funded team must include an experienced commercialization manager and a market specialist, while at least 35–40% of eligible expenditure is devoted to commercialization preparation, certification, customer discovery and market validation\. Stage\-gated financing, enterprise co\-funding, independent review and outcome\-based KPIs are proposed to improve additionality, reduce capture risk and convert research into regional industrial value\.
