# Adams, Nicholas Norman: Transmaxxing, Identity, and Online Male Subcultures: Toward a Feminist Analysis

## Descrição do feed

\_Deviant Behaviour\_ \(Online\):Online\. 2026This paper examines an emergent discourse within online male subcultures, in which some men frame a specific form of gender transition, transmaxxing, as a strategic response to perceived masculine failure and social marginalisation\. These men view themselves as underprivileged, understanding femininity as a route to social recognition, sexual access, and/or greater personal fulfilment than so\-called ‘alpha’, ‘traditional’, or ‘hegemonic’ masculinities could afford\. Unlike typical gender transitioning, transmaxxing is not commonly associated with gender dysphoria; rather, men’s motivations tend to centre on external factors, including perceived societal misandry and disillusionment with men’s social and sexual status following a feminist turn in contemporary culture\. This paper offers a conceptual analysis of transmaxxing as a gendered response to the perceived collapse of normative manhood within digital subcultures\. Rather than framing transmaxxing as ‘ironic’ or purely performative, we conceptualise transmaxxing as strategic femininity: an instrumentalisation of gender identity shaped by despair, rejection, and masculine ideological disaffection\. Acknowledging the growing online transmaxxing discourse, and grounded in men and masculinity theory, we situate it as both a modern conceptual critique and a symptom of hegemonic masculinity\. \(direct link \)
