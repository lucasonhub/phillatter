## Descrição do feed

Updated 4\.6\.26 \- New Addendum 6 God&\#x27;s Framework, Physical Processes, Human Choices and the Collapse Tree of Intent The Theory of Conscious Reality Convergence \(TCRC\) proposes that God’s continuous creative and providential action is physically realised through the convergent actualisation of quantum potentialities by conscious observers\. Following the orthodox consciousness\-collapse lineage \(von Neumann–London–Bauer–Wigner–Stapp\), TCRC identifies wave\-function collapse with the observer\-dependent selection of one real history from superposition\. Divine primary causality establishes all physically lawful potentialities; creaturely secondary causality—exercised through genuine conscious observation—selects which potentiality becomes actual, yielding a non\-interventionist, Thomistically coherent account of continuous creation and objective divine action\. TCRC solves the classic coordination problem by showing that multiple observers’ collapse events superpose instantaneously—like sound waves from separate instruments combining into one coherent waveform \(e\.g\., an entire orchestra captured in a single vinyl groove\)—producing one shared, stable timeline without ad hoc synchronisation\. Only genuine conscious understanding, not mere information processing, triggers reduction; current artificial intelligence therefore cannot function as a technical observer and does not collapse the state vector\. Crucially, TCRC distinguishes eternal, immaterial consciousness \(the image of God and true observer\) from temporal emotions and thoughts \(neural processes experienced by consciousness\)\. Moral responsibility resides in the conscious actualisation of configurations, not in affective states themselves, providing a compassionate framework for sin, virtue, mental illness, and eschatology\. TCRC thereby offers a unified solution to four longstanding problems: \(1\) the quantum measurement problem, \(2\) non\-interventionist divine action, \(3\) the hard problem of machine consciousness, and \(4\) the locus of moral responsibility—while generating novel, testable predictions concerning observer\-density effects, collaborative observation, and neural signatures of volitional collapse\.
