# DIGITAL TWIN AS AN INTEGRATED DECISION\-SUPPORT PLATFORM FOR SUSTAINABLE URBAN DEVELOPMENT: A CONCEPTUAL AND IMPLEMENTATION FRAMEWORK FOR TASHKENT

**Autores:** S\. Salimov; J\.Sh\. Inoyatxodjayev

**Data:** 2026-10-14 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Rapid urban growth increases the interdependence of transport, land use, utility infrastructure, environmental conditions, public services, and emergency\-management systems\. In Tashkent, these domains are increasingly supported by digital technologies, yet the fragmentation of data and sector\-specific information systems limits the ability of public authorities to evaluate the combined consequences of urban\-development decisions\. This paper develops an integrated conceptual and implementation framework for a cityscale Digital Twin designed to support sustainable urban development and data\-driven governance in Tashkent\. The study applies systems analysis, functional modelling, comparative analysis, and scenario\-based assessment to connect urban challenges with data acquisition, integration, simulation, decision support, and measurable outcomes\. The proposed framework consists of five interconnected layers: a spatial and physical model, dynamic urban data, data integration and governance, analytics and simulation, and decision\-support services\. These layers operate within a continuous decision cycle in which information from the physical city updates the Digital Twin, alternative scenarios are simulated, management decisions are implemented, and the resulting urban changes generate new data\. Priority applications include transport management, utility infrastructure, environmental monitoring, development control, and emergency management\. A four\-stage implementation roadmap is proposed for Tashkent, supported by governance, cybersecurity, open architecture, lifecycle financing, and outcome\-based performance indicators\. The framework shifts the role of the Digital Twin from urban visualization toward an operational mechanism for testing the consequences of decisions before physical implementation\.
