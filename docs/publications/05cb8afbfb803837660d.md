# Digital Wisdom and GenAI

**Autores:** Viviana Vinci; Pierangelo Berardi

**Data:** 2026-10-13 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Within the framework of the European Erasmus\+ STIAEM project, the authors of this study deconstruct the assumed homogeneity of “digital natives” regarding generative artificial intelligence adoption\. Utilizing a sample of 2,052 secondary school students, the research architecture applies confirmatory factor analysis, extended Bayesian information criterion graphical least absolute shrinkage and selection operator network analysis, and latent profile analysis\. Results identify distinct student profiles based on engagement and familiarity, revealing a profound relational decoupling between technological motivation and situated ethical choices\. Under academic deadline pressures, nearly all \(92\.74%\) of the sample participants succumbed to automation bias, uncritically accepting machine hallucinations\. This behavioral paradox demonstrates that superficial digital fluency masks severe deficits in epistemic validation and independent thought\. Consequently, in this paper the authors argue for shifting from product to process evaluation and integrating explicit algor\-ethics interventions within school curricula to develop reflexive, human\-centric algorithmic citizenship\.
