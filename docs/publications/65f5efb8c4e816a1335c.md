# An agentic AI system for multi\-framework communication coding

**Autores:** Bohao Yang; Rui Yang; Joshua M\. Biro; Haoyuan Wang; Jessica L\. Handley; Brianna N\. Richardson; Sophia Bessias; Nicoleta Economou\-Zavlanos; Armando D\. Bedoya; Monica Agrawal; Michael M\. Zavlanos; Anand Chowdhury; Raj M\. Ratwani; Kai Sun; Kathryn I\. Pollak; Michael J\. Pencina; Chuan Hong

**Data:** 2026-10-11 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Abstract Clinical communication is central to patient outcomes, yet large\-scale annotation of patient–provider conversations remains labor\-intensive, inconsistent, and difficult to scale\. Existing large language model \(LLM\)–based approaches typically rely on single\-task systems with limited robustness across communication frameworks and clinical domains\. In this study, we developed MOSAIC, a M ulti\-framew O rk S tructured Agentic AI system for scalable Clinical C ommunication coding\. MOSAIC combines dynamically few\-shot prompting, retrieval\-augmented generation \(RAG\), and a LangGraph\-based multi\-agent workflow for planning, annotation, and verification\. Performance was evaluated against gold\-standard annotations produced by trained human coders\. MOSAIC was developed on 54 annotated transcripts and evaluated on 120 transcripts spanning rheumatology and obstetrics/gynecology \(OB/GYN\)\. On the evaluation set, MOSAIC achieved an overall weighted F1 score of 95\.6%\. Performance was highest in rheumatology \(F1 = 95\.9%\) and strongest for bias and patient behaviors categories \(F1 = 96\.4% and 96\.1%, respectively\)\. These results demonstrate the potential of multi\-framework AI systems for reliable clinical communication analysis\.
