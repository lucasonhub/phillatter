# Italian adaptation and validation of the International Cognitive Ability Resource 16 sample test

**Autores:** Eva Tolomeo; Leognano Ceraudo; Marco Balducci; Marco Tullio Liuzza

**Data:** 2026-10-14 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

The International Cognitive Ability Resource 16 \(ICAR\-16\) is a public\-domain measure of cognitive ability\. To date, no Italian validation exists\. We translated and validated the ICAR\-16 in a sample of 486 Italian adults \(66\.4% female\), who completed the ICAR\-16 and the long version of the Cognitive Reflection Test \(CRT\-Long\) online\. Confirmatory factor analyses supported a second\-order latent variable \(SOLV\) structure \(CFI = 0\.98; TLI = 0\.98\)\. The ICAR\-16 showed strong associations with the CRT\-Long at both the observed and latent levels, providing partial evidence of convergent validity\. Internal consistency reliability was adequate\. Measurement invariance analyses supported partial strong second\-order factor invariance across sexes, indicating a largely comparable higher\-order structure despite sex differences in the intercepts of three first\-order cognitive domains\. Differential Item Functioning \(DIF\) analysis provided evidence of uniform DIF for only one item \(MR47\), indicating a consistent difference in item responses across sexes at comparable total score levels\. Overall, these findings support the Italian ICAR\-16 as a reliable, freely available instrument for cognitive assessment in research\.
