**Autores:** Nargizaxon Adiljanovna Ahmadjonova

**Data:** 2026-10-13 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

This article presents the results of the psychophysical training of passing the simulation installation of radiation safety conditions in the training of empoyees\. The analysis of the conducted tests is given\. The results of the transition without a special installation and with an installation in the presence of radiation risk were also psychologically analyzed\.
