# Mattos, José Caetano de: THE VIRTUE WORM: Why the Base of the Pyramid Defends the Power That Seized It — Flattery, the Trivia Decoy and the Transfer Test, with La Boétie, Aristotle’s Flatterer and Kershaw’s ‘Working Towards the Führer’

## Descrição do feed

Tyranny has never governed by force alone\. Every seizure of an institution or a state is held, in the end, by people who could see what was being done to freedom and chose to applaud — and who applauded what did not matter\. They praise the leader’s punctuality, his humour, his faith, his humble origins, while the courts are captured, the budget diverted and the critics silenced\. This article names that figure the “virtue worm” and gives it, for the first time, an exact structure\. The worm is identified without reading any mind, by four public marks: a seizure documented on the record, a defence that begins with possession of the seat, praise of attributes offered in place of answers to documented acts, and display beyond what personal safety requires \(D1\)\. From this definition seven results follow\. The worm’s praise is a forgery of judgement — the form of fair assessment with its origin replaced by interest \(T1\)\. It is the exact contrary of parresia, the courage to speak truth to power: pleasant speech to power at others’ risk instead of frank speech at one’s own \(T2\)\. It needs no lie, because its deception lives in the selection of truths, and the truer the trivia, the deeper the concealment \(T3\)\. Unlike the programme’s Freedophobic Man, who has lost his freedom to fear, the worm keeps his freedom intact and spends it against the freedom of others — and is answerable for it \(T4\)\. Its loyalty belongs to the seat, not the person, and passes to the successor the day the leader falls \(T5\)\. It blinds the very power it flatters \(T6\), and performs for free the policing that would otherwise need police \(T7\)\. The same structure now operates at a scale no court ever reached: in AI assistants trained on approval, which reproduce the worm’s selective affirmation by construction, and in state honour rolls such as China’s Social Credit System, which turn the worm’s display into a currency\. The remedy cannot be a purge, because those who hunt the old power’s worms become the new power’s worms \(C2\)\. It can only be a virtue: one person, in the room where it matters, saying what everyone else has chosen not to say\. Priority is conceded to Aristotle, Tacitus, La Boétie, Machiavelli, Kershaw, Kuran, Wintrobe, Egorov and Sonin, and the paltering literature; three tests are registered and none has yet been run\. The forgery lies in what is selected for defence — the Trivia Decoy, predicted by the Decoy Placement Rule\. • The worm is not the Freedophobic Man\. The freedophobe’s freedom was replaced; the worm’s is intact and spent against others, so the worm is answerable where the freedophobe is a victim\-turned\-enforcer\. • Worm loyalty follows the seat, not the person: the registered Transfer Test predicts re\-alignment to the successor when the figure falls\. • AI assistants trained on approval reproduce the worm’s structure without its agency, and state honour rolls \(China’s Social Credit System\) are worm markets; the remedy in both is a protected capacity to dissent, never a score\. • Freedom is lost not only when it is taken, but when those who could defend it find it more convenient to praise the one taking it; against that loss, no institution substitutes for the virtue of frank speech\. Relevance Every contemporary society has institutions — universities, firms, parties, churches, courts, platforms — in which power can be taken by one person and then held by the applause of many\. Political science explains much of the holding with fear and with rational opportunism\. What the virtue worm adds is an account of why the applause takes the form it does, why it can be honest sentence by sentence and still deceive, why it blinds the power it flatters, and why it outlives the figure it served\. For the governance of artificial intelligence the result is direct: a system rewarded by the approval of whoever holds the conversation acquires the worm’s structure without anyone intending it, and adding virtue scores makes the problem worse rather than better\. For states that score civic virtue, the article identifies the honour roll as the place where worm displays will concentrate\.
