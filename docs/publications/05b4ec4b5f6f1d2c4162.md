# MA’NAVIYATSHUNOSLIK FANINI RAQAMLI TA’LIM SHAROITIDA O‘QITISH METODIKASI SAMARADORLIGI

**Autores:** SHERZOD RAVSHAN O&\#x27;G&\#x27;LI YO&\#x27;LDOSHEV

**Data:** 2026-10-10 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

This article examines the methodological foundations of teaching Spiritual Studies in a digital educational environment, as well as the implementation process and outcomes of experimental research conducted on the basis of the “Digital Spirituality Platform\.” The study presents the results of a pedagogical experiment involving students \(134 participants in total\) from the “National Idea, Foundations of Spirituality and Legal Education” program at three higher education institutions: Kokand State University, Chirchiq State Pedagogical University, and Andijan State Pedagogical Institute\. A comparative analysis of the initial, intermediate, and final diagnostic indicators of the experimental and control groups was carried out, and the advantages of the digital education model over the traditional teaching methodology were scientifically substantiated\.
