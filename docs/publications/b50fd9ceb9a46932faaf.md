# TRANSFORMASI PEMBELAJARAN MELALUI PENDEKATAN DEEP LEARNING DAN QUESTION PROMPT SCAFFOLDING BERBANTUAN AI

**Autores:** Adinda Sekar arum Cahyani; Qurrotu Inayatil Maula; Jesika Yulianti; Nadia Putri; Dea Alya Agustina

**Data:** 2026-10-13 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Elementary/madrasah ibtidaiyah students are generally still accustomed to learning by memorizing facts, so they are less trained in understanding meaning, connecting concepts to real experience, and thinking critically\. This condition runs counter to the spirit of the Merdeka Curriculum, which promotes the implementation of deep learning that is joyful, meaningful, and mindful\. This activity aims to analyze the effectiveness of an AI\-assisted Deep Learning and Question Prompt Scaffolding approach in improving deep understanding, critical thinking skills, and learning engagement among fifth\-grade students at MI An\-Nuriyah, by integrating the context of Madurese local wisdom\. The activity was carried out through a participatory mentoring approach using a descriptive qualitative design\. Data were collected through observation, interviews, and documentation, then analyzed using an interactive model comprising data reduction, data display, and conclusion drawing, with data validity obtained through source and method triangulation\. The results show that students&\#x27; initial understanding was still superficial and they were not yet accustomed to reflective thinking\. After being introduced to a graded series of AI\-assisted question prompts, students showed a livelier learning process, especially when questions were connected to Madurese local wisdom they were familiar with, which increased their confidence and courage to ask questions\. Obstacles found included some students&\#x27; difficulty answering analysis\- and reflection\-level questions, as well as limited access to devices and the internet at home\. This activity concludes that a practice\-based approach using AI\-assisted graded questioning is more effective in building meaningful understanding than one\-way explanation, although its success depends on consistent teacher mentoring and sustained, adequate technological support\.
