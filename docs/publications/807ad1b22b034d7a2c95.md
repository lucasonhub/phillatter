# Executive Sensemaking of Generative AI Adoption at Enterprise Scale: A Reflexive Thematic Analysis of Cross\-Industry Executive Perspectives

**Autores:** Isidro T\. Aroy Jr; Rabel B\. Catayoc

**Data:** 2026-10-14 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

This qualitative study examines how senior executives from technology, financial services, and consumer goods publicly make sense of enterprise\-scale generative artificial intelligence \(GenAI\) adoption\. Using a purposively selected, bounded single\-case design, it applies reflexive thematic analysis to the complete transcript of one moderated industry panel\. Three research questions ask: \(1\) how leaders describe adoption trajectories, maturity, and organizational arrangements; \(2\) how they reconcile regulation with creative, operational, and governance innovation; and \(3\) how they frame workforce roles, emerging competencies, and human oversight\. Technology–organization–environment, diffusion\-of\-innovation, and organizational sensemaking perspectives inform interpretation\. Nine themes were developed: production\-grade diffusion; regulation as creative discipline; industrialized versus distinctive content; personalization and data trust; practitioner\-led implementation; divergent workforce responses; critical\-thinking demands; human\-in\-the\-loop brand safety; and a shift toward generative engine optimization\. Timestamped excerpts and explicit code\-to\-theme and theme\-to\-question mappings substantiate the analysis\. These findings describe public executive narratives, not independently verified organizational outcomes\. A single public transcript and single\-analyst interpretation limit transferability and preclude statistical generalization\. The principal contribution is a transparent, theory\-informed example of analyzing public executive discourse and a foundation for subsequent multi\-source research\.
