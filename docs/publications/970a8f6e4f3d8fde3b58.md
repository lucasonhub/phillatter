# Comparison of Wobble Board and Trampoline Training as an Adjunct on Proprioception, Kinesiophobia and Risk of Fall in Patients with Knee Osteoarthritis: A Randomised Clinical Trial Protocol

**Autores:** Prachi D Pandey; Amita S\. Aggarwal

**Data:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Introduction: Knee Osteoarthritis \(KOA\) is chronic degenerative, age\-related disorder leading to physical decline, balance and gait disturbance along with deformities\. Patients with KOA exhibit impaired joint position sense, fear of movement, poor proprioception and elevated fall risk which contributes to poor postural control, increased sway and higher risk of fall\. Need of the study: Proprioception training using unstable surface is essential component of rehabilitation\. Wobble board training is commonly used to improve balance and neuromuscular control, whereas trampoline training provides a dynamic, multidirectional challenge which might provide more better functional demand\. However, evidence comparing these two modalities as an adjunct in KOA is limited\. Aim: To compare the effect of wobble board and trampoline training as an adjunct to conventional therapy on proprioception, kinesiophobia and risk of fall in patients with KOA\. Materials and Methods: The present Randomised Clinical Trial \(RCT\) will be conducted in MM Super Specialty Hospital, Mullana, Ambala, Haryana, India, from March 2026 to March 2027\. A total of 36 patients with bilateral KOA of grade 2 and 3 \(Kellgren\-Lawrence grade\) will be recruited based on predefined inclusion and exclusion criteria\. Group A will receive wobble board training with conventional therapy, and Group B will receive trampoline training with conventional therapy for three sessions per week for four weeks\. Primary outcome measures include knee joint proprioception, Tampa Scale for Kinesiophobia, Fall Efficacy Scale\-International \(FES\-I\)and secondary outcome includes Knee injury and Osteoarthritis Outcome Score \(KOOS\) and Numeric Rating Scale \(NRS\)\. A paired t\-test will be performed for within group comparison for normally distributed using Independent t\-test and Wilcoxon Signed\-Rank Test for not normally distributed data\.
