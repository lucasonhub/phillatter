# From pragmatic inference to grammatical structure: A critical review of grammaticalization at the grammar–pragmatics interface in English

**Autores:** Azhar Wazir Thealib Al\-Mayali

**Data:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

The relation between pragmatic meaning and grammatical structure has been a major topic of research in linguistics, especially in the study of language change and grammaticalization\. This paper traces the development of contextual meanings and discourse\-based inferences into conventional grammatical structures in English\. In this paper, we study the mechanisms of structural constraints on pragmatic interpretation in the functional, cognitive and generative approaches, such as the semantic\-pragmatic abstraction, subjectification, frequency\-driven change, and syntactic reanalysis\. The review critically assesses previous historical studies and discusses three major areas of English grammatical development: the development of modal auxiliaries, the grammaticalization of connectives and subordinating structures, and the transition from flexible word order patterns to the more fixed Subject–verb–object \(SVO\) order of modern English\. The analysis shows that grammatical change cannot be explained in terms of one theoretical perspective but it is the result of the interaction of communicative practices, of cognitive processes and of structural reorganization\. The review also identifies current gaps in the literature, in particular the divide between corpus\-based historical studies and theoretical treatments of syntactic change, and the neglect of contemporary digital discourse and sociolinguistic variation\. The paper shows an integrated view of how contextual meanings become grammatical patterns in English\. Grammar and pragmatics are seen as steps in the paper, connected by the ongoing process of language change\.
