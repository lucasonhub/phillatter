# Research and Practice on Intelligent Management and Control of the Urban Drainage System in Pinggu District, Beijing Based on SWMM

**Autores:** Xingbo Peng

**Data:** 2026-10-14 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

This study presents a comprehensive research initiative focused on the intelligent management and control of urban drainage systems, specifically applied to Pinggu District in Beijing\. Utilizing the Storm Water Management Model \(SWMM\), the research integrates extensive literature reviews, comparative case studies of water utility management in major global cities, and rigorous on\-site field investigations\. A critical component of this work involves the construction and calibration of a high\-resolution SWMM stormwater model for the target drainage network\. To address common challenges such as missing data in legacy pipe networks and complex parameter calibration, a systematic section\-by\-section verification and zone\-by\-zone calibration methodology was implemented\. This approach significantly stabilized the model&\#x27;s operational environment, yielding simulation results that closely align with real\-world hydrological conditions\. Furthermore, the research incorporates insights from urban lake management, analyzing eutrophication treatment through a holistic source control, process interception, and end\-of\-pipe treatment framework, exemplified by a representative urban lake in Shenzhen\. By bridging theoretical hydrological modeling with practical engineering applications, this study enhances the understanding of water body dynamics under rapid urbanization pressures\. The findings demonstrate the efficacy of calibrated SWMM applications in optimizing urban drainage infrastructure\. Ultimately, this paper details the methodological framework, simulation outcomes, and practical implications of the intelligent drainage management system, while proposing strategic directions for future research in smart water infrastructure and sustainable urban water environment engineering\.
