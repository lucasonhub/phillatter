# SIMULATED ANNEALING ALGORITHM ENHANCED FOR PHOTOVOLTAIC PANELS MPPT UNDER PARTIAL SHADING CONDITIONS

**Autores:** Rabie Benaissa; Smail Mansouri; Omar Oueldali

**Data:** 2026-10-13 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

The primary goal of this article is to improve the performance of the maximum power point tracking \(MPPT\) controller using the simulated annealing algorithm \(SAA\) under partial shading conditions\. To achieve this, we propose a method combining SAA and the flower pollination algorithm \(FPA\) with a Cuk\-type DC\-DC converter for two photovoltaic panels connected in series under partial shading conditions\. First, we studied the photovoltaic panel, and then modelled two panels connected in series under partial shading\. Next, we modelled the SAA and FPA methods as well as the Cuk converter in continuous current mode \(CCM\)\. Finally, we implemented a simulation using Python to evaluate the efficiency and reliability of the proposed control\. Partial shading is an environmental phenomenon that causes several peaks in photovoltaic characteristics\. The Cuk converter was selected for its ability to invert the output voltage relative to the input\. The first panel is exposed to radiation of 1000 W/m2, while the second receives 400W/m2 at a constant temperature of 25 °C\. The results indicate that the short\-circuit current remains stable, while the short\-circuit voltage increases, resulting in higher overall system power\. The output power and voltage fluctuate and exhibit ripples\. The maximum power and efficiency for the MPPT controller methods are: SAA \(495\.29W, 86\.48%\), FPA \(546\.24W, 95\.4 %\), and SAA\-FPA \(547\.99W, 95\.74 %\)\. The SAA\-FPA method enhances responsiveness and performance when moniSIhe maximum power point of photovoltaic panels under partial shading conditions\.
