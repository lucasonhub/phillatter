# Loss‐Engineered Terahertz Pixelated Metasensors for Enhanced Molecular Vibrational Fingerprint Sensing

**Autores:** Chenglin Yang; Yuqing Cui; Donghai Han; Yushan Hou; Zhonglei Shen; Andreas Tittl; Liuyang Zhang; Ruqiang Yan; Xuefeng Chen

**Data:** 2026-10-14 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Molecular vibrational fingerprints in the terahertz \(THz\) regime offer unique opportunities for label\-free chemical and biological sensing\. Metasensors can amplify these vibrational signals through local near\-field enhancement\. However, current THz molecular fingerprint metasensors lack a systematic design and optimization methodology, which severely limits their potential\. Here, we unveil the crucial role of resonant loss rate in THz molecular fingerprint sensing and present a loss\-engineering methodology for optimized molecular vibrational sensing\. Based on the proposed methodology, we experimentally demonstrate a 12\.6\-fold enhancement of the molecular fingerprint signal with three\-dimensionally printed pixelated metasensors\. Furthermore, the pixelated metasensors support vibrational strong\-coupling, providing a unified platform for enhanced molecular sensing and strong light\-matter interaction\. Overall, the proposed metasensor could bridge THz molecular vibrational fingerprint sensing to end\-use applications in label\-free chemical and biomedical detection\.
