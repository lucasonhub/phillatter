# Development of an AI\-assisted maritime poetry writing book using tourism posters for junior high school students

**Autores:** Gatot Sarmidi; Riswanda Himawan; Fanny Farhanto Purwacaraka Candrajati; Alvian Guntur Prasetya Aziz; Rita Setiawati; Mohammad Naim Bahar

**Data:** 2026-10-13 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Background\- Poetry writing is an important productive literacy skill in the Merdeka Curriculum, yet instruction remains predominantly theoretical\. Students may experience difficulties in idea generation, diction, and imagination due to limited contextual and visual support\. Objectives\- This study aimed to \(1\) identify instructional needs; \(2\) develop an AI\-assisted maritime poetry writing book integrating illustrated data cards and marine tourism posters; and \(3\) evaluate its effectiveness\. Methods\- This Research and Development \(R&amp;D\) study using the ADDIE model\. The study involved experts, a teacher, and eighth\-grade students in experimental and control groups\. Data were collected through observations, interviews, questionnaires, documentation, and pretest\-posttest tasks\. The data were analyzed using qualitative and quantitative descriptive techniques\. Results\- The needs analysis indicated a strong need for step\-by\-step guidance and contextual visual stimuli in poetry\-writing instruction\. Expert validation indicated high feasibility \(material: 87%; media: 80%\)\. Teacher and student practicality ratings reached 92% and 94%, respectively\. Mean scores increased from 5\.086 to 8\.434, with significant differences between groups \(p &lt; 0\.001\) and a moderate effect \(d = 0\.54\)\. Diction richness \(78\.80%\) and figurative language \(74\.10%\) achieved the highest indicator scores\. Conclusions\- The developed book demonstrated high feasibility and practicality and was associated with improved poetry\-writing performance among students\.
