# ICHKI ISHLAR ORGANLARI XODIMLARIDA NIZOLARNI BOSHQARISHDA KONSTRUKTIV MULOQOT VA PSIXOLOGIK TA’SIR KOMPETENSIYASINI SHAKLLANTIRISH MEXANIZMLARI

**Autores:** SANJARBEK BOZIRBOY O&\#x27;G&\#x27;LI RAJABOV

**Data:** 2026-10-15 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

This article provides a scientific and theoretical analysis of the psychological mechanisms underlying the development of constructive communication and psychological influence competence among internal affairs officers in the process of managing conflict situations, including the interrelationships among emotional self\-regulation, social perception, communicative flexibility, and professional reflection\. Furthermore, it examines the priority areas for strengthening officers&\#x27; psychological resilience in conflict situations, constructively managing interpersonal disagreements, developing effective communication strategies, and enhancing competence in exercising psychological influence in accordance with legal and ethical standards\.
