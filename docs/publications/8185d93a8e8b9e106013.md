# Double Take: Using True\-False Exams to Deepen Student Learning

**Autores:** Angela Collins; Eric Nelson

**Data:** 2026-10-15 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

The Double Take True\-False Test is a proposed revitalization of a traditionally dismissed assessment format\. By embracing, rather than avoiding, intentional ambiguity, this article suggests that instructors craft deliberately provocative true\-false statements that lack definitive answers\. Students first respond individually, justifying their reasoning in writing, then reconvene in small groups to argue the opposite position\. Drawing on cooperative learning theory and two\-stage exam research, this approach develops critical thinking, perspective\-taking, and applied reasoning skills that prepare students for real\-world professional environments in which clear\-cut answers rarely exist\.
