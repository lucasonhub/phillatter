# Development and expression validation of an EGFP\-tagged human Kv1\.3 channel construct as a platform for functional studies

**Autores:** AMIRAH AQILAH AZIZ; Thye\-San Cha; Boon\-Teong Teoh; Sazaly AbuBakar; Suzana Misbah

**Data:** 2026-10-13 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Voltage\-gated potassium channel Kv1\.3 is a key regulator of membrane potential and ionic homeostasis, particularly in immune signalling\. Despite its importance, tools for directly visualizing Kv1\.3 dynamics in living mammalian cells are limited, which restricts the comprehensive evaluation of its biological functions\. To address this gap, we developed an enhanced green fluorescent protein \(EGFP\)\-tagged human Kv1\.3 construct for expression in mammalian cells, enabling fluorescence\-based visualization of the expressed fusion protein\. The full\-length human Kv1\.3 coding sequence was PCR\-amplified and subcloned into the pcDNA3\.1\-EGFP expression vector under the CMV promoter to generate an N\-terminal EGFP\-Kv1\.3 fusion protein for expression in mammalian cells\. Following transfection into HEK293 cells, live\-cell imaging revealed EGFP fluorescence consistent with the expression of EGFP\-Kv1\.3 fusion protein\. The protein expression was further verified using Jess™ Western blot system with anti\-EGFP and anti\-Kv1\.3 antibodies, both of which detected a distinct band corresponding to the EGFP\-Kv1\.3 fusion protein\. The successful development of the EGFP\-Kv1\.3 construct provides a fluorescence\-based tool for the expression and visualization of Kv1\.3 in mammalian cells, thereby facilitating future studies of Kv1\.3 expression, localization, and regulation using this fluorescently tagged channel protein\.
