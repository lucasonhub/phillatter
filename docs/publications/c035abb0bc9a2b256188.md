# Open and Closed Mappings in Quadruple Neutrosophic Soft Topological Spaces

**Autores:** Mohammad Ali Khalid; Hassan Dadas

**Data:** 2026-10-12 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

AbstractThis paper investigates open and closed mappings in Quadruple Neutrosophic Soft TopologicalSpaces \(NSQTS\)\. Precise definitions of complete and partial open and closed mappings areintroduced, along with fundamental theorems clarifying their relationships\. Numerical examplesand practical applications in artificial intelligence, medical diagnostics, and decision\-makingsystems are provided\. The results generalize classical topological concepts and demonstrate theflexibility of quad structures in handling uncertainty and multi\-criteria frameworks\. Keywords: Neutrosophic Set; Soft Set; Quadruple Topological Space; Open Mapping; ClosedMapping; NSQTS\.
