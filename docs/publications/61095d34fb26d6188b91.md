# Can Rényi Accountants Beat Lossless Accountants for DP\-SGD?

**Autores:** Jinho Bok

**Data:** 2026-10-12 · **Tipo:** preprint

## Resumo

Recent frameworks in differential privacy \(DP\), such as privacy loss distributions and $f$\-DP, provide lossless representations of the privacy leakage of mechanisms\. These approaches are often considered to yield tighter $\(\\varepsilon, δ\)$\-DP guarantees than those obtained from relaxed notions such as Rényi DP\. But is that always the case? We study this question for differentially private stochastic gradient descent \(DP\-SGD\) with fixed\-size batches under replace adjacency\. Our key observation is that in this setting, privacy accounting based on Rényi DP can use a pair of worst\-case distributions \(i\.e\., a dominating pair\) that differs from the one used in the lossless frameworks\. In particular, we characterize the worst\-case distributions as two\-dimensional Gaussian mixtures and derive an improved Rényi DP bound for DP\-SGD\. Numerical and asymptotic comparisons show that across various hyperparameter settings, a Rényi DP accountant based on the new bound can yield better privacy guarantees than those obtained from the lossless frameworks\.
