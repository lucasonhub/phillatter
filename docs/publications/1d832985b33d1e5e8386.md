# Methodology and methods for studying the image of the future state

**Autores:** Aleksandr A\. Grebenyuk; Ирина Волкова

**Data:** 2026-10-15 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

This article presents an attempt to generalize methods for analyzing images of the future, in particular, the image of the future state as a complex and largely irrational construct of collective consciousness recorded in textual, graphic, audio, or numerical format\. It examines the specific features of multilevel methodological approaches to analyzing images of the future \(thematic analysis, comprehensive socio\-humanitarian analysis, causal\-level analysis, and linguopolitical prognostics\), as well as the possibilities of applying selected sociological and psychological methods, hermeneutics, semiotics, archetypal analysis, cognitive science, and cybermetrics, using domestic and foreign studies as examples\.
