# Research on Multi\-source Data Fusion Quality Risk Prediction Model for Drug Registration Compliance

**Autores:** Zhenzhen Huang; Huan Gao

**Data:** 2026-10-13 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Drug registration compliance management faces practical challenges such as complex submission documents, dispersed risk sources, and diversified review stages\. Traditional manual review methods have difficulty identifying potential quality risks in a timely manner, especially when cumulative risk is formed by cross source, cross stage, and cross type information\. Against this background, a multi source data fusion quality risk prediction model was developed for the drug registration scenario\. The model integrated submission and review texts, historical deficiency records, inspection results, manufacturing change information, and regulatory provisions\. Unified preprocessing, feature extraction, fusion modeling, and stratified training were then applied to achieve risk level identification\. The results showed that the multi source fusion model outperformed single source models in Accuracy, Recall, F1, and AUC\. Among them, the fusion XGBoost model achieved the best overall performance and significantly reduced the miss rate of high risk cases\. The interpretability analysis further indicated that historical deficiency frequency, regulatory terminology deviation rate, abnormal inspection ratio, and manufacturing change density were the key factors affecting risk judgment\. This study provides a computable, verifiable, and early warning oriented technical path for drug registration compliance management, and offers more targeted digital support for enterprise pre review and regulatory assisted assessment\.
