# Ferumoxytol potentiates ferroptotic radiosensitization through NCOA4\-associated iron mobilization in brain\-metastatic lung cancer cells

**Autores:** Ziqiang Shen; Jiangrui Zhu; Yongliu Yang; Jing Yang; Lisha Rao; Chong Zhou; 夏铀铀

**Data:** 2026-10-14 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Background Brain metastasis remains a major cause of mortality in patients with advanced non\-small cell lung cancer, whereas effective radiosensitization strategies are constrained by heterogeneous tumor responses and the tolerance of normal brain tissue\. Ferumoxytol, a clinically used iron oxide nanoparticle formulation, has emerging translational potential as an iron\-modulating agent\. However, whether Ferumoxytol enhances radiosensitivity in brain\-metastatic lung cancer cells and the mechanisms underlying this effect remain unclear\. Methods PC\-9 lung adenocarcinoma cells and NCI\-H1915 brain\-metastatic lung adenocarcinoma cells were used to evaluate ferumoxytol under basal and irradiated conditions\. Ferroptosis\-associated phenotypes, oxidative stress, clonogenic survival, and cell\-cycle distribution were assessed\. TMT\-based proteomics and public single\-cell and spatial transcriptomic analyses were used for candidate prioritization and disease\-context support\. NCOA4 was evaluated by siRNA\-mediated loss of function, single\-dose clonogenic assays, time\-resolved NCOA4/FTH1 immunoblotting, bafilomycin A1\-based autophagic\-flux analysis, and endogenous NCOA4\-FTH1 co\-immunoprecipitation\. Results Ferumoxytol induced mitochondrial, biochemical, and molecular changes consistent with ferroptosis and enhanced irradiation\-associated ROS accumulation, lipid peroxidation, and loss of clonogenic survival\. Proteomic analysis prioritized NCOA4, while public transcriptomic analyses provided associative disease\-context support\. In H1915 cells, NCOA4 silencing attenuated combined\-treatment Fe 2\+ accumulation, oxidative stress, and γ\-H2AX induction\. The incremental reduction in colony formation produced by adding ferumoxytol to irradiation was also reduced after NCOA4 knockdown in H1915 cells and was no longer significant in PC\-9 cells\. Time\-resolved immunoblotting showed marked FTH1 accumulation rather than net FTH1 depletion, with a late reduction in NCOA4\. Bafilomycin A1 caused further LC3B\-II and SQSTM1/p62 accumulation under IR \+ F, consistent with ongoing autophagic turnover, whereas co\-immunoprecipitation showed greater normalized recovery of FTH1 in NCOA4 immunoprecipitates after IR \+ F\. Conclusion Ferumoxytol enhances ferroptosis\-associated oxidative injury and radiosensitivity in the tested lung cancer cell models\. The combined loss\-of\-function, autophagic\-flux, and protein\-interaction data support the involvement of NCOA4\-associated ferritin handling and iron mobilization\. However, the observed FTH1 accumulation and the absence of iron\-chelator rescue, cargo\-specific lysosomal tracking, and in vivo validation preclude a definitive claim of canonical ferritinophagy or clinical efficacy\.
