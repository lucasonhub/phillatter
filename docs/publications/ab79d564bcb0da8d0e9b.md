**Autores:** Ran Blekhman

## Resumo

In the age of generative artificial intelligence \(AI\), the assumption that writing a paper requires doing science no longer holds true\. Before deciding how to respond to the surge of AI\-generated papers, the field needs to state what biology research is actually for\.
