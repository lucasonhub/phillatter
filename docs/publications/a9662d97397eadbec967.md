# Laboratory\-derived inflammatory–nutritional exploratory phenogroups and their associations with functional limitation and 30\-day same\-center all\-cause unplanned readmission in patients with heart failure

**Autores:** Yilian Zhang; Xu Zhang; 宋鸿彬; Qiyu Hu; Jingzhu Nan

**Data:** 2026-10-09 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Background Heart failure \(HF\) is characterized by marked clinical heterogeneity, while conventional left ventricular ejection fraction \(LVEF\)\-based classification may not fully capture systemic inflammatory, nutritional, hematologic, and cardiorenal abnormalities\. We aimed to identify laboratory\-derived inflammatory–nutritional exploratory phenogroups in hospitalized patients with HF and evaluate their associations with functional limitation and 30\-day same\-center all\-cause unplanned readmission\. Methods This single\-center retrospective cohort included 1,588 hospitalized patients with HF, of whom 1,455 with complete data for 15 prespecified clustering variables were included in the primary analysis\. K\-means, Gaussian mixture models, and DBSCAN were compared, and K\-means with K = 3 was selected based on internal separation, stability, cluster size, and clinical interpretability, with K = 2 used as a sensitivity analysis\. Severe functional limitation was defined as NYHA class III–IV, and 30\-day same\-center all\-cause unplanned readmission was evaluated as the short\-term clinical outcome\. Multivariable logistic regression with HC3 robust standard errors was used to assess these associations, with multiple\-imputation and median\-imputation sensitivity analyses conducted in the full cohort\. Results Three phenogroups were identified: an inflammatory–cardiorenal–nutritional vulnerability phenotype, an anemia–immune dysregulation phenotype, and a relatively preserved laboratory phenotype\. LVEF was similar across groups, whereas severe functional limitation and 30\-day same\-center all\-cause unplanned readmission differed significantly\. After enhanced adjustment, compared with Cluster 3, the odds ratios for severe functional limitation were 3\.814 and 5\.594 for Clusters 1 and 2, respectively, and the corresponding odds ratios for 30\-day same\-center all\-cause unplanned readmission were 1\.966 and 2\.616\. Higher quartiles of both ALI and PNI were associated with lower odds of severe functional limitation; higher ALI quartiles and the highest PNI quartile were associated with lower odds of 30\-day same\-center all\-cause unplanned readmission\. Conclusion Routine admission laboratory measurements identified three exploratory HF phenogroups with distinct inflammatory, nutritional, hematologic, and cardiorenal profiles\. After adjustment for continuous LVEF and NT\-proBNP, the two vulnerable phenogroups remained associated with severe functional limitation and 30\-day same\-center all\-cause unplanned readmission, with directionally consistent findings in the full\-cohort and K = 2 sensitivity analyses\. ALI and PNI reflect laboratory\-derived inflammatory–nutritional vulnerability but cannot replace formal nutritional assessment or establish incremental predictive value\. These findings remain exploratory and require validation in prospective multicenter cohorts\.
