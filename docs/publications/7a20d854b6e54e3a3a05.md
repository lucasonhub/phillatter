# VISUAL ACTIVITY IN PEOPLE WITH MYOPIA AND FACTORS AFFECTING IT

**Autores:** Volodymyr Drozdov; О\. М\. Мацуга; Ірина Дроздова; Svitlana Martynenko; Ольга Володимирівна Полковенко

**Data:** 2026-10-17 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Introduction\. Myopic maculopathy worsens the prognosis in almost 40% of people with high myopia, leading to more than half of the cases of blindness or visual impairment among adults\. Significant differences in biometric parameters have been established in people with different degrees of myopia and axial length\. Compared with axial length, its ratio to the radius of curvature of the cornea has greater accuracy in myopia of all degrees and a significant correlation with the spherical equivalent\.Aim\. To assess changes in biometric parameters and factors affecting them in people with different degrees of myopia\.Materials and methods\. A total of 200 patients with myopia were examined, who were divided into 4 groups: 1 – 11 people without myopia; 2 – 77 people with mild myopia, 3 – 74 with moderate myopia; group 4 – 38 with high myopia\. Statistical analysis of the study results was carried out using methods of mathematical statistics, including descriptive statistics, correlation analysis, and multiple linear regression analysis\.Results\. In people with progressive myopia, a significant decrease in uncorrected distance visual acuity is observed in normal and cycloplegic conditions; vision correction Sphere \(D\) in normal and cycloplegic conditions, refractometry Sphere \(D\) in normal conditions are associated with an increase in uncorrected distance visual acuity\. Multiple linear regression analysis showed that the increase in uncorrected visual acuity in people with myopia is associated with refractometry Sphere \(D\) under non\-cycloplegic and cycloplegic conditions, Cylinder \(D\) in cycloplegia, age and gender; while its decrease – with axial length \(mm\), keratometry Cylinder \(D\) in cycloplegia, optical power of the weak meridian, pupil diameter \(mm\)\.Conclusions\. Clarification of the features of the relationships between eye parameters is the basis for clarifying the etiopathogenesis of myopia, the ability to predict complications and develop strategies for its prevention and correction\.
