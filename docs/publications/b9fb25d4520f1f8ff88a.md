# HbA1c\-Informed Pattern Recognition for Diabetes Mellitus Classification and Hospital Readmission Risk Analysis

**Autores:** Sarita B\. Ghansawant; Abhijeet G\. Dhepe; Mukta Dhopeshwarkar

**Data:** 2026-10-12 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Glycated hemoglobin \(HbA1c\) is an established indicator of longer\-term glycaemic exposure, but hospital readmission risk in patients with diabetes depends on additional clinical and healthcare\-utilization factors\. This paper presents a reproducible pattern\-recognition framework that incorporates HbA1c and structured clinical variables from the Diabetes 130\-US Hospitals for Years 1999–2008 dataset\. The resource contains 101,766 inpatient encounters across 130 U\.S\. hospitals and integrated delivery networks\. The proposed analysis addresses missing values, categorical encoding, patient\-level data leakage, class imbalance, and model calibration\. We compare logistic regression, decision trees, random forests, support vector machines, and gradient\-boosting classifiers\. Because the supplied source paper does not contain reproducible experimental predictions or raw model outputs, no new accuracy or area\-under\-the\-curve values are claimed\. Instead, the study motivates a transparent model\-validation protocol and uses published performance estimates strictly as contextual benchmarks\. The framework is intended for research and clinician\-supported risk stratification, not autonomous diagnosis\.
