# Eslami, Abolhassan: First time? Husserl, Merleau\-Ponty, and friends as hanged\-men \- The Lived Experience of a Mind at Light Speed

**Autores:** 

**Data de descoberta:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** unknown

## Descrição do feed

This paper explores the profound cognitive and philosophical meaning of relativistic velocities of consciousness, particularly light speed\. Drawing upon the findings of cognitive science, the philosophy of mind, and theoretical physics, we explore the paradoxical nature of subjective experience when space and time coordinates undergo radical transformations\. We explore how notions of qualia, embodiment, memory, and causality are rearranged or disintegrate entirely within a light speed reference frame\. Synthesizing the thoughts of philosophers like Nassim Nicholas Taleb, David Graeber, Maurice Merleau\-Ponty, Edmund Husserl, and Gilles Deleuze, we present a novel way of thinking about consciousness as not an object, but an active, relational process deeply entwined with the spacetime web\. The essay ends by positing that consciousness, far from being a cosmic constant, may only be given meaning in the &\#x27;middle speeds&\#x27; of life as a boundary condition between absolute motion and absolute stasis\. This inquiry challenges anthropocentric thinking in our understanding of mind and offers a speculative but scientific inquiry into the final nature of existence in a relativistic universe\. \(direct link \)
