# AI\-Driven Supply Chain Transformation in Bangladesh: An Integrated Multitech Approach and Empirical Evaluation of Readiness to Adopt

**Autores:** Tahmid Hossain

**Data:** 2026-10-13 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Abstract Bangladesh&\#x27;s industrial supply chains are spanning Ready\-Made Garments, pharmaceuticals, FMCG, logistics sectors continue to be hampered by structural bottlenecks, information asymmetry, and siloed operations, even as global markets shift toward Industry 4\.0/5\.0 digital integration\. While individual technologies such as Generative AI, blockchain, IoT, predictive analytics, and robotics have been studied separately, few frameworks integrate them into a single operational architecture, and no prior South Asian study has empirically compared adoption readiness across university students, academic faculty, and industry professionals\. This study addresses that gap using a cross\-sectional survey of 257 respondents, analyzed through the lens of the Technology Acceptance Model \(TAM\) and Resource\-Based View \(RBV\)\. Results show high Perceived Usefulness \(cost reduction, M=3\.96; risk reduction, M=3\.79\) and strong Individual Readiness \(M=3\.99\), positively correlated with usefulness \(r=\.665, p&lt;\.001\)\. However, Organizational Readiness was markedly lower \(M=3\.26\), driven by talent shortages \(44\.4%\) and capital constraints \(36\.6%\) \- revealing an &quot;Awareness\-Execution Paradox&quot; between individual enthusiasm and institutional capacity\. Chi\-square tests confirmed significant perception gaps between professionals and students on local AI awareness \(p&lt;\.001\) and integration ease \(p=\.049\)\. The study proposes a five\-layer Unified Multitech Smart Supply Chain Framework and offers practical implications for academia, industry, and policymakers seeking to accelerate digital supply chain transformation in Bangladesh\.
