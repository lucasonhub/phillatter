# BO&\#x27;LAJAK O&\#x27;QITUVCHINING REFLEKSIV\-PEDAGOGIK KOMPETENTLIGI: TUSHUNCHANING ILMIY\-NAZARIY EVOLYUTSIYASI VA TARKIBIY STRUKTURASI

**Autores:** Utkirbek Ilhomjon o&\#x27;g&\#x27;li Xakimov

**Data:** 2026-10-09 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

This study conceptualizes the scientific and theoretical trajectory of future educators&\#x27; reflective\-pedagogical competence while dissecting its structural framework\. The primary objective centers on tracing the conceptual trajectory of &quot;reflection&quot; and &quot;competence,&quot; substantiating the synthesized notion of &quot;reflective\-pedagogical competence,&quot; and offering a theoretical rationale for its four\-dimensional matrix comprising cognitive\-analytical, activity\- procedural, metacognitive\-reflective, and motivational\-value dimensions\.
