# Forest\-food supply chain resilience in mountain outdoor regions: a reconfigurability perspective

**Autores:** Qianlan Chen; Huan Liang; Xi Deng; Yuelin Ren

**Data:** 2026-10-13 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Introduction Forest\-based food supply chains face ecological seasonality, dispersed production, perishability, and uneven transport access\. This study examines how organizational capabilities combine to support forest\-based food supply\-chain resilience \(FFSCR\) from a reconfigurability perspective\. Methods The analysis draws on 409 managerial questionnaires nested within 50 firms in Zhangjiajie, Xishuangbanna, and Changbai Mountain\. Measurement quality was evaluated at the respondent level, and construct scores were aggregated to the firm level, supported by ICC\(1\) values of 0\.251–0\.342 and ICC\(2\) values of 0\.732–0\.809\. Necessary Condition Analysis \(NCA\) and fuzzy\-set Qualitative Comparative Analysis \(fsQCA\) were applied to examine necessary constraints and sufficient configurations\. Results Reconfiguration readiness was the only condition meeting both the substantive and statistical NCA criteria \( d = 0\.123, p &lt; 0\.001\) and the fsQCA necessity criterion \(consistency = 0\.921; coverage = 0\.848\)\. Five intermediate configurations generated high FFSCR, with overall consistency of 0\.945 and coverage of 0\.883\. Reconfiguration readiness appeared in all five configurations, while partner resilience was a core condition in four\. Robustness checks using 90th/70th/10th\-percentile recalibration reproduced the five configurations \(consistency = 0\.950; coverage = 0\.886\) and maintained the necessity of reconfiguration readiness \(NCA d = 0\.110, p &lt; 0\.001; fsQCA consistency = 0\.926\)\. Discussion Reconfiguration readiness functions as an enabling constraint rather than a sufficient condition on its own\. Relational, agile, financial, flexible, and technological capabilities provide alternative mechanisms through which firms convert readiness into resilient supply\-chain performance\.
