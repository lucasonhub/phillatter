# Johnson, Jamison: On Dreaming \- The Separation of Mind and Body During a Moment of Rest

**Autores:** 

**Data de descoberta:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** unknown

## Descrição do feed

When&\#x27;s the last time you got a decent night&\#x27;s sleep? This short lyric\-philosophical essay begins with an author who sleeps four hours on still days and soundly after long ones, and with a Jay caught during an episode of Rick and Morty\. It sets two literatures side by side: the rare inherited short sleepers found by the Fu and Ptáček group, and the dream yoga and Yoga Nidra traditions that keep awareness awake while the body sleeps\. It warns that sleeping less on purpose is not the same thing\. It imagines sleep as mind and body, two sides of one thing, resting on different schedules, and gathers the dream discoveries reported by Coleridge, Mary Shelley, Ramanujan, Kekulé, and Otto Loewi as the discoverers&\#x27; own accounts\. Dreams appear as the brain running on what it gathered, to be heard out and let go, with Tolkien&\#x27;s line that we make by the law in which we&\#x27;re made\. The essay closes on rest of both kinds\. \(direct link \)
