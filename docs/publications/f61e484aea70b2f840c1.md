# Prophylactic effects of wood rot fungus Ganoderma applanatum \(Pers\.\) Pat\. against acetaminophen\-induced hepatotoxicity

**Autores:** Ruby Varghese; Yogesh Bharat Dalvi; Prasad Y\. Lamrood

**Data:** 2026-10-12 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Objectives: The study investigates the role of Ganoderma applanatum \(Pers\.\) Pat\. against acetaminophen mediated hepatotoxicity in Swiss albino mice\. Materials and Methods: Acetaminophen \(150 mg/Kg\) was orally administered to the mice followed by the G\. applanatum water\-alcohol extract \(GAWE\)\. The extent of liver impairment was assessed by the blood serum parameter, antioxidant status, and lipid peroxidation assay\. The mode of prevention from acetaminophen\-induced damage at the molecular level was also analyzed\. Results: GAWE administration significantly increased blood serum parameters and a reduction in MDA \(Malondialdehyde\) levels was observed\. Up\-regulation of hepatic antioxidant enzymes, such as BCL\-2 \(B\-cell lymphoma 2\) and COX\-2 \(Cycloxygenase\) gene with the reduction in BAX \(BCL2 Associated X, Apoptosis Regulator\) mRNA expression was also observed in the GAWE administered group indicating hepatoprotection through hepatocyte regeneration Conclusion: GAWE contains active secondary metabolites with high antioxidant properties which mitigates acetaminophen toxicity and conceivably be relevant for treating hepatic ailments\. Further investigations on secondary metabolite composition are solicited\.
