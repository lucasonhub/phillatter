# Disputatio in the digital agora: Opening the doctorate through dialogue

**Autores:** Helen J\. DeWaard; Verena Roberts; Gabi Witthaus; Leo Havemann

**Data:** 2026-10-09 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

The authors of this collaborative autoethnographic research examine the tensions between ideas around research in open doctorate experiences and the realities of practice within traditional doctoral processes, presentations, and productions\. Using reflections and conversations, this research illuminates digital spaces, or agoras, where doctoral knowledge is shared and passed on through stories, writings, and lived experiences of those who agitate and rupture knowledge building, particularly as dissertations shift into the open\. Through the stories of disputatio, forms of discourse and dialogues in open and digital spaces, shared by the authors as practitioners and advocates for opening the dissertation, many inherent ethical values, risks, and challenges are revealed\. Nuanced questions and decisions are woven into the macro, meso, micro, and nano levels of opening a dissertation discussion\. In this paper, the authors explore where, when and with whom doctoral candidates can openly share, build connections and networks, join professional partnerships, enhance communications with media production tools, and draw on the feedback and support of others to innovate, collaborate in order to build knowledge and face tensions in open educational discourse and dialogue\.
