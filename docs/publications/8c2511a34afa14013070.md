# Asymptotic Analysis of Discrete\-Time Self\-Attention Dynamics in Transformers

**Autores:** Claudio Altafini

**Data:** 2026-10-09 · **Tipo:** preprint

## Resumo

In machine learning, a self\-attention dynamics describes the attention mechanism of transformers, and is obtained treating the layer index of a transformer as time index\. Even though naturally discrete\-time, this dynamical system is most often studied in continuous\-time\. Scope of this paper is to provide a thorough investigation of the discrete\-time version of the self\-attention dynamics: a complete classification of its equilibria is given, and their stability properties are studied, in both the single\-head and the multihead case\. We show that in both cases the main attractor of the dynamics is a consensus equilibrium aligned with the principal eigenvector of the value matrix \(in the single\-head case, a corresponding matrix of weights in the multihead case\), even though other locally asymptotically stable bipartite equilibria can be show to exist at least in the single\-head situation\. The behavior is confirmed by looking at the learned weight matrices of a real transformer\.
