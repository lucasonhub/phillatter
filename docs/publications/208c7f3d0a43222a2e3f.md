# Johnson, Jamison: On Completing \- On the felt moment, the forces you cannot feel, and knowing where to set the tools down

**Autores:** 

**Data de descoberta:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** unknown

## Descrição do feed

Have you ever worked so long on the how of something that you forgot the why? The finale of the On ——ing series writes one local equation for a felt moment — every sensory channel gated by change, met with a leaning toward or away, and bound into one field — and names it the anatomy of a Jay, with a life&\#x27;s accumulation of such moments as character\. It runs the four fundamental forces through that line: gravity is never felt directly, only the floor that resists it; electromagnetism has an unfelt band and a felt one; the nuclear forces lie wholly beyond any receptor&\#x27;s reach; and the leaning itself, valence, is the one force native to experience, the framework&\#x27;s claim that intent changes outcomes\. The essay marks where the testable part ends and states its final conviction — a designed and watched life — as philosophy rather than proof, then turns toward anxiety, interoception, and meditation as the near work that comes next\. \(direct link \)
