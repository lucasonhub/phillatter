# NON\-INVASIVE BIOMARKERS AND ADVANCED IMAGING FOR EARLY DETECTION OF ENDOMETRIOSIS: DIAGNOSTIC PERFORMANCE, REAL\-WORLD EVIDENCE, ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE, AND IMPLEMENTATION PRIORITIES

**Autores:** Xolboyeva Mashhura

**Data:** 2026-10-18 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Endometriosis is a chronic, heterogeneous inflammatory disease for which delayed recognition and unequal access to specialist imaging remain important clinical problems\. The transition from surgically centered diagnosis to an integrated, non\-invasive strategy has increased interest in expert transvaginal ultrasonography, magnetic resonance imaging, circulating biomarkers, and saliva\-based microRNA signatures\. Objective: To critically appraise the diagnostic role of these modalities and describe how real\-world data, causal inference and artificial intelligence can support safe implementation\. Methods:
