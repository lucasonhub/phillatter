# Digital\-green coupling coordination and firm labor productivity: evidence from Chinese listed companies

**Autores:** Juhao Lai; Guangxing Hu; Bin Li

**Data:** 2026-10-15 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Introduction This study examines whether the coordinated development of firms’ digital and green subsystems is associated with labor productivity\. Methods Using an unbalanced panel of Chinese A\-share listed firms from 2009 to 2023, we construct amultidimensional digital‐green coupling coordination \(DGCC\) index and estimate firm and year fixed‐effects models\. Results Higher DGCC is associated with higher labor productivity, and the result remains stable across alternative measures, fixed‐effects structures, inference procedures, additional controls, temporal‐ordering tests, and medium\-horizon changes\. The evidence further associates DGCC with a more highly educated workforce, a greater relative allocation of employees to technical functions, and a smaller administrative\-employment share\. The relationship varies with labor\-cost pressure, human‐capital levels, and routine‐task intensity\. Further analysis links DGCC to lower expense intensity and stronger cash realization\. Discussion These findings highlight the workforce, organizational, and operating adjustments that accompany firms’ coordinated digital and green development\.
