# Taqi, Mohamed: Null Monism : Why Qualia Arise from Nothing

**Autores:** 

**Data de descoberta:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** unknown

## Descrição do feed

A persistent intuition that motivates panpsychism — thereby generating the combination problem — holds that parts lacking experience cannot combine to produce a whole that has it\. We argue that this intuition rests on an unexamined assumption: that phenomenological contents compose by a null\-preserving operation\. We show that any composition rule adequate to preserve the structural sensitivity of experience generates non\-null composites from null parts\. Qualitative character turns out to be nesting topology — forced by the formalism, not assumed\. An argument from physical anchoring connects the set\-theoretic result to the physical world, revealing that nesting topology is causal topology, and showing that wherever causal organisation closes into an irreducible whole, qualia become a logical necessity\. In the discussion we draw out what follows once the null\-preservation assumption is dropped, for panpsychism and the explanatory gap\. Crucially, the argument begins from the methodological assumption that elementary parts carry no phenomenological content — which means panpsychism is not required\.
