# Italian Society of Obesity XII National Congress

**Autores:** 2026; :O1

**Data:** 2026-10-15 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Introduction: Excess White Adipose Tissue \(WAT\) deposition in obesity is characterized by low\-grade chronic inflammation; as such, WAT is one of the organs mostly affected by senescence, a cellular process characterized by cell\-cycle arrest and metabolic alterations\.Although cell senescence \(CS\) is a physiological processes, chronic or excessive CS is harmful for organisms and is a key driver of aging\.Senescent cells remain viable and release inflammatory cytokines which spread systemic tissue damage, thus contributing to the multi\-organ dysfunction of obesity, of which cardiovascular disease is the most common\.Reducing CS could therefore represent a valuable therapeutic tool toward obesity\-associated diseases\.We have previously shown that essential amino acids \(EAAs\), by increasing mitochondrial biogenesis \(MB\), have a positive outcome on obesity and diabetes, counteract doxorubicininduced cardiotoxicity, and also extend lifespan in mice 345 \.Here, we investigated whether the anti\-obesity and cardioprotective effects of EAAs are associated with reduced CS\.Methods: C57BL/6N mice were fed with chow \(CD\), a high\-fat diet \(HFD\-60% fat\), or a HFD in which the protein content was substituted with a mixture of EAAs \(HFD\-EAA\) for 10 weeks; afterwards, markers of CS and MB were analysed in epididymal WAT \(eWAT\)\.Furthermore, doxorubicin\-treated HL\-1 cardiomyocytes were used as in an in vitro model of cardiotoxicity\-induced senescence\.Results: After 10 weeks, body weight \(BW\) and glucose intolerance of HFD\-fed animals increased \(\+ 35% and \+ 56% vs CD, respectively\); however, HFD\-EAA diet prevented the increase in BW and improved glucose homeostasis of HFD\-fed mice \(\-25%, \-22% vs HFD\)\.This was associated with an increase in the expression of CS markers and β\-gal staining in eWAT of HFD mice, which were both blunted by HFD\-EAA diet\.Furthermore, in vitro supplementation of an EAA mixture with an equivalent formulation of HFD\-EAA diet also increased MB and oxygen consumption and normalized the doxorubicin\-induced increase in CS markers and β\-gal staining in HL\-1\.Conclusion: Our data suggest that EAAs mitigate CS in obesity and cardiotoxicity\.This nutritional strategy may therefore represent a promising approach for combating CS and improving outcomes in obesity and age\-related diseases\.
