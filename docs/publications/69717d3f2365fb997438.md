# PENGARUH PENGGUNAAN CHATGPT TERHADAP HASIL BELAJAR KOGNITIF MAHASISWA PROGRAM STUDI PAI IAIH PANCOR PADA MATA KULIAH METODOLOGI PENELITIAN

**Autores:** Siti Humaero&\#x27;; Siti Rauhillah

**Data:** 2026-10-13 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

The use of ChatGPT among university students is growing rapidly, yet empirical evidence on its effect on cognitive learning outcomes in courses that demand higher\-order thinking, such as Research Methodology, remains limited in Islamic higher education\. This study aimed to determine the effect of ChatGPT use on the cognitive learning outcomes of Islamic Religious Education \(PAI\) students at Institut Agama Islam Hamzanwadi Pancor in the Research Methodology course\. A quantitative approach with an ex post facto design was used\. The population was 487 students in the sixth and eighth semesters; the sample of 83 students was determined using the Slovin formula and proportionate stratified random sampling\. ChatGPT use was measured with a four\-point Likert questionnaire \(20 valid items\), while cognitive learning outcomes were taken from final course grades through documentation\. Data were analyzed with simple linear regression in SPSS after the normality, linearity, and heteroscedasticity assumptions were met\. The results show a regression equation of Y = 64\.465 \+ 0\.278X, with a t value of 17\.778 \(greater than the t table value of 1\.990; Sig\. 0\.000\) and an R² of 0\.796\. ChatGPT use therefore had a positive and significant effect on cognitive learning outcomes, accounting for 79\.6% of their variance\. These findings should be interpreted cautiously because of the ex post facto design, the narrow range of grades, and the measurement of usage intensity rather than usage quality\.
