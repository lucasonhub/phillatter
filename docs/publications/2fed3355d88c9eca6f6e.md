# A Bethe\-Ansatz Source of Early Token Correlations: A marginal\-preserving construction for parallel proposals

**Autores:** Abraham Chachamovits

**Data:** 2026-10-12 · **Tipo:** preprint

## Resumo

Parallel token proposals need a joint law, not merely accurate token marginals\. We construct coordinate\-Bethe\-ansatz laws from the periodic XXZ chain and compare them with their maximum\-entropy pairwise projections at identical one\- and two\-site moments\. In an eight\-site, three\-excitation sector, symmetry makes the full probability\-law difference one\-dimensional, determined by a contiguous three\-site moment $δ$\. Along the Bethe branch, this gap changes sign and vanishes exactly at $Δ=\(3\+\\sqrt3\)/2$, where the eigenstate law becomes pairwise\. A nonlinear two\-token readout preserves separate token marginals yet exposes a distance $2\|δ\|$; a direct three\-token readout preserves all one\- and two\-token marginals yet exposes $4\|δ\|$\. At $Δ=1\.5$ these deterministic binary distances are $0\.02654573$ and $0\.05309145$\. We give an exact target\-alignment formula and prove a strict speculative accepted\-prefix advantage throughout an unrestricted open neighborhood of a constructed target\. These are finite\-law existence results, not measured language\-model or wall\-clock gains\.
