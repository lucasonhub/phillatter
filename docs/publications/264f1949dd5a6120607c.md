# Liranza Diaz, Adonis: Operative Closure and Orchestrated Collapse: What the Quantum Physics of Consciousness Cannot Derive

**Autores:** 

**Data de descoberta:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** unknown

## Descrição do feed

Recent work by Michael Wiest on Orch OR theory \(Penrose/Hameroff\) revives, on new empirical grounds, the hypothesis of a quantum substrate for consciousness, and proposes to solve the problem of the separation of subjects through a mechanism of amplitude cancellation specific to each conscious body\. This article establishes that this mechanism presupposes the biological boundary it is supposed to explain \-a petitio principii that Wiest himself identifies without resolving, and that his own treatment of split\-brain patients renders untenable, and which recourse to the Markov blanket of active inference does not repair\. This mainly negative result is contrasted with a framework distinguishing two principles \-actualization and individuation, Àṣẹ and Orí in the vocabulary of Ifá philosophy \- derivable from the structure of the problem itself prior to any terminological borrowing\. This distinction accounts by construction for graded clinical states, such as depersonalization, which Wiest&\#x27;s one\-principle model cannot by construction explain, and opens, without imposing it, a reading of Orch OR data compatible with an interface model rather than with a local production of consciousness\.
