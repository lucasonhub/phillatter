# A TPACK–Bloom’s Taxonomy Hybrid Framework for Integrating Technology in Accounting Education in Sub\-Saharan African Countries

**Autores:** Emmanuel Kojo Oseifuah

**Data:** 2026-10-09 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

The rapid evolution of digital technologies necessitates a transformative approach to accounting education, particularly in developing countries\. Building on Mishra and Koehler’s \(2006\) foundational assertion that there is no universally accepted method for integrating technology into the educational curricula, this study proposes a TPACK\-Bloom’s Taxonomy framework to articulate how technology enhances learning in accounting education\. TPACK \(Technological Pedagogical Content Knowledge\) offers a multidimensional lens for aligning technological tools with pedagogical strategies and disciplinary content, ensuring that emerging technologies such as artificial intelligence and data analytics are contextually and instructionally relevant\. Bloom’s Taxonomy complements this by structuring cognitive development across six hierarchical levels, ranging from remembering to creating, within technology\-enhanced learning environments\. Together, these two frameworks provide a versatile tool for designing curriculum interventions that foster digital literacy, critical thinking, and professional competence\. This study makes three key contributions to the literature\. First, it bridges the gap between technology adoption and pedagogical effectiveness by linking digital tools to cognitive learning outcomes\. Second, it contributes to human capital development by promoting the integration of technical, cognitive, and digital competencies\. Third, it supports organisational transformation by offering a practical, scalable approach to technology\-enhanced learning in resource\-constrained contexts\.
