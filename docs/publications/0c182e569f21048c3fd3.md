# Moderating roles of strategic innovation compensation effect on new quality productive forces and firm performance: Evidence from Chinese A\-share listed companies

**Autores:** Kai Jiang; Haslinah Binti Muhamad; Mohammad Noor Hisham Osman

**Data:** 2026-10-15 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

New Quality Productive Forces \(NQPF\) have become China’s primary policy imperative to drive high\-quality development of its economy\. However, empirical evidence on the effects of NQPF on firm performance and the conditions that strengthen such effects is limited\. Building upon the resource\-based view and innovation compensation theory \(Barney, 1991; Porter &amp; van der Linde, 1995\), this study explores the link between NQPF and firm value and the moderating role of the innovation compensation effect \(ICE\)\. Using two\-way fixed effects and instrumental variable \(IV\) analysis, the study examines a panel dataset of 4,256 Chinese A\-share listed companies between 2015 and 2024\. It finds that NQPF has a positive and significant effect on the long\-term value of firms\. Although the ICE \(measured as the invention patent ratio\) does not directly increase the value of a firm, it significantly increases the positive effect of NQPF on firm performance\. Furthermore, this moderating role is significantly stronger in non\-state\-owned enterprises, high\-tech industries, and companies in the Eastern and Central regions of China\. The results contribute to the existing evidence on NQPF, as they provide micro\-level evidence on the value creation mechanism of NQPF and have practical implications for policymakers and firms\.
