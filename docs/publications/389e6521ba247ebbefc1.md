# Integrating Structured Writing Workshops with Metacognitive Instruction to Enhance Vietnamese Secondary Students’ Argumentative Writing

**Autores:** Milly Poon; Vinh Long Huy; Chen Lee Lin; Honjo Akira

**Data:** 2026-10-12 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Argumentative writing requires learners to coordinate reasoning, evidence, organization, audience awareness, language use, and revision, yet secondary\-school EFL students often receive limited support for regulating these interconnected processes\. This qualitative study investigated how integrating structured writing workshops with metacognitive instruction influenced students’ argumentative\-writing performance and their awareness and regulation of writing\. Four EFL teachers and 13 secondary\-school learners participated in the study\. Data were collected through semi\-structured interviews and classroom observations and analyzed thematically using deductive and inductive coding\. The findings indicated perceived improvements in the organization of arguments, thesis alignment, use of supporting evidence, consideration of counterarguments, and revision of higher\-order textual features\. Structured workshop stages, including planning, drafting, peer response, revision, and editing, provided external scaffolding that made argumentative writing more manageable\. Metacognitive prompts and tools supported learners in identifying goals, considering audience, monitoring claim–evidence relationships, recognizing weaknesses, and evaluating revision priorities\. However, the development was uneven\. Planning improved more consistently than monitoring and independent self\-evaluation, while less\-proficient learners continued to depend on teacher guidance, peer support, and checklists\. Peer feedback also varied in quality, indicating the need for sustained feedback\-literacy instruction\. The study concludes that combining workshop\-based scaffolding with explicit metacognitive regulation offers a promising framework for developing argumentative writing in secondary EFL settings\.
