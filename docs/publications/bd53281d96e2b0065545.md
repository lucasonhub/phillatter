# Book What Is Cancer Professional Expanded Manuscript by Prof\. Dr\. Zeeshan Qaiser

## Resumo

BOOK DESCRIPTION What Is Cancer? Professional Expanded Manuscript By Prof\. Dr\. Zeeshan Qaiser An In\-Depth Exploration of Cancer Biology, Genetics, Diagnosis, Prevention, Treatment, and the Future of Cancer Research Cancer is one of the most complex and challenging diseases in modern medicine\. To understand cancer is to explore the fundamental mechanisms of life itself—how normal cells grow, divide, repair damage, communicate, and maintain balance, and how these processes can become disrupted, leading to uncontrolled cellular growth and disease\. What Is Cancer? — Professional Expanded Manuscript is an educational and research\-oriented exploration of cancer, designed to develop a deeper understanding of its biological foundations, molecular mechanisms, clinical significance, and evolving scientific landscape\. Authored by Prof\. Dr\. Zeeshan Qaiser, this manuscript aims to connect foundational knowledge with modern developments in cancer science and the future possibilities of precision medicine\. The book explores the origins and development of cancer, the role of DNA mutations and genetic instability, oncogenes, tumour\-suppressor genes, cellular signalling pathways, inherited susceptibility, environmental risk factors, and the complex interactions between cancer cells and the human immune system\. It introduces readers to the major types of cancer, tumour development, invasion, metastasis, and the biological processes that distinguish malignant cells from normal tissues\. A major focus is placed on the evolution of cancer diagnosis and treatment, including histopathology, molecular diagnostics, cancer biomarkers, medical imaging, surgical oncology, radiotherapy, chemotherapy, targeted therapies, immunotherapy, hormonal therapy, and emerging approaches in personalised and precision oncology\. The manuscript also examines the importance of cancer prevention, evidence\-based screening, early diagnosis, genetic counselling, vaccination against cancer\-associated infections, and multidisciplinary cancer care\. Looking towards the future, the book considers promising research directions in cancer genomics, artificial intelligence\-assisted diagnostics, liquid biopsy, gene\-based approaches, next\-generation therapeutics, and personalised treatment strategies\. These developments are discussed as evolving areas of scientific investigation rather than guaranteed cures, highlighting both their potential and their limitations\. Beyond laboratory discoveries and clinical interventions, this manuscript recognises the human dimension of cancer: the psychological, social, ethical, and economic challenges experienced by patients and families\. It emphasises the importance of patient\-centred care, supportive and palliative services, equitable access to treatment, and international collaboration in advancing cancer research and healthcare\. Through a structured, multidisciplinary approach, What Is Cancer? seeks to provide a valuable foundation for students, researchers, healthcare professionals, medical educators, and readers interested in understanding the science of cancer and the direction of future oncology research\. Ultimately, this book presents cancer not merely as a disease to be studied, but as a scientific challenge that demands continuous discovery, responsible innovation, early intervention, and a global commitment to improving human health\. Author: Prof\. Dr\.
