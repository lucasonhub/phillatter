# Clinical characteristics of cadonilimab\-associated infusion reactions: a severity\-stratified analysis of 52 cases and preliminary management framework

**Autores:** Jinlan Li; Rui Qiu; Guisen Yin; Yong Pan; Po Chen

**Data:** 2026-10-09 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Objective To describe the clinical characteristics of infusion\-related reactions \(IRRs\) associated with cadonilimab in a real\-world cohort and propose preliminary management considerations based on these observations\. Methods A descriptive retrospective case series was conducted, involving data collection from 52 patients who received cadonilimab and subsequently experienced infusion\-related reactions at a tertiary hospital between July 2022 and December 2025\. Patients were classified into a common IRR group \(n=31\) and a severe IRR group \(n=21\) based on the severity criteria outlined in CTCAE 6\.0\. We conducted a comparative analysis of baseline characteristics, IRR onset timing, clinical manifestations, management strategies, and outcomes between the two groups\. Results The median time to IRR onset was 20 minutes post\-infusion, with the highest proportion of recorded IRR cases occurring during the second treatment cycle \(42\.3%\)\. Severe IRR manifested as multi\-organ involvement in 61\.9% of cases, predominantly affecting the respiratory and cardiovascular systems\. Patients with severe IRR more frequently received corticosteroids combined with antihistamines \(57\.1% vs\. 29\.0%\) and had higher rates of premedication \(76\.2% vs\. 45\.2%\)\. Twenty\-four patients \(46\.2%\) attempted same\-cycle infusion resumption; among these, 15 \(62\.5%\) successfully completed the infusion without symptom recurrence\. Among the 10 severe\-group patients who attempted same\-cycle infusion resumption, 6 \(60\.0%\) experienced recurrent symptoms; among all 21 severe\-group patients, the recurrence rate was 28\.6% \(6/21\)\. The clinically relevant same\-cycle recurrence rates were 9/24 overall \(37\.5%\), 3/14 \(21\.4%\) in the common group, and 6/10 \(60\.0%\) in the severe group\. Conclusion Cadonilimab is associated with early\-onset infusion\-related reactions frequently involving multiple organ systems\. Based on our institutional experience, we propose a preliminary management framework that requires prospective validation before it can be recommended for routine clinical practice\.
