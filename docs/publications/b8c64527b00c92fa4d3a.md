# Monsó, Susana: Science is based on assumptions\. Philosophy can make sure they’re the right ones\.

**Autores:** 

**Data de descoberta:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** unknown

## Descrição do feed

\_Science\_ 393 \(6814\):eael0147\. 2026When I was a postdoctoral researcher, I spent several years teaching at an interdisciplinary research institute in Vienna, Austria\. There, my main course sought to train master’s students—mostly biologists and veterinarians—to write a philosophical reflection on some aspect of the comparative cognition studies they were running\. The students would tend to arrive in my course heavily prejudiced, protesting the task of abandoning the “objectivity” of scientific data collection to engage in what they saw as mere opining\. Each year, however, their defenses would slowly unravel as they came to realize that not only was philosophical reasoning far from giving one’s opinion \(I forbid them from using phrases such as “I believe” and “I think” in their essays because they often stand in place of proper argumentation\), but also that the studies they were carrying out were full of underlying philosophical assumptions\. Learning to philosophize better helped them to be better scientists, enabling them to identify and question, where appropriate, these assumptions\. This is a lesson that can be extended beyond the classroom\. \(direct link \)
