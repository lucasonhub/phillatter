# Fujie, Naoto: What Does the Unity of a Scientific Image Add to Engagement with Reality? The Absolute Picture, Spaces of Reasons, History\-Reentry Generative Intervals, the Stellarization of Scientific Operational Shapes, and Xenoscience

**Autores:**

## Descrição do feed

\_Zenodo\_ 1\. 2026This article examines what the unity of a scientific image adds to engagement with reality\. It considers Peirce’s conception of inquiry and convergence, Sellars’s absolute picture, Brassier’s scientific realism, Hegelian systematicity, and Brandom’s space of reasons, while distinguishing the regulative significance of ultimate unity from the actual conditions of scientific observation, justification, and intervention\. The central argument is that the formation of a unified scientific image can generate new relations of comparison, inferential constraint, experimental coordination, and criticism\. Yet unification may also suppress distinctions, restrict observability, or alter the conditions under which earlier records can be reused\. Neither successful operational coupling nor local inferential integration, by itself, establishes the necessity of convergence upon a single absolute picture\. The article develops this argument through history\-reentry generative intervals and the proposed stellarization of scientific operational shapes\. It examines heterogeneous autonomous\-scientific configurations capable of modifying observational variables, experimental apparatuses, records, and subsequent possibilities of intervention\. A stronger conception of Xenoscience additionally raises questions concerning energy acquisition, material self\-reproduction, maintenance, operational suspension, structural and temporal death, and reactivation across distinct configurations\. The analysis also incorporates nihilistic operation as the possible loss of categorical privilege without treating extinction, meaninglessness, or negation as an ultimate principle\. It engages scientific pluralism and existing autonomous\-science research without claiming that the proposed configurations have been experimentally realized\. The resulting philosophical position recognizes the epistemic and practical contributions of scientific unity while rejecting any automatic inference from those contributions to the universal necessity of an ultimate unified picture\.
