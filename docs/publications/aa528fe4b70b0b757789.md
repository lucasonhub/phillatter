# Security and Privacy Challenges in Multi \-Cloud Environments : A Comprehensive

**Autores:** Kavita Yadav; Sheshang Degadwala

**Data:** 2026-10-14 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

The rapid adoption of multi \-cloud computing in which organizations distribute workloads and data across two or more cloud service providers \(CSPs\) has introduced a distinct and rapidly evolving class of security and privacy problems that differ materially from those of single \- provider or simple hybrid \-cloud deployments\. This paper presents a review of the security and privacy challenges that arise in multi \-cloud environments synthesizing recent academic literature and industry research published primarily between 2018 and 2026\. The review is organized around six pillars: data security and confidentiality, identity and access management \(IAM\) network security and attack \-surface expansion, privacy \-preserving computation, regulatory compliance and data sovereignty and the emerging role of Zero Trust Architecture \(ZTA\) as a unifying defense paradigm\. For each pillar representative studies are summarized, compared and critically discussed, culminating in a analysis table that maps challenges to proposed solutions and their limitations\. The review concludes by identifying research gaps, including the lack of standardized cross \-CSP security interfaces the shortage of empirically validated Zero Trust deployments in production multi \-cloud settings and the unresolved tension between regulatory data \- sovereignty requirements and the operational flexibility that motivates multi \-cloud adoption in the first place\. This review is intended as a foundation for researchers and practitioners seeking to design resilient privacy \-preserving and regulation \- compliant multi \-cloud architectures\.
