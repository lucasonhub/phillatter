# Equity in the Utilization of Artificial Intelligence Facilities Among Urban and Rural Public Secondary Schools in Benue State

**Autores:** Donatus Peter Ochai; Lawrence Linus Bodams; OJonoka Victoria Attah

**Data:** 2026-10-12 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

This study investigated equity in the availability and utilization of Artificial Intelligence \(AI\) facilities among urban and rural public secondary schools in Benue State, Nigeria\. The study was guided by two research questions and two hypotheses\. A descriptive survey design was adopted\. The population comprised 3,726 teachers in 128 public secondary schools, from which 361 teachers were selected using stratified random sampling\. Data were collected using the Equitable Utilization of Artificial Intelligence Questionnaire \(EUAIQ\), validated by three experts in the Faculty of Education, Veritas University, Bwari, Abuja\. The instrument yielded a Cronbach’s alpha reliability coefficient of 0\.88 based on a trial test involving 30 teachers\. Mean and standard deviation were used to answer the research questions, with a 2\.50 decision criterion, while independent\-samples t\-test was used to test the hypotheses at the 0\.05 level of significance\. The findings revealed significant differences between the mean ratings of respondents from urban and rural public secondary schools regarding both the availability and utilization of AI facilities for teaching and learning in Benue State\. The study concludes that disparities exist in the availability and utilization of AI facilities between urban and rural public secondary schools\. It recommends equitable provision of funding, reliable internet connectivity, stable electricity and AI\-enabled digital resources by government and relevant stakeholders, alongside continuous capacity\-building programmes for teachers on the effective use of AI facilities\.
