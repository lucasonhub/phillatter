# Exploring AI Literacy Self\-Efficacy among Pre\-Service EFL Teachers: A Case Study in the Vietnamese Context

**Autores:** Thi Thai Ha Pham; Thi Xuan Lien Cao

**Data:** 2026-10-15 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

This case study aims to explore Vietnamese pre\-service EFL teachers&\#x27; awareness of AI literacy, their AI self\-efficacy, and developmental needs\. Using an explanatory mixed\-methods approach, data were collected through a questionnaire administered to 85 pre\-service EFL teachers and follow\-up interviews with 08 of them at a public university offering initial teacher education in central Vietnam\. The findings show that these pre\-service teachers were generally familiar with the concept of AI literacy, well\-aware of its role in EFL instruction, and strongly supported the inclusion of formal AI literacy training in teacher education programs\. In addition, they perceived self\-efficacy of AI literacy to be moderate, particularly in dimensions such as a human\-centered mindset and AI application\. However, these pre\-service teachers still needed more structured, practice\-oriented support to develop their AI competence, with a preferred mode of blended training\. These findings can provide insights into pre\-service teachers’ perceptions of their self\-efficacy in AI\-assisted language education and inform the design and delivery of targeted AI literacy training for teacher education programs\.
