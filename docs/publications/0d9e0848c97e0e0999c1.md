# Time\-partitioning Gaussian Process Mixture Model

**Autores:** Guilherme Piantino; Hedibert F\. Lopes

**Data:** 2026-10-14 · **Tipo:** preprint

## Resumo

We propose the Time\-partitioning Gaussian Process Mixture Model \(TPGPMM\), a Bayesian nonparametric framework for identifying persistent regimes and modelling nonstationary dependence in time series\. The model combines an adaptive Pitman\-\-Yor prior on contiguous segmentations with regime\-specific Gaussian processes, without imposing Markovian within\-regime dependence\. Learning the Pitman\-\-Yor discount parameter allows adaptation to homogeneous and heterogeneous regime lengths\. A split\-\-merge Metropolis\-within\-Gibbs sampler with exact Gibbs boundary updates jointly learns change points and model hyperparameters, quantifying uncertainty over segmentations and within\-regime dynamics\. With an RBF kernel and a fixed, conservative observation\-noise level, TPGPMM favours sparse segmentations that distinguish persistent shifts from transient shocks\. Simulations demonstrate recovery of the regime structure\. Applications to the CBOE Volatility Index \(VIX\) and the 10\-year United States Treasury yield identify regimes aligned with major market and monetary\-policy events\. Variants that estimate observation noise improve predictive accuracy relative to the main specification, but can produce less sparse or less event\-aligned segmentations\. A variant with regime\-specific means fits better than a fitted stationary Gaussian process and forecasts about as accurately\. These results highlight a trade\-off between interpretable regime identification and predictive accuracy\.
