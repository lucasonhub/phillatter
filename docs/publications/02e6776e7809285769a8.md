# Spivak, Ihor: Who Sees Through Our Eyes? A Hypothesis on the Fundamental Nature of Individual Consciousness

**Autores:** 

**Data de descoberta:** 2026-10-19 · **Tipo:** unknown

## Descrição do feed

This paper presents an independently formulated scientific and philosophical hypothesis proposing that consciousness may be a fundamental property of the Universe and that individual consciousness may exist independently of the biological brain\. The model proposes the existence of distinct, persistent structures of individual consciousness, termed clusters\. Each cluster is assumed to possess a stable individual identity while retaining the capacity to accumulate information through experience\. The brain is considered a biological interface through which a cluster may interact with the physical world\. Such interaction is proposed to depend on the compatibility of the respective characteristics of the cluster and the nervous system\. The hypothesis allows for the possible persistence of individual consciousness after biological death and its subsequent interaction with another compatible nervous system, without requiring the transfer of a previous human personality\. The paper discusses conceptual parallels with other approaches to consciousness, unresolved scientific questions and possible avenues for empirical investigation\. The proposed model has not been experimentally confirmed and is presented for critical scientific and philosophical examination\. \(direct link \)
