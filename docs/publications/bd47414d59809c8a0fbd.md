# Beyond conventional ESG: configurational synergies and path effects on the return resilience of infrastructure REITs

**Autores:** Yinglin Wang; Hong Xu; Ziying Yan; Yuxin Zhao; Shengxu Shi; Yinhong Yang

**Data:** 2026-10-09 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

As sustainable development has gained increasing prominence, environmental, social, and governance \(ESG\) issues have become an important research topic in the field of infrastructure real estate investment trusts \(Infra\-REITs\)\. However, the three conventional ESG dimensions alone are no longer sufficient to fully explain the mechanisms through which ESG affects earnings resilience\. Drawing on the operational characteristics of the Infra\-REITs market, this study develops an “ESG\+” analytical framework comprising two dimensions: underlying assets and capital markets\. Using both questionnaire evidence and empirical data, it integrates fuzzy\-set qualitative comparative analysis \(fsQCA\) and necessary\-condition analysis \(NCA\) to systematically identify the configurational paths through which multiple factors jointly shape the earnings resilience of Infra\-REITs\. To mitigate potential subjective bias, a LASSO regression model is further employed to identify key variables containing incremental predictive information and to conduct a complementary comparison with the configurational results\. The results show that: \(1\) ESG elements such as green certification, energy efficiency, and social responsibility significantly enhance return resilience under specific contextual conditions, demonstrating strong multi\-factor synergies; \(2\) project cash flow and energy efficiency emerge as core conditions across several configurations, underscoring the foundational role of operational performance in shaping Infra\-REITs’ resilience; and \(3\) under imperfect legal and fiscal systems, market liquidity, pricing mechanisms, and information disclosure interact as critical complementary mechanisms that strengthen return stability\. The findings contribute to a more comprehensive understanding of ESG–resilience linkages and provide theoretical and practical guidance for optimizing investment management and improving institutional design in the Infra\-REITs market\.
