# The digital tutorship as mechanism of development of professional competences of medical workers

**Autores:** Lilia Mindanisova Sagdeeva; E\. K\. Smirnova; Гурцкой Лев Дмитриевич; N\. Sh\. Sarkhadov

**Data:** 2026-10-09 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

The article considers digital tutorship as modern mechanism for development of professional competencies of medical professionals in the context of digital transformation of health care and transition to model of continuing medical education\. The actuality of topic is conditioned by increasing complexity of clinical practice, expansion of application of digital technologies, development of telemedicine, medical information systems, artificial intelligence and remote education formats\. The purpose of the study is to substantiate significance of digital tutorship in system of professional development of medical personnel and to identify its main functional directions\. Based on results of analysis of official documents of the WHO, normative and strategic documents of the Russian Federation, as well as data from systematic reviews, it is demonstrated that digital tutorship can be considered not only as form of professional support, but also as tool to form clinical, digital, communicative, organizational and research competencies\. The conclusion is made that it is necessary to institutionalize digital tutorship in medical organizations and to integrate it into system of continuing medical and pharmaceutical education\.
