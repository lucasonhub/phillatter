# Thirty\-Two Theses and What Would Refute Them

**Autores:** Dirk de Pol

**Data:** 2026-10-17 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Abstract This register deposits thirty\-two theses on the Omegacene, a condition without precedent in which intelligent systems, biological and artificial, are beginning to read and deliberately rewrite their own foundations: genes, neural states, and program code\. Developed as part of the Omegacene project at omegacene\.com, these theses establish a framework for examining the emerging transformation of intelligence, life, and civilization\. They concern the concepts through which this condition can be understood, the six political spaces in which its future is being decided, the technologies driving it, and the institutions and social orders that must respond\. Each thesis is explained so that it can be understood without prior knowledge and carries four test entries: what it rests on, what would support it, what would refute it, and by when an answer seems possible\. Each is also classified according to whether future developments can decide its validity\. Cases known at the date of deposit serve to formulate the hypotheses; subsequent developments provide the basis for testing them\. For the first thesis to fall due, a prediction about Germany by the end of 2028 is deposited in advance, including case selection, measures, and decision rules\. The purpose of this deposit is to establish a timestamped record of the Omegacene&\#x27;s testable claims\. Two theses fall due in 2028, three by 2030 or 2032, nine by 2035, three by 2040, two by 2045, and one by 2046\. Four are observed continuously, one of them without scoring\. Eight carry no deadline because no period is known within which they could be decided\. The reckoning will be published on the stated dates, including results that contradict the author&\#x27;s expectations\. The register is part of the broader intellectual undertaking documented at omegacene\.com\. Its purpose is not to predict a predetermined future, but to make claims about the Omegacene explicit, traceable, and open to correction as that future unfolds\.
