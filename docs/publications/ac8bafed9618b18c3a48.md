# Niu, Gangbei: A Recursive\-Ontological Critique of Chalmers&\#x27; Hard Problem, Philosophical Zombies, and Subjectivity Privilege: AI Consciousness Ethics, Qualia, and Scientific Grain Size

**Autores:** 

**Data de descoberta:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** unknown

## Descrição do feed

This paper presents a recursive\-ontological critique of David Chalmers&\#x27; hard problem of consciousness, the philosophical zombie argument, and the ontological privilege granted to subjectivity\. Centered on the state\-update equation S\_n = f\(S\_\{n\-1\}, W\_n\), it deconstructs the first\-person privilege, symmetrically reviews zombie and &quot;philosophical God&quot; arguments, and proposes seven core principles \(subjectivity premise review, concept\-existence distinction, recursive increment, stopping condition, evidence consistency, grain\-size adaptation, pragmatic limitation\)\. It further offers a four\-level framework for AI consciousness judgment and a purpose\-directed grain\-size principle for scientific analysis\. The work dissolves pseudo\-problems in consciousness philosophy while providing a self\-restraining methodological toolkit for both human and artificial consciousness studies\.
