# Newspaper Vendor Problem: Some Insights Concerning the Expected Value of Perfect Information

**Autores:** Thomas Wedel

**Data:** 2026-10-14 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

The single\-period inventory problem with uncertain demand, also known as the Newspaper Vendor Problem, is a commonly encountered occurrence in industry and significant research literature exists on the subject\. The critical ratio or fractile method provides the decision maker with a method for quickly calculating the optimal stock quantity that either maximizes expected profit or minimizes the expected cost\. The critical ratio or fractile method is based upon the concept of finding the optimal balance between the opportunity costs of having excess inventory versus having a insufficient supply on hand\. Little research publication, however, exists concerning EVPI for the Newspaper Vendor problem\. This paper addresses that gap in literature\. Market research is often available to more accurately estimate next periods demand but usually it has an attached cost\. The Expected Value of Perfect Information \(EVPI\) represents the most that the decision maker would ever pay for market research\. It provides the scale for which all sources of information about future demand can be rated\. This paper demonstrates the procedure for finding EVPI for cases where demand is estimated by either a discrete or a continuous probability distribution\. Practical examples of both profit maximization and cost minimization are provided in the paper\.
