# Assessing the Impact of Digital Business Models on the Performance of Commercial Banks

**Autores:** Jurayev Jahongir Bahodir ugli

**Data:** 2026-10-14 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

The article assesses the impact of digital business models on the performance of commercial banks\. It compares the traditional branch\-based banking model with platform, open\-banking and ecosystem\-based digital models, and identifies the advantages \(lower costs, financial inclusion, new revenue streams\) and the risks \(cyber threats, technology and talent dependence, intensified competition, the digital divide\) of the transition\. The current state, regulatory framework and potential of digital business models in Uzbekistan&\#x27;s commercial banks are analysed, and recommendations for their development are proposed\.
