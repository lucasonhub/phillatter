# Paschalidis, Aristotelis Ioannis: Authorship as the reason for a text’s existence: without AIdeation, no authorlessness

**Autores:** 

**Data de descoberta:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** unknown

## Descrição do feed

\_Jme Practical Bioethics\_ 2 \(3\)\. 2026Earp et al identify five candidate views of authorship: composition, ideation, direction, sustained engagement, and accountability and authorisation\. I argue that ideation must be singled out as sufficient for authorship and necessary for first authorship, since it supplies the primary intellectual reason an essay exists\. Composition expresses the governing idea; direction governs its realisation; sustained engagement contributes to its development; and accountability ordinarily ratifies the antecedent intellectual ownership of whoever supplied the ideation\. I therefore propose an ideation\-centred hierarchy of authorial functions, a provisional definition of authorship as the primary intellectual reason for a text’s existence, and a corresponding model of byline attribution\. I then consider AIdeation—the outsourcing of ideation itself to AI systems—as the apparent exception\. Finally, I contend that ‘authorless essay’ is a misleading label for a criteria\-relative gap\. Without full AIdeation there is no authorlessness; with it, the essay is authorless under the ideation\-first account but cannot qualify as scholarship, although an undisclosed and undetected case may be published\. \(direct link \)
