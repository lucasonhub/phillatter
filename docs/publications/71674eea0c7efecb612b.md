# AI SMART AGRO SYSTEM: A WEB\-BASED AI\-ASSISTED DECISION SUPPORT PLATFORM FOR FARMERS

**Autores:** 

**Data:** 2026-10-17 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Farmers often have to decide on disease control, crop selection, soil management and irrigation without timely expert advice, which leads to yield loss and wastage of water and fertiliser\.This paper presents AI Smart Agro System, a web\-based decision\-support platform that brings five services into one dashboard: leaf disease detection from an uploaded photograph, crop recommendation, soil health analysis, irrigation advisory and weather\-based farming advice\.The system is built with PHP, MySQL and JavaScript, while disease detection and crop recommendation are handled by an external Python Flask AI service that is called through a secured REST API\.Soil, irrigation and weather advice are generated by transparent rule\-based logic using soil pH, N\-P\-K values, soil moisture, rainfall, temperature and humidity\.Every result is stored in the user&\#x27;s history, and notifications and an administrator panel support monitoring\.The prototype is designed to return the disease name, confidence, treatment and prevention tips for an uploaded leaf image, to recommend the three most suitable crops, and to give clear advice for soil and water management\.Quantitative evaluation of the AI models is left for future work\.
