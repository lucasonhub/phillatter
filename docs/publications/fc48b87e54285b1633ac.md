# The Degree of Chemistry Teachers&\#x27; Practice of Artificial Intelligence Applications and its Relationship to their Attitudes toward Teaching

**Autores:** تهاني العبوس

**Data:** 2026-10-14 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

The study aims to investigate the degree to of chemistry teachers&\#x27; practice of artificial intelligence \(AI\) applications\. Also, it identifies whether there were statistically significant differences in the degree of chemistry teachers&\#x27; practice of AI applications and their relationship to their attitudes toward teaching\. A descriptive correlational approach was used as it aligns with the nature and objectives of the study\. Two tools were employed to answer the study questions: The first was a questionnaire of 20 items divided into three domains \(planning, implementation, and evaluation\) to determine the degree of chemistry teachers&\#x27; practice of AI applications\. The second tool was a questionnaire designed to measure chemistry teachers&\#x27; attitudes toward teaching, consisting of 16 indicators\. The study population included all chemistry teachers in public schools under the Education Directorate of Wadi Al\-Seer District, totaling 49 teachers\. The study sample consisted of 34 male and female teachers selected randomly\. The study results show that the degree of chemistry teachers&\#x27; practice of AI applications was low, with a mean score of 1\.65, and their attitudes toward teaching were moderate, with a mean score of 2\.86\. Additionally, the results reveal a statistically significant positive correlation between the degree of chemistry teachers&\#x27; practice of AI applications and their attitudes toward teaching\. In light of the study&\#x27;s results, the researcher recommends conducting specialized training courses on AI applications and their integration into chemistry teaching for chemistry teachers\.
