# AEROX: A Smart Transforming Rover\-Drone for Surveillance and Field Operations

**Autores:** Samarth Pande; Atharv Nagpurkar; Apurva Lahudkar; Pooja Shelke

**Data:** 2026-10-13 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

In this paper, we present the designing of AEROX which is the name given to a novel and advanced robot that can work as a transforming rover and drone\. The robot is capable of performing tasks related to advanced surveillance as well as field works\. It combines both roving and flying properties in a single unit so that efficient performance is ensured regardless of the environment and the type of surface on which it is to perform tasks\. It functions in rover mode and drone mode depending on the needs of the task being performed\. Sensors, cameras, global positioning satellite, and wireless communication modules are some of the key components of the robot\. It can be used for purposes including but not limited to surveillance, agriculture monitoring, disaster management, and security measures\. Artificial intelligence is utilized to increase its capabilities\.
