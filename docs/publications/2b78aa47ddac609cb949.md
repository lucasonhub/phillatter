# Diffusion\-Selective Modulation Strategy for Enhanced Propane Dehydrogenation via Mixing Inert CHA Zeolite

**Autores:** Qiancheng Zhao; Yu\-Long Zheng; Lin Chen; Cheng Shang; Sicong Ma; Zhi‐Pan Liu

**Data:** 2026-10-10 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Abstract Achieving a high propane conversion in propane dehydrogenation to propylene \(PDH\) carries tremendous scientific and economic value, particularly if achievable with established industrial units\. Here we propose a diffusion\-selective modulation strategy driven by large\-scale machine\-learning computation, where the key to success lies in finding auxiliary materials to drive the PDH reaction forward by enlarging the diffusion\-rate difference between H2 and C3 species\. This strategy is demonstrated theoretically via high\-throughput computation and then validated experimentally through the introduction of inert siliceous CHA zeolites as a diffusion\-selective modulator within the catalyst bed\. By simply physically mixing CHA with various PDH catalysts, we show that the single\-pass propane conversion can increase by 1–5% from industrial space velocities \(10 h–1\) to significantly higher throughputs \(256 h–1\), consistently improving performance across multiple reported catalysts without compromising stability\. This “plug\-and\-play” methodology can be directly implemented in existing industrial PDH units without altering either the catalyst formulation or the reactor configuration, providing a readily implementable and scalable route for upgrading current PDH infrastructure\.
