# Lahiri, Avijit: The Connected Mind: From the Neuronal to the Social Synapse

**Autores:** 

**Data de descoberta:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** unknown

## Descrição do feed

This monograph attempts at understanding how the mind emerges in a process of self\-organized complexity involving a number of interacting complex systems, among which we focus principally on the hierarchy of neuronal networks in the brain\. At the same time, it looks at the complex structural and functional organization of the mind, in which the vast social web of interacting minds plays a pivotal role\.
