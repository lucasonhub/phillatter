# Integrating hippopotamus optimization algorithm into machine learning frameworks for predicting suspended sediment concentrations

**Autores:** Mohsen Nasrabadi; Yaser Mehri; Ali Abdolrazaq Sabbar; Mohammad Javad Nahvinia; Mohammad Hosein Omid

**Data:** 2026-10-17 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Abstract The vertical distribution of sediment concentration is a fundamental component of sediment transport hydraulics, directly impacting the estimation of sediment loads in riverine and channel systems\. Therefore, accurate characterization of this distribution remains a critical challenge in hydraulic engineering\. To address this, advanced intelligent modeling techniques offer a robust pathway for reliable prediction\. This study evaluates the performance of six distinct modeling approaches—ORELM, ORELM\-HOA, GPR, GPR\-HOA, GMDH\-ANFIS, and GMDH\-ANFIS\-HOA—to predict the relative sediment concentration distribution \(C/C a ​\)\. Results indicated that the integration of meta\-heuristic optimization significantly enhances the predictive precision of the GMDH\-ANFIS, ORELM, and GPR frameworks\. Comparative analysis reveals that the GMDH\-ANFIS\-HOA model achieved the highest predictive accuracy, yielding an RMSE of 0\.0434 and R 2 of 0\.9747 during the training phase, and an RMSE of 0\.0424 and R 2 of 0\.9727 during the testing phase\. The GPR\-HOA model followed as the second\-best performer\. Furthermore, sensitivity analysis identified the relative depth \(y/D\) as the most dominant factor influencing model accuracy\. These findings demonstrate that the optimized GMDH\-ANFIS\-HOA model provides a reliable and efficient computational tool for engineers and hydrologists, facilitating improved design and management of irrigation canals, river engineering projects, and sediment\-related environmental assessments\.
