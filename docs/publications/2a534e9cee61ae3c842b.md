# THE EFFECTIVENESS OF INTERACTIVE METHODS IN TEACHING FOREIGN LANGUAGES IN THE ERA OF MODERN TECHNOLOGIES

**Autores:** Norxidirov Dilshod

**Data:** 2026-10-10 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

This article examines the use of interactive teaching methods in foreign language learning in the modern digital era\. It focuses on the role of Artificial Intelligence, Gamification, and the Flipped Classroom model in improving students&\#x27; language skills and communicative competence\. The article also highlights the benefits of digital tools in the learning process\. However, it emphasizes that teachers&\#x27; guidance, communication, and personal support remain important for successful language learning\.
