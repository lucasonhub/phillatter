# VASPO: Self\-Distillation with Privileged Value Estimation

**Autores:** Fei Wu; Zhenrong Zhang; Jianshu Zhang; Jun Du

**Data:** 2026-10-14 · **Tipo:** preprint

## Resumo

On\-policy self\-distillation provides dense supervision for reasoning models via a privileged self\-teacher\. However, this supervision reflects local token preferences rather than the expected return of an unguided student continuation\. Recent methods combine this signal with GRPO\. They use GRPO for update directions and the teacher for signal scaling\. However, we demonstrate a more effective integration within PPO\. Specifically, we identify complementary positional dynamics: privileged information enhances a critic&\#x27;s value\-prediction accuracy toward the response&\#x27;s end, whereas self\-teacher guidance is strong initially, drops mid\-generation, and rebounds finally\. These insights motivate Value\-Augmented Self\-Distillation Policy Optimization \(VASPO\), wherein a self\-teacher and critic evaluate identical privileged information at student\-generated prefixes\. The critic computes token\-level PPO advantages from predicted continuation returns, while the self\-teacher modulates selected positive advantages via bounded probability ratios\. Across six mathematical reasoning benchmarks, VASPO improves mean accuracy over PPO by 2\.59 and 1\.81 percentage points on Qwen3\-4B and OLMo3\-7B variants, respectively, and achieves the highest mean accuracy among evaluated methods on Knights and Knaves\. Our code is available at https://github\.com/cii030/VASPO\.
