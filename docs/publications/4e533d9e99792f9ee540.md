# Maseda, Kini: Facing the problem of AI and human extinction\. Toward a Symbiotic Model of Future Intelligence

**Autores:** 

**Data de descoberta:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** unknown

## Descrição do feed

Recent discussions concerning advanced artificial intelligence frequently assume an eventual competition between biological and artificial agents for resources, autonomy, and influence\. This article explores an alternative possibility based on a structural theory of consciousness in which conscious systems are characterized not only by integration but also by structural richness\. I argue that if phenomenological richness arises from differentiated yet coherent relational structures, then biological and artificial intelligences may be understood as complementary rather than competing forms of cognitive organization\. Human beings contribute embodied, situated, and historically grounded perspectives, whereas artificial systems contribute computational, analytic, and integrative capabilities\. Under this framework, future human\-AI relations need not converge toward replacement or domination\. Instead, the coexistence of distinct forms of intelligence may increase the structural richness of the broader cognitive ecosystem, producing mutual benefits for both biological and artificial agents\.
