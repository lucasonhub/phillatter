# Simuladores interactivos y resolución de problemas: Mediaciones pedagógicas para el aprendizaje de la física

**Autores:** Victor Daniel Trillos Miranda

**Data:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Problem\-solving is a core competency in the education of students and individuals; at the same time, it is one of the challenges encountered when teaching physics\. This article reflects on the role of interactive simulators as pedagogical tools to strengthen this competency, integrating recent evidence on PhET, guided inquiry, problem\-based learning, digital literacy, artificial intelligence, and virtual laboratories\. A literature review of recent research and academic studies was conducted\. The findings indicate that simulations facilitate the visualization of abstract concepts, the manipulation of variables, and immediate feedback; however, their effectiveness does not depend solely on the technology itself\. Improvement occurs when the use of the simulator, in conjunction with activities proposed by the teacher, guides students to test their own hypotheses, rather than merely observing a result\. Limitations related to teacher training, infrastructure, cognitive load, and the risk of purely demonstrative uses were also identified\. We conclude that the value of simulators lies in their ability to transform problem\-solving into an activity involving modeling, inquiry, and argumentation, provided they are integrated through a deliberate and assessable instructional sequence\.
