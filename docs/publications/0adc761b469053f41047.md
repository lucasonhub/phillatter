# IMPACT OF ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE AND THE HUMAN ROLE IN THE FUTURE STOCK MARKET THEORETICAL AND IDEOLOGICAL EXPECTATIONS FOR UZBEKISTAN

**Autores:** Alisher Shavkidinov

**Data:** 2026-10-18 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Artificial intelligence can reduce the cost of processing financial information, yet its contribution to stock market development depends on the institutions that govern data, decisions and accountability\. This article examines that relationship for Uzbekistan through a critical synthesis of financial economics, research on human reliance on algorithms, international governance frameworks and national policy documents\. It distinguishes established findings from conditional expectations and develops a framework in which automated analysis remains subject to accountable human authority\. The central argument is that market quality depends on complementary investment in disclosure, professional competence and supervisory capacity\. An ideological comparison identifies tensions between technological efficiency, developmental objectives and investor autonomy\. Two analytical tables specify responsibilities and measurable criteria for future evaluation\. The proposed framework yields testable propositions concerning disclosure quality, meaningful review and common dependence on model providers\. For Uzbekistan, the defensible sequence begins with verified information and supervised analytical applications, followed by limited deployment only after independent validation\. These conclusions are theoretical; the article reports no original trading experiment or estimated investment returns\.
