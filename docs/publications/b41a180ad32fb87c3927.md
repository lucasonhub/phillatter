# Multi\-direction shift RWKV and frequency\-aware semantic network with for Ultrasound Image Segmentation

**Autores:** Jiangxiong Fang; Yiming Ge; Danjiang Huang; Wenbin Ji; Youyao Fu; Huaxiang Liu; Jie Jin

**Data:** 2026-10-14 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Abstract Ultrasound image segmentation plays an important role in computer\-aided diagnosis and clinical decision support\. Accurate delineation remains difficult because ultrasound images commonly exhibit speckle noise, low contrast, blurred boundaries, and substantial variation in lesion morphology\. Convolutional neural networks are effective at extracting local patterns but have limited capacity for modeling long\-range interactions, whereas Transformer\-based methods may incur high computational cost and lose fine local details\. To address these challenges, we propose a Multi\-Direction Shift RWKV and Frequency\-Aware Semantic Network \(MDS\-RWKV\) for ultrasound image segmentation\. First, a Multi\-Direction Shift RWKV \(MS\-RWKV\) module combines linear\-complexity WKV recurrence with multi\-direction spatial shifting, enabling each token to incorporate two\-dimensional neighborhood information before recurrent modeling\. Second, a Spatial and Spectral Cross Aggregation \(SSCA\) module integrates multi\-scale spatial features with Fourier\-domain representations to enhance lesion\-boundary and texture discrimination under speckle interference and weak contrast\. Third, a Global and Local Feature Decoder \(GLFD\) progressively fuses high\-level semantic context with fine\-grained spatial details for accurate mask reconstruction\. Experiments on three public ultrasound datasets, BUSI, BUS\-BRA, and TN\-SCUI, produced mIoU values of 71\.27%, 84\.18%, and 79\.87%, respectively\. The aggregate results show improved region overlap and boundary accuracy relative to the evaluated baselines, while the complete model should be regarded as a moderate\-size network rather than a lightweight architecture\.
