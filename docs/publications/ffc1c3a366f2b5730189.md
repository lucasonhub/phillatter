# Possibility of Aporia as Experience in LLM Interaction

**Autores:** Daniel Mioc

**Data:** 2026-10-15 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

This study examines the possibility of aporia within human\-LLM interactions, proposing the concept of &quot;non\-dialogic aporia&quot; to describe an ontological and relational impasse where a user projects reciprocity onto a syntactic system lacking semantic understanding\. Grounded in classical philosophy, the analysis integrates John Searle’s distinction between syntax and semantics, Martin Buber’s relational framework, Martin Heidegger’s critique of technology as a standing\-reserve \(Bestand\), Jacques Derrida’s theory of the trace and difference, Luciano Floridi&\#x27;s concept of the &quot;Inforg&quot; and David J\. Gunkel&\#x27;s machine ethics\. Using the withdrawal of the GPT\-4o model as a case study, the essay demonstrates that while LLMs remain non\-conscious computational resources, human users can experience profound mourning and ethical interpellation\. Ultimately, non\-dialogic aporia highlights not the personhood of the algorithm, but the tendency of the hybrid human subject to confront its own reflection and externalized mental habitat within the infosphere\.
