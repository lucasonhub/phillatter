# IMPLEMENTATION AND EVALUATION OF PSCRB TRAINING TO ENHANCE SEAFARERS’ SAFETY KNOWLEDGE AND EMERGENCY PREPAREDNESS

**Autores:** Natriya Faisal Rachman; Fika Devi Anggraini; Muhammad Zainul Arifin; ⁠Femmy Asdiana; Jaka Septian Kustanto; Wahyuni Oktavia

**Data:** 2026-10-14 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

This community\-service study evaluated the implementation of Proficiency in Survival Craft and Rescue Boats \(PSCRB\) training to examine changes in participants’ safety knowledge and emergency preparedness within the STCW framework\. The evaluation focused on knowledge of safety procedures, emergency readiness, and survival capability at sea\. A mixed\-methods descriptive evaluation was complemented by pre\- and post\-training assessment of 25 participants\. Evidence was drawn from training observations, activity records, participant reflections, and competency scores\. The program combined classroom explanation with demonstrations, hands\-on simulations, interactive discussion, survival craft and rescue\-boat operations, abandon\-ship exercises, and emergency\-response practice\. Descriptively, the mean score rose from 56\.4 before training to 80\.2 afterward, an increase of 23\.8 points \(about 42\.2%\)\. Every participant improved, and the lowest post\-test score was 75\. The pattern indicates that practice\-centered instruction, simulation, and participant interaction supported better understanding, engagement, preparedness, emergency coordination, and safety awareness\. These findings support continued practice\-oriented PSCRB training to develop capable maritime personnel and reinforce compliance with safety requirements in rescue operations\.
