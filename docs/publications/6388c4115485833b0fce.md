# Zhao, Xiangbin: The Law Group\-Form: The Group Map

**Autores:** 

**Data de descoberta:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** unknown

## Descrição do feed

Laws of nature, cognitive rules, and stable relational structures formed in learning systems are usually studied within different theoretical traditions\. The Law Group\-Form asks a common generative question: how can historical interaction form a law capable of organizing what happens next? This Group Map integrates eight independently completed form\-texts addressing the necessary structure of law, formal representation and solving, stability and substitution, construction of a minimal sedimentation field, construction of a law operating substrate, executable adjudication, the sufficient direction, and boundaries of lawhood\. At group level, under the explicitly stated conditions including C0, C2, and H1–H3, Law receives a bidirectional structural characterization in terms of the existence of a formed bearing organization that genuinely participates in subsequent generation and is supported for stable recurrence\. The sufficient direction is independently established, while the necessary direction retains its Conditional antecedents\. The group also distinguishes joint generation, generative precedence, field\-level office, and the running relation in which Principle may drive an already formed law\.
