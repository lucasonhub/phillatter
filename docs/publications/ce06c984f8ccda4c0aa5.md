# Development of EDP\.AI: An AI\-integrated engineering design module to enhance student engagement in STEM learning

**Autores:** Mellisa Anthony; Fariedah Lal Chan \-

**Data:** 2026-10-13 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

STEM education is a national priority in Malaysia; however, clear gaps in learning outcomes and student participation remain, particularly in rural areas\. In Sabah, very low Indeks STEM Murid \(ISM\) scores, coupled with declining enrolment in the STEM Package A stream, indicate persistent challenges in the quality and continuity of STEM learning at the lower secondary level\. These trends suggest that many students do not experience STEM learning as sufficiently meaningful or structured to build confidence and sustain their interest in STEM pathways\. This paper reports Phase 1A of a Design and Development Research \(DDR\) study, focusing on the needs analysis and conceptualisation of EDP\.AI, an AI\-assisted module designed to scaffold the Engineering Design Process \(EDP\) for lower secondary students in rural Sabah\. Document analysis was conducted to examine challenges related to STEM learning, EDP implementation, artificial intelligence in education, and Cognitive Load Theory \(CLT\) in low\-resource contexts\. The evidence was synthesised using design rationale mapping to translate theoretical and contextual insights into actionable design decisions\. The outcomes of Phase 1A include the identification of key needs and challenges, the formulation of preliminary design requirements and design principles, and the development of a conceptual model to guide the design of EDP\.AI\. The proposed module emphasises step\-by\-step scaffolding aligned with EDP stages, reduced cognitive load, low\-bandwidth accessibility, and support for both student reasoning and teacher workload\. Overall, this study provides a theoretical and contextual foundation for the subsequent development and expert validation of EDP\.AI to support more meaningful and sustainable STEM learning experiences in rural schools\.
