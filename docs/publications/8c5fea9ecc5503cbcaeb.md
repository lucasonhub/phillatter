# Determinants for Financial Literacy and Financial Well\-being of Social Work Students in Türkiye

**Autores:** Murat DİNÇER; Aslıhan Aykara

**Data:** 2026-10-13 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Objective: This study focused on investigating the financial literacy \(FL\) and financial well\-being \(FWB\) of Bachelor of Social Work \(BSW\) students in Türkiye, as well as the sociodemographic determinants of FL and FWB\. Methods: This cross\-sectional descriptive study was conducted with 392 BSW students from 6 different provinces in Türkiye\. Post\-stratification weighting was applied based on university type, gender, and province\. The survey released by the Organization for Economic Cooperation and Development in 2022 was used to assess the FL and FWB levels of the participants\. Data were analyzed using weighted hypothesis tests \(Wilcoxon rank\-sum and Kruskal–Wallis\) and weighted partial mediation models to investigate the relationship between each sociodemographic factor and FL and FWB\. Results: FL and FWB were found to be moderate determinants of each other\. The primary sociodemographic determinants of FL and FWB were the students&\#x27; year of study, maternal education, and maternal employment status\. As the year of study increased, FL levels rose; however, FWB generally tended to decrease\. Additionally, higher maternal education was indirectly related to FL and FWB as a supportive factor\. However, maternal labor force participation only increased FWB indirectly\. Conclusion: These findings suggest that FL and FWB content in social work education should be addressed not only as theoretical knowledge but also in conjunction with self\-efficacy and coping strategies\.
