# Detection of Aethina tumida \(Murray, 1867\) \(Coleoptera, Nitidulidae\) on non\-beekeeping substrate: cattle feed sacks

**Autores:** Erika Gleice Menezes do Nascimento; Victor C\. R\. Amorim; Edevaldo R\. da Silva; Viviane M\. L\. de Siqueira; Luiz G\. de Moraes; Marliton Rocha Barreto

**Data:** 2026-10-14 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

This study documents Aethina tumida \(Murray, 1867\) \(Coleoptera, Nitidulidae\) in silage bags and cattle feed\. The specimens were collected in the municipality of Rondonópolis, Mato Grosso state, during June 2025\. The identification was performed using qPCR conducted at LFDA\-GO\. This record contributes to the understanding of the species adaptability to different substrates\. Furthermore, this article records the first occurrence of A\. tumida in Alto Araguaia\.
