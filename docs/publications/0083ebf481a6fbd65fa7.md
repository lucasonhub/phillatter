# Public Sector Reforms and Sustainable Enterprise Development: Nigeria’s Quest for Sustainable Economic Development

**Autores:** Chibuzo Charles Nwosu; Dike C\. Obalum; Mathias Ozoemena Ananti

**Data:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Public sector reform is essential for fostering sustainable enterprise development and inclusive economic growth in developing economies, particularly in Nigeria\. Various reform initiatives have aimed to enhance institutional effectiveness, regulatory efficiency, public service delivery, and create a better environment for business and investment\. However, persistent challenges such as policy inconsistency, weak institutional capacity, corruption, and inadequate funding hinder successful reform implementation and enterprise sustainability\. This study explores the relationship between public sector reform and sustainable enterprise development in Nigeria by examining historical reform efforts, institutional changes, and contemporary governance initiatives\. Guided by Institutional Theory, it posits that strong, transparent, and accountable public institutions are crucial for nurturing sustainable business ecosystems and economic resilience\. The study highlights the impact of public sector reforms on enterprise sustainability and development\. In conclusion, it emphasizes that effective public sector institutions and governance reforms are vital for sustainable development and competitiveness, recommending policy continuity, capacity strengthening, enhanced transparency, institutional autonomy, and sustainable financing mechanisms\.
