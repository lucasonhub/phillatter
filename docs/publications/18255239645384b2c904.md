# Time Pressure and Limited Attention in Chess

**Autores:** Samuel Cheun

**Data:** 2026-10-15 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

This paper examines how individuals make decisions under cognitive constraints\. Using thousands of chess moves evaluated against computer\-generated benchmarks, Samuel Cheung provides evidence that time pressure significantly increases the likelihood of errors, reinforcing theories of bounded rationality and limited attention\. By combining economics with computational analysis, the paper demonstrates how strategic games can serve as powerful laboratories for understanding human decision\-making\.
