# Anthropogenic sinkholes in the urban fabric of Messina \(Sicily, Italy\)

**Autores:** Mauro Bonasera; Stefania Nisio; Luigi Guerriero

**Data:** 2026-10-15 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Urban anthropogenic sinkholes are increasingly reported in Messina \(Sicily, Italy\), where culverted torrents, legacy underground works, and dense utilities intersect a Holocene coastal/alluvial plain\. We present an updated, georeferenced inventory of sinkholes within the municipal urban area, built from local press/ web sources and field cross\-checks, and we assess spatial relationships with culverted drainage and known urban cavity entrances\. To frame site\-effects in a seismic setting, we consult the Italian catalogue of earthquake\-induced ground failures \(CEDIT\) and the Italian catalogue of earthquake environmental effects \(EEE\), which document widespread ground cracking, settlement and liquefaction during major historical earthquakes, notably the 1908 Messina event\. Our results highlight clusters along culverted stream corridors and in zones influenced by underground legacy, supporting future scenario\-based prioritization of inspections and post\-event checks\.
