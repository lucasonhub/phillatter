# An interpretable deep reinforcement learning framework for dynamic wind farm maintenance scheduling with enhanced power generation and stability

**Autores:** Feiyun Cong; Yulu Fan; Rong Wu; Xiaojie Lin; Wei Zhong

**Data:** 2026-10-10 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Wind farm maintenance scheduling is a challenging sequential decision\-making problem due to the dynamic operational environment, multi\-objective optimization requirements, and complex dependencies among turbine states, maintenance resources, and future power generation dynamics\. Existing optimization and deep reinforcement learning methods often suffer from limited interpretability and insufficient capability in proactively balancing energy production efficiency and output stability\. To address these challenges, this study proposes an interpretable deep reinforcement learning framework for dynamic wind farm maintenance scheduling based on temporal interaction modeling\. The proposed framework integrates a multi\-head self\-attention mechanism into a soft actor\-critic architecture to adaptively capture critical dependencies among historical SCADA observations, turbine operational conditions, and future power generation trajectories\. By dynamically allocating attention to maintenance\-sensitive turbines and fluctuation\-related temporal patterns, the framework achieves proactive maintenance resource scheduling under practical operational constraints\. In a SCADA\-driven simulation environment constructed from Penmanshiel wind farm data, the evaluated MEX\-DRL configuration generates 79,470 MWh of energy and achieves an aggregate output fluctuation of 1,973 kW\. Compared with the tested rule\-based and optimization\-based configurations, it increases energy generation by 280–770 MWh and reduces output fluctuation by 13\.3%–22\.5%\. Its shutdown\-event and abnormal\-event allocation rates are 93\.3% and 84\.9%, respectively\. The attention visualizations provide a descriptive view of the model’s attention patterns but do not, by themselves, establish decision faithfulness\. Overall, these results indicate that the proposed framework can support wind farm operation and maintenance scheduling while providing attention\-based transparency\.
