# Machine Learning\-Based Energy Demand Forecasting and Optimization of Renewable Energy Distribution in Smart Grids

**Autores:** QASEM SAMI YASER ALI OTHMAN

**Data:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Research Proposal Overview:This independent research proposal, prepared by Sami Yaser Ali Othman Qasem, presents a quantitative, simulation\-based study utilizing publicly available energy datasets\. The research focuses on integrating advanced machine learning techniques for accurate energy demand forecasting within smart grids, alongside developing an optimized framework for efficient and sustainable renewable energy distribution, management, and battery storage utilization\.
