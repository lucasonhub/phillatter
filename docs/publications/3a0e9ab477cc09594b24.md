# Inteligencia artificial en la administración de justicia: retos para el derecho ecuatoriano

**Autores:** Martha Cecilia Rosero\-Puetate; Jhon Sebastián Fuertes\-Realpe; Hamilton Fernando Lucero\-Arellano; Angie Michelle Velasco\-Campaña

**Data:** 2026-10-15 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Artificial intelligence is being incorporated into judicial systems to support management, case\-law search and outcome prediction, and it poses legal and ethical challenges\. The study analysed the challenges facing Ecuadorian law regarding its use in the administration of justice, with attention to risks for due process and regulatory gaps\. The research was qualitative and documentary, with an interpretive review of scholarship, domestic norms, international instruments and comparative studies\. The results indicate that the technology can ease the procedural burden, but it brings algorithmic opacity, bias and risks for reasoning, equality and data protection, and that no specific rule exists for the judicial sector\. It is concluded that regulation based on transparency, meaningful human oversight and training is required\.
