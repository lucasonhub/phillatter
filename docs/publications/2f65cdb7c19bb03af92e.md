# INNOVATIVE LENDING AS A TOOL FOR THE POST\-WAR MODERNIZATION OF UKRAINE&\#x27;S ECONOMY

**Autores:** Віолетта Харабара

**Data:** 2026-10-15 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

The prospects for the development of innovative lending in Ukraine depend to a large extent on the state’s ability to establish effective mechanisms for providing financial support to businesses that implement technological innovations\. Among such mechanisms, the ‘Affordable Loans 5\-7\-9%’ programme holds a special place, having proven its effectiveness in the challenging macroeconomic conditions of martial law\. Expanding this programme involves not only increasing the volume of budgetary funding for interest rate compensation, but also differentiating lending conditions depending on the nature of the innovation project, its degree of technological complexity, and its potential impact on the competitiveness of the national economy\.
