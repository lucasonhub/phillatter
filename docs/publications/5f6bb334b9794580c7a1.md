# GEYMIFIKATSIYA FORMATIV BAHOLASH VOSITASI SIFATIDA: XORIJIY TAJRIBA VA BOSHLANG‘ICH TA’LIM METODIKASI FANLARIDA QO‘LLASH ISTIQBOLLARI

**Autores:** ZUBAYDULLOYEVA MADINABONU KAMOL QIZI

**Data:** 2026-10-12 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

The article analyses the theoretical foundations and international experience of using gamification as a tool for formative assessment\. The functional correspondence between the key strategies of formative assessment and game mechanics is revealed, and the findings of international studies on gamified quiz platforms, including negative findings, are summarised\. An author’s definition of «gamified formative assessment» is given; a five\-stage assessment cycle and directions for its application in primary education methodology courses are proposed\.
