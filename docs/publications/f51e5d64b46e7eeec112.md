# Fauché, Nicolas: Bohm’s Implicate Order and the Persistence of Consciousness: Toward a Cosmopsychist Framework

**Autores:** 

**Data de descoberta:** 2026-10-19 · **Tipo:** unknown

## Descrição do feed

The nature of consciousness remains one of the major unresolved problems in the philosophy of mind\. This article explores the hypothesis that consciousness may not be reducible to brain activity, but could instead constitute a manifestation of a more fundamental level of reality\. Drawing on the double\-aspect monism and David Bohm’s theory of the implicate order, we examine a conception of reality in which observable phenomena emerge from a deeper, relational and dynamic structure\. The process algebra developed by Bohm and Hiley further suggests that physical entities can be understood as relatively stable structures emerging from an underlying process rather than as fundamentally independent objects\. This framework is then applied to consciousness through a dialogue with Integrated Information Theory and the physics of emergent phenomena\. We propose the concept of a “consciousness particle”, understood not as a fundamental particle in the standard physical sense, but as a stable dynamical organization whose identity depends on its underlying structure rather than on the persistence of its material constituents\. Within the implicate\-order framework, the disappearance of such a structure from the explicate order could potentially be interpreted as a transition into the implicate order rather than as absolute annihilation, thereby providing a conceptual basis for considering the possible persistence of consciousness beyond the brain\. Finally, we examine the implications of this hypothesis for panpsychism and cosmopsychism\. By analogy with quantum field theory, two possibilities are considered: consciousness particles could acquire experiential properties through interaction with a fundamental field of consciousness, or they could constitute the fundamental quanta of such a field\. Although highly speculative and not established as a physical theory, this framework offers a conceptual synthesis linking consciousness, emergence, the implicate order, and cosmopsychism, and provides a basis for investigating consciousness as a potentially fundamental feature of reality\.
