# Trepp, Tenzin C\.: Horizon of Construction, Latent Neuronal Potentials and the Emergence of Expression

**Autores:** 

**Data de descoberta:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** unknown

## Descrição do feed

The Horizon of Construction is used here to designate the distributed set of latent neural dispositions \- subthreshold activity, synaptic efficacy, ongoing network states, and plastic circuit configurations \- from which explicit perceptual, cognitive, and action\-related content can emerge\. The paper asks how such latent states become cognitively available without treating latent neural structure as already meaningful or conscious\. It develops a three\-part heuristic: activation selects and amplifies candidate patterns; binding coordinates distributed features into coherent assemblies; and integration embeds those assemblies within a wider cognitive context\. These operations are dynamically coupled rather than strictly serial\. Neural constructivism and work on oscillatory coordination, global neuronal workspace dynamics, and spontaneous brain activity provide the empirical background for the proposal\. Philosophically, the model supports a realistic form of cognitive constructivism: experience is actively constructed from neural dispositions and prior structure, but the resulting world\-model is constrained and corrected by mind\-independent causal input\. Neural latency therefore explains both the selectivity of conscious experience and its dependence on developmental history without implying that cognition constructs external reality itself\. The Horizon of Construction is best understood as a dispositional space of possible cognitive organization whose actualization is shaped jointly by internal dynamics, current context, and environmental constraint\. \(direct link \)
