# Spatiotemporal patterns and driving mechanisms of cultivated land multifunctionality evolution: evidence from county\-level data in Sichuan Province, China

**Autores:** Guangshun Zhang; Jing Zhang; Yi Su; Lin Qu; Kejun Zhang

**Data:** 2026-10-09 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

The pursuit of socioeconomic development, food security, and ecological sustainability has driven the multifunctional evolution of cultivated land\. Under the constraint of limited cultivated land resources, achieving synergy among the economic, food security, and ecological functions of cultivated land has become a major challenge for agricultural modernization in Sichuan Province\. This study evaluates the spatiotemporal evolution of cultivated land multifunctionality \(CLM\) and investigates the driving mechanisms underlying the CLM synergy level\. The results show that: \(1\) The overall CLM development level in Sichuan Province increased during the study period, with the economic contribution and food security functions serving as the primary drivers\. Nevertheless, the overall quality of CLM development remained relatively low, accompanied by substantial regional disparities, development imbalance, and functional disharmony\. \(2\) The CLM synergy level exhibited pronounced polarization and regional imbalance, together with significant spatial agglomeration\. \(3\) The explanatory power of regional background condition factors for the CLM synergy level gradually weakened, whereas that of economic development condition factors increased\. Six factors, regional GDP, rural per capita net income, rural employment population, construction intensity, urbanization rate, and cultivated land aggregation degree, showed significant explanatory power for the CLM synergy level\. To promote the synergistic development of CLM in Sichuan Province, policy efforts should focus on ensuring food production and supply, promoting high\-quality agricultural development, facilitating the value realization of landscape ecological functions, and implementing differentiated regional strategies\. This study provides scientific evidence and practical insights for improving CLM development in China and other regions facing similar cultivated land constraints\.
