# Easton, Hamilton: Panpsychism: The Raven Paradox of Consciousness

**Autores:** 

**Data de descoberta:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** unknown

## Descrição do feed

This paper argues that panpsychism does not answer the Hard Problem of consciousness, but relocates it\. Panpsychism begins from the real datum that consciousness exists, then spreads mentality, proto\-mentality, or consciousness\-readiness downward into matter as such\. This repeats a Raven\-style evidential error: a formal connection is allowed to outrun evidential landing\. Conscious beings are made of matter, but it does not follow that matter is conscious, proto\-conscious, or mind\-like in itself\. The evidence for consciousness lands where consciousness is actually known: in organised, persisting centres where memory, owned stakes, evaluation, resolution, and exposure close into a threshold structure\. Resolution Theory therefore offers a more disciplined answer: consciousness is not universal, but achieved\.
