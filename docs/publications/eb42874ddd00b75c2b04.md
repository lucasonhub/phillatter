# MaLiPre \- an explorative prototype evaluation of machine\-learning based lymphocyte infiltration prediction

**Autores:** Dennis Witt

**Data:** 2026-10-14 · **Tipo:** dissertation

## Resumo

This master&\#x27;s thesis, completed in 2023 as part of the M\.Sc\. Business Informatics programme at IU Internationale Hochschule \(Germany\), investigates the feasibility of predicting lymphocyte infiltration in tumour tissues from RNA expression data using machine\-learning methods\. The study follows an exploratory prototyping approach and evaluates several computational strategies, including models trained on real\-world tumour expression data and models trained on synthetic tissue mixtures generated from single\-cell RNA\-sequencing references using SimBu\. Classification and regression approaches are examined, and the resulting predictions are compared with histological lymphocyte assessments and the established quanTIseq deconvolution method\. The thesis documents the development and evaluation of the original MaLiPre prototypes, including preprocessing procedures, model configurations, quantitative evaluations, gene selection and supplementary methodological material\. It provides historical methodological background and feature\-selection provenance for subsequent research involving synthetic bulk RNA\-seq training data\.
