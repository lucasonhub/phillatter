# The effect of goal\-directed fluid therapy on endothelial glycocalyx and short\-term postoperative recovery quality in older patients undergoing laparoscopic hernia or gallbladder surgery: a randomized controlled double\-blind clinical study

**Autores:** Qinxian Wei; Man Zhou; Wenji Xu; Jiaxin Wan; Wuchang Fu; Fangjun Wang

**Data:** 2026-10-14 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Background This study aimed to observe the effects of intraoperative goal\-directed fluid therapy on postoperative recovery in older patients undergoing laparoscopic hernia or gallbladder surgery\. Methods A total of 60 older patients were randomly assigned to the control group \(C group\) and the goal\- directed fluid therapy group \(G group\)\. The primary outcome was the plasma concentration of syndecan\-1\. The secondary outcomes were the plasma concentrations of tumor necrosis factor alpha \(TNF\-α\), interleukin\-1 \(IL\-1\), intercellular adhesion molecule\-1 \(ICAM\-1\), and vascular cell adhesion molecule\-1 \(VCAM\-1\); mean arterial pressure; heart rate; regional cerebral oxygen saturation; intraoperative dopamine dosage; QoR\-15 scale score; hematocrit; and postoperative adverse events\. Results Compared with the C group, the G group showed decreased plasma concentrations of ICAM\-1 at the end of surgery and 24 h after surgery \( P = 0\.010 and 0\.005, respectively\), decreased plasma concentrations of syndecan\-1 at the end of surgery \( P = 0\.049\), more significantly decreased plasma concentrations of VCAM\-1 at the end of surgery and 24 h after surgery \( P &lt; 0\.001 and P = 0\.017, respectively\), and decreased plasma concentrations of IL\-1β at the end of surgery and 24 h after surgery \( P = 0\.009 and 0\.008, respectively\)\. The C group showed increased plasma concentration of TNF\-α at the end of surgery and 24 h after surgery compared with those before surgery \( P = 0\.003 and 0\.023, respectively\)\. Compared with the C group, the QoR\-15 scores were higher at 24, 48, and 72 h after surgery \( P = 0\.001, 0\.001, and 0\.005, respectively\) in the treatment group\. Conclusion Intraoperative goal\-directed fluid therapy in non\-critically ill older patients may alleviate endothelial glycocalyx degradation with a lower incidence of adverse events\. Clinical Trial Registration http://www\.chictr\.org\.cn/ , Chinese Clinical Trial Registry ChiCTR2500097999\.
