# Tail\-aware spatiotemporal diffusion for probabilistic forecasting of extreme traffic congestion

**Autores:** Lei Zhang; Xiaoqi Duan; Chuan Lin; Jianlong Wang

**Data:** 2026-10-13 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Extreme congestion occupies only a small portion of traffic records but carries disproportionate operational consequences for traffic\-management systems\. Conventional point and probabilistic forecasters are optimised primarily for average accuracy and therefore often underfit rare, severe speed reductions\. We present Tail\-Aware Spatiotemporal Diffusion \(TAD\-ST\), a probabilistic forecasting framework that couples a temporal convolutional and graph convolutional encoder with a conditional diffusion decoder\. A congestion\-aware denoising objective assigns greater training influence to future windows containing severe congestion without resampling the training data\. The framework forecasts the probability and evolution of speed\-derived extreme congestion from historical traffic states and network structure\. Evaluation on METR\-LA and PEMS\-BAY uses training\-only congestion thresholds, overall and event\-conditional probabilistic metrics, multiple random seeds, component ablations, calibration, transferability, and inference\-efficiency analysis\. In the reported comparisons, the largest separation from the architecture\-matched uniform\-loss baseline occurs on event\-conditional CRPS, whereas average point accuracy remains competitive\. The evaluation therefore emphasises calibrated tail risk rather than uniform dominance on every metric\.
