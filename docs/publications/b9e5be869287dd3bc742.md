# Enriched\-nonexpansive Plug\-and\-Play image restoration

**Autores:** Mohammad Akram; Umar Ishtiaq; Khaleel Ahmad; Ioan\-Lucian Popa

**Data:** 2026-10-09 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Plug\-and\-Play methods solve imaging inverse problems by inserting a learned denoiser into a proximal splitting algorithm\. Their convergence theory presently requires the denoiser, or its residual, to be nonexpansive or firmly nonexpansive, a constraint that curbs the network and, in turn, the reconstruction quality\. We prove that this requirement can be weakened to enriched nonexpansivity, a strictly larger operator class in which the map is nonexpansive only after averaging\. Using the displacement and transfer–return calculus for nonexpansive\-type operators, we show that when the composite Plug\-and\-Play operator is enriched nonexpansive an inertial Mann or Ishikawa iteration converges to a fixed point of the operator, that is, to a Plug\-and\-Play solution, and we identify the inertial correction as the exact quantity that reduces the enriched iteration to a classical iteration of the nonexpansive generator\. Around this reduction we build a certification theory\. The operator is b \-enriched exactly when the displacement I− T is infinitesimally cocoercive with modulus 1/\(2\( b \+1\)\), so that the smallest admissible constant is one less than the supremum of a Rayleigh\-type quotient of that displacement\. The characterization is attained on non\-scalar operators, is computable by automatic differentiation, and remains bounded in the near\-identity regime where the field\-of\-values bound degenerates\. A perturbation estimate converts spectral measurements on a finite sample into a bound over a segment or a covered set, which is what the Fejér argument needs and what a program can carry out\. We add a training objective that promotes the certified inequality, a safeguarded step\-size controller with a closed\-form greedy rule and an amortized policy trained by unrolled differentiation, sublinear and linear rates, stability under inexact denoiser evaluation, and a splitting\-agnostic extension covering Plug\-and\-Play ADMM\. Two experiments support the theory\. On a linear surrogate, for which every constant is available in closed form, the certificates are confirmed and the exact characterization widens the admissible step\-size window by about 31% over the field\-of\-values bound\. On Gaussian deblurring, motion deblurring and single\-image super\-resolution with trained denoisers, the certified constant is measured for each composite operator\. On the deblurring task a trained composite with Lipschitz constant 2\.08, admitted by no nonexpansive condition, is certified with constant 0\.60, where the characterization is more than fifteen times tighter than the field\-of\-values bound; it restores within four hundredths of a decibel of a nonexpansive composite of identical architecture, so the larger class is reached at no measured cost in quality\. The greedy relaxation halves the iteration count of the constant\-step scheme\. The acceleration is measured among safeguarded schemes; unrelaxed Picard, which carries no guarantee here, remains faster on the linear instance\.
