# Comparative transcriptomic analysis reveals the protective mechanism of ferulic acid against lipopolysaccharide\-induced ferroptosis in IPEC\-J2 cells

**Autores:** Jiayu Chen; Hongchao Li; Yaxuan Niu; Tao Guo; Xiangyu Wang; Yue Zhang; Wenwen Wang; Yuanqing Xu; Yuan Wang; Mingxing Chu

**Data:** 2026-10-12 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Lipopolysaccharide \(LPS\) is a key virulence factor that induces intestinal oxidative damage in livestock and poultry, and cellular ferroptosis is one of the triggers of oxidative stress\. Ferulic acid \(FA\) is a natural phenolic acid with antioxidant, anti\-inflammatory and anti\-ferroptosis properties\. This study aimed to investigate the protective effects of FA against LPS\-induced ferroptosis in the porcine small intestinal epithelial cell line \(IPEC\-J2\) and to elucidate the underlying mechanisms\. The experiment included four treatment groups: control group \(CON\), 50 μg/mL FA group \(FA\), 10 μg/mL LPS group \(LPS\), and 50 μg/mL FA combined with 10 μg/mL LPS group \(FA and LPS\), with all treatments lasting 24 h\. The results showed that FA inhibited ferroptosis in IPEC\-J2 cells\. LPS successfully induced ferroptosis, as evidenced by decreased cell viability, increased levels of ROS, MDA, and Fe 2\+ , downregulation of GPX4 and SLC7A11 , and upregulation of TFRC and ACSL4 \. FA effectively alleviated LPS\-induced ferroptosis in IPEC\-J2 cells, with an efficacy comparable to that of the ferroptosis inhibitor Fer\-1\. Transcriptomic analysis revealed that FA significantly reversed the gene expression changes induced by LPS, with differentially expressed genes enriched in pathways related to ferroptosis, TNF signaling, NF\-κB signaling, arachidonic acid metabolism, and PI3K\-Akt signaling\. Protein–protein interaction \(PPI\) network analysis indicated that genes such as HMGCS1 , CXCL8 , and IL\-18 may serve as key nodes mediating the protective effects of FA\. In conclusion, FA alleviates LPS\-induced ferroptosis in IPEC\-J2 cells by regulating glutathione metabolism, maintaining iron homeostasis, and inhibiting lipid peroxidation\. This effect is closely associated with the coordinated regulation of inflammatory responses, lipid metabolism, and ferroptosis\-related signaling pathways\.
