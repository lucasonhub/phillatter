# THE EL\-RAKHAWI PHYSICO\-CHEMICAL ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE FRAMEWORK: AI\-DRIVEN DISCOVERY OF ECO\-FRIENDLY CATALYSTS AND ADVANCED SUSTAINABLE MATERIALS

**Autores:** mohamed kamal arafa el\-rakhawi

**Data:** 2026-10-15 · **Tipo:** book

## Resumo

The El\-Rakhawi Physico\-Chemical AI \(EPC\-AI\) Framework revolutionizes sustainable material discovery by transcending the limitations of trial\-and\-error experimentation and computationally prohibitive Density Functional Theory\. Addressing industry&\#x27;s dependency on scarce, toxic rare\-earth metals, EPC\-AI integrates four novel modules: a Physics\-Informed Generative Material Model \(PIGMM\) using equivariant Graph Neural Networks constrained by quantum mechanical symmetries to generate thermodynamically stable structures; an AI\-Accelerated Reaction Pathway Mapper \(AARPM\) employing Machine Learning Potentials to predict catalytic mechanisms with near\-quantum accuracy; an Integrated Green Chemistry and Lifecycle Evaluator \(IGCLE\) that embeds the 12 Principles of Green Chemistry directly into the loss function as mathematical penalties, ensuring sustainability by design; and an Autonomous Closed\-Loop Validation Interface connecting AI predictions to robotic self\-driving laboratories\. Performance is quantified by the El\-Rakhawi Sustainable Material Discovery Metric \(ESMDM\), yielding bits per Joule to link discovery power directly to thermodynamic and ecological efficiency\. EPC\-AI accelerates the discovery of earth\-abundant, non\-toxic catalysts for critical applications like CO2 reduction and green ammonia synthesis, democratizing access to advanced materials while eliminating environmental harm\.
