# TURMUSH QURAYOTGAN YOSHLARNI OILAVIY MUNOSABATLARGA TAYYORLASHNING IJTIMOIY\-PSIXOLOGIK XUSUSIYATLARI

**Autores:** Maxbuba Alisherovna Turobova

**Data:** 2026-10-13 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

The article analyses the structure of young people’s socio\-psychological readiness for family relationships before marriage, the factors that shape it and the ways of preparing for it\. Based on a theoretical review of Uzbek and international family psychology research, the study identifies motivational, cognitive, emotional, communicative and behavioural \(role\) components of readiness and classifies the social and personal factors involved\. A stage\-based model of premarital psychological preparation is proposed\.
