# Molecular Structure Design of Heat\-Resistant Cycloolefin Polymer Based on Machine Learning

**Autores:** Yuxiao Liu; Qinghong Zhang; Baoquan Wan; Shao‐Long Zhong; Zhi‐Min Dang

**Data:** 2026-10-14 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Abstract Traditional biaxially oriented polypropylene \(BOPP\) is widely used in capacitors of converter valves, but its high\-temperature performance is gradually approaching its design limit\. Cycloolefin copolymer/polymer \(COC/COP\) materials are explored as promising alternatives due to their high glass transition temperature, wide band gap, thermal stability, and low conduction loss\. To overcome the lack of large\-scale COC/COP polymer databases and the limitations of experimental validation, molecular dynamics, and conventional machine\-learning screening, this work proposes a graph convolutional neural network \(GCN\)\-based workflow for inverse design and property prediction of cycloolefin polymer monomers\. An initial database of nearly 200,000 COC/COP candidates was constructed through library skeleton design and converted into molecular graph representations for model training, latent\-space sampling, and molecular generation\. Using a GCN\-based forward prediction model, we screened for 12 potential heat\-resistant polymers\. Combined with subsequent calculations of molecular dynamics \(MD\) and retrosynthetic analysis, we ultimately identified P9 and P11 as the final candidate heat\-resistant polymers\. This work provides a case\-study approach for the design and screening of high\-temperature\-resistant polymer dielectrics\.
