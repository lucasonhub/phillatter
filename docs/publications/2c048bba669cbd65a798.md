  What actually happens when a person does not use artificial intelligence merely
  to obtain an answer, but instead enters into a dialogue with it, challenges it,
  corrects it, provides evidence, and jointly constructs a new understanding of the
  problem itself? This evidential protocol examines precisely that process. It does
  not treat dialogue as a sequence of isolated questions and answers, nor does it
  define every correction made by an AI system as “learning.” Instead, it follows
  step by step whether the way the AI organizes knowledge within the conversation
  itself changes: the distinctions it uses, its basic assumptions and rules, the relations
  it recognizes, and the new conclusions it is subsequently able to generate. The
  theoretical basis is Epameinondas Xenopoulos’s Epistemology of Logic. In Xenopoulos’s
  original architecture, the real process Sπ, its cognitive reconstruction Sα, and
  the knowing subject Y(L,B,Θ) constitute distinct but interrelated levels. The new
  research move in the present protocol is simple but crucial: a contemporary conversational
  AI is placed functionally in the position of Y. The conversation itself is used
  as empirical material. Messages, corrections, objections, and new evidence are examined
  in the chronological order in which they actually occurred. The protocol tests whether
  the real development of the interaction corresponds to a transformation in the AI’s
  cognitive organization, and whether this transformation produces a subsequent cognitive
  stage. In this way, the study empirically examines the three-level correspondence
  presented by Xenopoulos on p. 281: the real level – the cognitive/propositional
  level – recursive construction. The analysis does not stop at a general resemblance.
  It also examines the synthetic transition from a position and its opposition toward
  a new synthesis with new properties, the expansion of the very capacity to relate
  elements, expanded induction, the recursive law of construction, and, wherever the
  empirical material permits, the specific source forms of N, Dubarle, and INRC. Where
  a correspondence cannot be demonstrated, this is stated explicitly; the theory is
  not imposed retrospectively on the data. The principal pilot finding is that, within
  continuous practical interaction, something more than the correction of individual
  answers can be observed. The organization of the problem changes, the network of
  relations expands, and new consequences are generated and subsequently used in further
  cognitive acts. This constitutes functional evidence of a cognitive upgrade of the
  artificial subject within the specific conversation. The significance of the problem,
  however, extends beyond a single dialogue. Contemporary AI systems are technically
  different expressions of a much broader, historically constituted human world of
  knowledge. Every day, billions of people interact practically with mobile phones,
  computers, and AI systems, producing new questions, corrections, texts, relations,
  categories, and digital traces. The present protocol therefore raises a broader
  research question: whether the cognitive upgrade observable within a single conversation
  may be a local manifestation of a wider historical-genetic transformation of humanity’s
  socially constituted Sα within the contemporary digital social BEING. The protocol
  does not claim that an AI “thinks like a human,” nor that human consciousness or
  permanent learning has been demonstrated. It proposes something more specific and
  testable: to examine the real conversation as a historical event and determine whether,

  the way in which it knows. Keywords: Epameinondas Xenopoulos, artificial intelligence,

# When Humans Transform AI Through Dialogue\(heteroiōsis / qualitatively transforms the AI\) \- The Historical\-Genetic Transformation of the Artificial Knowing Subject \- An Evidential Study Based on Epameinondas Xenopoulos&\#x27;s Epistemology of Logic

**Autores:** AKATERINH XENOPOULOU\-TYROKOMOU

## Resumo

When Humans Transform AI Through Dialogue\(heteroiōsis/qualitatively transforms the AI\) The Historical\-Genetic Transformation of the Artificial Knowing Subject An Evidential Study Based on Epameinondas Xenopoulos’s Epistemology of Logic Abstract What actually happens when a person does not use artificial intelligence merely to obtain an answer, but instead enters into a dialogue with it, challenges it, corrects it, provides evidence, and jointly constructs a new understanding of the problem itself? This evidential protocol examines precisely that process\. It does not treat dialogue as a sequence of isolated questions and answers, nor does it define every correction made by an AI system as “learning\.” Instead, it follows step by step whether the way the AI organizes knowledge within the conversation itself changes: the distinctions it uses, its basic assumptions and rules, the relations it recognizes, and the new conclusions it is subsequently able to generate\. The theoretical basis is Epameinondas Xenopoulos’s Epistemology of Logic\. In Xenopoulos’s original architecture, the real process Sπ, its cognitive reconstruction Sα, and the knowing subject Y\(L,B,Θ\) constitute distinct but interrelated levels\. The new research move in the present protocol is simple but crucial: a contemporary conversational AI is placed functionally in the position of Y\. The conversation itself is used as empirical material\. Messages, corrections, objections, and new evidence are examined in the chronological order in which they actually occurred\. The protocol tests whether the real development of the interaction corresponds to a transformation in the AI’s cognitive organization, and whether this transformation produces a subsequent cognitive stage\. In this way, the study empirically examines the three\-level correspondence presented by Xenopoulos on p\. 281: the real level – the cognitive/propositional level – recursive construction\. The analysis does not stop at a general resemblance\. It also examines the synthetic transition from a position and its opposition toward a new synthesis with new properties, the expansion of the very capacity to relate elements, expanded induction, the recursive law of construction, and, wherever the empirical material permits, the specific source forms of N, Dubarle, and INRC\. Where a correspondence cannot be demonstrated, this is stated explicitly; the theory is not imposed retrospectively on the data\. The principal pilot finding is that, within continuous practical interaction, something more than the correction of individual answers can be observed\. The organization of the problem changes, the network of relations expands, and new consequences are generated and subsequently used in further cognitive acts\. This constitutes functional evidence of a cognitive upgrade of the artificial subject within the specific conversation\. The significance of the problem, however, extends beyond a single dialogue\. Contemporary AI systems are technically different expressions of a much broader, historically constituted human world of knowledge\. Every day, billions of people interact practically with mobile phones, computers, and AI systems, producing new questions, corrections, texts, relations, categories, and digital traces\. The present protocol therefore raises a broader research question: whether the cognitive upgrade observable within a single conversation may be a local manifestation of a wider historical\-genetic transformation of humanity’s socially constituted Sα within the contemporary digital social BEING\. The protocol does not claim that an AI “thinks like a human,” nor that human consciousness or permanent learning has been demonstrated\. It proposes something more specific and testable: to examine the real conversation as a historical event and determine whether, through practical interaction, the artificial knowing subject functionally transforms the way in which it knows\. Η ίδια η πραγματική συνομιλία χρησιμοποιείται ως εμπειρικό υλικό\. Τα μηνύματα, οι διορθώσεις, οι αντιρρήσεις και τα νέα τεκμήρια εξετάζονται με τη χρονική σειρά με την οποία συνέβησαν\. Ελέγχεται αν η πραγματική εξέλιξη της αλληλεπίδρασης αντιστοιχεί σε μεταβολή της γνωστικής συγκρότησης του AI και αν από αυτή τη μεταβολή κατασκευάζεται επόμενη γνωστική βαθμίδα\. Με αυτόν τον τρόπο δοκιμάζεται εμπειρικά η τριπλή αντιστοιχία που παρουσιάζει ο Ξενόπουλος στη σ\. 281: πραγματικό επίπεδο – γνωστικό/προτασιακό επίπεδο – αναδρομική κατασκευή\. Μεταβάλλεται η οργάνωση του προβλήματος, διευρύνεται το δίκτυο των σχέσεων και παράγονται νέες συνέπειες που χρησιμοποιούνται στις επόμενες γνωστικές πράξεις\.
