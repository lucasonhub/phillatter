# Linha de cuidado para pessoas em crise de ansiedade na urgência e emergência: evidências e desafios na integração entre upa e a rede de atenção psicossocial

**Autores:** Ricardo Galvão Filho; Querem Evelyn da Silva Pereira; FABIANA RAQUEL FRIESS; Luiz Fernando Jun Aoyagi; Rebeca Ribeiro marques; Cesar Romero Lima Ferreira; Brenda Paes Bendassoli

**Data:** 2026-10-14 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Acute manifestations of psychological distress, particularly anxiety crises and panic attacks, represent highly prevalent demands in fixed pre\-hospital emergency care facilities, such as 24\-hour Emergency Care Units \(UPAs\)\. However, clinical management is frequently characterized by episodic interventions, covert stigmatization, symptomatic hypermedicalization, and severe disruption of longitudinal follow\-up\. This study aims to analyze scientific evidence and clinical\-organizational challenges in establishing an integrated care pathway for individuals experiencing acute anxiety in emergency settings, emphasizing the articulation between acute emergency services and the Psychosocial Care Network \(RAPS\), notably Psychosocial Care Centers \(CAPS\) and Primary Health Care\. This narrative literature review was conducted through a structured bibliographic search on the MEDLINE database via Europe PMC \(2015–2025\), complemented by official regulatory frameworks of the Brazilian Unified Health System\. The evidence indicates that acute emergency management requires rapid clinical stratification to rule out life\-threatening somatic mimics, relational reception grounded in active listening, judicious and strictly short\-term anxiolytic pharmacotherapy, and structured discharge planning\. Collaborative intersectoral matrix support and stepped\-care models emerge as decisive strategies to mitigate overcrowding, prevent rapid readmissions, and ensure person\-centered care\.
