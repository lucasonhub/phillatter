# Closing surveillance detection gaps for emerging digital drug discourse with ontology\-enhanced large language models

**Autores:** Danny Valdez; Damir Ćavar; Maria A\. Parker; Xue\-Jing Liu; Jon D\. Agley

**Data:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

LLMs are touted as a solution to enhance digital drug surveillance but lack domain knowledge to perform these tasks effectively\. Yet, when LLM architecture is enhanced by ontological frameworks, knowledge graphs, and retrieval\-augmented\-generation, we can evolve surveillance efforts to address a core issue contributing to surveillance lag—drug\-slang and coded\-language\. Integrating these established methods offers a novel, linguistically grounded approach to improving the validity and responsiveness of digital drug surveillance\.
