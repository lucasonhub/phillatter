# OLIY TA’LIM MUASSASALARIDA TA’LIM SIFATINI BOSHQARISH JARAYONINING PEDAGOGIK SHART\-SHAROITLARI

**Autores:** Latipova Zarina Oybek qizi

**Data:** 2026-10-10 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

The article examines the pedagogical conditions for effectively organizing education quality management in higher education institutions\. Alignment among educational objectives, curriculum content, teaching methods, and assessment criteria is considered a fundamental requirement for quality management\. The discussion addresses the development of teaching staff’s pedagogical competence, active student participation in the educational process, regular feedback, and internal quality monitoring\. It also explores the pedagogical significance of management decisions informed by learning outcomes, a collaborative learning environment, and continuous improvement of the educational process\.
