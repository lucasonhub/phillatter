# Service area extension of PPP\-B2b using clock bias prediction based on frequency stability and orbit age\-extension

**Autores:** Wenxuan Liu; Yize Zhang; Junping Chen; Yu Chao; Junsheng Ding; Ziyuan Song; Chenglong Zhang; Quanrun Cheng

**Data:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Abstract The PPP\-B2b service provided by the BeiDou\-3 Navigation Satellite System \(BDS\-3\) enables high\-precision real\-time precise point positioning \(PPP\) through corrections broadcast by satellites\. However, due to the regional distribution of tracking stations within the Operational Control System \(OCS\), its service area is mainly limited to the Asia\-Pacific region, leading to short correction arcs and data discontinuities near and beyond the service boundary\. To extend this capability beyond its nominal service area, this study proposes a service area extension strategy that combines clock bias prediction based on frequency stability with orbit correction age\-extension\. The clock bias prediction component mitigates arc discontinuity and tail degradation through local frequency estimation, stable estimate screening, starting point refinement, and prediction length control\. Based on 24\-hour clock prediction experiments with different modeling and prediction lengths, practical extrapolation constraints are determined, and clock prediction precision is maintained at the 0\.2 ns level\. Real\-time kinematic PPP experiments using the extended PPP\-B2b products show that the effective service area is extended to the Indochina Peninsula while maintaining centimeter level horizontal and decimeter level vertical accuracy, and further reaches inland Australia under a decimeter level kinematic positioning constraint\.
