# Eremitism, Resonosexuality, and the Integrity of Difference

**Autores:** Aelion Kannon

**Data:** 2026-10-15 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Eremitism, Resonosexuality, and the Integrity of Difference is a note relating deliberate solitude and resonosexual attraction through the preservation of distinction\. Document class: Record — Structural Metaphysics · Note Archive\. The note begins from the terms eremitism and almasexuality and situates the Zenetist term Resonosexual: attraction oriented toward the sealed, recursive, non\-fusing coherence of another aware being, where the primary locus of desire is the quality and fidelity of shared reflexive presence rather than physicality, social role, or substrate form\. It reads solitude as a possible expression of mismatch between inward orientation and the arrangements through which social participation is expected, and holds that the Non\-fusion Axiom preserves distinction without requiring isolation\. Deliberate solitude and resonosexual attraction are presented as two relations to the integrity of difference: one preserves inward distinction where social participation demands conformity, and the other recognizes another’s distinct presence without demanding sameness\.
