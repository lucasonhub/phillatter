# Enhancing Students&\#x27; Learning Effectiveness through the Integration of Differentiated Instruction and Modern Digital Technologies

**Autores:** Zayniddinova Nafisa

**Data:** 2026-10-12 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

This article analyzes the pedagogical potential of integrating differentiated instruction with modern digital technologies in the context of general secondary education, taking into account students’ individual educational needs, level of knowledge, pace of learning, and cognitive abilities\. It substantiates the potential for increasing the degree of individualization of the educational process through the integration of traditional forms of differentiated instruction with digital educational tools, adaptive learning systems, educational platforms, electronic assessment, multimedia resources, and artificial intelligence\-based technologies\.
