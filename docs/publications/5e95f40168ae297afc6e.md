# THE EFFECTIVENESS OF USING ELECTRONIC RESOURCES AND MULTIMEDIA TOOLS IN HIGHER EDUCATION

**Autores:**  Mo&\#x27;ydinova Mohlarbegim Abdullajon qizi; Worldly Knowledge Publishing Centre

**Data:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

This conceptual article examines the conditions under which electronic educational resources and multimedia tools can support meaningful learning in higher education\. Building on research in multimedia learning, constructive alignment, digital pedagogy, self\-regulated learning, and inclusive course design, the study compares the pedagogical functions of digital textbooks, academic databases, audiovisual materials, virtual laboratories, learning management systems, online assessments, and collaborative platforms\. A critical narrative synthesis is used to distinguish the accessibility of a resource from evidence of its educational effectiveness\. The principal contribution is a practical alignment framework linking learning outcomes, resource selection, active student tasks, assessment evidence, and subsequent instructional adjustment\. An illustrative university learning sequence demonstrates the application of this framework\. The analysis suggests that digital materials are most educationally valuable when they minimize unnecessary cognitive load, make thinking visible, provide actionable feedback, and remain accessible to students with different circumstances and abilities\. The article also examines unequal access, platform dependence, privacy, unreliable content, and uncritical uses of generative artificial intelligence\. No original student experiment is reported; the proposed framework should therefore be treated as a research\-informed design proposition rather than an empirically validated intervention\.
