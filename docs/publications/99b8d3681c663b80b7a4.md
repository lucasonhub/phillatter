# CogDrift\-4D: Hierarchical Multimodal Modeling of Temporal Behavioral Decay for MCI Detection

**Autores:** Sankalp Vaish; Sumukh Veeramalla; Rishank Singh; Caroline Summerour; John A\. Batsis; Xiaohui Liang

**Data:** 2026-10-14 · **Tipo:** preprint

## Resumo

Early detection of mild cognitive impairment remains a critical bottleneck in dementia care, demanding scalable tools capable of capturing subtle behavioral decay\. To capture the subtle temporal changes of cognitive decline, we introduce a behavioral dataset collected across seven unscripted multimodal interactions over an 18\-month horizon\. This dataset formalizes continuous, multi\-session interactions into a dense 4D tensor \(Batch \* Session \* Time \* Feature\) that explicitly preserves concurrent local dynamics alongside long\-term changes\. To effectively process these complex behavioral patterns, we propose CogDrift\-4D: a novel, hierarchical 4D Transformer framework\. To capture the complex trajectory of cognitive decline, CogDrift\-4D implements a dual\-level behavioral fatigue and drift modeling architecture\. At the intra\-session level, the model computes localized least\-squares regression slopes to quantify the acute \`\`fading&\#x27;&\#x27; of frame\-level facial action units and acoustic energy\. At the inter\-session level, a recursive cross\-attention feedback encoder acts as a cumulative fatigue accumulator, mapping the patient&\#x27;s continuous behavioral drift across successive sessions\. To mitigate environmental noise and track failures across sessions, a localized Quality Gate dynamically filters low\-integrity features before final fusion, also an early fusion that integrates semantic insights with raw physical dynamics\. Evaluated on structured verbal and linguistic interactions, our architecture successfully detects MCI with a classification accuracy of 0\.733 \+\- 0\.13 and an F1\-score of 0\.716 \+\- 0\.14, outperforming advanced baselines\. The framework provides a mathematically rigorous, scalable, and robust foundation for home\-based, real\-time dementia screening\.
