# Benefits and challenges of using AI in career counseling

**Autores:** KATSIOULA PANAGIOTA; BOUTSKOU LEMONIA; PEOVITI THEODORA; KATSIOULA THOMAIS

**Data:** 2026-10-13 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

The purpose of this paper is to examine the benefits and challenges arising from the use of AI in career counseling in secondary education\. A quantitative online survey was conducted using a questionnaire on a sample of 115 counselors from the regions of Western and Central Macedonia and Thessaly\. Overall, it was found that AI can be a very useful tool for career guidance counselors to provide personalized interpretation of assessment results, leading to personalized guidance that is also culturally sensitive, as well as to better match students&quot; skills and interests with academic and professional paths that are also in line with labor market requirements\. However, issues of accessibility, transparency, personal data protection, algorithm bias, and reduced trust have been identified, for example in the interpretation of results and the lack of human interaction\. Therefore, existing regulatory frameworks need to be strengthened, infrastructure needs to be enhanced to increase accessibility, and AI systems need to be continuously developed\.
