# La tecnología y las transformaciones del ser humano: un estudio psicosocial sobre la formación de actitudes e identidad en la era digital

**Autores:** Carlos Martinez Romero; Sergio Gómez Castillo

**Data:** 2026-10-10 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

This study examines how digital technology shapes individuals&\#x27; psychological and social attitudes from a social\-psychological perspective, with particular attention to the cultural specificity of Arab societies compared with Western contexts\. It adopts a critical narrative review of Arabic and international literature in social psychology and digital media studies\. The study is organized around three axes\. The first addresses psychological effects, including digital social comparison, the need for online approval, information overload, and the discrepancy between the digital and the actual self\. The second examines social dimensions: shifting patterns of belonging, cultural hybridization, political polarization, and the digital divide\. The third anticipates future implications related to artificial intelligence, virtual worlds, isolation, and changing moral frameworks\. Each axis closes with a critical reading of the evidence, addressing small effect sizes, the reliance on Western samples, and the contested echo\-chamber thesis\. The study concludes that digital technology is not a neutral medium, yet its effects on cognition, emotion, and belonging vary across individuals and cultural contexts and do not run in a single direction\. It recommends critical digital education, ethical digital governance, and Arab field research to test the propositions advanced here\.
