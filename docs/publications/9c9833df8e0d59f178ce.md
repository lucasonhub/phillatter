# Phillips, Randall: I1: Toward an Information\-First Unification of Mind and World

**Autores:** 

**Data de descoberta:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** unknown

## Descrição do feed

Sudden, non\-reconstructive insights — arriving whole, pre\-linguistic, without felt assembly — are a class of cognitive event standard generative models of mind do not explain\. This paper proposes an information\-first framework in which such events, termed chunks, are receptions rather than constructions: encounters with structure encoded on a holographic boundary, accessible when default mode network activity is reduced\. Information — the difference that makes a difference — is ontologically primary, with spacetime, matter, and consciousness as downstream projections\. The observer is a topological feature of this field, a localized pinch capable of approaching the boundary from which the interior is generated, rather than a separate agent\. This geometry explains why chunk reception must be complete rather than fragmentary, and why the brain’s scale\-invariant dynamics can instantiate it without quantum coherence in biological tissue\. A falsifiable neuroimaging prediction follows: integrated structural insight should precede self\-referential medial prefrontal activation, not accompany it\.
