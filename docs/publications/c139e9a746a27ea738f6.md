# Improving the Bounds on Approximating Weighted Maximin Share for Chores

**Autores:** Trung Thanh Nguyen; Khaled Elbassioni; Jörg Rothe

**Data:** 2026-10-11 · **Tipo:** preprint

## Resumo

We investigate the problem of allocating indivisible chores among agents with arbitrary positive weights and additive nonnegative cost functions\. An allocation is said to be $α$\-weighted\-maximin\-share \(WMMS\) fair if the cost incurred by each agent is at most $α$ times her weighted maximin share\. We establish that every instance admits a $4\.124234528$\-WMMS allocation\. Moreover, for instances in which all weights and costs are rational numbers represented in binary, we give an algorithm that computes a $4\.12424$\-WMMS allocation in time polynomial in the number of agents, the number of chores, and the total binary encoding length of the input\. Complementing these upper bounds, we construct a family of instances with strictly positive, identically ordered costs for which the optimal WMMS approximation factors converge to $e$\. Together, our results substantially narrow the previously best\-known gap of $\[2,12\]$ established by Li et al\.\~\\cite\{LWX26\} to $\[e,4\.12424\]$\.
