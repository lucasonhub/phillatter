# Encoder Selection and Segmentation Quality in U\-Net\-based Retinal Vessel Analysis

**Autores:** Jegan Sivaraman; Arjun Paramarthalingam; Arulnancy Thirunavukkarasu; Regan Rajendran

**Data:** 2026-10-14 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Retinal vessel segmentation through automation is a very significant area in retinal image processing, which can help in diagnosing many different ocular and systemic diseases\. In this research study, a comparative study of the encoder\-based U\-Net models is presented for retinal vessel segmentation\. Five different models including ResNet18\+U\-Net, ResNet34\+U\-Net, Traditional U\-Net, VGG16\+U\-Net, and VGG19\+U\-Net have been developed and trained on the retinal images belonging to the DRIVE and CHASE\_DB1 databases\. The proposed methodology involves image pre\-processing, image normalization, image augmentation, feature extraction from image using the encoder architecture, and U\-Net decoding with the skip connection for pixel\-wise segmentation of retinal vessels\. Among all models evaluated in this paper, the VGG19\+U\-Net model gives the highest value of Jaccard score of 81\.83%, F1 score of 89\.53%, Precision of 89\.47%, Accuracy of 98\.41%, and Specificity of 99\.13%, whereas the highest Sensitivity score of 90\.32% is achieved by the VGG16\+U\-Net model\.
