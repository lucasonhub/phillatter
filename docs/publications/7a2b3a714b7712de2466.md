# DeepPV\-MRF: End\-to\-End Partial\-Volume Fraction Estimation from MR Fingerprinting Time Series

**Autores:** Tianyi Ding; Yang Gao; Zhuang Xiong; Feng Liu; Martijn A\. Cloos; Jin Jin; Hongfu Sun

**Data:** 2026-10-14 · **Tipo:** preprint

## Resumo

Background: Estimating subvoxel tissue fractions from magnetic resonance fingerprinting \(MRF\) is challenging\. Conventional dictionary matching and segmentation pipelines can propagate errors from intermediate parametric maps into tissue fraction estimates\. Purpose: We introduce DeepPV\-MRF, a deep learning framework that directly estimates voxel\-wise tissue fractions from raw MRF signals\. Methods: A cascaded spatial\-temporal network was trained on subject\-derived Bloch simulations with radial k\-space undersampling\. The network combines temporal encoding and spatial U\-Net refinement to estimate cerebrospinal fluid \(CSF\), gray matter \(GM\), and white matter \(WM\) fractions without intermediate T1/T2 maps\. We compared DeepPV\-MRF with partial\-volume dictionary matching \(PV\-DM\) and pseudoinverse estimation \(PV\-Pinv\) using simulated and in vivo data\. Results: DeepPV\-MRF outperformed both methods across acquisition settings\. In single\-shot simulations, CSF/WM/GM mean absolute errors were 0\.0418/0\.0744/0\.0757, versus 0\.1281/0\.2443/0\.2958 for PV\-DM and 0\.2021/0\.2793/0\.2630 for PV\-Pinv\. Corresponding structural similarity values were 0\.960/0\.916/0\.922\. In vivo results showed sharper anatomical boundaries and smoother tissue transitions, while cross\-shot analysis indicated improved consistency across volunteers\. Conclusions: DeepPV\-MRF enables accurate and efficient direct tissue fraction estimation from MRF signals, particularly with limited shots\. Further validation is needed before clinical deployment\.
