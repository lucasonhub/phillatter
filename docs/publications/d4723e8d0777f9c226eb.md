# History, Adaptation, and Hidden Gain: Two Prospective Tests of Learned Predictive Control in a Synthetic Neural Population

**Autores:** Mahesh Reddy Pagadala

**Data:** 2026-10-09 · **Tipo:** preprint

## Resumo

We study learned one\-step predictive control of a synthetic population of 64 leaky integrate\-and\-fire neurons whose light sensitivity is scaled by a hidden factor s, using two prospective confirmations on plants simulated only after the analysis was frozen\. In the first \(50 plants, 11 conditions\), removing the 200\-ms input\-output history substantially worsened a parameter\-count\-matched neural predictor at low and moderate gain \(s = 0\.6, 0\.8; RMSE rose by 2\.47 Hz at s = 0\.6, 97\.5% CI 2\.43\-2\.51\)\. In secondary comparisons, recursively adapted ARX had the lowest mean error of the deployable controllers in 10 of 11 conditions, tuned PI with anti\-windup matched or beat the network for s &gt;= 0\.7, and training for 10,000 rather than 900 epochs lowered prediction error but worsened control\. Development work suggested the network&\#x27;s learned action sensitivity did not track the hidden gain\. A second protocol \(50 new plants, 9 sensitivities, 2 noise levels\) confirmed three pre\-specified predictions at the primary noise level\. Re\-estimating a single multiplicative input gain recovered 87% \(95% CI 86\-88%\) of the benefit of full adaptive ARX\. Supplying that online estimate to the network as an extra input reduced high\-gain tracking error by 1\.84 Hz \[1\.81, 1\.88\]\. Longer training worsened the plain network by 1\.09 Hz but improved the gain\-input network by 1\.07 Hz, a differential of 2\.16 Hz \[2\.13, 2\.20\]\. Under high process noise \(sigma = 2\.0\) the effects shrank to about 0\.04 Hz or less and gain\-only adaptation recovered only 24% of the adaptive\-ARX benefit\. In this synthetic plant, at the primary noise level, one\-parameter online gain estimation accounts for most of the gap between a history\-based network and full adaptive ARX, and for its prediction\-control mismatch\.
