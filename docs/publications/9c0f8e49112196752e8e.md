# THE EL\-RAKHAWI QUANTUM\-NEUROMORPHIC AGI ENGINE: ENGINEERING CONTINUOUS CAUSAL REASONING AND REAL\-TIME GLOBAL PROBLEM SOLVING VIA ENTANGLED COGNITIVE STATES AND HAMILTONIAN OPTIMIZATION

**Autores:** mohamed kamal arafa el\-rakhawi

**Data:** 2026-10-15 · **Tipo:** book

## Resumo

The El\-Rakhawi Quantum\-Neuromorphic AGI Engine \(QNAGI\) revolutionizes artificial intelligence by fusing quantum computing with neuromorphic cognitive architecture to solve planetary\-scale challenges\. Addressing the causal reasoning deficit, catastrophic forgetting, and thermodynamic inefficiency of classical AI, QNAGI introduces four novel modules: Entangled Knowledge Representation \(EKR\) mapping concepts to fault\-tolerant quantum states; a Cognitive Hamiltonian Reasoning Engine \(CHRE\) translating causal graphs into Hamiltonian operators to find optimal ground states; Thermodynamically Reversible Continuous Learning \(TRCL\) utilizing unitary Hilbert space expansion to prevent forgetting while approaching Landauer’s limit; and a Safe Quantum\-Human Translation Interface \(SQHTI\) encoding ethical values as quantum observables to guarantee alignment\. The framework is quantified by the Quantum Cognitive Efficiency Metric \(QCEM\), yielding bits per Joule, directly linking causal insight acquisition to fundamental thermodynamic limits\. QNAGI enables real\-time, ethically aligned optimization for global crises like pandemics, climate change, and logistics, transforming AI from a statistical pattern matcher into a true, energy\-efficient cognitive partner for humanity\.
