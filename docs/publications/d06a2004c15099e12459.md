# IoT\-Enabled Autonomous Agriculture Monitoring Rover for Smart Farming: A Comprehensive Literature Review

**Autores:** Chaitra A S; Venugopal Gowda V; Vinay H; Jeevan T; Puneeth M

**Data:** 2026-10-13 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

The rapid development of the Internet of Things \(IoT\), autonomous robotics, artificial intelligence \(AI\), embedded systems, wireless communication, and precision agriculture has created new opportunities for improving agricultural productivity and resource utilization\. Conventional agricultural monitoring relies extensively on manual field inspection, which is time\-consuming, labor\-intensive, and often unable to provide continuous information about crop and soil conditions\. Autonomous agricultural rovers provide an alternative approach by enabling mobile sensing, real\-time environmental monitoring, crop observation, navigation, and precision agricultural operations\. This paper presents a comprehensive literature review of IoT\-enabled autonomous agricultural rovers for smart farming, with particular emphasis on recent developments reported in IEEE literature\. Existing research is analyzed according to rover architecture, sensing technologies, navigation techniques, communication technologies, IoT platforms, agricultural applications, AI integration, and autonomy levels\. Recent studies have demonstrated the feasibility of ESP32\-based IoT rovers, ROS\-based agricultural monitoring robots, autonomous navigation systems, precision irrigation platforms, soil\-monitoring robots, and AI\-enabled agricultural rovers\. However, several limitations remain, including restricted autonomous navigation in unstructured environments, limited multi\-parameter sensing, communication reliability in rural areas, high computational requirements for AI, energy limitations, and insufficient integration of monitoring with autonomous decision\-making\. Based on the reviewed literature, a research gap is identified for a low\-cost, modular, IoT\-enabled autonomous agricultural monitoring rover capable of simultaneously performing soil and environmental sensing, crop monitoring, obstacle avoidance, real\-time wireless communication, location\-aware operation, and intelligent decision support\.
