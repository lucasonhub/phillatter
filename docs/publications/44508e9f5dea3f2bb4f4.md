# An AI\- and IoT\-Enabled Smart Food Donation Management System with Explainable Food\-Risk Prediction, Safe\-Time Forecasting and Automated NGO Assignment

**Autores:** Dr\. Manoj Singh S\. Mathavencie Sudhakar

**Data:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Surplus cooked food from restaurants, hotels and cafés is often discarded because donors cannot verify its safety or reach an NGO in time\. This paper presents a Smart Food Donation Management System that combines low\-cost IoT sensing, explainable machine learning and automated NGO allocation in one web platform\. An ESP32 node with DHT22 and MQ\-135 sensors streams storage conditions, and high gas readings flag or block spoiled food\. Six algorithms were compared with 5\-fold cross\-validation; the selected Gradient Boosting model classifies each donation as low, medium or high risk from temperature, humidity, food age and food type\. A safe\-time forecast estimates when food will become high risk, and exact Shapley values explain each prediction\. An allocation module offers each donation to one NGO at a time, filtering by remaining capacity and by whether the NGO can arrive before the food becomes unsafe\. On 600 heldout records the model achieved 97\.0% accuracy, a macro F1\-score of 0\.957 and 98\.7% high\-risk recall, with no high\-risk record classified as low risk\. In a 50\-day simulation the allocation method delivered 87\.2% of meals safely, against 83\.3% for nearest\-NGO allocation, and all 45 functional tests passed\.
