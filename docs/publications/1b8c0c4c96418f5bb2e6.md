# Sex differences in circulating endocannabinoid concentrations during adolescence: Associations with pubertal development

**Autores:** Julia M\. Evanski; Tristin Smith; Adriene M\. Beltz; Esmeralda Hidalgo‐Lopez; Christel Madeleine Portengen; Andrew Schrepf; Steven E\. Harte; Chelsea M\. Kaplan

**Data:** 2026-10-15 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Endocannabinoids contribute to sex\-related processes, including puberty\. In 1,159 adolescents, females showed significantly lower concentrations than males across five endocannabinoids \(AEA, 2\-AG, PEA, OEA, and 2\-OG\)\. Sex\-by\-puberty interactions were significant for AEA, PEA, and OEA\. Exploratory analyses revealed sex\-specific associations between endocannabinoids and self\-reported pain, stress, internalizing, and attentional problems\.
