# Emmerson, Parker: Articutechnica

**Autores:** 

**Data de descoberta:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** unknown

## Descrição do feed

Articutechnica collects fifty\-eight papers by Parker Emmerson \(who also publishes as Yaohushuason\) into a single continuous volume of 1,217 pages, arranged in nine parts: Preface; Prerequisites; Topology Briefings; Interactions Anterolateral; Q\-Daisy Networks and Merged Pipelines; The Book of Loops; Tachyons, Cauchy–Schwarz Developments, Cosmology and Extraordinary Particles; Connector Theory and The Pythagorean Operator; and Synthesis\. The journey runs from point\-set and non\-commutative topology, through the anterolateral algebra of energy\-tagged forms, into the self\-similar world of q\-daisy networks and their operator pipelines \(including the Infinity Diamond Matrices and the Diamond Matrix Method\), onward to phenomenological velocity, the PV–A∞ program, Majorana and Bell–CHSH developments, the loops and their fractal logical mitosis, and finally to Connector Theory and the Operator Pythagoras Theorem that gives the collection its subtitle\. A closing gestalt synthesis reads the corpus as one construction and proves a velocity–dimension bridge: the velocity of a split writes itself into the Hausdorff dimension of a Cantor boundary\. Every chapter is a complete paper, reproduced exactly as it compiles from its own source; papers are grouped by theme rather than by date\. The accompanying archive contains the full LaTeX source of all fifty\-eight papers with their figures and generating Python programs, the master file, and reproducible build and repair scripts, so that the volume can be rebuilt from source\. The edition is typeset with illuminated part pages and initials; the cover is computed from the corpus&\#x27;s own exotic\-loop map Z ↦ Z² − Z̄ \+ C\. KEYWORDS anterolateral algebra; q\-daisy networks; phenomenological velocity; connector theory; operator Pythagoras theorem; non\-commutative topology; energy numbers; fractal logical mitosis; exotic loops; choiciotomy; Infinity Diamond Matrices; Cantor boundary; Hausdorff dimension; Bell–CHSH; Majorana fermions; PV–A∞ transform; sweeping nets; mathematical physics; formal logic; collected papers ADDITIONAL NOTES The author also publishes under the name Yaohushuason\. Interior papers carry their original author lines\. The volume was assembled and typeset in August–September 2026; the source archive
