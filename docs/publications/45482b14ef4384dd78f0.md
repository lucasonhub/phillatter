# Nonlocal Evolution of Magnetic Helicity across the Magnetohydrodynamic–Kinetic Interface

**Autores:** Masatomi Iizawa; Yasuhito Narita; Tommaso Alberti; Stuart D\. Bale; Axel Brandenburg; Abraham C\.‐L\. Chian; H\. Comişel; Shuichi Matsukiyo; Nobumitsu Yokoi

**Data:** 2026-10-13 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Abstract Magnetic helicity is typically indistinguishable from noise in the solar wind inertial range\. Using Parker Solar Probe measurements \(0\.1–0\.8 au\), we report a robust bihelical structure emerging at the magnetohydrodynamic \(MHD\)–kinetic interface\. This structure, formed by a striking pair of positive and negative peaks, exhibits a systematic spectral migration toward lower frequencies with increasing heliocentric distance\. Crucially, this evolution deviates from local kinetic scaling laws, such as the radial decline of the ion cyclotron frequency, reflecting a nonlocal evolution of magnetic topology\. Our findings provide a new observational constraint on both nonlocal MHD transport processes and the nature of the helicity barrier in the kinetic regime\.
