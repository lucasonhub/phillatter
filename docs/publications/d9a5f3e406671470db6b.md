# The First Steps That Matter: A Guide to Neurodevelopment in Preterm Infants

**Autores:** Daiane Gartner

**Data:** 2026-10-10 · **Tipo:** book

## Resumo

Your baby came home before the due date\. Now what? The First Steps That Matter is a calm, science\-informed guide for the first months at home after the neonatal unit\. It shows parents how to prepare the nest, read the baby&\#x27;s signals, offer touch and rhythm, and support each step of development without rushing it\. Drawing on developmental neuroscience and clinical practice across occupational therapy, physiotherapy, speech\-language pathology, and optometry, the book brings together perception, regulation, movement, and connection in plain language\. Every chapter ends with a short table of what to observe and what to offer, and a page for the family&\#x27;s own notes\. This revised and expanded second edition adds two chapters: on corrected age and the first year of follow\-up, and on observing and describing what parents notice so the call to the pediatrician is made with confidence\.
