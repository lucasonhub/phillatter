# Coordinated inhibition of SOX9 and cell\-cycle progression by microRNA\-200 restricts sebaceous gland fate specification

**Autores:** Arpan Das; Yuheng C\. Fu; Haimin Li; Megan A\. Wong; Annalina Che; Anumeha Singh; Jimin Han; Glen A\. Bjerke; Dongmei Wang; Rui Yi

**Data:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Abstract The microRNA\-200 family \(miR\-200s\) is known for inhibiting epithelial\-to\-mesenchymal transition and cell\-cycle progression in cancer, but its function in epithelial development remains unclear\. Here we show that miR\-200s are highly enriched in hair matrix progenitors but absent from the upper hair follicle \(HF\), where sebaceous gland \(SG\) and HF stem cells are specified\. Ectopic miR\-200 induction abolishes SG specification while preserving hair morphogenesis\. Genome\-wide identification of miR\-200 targets reveals direct repression of negative regulators of WNT signaling and cell\-cycle regulators\. Single\-cell and spatial transcriptomic analyses uncover mutually exclusive domains of WNT activity and SOX9 expression in the upper HF that is disrupted by miR\-200 induction\. Genome\-wide mapping of SOX9 targets further uncovers a lipogenic and fatty\-acid metabolic program required for SG specification\. Together, these findings show that miR\-200 blocks SG fate by inhibiting SOX9 function and restricting cell proliferation, defining a spatially organized regulatory network essential for SG development\.
