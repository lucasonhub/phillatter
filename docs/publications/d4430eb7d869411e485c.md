# Crack Growth Prognostics with Particle Filtering Technique

**Autores:** Francesco Galeazzi

**Data:** 2026-10-15 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

This paper is the project deliverable for the MSc course entitled &quot;Artificial Intelligence and Advanced Simulation&quot;, held at Politecnico di Milano for the Master of Science in Nuclear Engineering\. It presents a simple application of a Particle Filtering Technique to track the crack degradation state of a 1D loaded plate, adopting a Bayesian framework and implementing a Sampling Importance Resampling algorithm\.\.
