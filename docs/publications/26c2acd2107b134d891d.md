# Design and Experimental Evaluation of a Low\-Cost Microcontroller\-Based Automatic A\.C\. Load Control System using Light\-Dependent Resistor Sensing

**Autores:** Chukwukodinaka G\. Awoh; Chinonso S\. Ezeonye

**Data:** 2026-10-11 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

In this paper, the development, realization and test of a microcontroller\-based A\.C\. load controller for automatic switching in accordance with ambient light level are presented\. The circuit consists of an LDR sensing unit, PIC16F84A microcontroller, relay switching stage, transistor driver and regulated power supply\. A 220 V A\.C\. was stepped down, rectified, filtered, and regulated to obtain the 5 V D\.C\. for the control circuit\. The 220 V, 60 W lamp was employed as the load for testing at both the subsystem and the full system levels\. The regulated power supply gave 5\.02 V D\.C at a ripple of 38 mV and the measured frequency for the 20 MHz crystal oscillator was 19\.994 MHz\. The relay coil current was 29\.6 mA and the operate time was 9\.8 ms, while the transistor driver and relay stages were found to be working in the designed region\. During complete system testing, Lamp turned ON when LDR was covered to represent as darkness and turn OFF, as light was cast on the sensor\. Repeated switching cycles gave more fine consistent results and no abnormal heating deforming occurred\. The results illustrate that the designed prototype successfully combines the features of illumination sensing, microcontroller based decision making, transistor driving and relay switching for automatic control of a 220 V A\.C load\. This technique gives a simple and effective approach to indoor local automatic load control with basic electronics components\.
