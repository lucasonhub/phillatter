# Reimagining Bucharest’s Central Station: PPP and Air Rights as Tools for Metropolitan Integration

**Autores:** Liviu\-Gabriel Baicu

**Data:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Railway\-station redevelopment is increasingly associated with transit\-oriented development, land\-value capture, and metropolitan regeneration, yet post\-socialist station areas remain insufficiently examined through an integrated spatial, operational, and financing lens\. This paper addresses that gap through a scenario\-based case study of the Gara de Nord \(Bucharest’s North Train Station\)–Basarab rail complex in Bucharest \(GN\-B\)\. The study combines GIS\-based spatial diagnosis, node–place assessment of Bucharest–Ilfov rail stations, purposive stakeholder interviews, survey evidence, and a seven\-criterion multi\-criteria analysis comparing six redevelopment scenarios \(S0–S5\)\. Results indicate that the train stations complex performs as a highly strategic transport node but remains weakly integrated as an urban place\. The preferred comprehensive scenario combines station modernization, intermodal restructuring, decking over parts of the railway yard, mixed\-use air\-rights development, public\-space creation, and phased PPP delivery\. The paper argues that PPPs and air rights can support station\-led regeneration in Bucharest only if embedded in transparent governance, public coordination, explicit value\-capture rules, and social safeguards\.
