# Autonomous Mobile Manipulators and AI\-Driven Cobots: Transforming Human\-Robot Collaboration in Next\-Generation Smart Factories

**Autores:** Neenu Ann Sunny

**Data:** 2026-10-09 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

The rapid evolution of Industry 4\.0 and 5\.0 demands flexible manufacturing systems that move beyond traditional rigid automation\. This paper presents an integrated framework for Autonomous Mobile Manipulators \(AMMs\) driven by Edge Artificial Intelligence \(AI\) and advanced computer vision\. By combining simultaneous localization and mapping \(SLAM\) with multi\-axis force\-torque sensing, the proposed system bridges the gap between autonomous mobility and precise robotic manipulation\. Experimental evaluations in a simulated smart\-factory environment demonstrate a 31% reduction in component transfer time, zero safety incidents during close human\-cobot interactions, and a 96% first\-attempt pick success rate for variable parts\. This study highlights the viability of deploying collaborative mobile manipulators to achieve highly adaptable, human\-centric production lines\.
