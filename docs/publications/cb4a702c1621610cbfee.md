# Dyer, Miroslav: Cybersemantic Consciousness: Positioning a Linguistic\-World Model Theory Relative to Chomsky and Searle

**Autores:** 

**Data de descoberta:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** unknown

## Descrição do feed

This paper situates Dyer&\#x27;s cybersemantic theory of consciousness\-according to which consciousness is a self\-corrective semantic world\-simulation implemented through an internal linguistic medium\-in relation to two major intellectual traditions: Chomsky&\#x27;s generative linguistics and Searle&\#x27;s biological naturalism\. Although all three perspectives engage with questions of language, meaning, and mental representation, they diverge sharply in domain, explanatory target, and ontological commitments\. This paper argues that cybersemantic theory synthesises certain insights from each thinker while ultimately establishing a distinct paradigm in which semantics becomes the fundamental ontology of conscious experience\.
