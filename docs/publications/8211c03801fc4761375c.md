# EKSTREMISTIK DUNYOQARASHDAGI MAHKUMLARDA IDENTIFIKATSIYA INQIROZINI PSIXOLOGIK KORREKSIYA QILISH YO‘LLARI

## Resumo

This article analyzes the psychological characteristics of personal and social identity crises among prisoners holding extremist worldviews and examines evidence\-informed approaches to their psychological correction\. Identity narrowing, psychological attachment to destructive groups, intensified “us–them” categorization, disruption of life meaning and future orientation, social stigma, and changes in social roles during imprisonment are considered major targets of intervention\.
