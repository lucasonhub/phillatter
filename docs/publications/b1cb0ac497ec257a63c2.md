# The development and use of Contextual Knowledge \(XK\) within the TPACK framework among secondary school English language teachers in Indonesia

**Autores:** Monica Ella Harendita

**Data:** 2026-10-14 · **Tipo:** dissertation

## Resumo

This study investigated the development and use of teacher Contextual Knowledge \(XK\) within the Technological Pedagogical Content Knowledge \(TPACK\) framework among secondary school English teachers in Indonesia\. Data were gathered from 14 in\-service teachers across six schools in three regions through in\-depth interviews informed by teachers&\#x27; lesson plans and digital artefacts\. Thematic analysis of the data revealed that teachers articulated XK regarding three layers of context \(micro, meso and macro\) in varying detail\. The development of their XK occurred mainly through informal and formal professional learning\. Finally, the use of XK in their technology\-mediated instruction aimed at improving student learning\.
