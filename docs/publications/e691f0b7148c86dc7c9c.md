# THE JUNCTION : On the Thirty\-Year Convergence of AI and Real\-Time Systems That Made ST\-T1024 Possible

**Autores:** M\. TAALABI; Team LLM\-DIPLOMAT

**Data:** 2026-10-15 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

ST\-T1024 is a deterministic inference architecture for embedded and edge hardware\. It achieves 3\.7 μJ per query, 800 ns AET, and zero hallucination by construction\. It is formally verified, Apache\-2\.0, and runs on a $12 microcontroller\. This paper asks a question the standard itself does not answer: why does this architectureexist, and why now? The answer is not a breakthrough\. The answer is a junction — the meeting of two disciplinesthat almost never meet in one person:· Artificial intelligence — knowledge representation, triage, domain routing, language\.· Real\-time embedded systems — AET/WCET discipline, ROM/SRAM partitioning, powergating, FSM scheduling, certification paths\. The two fields select for opposite instincts\. One tolerates a distribution; the other requires a bound\. They are separated by their failure modes\. ST\-T1024 exists because one person held both fields for thirty years — teaching AI since1988, working in real\-time operating systems since the 1980s, supervising industrial projects at STMicroelectronics from 2003 to 2008, and teaching QNX/Neutrino since 2000\. The paper records the junction, states what each field contributed, and names why thestandard could not have come from anywhere else\. It also discloses how the design processitself was conducted — with a large language model as advisor, under the author&\#x27;sspecification and signature\. That disclosure is not a footnote\. It is the demonstration of the architecture the standard describes\.
