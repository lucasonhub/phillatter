# Analisis Etika Penggunaan AI Generatif \(ChatGPT\) dalam penulisan Tugas Kuliah Studi Kasus : Mahasiswa Teknologi Informasi Untara

**Autores:** Saryani Saryani

**Data:** 2026-10-12 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

This study explores how effective and ethical the use of Artificial Intelligence \(AI\) tools, especially ChatGPT, is in helping students complete their academic assignments\. The research was conducted with 32 students from the Information Technology Study Program at Universitas Tangerang Raya, using an online questionnaire \(Google Form\) as the main data collection method\. The findings show that almost all students \(96\.9%\) have used AI in their coursework\. Most of them use it to find references \(93\.8%\), write or organize content \(31\.3%\), and check grammar \(21\.9%\)\. In addition, 78\.1% of students always review the AI\-generated results before submitting their work, which indicates a good sense of academic responsibility\. However, 62\.5% of respondents were neutral about whether using AI is ethical, suggesting that many students are still unsure about the ethical boundaries of AI use\. In general, the study concludes that tools like ChatGPT are effective learning aids that can make writing tasks easier and faster\. Yet, their use needs to be balanced with ethical understanding and critical thinking skills, so students can use AI responsibly and wisely in academic contexts\.
