# Ning, Huansheng: General Mind\-Thought Entanglement Theory \(MTE\): A Classical Field\-Theoretic Model — With a Note on the Concept and Mathematical Definition of the Conscious Yuanon

**Autores:** 

**Data de descoberta:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** unknown

## Descrição do feed

This paper proposes a Mind–Thought Entanglement Theory \(MTE\) aimed at providing a conceptual and mathematical framework for describing the structural relationships among the apparent unity, subjectivity, and intentional content in conscious experience\. While the theory draws terminologically from Eastern philosophical traditions of &quot;mind&quot; and &quot;thought,&quot; its goal is not to offer a direct interpretation of classical theories of mind, but rather to understand &quot;mind&quot; as an experiential field associated with the subject, and &quot;thought&quot; as distinguishable intentional, cognitive, or representational degrees of freedom within experience\. Based on this setup, the paper constructs a two\-field coupling model: an individualized mental field Ψ\(x,t\) in ordinary spacetime couples with a formalized primordial thought field φ\(y,τ\) defined in an auxiliary representational space of dimension n≠3\. The total action of the model consists of free dynamical terms for both fields and a holographic\-type coupling term, capturing cross\-space correlations between the experiential field and the structure of thought states\. Using variational principles, we derive the corresponding coupled field equations; further integrating out the auxiliary thought field yields a nonlocal effective dynamical equation for Ψ\. This nonlocal kernel formally represents the correlation structure among primitive thought degrees of freedom, offering a mathematical expression for the interconnection, holistic integration, and nonlocal organization of different intentional contents in conscious experience\. The paper also discusses several idealized solutions and symmetry properties to demonstrate the framework&\#x27;s capacity to formally characterize experiential stability and the differentiation of thought\-state structures\. Currently, MTE remains a conceptual and mathematical modeling attempt, not yet an experimentally verified theory, and its primary role lies in providing an initial formulation of the relationship between consciousness unity, the subject&\#x27;s experiential field, and the structure of intentional content\. The present study further identifies the coupled unity of the global mind field Ψ and the primordial thought field ϕ as the Yuanon—a fundamental nonlocal dynamical unit of conscious experience constituted by the coupled global mind field and primordial thought field\. The mathematical foundation of this definition is provided by the coupled two\-field equations, the nonlocal kernel, and the Green’s functions developed in this study\.
