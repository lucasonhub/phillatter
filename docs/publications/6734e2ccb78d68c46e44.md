# Accounting for Fiscal Governance in Risale\-i Felekiyye

**Autores:** Batuhan Güvemli; Muhsin Aslan; Özgür Muhittin Esen

**Data:** 2026-10-15 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

This research revisits Risale\-i Felekiyye – Kitab\-us Siyakat by applying a comparative analysis of seven sector\-specific accounting forms preserved in Chapter 8: stables, construction, rice cultivation, granaries, mint operations, sheep herding and treasury\. Rather than considering this document a comprehensive archive of Ilkhanid administrative practice, this study treats it as an instructional and practical work providing examples of accounting forms\. Using a standard analytical procedure, it explores who bears responsibility, what resource flow or transformation is recorded, how the accounting record is validated or reconciled, and what kind of accountability results from this\. This comparative analysis has demonstrated that Risale\-i Felekiyye did not require identical formats for the records of all state functions\. Different record formats depended on the physical properties of the resource, the nature of administrative relations, and the point where responsibility became reviewable\. Thus, the record format distinguished animal custody, project inputs, process losses, in\-kind distribution, metal conversion into money, changes in the flock size, and treasury transactions\. The article therefore interprets Risale\-i Felekiyye as evidence of sectoral accountability in fiscal governance\. Some records display paired presentations, residual balances and reconciliation, but they do not establish a unified modern double\-entry system\.
