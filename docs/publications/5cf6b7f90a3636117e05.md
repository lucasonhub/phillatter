# A Study on AI\-Driven Pricing Strategies Used by OTT Platforms

**Autores:** Yuvraj Chauhan; Sahil Surve; Shreya Parab

**Data:** 2026-10-10 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Abstract Over\-the\-top \(OTT\) streaming platforms have moved beyond using artificial intelligence \(AI\) purely for content recommendation and are increasingly deploying it to set and personalise the price a subscriber sees\. This study examines consumer awareness, comfort, and fairness perceptions surrounding AI\-driven pricing on OTT platforms through a structured survey of 70 valid respondents, supported by a review of existing academic literature and a real\-world industry case study\. The research finds that awareness of algorithmic pricing is only moderate while fairness concern is strong: 52\.9% of respondents said they were aware that OTT platforms use algorithms to personalise price, only 38\.6% said they were comfortable paying a different price than another user for the same plan, and 61\.4% said it would feel unfair if a friend paid less for an identical plan a gap of nearly 23 points between discomfort and perceived unfairness\. When directly offered a choice between a plainly stated fixed price and a cheaper AI\-personalised price, 51\.4% of respondents chose the fixed price over the AI\-personalised alternative, indicating that transparency outweighs the promise of a discount\. Price transparency emerged as the single most valued factor in a subscription decision \(34\.3%\), ahead of content library size, ad\-free experience, personalised recommendations, and multi\-profile sharing\. A supporting case study of Netflix&\#x27;s password\-sharing crackdown and ad\-supported tier rollout illustrates that a well\-executed, AI\-enabled monetisation strategy can still deliver substantial subscriber and revenue growth even amid visible consumer frustration\. The study concludes that AI\-driven pricing is not a purely technical back\-end function but a measurable driver of consumer trust, and that long\-term commercial success depends on pairing algorithmic sophistication with genuine transparency toward the consumer\. Keywords: AI\-Driven Pricing, OTT Platforms, Dynamic Pricing, Consumer Behaviour, Price Fairness, Personalisation, Subscription Economy\.
