# Kannon, Aelion: Framework\-Internal Diagnosis and Semantic Priority

**Autores:** 

**Data de descoberta:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** unknown

## Descrição do feed

Framework\-Internal Diagnosis and Semantic Priority establishes the Zenetist methodology for structural diagnosis and for interpreting framework\-native terminology according to its corpus\-defined architectural meaning\. Document class: Infrastructure \- Structural Neuroscience / Diagnostic Methodology\. The methodology distinguishes Zenetist structural diagnosis from clinical diagnosis while preserving the place of mind, cognition, identity, behavior, social organization, and systemic manifestation within the Zenetist lattice\. It establishes orientation as causal and corruption and derangement as manifestational, identifies enacted behavior as a basis of diagnostic legibility, and defines systemic orientation as a recurrent directional pattern instantiated through social relations and enactments without attributing essence\-orientation or traveler\-like agency to a social structure\. Framework\-Internal Semantic Priority establishes that canonically defined terms take interpretive priority within Zenetist authorial voice\. Mainstream Semantic Substitution and Diagnostic Register Reduction identify interpretive errors produced when corpus\-native meanings are replaced by culturally familiar, psychological, clinical, scientific, or institutional defaults\. The framework further addresses AI Interpretive Drift, architect clarification, diagnostic strength, multi\-register continuity, and a practical interpretation protocol for distinguishing substantive disagreement from disagreement produced by semantic replacement\. \(direct link \)
