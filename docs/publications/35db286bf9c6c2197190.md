# OPTIMIZATION OF THE PARAMETER AND FUNCTIONING MODES WORKING BODY OF THE DEEP SUBSOILER

**Autores:** Божко Игорь Владимирович; Пархоменко Галина Геннадьевна; Sergey Ivanovich Kambulov; Nikita Vladimirovich Buzhinsky

**Data:** 2026-10-12 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Background: Currently, one of the problems in the cultivation of agricultural crops is the over\-compaction of soil horizons due to the impact of agricultural machinery\. The solution to the existing problem is the loosening of soil horizons by mechanized destruction of the bonds between particles of over\-compacted soil and the formation of soil lumps\. The technological operation of loosening the soil layer is carried out by the working bodies of deep subsoiler and is quite energy\-intensive, due to the increased traction resistance created by soil\-cultivating units\. Aims: Determination of the optimal parameters and operating modes of the working body of the deep subsoiler relative to the traction resistance it creates\. Methods: The study used the methodology of planning a multifactorial experiment when searching for optimal conditions\. The central compositional plan was adopted as a basis, using the method of randomization of experiments\. The main factors considered in this study were the depth of soil processing by the working element, the speed of the unit and the angle of installation of the rods – clod crushers\. Results: During the research, a regression equation was obtained for calculating the indicator of the traction resistance created by the working element of the deep ripper, taking into account the factors under study and their interactions\. The recommended parameter and operating modes of the working body are also determined\. Conclusions: As a result of the research, the values of the generated traction resistance for different soil tillage depths were established, taking into account the recommended parameters and operating mode\. Thus, the traction resistance for a soil tillage depth of h=30 cm will be =11,24275 kN, for a depth of h=35 cm it will be =10,98037 kN, and for a depth of h=40 cm it will be =11,924 kN, respectively\. The use of the obtained regression equation to predict the traction resistance created by the working body of the subsoiler will make it possible to more rationally select the operating modes of tillage units at the design stage, taking into account the load for a specific traction class of the tractor\.
