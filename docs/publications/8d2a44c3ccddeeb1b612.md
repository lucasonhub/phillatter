# ONCship: A Large\-Scale Underwater Acoustic Corpus for Ship Recognition

**Autores:** Zhe Cao; Jichao Zhang; Xiao\-Lei Zhang

**Data:** 2026-10-14 · **Tipo:** preprint

## Resumo

Recent advances in deep learning have significantly improved underwater acoustic vessel recognition\. However, existing publicly available datasets are typically limited in scale, hindering large\-scale representation learning and downstream task evaluation\. To address this limitation, we introduce ONCship, a large\-scale underwater acoustic dataset comprising 4521 hours of curated recordings from Ocean Networks Canada \(ONC\) hydrophones and Automatic Identification System \(AIS\) data\. We evaluate the proposed dataset across three tasks: self\-supervised representation learning, vessel\-type classification, and MMSI\-Level Vessel Classification\. Experiments on DeepShip and ShipsEar demonstrate the effectiveness of ONCship for acoustic representation learning, with joint pre\-training on ONCship and AudioSet achieving a classification accuracy of 62\.45% on DeepShip through linear probing\. Furthermore, incorporating ONCship as auxiliary training data improves downstream vessel\-type classification performance\. Beyond conventional vessel\-type classification, we further investigate MMSI\-Level Vessel Classification, treating each vessel as a distinct identity\. Experimental results show that models fine\-tuned with different loss functions achieve Top\-1 accuracies exceeding 53% and Top\-5 accuracies approaching 69%, demonstrating the feasibility of distinguishing individual vessels through MMSI\-Level Vessel Classification\. Overall, ONCship provides a valuable resource for underwater acoustic representation learning and vessel recognition, while its rich vessel\-level metadata enables further exploration of MMSI\-Level Vessel Classification\.
