# Presenting ReadingFood and its infrastructure

**Autores:** Diana Santos; Michael Preminger; Elizaveta Khachaturyan; Åse Kristine Tveit; Slav Petrov

**Data:** 2026-10-15 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

In this paper, we provide general information on the ReadingFood project, discussing its architecture and infrastructure choice\(s\)\. After presenting the CorpusEye environment, we give some “appetizers” of the diverse studies that can be done with this system\. First, we present a taxonomy developed to cover food and drink in the three languages, and we explain the need to disambiguate for a fine\-grained classification in this domain\. We then illustrate different kinds of studies that ReadingFood allows, namely a\) lexical semantics of container words in Italian, b\) changes in drinking habits in Norway, and c\) differences between Portuguese and Brazilian societies in what concerns drinking\. We finish by a short discussion on how other infrastructural challenges for other parts of the project can be solved\.
