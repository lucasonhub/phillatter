# Rabies immunoglobulin utilization and delayed presentation following category III rabies exposure: a 10\-year retrospective study in Hangzhou, China

**Autores:** Jing Wang; Weiwei Cai; Jiaxin Chen; Jing Chen; Wanli Yang

**Data:** 2026-10-09 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Background Rabies is an almost invariably fatal, vaccine\-preventable zoonosis\. Rabies immunoglobulin \(RIG\) is recommended for Category III exposures, yet real\-world RIG utilization remains poorly characterized\. This study examined temporal trends and factors associated with RIG utilization and delayed presentation among Category III exposure patients in China\. Methods A retrospective study was conducted using clinical records of Category III exposure patients presenting to a high\-volume rabies prevention clinic in Hangzhou, China, from 2015 to 2024\. Calendar year 2018 was treated as a temporal reference year for descriptive comparison\. Multivariable logistic regression identified factors associated with RIG non\-administration and delayed presentation \(&gt;24 h post\-exposure\)\. Results A total of 14,270 eligible cases were analyzed\. Overall RIG utilization was 37\.54%, declining from 53\.09% in 2015 to 7\.14% in 2022\. RIG non\-administration was independently associated with male sex \(OR 1\.20, 95% CI 1\.11–1\.29\), age 18–59 years \(OR 3\.25, 95% CI 2\.95–3\.59\), exposure at home \(OR 1\.47, 95% CI 1\.33–1\.63\), presentation in 2018 \(OR 2\.38, 95% CI 2\.01–2\.82\) and in 2019–2024 \(OR 4\.49, 95% CI 3\.96–5\.09\), and delayed presentation \(OR 1\.43, 95% CI 1\.32–1\.55\)\. The overall delayed presentation rate was 34\.47%; independent risk factors included age 18–59 years \(OR 1\.37, 95% CI 1\.24–1\.52\), exposure at home \(OR 1\.57, 95% CI 1\.41–1\.74\), and presentation in 2018 \(OR 1\.61, 95% CI 1\.40–1\.86\) and in 2019–2024 \(OR 1\.77, 95% CI 1\.62–1\.94\)\. Conclusion RIG utilization was low and showed marked temporal variation during 2015–2024, and one third of patients presented more than 24 h after exposure\. Both outcomes were concentrated among working\-age adults and domestic exposure settings\. These findings describe service utilization patterns and identify subgroups in which gaps in PEP delivery are concentrated; targeted risk communication and routine monitoring of RIG utilization and presentation timeliness are needed\.
