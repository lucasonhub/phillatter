# Del eco algorítmico a la voz original: educación sensible y desfondamiento

**Autores:** Luis Manuel Martínez Domínguez

**Data:** 2026-10-15 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

The massive irruption of generative artificial intelligence \(GAI\) constitutes an ontological event that challenges contemporary education beyond mere technical efficiency or academic integrity\. This study justifies the critical urgency of addressing the structural &quot;groundlessness&quot; of the human person in the face of current algorithmic saturation and technological idolatry\. The main objective is to analyze, within the theoretical framework of Sensitive Education, the imminent risk of the &quot;synthetic echo&quot; supplanting the student&\#x27;s &quot;original voice,&quot; thereby atrophying their discursive agency and capacity for meaning\. A hermeneutic and phenomenological methodology is employed, articulating Leonardo Polo&\#x27;s transcendental anthropology, Hartmut Rosa&\#x27;s sociology of resonance, and the cultural criticism of authors such as Byung\-Chul Han and Éric Sadin\. The results reveal a deep existential fracture: AI imposes a &quot;perfect closure&quot; of data that collides with human &quot;radical openness,&quot; generating an alienation where discourse \(logos\) is traumatically dissociated from vital experience \(bios\)\. In conclusion, it is argued that true digital literacy requires a transition from the isolation of the digital &quot;false\-we&quot; towards a &quot;mature\-we&quot; capable of ethical resistance and real alterity\. Educational assessment must abandon the forensic approach to plagiarism to discern, through a &quot;resonant hearing,&quot; the vibratory authenticity of learning, thus guaranteeing the primacy of the spirit over automatism and restoring the classroom as an essential sanctuary of presence and listening\.
