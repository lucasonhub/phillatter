# THE USE OF ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE TOOLS IN ASSESSING STUDENTS&\#x27; SPEAKING PROFICIENCY

**Autores:** Zayniyeva Gulchaman

**Data:** 2026-10-11 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

The growing integration of artificial intelligence \(AI\) into language education has created new opportunities for assessing learners’ speaking proficiency\. AI\-powered speech recognition and automated speaking assessment tools can support the evaluation of pronunciation, fluency, grammatical accuracy, lexical use, and selected features of spoken interaction\. This thesis examines the potential educational benefits and limitations of using AI tools to assess students’ speaking proficiency, particularly in higher education\. It argues that such tools may provide timely feedback, increase opportunities for low\-stakes practice, and help teachers manage assessment workloads\. However, their results should be interpreted cautiously because recognition accuracy and scoring may vary according to accent, speech rate, recording quality, task type, and the characteristics of the learner\. The thesis therefore advocates a human\-in\-the\-loop approach in which AI\-generated feedback complements, rather than replaces, teacher judgement\. It also highlights the need for transparent criteria, data protection, accessibility, and empirical validation before AI\-based scores are used for high\-stakes decisions\.
