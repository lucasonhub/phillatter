# TRIM14 recruits BRCC3 to stabilize PD\-L1 by suppressing p62\-dependent selective autophagy in glioma

**Autores:** Yan Wu; Xujun Wang; Mengqiu Li; Rong Wang; Yaoxing Wu; Di Liu

**Data:** 2026-10-13 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Glioma remains largely refractory to immune\-checkpoint blockade, in part because tumor\-intrinsic mechanisms sustain programmed death\-ligand 1 \(PD\-L1\) and reinforce an immunosuppressive microenvironment\. Tripartite motif\-containing 14 \(TRIM14\) is an immune associated protein\. However, the role of TRIM14 in PD\-L1 homeostasis in glioma is incompletely defined\. Here we reported that TRIM14 was increased in malignancies and was particularly associated with overall survival in glioma\. Single\-nucleus analysis showed that TRIM14\-positive nuclei were predominantly malignant, indicating association between TRIM14 and glioma progression\. We also found TRIM14 upregulated PD\-L1 protein levels without materially changing CD274 mRNA in glioma cells\. Additionally, knockout of TRIM14 reduced tumor growth in vivo \. Mechanistically, TRIM14 recruits deubiquitinase BRCC3 to reduce K63\-linked ubiquitination of PD\-L1 in glioma cells, thereby inhibiting p62\-mediated autophagic degradation of PD\-L1\. This pathway prompts that TRIM14 or BRCC3 maybe candidate targets for therapeutic intervention in glioma\.
