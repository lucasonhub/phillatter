# The Ultimate Deduction of AI‑Driven Civilization Transition: The Collapse of the 200‑Year‑Old Industrial System, Deadlock of Interests and Risks of Historical Emergence\(V1\)

**Autores:** XiangKun Wang

**Data:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Ninety\-nine percent of modern human social forms are transitional byproducts of the 200\-year industrial era, not eternal configurations of civilization\. Education, law, working hours, professions, class structures, trade, finance, and currency all serve solely to facilitate industrial labor division; they can only buffer contradictions, not eradicate inequality at its root\. AI represents the first comprehensive cognitive substitution revolution in millennia—sufficient to dismantle the 200\-year industrial system and overturn the millennia\-old axioms of human labor value\. The velocity of AI iteration far outpaces the speed of institutional correction, causing the window for civilizational transition to shrink rapidly while urgency reaches critical levels\. The primary bottleneck in the transition between civilizations is not a technical barrier, but a structural deadlock of wealth and power, where the entrenched system lacks any intrinsic motivation for active reconstruction\. The two productivity leaps of the industrial age both achieved order reconstruction through world wars; today&\#x27;s underlying contradictions are a complete recurrence of historical patterns\. While structural contradictions exist with absolute certainty and continue to accumulate, history possesses inherent randomness and emergence, rendering the final outcome unpredictable\. No one desires turmoil, yet no one can arrest the natural release of historical laws and systemic potential energy\. Humanity is entering an era of ultimate AI transition: a total paradigm shift, an intractable deadlock of interests, and a future rendered completely unpredictable\.
