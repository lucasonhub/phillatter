# Beyond Loyalty: Gender, Timing, and Payment Preferences in Nigerian Consumer Decisions

**Autores:** O B Adewoyin; Taiwo David Adewoyin; Comfort Daramola; Olubunmi Faboya; Joshua Bature Hassan

**Data:** 2026-10-14 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Consumer behaviour modelling in African retail remains underexplored, with most predictive frameworks developed for Western or Asian markets\. This study addresses that gap by applying machine learning and probabilistic modelling to transactional data from Nigerian supermarkets\. The research investigates four behavioural dimensions: temporal decision triggers, customer loyalty segmentation, gender\-based preferences, and payment method influence\. Using Random Forest classifiers, regression analysis, and BG/NBD\-Gamma\-Gamma models, the study achieves high predictive accuracy in classifying customer types and estimating Customer Lifetime Value \(CLV\)\. Results reveal distinct purchase peaks at 7 PM and mid\-month, higher spending among loyalty programme members, gendered product and time\-of\-day preferences, and a surprising dominance of cash in high\-value transactions\. These findings demonstrate that consumer decisions are systematically patterned and contextually driven\. The study contributes a regionally grounded framework for behavioural analytics in emerging markets and offers actionable insights for retailers seeking to personalise engagement and optimise sales strategies\.
