# Mechanistic Understanding of Copper Electrodeposition and Additive Design Guidelines

**Autores:** Ralf Schmidt

**Data:** 2026-10-09 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Abstract Copper electrodeposition constitutes a pivotal process in semiconductor manufacturing and advanced packaging\. Further device miniaturization and increasing demands with regards to filling of recessed features and reliability require design of improved processes and profound understanding of the underlying mechanisms\. Models are available to describe deposit uniformities and filling of recessed features based on electrochemical properties of the organic additives\. In this perspective, a gap in literature was identified with regards to comprehension of functions of the additive on the molecular level\. A holistic approach to obtain understanding as foundation for derivation of additive design guidelines is proposed\.
