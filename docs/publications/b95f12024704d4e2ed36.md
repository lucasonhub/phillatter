# Digital Transformation and Corporate Green Mergers and Acquisitions: A Small\-Scale Panel Study of Chinese Listed Firms

**Autores:** Zongze Li

**Data:** 2026-10-13 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Digital transformation may help firms identify and integrate environmentally oriented acquisition targets\. This study examines its association with annual green acquisition counts in an unbalanced teaching panel of 250 Chinese listed firms, comprising 1,277 firm\-year observations from 2018 to 2023\. Ordinary least squares models progressively include seven financial controls and separate industry and year fixed effects, with standard errors clustered by firm\. Green acquisitions are infrequent: 92\.01% of observations record no acquisition\. In the full specification, the coefficient on the logged digital\-transformation index is 0\.291 \(standard error 0\.115; p = 0\.012\)\. A one\-standard\-deviation difference in the logged index corresponds to approximately 0\.077 additional acquisitions per firm\-year\. The association is statistically significant within the state\-owned subsample, whereas the non\-state\-owned estimate is imprecise; this difference in significance does not establish a statistically significant difference between ownership groups\. The findings provide descriptive evidence of a positive conditional association within the supplied sample\. They do not establish causality, verify proposed mechanisms, or demonstrate improvements in environmental or financial performance\. Extensions could assess count\-model specifications and formally test ownership differences\.
