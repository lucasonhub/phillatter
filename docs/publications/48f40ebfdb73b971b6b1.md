# Too Hot, Too Cold, or Just Right? The Effects of Temperature on Sleep

**Autores:** Jack R\. Gardner; Maria M\. Hadjimarkou

**Data:** 2026-10-12 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Sleep is necessary for optimal functioning and for overall health and wellbeing\. You might have heard advice on how to improve your sleep, such as reducing intense activities close to bedtime or keeping a regular sleep\-and\-wake schedule\. As many countries experience climate change, it is also important to be aware of how sleep may be affected when the temperature changes\. Sleep may be delayed or disturbed in a cold environment or a hot and humid one, and poor sleep can make it difficult to function well, especially if it happens often\. It is important to create a good sleep environment, keeping in mind that the temperature and humidity of the room, the air circulation, as well as the bedding and clothes worn to bed, play a role in a person’s ability to get the most out of the time spent in bed\.
