# Liang, Chuming: Always This One: The Boundaries and Recurrence of First\-Person Actuality

**Autores:** 

**Data de descoberta:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** unknown

## Descrição do feed

A census recording every public, personal, and experiential fact about the world&\#x27;s lives would record who was born when, who remembered what, and who died\. It would not record which of those lives is this one\. This paper asks what could draw that line\. The ordinary answer draws it twice: other lives are not this one because they belong to other persons; and this life is the only time this one is actual because it is a single occurrence that ends\. I argue that neither difference acquires boundary authority merely by being real\. For the first, a symmetry argument shows that if the granted structure does not distinguish among candidate lives whose complete content differs from the anchor&\#x27;s, either all such candidates fall within this\-one scope or none do; a line in between needs a relation that constitutes it\. For the second, I grant the strongest form of the first boundary—complete content fixes which occurrences are this one—and show that if actual succession continues past death, its later positions are realized by lives with genuine first\-person experience, and this one is not wholly replaced, the same complete content must receive a numerically new, later, non\-overlapping occurrence\. I call this first\-person recurrence\. The results are conditional, and the paper closes by mapping the main points at which the route can be left and what each exit commits one to\.
