# de Santiago, Dario Ricardo: Agency and Consciousness

**Autores:** 

**Data de descoberta:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** unknown

## Descrição do feed

Agency and Consciousness proposes a functional architecture of agency and consciousness\. It argues that both are emergent architectures that appear when sufficiently complex systems integrate resilient memory, limited perception, internal simulation, and self\-modeling\. Memory is not an archive of the past but the present physical state of a system shaped by prior interactions\. Perception does not capture reality in full but only the fraction accessible to the agent&\#x27;s architecture\. On that incomplete information, the system builds approximate simulations of its environment and of itself through what this work calls intelligent bias\. Consciousness appears when the system incorporates its own state within its internal simulation, and subjective experience is presented as a consequence of a structural asymmetry between internal modeling and external observation\. The work introduces a partition between architectural dominance and the zone of functional indifference: when the evaluation differential between trajectories exceeds the system&\#x27;s basal noise, the decision belongs to the architecture; when it does not, the resolution is contingent\. Agency resides in the strategy; the openness of the future, in the execution\. The position defended is relational: agency is not a property exclusive to the universe nor to the agent, but a property that emerges when a sufficiently complex architecture operates on a sufficiently stratified ontology\. The work builds on the three\-level architecture developed in Destiny and Will and engages with Metzinger&\#x27;s self\-model theory, Friston and Clark&\#x27;s predictive processing, and simulation theory in philosophy of mind\. It does not aim to resolve the strong phenomenological problem of subjective experience; its objective is more limited: to describe the minimal architectural conditions compatible with the existence of real agency and functional forms of consciousness\.
