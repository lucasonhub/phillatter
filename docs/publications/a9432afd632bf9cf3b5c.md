# XAVF GURUHIGA KIRUVCHI SHAXSLARNING PSIXOLOGIK PORTRETINI SHAKLLANTIRISH VA ULARNI YO‘LOVCHILAR OQIMIDA AJRATIB OLISH USULLARI

**Autores:** RASULBEK FARHOD O&\#x27;G&\#x27;LI EGAMBERGANOV

**Data:** 2026-10-12 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

The article explores the scientific, practical, and methodological foundations for the rapid identification of risk\-group individuals in high\-density passenger flows at aviation, railway, and border checkpoints using visual profiling methods\. Drawing upon the theoretical frameworks of P\. Ekman \(FACS\), J\. Navarro, and C\. Spielberger–A\. Hanin, a triad of non\-verbal markers reflecting cognitive\-emotional, vegetative\-somatic, and kinesthetic\-proxemic imbalances has been systematized\. A 4\-stage express diagnostic algorithm has been developed to eliminate subjective bias and ensure the protection of citizens&\#x27; rights and freedoms\.
