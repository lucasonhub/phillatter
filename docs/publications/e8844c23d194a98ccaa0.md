**Data:** 2026-10-15 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Architectural education is a field that uniquely targets the creativity and continuous development of architecture students\. This is especially true within environmental architecture, which relies on diverse spatial and temporal variables that create unique interactions for students, based on the specific concept of environmental design\.
