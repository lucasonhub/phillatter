# Association between TyG\-VAI and hypertension in children and adolescents based on machine learning algorithms

**Autores:** Jialiang Zhu; Chen Zhang; Qirui Zhang; Gang Tian; Jingge Zhao; Mengwei Wang; Zhanfeng Liu; Min Liu; Yibin Hao

**Data:** 2026-10-13 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Hypertension in children and adolescents is increasingly prevalent, and effective risk indicators are needed\. The triglyceride\-glucose index combined with visceral adiposity index \(TyG\-VAI\) has proved useful in adults, but evidence in the paediatric population remains limited\. In this cross\-sectional survey, 27,991 children and adolescents were enrolled in Henan Province, China, between 2023 and 2024 using multi\-stage sampling\. Multivariate logistic regression, subgroup and mediation analyses, and 11 machine learning models were used to examine the association between TyG\-VAI and hypertension and to assess its predictive value\. TyG\-VAI was positively associated with hypertension \(odds ratio \[OR\] = 2\.36 for the highest quartile, P &lt; 0\.001\), with greater sensitivity in males\. Waist\-to\-height ratio was the main mediator \(62\.2%\), followed by uric acid and the creatinine index\. The multi\-layer perceptron model incorporating TyG\-VAI showed the best predictive performance \(area under the curve \[AUC\] = 0\.7518\)\. TyG\-VAI was independently associated with hypertension in children and adolescents and improved risk prediction, providing a basis for early intervention in this population\.
