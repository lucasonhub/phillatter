# AI in Business Intelligence and Entrepreneurship

**Autores:** Hakikur Rahman

**Data:** 2026-10-10 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Artificial Intelligence \(AI\) has emerged as a transformative force in modern business environments, fundamentally reshaping how organizations collect information, generate knowledge, make decisions, and create value\. The convergence of AI and Business Intelligence \(BI\) enables enterprises to move beyond descriptive reporting toward predictive and prescriptive decision\-making frameworks\. Simultaneously, entrepreneurs and startups increasingly employ AI\-powered analytics, intelligent automation, and machine learning to identify market opportunities, optimize operations, and develop innovative products and services\. This article examines the evolving relationship between AI, Business Intelligence, and entrepreneurship, highlighting how organizations leverage data\-driven intelligence for sustainable competitive advantage and organizational innovation\. Drawing upon contemporary research and practical examples, the article explores technological foundations, leadership implications, entrepreneurial opportunities, global and local applications, and future challenges\. The discussion emphasizes that successful adoption of AI requires not only technical capabilities but also strategic leadership, ethical governance, and a culture of continuous innovation\.
