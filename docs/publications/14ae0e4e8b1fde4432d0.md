# From Emotion to Expression: The AL–DL–CL Framework for Understanding Communication in the Age of AI and Digital Media

**Autores:** Chacko P\. George

**Data:** 2026-10-17 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Rapid progress in digital media and artificial intelligence is transforming human communication, facilitating the rapid exchange of messages and minimizing the chances of reflective interpretation\. Existing theories in linguistics, psychology, and cultural studies address the structural, cognitive, or social aspects of language, yet fail to explain how emotional processing, internal linguistic structuring, and culturally mediated communication function as a coherent, dynamic system\. Through this model, the authors present the AL–DL–CL framework that explores how three layers are engaged in a process that results in communication, through a recursive interaction between the Automatic Layer \(AL\), the Deliberate Layer \(DL\), and the Cultural Language Layer \(CL\)\. In such a process, the affective or unconscious processes in AL are consistently reframed into structured internal representations via DL, and through CL, and translated back into culturally\-expressed language serves as a feedback mechanism, with a tendency to modify cognition and emotional response\. Based on a multi\-method exploratory approach of sequential cultural transmission, clinical case study analysis, and group\-based validation, the results indicate that language is not so much a static container for meaning as a process of reconstruction that is molded by the dynamic interaction between emotion, cognition, and culture\. This model also demonstrates how digitally mediated communication can amplify AL\-directed reaction and limit DL\-level thinking, which increases the potential for misinterpretation, polarization, or misinformation\. The AL–DL–CL framework provides guidance in a unified theoretical perspective around language emergence, responsible AI design and digital literacy and intercultural discourse based on the continuous transformation system of communication, and not on the linear encoding\.
