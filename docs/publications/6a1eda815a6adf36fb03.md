# The aesthetics of applying 3D printing in the design and manufacture of sustainable bags for women

**Autores:** Rania Shawky Ghazy; Afnan Abdallah Al\-Amri

**Data:** 2026-10-09 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Introduction Three\-dimensional \(3D\) printing has emerged as a promising digital manufacturing technology for fashion and accessories, providing opportunities for customization, design flexibility, and innovative production\. Integrating digital fabrication technologies with Saudi cultural heritage may contribute to the development of contemporary fashion products that retain distinctive cultural characteristics\. This study investigated the application of 3D printing in developing women’s handbags inspired by Saudi cultural heritage\. Methods The study aimed to develop contemporary handbag designs integrating Sadu\-inspired textile elements with 3D\-printed components and to evaluate their aesthetic quality, perceived economic potential, and functional performance\. Fourteen proposed handbag designs were evaluated using a structured questionnaire assessing aesthetic quality and perceived economic potential\. Twenty\-two specialists participated in validating the research instrument, while seven specialist evaluations per design were used for the design\-level analyses\. One\-way analysis of variance \(ANOVA\) was conducted to examine differences among the proposed designs\. Results The ANOVA results indicated statistically significant differences among the 14 designs in aesthetic quality, F \(13, 84\) = 36\.082, p &lt; 0\.001, perceived economic potential, F \(13, 84\) = 19\.680, p &lt; 0\.001, and overall evaluation, F \(13, 182\) = 45\.816, p &lt; 0\.001\. Design 11 achieved the highest overall quality coefficient \(92\.08%\), followed by Design 6 \(90\.65%\); both designs were fabricated using 3D printing\. Functional\-performance evaluation yielded quality coefficients of 88\.03% for Design 11 and 85\.30% for Design 6\. The difference between the two designs was not statistically significant, F \(1, 10\) = 2\.149, p = 0\.173\. Discussion The findings suggest that 3D printing can facilitate the development of contemporary women’s handbags that integrate aesthetic and functional requirements with elements of Saudi cultural heritage\. The results also demonstrate the potential of combining digitally fabricated components with Sadu\-inspired textile elements to create fashion products that balance cultural identity with contemporary design and production approaches\.
