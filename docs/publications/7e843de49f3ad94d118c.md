# An ARG1\-associated immunometabolic program in bone marrow PMN\-MDSC\-like cells in multiple sclerosis and experimental autoimmune encephalomyelitis

**Autores:** Hengchang Xie; Yiwei Fu; Yingchun Bi; Ranran Han; Pengfei Liu; 何柏林; Baoting Chen; Honglei Ren

**Data:** 2026-10-14 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Background Multiple sclerosis \(MS\) is a chronic inflammatory demyelinating disease of the central nervous system\. Recent work indicates that bone\-marrow hematopoietic niches sense CNS autoimmunity and enhance myeloid output\. However, whether MS bone marrow contains an immunometabolic PMN\-MDSC\-like state, and whether related ARG1\-associated observations can be examined in a cross\-species EAE context, remains unclear\. Methods We reanalyzed single\-cell RNA sequencing data from treatment\-naive patients with MS and healthy controls, focusing on myeloid states\. We used donor\-aware proportion analysis, trajectory inference, module scoring, donor\-pseudobulk pathway enrichment, expression\-based transcription\-factor prioritization, co\-expression analysis, ligand\-target inference, and ARG1 virtual perturbation\. We also quantified flow\-gated bone marrow PMN\-MDSCs and ARG1 protein in MOG35\-55\-induced EAE and tested systemic prophylactic oral nor\-NOHA\. Results MS bone marrow showed myeloid skewing and a heterogeneous PMN\-MDSC\-like transcriptional state, while donor\-level subtype proportions were comparable between groups\. Neutrophil degranulation showed the strongest BH\-supported donor\-pseudobulk enrichment, and expression\-based regulatory and communication analyses converged on an ARG1\-associated network\. In EAE, flow\-gated bone\-marrow PMN\-MDSCs and ARG1 protein were increased, and systemic prophylactic nor\-NOHA was associated with lower clinical scores and reduced spinal\-cord pathology\. Conclusions These data identify an ARG1\-associated immunometabolic program in bone\-marrow PMN\-MDSC\-like cells and connect the human transcriptional landscape with ARG1 protein expression and pharmacologic responses in EAE\. Together, the findings establish ARG1\-associated immunometabolism as a focused pathway for mechanistic and translational investigation\.
