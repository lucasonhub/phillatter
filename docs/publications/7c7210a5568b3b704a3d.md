# A domain knowledge graph\-driven joint recommendation system for wiring mode and automation configuration in differentiated power supply zones

**Autores:** Min Li; Junhua Gong; Jing Tan; Feipeng Huang; Xiaohong Tan

**Data:** 2026-10-12 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

INTRODUCTION: Distribution network planning requires jointly determining wiring modes and automation configurations, yet existing methods treat them independently without regulatory constraints\.OBJECTIVES: To develop a knowledge graph\-driven framework for joint wiring mode and automation configuration recommendation across differentiated supply zones\.METHODS: A BERT\-CRF pipeline constructs a knowledge graph from regulatory texts\. TransR\-initialized graph attention networks and CRF decoders with SWRL constraints are coupled via multi\-task learning, evaluated on 1,443 records and 12 field tasks\.RESULTS: Acc@3 of 0\.817, F1 of 0\.757, and CCR of 0\.733, surpassing the best baseline by 4\.3% and 10\.4% respectively\. Expert validation scores 4\.50/5\.00 with 73\.3% planning time reduction\.CONCLUSION: Integrating knowledge graph embedding, GNN aggregation, and constraint\-aware joint optimization yields accurate, compliant, and traceable distribution planning recommendations\.
