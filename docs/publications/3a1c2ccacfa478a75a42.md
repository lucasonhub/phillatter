# Development of Google Sites\-based interactive Arabic teaching materials for four language skills in senior high school

**Autores:** Achmad Affandy; Muhammad Nabil Rifa&\#x27;i; Nuroh Nuroh; Zibran Solehudin

**Data:** 2026-10-12 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Background\-Tenth\-grade students at Riyadhussholihiin Senior High School reported difficulties understanding existing Arabic textbooks due to limited explanations, exercises, multimedia support, and independent learning opportunities\. A needs analysis involving 29 students showed that 80% strongly needed more accessible and engaging digital materials\. Objectives\-This study aimed to \(1\) develop Google Sites\-based interactive Arabic teaching materials; \(2\) determine their feasibility; and \(3\) examine students’ reading, listening, writing, and speaking outcomes\. Methods\-This Research and Development study employed th ADDIE model\. Data were collected through observation, interviews, needs\-analysis questionnaires, expert\-validation sheets, and pretest\-posttest assessments\. Results\-The resulting Google Sites materials integrated texts, pronunciation audio, instructional videos, images, interactive exercises, and quizzes\. The material\-validation score was 87\.98% \(44/50\), whereas the media\-validation score was 82\.86% \(29/35\)\. Students provided positive responses to the materials, with an overall response score of 87\.98%\. The reported mean total score increased from 59\.30 before implementation to 73\.99 after implementation, an increase of 24\.77%; listening showed the largest percentage increase \(29\.45%\)\. Conclusions\-The materials were feasible for use and were associated with improved Arabic language learning\-outcome scores in the participating class\. The conclusion is limited to the study cohort and should not be interpreted as a separate claim about student motivation\.
