# ТРАДИЦИОННЫЕ МЕТОДЫ И МЕТОДЫ, ОСНОВАННЫЕ НА ИСКУССТВЕННОМ ИНТЕЛЛЕКТЕ, В ОБУЧЕНИИ РЕШЕНИЮ ТЕКСТОВЫХ ЗАДАЧ ПО ШКОЛЬНОЙ МАТЕМАТИКЕ: СРАВНИТЕЛЬНОЕ ИССЛЕДОВАНИЕ С ТОЧКИ ЗРЕНИЯ РАЗВИТИЯ НАВЫКОВ РЕШЕНИЯ ЗАДАЧ У УЧАЩИХСЯ

**Autores:** Салтанат Бахитжановна Ерназарова; Диёра Аюбханқызы Талипова

## Resumo

Digitalization of education creates new opportunities for mathematics teaching\. Solving word problems requires not only mathematical knowledge but also analytical skills, logical reasoning, and the ability to choose an appropriate solution strategy\. Therefore, comparing traditional teaching methods with artificial intelligence\-based approaches is becoming increasingly relevant\. This article examines traditional and artificial intelligence\-based approaches to teaching word problems in school mathematics\. Their potential for developing students problem\-solving skills is compared\. Particular attention is given to understanding the problem statement, constructing a mathematical model, and selecting a solution strategy\. The development of digital tools in mathematics education is also considered\. The study uses scientific literature analysis, comparative analysis and theoretical generalization\. The main characteristics of traditional and artificial intelligence\-based approaches are identified\. Traditional methods provide systematic development of mathematical knowledge and allow teachers to monitor the learning process\. Artificial intelligence can provide immediate feedback and adapt support to individual student needs\. However, the use of ready\-made solutions may reduce students independent thinking \. Therefore, artificial intelligence should be used as an additional learning tool rather than as a replacement for the teacher or students independent work\.
