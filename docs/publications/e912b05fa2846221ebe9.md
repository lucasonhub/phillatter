## Resumo

Since their introduction in the 1970s, breast implants have become a cornerstone of both aesthetic and reconstructive plastic surgery\. Breast augmentation and reconstruction are among the most commonly performed procedures worldwide and have been associated with improved patient satisfaction, self\-image, and quality of life\.
