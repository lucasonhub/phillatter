# In Situ Ion\-Exchange\-Mediated Dynamic Nanointerface Reconfiguration for Polarity\-Switchable Photoelectrochemical–Photothermal Biosensing

**Autores:** Hao Cheng; Zhen Yang; Dan Yang; Chuanping Li; Ren Cai; Weihong Tan

**Data:** 2026-10-12 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Abstract Conventional photoelectrochemical \(PEC\) systems typically rely on static semiconductor interfaces that merely translate biomolecular recognition into amplitude modulation of the pre\-existing photocurrent, thus limiting their analytical robustness and self\-validation capabilities\. Herein, we report in situ ion\-exchange\-mediated dynamic nanointerface engineering to fabricate an analyte\-programmed reconfigurable PEC–photothermal \(PT\) dual\-mode biosensor for the carcinoembryonic antigen \(CEA\) assay\. The Z\-scheme heterostructure of a plasmonic Au nanoparticle \(NP\) layer\-supported BiOI/AgI/indium tin oxide glass \(BiOI/AgI/Au NPs/ITO\) is constructed as the initial photoelectrode\. In the sensing process, a CEA recognition event directly reshapes the phase composition of BiOI/AgI, charge\-transfer behavior, and photoresponse polarity\. The synergistic integration of the BiOI/AgI heterostructure and the close\-packed layer of plasmonic Au NPs substantially enhance the light absorption, charge separation, and interfacial charge transfer, making BiOI/AgI/Au NPs/ITO a highly suitable photoelectrode for the PEC mode of the PEC–PT biosensor\. This analyte\-driven phase evolution reconstructs the semiconductor composition, band alignment, and charge\-transfer pathways to switch the photocurrent polarity from cathodic to anodic\. In addition, a photothermal imaging mode of the PEC–PT biosensor is triggered by regulating the Bi2S3 formation for intrinsic cross\-validation\. By integrating an artificial neural network \(ANN\)\-assisted analysis, PEC–PT biosensors enable robust multimodal quantification and improved analytical reliability, achieving &gt; 99\.8% prediction accuracy in real\-sample analysis\. This is the first example of integrating a dynamic nanointerface\-driven PEC–PT dual\-mode biosensor with artificial intelligence \(AI\)\-enhanced multimodal analysis for CEA detection and provides a dynamic nanointerface\-driven strategy for intelligent multimode biosensing\.
