# Multi\-swarm Improved Sparrow Search Optimized Variational Encoder with Kolmogorov\-Arnold Networks and Bidirectional Multiscale Dilated Convolutional Recurrent Architecture for Maize Leaf Disease Detection and Classification

**Autores:** Gaurav Singla; Vijay Laxmi; Sanjay Kumar

**Data:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Field\-deployable diagnosis of maize leaf disease is limited by shallow representations that do not separate visually similar lesions and by fixed activations that constrain decision boundaries\.This work proposes MS\-ISSA\-VAE\-KAN\-PMDC\-BGRU, one end\-to\-end pipeline: a variational encoder maps each leaf image to a 16 × 16 grid of 64\-dimensional latent codes whose Kullback\-Leibler term acts as an information bottleneck; a Parallel Multiscale Dilated Convolution block with dilation rates one, two and four samples that grid at three rates; a bidirectional gated recurrent unit encodes dependencies across the patch sequence; and a Kolmogorov\-Arnold Network with learnable Bspline edge activations replaces the multilayer\-perceptron head\.Five hyperparameters are tuned by the proposed Multi\-Swarm Improved Sparrow Search Algorithm\.All experiments use 20 independent runs under two protocols kept separate: a leakage\-controlled protocol \(perceptual\-hash de\-duplication with group\-aware splits\), primary here, and the conventional random split, retained only for comparability with published work\.Under the primary protocol the model attains 97\.86 ± 0\.29% on the PlantVillage maize subset and 96\.36 ± 0\.32% on the 4,188\-image Corn or Maize Leaf Disease dataset \(MDID\); the random\-split values are 98\.62 ± 0\.24% and 96\.98 ± 0\.27%\.Every baseline is reimplemented under one protocol and tuned by MS\-ISSA at an identical budget, four architectures additionally under random search and Bayesian TPE\.The leakage\-controlled margin over the strongest recent baseline is 0\.55 and 0\.70 points at 8\.4 M parameters against 86\.4 M\. The advantage is efficiency and stability rather than peak accuracy: the smallest loss when near\-duplicates are removed \(\-0\.76 / \-0\.62 against \-0\.88 to \-1\.33 points\) and the smallest crossdataset drop \(4\.31\-7\.52 against 6\.50\-13\.90points under the random\-split in\-domain reference; 3\.69\-6\.76against 5\.61\-12\.57under the primary leakage\-controlled reference\)\.Code, per\-seed results and the full baseline configuration are released\.
