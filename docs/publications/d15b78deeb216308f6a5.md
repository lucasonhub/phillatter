# Robust Covariance Estimation for Entry\-wise Contaminated and Partially Observed Data via Expectation Maximization Algorithm

**Autores:** Matys Savidan; Mohammed Nabil El Korso

**Data:** 2026-10-15 · **Tipo:** preprint

## Resumo

Robust covariance estimation under entry\-wise contamination and missing data is challenging in various signal processing applications\. Under the Student\-$t$ model, observation\-level reweighting induces a uniform attenuation across samples, which can lead to cumulative information loss even when most coordinates remain informative\. The alternative Student\-$t^\*$ model enables coordinate\-wise robustness, but its E\-step is not available in closed form\. We extend inference for the Student\-$t^\*$ model to incomplete observations through a stochastic approximation EM \(SAEM\) algorithm operating on a reduced latent space: stochastic updates of the coordinate\-wise scales are combined with Rao\-Blackwellized Gaussian conditional moments, which avoids explicit sampling of latent Gaussian variables while preserving a likelihood\-based framework\. Experiments on synthetic and real sensor data show a lower Fisher\-Rao error than Gaussian, Student\-$t$, imputation\-based and detect\-then\-estimate estimators at moderate to high outlier rates under diffuse contamination\.
