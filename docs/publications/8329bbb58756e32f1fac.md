# Digital Procurement Capabilities and Supply Chain Resilience: Building Competitive Advantage in the Era of Digital Transformation

**Autores:** Michael Georgiou

**Data:** 2026-10-09 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Digital transformation has repositioned procurement from a transaction\-processing function to a strategic source of information, coordination, risk intelligence and organisational adaptability\. Yet investments in electronic procurement platforms, artificial intelligence, cloud services, blockchain, supplier portals and advanced analytics do not automatically create resilient supply chains or durable competitive advantage\. This paper examines how digital procurement capabilities strengthen supply chain resilience and how resilience, in turn, helps firms translate digital investments into competitive advantage\. An integrative review of peer\-reviewed procurement, information\-systems and supply\-chain literature is organised around dynamic capabilities theory, the resource\-based view and organisational information\-processing theory\.
