# A Multi\-Parametric Risk Stratification Model for Early\-Pregnancy Prediction of Preeclampsia: Integrating First\- and Second\-Trimester Biomarkers with Machine Learning

**Autores:** Uğurcan Zorlu; Mert İshak Kaya; Hakkı Şerbetçi; Nevda Ceren Sonu; Ayşe Altındiş Bal; Fatma Doğa Öcal; Dilek Şahın

**Data:** 2026-10-17 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Preeclampsia remains a major contributor to maternal and perinatal morbidity\. This study aimed to develop and internally evaluate an interpretable machine\-learning framework integrating routinely available first\- and second\-trimester parameters for preclinical risk stratification\. This retrospective case\-control study included 524 singleton pregnancies \(262 with preeclampsia and 262 normotensive controls\)\. Seven prespecified predictors—12\-week mean arterial pressure \(MAP\), pregnancy\-associated plasma protein A \(PAPP\-A\), alpha\-fetoprotein \(AFP\), unconjugated estriol, eosinophil\-to\-lymphocyte ratio \(ELR\), neutrophil\-to\-lymphocyte ratio, and lymphocyte count—were evaluated using logistic regression, random forest, Gaussian naive Bayes, support vector machine, and k\-nearest neighbors\. Internal validation used stratified 10\-fold cross\-validation with identical fold assignments across classifiers\. A five\-variable weighted clinical risk score was also constructed\. MAP showed the highest univariable discrimination \(area under the receiver operating characteristic curve \[AUC\] = 0\.891\)\. In cross\-validation, Gaussian naive Bayes achieved the numerically highest mean AUC \(0\.948 ± 0\.024\), accuracy \(0\.885 ± 0\.031\), recall \(0\.893 ± 0\.044\), and F1 score \(0\.886 ± 0\.032\), while random forest had the highest mean precision \(0\.891 ± 0\.042\)\. The weighted clinical risk score achieved an AUC of 0\.885 and 81\.9% accuracy\. The framework showed strong internal discrimination but remains exploratory\. Because the score incorporates approximately 16\-week AFP and estriol measurements, it should not be interpreted as a purely first\-trimester aspirin\-selection model\. Prospective external validation, calibration, and subtype\-specific assessment are required before clinical use\.
