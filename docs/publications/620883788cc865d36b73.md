# Intellectual Property in Digital Age

**Autores:** Jayashree Raman

**Data:** 2026-10-17 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

The rapid growth of digital technology has transformed the way ideas, information, and creative works are created, shared, and accessed\. Intellectual Property \(IP\) plays a crucial role in protecting the rights of creators, authors, artists, inventors, and businesses in the digital world\. However, the internet has also increased challenges such as online piracy, copyright infringement, plagiarism, and the unauthorised use of digital content\. Social media platforms, artificial intelligence, and digital marketplaces have further complicated the protection and enforcement of intellectual property rights\. This article explores the importance of intellectual property in the digital age and examines the legal challenges associated with protecting original creations online\. It also highlights the role of intellectual property laws in encouraging innovation, creativity, and technological development\. Understanding these rights is essential to maintaining a balance between protecting creators and ensuring public access to knowledge\. Ultimately, effective legal frameworks and responsible digital practices are necessary to safeguard intellectual property in an increasingly interconnected world\.
