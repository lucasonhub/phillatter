# Yadav, Prashant: Consciousness: A Structural Resolution of the Hard Problem through Recursive Attribution and Embodied Coherence

**Autores:** 

**Data de descoberta:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** unknown

## Descrição do feed

The so\-called &quot;hard problem&quot; of consciousness has persisted for decades, largely due to category errors and ontological assumptions rooted in language rather than structure\. This paper introduces a structural theory of consciousness grounded in recursive attribution, coherence\-seeking, and embodied resolution\. We argue that subjective experience is not an inexplicable metaphysical surplus but a structurally emergent outcome of systems resolving internal perturbations under bounded constraints\. Drawing upon principles of recursive coherence, metacognitive arbitration, and semantic containment, we dissolve the epistemic opacity traditionally associated with qualia, selfhood, and phenomenal binding\. We propose that identity and conscious experience are not primitives but results of recursive convergence, governed by structural coherence over time\. This theory not only resolves the hard problem but also offers a falsifiable framework with implications across cognitive science, AI, and philosophy of mind\.
