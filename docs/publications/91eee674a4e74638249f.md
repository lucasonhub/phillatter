# Potential in vitro toxicity of fusel oil components

**Autores:** Junwei Zhao; Zezhi Li; Xiaoxia Xia; Xiang Li

**Data:** 2026-10-12 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Fusel oil is a by\-product during the fermentation of alcoholic beverage, mainly including n\-propanol, isobutanol, isopentanol, and others\. Long\-term or excessive intake of them may cause potential harm to human health\. In the present work, we preliminarily evaluated the cytotoxicity of n\-propanol and isobutanol, main components of fusel oil, in human fetal astrocytes \(SVGp12\), human microvascular endothelial cells \(HMEC\-1\), and human microglial cells \(HMC3\)\. The results showed that both n\-propanol and isobutanol exhibited cytotoxicity in all three types of cells, and the release of proinflammatory cytokines \(IL\-6 and IL\-8\) significantly increased in the cells exposed to fusel oil components\. The transepithelial electrical resistance \(TEER\) values were decreased and the permeability was increased in HMEC\-1 cells exposed to fusel oil components\. These results indicate that fusel oil components may cause potential harm to human health\.
