# THE ROLE OF ENGLISH IN MODERN EDUCATION

**Autores:** Soliyeva Zarina Botirovna; Nematova Mohinur Alisherovna

**Data:** 2026-10-15 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

This article highlights the relevance and importance of learning foreign languages in the modern world and education system\. In the process of globalization, international cooperation, the development of science and technology are making knowledge of foreign languages an important competence\. The article analyzes the role of learning foreign languages in personal development, academic success and intercultural communication\. It also considers issues of increasing the effectiveness of language learning through the use of innovative methods and information technologies in the educational process\.
