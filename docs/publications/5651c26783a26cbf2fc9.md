# Biodegradable electronic materials and devices

**Autores:** Gaurav Khandelwal; Shalu Rani; Venkateswaran Vivekananthan; Shashank Mishra; Kaliyannan Manojkumar; Daniel M\. Mulvihill; Themis Prodromakis; Ajay Giri Prakash Kottapalli

**Data:** 2026-10-10 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

The growing demand of electronic devices and their expeditious development in the past decade have generated a scientific challenge in terms of tackling e\-waste\. The growing e\-waste shifts the focus of the scientific community towards the development of biodegradable devices which can disintegrate and eventually mineralise in the natural environment\. There are numerous research papers published in the field of biodegradable devices, but so far there is no clear and comprehensive information on biodegradable electronic materials and devices\. Moreover, the titles of many research publications are misleading and create confusion for non\-experts or early\-stage researchers by using the term “biodegradable” even when a device is composed of one or more non\-biodegradable components\. This review paper aims at providing a well\-structured and accurate view on biodegradable electronic materials and devices\. A brief discussion on biodegradable insulating polymers and their degradation mechanism is discussed alongside biodegradable metals and conducting polymers\. Various biodegradable devices including mechanical energy harvesters \(triboelectric and piezoelectric nanogenerators\), energy storage \(supercapacitors and batteries\), sensors \(colorimetric, electrochemical and physical\), actuators and printed circuit boards are discussed in detail\. The review also discusses artificial intelligence \(AI\) as a transformative tool to accelerate research in the field of biodegradable electronic materials and devices\. Finally, the key challenges and future perspectives are discussed with some reflections on the use of term “biodegradable” and “biodegradable\-based” devices\.
