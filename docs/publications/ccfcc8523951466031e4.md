# MQDA\-Net: A Quantum\-enhanced Dual\-branch Framework for Unified Brain Tumor Segmentation, Classification and Structured Radiology Report Generation

**Autores:** subia salma; Sanjeev C\. Lingareddy; S\. Shilpashree; Vineet Kumar

**Data:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Automated brain tumor analysis from multi\-parametric MRI is typically split into three separately solved problems: volumetric segmentation, tumor\-type classification and radiology report generation\.The few unified systems chain these stages rather than optimizing them jointly, and inherit the weaknesses of every link: slice\-level segmenters that discard volumetric context, classification heads carrying tens of millions of parameters that overfit small neuro\-oncology cohorts, static knowledge graphs that cannot reason from image evidence, and report generators whose output barely overlaps clinician text\.We propose MQDA\-Net, which integrates all three tasks in one architecture, each pathway optimized on the cohort carrying its annotations and coupled to the others through shared representations and explicit conditioning\.A three\-dimensional encoder with block\-level Multi\-Scale Atrous Spatial Pyramid Pooling \(MSASPP\) widens the bottleneck receptive field\.A Dual\-Branch Discriminative Decoder couples a coarse binary localization branch to a fine\-grained semantic branch through cross\-attention, injecting an explicit localization prior into sub\-region assignment\.A twelve\-qubit variational classification head \(parameterized U3 rotations, controlled\-Z entanglement and Pauli\-X readout\) replaces the conventional stacked classifier, reducing classifier parameters from 67\.2 M to 7\.3 M\. A dynamic image\-grounded knowledge graph, built per case from segmentation masks and MNI atlas co\-registration and refined by a Graph Attention Network, conditions a Q\-Formerprojected LoRA\-adapted Llama 3\.2 11B Vision\-Instruct backbone that emits structured reports carrying volumetric measurements, anatomical localization and BT\-RADS risk stratification\.On BraTS 2023, MQDA\-Net reaches mean Dice 0\.91 and IoU 0\.84; on the harder BraTS 2024 post\-treatment cohort the framework reaches 79\.85 % Dice, exceeding a self\-supervised specialist baseline by 4\.61 points\.Classification accuracy reaches 99\.72 % on the Nickparvar composite benchmark, and report BLEU improves from 0\.068 to 0\.170 with ROUGE\-L F1 rising from 0\.0018 to 0\.068\.Differences against published values for the closest prior unified framework are reported descriptively; no matched statistical comparison is claimed\.
