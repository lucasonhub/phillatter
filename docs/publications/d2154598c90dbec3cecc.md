# A Stylistic Analysis of Responsibility and Moral Injury in U\.S\. Veterans’ Public Testimony on the 2003 Iraq War

**Autores:** \. Hajir Mahmoud Ibrahim Alallo; Azhar Hassan Sallomi; Muyyad Omran Chiad Muyyad Omran Chiad

**Data:** 2026-10-15 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Former soldiers suffer from moral injuries far harder than any physical injury they might have experienced during war\. Some of them may speak publicly about violence they committed or witnessed while in service, and they must find words to describe those acts which conflict with their own moral standards\. Such experience has been described as moral injury, the lasting distress that follows actions, or failures to act, which violate deeply held beliefs\. The present paper investigates the stylistic representation of trauma in veterans’ narratives about the U\.S\. 2003 Iraq War\.
