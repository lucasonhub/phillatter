# Medication\-related knowledge and behavior profiles are generally favorable, but counseling needs remain among pregnant and postpartum women in Taiyuan, China: a cross\-sectional study

**Autores:** Chaolong Zhang; 何荧居; Yuanyuan Yang; Li Zhang; Wenfei Zhang; Liuye Yang; Mengyao Ma

**Data:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Objective To characterize medication\-related Knowledge\-Attitude\-Practice \(KAP\) risk profiles, pregnancy\- and lactation\-specific medication familiarity, and demographic correlates among pregnant and postpartum women in Taiyuan, China\. Methods We conducted a hospital\-based cross\-sectional survey at Taiyuan Taihang Hospital from July to October 2024 using convenience sampling and an adapted medication\-related KAP questionnaire\. Higher knowledge\-, attitude\-, and behavior\-risk scores indicated less favorable profiles, whereas higher familiarity scores indicated greater self\-rated familiarity\. Internal consistency was assessed using Cronbach’s α\. Associations were examined using Pearson correlations, Welch’s analysis of variance with Games\-Howell post hoc tests, and multivariable ordinary least\-squares regression with HC3 robust standard errors\. Results Of 500 returned questionnaires, 497 were analyzed\. Cronbach’s α ranged from 0\.808 to 0\.957\. Mean knowledge\-risk and behavior\-risk scores were 59\.78 ± 18\.99 and 57\.22 ± 11\.29, respectively, both within the prespecified good category; the mean attitude\-risk score was 37\.34 ± 7\.94, within the pass category\. For attitude risk, 59\.0% were classified as pass, 16\.1% as fail, and 24\.9% as good or excellent\. Knowledge risk was inversely correlated with attitude risk \( r = −0\.258\) and positively correlated with behavior risk \( r = 0\.275\), while attitude risk was weakly positively correlated with behavior risk \( r = 0\.118; all p ≤ 0\.009\)\. In the adjusted behavior\-risk model, higher scores were observed among participants aged 35–49 years and those with other insurance or self\-payment\. The overall adjusted association between monthly income and behavior risk was not statistically significant \(Wald p = 0\.098\); an isolated contrast between the 4,000–6,000\-yuan and below\-2,000\-yuan groups reached nominal statistical significance and was treated as exploratory\. Familiarity scores across five pregnancy\- and lactation\-specific topics ranged from 2\.46 to 2\.60 on a five\-point scale\. Conclusion Favorable knowledge\- and behavior\-risk profiles coexisted with less favorable attitude\-risk classifications and limited topic\-specific familiarity\. Broad medication\-safety counseling should be integrated into antenatal and postpartum care, with proactive medication review considered for women aged 35–49 years, those with other insurance or self\-payment, and those with complex medication needs\. The income finding should be regarded as exploratory\. Multicenter prospective studies are needed to assess generalizability and evaluate this service approach\.
