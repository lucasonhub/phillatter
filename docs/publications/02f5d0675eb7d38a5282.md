# SCIENCE AND EDUCATION IN PROGRESS: CONTEMPORARY TRENDS, INNOVATIVE APPROACHES AND FUTURE PROSPECTS

**Autores:** U\. T\. Djalilova

**Data:** 2026-10-14 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

This article examines the role of science and education in the development of modern society, their close interrelationship, and their prospects for future development\. Particular attention is paid to the integration of digital technologies and artificial intelligence into the educational process, improving the effectiveness of scientific research, fostering innovative thinking, and unlocking the scientific potential of young people\. Based on the findings, recommendations are developed to improve the quality of education, support scientific activity, and create an environment conducive to innovation\.
