# Sarkar, Abhijeet: Dharmic Alignment: Toward a Normative Framework for Human\-Centered Artificial Intelligence

**Autores:** 

**Data de descoberta:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** unknown

## Descrição do feed

Artificial intelligence raises a fundamental philosophical question: what normative principles should guide the development and conduct of increasingly capable artificial systems? Existing debates on AI alignment address the relationship between machine behaviour and human intentions, values, or preferences, but these approaches also raise questions concerning moral pluralism, human dignity, responsibility, and the conditions of human flourishing\. This paper proposes Dharmic Alignment as a normative framework for human\-centered artificial intelligence, drawing critically on the philosophical resources associated with dharma while distinguishing their historical and cultural origins from principles intended for cross\-cultural application\. The framework examines human dignity, non\-harm, truthfulness, human agency, justice, compassion, accountability, transparency, restraint, and ecological responsibility as candidate principles for evaluating artificial intelligence\. It argues that alignment should be understood not merely as behavioural compliance or preference satisfaction, but also as a question of justified normative constraints, responsible agency, and the consequences of intelligent systems for human and ecological well\-being\. The paper further considers the potential application of this framework to language models, autonomous systems, and embodied intelligence, including the MANUSH Superintelligence initiative associated with Synaptic AI Lab Private Limited, founded by Abhijeet Sarkar\. It concludes by identifying conceptual challenges and questions for future philosophical analysis, technical implementation, and empirical evaluation\. \(direct link \)
