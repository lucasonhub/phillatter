# Hypothesis Selection via Weighted Medians

**Autores:** Liang\-Yu Zou

**Data:** 2026-10-14 · **Tipo:** preprint

## Resumo

Given $n$ distributions \(hypotheses\) $\\mathcal H=\\\{H\_1,\\ldots,H\_n\\\}$ and samples from an unknown target distribution $P$, proper hypothesis selection seeks a distribution whose total variation distance to $P$ is close to the best achievable within the known distributions\. We give an algorithm that, using $O\(\\varepsilon^\{\-2\}\\log\(n/δ\)\)$ target samples, running in $O\(n\\varepsilon^\{\-2\}\\log\(n/δ\)\)$ time, with probability at least $1\-δ$, returns $H\_\{\\hat i\}$ satisfying $d\_\\mathrm\{TV\}\(P,H\_\{\\hat i\}\)\\leq 3\\min\_\{i\\in\[n\]\}d\_\\mathrm\{TV\}\(P,H\_i\)\+\\varepsilon\.$ The algorithm achieves the optimal approximation factor and sample complexity\. The running time improves the dependence on the failure probability from polynomial to logarithmic in the state\-of\-the\-art bound $\\tilde\{O\}\(n\\varepsilon^\{\-2\}δ^\{\-1\}\)$ of Aamand, Aliakbarpour, Chen, and Silwal \(COLT 2026\), thereby answering their Question\~2\.
