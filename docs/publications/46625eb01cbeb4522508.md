# Wavelet\-based adaptive image fusion of CT and MRI with local neighborhood variance for nasopharyngeal carcinoma

**Autores:** Xinjian Qin; Haojiang Li; Shanshan Liu; Pengxing Huang; Lizhi Liu; Hong\-Bo Chen; Xin Nian Yang

**Data:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Multimodal imaging of nasopharyngeal carcinoma \(NPC\) requires complementary representation of CT\-derived bony anatomy and MRI\-derived soft\-tissue contrast\. Existing fusion methods may fail to preserve both structural detail and soft\-tissue information in a transparent and reproducible manner\. We present a wavelet\-based fusion framework that employs local neighborhood variance for adaptive weighting\. This interpretable variance\-driven strategy selectively integrates CT bony structures and MRI soft\-tissue textures according to regional information content\. The neighborhood configuration was selected through an ablation analysis of 232 clinical cases\. Quantitative evaluation showed improved image\-quality performance over the selected comparison methods across entropy, spatial frequency, average gradient, standard deviation, and edge information preservation factor, with respective gains of approximately 5\.5%, 2\.4%, 6\.7%, 5\.1%, and 3\.2% relative to the best\-performing baseline for each metric\. These findings support the proposed method as an image fusion methodology for preserving complementary multimodal information; direct effects on target delineation, workflow efficiency, or dosimetric outcomes require future clinical validation\.
