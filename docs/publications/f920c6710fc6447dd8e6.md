# Machine learning and first\-principles calculations for electronic and transport properties in p\-type Cd\-doped two\-dimensional Ga2O3

**Autores:** H Zeng; W Tang; C Ma; Xi Fu; Y R Xue; Meng Wu

**Data:** 2026-10-12 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Abstract Addressing the p\-type doping bottleneck in 2D Ga2O3 continues to be a crucial challenge for advancing next\-generation electronic materials\. In this study, the p\-type conductivity of 2D Cd\-doped Ga2O3 \(CdGaI\) originated from the strongly hybridized Cd\-4dxy\-O\-2p state at valence band maximum \(VBM\), along with a direct bandgap feature, are revealed by accurate Heyd\-Scuseria\-Ernzerh \(HSE\) hybrid density functional calculations\. The enhanced VBM band curvature lowers the hole effective mass and yields an ultrahigh hole mobility of \~417\.68 cm 2 V 1 s 1 , accompanied by a pronounced mobility anisotropy of 34\.86\. Using the highly efficient neuroevolution potential \(NEP\)\-based machine learning, the calculated lattice thermal conductivities of CdGaI configuration are decreased by \~30% compared with those in perfect 2D Ga2O3, which can be attributed to the increased atomic mass of Cd and the enhanced scattering of low\-frequency acoustic and optical phonons, both of which shorten the phonon mean free path\.Temperature\-dependent lattice thermal conductivity follows the power law as \~T\-1\.3 along x direction, indicating combined contributions of three\-phonon and four\-phonon scattering processes, whereas \~T\-1\.1 along y direction, where three\-phonon scattering dominate\. Notably, further band structure calculations under ±6% biaxial strains demonstrate that CdGaI retains a robustly direct bandgap, accompanied by high hole mobility and strong mobility anisotropy\.Particularly, CdGaI structure with 2% compressive strain exhibits the robust p\-type conductivity, direct bandgap, ultra\-high hole mobility and anisotropy, underlining its strong potential for optoelectronic device applications\.
