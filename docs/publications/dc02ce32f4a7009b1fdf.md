# Integrated Application of Intelligent Sensing and Data Visualization in Violin Instruction

**Autores:** Aihua Zhang; Qing Zhao

**Data:** 2026-10-13 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

The rise of web\-based and hybrid learning environments has increased the demand for scalable approaches to monitor violin performance and ensemble coordination\. This study proposes a web\-based instructional framework that integrates multimodal sensing, multimodal data fusion, three\-dimensional visualization, and closed\-loop feedback\. The framework collects kinematic and physiological signals in authentic teaching scenarios and analyzes relationships between key performance variables, including bow speed and bow pressure, through heatmap\-based analysis and distribution modeling\. It further constructs a collaboration index, C, to characterize ensemble coordination and provides interpretable feedback for teachers and learners\. Across several training cycles, the proposed framework demonstrated improvements in indicators related to accuracy, coordination, and expression, while revealing practical challenges in sensor integration and deployment in actual teaching settings\. This study provides a technology\-supported model for violin instruction and ensemble learning in web\-based and hybrid educational contexts\.
