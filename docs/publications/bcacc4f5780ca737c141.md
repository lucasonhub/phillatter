# MECHANICAL PROPERTIES OF PRECIPITATION\-STRENGTHENED ALUMINUM ALLOYS SYNERGISTICALLY MODIFIED BY Ti AND Cr

**Autores:** Shuai Sun; Xiyuan Huang; Ying Li

**Data:** 2026-10-13 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

This study investigates the synergistic modification effects of combined additions of 0\.25 w/% Cr and varying Ti contents \(0, 0\.5, 1\.0, 1\.5\) w/% on the microstructure and mechanical properties of an Al\-Si\-Cu\-Mg alloy\. The microstructure and phase constituents were characterized using scanning electron microscopy and X\-ray diffraction, while the mechanical properties were evaluated using a Vickers hardness tester and a universal testing machine\. Additionally, JMatPro software was employed to simulate the evolution of phase fractions, stress\-strain curves, and as\-cast strength\. The results indicate that Cr addition transforms the acicular β\-Al5FeSi phase into a blocky Al\-\(Cr,Fe\)\-Si phase, mitigating the detrimental cracking effect of brittle phases on the matrix\. Ti promotes the nucleation of α\-Al by forming Al3Ti via the peritectic reaction L \+ Al3Ti → α\-Al\. Moreover, Ti exhibits a synergistic effect with Cr, leading to a more uniform distribution of the blocky Cr\-containing phases\. Peak hardness is achieved at 0\.5 w/% Ti from grain refinement, but tensile strength further increases to 1\.5 w/% Ti \(10 % higher\) as Al3Ti strengthening compensates for grain coarsening, revealing their different sensitivities to grain size and second phases\. Excessive Ti addition leads to saturation of the grain refinement effect, resulting in a decrease in hardness and strength\. Appropriate combined additions of Ti and Cr synergistically optimize the microstructure and mechanical properties of Al\-Si\-Cu\-Mg alloys, providing a theoretical basis for the development of high\-performance cast aluminum alloys\.
