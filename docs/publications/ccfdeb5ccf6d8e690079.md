# Mapeamento da produção e colaboração acadêmica entre especialistas brasileiros em inteligência artificial e machine learning aplicados à saúde: uma análise bibliométrica

**Autores:** Bruno Cardoso Dantas; Jesús Pascual Mena‐Chalco

**Data:** 2026-10-09 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

The application of artificial intelligence in healthcare has shown great potential over recent decades\. In Brazil,this approach follows the expansion of research and academic collaboration in that field\. In this regard, thisstudy conducts an exploratory analysis of the scientific production and the collaboration among Brazilianresearchers working on artificial intelligence and machine learning applied to healthcare\. A quantitativeapproach was employed, using scriptLattes software to map data from the Lattes platform from 2008 to2024\. The analyses covered temporal evolution, geographical distribution, the most recurrent institutions and fields of concentration, keywords, and collaboration networks, assessing trends and gaps in the field\. The results demonstrate significant growth in scientific production, concentrated in the Southeast region, and predominance of terms associated with the technologies employed and diagnostic applications, withemphasis on covid\-19\. Furthermore, the need for greater interdisciplinary and interinstitutional collaboration as well as greater attention to ethics, governance, and regulation is evident\.
