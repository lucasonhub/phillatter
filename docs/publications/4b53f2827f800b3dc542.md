# Machine Learning Utilisation and Perceived Financial Reporting Quality among Employees of Selected Listed Deposit Money Banks in Nigeria: A PLS\-SEM Analysis

**Autores:** Yusuf Yahaya; Muhammad  Aminu Isa; Nasiru Abdulsalam Kao’je

**Data:** 2026-10-17 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Machine learning \(ML\) is increasingly used in accounting and assurance processes, yet focused evidence on its relationship with financial reporting quality \(FRQ\) in Nigerian banking remains limited\. This study examines the association between ML utilisation and perceived FRQ among employees of selected listed Deposit Money Banks \(DMBs\) in Nigeria\. A quantitative cross\-sectional survey design was used\. The study population comprised 243 eligible employees; 181 questionnaires were distributed after allowing for anticipated non\-response, 151 completed questionnaires were returned, and all 151 were valid for analysis, yielding a response rate of 83\.43%\. ML and perceived FRQ were measured with five indicators each on a five\-point Likert scale\. PLS\-SEM was implemented in SmartPLS 4\. The measurement model demonstrated strong indicator loadings, reliability, and convergent validity, although the very high reliability coefficients warrant assessment of possible item redundancy\. ML utilisation was positively associated with perceived FRQ \(β = 0\.719, t = 12\.63, p &lt; 0\.001, 95% CI \[0\.631, 0\.788\]\)\. The model explained 51\.7% of the variance in perceived FRQ \(R² = 0\.517; adjusted R² = 0\.514\), with a large effect size \(f² = 1\.07\)\. Because the design is cross\-sectional and perception\-based, the findings are interpreted as associations rather than causal effects\. The study contributes focused evidence on ML utilisation and perceived financial\-reporting quality in the Nigerian listed\-banking context\.
