# A dynamic pyramidal model of teaching excellence in higher education: a critical analytical reflection

**Autores:** Ariel Torres; Paloma González; Gisselle Trujillo; Mario Alomía; Martha Fors

**Data:** 2026-10-15 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Teaching excellence in higher education is often conceptualized through isolated dimensions such as instructional quality, assessment practices, or technological integration\. However, these approaches may overlook the dynamic and interdependent nature of educational processes\. This study presents a critical analytical reflection aimed at synthesizing high\-level evidence and proposing an integrative conceptual model of teaching excellence\. An interpretative synthesis was conducted using 35 high\-level studies \(systematic reviews, meta\-analyses, umbrella reviews, and scoping reviews\) published between 2015 and 2025, complemented by five international policy reports\. The analysis followed a thematic and iterative approach supported by an analytical matrix, coding procedures, and conceptual triangulation\. The findings reveal convergence across five interrelated dimensions: teacher–student interaction, higher\-order cognitive processes, pedagogical strategies, formative and authentic assessment, and digital technologies\. These dimensions are conceptualized as interconnected elements embedded within teaching and learning processes rather than as independent components\. Based on this convergence, the study proposes the Dynamic Pyramidal Model of Teaching Excellence, which conceptualizes excellence as an integrative construct organized through the interaction and alignment of multiple educational dimensions\. The model integrates relational, cognitive, pedagogical, evaluative, technological, contextual, and individual factors within a dynamic system characterized by continuous feedback\. The proposed framework integrates dimensions commonly addressed separately in the literature and provides a conceptual basis for future empirical validation across diverse higher\-education contexts\.
