# School Culture and Its Translation into Student School Climate Experiences: Basis for A Responsive School Management Framework

**Autores:** Liza Marie M\. Hipolito; Eloy P\. Aenlle

**Data:** 2026-10-10 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

This study explores students’ perspectives on school culture and its translation into student school climate experiences, to provide a basis for a comprehensive school management framework\. Using qualitative methods and the Collaizzi approach, ten student participants shared their experiences regarding academic and practical skill development, teacher\-student relationships, peer interactions, leadership and management practices, and the learning environment\. Findings reveal that students perceive academic and practical skill development as dependent on both the quality of resources and the support of teachers, while positive teacher\-student relationships and collaborative peer interactions enhance motivation, confidence, and engagement\. Strong leadership and inclusive management practices provide structure, encouragement, and fairness, shaping a positive school culture that balances academic rigor with student well\-being\. The study highlights the interconnection of social relationships, environmental resources, and leadership in fostering academic success and holistic development\. Based on these insights, a responsive school management framework is proposed that integrates leadership, teaching practices, resource allocation, and collaborative opportunities to cultivate an inclusive, motivating, and academically rigorous environment\. The framework aims to guide school policies and programs that support both student learning and personal growth, preparing students for life beyond the classroom\.
