# GLOBALIZATION, ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE AND LEGAL ENGLISH: TRANSFORMING PROFESSIONAL COMMUNICATION IN LAW ENFORCEMENT

**Autores:** Лариса Володимирівна Дудікова; Марина Костюк; Viktoriia Mikaielian

**Data:** 2026-10-15 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

The article examines changes in professional English\-language communication in law enforcement under the influence of globalization and the growing use of artificial intelligence \(AI\)\. Particular attention is given to Legal English as a means of international cooperation, professional information processing and interaction in intercultural settings\. The study argues that contemporary language training for law enforcement professionals should move beyond the acquisition of specialized terminology and develop the ability to understand legal concepts in context, adapt communication to different audiences, and use digital tools critically and responsibly\. Drawing on a narrative literature review and conceptual analysis of research in legal linguistics, intercultural police communication, AI\-assisted language learning and large language models in legal contexts, the article identifies an emerging gap between these research areas and proposes their integration into a four\-component framework of professional communicative competence\. The framework comprises linguistic, professional\-legal, intercultural and digital/AI competences\. The article also outlines practical applications for Legal English teaching, including AI\-assisted translation review and scenario\-based police communication\. Particular attention is given to accuracy, confidentiality, source verification and the limits of automated systems\. The proposed framework can support the design of more realistic, human\-centred and professionally relevant language training for future law enforcement professionals\.
