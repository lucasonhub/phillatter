# Athena Proctor \- Smart Proctoring System for Online Exam

**Autores:** ROOPA N A; SUBHASH K; UDITHA UDITHA; VEERBASAVANTH REDDY; Manohara Gowda

**Data:** 2026-10-10 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Online examinations provide flexibility and accessibility for students and educational institutions, but maintaining academic integrity in remote assessment environments remains a major challenge due to limited direct supervision\. Traditional examination methods may not effectively identify impersonation, unauthorized materials, suspicious movements, or attempts to access external resources during an examination\. To address these challenges, this paper presents a Smart Proctoring System for Online Examinations that integrates Artificial Intelligence \(AI\), Machine Learning \(ML\), computer vision, audio monitoring, and web technologies to support automated examination supervision\.
