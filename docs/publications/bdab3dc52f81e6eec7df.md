# Carranza\-Pinedo, Victor: Borderline consciousness

**Autores:** 

**Data de descoberta:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** unknown

## Descrição do feed

\_Erkenntnis\_\. forthcomingRecent work on animal consciousness urges a multidimensional picture: species differ in their consciousness profiles across several dimensions, yet there is taken to be an ontological edge between conscious and non\-conscious organisms\. I argue that, if we understand the multidimensional idea as a claim about the structure of consciousness, this edge cannot be uniquely sharp\. On that understanding, phenomenal consciousness depends on several partly independent dimensions, admits overall degrees with a minimal threshold, and any such degree must result from an aggregation rule\. Plausible structural constraints leave room for multiple admissible aggregation rules that diverge on some low but non\-trivial profiles\. Hence, there are borderline cases at the transition between non\-conscious and minimally conscious systems\. I then consider the implications of such borderline consciousness for precautionary reasoning and regulatory decision\-making\. For some taxa and systems, further empirical evidence about their profiles may not remove all indeterminacy in the conscious/non\-conscious classification\. In such cases, borderline status is not merely a temporary product of incomplete evidence, but a structural feature that practical decision\-making may need to represent when considering proportionate precautionary safeguards\.
