# Johnson, Jamison: On Existing \- On the Ruler, the Sum Over Selves, and What Is Left When the Perspectives Cancel

**Autores:** 

**Data de descoberta:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** unknown

## Descrição do feed

What if someone tried to put a ruler to subjective experience? This lyric\-philosophical essay argues that there is no outside from which to measure it: the ruler is the seeing, and attention is the ruler coming down at every instant\. Taking Feynman&\#x27;s sum over histories as a formal image, it writes existence as a sum over selves, the author&\#x27;s coherent\-existence functional, in which each perspective enters with an intensity and a facing and the gathering is held in proportion, a weighted form of Kuramoto&\#x27;s order parameter\. The essay reads what that arithmetic hands back to one person: a reading set by how sharp the room is, taken through the angle between one&\#x27;s own facing and the gathering, with no separate term for how hard one tried\. Laid against oneself alone, the ruler returns one at every moment, an empty reading that says only that one is present, pictured as a Dirac comb and as a string of Jays, single felt moments; laid against everyone, it reads the room through how one is turned, and the turning is the part a person can choose\. A floor of about one over the square root of the crowd is the typical size a scattered gathering falls toward; exact zero takes a perfect conspiracy\. A supplemental note, Parallels and Lineage, situates the framework beside the path integral and quantum cosmology, Integrated Information Theory and the 2025 adversarial collaboration, participatory and relational interpretations of quantum mechanics, cosmopsychism, its classical ancestor Spinoza and the individuation problem, and Whitehead&\#x27;s actual occasions and Teilhard&\#x27;s noosphere, as family resemblances rather than derivations\. \(direct link \)
