# Epistemic Hidden Defects in Artificial Intelligence: Classical ʿIlal al\-Ḥadīth as a Framework for AI Verification

**Autores:** Ghulam Hasan Awan

**Data:** 2026-10-15 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

This working paper examines whether the classical methodology of ʿilal al\-Ḥadīth—the detection of hidden defects in apparently sound reports—can provide a useful conceptual framework for evaluating artificial\-intelligence outputs in specialist knowledge environments\. It develops the concept of the “epistemic hidden defect”: a weakness in provenance, attribution, inference, context, evidential hierarchy, or scholarly status that may remain concealed beneath a fluent and apparently authoritative AI\-generated response\. Drawing on classical Hadith\-critical methodology and contemporary AI research, the paper distinguishes computational capability from scholarly authority\. It develops the concepts of the Authority Ceiling, Digital Isnād, competence\-specific human oversight, and the No\-Autonomous\-Grading Principle as safeguards for AI\-assisted scholarship\. The paper argues for an AI\-assisted rather than AI\-autonomous model of specialist research, in which computational systems widen the evidential field while consequential scholarly judgement remains traceable, reviewable, and accountable\.
