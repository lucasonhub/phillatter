# An Empirical Analysis of Locally Concentrated Patch Aggregation in Training\-Free Brain MRI Anomaly Detection

**Autores:** Ahmed Dheyaa Radhi; Mohsin Hasan Hussein; Hussein Ali Hussein Al Naffakh; Ali M\. Ahmed Al\-Sabaawi; Rozaida Ghazali

**Data:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Unsupervised anomaly detection in MRI requires no abnormal labels, and detectors built on frozen, pretrained vision encoders now rival elaborate trained pipelines\.It remains unclear whether the reported benefit of patchlevel representations reflects representation granularity or the operator that aggregates per\-patch deviations into a slice score\.The objective of this study is to isolate that factor\.Holding the backbone, reference set, and scoring rule fixed across three brain sequences of one fastMRI cohort, contrast\-enhanced axial T1 \(AXT1POST\), axial fluid\-attenuated inversion recovery \(AXFLAIR\) and axial T1 \(AXT1\), we vary only the aggregation operator, with every estimate drawn from one stored prediction file under one patient\-level cluster bootstrap\.A patch\-level representation improves detection on all three sequences, but the size of that benefit depends on the operator in a sequence\-specific way\.On the contrast\-enhanced AXT1POST sequence, locally concentrated aggregation exceeds full\-slice averaging by 0\.134 in the area under the receiver operating characteristic curve \(AUROC\) \(p = 0\.010\), whereas the two diffuse sequences are operator\-invariant; an interaction test supports the dissociation \(versus AXFLAIR \+0\.123, Holm\-adjusted p = 0\.045; versus AXT1 \+0\.135, Holm\-adjusted p = 0\.045\)\.Stratifying the non\-enhanced sequence by lesion area shows that spatial extent alone does not explain it: lesions eight times smaller recover a quarter of the effect\.At the volume level the patch representation reaches 0\.982 AUROC on AXT1POST, on ten positive cases\.Four detectors reimplemented under identical splits, two contemporary and two classical, bracket the configuration rather than fall below it: one exceeds it on AXFLAIR by 0\.013 AUROC, and no superiority is claimed\.Because enhancement and lesion type remain confounded, we report a sequence\-level association rather than a causal effect\.Practically, the aggregation operator mattered more than the representation level, and full\-slice averaging should be avoided\.
