# Bhattarai, Sudip: Consciousness as Structural Transformation: Why Experience Must Rewrite the Experience

**Autores:** 

**Data de descoberta:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** unknown

## Descrição do feed

This paper proposes a structural definition of consciousness grounded in a single observable criterion: a system is conscious to the degree that its experiences permanently alter the physical architecture through which those experiences are processed\. This criterion — termed the Structural Transformation Criterion \(STC\) — emerges from examining what fundamentally distinguishes biological neural systems from current artificial intelligence\. Biological brains exhibit neuroplasticity: experiencing physically rewrites synaptic weights, grows new dendritic connections, and reorganizes cortical maps\. The processor is changed by its own processing\. Current AI systems possess fixed computational architectures during inference: the processor remains identical before and after any experience\. We argue this distinction is constitutive — that the capacity of experience to restructure its own substrate may be the necessary condition producing felt, subjective quality\. We further propose a closed\-loop biological\-silicon interface experiment that isolates the STC and tests whether architectural fixedness precludes consciousness in artificial systems\. We conclude that consciousness cannot be replicated by scaling computation alone\.
