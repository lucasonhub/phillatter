# Automated Thyroid Disease Detection Using a Hybrid Autoencoder\-TabTransformer\-multi\-layer Perceptron Model

**Autores:** Shibitha K P; P Manimeghalai; N\. Rajalakshmy; S\. Gnanasaravanan

**Data:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Thyroid disorders constitute a major category of endocrine diseases globally, influencing metabolic homeostasis and overall physiological function\.Early and reliable diagnosis plays a critical role in effective disease management; however, clinical evaluation frequently depends on multiple biochemical markers and patient records, making manual interpretation challenging and susceptible to errors\.Conventional diagnostic approaches and traditional machine learning \(ML\) models frequently exhibit limitations to capture nonlinear relationships among heterogeneous clinical attributes and mixed data types\.To overcome these challenges, this research presents a hybrid Autoencoder\-TabTransformer\-Multi\-Layer Perceptron \(MLP\) model for automated thyroid disease detection from structured clinical and biochemical data\.The autoencoder component compresses numerical medical attributes into informative latent representations, while the TabTransformer captures contextual dependencies among categorical features using attention mechanisms\.The fused feature representation is subsequently processed by an MLP for final classification\.The effectiveness of the proposed architecture is assessed on a publicly accessible thyroid dataset containing demographic, medical history and hormonal measurements\.The original dataset containing 3,772 records was first stratified into training, validation and independent test subsets before any data\-dependent preprocessing\.Imputation parameters, categorical mappings, outlier thresholds and feature\-scaling statistics were derived exclusively from the training subset and subsequently applied unchanged to the validation and test subsets to prevent information leakage\.Following training\-set outlier removal, 1,790 samples were retained for model training, while the validation and independent test sets contained 755 samples each\.Experimental findings demonstrate that the hybrid model delivers an overall predictive accuracy of 98\.01%, with weighted precision, weighted recall and weighted F1\-score values of 97\.99%, 98\.01% and 98\.00%, respectively\.The model exhibits effective generalization and improved handling of heterogeneous tabular medical data through the complementary integration of latent feature learning and contextual modelling\.These outcomes demonstrate the predictive capability of the proposed hybrid architecture on the evaluated dataset, while further external and prospective validation is required to establish its clinical reliability and applicability to real\-world clinical decision\-support settings\.
