# TRACER: Trajectory\-Aware Tool Retrieval for LLM Agents

**Autores:** Tanish Kher; Sarvesh DK; Abedelkadir Asi; Sparsha Chennarayapatna Sundaresh; Dezhi Hong

**Data:** 2026-10-15 · **Tipo:** preprint

## Resumo

Modern agents select tools from registries, but exposing entire registries incurs high token costs and can impair selection as they grow\. We present TRACER, a retrieval front\-end that indexes observed tool\-call sequences in a trie and ranks continuations using a learned blend of empirical and contextual priors\. On a mixed retail\-and\-telecom $τ^2$ environment, TRACER improves GPT\-5\.4&\#x27;s tool\-selection accuracy by 4\.7 points while reducing input tokens per step by 80%, exposing just 5 of 128 tools\. In autonomous full\-turn execution, it matches GPT\-5\.4&\#x27;s full\-registry task success with 61% fewer input tokens per step\. Across six environments and four models, TRACER improves tool\-selection accuracy over full\-registry selection in 21 of 24 model\-environment pairs, with average gains of 6 to 10 points; the three exceptions trail by at most 2\.2\. We analyze failures in both regimes to identify directions for future work\.
