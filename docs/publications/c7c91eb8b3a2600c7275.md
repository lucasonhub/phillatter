# Experimental Protocols for Developing and Validating Explainable AI Models in Secure Healthcare Diagnostics

**Autores:** Sonali Patil; Meghana Lokhande; Shweta Ashish Koparde; Sonali Kothari; Sudan Jha

**Data:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Artificial Intelligence \(AI\) has drastically enhanced healthcare diagnostics with automated disease detection, predictive analytics, and intelligent systems for clinical decision support\. Nevertheless, the black\-box nature of deep learning models hampers interpretability, clinical trust, and regulatory acceptance and raises concerns about data privacy and cybersecurity\. This paper presents comprehensive experimental frameworks for developing and evaluating explainable AI models for secure healthcare diagnostics\. The suggested model combines responsible data management, multimodal preprocessing, secure deep learning models, explainable artificial intelligence \(XAI\) methods, and reproducible validation procedures in a single healthcare diagnostic pipeline\. Methods of explaining, such as Grad\-CAM, SHAP, LIME, and counterfactual analysis, were used to make the models more transparent and understandable for clinicians\. At the same time, federated learning and differential privacy methods were used to securely manage highly sensitive patient data\. Experimental evaluation has shown 96% classification accuracy, 94% sensitivity, and 95% specificity\. These results were consistent in heterogeneous healthcare datasets\. Compared to traditional black\-box models, the explainability framework not only enhanced the interpretability of the results but also more accurately identified the regions relevant for diagnosis\. Moreover, the secure federated learning architecture minimized privacy leakages without sacrificing the predictive performance and remained robust even when exposed to adversarial perturbations\. An external evaluation also confirmed the reproducibility and generalizability of the protocols involved\. This methodology offers a comprehensive and reliable foundation for deploying AI\-powered diagnostics across multiple healthcare domains: radiology, pathology, oncology, cardiology, neurology, and precision medicine\.
