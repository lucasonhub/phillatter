# Kim, GeonWoo: Beyond Individual Formation: Participatory Reachability and the Public Duty of Education

**Autores:** 

**Data de descoberta:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** unknown

## Descrição do feed

A dialysis patient may understand a medical allocation model perfectly and receive a reasoned institutional response, yet lack a sustained opportunity to examine access with other affected people\. Has public education done its part? This paper defends a conception of education on which developing capacities and supporting people’s own examination of common problems are complementary formative tasks\. A controlled contrast holds complete individual formation, perfect tutoring, institutional responsiveness, and policy outcomes fixed while varying access to reciprocal inquiry\. The contrast isolates a difference that capacities alone cannot capture; the educational argument concerns why opening that inquiry to participants belongs within education’s purpose even when it adds no capacity\. Where knowledge and technology reshape basic conditions of life, the paper joins this account to affected people’s equal standing to argue that a polity committed to public answerability owes adequate and fair access to such inquiry\. It names the combined condition of individual formation and such access participatory reachability, a proposed criterion of public educational success distinct from institutional consideration of and response to people’s reasons\.
