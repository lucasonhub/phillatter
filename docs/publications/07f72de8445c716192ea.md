# TARBIYA FANIDA GENERATIV SUN’IY INTELLEKT TEXNOLOGIYALARI ASOSIDA MADANIYATLARARO MULOQOT KOMPETENSIYASINI RIVOJLANTIRISHNING PEDAGOGIK MEXANIZMLARI

**Autores:** Maftuna Rahmatova

**Data:** 2026-10-14 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

This article examines the pedagogical mechanisms for developing students&\#x27; intercultural communication competence through the use of generative artificial intelligence technologies in the teaching of the Tarbiya \(Moral Education\) subject\. It scientifically substantiates the potential of generative artificial intelligence in the educational process and highlights its role in fostering learners&\#x27; communicative, cultural, and social competencies\. Furthermore, the article describes interactive teaching methods based on artificial intelligence, approaches to designing individualized learning trajectories, and strategies for developing effective communication skills for interaction with representatives of diverse cultures\.
