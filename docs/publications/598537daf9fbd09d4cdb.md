# Large language models in healthcare: Mapping the global research landscape through bibliometric and science mapping analyses \(2022–2025\)

**Autores:** Rachel T\. Alegado

**Data:** 2026-10-10 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

The rapid emergence of Large Language Models \(LLMs\) has fundamentally transformed healthcare research by enabling advances in clinical decision support, biomedical text mining, medical education, electronic health record analysis, and healthcare communication\. Despite the explosive growth of this research area, a comprehensive understanding of its scientific development, intellectual structure, collaboration patterns, and emerging research directions remains limited\. This study provides a bibliometric and science mapping analysis of the global research landscape on LLMs in healthcare published between 2022 and 2025\. A bibliometric analysis was conducted using publications indexed exclusively in the Scopus database from 2022 to 2025\. Data were analyzed using Bibliometrix/Biblioshiny for descriptive bibliometric indicators and VOSviewer for science\-mapping analyses, including author cocitation, keyword co\-occurrence, overlay visualization, and international collaboration networks\. Scientific productivity, citation impact, leading journals, authors, institutions, countries, intellectual structure, conceptual evolution, and emerging research frontiers were systematically examined\. The final dataset comprised 12,408 publications from 3,353 scholarly sources involving 51,920 authors and 889,155 references, with an exceptional annual publication growth rate of 176\.41%\. Scientific production increased exponentially following the introduction of generative AI technologies, demonstrating the rapid diffusion of LLMs across healthcare disciplines\. Citation analysis revealed that publications from 2023 achieved the greatest scientific impact, establishing the intellectual foundation of the field\. The Journal of Medical Internet Research, Scientific Reports, and NPJ Digital Medicine emerged as the leading publication venues, while the United States and China dominated global scientific production and international collaboration\. Science mapping identified three major intellectual communities centered on clinical artificial intelligence, foundation model development, and responsible AI implementation, together with four interconnected conceptual domains encompassing technological foundations, clinical applications, healthcare education, and responsible artificial intelligence\. Overlay visualization demonstrated a clear thematic evolution from conventional healthcare AI toward multimodal foundation models, including GPT\-4o, Gemini, Claude, DeepSeek, and Copilot, accompanied by increasing emphasis on clinical implementation, privacy, explainability, evaluation, and trustworthy AI\. Healthcare LLM research has evolved into a mature interdisciplinary scientific domain characterized by rapid publication growth, expanding international collaboration, and increasing intellectual integration across artificial intelligence, biomedical informatics, and clinical medicine\. The field is transitioning from proof\-of\-concept investigations toward clinically deployable, ethically governed, and multimodal foundation models\. These findings provide a comprehensive overview of the global research landscape and offer valuable evidence to guide researchers, healthcare professionals, policymakers, funding agencies, and technology developers in advancing the safe, effective, and responsible implementation of Large Language Models in healthcare\.
