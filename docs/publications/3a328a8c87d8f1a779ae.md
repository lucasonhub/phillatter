# Carranza\-Pinedo, V\.: How precaution should track uncertainty in animal sentience judgements

**Autores:** 

**Data de descoberta:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** unknown

## Descrição do feed

\_Philosophical Quarterly\_\. forthcomingDebates about animal sentience often appeal to precaution: where the evidence of sentience is strong enough, taxa should be treated as sentient in law and policy\. I argue that what is called ‘evidence of sentience’ in fact has two dimensions—robustness of indicators and credibility of the indicator–sentience link—and that standard, threshold\-based formulations of precaution ignore this structure\. Using a simple model, I show that conjunctive high\-threshold rules are over\-conservative and excessively fragile, and I sketch an inverse\-threshold\-with\-floors principle better suited to sentience policy\. \(direct link \)
