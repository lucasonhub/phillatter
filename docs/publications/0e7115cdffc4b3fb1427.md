# Artificial Intelligence in Periodontics and Oral Implantology

**Autores:** Dr\. Shivam Agarwal

**Data:** 2026-10-14 · **Tipo:** book

## Resumo

Artificial intelligence \(AI\) is rapidly transforming the landscape of modern dentistry, offering new opportunities to enhance diagnosis, treatment planning, clinical decision\-making, and patient care\. In periodontics and oral implantology, where accurate assessment, risk prediction, individualized treatment planning, and long\-term monitoring are fundamental to successful outcomes, AI\-based technologies have emerged as valuable adjuncts to clinical expertise\. From automated interpretation of radiographic and clinical data to predictive analytics, image\-based diagnosis, computer\-assisted implant planning, and personalized treatment strategies, the integration of AI is opening new avenues for precision and efficiency in periodontal and implant practice\. This book, ‘Artificial Intelligence in Periodontics and Oral Implantology’, aims to provide a comprehensive and clinically relevant overview of these evolving applications while placing them within the broader context of contemporary evidence\-based dentistry\. The purpose of this book is not to present artificial intelligence as a replacement for the clinician, but rather as a powerful tool that can augment professional judgment, improve diagnostic accuracy, facilitate informed decision\-making, and support patient\-centered care\. It brings together fundamental concepts of AI with their practical applications in periodontics and oral implantology, addressing current advances, clinical opportunities, limitations, ethical considerations, and future directions\. As AI continues to evolve, clinicians will increasingly need both technological awareness and critical appraisal skills to determine where these innovations can be safely and effectively incorporated into practice\. We hope this book serves as a useful resource for undergraduate and postgraduate students, researchers, periodontists, implantologists, and dental practitioners seeking to understand and responsibly embrace the emerging role of artificial intelligence in periodontal and implant dentistry\.
