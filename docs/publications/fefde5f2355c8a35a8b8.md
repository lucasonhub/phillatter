# Bulgaria, Jamel: Theoretical Convergence in Affective Ontologies, Psycho\-Historical Agent Tracking, and Moral Epistemology: A Unified Systems Framework for Multi\-Dimensional Detangling

**Autores:** 

**Data de descoberta:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** unknown

## Descrição do feed

The integration of affective computing, cognitive psychology, and moral epistemology reveals a profound structural parallelism between how human systems represent internal emotional states, track environmental agents, and validate normative beliefs\. Historically, affective models have operated independently of epistemic theories of person\-identification, while moral philosophy has often treated normative justification in isolation from its psychological and somatic substrates\. However, by linking the Core Emotion Framework with the psycho\-historical theory of agent\-identification and the principles of distributed truth\-telling, a unified, multi\-dimensional architecture is established\. This framework demonstrates that emotional self\-regulation and historical tracking are not distinct cognitive functions, but are instead deeply coupled systems designed to stabilize human agency amidst complex environmental, social, and moral landscapes\. \(direct link \)
