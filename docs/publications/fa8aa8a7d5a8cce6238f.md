# Repurposing Approved Chemotherapeutics as Intrinsic Photosensitizers for Single\-Molecule Chemophototherapy

**Autores:** Giulia Greco; Giulia Giugliano; Alessia Marconi; Paolo Emidio Costantini; Michele Trivini; Giulia Neggiani; Francesca Maffei; Manuele Di Sante; Tainah Dorina Marforio; Alessandro Calza; Manuela Sollazzo; Anna Maria Porcelli; Giuseppe Gasparre; Francesco Zerbetto; Alberto Danielli; Julia Westermayr; Matteo Di Giosia; Carmela Fimognari; Eleonora Turrini; Edoardo Jun Mattioli; Matteo Calvaresi

**Data:** 2026-10-12 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Abstract Chemophototherapy \(CPT\), combining chemotherapy and photodynamic therapy \(PDT\), is a promising strategy to enhance anticancer efficacy while reducing systemic toxicity\. Many approved drugs contain chromophoric moieties that absorb visible light, suggesting their potential as intrinsic photosensitizers\. Herein, we introduce a single\-molecule chemophototherapy \(SM\-CPT\) approach based on computational screening of approved anticancer drugs for repurposing as photosensitizers\. A curated library of 238 small\-molecule drugs was screened using time\-dependent density functional theory to identify compounds with favourable photophysical properties\. Fifteen candidates were identified, and mitoxantrone, pixantrone, daunorubicin, and epirubicin were selected for experimental validation\. All compounds generated reactive oxygen species upon irradiation and showed enhanced cytotoxicity in breast cancer cell models, with up to an order\-of\-magnitude reduction in half\-maximal inhibitory concentration values\. The approach was further validated in ovarian cancer models, including platinum\-resistant cells and patient\-derived ascites, highlighting SM\-CPT as a translationally attractive light\-activated cancer therapy\.
