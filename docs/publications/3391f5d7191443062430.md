# Engineering Anthropology: Books I–VI\. A General Engineering Theory of Human Coherence

**Autores:** Oleg Kvashuk; Violetta Podets

**Data:** 2026-10-13 · **Tipo:** book

## Resumo

Engineering Anthropology — Books I–VI A General Engineering Theory of Human Coherence Engineering Anthropology presents a developing theoretical framework for describing human consciousness, cognition, behaviour, and interpersonal relationships using the conceptual language of engineering and systems theory\. This collected English\-language edition brings together the first six books of the series\. The human being is examined as a system of signal reception, interpretation, filtering, feedback, and regulation\. Key concepts include the operator of consciousness, signal and noise, cognitive filters, coherence, and the conditions under which a system becomes capable of recognising and processing differences\. The six books develop the framework progressively: Book I introduces the general engineering theory of human coherence and the informational model of consciousness\. Book II explores the human being as a generator of coherence and examines the relationship between individual states and the surrounding environment\. Book III develops a fractal model of coherence and investigates love and complementary human relationships as mechanisms through which cognitive filters become observable\. Book IV presents an applied methodology for diagnosing and adjusting coherence, including a multidimensional map of human functioning\. Book V examines the engineering mechanics of the dyad, including distributed regulation, asymmetry, feedback, and the conditions for sustainable interaction\. Book VI investigates the admission threshold: the conditions under which a cognitive system allows new information or differences to enter processing rather than rejecting them through established filters\. The work integrates perspectives from cognitive science, psychology, information theory, philosophy, and comparative religious traditions\. Its engineering models are presented as theoretical and methodological hypotheses, not as experimentally validated descriptions of the ultimate nature of consciousness\. The collected edition documents the evolution of the conceptual framework across its first six books and is intended for researchers and readers interested in consciousness, human development, systems thinking, artificial intelligence, and interdisciplinary anthropology\.
