# Novel indole derivatives containing a thiosemicarbazide moiety as potential antioxidant agents for fresh beef preservation

**Autores:** Tao Wang; Lingling Xiao; Yanling Ren; 吴茂钊; Yongfei Liu; Jian Tang; Shoumei Wan; Jing Peng

**Data:** 2026-10-12 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Oxidative stress is implicated in numerous pathological processes\. Developing efficient, safe small\-molecule antioxidants is of great medicinal interest\. In this work, ten new indole derivatives bearing a thiosemicarbazide moiety \( 4a–4j \) were synthesized and fully characterized by 1 H NMR, 13 C NMR, and HRMS\. In vitro antioxidant assays indicated that all target compounds showed obvious free radical scavenging potency\. Among them, compound 2\-\(3\-\(1 H \-indol\-3\-yl\)propanoyl\)\- N \-\(4\-chlorophenyl\)hydrazine\-1\-carbothioamide \( 4i \) displayed the strongest in vitro ABTS radical scavenging activity with an IC 50 = 0\.96 μg/mL, remarkably lower than that of vitamin C \(1\.87 μg/mL\)\. Compound 4i also exhibited excellent in vitro DPPH scavenging efficiency \(IC 50 = 2\.95 μg/mL\)\. Molecular docking and 100 ns molecular dynamics simulations predicted that compound 4i stably bound to the catalytic pocket of human peroxiredoxin 5 \(PDB ID: 1HD2\)\. Meanwhile, in silico ADMET and drug\-likeness predictions suggested favorable physicochemical properties, acceptable safety, and compliance with Lipinski’s rule of five\. Furthermore, application in a raw beef patty model revealed that compound 4i effectively retarded myoglobin oxidation, suppressed pH elevation, and preserved red\-color stability during refrigerated storage, exhibiting superior color\-protection performance\. Collectively, these findings highlight compound 4i as a promising dual\-purpose candidate, deserving further investigation both as an antioxidant lead for drug discovery and as a potential protective agent for raw meat preservation, with additional enzyme\-level and food\-safety assessments required\.
