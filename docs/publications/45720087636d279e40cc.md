# Preventive Real Estate Legal Governance: Litigation, Risk, and Value Preservation\.

**Autores:** Ana Paula Costanzo

**Data:** 2026-10-15 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Real estate assets are subject to regulatory, registration, urban planning, environmental, contractual, economic, and family\-related factors that cannot be adequately understood in isolation\.The fragmentation of these dimensions contributes to the accumulation of inconsistencies that often only become apparent during sales, financing, development, or estate succession\.In light of this problem, this article analyzes how preventive real estate legal governance can help reduce vulnerabilities and litigation, thereby preserving value, asset continuity, and the quality of decisions related to real estate\.Methodologically, a qualitative, exploratory, and descriptive study was conducted, based on a narrative and integrative literature review and documentary analysis\.Priority was given to scientific articles, specialized books, technical standards, and institutional documents published between 2016 and 2026\.The discussion was organized around three themes: recurring sources of litigation and vulnerability; preventive governance as a framework for identifying and mitigating risks; and the preservation of value, asset continuity, and decision\-\-making efficiency\.The results indicate that real estate security does not depend exclusively on registered title, but rather on the compatibility between registration, cadastral records, possession, physical reality, regulatory compliance, and succession planning\.It is concluded that preventive governance can transform fragmented information into decision\-making criteria, responsibilities, and follow\-up measures\.The LEGACY Method™ is discussed as an original proposal for integrating these dimensions, although it still requires independent empirical validation\.
