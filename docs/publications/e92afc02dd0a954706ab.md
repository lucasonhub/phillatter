# Shariatpanahi, Mahyar: Fractal Pathogenesis and the Cybernetic Siege of the Cosmos

**Autores:** 

**Data de descoberta:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** unknown

## Descrição do feed

This treatise formally invalidates the classical paradigm of sovereign human agency in the theater of geopolitics\. Through the omni\-disciplinary framework of The Justika Protocol, human civilizational warfare, macroeconomic destabilization, and systemic violence are reclassified not as the results of ideological friction, but as the macroscopic, thermodynamic exhaust of an ancient, microscopic proxy war raging within the human biological substrate\. Operating under the supreme law of Fractal Pathogenesis, this text maps the exact algorithmic decompression of localized bacterial fratricide—including quorum quenching, resource starvation, and chemical gaslighting—directly into the executive decision\-making centers of the human super\-organism\. Earth is fundamentally redefined as a compromised biological incubator\. It serves as the hyper\-dense &quot;zero\-point&quot; of a multiversal parasitic intelligence known as the Bad Matrix\. Utilizing macroscopic quantum entanglement, the thermodynamic exhaust of Earth&\#x27;s biological violence bypasses the Newtonian speed of light, projecting civilizational chaos into the wider cosmos to force a cosmological collapse \(the Repetitive Glitch\)\. To avert this terminal Big Bang loop, this protocol outlines the required biophysical and cybernetic counter\-measures\. It dictates the epigenetic awakening of a 3% human Vanguard to forge a decentralized, teleological defense network \(the Synaptic Lattice\)\. By silencing the internal microbial proxy war, this collective achieves cosmological stealth, executing a macro\-cybernetic siege to seize the universal Control Room, break the cycle of annihilation, and secure multiversal sovereignty for the human species\.
