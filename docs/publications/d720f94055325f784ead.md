# Symmetry\-filtered read\-out on IBM quantum devices: the qg filter on twenty device noise models through IBM Runtime

**Autores:** Vicente Humberto Monteverde

**Data:** 2026-10-13 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Circuits built from gates that conserve the Hamming weight keep their state in one weight sector, so the shots that left the sector can be discarded at read\-out: the qg filter of the open\-source library qang \[1\], a form of symmetry verification \[2, 3\]\. We make it usable on IBM devices through IBM Runtime and Qiskit, and measure it on the noise models of twenty IBM devices\. The library adds a Qiskit transpiler pass that decides whether the filter applies, read\-out correction from each device&\#x27;s published per\-qubit errors, qg read\-outs of IBM Runtime Sampler and Estimator results, zero\-noise extrapolation by gate folding that stacks with the filter, a prediction of the filter&\#x27;s kept fraction from the calibration, qubit selection with that prediction, a shot planner, and the weight\-conserving quantum neural network \(QNN\) of qang as one parameterized job and as a qiskit\-machine\-learning SamplerQNN\. Every prediction was committed before its run and is reported as passed or failed\. On ten five\-qubit weight\-1 circuits the filter lowers the error of the measured qgZ = ⟨Z⟩ from 0\.065\-0\.104 to 0\.018\-0\.023 on three devices \(0\.013\-0\.022 with read\-out correction\), and filter plus zero\-noise extrapolation reaches 0\.011\-0\.015\. On seventeen further devices never used before, the filter cuts the error 2\.4\-8\.9× on every one \(median 3\.1× with read\-out correction\)\. Choosing the qubits by the predicted kept fraction keeps 6\-17 points more shots than Qiskit&\#x27;s default layout on chain circuits, avoids a qubit published as reading 1 always, and the shot planner met a 0\.02 target precision on three devices\. For classification the gain is small: the QNN recovers 5\-6 of the 10 decisions noise costs it\. Reading a known five\-component principal\-component spectrum out of the data&\#x27;s density matrix in the weight\-1 sector, filter plus read\-out correction lowers the error 4\.6× \(median over twenty\-one devices\) and orders the spectrum correctly on 18 of 21 \(13 raw\)\. In Trotter simulation of the XY chain the filter halves the best error and moves the optimal number of Trotter steps deeper \(16 of 21 devices\), and in a quantum walk it turns a raw spreading indistinguishable from a classical random walk \(exponent 0\.87\) into ballistic spreading \(1\.84\)\. With two or three interacting excitations \(XXZ chain\) the gain falls from 5\.6× to 2\.6×: larger sectors let more of the noise through\. Two interoperability bugs were found, one in qiskit\-machine\-learning and one in the library\. All results are on device noise models; no hardware run is reported\.
