# ANN\-GA\-based optimization of a hybrid battery thermal regulation system with helical liquid cooling, PCM, and copper foam

**Autores:** Sonia Naghizadeh Mahjoub; Mohammad Vajdi; Kourosh Vaferi; Farhad Sadegh Moghanlou; Ebrahim Abdi Aghdam

**Data:** 2026-10-14 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Effective thermal management is essential for ensuring the safety, performance, and longevity of Lithium\-ion batteries \(LIBs\)\. In this study, an optimized hybrid Battery Thermal Regulation System \(BTRS\) is proposed, integrating helical liquid cooling mini\-channels and phase change material \(PCM\) embedded in a copper foam matrix \(CPCM\) to enhance heat dissipation\. To efficiently explore the complex design space, a coupled framework integrating numerical simulations, a Genetic Algorithm \(GA\), and Artificial Neural Networks \(ANN\) was developed\. The approach successfully identified an ideal configuration \(OCD1\) characterized by a 10 mm PCM thickness, a 0\.08 m/s inlet velocity, and a \\\(65^\\circ \\\) helical angle\. Under a severe 3C discharge condition, this optimal design achieved a maximum temperature reduction of 21\.27 K, representing an 82\.25% improvement over a standalone cell\. Furthermore, the optimized hybrid system demonstrated superior cooling efficacy compared to individual strategies, outperforming case A \(liquid cooling\) by 33\.67%, case B \(pure PCM\) by 69\.35%, and case C \(CPCM\) by 45%\. The developed ANN model exhibited high predictive accuracy, with R 2 values exceeding 0\.99 for both thermal responses\. These findings provide a high fidelity and scalable framework for advanced thermal management of next\-generation electric vehicle battery systems\.
