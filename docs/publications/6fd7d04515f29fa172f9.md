# INTER\-SECTORAL TRANSFORMATION PROCESSES AND INNOVATIVE DEVELOPMENT MECHANISMS IN THE CONTEXT OF THE DIGITAL ECONOMY

**Autores:** Shoymardonov Akhrorjon

**Data:** 2026-10-13 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

This study analyzes the transformation of economic sectors driven by digital technologies\. It aims to determine how artificial intelligence, big data, cloud technologies and the Internet of Things \(IoT\) affect the economy and to develop recommendations for making the transformation more effective\. The study uses systems analysis, comparison, statistical analysis, logical analysis and synthesis\. The results show that digital transformation enhances the competitiveness of enterprises, ensures efficient use of resources, creates new business models and accelerates innovation\. At the same time, problems related to workforce skills, infrastructure development and cybersecurity were identified, and recommendations for overcoming them are provided\.
