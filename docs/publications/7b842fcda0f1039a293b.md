# Analysis and Modelling of Faults in Solar Power Plant

**Autores:** Emilija Bareikaitė; Aušrinė Budzevičiūtė; Rūta Levulienė

**Data:** 2026-10-13 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Considering the rapid increase in electricity consumption and the necessity to transition to sustainable energy sources, the reliable operation of solar power plants has become particularly crucial\. This study analyses time\-series data from an active solar power plant, consisting of electrical parameters recorded every 5 minutes and corresponding fault labels\. The primary focus is on the investigation of anomalies and the development of significant covariates that contribute to power plant failures\. Subsequently, these variables are utilized in logistic regression models to predict operational disruptions, with the best model achieving an accuracy of 96\.5%\. The study is further enhanced by the application of a machine learning technique – random forests – which attained an accuracy of 98\.4%\. This research contributes to the more effective management of solar power plants, and aids in ensuring their reliable operation over the long term\.
