# Coraij, Jori: Perspective

**Autores:** 

**Data de descoberta:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** unknown

## Descrição do feed

This account argues that perspective, the irreducible situatedness of a position in a relational field, follows from constitutive relational ontology rather than from organizational complexity\. Under Ontic Structural Realism, situatedness is what relational structure requires by virtue of being relational\. Organizational complexity explains how this minimal situatedness becomes differentiated and integrated into what we recognize as conscious experience\. Five organizational conditions for perspectival integration are proposed and distinguished from existing accounts in integrated information theory, predictive processing, higher\-order thought theories, and global workspace theory\. Philosophical defeaters and empirical falsifiers are specified\. The ontological foundation of this account dissolves the presupposed gap between organized structure and perspectival character as formulated by the hard problem of consciousness, without claiming to explain why there is experience at all\.
