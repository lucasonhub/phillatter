# A synthetic\-data and machine\-learning digital shadow foundation for end\-of\-life magnet recycling

**Autores:** Nikolaos Sifakis; Alexandros Chachalis; George Tsinarakis; Maria Aryblia; Dimitrios Cholidis; George Arampatzis

**Data:** 2026-10-14 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

ABSTRACT Digital twins need data that are physically consistent, temporally realistic and honest about uncertainty\. For end\-of\-life permanent\-magnet recycling, central to rare\-earth supply security yet poorly characterised, they are absent\. Generated data can substitute, but resemblance, utility and privacy criteria are defined against the missing data themselves, so a synthetic foundation cannot be certified\. This study builds one for an eleven\-process neodymium\-iron\-boron and ferrite recycling chain\. The generator combines autoregressive noise, dual seasonality, a wear trend, noisy conversion ratios and two\-pass mass\-balance conservation over 500 replicates\. Seven structural fits recover the configured ratios to within 0\.11% \(R² 0\.979\-0\.999\), and 85\.4% of an 82\-model screen satisfy five diagnostic criteria \(best\-per\-process R² 0\.24\-0\.98\)\. Four machine\-learning families all degrade mean R² \(0\.000\-0\.041\), as a linear generating process predicts; this linearity\-recovery test is proposed as a within\-synthetic criterion of internal consistency, not of external validity\. Chain demand is 442\.1 kWh per hour and energy intensity 9\.58 kWh per kilogram of magnet product against 1\.19 for ferrite; no saving against primary neodymium is claimed, the pellet&\#x27;s composition being unresolved\. Relative dispersion rises from 3\.46% to a 4\.62\-4\.69% plateau, so conservation bounds variance propagation\. Its cost follows in closed form: renormalisation attenuates each stage&\#x27;s injected dispersion by a factor set by its conversion ratios, producing the near\-unit coefficients of determination and erasing the specified seasonal and wear\-trend structure\. With near\-uniform dispersion, a binary\-knapsack reallocation of the control budget funds high\-throughput upstream stages, not the energy\-dominant Furnace: the uncertainty and energy layers order the chain oppositely\.
