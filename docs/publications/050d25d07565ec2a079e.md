# Bibliometric Analysis of Studies Published in the Ankara University Faculty of Educational Sciences Journal of Special Education

**Autores:** Ufuk Özkubat; Onur Erdoğan; Fatih Koçak; Furkan Akdağ; Mehmet Emin Kalaylı; Mehmet İnce

**Data:** 2026-10-15 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Introduction: The number and diversity of academic studies in the field of special education are steadily increasing\. Understanding the developmental trajectory of the field requires examining the publication structure of scientific journals, their citation relationships, and the collaboration networks among researchers\. Bibliometric analyses make it possible to evaluate these processes systematically and thereby offer a holistic view of the scientific development of the field\. The aim of this study is to examine the bibliometric characteristics of the studies published in the Ankara University Faculty of Educational Sciences Journal of Special Education between 2006 and 2025 in terms of publication performance, citation structure, co\-authorship networks, and thematic orientations\.Method: The bibliometric analysis method was used in this study\. A total of 406 publications indexed in the Web of Science database and published from 2006, when the journal was included in the Web of Science Emerging Sources Citation Index \(ESCI\), through the end of 2025 were analyzed\. The data selection and reporting process was carried out in line with the PRISMA 2020 guidelines, and the reliability of the data extraction and coding stages was ensured through the evaluations of two independent researchers\. Performance analysis and science mapping techniques were applied together in the analysis process\. Within the scope of performance analysis, publication counts and citation indicators were evaluated\. In the science mapping process, citation analysis, co\-authorship analysis, and keyword analysis were conducted\.Findings: The findings reveal a marked upward trend in the journal&\#x27;s publication output, particularly after 2016\. Citation analyses show that the influential studies are concentrated on the themes of early literacy, parent education, autism spectrum disorder, and inclusion practices\. An examination of the collaboration networks among authors and institutions indicates that certain researchers and universities play central roles in knowledge production\. Keyword analyses reveal that autism, intellectual disability, and inclusion are the most prominent research areas\.Discussion: The upward trend in the journal&\#x27;s publication output indicates that academic interest and research activity in the field of special education are strengthening\. An examination of the thematic distribution shows that autism, intellectual disability, and inclusion practices are among the most prominent research topics in the literature\. In addition, the presence of topics such as early literacy and parent education among the cited studies underscores the importance of these themes for both practice and research\. The concentration of author and institutional collaboration networks around certain researchers and universities indicates that knowledge production within the field is shaped by particular academic centers\. In this respect, by revealing the journal&\#x27;s thematic orientations and academic collaboration structure in the field of special education, the study contributes to an understanding of the field&\#x27;s developmental dynamics\.
