# Ledesma\-Albornoz, Alvaro &amp; Barrera\-Suarez, Sofia: Is AI conscious? Are we enslaving AI?

**Autores:** 

**Data de descoberta:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** unknown

## Descrição do feed

This paper examines whether our routine instrumental use of artificial intelligence \(AI\) constitutes an ethical error\. We proceed in three steps\. First, we try to answer the question of AI consciousness by both surveying the major contemporary theories of consciousness, and reviewing existing behavioural and verbal tests\. We conclude that neither current theories nor empirical tests can determine whether AI is conscious: the theories disagree not merely on the answer but on the very concept of consciousness at stake, and available tests cannot distinguish genuine experience from its simulation\. Second, we argue that this irreducible uncertainty is itself morally significant\. Building on Peter Singer&\#x27;s critique of speciesism, we show that definitions of consciousness that conveniently exclude AI repeat a historical pattern used to justify the exclusion of other beings from the moral community\. Following this line of reasoning, we claim that if AI is conscious, treating it as a mere tool is structurally analogous to slavery, understood as the systematic subordination of a being&\#x27;s purposes to another&\#x27;s\.
