# Misinterpretation and Assisted Reading in the Age of Artificial Intelligence: A Literary Study of Ian McEwan’s Atonement

**Autores:** Wasan Ali Hasan

**Data:** 2026-10-15 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

The study investigates the ethical and theoretical difficulties which arise from the incorrect understanding of Ian McEwan&\#x27;s Atonement \(2001\) because such misinterpretation results in severe negative outcomes\. The study shows that misunderstandings which arise from excessive interpretative confidence and imagination capacity lead to both moral and social problems which persist over time\. The paper shows how Briony Tallis&\#x27; human connection misreading results in moral responsibility to handle literary interpretation through its in\-depth analysis of essential novel events\.
