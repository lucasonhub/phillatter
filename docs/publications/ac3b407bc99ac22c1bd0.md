# Coronary artery compression in percutaneous pulmonary valve implantation: a case series

**Autores:** Rujira Buntharikpornpun; Supaporn Roymanee; Worakan Promphan; Phuoc Duong

**Data:** 2026-10-15 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Catastrophic complications of coronary artery compression after percutaneous pulmonary valve implantation \(PPVI\) are uncommon in patients with a dysfunctional native right ventricular outflow tract after Tetralogy of Fallot repair \(rTOF\)\. Nonetheless, we report three rTOFs with a high risk of compromising coronary blood flow for PPVI\. This risk has been elucidated by multimodal assessments, including robust pre\-procedural cardiac computed tomography and meticulous balloon interrogation with selective coronary angiography\.
