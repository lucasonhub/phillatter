# Simulação computacional aplicada à avaliação de desempenho de iluminação integrativa

**Autores:** Flávia Alexandrino de Mattos; Cintia Akemi Tamura; Eduardo Leite Krüger

**Data:** 2026-10-09 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Desynchronization with light\-dark cycles caused by inadequate exposure to natural light can result in disruptions ranging from changes in sleep\-wake cycles to impairments in neurological, immunological, and metabolic functions\. In this context, circadian metrics emerge, establishing environmental lighting parameters as references for circadian homeostasis\. This study evaluated the accuracy of computer simulations in predicting the circadian potential of different environmental lighting configurations\. Results obtained with ClimateStudio v\.2\.1 software were compared to on\-site measurements conducted in a climate chamber in Karlsruhe, Germany\. Equivalent Melanopic Lux \(EML\) values for predicted and measured data showed higher congruence under electric lighting conditions, while natural lighting scenarios exhibited greater discrepancy\. This result highlights the difficulty of basing circadian metric calculations on variations in weather conditions and occupant behavior, reinforcing the need for further research into integrative lighting\.
