# Eye, Robot: Learning Expressive Gaze Dynamics via a VLM\-Guided Transformer\-VAE

**Autores:** Yuhang Hu; Junlin Lyu; Yuan Fang; Xiaoying Shen; Lechen Zhang; Jiong Lin; Judah Goldfeder; Steven Tian; Hod Lipson

**Data:** 2026-10-14 · **Tipo:** preprint

## Resumo

Gaze coordinates attention in human interaction through coupled eye, head, and eyelid motion\. A humanoid robot must choose where to look while generating a suitable movement toward that target; fixed tracking rules alone do not model the temporal structure of human gaze\. We separate these tasks with an asynchronous vision\-language model that selects a scene referent, a reactive perception module that maintains fixation, and a Realistic Gaze Transformer \(RGT\) that generates target\-conditioned motion\. RGT is a Transformer\-VAE trained on markerless human recordings retargeted to the robot&\#x27;s 11\-DoF joint space: self\-supervised pretraining encodes recent full\-head history, and autoregressive fine\-tuning exposes the model to its own predictions during rollout\. At inference, a four\-dimensional binocular\-eye trajectory conditions coordinated neck and eye motion; an auxiliary blink predictor triggers eyelid templates during playback\. On a two\-second benchmark in which all controllers receive the same ground\-truth target, RGT\-Base obtains the lowest reported overall seven\-joint MSE, and physical demonstrations show the integrated targeting and control pipeline\. The evidence establishes robot\-space kinematic fidelity and real\-time RGT inference; perceived naturalness and open\-vocabulary target\-selection accuracy remain questions for controlled evaluation\.
