## Descrição do feed

We formulate the architectural foundation of Discrete Sequential Superdeterminism \(DSSD\), an emergent physical framework modeling spacetime as an isotropic, discrete cellular network evolving across countable execution epochs\. DSSD addresses causal paradoxes in retrocausal topologies via Periodic\-Gauge Causal Consistency Theorem \(PGCCT\), demonstrating that within a discrete helical manifold, quotiented involution collapses contradictory interventions into an invariant quotient\-class macroscopic Period\-2 limit cycle attractor \(\{\[1\], \[0\],\.\.\.\}\), strictly preserving global thermodynamic parity with zero inter\-epoch mass transport through localized anchor dissipation\. The classical challenge of Poincaré recurrence in bounded finite\-state universes is resolved via Permutation Recurrence Duality Theorem \(PRDT\): while the extended state manifold enforces unconditional kinematic non\-recurrence, the spatial lattice achieves deterministic, infinite aperiodicity across macroscopic iterations when conditionally coupled to algorithmically incompressible boundary digit streams above the critical threshold \(coupling parameter Λ\_c ≈ 0\.892\)\. In the relativistic kinematics sector, Execution\-Budget Framework \(EBF\) establishes computational resource partitioning as a foundational kinematic postulate, from which coordinate time dilation \(dτ = dt / γ\) and continuous Lorentz invariance emerge as infrared limits on the cubic lattice, with Lorentz Invariance Violations suppressed below 10⁻⁷⁰\. Because spacetime geometry and metric tensors are modeled as emergent macroscopic hydrodynamic variables, geometric singularities and micro\-black hole collapse are naturally precluded at the bare cellular scale \(below 10⁻⁵⁴ meters\)\. Inhomogeneous computational processing latency models the weak\-field metric components and recovers relativistic geodesics via the Principle of Extremal Computation\. In the quantum foundations sector, Phase\-Invariant Subroutine Embedding Theorem \(PISET\) introduces an operational microcanonical framework over ancestral Cauchy hypersurfaces\. Utilizing an operational spinor boundary projection, PISET analytically reproduces continuous SU\(2\) singlet correlations E\(a, b\) = −cos\(a − b\) and algebraically saturates the Tsirelson bound \(2√2\) under local\-deterministic dynamics\. This bounds the global setting\-averaged Shannon mutual information to merely 0\.0271 bits—refuting conspiratorial fine\-tuning while establishing the operational foundation for an ab\-initio combinatorial completion\. In the gravitational and cosmological sectors, Margolus Number\-Conserving Reversible Cellular Automaton \(NCRCA\) dynamics enforce discrete 4\-current continuity, yielding covariant stress\-energy conservation in the continuum limit\. Cauchy surface projection stereology proves boundary cell intersection density to be strictly orientation\-invariant, establishing the constitutive dictionary mapping Newton&\#x27;s gravitational constant directly to cellular bit capacity: G = c³ · l\_sub\-P² / \[4 ln\(2\) ℏ\]\. Vanishing irreversible dissipation on stationary local Rindler horizons saturates Landauer&\#x27;s bound, deriving the Clausius equation of state and establishing an emergent thermodynamic pathway toward the non\-linear Einstein Field Equations\. Finally, evaluating the sub\-Planckian boundary impedance kernel through full numerical Boltzmann line\-of\-sight integration \(CAMB\) yields a theoretical forward\-model prediction of a high\-multipole CMB power deficit of \~ \-2\.4% \(−2\.44% in the core resonance window ℓ ∈ \[3000, 4000\], and −2\.40% across the full CMB\-S4 window ℓ ∈ \[2500, 4500\]\), demonstrating that last\-scattering projection smearing dampens high\-frequency ripples and establishing explicit falsification benchmarks for forthcoming CMB\-S4 and LiteBIRD surveys\. \(direct link \)
