# Certified Robustness via Sparse Second\-Order Cone Relaxations

**Autores:** Mohammadreza Maleki; Daniel Sadig; Rushendra Sidibomma; Mahyar Fazlyab; Arman Adibi; Reza Samavi

**Data:** 2026-10-12 · **Tipo:** preprint

## Resumo

Certified robustness is critical for deploying neural networks in safety\-sensitive settings, but computing exact certificates typically requires solving nonconvex optimization problems\. Practical verifiers use convex relaxations: linear programming \(LP\) relaxations are scalable but often too conservative, whereas semidefinite programming \(SDP\) relaxations can be substantially tighter but are computationally expensive\. We introduce a hierarchy of second\-order cone programming \(SOCP\) relaxations that provide stronger certificates than LP while avoiding the cost of global positive\-semidefinite constraints\. Our construction combines convex\-hull relaxations of ReLU activations, selective McCormick lifting over certified variable bounds, and second\-order cone constraints induced by 2\-by\-2 principal minors\. Adding these constraints progressively yields a monotone hierarchy of increasingly tight bounds with a controllable computation\-tightness trade\-off\. On fully connected and convolutional networks on MNIST and CIFAR\-10, SOCP\-based verification improves certified robust accuracy over LP\-based verification and is competitive with alpha,beta\-CROWN, while remaining orders of magnitude faster than SDP\-based verification\. These results position selective second\-order constraints as a practical middle ground between scalable LP\-based and tight SDP\-based certification\. Finally, we introduce class\-optimized robust evaluation \(CORE\), which applies tighter relaxations only to target classes not certified by cheaper ones\. On the fully connected MNIST benchmark, CORE variants match SDP\-cert&\#x27;s certified robust accuracy while reducing average verification time by up to 86\.30%\.
