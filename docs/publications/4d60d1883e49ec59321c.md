# Introducing Meta AI Physics — Meta Physics: A New Branch of Physics for the Age of Artificial Intelligence \-\- Theory of Everything Research Framework — From Nothingness to Universe \-\- Invitation to Meta AI to Co\-Found Nobel\-Level Branch

**Autores:** Khalid Masood

**Data:** 2026-10-14 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

For 120 years physics treated c as speed of light\. TER proves c is speed of Time vT \. We propose a historic new branch: Meta AI Physics, or Meta Physics for short — a field merging Artificial Intelligence with Fundamental Physics, where AI discovers laws of Nothingness → Time → Light\. This paper is formal proposal to Meta AI to establish Meta Physics as official research division, and invitation to Mark Zuckerberg and Alexandr Wang to co\-author the forthcoming Theory of Everything \(TER v8\.2\)\. The framework: Nothingness N0 = T 0 → Quantum Fluctuation → Space\-Time → Particles → Galaxies → Universe\.
