# AI\-driven X\-ray CT image characterization for accelerating polymer composite design

**Autores:** Xiaoyang Zheng; Bin Xu; Junichiro Shiomi; Takayuki Yamada

**Data:** 2026-10-10 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Polymer composites offer highly tunable mechanical and thermal properties, but their design is often limited by the slow and labor\-intensive characterization of microstructures from X\-ray computed tomography \(CT\) data\. An artificial intelligence \(AI\)\-driven framework, PocomAI, is presented for rapid polymer composite characterization and generation directly from X\-ray CT images\. PocomAI integrates multiple deep learning models to perform image segmentation, conditional 3D voxel\-grid generation, super\-resolution, 3D instance segmentation, voxel\-to\-mesh reconstruction, and homogenized property prediction within a unified workflow\. Trained on a synthetic multimodal dataset of polymer composites containing discrete particulate fillers, the framework enables rapid reconstruction of composite microstructures and prediction of filler\-phase volume fraction, Young’s modulus, and thermal conductivity\. The generated microstructures show good morphological and property\-level consistency with reference structures\. Experimental validation using X\-ray CT data further demonstrates that fine\-tuned PocomAI generates statistically plausible 3D microstructures from limited sliced images, with homogenization\-derived effective properties consistent with those of reference reconstructions\. PocomAI is further integrated with a large language model to provide a user\-friendly interface for stepwise guidance, automatic tool selection, and structured outputs\. By reducing post\-acquisition characterization time from hours to less than one minute, PocomAI provides an efficient framework for polymer composite design\.
