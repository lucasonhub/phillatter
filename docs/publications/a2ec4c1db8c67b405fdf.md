# Disease Duration Differentiates Freezing of Gait Response to Subthalamic and Nigral Stimulation for Parkinson&\#x27;s Disease in an Exploratory Multicenter Controlled Trial

**Autores:** Daniel Weiß; Idil Cebi; Marion Schütt; Imma Fischer; Lukas Pavelka; Pierre Luc Kolber; Monika Pötter‐Nerger; Wolfgang Hamel; Andrea A\. **Data:** 2026-10-12 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

BACKGROUND: Freezing of gait \(FoG\) belongs to most pressing unmet symptoms in the treatment of Parkinson&\#x27;s disease\. OBJECTIVES: To explore whether combined subthalamic and nigral \(STN \+ SNr\) deep brain stimulation improves FoG compared with optimized STN stimulation alone; to identify clinical variables associated with therapeutic response\. METHODS: In this exploratory multicenter randomized controlled double\-blind trial, we recruited patients from our existing cohorts and randomized them 1:1 to STN or STN \+ SNr\. After baseline, patients were followed with close interim visits \(visits 1\-5\) for 90 days \(visit 6\)\. The Freezing of Gait Assessment Course \(FoG\-AC\) was the primary endpoint and was evaluated at P &lt; 0\.2 with exploratory intent\. Secondary endpoints assessed responder rates, treatment dynamics, predictors of response, and patient preferences\. RESULTS: At V6, STN \(n = 23\) and STN \+ SNr \(n = 22\) FoG\-AC outcomes did not differ significantly\. We identified responders with clinically meaningful FoG improvement in both the STN \(n = 7\) and STN \+ SNr \(n = 8\) groups\. In STN \+ SNr responders, mean disease duration was \~12 years, which was \~4\.5 years shorter than in non\-responders, but there were no differences in motor and cognitive functions\. Treatment dynamics suggested that FoG responders improved slowly and gradually\. The STN \+ SNr responder group showed numerically stronger FoG improvement compared with STN responders\. Additionally, 15 STN compared to 10 STN \+ SNr patients wished to change treatment after the RCT phase and before unblinding\. Both STN and STN \+ SNr treatment was safe\. CONCLUSIONS: Future trials evaluating adjunctive STN \+ SNr stimulation should stratify patients by disease duration to optimize outcomes\.
