# Administrative Law Innovation For Automated Artificial Intelligence Social Assistance Governance

**Autores:** Azilfi Fatma; Abelia Rahma Ananda; Syaita Novidia; Fathia Dwi Maharani; Melyana Melyana; Wandari Syahputri

**Data:** 2026-10-18 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

General Background The integration of digital technologies into government systems aims to optimize public service precision and national resource distribution\. Specific Background The INA Digital portal accelerates the distribution of safety nets in Indonesia using automated decision\-making systems to minimize targeting errors and prevent state budget leakage\. Knowledge Gap Although computational processing offers efficiency, the existing regulatory framework experiences a significant vacuum regarding algorithmic auditing mechanisms, the obligation to provide logical explanations, and digital objection schemes\. Aims This study analyzes the readiness of national administrative jurisprudence instruments to govern the utilization of electronic agents in determining welfare recipient eligibility\. Results Normative legal analysis reveals that current legislations are inadequate to independently oversee automated machine decisions\. The absence of human verification within the data matching system risks violating the General Principles of Proper Governance and triggers bureaucratic authority defects\. Novelty This research specifically proposes the formulation of governmental algorithmic accountability regulations that mandate human intervention principles within legally binding digital policies\. Implications Establishing new legislative instruments coordinated by relevant institutions is essential to guarantee system operational transparency, jurisprudential certainty, and the constitutional rights protection of affected citizens\. Highlights Bureaucratic decision\-making without human verification triggers potential violations of jurisprudential certainty principles\. Existing regulations lack necessary mechanisms for digital objections and public computational system audits\. The state requires new legislative instruments mandating operational transparency for integrated public service portals\. Keywords Administrative Law Innovation; Artificial Intelligence Governance; Automated Decision Making; INA Digital Portal; Algorithmic Accountability
