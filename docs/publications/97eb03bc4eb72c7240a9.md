# Composite power quality disturbance identification method considering confidence correction

**Autores:** Biaolong Su; Dashi Luo; Shuangyin Dai; Bo Dong; Yi Wang; Fan Ni

**Data:** 2026-10-15 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

To effectively identify multi\-source composite power quality disturbance \(PQD\) signals in distribution networks, this paper proposes a novel PQD recognition method based on a Fully Convolutional Network\-Gated Recurrent Unit\-Attention \(FCN\-GRU\-Attention\) architecture combined with a confidence correction mechanism\. Composite PQDs, formed by the simultaneous coupling of transient and continuous disturbances, exhibit severe feature overlap and masking effect, making key discriminative features easily submerged\. To tackle this challenge, a three\-dimensional synergistic framework is constructed: the FCN extracts multi\-scale spatial and temporal\-frequency representations from raw time\-series signals without manual feature extraction; the Gated Recurrent Unit \(GRU\) models long\-term dynamic evolution characteristics; and the Attention mechanism adaptively weights critical transient disturbance windows\. Furthermore, a disturbance recognition enhancement mechanism using explicit and implicit confidence factors is designed to adaptively refine multi\-label confidence predictions, enabling the model to achieve superior generalization to unseen composite disturbances beyond the training distribution\.
