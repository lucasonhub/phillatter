# Comparative evaluation of plaque removal efficacy of manual and electric interdental brushes around orthodontic brackets: A randomized clinical study

**Autores:** Prachi Agarwal; Vijay Agarwal; Hari Narayan Choudhary; Pallavi Goswami; Bhavya Gupta; Kanika Agarwal

**Data:** 2026-10-17 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Introduction: Fixed orthodontic appliances increase plaque accumulation owing to the presence of brackets and arch wires, making oral hygiene maintenance difficult and increasing the risk of gingivitis and enamel demineralization\. Adjunctive interdental cleaning tools, including interdental brushes \(IDBs\), are recommended\.Aim and Objectives: To compare the plaque removal efficacy of manual and electric interdental brushes around orthodontic brackets\.Materials and Methods: A randomized crossover controlled trial was conducted on 60 patients \(aged 12–35 years\) undergoing fixed orthodontic treatment\. Participants were allocated into two groups \(n = 30 each\): Group I used a manual IDB \(Stim IDB\) and Group II \(U Brush\) used an electric IDB\. Dental plaque was disclosed and scored across eight classified buccal tooth surface zones per tooth at two time intervals \(T1 and T2, four weeks apart\), using a binary plaque index\. The Mann–Whitney U test and Wilcoxon signed\-rank test were applied for intergroup and intragroup comparisons, respectively \(p &lt; 0\.05\)\.Results: The two IDBs showed statistically significant plaque reduction at T1 and T2 levels \(p &lt; 0\.001\)\. However, the electric IDB showed a significantly higher degree of plaque reduction in all surface regions compared to the manual IDB\. The mean plaque reduction around brackets was twice that of the manual IDB \(0\.708 ± 0\.045 vs 0\.353 ± 0\.128\)\.Conclusion: It can be concluded that manual as well as electric interdental brushes both have the capacity to decrease the plaque deposits around the orthodontic brackets\. Electric IDB proved to be much better in plaque removal especially in the area around the brackets\.
