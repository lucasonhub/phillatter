# Algorithmic Anxiety and Trust Breakdown in Automated Health and Financial Triage

**Autores:** Saha Aritro

**Data:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** book

## Resumo

The rapid deployment of conversational artificial intelligence and automated decision\-tree gatekeepers across consumer healthcare and retail financial services has fundamentally reorganized how individuals access life\-critical triage\. While institutional operators justify automated triage through the dual imperatives of labor containment and rapid routing, consumer interactions frequently degenerate into acute socio\-technical friction\. In this paper, we formalize and empirically investigate algorithmic anxiety—the profound psychological distress, perceived institutional entrapment, and anticipatory helplessness experienced by individuals subjected to opaque, automated gatekeepers in high\-stakes domains\. We execute a large\-scale computational netnography analyzing N = 7,140 verified, unstructured user narratives drawn from seven public grievance ecosystems spanning 2021 to 2026, including Reddit specialized clinical and financial forums, mobile application store reviews, Consumer Financial Protection Bureau \(CFPB\) regulatory complaints, HackerNews, and patient advocacy boards\. Leveraging an inductive\-deductive 3\-Stage Hybrid Neuro\-Symbolic qualitative coding ensemble \(coupling local open\-weights SLMs Meta Llama 3\.2 1B and Alibaba Qwen 2\.5 1\.5B with a deterministic lexical rubric router\) achieving publication\-grade inter\-rater reliability \(kappa = 0\.824, alpha = 0\.821\) validated against an expert human benchmark \(Author test\-retest stability: kappa = 0\.894, alpha = 0\.888\), we construct an eight\-dimensional taxonomy of trust breakdown comprising six core failure modes and two newly discovered emergent modes: Asynchronous Limbo and Biometric/Authentication Lockouts\. Cross\-domain statistical testing demonstrates significant domain\-specific asymmetries \(chi\-square = 206\.62, p &lt; 0\.001, Cramer&\#x27;s V = 0\.170\), with healthcare triage predominantly plagued by performative empathy and opaque clinical rejections, whereas financial triage is dominated by recursive containment loops and gatekeeping dark patterns\. Kaplan\-Meier survival modeling and Cox proportional hazards regressions reveal that legacy opaque rule\-trees and unconstrained conversational agents induce median user abandonment at Turns 5\.0 and 7\.0, accelerating abandonment hazards by 2\.84x and 1\.96x relative to transparent baselines\. Finally, we translate our empirical findings into an actionable systems blueprint—the Glass\-Box Triage Architecture—incorporating deterministic state\-handoff schemas, entropy\-based circuit breakers, and calibrated tone modulation to restore human agency in automated service delivery\.
