# Is there a future for leadership in a world of AI?

**Autores:** Stan Skipworth

**Data:** 2026-10-15 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Over the last few years, the ever‐accelerating world of artificial intelligence has seemingly established a stepping‐off point from technological advances as we have understood them\. Now, AI offers the possibility of significant milestones that represent automation in ways we do not yet fully understand; AI is driving us to a future that is uncertain\.
