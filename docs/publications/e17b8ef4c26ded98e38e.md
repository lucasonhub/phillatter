# Artificial Intelligence in University Education: Exploring the Attitudes of Science Education Students in Makurdi, Nigeria

**Autores:** Samuel OKANCHE; Benedict Ojedor Onah

**Data:** 2026-10-10 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

The study investigated Artificial Intelligence in University Education: Exploring the Attitudes of Science Education Students in Makurdi, Nigeria\. The study employed a descriptive survey research design\. The population comprised 670 \(374 males and 296 females\) 400\-level science education students of Rev\. Fr\. Moses Orshio Adasu University and Joseph Sarwuan Tarka University for the 2025/2026 academic session\. A sample size of 237 \(102 males and 135 females\) students was selected using purposive sampling technique\. The instrument for data collection was &quot;Students&\#x27; Attitude toward Artificial Intelligence Integration Questionnaire \(SAAIIQ\)\.&quot; Validated by two experts in Science Education\. Cronbach&\#x27;s Alpha was used to determine the reliability coefficient of the instrument, and the reliability coefficient obtained was 0\.85\. Two research questions and one hypothesis was tested\. Descriptive statistics of mean and standard deviation were used for answering the research questions, while inferential statistics of independent t\-test was used for testing the hypothesis at 0\.05 level of significance\. The findings revealed that science education students exhibited a positive attitude toward the integration of Al in universities with a cluster mean of 3\.14\. The study also found no significant difference between the attitudes of male and female students toward Al integration\. Students demonstrated a moderate perception toward the integration of Al in universities with a cluster mean of 2\.98\. The study concluded that students generally accept the use of AI in university, adequate infrastructure, institutional support, and training are necessary for its effective implementation\.
