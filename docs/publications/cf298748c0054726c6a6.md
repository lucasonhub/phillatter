# Assessing the overheating resilience of a novel earth walled \(CobBauge\) building across different climate zones in Europe using a calibrated building performance simulation model

**Autores:** Halat Alnaqshabandy; Matthew Fox; Steve Goodhew

**Data:** 2026-10-14 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

CobBauge is a dual\-layer earthen construction material, optimized to meet Part\-L of the Building Regulations in England and Wales\. A classroom constructed with CobBauge demonstrated high resilience to overheating during two heatwaves in Plymouth, highlighting its potential as a sustainable and ultra\-low\-carbon material\. This study assesses its overheating resilience using a simulation model, calibrated to achieve 7\.30% CV\(RMSE\) and 1\.34°C RMSE against measured indoor temperatures\. Plymouth&\#x27;s future climate and nine European locations representing different Köppen climate classifications were incorporated into the model to analyse the building&\#x27;s thermal performance and its compliance with CIBSE TM52 criteria\. Results showed no overheating risk under Plymouth&\#x27;s future climate\. Similar performance was observed across all European locations except Murcia, where outdoor temperatures reached 42°C\. Nevertheless, indoor temperatures remained 9°C below outdoor peaks without active cooling\. This study recommends CobBauge as an ultra\-low\-carbon and resilient material to mitigate indoor overheating across diverse European climates\.
