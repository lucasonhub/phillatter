# Quality monitoring of the lautering process in microbreweries using machine learning

**Autores:** Carolina Rosseti Matheus; Rodrigo Nicoletti; André Luís Dias; Jean Carlos Rodrigues da Silva

**Data:** 2026-10-12 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Industry 4\.0 and artificial intelligence offer opportunities to improve the operation of small and medium\-sized enterprises, including microbreweries, which often rely on limited automation and are subject to operational failures\. Intelligent anomaly diagnosis can contribute to process reliability and product quality\. This study investigates the use of machine learning to monitor the lautering process through operational data from a lautering pump for fault diagnosis purposes\. The results showed that torque, current, and power factor exhibited consistent dynamic patterns associated with the lautering stage operation\. Although turbidity and dry mass were affected by the operating conditions, their relationship with pump speed was not consistent across all bed configurations\. A Support Vector Machine classifier was developed to identify normal and anomalous lautering conditions\. The model achieved 98\.1% accuracy, with 98\.9% specificity in fault detection\. These results demonstrate that electrical variables obtained from the frequency inverter can capture relevant dynamic changes in the lautering process and support low\-cost, non\-invasive real\-time monitoring of filtration anomalies in microbreweries\.
