# Enhancing physiological performance and antioxidant defense in Mexican lime \(Citrus aurantifolia\) under water\-deficit: synergistic effects of PGPB and KNO₃ application

**Autores:** Soraya Moallaye Mazraei; Esmaeil Khaleghi; Naeimeh Enayatizamir

**Data:** 2026-10-13 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Drought stress severely restricts citrus seedling growth, necessitating sustainable strategies to mitigate water\-deficit damage\. This study investigated the synergistic effects of plant growth promoting bacteria \(PGPB\) and foliar application of potassium nitrate \(KNO₃\) on the physiological performance and antioxidant defense mechanisms of Mexican lime \( Citrus aurantifolia \) under water\-deficit conditions\. The experiment was conducted using a randomized complete block design \(RCBD\) with three irrigation levels \(100%, 70%, and 40% of crop evapotranspiration, ETc\), five bacterial treatments \( Enterobacter cloacae E1, Bacillus subtilis CS1, Enterobacter cloacae R33, a bacterial consortium \(COB\), and a non\-inoculated control\), and KNO 3 foliar spray \(0 and 2% w/v\)\. Physiological traits, osmotic adjustment indicators, and antioxidant responses were evaluated at 60, 120, and 180 days after treatment application\. Water\-deficit significantly reduced leaf relative water content \(RWC\) and increased electrolyte leakage \(EL\), indicating impaired plant water status\. However, PGPB inoculation, particularly in combination with KNO 3 , effectively mitigated EL under both moderate and severe water\-deficit conditions\. Drought stress also triggered an increase in total soluble carbohydrates \(TSC\) and a concomitant decrease in starch content, reflecting osmotic adjustment\. The combined PGPB \+ KNO 3 treatments further enhanced TSC accumulation and partially restored starch reserves, contributing to improved carbon metabolism stability under water\-limited conditions\. Under severe water\-deficit \(40% ETc\), the combination of Bacillus subtilis CS1 and 2% KNO 3 resulted in a 49\.8% increase in catalase \(CAT\) activity, an 83\.4% increase in peroxidase \(POD\) activity, and a 9\.8% increase in total phenolic content compared to the control\. Likewise, Enterobacter cloacae R33 combined with KNO 3 reduced malondialdehyde \(MDA\) content by 24\.1% and increased leaf protein levels\. Total antioxidant capacity \(TAC\) increased by 6\.9% in plants treated with KNO 3 and the COB under severe drought stress\. Under moderate water\-deficit \(70% ETc\), the combined application of KNO 3 and the COB produced the highest flavonoid content\. Overall, these findings indicate that the integration of PGPB with KNO 3 substantially enhances antioxidant enzyme activities and physiological resilience in Mexican lime under water\-deficit\. This integrated approach represents a cost\-effective and environmentally sustainable strategy for mitigating drought\-induced damage and improving crop productivity\.
