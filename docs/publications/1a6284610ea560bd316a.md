# Hendel, Tal: Why interactionist fields are \(almost\) invisible to physics: A phase\-transition account

**Autores:** 

**Data de descoberta:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** unknown

## Descrição do feed

This paper argues that panpsychist and panqualityist theories are particularly well aligned with contemporary fundamental physics when formulated in field\-theoretic terms\. Applied to non\-monistic interactionist versions of these views, this motivates the postulation of a pervasive qualitative/experiential field with its own dynamics and weak local coupling to physical systems, allowing energy and momentum to be exchanged between the two domains\. Such theories face an invisibility problem: why are the effects of the posited psychophysical interaction not ordinarily observed? I propose that the answer lies in a phase transition between disordered and ordered regimes of the field\. In the disordered regime, local psychophysical contributions cancel statistically, preventing macroscopic effects; in the ordered regime, this cancellation breaks down, allowing the same interaction to become macroscopically significant\. Accordingly, departures from the expected physical energy or momentum balance are predicted in the ordered regime\. The transition is argued to be more plausibly understood as a driven non\-equilibrium process than as a conventional equilibrium transition: physical processes provide the drive, while a state variable within the qualitative/experiential field serves as the control parameter and feedback helps sustain the resulting ordered regime\. Consciousness in brains is proposed to depend on their ability to drive and sustain this ordered regime\. Because the field carries energy and momentum, it is expected to be gravitationally active while remaining largely hidden from non\-gravitational detection\. The qualitative/experiential field may therefore contribute to gravitational phenomena currently attributed to dark matter\.
