# ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE IN TEACHING NATURAL SCIENCE AND MATHEMATICS DISCIPLINES: SURVEY RESULTS ON TEACHERS&\#x27; AND STUDENTS&\#x27; READINESS, BARRIERS AND NEEDS

**Autores:** Violetta Hurchonok

**Data:** 2026-10-11 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

The article presents the results of a survey of 99 teachers of natural science and mathematics disciplines and 195 students in grades 7–11 in Poltava region on the use of AI tools in the educational process\. It was found that 96% of teachers and 93\.8% of students already use AI, but 64\.6% of students did so without any pedagogical guidance\. The main systemic barrier for teachers is work overload \(66\.7%\), despite a strong willingness to learn \(81\.8%\)\. A thematic analysis of students&\#x27; open\-ended responses revealed a dominant message: AI is used primarily to understand material, not to copy it\. A critical research gap was identified regarding the specific impact of AI on adolescents as a distinct age group\.
