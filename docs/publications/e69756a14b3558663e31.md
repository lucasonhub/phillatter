# Antimicrobial Resistance in Zoonotic Tuberculosis: CurcuminLoaded Polymeric Nanoparticles as a Next\-Generation Therapeutic Strategy

**Autores:** 

**Data:** 2026-10-14 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Resistant tuberculosis is a significant epidemic globally and requires new adjunct treatment measures to be made\.This review focuses on examining curcumin\-polymeric nanoparticles loaded as a host\-directed and synergistic method of managing tuberculosis\.The paper is grounded on the critical analysis of recent preclinical and translational research on nanoparticle formulation, macrophage targeting, intracellular delivery, and combination therapy with first\-line antituberculosis drugs\.The polymer composition, particle size, surface functionalization, drug\-loading efficiency, and release kinetics are some of the key formulation parameters analyzed\.Curcumin\-loaded nanoparticles induce macrophage autophagy by upregulating LC3 and modulating p62, thereby facilitating the enhanced intracellular killing of Mycobacterium tuberculosis in a mechanistic manner\.Simultaneously, when used together with drugs like isoniazid and rifampicin, the intracellular drug accumulation is enhanced, the sustained release is achieved and the synergistic bactericidal activity is observed, leading to significant decreases in intracellular colony\-forming units\.Macrophage models prove to be more effective than free curcumin or drugs on their own, as well as showing better safety profiles\.The results indicate the possible potential of nanoparticle\-mediated delivery of curcumin to solve the shortcomings of traditional therapy\.The next steps are inhalable preparations, standard preparation, and in vivo testing\.To sum up, polymeric nanoparticles with curcumin as an adjunct could become a great solution to improve the outcome of tuberculosis therapy and counteract antimicrobial resistance in every corner of the world\.
