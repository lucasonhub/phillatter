# Biodigital Transformation in Dentistry: From Diagnosis and Biological Preservation to Multidisciplinary Rehabilitation

**Autores:** Gizem Yazdan Özen; Nebiha Hilal Bilge; Feride Aktaş; Bekir Haseki; Yücel Aydemir; Nur Sima Eken; Kübra Aslantaş Akar; Sevgi Yaşar; Ceren Yeniay; Berk Kaya; Nedim Güneş; Ladise Ceylin Has; Mevlüt Furkan Sadeler; Ali Kağan Özen; Nesli Damla Deniz; Muhammed Mustafa Akar; Alptuğ Kendirci; Zehranur Kızıldağ

**Data:** 2026-10-09 · **Tipo:** book

## Resumo

Advances in digital technologies have expanded diagnostic and treatment possibilities in dentistry while making the preservation of biological tissues and collaboration among specialties increasingly important\. This book, \*Biodigital Transformation in Dentistry: From Diagnosis and Biological Preservation to Multidisciplinary Rehabilitation\*, was prepared to address these developments from an integrated perspective\. It follows a scientific progression from biodigital diagnosis and clinical decision\-making to preventive care in pediatric dentistry; from the preservation of the pulp and natural dentition to periodontal and orthodontic treatment; and from digital prosthetic planning to implant\-supported rehabilitation\. Written by contributors from different dental specialties, the chapters examine both the clinical applications and the limitations of current technologies\. We hope this book will serve as a valuable resource for dental students, residents, academics, and clinicians, and we thank all the authors for their knowledge, time, and contributions\.
