# Artificial Intelligence Related Sentiments and Perceptions on Federated Social Networks: A Computational Text Analysis

**Autores:** Sadettin Demirel

**Data:** 2026-10-15 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

This study aims to examine public perceptions of artificial intelligence \(AI\) on federated social networks, specifically Bluesky and Mastodon\. While existing research focuses on centralized platforms, a significant gap exists regarding AI perception within decentralized networks\. This study addresses this gap through computational analysis of AI related posts in Bluesky and Mastodon\. A text\-as\-data approach was employed to analyze 61,285 posts collected from Bluesky and Mastodon between January 1, 2023, and January 1, 2025\. The methodology integrated topic modeling, sentiment analysis, and text analysis to identify prevalent themes, sentiment polarity, and discussion patterns within AI\-related discourse\. Findings demonstrate predominantly positive sentiment towards AI across both platforms, with trust, anticipation, and joy as primary emotional responses, accompanied by underlying fear and anxiety\. Platform\-specific variations emerged: Mastodon discourse centers on creativity, digital art, and societal\-ethical implications including regulation, bias, and disinformation concerns\. Conversely, Bluesky posts emphasize economic and geopolitical dimensions, featuring startups, funding, stock markets, and international policy\. The analysis reveals significant fragmentation in public AI perception, characterized by discourse division into distinct economic and ethical spheres across platforms\. While this fragmentation accommodates diverse social group perspectives, it presents challenges for establishing comprehensive societal consensus on AI governance\. The analytical framework provides insights for understanding decentralized social network dynamics and implications for policy makers examining AI perception in federated digital environments\.
