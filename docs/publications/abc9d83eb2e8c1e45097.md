# AI\-ORIENTED LEADERSHIP AND EMPLOYEE INNOVATIVE WORK BEHAVIOUR: THE ROLE OF PSYCHOLOGICAL EMPOWERMENT AND AI ANXIETY

**Autores:** Qudsia Arshad; Zehra Afreen Sayyid; Ghulam Hussain Wagan

**Data:** 2026-10-10 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

The rapid spread of artificial intelligence \(AI\) in organizations has made employee innovation an important concern, yet many employees remain uncertain or anxious about how AI will affect their work\. Drawing on conservation of resources \(COR\) theory, this study examines how AI\-oriented leadership influences employees&\#x27; innovative work behaviour through psychological empowerment, and whether AI anxiety moderates both the relationship between AI\-oriented leadership and psychological empowerment and the relationship between psychological empowerment and innovative work behaviour\. Data were collected through a three\-wave, time\-lagged survey of 302 employees working in organizations that use AI\-based tools in Pakistan, in the banking, information technology, telecommunications and higher education sectors, and the hypotheses were tested using partial least squares structural equation modelling \(PLS\-SEM\) in SmartPLS 4\. The results show that AI\-oriented leadership has a positive effect on both innovative work behaviour and psychological empowerment, and that psychological empowerment has a positive effect on innovative work behaviour and partially mediates the relationship between AI\-oriented leadership and innovative work behaviour\. AI anxiety weakens the model at both stages, as it reduces the empowerment employees gain from AI\-oriented leaders and also reduces the extent to which empowered employees engage in innovative behaviour\. The study contributes to the leadership literature by treating AI\-oriented leadership as a distinct form of leadership suited to technology\-driven change and by explaining how and when it encourages innovation\. The findings suggest that organizations should develop leaders who support employees in using AI, give employees autonomy in applying AI to their tasks, and address AI\-related fears through open communication and reskilling programmes\. Keywords: AI\-oriented leadership; innovative work behaviour; psychological empowerment; AI anxiety\.
