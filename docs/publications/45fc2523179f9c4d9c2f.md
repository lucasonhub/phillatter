# Predictive Policing dan Negara Hukum: Analisis Yuridis Penggunaan Artificial Intelligence dalam Pencegahan Kejahatan di Indonesia

**Autores:** Abdul Latif

**Data:** 2026-10-10 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Predictive policing based on artificial intelligence is shifting crime prevention from reactive response toward data\-driven risk identification, pattern recognition, and resource prioritization\. This transformation creates a legal dilemma: the same systems that may improve analytical capacity can also amplify historical bias, enable opaque profiling, intrude upon privacy, and influence coercive decisions without adequate procedural safeguards\. This article analyzes the legal position of predictive policing in Indonesia and formulates a rights\-based governance model for police use of artificial intelligence\. The research applies normative juridical analysis using statutory, conceptual, comparative, and policy approaches\. The analysis focuses on the 2022 Personal Data Protection Law, the 2023 Criminal Code, the 2025 Criminal Procedure Code, the 2026 amendment to the Police Law, and Indonesia&\#x27;s emerging national AI governance framework\. The study finds that Indonesia already possesses fragmented legal bases relevant to AI\-supported policing, but lacks a dedicated risk\-based framework that defines prohibited uses, high\-risk systems, human oversight, auditability, contestability, and allocation of responsibility\. The article proposes a four\-layer governance model: constitutional safeguards, statutory legality, AI governance controls, and operational human review\. Predictive outputs should function as decision\-support rather than autonomous grounds for coercive action\.
