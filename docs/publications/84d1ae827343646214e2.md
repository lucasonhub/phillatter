# Bulgaria, Jamel: Unified Psychophysiological Architecture: Scaling the Core Emotion Framework through Neurosensorimotor Integration, Computational AI Dynamics, and Somatic Engineering

## Descrição do feed

This manuscript presents a unified psychophysiological architecture that integrates the Core Emotion Framework \(CEF\) with developmental neurosensorimotor reflex theory, autonomic vestibular modulation, somatic mechanical engineering, and complex systems computational modeling\. Drawing on the tri centric organization of the cephalic, cardiac, and enteric nervous systems, the paper demonstrates that these biological hubs map precisely onto the Ajna, Anahata, and Manipura chakras and the Daoist Upper, Middle, and Lower Dantians\. The analysis reframes the classical yogic Granthis as the physiological consequences of retained primitive reflexes—specifically the Fear Paralysis, Moro, and Asymmetric Tonic Neck Reflexes—showing how these unintegrated circuits generate chronic autonomic dysregulation, myofascial bracing, and operator level emotional fusions\. The manuscript further introduces a mechanistic model of somatic mechanical actuation and vestibular stimulation, illustrating how rotational, linear, and oscillatory movements reorganize brainstem pathways, restore ventral vagal braking, and dissolve pathological attractor basins\. A formal computational treatment using Bloch sphere qubit dynamics, non commuting operator projections, Kuramoto synchronization, and holographic boundary–bulk mappings establishes the CEF as a mathematically tractable and empirically testable paradigm\. Clinical case deconstructions demonstrate how operator fusions manifest in psychopathology and metabolic disorders, while engineering validation protocols outline the safety, fidelity, and ergonomic requirements for somatic mechanical interfaces\. Together, these contributions provide a comprehensive, multi level framework for understanding and modulating the coupled cognitive emotional somatic system, enabling next generation psychophysiological therapies, affect aware AI systems, and integrative consciousness research\.
