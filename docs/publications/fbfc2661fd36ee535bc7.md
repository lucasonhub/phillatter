# Academic Burnout

**Autores:** Haley Rebecca May Toll; Cecile Badenhorst; Heather Skye McLeod

**Data:** 2026-10-14 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Due to institutional pressures within neoliberal university environments, faculty are experiencing heightened burnout\. Exploring ways to reduce burnout in accessible and creative communal ways is significant\. To counter experiences of burnout, two professors and one emerging academic examined the three tenets of the self\-compassion framework through arts\-based inquiry\. We created postcard images, mailed them to one another, and investigated our experiences using Maslach’s Burnout Inventory\. We analyzed written narratives about the artmaking, receiving, and assessment answers within the self\-compassion framework\. We found mail art increased common humanity, self\-kindness, and mindfulness\. We recommend future studies explore self\-compassion\-focused artmaking while integrating assessments\.
