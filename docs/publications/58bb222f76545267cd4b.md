# Artificial intelligence and digital trust: a comparative bibliometric analysis of e\-reputation research in scopus and web of science \(2015\-2025\)

**Autores:** Nouhaila SABIR; Nouhaila EL ACHHAB; Ismail ELBAZI; Abdelmajid CHARRASS; Asmaa Abyre

**Data:** 2026-10-14 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

In the Digital Age, artificial intelligence, consumer trust and e\-reputation are the focus of the factors determining an organization&\#x27;s competitiveness and brand equity\. However, there is still no synthesis on a large number of major of indexing tools that relates technological infrastructure, perceptions and consumer behavior\. This paper analyzes the evolution of studies on AI, e\-reputation and consumer trust from 2015 to 2025 with comparative bibliometric design on Scopus \(n = 541\) and Web of Science \(n = 293\)\. This analysis tracks publication volume by year, measuring prolific authors, publication sources, international co\-authorship and keyword co\-occurrence clusters\. Results show that Scopus is a technological radar focusing on applied algorithms, blockchain verification and machine learning\. Web of Science is a filter consolidating theory\-driven economic concept, consumer behavior, brand management and relational trust\. Consumer trust is the one common bridge that links technological reliability with behavioral commitment\. In the end, this study offers a unified multi\-theoretical lens \(Signaling Theory, the Technology Acceptance Model and Multi\-Dimensional Trust Theory\) to reconcile algorithmic control and relational trust management\.
