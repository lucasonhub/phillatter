# Emerging Role of Artificial Intelligence in High\-Performance Liquid Chromatographic Analysis: A Mini Review

**Autores:** Kalyani Kudupale, Saloni Raut, Saloni Bhoyar, Komal Masaram; Nilesh Gandhare Disha Bandre; Parvez Ali Ashok Shende

**Data:** 2026-10-13 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

High\-performance liquid chromatography \(HPLC\) is one of the most widely employed analytical techniques for the qualitative and quantitative determination of compounds\. Despite its exceptional sensitivity and reliability, conventional HPLC method development and data interpretation often require extensive experimental optimization, expert knowledge, and considerable time\. Furthermore, peak detection, integration, and identification in chromatograms can be automated by artificial intelligence \(AI\) methods like machine learning and deep learning, allowing for quicker and more precise analysis of complicated datasets, resulting in the identification of compounds and the comprehension of complex reactions more quickly and creatively\. From an economic perspective, these developments promote sustainable analytical chemistry and effective resource recovery\. Moreover, AI can increase productivity and reduce the possibility of human error by automating chromatographic analysis\. Additionally, AI has played a key role in enhanced analysis of complicated spectra in fields like spectroscopy and chromatography, as well as in handling heterogeneous data\. This mini review discusses recent advancements in artificial intelligence and machine learning \(ML\) that enable scientists to address complex analytical challenges more effectively\. It highlights the growing application of these technologies in chromatographic predictions, particularly in liquid chromatography method development\. The present review further examines key AI\-driven areas, including automated method development, retention time prediction, peak deconvolution, predictive maintenance, and intelligent data processing\.
