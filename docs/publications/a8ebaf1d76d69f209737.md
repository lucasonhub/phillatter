# Inteligencia artificial y razonamiento clínico en educación médica: una revisión sistemática

**Autores:** Rafael Emiliano Sulca Quispe; Juan Miguel Purilla Janto; María del Rosario Jáuregui\-Figueroa; María del Rosario Figueroa Bejarano De Jauregui

**Data:** 2026-10-12 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Context: Artificial intelligence has transformed learning environments in the health sciences and prompted debate about its role in developing clinical reasoning\. Objective: To analyze the role of artificial intelligence in developing clinical reasoning in medical education\. Methods: A systematic review followed PRISMA 2020 guidelines\. Twenty\-two publications, including primary studies, reviews, and reflective papers, published between 2021 and 2026 were selected\. Results: The included meta\-analysis by Takita et al\. \(2025\) reported an overall diagnostic accuracy of 52\.1% across 83 studies\. In Gordon et al\. \(2024\), 6\.8% \(13/191\) of articles/innovations focused on teaching or assessing clinical reasoning\. Arcos Huaman et al\. \(2025\) reported 31% regulatory barriers in Latin American cardiovascular diagnosis, without assessing their curricular prevalence\. Conclusions: Artificial intelligence is a valuable complementary educational resource whose curricular integration requires active faculty supervision, explicit ethical frameworks, and ongoing empirical validation\.
