# TWG6 Consolidated Recommendations: Women Shaping the AI Economy\. Working Paper for Discussion

**Autores:** Ruth Martínez; Ilker Demirkol; Caroline de Cock; Alexander Galt; Ana Lima; Catherine Mulligan; Ciara O&\#x27;Buachalla; Chandrakumar Pillai; Sanja Ramic; Mara Saenger; Roberta Scrofani; Gilda Seddighi; Rania Souhlal; Krzysztof Supowicz; Georgia Voudoulaki; Irene Emanuela Falcone

**Data:** 2026-10-11 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

This working paper brings together draft recommendations developed by the five Task Forces of Thematic Working Group 6 \(TWG6\), “Women Shaping the AI Economy,” within the WIDCON project\. It identifies policy and organisational actions to support women’s participation, quality employment and opportunities in an economy increasingly shaped by artificial intelligence\. The document covers five interconnected areas: Evidence, monitoring and accountability\. Regulatory scope and workforce impact governance\. Good jobs, just transition and entrepreneurship\. AI design and supply\-side accountability\. Structural barriers, skills and lifelong learning\. A common framework connects the problems and available evidence to responsible actors, proposed actions, implementation routes, expected outcomes and monitoring signals\. The document also records evidence limitations, external review status, illustrative practices and outstanding questions\. Evidence strength and review maturity vary across the Task Force contributions\. The working paper is prepared for discussion at the Dublin Summit, 15\-16 October 2026, including the TWG6 session “AI for All and by All?”\. It provides a basis for feedback from policymakers, employers, AI providers, social partners, researchers, education and training organisations, and civil society\. This is an interim working paper for discussion\. Summit feedback and subsequent review will inform further refinement of the recommendations\. Final validation and formal adoption as a TWG6 position remain pending\.
