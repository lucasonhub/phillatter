# Rendón\-Aldaraca, Oscar: The Consciousness Feedback Loop: A Reinterpretation of Karma through Quantum Neutrality and Information Conservation

**Autores:** 

**Data de descoberta:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** unknown

## Descrição do feed

This essay proposes a logical alternative to traditional quantum mysticism\. By shifting moral judgment away from the cosmos and restoring it to the subject, we redefine karma not as a punitive divine law, but as a feedback loop where the Self ethically evaluates the effects of its own actions upon an informationally neutral physical substrate originated at a subquantum level\. Consequent to this framework, conscious beings are positioned as the ultimate sovereigns over the material universe\.
