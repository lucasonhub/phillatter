# The Multiple Economic Value of Information in the Digital Economy: Toward a multidimensional framework for information, and artificial intelligence

**Autores:** Alessandro Aveni

**Data:** 2026-10-09 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

In the digital economy information has taken on the role of a key economic resource, even though its economic value does not have to be the same as its market price\. The body of work in information economics has provided a number of important but mostly separate explanations of information asymmetry, uncertainty reduction, signaling, property rights, pricing, network effects, organizational information and intangible capital\. The present paper looks at these contributions again by means of a qualitative, theory\-focused review of the literature referred to in the source text and puts forward an integrative interpretation of informational value\. It is argued that information should be regarded as a multidimensional economic resource whose value can at the same time include aspects relating to use, exchange, organization, network, social and temporal dimensions\. Market price is thus only one possible form of informational value and not a complete measure of it\. The digital economy makes this distinction more apparent since digital information can be copied and distributed at a very low marginal cost, can produce consumer and network benefits which are not entirely reflected in transactions, and can at the same time act as both a private asset and a shared resource\. Artificial intelligence enhances these features by increasing the scale, speed and complexity of information processing\. The paper does not assert that economics as a whole is inadequate; instead it maintains that the various theories of information should be taken into account together when the economic value generated by information as a whole is the object of analysis\.
