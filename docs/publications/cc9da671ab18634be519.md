# Protocol: From Pilots to Practice: A Scoping Review of Artificial Intelligence Adoption in the Healthcare Sector of the GCC Countries and Egypt

**Autores:** Ahmed Abdelalim

**Data:** 2026-10-17 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Protocol \(version 0\.6, 17 October 2026\) for a scoping review mapping the adoption of artificial intelligence across five healthcare sectors in Saudi Arabia, the United Arab Emirates, Qatar, Kuwait, Bahrain, Oman and Egypt \(2015–2026\): hospitals and providers; pharmaceutical companies and manufacturers; pharmacies and distribution; government and regulators; and research and universities\. The review follows JBI guidance and PRISMA\-ScR\. It searches PubMed, Europe PMC, OpenAlex, Crossref, DOAJ, Semantic Scholar, Arabic and regional sources and official websites\. An independent, blinded large language model acts as second screener on a 20% sample, and contested records are adjudicated by an AI reviewer under the author&\#x27;s delegation\. Synthesis covers descriptive mapping, NASSS\-based barriers, pilot versus practice, an OECD/JRC\-style readiness index, and use / pilot\-with\-caution / avoid recommendations\. Registration statement: this protocol is registered retrospectively\. Searching, screening, charting and a first synthesis were completed on 16–17 October 2026, before this deposit\. A dated amendments log \(v0\.1–v0\.6\) is included\. The document contains no results\.
