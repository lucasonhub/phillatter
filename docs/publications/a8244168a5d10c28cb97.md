# e MolFrag 2 and e Synth 2: Sound Fragment\-Based Molecular Decomposition and Reconstruction

**Autores:** Adam Bess; Sean Rowland; Chris Alvin; Meisam Adibifard; Supratik Mukhopadhyay

**Data:** 2026-10-14 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Abstract We present eMolFrag2 and eSynth2, enhanced successors to eMolFrag and eSynth for fragment\-based molecular decomposition and reconstruction\. eMolFrag2 is a complete reimplementation of eMolFrag in Python, using RDKit and graph\-based molecular representations to preserve connectivity, identify free atoms, combine adjacent linkers, and avoid aromaticity ambiguities that impair downstream reconstruction\. eSynth2 generalizes fragment recombination by removing hard\-coded molecular\-property filters, supporting multiple bonds to a single brick atom, and adding a targeted reconstruction mode for soundness validation\. Across 36,534 nonredundant DUD\-E active compounds, the eMolFrag2 → eSynth2 workflow reconstructed every input molecule exactly \(Tanimoto coefficient, TC = 1\.0\), whereas the original eMolFrag→ eSynth workflow reconstructed 51 of the 100 molecules in a benchmark subset\. The upgraded workflow also produced broader recombinant chemical spaces, including higher entropy in a four\-molecule diversity analysis \(2\.78 versus 2\.70\)\. Both tools are open\-source, platform\-agnostic, and available for academic and commercial use\.
