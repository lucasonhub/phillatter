# A Physics\-Hybrid Alpha Network \(PHAN\) approach for soil moisture retrieval using time series optical and SAR imagery

**Autores:** Zhuqiang Li; Xiao Chen; Lisai Cao; Zhijun Zhen; Shengbo Chen; Liqiang Zhang

**Data:** 2026-10-15 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

The coarse spatial resolution hinders the application of soil moisture \(SM\) in elucidating the interactions among water, energy and carbon cycles at regional and global scales\. We propose a physics\-hybrid differentiable network, termed the Physics\-Hybrid Alpha\-Net \(PHAN\), that integrates time\-series C\-band dual\-polarization SAR and optical observations to retrieve 10\-metre high\-resolution SM maps\. PHAN extends the Alpha approximation by enforcing a physics\-based regularization on retrieved soil moisture \(SM\) and adding a cross\-attention module that implicitly reduces vegetation\-canopy scattering\. By using optical and SAR imagery from 2019 to 2020 of Shandian River Basin, Naqu and Twente, PHAN outperformed machine learning \(ML\), deep learning \(DL\) and physics\-guided baselines, achieving Pearson correlation coefficients \(R\) = 0\.821, 0\.861, 0\.856 and unbiased root mean square error \(ubRMSE\) = 4\.62, 5\.54, 8\.38 vol%\. PHAN can improve applicability and accuracy while avoiding reliance on hard\-to\-obtain microwave driving parameters or opaque, purely data\-driven methods\. This physics\-hybrid paradigm can also inspire future methodological extensions to the retrieval of other land surface parameters\.
