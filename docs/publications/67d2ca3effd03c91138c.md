# Co\-GRFuzz: Shared\-Corpus Cooperative Fuzzing for Python Libraries

**Autores:** Anh Le Minh Viet; Hiệu Nguyễn Huy; Thang Hoang Nam; Hai Anh Tran; Truong Tran Xuan

**Data:** 2026-10-15 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Parallel fuzzing can exchange successful inputs, but the behavior of different Python mutation strategies under a shared corpus is not well characterized\. We evaluate four Pythonfuzz\-anchored two\-worker configurations\-PP, PD, PO, and PG\-on five Python libraries and compare them with isolated single\-process references under one common sys\.settrace\-based line\-transition coverage representation\. The observed outcomes are target\-dependent and do not favor GRPO as a cooperative partner\. On python\-dateutil, PD and PO each reach a global union of 3407 edges, whereas PG reaches 2913 despite similar local coverage of the learning\-guided worker\. On cryptography, PD and PO reach a 47\-edge union while PG shows no union gain\. A provenance\-enabled PG run records 106 global\-first discoveries whose direct parent was produced by the peer worker, documenting within\-run seed reuse without establishing a causal benefit of sharing\. Each configuration is represented by one six\-hour campaign; the reported values are therefore individual campaign observations rather than statistical comparisons\. Two previously undocumented exceptions are also reported as qualitative bug\-finding evidence\.
