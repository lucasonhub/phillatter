# CORA\-CSS: Correlation\-Aware Reputation and Influence\-Capped Fusion for Collusion\-Resilient Cooperative Spectrum Sensing

**Autores:** Pavani Priya Vyshnavi Nandanavanam

**Data:** 2026-10-17 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Cooperative spectrum sensing improves primary\-user detection through spatial diversity, but collaborative fusion also creates an attack surface: multiple Byzantine radios can coordinate their reports so that individually plausible observations become collectively dominant\. Existing reputation methods attenuate persistently unreliable radios, while SENTRY\-CSS combines adaptive reliability with selective re\-sensing; however, protected reference labels and arbitrary collusion remain important limitations\. This paper introduces CORA\-CSS, a reference\-free fusion method that combines a rolling residual\-correlation graph, consensus\-based reputation, and a sublinear group\-influence cap\. Each radio is scored against the instantaneous robust median, its reputation is updated from agreement history, and radios exhibiting strongly correlated residuals with sustained deviation receive a multiplicative influence cap before soft fusion\. A reproducible Monte Carlo study models twelve heterogeneous sensing radios, fading\-like sensing variation, noise uncertainty, primary occupancy imbalance, and four attack families\. In the main ten\-seed condition with four colluding Byzantine radios, CORA\-CSS achieves 0\.9659±0\.0100 balanced accuracy, 0\.9677±0\.0103 detection probability, 0\.0358±0\.0125 false\-alarm probability, and 0\.9515±0\.0145 F1\. Reference\-free reputation without the correlation cap reaches 0\.9454 balanced accuracy, while equal\-soft, median, majority, trimmed\-mean, and clean\-calibrated SVM fusion degrade substantially\. CORA\-CSS remains above 0\.96 balanced accuracy with four colluders in the attacker\-count sweep and retains useful performance with five\. A stress test also exposes the boundary of the method: under weak stealth attacks that preserve plausible independent\-looking reports, ordinary equal\-soft fusion can outperform CORA\-CSS\. The contribution is therefore a collusion\-focused robustness mechanism, not a universal replacement for conventional fusion\.
