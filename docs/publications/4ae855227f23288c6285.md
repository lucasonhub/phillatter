# Adaptive natural ventilation for sustainable green campus buildings in the humid tropics: A stochastic approach at Telkom University office building in Bandung, Indonesia

**Autores:** I Ilhamdaniah; Bayu Erfianto; Ravi Shankar Srinivasan

**Data:** 2026-10-11 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Telkom University Bandung, as one of the top\-ranked green campuses in Indonesia, strives to meet the assessment criteria in campus sustainability instruments such as the UI GreenMetric World University Rankings\. As energy and climate change constitute one of the most heavily weighted categories in this metric, efforts to save energy and adapt to climate change are essential\. In tropical campuses like Telkom University, space cooling dominates operational energy use, and reliance on air conditioning can undermine both energy performance and indoor environmental quality\. This study proposes a proactive and autonomous natural ventilation strategy for tropical campus buildings that combines low\-cost indoor climate sensing with a mean\-reverting stochastic Ornstein–Uhlenbeck \(OU\) model\. The stochastic OU model forecasts the Predicted Mean Vote \(PMV\) in a building located in a tropical climate 15–30 minutes ahead\. The framework was validated using sensor data from an office building at Telkom University in November 2025\. The OU model tracked the observed thermal dynamics with high accuracy and generated stable response signals that opened windows during favorable cooling periods and closed them during periods of peak heat\. This experiment on adaptive natural ventilation for cooling building indoor temperatures offers the Telkom University campus a scalable pathway to reduce cooling energy demand\. This experiment also has the potential to strengthen Telkom University’s performance in the UI GreenMetrics World University Rankings\.
