# SATRIA, an adaptive statistics application with limited AI assistance: Development and effects on vocational students’ numeracy and motivation

**Autores:** alif basuki normansyah

**Data:** 2026-10-15 · **Tipo:** dissertation

## Resumo

Vocational students often meet statistics as a set of formulas, yet their own field asks them to read network data, compare services, and judge claims\. This study developed SATRIA, a web\-based statistics application for Grade XI students of Computer and Telecommunication Network Engineering, and examined changes in numeracy and learning motivation\. SATRIA combines mastery learning, rule\-based adaptive pathways, and AI hints limited to five per task that never reveal final answers\. An embedded mixed methods design was used: the application was developed in three stages, reviewed by three content and three media experts, and tried out in a nonequivalent control group quasi\-experiment with 32 experimental and 30 control students over six meetings\. Experts supported its validity \(content 94\.95%, media 84\.76%\), and students rated it very practical \(84\.32% in the limited trial, 89\.20% in the main use\)\. Mean numeracy change was 54\.30 points in the SATRIA class and 35\.83 in the regular class, t\(60\) = 5\.905, p &lt; \.001, d = 1\.50\. Motivation rose in both classes without a reliable difference between them \(p = \.389\)\. Interviews and student work pointed to clearer learning pathways and better data reasoning, alongside calls for smoother network access, simpler hints, and teacher guidance\.
