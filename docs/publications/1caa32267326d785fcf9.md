# Texture Analysis of Tumor Spect Images Using Tumorotropic Radiopharmaceuticals

**Autores:** Николай Александрович Николов; Sergiy Makeyev; Tatiana Novikova; Denis Myronchuk; Yevhen Rozum; Iryna Kozubska

**Data:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Background\. Single Photon Emission Computed Tomography \(SPECT\) is one of the most sensitive methods for investigating brain tumors\. Processing and analyzing 3D images require specific segmentation methods, resource\-intensive calculations, and specialized software\. Today, the most popular strategy for solving these problems is the use of artificial intelligence \(AI\)\. At the same time, training AI requires hundreds to thousands of cases in databases, which makes correct training and validation of segmentation methods impossible due to data scarcity\. One solution to the problem of insufficient data is augmentation and artificial generation of images with the requisite properties\. However, quantitative criteria for the adequacy of texture properties of model SPECT images have not yet been defined\. Objective\. The aim of this work is the texture analysis of brain tumor SPECT images and the determination of their main quantitative characteristics for the subsequent assessment of the correctness of artificial tumor generation or augmentation methods\. Methods\. Brain SPECT studies were performed using 99mTc\-pertechnetate and 99mTc\-MIBI\. A total of 94 tumor cases were analyzed, including anaplastic astrocytoma \(n=11\), glioblastoma \(n=22\), metastases \(n=12\), meningioma \(n=4\), and others\. Texture analysis included geometric, statistical \(independent voxel intensity\), and topological \(voxel relationships\) metrics, as well as indices dependent on background cutoff level\. Approximately 45 parameters were calculated per region of interest\. Results\. The data presented in this work systematize the list of texture characteristics of tumor SPECT images using tumorotropic radiopharmaceuticals\. The presented mean values, data dispersion levels, mutual correlation, and regression analysis of these indices allow for a comprehensive evaluation of the adequacy of model 3D SPECT tumor images\. Conclusions\. Quantitative texture characteristics of brain tumor SPECT images were obtained\. To quantitatively assess the degree of neoplasm perfusion and contour indistinctness, a number of new indices were proposed, based on: comparing radiopharmaceutical activity in the tumor with the confluens sinuum area; and evaluating the characteristics of the sigmoid function approximation of parameters dependent on the α background cutoff level\. It was shown that the vast majority of quantitative texture characteristics of tumor SPECT images using tumorotropic agents significantly correlate with the corresponding values of conditionally undamaged tissues\. This relationship is interpreted as being influenced by image quality and the proximity of tumors and the symmetrical control zone to areas with high radiopharmaceutical uptake, particularly skull regions\. For a correct comparison of regions of interest, index normalization accounting for such a correlation is proposed\.
