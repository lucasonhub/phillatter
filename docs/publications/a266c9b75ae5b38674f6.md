# AGC\-YOLO: adaptive gated channel network for rice leaf disease detection

**Autores:** Fei Wang; Yuan Lv; Li Zhao; Jun Zhang; Wen Yang; Guo Li; Xianyao Zhang; Yuxiang Zhang

**Data:** 2026-10-15 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Accurate detection of rice leaf diseases is critical for intelligent crop protection, yet class imbalance, degradation of multi\-scale feature information, and the limited discriminative capability of feature fusion continue to hinder effective monitoring under field conditions\. To address these challenges, this study presents AGC\-YOLO, an improved detection model based on the YOLOv8s framework\. First, a semantic\-aware data augmentation network termed SAICP Net is devised to alleviate class imbalance and the scarcity of cross\-class co\-occurrence samples through a weakly supervised soft spatial prior and semantically constrained cross\-category instance pasting\. Second, a gated hybrid pooling module named GHP\-SPPF is proposed to adaptively integrate max pooling and soft pooling via a learnable gating mechanism, thereby strengthening the discriminative representation of multi\-scale lesions\. Finally, a channel\-adaptive fusion module termed CAFF is constructed to replace standard concatenation with channel\-wise learnable weights and gated residual refinement, boosting the discriminative power of cross\-scale feature fusion\. Experimental results demonstrate that AGC\-YOLO achieves an mAP@0\.5 of 93\.5 ± 0\.6% on the rice disease dataset, representing a 3\.0 percentage\-point improvement over the baseline, and additional experiments on two public datasets further demonstrate the effectiveness of the proposed method for disease detection\.
