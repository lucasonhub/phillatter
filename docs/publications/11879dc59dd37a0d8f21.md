# Fenomenul guvernării\. Continuități doctrinare în memoria profesoarei Daiana Maura Vesmaș

**Autores:** Andreea\-Nicoleta Dragomir

**Data:** 2026-10-13 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

The study analyses the contemporary relevance of the concept of the „phenomenon of governance”, starting from the doctrinal contribution of Professor Daiana Maura Vesmaș and from the possibility of further developing this theme in the context of contemporary European governance\. The paper is based on the idea that governance cannot be reduced to the formal exercise of executive power or to the mere application of the law, but must be understood as a complex phenomenon situated at the intersection of the state, law, public administration, politics and society\. The methodology used is doctrinal and analytical, relying on Romanian legal literature, relevant European documents and legal instruments concerning digitalisation, data protection, cybersecurity and artificial intelligence\. The study highlights the continuity between the reflection on governance in the contemporary state and the current challenges of public administration\. Particular attention is given to the security of governance and smart governance\. The security of governance is analysed as the legal, coherent, predictable and resilient functioning of public administration\. Smart governance is understood as a form of administrative modernisation in which technology, data and artificial intelligence must be integrated into a legal framework capable of ensuring transparency, control, accessibility and the protection of citizens&\#x27; rights\.
