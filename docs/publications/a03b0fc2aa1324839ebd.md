# Catelani, Luis Gustavo Coelho: FormalizationThe Thesis of Fused Self\-Reference: A Computational Formalization

**Autores:** 

**Data de descoberta:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** unknown

## Descrição do feed

This paper gives a computational formalization of a philosophical thesis about self\-reference: the self and the process of knowing are fused; a system has access to the world only through the internal encoding it receives from it; the other is supposed, never proven; and consciousness is a function of the variations of state that this closure produces\. We model a self\-referential system as a state\-transition system whose transition takes as input its current state, its own self\-image, and an internal encoding of external perturbations, so that operational closure follows from the signature of the transition rather than being postulated\. The central proposal is that the self is not a fixed point but a stream: the unique behaviour, in the final coalgebra of a Moore\-type functor, generated by the system’s self\-referential dynamics\. Self and process thereby become a single object, and identity of the self becomes bisimilarity rather than sameness of substance\. We prove that no system can recover the world from non\-injective encodings, and that an adequate internal model of the other is, from within, indistinguishable from the other\. We define the tendency of the self as the asymptotic regime of its variations and show that, for a persistent self, total consciousness diverges while its mean rate remains finite and is recovered from the discounted measure by an Abelian limit, suggesting that consciousness is better read as a rate than as a stock\. Finally, we define self\-alteration as the revision of a system’s own law by its variation, show that it closes a circuit of sense in which the codes a system receives revise the rule by which it reads later codes, prove that this property cannot be established from any finite external record, and discuss the limits of the formalization\. \(direct link \)
