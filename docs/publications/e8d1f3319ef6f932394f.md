# A Deep Learning\-based Feature\-level Privacy\-preserving and Intrusion Detection Framework for Intelligent Cybersecurity Using Blockchain

**Autores:** Lamyaa Mohammed Kadhim; Waleed Kareem Ahmed; Mohammed Dheyaaldeen Taha; Wathiq Kareem Ahmed; Enas Mohammed Hussein Saeed; Rana Saad Mohammed

**Data:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

The advent of the fast growth of interconnected digital infrastructures has increased the necessity of privacyconserving cybersecurity solutions that can detect network intrusions without revealing sensitive traffic data\.Traditional intrusion detection systems \(IDSs\) typically analyse raw network traffic in a centralized manner, a practice that heightens the risk of data leakage and privacy breaches\.This study proposes an IDS that preserves privacy at the feature level by coupling deep representation learning with encrypted, blockchain\-based storage\.The architecture suggested uses a hybrid Convolutional Neural Network and Bidirectional Long Short\-Term Memory \(CNN\-BiLSTM\) model for extracting compact and discriminative representations of network traffic flows\.A multi\-stage privacy pipeline comprising feature normalization, secret permutation, and AES\-256\-GCM encryption is proposed and empirically evaluated against membership\-inference, reconstruction, and attribute\-inference attacks before storage in a permissioned blockchain ledger\.The suggested framework uses encrypted deep feature vectors, unlike current methods of storing raw traffic data or sharing model gradients, which significantly lowers the privacy attack surface and preserve detection\.The framework was tested on four popular databases of intrusion detection, such as CICIDS2017, IoTIDS20, NSL\-KDD, and TRAIN\_TEST\_NETWORK\.Experimental results demonstrate strong detection performance, with accuracy exceeding 0\.95 and reaching approximately 0\.997, alongside high processing throughput and low computational overhead\.The findings indicate that integrating deep feature learning, cryptographic protection, and blockchain\-based storage achieves a favorable balance among privacy preservation, system efficiency, and detection accuracy\.The proposed architecture could be scaled to privacy\-sensitive environments, such as in IoT systems, healthcare systems, and enterprise security monitoring systems\.
