## Descrição do feed

Modern large language models exhibit non\-deterministic hallucinations and structural instability under high\-burden reasoning tasks\. We introduce Axiom\-1 \(Sovereign Matrix\), a post\-generation structural reliability framework that shifts AI output governance from empirical probabilistic patching to formal topological validation\. Axiom\-1 routes candidate outputs through a six\-stage filtering process governed by spectral stability analysis of first\-order Markov transition matrices and a continuous 12\.8 Hz internal pulse operator\. We mathematically prove that structural hallucinations collapse the spectral eigenvalue spectrum, enabling real\-time deferral or rejection prior to release\. Live empirical validation demonstrates 100% topological stability maintenance under maximum logical burden, providing a practical pathway toward sovereign, hallucination\-resistant artificial intelligence\. \(direct link \)
