# Ibaguner, Sinan: The Equation of Genuine Love: A Philosophical, Sociological, and Psychological Inquiry

**Autores:** 

**Data de descoberta:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** unknown

## Descrição do feed

The formulation \{Genuine Love\} = \{Love for Self\} \+ \{Love for Others\} appears deceptively simple\. Yet embedded within it lies a radical claim: that love is not a zero\-sum resource to be divided between self and other, but a composite phenomenon requiring both orientations simultaneously\. This article examines the equation through three lenses\-philosophy, sociology, and psychology\-arguing that genuine love is neither pure altruism nor pure self\-interest, but a dialectical synthesis that collapses the false binary between the two\. \(direct link \)
