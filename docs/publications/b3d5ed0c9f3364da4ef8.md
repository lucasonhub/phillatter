# Case Report: transient migratory pulmonary nodules with bronchial artery malformations in an autosomal dominant polycystic kidney disease patient: a case of recurrent hemoptysis

**Autores:** Yi Hu; Jie Zhang; Ting Wang; Bing Xue

**Data:** 2026-10-14 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Autosomal dominant polycystic kidney disease \(ADPKD\) is associated with vascular malformations, but bronchial artery involvement with migratory pulmonary nodules has not been reported\. We describe an 81\-year\-old man with recurrent hemoptysis and migratory nodules on chest CT\. Digital subtraction angiography revealed tortuous, dilated bronchial arteries; embolization controlled bleeding and nodules resolved\. He had polycystic kidney and liver disease, a prior intracranial aneurysm, and a PKD1 missense variant \(c\.8426C &gt; T\)\. Common causes of hemoptysis were excluded\. This case describes co\-occurrence of bronchial artery malformation, recurrent hemoptysis and migratory pulmonary nodules in a patient with genetically confirmed ADPKD, raising the possibility that bronchial arterial involvement could be a rare, under\-recognized vascular manifestation associated with ADPKD\.
