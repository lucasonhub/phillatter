# EXPERIMENTAL ANALYSIS OF ERROR STRUCTURE AND LATENCY OF FACE DETECTORS AND CONSTRAINED DETECTOR\-TIER SELECTION FOR REAL\-TIME BIOMETRIC SYSTEMS

**Autores:** Feruza Ortiqova

**Data:** 2026-10-12 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Five face detector configurations \(YuNet, Dlib HOG without and with upsampling, OpenCV DNN, RetinaFace\) are evaluated on 1 895 annotated faces in overall and dominant\-face modes\. Error structure, resolution\-dependent latency and efficiency are quantified, and a constrained criterion for detector\-tier selection is proposed\.
