# Zeng, Xiaozhou: A Graph\-Theoretic Characterization of Consciousness in G\_D: Sixteen Necessary and Sufficient Conditions, Three Extensions, and a Cross\-Chain Personal Identity Criterion under Structural Determination Theory

**Autores:** 

**Data de descoberta:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** unknown

## Descrição do feed

This paper develops a graph\-theoretic characterization of consciousness within the determination dependency graph G\_D of Structural Determination Theory \(SDT\)\. The result is a meta\-structural identification criterion with four substantive claims and a cross\-chain personal identity criterion\. \(1\) Four\-fold equivalence\. A sub\-graph C of G\_D is consciousness if and only if it satisfies sixteen identification conditions IC\_1 through IC\_16 together with a local\-maximum principle\. Three extension equivalences add reflection \(IC\_17\), free will \(IC\_18\), and consciousness\-level memory \(IC\_19\) as independent extension conditions\. \(2\) Cross\-chain personal identity\. A cross\-chain criterion PI\_1 governs diachronic identity between distinct consciousness chains via substrate continuity, formalizing the N9 signature of Biology Identification within the consciousness framework and completing the deferred work of Distributed Identity \(§7\.1\.3\)\. \(3\) Two\-directional strength\. The necessity direction is established at mathematical\-proof level within the SDT framework, drawing on the consciousness\-is\-structure derivation of Bounded Free Will \(§3\.2\) and canonicity arguments establishing each IC\_i as the unique G\_D\-formalization of the corresponding ontological commitment\. The sufficiency direction is established as the SDT\-internal strongest identification claim via structural\-space analysis showing that the non\-conscious satisfaction space is empty\. \(4\) Coordination and application\. The framework yields determinate verdicts on nine boundary cases \(persistent vegetative state, split\-brain, anesthesia, infants, dementia, mammals, octopus, large language models, mind upload\), with concept\-level paradigm verification on adult awake human consciousness and instance\-level verification through an explicit minimal G\_D construction satisfying all sixteen identification conditions jointly\. The framework resolves classical personal\-identity puzzles \(Locke&\#x27;s circularity, Olson&\#x27;s thinking\-animal, Parfit&\#x27;s fission, Williams&\#x27;s body\-priority, Parfit&\#x27;s &quot;what matters&quot;\) under a dual\-signature framework, and is differentiated from Integrated Information Theory, Global Workspace Theory, Higher\-Order Theory, and Predictive Processing\. The paper does not address the explanatory aspect of the hard problem \(Chalmers 1996\) — why conscious structure has phenomenal character lies outside SDT&\#x27;s scope\. The ontological aspect, namely what consciousness fundamentally is, is addressed via the IC characterization of the structure that consciousness is \(Bounded Free Will §3\.2\)\.
