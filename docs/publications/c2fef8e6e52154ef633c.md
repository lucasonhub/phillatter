# Martinez Garcia, Gustavo: Network Panpsychism: A Metatheoretical Framework of Networked Consciousness and Its Cosmic Function

**Autores:** 

**Data de descoberta:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** unknown

## Descrição do feed

The enduring challenge of the &quot;hard problem&quot; of consciousness lies in explaining how physical processes give rise to subjective experience\. This paper proposes and defends Network Panpsychism, a comprehensive metatheoretical framework designed to reframe this problem by synthesising core elements of traditional Panpsychism, Functionalism, and Integrated Information Theory \(IIT\)\. Network Panpsychism posits that \(1\) consciousness, or proto\-consciousness, is a fundamental property of information\-processing structures, where experience is constitutive of information processing itself; \(2\) individual conscious entities are nodes within hierarchically integrated networks termed &\#x27;Psychospheres&\#x27;, a structure that effectively resolves the combination problem by replacing the logic of mereological aggregation with one of network integration; and \(3\) subjective experience \(qualia\) is not an epiphenomenon but possesses a teleological function: to facilitate high\-fidelity information transfer and maintain stability within complex, nested cosmic systems\. We argue that this framework provides a more coherent, unified, and empirically suggestive account of consciousness, bridging the explanatory gap and offering novel pathways for future research across philosophy, cognitive science, and physics\.
