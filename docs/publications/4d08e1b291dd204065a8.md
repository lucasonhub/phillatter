# Accuracy Preserving Selective Rare Attack Expert with Deep Cross Residual Learning for Imbalanced Network Intrusion Detection

**Autores:** Mustafa Mahmood Yahya; Sarah Najm Abdulwahid; Yaseen Ahmed Mohammed Alsumaidaee

**Data:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Severe class imbalance can make multiclass intrusion detectors appear reliable while rare attacks remain poorly recognized\.This study proposes an Accuracy\-Preserving Selective Rare Attack Expert \(AP\-SRAE\) built on a Deep Cross Residual Network \(DCRN\) backbone\.The term accuracy\-preserving is operational and validationtolerance\-bounded: candidate gates may reduce calibrated\-backbone validation accuracy by at most 0\.15 percentage point, but no test\-set guarantee is implied\.On the original 2,280,090\-flow full\-context random benchmark, AP\-SRAE achieves 98\.28% accuracy and raises macro F1 from 61\.85% to 65\.76%; exact paired predictions show 191 incorrectto\-correct and 124 correct\-to\-incorrect transitions, for a net gain of 67 correct decisions\.In an identifier\-reduced rerun, macro F1 rises from 53\.60% to 58\.35% and balanced accuracy from 56\.24% to 62\.33%, with a 0\.09\-percentage\-point accuracy decrease\.On the official UNSW\-NB15 split, five independent seeds yield balanced accuracy of 48\.53% \+/\-0\.55% for AP\-SRAE versus 47\.56% \+/\-0\.52% for the backbone \(paired t\-test p=0\.026\), while accuracy decreases from 72\.96% \+/\-0\.59% to 72\.60% \+/\-0\.77%\.Protocol\-controlled IDS\-MTran and TabTransformer reimplementations obtain higher official\-split headline accuracy, but AP\-SRAE is computationally lighter in the controlled run\.An independent component\-wise ablation further shows that the final gated and weighted expert improves over the calibrated backbone in macro F1 and balanced accuracy while leaving Normal\-class F1 unchanged\.
