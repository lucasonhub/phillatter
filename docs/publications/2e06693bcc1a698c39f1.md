# Portman, Weston: What Counts as Physical? Why Single\-Aspect Monisms Must Be False

**Autores:** 

**Data de descoberta:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** unknown

## Descrição do feed

This paper proposes a definition of the physical in terms of in\-world efficient causation: a physical difference is a difference within a world that produces another difference within that world according to laws or regularities\. I argue that this distinction clarifies the limits of physical explanation and exposes problems for physicalism and other single\-aspect monisms\. Through cases involving a video\-game &quot;glitch,&quot; phenomenal redness, and quantum measurement, I distinguish law\-governed transitions within a world from differences whose sufficient causes cannot be located within that world\. I then argue that consciousness and personal identity introduce differences not captured by a complete description of in\-world physical relations\. The resulting framework preserves a meaningful distinction between physical and mental causation while motivating a dual\-aspect idealist ontology\.
