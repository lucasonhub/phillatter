# XPERIMENTAL INVESTIGATION OF THE PARAMETERS OF A WORKING TOOL FOR REMOVING THE DISTURBED SOIL LAYER FROM THE CENTER OF A CRATER

**Autores:** Oleksandra Trokhaniak; L\.P\. Kolomiiets

**Data:** 2026-10-12 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

The article presents the results of theoretical and experimental studies of the process of removing a damaged soil layer inside a crater by means of a specially developed working tool\. The structural scheme and the general configuration of the working tool are described in detail\. For performing the technological operation, the tool was mounted on the boom of an excavator, which provided the required positioning, movement trajectory and interaction with the treated soil surface\. The design of the working tool includes the use of channel flanges and angle\-section elements that ensure gradual layer\-by\-layer cutting, separation and removal of the damaged soil from the inner surface of the crater\. Such a design makes it possible to control the depth of soil removal and to reduce excessive disturbance of the underlying undamaged layers\. To determine the soil cutting force and to evaluate the nature of the mechanical loading during dynamic experimental studies, an electronic dynamometer equipped with an SBA interface was used\. The dynamometer strain\-gauge sensor was installed directly on the brackets of the working tool, which ensured continuous and reliable registration of force parameters during the cutting process\. The obtained experimental data made it possible to assess the actual loading conditions of the tool and to compare them with the values predicted by the theoretical model\. A comparative analysis of theoretical calculations and experimental results was carried out\. It was established that the discrepancy between the calculated and experimentally measured values of the soil cutting force ranged from 4\.5 to 8\.6%\. Such a level of deviation indicates a sufficiently high adequacy of the adopted mathematical model to the real technological process\. The comparison also confirmed that the previously determined values of cutting force and energy intensity for soil removal by the channel flanges and angle\-section elements adequately describe the actual loading pattern during the cleaning of the damaged soil layer inside the crater\. The obtained results can be used for further improvement of the working tool design, substantiation of its principal geometric and operating parameters, optimization of cutting modes, and reduction of energy consumption\. In addition, the developed approaches may contribute to increasing the efficiency, reliability and technological effectiveness of mechanized soil\-cover restoration operations under conditions involving local soil damage and crater formation\.
