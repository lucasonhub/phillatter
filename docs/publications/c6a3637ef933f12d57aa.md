# Davis, Aaron Brian: Disability Theology: An Introduction

**Autores:** 

**Data de descoberta:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** unknown

## Descrição do feed

Is disability a natural evil destined to be eliminated by God? What does it mean to be disabled, anyway? Can being disabled prevent you from being a flourishing person, or even a person at all? Could God actually be disabled somehow? Questions like these have occupied the minds of disability theologians since the field’s inauguration in the mid\-1990s\. The intersection of theological concepts and ideas with those related to notions of disability, disabled identity, and disability justice make up disability theology’s core\. However, until now there has been no single volume introduction to disability theology to which interested persons could turn to learn more about this dynamic area of study\. &quot;Disability Theology: An Introduction&quot; changes this by offering a first\-of\-its\-kind primer on the subject\. Here you’ll find considerations of disability’s nature, the presentation of disability in Christian scripture, the relationship between disability and concepts of salvation, resurrection, and Church, and much more\. Distilling insights from across the field’s breadth, &quot;Disability Theology: An Introduction&quot; is an all\-in\-one primer for students of theology approaching this subject for the first time\. And yet, it also offers constructive insights for those already familiar with disability theology who seek clarity on key terms, concise models of complex topics, and critical appraisals of what can be found across the field’s diverse landscape\. As such, &quot;Disability Theology: An Introduction&quot; can be a welcome addition to reading lists and libraries of all sorts\.
