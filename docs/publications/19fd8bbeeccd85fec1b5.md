# Bayesian predictive inference for the aggregate claims distribution and ruin probability

**Autores:** Mark Corrado; Douglas E\. Johnston

**Data:** 2026-10-09 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Compound process models are widely used to model the sum of claims against an insurance portfolio or total withdrawals from a bank\. The classic compound Poisson\-Exponential model is often used, and the standard approach estimates unknown parameters from historical data using maximum likelihood estimation\. Unfortunately, this method can significantly underestimate risk\. We use a Bayesian approach to compute a predictive distribution for cumulative loss and the ruin probability \(i\.e\., bankruptcy\) and obtain closed\-form, analytical solutions\. We examine the performance of these methods and show, under a range of sample sizes, that our method outperforms traditional approaches, and we provide statistical evidence that it achieves zero coverage error, improving risk assessment\.
