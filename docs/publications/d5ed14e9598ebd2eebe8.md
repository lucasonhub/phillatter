# Operational\-Response\-Aware Two\-Stage Stochastic Scheduling of Renewable Port Microgrids under Arrival\-Driven Congestion and Demand Amplification

**Autores:** Seyed Reza Samaei; K\.S\. Reddy; James Riffat

**Data:** 2026-10-13 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Electrified container ports are coupled logistics–energy systems in which uncertain vessel arrivals determine berth occupancy, shore\-power activation, cargo\-handling intensity, and the residual demand balanced by renewable generation, battery storage, and the utility grid\. This study develops a scenario\-based stochastic framework linking a non\-homogeneous vessel\-arrival process to queue\-consistent berth occupancy, congestion\-responsive demand, photovoltaic and wind uncertainty, battery dispatch, and grid exchange\. Physical arrival variability is separated from one\-sided delayed Automatic Identification System \(AIS\) information, while a Gaussian\-copula construction preserves adverse traffic–demand–renewable dependence\. Five thousand stratified scenarios are reduced to 60 weighted representatives; the conditional extreme\-congestion benchmark uses the nine retained Scenario\-IV representatives\. Across 30 independent paired out\-of\-sample replications, the proposed schedule reduces operating cost by 13\.9% \(95% Student\-t CI: 13\.60–14\.20%\), grid\-import\-based operational CO2 emissions by 17\.1% \(16\.76–17\.44%\), probability\-weighted scenario peak grid import by 18\.4% \(18\.03–18\.77%\), and cumulative operational response by 34\.1% \(33\.50–34\.70%\) relative to the deterministic reference\. Battery throughput is 29\.4% lower than under cost\-only stochastic dispatch \(28\.73–30\.07%\)\. In the representative seed\-2026 benchmark, the corresponding in\-sample reductions are 14\.8%, 18\.2%, 19\.9%, and 36\.4% relative to B0, while battery throughput is 31\.6% lower than under B3\. The formulation is an optimistic two\-stage perfect\-continuous\-recourse benchmark rather than a deployable rolling\-horizon controller\. Within the representative synthetic system, vessel\-arrival uncertainty is therefore an operational energy disturbance rather than a purely logistical input\.
