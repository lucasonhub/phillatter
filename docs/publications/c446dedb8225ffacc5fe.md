# Surface Damage Morphology Prediction Method with the Rule\-Prior\-Conditioned Generative Adversarial Network

**Autores:** Bin Yu; Haifeng Zhao

**Data:** 2026-10-10 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Open\-hole features are widely used in composite structures but inevitably alter intrinsic load\-bearing behavior and fracture characteristics\. This study develops an artificial intelligence\-based method for surface damage morphology prediction of multi\-hole carbon fiber reinforcement thermoplastic laminates\. A pix2pix conditional generative adversarial network incorporating a rule\-based ideal fracture mask is established to predict geometry\-dependent fracture morphologies and evaluate its predictive capability for unseen geometric configurations\. Four global pixel\-based and five morphology\-specific metrics are employed to assess the agreement between predicted and experimental damage morphologies\. The results show that the proposed network successfully reproduces the dominant crack paths, critical\-hole locations, and distributed surface\-splitting patterns observed experimentally\. Ablation analysis further confirms that ideal fracture mask provides effective guidance for fracture morphology prediction\. Despite the limited training dataset, the model retains reliable predictive capability for unseen regions of the geometric parameter space and accurately identifies critical failure holes\. Quantitative comparison of single\-hole laminates further shows similar fracture characteristics under quasi\-static and fatigue loading, indicating potential extension to fatigue applications\. However, broader applicability requires validation using datasets with multi\-hole fatigue specimens\. Overall, the proposed method provides an image\-based framework for predicting surface damage morphology under quasi\-static loading and localizing fracture\-critical regions within the predicted morphology\.
