# AI Adoption and the Digital Divide in Colombia’s Third Sector: Unintended Consequences, Organizational Readiness, and Ethical Tensions

**Autores:** Sara Calvo; Andrés Morales

**Data:** 2026-10-15 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

This study examines the adoption of artificial intelligence \(AI\) in Colombia’s third sector through a mixed\-methods study of 104 survey respondents and 20 semi\-structured interviews\. Findings reveal uneven, largely incremental, task\-oriented adoption, constrained by infrastructure, digital skills, governance capacity, and institutional support\. While AI offers opportunities for efficiency, communication, and resource optimization, concerns remain around privacy, misinformation, technological dependency, and the erosion of human\-centred practice\. Drawing on theories of the digital divide, organizational readiness, and unintended consequences, the study conceptualizes AI as both a stratifying and moral technology, highlighting the need for context\-sensitive governance, capability building, and equitable institutional support\.
