# Predicting Urban Growth and Future Land Use/Land Cover Change Using GeoAI A Case Study of Indore District, Madhya Pradesh, India

**Autores:** Bhawna Dandotiya; Suresh Awase

**Data:** 2026-10-15 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

1\. Abstract Rapid urbanisation is transforming the spatial structure of Indore and its surrounding peri\-urban landscape\. This proposed study aims to develop a GeoAI\-oriented framework for analysing historical urban expansion and predicting future land\-use/land\-cover \(LULC\) change\. The study will integrate Earth\-observation data, GIS layers, demographic information, accessibility variables, terrain characteristics, environmental constraints, and machine\-learning methods within a reproducible spatial workflow\. Verified secondary evidence indicates that Indore district covers 3,898 km² and had a population of 3,276,697 in 2011, including 2,427,709 urban and 848,988 rural residents\. Published analysis of the Indore urban/peri\-urban area reports an increase in built\-up area from 15\.30 km² in 1991 to 63\.57 km² in 2001 and 158\.11 km² in 2011\. These trends demonstrate the need for spatially explicit urban\-growth modelling\. The proposed study will identify the principal spatial drivers of urban expansion, classify historical LULC, develop and validate a GeoAI\-based prediction model, and generate a future urban\-growth probability surface for a selected prediction year, such as 2035 or 2045\. Final satellite\-derived LULC areas and model accuracy statistics will be calculated from the selected datasets and independent validation samples before the study is presented as a completed empirical analysis\.
