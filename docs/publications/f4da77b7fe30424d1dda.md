# Beyond Fun: Gamified Multidomain Reading Assessment for Children

**Autores:** Angela Pasqualotto; Manon Bochud; Jessy Damas; Zeno Menestrina; Irène Altarelli; Martina Röthlisberger; Johannes Christoph Ziegler; Pascal Zesiger; Silvia Brem; Daphné Bavelier

**Data:** 2026-10-17 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

This study presents the Computerized Reading\-related Assessment Battery \(CRAB\), a multidomain, child\-centered tablet platform designed to support scalable, school\-based assessment of linguistic and cognitive skills relevant to early reading\. We report results from a large\-scale school administration of CRAB to 310 second\-grade children in France and Italy, implemented in school settings with minimal adult supervision\. Feasibility was assessed through task\-by\-task completion rates and system\-logged durations: completion was high across tasks \(93–100%\), and task durations were developmentally appropriate \(median 2\.6–7\.5 min\)\. Usability and engagement were examined using embedded child\-friendly questionnaires and post\-assessment focus\-group feedback; children rated the battery as enjoyable and easy to use, and qualitative responses highlighted intuitive interaction and effective onboarding\. We also report task performance patterns showing that associations among CRAB outcomes were consistent with theoretically expected relationships across reading, reading\-related, and cognitive measures, supporting coherent functioning across the battery within a unified digital environment\. Constraints were concentrated in speech\-based tasks, reflecting ongoing challenges for speech input technologies with young children\. Overall, findings demonstrate the feasibility of implementing a multidomain, gamified assessment in authentic school contexts and provide empirically grounded design principles for scalable serious game–based digital assessment in education\.
