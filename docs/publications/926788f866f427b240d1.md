# Latent\-Space Importance Weighting under Covariate Shift

**Autores:** Yuling Jiao; Lican Kang; Yang Xiang; Jerry Zhijian Yang; Fusheng Zhou

**Data:** 2026-10-11 · **Tipo:** preprint

## Resumo

In this work, we propose a transfer learning algorithm for covariate shift, integrating sample reweighting with representation learning\. A key feature of our approach is the use of a lower\-dimensional latent space for modeling, in contrast to conventional methods that operate directly in the high\-dimensional ambient space\. Our method estimates the density ratio of learned latent features for downstream weighted prediction\. We establish explicit target\-domain excess\-risk rates with independent data folds for strictly sufficient learned representations with regular learned\-space prediction scores and density ratios\. We give dimension comparisons under stated smoothness conditions and a variance comparison for true weights and a fixed predictor\. General finite\-sample inequalities retain representation\-quality errors\. Experiments on simulated and real\-world datasets demonstrate gains from combining learned representations and latent weighting, and reveal sensitivity to initialization and domain choice\.
