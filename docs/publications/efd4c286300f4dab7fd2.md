# Teaching Competency in the Age of Artificial Intelligence: A Review of Teachers’ Attitudes, Adaptability, and Professional Commitment

**Autores:** Pratima devi; Anjana sonker

**Data:** 2026-10-17 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

The integration of Artificial Intelligence \(AI\) into in these aspects of educational environments has initiated more of a paradigm shift in pedagogical structural methodologies, assessment strategies, and the use of fundamental role of educators\. This comprehensive review always seems to be examined the current state of teaching competency in the age of AI, focusing here on three pivotal dimensions: teachers&\#x27; attitudes towards AI, their adaptability of such aspects in utilizing intelligent tools, and their professional commitment in an era of rapid technological disruption\-based aspects\. Drawing upon a more natured based synthesis of recent literature, we therefore explore how factors such as AI literacy, generative based AI competence, &amp; as also AI\-TPACK \(Technological Pedagogical Content Knowledge\) influence here in the successful integration of AI into classrooms everywhere\. The review in this aspects highlights that while educators here are increasingly by recognizing the potential of AI to more enhance personalized learning as well as administrative efficiency, there remain in this way to stay neutral significant barriers related to AI anxiety as also, ethical concerns, &amp; most of all a lack of targeted based on professional development\. Adaptability is conceptualized not merely as herein technical proficiency, but as a continuous pedagogical evolution requiring robust organizational support\-based aspects\. Furthermore, professional aspects of commitment in this nature always is analyzed through the lens of shifting teacher identities &amp; the ethical imperative to foster critical thinking and human\-centric values based through in AI\-augmented learning environments\. A prominent table\-based data related structure is provided to synthesize the thematic findings, mapping core competencies and against professional development needs\. The paper in this manner concludes with actionable recommendations for policymakers, teacher training programs, &amp; future research directions to ensure that educators are adequately equipped here to navigate the complexities of AI in education\.
