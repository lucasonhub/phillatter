# Builes, David: Why Non\-Aristotelians Are Out of Their Minds

**Autores:** 

**Data de descoberta:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** unknown

## Descrição do feed

\_Synthese\_\. forthcomingThe goal of this paper is to argue for an “Aristotelian” view of natural necessity, according to which it is metaphysically necessary that fundamental physical properties play the nomic role that they do, and moreover that this metaphysical necessity is explained in terms of the nature of the relevant fundamental properties\. The argument builds on an earlier argument defended by Hawthorne \(2004\) and Friend \(2023\), according to which Humean theories of natural necessity are incompatible with two highly intuitive claims about phenomenal consciousness\. However, I will be further arguing that popular non\-Humean accounts of natural necessity, except for an Aristotelian view, are also incompatible with the same two facts about phenomenal consciousness\.
