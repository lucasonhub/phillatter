# Issues of improving service quality in the hotel and restaurant industry

**Autores:** Ольга Олійник; Volodymyr Kotsiuk

**Data:** 2026-10-14 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

The hospitality industry plays a vital role in the global economy, providing services that require the highest standards to ensure customer satisfaction and operational success\. Managing quality in this environment involves a combination of effective, efficient operations\. This article explores the challenges and best practices of quality assurance in the hospitality industry, highlighting the importance of customer service delivery, employee training, and technological advancements\. A key component of quality control is the use of standardized methods\. These frameworks help businesses maintain operational efficiency, reduce risk, and improve the customer experience\. Furthermore, training and motivating employees play an important role in ensuring service quality\. Trained employees can effectively address customer needs, solve problems, and contribute to a successful brand\. Another important factor is customer feedback and continuous improvement\. The ability to receive, analyze, and adapt to customer feedback is critical to maintaining a competitive advantage\. In the digital age, online reviews and ratings have a major impact on customer decisions, making quality management a top priority for businesses\. Technological innovations such as artificial intelligence, automation, and intelligent service systems have revolutionized the quality of the hospitality industry\. AI\-powered chatbots, automated reservation systems, and data analytics enable businesses to personalize customer experiences, streamline processes, and improve operational efficiency\. However, despite technological advances, challenges such as employee turnover, cultural differences, and regulatory compliance still plague quality management\. A successful strategy involves balancing resource integration and people\-centric service delivery, ensuring that hospitality companies not only meet industry standards but also exceed customer expectations\. The article concludes that quality management is a dynamic and ongoing process that requires strategic planning, employee engagement, and adaptation to changing customer preferences\. The future of successful management in the hospitality industry will be shaped by digital transformation, sustainable development initiatives, and an increased focus on personalized service\.
