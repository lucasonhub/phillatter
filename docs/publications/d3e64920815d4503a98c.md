# AI Based Marketing Strategy to Improve Sales Performance of PT Ramayana Lestari Sentosa Tbk: A Technology\-Organization\-Environment \(TOE\) and Generative AI Recombination\-Targeting\-Iteration \(RTI\) Approach

**Autores:** Kevin Stevanus Irwanto; Regina Violyn Widjaja; Anastasia Christy Arviana Montana; Cindy Valentina Tamrin

**Data:** 2026-10-15 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

PT Ramayana Lestari Sentosa Tbk \(RALS\), one of Indonesia&\#x27;s oldest department store retailers, has experienced a consistent decline in sales performance and net profit in recent years, whereas comparable competitors have demonstrated financial growth over the same timeframe\. This study formulates an Artificial Intelligence \(AI\)\-based marketing strategy framework for RALS by integrating the Technology\-Organization\-Environment \(TOE\) model to assess organizational technology adoption readiness with the Generative AI Recombination\-Targeting\-Iteration \(RTI\) framework to structure its strategic deployment\. A descriptive qualitative approach was conducted using literature review synthesis and secondary document analysis encompassing annual reports, Public Expose presentations, and financial disclosures\. The findings reveal that RALS&\#x27;s readiness across the TOE dimensions is asymmetrical: the Technology and Organization dimensions remain at a moderate level, whereas competitive and macroeconomic pressures within the Environment dimension are critically high\. Consequently, a phased Gen\-AI RTI marketing strategy is formulated across three core pillars: \(1\) recombination, integrating fragmented legacy data assets \(Point of Sale, mobile membership, transaction records\) with Generative AI capabilities via a cost\-effective API gateway architecture; \(2\) targeting, prioritizing high\-ROI operational use cases across inventory forecasting, customer segmentation, and collaborative Sales &amp; Operations Planning; and \(3\) iteration, embedding continuous model calibration driven by structured human\-in\-the\-loop operational feedback mechanisms\. This research provides an adaptable implementation roadmap aligned with RALS&\#x27;s fiscal and workforce constraints, enriching the empirical discourse surrounding generative technology diffusion within conventional department store retail ecosystems in emerging markets\.
