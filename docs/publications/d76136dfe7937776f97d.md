# Artificial Intelligence in Livestock Farming: From Smart Monitoring to Smarter Animal Management

**Autores:** Mandhale Shubham and Aditya Adgaonkar Rathod Rohit Kailasrao

**Data:** 2026-10-12 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Livestock farming has always depended heavily on observation and experience\. Farmers learn to recognize subtle changes in an animal&\#x27;s appetite, movement, appearance, behaviour and productivity\. Veterinarians use clinical examination and diagnostic tests to identify disease, while farmers make daily decisions about feeding, breeding and herd management based on years of practical knowledge\. However, as livestock farms become larger and production systems become more complex, continuously monitoring every individual animal is becoming increasingly difficult\. This is where Artificial Intelligence \(AI\) is emerging as a powerful decision\-support technology\. Advances in Computer Vision, Machine Learning, Deep Learning, Internet of Things \(IoT\), smart sensors, Edge AI and robotics are creating new possibilities for monitoring animals in real time\. AI systems can analyse photographs, videos, movement patterns, environmental conditions and other farm\-generated data to identify abnormalities, estimate body weight, monitor behaviour and support health and production decisions\. The objective is not to replace farmers or veterinarians, but to provide them with timely and data\-driven information for better decision\-making\.
