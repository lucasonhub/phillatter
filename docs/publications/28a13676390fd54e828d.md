# Digital Health in Nursing: Opportunities, Challenges and the Road Ahead An Integrative Review

**Autores:** Vasudevan Nattamai Jothilal; Amal Al\-Alawi; A beer Al Maamari; Laila Al Hasani

**Data:** 2026-10-12 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Digital Transformation is bringing challenges to health systems in healthcare in all over the world where nurses makes a majority of the workforce in health systems and play an essential role in the sweep out process\. Gradually but definitely technologies like electronic health records, artificial intelligence for the clinical decision making, telemedicine, remote sensors and robotic assistance plays a significant role and a game changing process in which patient care is given and evaluated\. In spite of pandemic makes the digitalisation has driven forward the health sector little is known about its effects on nursing research today\. This integrative review was undertaken to synthesis the various evidence on digital transformation in nursing examining the opportunities its presents the challenges its poses and its impact on nursing roles and the directions its signal for future practices\. Authors performed an integrative review to fill this gap also it brings attention to study the data on digital information on nursing which mainly focuses on opportunities, challenges, impact the roles and practice and future innovative directions\. Researches from January 2014 to December 2023 were searched intensely through various databases such as CINAHL, PubMed, Scopus and Web of Science\. The review is mainly focused on problem identification, literature search, quality assessment, dataanalysis and thematic synthesis\. From 78 studies, five main themes emerged: The expanding role digital technologies in nursing, which brings new opportunities at both practice and system levels ongoing structural and ethical barriers evolving the nursing key roles and identity and future priorities for direction for education, policy and practice\. Evidence also states the digital tools improve care coordination, patient safety, clinical decision making and work force improvement\. Though there are few nurses who are digitally illiterate, similarly another challenge is unequal access part for workflow interruptions\. Ethical consideration is also another challenges such as data privacy, algorithm bias accountability and others\. Moreover, the review also understands adjustments in variation of nursing practice where advancedroles and informatics develop alongside concerns about maintaining the personal human side of the care\. Overall, digital transformation is a complex and evolving force in nursing that promises significant innovation and growth but requires careful attention to ethical, educational and systemic issues\. Successful integration depends on equitable technology access and establishing strong governance that preserves both clinical quality and the core human values of nursing\.
