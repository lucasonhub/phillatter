# Artificial Intelligence Marketing dan Consumer Trust sebagai Determinan Purchase Intention Generasi Z

**Autores:** Bambang Bambang

**Data:** 2026-10-13 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

This study aims to analyze the influence of Artificial Intelligence Marketing and Consumer Trust on Purchase Intention in Generation Z consumers\. A sample of 100 Generation Z respondents who actively use digital media and are exposed to AI\-based marketing was selected using purposive sampling techniques\. Data were collected through a Likert scale questionnaire of 1–5 and analyzed using multiple linear regression, t\-test, F test, and determination coefficient \(R2\) test\. The results showed that partially, Artificial Intelligence Marketing \(t = 12\.021; p = 0\.000\) and Consumer Trust \(t = 7\.873; p = 0\.000\) had a positive and significant effect on Purchase Intent\. Simultaneously, Artificial Intelligence Marketing and Consumer Trust had a significant effect on Purchase Intention \(F = 278\.811; p = 0\.000\)\. A determination coefficient value \(R2\) of 0\.852 showed that 85\.2% of the variation in Purchase Intention was influenced by these two variables, while the remaining 14\.8% was influenced by other factors outside the model\. This study concludes that optimizing personalization\-based AI marketing strategies coupled with maintaining consumer trust is a key determinant in encouraging Generation Z&\#x27;s buying interest\.
