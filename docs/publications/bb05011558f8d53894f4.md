# Use of Machine Learning to Predict Mental Health Disorders Using EEG Data: Selected Research Excerpts

**Autores:** Aiwanseosa Israel Omo\-Imade

**Data:** 2026-10-17 · **Tipo:** dissertation

## Resumo

This record contains selected, unedited excerpts from research completed in 2024 as part of an MSc in Artificial Intelligence at the University of Wolverhampton\. The study investigated the use of machine\-learning models, including Logistic Regression, Random Forest, Support Vector Machine and a Soft Voting Ensemble, for predicting mental health disorders using electroencephalography \(EEG\) data\. The selected excerpts cover the research rationale, methodology, model evaluation, interpretation of findings, clinical implications, methodological limitations and recommendations for future work\. This public portfolio edition has been prepared as a concise research and analytical writing sample\. It does not represent the complete dissertation and has not undergone peer review\.
