# PSYCHOLOGICAL MECHANISMS OF YOUNG PEOPLE&\#x27;S PSYCHOLOGICAL WELL\-BEING AND PERSONAL SELF\-REGULATION IN THE CONTEXT OF INTERACTION WITH ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE

**Autores:** Ibrohimova Feruza

**Data:** 2026-10-09 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

The thesis examines the psychological mechanisms through which interaction with artificial intelligence \(AI\) affects the psychological well\-being and personal self\-regulation of young people\. Four bivalent mechanisms are identified: cognitive offloading, transformation of basic psychological needs, emotional co\-regulation and reflexive calibration of trust\. It is argued that AI may either scaffold or substitute the young person&\#x27;s own regulatory resources, and that this determines its effect on well\-being\.
