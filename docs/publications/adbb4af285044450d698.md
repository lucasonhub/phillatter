# Kakos, Spyridon: Non\-local consciousness NDEs and other arguments against the brain

**Autores:** 

**Data de descoberta:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** unknown

## Descrição do feed

\_Harmonia Philosophica Papers\_\. 2026What is consciousness? Even though the current mainstream view suggests that the brain is the producer of consciousness in a materialistic and reductionist context, there is ample evidence supporting an alternative model: that the brain acts as a receiver of consciousness instead\. This paper discusses evidence from cases where consciousness exists despite a non\-working brain, from cases where the brain is nearly absent and from data related to non\-local consciousness\. All this evidence points towards a different viewpoint than the materialistic one that is currently accepted: The brain acting as a receiver or filter of consciousness\. Such a model fits perfectly with all the currently available data and at the same time, solves the problems the current local consciousness model cannot explain\. Without claiming to offer a definite solution to the problem, one thing is certain: The evidence calls for a more open mind when viewing the problem of consciousness\. I am me\. That I know\. But where does that stem from? Let’s keep our mind open to everything and we might discover that the world is more exciting than we could ever consider\. \(direct link \)
