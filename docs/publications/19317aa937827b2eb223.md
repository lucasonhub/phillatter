# A comparative analysis of the effect of different drying treatments on the volatile organic compounds of polygonati odorati rhizoma

**Autores:** Haili Tang; Shijia Zhang; Ziran Yu; Qiuye Liu; 左亚杰

**Data:** 2026-10-15 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Introduction Polygonati Odorati Rhizoma \(POR\) is a widely recognized functional food, volatile organic compounds \(VOCs\) as one of their main active metabolites\. Drying is a crucial link in the front\-end of the product production chain\. There have been no comparison investigations on the effects of different drying methods on the VOCs of POR\. Methods Gas chromatography\-ion mobility spectrometry \(GC\-IMS\) was utilized to detect and analyze the VOCs of POR samples treated with vacuum freeze\-drying \(VFD\), vacuum drying \(VD\), sun drying \(SD\), hot\-air drying \(HAD\), and microwave drying \(MD\)\. Analytical methods including Principal Component Analysis \(PCA\), Cluster Analysis \(CA\), and Partial Least\-Squares Discriminant Analysis \(PLS\-DA\) were used\. Results A total of 56 VOCs were detected\. Principal component analysis \(PCA\), cluster analysis \(CA\), and partial least\-squares discriminant analysis \(PLS\-DA\), consistently showed that the VOCs of VFD\- and MD\-treated samples were the closest to those of SD samples, whereas VD and HAD differed more markedly\. Representative drying\-specific markers tentatively identified as \(E\)\-2\-heptenal and ethyl hexanoate in SD, 2,5\-dimethyl thiophene in HAD, \(E\)\-2\-butenal in MD, 2\-butanol in VD, and 3\-methyl\-3\-buten\-1\-ol in VFD were observed\. Conclusions These results indicate that different drying methods significantly affect the VOC profile of POR\. VFD and MD are promising for VOC retention\.
