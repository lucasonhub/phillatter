# Collected Papers, Vol\. XVIII \(Frontiers of Neutrosophy: Continuum Systems, Contradiction, Uncertainty, and Interdisciplinary Applications\)

**Autores:** Florentin Smarandache

**Data:** 2026-10-13 · **Tipo:** book

## Resumo

Collected Papers, Vol\. XVIII – Frontiers of Neutrosophy: Continuum Systems, Contradiction, Uncertainty, and Interdisciplinary Applications is a substantial and wide\-ranging volume, bringing together 79 articles organized into ten thematic chapters\. The contributions, originally published in a variety of international scientific journals, reflect the breadth, diversity, and continuing evolution of contemporary research in neutrosophy, plithogeny, uncertainty modeling, and related interdisciplinary domains\. The volume explores a rich spectrum of scientific and philosophical fields, including continuum neutrosophic systems, logic, mathematics, statistics, information geometry, decision theory, artificial intelligence, physics, complex adaptive systems, topology, graph theory, cognitive science, medicine, architecture, and the philosophy of science\. Particular attention is devoted to the study of uncertainty, indeterminacy, contradiction, and the integration of heterogeneous forms of knowledge through neutrosophic and plithogenic frameworks\. Several chapters advance foundational developments in continuum neutrosophic sets, logic, probability, statistics, and information geometry, while others investigate emerging directions in infinitesimally punctured physics, multi\-criteria decision making, generalized uncertainty structures, and the mathematical foundations of neutrosophic graphs, topologies, algebras, and superhyperstructures\. The volume also extends beyond the traditional boundaries of science and mathematics, incorporating contributions on personality theory, evolution, consciousness studies, paradoxism, anti\-art, Meta\-Garde theory, and pluriversal approaches to culture, law, and anthropology\. All papers are authored or co\-authored by Florentin Smarandache, either individually or in collaboration with 47 distinguished co\-authors from diverse scientific and cultural backgrounds\. This collaborative dimension underscores both the international reach of neutrosophic research and its capacity to stimulate dialogue across disciplines\. The collected works reveal a coherent intellectual vision centered on the systematic study of uncertainty and contradiction, while simultaneously demonstrating the remarkable versatility of neutrosophic methodologies in addressing theoretical and practical challenges\. Taken together, the papers assembled in this eighteenth volume provide both a comprehensive reference and a forward\-looking perspective on the expanding role of neutrosophic, plithogenic, and multi\-valued approaches in contemporary science, mathematics, technology, and philosophy\. They document ongoing efforts to develop more flexible and realistic models of complex phenomena and offer new avenues for understanding systems characterized by incomplete, inconsistent, or evolving information\.
