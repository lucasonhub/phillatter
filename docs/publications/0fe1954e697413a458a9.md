# Indian Philosophy: Ancient Wisdom for the Modern World: Critical Epistemology, Comparative Thought, and Contemporary Applications

**Autores:** Ras Bihari Sharma; Smt\. Anuradha Kumari

**Data:** 2026-10-17 · **Tipo:** book

## Resumo

Indian Philosophy: Ancient Wisdom for the Modern World explores the major traditions, concepts, and debates of Indian philosophical thought and their relevance to contemporary intellectual life\. It examines classical Indian systems through critical epistemology, comparative philosophy, logic, metaphysics, consciousness, ethics, and contemporary applications\. The book covers the six orthodox schools of Indian philosophy, heterodox traditions including Buddhism, Jainism, and Charvaka, theories of knowledge \(pramana\), logical reasoning, language and meaning, the nature of the self, consciousness, karma, dharma, and liberation\. It also compares Indian and Western philosophical perspectives and discusses connections with artificial intelligence, ecology, sustainable living, democracy, and social justice\. Designed for students, teachers, researchers, and general readers, this book provides a structured introduction to Indian philosophical traditions, their historical development, internal debates, and continuing significance in the modern world\.
