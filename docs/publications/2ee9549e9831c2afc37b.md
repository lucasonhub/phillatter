# Spermidine combined with resistance exercise ameliorates myocardial injury and mitochondrial dysfunction via AMPK\-SIRT1\-PGC\-1α in D\-galactose\-induced aging rats

**Autores:** Kunda Yang; Lili Sun; Yu Cheng; Kunxiang Liang; Nixi Huang; Yunshuang Liu; Yewenqian Zhang; Chuangye Liu; Lina Peng

**Data:** 2026-10-17 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Age\-related decline in myocardial structure and function is closely linked to dysregulated mitophagy, impaired mitochondrial biogenesis, and elevated apoptosis\. Spermidine \(SPD\), a naturally occurring polyamine, and resistance exercise \(RE\) are both known to induce autophagy and exert cardioprotective anti\-aging effects\. In the present study, we investigated whether combined SPD and RE intervention ameliorates myocardial injury and mitochondrial dysfunction in D\-galactose\-induced aging rats, and explored the underlying mechanisms\. With the exception of a control group, all rats received 6 weeks of daily intraperitoneal D\-galactose injections to induce aging\. Successful model establishment was verified by quantifying expression of the senescence\-associated genes p53, p21, and p16, and by conducting hematoxylin–eosin \(H&amp;E\) and Masson’s trichrome staining\. Following model validation, rats in the aging cohort were assigned to three intervention groups treated for 8 weeks: RE alone, RE \+ vehicle \(VRE\), and SPD \+ RE\. Myocardial tissues were assessed using H&amp;E staining, Masson’s trichrome staining, and transmission electron microscopy\. Myocardial levels of adenosine triphosphate \(ATP\), citrate synthase \(CS\), cytochrome c oxidase subunit I \(COX I\), and mitochondrial membrane potential \(MMP\) were quantified\. Markers of mitochondrial biogenesis, mitophagy, and apoptosis were also quantified\. Molecules of the AMPK\-SIRT1\-PGC\-1α pathway were measured using reverse transcription\-quantitative polymerase chain reaction \(RT\-qPCR\), Western blotting, and immunohistochemistry\. Combined SPD \+ RE intervention significantly ameliorated pathological myocardial structural alterations, mitochondrial morphological abnormalities, and functional impairment\. It elevated myocardial ATP, CS, COX I, and MMP levels, and activated the AMPK\-SIRT1\-PGC\-1α pathway\. Specifically, it upregulated p\-AMPK, SIRT1, PGC\-1α, NRF1, and TFAM to promote mitochondrial biogenesis\. Enhanced mitophagy was confirmed by an increased LC3B\-II/LC3B\-I ratio and reduced p62 expression, while suppressed apoptosis was verified by upregulated Bcl\-2 and downregulated Bax\. In conclusion, combined SPD and RE intervention ameliorates age\-related myocardial injury and mitochondrial dysfunction by activating the AMPK\-SIRT1\-PGC\-1α signaling pathway, promoting mitochondrial biogenesis and mitophagy, and inhibiting apoptosis\.
