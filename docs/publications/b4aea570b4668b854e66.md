# Mihal, Jan &amp; Black, C\. F\.: Flowing with AI: Towards a New Jurisprudence

**Autores:** 

**Data de descoberta:** 2026-10-19 · **Tipo:** unknown

## Descrição do feed

This Element introduces a new jurisprudence for exploring artificial intelligence \(AI\) and law in an open and creative way\. Drawing upon the backgrounds of, respectively, an Indigenous jurisprudent and poet \(Black\) and a legal philosopher \(Mihal\), the Element develops a &\#x27;mythosophical&\#x27; approach informed by narrative theory, Indigenous jurisprudence, and original interpretations of stories from the past\. To imagine possible lawful futures with AI, the Element weaves a stream of what it calls &\#x27;flows&\#x27;, designed to allow readers openness and flexibility as they engage with the ideas underpinning this new jurisprudence\. As worldwide changes sweep us into a vortex of ceaseless innovation, the Element provides a cultural scaffolding through which to relate to AI as an autonomous, cunning, &\#x27;non\-human&\#x27; intelligence in lawful terms\. It offers insights both old and new which assist in reconceptualising problems and aligning with the paradigm\-shattering changes of the present day\. \(direct link \)
