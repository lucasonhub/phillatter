# Cbp, Hn: AAA\-08 \- The Illusion of Choice in Personalized Worlds

**Autores:** 

**Data de descoberta:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** unknown

## Descrição do feed

\_Agency in the Age of Algorithms\_\. 2026Contemporary systems do not eliminate choice — they displace authorship\. This paper argues that algorithmic personalization operates as a specific form of environmental design: unlike nudging or behavioral architecture, it re\-organizes the field of possible actions before intention has occasion to form, producing a mode of selection that retains the phenomenology of freedom while dissolving the conditions under which choices can be owned\. Within personalized environments, preferences become recursively self\-confirming, counterfactual imagination erodes, and deliberative time collapses into guided flow\. The cost of being wrong is lowered — but so is the weight of being right\. Responsibility disperses not because agents refuse to claim it, but because no participant occupies a position from which ownership of the decision could plausibly be asserted\. The paper&\#x27;s central claim is that the philosophically significant question is not whether algorithmic systems influence decisions — they demonstrably do — but whether conditions remain under which a decision can be stood behind as one&\#x27;s own\. This question belongs to the philosophy of action: specifically, to debates about what structural conditions are required for intentional action to generate genuine authorship rather than mere behavioral output\. Where those conditions dissolve, choice becomes an interface event rather than a moral act\. Drift, sustained at scale, reshapes societies more profoundly than force ever could\. \(direct link \)
