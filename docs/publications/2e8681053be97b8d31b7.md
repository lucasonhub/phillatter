# Exploraciones digestivas\. La relación estética y conceptual entre cine y performance en la película Crímenes del futuro de David Cronenberg

**Autores:** Mayra Citlalli Rojo Gómez

**Data:** 2026-10-09 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

The paper argues that the film Crimes of the Future opens up a critical dialogue on the relationship between the body and technology through two controversial figures in the world of performance art: Orlan and Stelarc\. The tensions arising from this relationship are framed by the premise that the evolution of bodies is not to be found in the body\-machine hybridisation and prosthetics; rather, it lies in the mysterious rebellion of the organs: the organ in question—or rather, the system in conflict—is here the digestive system\. Digestion is therefore explored as the film’s acoustic identity, as an aesthetic experience, and as the conceptual framework for the analytical writing of this text\.
