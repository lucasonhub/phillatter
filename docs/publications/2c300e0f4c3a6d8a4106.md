# Methylation\-associated downregulation of CRABP1 marks an aggressive malignant epithelial cell state in thyroid carcinoma

**Autores:** Tiancheng Fu; Yudong Li; 黄国香; Xiarong Hu; Guangfeng Luo; Chengcheng Wang; Yanze Wang; Ziyang Ding; Jinghua Zhou; Zhen Li

**Data:** 2026-10-14 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Papillary thyroid carcinoma \(PTC\) is the most common thyroid malignancy, yet molecular features that distinguish indolent tumors from those with progression\-associated clinicopathological features remain limited\. Here, we integrated single\-cell RNA sequencing of 17 PTC samples with copy\-number inference and identified a CRABP1\-positive malignant epithelial cell population that retained epithelial features \(CDH1, KRT19\) and exhibited a distinct transcriptional program\. In TCGA\-THCA bulk data, lower CRABP1 expression was associated with lymph\-node metastasis and pathological T\-category variation\. Functional experiments in complementary thyroid carcinoma cell\-line models showed that CRABP1 knockdown increased migration and invasion and was accompanied by an EMT\-related transcriptional shift, whereas CRABP1 overexpression showed the opposite pattern\. Public methylation analyses showed higher aggregate promoter\-region methylation in tumors, whereas methylation of cg02276974, an intron\-2/gene\-body CpG, was weakly inversely associated with CRABP1 expression \(r = −0\.2069, R 2 = 0\.0428; P &lt; 0\.001\)\. Decitabine altered migration, invasion, Vimentin\-associated, and xenograft phenotypes in CRABP1\-manipulated models\. Collectively, these results identify a CRABP1\-positive malignant epithelial cell state associated with clinicopathological features in PTC and support an invasion\-associated suppressive role for CRABP1 in thyroid carcinoma models\. The methylation and decitabine findings define a methylation\-associated pharmacological context; they do not establish CRABP1\-specific or locus\-specific epigenetic causality\.
