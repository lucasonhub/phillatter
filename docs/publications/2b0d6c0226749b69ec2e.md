# MAIN DIRECTIONS AND RESULTS OF SCIENTIFIC RESEARCH IN THE FIELD OF ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE

**Autores:** Utkirbek Mutalibjonovich Muxtarov

**Data:** 2026-10-12 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

This scientific article highlights the main directions of scientific research in the field of artificial intelligence, the results achieved, and their significance in the development of modern society\. It analyzes the developmental characteristics of areas such as machine learning, deep learning, natural language processing, computer vision, robotics, and generative artificial intelligence\. Opportunities arising from the application of artificial intelligence technologies in the fields of education, medicine, industry, economics, and information technology will also be considered\.
