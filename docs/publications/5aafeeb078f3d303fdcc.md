**Autores:** Amin Jajarmi

## Resumo

ABSTRACT In this paper, the dynamics and regulation of a generalized fractional‐order memristive Chua circuit are investigated from practical, mathematical, and data‐oriented perspectives\. The proposed system involves the ‐Caputo fractional derivative, including a general kernel function , which allows it to describe heterogeneous memory effects and non‐uniform time scaling\. The paper proves the existence and uniqueness of solutions for the ‐fractional system via Volterra‐type integral equations and the Banach fixed‐point theorem\. Moreover, we show that after a full equilibrium analysis, a memristive element generates a line of equilibria along the internal state variable \(‐axis\)\. Furthermore, Matignon&\#x27;s stability criterion defines the stability region in the complex plane and determines the local asymptotic stability of equilibrium points\. The numerical implementation is further validated using an exact‐solution benchmark and compared with an independent L1 predictor‐corrector method\.
