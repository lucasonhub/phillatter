# Physics\-informed learning detects islanding and discriminates power\-quality disturbances in grid\-forming inverter\-dominated distribution networks

**Autores:** Yuanyuan Xu; Ehab Seedahmed; Essa Alyounis; Naoufel Kraïem; Mohammad Nazir Ahmad

**Data:** 2026-10-12 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

The proliferation of grid\-forming \(GFM\) inverters in modern distribution networks has fundamentally altered the islanding\-detection problem\. Unlike grid\-following converters, droop\- and virtual\-synchronous\-generator \(VSG\)\-controlled GFM units actively regulate voltage and frequency, suppressing the very deviations that conventional passive schemes rely upon and thereby enlarging the non\-detection zone \(NDZ\) under near\-balanced power conditions\. This paper proposes a Physics\-Informed Lightning\-Search\-Optimised Temporal Attention Network \(PI\-LSO\-TAN\) that jointly detects unintentional islanding and discriminates it from power\-quality disturbances \(PQDs\) and non\-islanding faults in GFM\-dominated feeders\. The framework fuses a physics\-informed feature set rate\-of\-change\-of\-frequency \(ROCOF\) estimated from the analytic signal, total harmonic distortion, phase\-jump and current\-imbalance descriptors with a raw\-window convolutional embedding processed by a self\-attention pooling layer\. A Lightning Search Algorithm \(LSA\) simultaneously optimises the network hyperparameters and a physics\-informed decision threshold, removing manual tuning\. Across 10 independent dataset realisations of a documented, fully reproducible synthetic GFM feeder operated in the near\-balanced NDZ regime, PI\-LSO\-TAN attains a four\-class test accuracy of 96\.34% \(SD 0\.96 percentage points\), a macro\-F1 of 0\.9636 and an islanding recall of 93\.49% at an islanding false\-positive rate of 0\.70%, misdeclaring 2\.40 of 175 power\-quality windows as islanding\. Two findings define what the method does and does not contribute\. First, when ten baselines spanning kernel, ensemble, convolutional, recurrent, attention\-based and graph architectures are given the identical physics\-informed side\-channel, all 11 models fall within 1\.20 percentage points of one another; none of the 6 deep sequence architectures differs significantly from the proposed model under Holm\-corrected Wilcoxon tests, and of the ten baselines only 1 differ significantly at all MLP\-SVM hybrid, ahead by up to 0\.73 percentage points these being among the simplest feature\-space classifiers in the comparison\. On this task the discriminative power resides in the physics\-informed representation rather than in the temporal\-modelling mechanism\. Substituting an alternative fourteen\-dimensional representation confirms this directly principal components of the raw window reach 52\.37%, wavelet band energies 73\.60%, generic statistics 62\.94%, and a purely learned embedding 30\.17%, against 96\.20% for the physics\-informed set\. Second, noise resilience is a property of the training distribution, not of the architecture: training at a single SNR leaves accuracy at 52\.18% when the measurement noise floor moves to 30dB, whereas sampling the training SNR across 15 dB to 50 dB restores 88\.79% at 30dB and 84\.54% at 20dB for a cost of − 6\.14 percentage points at the nominal operating point\. The practical contribution is protective rather than architectural\. A security\-calibrated passive over/under\-frequency, ROCOF and over/under\-voltage relay detects none of the 3872 scanned near\-balanced islanding events, leaving a 100\.0% non\-detection area, whereas the proposed detector reduces it to 0\.00%, with no cell of the power\-mismatch plane falling below 87\.5% detection\. Measured sliding\-window detection latency is 80 ms on average and 130 ms at the 95th percentile, inside the 2 s envelope of IEEE 1547\-2018 and IEC 62116, at 337 μsof computation per window and 28\.7 kB of parameters\. The results indicate that reliable anti\-islanding protection in inverter\-dominated grids is obtained by pairing a physics\-informed representation with noise\-augmented training, and that the resulting detector covers a region of the operating plane in which conventional passive protection is blind by construction\.
