# Künstliche Intelligenz – Chance oder Risiko? Vortrag beim Katholischen Bildungswerk Frankenburg am Hausruck

**Autores:** Alexander Jung

**Data:** 2026-10-13 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

lides of a general\-audience evening talk \(about 90 minutes plus discussion\), given inGerman at the Catholic adult\-education centre \(Katholisches Bildungswerk\) inFrankenburg am Hausruck, Upper Austria, on 13 October 2026\. The talk explains, without formulas, what artificial intelligence can and cannot dotoday\. Part 1 shows where AI already sits in everyday life \(timetables, weather,navigation, licence\-plate recognition, recommendations\)\. Part 2 covers data as the rawmaterial, from monastic tithe registers to social media, the planned Google datacentre in Kronstorf, and open government data\. Part 3 demonstrates AI agents on threelive examples: analysing the Frankenburg municipal council minutes 2021–2026, planninga bicycle tour, and a study of Alpine pumped\-hydro storage\. Part 4 contrastsopportunities \(retinal\-image diagnostics\) with risks: hallucination, deepfakes,accountability, military use\. It closes with three take\-home messages\. Includes the video shown in the talk: an AI\-agent\-generated flood visualisation\(Perschling valley, September 2024\) together with the slide explaining its limits\.Text and the author&\#x27;s own figures are CC BY 4\.0; third\-party images and screenshotsare credited on the final slide and excluded from the licence\.
