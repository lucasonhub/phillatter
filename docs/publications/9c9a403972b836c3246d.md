# Dual\-Channel Information Topology in the Chrysene Formalism: Noncommutative Transport, Latent\-State Dynamics, and a Falsifiable Cognitive Interpretation

**Autores:** Charles D\. Schaper

**Data:** 2026-10-09 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

We develop a dual\-channel information topology as a model\-relative extension of the twelve\-axiom Chrysene Formalism\. The inherited molecular structure supplies inequivalent C3\-\-C9 and C12\-\-C6 directional data, but does not by itself imply Euclidean orthogonality, electronic isolation, transfer anisotropy, or cognitive function\. We therefore separate four levels: the protected Chrysene foundation; definitional channel mathematics; realization\-specific transport and hidden\-state hypotheses; and formal cognitive/neuromorphic interpretation\. The mathematical extension introduces channel\-saturation topologies, a four\-state Boolean channel lattice distinct from the inherited four\-state cipher, a direct\-sum transport model with quantitative residual leakage, and a three\-sector crosspoint space comprising a C3\-\-C9 control pathway, a persistent internal\-state sector, and a C12\-\-C6 readout pathway\. Eliminating the hidden block yields an exact memory\-kernel representation of the accessible dynamics; a finite\-time linear observation space characterizes state discrimination, while a separately defined generated dynamical algebra records multiplicative growth\. Oriented reachability yields a complete Heyting algebra and non\-Boolean intuitionistic semantics\. A corrected four\-terminal bipartite/Hückel core model further shows that both intended terminal pairs are transmission\-permitted but electronically inequivalent, with selective cross\-terminal interference zeros on one delocalized chrysene π substrate\. We then prove a finite neuromorphic consistency result in which unequal response scales, persistent hidden state, bounded nonzero direct cross\-talk, state\-mediated influence, rapid automatic processing, and later reportable accessibility can coexist without assigning consciousness to a molecular channel\. Physical realization, transport\-time ordering, and cognitive adequacy remain independently testable claims\.
