# FROM AI ADOPTION TO INTELLIGENT PHARMACEUTICAL OPERATIONS

**Autores:** Dr\. Parag Das, Animesh Maity,  Manashi Das

**Data:** 2026-10-14 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Artificial intelligence \(AI\) and machine learning \(ML\) are increasingly being considered across pharmaceutical manufacturing, quality management, maintenance, supply chain, regulatory activities and product development\. However, the central challenge for pharmaceutical organizations is no longer whether AI can generate technical possibilities, but how AI can be converted into reliable, measurable and compliant operational value\. This conceptual paper develops an AI\-to\-Value framework for pharmaceutical operations by integrating five dimensions: use\-case relevance, data readiness, model credibility, GMP and governance controls, and human decision accountability\. The framework proposes that AI value creation is mediated by data quality and organizational readiness and moderated by risk\-based governance and human oversight\. Illustrative use cases are discussed across tablet manufacturing, visual inspection, predictive maintenance, supply planning, deviation management and workforce development\. The paper further proposes research propositions that can be empirically tested using case studies, surveys or quantitative modelling\. The contribution is a practical conceptual architecture that connects AI adoption with operational excellence, rather than treating AI implementation as a technology acquisition exercise\. The framework suggests that successful pharmaceutical AI requires a progression from isolated pilots to governed, measurable and scalable intelligent operations\.
