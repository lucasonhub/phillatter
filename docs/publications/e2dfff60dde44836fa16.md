# Overcoming the strength\-ductility trade\-off in the Ni46Co23Cr23Al4Ta4 multi\-principal element alloys through hierarchical grain\-precipitate\-interface engineering

**Autores:** Pingkai Luan; Fachang Zhao; Dongqi Hu; Guoke Liang; Ruixin Wang; Honggang Sun; Jianhong Gong; Xiaoliang Han; Chongde Cao; J\.C\. Qiao; Kaikai Song

**Data:** 2026-10-09 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Overcoming the strength\-ductility trade\-off remains a major challenge in the development of face\-centered cubic \(FCC\) multi\-principal element alloys \(MPEAs\) for structural applications\. In this work, a hierarchical grain\-precipitate\-interface heterostructure was developed in a Ni 46 Co 23 Cr 23 Al 4 Ta 4 MPEA through a multistep thermomechanical route that enables the synergistic regulation of recrystallization, dislocation substructures, and precipitation\. Hot extrusion produces a lamellar precursor microstructure with a high dislocation density and facilitates the pre\-precipitation of L1 2 nanoparticles\. Subsequent cold rolling and annealing generate a heterogeneous microstructure comprising interspersed recrystallized fine\-grained regions and non\-recrystallized coarse\-grained domains, together with pronounced dislocation\-density gradients and spatially heterogeneous L1 2 \(nano\)precipitates\. Coherent L1 2 nanoprecipitates are distributed within both recrystallized and non\-recrystallized regions, whereas submicron L1 2 particles appear at grain boundaries \(GBs\) in the recrystallized regions\. The high yield strength arises primarily from coherent L1 2 precipitation strengthening and dislocation strengthening, particularly from dislocations accumulated near heterogeneous interfaces\. The hierarchical architecture promotes strain partitioning between relatively soft and hard regions, generating pronounced hetero\-deformation\-induced \(HDI\) stress and thus maintaining a high strain\-hardening capacity\. As a result, the alloy exhibits a yield strength of 1580 ± 30 MPa, an ultimate tensile strength of 1700 ± 20 MPa, and a total elongation of approximately 15%\. These findings establish an effective grain\-precipitate\-interface engineering strategy for developing FCC\-type MPEAs with a favorable combination of strength and ductility\.
