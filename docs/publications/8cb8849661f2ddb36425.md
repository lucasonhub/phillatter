# Achieving sustainable desertification control: insights from symbiosis between the Taklamakan Desert\-fringe shelter\-belt enclosure project and desert\-based industries

**Autores:** Hua Li

**Data:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

There is an inherent trade\-off between ecological restoration goals and the survival needs of inhabitants in desertification control\. From an ecological perspective, large\-scale planting of trees and grasses, sand fixation through enclosed forest cultivation, and rotational cropping are core measures for curbing shifting sands, restoring soil and water, and building robust ecological barriers to prevent further desertification\. From an economic perspective, local residents in desert areas rely heavily on traditional grassland grazing, desert reclamation, and the harvesting of desert shrubs for fuel and forage\. This gives rise to the tangible dilemma of “ecological restoration versus subsistence survival” in desertification control\. Desert\-based industries are considered conducive to addressing the trade\-off dilemma between ecological restoration and economic gain\. This study was conducted through a multi\-case study of the symbiosis between the Taklamakan Desert\-fringe Shelter\-belt Enclosure Project \(TDSEP\), a part of China’s Three Norths Shelterbelt Program \(TNSP\), and six types of desert\-based industries embedded in the TDSEP, including the Yutian rose industry, Kunyu jujube industry, Hetian walnut \+ jujube industry, Shache Eucommia Industry, Shaya centralized photovoltaic industry, and Shaya “saxaul \+ cistanche deserticola \+ distributed photovoltaic” industry\. It was found that the factors and mechanisms impacting the symbiosis between TDSEP and industry\-based industries are categorized into three categories: the symbiotic environment and material factors of production, the technological factors of production, and the transformative agencies enabling symbiosis, thus responding to the research questions proposed in the introduction section\. Therefore, policy insights and implications may involve fostering a favorable environment for symbiosis, selecting adaptable symbiotic units, promoting technological advancements, and forming an inclusive participating network among stakeholders\.
