# JoyInside\-VLM\-Agent: Product\-Conditioned Multimodal Policy Learning for Embodied Agents

**Autores:** Gangyi Li; LuYao Wang; Jingfu Sun; Jingyi Yuan; Hang Liu; Hao Zong; Yujia Hu; Tingting Gao; Jianyong Wang; Xu Yang

**Data:** 2026-10-09 · **Tipo:** preprint

## Resumo

Embodied agents must translate multimodal observations and user requests into executable actions while adapting to product constraints, changing goals, and execution feedback\. We present JoyInside\-VLM\-Agent, a ReAct\-style multimodal agent developed through product\-conditioned trajectory construction and staged policy learning for robotic platforms and smart hardware\. A closed\-loop sandbox generates multimodal interaction trajectories covering goal revisions and failure recovery, which are checked against execution outcomes and semantic criteria\. Supervised fine\-tuning and multi\-teacher on\-policy distillation precede reinforcement learning with DPER \(Dual\-Source Privileged On\-Policy Self\-Distillation with Token\-Level Evidence Routing\)\. DPER routes token\-level evidence between a frozen privileged teacher and a privileged student sharing the rollout policy&\#x27;s parameters, modulating reward\-derived token advantages while preserving their signs\. Training rewards combine generative process evaluation, multi\-judge rankings, and task\-queue consistency\. JoyInside\-VLM\-Agent achieves a BFCL v4 overall score of 77\.44, exceeding the evaluated baselines; results on the other public benchmarks are mixed\. On JoyInside\-Devices and JoyInside\-Robotics, the evaluated system, which includes domain\-specific skill refinement, leads the baselines on all reported metrics\. Two real\-world quadruped case studies illustrate visually grounded search, replanning after execution failure, and explicit failure reporting\.
