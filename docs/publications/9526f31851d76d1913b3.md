# BOSHLANG‘ICH SINF O‘QUVCHILARIDA O‘QISH SAVODXONLIGI DARSLARI ORQALI MUSTAQIL O‘QISH KO‘NIKMALARINI SHAKLLANTIRISHNING PEDAGOGIK\-PSIXOLOGIK ASOSLARI

**Autores:** JO&\#x27;RABEK NORMENGOV

**Data:** 2026-10-13 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

This article presents a scientific and theoretical analysis of the pedagogical and psychological foundations of developing independent reading skills in primary school pupils in “Native Language and Reading Literacy” lessons\. Vygotsky’s zone of proximal development, Elkonin and Davydov’s theory of learning activity, Egorov’s stages of reading skill acquisition, Chall’s stages of reading development and the Simple View of Reading are considered as Ta’limda istiqbolli izlanishlar \- xalqaro ilmiy\-metodik jurnal № 9/2026 ISSN: 3060\-527X interrelated frameworks\. A holistic model of independent reading skill is proposed, comprising four structural components \(technical, semantic, metacognitive and motivational\-value\), together with a technology for its stage\-by\-stage development in grades 1–4, a sample lesson and assessment criteria\.
