# STRATEGI GURU DALAM MENGHADAPI TANTANGAN PEMANFAATAN ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE \(AI\) UNTUK MENUMBUHKAN KEMAMPUAN BERPIKIR KRITIS SISWA: PEMBELAJARAN BAHASA INDONESIA

**Autores:** Raudhatil Maulani Maulani; Baiq Rismarini Nursaly; Ahmad Sirajuddin

**Data:** 2026-10-14 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

The use of Artificial Intelligence \(AI\) in Indonesian language learning provides convenience in accessing information and completing assignments, but also presents challenges for teachers in ensuring appropriate AI use and fostering students’ critical thinking skills\. This study aims to describe the challenges faced by teachers in utilizing AI and the strategies employed to foster students’ critical thinking skills in Indonesian language learning at the senior high school level\. This qualitative study collected data through interviews with Indonesian language teachers\. The findings indicate that the main challenges include limited supervision of students’ AI use outside school, students’ tendency to copy AI\-generated outputs without understanding or processing the information, difficulties in assessing the authenticity of students’ work, and the need for consistency in developing critical thinking skills\. Teachers’ strategies include providing guidance and limitations on AI use, using AI\-generated outputs as materials for critical evaluation, designing tasks that require thinking processes and contextual understanding, and shifting assessment orientation from products to learning processes\. These strategies can encourage students to analyze, evaluate, verify, and express opinions about information obtained through AI\. Thus, AI can serve as both a learning resource and an object of critical examination without replacing students’ thinking processes\.
