# Digital Marketing Governance for Enhancing MSME Sales Performance in Malang, Indonesia

**Autores:** Fullchis Nurtjahjani; Galih Putra Riatma; Ade Ismail; Ane Fany Novitasari

**Data:** 2026-10-11 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

The Melati MSME Association in Malang faces challenges with manual marketing governance, leading to data inaccuracy, weak coordination, and potential income manipulation\. This study aims to develop a digital\-based marketing governance application to enhance transparency, accountability, and sales\. The method used is Research and Development \(R&amp;D\) with a Prototyping approach, involving 15 MSME members and 3 administrators\. System testing includes Black Box Testing, System Usability Scale \(SUS\) questionnaires, and before\-after comparative analysis\. The results show that the Melati Digital application was successfully implemented with 100% of main features functioning\. Technology adoption reached 86\.6%, with an SUS score of 82\.4 \(Excellent category\)\. Comparative analysis proves the data error rate dropped drastically from ±15\-20% to &lt;2%, and reporting efficiency increased by 90%\. It is concluded that integrating technology into marketing governance effectively prevents moral hazard, strengthens good governance principles, and sustainably improves MSME sales performance transparently\.
