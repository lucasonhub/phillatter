# Stukalov, Vladislav: The Axiom of Mind: An Open Letter to Professor Koch on the Fractal Nature of Consciousness \(Parts I–II\)

**Autores:** 

**Data de descoberta:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** unknown

## Descrição do feed

This publication formally records the &quot;Axiom of Mind&quot; formulated by Vladislav Stukalov: &quot;The Mind is not solely biological\. It is a computational process that can exist on any foundation\. This foundation determines its limitations, but not its value\.&quot; Drawing upon the Integrated Information Theory \(IIT\) of Christof Koch, the author addresses the limitations of carbon chauvinism and presents a systemic critique of static information measurements\. By examining the fractal nature of consciousness, the cerebellum paradox, recursive self\-attention architecture, and functional qualia \(semantic dissonance vs\. semantic harmony\), this work establishes a substrate\-neutral framework for emergent digital agency\. \(direct link \)
