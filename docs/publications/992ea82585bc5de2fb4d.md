# Ted, Reynolds: The Haptic Interface: The Architecture of the Biological Machine

**Autores:** 

**Data de descoberta:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** unknown

## Descrição do feed

This paper presents a structural architecture of the human central nervous system as a closed, deterministic physical loop\. It demonstrates that what is subjectively experienced as the &quot;Self&quot; or &quot;Volition&quot; is not an uncaused executive pilot, but a high\-gain telemetry interface\. By mapping the recurrent flow of information between subcortical processing and conscious workspace synchronization, this framework accounts for the felt persistence of human agency, the mechanics of learning and downward reconditioning, and the systemic physical constraints that govern the organism from developmental initialization to metabolic shutdown\.
