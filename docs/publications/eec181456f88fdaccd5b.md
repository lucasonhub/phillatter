# Organisational culture and technology acceptance as catalysts for artificial intelligence adoption towards human resource management effectiveness in Thailand’s industrial hubs

**Autores:** Kantima Pongnairat

**Data:** 2026-10-14 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Orientation: Artificial intelligence \(AI\) is becoming increasingly important in human resource management \(HRM\), particularly in Thailand’s Eastern Economic Corridor \(EEC\), a rapidly developing industrial and economic zone\. Identifying the key factors that influence AI adoption can help organisations improve the effectiveness of their HRM practices\. Research purpose: To test how organisational culture, technology acceptance \(TA\) and technology readiness \(TR\) are associated with AI use in HRM use of technology \(UT\), and how UT is associated with HRM effectiveness \(EF\), including UT’s mediating role\. Motivation for the study: Despite strong promotion of AI for HRM efficiency, limited evidence explains which organisational and behavioural factors most drive AI adoption in industrial estates, particularly in the EEC\. Research approach/design and method: A cross\-sectional survey of 500 HR professionals in EEC industrial estates \(Chachoengsao, Chonburi, Rayong\) was analysed using structural equation modelling \(SEM\) with SmartPLS\. Main findings: Organisational culture and TA were positively associated with UT, while TR has no effect\. Use of technology showed a strong positive association with EF\. Use of technology mediated the relationships of OC and TA on EF, but not TR\. Practical/managerial implications: Organisations should strengthen digital\-supportive culture and employee TA, as these are more impactful than focusing only on readiness and infrastructure\. Contribution/value\-add: The study shows OC and TA are stronger drivers of AI\-HRM adoption than TR and provides evidence\-based guidance for improving HRM effectiveness in the EEC through AI\.
