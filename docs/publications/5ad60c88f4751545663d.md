# Measurement\-informed finite element damage identification under modal uncertainty using hybrid QPSO–SA

**Autores:** M\.Q\. Tran; Thuc Ngo Van; Hélder Silva Sousa; Jose Campos Matos; Huan Xuan Nguyen; Seyedmilad Komarizadehasl

**Data:** 2026-10-09 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Vibration\-based damage estimates depend not only on the inverse algorithm but also on the quality, completeness, and modal reliability of the measured data\. This study presents a measurement\-informed finite element model updating framework that combines quantum\-behaved particle swarm optimization with conditionally activated simulated annealing\. Field acceleration measurements from a steel truss span of the Chuong Duong Bridge in Hanoi were processed by covariance\-driven stochastic subspace identification and used to calibrate the healthy\-state model\. Damage\-identification performance was then assessed using field\-calibrated numerical scenarios with 1 to 4 stiffness\-reduced members\. Controlled tests represented degraded modal information through nominal 0–10% perturbations, mode\-confidence weighting, and sensor layouts reduced from 34 to 6 locations\. The calibrated model achieved a maximum frequency discrepancy of 1\.789% and a minimum modal assurance criterion of 0\.992\. In the four\-damage case, the mean cumulative normalized severity error of QPSO–SA increased from 76\.1% at 0% noise to 93\.9% at 5% noise and 133\.0% at 10% noise\. Its error remained lower than that of every benchmark algorithm at each investigated noise level\. Reducing the sensor layout to six locations increased its mean error by 6\.6 percentage points, compared with 10\.7–17\.6 percentage points for the alternatives\. The additional simulated\-annealing search increases computation time, but the results demonstrate a favorable stability–cost trade\-off for periodic, measurement\-informed condition assessment\. The study is a field\-calibrated numerical validation, measured damaged\-state responses and environmental variability remain outside its scope\.
