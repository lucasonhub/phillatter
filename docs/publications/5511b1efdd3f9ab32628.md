# The Seasons of a Career in Physical Chemistry: Himansu S\. Biswal

**Autores:** Himansu S\. Biswal; Gemma C\. Solomon; Joan‐Emma Shea; Gregory D\. Scholes

**Data:** 2026-10-15 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Recommendations &quot;The Seasons of a Career in Physical Chemistry&quot; is an interview series published across ACS Physical Chemistry Au, The Journal of Physical Chemistry A/B/C, and The Journal of Physical Chemistry Letters\.The series celebrates the people behind physical chemistry, giving researchers at all career stages the opportunity to tell their own stories: how they came to be where they are today, the joys and challenges they have encountered along the way, and what they imagine as they look to the future\.Whether reflecting on a lifetime of work or navigating the uncertainties of an earlier career stage, each interviewee offers a window into research environments and trajectories as varied as the science itself, with the hope of providing inspiration for the broader physical chemistry community\.
