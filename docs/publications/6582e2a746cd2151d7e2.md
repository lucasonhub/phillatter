# Investigating the Role of Micromobility in Sustainable Last\-Mile Delivery

**Autores:** Ilgın Gökaşar; Volkan Yıldız

**Data:** 2026-10-11 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

The growth of e\-commerce, food delivery, grocery delivery, and quick\-commerce services has increased the importance of last\-mile logistics in dense urban areas\. At the same time, micromobility vehicles such as e\-scooters, e\-bikes, e\-mopeds, and cargo bikes have become increasingly common for short urban trips\. This study assesses whether micromobility can be a viable option for last\-mile logistics and under which conditions it can be effectively used\. For this purpose, data were collected from logistics company reports, delivery platform reports, micromobility operator reports, and city\-level shared micromobility evidence\. The findings suggest that micromobility is not a universal replacement for conventional delivery systems, but it can be a complementary solution for short\-distance, high\-density, and small\-parcel deliveries\. E\-scooters are suitable only for very small and light deliveries under safe conditions, while e\-bikes provide a more balanced option for food, pharmacy, and small e\-commerce deliveries\. Cargo bikes appear to be the strongest micromobility vehicle for logistics, especially for grocery, multiple\-package, and medium\-parcel deliveries in dense urban areas\. Overall, micromobility is most viable when suitable parcel types, short delivery distances, high order density, safe infrastructure, and supportive urban policies exist together\.
