## Descrição do feed

In Real Time: A Reinvention in British Metaphysics 1883\-1928\. Oxford University Press\. 2026In 1908, J\. M\. E\. McTaggart wondered if the ‘real nature of time only contains\.\.\. the B series’\. If B\-theory is understood as affirming this then, along with many other scholars, the author read Russell as a B\-theorist\. This chapter focuses on a key part of Russell’s B\-theory: his distinction between ‘physical’, objective time, comprising simultaneity, before, and after; and ‘mental’, subjective time, comprising past, present, and future\. This original distinction first appears in Russell’s well\-cited Monist paper, “On the Experience of Time”\. This chapter contextualises “On the Experience of Time” within Russell’s earlier work, before digging into the paper itself\. It argues Russell was spurred to write the paper out of frustration with recent accounts of time produced by four of his contemporaries: J\. M\. E\. McTaggart, G\. E\. Moore, Henri Bergson, and Karin Costelloe\-Stephen\.
