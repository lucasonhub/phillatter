# Effects of myofascial release on clinical outcomes and muscle viscoelastic properties in patients with breast cancer: A randomized controlled trial

**Autores:** Özlem Feyzioğlu; Selvi Dinçer; Özgul Öztürk; Tülay Çevik Saldıran

**Data:** 2026-10-10 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Evidence regarding the effects of myofascial release \(MFR\) on clinical outcomes in breast cancer patients is inconsistent, and its effects on muscle viscoelastic properties have not been well established\. To evaluate the effects of MFR on the viscoelastic properties of the pectoralis major \(PM\) and sternocleidomastoid \(SCM\) muscles and on clinical outcomes in breast cancer patients undergoing radiotherapy\.Thirty\-one breast cancer patients undergoing radiotherapy after surgery were randomly assigned to a control group \(CG\) or an MFR group \(MFRG\)\. Both groups performed a home\-based exercise program for six weeks, while the MFRG additionally received MFR therapy twice weekly\. Shoulder mobility was assessed using a digital goniometer, muscle strength using a handheld dynamometer, pain intensity using the Visual Analog Scale, quality of life using the Functional Assessment of Cancer Therapy–Breast, and kinesiophobia using the Tampa Scale for Kinesiophobia\. The viscoelastic properties of PM and SCM muscles were evaluated using the MyotonPRO device\. PM muscle tone increased in the MFRG; however, no significant group × time interactions were found for PM or SCM viscoelastic properties \(p&gt;0\.05\)\. The MFRG showed significant improvements in most clinical outcomes except quality of life\. Significant group × time interactions favored the MFRG for shoulder mobility, pain, and shoulder flexion and abduction strength \(p&lt;0\.05\)\. The addition of therapist\-delivered MFR to home exercise was associated with greater improvements in shoulder mobility, muscle strength, and pain, but did not significantly affect PM or SCM viscoelastic properties\. The specific contribution of MFR remains uncertain due to additional therapist contact in the MFRG\. Trial registration number and date\. www\.clinicaltrials\.gov \(registration number NCT06764394, 01\.02\.2025\)
