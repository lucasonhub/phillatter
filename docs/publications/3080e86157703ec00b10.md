## Descrição do feed

In Real Time: A Reinvention in British Metaphysics 1883\-1928\. Oxford University Press\. 2026Across his career, A\. N\. Whitehead holds realism about time\. As he charmingly puts it in a 1922 symposium, he admits the ‘spaciness’ and ‘time\-iness’ of nature\. Yet for Whitehead, time derives from events—temporally extended slabs of nature\. Events include molecule vibrations in my hot mug of tea, the life of a tree across a sunny afternoon, and a rotation of the Earth around the sun\. This chapter will consider Whitehead’s earliest metaphysics of time and events, exploring their genesis and development during his London era, especially from 1911 to 1920\. Although Whitehead’s London texts are usually taken to defend eternalism, this chapter shows his remarks on temporal passage create serious tensions for this reading, for they forcefully point to an unreal future\. Further, this chapter argues that if Whitehead borrowed anything from Henri Bergson during this period, it is the notion that the passage of time is ontologically creative\.
