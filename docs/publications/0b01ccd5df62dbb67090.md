# Evaluating Semantic and Relational Knowledge Preservation under Fair\-budget Multi\-document Compression

**Autores:** Shaufiah Abdullah Junaidi; Yuefeng Li; Richi Nayak

**Data:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Compressing multi\-document text before structured\-knowledge extraction can reduce processing cost, but it can also remove relation\-bearing evidence\.This study evaluates how a frozen topic\-pattern extractive selector differs from fair\-budget comparators in preserving semantic and relational information under matched word budgets\.The selector combines latent dirichlet allocation topic relevance, FP\-Growth lexical\-pattern support, and a redundancy penalty\.A 120\-configuration validation search was completed before definitive test analysis; Run82 \(α=0\.30,β=0\.70, δ=1\.00, 15 sentences, 25 topics\) was then frozen and transferred unchanged to held\-out evaluation\.On 5,621 Multi\-News test clusters, Run82 produced statistically reliable but small improvements in concept F1 \(0\.4550 vs\. 0\.4405\) and relation F1 \(0\.4395 vs\. 0\.4251\), while exact\-triple F1 was effectively unchanged \(0\.3994 vs\. 0\.3990\)\.A prespecified 500\-cluster PRIMERA comparison under the same exact word budget produced lower dependency\-based structured\-preservation scores than both Run82 and Lead\-W, but an independent 500\-cluster REBEL analysis reversed the Run82\-Lead\-W ordering \(exact\-triple F1 0\.1779 vs\. 0\.4094\), demonstrating extractor sensitivity\.Independent WebNLG verification reproduced the same component\-composition gap: subject, object, and predicate coverage improved, whereas complete semantic\-triple recovery did not\.Across 100 alternative WebNLG regroupings, subject, object, and predicate effects remained positive in every regrouping, whereas complete semantic\-triple effects were smaller and less consistently significant\.Preservation also declined sharply with increasing compression severity and source\-graph size\.Ablation further showed non\-uniform component contributions and budget\-length trade\-offs\.Across both datasets, improvements at the concept and relation\-vocabulary levels did not propagate to argument pairing or complete triple recovery\.We therefore characterize the component\-structure preservation gap as a pipelineconditional empirical pattern rather than a universal hierarchy\.
