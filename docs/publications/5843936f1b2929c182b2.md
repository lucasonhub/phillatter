# The Role of Photorefractive Keratectomy in Modern Refractive Surgery

**Autores:** Çağatay Çağlar; Fatih Güven

**Data:** 2026-10-13 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Photorefractive keratectomy \(PRK\) remains a cornerstone of modern refractive surgery and retains its clinical relevance in the era of small\-incision lenticule extraction because of recent technological advances such as transepithelial techniques and optimized ablation profiles\. This review comprehensively examines the historical development, contemporary techniques, and role of PRK in modern refractive surgery\. PRK is based on excimer laser ablation following removal of the corneal epithelium\.
