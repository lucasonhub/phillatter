# Implications and Inferences: On the Organised Irresponsibility of Generative Artificial Intelligence

**Autores:** Jack Stilgoe

**Data:** 2026-10-18 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

We are told we are living in the “Age of AI\.” As social scientists, we are often expected to explore and explain the implications of this new technology\. In this article, based on a pair of recent lectures, I argue that, rather than focussing on what the technology implies, we should instead study what it infers\. We need to take our sociological tools upstream, to understand how generative AI technologies are being constituted\. Those of us working with terms such as “responsible AI” and “responsible innovation” should first consider how irresponsibility is organised as part of the emerging politics of AI\.
