# Statistical analysis of ANOVA\-RSM optimization with heat and mass transport in hyperbolic tangent fluid flow on an inclined cylinder

**Autores:** Muhammad Naveed Khan; Maham Mujahid; Nurul Amira Zainal; Sami Dhahbi

**Data:** 2026-10-09 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Background Conventional numerical parametric studies of strongly coupled non\-Newtonian transport systems mainly describe one\-parameter trends and provide limited information about nonlinear interactions, relative statistical significance, and prediction of engineering responses\. To address these issues, this study integrates Response Surface Methodology \(RSM\) with Analysis of Variance \(ANOVA\) to quantify and model the skin\-friction response of magnetohydrodynamic hyperbolic tangent fluid flow over an inclined stretching cylinder\. The framework is selected to evaluate the individual, quadratic, and interaction effects of the governing parameters while retaining the coupled heat, mass, and bioconvective transport physics\. Methods The principal equations for momentum, energy, concentration, and microorganisms&\#x27; density are first converted to a set of nonlinear ordinary differential equations by means of appropriate similarity transformations\. The resultant boundary value problem is reduced to an initial value problem and solved numerically using the shooting method in combination with the well\-known fourth\-order Runge\-Kutta scheme\. The impact of the dimensionless parameters on velocity, temperature, concentration, microorganism density, skin friction coefficient, Nusselt number, Sherwood number, and streamlines has been analyzed systematically\. Significant Findings Velocity increases due to Darcy number and buoyancy, whereas Weissenberg number and magnetic parameter make velocity decrease\. Temperature increases when thermal radiation, Dufour, Eckert, and Biot numbers get higher\. Concentration increases due to the effect of Soret number and activation energy but decreases due to the effect of chemical reaction\. The density of gyrotactic microorganisms decreases due to the effect of Peclet number and curvature parameter\. RSM\-ANOVA model gives excellent results, which are in agreement with numerical values \(R^2 = 0\.99998825\)\.
