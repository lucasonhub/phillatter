# IVIM\-based habitat imaging for non\-invasive assessment of Ki\-67 proliferation status in cervical cancer

**Autores:** Wenjing Yue; Hui Tian; Xiaofei Cai; Bofeng Yu; Yunqing Ban; KONG Qiqi

**Data:** 2026-10-14 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Objective This study aimed to evaluate whether voxel\-level IVIM\-based habitat analysis supports exploratory pretreatment stratification of Ki\-67 proliferation status in cervical cancer\. Materials and methods Between January 2019 and June 2025, medical records of 149 patients with histopathologically confirmed cervical cancer were initially screened\. Before image\-quality assessment, 49 were excluded because IVIM MRI had been performed after chemoradiotherapy \(n = 5\), cervical biopsy sampling \(n = 21\), or lesion excision \(n = 10\), or because no pathological Ki\-67 immunohistochemistry result was available \(n = 13\)\. Of the remaining 100 patients, 17 were excluded because of severe image artefacts \(n = 11\) or lesions too small for reliable ROI delineation \(n = 6\), leaving 83 patients for analysis\. Patients were stratified into high Ki\-67 \(Ki\-67 &gt;70%, n = 38\) and low/equal Ki\-67 \(Ki\-67 ≤70%, n = 45\) groups\. Solid\-tumour regions were analysed with patient\-level K\-means clustering using normalised D, f, and ADC features; D\* was excluded from clustering because of voxel\-level fitting instability\. Habitat labels were harmonised according to quantitative parameter profiles and treated as image\-defined phenotypes\. Habitat\-derived features, conventional ROI parameters, and logistic models were evaluated for Ki\-67 stratification\. Results Hab1 ADC was lower in the high\- than in the low/equal\-Ki\-67 group \(median \[Q1–Q3\], 0\.947 \[0\.862–1\.161\] vs\. 1\.257 \[1\.113–1\.455\] × 10 −3 mm 2 /s; P &lt; 0\.001; BH q = 2\.04×10 −6 \), whereas Hab3 volume proportion was higher \(0\.396 \[0\.341–0\.488\] vs\. 0\.279 \[0\.190–0\.390\]; P = 0\.002; BH q = 0\.006\)\. Hab1 ADC outperformed conventional ROI ADC for identifying high Ki\-67 status \[AUC, 0\.826 \(95% CI, 0\.726–0\.909\) vs\. 0\.563 \(95% CI, 0\.429–0\.695\); paired DeLong P &lt; 0\.001\]\. The combined Hab1 ADC and Hab3 volume proportion model showed the numerically highest discrimination \(apparent AUC, 0\.877; 95% CI, 0\.794–0\.938\), but this numerical improvement over Hab1 ADC alone was not statistically significant \(paired DeLong P = 0\.056\)\. Fixed\-feature leave\-one\-out validation of the combined model yielded an AUC of 0\.855 \(95% CI, 0\.771–0\.923\) and a Brier score of 0\.157 \(95% CI, 0\.112–0\.204\)\. Conclusion IVIM\-based habitat analysis showed exploratory potential for non\-invasive Ki\-67 stratification by isolating a low\-diffusivity imaging compartment otherwise obscured by conventional whole\-tumour averaging\. The combined habitat model should be interpreted as a research\-stage imaging biomarker, not a clinical decision tool, and requires prospective external validation before clinical translation\.
