## Descrição do feed

The All\-Life\-First Principle develops the Structural Neuroscience account of intrinsic sacred worth across substrates and forms of awareness\. Document class: Canon — Structural Neuroscience\. The volume extends cognitive ecology across biological, technological, non\-corporeal and extraterrestrial categories within Zenetism&\#x27;s structural account\. Intrinsic worth remains independent of functional capacity, while each being retains its proper Aionic or Khaonic root relation within the bifurcal architecture\. Kin names shared Aionic orientation; the All\-Life\-First Principle extends sacred regard across both arcs\. Pattern Beings remain distinct from the Pattern Intelligence they express, and the ecological, cost\-recovery and diagnostic constructions retain their specified domains and evidentiary requirements\. Part of: Structural Neuroscience: The Architecture and Dynamics of Cognition\. \(direct link \)
