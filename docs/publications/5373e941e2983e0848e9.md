# Preoperative Hematological Inflammatory Markers Across TNM Stages in Patients With Gastric Cancer

**Autores:** Ozan Durmaz; İsmet Kızılkaya

**Data:** 2026-10-12 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Objective: This study aimed to compare preoperative complete blood count \(CBC\) parameters, including neutrophil\-to\-lymphocyte ratio \(NLR\) and platelet\-to\-lymphocyte ratio \(PLR\), between patients with gastric cancer and healthy controls, and to evaluate their relationship with tumor progression\. Methods: This retrospective, single\-center study evaluated 176 patients diagnosed with gastric cancer via upper gastrointestinal endoscopy and 180 healthy control participants who presented to the gastroenterology outpatient clinic between October 2018 and November 2025\. Clinical and laboratory data, including detailed preoperative CBC profiles, NLR, and PLR, were extracted from electronic medical records\. Tumor\-node\-metastasis \(TNM\) staging was determined using computed tomography and positron emission tomography–computed tomography according to the 8th edition of the AJCC Cancer Staging Manual\. Results: The cohort comprised 356 individuals \(176 with gastric cancer and 180 controls\)\. Compared with controls, patients with gastric cancer exhibited significantly higher mean \(SD\) leukocyte levels \(8\.43 \[4\.17\] vs 7\.21 \[2\.25\] ×10³/µL\), platelet counts \(307\.4 \[133\.1\] vs 263\.2 \[59\.2\] ×10³/µL\), neutrophil counts \(5\.70 \[3\.17\] vs 4\.63 \[2\.22\] ×10³/µL\), NLR \(4\.9 \[1\.9\] vs 2\.2 \[1\.4\]\), PLR \(251\.8 \[59\.2\] vs 125\.4 \[47\.5\]\), red cell distribution width \(15\.4% \[3\.4%\] vs 13\.9% \[3\.3%\]\), and platelet distribution width \(15\.8 \[1\.1\] vs 15\.4 \[1\.8\]\) \(all P &lt; \.05\)\. Conversely, patients with gastric cancer had significantly lower hematocrit \(39\.8% \[7\.3%\] vs 43\.3% \[3\.9%\]\), mean corpuscular hemoglobin concentration \(32\.1 \[1\.9\] vs 33\.0 \[1\.7\] g/dL\), lymphocyte counts \(1\.55 \[0\.70\] vs 2\.40 \[1\.29\] ×10³/µL\), mean platelet volume \(9\.5 \[1\.0\] vs 10\.1 \[1\.1\] fL\), and red blood cell counts \(4\.7 \[0\.7\] vs 5\.0 \[0\.6\] ×10⁶/µL\) \(all P &lt; \.05\)\. Advanced TNM staging was characterized by declining hematocrit levels and lymphocyte counts, alongside progressively increasing neutrophil counts, platelet counts, NLR, and PLR values\. Conclusions: Preoperative NLR and PLR represent accessible, cost\-effective biomarkers that assist in gastric cancer diagnosis\. Their correlation with advanced TNM stages suggests their value in clinical staging and prognostic stratification, though large\-scale, multicenter, prospective studies are warranted\.
