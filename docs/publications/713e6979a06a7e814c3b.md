# Explainable machine learning integrating CT\-derived intratumoral heterogeneity for histological subtype prediction in pure ground\-glass lung adenocarcinoma: multicenter validation and transcriptomic characterization

**Autores:** Ying Deng; Jiajun Liu; Dinghua Li; Bingyi Zhang; Xiang Bin; Wanyin Qi; Sanhong Zhang

**Data:** 2026-10-12 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Background Preoperative differentiation of lepidic\-predominant adenocarcinoma from other histological subtypes in lung adenocarcinoma presenting as pure ground\-glass nodules remains challenging\. We evaluated whether a CT\-derived intratumoral heterogeneity score provides complementary predictive and biological information beyond routine clinical and CT features\. Methods This retrospective multicenter study included 533 patients, divided into a training cohort \(n = 373\) and a held\-out validation cohort \(n = 160\)\. Twelve supervised algorithms were compared exclusively within the training cohort using stratified five\-fold cross\-validation and grid\-search optimization; predictors, hyperparameters, and the decision threshold were locked before the held\-out cohort was evaluated once\. Incremental value of the ITH score and leave\-one\-center\-out internal–external validation were additionally assessed\. Segmentation reproducibility was evaluated in 100 lesions\. An independent radiogenomic cohort of 43 pathological Tis/T1 lung adenocarcinomas was analyzed by differential\-expression and Hallmark gene\-set enrichment analyses after median ITH\-score stratification\. Results XGBoost achieved the highest training\-set cross\-validation AUC \(0\.908 ± 0\.021\) and was selected before held\-out evaluation\. In the validation cohort, the AUC was 0\.919 \(95% CI, 0\.870–0\.968\), with accuracy 0\.850, precision 0\.857, sensitivity 0\.545, specificity 0\.966, and Brier score 0\.098\. Adding the ITH score to clinical and CT features increased AUC from 0\.867 to 0\.919 \(ΔAUC = 0\.052; DeLong P = 0\.012\), increased PR\-AUC from 0\.731 to 0\.824, and reduced Brier score from 0\.126 to 0\.098\. Leave\-one\-center\-out AUCs ranged from 0\.824 to 0\.882\. The ITH score showed good\-to\-excellent repeat\-segmentation reproducibility\. In the radiogenomic cohort, a high ITH score was associated with coordinated enrichment of G2M checkpoint \(NES = 1\.86, FDR &lt;0\.001\), E2F targets \(NES = 1\.86, FDR &lt;0\.001\), and epithelial–mesenchymal transition \(NES = 1\.55, FDR = 0\.001\)\. Conclusion CT\-derived ITH provided incremental information for histological subtype prediction and was associated with proliferation\-, cell\-cycle\-, and EMT\-related transcriptional programs\. The moderate sensitivity at the prespecified operating point and the retrospective design warrant prospective external validation before clinical implementation\.
