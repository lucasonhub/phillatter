# Ethical leadership and job crafting: the role of intrinsic motivation and job feedback

**Autores:** Pei Wang; Yu Zhang; Kun Yu

**Data:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Introduction Drawing on Self\-Determination Theory \(SDT\) and its sub\-theory Cognitive Evaluation Theory \(CET\), the present study examines how ethical leadership relates to employees’ job crafting through intrinsic motivation, and how this indirect relationship is contingent on job feedback\. We proposed that ethical leadership is positively related to job crafting via intrinsic motivation, and that job feedback moderates this indirect effect by providing competence\-relevant informational cues\. Methods We tested our hypotheses using two\-wave data collected from 102 employees in the customer service department of a gaming company in northern China\. Results The results provided preliminary evidence of a moderated mediation pattern, suggesting that the indirect association between ethical leadership and job crafting through intrinsic motivation varied as a function of job feedback\. Discussion These findings extend research on ethical leadership and job crafting by clarifying intrinsic motivation as the motivational mechanism and job feedback as a key boundary condition\. The theoretical and practical implications are discussed\.
