# A Dual‐Excitation Light‐Harvesting System Based on a Flexible Silicon Composite Containing Ca 2 Sr\(PO 4 \) 2 : Ce 3\+ , Sm 3\+ , Designed for Multimodal Anti‐Counterfeiting and Secure Information Encryption

**Autores:** Marek Adaszyński; Kacper Adamczak; Dagmara Stefańska

**Data:** 2026-10-14 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

ABSTRACT Advanced anti‐counterfeiting technologies require luminescent materials capable of generating dynamic and difficult‐to‐reproduce optical responses\. The research focuses on a dual‐excitation light‐harvesting system based on flexible silicone composites containing Ca 2 Sr\(PO 4 \) 2 : Ce 3\+ , Sm 3\+ phosphors, designed for multimodal anti‐counterfeiting protection and secure information encryption The co‐doped phosphate exhibits excitation‐dependent emission resulting from efficient Ce 3\+ → Sm 3\+ energy transfer and multisite occupation of dopant ions, enabling tunable luminescence under different excitation wavelengths\. Temperature‐dependent studies additionally reveal a phonon‐assisted energy transfer process leading to enhancement of Sm 3\+ emission\. The combination of excitation‐ and temperature‐controlled luminescence enables reversible color switching and generation of complex optical signatures that are difficult to counterfeit\. As a proof of concept, the phosphor is incorporated into a flexible silicone security label displaying distinct emission patterns observable using a simple UV source and camera detection\. Furthermore, a novel encoding platform is proposed, enabling message encryption through the selection of material composition and controlled excitation conditions\. The developed dual‐excitation composite represents a promising strategy for next‐generation flexible anticounterfeiting systems, adaptive optical labels, and secure data encryption technologies\.
