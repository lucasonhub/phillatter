# A model for prediction of a twin live birth following in vitro fertilization–embryo transfer in women of advanced age

**Autores:** Siyue Xu; Ruolei Wang; Cuilian Zhang; Meng Li; 张少娣

**Data:** 2026-10-13 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Introduction The aim of this study was to construct a model for prediction of the probability of a twin live birth in women of advanced age in vitro fertilization and embryo transfer cycles\. Methods This retrospective analysis included 10,089 transfer cycles from women aged ≥35 years, stratified by whether they had a live twin birth\. Taking a twin live birth as the positive outcome \(Y = 1\) and cycles with a singleton or no live birth as the negative outcome \(Y = 0\)\. A multivariate logistic regression analyses was built based on candidate variables, followed by nomogram construction\. The discrimination ability of the model was assessed by the area under the receiver\-operating characteristic curve, with 500 bootstrap resamples for internal validation\. Results There were significant differences in age, endometrial thickness, number of usable embryos, number of embryos transferred, and embryo type between the groups \(all P &lt; 0\.05\)\. Multivariate logistic regression identified the following five predictors of a twin live birth: age \(adjusted odds ratio \[aOR\] 0\.69, 95% confidence interval \[CI\] 0\.66–0\.73, P &lt; 0\.001\), endometrial thickness ≥7 mm \(aOR 2\.79, 95% CI 1\.30–5\.99, P = 0\.009\), blastocyst transfer \(aOR 1\.88, 95% CI 1\.36–2\.60, P &lt; 0\.001\), two good quality embryos \(aOR 2\.20, 95% CI 1\.71–2\.82, P &lt; 0\.001\), and two embryos transferred \(aOR 19\.45, 95% CI 11\.57–32\.69, P &lt; 0\.001\)\. The nomogram built on these variables yielded an area under the curve of 0\.8462\. Bootstrap internal validation \(500 resamples\) produced a corrected area under the curve of 0\.8443\. Conclusions The likelihood of a twin live birth correlated positively with two embryos transferred, two good quality embryos, blastocyst transfer, and endometrial thickness ≥7 mm, and correlated negatively with maternal age\.
