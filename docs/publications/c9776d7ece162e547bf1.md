## Resumo

This symposium celebrates the 50th anniversary of Star Wars \(1977\) with nineteen short essays from scholars who discuss its meaning, impact, and legacy\. The essays examine the migrations and manifestations of the Star Wars universe across media, including animation, video games, novels, comics, collectibles, and television\. The scholars in this symposium explore topics such as race, ethnicity, sexuality, fandom, artificial intelligence, and animals in Star Wars texts\.
