# K\., Manukrishna: Automated Cognition and the Crisis of Human Dignity

**Autores:** 

**Data de descoberta:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** unknown

## Descrição do feed

This paper presents a preliminary Heideggerian critique of generative artificial intelligence, examining the ontological consequences of reducing human expression to synthetic output\. By framing algorithmic extraction as the reduction of thought to Bestand \(standing\-reserve\), it contrasts statistical symbol manipulation with Dasein’s essential structure of being\-in\-the\-world \(In\-der\-Welt\-sein\) and care \(Sorge\)\. The text argues that automated systems lack the existential finitude and intentional involvement required for authentic Poiesis\. Intended as a short initial inquiry, this working draft outlines foundational conceptual distinctions to be expanded in future research\. \(This is a short working paper intended for preliminary circulation and discussion\. The arguments and framework presented here are subject to further expansion and refinement in a future, full\-length study\)
