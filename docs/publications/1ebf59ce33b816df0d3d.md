# Thomas, Emily: C12249Samuel Alexander’s ‘Generative’ Time and Its Eternalist Universe 1911–1920

**Autores:** 

**Data de descoberta:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** unknown

## Descrição do feed

In Real Time: A Reinvention in British Metaphysics 1883\-1928\. Oxford University Press\. 2026From around 1911, Alexander began developing a sweeping new metaphysic\. This culminated in two series of Gifford lectures, delivered in 1917 and 1918, published in 1920 as Space, Time, and Deity\. It unites space and time as the grand entity ‘Space\-Time’, the eternalist emergence base of all else\.Existing scholarship on Alexander’s account of time focuses on Space, Time, and Deity, alongside later texts\. This chapter undertakes a new enquiry, focusing on earlier texts\. These include Alexander’s published articles; his 1914 and 1915 Samuel Hall lectures, unpublished in his lifetime; and Alexander’s correspondence with emergentist C\. Lloyd Morgan, which remains unpublished and has not been previously discussed by scholars\. It considers Alexander’s engagement with Henri Bergson, Bertrand Russell, and physics\. It will be shown that Alexander defends eternalism against Bradley’s timeless block universe and against growing block theory and presentism\.
