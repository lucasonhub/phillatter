# A hierarchical dual\-stream POMDP–IPSO framework for multi\-UAV cooperative inspection scheduling and control

**Autores:** Zijie Zheng; Zhijun Zhang

**Data:** 2026-10-13 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Large\-scale power\-transmission\-line inspection requires coordinated task allocation and safe local motion control\. This study presents a hierarchical dual\-stream partially observable Markov decision process \(POMDP\)–Improved Particle Swarm Optimization \(IPSO\) framework for multi\-UAV inspection\. At the lower level, a centralized\-training/decentralized\-execution Multi\-Agent Proximal Policy Optimization \(MAPPO\) algorithm trains a dual\-stream actor that separates short\-horizon collision perception from communication\-topology coordination; a sigmoid curriculum fuses the streams and a differentiable safety filter enforces soft separation, boundary, velocity, and acceleration constraints\. At the upper level, IPSO jointly optimizes task\-to\-UAV assignment and visit order under time, energy, priority\-coverage, battery, and time\-window criteria\. Closed\-loop feedback triggers replanning when tasks are completed, deadlines are threatened, energy reserves are low, or a route segment becomes infeasible\. In addition to the restricted operational scenarios, we provide a reproducible benchmark with 19 scenarios, 574 tasks, 30 scheduling seeds, and 30 controller test seeds\. Under equal evaluation budgets, IPSO reduced mean fitness by 4\.8% relative to standard PSO, while the reconstructed dual\-stream MAPPO controller achieved 89\.3% ± 10\.1% task success\. Across the evaluated cases, the proposed framework achieved better average performance in most scenarios rather than uniformly dominating every baseline\. The code, generated scenarios, raw run\-level data, and 600\-dpi figures are supplied for independent reproduction\. Clinical trial number Not applicable\.
