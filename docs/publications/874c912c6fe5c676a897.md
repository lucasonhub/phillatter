# Mundus Est Non\-Linearis: The Failure of Reductionist AI and Physics

**Autores:** Kolesnikov Igor

**Data:** 2026-10-10 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

This manifesto presents a philosophical and theoretical position on non\-linear dynamics, artificial intelligence, and modern physical theory\. The document argues that many limitations observed in contemporary computational systems arise from the widespread use of simplified linear and reductionist modeling approaches\. It advocates a transition toward explicitly non\-linear frameworks capable of representing complex, self\-interacting, and multi\-scale phenomena\. \[Mundus\_Est\.\.\.ifesto\.pdf \| PDF\] The manuscript introduces the central principle“Mundus est nonlinearis” \(&quot;The world is non\-linear&quot;\), proposing that linear equations and simplified algorithmic structures should be regarded as approximations of a fundamentally dynamic and non\-linear reality\. The text discusses implications for AI architectures, wave\-based models, field dynamics, and broader scientific methodology\. \[Mundus\_Est\.\.\.ifesto\.pdf \| PDF\]
