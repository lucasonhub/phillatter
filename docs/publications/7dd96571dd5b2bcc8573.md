# Generatieve AI\-gestuurde pedagogieën voor de ontwikkeling van interculturele competentie in het Chinese hoger onderwijs

**Autores:** Yiwen Jin

**Data:** 2026-10-09 · **Tipo:** dissertation

## Resumo

This doctoral project investigates how generative artificial intelligence can support the development of intercultural competence in Chinese higher education\. It focuses on the use of tools such as ChatGPT and DeepSeek in intercultural language learning, particularly in activities involving critical incidents and role\-play\. The project addresses three main areas\. First, it examines existing research on technology\-supported intercultural competence education and GenAI\-based interventions in higher education\. Second, it develops and validates an assessment instrument for measuring intercultural competence among Chinese university students\. Third, it explores how GenAI\-supported learning activities can be designed and implemented to support students&\#x27; intercultural competence development\. Using mixed\-methods research, the project combines quantitative measures with qualitative data such as student reflections and interviews\. It aims to clarify the pedagogical affordances and limitations of GenAI in intercultural education\. In particular, the project considers how AI can provide scalable, interactive, and personalized learning opportunities, while also highlighting the need for teacher guidance, critical reflection, and careful assessment\. Overall, this PhD contributes to research on intercultural competence, language education, and technology\-enhanced learning by developing both empirical evidence and pedagogical insights for the responsible integration of generative AI in higher education\.
