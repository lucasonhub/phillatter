# Explainable deep learning framework for real\-time insect pest detection in smart agriculture

**Autores:** J\. Briskilal; M\. K\. Vidhyalakshmi; Aswathy K Cherian; S\. Neduncheliyan; Ajay Kumar; Rakesh Kumar

**Data:** 2026-10-12 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Abstract Global losses of crop production due to insect pest infestations are estimated to be around 20–40% annually, which represents one of the highest threats to agricultural productivity, farmer livelihoods and food security\. Current manual scouting is labor intensive, subjective and non\-scalable, and many automated scouting methods suffer when they come to deal with different field conditions and offer little insight into decision making\. To tackle these challenges, this study proposes Crop Protection in Smart Agriculture—Real\-time Insect Pest Detection using Continual Spatio\-Temporal Graph Convolutional Network \(CPSA\-RIPD\-CSTGCN\), a unified and interpretable deep learning framework that combines the four synergistic components: \(i\) the Ensemble Transform Schmidt–Kalman Filter \(ETSKF\) for image preprocessing, including resizing, normalization, and noise suppression; \(ii\) data augmentation using rotation, flipping, scaling, shearing, and contrast adjustment, which enhances generalization and reduces overfitting; \(iii\) the Continual Spatio\-Temporal Graph Convolutional Network \(CSTGCN\) that jointly models the spatial dependency across crop regions and temporal evolution of pest dynamics to achieve robust feature extraction and classification; and \(iv\) the Revolution Optimization Algorithm \(ROA\) for hyperparameter optimization of CSTGCN weights to adaptively adjust the network\. Further, Gradient\-weighted Class Activation Mapping \(Grad\-CAM\) visualization is introduced to give post\-hoc explainability as it shows regions that are relevant to the biological process and areas that are affected by pests\. This framework was tested on IP102 benchmark dataset with 60%\-40%\-split train\-test with 10 economically important classes of pests with 97% accuracy, 98% precision, 98% recall and 98% F1 score, mean ± 0\.32% accuracy, across 5\-fold cross validation\. Among other baseline models, these results consistently outperform VGG16 \(82%\), InceptionV3 \(85%\), MobileNetV2 \(87%\), DenseNet121 \(88%\), EfficientNetB0 \(89%\) and task\-specific models like EDCP\-DIA\-RNN\(Early Disease and Crop Pest—Diagnosis/Detection using Image Analysis RNN\) \(94%\), EPCY\-TPA\-ANN \(Efficient Prediction of Crop Yield—Through Parameter Analysis ANN\) \(91%\) and ICPM\-ESA\-CNN \(Interactive Crop and Pest Management Enhanced Soil Analysis CNN\) \(90%\)\. The results of ablation studies showed the solitary effects of each module of the Ensemble Transform Schmidt–Kalman Filter pre\-processing and Revolution Optimization Algorithm optimization, and the confusion matrix analysis demonstrated high per\-class reliability and only a small amount of misclassification, implying that the proposed method could be applied to real\-time, large\-scale smart agriculture deployments\.
