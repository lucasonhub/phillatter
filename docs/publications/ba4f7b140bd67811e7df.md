# La Alfabetización Digital en las Universidades, la IA y su Impacto en el Mundo del Trabajo

**Autores:** Rosa Elia Robles Medina

**Data:** 2026-10-15 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

This research highlights the need to incorporate the teaching and learning of artificial intelligence, particularly big data, into the educational process\. In addition to developing digital literacy, students must acquire knowledge of the technological tools required by companies, which may become their future sources of employment\. Furthermore, this research explores the practical application of big data in the world of work\.
