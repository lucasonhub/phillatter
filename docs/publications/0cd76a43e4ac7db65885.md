# Penguatan Kapasitas Pelayan Gereja dalam Pemuridan Generasi Digital: Pendampingan Remaja, Misi Media Sosial, dan Discernment Alkitabiah di GSPDI Purwakarta

**Autores:** Fransisca Putri Cyndivania; Fendi Fendi; Samuel Chandra Handoyo; Ferry Simanjuntak

**Data:** 2026-10-14 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Generation Z has grown up as native inhabitants of digital space, and this reality shapes how they form identity, read Scripture, and view involvement in church ministry\. Many local churches face three problems at once: adolescents withdrawing from ministry because of a pressurising evaluation culture, youth ministry that has not yet worked digital space missionally and ethically, and young believers who lack adequate tools for testing spiritual content in a post\-truth culture\. This community service programme aimed to strengthen the capacity of adolescents, young adults, and their ministry mentors at GSPDI Purwakarta in these three areas\. The activity took the form of a one\-day seminar held on Saturday, 3 October 2026, using interactive lectures and case discussion, with each session structured as 30 minutes of presentation followed by 15 minutes of moderated discussion\. The material was drawn from three complementary studies: a practical\-theology model of adolescent discipleship, a reconstruction of youth ministry through social media with ethical control over artificial intelligence, and a framework of digital discernment rooted in the authority of Scripture\. Evaluation used pre\-test and post\-test instruments and participation observation sheets\. The programme produced a single roadmap for digital discipleship that combines a safe ministry space, missional digital outreach, and critical content evaluation, together with follow\-up commitments from the partner church\.
