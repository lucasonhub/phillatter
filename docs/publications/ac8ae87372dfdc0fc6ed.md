# Bernini, Marco: Personification as \_Élanification\_: Agency Combustion and Narrative Layering in Worlding Perceived Relations

**Autores:** 

**Data de descoberta:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** unknown

## Descrição do feed

In Stephan Besser &amp; Flora Lysen, Worlding the Brain: Neurocentrism, Cognition and the Challenge of the Arts and Humanities\. Berlin and Leiden: Brill\. pp\. 113\-128\. 2023This chapter proposes a cognitive and phenomenological account of personification as part of a broader human capacity for worlding perceived relations, which I term élanification\. Drawing on recent frameworks in cognitive science, philosophical panpsychism, new materialism, and cognitive literary studies, the chapter contributes to current debates on human entanglement with the world by focusing on the dynamic interplay between individual agency and the ascription of vitality, mentality, and intentionality to real, imaginary, or fictional beings and objects\. The chapter advances two interlinked proposals\. The first is what I call the personification as agency combustion \(PAC\) principle: rather than co\-existing independently, individual agency and personification stand in an energetic, resource\-based relationship, whereby personification consumes individual agency as its fuel\. The more subjective agency diminishes, the more personifying dynamics are unleashed\. The PAC principle is shown to operate across a spectrum of experiences — literary writing and reading, child play with imaginary companions, religious and mystical states \(drawing on Simone Weil\), and hallucinatory phenomena such as voice\-hearing and felt presence — where the lessening of agentive control is inversely proportional to the autonomy and vividness of perceived personified agents\. The second proposal is that this combustion of agency unfolds temporally, and that narrative plays a key scaffolding role in progressively layering mentality and intentionality into perceived agents\. \(direct link \)
