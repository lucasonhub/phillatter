# The Effects of Instructional Leadership and AI\-Assisted Teacher Teaching Performance on Students’ Learning Motivation

**Autores:** Alvin Widiyarto; Achmad Buchori; Endang Wuryandini

**Data:** 2026-10-15 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

This study aims to examine the effects of principals’ instructional leadership and AI\-assisted teacher instructional performance on students’ learning motivation in Economics subjects at senior high schools in Sub Rayon 03, Semarang Regency\. The study employed a quantitative approach with a survey method and a correlational design\. Primary data were obtained from students’ questionnaire responses\. The population comprised 184 students from four senior high schools, with a sample of 126 students determined using Slovin’s formula at a 5% margin of error\. Respondents were selected using \[insert the sampling technique used\]\. Data were collected through a closed\-ended questionnaire using a five\-point Likert scale\. Multiple linear regression analysis was conducted using SPSS version 25, with t\-tests, an F\-test, and the coefficient of determination assessed at a 5% significance level\. The results showed that principals’ instructional leadership had a positive and significant effect on students’ learning motivation \(B = 0\.622; t = 8\.358; p &lt; 0\.001\), whereas AI\-assisted teacher instructional performance had a negative and significant effect \(B = −0\.364; t = −3\.693; p &lt; 0\.001\)\. The overall regression model was statistically significant \(F = 39\.706; p &lt; 0\.001\) and explained 39\.2% of the variance in students’ learning motivation \(R² = 0\.392\)\. These findings emphasize the importance of strengthening instructional leadership and evaluating AI implementation in teaching while maintaining meaningful pedagogical interactions between teachers and students\.
