# Extrachromosomal circular DNA drives focal amplification of AGAP2\-AS1 to promote cutaneous tumorigenesis

**Autores:** Jin Zhang; Zeying Liu; Jinzhi Zhao; Xun Liu; 刘之英; Huiting Xue; Tingting Lin; Limin Zhu

**Data:** 2026-10-14 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Background Extrachromosomal circular DNA \(eccDNA\) represents a pervasive structural continuum of genomic instability that drives intratumoral heterogeneity; the topography of the eccDNA\-encoded non\-coding transcriptome remains obscure in ultraviolet \(UV\)\-driven malignancies\. Methods We profiled eccDNA from Basal Cell Carcinoma, Inverted Follicular Keratosis, Merkel Cell Carcinoma, and matched adjacent normal tissues using Circle\-Seq\. Structural data were integrated with the eccDNA Gene Analyzer \(ECGA\) and The Cancer Genome Atlas \(TCGA\) clinical cohorts\. In vitro siRNA\-mediated knockdown assays were used to determine the oncogenic functionality of identified lncRNAs\. Results Circle\-Seq revealed genome\-wide remodeling of circular DNA with focal hyper\-amplification, GC\-rich compositional biases, and recurrent hotspots on chromosomes 9, 16, and 20\. Integrative screening identified AGAP2\-AS1 as a clinically significant, copy\-number\-associated lncRNA, whose expression correlated with cellular responses to UV radiation and oxidative stress\. Depletion of AGAP2\-AS1 impaired tumor cell proliferation, migration, and invasion in vitro\. Conclusion The eccDNA landscape serves as a reservoir of functional non\-coding elements; focal amplification of AGAP2\-AS1 contributes to malignant progression and stress adaptation in cutaneous neoplasia\.
