# Causal\-inspired Cross\-asset Contrastive Multimodal Maintenance Network: A Proxy\-benchmark Study for Renewable\-energy Maintenance

**Autores:** Dona Amange Franci; Chra Ismael Doghramachy; Haneen Khalid Dany; Maikey Zaki Bia; Shahab Wahhab Kareem

**Data:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

This paper proposes a Causal\-Inspired Cross\-Asset Contrastive Multimodal Maintenance Network \(C3M\-Farm\) for predictive\-maintenance research in renewable\-energy systems\.The architecture combines modality\-specific encoders, environment\-aware latent disentanglement, cross\-modal contrastive learning, attention\-based fusion, and graph reasoning\.NASA C\-MAPSS is used as a turbofan prognostics proxy rather than as direct renewable\-energyfarm validation\.The reported classification experiment achieved 96\.0% accuracy, 95\.7% precision, 95\.2% recall, 95\.4% F1\-score, and 97\.0%AUC\.In the controlled same\-pipeline RUL evaluation, C3M\-Farm achieved RMSE values of 2\.94 ± 0\.12, 2\.11 ± 0\.08, 2\.34 ± 0\.09, and 2\.94 ± 0\.11 on FD001, FD002, FD003, and FD004, respectively\.The paper reports lower RMSE values for C3M\-Farm than for the evaluated baselines on the subsets for which corresponding results are available\.These results are interpreted descriptively\.The preliminary archived experiments \(Tables 3456\) are retained only for provenance and do not support superiority claims\.The study therefore demonstrates proxy\-benchmark performance while retaining explicit limitations concerning cross\-study protocol equivalence, proxy\-modality construction, physical farm\-topology reasoning, and validation on genuinely heterogeneous renewable\-energy assets\.
