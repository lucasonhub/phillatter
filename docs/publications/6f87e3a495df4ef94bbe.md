# A Critique of the Argument View:

**Autores:** Roberto Schmitz Nitsche; Tiegüe Vieira Rodrigues

**Data:** 2026-10-14 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

The Argument View posits that thought experiments are merely arguments in disguise, deriving their epistemic power from logical inference\. This paper challenges this view, arguing that it rests on a fundamental category error by mischaracterizing the cognitive activity of thought experimentation\. We demonstrate that the process of running a thought experiment is phenomenologically and structurally distinct from executing an inference\. Specifically, it fails to satisfy the necessary taking condition – where a thinker must take their premises to support their conclusion – a core feature of genuine, person\-level reasoning\. This critique is shown to be decisive against the strong Execution Thesis\. We then extend our analysis to the weaker Reconstructibility Thesis, arguing that it severs the link between a subject and their justification and makes a fatal concession about the indispensable epistemic role of the narrative itself\. We conclude that the Argument View is an untenable model because it conflates the imaginative process of discovery with its potential post\-hoc logical validation\.
