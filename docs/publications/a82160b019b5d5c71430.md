# Agricultural Communication

**Autores:** Meetali Bharti; Bhavnesh Jaint; Rohitashw Kumar; N\. R\. Chaithra; Vijay P\. Singh

**Data:** 2026-10-15 · **Tipo:** book

## Resumo

Agricultural Communication: Advances in Agricultural Knowledge and Information Systems examines the transformative role of communication technologies in modern agriculture\. It highlights the critical need for effective dissemination of agricultural knowledge and technologies to farmers, addressing the challenges posed by poor linkages between research and advisory services\. The book explores the integration of indigenous technical knowledge \(ITK\) with advanced information and communication technologies \(ICT\) to empower farmers, enhance decision\-making, and promote sustainable agricultural practices\. Covering topics such as precision agriculture, drones, artificial intelligence, and mobile technology, it emphasizes the importance of timely and accurate information in overcoming market complexities, climate change, and resource constraints\. Examines emerging trends such as robotics, AI, and precision farming, emphasizing their potential to revolutionize agriculture and address global challenges like climate change and food security\. Addresses real\-world challenges faced by small farmers, offering actionable knowledge on decision\-making, market dynamics, and resource optimization to improve livelihoods\. Highlights the importance of ITK, showcasing its role in sustainable farming practices and its integration with modern technologies for enhanced agricultural productivity\. Offers best practices for different agroclimatic zones\.
