## Descrição do feed

\_Matters of Mind\_ examines the mind\-body problem\. It offers a chapter by chapter analysis of debates surrounding the problem, including visual experience, consciousness and the problem of Zombies and Ghosts\. It will prove invaluable for those interested in epistemology, philosophy of mind and cognitive science\.
