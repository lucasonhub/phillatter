# Engineering a multifunctional bioactive coordination polymer nanoplatform for inflammation\-responsive bone regeneration

**Autores:** Xipu Chen; 秦晓东; Zhong He; Yi Chen; Zezhang Zhu

**Data:** 2026-10-10 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Inflammatory bone diseases are closely associated with impaired osteogenic differentiation of mesenchymal stem cells \(MSCs\) and excessive inflammatory signaling\. Sinomenine \(SIN\) exhibits anti\-inflammatory activity; however, its therapeutic application is limited by poor bioavailability\. In this work, a multifunctional nanoplatform \(CS\-1@CP1@SIN\) was constructed for SIN delivery and electrochemical sensing applications\. The nanocomposite exhibited a mesoporous structure, structural stability, and improved electrochemical response characteristics\. In a TNF\-α\-induced inflammatory MSC model, CS\-1@CP1@SIN improved cell viability, reduced the expression of pro\-inflammatory cytokines \(IL\-1β and IL\-6\), and enhanced the expression of osteogenic markers \(Runx2, ALP, and OCN\)\. In addition, the CS\-1@CP1@SIN\-modified electrode exhibited enhanced electrocatalytic activity toward tert\-butylhydroquinone \(TBHQ\), enabling sensitive and reliable electrochemical detection\. These results demonstrate that the proposed nanoplatform integrates anti\-inflammatory therapy and electrochemical sensing functions, providing a multifunctional strategy for inflammatory bone disease treatment and food antioxidant monitoring\.
