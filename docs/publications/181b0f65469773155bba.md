# Tan, Kwan Hong: Architecting Liberty: Recursive Freedom in the Age of Digital Concentration

**Autores:** 

**Data de descoberta:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** unknown

## Descrição do feed

This thesis critically examines the concept of freedom in the context of the networked age, arguing that traditional liberal paradigms of negative and positive liberty are no longer sufficient to address the unique challenges posed by algorithmic governance, surveillance capitalism, and platform monopolization\. The pervasive and often invisible forms of control embedded in digital architectures necessitate a fundamental rethinking of what it means to be free\. This work introduces a novel theoretical framework—Recursive Freedom—which re\-conceptualizes liberty as the capacity for individuals and collectives to actively and continuously reshape the socio\-technical systems they inhabit\. Recursive freedom is predicated on three interdependent pillars: Architectural Awareness, the ability to perceive and critique system rules; Collective Agency, the capacity for organized action to challenge power structures; and Contestability by Design, the normative principle that digital systems should be built for ongoing negotiation and democratic oversight\. Through a comprehensive analysis of empirical data on digital market concentration, case studies of platform governance failures, and global trends in internet freedom, this thesis demonstrates the inadequacies of existing governance models\. It provides evidence of a systemic erosion of digital rights and a concentration of power that undermines both individual autonomy and collective self\-determination\. The research culminates in a persuasive argument for the adoption of a recursive freedom framework as a necessary condition for a more democratic digital future\. By shifting the focus from individual choice within fixed systems to the collective power to remake those systems, this thesis offers a unique and vital perspective on one of the most pressing issues of our time\. \(direct link \)
