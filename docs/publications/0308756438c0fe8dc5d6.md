# How Learning to Write Helped Me Learn to Think

**Autores:** Nicholas D\. J\. Strzalkowski

**Data:** 2026-10-14 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Write without stopping for five minutes, whatever comes to mind\. This simple task transformed how I write and teach writing in my undergraduate science classroom\. Scientists can spend their whole careers without thinking deeply about writing\. I was this scientist\. I was a mimic without my own voice\. Learning freewriting expanded my view of writing from a means of communication to a method of exploration\. Writing freely for oneself reveals what is known and unknown\. It makes thinking visible and creates a powerful classroom and scholarly practice for generating and refining ideas\. In this reflective essay, I describe how learning freewriting through a faculty community of practice transformed my identity as a writer, scientist, and educator\. I discovered that writing is more than a means of communication—it is a valuable process for developing and refining understanding\. As AI\-assisted writing becomes more pervasive, preserving writing as a tool for thinking, understanding, and developing voice is increasingly important\. I present a framework that positions freewriting along a spectrum from introspection and reflection to creation and dissemination\. Freewriting, across this spectrum, helps writers develop understanding before feeling the pressure to communicate it\.
