# HUBUNGAN PENGGUNAAN GAME TIDAK SESUAI USIA DENGAN KEMAMPUAN BAHASA ANAK USIA 4–6 TAHUN

**Autores:** Sabrina Aulia Nabila; Usep Kustiawan

**Data:** 2026-10-09 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

The rapid advancement of digital technology has increasingly exposed young children to various online games, including those originally designed for older age groups\. Exposure to age\-inappropriate games is suspected to affect children&\#x27;s language ability, given that children aged 4–6 years are in a stage of imitating and absorbing linguistic stimuli from their surrounding environment\. This study aims to analyze the relationship between the use of age\-inappropriate games and the level of language ability of children aged 4–6 years at TK IT As\-Salam, Malang City, based on parents&\#x27; perceptions\. The study employed a quantitative correlational approach with a purposive sampling technique involving 73 parents as respondents\. Data were collected through a Likert\-scale questionnaire that had been tested for validity and reliability, then analyzed using normality tests, linearity tests, and Pearson Product Moment correlation with the assistance of SPSS\. The results showed that the data were normally distributed and the relationship between variables was linear\. The Pearson correlation test produced a correlation coefficient of 0\.798 with a significance value of 0\.000 \(p &lt; 0\.05\), indicating a significant, strong, and positive relationship between the use of age\-inappropriate games and children&\#x27;s language ability\. These findings underscore the importance of parental supervision and collaboration with educators in selecting age\-appropriate digital game content to support optimal child language development\. Paparan terhadap game yang tidak sesuai usia diduga dapat memengaruhi kemampuan bahasa anak, mengingat anak usia 4–6 tahun berada pada tahap meniru dan menyerap rangsangan bahasa dari lingkungan sekitarnya\.
