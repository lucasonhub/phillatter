# The Effectiveness of Social Media Influencers in Driving Beauty Product Purchase Decisions through Content Quality

**Autores:** Isalman

**Data:** 2026-10-14 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

The increasing adoption of social media influencers in digital marketing strategies underpins this research, particularly in triggering purchase decisions for Glad2Glow beauty products\. This research tests the argument that an influencer&\#x27;s effectiveness depends not only on their popularity but also on the quality of the content they present\. The purpose of this study is to analyze the influence of social media influencers on content quality and purchase decisions and to confirm the role of content quality as a mediating variable\. A quantitative approach was applied in this study by collecting data from 168 female consumers who use Glad2Glow products\. The analysis was conducted using structural equation modeling to test the relationship between variables\. The research findings show that the presence of social media influencers can significantly increase content quality but does not have a significant direct influence on purchase decisions\. Furthermore, content quality was found to have a significant positive effect on purchase decisions and was proven to strongly mediate the intervening relationship\. Practically, this study emphasizes that corporations must ensure that the content produced by influencers is relevant, authentic, and persuasive, rather than simply relying on the credibility of the figure\.
