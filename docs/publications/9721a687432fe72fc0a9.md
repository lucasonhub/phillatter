# METAMORPHIC TESTING OF SOFTWARE FOR DETECTING FALSIFIED DIGITAL INFORMATION

**Autores:** Артем Ховрат

**Data:** 2026-10-15 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

The problem of verifying software for detecting falsified digital information in the absence of a test oracle, when the correct answer for new messages of the stream is unknown, is considered\. A system of seven metamorphic relations is proposed for cascade software in which classifiers of different cost form successive tiers\. The relations are divided into three groups\. The first covers invariance of the decision to paraphrasing, neutral insertions, orthographic noise and duplication of text\. The second concerns robustness to humanization of machine\-generated text\. The third checks monotonicity of routing with respect to the confidence threshold and independence of results from the processing order\. Each relation is associated with the component in which a detected violation should be localized\. A testing procedure on unlabelled messages is described, with the release decision based on the upper bound of the confidence interval of the violation rate\.
