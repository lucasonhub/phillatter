# Boundary\-aware multi\-scale residual learning with hybrid radiomic–deep fusion for lung tumor segmentation and classification

**Autores:** M\. Sutharsan; K\. R\. Kavitha

**Data:** 2026-10-10 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Accurate lung tumor segmentation and classification from computed tomography \(CT\) images remain challenging because of heterogeneous tumor appearance, indistinct boundaries, and inter\-class similarity\. This study presents BAMR\-HF, an integrated framework combining boundary\-aware segmentation, hybrid radiomic–deep feature fusion, and context\-aware classification\. BAMR\-Net performs tumor segmentation using multi\-scale residual learning and boundary refinement, while TRFF\-Net combines radiomic and deep features through learnable weighted fusion, while MTCA\-Net incorporates tumor and peritumoral contextual information\. The framework was evaluated on the processed Lung Cancer Segmentation Dataset with Lung\-RADS Class comprising 972 CT images, with 708 images in the training partition and 264 images in the repository\-defined held\-out test partition\. The source labels were consolidated into Mild \(LR2\), Moderate \(LR3\), and Severe \(LR4A–LR4B\) categories\. BAMR\-Net achieved a test Dice score of 0\.8563\. TRFF\-Net and MTCA\-Net achieved test accuracies of 0\.9038 and 0\.9327, respectively, while the complete BAMR\-HF framework attained an accuracy of 0\.9478, F1\-score of 0\.9476, AUC of 0\.9872, and MCC of 0\.9215\. These findings support the complementary use of boundary\-aware segmentation, hybrid feature representation, and contextual modeling, while broader validation on independent multi\-institutional data remains necessary\.
