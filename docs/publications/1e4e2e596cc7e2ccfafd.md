# Chen, Jijiang: Topological Structure, Information Integration, and the Nature of Consciousness: An Integrated Framework Based on Panpsychism and IIT

**Autores:** 

**Data de descoberta:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** unknown

## Descrição do feed

Mainstream consciousness theories regard consciousness as a dynamic process of neural firing, but the results of split\-brain experiments pose a fundamental challenge to this view: after the corpus callosum is severed, the two cerebral hemispheres exhibit behavioral conflicts, yet subjects still report “I am one I”—the unity of consciousness does not disappear with the interruption of physical connectivity\. This paper proposes an alternative framework: consciousness is an intrinsic property of matter \(panpsychism\), but its unity is neither pre\-given nor emerges from nothing; rather, it is constituted by the coupling relations established in the process of information integration\. The topological structure of matter determines the pattern and strength of information coupling \(the Φ value\), thereby modulating the unity and richness of consciousness \(Integrated Information Theory\)\. Under this framework, the difference between human beings and inanimate matter lies not in material composition but in the capacity of topological structure to support information coupling\. The paper further examines this framework through three test cases—the sleep state, Libet&\#x27;s experiment, and the cerebellum paradox—and infers that the large\-scale network structure of the cosmos may possess forms of consciousness far surpassing those of human beings\. Finally, the paper discusses the falsifiability of the framework and potential empirical pathways through brain–computer interface experiments\.
