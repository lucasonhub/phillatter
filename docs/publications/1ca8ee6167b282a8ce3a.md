# Multi\-Sensitive Distributional Fairness: Joint Wasserstein Audits and Shapley Allocation

**Autores:** Agathe Fernandes Machado; Charlotte Jamotton; Arthur Charpentier

**Data:** 2026-10-13 · **Tipo:** preprint

## Resumo

Fairness audits with several sensitive attributes are often performed one coordinate at a time\. This can miss purely intersectional disparities: a score may be independent of each sensitive coordinate while depending on their joint profile\. We study distributional demographic parity for a real\-valued score using one\-dimensional Wasserstein geometry\. We distinguish two complementary constructions\. Conditional and sequential audits characterize joint parity through their zero sets, but their positive values do not generally decompose a fixed joint Wasserstein disparity\. By contrast, full\-cell conditional quantile functions define a Hilbert\-valued random variable whose variance is the joint disparity\. Conditional expectations of this quantile process induce a monotone information game: every reveal order yields an exact martingale decomposition of the same joint disparity, while Shapley values provide an order\-free attribution across sensitive attributes\. Under independent sensitive attributes, the game reduces to a Hoeffding\-ANOVA decomposition and Shapley splits each interaction energy equally among its members; under dependence, orthogonality is lost and explicit formulas reveal suppression and redundancy effects that marginal audits can hide\. We give fixed\-grid estimators and permutation calibration, and illustrate the framework on synthetic examples and ACSIncome\. The resulting framework separates three questions that are often conflated: whether joint distributional parity holds, how a fixed joint disparity decomposes along an information path, and how that disparity can be attributed across sensitive coordinates without privileging an ordering\.
