# A Computationally Efficient Radar Sensor Modeling Methodology Incorporating Rain\-Induced Propagation Attenuation for Virtual Validation of Autonomous Driving Systems

**Autores:** Seunghwan Chung; Chan\-Jung Kim

**Data:** 2026-10-15 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

This paper presents a computationally efficient radar sensor modeling methodology incorporating rain\-induced propagation attenuation for simulation\-based validation of autonomous driving systems\. The proposed framework combines the classical radar equation with the rain attenuation model specified in ITU\-R Recommenda\-tion P\.838\-3 to estimate received signal power degradation and target\-range estimation errors under rainfall conditions\. A stochastic False Alarm Probability Generation \(FAPG\) algorithm is also introduced to reproduce intermittent tar\-get detection according to user\-defined probabilities of false alarm and detection\. The proposed methodology was implemented through real\-time co\-simulation between MATLAB/Simulink and IPG CarMaker\. Simulation results demonstrated that the proposed framework successfully reproduced rain\-induced propagation attenuation, target\-range estimation errors, received signal power degradation, and stochastic target detection behavior while maintaining computational efficiency suitable for real\-time simulation\.
