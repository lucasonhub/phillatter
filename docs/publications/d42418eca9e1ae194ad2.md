# Post\-Generative

**Autores:** Benedikt Brünner; Martin Ebner; Sandra Schön; Kathryn A\. Hoffmann; Christian Ortner; Philipp Leitner

**Data:** 2026-10-09 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

This A3 poster proposes the term “post\-generative”\. It describes a societal condition in which generative artificial intelligence permeates everyday life\. Drawing on the idea of the post\-digital, the term denotes a world that generative AI has already transformed\. A graphic shows eight areas of society in which generative AI is at work\. Nine indications from education, science, software development, the creative sector and research support this diagnosis of the present\. A companion sheet sets out all 19 sources and their scope\. More recent findings are collected at postgenerative\.at\.
