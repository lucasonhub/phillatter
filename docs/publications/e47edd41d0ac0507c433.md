# Genesis Steering: Better Activation Steering Through Training Data

**Autores:** Tora Bodin; Eric Aubinais; Ilaria Torre; Chih\-Hong Cheng

**Data:** 2026-10-13 · **Tipo:** preprint

## Resumo

Activation steering controls large language models at inference time, but its effectiveness depends on the choice of reference data\. We introduce genesis steering, which derives a genesis vector from contrastive examples in the model&\#x27;s own fine\-tuning data\. When these data are unavailable, we draw on the membership inference literature to identify likely training examples and construct a proxy dataset\. We establish two theoretical results on optimal displacement to the decision boundary and reduced proxy\-estimation bias\. We evaluate genesis steering and membership\-based proxy construction on Tülu\-3\-8B, whose fine\-tuning data are publicly available, and Llama\-3\.1\-8B\-Instruct as a control\. We find that safety data from Tülu\-3\-8B&\#x27;s training mixture yield stronger steering than external datasets on both models\. On Tülu\-3\-8B, membership scores largely agree in identifying training examples, and vectors built from top\-ranked examples achieve competitive performance against random subsets of the same size\. In contrast, the results on Llama\-3\.1\-8B\-Instruct hint that a MIA method will only have a positive effect if the mean direction shift with MIA\-filtered in\-distribution data is much less severe than the direction shift with OoD data\.
