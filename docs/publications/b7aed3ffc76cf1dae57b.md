# Evaluasi Implementasi Sistem Informasi Arsip dan Persuratan Berbasis Network Attached Storage di Sekolah Pascasarjana, IPB University

**Autores:** Primus Sanbein; Rudi Irawan; Titi Candra Sunarti; Alan Sunarya

**Data:** 2026-10-10 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

The implementation of a digital archival information system by the Graduate School of IPB University represents a strategic step toward strengthening administrative governance in higher education\. This study focuses on dynamic records management through the application of the Archival and Correspondence Information System \(SIAP\) based on Network Attached Storage \(NAS\)\. The system was developed to address limitations of manual archiving, such as retrieval delays and the risk of data loss\. The evaluation was conducted across four dimensions: system effectiveness, user satisfaction, operational challenges, and human resource agility\. A mixed\-methods approach was employed using an online questionnaire involving 30 respondents from two groups: administrators \(archive managers at the Graduate School\) and internal users representing faculties or other work units within IPB who utilize the Graduate School’s archival services\. The results indicate that the system is reasonably effective in supporting digital archive services, although challenges remain, including the absence of standard procedures, limited training, uneven technical capacities, and lack of an archive tracking feature\. This study highlights the importance of aligning technology, human resources, and institutional policies to develop an adaptive and sustainable digital archiving system\.
