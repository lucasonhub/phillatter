# Kim, GeonWoo: Political Attribution Does Not Entail Sufficient Specifying Capacity: Genuine Judgment and the Conditions of Public Reconsideration

**Autores:** 

**Data de descoberta:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** unknown

## Descrição do feed

Independent judgment and public answerability are central concerns in debates over representation and democratic contestation\. Both can be secure while a representative relies on advisers to specify what her decisions protect\. Does securing her judgment also secure the capacity to revise those terms when the governed contest them? A representative can genuinely reject advice while still needing help to formulate a reasoned alternative\. A thought experiment compares two realizations of one representative with the same authority and responsibility\. Both understand and critically adopt the same advice for the same reasons\. So advised, both assign the same protections and exceptions across related cases\. In one, the adviser performs work the representative could also perform\. In the other, it supplies a recurring specifying function she does not sufficiently command\. I call the second arrangement purposive dissociation\. When citizens’ challenges require new terms of protection, effective reconsideration depends on access to specifying work\. Making decisions answerable, governing advice, and developing specifying capacity can therefore be distinct institutional achievements\. A shared process can likewise supply work that several representatives cannot sufficiently perform\. Changes to that process may reshape the protections they authorize while their votes remain opposed\.
