# Medication Adherence: Digital Health Interventions

**Autores:** Asmita Bhosale, Diksha Jadhav, Dhanashri Ghotekar, Rajeshwari Gangad

**Data:** 2026-10-12 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Medication adherence plays a crucial role in achieving the best possible therapeutic results while minimizing morbidity, mortality, and healthcare expenditures\. Nonetheless, patients suffering from both acute and chronic conditions frequently face significant hurdles when it comes to following their prescribed medication regimens\. Various factors, including forgetfulness, complicated medication schedules, side effects, insufficient understanding, financial constraints, and restricted access to healthcare, can lead to suboptimal adherence to medications\. The swift advancement of digital health technologies has opened up new avenues for enhancing medication adherence and providing support to patients during their treatment journey\. Interventions in digital health, such as mobile health applications, text message reminders, electronic monitoring systems for medications, telemedicine services, wearable technology, and tools powered by artificial intelligence, can offer tailored reminders, educational resources, monitoring capabilities, and foster communication between patients and healthcare providers\. These tools may boost adherence by fostering patient involvement, aiding in self\-management, and facilitating the early detection of issues related to adherence\.
