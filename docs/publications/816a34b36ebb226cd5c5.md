# Machine Learning–Assisted Fluorescent Eu@ZnMOF for the Enoxacin and Glyphosate Detection in Food by Suppressing Sensitization and Ligand–Metal Charge Transfer

**Autores:** Ze Li; Yan Man; Qingju Liu; Jilong Han; Zhikun Zhang

**Data:** 2026-10-14 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

ABSTRACT The antibiotics and pesticides in the ecosystem environment, owing to their extensive application in veterinary medicine and insecticides, present a significant hazard to public health\. Herein, we developed a novel bimetallic metal‐organic framework \(MOF\) as a ratiometric fluorescent sensor for the simultaneous monitoring of enoxacin \(ENX\) and glyphosate \(GLY\)\. To fabricate a dual‐responsive probe, we synthesized the ZnMOF by reacting Zn 2\+ ions with the ligands \(2,2′‐bipyridine‐5,5′‐dicarboxylic acid, 8‐hydroxyquinoline, and 2‐aminoterephthalic acid\)\. Subsequently, the bimetallic Eu@ZnMOF was synthesized by loading europium \(Eu 3\+ \) ions into the ZnMOF based on the coordination with the 2,2′‐bipyridine‐5,5′‐dicarboxylic acid sites\. The Eu@ZnMOF‐based detection system exhibits a distinct green emission due to the suppression of excited‐state intramolecular proton transfer in 8‐hydroxyquinoline by Zn 2\+ ions\. In the detection system, ENX enhances the red emission of Eu@ZnMOF by sensitizing Eu 3\+ ions\. Accordingly, the ENX fluorescent sensing system was fabricated\. Moreover, ENX‐modified Eu@ZnMOF was fabricated\. Upon the addition of GLY, suppression of sensitization and ligand‐to‐metal charge transfer \(LMCT\) occurs, which quenches the red emission of Eu 3\+ and boosts the blue emission, resulting in a distinct color transition from red to blue\. The ratiometric fluorescent sensor allows detection limits of 0\.05 and 0\.10 mg/L for ENX and GLY, respectively\. Moreover, we integrated this material into a microfluidic chip\. By combining this platform with smartphone imaging and machine learning algorithms, we constructed a rapid detection protocol capable of simultaneous multi‐sample analysis\. These results demonstrate the promising application potential of Eu@ZnMOF for advanced environmental and food‐safety testing\.
