# DO GUIA ORIENTADO A PRINCÍPIOS À SÍNTESE INTEGRADA: ANÁLISE COMPARATIVA ENTRE A 7ª E A 8ª EDIÇÕES DO GUIA PMBOK

**Autores:** Henderson Matsuura Sanches; Joelson Carvalho Junior

**Data:** 2026-10-15 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

The PMBOK Guide, published by the Project Management Institute \(PMI\), is the leading international reference in project management\.Its 7th edition \(2021\) broke away from the process\-based model by adopting twelve principles and eight performance domains, which drew criticism for the lack of practical guidance\.In November 2025, PMI released the 8th edition, which aims to reconcile both approaches\.This article aims to compare the 7th and 8th editions of the guide, identifying what has changed, what is new and what remains in common\.This is a qualitative, descriptive and documentary study based on PMI publications and secondary technical sources published between 2025 and 2026\.The results show that the 8th edition consolidates the twelve principles into six, reduces the performance domains from eight to seven, reintroduces 40 non\-\-prescriptive processes mapped to five focus areas and brings together, in a single volume, content previously split with the companion process groups guide\.The main novelties are the sustainability principle, the three\-dimensional mindset framework and the appendices on artificial intelligence, project management offices and procurement\.Value orientation, the value delivery system, tailoring and neutrality regarding the development approach remain in common\.It is concluded that the 8th edition represents a synthesis, rather than a rupture, between prescription and flexibility\.
