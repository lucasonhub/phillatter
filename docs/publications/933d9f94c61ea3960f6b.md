# The Importance of Utilizing Innovative Methods in the Management of Cultural Institutions

**Autores:** Xolmuminov Muminmirzo

**Data:** 2026-10-15 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

This article analyzes the significance of employing innovative methods and approaches to enhance the management framework of cultural institutions under contemporary conditions, alongside the practical issues surrounding their implementation\. The study provides a scientific rationale for transitioning the cultural sector toward financial autonomy and self\-sustainability\. Furthermore, it scientifically and practically substantiates the modernization of organizational and economic mechanisms in the field, including the integration of “Art Management” principles, the expansion of “Creative Industry” sectors, and the acceleration of scientific\-practical “Project Commercialization” processes\. Additionally, the paper comprehensively highlights mechanisms for improving managerial efficiency through the establishment of mutually beneficial public\-private partnership models and the effective utilization of preferential conditions created in the sector\.
