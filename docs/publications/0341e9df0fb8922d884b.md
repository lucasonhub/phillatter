# Triage\-PiEvo: Evidence Admission for Principle\-Evolving Scientific Agents

**Autores:** Peize Li; Yingming Pu; Xingguo Xu; Yiming Cui; Yuansheng Gao; Tao Lin

**Data:** 2026-10-10 · **Tipo:** preprint

## Resumo

Large language model \(LLM\)\-based AI Scientists advance scientific discovery, yet they suffer from inefficiencies due to noisy observations\. Existing principle\-evolving agents act on every anomaly, leaving its source unresolved: a new mechanism or an evaluation artifact, so invalid revisions bias evolution\. To address this, we formulate revision as evidence admission: anomalies steer search immediately, but only independently verified evidence may rewrite persistent principles, and contradicted principles are retracted\. We realize this in TRIAGE\-PIEVO , which pairs an anomaly\-responsive search portfolio with a staged verification gate that alone controls what enters principle state\. Across four scientific tasks with three backbones, TRIAGE\-PIEVO \(1\) attains the highest average solution quality with \+7\.5%\~11\.8% over the strongest baseline per backbone, matching the strongest baseline&\#x27;s full\-budget quality with one\-third of the evaluation budget on Qwen3\-235B; and additional stress tests and cross\-harness deployment demonstrate that \(2\) in constructed stress tests, it admits none of the corrupted, hidden\-source, or exploit evidence that ungated variants admit, while missing no planted mechanism, and \(3\) plugged unchanged into three external research harnesses, it cuts false evidence admissions by over 60% and raises verified scores by 31%\~43%\.
