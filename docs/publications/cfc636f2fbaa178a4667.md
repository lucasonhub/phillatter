# Functional voxel modeling of the radar detection zone considering the terrain influence by the R\-function apparatus

**Autores:** A\. B\. Yarov

**Data:** 2026-10-11 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

The article addresses the problem of functional\-voxel modeling of a radar detection zone considering terrain effects\. The author describes a principle for recalculating the local characteristics of the updated function by the use of local functions represented as V\-representations of the radar detection zone and the terrain model\. Moreover, the author defines the modeling space as a bounded domain and approximates by a regular discrete grid\. The article performs the integration of the models using the R\-function apparatus developed by Rvachev, which enables the implementation of set\-theoretic operations such as intersections and joins in terms of ocal functions\. This approach provides the construction of the resulting model in the form of a unified local analytical equation and ensures proper consideration of terrain\-induced shielding effects in the formation of the detection zone\. The software implementation demonstrates the applicability of the method with the discretization of 2003 voxels\. The proposed approach can be applied to the analysis of spatial characteristics of radar coverage and to the development of software tools for modeling complex geometric stages\.
