# DEVELOPING STUDENTS&\#x27; INFORMATION SECURITY COMPETENCE IN THE AGE OF GENERATIVE AI: A QUASI\-EXPERIMENTAL STUDY IN UZBEK HIGHER EDUCATION

**Autores:** Mustafoyev Shahzod

**Data:** 2026-10-14 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Generative AI tools are now part of students&\#x27; everyday academic work, but the information security risks involved are rarely covered in curricula\. Students leak their own and other people&\#x27;s data into external systems, rely on fabricated output and fall for AI\-enhanced social engineering\. This paper reports a quasi\-experimental study of a pedagogical model designed to develop students&\#x27; information security competence in the context of AI tools\. The model is based on a pedagogical classification of AI related threats into four groups \(input data, output, access and account, cognitive\-behavioural\), a five\-stage learning technology in which simulation and reflection are mandatory, and five pedagogical conditions\.
