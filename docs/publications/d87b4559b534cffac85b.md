# Lie\-theoretic structure of parameter gradients in variational quantum circuits

**Autores:** María\-Luisa Pérez\-Delgado; Roberto Casado Vara

**Data:** 2026-10-13 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Abstract Variational quantum circuits are a central paradigm of quantum machine learning and near\-term quantum computing, yet their practical optimization is often limited by strongly suppressed parameter gradients\. Dynamical Lie algebras provide a rigorous description of the infinitesimal directions accessible to a variational architecture, but dynamical accessibility alone does not determine the gradient signal exposed by an actual circuit parametrization\. In this work, we develop a Lie\-theoretic description of parameter\-gradient structure that separates four successive mechanisms: dynamical accessibility, observable visibility, state\-dependent activation, and local parameter realization\. The optimization observable determines which accessible directions can influence the objective and how strongly they couple to it, while the quantum state selects which of these response modes are locally activated\. This information is then pulled back through the circuit parametrization to recover the actual parameter gradients\. The resulting construction yields an exact modal decomposition of the accessible gradient signal, a hierarchy of structural bounds, and a natural description of state averaging\. An analytical single\-qubit example and controlled numerical experiments validate the successive levels of the hierarchy and show that accessible Lie dimension alone is insufficient to characterize the parameter\-gradient scale\. The results support a mechanism\-based view of variational trainability in which parameter gradients emerge from the interaction between accessible dynamics, observable coupling, quantum\-state activation, and circuit parametrization\.
