# Associations between change fatigue and teaching enthusiasm among Shanghai teachers: the roles of teaching motivation and job satisfaction

**Autores:** Zhang Ziying; Shiyu Li; Yuxuan Bao; Yipu Hao

**Data:** 2026-10-14 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Introduction In recent years, frequent educational reforms have placed growing demands on teachers and may contribute to change fatigue\. Drawing on Conservation of Resources theory, this study examines how change fatigue relates to teaching enthusiasm and whether teaching motivation and job satisfaction serve as independent and chained mediators\. Methods Data were drawn from Shanghai teachers who participated in the OECD&\#x27;s 2024 Teaching and Learning International Survey \(TALIS\)\. Validated TALIS\-based scales measured change fatigue, teaching motivation, job satisfaction, and teaching enthusiasm\. Confirmatory factor analysis, correlation analysis, structural equation modeling, and bootstrap\-based mediation tests were used to examine the hypothesized relationships\. Results Change fatigue was negatively associated with teaching motivation and job satisfaction\. Teaching motivation was positively associated with job satisfaction and teaching enthusiasm, and job satisfaction was positively associated with teaching enthusiasm\. Teaching motivation and job satisfaction each showed an independent indirect association and together showed a chain indirect association between change fatigue and teaching enthusiasm\. After including the mediators, no direct association was found between change fatigue and teaching enthusiasm; instead, change fatigue was indirectly and negatively related to teaching enthusiasm through teaching motivation and job satisfaction\. Conclusion These findings suggest that change fatigue is indirectly associated with lower teaching enthusiasm through lower teaching motivation and job satisfaction\. Schools and policymakers should manage the pace of reform, provide resource compensation and professional support, and strengthen teachers&\#x27; motivation and job satisfaction to sustain teaching enthusiasm\.
