## Descrição do feed

\_Zenodo\_ 1\. 2026This paper asks how immediacy, experience, and rationality are generated rather than presupposed\. It argues that what appears as immediate experience or self\-standing rational access is often the compressed result of prior mediation by perceptual organization, conceptual inheritance, historical stabilization, social practices, instrumentation, and inferential structures\. The paper develops an operational account in which immediacy is not treated as the absence of mediation, but as the local disappearance of visible mediation within a sufficiently stabilized configuration\. Experience is analyzed as an operationally organized field in which differences become selectively observable, retainable, and action\-guiding, while rationality emerges only within intervals in which reasons, incompatibilities, commitments, and revisions become available as non\-redundant constraints\. Transcendental reflection is reconstructed not as access to timeless conditions of possibility, but as a method for identifying the historically and materially generated conditions under which particular forms of experience and rational assessment become possible\. The concepts of historical compression and invisible mediation explain how layered processes can become phenomenologically or epistemically immediate once their enabling operations are no longer encountered as separate steps\. Generated observability extends this analysis to scientific and artificial systems, where new sensors, scales, interfaces, and inferential architectures can produce previously unavailable objects and distinctions\. Xenoscience generalizes the argument by treating immediacy, experience, and rationality as revisable operational achievements rather than universal starting points\. \(direct link \)
