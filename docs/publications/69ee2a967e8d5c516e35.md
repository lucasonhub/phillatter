# MediNova AI

**Autores:** Prof\. Shraddha Raut; Utkarsh Udapurkar; Sameer Ashtikar; Harsh Chorode; Pratik Mule; Pawan Dhengre; Harshal Telrandhe

**Data:** 2026-10-12 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Rural And disadvantaged communities often have difficulty getting access in time\. Quality healthcare due To long travel distances, Shortage of specialist doctors, limited diagnostic facilities, Problems with the availability of medicines, delayed referrals And poor continuity of patient records\. This plan suggests an integrated healthcare support system connecting patients, frontline health workers and healthcare facilities through a digital platform\. The proposed system Assistant teleconsultation includes, digital patient records, Agreement and queue management, digital triage, referral tracking, diagnostic coordination, Medicines availability and follow\- up support for high\- risk patients\. The system is designed to work\. Low\- connectivity environments And deliver multilingual support To improve accessibility\. The methodology Collection included\. Patient information, Identity healthcare requirements, Connect patients appropriate healthcare services and maintenance of follow\- up information digitally\. The proposed solution Expected to decline\. Unnecessary travel And the wait, the better referral completion and patient to follow, to provide better visibility of medicines And diagnostic services, Improve coordination between and healthcare facilities\. The system aims to strengthen the existing public healthcare infrastructure and provide more accessible, consistent and responsive\. Healthcare services To rural communities
