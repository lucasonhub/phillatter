# Análisis cromático para caracterización de dipterofauna de la familia Piophilidae con fines forenses

**Autores:** Emanuel Emilio Valera\-Hurtado; Andrea Alicia Yépez\-Peñaherrera; Rosa Doménica Peñafiel\-Guatán; Denisse Alexandra Guartambel\-Cruz

**Data:** 2026-10-15 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Forensic entomology is an essential discipline for determining the Postmortem Interval; however, the description of color in species of the Piophilidae family remains a subjective process that depends on the expert&\#x27;s perception and lighting conditions\. This study addresses this issue throught chromatic analysis to standardize the characterizing of forensic\-interest Piophilidae entomological samples in Ecuador\. To this end, an exploratory documentary approach and a systematic review were employed, integrating objective colorimetric methodologies such as the Munsell and CIELAB systems\. Various anatomical structures and appendages of the Neottiophilinae and Piophilinae subfamilies were analyzed in detail, translating visual features into specific digital codes such as HEX and RGB\. The results establish a precise color classification by tagmata, providing a comprehensive reference tool that minimizes inconsistencies in taxonomic identification\. It concludes that creating a color scale based on color analysis turns coloration into an objective and verificable forensic data point for this family, strengthening the scientific validity of technical reports\. This standardization not only optimizes the reliability of entomological analyses, but also provides a methodological rigor necessary for the administration of justice in the national context
