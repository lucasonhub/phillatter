# Research on the Impact of Carbon Accounting Information Disclosure on Corporate Reputation

**Autores:** Yiran Li

**Data:** 2026-10-09 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

At present, the concept of environmental protection has become deeply rooted in people&\#x27;s minds, and enterprises should therefore assume their social responsibilities\. Corporate reputation, as an important intangible asset, is increasingly attracting the attention of stakeholders\. This article mainly analyzes the significance of carbon accounting information disclosure on corporate reputation, and explores the process mechanism by which carbon accounting information disclosure affects corporate reputation\. It provides a reference for enterprises to optimize their information disclosure strategies and enhance their reputation levels in the context of low\-carbon transformation\.
