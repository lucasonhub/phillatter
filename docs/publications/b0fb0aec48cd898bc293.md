# Strisower, John: The Consciton Hypothesis

**Autores:** 

**Data de descoberta:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** unknown

## Descrição do feed

This document proposes that consciousness is tied to a universal, non\-local, two\-way phenomenon, the consciton, that is not a known force and not ordinary quantum entanglement\. It sets the hypothesis beside deflationary, structural and mysterian positions, states what is unresolved \(no equation, no coupling rule, no test yet\), and lists conditions that would count against it\. It is a living hypothesis; this is Version 1\.0\. Drafted with AI assistance \(Claude\); concept and direction by the author\. Not peer reviewed\. \(direct link \)
