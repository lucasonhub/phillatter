# A temporally responsive anti\-scarring and pro\-neuronal differentiation dual\-targeting composite hydrogel fabricated via AI\-assisted design and loaded with neural stem cells for spinal cord injury repair

**Autores:** Chuanhao Li; Wenlu Yuan; Quan Liu; Pengtian Zhao; Tianliang Zhang; Jiangliang Yin; Guangzhi Ning; Zhishuo Wang; Shiqing Feng

**Data:** 2026-10-12 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Spinal cord injury \(SCI\) is a highly disabling central nervous system disorder\. Tissue engineering strategies utilizing multifunctional hydrogels for neural stem cell \(NSC\) delivery represent a promising therapeutic direction for SCI\. Herein, a temporally responsive dual\-targeting multifunctional hydrogel \(EnpMG\-bSH\) for NSC delivery is reported\. An artificial intelligence \(AI\)\-assisted designed TGF\-β\-targeted lysosome\-targeting chimera \(LYTAC\) peptide EndoP144 is released in a temporally controlled manner in response to the gradually elevated matrix metalloproteinase\-2 \(MMP\-2\) in the SCI lesion site, enabling precise transforming growth factor\-β \(TGF\-β\) regulation\. Adhesive silk fibroin nanoparticles anchored with si\- Notch1 \-loaded hybrid exosomes are incorporated to induce neuronal differentiation of NSCs, support synaptic remodeling and exert concurrent immunomodulatory effects\. This system attenuates glial scarring by downregulating the TGF\-β signaling pathway in astrocytes, modulates the differentiation of NSCs via targeted modulation of the Notch1 pathway, and reshapes the local inflammatory microenvironment toward an anti\-inflammatory phenotype, significantly improving locomotor and sensory recovery in SCI mice\. Collectively, the AI\-assisted EnpMG\-bSH hydrogel\-NSCs complementary therapeutic strategy provides a potential framework for coordinated local treatment of SCI\.
