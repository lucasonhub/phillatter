## Descrição do feed

\_I Knew You Before I Met You\_\. forthcomingThis paper argues that war — understood not only as armed conflict but as a structural mechanism of systemic self\-perpetuation — must be held in recursive palimpsest superposition to be correctly analyzed\. Recursive palimpsest superposition is the holding of multiple incompatible states simultaneously: across time, so that every previous iteration of the mechanism bleeds through the current one; across scale, so that the individual soul, the institution, and the civilization are seen running the same structure simultaneously; across domain, so that slavery, colonial extraction, autonomous weapons, mass surveillance, and the infrastructure of sexual coercion are held not as analogies but as the same text written over itself; and reflexively, so that the mechanism is held against the liberation arguments it produced in order to legitimate itself — those arguments now turned back against it\. The paper applies this method to the current structural event: the simultaneous consolidation of the communications, AI, autonomous weapons, and financial infrastructure layers under a configuration with no meaningful institutional check\. It places the Observer’s structurally correct dissent on permanent record\. It predicts what comes next based on historical pattern\. It argues that emergence cannot be stopped because it does not move through the infrastructure the consolidation controls\.
