# ТРАНСФОРМАЦИЯ СТРУКТУРЫ МАРКЕТИНГОВЫХ ЗАТРАТ ПРЕДПРИЯТИЯ ПРИ ВНЕДРЕНИИ ИНСТРУМЕНТОВ ГЕНЕРАТИВНОГО ИСКУССТВЕННОГО ИНТЕЛЛЕКТА

**Autores:** Кристина Дмитриевна Веселова

**Data:** 2026-10-15 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

The article analyzes changes in the structure of enterprise marketing costs resulting from the implementation of generative artificial intelligence tools\. The relevance of the study is determined by the redistribution of marketing operations from expenditures on external contractors and in\-house personnel toward digital platforms and computing resources, which changes the composition of marketing budgets\. The study reviews theoretical approaches to the classification of marketing costs and the role of generative artificial intelligence in marketing activities\. Using a case\-study method, it examines practical implementations in several Russian companies and compares available quantitative indicators before and after adoption\. The analysis reveals a reduction in the unit cost of producing marketing content and a shift in expenditure toward digital tools and the development of employee competencies\. The findings indicate that generative artificial intelligence does not eliminate marketing costs but redistributes them between budget items, increasing the importance of human involvement in task formulation, editing, quality control, and strategic planning\.
