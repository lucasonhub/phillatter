# AI\-Based Smart Interview Analyzer System

**Autores:** Miss\. Dhyanhvi Kale; Mr\. Abhishek Padole Mr\. Aditya Chandure; Dr\. Abrar S\. Alvi

**Data:** 2026-10-14 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

The AI\-Based Smart Interview Analyzer System is an artificial intelligence\-based web application developed to help students and job seekers improve their interview skills through interactive mock interviews, automated performance evaluation, and personalized feedback\. In today’s competitive employment environment, candidates often face challenges such as nervousness, poor communication skills, lack of confidence, and insufficient interview practice\. Traditional preparation methods may not provide immediate and consistent feedback, creating a need for an intelligent system that supports effective and personalized interview preparation\. The proposed system integrates Artificial Intelligence \(AI\), Machine Learning \(ML\), and Natural Language Processing \(NLP\) techniques to support interview practice and analyze candidate responses\. It provides job\-role\-based interview preparation, maintains interview history, and helps users identify their strengths and weaknesses through feedback\. The application consists of two main modules: the Admin Module and the Student Module\. The Admin Module manages student information, job roles, interviews, and feedback, while the Student Module allows users to access their dashboard, practice interviews, review previous sessions, view feedback, and manage their profiles\. The system aims to provide an accessible and structured environment for continuous learning and performance improvement\. By combining AI\-based assessment with a user\-friendly web interface, the AI\-Based Smart Interview Analyzer System can help candidates develop better communication skills, increase confidence, and improve their readiness for real\-world job interviews\. Future enhancements may include advanced speech analysis, facial expression recognition, body\-language assessment, multilingual support, and more comprehensive real\-time performance evaluation
