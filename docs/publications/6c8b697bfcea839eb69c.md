# Intelligent Academic Software: Transforming Higher Education through AI\-Driven Learning Ecosystems

**Autores:** Sandeep Hanumant Bhokare; Siddhant kumar Vasantrao Wadmare

**Data:** 2026-10-17 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

The digital transformation of higher education has accelerated with the integration of Artificial Intelligence \(AI\) technologies into academic software systems\. Intelligent Academic Software \(IAS\) represents a paradigm shift from static learning management systems toward dynamic, data\-driven learning ecosystems capable of personalization, predictive analytics, automated assessment, and institutional decision support\. This study proposes a comprehensive AI\-driven learning ecosystem model integrating adaptive learning engines, intelligent tutoring systems, predictive analytics modules, and administrative automation frameworks\. A multi\-institutional dataset comprising 1,200 student records was analyzed using supervised and unsupervised machine learning algorithms to evaluate performance prediction, engagement analytics, and risk detection accuracy\. Experimental results demonstrate improved academic performance forecasting \(92% accuracy\), enhanced learner engagement, and optimized resource allocation\. The study contributes to AI\-enabled higher education transformation by presenting a scalable intelligent software architecture aligned with ethical AI governance and institutional digital strategies\.
