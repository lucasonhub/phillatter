# Robust Langevin Sampling under State\-Dependent Heavy\-Tailed Noise with Applications to LLM Fine\-Tuning

**Autores:** M\. Berk Sahin; Ege C\. Kaya; Arda Fazla; Behzad Sharif; Abolfazl Hashemi

**Data:** 2026-10-09 · **Tipo:** preprint

## Resumo

Existing convergence guarantees for non\-log\-concave sampling with stochastic gradient Langevin Monte Carlo \(SG\-LMC\) typically assume globally Lipschitz gradients and uniformly bounded noise variance\. Both assumptions can fail even in elementary sampling problems, and relaxing them better reflects practical applications such as Bayesian fine\-tuning of large language models \(LLMs\)\. We relax both assumptions through generalized smoothness and our proposed state\-dependent heavy\-tailed oracle with finite $p$\-th moments, $1&lt;p\\leq 2$\. The oracle permits infinite variance when $p&lt;2$ and includes the Blum\-Gladyshev \(BG\-0\) condition\. We study these relaxations separately and jointly, proposing three methods\. Under globally Lipschitz gradients, robust LMC \(R\-LMC\) handles the proposed oracle through geometric\-median centering and residual clipping\. With exact gradients, adaptive LMC \(A\-LMC\) controls discretization error under generalized smoothness\. Robust adaptive LMC \(RA\-LMC\) addresses both relaxations through robust estimation and a noise\-aware step size\. For all three methods, we establish non\-asymptotic convergence guarantees in Fisher information \(FI\) without assuming dissipativity or a functional inequality\. Synthetic experiments demonstrate FI convergence and the benefit of each mechanism\. Bayesian fine\-tuning experiments with LLMs and vision\-language models support our oracle model and show that RA\-LMC matches or outperforms state\-of\-the\-art baselines in accuracy and uncertainty quantification\.
