# AI for Engineers: A Beginner&\#x27;s Guide with Power System Examples and Real\-Data Case Studies

**Autores:** Muhammad Ayaz

**Data:** 2026-10-11 · **Tipo:** book

## Resumo

AI for Engineers: A Beginner’s Guide with Power System Examples and Real\-Data Case Studies is a first introduction to artificial intelligence for engineering students\. It assumes no background in AI, machine learning or programming\. Every idea is explained in plain language, first with an example from everyday life and then with an example from electrical power engineering, such as load forecasting, protection relays, transformer monitoring, smart meters and the inspection of power lines\. The book has eleven chapters\. Chapters 1 to 3 cover the foundations: what AI is, how machines learn, and how to work with data\. Chapters 4 to 7 cover the classical methods: linear regression, classification, decision trees and random forests, clustering and anomaly detection\. Chapters 8 and 9 introduce neural networks and their use with images and language\. Chapter 10 deals with the ethical and responsible use of AI\. Chapter 11 is a guide to the research literature on AI in power systems\. Each chapter states its main ideas as numbered equations with small worked examples, using only school\-level algebra\. Five chapters include a case study on real, publicly available data: the hourly load and temperature data of the Global Energy Forecasting Competition 2014\. The teaching examples elsewhere use illustrative numbers and are labelled as such\. The book also contains review questions with answers, a glossary and a reference list\. The Python code and data behind every figure and case\-study result are included with this record\. This book grew out of the lecture notes for the course COMP\-140 AI for Engineers\.
