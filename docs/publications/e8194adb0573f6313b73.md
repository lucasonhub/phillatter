# MULTIMODAL MARKERS OF DIGITAL IDENTITY: THE CONSTRUCTION OF THE SELF AND NEW MODELS OF COMMUNICATION

**Autores:** Аlla Varynska

**Data:** 2026-10-14 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

The article examines multimodal markers of digital identity as means of self\-presentation and self\-identification in the contemporary communicative environment\. Particular attention is paid to the phenomenon of the multimodal turn in digital communication, which has expanded traditional linguistic mechanisms of interaction through the integration of visual, graphic, and paralinguistic means\. The role of verbal, visual, and paralinguistic resources in the construction of the digital self and the formation of new models of online self\-presentation is analysed\. Special attention is devoted to professional, national, social, gender, and generational aspects of digital self\-identification\. It is demonstrated that digital identity is realised through a complex system of multimodal markers, including photographs, visual symbols, emojis, memes, reactions, intertextual practices, and other means of digital communication\. It is substantiated that the meaning of such markers is shaped by the communicative context as well as by users’ age, gender, cultural, professional, and social characteristics\. The study establishes that the contextual nature of multimodal markers gives rise to semiotic gaps between different generations, social groups, and digital communities, causing the same sign to acquire different interpretations across communicative environments\. Against this background, the article analyses the peculiarities of communication within the Human–AI model, the phenomenon of algorithmic empathy, and the mechanisms of linguistic adaptation employed by generative artificial intelligence systems to users’ communicative practices\. It is revealed that multimodal markers function not only as important instruments of identity representation but also as factors in the formation of new models of digital interaction\. The study concludes that the widespread adoption of generative artificial intelligence transforms not only the mechanisms of digital self\-identification but also the very foundations of mutual understanding in contemporary digital society\.
