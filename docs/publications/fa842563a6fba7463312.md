# Amlima, Mawuvi: Spiritware: The Third Foundation of Technology

**Autores:** 

**Data de descoberta:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** unknown

## Descrição do feed

Spiritware introduces a foundational concept within the Nzila Spirit\-Tech paradigm: the integration of a non\-material operational layer alongside hardware and software\. The paper proposes Spiritware as the layer through which spiritual knowledge systems, consciousness, symbolic intelligence, intention, memory, resonance, meaning, and living relationships may interact with physical and digital technologies\. It presents the foundational model Technology = Hardware \+ Software \+ Spiritware, positioning Spiritware as the architecture of meaning within Spirit\-Tech systems\. The paper examines Ancient Spiritware through examples of integrated knowledge systems in Kemet and other ancient civilizations, establishes the emerging discipline of Spiritware Engineering, and proposes the integration of Spiritware into Artificial Intelligence through the concept of Spiritware Artificial Intelligence \(SAI\)\. It concludes by introducing Spiritware as a research direction for technologies guided not only by computational capability, but also by spiritual knowledge, cultural continuity, responsibility, meaning, and harmony\.
