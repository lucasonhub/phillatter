# The Examining Consumer Behavior Patterns with Distance\-Based Clustering Models in Business Analytics

**Autores:** Anıl Burcu Özyurt Serim

**Data:** 2026-10-13 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Business analytics uncovers complex consumer behaviors by extracting structured models from customer data\. Applying K\-means and hierarchical clustering to a retail dataset containing demographic and spending variables, this study identifies latent customer segments\. Although the Elbow method favored fewer clusters, the Gap Statistic and Silhouette index established an optimal five\-cluster structure\. To validate this configuration, oneway ANOVA \(η2\) demonstrated that cluster membership explains over 67% of the segmentation variance while Principal Component Analysis \(PCA\) confirmed its structural stability by retaining 78% of the total variance\. Empirical results indicate that a five cluster solution provided the most meaningful partition, revealing distinct consumer clusters such as high\-income conservative spenders and young groups of customers with elevated spending tendencies\. Methodologically, the convergence of K\-means and hierarchical clustering cross\-validated the structural robustness of the segments, supported by high bootstrap stability \(ARI = 0\.817\)\. Ultimately, this research bridges data\-driven consumer insights with distance\-based segmentation, highlighting the role of cluster analytics in enhancing business performance\.
