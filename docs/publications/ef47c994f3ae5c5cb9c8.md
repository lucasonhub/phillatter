# Logical rationale and practical paths of digital\-intelligence empowerment for improving health education literacy of college physical education teachers

**Autores:** Jiangsong Chen

**Data:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

With the continuous advancement of educational digitalization and health education in colleges and universities, new requirements have been put forward for the professional literacy of college physical education teachers\. Based on relevant policy documents and existing studies, this paper analyzes the connotation, improvement logic, realistic adaptation and practical paths of digital\-intelligence empowerment for the health education literacy of college physical education teachers\. The study holds that this literacy can be understood from four dimensions: health knowledge integration, health information interpretation, health education transformation, and digital\-intelligence application and ethics\. Its improvement stems from the expansion of health education goals, the integration of physical education and health knowledge, and the collaboration between professional experience and data evidence\. At present, there is still room for further improvement in the embedding of health education roles, the connection between physical education and health knowledge, the educational transformation of health data, and relevant support mechanisms\. To this end, it is necessary to strengthen the professional role cognition oriented by health promotion, build a knowledge system integrating physical education and health, form a capability chain of &quot;data acquisition – information interpretation – educational decision\-making – teaching intervention – feedback adjustment&quot;, and improve the support mechanisms of training, teaching and research, interdisciplinary collaboration and evaluation\.
