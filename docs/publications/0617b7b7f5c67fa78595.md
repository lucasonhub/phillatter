# Johnson, Jamison: On Explaining \- Understanding What it is to be Human, From all Sides of the Equation

**Autores:** 

**Data de descoberta:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** unknown

## Descrição do feed

What happens when you are confidently right and then learn otherwise? This lyric\-philosophical essay takes that moment as its starting point and treats knowledge as something alive: what is certain one day can change the next\. It seats the twelve symbols of the author&\#x27;s coherent\-existence functional, each in the role it already holds in standing mathematics: Ψ for the gathering of perspectives, α and φ for intensity and facing, Θ for one perspective&\#x27;s angle to the gathering, π for cancellation and return, λ for the settled reading, τ for within\-time, the clock a life keeps on the inside, Δ for the difference that keeps the gathering changing, Ω for everyone present, Ξ for the total intensity that holds the gathering in proportion, and Λ for the floor beneath cancellation\. The gathering held in proportion has the shape of Kuramoto&\#x27;s order parameter for coupled oscillators, with an intensity weighting that is the framework&\#x27;s own\. The essay shows that intensity buys only a share of the average, never agreement past its ceiling, and that a loud voice turned crosswise bends the room rather than joining it; the per\-person reading carries no separate term for effort, which enters as weight\. It reads the golden ratio&\#x27;s continued fraction of ones against the Dirac comb as two shapes built from the same ones, offers one original structural claim, offered as a lens: that coherence defined against a target outlives it, if at all, only in infamy, while self\-referenced coherence can be carried forward freely, and closes on the borrowed lineage of every symbol\. The mathematics is used as form and lens, not asserted as a physics of mind\. \(direct link \)
