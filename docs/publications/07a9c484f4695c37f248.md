# Hybrid CNN–Random Forest Architecture with Residual Connections and Attention for Real\-Time Facial Emotion Recognition

**Autores:** P\. Penchala Prasad; Y\. Naga Purushotam; T\. Raju; V\. Lokesh Yadav

**Data:** 2026-10-09 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

The problem of facial emotion recognition \(FER\) is one of the primary subjects studied by the community of affective computing\. Its possible applications include many disciplines such as human\-computer interaction, diagnosis of mental disorders, and surveillance\. Within this work, a new model for emotion recognition using convolutional neural networks with residual connections, channel attention mechanism, and random forest classifier for recognition of seven emotion classes is suggested for use in the context of FER tasks\.
