## Descrição do feed

\_Zenodo\_ 1\. 2026This paper asks how governance changes once artificial intelligence becomes embedded not merely as a decision\-support tool but as an active component of scientific, economic, infrastructural, and institutional coordination\. Rather than treating governance as the exercise of authority by human agents over stable social systems, the paper analyzes governance as an operational configuration in which observation, prediction, resource allocation, scientific reachability, responsibility, and intervention are distributed across heterogeneous human and machine systems\. It introduces responsibility points as the minimal locations at which prior changes become coupled to downstream consequences in ways that alter subsequent possibilities, without presupposing that responsibility must be concentrated in a single human subject\. The paper further develops the concepts of scientific reachability and Xeno\-Capital to examine how AI\-mediated systems can reorganize which scientific problems, resources, interventions, and economic pathways become accessible or inaccessible\. Governance after AI therefore concerns not only algorithmic decision\-making but the changing architecture through which capacities, constraints, risks, and resources are generated and redistributed\. Xenoscience provides the broader framework for tracking these transformations across changing boundaries, scales, temporal windows, institutions, and feedback structures\. The paper concludes that governance under advanced AI should be analyzed as the reconfiguration of reachability and responsibility across coupled systems rather than as the simple transfer of human authority to machines\. \(direct link \)
