# PENGARUH CITA RASA, VARIASI RASA, DAN HARGA TERHADAP KEPUTUSAN PEMBELIAN KOPI KELILING JAGO DI JALAN GURU MUGHNI JAKARTA SELATAN

**Autores:** Mahrizal; Hidayat Syah; Wanda Yulia Utami; Mahrizal

**Data:** 2026-10-14 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

This study aims to determine the effect of taste, flavor variety, and price on purchasing decisions for Kopi Keliling Jago on Jalan Guru Mughni, South Jakarta\. This study employed a quantitative approach involving 95 respondents\. Data were collected through questionnaires and analyzed using multiple linear regression with the assistance of SPSS 25\. The results showed that, partially, taste had a positive and significant effect on purchasing decisions, with a regression coefficient of 0\.267, a t\-value of 2\.204, and a significance value of 0\.030\. Flavor variety also had a positive and significant effect, with a regression coefficient of 0\.449, a t\-value of 4\.411, and a significance value of 0\.000\. Price had a positive and significant effect, with a regression coefficient of 0\.267, a t\-value of 3\.212, and a significance value of 0\.003\. Simultaneously, taste, flavor variety, and price had a significant effect on purchasing decisions, with an F\-value of 16\.395 and a significance value of 0\.000\. Therefore, improving taste quality, providing greater flavor variety, and offering appropriate prices can increase consumers’ purchasing decisions for Kopi Keliling Jago\.
