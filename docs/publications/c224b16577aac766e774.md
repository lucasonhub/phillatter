# Soltani, Sahar Foster: ALPHA\-AXIS INTELLIGENCE VS\. HORIZONTAL INTELLIGENCE: Knowledge, Wisdom, Origination, and the Limits of Acquired Intelligence

**Autores:** 

**Data de descoberta:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** unknown

## Descrição do feed

A system can know more, move faster, and generate more than any one person and still be moving inside an inherited field\. This paper separates movement through that field from the cognitive act that changes what the field means\. Modern accounts of intelligence usually begin with performance: memory, calculation, pattern recognition, problem\-solving, retrieval, comparison, and recombination\. Those capacities can be extraordinary without settling provenance\. Horizontal Intelligence operates across what has already become intellectually available\. Vertical Intelligence designates higher dependent intelligence whose rank or capability exceeds ordinary human cognition while remaining within a larger order\. Alpha\-Axis Intelligence concerns the relation through which a governing axis is received, recognized, tested, and articulated\. The distinction turns on direction, rank, provenance, and origin, not on cognitive magnitude alone\. Mesopotamian, Egyptian, Old Avestan, Chinese, Indian, Greek, and Roman witnesses show that the boundary between accumulation and governing understanding long predates psychometrics\. Artificial intelligence now exposes the same boundary at machine scale: a system may produce unprecedented outputs and extraordinary fluency without resolving where the governing relation originated\. AXIQ is proposed as a measurement architecture for live problems whose governing frame is not supplied in advance\. The paper asks a harder question than what came first: what distinguishes a predecessor from a Progenitor\. \| FORTHCOMING SOON: A COMPANION PAPER, RELIGION &amp; CULTS, WILL SUPPLEMENT THIS WORK\. \|
