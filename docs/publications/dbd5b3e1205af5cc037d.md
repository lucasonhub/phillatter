# Interpretable machine learning identifies predictors of IGF\-1 during growth hormone therapy in boys

**Autores:** Helena‐Jamin Ly; Juulia T\. Suvilehto; Beatrice Skyman; Carina Ankarberg‐Lindgren; Hans Fors; Jesper Havsol; Jovanna Dahlgren

**Data:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Objectives Insulin\-like growth factor\-1 \(IGF\-1\) is widely used to guide growth hormone \(GH\) therapy, yet its predictors during the maintenance phase and puberty remain incompletely understood\. We aimed to predict IGF\-1 standard deviation scores \(SDS\) at 3 and 12 months in GH\-treated boys and to identify key predictors of IGF\-1 dynamics using interpretable modeling approaches\. Methods Data from 72 boys with growth hormone deficiency or idiopathic short stature from a previous Swedish clinical trial were analyzed using early\-life characteristics, GH treatment variables, and puberty\-related measures\. Linear regression, Elastic Net regression, and explainable boosting machine \(EBM\) models were evaluated with nested five\-fold patient\-grouped cross\-validation\. Performance estimates were patient\-balanced, with 95% confidence intervals based on 2,000 patient\-cluster bootstrap resamples\. Results Elastic Net had the highest point estimates at 3 months, but confidence intervals did not establish a difference between the primary models\. No clear differences between models were found at 12 months\. Current IGF\-1 SDS was the most prominent predictive feature, while additional treatment\- and puberty\-related variables were also selected across cross\-validation models\. Conclusion Interpretable modeling approaches provided moderate predictive performance for future IGF\-1 SDS during the maintenance phase and puberty\. The findings indicate that prediction is informed by current IGF\-1 SDS together with additional clinical variables, but further validation in independent cohorts is required before clinical application\.
