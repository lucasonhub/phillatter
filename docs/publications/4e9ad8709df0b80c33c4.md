# Innovative Technologies in Korean Language Teaching

**Autores:** Ataniyazova Muxabbat Muratbay qizi; Worldly Knowledge Publishing Centre

**Data:** 2026-10-11 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

In the modern world, innovative technologies are becoming an essential part of education\. They provide new opportunities for teachers and students, making the learning process more effective, interactive, and accessible\. Korean language teaching is also influenced by technological development\. Mobile applications, artificial intelligence, online platforms, virtual reality, and multimedia resources help learners improve their speaking, listening, reading, and writing skills\. These technologies make it possible to study Korean independently, communicate with native speakers, and explore Korean culture\. This article examines the role of innovative technologies in Korean language teaching, their advantages and challenges, and their influence on the development of modern language education\.
