# An enhanced YOLOv8\-pose network for joint image\-based tennis action classification and human pose estimation

**Autores:** Bai Zhen; Yijun Bai; Qiqi Liu; Cui Cui

**Data:** 2026-10-17 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Abstract This study addresses joint single\-frame player\-instance tennis action classification and human pose estimation from individual RGB images\. We propose an enhanced YOLOv8\-Pose network that jointly predicts player bounding boxes, action labels, and 17 human keypoints\. The network combines an enhanced StarNet backbone for efficient multi\-scale representation, a motion\-relevant attention module that emphasizes action\-discriminative body configurations visible in one image, and a composite pose loss for accurate keypoint localization\. On the primary four\-class Tennis Action dataset, the proposed model obtains Pose AP50 and AP50:95 values of 0\.975 and 0\.746, respectively, together with a matched\-instance action accuracy of 0\.990, matching coverage of 1\.000, 3\.08 million parameters, 8\.71 GFLOPs, and 51\.7 FPS on an NVIDIA GeForce RTX 5070 GPU\. To determine whether the pose\-estimation gain persists under a second independent training setting, the model is separately trained, validated, and tested on the two\-class tennis\-player\-pose dataset using its official split, where it achieves Pose AP50 and AP50:95 values of 0\.820 and 0\.549\. The results indicate that the proposed architecture maintains a favorable accuracy–efficiency trade\-off in both independently trained dataset settings\.
