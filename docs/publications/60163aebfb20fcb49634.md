# The minimal width of neural networks and immersions of real projective space

**Autores:** BaoZhi Chu; Ben Weinkove

**Data:** 2026-10-14 · **Tipo:** preprint

## Resumo

We investigate the minimal width of deep neural networks that can approximate all continuous functions from $\\mathbb\{R\}^n$ to $\\mathbb\{R\}^m$ in the topology of uniform convergence on compact subsets\. We show that whenever the real projective space $\\mathbb\{RP\}^\{n\-1\}$ admits a smooth immersion into $\\mathbb\{R\}^n$, the minimal width is at least $n\+\\min\\\{n,m\\\}$, assuming that the activation function is continuous and monotone\. As a consequence, the minimal width of ReLU networks is $n\+m$ whenever $n$ is equal to 2, 3, 4, 7 or 8 and $1\\le m \\le n$, extending results of Hanin\-Sellke and Hwang\. This suggests an intriguing connection between the approximation theory of neural networks and differential topology\.
