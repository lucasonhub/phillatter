# Ren, Songyao: Dispassion and the Good Life: A Study of Stoicism and Zhuangism

**Autores:** 

**Data de descoberta:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** unknown

## Descrição do feed

To the modern ear, &quot;dispassion&quot; carries a negative connotation and is often associated with apathy—a a lack of feeling, motivation, or concern—or an indifference to human affairs\. This book, however, argues that dispassion is not apathy, but remains relevant to contemporary debates about the good life\. Through a comparison of Stoicism and Zhuangism, it shows that dispassion offers emotional equanimity, enabling us to escape emotional upheaval without devolving into impassivity\. The Stoics propose maintaining equanimity by identifying with reason, the only thing impervious to the blows of fortune\. However, while this lifts us above worldly vicissitudes, it risks impoverishing life to the point that it may no longer be worth living\. By contrast, the Zhuangists advocate preserving equanimity by extending ourselves out over different parts of the volatile world in a responsive manner\. This allows us to take flourishing into our own hands while acknowledging our embeddedness in the world of change\. \(direct link \)
