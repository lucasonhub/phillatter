# Emerging Trends in Management and Organization: Strategic Adaptation in Volatile Global Markets

**Autores:** 

**Data:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** book

## Resumo

In the contemporary business environment, organisations are faced with a complex set of challenges, including geopolitical tensions, increasing sustainability regulations, rapid digital transformation, and the integration of artificial intelligence into various aspects of professional activities\. In such circumstances, the degree of competitive advantage is less dependent on fixed plans and more dependent on the capacity to adapt\. This edited volume brings together seven conceptual chapters that examine strategic adaptation across three levels of analysis\. The initial section of the text concerns itself with the subject of digital transformation in the context of uncertainty\. The text revisits universalist, contingency and structuralist perspectives, discussing how digital strategy helps international firms detect, respond to and recover from geopolitical risk\. It also analyses artificial intelligence as an organisational actor that reshapes power, authority and responsibility\. The second part of the study examines legitimacy and sustainability in global markets\. It asks when ESG functions as a source of competitive advantage or merely as a market entry requirement\. It also explores how women\-focused diversity policies can decouple into pinkwashing\. The third part of the text focuses on human\-based capabilities, establishing a link between organisational emotional memory and the concepts of learning and sustainability\. The text also explains how social intelligence can become a strategic advantage through the medium of intrapreneurship\. The chapters draw on institutional theory, the resource\-based view and dynamic capabilities to argue that adaptation is not a reactive adjustment, but rather a strategic capability built through structures, people and legitimacy\. The book offers integrative frameworks for researchers, graduate students and managers seeking to understand management and organisation under uncertainty\.
