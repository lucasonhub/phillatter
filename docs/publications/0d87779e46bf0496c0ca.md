# Advanced visualization workflows for gyrokinetics simulation data

**Autores:** Rui Costa; William Hornsby; Daniel Kennedy; Lorenzo Zanisi; Nitesh Bhatia; Stanislas J\. P\. Pamela; Alejandra N Gonzalez\-Beltran; R\. Akers

**Data:** 2026-10-12 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Gyrokinetic simulations are central to the study of turbulent transport in magnetized fusion plasmas, but their output remains difficult to visualize because the data are complex and must be transformed into real space\. This has limited the routine use of advanced three\-dimensional visualization in gyrokinetic analysis\. We present reproducible workflows for visualizing gyrokinetic simulation data, with a focus on post\-processed quantities in three\-dimensional physical space\. These workflows are designed for use on high\-performance computing systems and support a myriad of gyrokinetic codes including GKW, GyroSwin, GENE, CGYRO, and other workflows based on Pyrokinetics\. In the proposed workflows, the magnetic\-field\-aligned data are transformed into real space and written as Visualization Toolkit \(VTK\) datasets\. The intermediary Visualization Toolkit datasets provide flexibility and versatility to target different visualization platforms \(e\.g\., ParaView, Blender, NVIDIA Omniverse\) suited to different needs and end users\. This flexibility allows for both single\-field and multivariate views of potential, density, and temperature fluctuations, and enables the data to be shown within tokamak geometry\. The workflows proposed in this work provide a practical basis for further visualization work in gyrokinetics\.
