# Risguardi: la lettura relazionale come pratica incarnata e distribuita

**Autores:** Chiara Tullio

**Data:** 2026-10-11 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

This paper offers a theoretical and pedagogical reflection on relational reading as an embodied and distributed experience, emerging through the cooperation of plural narrating bodies\. Drawing on the perspective of embodied education \(Damiani, 2024\), cognitive narratology \(Bernini &amp; Caracciolo, 2013; Caracciolo &amp; Kukkonen, 2021\), and Bateson&\#x27;s \(1972\) ecology of mind, reading is conceptualized as a relational practice in which meaning emerges through the interaction of bodies, languages, cultural artefacts, and contexts\. Engaging with Mauss&\#x27;s \(1934\) notion of techniques of the body, the paper introduces the concept of plural narrating bodies, understood not simply as the coexistence of different subjects but as the plurality of expressive modes through which narration takes shape\. From this perspective, relational reading extends beyond accessibility, becoming a pedagogical device that makes the embodied nature of narrative participation visible\. The paper presents Risguardi, a pedagogical device developed within the Includere leggendo project, as a context in which this perspective is explored\. It argues that the plurality of narrating bodies should not be understood as a variable of the narrative experience but as a pedagogical category through which narrative relationships can be rethought and educational devices designed to transform imaginaries of participation and citizenship\.
