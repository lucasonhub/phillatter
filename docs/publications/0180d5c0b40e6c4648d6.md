# From Awareness to AI Readiness: Artificial Intelligence Literacy, Gendered Barriers, and Future Opportunities For Women in Higher Education

**Autores:** Ikhlas Ahmad; Mohammad Zafar Ayat; Rabiya Khatoon; Alok Gardia

**Data:** 2026-10-10 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo
