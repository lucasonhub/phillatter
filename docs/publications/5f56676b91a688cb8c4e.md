# 권기원\_통합연구논문\_2026\-10\-08

**Autores:** Gi Won Kwon

**Data:** 2026-10-09 · **Tipo:** dissertation

## Resumo

This conceptual and design\-oriented study explores how artificial intelligence can support a context\-sensitive understanding of human emotions, dispositions, intentions, circumstances, and processes of awareness\. Drawing on personal reflection and AI system development, the author proposes a five\-dimensional framework for human understanding and the concept of Jeoksim \(適心\), or context\-sensitive selection of interpersonal attitudes\. The study emphasizes evidence\-based judgment, counterevidence, and iterative reassessment\. It does not claim that AI possesses human\-like feelings or consciousness\. The proposed framework remains preliminary and requires future empirical validation\.
