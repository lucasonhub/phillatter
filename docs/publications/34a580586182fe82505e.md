# Statistical Downscaling of Daily Precipitation from ERA5 Atmospheric Fields to IMERG over West Africa

**Autores:** David Sagna; Redouane Lguensat; Samo Diatta

**Data:** 2026-10-12 · **Tipo:** preprint

## Resumo

Global reanalyses are too coarse to resolve the convective systems that produce most West African monsoon rainfall\. In this work, we use U\-Net based architectures to downscale daily ERA5 fields \(0\.25°\) to IMERG V07 satellite precipitation \(0\.1°\) over West Africa for the June to October seasons of 2000 to 2024, using geopotential, temperature, humidity and wind at three pressure levels together with elevation and land fraction\. Adding ERA5 precipitation as input slightly improves performance for this particular challenging region\. We find that all the deterministic networks investigated for this work are too dry and lose almost all observed variance below a few hundred kilometers, which motivated our use of a GAN\-based alternative\. We also investigated permutation importance for the U\-Net that ranks 500 hPa specific humidity first, consistent with the sensitivity of deep convection to mid\-tropospheric moisture\.
