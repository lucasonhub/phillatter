# Digital mental health: Foundations, evidence\-based interventions, artificial intelligence, research methods, and ethical practice\.

**Autores:** Henrique Pereira

**Data:** 2026-10-13 · **Tipo:** book

## Resumo

Digital Mental Health: Foundations, Evidence\-Based Interventions, Artificial Intelligence, Research Methods, and Ethical Practice, by Henrique Pereira \(2026\), is a comprehensive academic manual exploring the rapidly evolving intersection of psychology, mental healthcare, and digital technologies\. Organised into 22 chapters across seven thematic parts, the book critically examines evidence\-based digital psychological interventions, telepsychology, mobile health applications, artificial intelligence, digital phenotyping, and innovative research methodologies\. Particular attention is given to clinical effectiveness, ethical challenges, data privacy, digital inclusion, and the responsible integration of emerging technologies into mental healthcare\. Combining scientific evidence, theoretical frameworks, practical case studies, and critical perspectives, this volume provides a valuable educational resource for postgraduate students, researchers, psychologists, mental health professionals, and policymakers seeking to understand, evaluate, and advance digital mental health in an increasingly technology\-driven world\.
