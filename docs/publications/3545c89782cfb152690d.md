# Multimodal Evaluation of Ouret lanata \(L\.\) Kuntze against Scopolamine\-induced Alzheimer’s\-like Neurocognitive dysfunction in Wistar Albino Rats

**Autores:** A\. Thanga Thirupathi; A\. Shenbagavel; J\. Boopathy Rajakumar; J\. Ram Ganesh; M\. Bhavithra

**Data:** 2026-10-17 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Alzheimer’s disease \(AD\) is a progressive neurodegenerative disorder associated with cognitive decline, cholinergic dysfunction, oxidative stress, and neuronal damage\. The present study evaluated the neuroprotective and anti\-amnesic potential of Ouret lanata \(L\.\) Kuntze stem extracts against scopolamine hydrobromide\-induced AD\-like neurocognitive dysfunction in Wistar albino rats\. Petroleum ether, chloroform, ethanolic, aqueous, and total aqueous extracts were prepared and subjected to physicochemical, phytochemical, TLC, FTIR, and LC\-MS analyses\. In vitro acetylcholinesterase \(AChE\) inhibitory and DPPH radical\-scavenging activities were assessed\. Acute oral toxicity of the total aqueous extract was evaluated according to OECD 423\. Cognitive and behavioral functions were assessed using the Morris water maze, T\-maze, Y\-maze, novel object recognition, open\-field, and locomotor activity tests\. Brain AChE, lipid peroxidation, superoxide dismutase, and reduced glutathione were estimated, followed by hippocampal histopathological examination\. The total aqueous extract showed the highest extraction yield \(3\.48%\), while LC\-MS analysis identified several bioactive constituents in the ethanolic extract, including naringenin, catechin, acacetin, an isorhamnetin derivative, a kaempferol\-derived conjugate, and a rutin\-type glycoside\. The ethanolic extract exhibited strong AChE inhibition \(87% at 400 µg/mL; IC₅₀ 33\.04 µg/mL\), while ethanolic, aqueous, and total aqueous extracts showed antioxidant activity\. Treatment improved cognitive and behavioral performance and attenuated scopolamine\-induced alterations in AChE, lipid peroxidation, SOD, and GSH levels\. Histopathology further supported neuronal preservation\. Overall, Ouret lanata \(L\.\) Kuntze stem extracts, particularly the ethanolic and total aqueous extracts, demonstrated promising anti\-amnesic, antioxidant, and neuroprotective effects\.
