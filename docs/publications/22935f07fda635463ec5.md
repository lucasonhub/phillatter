# Hindsight\-Privileged RLVR: Single\-Rollout Token Credit Without Group Sampling

**Autores:** Wei Chen; Yubing Wu; Zhou Xu; Haoyue Zhang; Delu Zeng; Jiahao Wang; Junmei Yang; Pan tiewen; Jingfeng Liu; Zhiheng Zhou; Bo Li; Min Chen

**Data:** 2026-10-14 · **Tipo:** preprint

## Resumo

Reinforcement learning with verifiable rewards \(RLVR\) trains language models from checked task outcomes, but assigning a terminal reward to individual reasoning tokens or actions remains difficult\. Group\-relative methods such as Group Relative Policy Optimization \(GRPO\) sample several attempts per prompt, but lose their reward\-based learning signal when all attempts receive the same outcome\. We propose Hindsight\-Privileged RLVR \(HP\-RLVR\), a single\-rollout method that turns a completed trajectory&\#x27;s own future into token\-level update weights\. The generating policy snapshot scores each eligible token under its causal history and under a hindsight context containing the realized future and verifier feedback\. A normalized Jensen\-\-Shannon divergence between the two views weights the reward relative to a historical baseline, yielding bounded token credit for both successful and failed attempts, with no reference solution or learned critic\. In paired Qwen3\-8B runs over a shared $100$\-step schedule, HP\-RLVR trains $1\.21\\times$, $1\.46\\times$, and $2\.30\\times$ faster than GRPO on mathematics, code generation, and ALFWorld, using one eighth the rollouts per prompt\. Accuracy is comparable overall, with HP\-RLVR ahead on mathematics at all three sizes and GRPO ahead on code and ALFWorld\. In ALFWorld, $43\.2\\%$ \(4B\) and $49\.0\\%$ \(8B\) of GRPO&\#x27;s gradient\-norm records are zero, versus none for HP\-RLVR\.
