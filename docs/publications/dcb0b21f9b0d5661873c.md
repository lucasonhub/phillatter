# Evaluation of Mechanical and Durability Properties of Polypropylene\-Basalt Hybrid Fibre\-Reinforced Concrete

**Autores:** Ram Reddy; Jayarami Reddy

**Data:** 2026-10-12 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Hybrid fibre\-reinforced concrete is an effective approach for improving the mechanical performance, deformation capacity, and durability of concrete by combining fibres with complementary mechanical characteristics\. Basalt fibres provide high stiffness and tensile strength, whereas polypropylene fibres contribute to crack control and post\-cracking deformation\. However, the combined influence of total hybrid fibre volume and fibre proportion on the mechanical and durability performance of polypropylene\-basalt hybrid fibre\-reinforced concrete requires further investigation\. Therefore, this study experimentally evaluates the mechanical and durability properties of M40\-grade polypropylene\-basalt hybrid fibre\-reinforced concrete with different total fibre volume fractions and fibre proportions\. Twelve hybrid mixtures were prepared by varying the total fibre volume from 0\.25% to 1\.0% and the polypropylene\-to\-basalt fibre ratio as 25:75, 50:50, and 75:25, along with a plain concrete control mix\. Compressive and flexural strengths were evaluated at 3, 7, 28, and 90 days, while splitting tensile strength and water absorption were assessed at 28 days\. Compressive stress\-strain behaviour and deformation characteristics were also examined\. The results showed that compressive strength was most effective at low to moderate fibre contents of 0\.25\-0\.50%, particularly in basalt\-rich mixtures, whereas higher fibre contents reduced compressive strength because of fibre interaction, reduced workability, and dispersion limitations\. Flexural strength and deformation capacity improved substantially with hybrid fibre incorporation, with basalt\-rich mixtures providing higher peak strength and polypropylene fibres contributing to post\-cracking deformation\. The 28\-day splitting tensile strength increased by nearly 30% at 0\.75% total fibre volume in the basalt\-rich mixture\. Water absorption progressively decreased with increasing fibre content, reaching a minimum of 1\.07% at 1\.0% total fibre volume in the basalt\-rich mixture\. Overall, polypropylene\-basalt hybridisation effectively balances strength, ductility, tensile resistance, and durability, with the optimum fibre content depending on the targeted performance requirements\.
