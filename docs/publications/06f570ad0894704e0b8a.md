# Generation Z and Digital Identity: The Relationship Between Online and Offline Personalities

**Autores:** Khushvinder Kaur

**Data:** 2026-10-11 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Abstract Generation Z has grown up in a rapidly transforming digital environment where smartphones, social media platforms, online communities, artificial intelligence and digital communication have become integral parts of everyday life\. For this generation, identity is no longer formed only through family, educational institutions, friendship groups and physical communities\. Digital platforms have created additional spaces where young people express their personalities, establish relationships, seek recognition, share experiences and construct images of themselves\. As a result, digital identity has become an important dimension of contemporary youth identity\. The present study examines the relationship between the online and offline personalities of Generation Z\. It focuses on digital identity, social media\-based self\-presentation, profile construction, photographs, digital content, social approval, self\-confidence, privacy, influencers, social relationships, emotional experiences, personal branding and career opportunities\. The study also considers the emerging influence of artificial intelligence on the construction and management of digital identity\.
