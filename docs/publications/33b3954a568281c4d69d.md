# Effect of Strategic Human Resource Planning on Organizational Performance in Rwanda: A Case of RwandAir

**Autores:** 

**Data:** 2026-10-11 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

This study assessed the effect of strategic human resource planning on organizational performance at RwandAir in Kigali, Rwanda\.It used a descriptive and correlational design\.The target population was 1,325 employees, from whom 307 respondents were determined with the Yamane formula and selected purposively\.Data were collected with a questionnaire, an interview guide and documentary review, and analysed in SPSS version 26 using means, standard deviations, Pearson correlation and multiple regression\.The four planning practices jointly explained 43\.2% of the variance in performance \(R = \.657,R² = \.432\)\.Predictive employee training \(B = \.451,p &lt; \.001\),predictive workforce forecasting \(B = \.428,p = \.001\),strategic talent sourcing \(B = \.306,p = \.006\)and performance\-linked employee retention \(B = \.376,p = \.028\)each had a positive, statistically significant effect, although retention showed no significant bivariate correlation with performance\.The study concluded that strategic human resource planning contributes to the airline&\#x27;s performance\.It recommends that RwandAir invest in workforce analytics, build an employer brand and partnerships with aviation training institutions, adopt competency\-based training, and tie rewards and career paths to measured performance\.
