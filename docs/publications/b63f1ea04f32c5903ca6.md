# Sustainable Water and Environmental Technologies of Global Relevance

**Autores:** 

**Data:** 2026-10-13 · **Tipo:** book

## Resumo

The following reprint focuses on Sustainable Water and Environmental Technologies of Global Relevance to promote promising solutions to complex international challenges\. The highlighted solutions are of evident international relevance and advance at least one United Nations Development Goal, such as clean water and sanitation, affordable and clean energy, sustainable cities and communities, and climate action\. This reprint provides an advanced forum for the latest knowledge in technological studies to both researchers and professionals in water and environmental technologies\. Targeted technologies relate to monitoring, conservation, and modelling\-related advances associated with the Internet of Things, neural networks, and machine learning\. This reprint publishes papers with global relevance and a clear soft and/or hard technology focus\.
