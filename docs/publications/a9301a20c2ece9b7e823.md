# RAHBAR–XODIM MUNOSABATLARIDA O‘ZARO ISHONCH SHAKLLANISHINING IJTIMOIY\-PSIXOLOGIK MEXANIZMLARI

**Autores:** A\. S\. \(Azamat\) Nazarov

**Data:** 2026-10-10 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

This theoretical and analytical article examines how mutual trust develops in leader–employee relationships\. Trust is understood as the parties’ willingness to rely on each other in professional interaction rather than as a general favorable judgment of a leader’s personality\. The author’s publications on management psychology and decision\-making provide the local scholarly context, while international research supports distinctions between trust, trustworthiness, and psychological safety\. A conceptual framework links the professional situation, interpretation of the other party’s behavior, assessment of interpersonal risk, and revision of accumulated relational experience\. The analysis explains why employees’ trust in leaders and leaders’ trust in employees differ in content despite their reciprocal nature\. Practical recommendations address feedback, fair evaluation, explicit delegation arrangements, and constructive discussion of mistakes\. The proposed framework is a theoretical contribution that requires further empirical testing\.
