# EGFR\-TKI\-associated colitis in non\-small cell lung cancer: a retrospective characterization of clinical, radiographic, endoscopic, and histopathologic features

**Autores:** Shujun Wang; Chen Tan; Pufang Shi; Ya Li; Hefeng Zhan; Jiajing Li

**Data:** 2026-10-12 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Objective Epidermal growth factor receptor tyrosine kinase inhibitors \(EGFR\-TKIs\) are standard targeted therapies for EGFR\-mutant non\-small cell lung cancer \(NSCLC\), but clinically documented EGFR\-TKI\-associated colitis remains incompletely characterized\. We aimed to describe its clinical, radiographic, endoscopic, histopathologic, management, and exploratory survival features\. Methods We conducted a single\-center retrospective case\-control analysis nested within an institutional cohort of 3,285 patients with NSCLC treated with EGFR\-TKIs between January 2018 and July 2025\. The analysis included 43 patients with documented radiographic and/or endoscopic colitis and 223 selected asymptomatic controls\. Clinical, imaging, colonoscopic, histopathologic, treatment, and survival data were reviewed\. Ever\-smoking was examined in an exploratory univariable logistic analysis\. Drug\-specific EGFR\-TKI odds were not estimated because a common time\-aligned exposure definition for cases and controls could not be reconstructed, and overall survival was examined exploratorily\. Results Clinically documented EGFR\-TKI\-associated colitis occurred in 43/3,285 treated patients \(1\.3%\)\. Ever\-smoking was more frequent among cases than selected controls \(39\.5% vs\. 20\.2%; univariable odds ratio = 2\.586, 95% confidence interval: 1\.293–5\.173, P = 0\.007\)\. Drug\-specific odds were not estimated because a common time\-aligned exposure definition could not be reconstructed\. Median symptom onset was 3 months\. Diarrhea occurred in 40/43 patients \(93\.0%\); three patients had grade 0 diarrhea\. Among 39 patients undergoing colonoscopy, 14 had normal\-appearing mucosa; 11 of these patients underwent biopsy and showed histologic inflammation\. Of 35 classifiable biopsies, 10 \(28\.6%\) showed a microscopic pattern, 14 \(40\.0%\) an inflammatory/IBD\-like pattern, and 11 \(31\.4%\) an ischemic\-like pattern\. In exploratory survival analysis, no statistically significant association between documented colitis and overall survival was detected \(log\-rank P = 0\.538; adjusted HR = 1\.184, 95% CI: 0\.782–1\.793, P = 0\.426\)\. Conclusion Clinically documented EGFR\-TKI\-associated colitis showed heterogeneous radiographic, endoscopic, and histopathologic patterns\. Normal\-appearing mucosa did not exclude histologic inflammation in biopsied patients\. The 1\.3% value represents documented cases in this institutional cohort rather than population incidence\. The recorded smoking difference, severity\-stratified findings, treatment outcomes, and survival estimates should be interpreted as exploratory; no drug\-specific colitis association is inferred\.
