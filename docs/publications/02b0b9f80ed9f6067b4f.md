# Mortalidad infantil y residuos sólidos en los cantones del Guayas, Ecuador \(2016\-2023\)

**Autores:** Cinthya Succety Patiño Rojas; Carmen Graciela Urgilés Carabajo

**Data:** 2026-10-12 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

The study analyzes the socio\-environmental and economic determinants of infant mortality in the 25 cantons of the province of Guayas between 2016 and 2023\. The research is based on the persistence of territorial inequalities in child health associated with solid waste management and local economic capacity\. The study uses a longitudinal ecological design based on panel data for the cantons, combining descriptive statistics, thematic mapping, and the estimation of a fixed\-effects model with robust standard errors grouped by unit\. The model specification was tested using heteroscedasticity, autocorrelation, and cross\-sectional dependence tests to ensure the consistency of the estimates\. The results show that the rate of solid waste per capita is positively and significantly linked to income\-related infant mortality, while cantonal GDP shows a similarly significant negative relationship\. Taken together, these associations suggest that children&\#x27;s health is determined both by exposure to degraded environments and by the economic capacity of the territories\. Hence, the priority is to strengthen waste management, investment in sanitation, and institutional capacities to reduce health gaps between cantons\. It is also proposed that future research incorporate microenvironmental variables and spatial indicators to better understand how environment, territory, and child health are intertwined\.
