# Design and Implementation of a Smart Control Panel Using Cloud Computing for Real\-Time Electrical and Environmental Monitoring

**Autores:** Ms\. Amisha Moon Dr\. Rashmi A\. Keswani; Mr\. Om Guru Ms\. Khushi Bhongade; Chaitanya Futane Mr\. Rahul Trivedi

**Data:** 2026-10-13 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

In the pursuit of efficient and reliable operation of electrical systems, this paper presents the design and implementation of a cloud\-connected smart control panel for real\-time monitoring and remote control of electrical devices in a poultry\-farm application\. The proposed system enables continuous monitoring of electrical and environmental parameters, including voltage, current, power, temperature, humidity, and vibration, with the acquired data transmitted to a cloud server for remote observation\. In addition, the system facilitates remote control of connected electrical devices through relay\- and contactor\-based switching\. The proposed control panel integrates real\-time monitoring and remote load control within a unified platform, providing centralized access to the operating conditions and connected devices of the electrical system\.
