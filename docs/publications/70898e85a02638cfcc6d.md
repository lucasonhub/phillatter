# HEALTHCARE AI

**Autores:** Tanuja Sakhare

**Data:** 2026-10-18 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Artificial Intelligence \(AI\) is increasingly being explored in healthcare to support access to information, patient engagement, administrativeefficiency, and selected clinical workflows\. This paper proposes a Healthcare AI framework that organizes healthcare\-related requests andprovides structured, understandable information through a digital interface\. The framework combines a user interface, request classification,retrieval from approved healthcare information sources, privacy and safety checks, and a response\-generation layer\. It can support generalhealth education, service navigation, and appointment\-related guidance where authorized services are available\. The system is designed tocommunicate uncertainty, protect sensitive information, and direct users to qualified professionals when a request is outside its scope\. It is notintended to diagnose conditions, prescribe treatment, or replace clinical judgement\. The proposed design is modular and can be evaluatedthrough documented software, usability, source\-quality, privacy, and safety tests
