# Efflux modulation as an antimicrobial strategy: mechanisms and translational perspectives

**Autores:** Waleska Stephanie da Cruz Nizer; Cesar Augusto Roque‐Borda; Chongbing Liao; Heitor Leocádio de Souza Rodrigues; Subham Kumar Vishwakarma; Zidane Qriouet; Andréía Bagliotti Meneguin; Fernando Rogerio Pavan

**Data:** 2026-10-12 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Efflux pumps are widely viewed as antibiotic resistance determinants, yet their roles in stress adaptation, virulence, quorum sensing, and biofilm formation make them context\-dependent therapeutic targets\. This Perspective distinguishes four efflux intervention strategies: direct inhibition, bioenergetic or membrane\-mediated suppression, regulatory modulation, and dual\-effect approaches\. We examine why promising in vitro findings rarely translate clinically and argue that efflux\-targeting strategies require defined therapeutic objectives and integrated multidisciplinary approaches\.
