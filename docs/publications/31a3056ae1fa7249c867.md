# O‘SPIRINLIK DAVRIDA KOMMUNIKATIV KOMPETENSIYALARNI RIVOJLANTIRISHNING PEDAGOGIK\-PSIXOLOGIK ASOSLARI

**Autores:** MURODBEK QO&\#x27;ZIBOY O&\#x27;G&\#x27;LI QURBONOV

**Data:** 2026-10-12 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

This article analyzes the pedagogical and psychological foundations for developing communicative competencies during adolescence\. Factors influencing the processes of interpersonal communication, communicative activity, and social adaptation of adolescent students are scientifically highlighted\. Additionally, the importance of interactive methods, collaborative educational technologies, and psychological training in the formation of communicative competencies in the educational process is substantiated\.
