# \(RE\)CREATING GENDER THROUGH HYBRID TRANSLATION PRACTICES

**Autores:** Andrei MOHANU

**Data:** 2026-10-13 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

This paper examines how artificial intelligence\-mediated and human translation practices co\-construct gendered meaning within digital environments\. Approaching translation as an active reconfiguration of linguistic and multimodal cues, the study contrasts the tendency of computational systems to reproduce normative gender binaries with the human capacity to negotiate contextual nuance\. Through an empirical analysis of English\-to\-Romanian translations in the beauty sector, the research exposes the ethical and cognitive implications of automated workflows, particularly the amplification of embedded cultural biases\. Integrating sociocultural semiotics and algorithmic auditing, the paper proposes a collaborative model of translation\. This framework leverages the efficiency of neural machine translation while relying on practitioner reflexivity to mitigate systemic distortions\. Ultimately, human expertise remains an essential mechanism for guiding automated systems toward culturally responsible representations of identity in automated translational landscape\.
