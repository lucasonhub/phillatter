# LangNCO: Compiling Natural\-Language Constraints into Inference\-Time Interventions for Neural Combinatorial Optimization

**Autores:** Hironori Ohigashi

**Data:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** preprint

## Resumo

Neural combinatorial optimization \(NCO\) rapidly produces high\-quality solutions for problem structures encountered during training, but operational requirements may change after deployment and be communicated in natural language\. We propose LangNCO, a framework that compiles instance\-specific requirements into structured inference\-time interventions for a frozen NCO solver, without changing the pretrained policy&\#x27;s architecture, learned input representation, or parameters\. A pretrained LLM serves as a replaceable semantic front end, translating each requirement into a schema\-validated domain\-specific language \(DSL\) rule set\. A deterministic Rule Executor applies the rules as nonexpansive hard action masks\. Because local masking prevents immediate violations but does not ensure that a complete feasible continuation remains reachable, Critical\-Offset Decoding extracts rule\-referenced critical nodes and reallocates part of a fixed rollout budget to multi\-strength biased rollouts\. Using controlled task\-specific language to isolate solver adaptation from open\-ended language robustness, we evaluate a CVRP policy on road closures, time windows, vehicle assignments, visit\-order requirements, all pairwise combinations, and twelve additional constraint types\. LangNCO achieves 100% feasibility on the four primary datasets with mean reference gaps of 1\.63\-\-3\.26%, 97\.33\-\-100% feasibility when separately generated rules are composed for pairwise constraints, and 98\.67% feasibility across the additional constraints\. These results demonstrate modular post\-training adaptation of a frozen NCO solver through inspectable language\-derived rules, without retraining\.
