# Pharmacogenomics in the era of precision healthcare

**Autores:** Shiva Mishra

**Data:** 2026-10-12 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Pharmacogenomics, the study of how genomic variations influence drug response has emerged as a cornerstone of precision medicine\. Interindividual differences in drug efficacy and toxicity are often attributable to genetic variations affecting drug\-metabolizing enyzmes, transporters, and therapeutic targets\. Advances in genomic technologies, including next\-generation sequencing, and genome\-wide association studies have enabled the identification of genetic biomarkers that can guide individualized drug therapy\. Pharmacogenomic testing has shown significant clinical utility in various therapeutic areas, including oncology, cardiology, psychiatry and infectious diseases, by optimizing drug selection, dosing, and minimizing adverse drug reactions\. Despite its promising potential, the widespread implementation of pharmacogenomics faces challenges related to cost, accessibility, ethical considerations and integration into routine clinical practice\.The integration of pharmacogenomic information into healthcare has the potential to enhance therapeutic outcomes, improve patient safety, and facilitates the transition from a one\-size\-fits all approach to truly personalized medicine\.
