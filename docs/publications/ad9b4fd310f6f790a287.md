## Resumo

Nigeria has 350 government schools where a single teacher taught math to five different skill levels at once — until an AI tutor helped push the district&\#x27;s pass rate to 96\.4 percent in six weeks\. A radiologist in Scotland caught a six\-millimeter tumor that human eyes alone had missed\. A smallholder farmer in Kenya saved his maize crop by pointing his phone at a leaf\. None of these are science fiction\. They happened in the last three years\.THE AI AGE is the book for anyone who wants to understand what artificial intelligence is actually doing to the world right now not the hype, not the panic, but the real, documented transformation already underway across medicine, education, work, government, and daily life\.Shehu Ibrahim traces the full arc: from the ancient dream of thinking machines, through decades of AI winters and quiet breakthroughs, to the sudden acceleration that produced ChatGPT, AlphaFold, and a global race for AI supremacy\. Then he turns to the questions that matter most for the next fifty years — who wins, who is left behind, and what humanity owes itself as it builds something smarter than any tool it has ever made\.This is not a Silicon Valley book\. It is a genuinely global one — grounded as much in Kenyan farms, Rajasthani classrooms, and Nigerian newsrooms as in California labs, written by an author whose own path from rural northern Nigeria into technology mirrors the book&\#x27;s central argument: that this technology&\#x27;s benefits must reach everyone, not just those already closest to power\.Inside, you&\#x27;ll find:• The real history of AI, from Ada Lovelace&\#x27;s first program to the deep learning breakthrough of 2012• How AI is already reshaping healthcare, education, work, business, and government — with real numbers and real case studies• A clear\-eyed account of the global AI race between the US, China, and the rest of the world• Africa&\#x27;s emerging role in the AI revolution — leapfrogging opportunities most global coverage ignores• An honest reckoning with AI&\#x27;s risks: job disruption, inequality, misinformation, and long\-horizon questions• What AI means for human identity, creativity, and authorship• Two grounded scenarios for the world in 2050 and 2100• A final chapter on the choice still in front of usWritten for citizens, not specialists, THE AI AGE is a comprehensive, research\-driven guide to the most consequential technological shift of our time\.If you want to understand artificial intelligence&\#x27;s history, its present reach, and its stakes for your own future, This is the BOOK \!\!\!\.
