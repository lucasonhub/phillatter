# AgriEase: One Click Solution for Farmers

**Autores:** Arti Zade; Aadi Nagpure; Lakshmi Soni

**Data:** 2026-10-14 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Farmers often face difficulties in finding and purchasing essential farming equipment and agricultural products such as seeds, fertilizers, tractors, pesticides, and tractor accessories\. They may have to visit multiple shops to compare prices, check product availability, and find suitable equipment\. This process takes time, increases transportation costs, and makes it difficult for farmers to find affordable and quality products\. The proposed project aims to develop an online platform that helps farmers easily search, compare, and purchase agricultural products and equipment in one place\. It will provide product details, prices, availability, and easy access to Essen The methodology of the AgriEase project involves designing and developing a user\-friendly web\-based platform that provides farmers with easy access to agricultural products and useful farming information\. The project follows a systematic approach, including requirement analysis, system design, website development, database management, and testing\. AgriEase aims to provide a convenient digital platform that connects farmers with essential agricultural products and information, helping reduce the effort required to access farming resources
