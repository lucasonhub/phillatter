# ENHANCING COMMUNITY ENTREPRENEURSHIP COMPETITIVENESS: THE EVOLUTION TO THE AI\-INTEGRATED SPEED\-X MODEL

**Autores:** Khatawut Sangkhamart

**Data:** 2026-10-13 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

This study empirically evaluates the efficacy of the SPEED Model strategy—comprising Social networking, Perception, Extra value, Excitement, and Design—in strengthening community entrepreneurship and business competitiveness\. Utilizing a mixed\-methods quasi\-experimental design, empirical data were gathered from 50 targeted community entrepreneurs in Pak Kret Municipality, Thailand\. Quantitative analysis using paired\-samples t\-tests revealed that entrepreneurial capabilities improved significantly post\-implementation, particularly in the critical domains of strategic business and risk management\. Furthermore, qualitative insights derived from intensive focus group discussions strongly validated these statistical enhancements but simultaneously uncovered a crucial necessity for rapid digital adaptation to sustain long\-term market relevance\. Consequently, this study proposes an evolved strategic framework, the &quot;SPEED\-X Model,&quot; which fundamentally integrates Artificial Intelligence \(AI\) to augment the original five dimensions\. Ultimately, the findings provide a robust, actionable roadmap for policymakers and community enterprises, demonstrating that systematically combining foundational strategic models with modern AI technologies is essential for achieving sustainable economic competitiveness\.
