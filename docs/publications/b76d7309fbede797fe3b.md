# Construction and Evaluation of a Ketosis Prediction Model Based on Milk MIR Spectra and Feature Wavenumber Selection in Dairy Cows

**Autores:** Xiaoli Ren; Zhuo Yang; Aote Han; Yikai Fan; Yi Zhang; Shaohu Chen; Zhen Zhang; Lei Yan; Zheng Cao; Wenjiao Liu; Xiaofeng Huang; Dongwei Wang; 张桂香; Qingxia Yan; Shujun Zhang

**Data:** 2026-10-12 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Ketosis is a common metabolic disorder in dairy cows and occurs mainly during early lactation\. Blood β\-hydroxybutyrate \(BHB\) is widely used for diagnosis, but large\-scale testing is limited by sampling requirements and cost\. We therefore studied lactating Holstein cows from 12 large\-scale dairy farms in central China\. Milk samples, milk composition traits, and milk mid\-infrared \(MIR\) spectra were collected through the Dairy Herd Improvement \(DHI\) testing system from April 2019 to December 2022, and ketosis diagnoses were obtained from farm health records; samples were labeled as healthy or ketotic based on the temporal relationship between diagnosis and sampling\. A total of 2206 samples were used to build the calibration and validation datasets, in which five feature wavenumber selection methods and five machine learning algorithms were compared\. Ketotic cows had a higher milk fat percentage and fat\-to\-protein ratio and a lower protein and lactose percentage \(p &lt; 0\.01\)\. The best combination was PLS\-DA based on 104 CARS\-selected wavenumbers, which achieved a sensitivity of 0\.83, a specificity of 0\.79, a balanced accuracy of 0\.81 and an area under the receiver operating characteristic curve \(AUC\) of 0\.88 in validation\. MIR spectroscopy combined with machine learning can therefore support large\-scale ketosis phenotyping within the DHI system\.
