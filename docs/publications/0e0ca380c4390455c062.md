# Workplace Safety Judgment from Images: Comparing AI Assistants Across Five Occupational Contexts

**Autores:** Mehmed Zahid Çögenli; Yılmaz Karadaş

**Data:** 2026-10-15 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

This study compared the image\-based workplace safety judgments of three general\-purpose AI assistants across five occupational contexts\. Fifteen workplace images representing construction, workshop/manufacturing, maintenance/technical service, warehouse/logistics, and laboratory settings were evaluated by ChatGPT GPT\-5\.5, Claude Sonnet 5, and Gemini 3\.5 Flash\. Each model completed 15 single\-image assessments and five within\-context relative\-ranking tasks, yielding 60 outputs\. Two occupational safety specialists independently rated the outputs on five 0–5 dimensions and six binary error patterns\. The raters showed exact or near\-exact score agreement in 81\.8% of the ordinal ratings; pooled quadratic weighted agreement was moderate, κ = 0\.479, 95% CI \[0\.340, 0\.595\]\. Agreement was complete for pairwise ranking accuracy and exact final ordering\. Based on the two\-rater means, ChatGPT led in visible evidence coverage \(4\.37\), visual fidelity \(4\.43\), prioritization \(3\.73\), and uncertainty discipline \(4\.70\), whereas Claude obtained the highest descriptive control\-guidance mean \(4\.40\)\. Between\-model differences were statistically significant in the other four dimensions but not in control guidance\. No rater\-confirmed problematic control recommendation was observed\. All three models achieved the same final\-match performance: four of five context rankings were fully correct and 14 of 15 pairwise relations were correct\. The shared failure occurred in the workshop/manufacturing context, where the image containing a visible person at height was ranked below a dusty but static scene\. The findings support supervised first\-pass use of AI assistants but not autonomous workplace safety evaluation\.
