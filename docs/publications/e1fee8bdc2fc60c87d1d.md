# Machine learning\-based prediction of HCV\-related cirrhosis and the exploratory analysis of drug use influence

**Autores:** Shuanglin Gu; Lingling Ye; Jiasheng Tang; Wenxian Qian; Zheng Hu; Xiuchang Ma; Apeng Chen; Qin Zheng; Lei Wang; Guoping Yin; Changhua Yi

**Data:** 2026-10-13 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Introduction Chronic hepatitis C virus \(HCV\) infection easily progresses to liver cirrhosis, and drug users represent a special high\-risk population with accelerated liver damage and insufficient early screening strategies\. Methods This study aimed to develop a machine learning model for identifying HCV\-related cirrhosis, with stratified analysis between drug\-using and non\-drug\-using subgroups\. We enrolled patients with chronic HCV infection and collected blood routine, liver function, lymphocyte subsets, and drug use data\. Results Among the seven models compared, the XGBoost model achieved the best performance, with an AUC of 0\.86 on the test set\. Stratified analysis showed significantly better predictive performance in drug users \(AUC = 0\.87\) than in non\-drug users \(AUC = 0\.72, p &lt; 0\.05\)\. Drug use did not independently improve model performance and showed significant interactions with only a limited number of key features \(e\.g\., ADA\)\. SHAP and decision curve analysis validated the model’s reliability and clinical benefit, especially in drug users\. Discussion The XGBoost model enables noninvasive and accurate cirrhosis prediction and is suitable for early screening in high\-risk drug\-using populations\.
