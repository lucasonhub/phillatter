# Momentum, Commentary Tone, and Earnings Surprise in LLM Financial Forecasts

**Autores:** Alina Khaybullina

**Data:** 2026-10-15 · **Tipo:** preprint

## Resumo

Which inputs drive a language model&\#x27;s financial forecast? We construct synthetic earnings announcements that independently reverse 20\-day momentum, non\-informational commentary tone, and whether fixed actual EPS beats a manipulated consensus\. Six instruction/chat checkpoint conversions from four families \(0\.49\-7\.61B base parameters\) receive 2,016 prompts each under a direct\-answer Q8\_0 protocol\. The design includes 24 numerical blocks, six commentary pairs, profits and losses, rising and falling annual EPS, and two surprise sizes\. Both A/B answer assignments and U/D cover all 384 primary scenarios per conversion\. We estimate matched changes in conditional answer\-prefix scores, not calibrated market probabilities\. Across these checkpoints, the primary EPS contrast ranges from \-0\.19 to 86\.80 percentage points\. The magnitude of the primary signed tone contrast exceeds that of EPS in 5 of six models, and momentum exceeds it in 5\. Natural\-word numerical diagnostic accuracy ranges from 51\.0 to 95\.8 percent\. Answer\-encoding, wording, context, and conversion controls qualify these results\. The experiment measures observable input reliance and numerical recognition; it establishes neither an internal reasoning mechanism nor realized return predictability\. Public benchmark prompts, recorded scores, and an interactive recorded\-results explorer accompany the study\.
