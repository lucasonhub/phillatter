# First record of Parbatocamptus jochenmartensi Dumont &amp; Maas, 1988 \(Copepoda, Harpacticoida, Phyllognathopodidae\) from a cave in Thailand, with notes on the first known female

**Autores:** Santi Watiroyram; Kamonwan Koompoot

**Data:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Parbatocamptus jochenmartensi Dumont &amp; Maas, 1988 \(Copepoda, Harpacticoida, Phyllognathopodidae\) was collected from dripping\-water pools in a cave in northern Thailand\. This represents the first record of the species from Thailand and only the second record worldwide\. The first known female of the species is reported and briefly documented\. In addition, an additional morphological observation on the male is provided based on newly collected material\.
