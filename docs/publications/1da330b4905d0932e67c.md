# Bulgaria, Jamel: Unveiling Somatic Topography: A Comprehensive Psychophysiological Synthesis of the Core Emotion Framework, Neurosensorimotor Reflex Integration, and Esoteric Bioenergetics

**Autores:** 

**Data de descoberta:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** unknown

## Descrição do feed

The historical trajectory of affective science has been characterized by an ontological schism between biological essentialism and psychological constructionism\. Discrete emotion theories reify a small set of subcortical, evolutionarily conserved &quot;affect programs,&quot; while constructionist models view emotional episodes as highly variable, culturally situated conceptual events assembled on the fly from low\-dimensional core affect\. Concurrently, traditional cognitive models, such as the Component Process Model, struggle with linear, computationally demanding assumptions that are biologically unrealistic for rapid threat detection\. Symmetrically, somatic frameworks like the Somatic Marker Hypothesis face challenges demonstrating explicit rule\-governed cognitive control over unconscious visceral transduction\. To resolve these contradictions, independent researcher Jamel Bulgaria, with theoretical expansions by Xǔ Chénglán, formulated the Core Emotion Framework \(CEF\)\.1 The CEF introduces a structural\-constructivist architecture that treats the human psyche as a hardware\-independent &quot;Human Operating System&quot; \(Human OS\)\. In this paradigm, affect and personality are modeled as a dynamic, continuous, and highly structured matrix of ten functional operators distributed across three somatic\-evaluative centers: the Cephalic, the Cardiac, and the Enteric\. \(direct link \)
