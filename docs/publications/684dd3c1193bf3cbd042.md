# Imbalance and transition: a textual analysis of 125 ecological restoration policy documents in the Yangtze River Basin

**Autores:** Kanglan Fang; Lianghang Wu; Kun Qin

**Data:** 2026-10-13 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

The Yangtze River Basin is central to China’s socio\-economic development\. Its ecological restoration is key to regional ecological security, sustainable economic and social progress, and national strategic implementation\. By establishing a three\-dimensional framework encompassing policy tools, themes, and development stages, this study conducts a quantitative text analysis of 125 central and provincial policy documents \(comprising 349 policy clauses\) to systematically examine the patterns and evolutionary characteristics of the Yangtze River Basin’s ecological restoration framework\. The findings show an uneven distribution in the use of policy tools and themes: environmental\-oriented tools dominated \(65\.62%\), while Planning for Ecological Conservation and Restoration was the most frequent theme \(57\.60%\), and public participation and industrial integration remained marginal\. Across development stages \(slow: 2001–2015; rapid: 2016–2020; flourishing: 2021–2024\), the policy mix shifted\. From the rapid development stage \(2016–2020\) to the flourishing development stage \(2021–2024\), the combined share of supply\- and demand\-oriented tools increased from 33\.33% to 34\.59%\. As a text\-based analysis, this study focuses on policy design features and stage transitions, offering insights for sustainable policy formulation and methodological guidance for future research in related fields\.
