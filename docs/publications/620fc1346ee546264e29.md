# Pengaruh Persepsi Harga, Kualitas Produk dan Citra Merek terhadap Keputusan Pembelian Pakaian Thrift pada Kalangan Gen Z di Kota Jambi

**Autores:** Uly Deslianti; Hansen Rusliani; Beid FitrianovaAndriani

**Data:** 2026-10-11 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

This study aims to analyze the partial and simultaneous effects of price perception, product quality, and brand image on thrift clothing purchasing decisions among Generation Z in Jambi City\. This quantitative research with a causal\-associative approach involved 100 Gen Z respondents residing in Jambi City, selected using a purposive sampling technique\. Primary data were collected through questionnaires and analyzed using Multiple Linear Regression Analysis\. The findings indicate that partially, price perception \( = 0\.004 &lt; 0\.05\) and product quality \( = 0\.009 &lt; 0\.05\) have a positive and significant effect on purchasing decisions, with product quality being the most dominant factor \( = 0\.323\)\. Conversely, brand image partially has no significant effect \( = 0\.930 &gt; 0\.05\) because Gen Z consumers prioritize functionality, comfort, and product uniqueness over brand prestige\. Simultaneously, all three independent variables significantly influence purchasing decisions \( = 0\.001 &lt; 0\.05\), contributing 26\.5% \(Adjusted R Square = 0\.265\) to the variance in purchasing decisions, while the remaining 73\.5% is influenced by other factors outside the research model\.
