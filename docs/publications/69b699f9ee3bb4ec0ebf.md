# SACRED PLACES IN A SHADOW AS A RESOURCE FOR THE DEVELOPMENT OF RELIGIOUS TOURISM: A CASE STUDY IN SOUTHWESTERN BULGARIA

**Autores:** Petrana STOYKOVA; Teodora Kiryakova\-Dineva

**Data:** 2026-10-12 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

This study aims to analyze less familiar Orthodox sites in Blagoevgrad District of Southwestern Bulgaria, defined here as “sacred places in a shadow” as a factor for religious tourism development, specifically through community\-led charitable activities for their renovation\. Observations and visual data serve as the starting point of the analysis\. Next, the study applies the mixed\-methods approach, integrating field observation and visual documentation of six selected sites with 34 in\-depth interviews\. These were conducted with adult Bulgarian citizens residing in the territory of Blagoevgrad district\. The interviewees included persons directly involved in local church activities \(e\.g\. priests, church members, lay officials \(klisari\)\), which made it possible to outline the different aspects of the research problem\.The results are contextualized through an analysis of a national representative survey \(n =1283\) to evaluate broader trends in religion and socialization\. The findings suggest that the “sacred places in shadow” can function as vital tools for social cohesion, evidenced by localized efforts and volunteer\-led renovations\. These activities demonstrate that besides the scope of tourism, these sites are a tool for collective philanthropy and community spirit\. The data indicate that these “sacred places in a shadow&quot; contribute to the local society spirit and the are factor for the development of religious tourism in the region\. The study concludes that their integration into the tourism product profile can strengthen regional identity and better align with the principles of community social value\. This approach offers a replicable framework for peripheral regions seeking to balance cultural heritage preservation with sustainable economic growth\. By repositioning overlooked spiritual landmarks for civic engagement, the research underscores the profound interconectedness of faith and regional development\.
