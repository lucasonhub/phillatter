# Perušić, Luka: Suicide and the Moral Implications of AI\-Driven Life Value Assessment

**Autores:** 

**Data de descoberta:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** unknown

## Descrição do feed

In Steven Gouveia, AI Ethics Explored: A Multidisciplinary Approach\. pp\. 147\-260\. 2026The paper discusses the interrelation between social disengagement processes characterised by suicidal tendencies and the personal use of artificial intelligence systems for life value assessment in critical existential situations\. It provides arguments in favour of the conclusion that dialogic AI systems may add to the existential deterioration and ultimately contribute to suicidal tendencies in individuals\. Children, young people, and mentally vulnerable individuals in general are particularly emphasised as the members of society to be most affected by the presence of AI in social disengagement processes\. Thus, family members, guardians and professionals should be more vigilant in monitoring the use of non\-supervised dialogic AI systems\.
