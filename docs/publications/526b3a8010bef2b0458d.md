# Analysis of China&\#x27;s new materials industry landscape and corporate strategic planning

**Autores:** Jiusheng Wu

**Data:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

The new materials industry is a strategic pillar industry underpinning China&\#x27;s pursuit of technological self\-reliance and self\-strengthening\. It plays a vital role in the development of advanced manufacturing, the achievement of the &quot;dual carbon&quot; goals, and the security of industrial and supply chains\. The deep integration of Artificial Intelligence \(AI\) with materials Research and Development \(R&amp;D\) is continuously reshaping the innovation and production models of the industry\. Existing literature has extensively examined new materials technologies and AI\-driven innovation, but relatively little research has systematically analyzed the competitive landscape of the industry from the perspective of industrial economics\. Clarifying the characteristics of industry competition can provide practical insights for domestic industrial policymaking and overseas industrial deployment under the framework of the Belt and Road Initiative\. Using Porter&\#x27;s Five Forces model as the analytical framework, this study examines the competitive characteristics of China&\#x27;s new materials industry by drawing on publicly available industry reports, existing literature, and online interviews with industry practitioners, given the constraints on obtaining first\-hand corporate research samples\. The findings indicate that the industry as a whole has high entry barriers, while supplier bargaining power varies significantly across different segments\. Buyers and substitutes exert moderate competitive pressure, and the intensity of intra\-industry competition differs substantially between low\-end and high\-end segments\. Based on these findings, corresponding industrial policy recommendations are proposed, followed by a discussion of the limitations of this study and directions for future research\.
