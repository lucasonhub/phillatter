# METHODOLOGY FOR DEVELOPING INFORMATION AND ANALYTICAL COMPETENCE OF FUTURE LIBRARY, INFORMATION, AND ARCHIVAL PROFESSIONALS THROUGH GENERATIVE ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE

**Autores:** Tur O\.; Shabunina V\.

**Data:** 2026-10-15 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

The study examines the impact of global oil volatility on the financial performance of banks in Kazakhstan\. The aim of the study is to assess the sensitivity of key indicators of the financial stability of the banking sector, such as ROA, ROE, and CAR, to changes in oil volatility\. This study uses data from the banking sector, obtained through manual collection from the financial statements of 16 banks in Kazakhstan for the period from 2002 to 2024\. This issue is studied using the Generalized Autoregressive Conditional Heteroskedasticity model \(hereinafter – GARCH\), as well as Fixed or Random Effects models \(hereinafter – FE or RE models, respectively\)\. Preliminary results failed to prove the existence of a statistically significant relationship between oil volatility and the selected indicators of banks’ financial stability\.
