# ChatGPT and employability in higher education: exploring creativity, critical thinking, and problem\-solving

**Autores:** Cristina Calle\-Martínez; Marta Silvera\-Roig; Nuria Recuero Virto; Marta Paredes

**Data:** 2026-10-15 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Generative AI tools are reshaping higher education and employability preparation\. This study examines how using versus not using ChatGPT influences university students’ perceptions of employability, focusing on complex problem\-solving, critical thinking, and creativity\. Thirty students from the Complutense University of Madrid completed a contingency\-plan task and participated in eight focus groups analyzed through Grounded Theory \(ATLAS\.ti\)\. Results show that ChatGPT use is associated with efficiency, output refinement, and assisted creativity, while non\-use is associated with originality, deliberation, and teamwork\. The findings reveal preliminary patterns in students’ experiences with and without AI, warranting further quantitative investigation rather than claims of improved skills or employability\.
