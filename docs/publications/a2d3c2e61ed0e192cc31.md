# Writing a clinical case report based on international guidelines

**Autores:** Евгений Олегович Куневич; Mariia Mikhaleva; Сергей Владимирович Волошин; Victoria Olegovna Polyakova; Sergey M\. Alekseev

**Data:** 2026-10-14 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Introduction\. Over 10,000 of articles describing clinical cases or case series are published annually in the medical journals world\-wide\. However, the lack of unified requirements for manuscript preparation and varying author guidelines across medical journals often result in incomplete presentation of clinical information, reduced transparency in reporting, and substantial challenges in critically appraising the presented observations\. Objective\. This review aimed to analyze the requirements of the major international guidelines that govern the reporting of clinical cases in peer\-reviewed medical journals\. Materials and methods \. A structured literature search was conducted in the EQUATOR Network website and the PubMed database to identify articles addressing recommendations for clinical case reporting\. Results\. The current reporting guidelines for clinical cases in general clinical medicine \(CARE\), surgery \(SCARE\), and radiology \(CARE\-radiology\) were systematically reviewed\. We examined the structure and content of the checklists along with specific requirements for formatting the core sections of the publication and documenting medical data\. Moreover, we presented practical examples of patient image processing and methods for achieving anonymization\. The utility of graphical visualization of disease trajectories, specifically through the use of a chronological timeline, is also demonstrated\. Conclusion \. Adherence to the CARE statement and CARE\-based guidelines will promote standardization in the preparation of clinical case reports, improve the quality of case descriptions, enhance the completeness and transparency of published reports, and facilitate their reproducibility\. This review may serve as a practical reference for authors, peer reviewers, and journal editors in the preparation, critical evaluation, and publication of clinical case reports\.
