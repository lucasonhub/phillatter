# Generative AI Decision Support in B2C E\-Commerce: A Consumer Buying Behaviour Framework

**Autores:** Galina Ilieva

**Data:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Decision making in business\-to\-consumer \(B2C\) e\-commerce connects company and consumer activities before, during and after purchase, whereas many existing generative artificial intelligence \(GAI\) applications focus on specific tasks or functions\. This article develops a conceptual framework linking employee responsibilities, consumer choices, GAI support, information sources, permissions and two\-sided evaluation through the five\-stage Consumer Buying Behaviour \(CBB\) process\. It connects need clarification, information gathering, feasible\-offer comparison, transaction review and post\-purchase assessment through decision gates and bounded support functions\. Five employees and three consumers participated in a fixed\-order ChatGPT 5\.6 demonstration to examine the administration and perceived value of the proposed decision\-support approach\. Following a brief GAI introduction, participants observed ordinary prompt\-response support and then a structured CBB\-guided sequence within the same general e\-commerce decision context\. Questionnaire ratings were generally more favourable following CBB guidance, particularly for perceived comparison support and mental effort\. The study\-specific items are descriptive indicators, and fixed ordering confounds condition with practice, familiarity, novelty, fatigue, expectancy and task\-set effects\. The contribution is strictly conceptual and methodological; the eight\-participant exercise supplies procedural illustration and design input, without establishing effectiveness, deployment readiness or validated system behaviour\. Future evaluation requires an implemented system, versioned tasks and outputs, independently scored task\-level records, and randomised or counterbalanced comparison\.
