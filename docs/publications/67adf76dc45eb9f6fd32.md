# IMPROVING METHODS FOR OPTIMIZING THE HYDRAULIC REGIMES OF URBAN WATER DISTRIBUTION NETWORKS BASED ON ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE AND MATHEMATICAL MODELING

**Autores:** SHERZOD AKROM UGLI KHUDAYNAZAROV

**Data:** 2026-10-15 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

This article reviews scientific approaches to mathematical modeling and artificial intelligence\-based optimization of hydraulic regimes in urban water distribution networks\. It considers uneven pressure distribution, hydraulic losses, water leakage, and energy consumption\. Traditional hydraulic models, evolutionary optimization, machine learning, and digital twins are compared\. The review proposes a conceptual hybrid framework combining physics\-based simulation, sensor data, demand forecasting, and multi\-objective optimization\. The framework is presented as a research direction rather than an experimentally validated solution
