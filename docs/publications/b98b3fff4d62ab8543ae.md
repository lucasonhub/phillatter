# Trainable at Depth, Solvable at Small Size: What the Loss Surface of a Polynomial Activation Dictates for Designing, Initializing and Training Networks

**Autores:** Xiuyi Yang

**Data:** 2026-10-14 · **Tipo:** preprint

## Resumo

What makes a deep network train, and what shape is its loss surface? We answer both with one construction at two scales: a polynomial activation f \(z\) = z \+ λz 3 , clamped and projected onto the unit sphere, plus an unnormalized identity path\. As a deep stack it trains to one hundred layers on MNIST with no normalization machinery; as a small model its square loss is a polynomial system we solve exactly\. The small scale explains the large one: it prescribes how to design, initialize and train the stack\.
