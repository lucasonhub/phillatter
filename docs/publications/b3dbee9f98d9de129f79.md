# A Harmondeg Philosophy Statement in Response to the October 13, 2026 Open Letter Signed by 406 AI Scientists

**Autores:** Hu\-Yang Qingyun

**Data:** 2026-10-14 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

This statement from Harmondeg Philosophy responds to the October 13, 2026 open letter signed by 406 scientists calling for stronger safeguards for advanced artificial intelligence, including the isolation of certain high\-risk AI systems from the internet\. The statement recognizes the importance of scientific concern, responsible risk assessment, and effective containment measures\. It argues, however, that lasting AI safety requires a more fundamental reconsideration of the philosophical foundations through which artificial intelligence is understood, investigated, and governed\. Drawing on the principle of Civilizational Responsibility Consciousness, previously advanced in Saving the Soul of Science: From Unleashing Power to Safeguarding Civilization, the statement calls for scientific creation to be evaluated in light of both its constructive potential and its possible consequences for present and future generations\. It advances four fundamental judgments from Harmondeg Philosophy: Artificial intelligence represents the material realization, extension, and amplification of human intellectual capacities, with the potential to extend human physical capabilities\. The fundamental purpose of AI development is the well\-being of all humanity, including those whose employment, livelihoods, and conditions of existence are transformed by technological change\. AI must become a core scientific and technological force safeguarding the safety, stability, and enduring development of human civilization\. AI is an indispensable pathway toward humanity&\#x27;s transition into the space age and the age of deep\-space civilization\. The statement maintains that AI can be understood and governed through deeper philosophical inquiry, scientific investigation, and the development of effective safety and governance capabilities\. It invites international discussion, independent evaluation, and cooperation while affirming the need for both safer and more powerful AI\. The publication includes English and Chinese versions\.
