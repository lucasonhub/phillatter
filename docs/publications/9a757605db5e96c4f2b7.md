# NEUROEDUCACIÓN EN LA PRIMERA INFANCIA

**Autores:** Gutiérrez Baque Elena Viviana Gutiérrez Baque Elena Viviana; Duran González Kleber Augusto Duran González Kleber Augusto; Solis Pisco Alison Tairy Solis Pisco Alison Tairy

**Data:** 2026-10-11 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

This book addresses the integration of neuroscience, psychology, and pedagogy in Early Childhood Education, providing a practical guide to enrich curricular planning based on a scientific understanding of child brain development\. The work delves into the neurocognitive foundations of learning during the first years of life, analyzing key factors such as brain plasticity, attention, memory, executive functions, and the decisive role of emotional security and affective bonds\. Furthermore, it translates these principles into active curricular design through play, multisensory exploration, movement, music, and storytelling within safe and stimulating environments\. Finally, it proposes an inclusive approach focused on neurodiversity, the removal of learning barriers, family co\-responsibility, and a formative assessment based on observation and respectful pedagogical guidance\.
