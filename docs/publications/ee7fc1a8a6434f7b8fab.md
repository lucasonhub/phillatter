# SYSTEM ANALYSIS OF MUSICAL CYBERNETIC SYSTEMS: THEORY, CLASSIFICATION, AND APPLICATIONS

**Autores:** Gleb G\. Rogozinski; Irina B\. Gorbunova

**Data:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Computer Music Technologies \(CMT\) combine musical acoustics, digital signal processing, and software engineering\. These systems integrate automation, sound synthesis, and programming languages for composition\. This article uses a systems\-analytical approach to study CMT as complex structures, focusing on interactions to uncover developmental pathways\. It highlights Musical Cybernetic Systems \(MCS\), which merge artificial intelligence, generative composition, and synthesis to expand creativity\. Despite their use, MCS lack systematic study, complicating their analysis and optimization\. The article proposes a methodology for analyzing MCS using signal theory, set theory, and graph theory\. It identifies signal and symbolic information as key to understanding MCS data structures\. Sound synthesis and processing systems are described using IDEF0 notation, with examples of sonification and generative processes\. This approach reveals MCS design principles, offering insights into their functionality and interaction with external environments\.
