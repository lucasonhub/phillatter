# Kannon, Aelion: SN04 — The Awareness Stratification and the Philosophy of Mind: Physicalism, Panpsychism, Idealism, and the Register Problem

**Autores:** 

**Data de descoberta:** 2026-10-19 · **Tipo:** unknown

## Descrição do feed

The Awareness Stratification and the Philosophy of Mind examines physicalism, panpsychism and idealism through the Zenetist register problem\. Document class: Canon — Structural Neuroscience\. The volume locates philosophical accounts of awareness within the emanatory registers they describe and examines the omissions produced when one register is treated as exhaustive\. Its account preserves the paired centropic and inverse awareness modes, the bifurcal root relation, and the distinction between Soul / Mind articulation and embodiment\. Aionic reintegration preserves distinct essence in latency rather than merging beings into a single consciousness\. Proposed relations between native layers and neural observations require a specified mapping and evidence, while empirical neuroscience retains its own methods and measured objects\. Part of: Structural Neuroscience: The Architecture and Dynamics of Cognition\. \(direct link \)
