# Cullinane, James: What? Consciousness, the Self, and the Question of Being

**Autores:** 

**Data de descoberta:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** unknown

## Descrição do feed

What? is a philosophical inquiry into the nature of consciousness, selfhood, and existence, approached through a synthesis of analytic philosophy, phenomenology, cognitive science, and speculative thought\. Rather than advancing a single doctrinal theory, the work examines the question “What am I?” as an unresolved and structurally open problem, one that resists reduction to biological, computational, or metaphysical accounts alone\. Across a sequence of conceptual investigations, the book explores issues including personal identity over time, the relationship between mind and body, the emergence of moral experience, the role of language in shaping thought, the limits of introspection, and the implications of artificial intelligence for theories of awareness\. Classical problems such as the observer’s paradox, the continuity of self, and the nature of subjective experience are revisited alongside contemporary questions concerning machine consciousness, distributed cognition, and systems theory\. The work adopts a cumulative structure in which thought experiments, metaphors, and interdisciplinary perspectives are used not to resolve philosophical uncertainty but to clarify its contours\. Consciousness is treated as a process rather than an object, and the self as a dynamic pattern rather than a fixed entity\. In doing so, What? argues that uncertainty is not a failure of inquiry but a fundamental feature of any honest engagement with the question of being\.
