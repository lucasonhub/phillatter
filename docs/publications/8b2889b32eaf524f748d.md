# Associations Between Temporal Linguistic Features of Occupational Interviews and Personal Recovery Among Users of Psychiatric Home\-Visit Nursing Services

**Autores:** Ichiro Kutsuna; Masanao Ikeya; Akane Fujii; Aiko Hoshino; Kazuya Sakai

**Data:** 2026-10-12 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Using natural language processing, linguistic features of Occupational Performance History Interview\-II utterances concerning past, present, and future occupations among users of psychiatric home\-visit nursing services were quantified and examined for associations with personal recovery\. Utterances from 27 participants were classified by temporal context, analyzed using Japanese Linguistic Inquiry and Word Count, and related to Recovery Assessment Scale scores using generalized estimating equations\. Present\-oriented work/school\-related vocabulary was associated with hope, reliance on others, and willingness to ask for help, whereas future\-oriented cognitive vocabulary was associated with hope and personal confidence\. These findings suggest recovery\-related linguistic patterns may differ across temporal contexts\.
