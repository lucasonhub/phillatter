# The influence of AI adoption on decision making, forecasting, and performance: Evidence from solar and telecommunication sectors in Pakistan and China

**Autores:** Maureen Quaid

**Data:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

This study investigates how Artificial Intelligence \(AI\) adoption builds sustainable supply chain performance in the renewable energy and telecommunications sectors, focusing on the mediating mechanisms of decision\-making and forecasting\. Using a comparative design, data was collected from 214 companies in Pakistan and China\. The proposed model was analyzed using Partial Least Squares Structural Equation Modeling \(PLS\-SEM\), integrating the Technology\-Organization\-Environment \(TOE\) framework and Resource\-Based View \(RBV\)\. Results confirm a fully mediated pathway, AI adoption is positively associated with decision making and forecasting capabilities, which are positively associated with operational and financial performance, and are associated stronger supply chain performance\. A key finding is that these capabilities exert no direct effect, revealing that AI&\#x27;s sustainability impact is realized indirectly through core business outcomes\. The comparative analysis highlights contextual influence, with China&\#x27;s mature ecosystem fostering deep AI integration and Pakistan&\#x27;s emerging context showing reliance on external tools\. This study provides a novel, integrated TOE\-RBV model that unpacks the &quot;black box&quot; of AI&\#x27;s impact on sustainability\. It offers unique empirical evidence from contrasting emerging economies, demonstrating that the path to AI\-driven sustainable supply chains is mediated and context\-dependent\. For managers, embedding AI into core processes is critical for sustainability gains\. For policymakers, strategies must be context\-specific, focusing on foundational digital infrastructure in emerging economies and advanced innovation ecosystems in mature ones\.
