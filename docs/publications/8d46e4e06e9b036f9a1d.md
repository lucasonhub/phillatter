# Horizon\-aware group\-conditional conformal prediction: Post\-hoc shrinkage for sparse multi\-horizon coverage auditing

**Autores:** Jitong Zou; Enmiao Feng; Pengfei Li; Gaike Wang

**Data:** 2026-10-09 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Conformal prediction turns any forecaster into a generator of prediction intervals with a distribution\-free marginal coverage guarantee, but this guarantee is silent about individual subgroups, about individual forecast horizons, and about the sparse \(group, horizon\) cells in which the stratified empirical quantile is undefined\. We propose structured shrinkage for this sparse multi\-horizon auditing problem\. Our primary method, Horizon\-Aware Conformal Calibration \(HACC\), shrinks each cell’s empirical conformity quantile toward a multiplicative log\-additive anchor with a cross\-validated shrinkage strength; a complementary variant, AdditiveFE, fits a log\-linear group\-and\-horizon model to the whole quantile matrix\. Both always return finite intervals\. We prove a finite\-sample coverage bound for fixed shrinkage and, new in this version, show that the same bound applies pointwise to the cross\-validated shrinkage strength selected on the calibration data itself, removing the need for sample splitting\. On a 30\-seed synthetic benchmark the two variants attain the highest worst\-cell coverage and the most uniform coverage matrices among finite\-width methods, and on an Our World in Data child\-mortality panel with 15 intersectional groups and five horizons they raise minimum\-group coverage from 0\.28–0\.31 \(stratified baselines\) to 0\.51–0\.72 with country\-cluster bootstrap confidence intervals\. Finite back\-off variants of the stratified baselines, a sample\-splitting variant of HACC, and a rolling\-origin deployment protocol confirm that the gains are not artefacts of the undefined\-quantile convention or of retrospective pooling\.
