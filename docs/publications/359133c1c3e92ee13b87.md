# A phase\-velocity\-based 3D ray tracing method for transversely isotropic media

**Autores:** Jianen Xiao; Ning Qin

**Data:** 2026-10-15 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Seismic anisotropy is common in subsurface media and significantly affects seismic wave propagation, and although ray tracing provides direct access to kinematic quantities such as ray paths and traveltimes, conventional anisotropic ray\-tracing methods often require repeated stiffness\-tensor rotations and Christoffel eigensystem calculations that increase their algebraic and implementation complexity\. To address this, we propose a phase\-velocity\-based 3D ray\-tracing method for qP waves in transversely isotropic \(TI\) media, in which the ray equations are constructed directly from the qP\-wave phase velocity and its spatial and directional derivatives, thereby avoiding repeated Bond transformations and Christoffel eigensystem calculations, and the resulting equations are integrated using a fourth\-order Runge–Kutta scheme\. Numerical experiments using homogeneous, layered, and structurally complex models show good agreement with analytical solutions, fast\-marching results, and simulated qP\-wavefronts\. These results demonstrate the accuracy and applicability of the proposed method for 3D qP\-wave ray tracing in TI media\.
