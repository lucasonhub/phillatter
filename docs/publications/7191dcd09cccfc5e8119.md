# RiskPercept\-X: Explainable Transaction\-level Prediction of Investor\-perceived Risk on E\-investment Platforms

**Autores:** Shubhi Saxena; Garima Bhardwaj; Loveleen Gupta

**Data:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Retail investing has gone mobile, yet almost everything known about perceived risk in it comes from crosssectional questionnaires analysed with structural equation models\.Such designs count associations in one cross\-section rather than predicting an unseen case, ignore the risk statements accumulating in public platform reviews, and use taxonomies blind to the frictions of digital brokerage\.RiskPercept\-X removes all three by making the platform event the unit of analysis: a risk\-adaptive encoder and taxonomy\-prior gate turn an event into a seven\-dimensional risk profile, learned per\-dimension prototypes align review text, sentiment benchmarks and survey records without cross\-source pairing, an ordinal cumulative\-link head treats long\-tailed intensity as a graded response, and a recency\-weighted escalation operator turns a history into a churn early warning\.On a temporally held\-out split it attains 89\.0% accuracy and 86\.8% macro\-averaged F1 \(macro\-F1\) on risk dimensions, 0\.841 area under the receiver operating characteristic curve \(AUC\) on investor outcomes with an increment of 0\.033 from transferred signal, and 92\.3% accuracy on the Financial PhraseBank, where the comparison is with published reference values; against the strongest baseline reimplemented under an identical protocol it improves by 1\.9 macro\-F1 points on D1 and by 2\.9 on D2\.A leakagecontrolled replication confining every unsupervised and calibration stage to pre\-cut\-off data reproduces the ordering at 85\.9% macro\-F1 and 0\.834 AUC\.Three platform\-native dimensions absent from classical instruments carry 34\.0% of risk\-bearing reviews, supply the two highest\-ranked outcome drivers and together hold 0\.338 of the attribution mass, and escalation scores are associated with subsequent disengagement up to six weeks ahead\.Code and result files are released publicly\.
