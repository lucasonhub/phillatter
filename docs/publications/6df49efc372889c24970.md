# The Impact of Configurational Fit of Product and Service on Customer Decision\-Making: Evidence from an Event\-Related Potential Experiment

**Autores:** Meina Zhao; Mingming Xu

**Data:** 2026-10-11 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Artificial intelligence provides new approaches for smart product–service systems\. As such, an increasing number of service\-oriented manufacturing enterprises are leveraging artificial intelligence \(AI\) to achieve value co\-creation with customers\. In the process of intelligently matching additional services to products, measuring the congruity of product–service systems remains to be explored\. This study employs an event\-related potential \(ERP\) methodology to examine how product–service congruity influences customer purchase intentions associated with cognitive processing\. ERPs elicited by high\-congruity product–service combinations were significantly different from low\-congruity combinations\. By proposing a method to determine product–service congruity, this study provides insights for matching product–service combinations in an AI\-driven context\. These findings can guide the delivery of enhanced personalized product–service combinations in platform economies and AI ecosystems, enabling enterprises to achieve value co\-creation\.
