# Instance\-Adaptive Online Multiclass Multicalibration

**Autores:** Zhiming Huang; Jamie Morgenstern; Aaron Roth; Claire Jie Zhang

**Data:** 2026-10-11 · **Tipo:** preprint

## Resumo

We study online multiclass multicalibration over $T$ rounds, and give an algorithm with beyond\-worst\-case guarantees\. With $n$ possible outcomes, forecasts lie in a simplex of dimension $d=n\-1$\. In the worst case, cumulative $\\ell\_1$ multicalibration error scales with $\\widetildeΘ\(T^\{\(d\+1\)/\(d\+2\)\}\)$, which becomes untenable once $d$ exceeds a small constant\. Our algorithm adapts to the geometry of the realized sequence: if the sequence of adversarially selected outcome means lies in an affine subspace of dimension $s$, it obtains error $\\widetilde O\(n^\{1/\(s\+2\)\}T^\{\(s\+1\)/\(s\+2\)\}\)$ with high probability\. The algorithm adapts to this structure without knowing the subspace or its dimension\. For $T\\ge n\\ge2$, this rate is minimax optimal up to polylogarithmic factors\. For a stochastic instance with a fixed conditional mean, the bound specializes to $\\widetilde O\(\\sqrt\{nT\}\)$, giving a square\-root dependence on the horizon\.
