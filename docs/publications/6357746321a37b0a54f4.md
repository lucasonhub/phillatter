# Análisis de series de tiempo en la investigación en salud: visión general actualizada y breve guía práctica

**Autores:** José Moreno‐Montoya; Laura Andrea Rodríguez\-Villamizar

**Data:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

The era of longitudinal biomedicine derived from health records demands sophisticated temporal analysis\. However, traditional statistical techniques, such as ordinary least squares regression, are hampered by autocorrelation and heteroscedasticity in health time series data\.This essay examines the main characteristics of time series analytical models from autoregressive integrated moving average models to more recent machine learning\-based approaches and highlights the importance of implementing sophisticated techniques according to the nature of the health research question and data to obtain reliable statistical inferences\. It is argued that applying robust time series analysis is essential for effectiveimplementation\.
