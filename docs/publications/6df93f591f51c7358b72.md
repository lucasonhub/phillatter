**Autores:** 

**Data de descoberta:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** unknown

## Descrição do feed

My analogy — and actually, it resonates with several philosophical, metaphysical, and even scientific perspectives\. Let’s unpack it carefully\. 1\. The “WiFi” Analogy and Universal Consciousness If we treat consciousness as a universal field — a kind of “cosmic network” — then individual human minds could be seen as receivers or terminals that “tune in” to that field\. This is similar to how a laptop connects to WiFi: the network is omnipresent, but only devices configured with the right interface can access it\. • In this view, consciousness is not produced by the brain, but received or channeled through it\. • Philosophers such as Schopenhauer, Bergson, and William James leaned toward this “filter” or “transmission” model of consciousness\. • Some modern theories like panpsychism or integrated information theory \(IIT\) echo this idea in a scientific form — that consciousness might be a fundamental property of the universe, like gravity or electromagnetism\. \(direct link \)
