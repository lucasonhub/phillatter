## Descrição do feed

\_Inmediaciones de la Comunicacion\_ 21 \(2\):49\-72\. 2026Peirce’s semiotic may help in explaining the process of mediation, key to the study of media\. As an essential feature of semiosis, signs mediate between two determinative processes: the sign by the object and the interpretant by the sign\. The determinative process is transitive\. The object determines the sign, and the sign determines the interpretant in a way that the object representatively determines the interpretant\. That which is carried through the transitive relation is the form of the dynamical object, initially meaningless but, once represented in the sign in an interpretable manner, can produce a meaningful effect, an interpretant for some sign agency\. Additionally, I argue that the two processes of determination are themselves mediative processes\. The sign’s medium—the sign vehicle as Peirce calls it\-\-mediates the determination of signs by objects, and what Peirce calls collateral information, mediates the determination of interpretants by signs\. The latter is understood as the relevant collection of existing interpretants that act like a schema for interpretation\. I conclude by suggesting that the fault in Peirce’s semiotic is lack of appreciation for the role of the sign’s medium in mediation, which can be supplemented by the good work of media studies\. \(direct link \)
