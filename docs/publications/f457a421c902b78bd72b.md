# Nutritional strategies for bone stress injury prevention in military trainees: historical evolution, current evidence, and future directions

**Autores:** Nicole M\. Sekel; Stefan M\. Pasiakos

**Data:** 2026-10-14 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Bone stress injuries exist along a continuum from stress reactions to stress fractures\. They carry prolonged recovery times, high recurrence risk, and substantial economic burden, and they pose a direct threat to military readiness\. Nutritional status modifies injury susceptibility by influencing skeletal adaptation to mechanical loading, bone modeling and remodeling, and structural integrity\. Yet the historical evolution and effectiveness of nutritional policies intended to reduce the bone stress injury prevalence in the military remain poorly understood\. In this narrative review, we critically evaluate that evidence base and subsequently propose novel strategies to mitigate injury risk and promote skeletal resilience in military personnel\.
