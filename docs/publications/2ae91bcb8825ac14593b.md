# A Hybrid ConvLSTM Transfer Learning Framework for Cross\-regional Prediction of Arecanut Disease Intensity

**Autores:** Bharathi K; Ashwini B; Shrikanth Rao S K; Prathibha V H

**Data:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Accurate prediction of arecanut fruit\-rot disease risk is challenging because disease development is influenced by spatial variation, temporal weather dynamics, and delayed meteorological effects\.This study proposes a Hybrid Convolutional Long Short\-Term Memory \(ConvLSTM\) transfer\-learning framework for cross\-regional prediction of arecanut disease\-risk levels using Karnataka as the source domain and Kerala as the target domain\.The framework jointly models spatio\-temporal meteorological sequences and lagged weather information, with disease intensity classified into Low, Moderate, and Severe risk categories\.The Karnataka model was trained chronologically using 80% of observations for training and 20% for validation\.The optimized source\-domain weights were transferred to Kerala for target\-domain adaptation\.Karnataka and Kerala were represented using 4 × 5 and 2 × 5 spatial grids, respectively\.Experiments used seeds 42, 52, and 62\.The Hybrid ConvLSTM achieved mean 94\.53 ± 2\.32% accuracy and 98\.26 ± 0\.51% ROC\-AUC on Karnataka\.On Kerala, the scratch\-trained Hybrid ConvLSTM achieved mean 84\.69 ± 1\.09% accuracy, 68\.43 ± 2\.92% balanced accuracy, 69\.32 ± 2\.26% macro F1\-score, and 88\.99 ± 1\.09% ROC\-AUC\.The Karnataka\-to\-Kerala transfer model achieved mean 89\.63% accuracy, 83\.78% balanced accuracy, 83\.35% macro F1\-score, 89\.63% weighted F1\-score, and 94\.24% ROC\-AUC across the three seeds\.The best transfer result, obtained with seed 42, achieved 90\.735% accuracy, 84\.965% balanced accuracy, 85\.052% macro F1\-score, and 94\.513% ROC\-AUC\.The results demonstrate that learned Hybrid ConvLSTM representations can be adapted across geographically distinct regions\.Although transfer learning did not outperform the strongest Kerala\-only baselines, it improved the Hybrid ConvLSTM over scratch training and demonstrated effective cross\-regional parameter transfer despite regional distribution shift\.The framework provides a basis for transferable weather\-driven disease\-risk prediction where targetdomain observations are comparatively limited\.
