# RAQAMLI QURILMALARDAN FOYDALANISHNING OILAVIY MULOQOT SIFATIGA IJTIMOIY\-PSIXOLOGIK TA’SIRI

**Autores:** MUSHTARIY ISAYEVA

**Data:** 2026-10-13 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

This article provides a theoretical analysis of how digital\-device use affects the quality of family communication\. It examines the dual effects of smartphones, tablets, computers and other internet\-connected tools and clarifies the concepts of technoference, phubbing and parental phubbing\. The mechanisms through which digital devices influence emotional closeness, active listening, mutual trust, conflict regulation, parent–child interaction and couple relationships are systematized\. The analysis argues that the key determinant is not the device itself but the purpose, context and duration of use, family norms, and the extent to which technology interrupts face\-to\-face interaction\. Practical recommendations for developing a healthy digital balance within families are proposed\.
