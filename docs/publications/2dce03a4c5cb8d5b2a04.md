# Saraiva Dos Anjos, Gustavo: Temporal Extension and Temporal Access: The Experienced Present as Biological Localization

**Autores:** 

**Data de descoberta:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** unknown

## Descrição do feed

An organism is a physical process extended over an interval of time, but the interval to which it has access at any location in that extension is vastly smaller than the interval it occupies\. This paper takes that asymmetry as its subject\. It distinguishes temporal extension, temporal access, and temporal representation, and argues that the experienced present belongs to the third, is constrained by the second, and is not by itself a report on the first\. The framework models the experienced present as the output of a biological localization operator, Eτ = Bθ\(Sτ, Rqτ, Iτ\)\. Sτ is the organism’s currently transduced and integrated state, Rqτ is the subset of causally preserved records retrieved at τ, and Iτ is the set of model\-generated inferences presently instantiated at τ, including both retrodictions and forward projections\. Memory, perception, and anticipation are therefore distinguished by causal provenance, semantic orientation, and functional role rather than by physical contact with three regions of time\. Relativistic causal structure constrains how information becomes locally available; thermodynamic boundary conditions help explain the prevalence of records directed toward earlier interactions; and biological architecture determines the width and organization of integration\. A restricted philosophical consequence follows: among temporal ontologies that preserve the same local causal, thermodynamic, and biological conditions, the access structure described here does not by itself discriminate between dynamic and static accounts\. The paper closes with a field\-sensitive update principle for artificial agents and a prospective experiment testing its engineering consequence\. The conceptual, phenomenological, and engineering claims are explicitly separated; no experiment has yet been run\.
