# Barack, David: The Perils and Prospects of NeuroAI

## Descrição do feed

In The Role of Artificial Intelligence in Science: Methodological and Epistemological Studies\. Routledge\. forthcomingNeuroAI refers to a range of projects that seek to leverage the explanatory similarity between brains and artificial neural networks\. This broad movement has different goals, and in this chapter I will focus on how NeuroAI seeks to understand the mind and brain by leveraging insights from artificial intelligence\. This project can be usefully decomposed into Strong and Weak NeuroAI\. Strong NeuroAI contends that complex neural network\-like systems, including the brain, can only be understood in virtue of their objective functions, learning rules, and architecture\. I argue that Strong NeuroAI presents a kind of quietism about our capacities for achieving our epistemic aims with regard to the brain, prematurely forecloses options for achieving those aims, and should be adopted only at the end, not the outset, of inquiry\. In contrast, Weak NeuroAI contends that artificial neural networks serve as a source of hypotheses and as a digital experimental testbed, allowing for experimental investigation of neural processing\. I argue that Weak NeuroAI is much more promising and already bearing fruit\.
