# Fractional Optimal Control of AI\-TPACK Learning Dynamics with FBSM Numerical Study

**Autores:** Moosa Ebadi

**Data:** 2026-10-15 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

In this study, we develop a fractional optimal control model for teachers&\#x27; artificial intelligence\-based technological pedagogical content knowledge \(AI\-TPACK\)\. Four state variables: AI\-related technological knowledge \(AI\-TK\), technological content knowledge \(AI\-TCK\), technological pedagogical knowledge \(AI\-TPK\), and integrated AI\-TPACK are used in the model\. We used two bounded decision variables: technology\-integration workshops and specialized technology courses\. A direct\-method proof establishes the existence of an optimal pair\. Pontryagin conditions yield the fractional adjoint and the classical pseudo\-state system used numerically, as well as separate projection formulas for both interventions\. A local contraction theorem gives sufficient conditions for convergence of the relaxed forward\-backward sweep method \(FBSM\)\. The results support a staged policy: moderately stronger technology\-integration workshops, complemented by specialized courses, provide a more efficient pathway than either intervention component alone\. The scenario coefficients are used in this work to solve the AI\-TPACK optimal control model numerically\.
