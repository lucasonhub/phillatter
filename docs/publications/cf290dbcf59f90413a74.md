# Sergent, Brandon M\.: Bradley Found Experience and Then Rebuilt the Outside: How Retained Time Generated the Absolute

**Autores:** 

**Data de descoberta:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** unknown

## Descrição do feed

F\. H\. Bradley&\#x27;s \*Appearance and Reality\* presents a substantially closer historical precedent to Experiential Empiricism than my prior genealogical work recognized\. Bradley argues that reality is an all\-inclusive whole beyond which there is nothing, identifies the material of reality with sentient experience, denies that anything non\-experiential can coherently occupy another ontological region, and challenges the reader to produce a fact separable from sentience\. These claims overlap strikingly with arguments I later developed independently under Experiential Empiricism, especially the Experientia Est challenge and the claim that reality cannot possess an exterior\. Bradley was absent from my previous historical surveys, and that omission requires correction\. Yet the comparison also exposes the precise point at which Bradley fails to complete the subtractive move\. Bradley retains temporal succession and relational persistence long enough to generate contradictions involving change, identity, permanence, and succession\. He then interprets those contradictions as evidence that ordinary experience is merely appearance and must be reconciled within a metaphysically privileged Absolute\. This paper argues that the Absolute is unnecessary\. Bradley correctly identifies the coherence requirement but mislocates the contradiction\. The contradiction arises from treating external time and relations across temporally distinct states as features of reality rather than as present experiential structure\. Once external time is removed, each experiential occurrence is internally coherent and no higher Absolute is required to reconcile it\. Bradley therefore represents an unusually powerful historical near\-hit: he reaches experiential totality, then manufactures the need to escape it by retaining exactly the externalist machinery that complete subtraction removes\.
