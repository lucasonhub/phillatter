# Bulgaria, Jamel: Uncovering Somatic Topography: A Unified Psychophysiological Synthesis of the Core Emotion Framework, Neurosensorimotor Reflex Integration, and Esoteric Bioenergetics

**Autores:** 

**Data de descoberta:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** unknown

## Descrição do feed

This manuscript presents a unified psychophysiological synthesis that integrates the Core Emotion Framework \(CEF\), developmental neurosensorimotor reflex theory, and classical Eastern esoteric energy systems into a single tri\-centric somatic architecture\. Drawing on neuroanatomical evidence from the cephalic, cardiac, and enteric nervous systems, the paper demonstrates that these three biological hubs map precisely onto the Ajna, Anahata, and Manipura chakras and the Daoist Upper, Middle, and Lower Dantians\. The analysis reframes the yogic Granthis as the physiological consequences of retained primitive reflexes—specifically the Fear Paralysis, Moro, and ATNR reflexes—showing how these unintegrated circuits generate chronic autonomic dysregulation, myofascial bracing, and emotional fusion patterns\. The manuscript further introduces a mechanistic model of somatic\-mechanical actuation and vestibular modulation, illustrating how rotational, linear, and oscillatory movements reorganize brainstem pathways, re\-engage the ventral vagal brake, and dissolve pathological attractor basins\. A mathematical formalization using Bloch\-sphere qubit dynamics, non\-commuting operator projections, Kuramoto synchronization, and holographic boundary–bulk mappings establishes the CEF as a testable, computationally tractable paradigm\. This synthesis provides a comprehensive framework for understanding how cognitive, emotional, and somatic processes co\-regulate across biological, energetic, and mathematical domains, offering a foundation for next\-generation somatic therapies, psychophysiological instrumentation, and consciousness research\.
