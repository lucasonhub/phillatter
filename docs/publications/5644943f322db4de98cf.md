# Digital tax governance and resilient agricultural machinery investment: a lifecycle analysis of an upstream pathway to sustainable food systems in China

**Autores:** Mingya Wei

**Data:** 2026-10-12 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Introduction Tax incentives can support agricultural\-machinery investment, but their value depends on taxable capacity, documentary evidence, and timing\. Methods We use a policy\-calibrated single\-case simulation of Company A to evaluate 432 prespecified lifecycle rules across ten demand\-policy stress paths for 2026–2033\. The model integrates value\-added tax, corporate income tax, R&amp;D incentives, tax\-loss and credit vintages, investment timing, and model\-implied adjustment exposure\. Scenario weights are judgmental rather than estimated frequencies\. Results The constrained rule combines 7% R&amp;D, equipment and retrofit in 2026, straight\-line tax depreciation, and no exemption\-based separate accounting\. Its expected NPV is CNY 16\.82 million and its 20% lower\-tail CVaR is CNY 12\.61 million\. Expected NPV is 5\.40% higher than under Company A’s static plan but only 1\.51% higher than under baseline compliance\. Relative to baseline, CVaR is 5\.82% lower and risk\-adjusted NPV is essentially unchanged\. Discussion These results are conditional on the stated parameters\. The proposed food\-system pathway is theoretical because machinery adoption, farm output, prices, diets, and nutrition are not observed\.
