# Rezazadeh, Farhad: Artificial Intelligence as Humanity&\#x27;s Ultimate Aspiration: Building the Virtuous City and Why Its Advance Cannot Be Stopped

**Autores:** 

**Data de descoberta:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** unknown

## Descrição do feed

The wish for a made intelligence is older than the science that now fulfills it\. This philosophical paper argues, from the standpoint of a practicing AI researcher and engineer, that artificial intelligence is humanity&\#x27;s ultimate aspiration; the one project whose success enlarges our power to pursue every other\. It defends two theses by conceptual analysis, textual interpretation and one stylized model\. The first is constructive\. Reading al\-Farabi&\#x27;s tenth\-century treatise on the Virtuous City as a specification, in a deliberately modern reconstruction, I argue that what it requires is what machine intelligence can help to supply\. The second is structural\. The general advance of AI cannot be stopped, in the defined sense that a global and lasting halt would need monitoring and sanctions that a free society could not accept; particular uses can be restricted and the frontier slowed\. A game\-theoretic model gives the condition that any enforced limit must meet\. I separate what the argument establishes, that an unenforced halt is unstable and that no enforceable one has been shown feasible under present incentives, from the stronger thesis, which rests on two stated premises\. I answer six objections and conclude that inevitability concentrates responsibility; if the advance cannot be halted, its direction is the whole of the moral question\.
