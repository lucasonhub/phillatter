# AXBOROT MAYDONI GLOBALLASHUVINING SHAXS MA’NAVIY MADANIYATI SHAKLANISHIGA TA’SIRI

**Autores:** ZAMIRA BAXTIYOROVNA RAXMATOVA

**Data:** 2026-10-14 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

This article examines the impact of the globalization of the information sphere on the formation of an individual’s spiritual culture\. It analyzes the interrelation among globalization, spirituality, and culture, and discusses the significance of factors that emerge in connection with mass culture, individualism, and egocentrism—phenomena inherently linked with globalization\. Reflections are offered on their role in contemporary sociocultural development\.
