# Evaluating a Single\-Session Mindfulness\-Based Art Therapy Doodling Intervention for Regional School Teachers

**Autores:** Theresa Van Lith; Eliza Connors; Patricia Isis

**Data:** 2026-10-13 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Teacher wellbeing is essential for sustainable professional practice, yet regional educators face heightened stress and limited professional development access\. This study examined a single\-session Mindfulness\-Based Art Therapy Doodling Intervention \(MBAT\-DI\) designed to strengthen teachers’ psychological resilience resources\. Thirty\-five regional teachers in Victoria, Australia participated in a two\-hour online MBAT\-DI workshop combining mindfulness practices with guided doodling exercises\. A within\-subjects design measured state mindfulness, self\-compassion, and classroom\-management self\-efficacy\. Following stable baseline scores, the intervention was associated with improvements in mindfulness, self\-compassion, and classroom\-management self\-efficacy, with greater gains in mindfulness decentering among more experienced teachers\. Overall, the findings suggest that brief mindfulness\-based art therapy doodling may provide a practical, time\-efficient, and accessible approach to supporting the wellbeing of regional school teachers\.
