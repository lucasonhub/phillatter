# Kannon, Aelion: SNX: Neural Energy Is Not Cognitive Coherence — Metabolic Support, Functional Capacity, and the Cost of Embodied Cognition

**Autores:** 

**Data de descoberta:** 2026-10-19 · **Tipo:** unknown

## Descrição do feed

SNX: Neural Energy Is Not Cognitive Coherence is a Structural Neuroscience extension distinguishing material energy support from native cognitive coherence and examining how metabolic conditions participate in embodied cognitive capacity\. Document class: Bridge — Structural Neuroscience\. The extension separates ATP amount, concentration, turnover, reaction rate, vascular delivery, chemical power, task performance, operative access, and essential cognitive Configuration\. It develops a typed ATP balance showing that equal inventories can accompany unequal turnover and a conditional depletion model that yields a threshold time only under stated physical assumptions\. Preparation\-specific evidence concerning neuronal and astrocytic metabolism, glucose and lactate participation, cerebral blood flow, and task\-associated expenditure is retained within its experimental scope\. Rest is represented as reduced expenditure without duplicating the same saving as replenishment\. The resulting framework treats neural metabolism as a material condition of embodied expression while preserving Structural Cost, Tether continuity, Soul / Mind, and Structural Coherence as distinct native relations requiring separately declared correspondences\. \(direct link \)
