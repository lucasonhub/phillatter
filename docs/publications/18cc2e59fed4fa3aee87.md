# Skorburg, Joshua August &amp; Sinnott\-Armstrong, Walter: The alignment problem&\#x27;s problem

**Autores:** 

**Data de descoberta:** 2026-10-19 · **Tipo:** unknown

## Descrição do feed

The GPT\-4o incident can be seen as a natural experiment, of sorts, in AI alignment\. It showed what can happen when user preferences – what users say they like, what their feedback signals reward – are taken as the target of alignment\. Aligning AI to user preferences seems more likely to give us systems that agree with us, rather than systems that get things right\. We argue that this situation vindicates a philosophical position that has been too hastily dismissed\. Namely, that a proper target of AI alignment is idealized preferences\.
