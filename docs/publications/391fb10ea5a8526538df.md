# Review of the 2025 Year of Elections

**Autores:** Khushbu Agrawal; Erik Asplund; Bianca Canevari; Sonali Campion; Holly Ann Garnett; Toby S\. James; Tim Murithi; Daniella Paulin; Therese Pearce Laanela; Amali Ranavi Thanthrige; Julia Thalin; Julian Tucker; Vasil Vashchanka

**Data:** 2026-10-12 · **Tipo:** book

## Resumo

This report examines the key challenges and innovations that shaped elections during the 2025 calendar year\. It provides practitioners, researchers and the wider public with an overview of global trends in electoral integrity; analyses emerging challenges affecting the conduct of elections; and showcases innovative approaches and good practices from around the world\. Produced through close collaboration between International IDEA and the Electoral Integrity Project, the report draws on extensive discussion, global comparative analysis and expert reflection\. It synthesizes the key trends observed throughout 2025 and offers evidence\-based insights to inform future policy, practice and research\.
