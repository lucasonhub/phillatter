# Il laboratorio di Corpo in Movimento e Laban Movement Analysis nella formazione iniziale dei docenti: un progetto pilota per lo sviluppo di consapevolezza corporea, autoregolazione emotiva e competenze so\-cio\-emotive\.

**Autores:** Loredana Cervino

**Data:** 2026-10-11 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

We present the results of a single\-group pilot project aimed at exploring the feasibility and preliminary effectiveness of a workshop based on Laban Movement Analysis \(LMA\) and educational dance in initial teacher education\. The sample consists of 154 second\-year students enrolled in the degree programme in Primary Education Sciences and 54 students enrolled in the Specialization Course for Support Teaching Activities at the University of Palermo\. The main objective is to verify whether systematic exposure to intentional movement practices, structured according to the four motion factors of LMA, produces significant improvements in five interconnected dimensions\. From a theoretical perspective, the study is grounded in the paradigm of embodied cognition and integrates the process model of emotional self\-regulation with the interoceptive approach of Mindful Awareness in Body\-Oriented Therapy\. The workshop is organized into three sequential phases: guided bodily experience, metacognitive reflection, and didactic transfer, with activities built around Labanian parameters as intentional tools for modulating internal states\. Outcomes are assessed through a pre/post questionnaire with items rated on a five\-point Likert scale\. Preliminary results and implications for inclusive teaching, with particular reference to students with special educational needs, are discussed from a transformative research perspective\.
