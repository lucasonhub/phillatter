# New requirements for medical device safety monitoring: key challenges for healthcare organizations\.

**Autores:** D\. V\. Piven; Igor V\. Ivanov; E\. A\. Trofimova

**Data:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Relevance\. Medical device safety monitoring is one of the consistently and strictly controlled mandatory requirements imposed on healthcare organizations\. Objective: to analyze the new Procedure for the implementation of medical device safety monitoring and the new Procedure for reporting adverse events in the field of medical device circulation with a view to identifying the new tasks now facing healthcare organizations\. Results\. It is noted that the new orders of the Ministry of Health of the Russian Federation have significantly clarified and detailed the requirements for information, the collection, processing, and recording of which are carried out during medical device safety monitoring\. This includes, inter alia, data transmitted by software employing artificial intelligence technologies that constitutes a medical device; information on adverse events in the field of medical device circulation in other states; and information contained in an adverse event report\. Attention is drawn to the increased significance of the Classification of adverse events related to the circulation of medical devices\. Conclusion\. Healthcare organizations should pay special attention to the fact that the identification of adverse events in the field of medical device circulation must now be carried out in strict accordance with the Classification of adverse events approved by Order No\. 4513 of the Federal Service for Surveillance in Healthcare \(Roszdravnadzor\) dated May 20, 2021\. In accordance with the new Procedure for the implementation of medical device safety monitoring, each healthcare organization is required to appoint an official responsible for organizing the work of collecting information in order to ensure the safe use of medical devices and to establish the procedure for this work\.
