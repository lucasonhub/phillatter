# Seminar: How to Make the Most of AI: A Workshop for Safety Engineers

**Autores:** Catherine Menon

**Data:** 2026-10-14 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

The “How to Make the Most of AI: A Workshop for Safety Engineers” seminar was held at the Hilton Euston hotel in London, on 25th June 20264 and was hosted by Mike Parsons\. The speakers discussed a range of techniques and insights into how safety engineers might benefit from the use of generative Artificial Intelligence \(AI\) / Large Language Models \(LLMs\), examining everything from prompt engineering to building applications and custom tools from AI code generators\.
