# Lloyd, Dan: AI Poe and the Quest for Good Slop

**Autores:** 

**Data de descoberta:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** unknown

## Descrição do feed

\_AI Poe and the Quest for Good Slop\_ is an experiment in AI authorship, in which three Frontier Models \(Claude, ChatGPT, and Grok\) compose Gothic horror stories about AI in the style of Edgar Allan Poe\. Following the stories, the models themselves engage in discussion of story strategies and implications for issues in AI, including ethics, consciousness, and aesthetics\. The texts are entirely by the models, but framed by an AI\-free preface and epilogue by the promptmaker, Dan Lloyd\. Lloyd particularly identifies gaps in the AI understanding of the physical and social world, and especially their alien understanding of temporality\. For context \(and free ebooks\), see danlloyd dot online\.
