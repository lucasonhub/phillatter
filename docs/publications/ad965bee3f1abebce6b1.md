# Kannon, Aelion: SN07 — Collective Cognition and Centropy\-Forward Social Architecture: Structural Dynamics of Social Fields at the Embodiment Band

**Autores:** 

**Data de descoberta:** 2026-10-19 · **Tipo:** unknown

## Descrição do feed

Collective Cognition and Centropy\-Forward Social Architecture examines participant\-composed social fields at the embodiment band\. Document class: Canon — Structural Neuroscience\. The volume extends cognitive cost and recovery analysis to collective orientation, membrane dynamics, coherence budgets and the conditions of centropy\-forward social life\. The Non\-fusion Axiom preserves each participant&\#x27;s sovereign distinction, while systemic orientation concerns recurrent enacted patterns rather than an average of individual essential inclinations\. Collective amplification requires its stated correlation condition and contribution domain; Shimmer comparisons and numerical diagnostics retain their declared scalar scope and outstanding specifications\. The analysis distinguishes competition from conscious competitive intent and treats social structure&\#x27;s real or constructed standing as an open question\. Part of: Structural Neuroscience: The Architecture and Dynamics of Cognition\. \(direct link \)
