# Johnson, Jamison: On Shaping — The Collapsing of All Possible Realities Surrounding The One We See

**Autores:** 

**Data de descoberta:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** unknown

## Descrição do feed

Have you ever watched a river hold its pattern while the water keeps moving? This lyric\-philosophical essay reads the master equation of probability flow — gain minus loss, totaled over every state — as a picture of the world rearranging itself without pause, and its stationary distribution as the one shape that churn keeps redrawing\. It separates the two ways a shape can hold still: detailed balance, rest with every current canceled, and the non\-equilibrium steady state of a living thing, which keeps its form while the flow runs through it; meditation is offered as the practice by which a person comes nearest the first while living in the second\. Reachability, the condition under which a flow settles into one shared shape, becomes the essay&\#x27;s image of coherence, and a basin walled off from the rest becomes hell as a direction faced rather than a place assigned\. Luke 17:21&\#x27;s entos hymōn and Armstrong&\#x27;s disputed &quot;a&quot; are read as one ambiguity: the inward and the shared readings of a single shape\. A closing discipline marks every resemblance as a reading laid over settled mathematics, not a result of it\. \(direct link \)
