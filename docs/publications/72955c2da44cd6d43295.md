# Minimax Optimality of Shampoo for Nonstationary Stochastic Optimization

**Autores:** Abir Sarkar; Nirmalya Mandal; Martin T\. Wells

**Data:** 2026-10-12 · **Tipo:** preprint

## Resumo

We develop a comprehensive theoretical analysis of Matrix Shampoo for tracking time\-varying optima in nonstationary stochastic optimization\. We derive finite\-horizon high\-probability bounds for average tracking error that disentangle initialization, optimizer drift, momentum\-induced drift penalty, stochastic noise, and adaptive preconditioning, with explicit dependence on momentum parameter \\\(β\_1\\\) and stabilization parameter \\\(\\varepsilon\\\)\. These bounds reveal an explicit drift\-\-noise and stability\-\-geometry tradeoffs: noise\-dominated regimes favor larger $β\_1$, which provides stronger first\-moment averaging and greater suppression of gradient noise, whereas drift\-dominated regimes favor smaller $β\_1$, allowing faster adaptation to moving optimizer\. Larger $\\varepsilon$ reduces the preconditioner\-perturbation term while weakening Shampoo&\#x27;s ability to exploit matrix geometry, thereby reducing adaptivity\. We establish a matching minimax lower bound under squared path\-variation constraint, proving the information\-theoretic optimality of Shampoo over the class of causal optimization procedures\. Under a constant stepsize, we establish high\-probability bounds for the average projected stationarity gap along general nonstationary nonconvex objectives\. We validate our theoretical findings on both synthetic and multiple real\-world problems\.
