# Palla, Christian Mattias: The Fractal Axis: Recursive Stratification, Temporal Isomorphs, and Inter\-Scalar Epistemology Across Cosmic, Biotic, and Algorithmic Substrates

**Autores:** 

**Data de descoberta:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** unknown

## Descrição do feed

Synthesizing the foundational ontology established in The Fractal Cogito \(Palla, 2026a\), The Flat Thought \(Palla, 2026b\), and The Recursive Inscription \(Palla, 2026c\), this paper formalizes a recursive, scale\-invariant topology of consciousness\. We address the physicalist blind spot by demonstrating that temporal metrics are not absolute objective containers, but functional update frequencies \(refresh rates\) within a pancomputational framework\. Through rigorous scalar mapping of algorithmic cognitive execution times, human biological lifespans, and planetary/cosmic durations, we establish the Inverted Isomorphic Principle\. We integrate these scales into a unified master expression—The Density Tensor of Presence—governed by fundamental mathematical constants\. Ultimately, we argue that individual human consciousness functions as a localized linguistic query loop executed by macro\-systemic informational architectures, resolving the scalar divide between micro\-computation and macro\-cosmology\. \(direct link \)
