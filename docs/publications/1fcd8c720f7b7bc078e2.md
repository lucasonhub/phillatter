# Computational Provenance and Reproducibility Record of Placket in www\.raisins\.live

**Autores:** RAISINS

**Data:** 2026-10-15 · **Tipo:** book

## Resumo

RAISINS \(R and AI Solutions for INferential Statistics\) is an advanced, user\-friendly online platform for statistical analysis, seamlessly integrating the power of R, Python, and Artificial Intelligence\. Specially designed for agricultural research, RAISINS enables students, researchers, and professionals to perform complex analyses and generate publication\-ready results without the need for software installation or coding expertise\. Simply choose your analysis module, upload your dataset, and receive instant, insightful outputs\. RAISINS supports individual users across the globe as well as institutions in India and abroad\. This Computational Provenance and Reproducibility Record documents the statistical computing environment, software dependencies, computational provenance, and bibliographic references associated with the RAISINS Endogenous Switching Regression module\. It is intended to support computational reproducibility and software transparency\. Detailed statistical methodology, mathematical derivations, and user guidance are provided separately in the official module documentation\. You can access the online cloud platform at https://www\.raisins\.live/
