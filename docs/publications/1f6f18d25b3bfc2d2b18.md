# Artificial Intelligence and Working Capital Management: Implications for Financial Efficiency and Financial Decision\-Making in Nepal

**Autores:** Ph\.D\. Abdul Rahman

**Data:** 2026-10-12 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

This study examines the role of artificial intelligence \(AI\) in working capital management \(WCM\) and investigates how effective WCM contributes to financial efficiency and financial decision\-making in modern businesses\. The study adopts a quantitative research design and focuses on finance and business professionals involved in financial decision\-making\. Data were structured around AI\-based financial forecasting, AI\-based decision support, working capital management, financial efficiency, and financial decision\-making\. The proposed relationships were examined using Partial Least Squares Structural Equation Modeling \(PLS\-SEM\) through SmartPLS 4\. The results indicate that AI\-based financial forecasting has a significant positive effect on working capital management \(β = 0\.354, p &lt; 0\.001\), while AI\-based decision support also has a significant positive effect on WCM \(β = 0\.208, p &lt; 0\.001\)\. Working capital management significantly improves financial efficiency \(β = 0\.533, p &lt; 0\.001\) and financial decision\-making \(β = 0\.389, p &lt; 0\.001\)\. The model explains 21\.6% of the variance in WCM, 28\.4% in financial efficiency, and 15\.2% in financial decision\-making\. The findings suggest that integrating AI\-based forecasting and decision\-support capabilities with working capital practices can strengthen financial planning, improve the management of short\-term financial resources, and support more informed financial decisions\. The study connects AI\-enabled financial capabilities with working capital management and its downstream effects on financial efficiency and decision\-making, providing an integrated framework for understanding the role of AI in contemporary financial management\.
