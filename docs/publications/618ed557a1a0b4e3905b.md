# Ideal Convergence of Regular Uncertainty Distributions in $L^p$\-Distance

**Autores:** Ömer Kı̇şı̇; Mehmet Gürdal

**Data:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Let $1\\leq p&lt;\\infty$\. On the class of regular uncertainty distributions with $p$\-integrable inverse distributions, we consider the $L^p$\-distance between the inverse distributions\. Using the standard quantile representation, we identify the completion of this metric space with the closed cone of almost\-everywhere equivalence classes of nondecreasing functions in $L^p\(0,1\)$ and characterize its relatively compact subsets by uniform $p$\-integrability\. For an admissible ideal $I$ on $N$, the Cauchy criterion for complete metric spaces gives the equivalence of ideal convergence and the ideal Cauchy condition in the completed space\. The classical additive\-property criterion also applies: ideal convergence is realized along a subsequence indexed by some $M \\in\\mathcal F\(I\)$ for every sequence if and only if $I$ has the additive property\. Under uniform $p$\-integrability, convergence in $d\_p$ is equivalent to pointwise ideal convergence of the associated distribution functions at every continuity point\. An endpoint\-concentration example shows that pointwise distributional convergence alone does not imply convergence in $d\_p$\. Finally, expected values satisfy a sharp Lipschitz estimate, and $d\_2$ reduces to the Euclidean distance between the location and scale parameters of normal uncertainty distributions\.
