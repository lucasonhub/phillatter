# A patenting perspective of IKZF2 molecular glue degraders \(2020\-present\)

**Autores:** Xuqing Zhang

**Data:** 2026-10-12 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

INTRODUCTION: IKZF2 \(Helios\) maintains the suppressive function of regulatory T cells \(Tregs\) and is an emerging immuno\-oncology target\. Its degradation destabilizes Tregs, strengthens antitumor immunity, and may remodel the tumor microenvironment \(TME\) with reduced off\-target toxicity\. Recent patents focus on selective cereblon \(CRBN\)\-recruiting molecular glues, combination therapies, and next\-generation degraders\. AREAS COVERED: SciFinder and Google Patents were searched for IKZF2 molecular glue degraders reported in patents and peer\-reviewed journals from January 2020 to September 2026\. EXPERT OPINION: The immunomodulatory drug \(IMiD\)\-based degrader field is increasingly competitive, with growing emphasis on selectivity, alternative ligases, and artificial intelligence\-enabled discovery\. Because small structural changes can strongly affect ternary\-complex formation and selectivity among IKZF proteins, structure\-guided optimization remains essential\. Dual degradation of IKZF2 and IKZF4 \(Eos\) may offer further opportunities, but programs must demonstrate differentiated biology, biomarker\-guided patient selection, and clear clinical value\.
