# IMPLEMENTASI BAHAN AJAR BERBASIS GAMIFIKASI DAN KECERDASAN ARTIFISIAL DENGAN PENDEKATAN PEMBELAJARAN MENDALAM DALAM MENGEMBANGKAN LITERASI SISWA SEKOLAH DASAR

**Autores:** Husnul Khatimah; Intan Fandini Intan Fandini; M Irmawati; Sulistiyawati Sulistiyawati; Nurpadila Nurpadila

**Data:** 2026-10-12 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

The integration of gamification and artificial intelligence \(AI\) into instructional materials offers opportunities to support more interactive reading literacy instruction\. This study examined changes in primary school students’ reading literacy after using gamification\- and AI\-based instructional materials informed by a pedagogical deep learning approach\. A quantitative approach with a pre\-experimental one\-group pretest–posttest design was employed\. Twenty\-one students from one class participated, while paired analyses included 20 students with complete pretest and posttest data\. Reading literacy was measured using a 10\-item test, with total scores ranging from 0 to 100\. Data were analysed using descriptive statistics, the Shapiro–Wilk test, the Wilcoxon signed\-rank test, paired rank\-biserial correlation, and normalised gain \(N\-gain\)\. Because the difference scores were not normally distributed, pretest–posttest differences were examined using the Wilcoxon signed\-rank test\. The mean score increased from 51\.05 \(SD = 17\.99\) at pretest to 84\.85 \(SD = 3\.76\) at posttest\. Posttest scores were significantly higher than pretest scores, W = 0, p &lt; \.001\. All students included in the paired analysis improved, yielding a paired rank\-biserial correlation of 1\.00\. The class\-level N\-gain was 0\.691, indicating a moderate improvement\. These findings provide preliminary evidence of improved reading literacy following the use of the instructional materials\. However, the absence of a control group limits causal attribution, and further evaluation using a more rigorous design is required\.
