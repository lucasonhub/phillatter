# The Teleology of Libido Regression: A Clinical and Archetypal Analysis of Symbolic Rebirth

**Autores:** Merab Kintsurashvili

**Data:** 2026-10-13 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Abstract The insidious collapse of a 38‐year‐old businessman’s hyper‐rational life into a midlife marital crisis compels his psyche to demand a terrifying return to its origins\. This paper maps his psychological rebirth by tracing the explicit structural parallels between the stages of the analytical process and the ancient Egyptian solar myth of transformation, illustrating how dream imagery shapes the analytic process\. Guided by a series of vivid, archetypal dreams, beginning with the scarab beetle \(Khepri\) signalling ego‐decapitation and the initiation of descent, the analytic encounter becomes a generative matrix for integration\. The narrative moves through the oppressive grip of the Kronos complex to a vital confrontation with a pregnant wolf that reawakens long‐repressed Eros\. The study further bridges clinical observation and neuro‐archetypal perspectives, linking primate neurobiology, specifically vocalisation patches and joint attention mechanisms, to the restoration of the ego‐Self axis\. As &quot;sacred chaos&quot; erupts through the nocturnal threshold of Egyptian solar baboons, the journey mirrors Ra&\#x27;s night sea journey, turning painful regression into living renewal\. Ultimately, through the unfolding dream imagery and the gentle emergence of the anima, the patient surrenders his defensive posture, discovering a deeper capacity for introverted feeling\. Bridging Jungian theory with contemporary neuro‐psychoanalysis, this study demonstrates that libido regression follows a purposeful, archetypally structured pathway toward new psychic connections\.
