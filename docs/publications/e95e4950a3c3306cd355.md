# AI Data Centers and Climate Change: Developing a Field Guide for Library and Archival Workers

**Autores:** Lisa Spiro; Sarah K\. Tribelhorn; Justin Wadland

**Data:** 2026-10-13 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

The DLF Climate Justice Working Group seeks feedback on a work\-in\-progress “field guide” for the digital library community to the climate implications of hyperscale data centers to advance artificial intelligence\. We will share what we’ve learned and discuss with participants how these impacts are showing up in our work\.
