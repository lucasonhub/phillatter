# Biodiversity Disclosure and Analyst Forecast Accuracy: Evidence From Chinese A‐Share Firms

**Autores:** Han Sun; Hyoung‐Goo Kang; Jae\-Ho Lee; Doojin Ryu

**Data:** 2026-10-13 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

ABSTRACT We examine whether biodiversity‐related disclosure in annual reports is associated with analyst forecast accuracy among Chinese A‐share firms\. Using 20,038 firm‐year observations from 2011 to 2023 and a dictionary‐based measure validated against independent manual coding, we find that greater disclosure intensity is associated with lower subsequent forecast error after accounting for firms&\#x27; broader ESG and textual‐reporting characteristics\. The negative association is evident in firm‐grouped cross‐fitted estimates and in forecasts issued within 30 days of annual‐report publication\. Evidence for forecast dispersion is weaker\.
