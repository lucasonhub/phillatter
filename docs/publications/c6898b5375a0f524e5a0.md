# Kadsura coccinea \(Schisandraceae\), a New Record for India From Arunachal Pradesh

**Autores:** Dipankar J\. Borah; Madhusudhan Khanal; Dixit Bora; Neelam Gap

**Data:** 2026-10-12 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

ABSTRACT Kadsura coccinea \(Lem\.\) A\.C\.Sm\. \(Schisandraceae\) is being reported here for the first time from India, based on a collection from Keyi Panyor district of Arunachal Pradesh\. A detailed taxonomic description, along with a photo plate and a comparison table with species known from India, has been provided to help in easy identification of this species\.
