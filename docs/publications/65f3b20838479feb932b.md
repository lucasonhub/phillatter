# Estimation of rubber yield on Hainan Island, China using multi\-source data during 2021\-2025

**Autores:** Ruofan Jing; 廖静娟; Li Zhang; 尧德明; X\. Liu

**Data:** 2026-10-17 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Rubber is both a strategic material and an essential industrial raw commodity, making accurate yield estimation critically important\. Current remote sensing\-based physical models require enhancement to converting potential yield into actual yield using limited field samples, while improving parameter applicability and estimation accuracy\. Integrating DEM, Sentinel\-2 imagery, and meteorological data, this study develops a multi\-stage framework that couple the CASA model, SOLARFLUX, a conversion module, and a parameter calibration scheme\. Calibrated and validated with field\-measured data, the model enables high\-resolution dynamic monitoring of dry rubber yield across Hainan Island\. Validation demonstrates strong model performance \(R2 &gt; 0\.95\), with relative error \(RE\) reduced from 21\.99 % to 16\.62 % after refinement\. The model substantially outperforms machine learning approaches\. Results reveal a year\-on\-year decline in total rubber yield from 2021 to 2023, followed by a marked rebound during the 2023\-2024\. The proposed framework offers a robust and practical solution for data‑scarce regions, and its products provide valuable support for the sustainable development and ecological monitoring of Hainan’s rubber industry\.
