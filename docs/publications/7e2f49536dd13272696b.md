# De Silva, Frank: Mind to mind?

**Autores:** 

**Data de descoberta:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** unknown

## Descrição do feed

This short essay establishes the fact that all human experiences is thoughts as they are a product of the brain and mind via a thought experiment\. This one fact implies that everything we know, space, time, hardness, weight etc are all thoughts in the context of our experience\. This observation leads to the question, do things in the world, that is outside our senses, exactly correspond to our experience of them\. It is noted that the definitive answer is no as flat surfaces, or straight lines do not exist\. Next the question, is the mind is a product of the brain or brain activity is considered\. It is noted that there is a singular property of the mind that of its ability to connect simultaneous events make it impossible to be a product of the brain\. On the basis of this evidence, it is suggested all material things such as atoms, molecules are thoughts in The Mind and they are used to create visible things such as mountain which are also Thoughts in The Mind and will again look like mountain when recreated in other minds\. The atoms and molecules acting like the transmission from a TV station and the screen like other minds\. A mind is needed to translate the thought to completion\. Finally, it is show that the set of all thoughts or the universal set of thoughts has the properties of God\.
