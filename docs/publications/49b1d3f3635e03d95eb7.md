# Ascent Conditions for Length\-Normalized Single\-Rollout Policy Updates

**Autores:** Levin David Schwab

**Data:** 2026-10-10 · **Tipo:** preprint

## Resumo

Dividing each trajectory&\#x27;s score contribution by its realized length can change the direction of a reward\-centered policy update\. In a finite trajectory model, we derive an exact ascent threshold for a three\-outcome softmax policy and exhibit a binary\-reward example in which the expected Euclidean update decreases task success\. Natural\-gradient ascent holds for fully controllable outcome distributions with deterministic binary labels, but can fail under a one\-parameter restriction\. A third\-order U\-statistic estimates the first\-order return change without bias from independent current\-policy trajectories, one per request\. In the example, a same\-sample plug\-in estimator instead has the opposite sign in expectation for batch sizes two through forty\-nine\. Exact policy\-grid enumeration confirms the local sign reversal\. In a branching process, length\-normalized learning initially loses return, then recovers and exceeds the unnormalized comparator\. The analysis concerns the expected update field at the current policy\.
