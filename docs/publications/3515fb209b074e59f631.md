# INNOVATSION TA’LIM MUHITINI YARATISH ASOSIDA TA’LIM OLUVCHILARNING KREATIVLIGINI RIVOJLANTIRISH

**Autores:** Gulimatova Dilobar Davronbek qizi

**Data:** 2026-10-12 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

This article examines the pedagogical and psychological characteristics of developing learners&\#x27; creativity through the creation of an innovative educational environment, the factors influencing the formation of creative thinking, the didactic potential of modern educational technologies, and the scientific and methodological foundations for stimulating creative activity\. Furthermore, the study analyzes the opportunities for developing learners&\#x27; independent thinking, creative initiative, and ability to find unconventional solutions to problems through innovative pedagogical approaches, evaluates changes in creativity levels based on the statistical indicators of pedagogical research, and develops practical recommendations aimed at improving the effectiveness of the educational process\.
