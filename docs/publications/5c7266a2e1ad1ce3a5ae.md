# Gadolinium\-Based Contrast Agent Use in MRI and Projected Gadolinium Discharge: A Ten\-Country Modeling Study to 2040

**Autores:** Hakan Ayyıldız; Şükrü Mehmet Ertürk

**Data:** 2026-10-12 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Objective: Gadolinium\-based contrast agents are excreted essentially unmetabolized and pass\-through conventional wastewater treatment, making magnetic resonance imaging \(MRI\) the dominant source of anthropogenic environmental gadolinium\.We projected national gadolinium discharge from MRI through 2040 for ten countries under business\-as\-usual \(BAU\) and stewardship scenarios\.Methods: Using publicly available aggregate data \[Organisation for Economic Co\-operation and Development\-\(OECD\), Türkiye Ministry of Health, World Bank\], the annual elemental gadolinium discharge was estimated for the United States, United Kingdom, Germany, France, Italy, Spain, the Netherlands, Austria, Chile, and Türkiye from national MRI volumes, a contrast\-enhanced fraction of 0\.375, and a mean dose of 7\.5 mmol per enhanced examination\.Utilisation was extrapolated linearly to 2040, excluding data from 2020, under three scenarios: BAU; substitution with low\-dose, high\-relaxivity agents \(3\.75 mmol; logistic adoption, ceiling 0\.70\); and substitution plus contrast avoidance \(up to 20% reduction in the enhanced fraction\)\.Sensitivity analyses varied the contrast fraction, dose, and adoption ceilings\.Results: The ten\-country aggregate discharge for the latest available year is approximately 43\.5 t/year \(sensitivity range: 28\-63 t\)\.The United States contributes the largest absolute load \(15\.8 t/year\), while Türkiye has the highest per capita burden among the included countries \(108 mg/person/year, more than double that of the United States\)\.Under BAU, discharge rises in all countries, reaching 69\.3 t/year by 2040\.Combined stewardship produces an aggregate peak around 2028\-2029 and reduces the 2040 discharge to 37\.1 t/year, representing an avoidable load of 32\.2 t/year \(\~46%\)\.Conclusion: MRI\-derived gadolinium discharge is projected to continue to rise through 2040 under the assumed linear\-utilisation model unless low\-dose agents and contrast avoidance are adopted\.Stewardship could avoid nearly half of the projected load, making the radiology department the practical point of intervention\.
