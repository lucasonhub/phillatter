# GENERATIV SUN’IY INTELLEKT TEXNOLOGIYALARI VOSITASIDA TALABALARNING MUSTAQIL TA’LIM FAOLIYATINI RIVOJLANTIRISHNING PEDAGOGIK\-METODIK IMKONIYATLARI

**Autores:** DILAFRUZ RUSTAM QIZI NAIMOVA

**Data:** 2026-10-09 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

This article theoretically examines the pedagogical and methodological opportunities, organizational mechanisms, and didactic conditions for developing students’ independent learning activities in vocational education institutions through the use of generative artificial intelligence technologies\. The research findings present methodological recommendations developed on the basis of theoretical analysis and substantiate the need for further experimental studies to determine their empirical effectiveness\.
