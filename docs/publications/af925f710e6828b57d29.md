# The Impact of Artificial Intelligence on Writing Skills for First\-Year English Department Students at the University of Kerbala

**Autores:** Huda Jalail Kadhim; Manar Amer Saleh

**Data:** 2026-10-15 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Writing is the most important language skill\. It requires experiences of grammar, vocabulary, coherence, and logical organization\. With the development of combining technology and education, Artificial Intelligence \(AI\) tools have become necessary for English as a Foreign Language \(EFL\) writing instruction\. The study explains how the students at a University of Kerbala, College of Education for the Humanities, First\-Year English Department, realize the benefits and drawbacks of using AI technologies to improve their writing skills
