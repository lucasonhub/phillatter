# STATISTICAL ANALYSIS OF SIGNAL PROCESSING TECHNIQUES FOR DRONE COMMUNICATION SYSTEMS

**Autores:** Taqi Mohammed Khattab Al\-Rubaye

**Data:** 2026-10-10 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

The technology of unmanned aerial vehicles \(UAVs\), or drones, has witnessed tremendous advancements in recent years, making them indispensable tools across various applications, including transportation\. These aircraft are characterized by their mobility and flexible deployment, making them an ideal choice for enhancing the efficiency and safety of transportation systems in Iraq\. Importance of UAVs in Iraq Infrastructure Improvement:\* UAVs can be utilized for inspecting and maintaining infrastructure, such as roads and bridges, which helps reduce costs and improve safety\. Disaster Management: UAVs can play a crucial role in disaster management, as they can provide accurate and rapid information about affected areas, aiding in directing rescue and relief efforts\. UAVs can be employed for security surveillance of vital installations and border areas, contributing to enhanced security and crime prevention\. Logistical Delivery: UAVs can be used for delivering goods and parcels in remote and distant areas, which helps improve logistical services and reduce costs\. Challenges of UAV Utilization in Iraq: Infrastructure Iraq needs to develop advanced infrastructure to support the use of UAVs, including communication and air navigation systems\. Iraq needs to establish clear legislation and laws that regulate the use of UAVs, including specifying permitted and prohibited areas, and setting safety and security rules\. Iraq needs to train and qualify specialized personnel in operating and maintaining UAVs\. Statistical Analysis of Signal Processing Techniquesو Signal analysis is considered a fundamental element in UAV communication systems, as it helps improve communication quality and reduce noise and interference\. This analysis aims to study and evaluate various signal processing techniques used in these systems, and determine the best ones for use in Iraq\. Study Objectives, Identify Best Signal Processing Techniques:\* The study aims to identify the best signal processing techniques that can be used in UAV communication systems in Iraq, taking into account local conditions and existing challenges\. The study aims to improve the quality of communication in UAV systems, and reduce noise and interference\. The study aims to develop local capacities in the field of UAV technology, and qualify specialized personnel in this field\.
