# Artificial intelligence and machine learning in contemporary neuroeconomics: Data analysis and modeling of economic behavior

**Autores:** Maxim V\. Fedorov; Ольга Юрьевна Клевцова; Сергей Александрович Игнатьев

**Data:** 2026-10-14 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Aim\. To systematize contemporary understandings of neuroeconomics as an interdisciplinary field of research, and to determine the role of artificial intelligence \(AI\) and machine learning technologies in analyzing economic behavior based on a comparison of international and Russian studies\. Objectives\. To trace the genesis and key theoretical models of neuroeconomics; to characterize the state and specifics of neuroeconomic research by Russian scholars; to systematize the main research directions on individual, social, and financial economic behavior conducted by international authors; to examine the forms of AI application both as a data analysis tool and as an independent computational model of decision\-making; to compare the degree of maturity and institutional support of the areas under consideration\. Methods\. A qualitative comparative\-analytical review of peer\-reviewed international and Russian research on neuroeconomics, computational neuroscience, behavioral economics, and the application of AI in the analysis of economic behavior is employed\. The sources were selected by the authors according to their relevance to three thematic blocks: theoretical and methodological foundations of neuroeconomics, empirical studies of the neural mechanisms of choice, and works on the application of machine learning to the analysis and modeling of economic behavior\. The comparison of international and Russian directions is conducted according to the criteria of theoretical foundation, empirical methods, and the degree of integration of AI and machine learning\. Resul ts It is established that neuroeconomics is emerging as an independent discipline that explains decision\-making through the interaction of automatic and controlled neural processes\. International researchers take into account social preferences and financial behavior, while Russian authors focus predominantly on social influence, conformity, rehabilitation neurotechnologies, and are still weakly integrated into economic theory\. The dual role of AI as a data analysis tool and a computational model of choice is demonstrated, and the ethical frameworks for the application of neurotechnologies and AI in economic research are examined \. Conclusions\. Neuroeconomics represents a mature interdisciplinary field with a pronounced asymmetry between international and Russian research in terms of integration into economic theory\. AI enhances the analytical capabilities of neuroeconomics but requires methodological caution and institutional ethical regulation\. A promising direction for development is the creation in Russia of specialized interdisciplinary programs that combine economics, neuroscience, and data analysis
