# INTEGRATION OF GENERATIVE AI INTO DIGITAL MARKETING VIDEO PRODUCTION: AN APPLIED SYSTEMATIZATION AND USE SCENARIOS

**Autores:** Яна Сало; Viktor Fedirko

**Data:** 2026-10-09 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

The rapid development of generative artificial intelligence is changing not only video generation itself but also the organization of digital marketing video production\. The relevance of the study is determined by the growing need for scalable content production, localization, personalization and continuous creative adaptation, while existing research tends to examine either technical video\-generation architectures or separate marketing applications\. The purpose of the article is to systematize the applied formats of generative AI use in digital marketing video production and relate them to the stages of the marketing workflow and practical use scenarios\. The study applies analysis and synthesis, comparison, systematization and conceptual generalization\. Peer\-reviewed publications and official materials of developers and digital platforms published mainly between 2022 and July 2026 were selected according to their direct relevance to video generation, AI\-assisted production and digital marketing applications\. The study does not aim to provide an exhaustive ranking or chronology of rapidly changing models\. As the main scientific result, a two\-level applied systematization is proposed\. The first level includes four modality\-oriented generation formats: text\-to\-video, image\-to\-video, text\-and\-image\-to\-video and video\-to\-video\. The second level includes two functional application formats: avatar\-based video and AI\-assisted video editing\. This distinction avoids treating categories from different classification levels as a homogeneous technical taxonomy\. The formats are mapped to a marketing video workflow covering ideation, scripting, prompting, generation, evaluation, editing, adaptation, publication and performance analysis\. A matrix of practical scenarios is also developed for advertising creatives, short\-form vertical video, UGC\-like communication, e\-commerce product presentation, explanatory content, localization, personalization, prototyping and cross\-channel adaptation\. The analysis further shows that generative AI is increasingly embedded in professional editing software, mass\-market content tools and advertising platforms, reducing the technological separation between production and distribution\. The practical value of the results lies in supporting the choice of an appropriate format according to available source materials, communication objectives, required product fidelity and visual control, production scale and channel requirements\. The findings also emphasize that automated generation should be combined with human verification when factual accuracy, brand consistency or authentic representation of a product is important\.
