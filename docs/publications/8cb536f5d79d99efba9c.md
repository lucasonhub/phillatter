# Morozov, Konstantin: Dilemma for Strong Illusionism in the Philosophy of Mind

**Autores:** 

**Data de descoberta:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** unknown

## Descrição do feed

In Philosophy in the 21st Century: An Interdisciplinary Dialogue\. Moscow: National Research University of Electronic Technology\. pp\. 145\-151\. 2026Phenomenal consciousness is often defined as the capacity for subjective experience\. Strong illusionism is a physicalist theory of consciousness, according to which phenomenal consciousness does not exist, but is merely an introspective illusion of its presence\. This view is countered by the argument that introspective illusion is itself a subjective experience, and therefore strong illusionism is incoherent\. In response, illusionists distinguish phenomenality from subjectivity\. But this approach leaves them with a dilemma\. If illusionists accept the possibility of a theoretically unburdened concept of subjectivity, they are forced to abandon antirealism and embrace weak illusionism\. If they reject such a concept of subjectivity, they must embrace a more radical eliminative materialism\. \(direct link \)
