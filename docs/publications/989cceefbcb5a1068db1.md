# Pengaruh Voluntary Disclosure, Income Smoothing dan Pertumbuhan Laba Terhadap Kualitas Laba

**Autores:** Widya Eka Utami; Abdullah Mubarok

**Data:** 2026-10-13 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Penelitian ini bertujuan untuk menganalisis pengaruh voluntary disclosure, income smoothing, dan pertumbuhan laba terhadap kualitas laba\. Sampel penelitian terdiri dari 27 perusahaan sektor consumer non\-cyclicals yang terdaftar di Bursa Efek Indonesia \(BEI\) periode 2020–2024, yang dipilih menggunakan teknik purposive sampling\. Data sekunder berupa laporan keuangan tahunan dianalisis menggunakan EViews 12 dengan Common Effect Model \(CEM\) sebagai model terbaik\. Hasil penelitian menunjukkan bahwa secara parsial, voluntary disclosure dan income smoothing tidak berpengaruh terhadap kualitas laba, sedangkan pertumbuhan laba berpengaruh signifikan terhadap kualitas laba\. Namun secara simultan, ketiga variabel independen tersebut berpengaruh signifikan terhadap kualitas laba\. This study aims to analyze the effect of voluntary disclosure, income smoothing, and earnings growth on earnings quality\. The research sample consists of 27 consumer non\-cyclicals sector companies listed on the Indonesia Stock Exchange \(IDX\) during the 2020–2024 period, selected using a purposive sampling technique\. Secondary data in the form of annual financial statements were analyzed using EViews 12, with the Common Effect Model \(CEM\) identified as the best\-fit model\. The results indicate that partially, voluntary disclosure and income smoothing have no significant effect on earnings quality, whereas earnings growth has a significant effect on earnings quality\. However, simultaneously, voluntary disclosure, income smoothing, and earnings growth significantly affect earnings quality\.
