# Target\-focused supervision and fine spatial evidence improve sparse landslide detection in remote\-sensing imagery

**Autores:** Changhao Lin; Sai Liang; Hongjie Qin; Qinsonghao Zheng; Jianghai Shen

**Data:** 2026-10-14 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Introduction Regional landslide screening must retrieve sparsely annotated failures among visually similar terrain while limiting false review candidates\. Small scars can lose spatial detail during downsampling, and extensive background regions can dominate semantic supervision\. Methods We developed the Sparse Landslide Detection Transformer \(SLD\-DETR\) by adapting RT\-DETRv4\. It combines scale\-guided focused distillation \(SGFD\), which concentrates teacher–student alignment near annotated landslides; high\-resolution scale\-aware supervision \(HRSS\), which supervises stride\-4 features; and stride\-4 decoder memory \(S2\-Mem\), which exposes fine spatial features to query selection and cross\-attention\. We evaluated five independent training seeds on a source\-scene\-disjoint 6:2:2 split of the Multi\-Scale Landslide Dataset \(MSLD\)\. For screening performance, confidence thresholds were calibrated on validation to a false\-positive rate of no more than 0\.10 false positives per image \(FPPI\), then frozen for testing\. We also assessed zero\-shot transfer to LRSTTC\-New\. Results On the held\-out MSLD test split, SLD\-DETR achieved a mean average precision of 58\.07 ± 1\.10 % across IoU thresholds 0\.50–0\.95 \(mAP@50:95\) and 63\.43 ± 1\.23 % mAP@75, compared with 53\.90 ± 0\.96 % and 58\.34 ± 2\.65 % , respectively, for RT\-DETRv4\-S\. At the validation\-calibrated operating points, test miss rate was 15\.82 ± 2\.18% for SLD\-DETR and 18\.63 ± 0\.99% for the baseline\. Under zero\-shot cross\-region transfer, all detectors degraded substantially; SLD\-DETR had the highest AP within the RT\-DETRv4 family, while RF\-DETR\-S performed best overall\. Discussion The coordinated modules improved box\-level screening on the evaluated source\-disjoint MSLD split\. SGFD and HRSS are training\-only, whereas S2\-Mem remains at inference and adds approximately 0\.06 million parameters\. Runtime and memory costs increase with image resolution\.
