# Adopting Smart Technologies for Sustainable Tourism: Challenges and Opportunities for Nashik Kumbh Mela

**Autores:** Chandrashekhar Pawar; Yogesh Gaikwad

**Data:** 2026-10-15 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Adopting Smart Technologies for Sustainable Tourism: Challenges and Opportunities for Nashik Kumbh Mela Chandrashekhar Pawar Research Scholar, MET’s Institute of Management \(Affiliated to Savitribai Phule Pune University\), Nashik, Maharashtra, India Dr\. Yogesh Gaikwad Professor &amp; Research Guide, MET’s Institute of Management \(Affiliated to Savitribai Phule Pune University\), Nashik, Maharashtra, India Abstract Tourism is the key tool for socio\-economic development\. The Nashik Kumbh Mela is one of India’s major religious gatherings, attracting millions of pilgrims and creating substantial demands on transportation, sanitation, accommodation, public safety, waste management, and environmental resources\. This study examines the adoption of smart technologies for promoting sustainable tourism during the Nashik Kumbh Mela, with particular emphasis on both opportunities and implementation challenges\. Technologies such as artificial intelligence, Internet of Things \(IoT\), smart mobility systems, GIS\-based crowd monitoring, digital payment platforms, mobile applications, and data analytics can improve visitor management, resource efficiency, safety, and tourist experiences\. Their integration may also support waste reduction, traffic control, energy conservation, and effective destination governance\. The inadequate digital infrastructure, implementation costs, cybersecurity risks, digital literacy gaps, interoperability issues, and stakeholder coordination remain significant challenges\. The study highlights the need for an integrated smart\-tourism framework combining technological innovation, environmental sustainability, inclusive governance, and community participation to facilitate efficient and sustainable management of the Nashik Kumbh Mela\. Keywords: Smart Tourism, Sustainable Tourism, Nashik Kumbh Mela, Smart Technologies, Artificial Intelligence, IoT, Crowd Management, Destination Governance
