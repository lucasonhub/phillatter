# Uncertainty‑weighted neural networks in materials testing: Application to asphalt mixture volumetric properties

**Autores:** Mohammad Abbas; Mārtiņš Zaumanis

**Data:** 2026-10-09 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Machine learning is increasingly used to predict material properties, yet measurement uncertainty defined in test standards is rarely incorporated into model development or evaluation\. In this paper we address that gap by proposing an uncertainty\-weighted neural network \(UWNN\) to predict the bulk density and air voids of asphalt mixtures\. First, a large dataset comprising 3981 Swiss Marshall mixture test results was assembled for model development, then preprocessed and subjected to correlation analysis\. Next, the uncertainty\-weighted neural network was introduced, integrating test uncertainty into both the neural network loss function and the performance evaluation metric\. Bayesian optimization was then applied to a classical artificial neural network \(ANN\), and its performance was compared with that of the UWNN\. Results showed that, on the full dataset, the UWNN provided only a slight improvement over the ANN, with both achieving reproducibility\-adjusted R\-squared values close to 0\.95\. The UWNN was also able to predict air voids with similarly high coverage of 95%\. The UWNN was more robust than the ANN when training samples with high measurement uncertainty were corrupted, showing statistically significant improvements at 10% and 20% corruption levels according to both paired t\-tests and Wilcoxon signed\-rank tests\. The proposed approach is simple to implement, does not reduce predictive performance, and offers a practical pathway for incorporating measurement uncertainty into material testing models\.
