# ALISHER NAVOIYNING LIRIK MEROSI VA SOQIYNOMA JANRINING O‘RNI

**Autores:** DURDONA KOMIL QIZI RAJABOVA

**Data:** 2026-10-12 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

To ensure that Alisher Navoi&\#x27;s soqiynomas are understandable and readable, grammatical labeling and lexical and grammatical analysis are necessary\. The lexicology of the period in which Navoi lived and the current lexicon, the emergence of archaic and historicist words and the radical change in the meanings of many words, the fact that words that had different morphological characteristics during Navoi&\#x27;s time have undergone a change in meaning, narrowed in meaning, or have taken on a different meaning of the word today, indicate the importance of grammatical analysis of Navoi&\#x27;s soqiynomas\. This article provides a scholarly analysis of the lyrical heritage of Alisher Navoiy, a prominent figure in 15th\-century Uzbek classical literature, and the literary and aesthetic role of the “Soqiynoma” genre\. The study highlights the genre scope, thematic range, and artistic features of the poet&\#x27;s lyrics, and substantiates its incomparable role in the development of Turkic poetry\. In particular, the genres of the collection “Khazoyin ul\-maoniy”, the traditions of ghazal writing, and the mystical interpretation of the concept of love are analyzed\. The article examines the process of formation, symbolic\-figurative system, and ideological direction of the “Soqiynoma” genre in Eastern literature, highlighting its comparative aspects with the works of Nizami Ganjavi and Hafiz Shirazi\. It is concluded that Navoi’s “Soqiynoma” not only continued the traditions of the genre, but also enriched it with deep socio\-philosophical content and occupies a special place in Uzbek literature as a perfect example\.
