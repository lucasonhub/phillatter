# Injection‐Related Pain During Nemolizumab Treatment With Standardized Vibration‐and‐Cooling Analgesia in Children With Atopic Dermatitis: A Real‐World Observational Study

**Autores:** Tomoaki Kunitsu; Kana Hamanaka; Midori Tagaya; Kazuo Nonomura

**Data:** 2026-10-12 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

BACKGROUND/OBJECTIVES: Injection pain and needle\-related anxiety may limit acceptance and continuation of biologic therapy in children with atopic dermatitis \(AD\)\. Real\-world data on injection pain during nemolizumab treatment is limited\. We aimed to longitudinally quantify injection pain during nemolizumab treatment under standardized vibration\-and\-cooling analgesia and evaluate early clinical outcomes\. METHODS: This retrospective observational cohort study enrolled children aged 6\-12 years with moderate\-to\-severe AD who had completed 16 weeks of nemolizumab treatment \(30 mg every 4 weeks\) between June 2024 and July 2025\. All injections were administered with standardized vibration\-and\-cooling analgesia\. Injection pain was assessed immediately after each injection using the Wong\-Baker FACES Pain Rating Scale \(0\-10\)\. Secondary outcomes included the Peak Itch Numeric Rating Scale \(NRS\), Patient\-Oriented Eczema Measure \(POEM\), and Eczema Area and Severity Index \(EASI\) scores\. RESULTS: The 13 children provided 62 of 65 planned pain ratings\. Injection pain was consistently minimal \(median 0; interquartile range \[IQR\] 0\-2\); no child reported severe pain \(score ≥ 8\)\. At Week 16, the median EASI reduction was 87\.5% \(IQR, 73\.7\-90\.3\); EASI\-75 was achieved by 9 of 13 \(69\.2%\) participants\. Median peak itch NRS improved by 5\.0 points \(IQR, 3\.0\-6\.0\), and median POEM improved by 7\.0 points \(IQR, 4\.0\-12\.0\)\. No serious adverse events occurred\. CONCLUSION: Under standardized vibration\-and\-cooling analgesia, nemolizumab administration was associated with minimal injection pain and substantial early clinical improvement; these findings do not represent the intrinsic injection pain of nemolizumab without analgesia\.
