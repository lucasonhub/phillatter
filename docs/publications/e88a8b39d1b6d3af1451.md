# EFL Learner Engagement With CALL

**Autores:** Amna Mohammed AlSaadi; John Mathew; Amal Salim Ali Al Sheidi; Sree Lakshmi Ammanamanchi; Salwa Al Hadidi

**Data:** 2026-10-15 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

This mixed\-methods study investigated Omani EFL university students&\#x27; engagement, motivation, and perceptions of the Spark digital platform in a blended learning context\. Using Self\-Determination Theory \(SDT\), the Technology Acceptance Model \(TAM\), and the Motivated Strategies for Learning Questionnaire \(MSLQ\) frameworks, the study analyzed survey data, platform analytics, and focus group discussions from 100 students\. The results showed that extrinsic, grade\-oriented motivation mainly led to moderate engagement\. Although analytics suggested high activity, qualitative findings revealed frustration, redundancy, and time pressure\. Students reported moderate levels of satisfaction\. The study identifies an engagement\-compliance paradox, where observable use masks low intrinsic motivation, and recommends a shift to structured in\-class use, targeted curriculum alignment, and AI\-powered feedback to achieve meaningful and sustained learner engagement\.
