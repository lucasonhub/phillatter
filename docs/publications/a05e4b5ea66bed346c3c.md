# Sheikh, Aarez: The Algorithmic Architecture of Analytic Idealism: A Three\-Tiered Model of Conscious Reality and Vibrational Resonance

**Autores:** 

**Data de descoberta:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** unknown

## Descrição do feed

This paper proposes a systematic refinement of Analytic Idealism by introducing a nested, algorithmic framework for reality generation\. Rather than treating physical reality as an unyielding, materialistic machine or a chaotic mental illusion, we model the cosmos as a singular Super\-Mind operating a dynamic, software\-like environment\. Individual conscious entities function as localised sub\-minds \(psychic alters\) possessing varying administrative privileges\. We outline a strict three\-tier taxonomy of reality: Level 1 \(The Local Overwrite\), Level 2 \(Social Consensus/Delayed Synchronisation\), and Level 3 \(The Root Code\)\. Crucially, we introduce cosmic vibrations as the fundamental data transmission protocol linking these nodes, and explore how modern digital networks act as cybernetic amplifiers that accelerate reality updates\. Finally, we account for the conservation of unshared data, arguing that private ideas remain permanently vaulted within the Super\-Mind&\#x27;s hardware upon physical dissolution\. This model resolves long\-standing critiques of idealism by explaining the robust solidity of physical laws as the compounding mathematical and historical consensus of billions of converging conscious nodes, while preserving the capacity for localised, mind\-driven updates to the cosmic framework\.
