# Bower, Matt: The Phenomenology of Self\-Conscious Perception

**Autores:** 

**Data de descoberta:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** unknown

## Descrição do feed

When I perceive, I not only experience the scene before me, but also my seeing of that scene\. I perceive self\-consciously\. Classical phenomenologists understand self\-consciousness as intrinsic to the experience, not the result of a separate, self\-monitoring state\. They also suppose that self\-consciousness is different in kind from the consciousness I have of the scene before me\. The latter is an intentional act, the former isn’t\. This conception, which I take as my starting point, faces two difficulties\. First, perception primarily reveals the world, not itself\. It is ‘transparent\.’ If that’s so, it is unclear what exactly I might be aware of in being aware of my experience\. Second, granting that no separate mental state is needed to render perception self\-conscious and that its self\-consciousness isn’t a form of self\-observation, it remains mysterious what does make perception self\-conscious\. I critically review extant proposals and present an alternative, inspired by both classical phenomenology and recent work\. Both difficulties can be addressed, I argue, by appreciating the normative character of perception, that part of perceiving is experiencing how well I am perceiving in bringing discriminative and recognitional capacities to bear on the world\.
