# Bringing Organization Back In: Rethinking Technology–Organization Nexus in Digital Government

**Autores:** Yinhao Yang

**Data:** 2026-10-15 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Digital government scholarship has become increasingly sophisticated about technology, yet surprisingly thin about organization\. Recent studies have shown how algorithms shape administrative discretion, how artificial intelligence changes public decision\-making, and how digital systems affect frontline work, accountability, and public values\. These advances matter\. But they also reveal a conceptual imbalance\. Too often, digital transformation is treated as the introduction of a new technology into an otherwise stable administrative setting\. When performance changes, the technology receives the explanatory credit; when implementation fails, organizational capacity is added as a contextual condition\. This perspective argues for a different starting point: digital government should be understood as a process of organizational transformation produced through the interaction between technology and organization\. Put simply, we need to bring organization back into digital government research\.
