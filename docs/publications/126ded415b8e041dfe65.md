# Tourism, Transport and Travel Management

**Autores:** M\. R\. Dileep

**Data:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** book

## Resumo

Fully revised and updated in its second edition, Tourism, Transport and Travel Management analyses the structure, functions, activities, strategies and practices of each of the sectors in the travel industry, including airlines, airports, tour operators, travel agencies and cruises\. The book comprises a six\-part structure, covering air, land, and water transportation, travel intermediation, the tour operation business, and impacts and prospects for the future\. Updated content featured in the second edition includes geographical perspectives on tourism transport, nuances of the intricate relationship between air transport and tourism, in\-depth coverage of sustainability, and the impact of COVID\-19 on transport and travel operations\. Written in an accessible and engaging style, this is an invaluable resource for students, scholars and academics within the fields of tourism, hospitality, transport and travel management\.
