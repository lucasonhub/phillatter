# Bulgaria, Jamel &amp; Xǔ, Chénglán: Pedagogical Affective Structuralism: A Comprehensive Functional\-Computational Analysis of the Nurture Paradigm within the Core Emotion Framework

**Autores:** 

**Data de descoberta:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** unknown

## Descrição do feed

The field of affective science has been historically polarized between discrete emotion theories and dimensional or constructionist models, producing a century long theoretical impasse\. The Core Emotion Framework \(CEF\), developed by Jamel Bulgaria and expanded mathematically by Xǔ Chénglán, offers a structural constructivist resolution by modeling emotional life as dynamic configurations of ten irreducible functional operators distributed across three somatic centers—Head, Heart, and Gut\. As stated in the manuscript, “the CEF proposes that human emotional experiences…are constructed from a finite set of ten irreducible ‘primal powers’” and that these operators form “a top down, mathematically tractable sequence for healthy psychological flow” \(document\)\. This article extends the CEF into developmental and pedagogical domains, demonstrating how a teacher’s moment to moment execution of operators constructs the child’s regulatory architecture\. We formally decompose the pedagogical loop—diagnostic perception, commitment, relational calibration, executive coordination, and environmental output—into operator level state vectors\. We further integrate structural psychopathology \(e\.g\., the GoodPerson Anxiety Pattern\) and somatic intervention protocols \(e\.g\., the 7 Step Detangling Method\) to show how maladaptive operator fusions disrupt developmental trajectories\. Finally, we situate these analyses within the OSF registered empirical validation program, which tests the ten factor operator structure using vignette based psychometrics and confirmatory factor modeling\. This synthesis positions the CEF as a unified computational somatic architecture for understanding nurture, regulation, and affective development\.
