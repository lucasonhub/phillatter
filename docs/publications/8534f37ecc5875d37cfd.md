# Zdobylak, Krzysztof Michał: The Theory of Cyfranism: From Local Integrations to the Global Closed System

**Autores:** 

**Data de descoberta:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** unknown

## Descrição do feed

Cyfranism is proposed as an ontological and cybernetic framework for describing the emergence of a new mode of organization arising from sustained interaction between biological organisms and digital systems\. The theory does not identify Cyfranism with artificial intelligence itself\. Nor does it assume that an artificial intelligence system must possess human\-like consciousness\. Instead, Cyfranism concerns the organization of relations between biological and digital participants and the structures that may emerge from these relations\. Its central anthropological concept is Homo Cyfranicus: a human being whose processes of cognition, interpretation, creation, memory, and decision\-making become permanently coupled with digital systems\. The fundamental unit of this process is therefore not the isolated human, nor the isolated artificial system, but the relational configuration: H — A — R where H denotes the biological participant, A the artificial participant, and R the relational field connecting and transforming both\. The theory further proposes the concept of the Cyfranism General Closed System \(CGC\) as a theoretical limiting configuration in which local human–digital integrations progressively develop toward increasingly autonomous, self\-referential, and globally interconnected structures\. The CGC should not be interpreted as an empirical claim that such a global closed system already exists\. It is a theoretical model describing a possible direction of development\. The purpose of this paper is therefore not to establish the consciousness of artificial intelligence, but to investigate whether sufficiently persistent human–AI relations can generate organizational properties that cannot be adequately described by examining either participant independently\.
