# 3D Digital Modeling Empowering Sculpture Education

**Autores:** Lukai Li

**Data:** 2026-10-14 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

This paper explores the application of 3D modeling software in sculpture education and proposes a data\-driven framework called “Adaptive Sculpture\-3D,” aimed at addressing the lack of real\-time feedback and the challenges of cognitive load management in traditional teaching\. Through case analysis and quantitative experiments, the study validates the effectiveness of the framework in improving students&\#x27; creation accuracy \(with a 21\.8% reduction in vertex error rate\) and self\-efficacy \(medium effect size of 0\.74\), while also revealing limitations in enhancing operational efficiency \(effect size of 0\.38\)\. The experimental results demonstrate that the real\-time feedback mechanism of the framework significantly optimizes the learning process, though caution is needed to avoid learner dependency caused by excessive prompts\. This research provides empirical support for the digital curriculum design in art institutions and envisions future developments integrating generative artificial intelligence and multimodal data\.
