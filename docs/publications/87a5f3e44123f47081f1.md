# ประสทธผลของโปรแกรมกระตนศกยภาพสมองตอการทำหนาทดานการรคด ของผสงอายทมภาวะการรคดบกพรองเลกนอย: การศกษาตดตามผลระยะยาว

**Autores:** Jiraporn Sripalakich; Saowaluk Netchang

**Data:** 2026-10-12 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

BACKGROUND: Mild cognitive impairment is common among older adults and a significant risk factor for future dementia\. Cognitive stimulation activities may help promote and maintain cognitive function and delay cognitive decline\. OBJECTIVES: To compare cognitive function scores among older adults with mild cognitive impairment before intervention baseline, after a 2\-week intervention, and at 3\- and 6\-month follow\-ups\. METHODS: This quasi\-experimental study used a one\-group pretest\-posttest design with long\-term follow\-up\. The sample included 30 older adults with mild cognitive impairment from the catchment area of Phai Lom Sub\-district Health Promoting Hospital, Lap Lae District, Uttaradit Province, who met the inclusion criteria assessed by the MoCA, TGDS\-15, and ADL scales\. Data were collected using a demographic questionnaire and the MMSE\-Thai 2002 to assess cognitive function\. The cognitive stimulation program &quot;Challenge Your Mind,&quot; developed based on the concept of Spector et al\., covered all six cognitive domains and was delivered over 2 weeks, 3 times per week, with 45\-60 minutes per session\. Data were analyzed using repeated\-measures ANOVA with Bonferroni\-corrected post hoc tests\. RESULTS: Cognitive function scores differed significantly across time points \(F=28\.64, p&lt;0\.001\)\. Mean scores rose from 16\.8±3\.4 at baseline to 21\.3±3\.8 after the 2\-week intervention and remained significantly higher than baseline at the 3\-month \(20\.7±3\.9\) and 6\-month \(19\.9±4\.1\) follow\-ups\. The program demonstrated a large effect size \(partial eta squared=0\.50\)\. CONCLUSIONS: The &quot;Tha Khwam Khit&quot; \(Challenge Your Mind\) program effectively improved and sustained cognitive function among older adults with mild cognitive impairment for up to 6 months\. It can be applied in healthcare settings and communities to prevent and delay dementia\. However, because this study used a one\-group pretest\-posttest design without a control group, along with purposive sampling and a relatively small sample size, further studies with a control group and larger sample size are warranted to confirm the effectiveness of this innovation before broader application\.
