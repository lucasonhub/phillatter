## Descrição do feed

\_Zenodo\_ 1\. 2026This paper examines the scope of Kantian transcendental synthesis through the Transcendental Deduction, the Schematism, and the Principles of Pure Understanding, with particular attention to the Second Analogy and the determination of objective temporal succession\. It develops an operational reconstruction of the relation between transcendental conditions of objectively valid experience and the empirical identification of events across heterogeneous observational arrangements\. While Kantian principles concern the objective temporal determination of experience, the identification of a common event across independently configured scientific systems requires additional empirical constraints\. The paper investigates how uncertain event identifications can guide interventions that subsequently modify observational conditions, evidential accessibility, and the possibilities of further scientific inquiry\. These processes are examined through the framework of real operational shapes, reality penetration, and Xenoscience\. Three hypothetical cases address variable generation, heterogeneous temporal records, and the persistence of operational consequences following epistemic correction\. The philosophical analysis is supplemented by a PyTorch synthetic experiment comprising 90,000 trials across nine conditions\. The experiment examines event identification, confidence\-based intervention, information acquisition, record loss, and history\-dependent estimation\. The computational results demonstrate model\-dependent changes in identification accuracy and evidence retention under different intervention conditions\. They do not establish autonomous scientific variable generation, confirm strong Xenoscience, or empirically validate Kant’s transcendental arguments\. Engaging Kant, Wilfrid Sellars, and Ray Brassier, the paper contributes a comparative reconstruction of transcendental synthesis and heterogeneous scientific operations while distinguishing transcendental validity, empirical justification, and material consequences\. \(direct link \)
