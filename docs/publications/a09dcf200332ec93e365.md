# Suzume, Suzume: Retroactive Semantic Computation and Closed\-Text Interpretation: Toward a Verifiable Proof\-Assistant Model of Interpretation

**Autores:** 

**Data de descoberta:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** unknown

## Descrição do feed

This paper proposes a formal model of literary and philosophical interpretation as a process of verification, comparison, and retroactive revision within a fixed evidence boundary\. The model separates hypothesis generation from hypothesis verification: a large language model or other generator may be influenced by external knowledge, while the verifier restricts admissible evidence to the input text, time\-indexed core annotations, and predeclared interpretive rules\. The framework integrates defeasible admissibility, a support preorder, textual provenance, hypothesis\-conditional truth values, retroactive reclassification, and switching among predeclared evaluators\. It distinguishes counterevidence against an interpretation from cases in which an earlier utterance becomes false under an otherwise admissible interpretation\. Four synthetic tests based on familiar motifs from Greek myth illustrate distinct operations: referential competition and retroactive truth evaluation, speech\-act reclassification, evaluator switching and meta\-level reinterpretation, and the filtering of hypotheses generated from external cultural priors\. The aim is not to identify a uniquely correct interpretation or to exhaust all possible readings\. Rather, the paper develops a candidate architecture for an interpretive proof assistant: a system designed to make explicit which textual fragments and declared rules support a submitted interpretation at a given stage of reading, and how that interpretation changes as later textual evidence becomes available\. \(direct link \)
