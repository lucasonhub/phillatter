# Panda, Lokanath: The Evolutionary Narrative Generator: Existential Philosophy as an Out\-of\-Domain Cognitive Artifact

**Autores:** 

**Data de descoberta:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** unknown

## Descrição do feed

This paper proposes a formal framework conceptualizing existential philosophy not as an objective truth\-discovery system, but as an evolutionarily optimized cognitive reg\- ulation mechanism\. We argue that while human consciousness is highly optimized for processing objectively corrigible environmental feedback to maximize functional survival, it encounters an out\-of\-domain computation when directed toward existential questions lacking external error\-correcting codes\. In the absence of empirical constraints, existen\- tial cognition diverges rather than converges, acting as a narrative generator designed to minimize internal cognitive entropy and translate existential anxiety into psychological equilibrium\. We formalize this framework through nine fundamental postulates and tie our conclusions to contemporary evolutionary debunking arguments\.
