# Consistency and correlation of a domestic Kehua serum free light chain assay with two imported analytical systems

**Autores:** Yansong Ren; Shoulei Wang; Limin Han; 王艳生; Qian Ren; Mengxin Wang; Yonghui Xia; Guoqing Zhu

**Data:** 2026-10-15 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Background Serum free light chain \(sFLC\) testing is central to the diagnosis and monitoring of plasma cell disorders, but assay availability and implementation can vary by laboratory setting\. We evaluated a domestic latex\-enhanced immunoturbidimetric Kehua serum sFLC assay against Freelite and N Latex systems\. Methods Residual serum samples from 133 adults with suspected or established monoclonal gammopathies were measured in parallel by Kehua and Freelite; 122 were also tested by N Latex\. Precision was assessed with low\- and high\-level serum controls\. Method agreement was evaluated using correlation, Passing\-Bablok regression, Bland\-Altman analysis, qualitative concordance and comparator\-defined ROC analysis\. Results Kehua within\-run CVs were &lt;5% for κ and λ controls\. Agreement with Freelite was excellent for FLC\-κ, FLC\-λ and κ/λ ratio \(Pearson r = 0\.988\-1\.000; CCC = 0\.983\-0\.999\), with Passing\-Bablok slopes close to unity\. Agreement with N Latex was also strong \(r = 0\.991\-0\.998\), although κ/λ ratio showed proportional bias\. Total qualitative concordance was 89\.47\-93\.23% versus Freelite and 83\.61\-91\.80% versus N Latex, with Cohen’s κ of 0\.67\-0\.85\. Comparator\-defined ROC AUCs were 0\.994\-0\.999 for elevated FLC\-κ or FLC\-λ and 0\.796\-0\.869 for κ/λ ratio abnormality\. Conclusion The Kehua serum assay demonstrated acceptable precision and good clinical agreement with two imported methods\. It may serve as a domestic option for routine serum sFLC quantification, provided method\-specific intervals are maintained and the same platform is used for serial monitoring\.
