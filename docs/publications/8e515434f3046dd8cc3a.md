# From AI\-enabled materials informatics to sustainable innovation in Asian global value chains: the bridging role of inter\-organisational collaboration

**Autores:** An\-Chin Cheng

**Data:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

The twin transition of digitalisation and sustainability requires manufacturers in Asian global value chains \(GVCs\) to convert internal artificial intelligence \(AI\) capability into outcomes that depend on cross\-boundary coordination\. Drawing on the Dynamic Capabilities View and GVC governance research, this study examines how AI\-enabled materials informatics \(MI\) capability relates to sustainable innovation performance \(SIP\) through inter\-organisational collaboration\. Survey data from 205 senior technology executives in Taiwan&\#x27;s high\-technology manufacturing sector were analysed using a complementary quantitative dual\-analytic strategy combining PLS\-SEM and fsQCA\. PLS\-SEM shows positive associations between AI\-enabled MI capability and collaboration capability \(β = 0\.612, p &lt; \.001\), collaboration capability and SIP \(β = 0\.485, p &lt; \.001\) and AI\-enabled MI capability and SIP \(β = 0\.215, p = \.002\)\. The specific indirect association is significant \(β = 0\.297, p &lt; \.001\), and sensitivity analysis excluding SIP\_2 yields materially unchanged results\. fsQCA identifies three sufficient configurations for high SIP, including an Eco\-Collaborative pathway combining weak internal AI capability with strong data\-governance and regulatory\-sensing practices\. The findings highlight complementary net\-association and configurational perspectives on sustainable innovation\.
