# The role of bacterial microbiota of the respiratory tract in the onset and progression of chronic obstructive pulmonary disease

**Autores:** Vladislav Yu\. Buslaev; Vladimir Gennadyevich Druzhinin; Andrey V\. Shabaldin

**Data:** 2026-10-15 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

In this review data about contribution of lung microbiome in chronic obstructive pulmonary disease development are systematized\. For systematic review systems like PubMed, Scopus, Web of Science, Embase, Cochrane CENTRAL, Google Scholar, e\-library were used for search of scientific articles\. As result 35 publications were included in review\. Information was given about dominative taxa of respiratory microbiota, involved in disease development and also about possible mechanisms providing this linkage\. It was shown that the basis of this phenomena is linked with dysbacteriosis which expressed in microbial diversity decrease and also in enhancement of recent taxa size\. It was noticed potential ability of certain bacteria to synthesize of toxins and adhesins for adaptation to immunity activity\. In perspective these data can be used to expand our view about diagnostics and therapy of chronic obstructive pulmonary disease\.
