# FunDM: Cheap &amp; Label\-Free Decision Models from Frozen Language Models

**Autores:** Ujjawal K\. Panchal

**Data:** 2026-10-12 · **Tipo:** preprint

## Resumo

Decision models such as Jev score candidate answers or actions\. A small decision head trained label\-free can recover about 99% of a frozen language model&\#x27;s multiple\-choice accuracy while using only two\-thirds of its transformer layers\. It learns the full model&\#x27;s answer probabilities with KL divergence, without correct\-answer labels for training\. One backbone pass reads the question and all options together; the head returns probabilities for every option\. We call this model FunDM \(Fun Decision Model\)\. Using 24 of Qwen3\-8B&\#x27;s 36 layers and skipping the LM head, its \+1T variant reaches 75\.0% mean accuracy over eight benchmarks, versus 75\.7% for the full model\. On MMLU\-Pro, both heads achieve 1\.56 to 1\.57 times batched vLLM throughput with about one\-third lower peak GPU allocator memory\. Both heads score higher than released Contrastive Language Models \(CLM\), fine\-tuned CLM, and CLM\-shaped heads trained from scratch on the eight multiple\-choice benchmarks and coding\-agent selection\. Separately trained agent heads learn judge probabilities without pass/fail labels and select passing runs for 74\.4 to 74\.6 of 100 held\-out tasks on average, versus 74 for the full\-model judge and 52\.0 for a CLM\-shaped action\-trained head\. Code is available at: https://github\.com/Ujjawal\-K\-Panchal/FunDM
