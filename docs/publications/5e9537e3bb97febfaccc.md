# The Integration of Orff Teaching Method and Intelligent Software

**Autores:** Han Wei

**Data:** 2026-10-15 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

With the development of information technology, music education is facing profound changes\. Orff teaching method, which emphasizes perceptual experience and emotional participation, has received increasing attention for music education in Chinese colleges and universities\. At the same time, the emergence of intelligent educational software provides more personalized and interactive support for teaching\. This study explores the influence of Orff teaching method combined with intelligent education software on students&\#x27; sense of happiness and self\-worth\. Through questionnaires and classroom observation, this study analyzes the trend of students&\#x27; psychological changes in the integrated teaching environment\. The results show that this integration model helps to enhance students&\#x27; positive emotional experiences and self\-worth\. This study enriches the application of Orff teaching method in the modern educational environment and provides new ideas for the reform of music teaching in colleges and universities\.
