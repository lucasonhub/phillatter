# THE EL\-RAKHAWI DOCTRINE THE COMPREHENSIVE ENCYCLOPEDIA OF CRIMINAL PSYCHOLOGY, FORENSIC MEDICINE, AND NEUROSCIENCE IN LAW

**Autores:** mohamed kamal arafa el\-rakhawi

**Data:** 2026-10-11 · **Tipo:** book

## Resumo

The El\-Rakhawi Doctrine synthesizes criminal psychology, forensic medicine, and neuroscience into a unified framework for modern legal practice\. Moving beyond traditional jurisprudence, it introduces an empirically grounded biopsychosocial model, showing that human behavior results from interacting biological, developmental, and cognitive systems\. The work establishes rigorous methodological standards for forensic neuropsychology, ensuring neurocognitive data meets strict evidentiary gatekeeping\. It details protocols for courtroom translation, expert testimony, and multidisciplinary collaboration\. Furthermore, the text maps neurological conditions onto legal defenses, competency evaluations, and proportional sentencing, aligning accountability with cognitive capacity\. Addressing future challenges, it sets ethical boundaries for neurotechnologies, AI analytics, and cognitive liberty\. Ultimately, this doctrine transforms subjective legal intuition into scientifically defensible judicial practice, balancing public safety with biological truth\.
