# From Cancer Dependency Maps to Antiviral Structures: Highlights of Recent Advances in Medicine and Biology — A Research Brief on Frontier Progress, September 2026

**Autores:** Dan\-Dan Wang

**Data:** 2026-10-15 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

The September 2026 issues of a leading international science journal present a rich harvest of biomedical research\. This communication selects 34 original research articles with close relevance to medicine and the medical biosciences, and organizes them into six themes: cancer biology and translational oncology; neuroscience and brain disorders across the lifespan; neuroimmunology; metabolism, ageing and the microbiome; infection, haematology and global health; and broader biological advances that underpin future medicine\. For each study we provide a concise summary and brief commentary, aiming to offer clinicians and researchers a panoramic view of current progress\.
