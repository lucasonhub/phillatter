# Improving Highway Network Performance Using the VIKOR Method: Multi\-Criteria Analysis of Road Infrastructure

**Autores:** Mani Sathishkumar; M Ramachandran; Saravanan Vimala

**Data:** 2026-10-17 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

A model\-based predictive control strategy is presented to identify the optimal path and speed for vehicles in a platoon in an automated highway system \(AHS\)\. It also addresses the challenge of determining the optimal exit times for vehicles entering the system, with the goal of improving overall traffic efficiency\. The model considers factors such as dynamic speed limits, lane distribution, and gradient measurements to estimate the total time spent within the system\. Furthermore, it examines investment challenges in highway networks affected by earthquakes, aiming to improve connectivity and subsequently reduce travel costs\. A key focus is on rehabilitating fragile infrastructure, particularly bridges, whose failure can cause long\-term traffic disruptions\. The research highlights the importance of strategic investments to facilitate rapid recovery and minimize network disruptions\.This study presents a model\-based predictive control method designed to optimize the paths and speeds of vehicles in a platoon within an automated highway system \(AHS\)\. This approach also addresses the challenge of determining the optimal exit times for vehicles entering the system, a key factor in improving traffic efficiency\. By factoring in variables such as dynamic speed limits, lane allocation, and gradient, the model calculates the total time spent within the system, providing important insights for optimizing traffic flow\. These findings are essential for the successful implementation of future AHS systems and improving overall highway network performance Road A, B, C, D, E, F\.
