# Mapping natural sinkhole susceptibility in Italy: integrating a national inventory with machine learning

**Autores:** Livio Ruggiero; Giancarlo Ciotoli; Federico Mori; Sergio Madonna; Stefania Nisio

**Data:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Natural sinkholes are a widespread geohazard in Italy, complicating landuse planning and risk reduction\. This study develops the first national\-scale susceptibility map for natural sinkholes using an updated inventory and data\-driven modelling\. Building on the ISPRA National Sinkhole Database, we assembled more than 14,000 documented features from literature, historical records, imagery and regional datasets\. Geological, geomorphological, structural, hydrogeological and geochemical factors were converted into gridded predictors and combined with the inventory through a machine\-learning regression framework estimating a continuous susceptibility score\. The calibrated model, evaluated on a held\-out 20% test subset, produces a susceptibility surface for the Italian territory\. Interpretation tools emphasize the importance of soluble rocks, fault\-controlled permeability, deep gas emissions and mineralized springs, consistent with previous studies\. High\-susceptibility zones align with major karst and evaporitic provinces, including the Friuli\-Veneto piedmont, northern Apulia, Latium travertine basins and central Sicilian evaporites, whereas western Alpine sectors and Sardinia show very low scores\. The map offers a coherent national overview of where natural sinkholes are more likely to occur and provides an operational basis for prioritizing surveys, monitoring programmes and planning measures, while the methodology can be refined to incorporate time\-dependent drivers and multi\-hazard interactions\. These advances support more resilient and sustainable territorial development\.
