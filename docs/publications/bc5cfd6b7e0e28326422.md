# Procesamiento térmico y acidificación: un ejercicio de laboratorio de grado

**Autores:** José Chaves\-Quesada; Oscar Acosta\-Montoya

**Data:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Food engineering programs should provide students with practical activities related to the analysis of unit operations, within the context of food processing\. Both effectiveness and efficiency should be evaluated, so students can recognize the importance of achieving an objective and quantifying resources employed to achieve the goal\. We describe an undergraduate\-level food engineering laboratory exercise divided in two sections\. Both use carrot cylinders in vinegar solutions, packed in glass jars with metal lids, thermally processed in boiling water\. In the first section \(thermal processing\) students collect time and temperature data during a heat penetration study\. The experimental variable is the process time, and the response variable is the accumulated lethality \(Tref = 93\.3ºC, z = 8\.9ºC\)\. In the second section \(acidification\), students collect time and pH data during an acidification study\. The experimental variables are the acetic acid concentration and the acidification time, and the response variable is the carrot cylinders’ pH\. In both sections, students perform basic statistical analyses, carrying out calculations related to non\-linear regression, prediction intervals, inverse prediction, and model verification\. This laboratory exercise can assist in illustrating concepts, techniques, measurements, calculations, model development, and statistical analyses related to thermal processing and acidification of food\.
