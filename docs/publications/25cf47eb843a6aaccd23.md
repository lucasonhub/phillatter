# From code to cure: Harnessing AI for antibiotic discovery and design

**Autores:** Aarti Krishnan; Hanqun Cao; James J\. Collins; César de la Fuente‐Núñez

**Data:** 2026-10-14 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Artificial intelligence \(AI\) now enables the exploration of vast chemical and sequence spaces, the design of synthesizable antibiotics, and the prioritization of safe, mechanism\-diverse candidates through integrated modeling and accelerated testing\. Here, we survey recent advances in AI for both small\-molecule and peptide antimicrobial drugs, highlight emerging data and reporting standards, and outline translational pathways and regulatory hurdles needed to bring these agents to patients\.
