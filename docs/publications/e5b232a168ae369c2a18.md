# Generative Artificial Intelligence Empowering Knowledge Work: The Impact of Human\-AI Collaboration on the Efficiency and Quality of Managerial Decision\-Making

**Autores:** Jingwei Guo; Zhenguang Zhong; Weiqiang Lyu

**Data:** 2026-10-15 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

The rapid iteration and industrialization of Generative Artificial Intelligence \(GenAI\) have thoroughly changed the paradigm of knowledge work in modern organizations, and thus the process of managerial decision\-making has evolved from heuristic judgment dominated by human managers to the new paradigm of human\-AI collaborative creation\. This paper seeks to investigate how GenAI\-assisted human\-AI collaboration would affect the effectiveness and quality of organizational managerial decision\-making\. Based on the knowledge\-based view and collaborative intelligence, a theoretical framework including the technology of GenAI, modes of human\-AI collaboration and decision\-making performance is established in this paper\. By quantitative simulation experiments and empirical analysis with Python, the impacts of GenAI assistance on decision\-making time cost, information processing comprehensiveness, decision accuracy and risk identification ability are verified in this paper\. It can be found that moderate human\-AI collaboration will contribute to managerial decision\-making effectiveness and quality; too much or not enough GenAI assistance may weaken the positive impacts of human\-AI collaboration on managerial decision\-making\. GenAI contributes to managerial decision\-making effectiveness and quality mainly through reducing information searching costs, improving data analysis efficiency and compensating human cognitive limitations\.
