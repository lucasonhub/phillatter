# Optimal driving assignment strategies for plug\-in hybrid electric heavy duty trucks

**Autores:** Javier Jareño; Bernabè Dorronsoro; P\. Ruiz

**Data:** 2026-10-10 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

The transportation sector faces critical challenges in reducing CO 2 emissions, particularly from heavy\-duty trucks \(HDTs\), which account for 25% of transportation\-related emissions\. Plug\-in hybrid electric \(PHE\) HDTs offer a sustainable solution for long\-distance freight, yet optimizing their battery management under variable route conditions such as topography, load, and speed constraints remains a complex problem\. This study addresses this challenge through an integrated route\-aware optimization framework for PHE HDTs\. The framework combines route and elevation data, a power\-constrained speed–acceleration estimator, regenerative braking, charging opportunities, and a Cellular Genetic Algorithm for Multiobjective Optimization \(MOCell\) to determine the driving\-mode assignment between the electric motor and the internal combustion engine\. A deterministic constructive heuristic, GreenRoute, is also included as a baseline for assessing the benefits of multi\-objective optimization\. Validation across three long\-haul routes \(Cádiz\-Madrid, Madrid\-Donostia, Paris\-Berlin\) demonstrates significant improvements: MOCell achieves up to 9% greater electric range and 17\.5% lower emissions compared to the heuristic GreenRoute\. For instance, on the Cádiz\-Madrid route, MOCell reduces emissions by 5\.7% and increases electric range by 4\.6% when en\-route charging is not considered, with further gains \(6\.6% and 4\.1%, respectively\) when charging is included\. The study also reveals that heavier trucks benefit from enhanced regenerative braking, extending electric range despite higher total CO 2 emissions\. These results underscore the efficacy of metaheuristic approaches in balancing emissions reduction and operational efficiency, advancing sustainable freight transportation aligned with global climate goals\.
