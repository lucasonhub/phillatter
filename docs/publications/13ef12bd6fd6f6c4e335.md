# Inteligencia artificial en la investigación penal y sus límites procesales en Ecuador

**Autores:** Ignacio Fernando Barcos\-Arias; Luis Alberto Andrade\-Monar; Fredy Geovany Guachi\-Soria

**Data:** 2026-10-15 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Artificial intelligence is increasingly used to support criminal investigation, in the analysis of large data volumes, digital forensics and case management, but it poses challenges for due process, the presumption of innocence, evidentiary transparency and personal data protection\. The aim was to analyze its use in Ecuadorian criminal investigation, its applications, regulatory gaps and the procedural limits arising from the Constitution, the criminal code and international standards\. A qualitative, descriptive\-analytical approach was applied, with a legal hermeneutic method, document review and five exploratory expert interviews\. Criteria for a rights\-based incorporation are proposed: express regulation through protocols, algorithmic explainability, judicial control of results, training of legal operators and a ban on delegating decision\-making functions to automated systems\.
