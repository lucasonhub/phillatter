# Innovación en Inteligencia Artificial y Nuevas Tecnologías en la Medicina del Deporte y el Fútbol

**Autores:** Antonio Eugenio Rivera Cisneros; Jimena Prado Peláez; Gloria Vargas Sánchez; Amalia Marcela Lara Gonzalez; FELIPE HOMERO GÓMEZ BALLESTEROS

**Data:** 2026-10-14 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Background: Innovation in artificial intelligence \(AI\) in sports medicine integrates algorithms, sensors, computer vision, predictive analytics and decision\-support systems to improve athlete health, performance, safety and competitive fairness\. Objective: To reconstruct the historical development of AI and emerging technologies in sport, assess applications, accuracy, benefits, limitations, ethical dilemmas and rule\-related impact, with a focus on association football and the 2026 FIFA World Cup\. Methods: A rapid historical systematic review was conducted according to PRISMA 2020\. PubMed/MEDLINE, Scopus, Web of Science, SPORTDiscus, IEEE Xplore and official FIFA, IFAB, UEFA, IOC and FIMS sources were searched from 1950 to July 2026\. Results: Eighty\-two sources were synthesized; 47 provided quantitative information\. Mature applications included player tracking, video analysis, workload monitoring and officiating support\. Injury prediction remained promising but vulnerable to overfitting, poor external validation and dataset shift\. Conclusions: AI is most useful as an augmented\-intelligence layer supporting clinical, coaching and refereeing expertise\. Responsible adoption requires prospective validation, explainability, cybersecurity, athlete consent and clear accountability\.
