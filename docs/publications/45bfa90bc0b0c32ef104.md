## Descrição do feed

In Anna Giustina, The Routledge Handbook of Introspection\. Routledge\. 2026This chapter discusses aesthetic introspection, namely, the introspection of aesthetically relevant mental states and events\. It distinguishes three types of aesthetic introspection—type‑1, type‑2, and type‑3—in terms of their functional roles\. Type‑1 aesthetic introspection is inward attention that contributes to the processes that lead from aesthetic perception to an aesthetic judgment\. Type‑2 aesthetic introspection consists in providing a second‑order affective experience that represents a conscious experience as having aesthetic properties\. Type‑3 aesthetic introspection is to produce judgments about conscious experiences that represent their objects as having aesthetic properties\. By exploring each type of aesthetic introspection and listing several key questions about them, this chapter shows that aesthetic introspection is a promising yet neglected research topic\.
