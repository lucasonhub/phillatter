# Full Lyapunov Spectra of Random Neural Networks, Part I: Finite\-Time and Asymptotic Theory

**Autores:** Haoyu Albert Wang; Wei P\. Dai

**Data:** 2026-10-10 · **Tipo:** preprint

## Resumo

We develop a dynamical mean\-field framework for the full finite\-time and stationary asymptotic Lyapunov spectra of random recurrent neural networks\. At finite observation time, an exact log\-determinant representation of tangent\-propagator singular values leads to a closed\-contour matrix Dyson equation for the smoothed spectral tail\. In the stationary setting, a cocycle representation yields an operator equation for the asymptotic cumulative spectrum\. Both formulations retain complete local gain histories and the repeated action of quenched connectivity\. Direct singular\-value calculations and network QR spectra support the predictions in the regimes examined, with sampling uncertainty and numerical cutoffs assessed separately\. The stationary equation also recovers the known maximal exponent, and controlled gain\-history replay illustrates how temporal correlations redistribute instability\. This work was carried out in collaboration with ChatGPT 5\.6 Sol\. Toward the end of preparation we became aware of David G\. Clark&\#x27;s contemporaneous work, \\emph\{Lyapunov spectrum of random neural networks\}\[arXiv:2610\.12426\], which reports overlapping mean\-field results for the full Lyapunov spectrum using a cavity approach\.
