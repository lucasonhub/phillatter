# Diagnostic Accuracy of American College of Radiology Ovarian\-Adnexal Reporting and Data System \(ACR O\-RADS\) Ultrasound for Characterizing Adnexal\-Ovarian Masses: A Histopathological Correlation Study

**Autores:** Dr\. Rupanshi Jain; Saurav Bhagat; B Sampath Akhila; Sakshi Aggarwal; Sarthak Jain; Urvi Jangir; Sarthak Jain

**Data:** 2026-10-12 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

BackgroundAccurate preoperative characterization of adnexal\-ovarian masses is essential for appropriate clinical management\.The American College of Radiology Ovarian\-Adnexal Reporting and Data System \(ACR O\-RADS\) ultrasound provides a standardized ultrasound\-based framework for malignancy risk stratification\. ObjectiveThe objective of this study is to evaluate the diagnostic accuracy of transvaginal ultrasonography \(TVUS\) using ACR O\-RADS for adnexal\-ovarian masses, with histopathological diagnosis as the reference standard\. Materials and methodsThis prospective, single\-center, hospital\-based observational diagnostic\-accuracy study with a crosssectional index\-test/reference\-standard comparison was conducted over 18 months, from May 2023 to November 2024\.Women with suspected adnexal\-ovarian masses underwent TVUS, and each lesion was categorized according to the ACR O\-RADS ultrasound system\.Histopathological examination following surgical excision served as the reference standard\.Sensitivity, specificity, positive predictive value \(PPV\), and negative predictive value \(NPV\) were calculated and reported with Wilson 95% CIs\. ResultsAmong the 34 adnexal\-ovarian masses, histopathological examination classified 16/34 \(47\.1%; 95% CI: 31\.5%\-63\.3%\)as malignant, 15/34 \(44\.1%; 95% CI: 28\.9%\-60\.5%\)as benign, and 3/34 \(8\.8%; 95% CI: 3\.0%\-23\.0%\)as borderline\.When O\-RADS categories 4 and 5 were considered test\-positive and malignant/borderline lesions were combined as disease\-positive, O\-RADS demonstrated a sensitivity of 100\.0%\(95% CI: 83\.2%\-100\.0%\),specificity of 66\.7% \(95% CI: 41\.7%\-84\.8%\),PPV of 79\.2% \(95% CI: 59\.5%\-90\.8%\),and NPV of 100\.0%\(95% CI: 72\.2%\-100\.0%\)\.O\-RADS category was significantly associated with histopathological classification \(p &lt; 0\.001\); however, the proportion of malignant lesions was identical in O\-RADS 4 and O\-RADS 5 \(66\.7% each\)\. ConclusionACR O\-RADS demonstrated promising diagnostic performance for characterizing adnexal\-ovarian masses in this cohort\.These findings require confirmation in larger, consecutively recruited populations before broader conclusions regarding clinical utility can be made\.
