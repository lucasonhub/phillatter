# Zero\-Shot Cross\-Modality Dose Prediction in Particle Therapy via Physics\-Informed Ensembles

**Autores:** Muhammed Emin Bedir

**Data:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Purpose: Deep learning can accelerate radiotherapy dose prediction, but particle\-therapy datasets remain scarce\. We evaluated whether a physics\-informed ensemble trained exclusively on photon plans could predict proton and carbon\-ion dose distributions without particle\-dose training\.Methods: Using matRad, 90 reference plans \(photons, protons and carbon ions\) were generated for five anatomies and six beam configurations\. A 13\-channel tensor combining CT, structure masks and modality\-specific ray\-tracing/depth\-dose channels was input to SOTA\-UNet3D, Res\-UNet3D and Trans\-UNet3D; their voxel\-wise mean formed the Voltron ensemble\. Leave\-one\-anatomy\-out evaluation used four test anatomies with photon\-only training\. Performance was assessed with PyMedPhys gamma analysis \(3%/3 mm and 2%/2 mm; 10% low\-dose threshold\) and mean absolute error \(MAE\)\.Results: Across four held\-out anatomies, mean 3%/3 mm gamma pass rates were 23\.5% for photons, 23\.3% for protons and 21\.5% for carbon ions; 2%/2 mm rates were 15\.8%, 15\.5% and 14\.5%\. Mean MAE was approximately 5\.2–5\.3 Gy\. Gamma values reported in an earlier version of this work \(73\.2/70\.8/69\.1%\) were computed with a non\-standard lenient criterion; all values reported here use the standard gamma index\.Conclusion: Photon\-only training with physics\-informed inputs provides a reproducible feasibility baseline for zeroshot particle\-dose prediction\. Performance is not clinical\-grade, but the small cross\-modality degradation suggests that anatomical and geometric features learned from photon data can transfer to particle therapy\.
