# An end‑to‑end multi‑source heterogeneous data fusion and spatiotemporal graph convolutional network for enterprise financial crisis early warning

**Autores:** Yang Qu

**Data:** 2026-10-13 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

In the early warning of enterprise financial crisis, the difficulty of multi\-source heterogeneous data fusion and the complexity of risk transmission effect modeling are the key challenges\. To this end, this study proposes an end\-to\-end deep learning framework named MFGCN to achieve accurate early warning\. This paper proposes an MFGCN model that fuses a multi\-source heterogeneous data fusion module \(using a GNN\-based pattern alignment and attention weighting mechanism\) and a spatiotemporal graph convolution early warning module \(combining R\-GCN and GRU to capture spatiotemporal dependencies\)\. On a multi\-source dataset covering 15,000 companies around the world, the model achieved an accuracy of 89\.1%, an F1\-score of 0\.862, and an AUC value of 0\.896, significantly outperforming the baseline models\. Moreover, the model shows excellent robustness \(performance degradation under noise is only 4\.2%\), long\-term predictive capability \(maintaining an AUC of 0\.861 at a lead time of Δ=4 quarters\) and domain generalization \(no domain performance degradation &lt; 4\.52% is seen\)\. The conclusion shows that the MFGCN framework provides an efficient and reliable solution for enterprise financial crisis early warning through end\-to\-end fusion, multi\-relationship risk transmission modeling, and a dynamic adaptive mechanism\. Its core contribution is to realize end\-to\-end learning from raw multi\-source data to accurate early warning\. Although the computational complexity is high, it points out the direction of optimizing efficiency and introducing federated learning for subsequent research\.
