# Comparing GenAI to Machine Learning and Manual Review for eDiscovery

**Autores:** Gordon V\. Cormack; Maura R\. Grossman; Jeremy Pickens

**Data:** 2026-10-12 · **Tipo:** preprint

## Resumo

Keeling et al\. \[Keeling2026Generative\] report on a case study recently conducted by Redgrave LLP in which they assert that Relativity&\#x27;s Generative AI Review tool \(aiR\) achieved 88% recall while Relativity Assisted Learning \(RAL\), its machine\-learning tool, achieved 64% recall\. These results are not comparable because the methods employed have different objectives, because of inter\-assessor disagreement, and because they represent different recall\-precision and cost\-benefit trade\-offs\. Our analysis of the Redgrave data indicates that, when these differences are taken into account, aiR and manual review are comparable\. Furthermore, established machine\-learning models employing Continuous Active Learning \(CAL\) show state\-of\-the\-art effectiveness, and superior efficiency, when trained only on a sample of 2,000 first\-pass\-reviewer responsiveness determinations\.
