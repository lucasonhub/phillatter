# INGLIZ TILI TA’LIMIDA KATTA YOSHDAGI O‘RGANUVCHILARNING KOGNITIV KOMPETENSIYALARINI RIVOJLANTIRISHDA METAKOGNITIV STRATEGIYALARNING METODIK IMKONIYATLARI

**Autores:** YOQUBJANOVA DIYORA DILSHODBEK QIZI

**Data:** 2026-10-10 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

This article examines the theoretical foundations and methodological potential of metacognitive strategies for developing adult learners’ cognitive competencies in English Zamonaviy jamiyat va innovatsiyalar ilmiy nazariy jurnali 2026\-yil, 9\-Son, 2\-Qism\. ISSN 3060\-544X language education\. Cognitive competencies are understood as the abilities to interpret language input, identify semantic relationships, analyse information, draw inferences, and transfer knowledge to new communicative situations\. Metacognitive strategies are examined as tools for planning, monitoring, evaluating, and adjusting these processes\. The article explores the relationship between andragogy, metacognitive knowledge, and self\-regulated learning\. Based on an analysis of relevant literature, a methodological model is proposed that incorporates diagnosis, strategic orientation, guided practice, independent application, and reflective evaluation\. The analysis highlights the need to combine self\-reported awareness with evidence from learning processes and performance\. The potential of the proposed model and areas requiring further empirical investigation are identified\.
