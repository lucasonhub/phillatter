# BIOECOLOGICAL CHARACTERISTICS, DISTRIBUTION RANGE, CHEMICAL COMPOSITION AND MEDICINAL PROPERTIES OF BOG BILBERRY \(VACCINIUM ULIGINOSUM L\.\)

**Autores:** Zilola Jabborova

**Data:** 2026-10-15 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

The article analyses the scientific literature on the bioecological characteristics, distribution range, chemical composition and medicinal properties of wild bog bilberry \(Vaccinium uliginosum L\.\), which grows in the cold regions of the Northern Hemisphere, and of the closely related cultivated species, highbush blueberry \(V\. corymbosum L\.\)\. Methods of systematic literature review, comparative analysis and generalisation were applied\. Bog bilberry is a circumboreal species that is highly frost\-hardy and thrives only on acidic \(pH 3\.5–5\.5\) peaty soils\. Per 100 g, the berries contain 57 kcal of energy, 14\.49 g of carbohydrates, 2\.4 g of dietary fibre, 9\.7 mg of vitamin C and 77 mg of potassium; in addition, they contain anthocyanins, flavonols, chlorogenic acid, iridoids and triterpenes\. Clinical and experimental evidence on the reduction of visual fatigue, the improvement of cardiovascular and metabolic parameters, and neuroprotective, anti\-inflammatory and antitumour effects is summarised\. The safety of bog bilberry, its contraindications, the storage conditions of the berries and the problem of V\. uliginosum and V\. corymbosum being referred to by the same name in the scientific literature are also considered separately\. The need to grow blueberries in containers on an acidic substrate and to conduct local introduction studies under the calcareous soils and hot climate of Uzbekistan is substantiated\.
