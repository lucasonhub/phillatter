# TREATING THE CHALLENGE OF PNEUMOCOCCAL MENINGITIS USING NOVEL DRUG DELIVERY SYSTEMS

**Autores:** Vaishnavi \. A \. Ghormade; Harsha Sonaye; Jignesh M\. Doshi

**Data:** 2026-10-17 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Even though vaccinations and effective treatments are accessible, pneumococcal meningitis caused by Streptococcus pneumonia is still one of the major determinants of morbidity and mortality worldwide\. As a result of the rapid progression of disease, increased resistance to antibiotics, and low medication permeation through the blood\-brain barrier \(BBB\), conventional methodologies to treatment are often limited\. Treatment methodologies for CNS infections were totally revolutionized by the recent developments in innovative drug delivery systems \(NDDS\)\. Delivery approaches like intranasal administration, liposomes, niosomes, dendrimers, micelles, nanoparticles, and polymer\-based drug delivery are known to offer better drug bioavailability, extended drug\-release profile, and better drug permeability via the BBB\. The pathology behind pneumococcal meningitis, existing problems with treatment, and the new advances in NDDS have been elaborated on in this review to facilitate effective management of the condition\. The combination of effective antibiotics and advanced delivery systems can bring considerable improvements in the treatment of the disease, reducing its mortality rates and combating antibiotic resistance\.
