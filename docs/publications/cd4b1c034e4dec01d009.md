# Developing a Project Management System in Construction: The Role of Project Management Body of Knowledge \(PMBOK\) for UA Design

**Autores:** Marius Gene Cassius Arce; Rowen Neil Enriquez; Vince Caleb Tadiwan

**Data:** 2026-10-17 · **Tipo:** dissertation

## Resumo

Construction organizations that still plan and track work through spreadsheets, email, and paper records have limitedvisibility of schedule, cost, resources, and risk\. This paper reports the design, implementation, and evaluation of aweb\-based project management system for UA Designs, a design\-and\-build contractor, aligned with six knowledge areas ofthe Project Management Body of Knowledge \(PMBOK\): integration, schedule, cost, resource, risk, and stakeholdermanagement\. The system is a React and Node\.js application with critical\-path scheduling, earned\-value indicators, adeterministic forecasting engine, and a tool\-grounded artificial intelligence assistant whose suggested changes to projectrecords require human approval\. Software verification covered functional, usability\-walkthrough, performance, andPMBOK\-alignment tests\. A descriptive evaluation with two key company users produced a functionality mean of 4\.83 and ausability mean of 4\.92 on a five\-point scale, both interpreted as Excellent, and a System Usability Scale average of 60\.0,below the commonly cited benchmark of 68\. The findings support the system’s fitness for the tested UA Designs workflowsand identify login reliability, connectivity, and the size of the user sample as limits on what can be claimed\.
