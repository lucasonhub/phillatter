# Physics\-Informed Multi\-Parameter Optimization of Oxide\-Based Memristor Characteristics Using Genetic Algorithms

**Autores:** Armin Gooran\-Shoorakchaly; Safura Sharifi; Yaser Mike Banad

**Data:** 2026-10-12 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Abstract We present a multiobjective, physics\-informed optimization framework for oxide\-based memristive devices that jointly minimizes forming voltage \(Vf\) and maximizes memory contrast \(HRS/LRS\)\. The proposed approach integrates COMSOL Multiphysics with MATLAB through a genetic algorithm \(GA\), enabling self\-consistent electrothermal simulations and systematic exploration of a high\-dimensional design space encompassing geometrical, electrical, thermal, and operational parameters\. By optimizing 13 key variables, the GA efficiently identifies optimal trade\-offs between switching efficiency and resistance ratio\. The optimization results demonstrate a substantial enhancement in device performance, with the high\-resistance\-state to low\-resistance\-state ratio \(HRS/LRS\) improved by up to 21× relative to the reference device, while alternative optimized configurations achieve a reduction in Vf down to 0\.7× of the reference value\. A parameter\-importance analysis based on a random forest model highlights oxide thickness and effective electrical and thermal conductivities as influential parameters\. Importantly, the optimization further reveals nontrivial multiparameter trade\-offs, demonstrating that reductions in Vf and enhancements in HRS/LRS can be simultaneously achieved through coordinated tuning of geometrical, material, and pulse\-related parameters beyond simple intuitive design rules\. Overall, the MATLAB–COMSOL co\-optimization strategy provides a scalable and generalizable computational design methodology for next\-generation low\-power, high\-contrast RRAM devices, and offers quantitative insights into the coupled physical mechanisms underlying filament formation and rupture\.
