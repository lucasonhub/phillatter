# BO‘LAJAK O‘QITUVCHILARNI XULQ\-ATVOR KORREKSIYASIGA TAYYORLASHDA SUGGESTIV METODLARNING ADAPTIV PEDAGOGIK IMKONIYATLARI

**Autores:** Shermatova Manzura Ikromjanovna

**Data:** 2026-10-13 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

This article investigates the theoretical and methodological potential of integrating suggestive communication techniques into adaptive teacher education for responding to challenging behaviour\. Particular attention is given to preservice teachers’ ability to analyse complex educational situations, select contextually appropriate communication strategies, consider learners’ individual needs, and critically evaluate their professional decisions\. The study draws on experiential learning, situated learning, differentiated instruction, Universal Design for Learning, and reflective practice\. The contributions of Uzbek scholars R\. Mavlonova, B\. Xodjayev, and J\. Jalolov are discussed in relation to the organization of pedagogical processes and the development of teaching competence\. An adaptive training framework is proposed that integrates initial diagnostic assessment, simulated teaching situations, individualized methodological support, professional autonomy, and reflective redesign of instructional decisions\. The framework conceptualizes suggestive communication as one ethically regulated component of pedagogical interaction rather than as an independently validated intervention\. Its effectiveness remains a subject for future empirical investigation\.
