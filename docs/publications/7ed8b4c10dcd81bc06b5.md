# Nanopore Signatures of High\-Molecular\-Weight Polysaccharides with Recurring Structural Motifs

**Autores:** Wenzheng Li; Yunqi Xiao; Xinmeng Gao; Panke Zhang; Shuo Huang; Kefan Wang

**Data:** 2026-10-17 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Abstract Polysaccharides in nature possess profound biological significance, and their structural complexity renders them of great research value\. However, the structural heterogeneity of complex polysaccharides has long posed a great challenge for analysis\. Here, a nanopore profiling strategy was developed for polysaccharide characterization\. Experimentally, polysaccharides are hydrolyzed into informative fragments, and the distribution of their resulting nanopore events forms a unique profile that serves as a reliable molecular signature\. Seven distinct polysaccharides, including xanthan gum, gum arabic, agarose, sodium alginate, gellan gum, heparin, and chondroitin sulfate A, were successfully identified and discriminated\. A profile\-based machine learning classifier was also developed, which utilizes the overall distribution profile of fragment events as training input, yielding 100% validation accuracy\. Further application of the fully constrained least\-squares \(FCLS\) algorithm enabled accurate quantification of polysaccharide mixture composition, allowing for unambiguous analysis of mixtures of polysaccharides in the same assay\. Collectively, this work establishes a universal, label\-free, and high\-resolution framework for decoding the structural complexity of polysaccharides\.
