# An Explainable WO\-Performer\-TabM Framework with Cross\-attention Feature Fusion for Pregnancy Outcome Prediction

**Autores:** Divya Mohan; M\. Murugan

**Data:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Accurate prediction of pregnancy outcomes is essential for supporting clinical decision\-making and improving reproductive healthcare\.However, pregnancy outcome prediction remains challenging because clinical datasets comprise heterogeneous demographic, reproductive, hormonal, metabolic, ultrasound, lifestyle and medical variables that exhibit complex nonlinear relationships\.This research presents a hybrid deep learning \(DL\) approach that integrates a Performer Transformer encoder and a TabM network through a Cross\-Attention Feature Fusion to jointly capture global feature dependencies and nonlinear feature interactions from structured clinical data\.To identify an optimal hyperparameter configuration, the Walrus Optimization \(WO\) algorithm is employed for automatic hyperparameter optimization, enabling efficient exploration and exploitation of the search space\.The framework was developed and evaluated using a publicly available clinical pregnancy dataset comprising 50,000 patient records with 40 predictor variables\.Experimental evaluation demonstrated a classification accuracy of 98\.60%, with 99\.11% precision, 98\.86% recall, 98\.98% F1\-score, 98\.03% specificity, 96\.74% Cohen&\#x27;s Kappa, indicating reliable agreement between predicted and actual pregnancy outcomes\.Furthermore, the framework reached an Area Under the Receiver Operating Characteristic Curve \(ROC\-AUC\) of 99\.60%, demonstrating excellent discriminative capability across different decision thresholds\.Comparative investigations and ablation study further verified the effectiveness of the hybrid architecture and WO\-based hyperparameter optimization\.Finally, SHapley Additive exPlanations \(SHAP\) were incorporated to provide interpretable explanations of the model predictions\.The developed framework demonstrates high predictive accuracy, robustness and interpretability, making it an effective decision\-support tool for pregnancy outcome prediction in reproductive healthcare\.
