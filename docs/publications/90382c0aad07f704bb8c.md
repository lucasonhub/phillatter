# Who is in the data? Demographic and geographic reporting in public datasets used to develop artificial intelligence for neurosurgery: protocol for a meta\-research audit

**Autores:** Omar Farghal; Mohammad Al\-Darawsheh; Mohammad Telfah; Leen Basha

**Data:** 2026-10-17 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Protocol for a cross\-sectional meta\-research audit of how public datasets used to develop artificial intelligence \(AI\) for neurosurgery report patient demographics and data origin\. The frame comprises 110 datasets: the 84 unique datasets catalogued by Chan et al\. \(J Clin Med 2025\) and 26 pre\-specified, widely used datasets\. Fourteen items are extracted from each descriptor paper, including age, sex, race/ethnicity, socioeconomic status, country of data origin and inclusion of children\. A large language model produced a first\-pass extraction with verbatim quotes\. Two investigators verify every item independently and reconcile any disagreements, and two further reviewers double\-extract a random 20% sample\. Countries are mapped to World Bank income groups\. This record time\-stamps the protocol, codebook, sampling frame and analysis code before human verification began\. The AI outputs and verification workbooks are withheld until verification is complete, to keep the second reviewers blind\.
