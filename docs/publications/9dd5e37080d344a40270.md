# Tail\-risk\-constrained portfolio optimization with cluster pre\-selection and machine learning: risk\-adjusted evidence from the S&amp;P 500

**Autores:** David Toscano; Juan Carlos Roca

**Data:** 2026-10-15 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Purpose Standard Gaussian risk frameworks underestimate extreme joint losses in large equity universes, exposing investors to unbudgeted drawdowns\. This article develops and tests a portfolio\-construction pipeline that disciplines tail risk while exploiting forward\-looking return signals\. Design/methodology/approach The four\-stage pipeline integrates a rolling tail\-index \(Hill\) filter, cluster pre\-selection, a Conditional Value\-at\-Risk \(CVaR\) risk\-parity optimizer with Generalized Autoregressive Conditional Heteroskedasticity \(GARCH\) and extreme value theory \(EVT\) marginals and a Student\-t copula, and an eight\-classifier machine\-learning ensemble that reallocates weights within the risk budget\. It is tested on S&amp;P 500 constituents across 75 monthly walk\-forward windows \(January 2020–January 2026\), net of transaction costs\. Findings Among twelve strategies, the pipeline delivers the highest Sharpe ratio \(1\.106\) and monthly win rate \(76\.0%\), surpassing risk parity, Hierarchical Risk Parity and the Gaussian CVaR specification it generalizes; block\-bootstrap inference confirms its advantage over passive exposure \(resampled mean Sharpe 1\.316\)\. Risk parity accumulates greater terminal wealth, a trade\-off, since tail constraints curb high\-momentum exposure in bull markets\. Research limitations/implications The analysis is limited to S&amp;P 500 constituents and a January 2020–January 2026 evaluation period\. Results may differ in other asset classes, international markets or under alternative transaction\-cost assumptions\. Future research could examine adaptive clustering schemes, alternative dependence structures and real\-time implementation constraints\. Practical implications For investors prioritizing risk\-adjusted efficiency over cumulative wealth, the framework is a transparent capital\-allocation tool whose tail discipline holds at roughly 97 basis points annually\. Originality/value To the authors&\#x27; knowledge, this is the first to combine tail\-index screening, clustering, GARCH\-EVT\-copula CVaR optimization and a machine\-learning overlay within one framework, jointly addressing the dimensionality, distributional misspecification and information\-neglect problems that defeat these tools in isolation\.
