# THE CONCEPT OF ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE AND ITS CIVIL\-LAW SIGNIFICANCE

**Autores:** Turg&\#x27;unboyev Abrorjon

**Data:** 2026-10-11 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

The thesis explains in plain language the concept of artificial intelligence \(AI\), its key features and its legal nature\. It analyses the role of AI in civil\-law relations, in particular in contracts, intellectual property, personal data and liability for harm\. Based on the legislation adopted in Uzbekistan in 2026, it argues that AI should be treated as an object of law rather than a legal subject, and offers proposals for developing the legislation\.
