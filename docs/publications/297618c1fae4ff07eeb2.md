# MODERN INNOVATIONS AND PROSPECTIVE APPROACHES TO SPEECH ECONOMY IN DIGITAL NEWSPAPER DISCOURSE

**Autores:** Ахмадова Мадина

**Data:** 2026-10-10 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

This article examines modern innovations and prospective approaches to speech economy in digital newspaper discourse\. Particular attention is given to the transformation of speech economy under the influence of digital publication environments, including search engines, news feeds, mobile alerts, social media platforms, and multimodal formats\. The study considers the ways in which digital journalism requires headlines to achieve extreme brevity, immediate recognizability, and communicative impact while preserving essential information\. The analysis focuses on text reduction in push notifications, tweets, and multimodal headlines, as well as on the influence of audience expectations and editorial ideology on the selection and compression of linguistic material\. The development of artificial intelligence is examined as the latest technological stage in the evolution of speech economy, particularly through algorithmic headline generation, A/B testing, and platform\-specific optimization\. Overall, speech economy in digital newspaper discourse is presented not simply as mechanical reduction of linguistic form, but as a dynamic communicative and ideological practice shaped by technology, audience expectations, editorial strategies, and increasingly by artificial intelligence\.
