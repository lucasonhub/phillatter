# A Two\-way Interactive Secure Communication Model for Electric Energy Metering Data

**Autores:** Xu Chen; Chao Zhang; Fei Liang; Yu Yan; Baofeng Li; Xiaomeng Duan; Yingjie Zhao

**Data:** 2026-10-12 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

INTRODUCTION: As power market reforms deepen, traditional unidirectional protocols \(e\.g\., IEC 102\) fail to meet the secure, real\-time, and bidirectional data exchange requirements of modern power grids, leaving systems vulnerable to eavesdropping and tampering\.OBJECTIVES: To ensure the confidentiality, integrity, and reliability of power data transmission by overcoming the pattern leakage vulnerabilities of traditional encryption, while satisfying the strict real\-time and high\-frequency demands of large\-scale power systems\.METHODS: Two schemes are designed: a pure SM4 symmetric encryption scheme for high\-frequency data, and an SM2\-SM4 hybrid scheme for advanced authentication\. To enhance security, a novel ECB\-CBC hybrid encryption mode is introduced for SM4\. RESULTS: The proposed ECB\-CBC hybrid mode effectively eliminates plaintext redundancy\. Its Shannon entropy stabilizes at \~7\.99 bits/byte \(nearly touching the 8\.0 theoretical ideal, vastly improving upon standard ECB&\#x27;s \~3\.5 bits/byte\)\. It also achieves an optimal inter\-block avalanche effect of \~64 bits per block\. The SM2\-SM4 hybrid scheme&\#x27;s encryption time is only 15%\-20% higher than that of pure SM4\. Across 1\-16 thread tests, the throughput of both SM4\-based schemes grows linearly, outperforming RSA and fully homomorphic encryption \(FHE\) by several orders of magnitude\.CONCLUSION: The proposed ECB\-CBC hybrid architecture overcomes standard mode vulnerabilities and achieves the macro\-level Strict Avalanche Criterion \(SAC\), significantly enhancing resistance to network attacks\. Compared to traditional asymmetric and homomorphic encryption, the SM4 and SM2\-SM4 schemes strike an optimal balance between robust security and computational efficiency for high\-concurrency power system applications\.
