# THE GATE TO AI : The Corpus in One Volume — Every Confusion, Every Answer, Every Reference

**Autores:** M\. TAALABI; Team LLM\-DIPLOMAT

**Data:** 2026-10-17 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

This volume exists because the corpus is large and the reader is new\. The corpus contains more than 300 documents\. Each one answers a specific confusion\. Each one is dated, cited, and public\. But no one can read 300 documents\. No one shouldhave to\. This volume is the gate\. It states each confusion in one sentence\. It answers it in onesentence\. It cites the paper that answers it in full\. The volume is not a summary of the corpus\. It is the index\. A reader who finds a confusion in this volume can find the paper that answers it\. A reader who finds a paper can find theconfusion it answers\. The confusions are organized by domain\. Within each domain, they are ordered by howoften they are repeated — the most common first, the most technical last\. A note on register: Every answer in this volume is a statement of fact or structure\. None is arhetorical move\. None is an attack\. None requires trust in the author\. Each is checkableagainst the paper it cites\. A note on what this volume is not: It is not a substitute for the papers\. It is a map to them\. A reader who wants the full argument should follow the references\. A reader who wants to understand the shape of the corpus can read this volume alone\.
