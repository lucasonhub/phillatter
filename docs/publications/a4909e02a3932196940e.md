# From academic burnout to career withdrawal: the progressive path and intervention strategies of professional identity crisis among medical students

**Autores:** Zhigong Wei; Nianze Li; Xingchen Peng

**Data:** 2026-10-13 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Professional identity crisis among medical students represents a major challenge in contemporary medical education\. In this narrative review, we synthesize its multidimensional determinants and propose a three\-stage developmental framework comprising academic burnout, professional identity erosion, and career withdrawal intention\. These stages represent a progressive broadening of disengagement, from strain centered on medical training, to destabilization of the professional self, and ultimately to reconsideration of a future in medicine\. We further organize intervention strategies at the individual, educational, and social/policy levels according to the major problems arising across these stages\. By integrating previously fragmented evidence on burnout, professional identity, and career disengagement, this framework provides a conceptual basis for stage\-informed intervention and a testable structure for future longitudinal research\.
