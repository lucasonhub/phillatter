# The pedagogical impact of AI\-Assisted idea generation on self\-efficacy and idea development skills in general foundation program students&\#x27; research\-based essay

**Autores:** Ahoud Yaqoob Al\-Kuwaili; Sheema Amtul Hai

**Data:** 2026-10-17 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Foundation Program students often struggle to generate research ideas and organise their thoughts when writing research\-based essays, which can undermine their confidence and academic performance\. Traditional assessment methods provide limited insight into students&\#x27; independent development of ideas\. However, recent advances in educational technology, particularly Artificial Intelligence \(AI\), offer new opportunities to support learning\. This study aims to investigate the pedagogical impact of AI\-assisted idea generation on General Foundation Program \(GFP\) students&\#x27; self\-efficacy and idea\-generating skills in research\-based essay writing\. The study adopts a quantitative research design using a single\-group pre\-test and post\-test approach\. The sample consists of 25 randomly selected GFP students\. The intervention is conducted over a period of 4\-6 weeks\. Students use ChatGPT as their primary AI tool for brainstorming, outlining, and refining ideas\. Students&\#x27; self\-efficacy is measured based on a structured questionnaire, while idea development skills are assessed through a writing rubric\. Data is analysed using paired\-sample comparison\. The results indicate improvement in both self\-efficacy and idea development\. The study highlights the importance of integrating AI tools into writing instruction to enhance student confidence and performance\.
