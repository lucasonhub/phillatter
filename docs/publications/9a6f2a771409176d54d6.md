# Adaptive preconditioned Uzawa methods for double saddle\-point systems

**Autores:** Siqi Liang; Na Huang; Yitian Xu

**Data:** 2026-10-09 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Uzawa methods are known for their simplicity and low storage requirements\. In this work, we propose preconditioned Uzawa methods for solving double saddle\-point systems, which represent a class of block three\-by\-three linear systems\. By fully exploiting the special structure of these systems, we derive a preconditioned Uzawa method with three fixed parameters and establish its convergence\. To avoid the choice of parameters, we introduce an adaptive technique that requires only simple matrix\-vector multiplications\. Additionally, we analyze the convergence of the adaptive preconditioned Uzawa method under reasonable assumptions\. Numerical experiments on test problems from the liquid crystal director model, considering both the off\-state and on\-state conditions, demonstrate the feasibility and efficiency of our adaptive preconditioned Uzawa methods\.
