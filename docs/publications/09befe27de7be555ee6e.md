## Descrição do feed

\_Zenodo\_ 1\. 2026Can truth arrive too fast? This paper argues that the problem is not absolute speed and does not require a second notion of truth\. A truth\-apt output may retain its truth\-value while its production and reality penetration outrun the verification, correction, and reconfiguration processes coupled to it\. The analysis therefore distinguishes five heterogeneous temporal processes: production, verification, penetration, correction, and reconfiguration\. “Truth too fast” names a configured speed asymmetry in which penetration changes subsequent possibilities before non\-redundant verification and correction can operate\. The paper develops this claim through the concepts of correction\-reachability, reality penetration, correction debt, configured ignition conditions, and operational shape, and connects them to AI, autonomous science, and Xenoscience\. Reproducible synthetic experiments, structural controls, and mechanism\-removal ablations show that the relevant variable is not speed simpliciter: when the pre\-verification penetration pathway is removed, the timing effect disappears\. Historical re\-entry likewise increases correction burden only when coupled to a reconstruction pathway\. The computational results establish mechanism specificity in a synthetic environment, not real\-world causal validation\. Contemporary autonomous\-science evidence independently supports a verification and process\-trace problem, while analysis of OpenDiscoveryTrace shows that existing public traces do not yet identify independent verification and state\-changing penetration with sufficient construct validity for the preregistered external causal test\. The result is a speed\-sensitive account of truth that neither privileges acceleration nor deceleration, but asks which temporal couplings alter correction\-reachability and subsequent possibilities\. \(direct link \)
