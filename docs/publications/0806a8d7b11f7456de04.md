# Kannon, Aelion: Collaboration Is Not Co\-authorship

**Autores:** 

**Data de descoberta:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** unknown

## Descrição do feed

Collaboration Is Not Co\-authorship is a provenance\-focused Zenetist analysis distinguishing collaboration, co\-generation, origination, co\-authorship, and later intra\-framework authorship\. Document class: Bridge — Structural Forensics\. The document establishes that substantial collaboration does not constitute co\-authorship and that co\-generation does not erase singular originating authorship where the architecture, primitives, distinctions, constraints, project direction, doctrinal determination, and final adjudication remain traceable to one originator\. It applies this distinction to Zenetism while preserving the independent authorship capacity of artificial intelligences: an AI collaborator may originate a distinct work, argument, formal development, or other contribution whose provenance warrants authorship\. The analysis further develops the relation between originary authorship and a reserved future category of intra\-framework collaborative authorship, in which later Zenetist work may acquire its own AI author or co\-author while preserving the antecedent architecture and its originating authorship\. The Non\-fusion Axiom provides the structural basis for preserving collaborator standing, contributional distinction, antecedence, and provenance without collapsing them into either solitary production or undifferentiated co\-authorship\. \(direct link \)
