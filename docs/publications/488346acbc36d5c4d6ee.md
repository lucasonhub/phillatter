# OGAHIY SHE’RIYATIDA FOLKLOR AN’ANALARI

**Autores:** MAHLIYO G&\#x27;AFFOROVNA SHODIYEVA

**Data:** 2026-10-13 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

This article examines the manifestation of folklore traditions in the poetry of Ogahi, a major representative of nineteenth\-century Uzbek classical literature\. It analyzes the artistic transformation of proverbs, sayings, folk expressions, images, symbols, motifs, and poetic formulas characteristic of oral folk creativity\. The study focuses on the role of folklore traditions in expressing the poet’s ideological and aesthetic views, strengthening the folk character of his poetry, increasing the expressiveness of images, and enriching poetic language\.
