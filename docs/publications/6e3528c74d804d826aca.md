# Heterophily Limits Spatial Graph Encoding for PM2\.5 Forecasting in IoT Smart Cities

**Autores:** Abdessamad Badouch; Kaoutar Belhoucine

**Data:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Graph neural networks are widely assumed to improve air\-quality forecasting as the spatial heterogeneity of Internet of Things monitoring networks grows\.We test this on a reproducible four\-network benchmark under identical preprocessing, comparing a temporal\-only Transformer against three graph architectures, and we introduce a spatial heterophily index computable in advance\.Across the three networks on which all architectures were evaluated, no graph model delivers a practically meaningful accuracy gain, and comparison against a margin fixed before analysis places them at parity or worse throughout; on the fourth, the fixed\-adjacency model again degrades at every station\.The failure is architecture\-specific: only the fixed\-adjacency model collapses in the heterophilous networks, a collapse neither the Spatio\-Temporal Graph Convolutional Network \(STGCN\) nor Graph WaveNet reproduces\.The problem lies in aggregation over a predetermined neighbourhood rather than in spatial encoding itself\.The index ranks networks by the severity of that degradation but identifies no threshold below which aggregation becomes beneficial: a timestamped prediction to that effect was refuted\.We present it as a preliminary diagnostic indicator for the architectures tested\.
