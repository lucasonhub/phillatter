# THE USE OF GENERATIVE ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE IN STUDENTS&\#x27; INDEPENDENT LEARNING

**Autores:** Sh\.F\. Abdullayeva

**Data:** 2026-10-09 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Today, the effective use of digital technologies in higher education is one of the key areas for developing students&\#x27; independent learning activities\. In engineering education in particular, students need to acquire theoretical knowledge independently, analyze technical problems, find solutions to practical tasks, and evaluate their own performance\. From this perspective, the use of generative artificial intelligence technologies offers new opportunities for organizing independent learning\.
