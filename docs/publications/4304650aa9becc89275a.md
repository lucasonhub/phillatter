# From peri\-implant distance ranges to spatial niches: a resolution\-aware analytical framework for the intracortical microelectrode–brain interface

**Autores:** Miaozhe Huo; Zhengyang Xue; Yifei Sheng; Shiying Li

**Data:** 2026-10-12 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Intracortical microelectrodes \(IMEs\) record local neural activity from within the cortex\. Current studies of IME tissue responses provide whole\-transcriptome multicellular spots, targeted transcript coordinates, regional ribonucleic acid \(RNA\) or protein profiles, registered morphology, and exploratory associations between tissue measurements and recording performance\. Implant\-centered distance locates these measurements relative to the device\. This methodological framework review extends implant\-centered analyses by defining spatial niches that characterize local cellular, molecular, and structural context at defined neighborhood scales\. We organize niche\-level analyses into niche identification, implant\-referenced spatial organization, and niche\-conditioned molecular relationships, with platform\-specific guidance for Visium, Xenium, and GeoMx inputs\. Finally, we propose candidate relationships between niche\-derived features and recording outcomes at matched functional scales for testing in future IME studies\.
