# AI\-ENHANCED FLIPPED EFL LEARNING AND VOCABULARY ACQUISITION

**Autores:** Арайлым Тұрусбекова; Arailym Turusbekova; S\. G\. \(Seydalieva\) Omarovna

**Data:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Abstract\. The article discusses the influence of the &quot;inverted class&quot; model, enhanced by artificial intelligence tools in teaching English as a foreign language, on mastering the lexical stock\. The focus on three\-stage learning, which is based on independent repetition using artificial intelligence after lessons, recognizing new vocabulary before class, actively using it in the classroom in a semantic context, rather than the model of memorizing words a traditional lesson\. The methodological basis of the study was a comparative analysis of the works of Kazakhstani and Russian researchers published in 2023\-2025, systematization of the didactic activities of digital tools and pedagogical design of Model tasks for the EFL lesson\.The result of the analysis showed that artificial intelligence is more productive as a means of connecting pre\-training, level adjusting training, instant feedback and interval repetition than as a separate technology that automatically &quot;trains&quot; vocabulary\.In the practical part, samples of identifying new words from the text,creating context, conducting a microdiologue with AI\-chat,comparing incorrect answers, forming an individual dictionary map snd deferred verification were presented\.
