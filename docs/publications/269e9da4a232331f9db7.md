# Near\-quantitative nickel recovery and selective chromium preconcentration from complex industrial wastewaters by pyrrolidinium ionic\-liquid dispersive liquid–liquid microextraction

**Autores:** DHANALAKSHMI MARIAPPAN; V\. Aruna Janani; Saravana Kumar Ganesan; A\. Saravana Raj

**Data:** 2026-10-13 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Industrial effluents from electroplating and allied surface\-finishing operations may contain chromium \(Cr\) and nickel \(Ni\) at concentrations that restrict water reuse while representing potentially recoverable metal resources\. This study evaluated dispersive liquid–liquid microextraction \(DLLME\) using 1\-butyl\-1\-methylpyrrolidinium bis\(trifluoromethylsulfonyl\)imide \(\[BMPyrr\]\[TFSI\]\) for Cr recovery/preconcentration from industrial wastewater and Ni aqueous\-phase removal from a separately investigated aqueous feed\. Cr and Ni were investigated using independent single\-factor experimental designs covering metal concentration, ionic\-liquid volume, centrifugation conditions and NaCl addition\. Five real industrial wastewaters were screened for total Cr by flame atomic absorption spectroscopy \(AAS\), and the selected metal\-plating wastewater contained 10\.31 ppm Cr\. Post\-extraction \[BMPyrr\]\[TFSI\] and recovered Cr\-containing material were evaluated by reuse testing over three cycles, Fourier\-transform infrared spectroscopy \(FTIR\) and scanning electron microscopy coupled with energy\-dispersive X\-ray spectroscopy \(SEM\-EDX\)\. Chromium recovery reached approximately 89% using 400 μL \[BMPyrr\]\[TFSI\] at pH 6, 2000 rpm centrifugation, 15 min phase separation and 0\.4 g NaCl\. In the Ni experiments, the highest measured aqueous\-phase removal was 98\.2% at 40 ppm Ni, pH 10, 5 mL ionic liquid, 2500 rpm and 2\.4 g NaCl\. Because hydroxo\-speciation and Ni\(OH\) 2 precipitation/co\-sedimentation may contribute at pH 10\. FTIR retained the principal TFSI\-anion and pyrrolidinium signatures after extraction\. SEM\-EDX showed Cr enrichment in the recovered solid\-associated material, with approximately 12\.9 wt% Cr and 8\.6 wt% Fe, indicating concomitant recovery of competing species\. Reuse performance decreased from 96\.1% in the first cycle to 81\.8% and 65\.6% in the second and third cycles, respectively\. \[BMPyrr\]\[TFSI\]\-based DLLME provided substantial Cr recovery/preconcentration and high Ni aqueous\-phase removal under the investigated conditions\.
