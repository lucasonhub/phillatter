# Multitarget Drug Design for Cardiovascular Disease: Bridging Chemistry and Medicine

**Autores:** Gyuzel Yu\. Mitronova; Vladimir N\. Belov; Constanze Schmidt; Stephan E\. Lehnart

**Data:** 2026-10-13 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

This review provides an overview of recent advances in the development of multitarget drugs \(MTDs\) for cardiac diseases, with a particular focus on emerging molecular scaffolds and their pharmacological properties\. We discuss the fundamental principles of cardiac polypharmacology and multitarget drug design, highlighting key compound classes, such as 1,4\-benzothiazepines, 1,4\-dihydropyridines \(DHPs\), and polyphenol derivatives\. Particular emphasis is placed on the relationship between the structural features and the pharmacological activity, efficacy, and therapeutic potential of these compounds\.
