# AI in Enterprise Software 2026: What the Data Shows

**Autores:** David Ohnstad

**Data:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Enterprises spend billions on artificial intelligence, yet half of new models never leave the sandbox\. According to recent data, only forty\-eight percent of pilot projects reach production within twelve months\. David Ohnstad examines this deployment paradox, revealing why feedback loops and real\-world data are essential to bridging the gap between heavy investment and actual enterprise value\.
