# ECG\-RSI: Recursive Self\-Improvement through Targeted Electrocardiogram Waveform Evolution

**Autores:** Xiaoliang Wang; Zhuo Chen; Chuanyang Zheng; Jiangwei Lao; Quanbin Wang; Honglin Lin; Jiajia Liu; Zhihong Lu; Hao Jin; Jian Wang; Lijun Wu

**Data:** 2026-10-13 · **Tipo:** preprint

## Resumo

Electrocardiogram \(ECG\) models need supervision that targets evolving weaknesses and remains faithful to waveform evidence\. We introduce ECG\-RSI, a recursive self\-improvement framework combining verified waveform synthesis with feedback\-guided intervention search\. Signal remeasurement verifies waveform edits; construction errors guide generator repair, while learner errors motivate interventions compared on a separate selection set\. Across seven benchmarks, three rounds raise Qwen3\.5\-35B\-A3B&\#x27;s mean question\-answering accuracy from 34\.57% to 56\.04% and mean classification F1 from 24\.27 to 34\.03\. Across the retained trajectory, the preferred supervision shifts from direct reinforcement to confusion\-focused contrasts\. Continued feedback correction yields diminishing external gains\. These results motivate separating error diagnosis from intervention selection and checking whether gains on the feedback set transfer to independent benchmarks\.
