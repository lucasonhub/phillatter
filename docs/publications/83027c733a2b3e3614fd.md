# CULINARY HERITAGE OF UKRAINE: POST\-WAR TRANSFORMATION AND DIGITAL APPROACHES TO PRESERVATION

**Autores:** K\. Svidlo; Nataliia M\. Bogdan; Larysa V\. Obolentseva; Лідія Піддубна; Олена Каролоп

**Data:** 2026-10-12 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

In the context of the post\-war transformation of rural areas in Ukraine, culinary tourism is considered an important tool for sustainable development and regional recovery\. The preservation and promotion of culinary heritage through tourism practices create opportunities for economic diversification, support of local communities, and strengthening of cultural identity\. The aim of the study is to analyze the processes of integrating Ukrainian culinary heritage into the tourism sector and to assess its impact on the development of rural areas under current conditions\. The methodological framework combines qualitative and quantitative approaches: semi\-structured interviews were conducted with stakeholders \(entrepreneurs, experts, and community representatives\), along with an analysis of statistical and analytical data for 2024–2025\. The results of the study show that culinary heritage is a key element of national identity, as it includes traditional recipes, cooking technologies, local ingredients, and gastronomic practices\. At the same time, its preservation is challenged by globalization, urbanization, and military conflict, which lead to the loss of cultural practices and the destruction of infrastructure\. It has been found that the effectiveness of culinary tourism development depends on the level of coordination among stakeholders and the involvement of local communities\. Insufficient public participation and fragmented policies reduce the potential of such initiatives, whereas participatory approaches enhance their effectiveness\. Culinary tourism contributes to rural development through job creation, the formation of local branding, and support for creative industries\. Digital technologies, including online archives and artificial intelligence tools, play a significant role in documenting and promoting heritage\. Culinary heritage has strong potential as a resource for the post\-war recovery of Ukraine; however, its effective use requires integrated governance, digitalization, and active community participation\.
