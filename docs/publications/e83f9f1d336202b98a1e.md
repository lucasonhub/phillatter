# Forensic Data Analytics and Anti\-Money Laundering: Panacea for Fraud Prevention in Nigeria and United States of America

**Autores:** Chukwuchebem Christian Umahi

**Data:** 2026-10-13 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

The study examined the effect of forensic data analytics on anti\-money laundering efforts and its effectiveness in preventing financial fraud in Nigeria and the United States of America \(U\.S\.A\.\)\. Specifically, the study sought to evaluate the role of forensic data analytics in detecting suspicious financial transactions and to compare its effectiveness in enhancing anti money laundering measures in the two countries\. The study equally compared the effectiveness of forensic data analytics on anti\-money laundering efforts between financial institutions in the United States and Nigeria\. A survey research design was employed, targeting a population of 200 top\-level bankers, with 100 respondents from Nigeria and 100 from the United States of America, selected through a snowball sampling technique\. Primary data were collected using a structured electronic questionnaire\. The hypotheses were tested using student t\-test and Spearman’s ranked order correlation at a 5% significance level to determine the relationships between forensic data analytics, anti\-money laundering, and fraud prevention\. The findings revealed that: forensic data analytics did not have a significantly stronger effect on anti\-money laundering in the United States compared to Nigeria \(t = 1\.731, p = \.085\); forensic data analytics positively influenced anti\-money laundering in both countries \(beta = 0\.499, p &lt; \.001\); forensic data analytics strongly enhanced fraud prevention in both countries \(beta = 0\.817, p &lt; \.001\)\. Therefore, investments in forensic data analytics contribute to the development of more robust monitoring structures, improve the capacity of institutions to respond to complex financial crimes, and reinforce accountability mechanisms\. The study recommended that compliance officers and risk management teams should actively incorporate advanced forensic data analytics techniques into their monitoring and reporting processes\. By leveraging data\-driven insights, these teams can enhance the accuracy of transaction monitoring, improve the identification of suspicious activities, and strengthen overall regulatory compliance\.
