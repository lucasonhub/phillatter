# AUTHENTIC LEADERSHIP AND SDG 16: THEORETICAL EVOLUTION, CRITICISMS, AND FUTURE AVENUES FOR RESEARCHERS

**Autores:** Jorge Salcedo; Franco Gandolfi; Astrid Garza; Siti Khadijah Mohd Nasrah

**Data:** 2026-10-13 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Objective: This article critically examines the theoretical evolution, empirical contributions, and principal criticisms of authentic leadership, exploring its relevance to Sustainable Development Goal 16 \(SDG 16\)\. Theoretical Framework: The review traces authentic leadership’s historical origins and conceptual development, examining foundational contributions by George, Luthans and Avolio, Gardner and colleagues, and Walumbwa and colleagues\. It considers integrity, ethical responsibility, and commitment to core values as central elements of the construct\. Method: An integrative literature review synthesizes foundational and contemporary scholarship on authentic leadership, including its theoretical foundations, measurement approaches, empirical contributions, and critical debates\. Results and Discussion: The review examines the formalization of authentic leadership and the development, validation, and widespread use of the Authentic Leadership Questionnaire \(ALQ\)\. It identifies persistent debates concerning conceptual clarity, measurement, and normative assumptions\. It also examines authentic leadership’s potential contribution to SDG 16 through ethical leadership and accountable, transparent, and inclusive institutions\. Research Implications: Future research should strengthen conceptual precision, address measurement limitations, and examine authentic leadership across diverse cultural and institutional contexts\. Further investigation is needed to assess its contribution to institutional accountability and the objectives of SDG 16\. Originality/Value: The article integrates theoretical development and critical perspectives with an explicit focus on SDG 16, offering a foundation for advancing authentic leadership research and examining its institutional relevance\.
