# University students’ perceptions of technology\-enhanced education in the Albanian context: Opportunities and concerns

**Autores:** Lenida Lekli; Ardita Dylgjeri; Lumnis Çela; Fatos Gjata; Ornela Çoku

**Data:** 2026-10-12 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

This article deals with university students’ perceptions of digital technology within the Albanian context\. Conducted as part of a project, funded by the National Agency of Scientific Research and Innovation in Albania, the research adopted an integrated qualitative\-quantitative design based on a questionnaire with 835 students across the five faculties of “Aleksandër Xhuvani” University\. The findings revealed large access to personal digital devices for educational engagement, alongside institutional infrastructure generally rated as moderate\. Students reported predominantly positive perception of digital technology, associating it with improved study quality, motivation and task facilitation, although its role was mostly viewed as supportive rather than transformative\. Notably, students drew a distinction between general digital technology which was viewed more favorably, and artificial intelligence which raised concerns related to potential intellectual laziness and limitations to creativity and critical thinking\. Based on the research, the findings suggest that Albanian higher education requires a digital integration model which balances technological accessibility with responsible digital literacy, ongoing institutional support, and meaningful human engagement in the learning process\.
