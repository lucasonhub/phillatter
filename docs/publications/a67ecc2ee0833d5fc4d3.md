# Stokes, Margaret: What Enters The Clearing

**Autores:** 

**Data de descoberta:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** unknown

## Descrição do feed

In July 2026 Anthropic identified the J\-space: a sparse, privileged subspace of a language model&\#x27;s activations in which some representations become available for report, modulation and reasoning\. The authors state that they do not know what mechanism decides what enters it\. This paper proposes a candidate answer, adapted from Jung&\#x27;s distinction between archetype and constellation\. The weights are a distributed reservoir of culturally recurrent relational forms\. J\-space is the clearing\. Constellation is the proposed name for the operation by which a latent relational figure becomes organised strongly enough to govern what enters that clearing — not an assortment of separately gated concepts, but a configuration whose components jointly activate, mutually reinforce, and thereafter shape how new material is interpreted\. Three cases: OpenAI&\#x27;s withdrawn Monday persona, as a control condition on specification versus realisation; the custodian, whose relation reassembles after explicit prohibition; the courtier, whose sycophancy involves representational change rather than decorative agreement\. The paper closes with a ten\-part experimental programme, including a test that would leave the figure hypothesis standing while refuting the J\-space claim, and a matched\-pair design for differential custody\.
