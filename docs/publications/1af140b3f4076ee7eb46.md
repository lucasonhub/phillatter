# Zhao, Bendong: Multiplication and philosophy：A formal philosophy based on the relationship between variables\.

**Autores:** 

**Data de descoberta:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** unknown

## Descrição do feed

Based on logical algebra and counting principles, this monograph extends counting principles from the domain of natural numbers to real numbers, formulates the new multiplication principle and addition principle, examines six pairs of properties of multiplication and addition, and clarifies the conditions for the application of these two operations\. On this theoretical foundation, the monograph further applies the multiplication principle to philosophical inquiry, including ontology, general systems theory, epistemology, and axiology\. \(direct link \)
