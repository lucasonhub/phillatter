# Didehvar, Farzad: Classification of What AI Does and What AI Does with Texts

**Autores:** 

**Data de descoberta:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** unknown

## Descrição do feed

Here, we try to categorize different ways in which AI takes action, as well as the ways in which scientific issues can be published using AI\. We first present a general classification of the actions of AI and then consider the classification of AI activities involving texts as a part of this general classification\. We also consider different degrees of human supervision and introduce the notion of a finite series of activities\. Finally, we propose a general tree that places the classification of activities involving texts within the broader classification of actions performed by AI\. \(direct link \)
