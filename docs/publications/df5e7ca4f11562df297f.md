# Land, culture, voice, and economics: an Indigenous land based framework for STEM and agricultural education

**Autores:** Quintana M\. Clark; Olivia Perez; Jennifer L\. Freeman

**Data:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

As Tribal Nations explore emerging opportunities in bio\-based economies, hemp education offers a meaningful platform for integrating land stewardship, cultural knowledge, community priorities, and workforce development\. Yet existing science, technology, engineering, and mathematics \(STEM\) and agricultural education models rarely provide culturally grounded approaches that integrate Indigenous epistemologies, land\-based pedagogy, and sovereignty\-centered economic development\. This article introduces a conceptual Indigenous land\-based curriculum framework for culturally grounded STEM and agricultural education, using hemp education as an applied and relational learning context\. Grounded in Indigenous research methodologies and guided by a Two\-Eyed Seeing orientation, the framework was developed through qualitative conceptual inquiry and iterative synthesis of scholarship on Indigenous epistemologies, culturally grounded STEM education, land\-based learning, and community\-centered economic development\. Centered on Land, Culture, Voice, and Economics as foundational dimensions of curriculum design, the model is represented through a flower metaphor that emphasizes holistic, experiential, place\-based, community\-based, and intergenerational learning\. Indigenous values of reciprocity, relationality, responsibility, and respect further shape how teaching and learning are enacted within the framework\. By articulating a cohesive pedagogical structure that bridges Indigenous knowledge systems, applied STEM learning, and sustainable bio\-based economic futures, the article offers a conceptual model for designing culturally grounded curricula, advances scholarship on Indigenous land\-based curriculum design, and provides a foundation for future curriculum development and research\.
