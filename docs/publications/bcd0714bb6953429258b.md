# Madurez del marco normativo para la IA en la educación superior ecuatoriana

**Autores:** Jorge Maldonado‐Mahauad; Juan Pablo Carvallo; María Cristina León\-Carvajal

**Data:** 2026-10-14 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

The rise of artificial intelligence \(AI\), particularly generative AI, has transformed teaching, research, and higher education management at a pace that outstrips the responsiveness of Latin American regulatory frameworks\. This study diagnoses the maturity level of Ecuador&\#x27;s regulatory framework on AI in higher education and identifies its critical gaps\. A systematic analysis was conducted on 81 regulatory instruments \(pending laws, regulations, resolutions, guidelines, and institutional policies\) registered in the Regional AI in Education Observatory of the Erasmus\+ GENIAL project, as of May 2026\. Instruments were coded using a six\-dimension framework \(actor, object, mechanism, purpose, approach, and maturity\) and synthesized into the Regulatory Maturity Index \(RMI\), which weights regulatory quality\. Ecuador achieves an RMI of 70\.6/100 \(very high level\), ranking third among the 28 countries in the Observatory\. However, this leadership stems from the breadth of the corpus and university\-level activity rather than ecosystem consolidation\. Half of the instruments remain at the stage of a declaration of intent, and maturity is the most lagging dimension\. Institutional\-level instruments account for 87\.7% of the corpus, revealing a bottom\-up regulatory construction in the absence of specific national legislation\. Administrative and data\-protection domains concentrate 70\.7% of the instruments; only 7\.3% explicitly regulate generative AI\. A differentiated policy agenda is proposed for CES, the Ministry of Education, CACES, and higher education institutions\.
