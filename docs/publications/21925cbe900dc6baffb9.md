# Optimization of Medium Conditions for Removal of Vat Green 1 by Bentonite Using Response Surface Methodology

**Autores:** Burçin Yıldız; Ayşe Özgüven; Vahap Yönten

**Data:** 2026-10-12 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

In this study, the removal conditions of Vat Brilliant Green FFB \(C\.I\. Vat Green 1, VG1\) dye were optimized using a two\-stage experimental design\. The Plackett–Burman Design program was used to eliminate independent parameters such as contact time \(min\), initial concentration \(mg/L\), adsorbent dose \(g\), temperature \(°C\), pH and shaking speed \(rpm\)\. This method was also used to measure the effects of independent parameters on the response\. As a result of the PBD, initial concentration, adsorbent dose, contact time, and pH were selected as the most effective parameters in dye removal\. A central composite design was applied to determine the maximum response values and effective parameters\. As a result of the obtained linear model and equations, a maximum removal rate of 65% was achieved; this indicates that VG1 is difficult to remove in aqueous solutions\. In this study, the highest experimentally observed removal efficiency was observed at an initial concentration of 1100 mg/L, a contact time of 27\.5 minutes, an adsorbent dose of 1\.0 g, and a pH of 7\. While this study demonstrates the recalcitrant nature of VG1 dye within statistical boundaries, the developed optimization model provides a practical, cost\-effective, and a preliminary statistical framework for designing industrial wastewater treatment plants and optimizing operational conditions\.
