# Preface

**Autores:** Wenbing Wu

**Data:** 2026-10-10 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

The second edition of ACES brings together interdisciplinary studies addressing emerging challenges in intelligent infrastructure, construction technology, built heritage, material behavior, energy\-responsive buildings, and extraterrestrial engineering environments\. The contributions demonstrate how retrieval\-augmented and agentic large language models can support bridge operation and maintenance, construction quality assessment, and knowledge\-driven engineering decision\-making\. Other studies examine the conservation and contemporary interpretation of traditional wooden architecture and urban industrial heritage landscapes, while investigations into lightweight building envelopes and grid\-interactive commercial buildings explore energy performance and climate\-responsive design\. The volume also presents experimental and numerical analyses of fractured sandstone under water immersion and freeze\-thaw conditions, together with research on thermal coring, water\-vapor phase transitions, and oxygen production from lunar regolith\. In addition, AI\-IoT solutions for smart venues and urban traffic are discussed\. Collectively, these studies illustrate the growing integration of artificial intelligence, experimental mechanics, heritage studies, energy systems, and space\-resource engineering in the development of advanced and adaptable civil infrastructure\.
