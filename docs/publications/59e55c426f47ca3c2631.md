# Niami, Morteza: Beyond Awareness: A Non\-Circular Criterion for Automatic and Dynamic Masking in the Regulation of Determination

**Autores:** 

**Data de descoberta:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** unknown

## Descrição do feed

Within the framework of determination regulation, the automatic mask and the dynamic mask are distinguished not by the presence or absence of conscious awareness but by adaptive capacity: the automatic mask fixes determination through ontological operational memory, while the dynamic mask preserves the possibility of readjustment\. This distinction, however, faces a serious methodological objection\. If adaptive capacity is inferred from observed readjustment, and readjustment is precisely what the distinction is meant to explain, the criterion risks vicious circularity\. This article confronts that objection directly\. It argues that the apparent circularity dissolves once adaptive capacity is treated as a genuine disposition, analyzed counterfactually rather than inferred post hoc from occurrent behavior\. Drawing on the standard treatment of dispositional predicates in analytic metaphysics, the article proposes a perturbation criterion: masking structures are classified not by whether they happen to readjust under ordinary conditions, but by how they respond to a calibrated increase in erosive load relative to the threshold of sufficiency, an independent variable specified prior to and apart from the readjustment it is meant to predict\. Three response\-profiles are identified under such perturbation: rigid persistence, adaptive reorganization, and structural collapse, corresponding respectively to automatic mask dominance, dynamic mask engagement, and productive rupture\. The article situates this solution against debates on the reflexivity of habitus in Bourdieu and his critics, and against dispositional essentialism in contemporary metaphysics, arguing that the resulting criterion is no more circular than the standard analysis of fragility or solubility, while remaining a philosophical rather than an empirical criterion\. Keywords: automatic mask, dynamic mask, dispositional capacity, determination regulation, threshold of sufficiency, productive rupture, habitus, reflexivity \(direct link \)
