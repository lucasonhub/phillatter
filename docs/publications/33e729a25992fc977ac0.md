# A comparative analysis of direct and indirect strategy\-based interventions for improving ESL learners’ speaking proficiency

**Autores:** S\. Mekala; R\. K\. Dharini; M\. P\. Shabitha

**Data:** 2026-10-15 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

This study examines the impact of Direct and Indirect strategy\-based interventions on the speaking performance of ESL learners\. The participants are undergraduate students at Periyar Government Arts and Science College, Tiruchirappalli\. The study employs a quasi\-experimental design involving two experimental groups and a control group\. Data have been collected through questionnaires and speaking tasks, while classroom observations have also been recorded by the researcher to provide additional contextual information about learners’ participation and engagement\. The data have been analyzed using correlation analysis, one\-way ANOVA, and ANCOVA\. The results have shown significant improvements across the assessed dimensions of speaking performance following both Direct and Indirect strategy\-based interventions, with learners receiving the Indirect strategy\-based intervention showing greater gains\. Among them, cognitive, metacognitive, and social strategies have correlated well with speaking components, highlighting the vital role of these strategies in improving the learners’ speaking skill\. Within the context of the present study, these findings suggest that guided pedagogical intervention may play an important role in supporting improvements in ESL learners’ speaking performance across the aspects of fluency, coherence, grammatical acceptability, idea expansion and volume\. The study supports the inclusion of structured learning strategies in language instruction and reinforces the pedagogical shift towards learner\-centered, strategy\-informed teaching frameworks\.
