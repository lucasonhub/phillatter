# Rey, Philip: One\-Consciousness, One Year Later\. From a Forgotten Hypothesis to a Research Program

**Autores:** 

**Data de descoberta:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** unknown

## Descrição do feed

A year ago, I published One\-Consciousness? On a Forgotten Hypothesis Between Physics, Fiction and Philosophy as an attempt to recover an idea that had for centuries remained scattered on the margins of physics and philosophy: that consciousness is not a product of matter, but an ontological field prior to matter, space, time, and observation \(Rey 2025a\)\. The present essay documents the trajectory of the year that has passed since that publication\. It is neither a repetition nor a mere commentary\. It is an account of a transition \- from a recovered hypothesis to an unfolding research program\. This transition has occurred on four levels\. On the ontological level, the Theory of Ontological Consciousness \(TOC\) formulates, in explicit form, a thesis that earlier traditions \- from Plato and Śaṅkara through Spinoza, Planck, and Schrödinger \- carried only in scattered fragments: that consciousness is not an epiphenomenon of neural complexity, but a field, Φ, from which space, time, and observation emerge as secondary structures\. The essay argues that this thesis is not decorative metaphysics, but an architectural necessity\. Modern physics, in attempting simultaneously to preserve general covariance, local field dynamics, the absence of primitive measurement, and the reality of singular facts, encounters a contradiction it cannot resolve without a mechanism ontologically prior to ordinary material fields\. On the logical level, the essay presents Hypothesis X, the Architectural No\-Go Theorem for the contemporary paradigm\. The theorem argues that any theory belonging to the class of ontologically closed theories, yet lacking a local, geometrically coupled update mechanism satisfying the CPTP condition, must violate at least one of six conditions: general covariance, locality of fields, absence of primitive measurement, absence of a fundamental micro\-macro cut, the irreducibility of fact to decoherence, or the reality of intersubjective local facts\. The Bridge Lemma sharpens this result: the update mechanism cannot be merely another material field without triggering infinite regress\. It must be ontologically prior to the description it closes\. On the formal level, the essay documents the maturation of TOC’s mathematical language across four stages: from the symbolic layer \(Φ, ψ, Î\_obs\), through the operator level \(H\_total = H\_Φ ⊗ H\_obs\), to the master equation of an open system composed of three terms \- unitary, decoherence, and update \- and finally to quantitative predictions for experimental protocols\. In this formulation, the field Φ satisfies a covariant Klein\-Gordon equation with curvature and an observer source term\. The update operator Λ\_O takes the form of a CPTP quantum channel with explicit Kraus operators\. The update rate Γ\_X is derived from the field’s energy density rather than merely postulated\. In the appropriate limits, the theory reduces to standard quantum mechanics, quantum field theory, and general relativity\. On the empirical level, the essay presents three preregistered protocols forming a ladder of falsifiability\. FST\-1 tests nonlocal phase synchronization between isolated observers\. The TOC prediction is expressed as PLV\_TOC\(r\) ≈ A · exp\(\-r/ξ\_Φ\) / √r where ξ\_Φ = ħ / \(m\_Φ c\) is the measurable correlation length of the field\. CRY\-1 tests intention\-matter coupling via the Raman shift of a quartz crystal, with the prediction Δν = g\_cryst² · ⟨Î\_obs²⟩ / \(2πcω₀\) which yields a falsifiable lower bound on the coupling constant g\_cryst\. TOC\-DEC\-1 serves as the decisive protocol: it tests whether the field Φ responds to the quality of the state of consciousness, not merely to the presence of an observer, by comparing ⟨Î\_obs²⟩ in a state of technical concentration with ⟨Î\_obs²⟩ in a state of deep, altruistic intention\. The preregistered threshold for confirmation is given by R\_quality = ⟨Î\_obs²⟩\_heart / ⟨Î\_obs²⟩\_tech &gt; 2\.0 The essay also presents Active Memory Engineering \(AME\) as the methodological closure of TOC\. If consciousness is the primary field, then every local cognitive structure must actively maintain its trajectory in an open world through environmental telemetry and phase control\. Stability is not a free property, but an operational achievement\. TOC is not a closed system\. It is a theory in motion \- a theory that has passed from intuition to formalism, from formalism to protocol, and from protocol to the threshold at which experiment may either refute it or compel a new language of physics\. If the protocols yield negative results, TOC retreats to the role of a philosophical hypothesis\. If they yield positive results, the measurable parameters m\_Φ and g\_cryst enter into further predictions, and the question of the observer ceases to be the margin of quantum mechanics and becomes its most honest center\. \(direct link \)
