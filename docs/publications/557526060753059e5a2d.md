# LOGOPEDIYANING BOSHQA FANLAR BILAN ALOQASI: NAZARIY ASOSLARI VA AMALIY AHAMIYATI

**Autores:** Eshboyeva Ozoda Yuldashevna

**Data:** 2026-10-12 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

The article analyses the place of speech therapy \(logopedics\) within the system of sciences and its relationships with pedagogy, special education, psychology, psycholinguistics, linguistics, anatomy, physiology, neurology, otorhinolaryngology and audiology\. Intra\-system \(pedagogical and psychological\) and inter\-system \(medical\-biological and linguistic\) links of speech therapy are distinguished, and their role in identifying, differentially diagnosing, correcting and preventing speech disorders is shown\. Drawing on A\.R\. Luria’s theory of the functional blocks of the brain and A\.A\. Leontiev’s model of speech production, the psycholinguistic foundations of speech and language assessment are described\. A five\-stage model of speech therapy support based on interdisciplinary integration is proposed, together with recommendations for improving professional collaboration in inclusive education\.
