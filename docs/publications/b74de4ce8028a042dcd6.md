# de Vries, Bouke: AI, Political Polarization, and the Risk of Value Lock\-In

**Autores:** 

**Data de descoberta:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** unknown

## Descrição do feed

Discussions of AI\-enabled value lock\-in have focused mostly on competition between AI companies and geopolitical rivals, particularly the United States and China\. This article highlights a neglected yet plausible alternative pathway: domestic political competition within the US\. Advanced AI could turn the permanent entrenchment of one side&\#x27;s preferred values in America&\#x27;s culture war into a realistic possibility, particularly if the federal government acquires control over frontier AI systems through the formal or de facto nationalization of the companies developing them\. Against this background, I argue that both progressives and conservatives may plausibly come to fear that their opponents will eventually use advanced AI to secure lasting control over society&\#x27;s moral and political trajectory\. Such fears could create strong incentives to pursue lock\-in pre\-emptively, apart from any independent appeal that permanently entrenching one&\#x27;s values might have for some\. At the same time, I find that these divisions create opportunities for cooperation\. Because neither side knows who will ultimately control advanced AI, each has reasons to accept reciprocal constraints on its ability to pursue lock\-in\. Drawing on the work of Finn Moorhouse, two complementary forms are considered: normative constraints on how advanced AI may be used and power\-sharing arrangements limiting unilateral lock\-in\. Given current cross\-partisan concern about AI and support for its regulation, I further suggest that there may now be a limited window for such agreements, which could narrow if AI governance becomes more partisan or if broader political polarization further reduces willingness to cooperate\.
