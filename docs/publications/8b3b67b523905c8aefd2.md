# RDW and Immature Granulocytes as Predictors of Hemolysis and Immune Dysfunction in VV\-ECMO Patients

**Autores:** Emre Oteyaka; Mustafa Sedat Yıldız; Berk Durak; Mustafa Eren; Aykun Hakgör; Yahya Yıldız; Halil Türkoğlu

**Data:** 2026-10-13 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Shear stress can cause severe blood trauma during venovenous \(VV\) extracorporeal membrane oxygenation \(ECMO\), primarily because of high\-flow rates and contact with foreign surfaces\.Therefore, optimizing the timing of oxygenator exchange is critical for mitigating these hematological complications\.Herein, we present the case of a patient who required VV\-ECMO for refractory acute respiratory distress syndrome and septic shock following emergency coronary artery bypass grafting\.During ECMO support, C\-reactive protein, red cell distribution width, and immature granulocytes served as important biomarkers\.These markers facilitated early detection of filter dysfunction, guided oxygenator exchange, and enabled monitoring of shear stress\.Ultimately, monitoring these biomarkers may help optimize the management of complications associated with high\-flow ECMO and provide a basis for further clinical research\.
