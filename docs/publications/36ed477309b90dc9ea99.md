# The Concept of the Future and Genre Evolution in Modern English and Uzbek Dystopian Literature

**Autores:** Isanova Visola Oybekovna

**Data:** 2026-10-11 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

This article provides a comparative\-typological study of the artistic interpretation of the future concept and the evolutionary features of the dystopian genre in 21st\-century English and Uzbek literature\. Unlike classical 20th\-century anti\-utopias, modern dystopian prose reflects how global technological networking, artificial intelligence, ecological crises, and information manipulation have transformed the imagery of the future\. The research comparatively analyzes that while English dystopian literature predominantly emphasizes technological determinism and post\-humanism, modern Uzbek dystopia centers on the preservation of national identity, moral\-ethical values, and historical memory\. The research findings serve as a valuable theoretical resource for comparative literary studies and the analysis of contemporary literary processes\.
