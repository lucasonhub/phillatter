# Study on microgrid \- strength parameters of the soil\-geosynthetic interface based on direct shear tests

**Autores:** Thiago de Sá Sena; Ana Karine Santiago Bessa; Anderson Borghetti Soares; Marcos Fábio Porto de Aguiar

**Data:** 2026-10-17 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Soil reinforcements positively influence the mechanical behavior of soil by inserting external elements, such as geosynthetics, which improve strength and reduce deformability of the composite\. Over the years, new geosynthetics have been developed and upgraded\. In the last decade or so, microgrids have emerged as an appropriate choice for strengthening and separating soil layers\. This research aimed to evaluate the strength parameters of the soil‒microgrid interface using direct shear tests under different testing conditions and to compare the results with those for other geosynthetics \(geomembrane and geotextile\)\. The support base type \(rigid and soil\) for the geosynthetics was varied in the direct shear tests\. When a soil base was used beneath the microgrid, performance was more efficient than with a rigid wooden base due to the higher soil friction angle\. The results for all soil–geosynthetic interface strength parameters obtained using the rigid base indicated that the microgrid exhibited higher strength than the geomembrane and comparable strength to the geotextile, though slightly lower\.
