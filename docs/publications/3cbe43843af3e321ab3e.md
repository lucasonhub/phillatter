# Recent progress in physical properties and power electronic applications of ε\(κ\)\-Ga2O3

**Autores:** Yanfang Zhang; Jinggang Hao; Conggui Huang

**Data:** 2026-10-15 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Gallium oxide \(Ga2O3\) has emerged as a promising candidate for next\-generation power electronics due to its ultra\-widebandgap and superior critical breakdown field\. The emergent spontaneous polarization and ferroelectric characteristics of ε\(κ\)\-Ga₂O₃ are anticipated to enable the development of advanced power devices with both low power consumption and high efficiency\. In this review, we describe the development on the physical properties and power electronic device applications of ε\(κ\)\-Ga2O3, covering its crystal structure and polarization properties, optical characteristics, thermal transport behavior, and electrical properties\. Then, we highlight recent progress in ε\(κ\)\-Ga₂O₃\-based power electronic devices, with particular focus on Schottky barrier diodes, p\-n heterojunctions diodes, and metal\-oxide\-semiconductor field\-effect transistors\.
