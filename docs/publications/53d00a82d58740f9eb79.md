# Boundary\-aware Mixture\-of\-Experts with Calibrated Bayesian Uncertainty for Skin Cancer Detection and Multi\-class Classification

**Autores:** Preeti Kathuria; Deepa Nehra

**Data:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Dermoscopic analysis is held back by three coupled problems: detection and classification are trained separately, one monolithic backbone must serve lesions whose evidence ranges from keratin micro\-texture to longrange pigment topology, and its confidence is too poorly calibrated to drive a referral\.BMoE\-Net is a single network that localises a lesion, dispatches it to morphology\-specialised experts and returns a calibrated posterior with a referral flag\.Its novelty is the routing signal: prior vision mixtures\-of\-experts gate on the same appearance features their experts consume, which is close to circular, whereas BMoE\-Net computes a Contour Fingerprint Descriptor from the predicted lesion boundary and conditions expert selection on geometry\.It is shown information\-theoretically that this cannot increase the conditional entropy of the optimal routing variable, and the effect is isolated against permuted, randomised and dimension\-matched control gates: routing entropy falls from 1\.061 to 0\.694 nats, with interpretable unsupervised expert specialisations that no control reproduces\.Under lesion\-disjoint partitions BMoE\-Net reaches balanced accuracies of 89\.6%, 86\.9% and 85\.4% on HAM10000, ISIC 2019 and BCN20000 with macro\-AUCs of 0\.979, 0\.973 and 0\.966, beating fifteen baselines under paired DeLong testing with Holm\-Bonferroni correction \(adjusted p &lt; 0\.001\), and attains an AUROC of 0\.969 on ISIC 2024 SLICE\-3D under a patient\-level split\.Expected calibration error on HAM10000 is 0\.034 without rescaling, and at matched metadata availability boundary routing narrows the AUC gap between Fitzpatrick I\-II and V\-VI skin by 7\.2 points\.Code, splits, seeds, evaluation scripts and raw predictions are at https://github\.com/prtkathuria89\-png/BMOE\-NET\.
