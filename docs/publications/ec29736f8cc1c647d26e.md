# Beyond Digital Adab: A Conceptual Model of Ta’dib\-Oriented Character Pedagogy in Islamic Education

**Autores:** Abidul Haq; MUHAMMAD KHAQIM

**Data:** 2026-10-15 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Digital Islamic learning presents challenges involving unverified religious claims, artificial intelligence outputs, privacy concerns, and unequal access\. This study aims to reconstruct the philosophy of ta’dib as a conceptual foundation for character education in digitally mediated Islamic learning\. It employs a qualitative conceptual approach through purposive document analysis and an integrative synthesis of literature on educational philosophy and digital ethics\. The findings propose the Ta’dib\-Oriented Digital Character Pedagogy \(TDCP\) model, comprising five dimensions: epistemic integrity, dignified interaction, responsible digital agency, equitable participation, and reflective self\-discipline\. These dimensions are implemented through a cycle of inquiry, deliberation, action, and reflection to support formative assessment and protect learner privacy\. The model offers a pedagogical framework for developing ethical reasoning without equating observable behavior with piety\. Expert validation and empirical testing are needed to assess its applicability in educational settings\.
