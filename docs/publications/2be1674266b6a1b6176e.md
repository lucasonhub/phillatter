# Yonder, Yon: Non\-algorithmicity

**Autores:** 

**Data de descoberta:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** unknown

## Descrição do feed

Through physical unpredictability, conceptual deficiencies, including untheorizability reasons, and modelling predicaments, non\-algorithmicity is gestured toward\. Lifeforms are machines at least in simulations, and are oracles by their manifestations\. The continuing progress despite persisting epistemic deficiency is a historical proof of non\-algorithmicity\.
