# Operation state gated topology aware multimodal fusion for simulation based active distribution network risk assessment

**Autores:** Lei Feng; Baolin Liu; Chong Du; Yanru Duan; Xianmin Wang

**Data:** 2026-10-17 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Active distribution planning requires day\-ahead identification of buses exposed to voltage and loading stress\. Separating injection forecasting from feeder simulation leaves the contribution of multimodal fusion to node\-risk estimation unresolved\. We develop OSTMF\-Net, an operation\-state\-gated topology\-aware multimodal fusion network that combines temporal and graph experts through convex, residual and direct heads selected on a chronological validation holdout\. Public load and NASA POWER weather variables are mapped to the IEEE 123\-node OpenDSS feeder\. Quasi\-static time\-series simulations generate deterministic 24\-h absolute\-risk and positive\-increment targets under base, high\-photovoltaic, heavy\-load and combined\-stress conditions\. Evaluation measures agreement with OpenDSS\-derived risk surrogates within this reproducible simulation benchmark\. Among matched fusion methods across eight correlated scenario\-target cases, OSTMF\-Net obtains the lowest root mean square error \(RMSE\) in six and remains within 1% of the lowest value in seven\. Mean case\-wise RMSE reductions are 9\.77%, 17\.37% and 3\.72% relative to a validation\-selected expert, uniform averaging and linear stacking, respectively; gains over stacking concentrate in absolute\-risk cases\. A matched state\-adaptive control yields mean RMSE 0\.03460 versus 0\.03080 for OSTMF\-Net\. Forecast perturbations, sensor\-input corruption and label sensitivity assess robustness to input and target changes\. On a disjoint 2013 chronology on the same feeder, OSTMF\-Net records RMSE 0\.03067, high\-risk F1 0\.795 and Top\-10 agreement 1\.000\. The results connect complementary temporal and topology information with state\-dependent correction, supporting auditable day\-ahead bus\-risk screening on the evaluated OpenDSS benchmark\.
