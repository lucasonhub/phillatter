# The Digital Responsibility Index: Quantifying Perceived Multi\-Layered Responsibility in Cybersecurity

**Autores:** Mircea Ţălu

**Data:** 2026-10-13 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

In the evolving digital landscape, cybersecurity responsibility extends beyond technical safeguards to include individual behavior, organizational governance, and societal impact\. This study introduces the Digital Responsibility Index \(DRI\), a novel metric that quantifies multi\-layered accountability across four domains: technical, individual, organizational, and societal\. Data were collected through scenario\-based Likert\-scale surveys with 120 cybersecurity professionals, supplemented by semi\-structured interviews with senior leaders\. Results reveal that while technical and organizational responsibilities are consistently prioritized, individual and societal accountability remain under\-recognized\. Leadership engagement significantly enhances responsibility scores across all layers\. The proposed DRI framework provides a measurable and potentially actionable basis for assessing and improving responsible cybersecurity practices, while the present findings represent an initial empirical validation of the framework\.
