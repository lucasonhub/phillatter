# DIGITAL PEDAGOGY IN HIGHER EDUCATION: DEVELOPING 21ST\-CENTURY CRITICAL THINKING SKILLS

**Autores:** Abdusalomova Barchinoy Jo&\#x27;rabek qizi; Xudoyqulov Abdulla Eshquvvatovich

**Data:** 2026-10-18 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Digital pedagogy has become an essential component of higher education, offering new opportunities to develop students’ critical thinking, analytical reasoning, problem\-solving, and independent learning skills\. This article examines how digital learning environments and technology\-enhanced teaching strategies can support the development of critical thinking among university students\. Particular attention is given to blended learning, problem\-based learning, collaborative digital platforms, and artificial intelligence\. The discussion draws on existing research and identifies practical approaches for integrating digital tools into higher education\. The article argues that technology alone does not guarantee improved learning outcomes; effective digital pedagogy requires purposeful instructional design, active student participation, and continuous feedback\.
