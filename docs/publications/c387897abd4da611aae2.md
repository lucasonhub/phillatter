# A Knowledge\-Driven Multi\-Evaluation Framework for Art Pedagogy in the Digital Era

**Autores:** Wanqi Zheng

**Data:** 2026-10-09 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Traditional art teaching evaluation, relying on subjective judgment and uniform criteria, fails to support individualized learning\. Grounded in knowledge management principles, this study develops a knowledge\-driven, multi\-agent framework for art teaching evaluation that integrates multiple data sources, diagnostic, formative, and summative assessment phases, and a closed\-loop feedback mechanism with dynamic weight adjustment, converting behavioral data analytics into actionable pedagogical intelligence\. A quasi\-experimental comparison of 186 students across two art courses shows that the framework significantly enhances creativity and engagement relative to traditional score\-based evaluation, while fostering collaborative knowledge sharing among instructors\. Implementation nonetheless faces data\-integration and platform\-reliability challenges that increase instructors&\#x27; workload\. Future research will strengthen the platform&\#x27;s anomaly\-detection and data\-repair capabilities and further validate the framework&\#x27;s bias\-correction mechanisms and generalizability\.
