# Non\-Inductive Calibration and State Evaluation Model for Transformers in Low\-Voltage Distribution Networks

**Autores:** Liwen Chen; Shubin Dong; Peigang Shen

**Data:** 2026-10-12 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

The transformers in low\-voltage distribution areas are easily influenced by environmental aging and nonlinear load in long\-term operation, and the traditional on\-site off\-line calibration method has a high cost and low efficiency\. Aiming at these problems, a model of online non\-inductive calibration and health evaluation of transformer errors based on multi\-source data fusion is proposed\. In this study, the dataset of low\- and medium\-voltage energy distribution networks in Norway is used; meanwhile, the unstructured data in the format of Common Grid Model Exchange Specification is analyzed to construct a structured network topology model\. Using the high\-frequency active and reactive power load curves uploaded by the advanced metering infrastructure \(AMI\) system, combined with Kirchhoff&\#x27;s law, the mathematical model of power conservation and line loss correction in the substation is established; the error parameter identification equation is built by using the weighted least squares \(WLS\) method; the transformer ratio and phase error are iteratively solved without hardware intervention\. The model further integrates meteorological environmental factors and constructs multi\-dimensional state feature vectors to quantitatively evaluate the health of equipment\. The improved adaptive WLS algorithm reduces the mean absolute percentage error of transformer ratio error to 0\.063%, significantly better than the traditional method\. Under the interference of a total harmonic distortion rate as high as 20%, the convergence success rate of the algorithm is still 92\.5%, and the root mean square error of transformer ratio error is 0\.253%; this shows strong robustness to nonlinear load interference\. The coefficient of determination \(R²\) on the test set of the proposed health index state evaluation model integrating temperature, humidity and load rate data reaches 0\.915\. The state evaluation model with multi\-dimensional environmental characteristics has a trend capture accuracy of 96\.8% on the test set\. Thus, it effectively realizes accurate prediction of the equipment health state\.
