# TDD\-Net: A Metadata\-augmented Transformer Framework for Depression Detection from Primary Social Media Data

**Autores:** V\. Lavanya; Ashok Kumar T\. A\.

**Data:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Mental illnesses are on the rise all over the world and there is a need to detect depression through digital footprints in an automated and early manner\.The traditional Artificial Intelligence \(AI\) paradigms do not usually have context\-based and user behavioral information\.To overcome these shortcomings, this research paper suggested the Transformer\-Based Depression Detection Network \(TDD\-Net\) that combined textual information with user\-centric behavioral metadata built on a fully primary and consented social media dataset\.The data included self\-written posts, captions, comments, patterns of interactions and anonymized profile data\.Text cleaning, sentiment normalization and tokenization were done by using a systematic preprocessing pipeline\.A RoBERTa encoder was used to learn the semantic representation of text, and a metadata embedding network was used to learn longitudinal behavioral signals, including posting frequency, intensity of engagement, and diurnal activity patterns\.The linguistic and metadata vectors were fused with a fusion layer and a multi\-head attention classification head was applied to predict the risk of depression\.This transformer fusion, which is metadata\-enhanced, boosted personalization in depression detection\.An experimental assessment revealed an accuracy of 97\.18, and better precision, recall, and F1\-scores\.These findings show that the model has high predictive accuracy, and it is used in practice in mental health monitoring\.TDD\-Net is a big improvement over the conventional text\-only approach by making good use of extensive behavioral cues\.
