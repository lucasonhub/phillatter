# CHALLENGES OF PROTECTING THE RIGHT TO PRIVACY AMID THE DIGITALIZATION OF PUBLIC\-LAW RELATIONS AND THE INTRODUCTION OF ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE TECHNOLOGIES

**Autores:** Bakhriddinova Behruza Bakhreddinovna

**Data:** 2026-10-15 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

The development of public\-law institutions in virtually any democratic country is difficult to envisage without the widespread adoption of digital technologies and the growing integration of artificial intelligence \(AI\) into the social and political life of the state\. At the same time, AI technologies may pose risks and threats to the protection of the individual&\#x27;s right to privacy\. This article examines international standards in this field\.
