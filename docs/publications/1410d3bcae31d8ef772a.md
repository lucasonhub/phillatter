# Desarrollo de un agente conversacional con RAG para el apoyo en la toma de decisiones en la empresa Acopio, Beneficio y Torcido de Tabaco Granma

**Autores:** Marcos Antonio Lozada Pérez; Juan Antonio Plasencia Soler

**Data:** 2026-10-15 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

This research addresses the challenges of decision\-making in the area of ​​quality control for exported hand\-rolled tobacco at the company &quot;Acopio, Beneficio y Torcido de Tabaco Granma,&quot; where the growing volume of production data hinders efficient analysis\. The main objective of this paper is to develop a conversational agent based on Recall Augmented Generation \(RAG\) technology, integrating a linguistic rule generation module capable of leveraging local knowledge bases to provide reliable, evidence\-based responses\. The study focuses on generative artificial intelligence applied to quality management\. The methodological strategy combined theoretical methods such as analytical\-synthetic, inductive\-deductive, and document analysis, along with empirical methods such as interviews\. The system was designed using the Scrum agile approach, incorporating design patterns, modular architecture, and coding standards\. The results obtained demonstrate that the conversational agent improves efficiency and objectivity in decision\-making by transforming historical data into structured and understandable information through linguistic summarization and rules\. The tests conducted, along with expert validation, confirmed the solution&\#x27;s effectiveness\.
