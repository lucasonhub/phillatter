# Thomas, Emily: C15341Arthur Eddington and Time’s Arrow into the Progressive Future 1919–1928

## Descrição do feed

In Real Time: A Reinvention in British Metaphysics 1883\-1928\. Oxford University Press\. 2026Arthur Eddington is best known as an early advocate of Einstein’s relativity theories\. Yet he also produced a complex metaphysic of time, developed across works from 1919 onward and blooming in his 1927 Gifford lectures, published in 1928 as The Nature of the Physical World\. At first glance, this metaphysic appears to be in the style of Bertrand Russell\. Eddington defends \(what he takes to be\) the world described by physics: a ‘block’ containing a real past, present, and future\. Yet Eddington swiftly complicates that picture, via his directly apprehended, progress\-indicating ‘arrow’ of time\. This chapter explores his account of time’s directionality and its origins, arguing it lies not in the work of Henri Bergson\. Instead, it’s sourced in the Victorian empiricists W\. K\. Clifford and Karl Pearson, and mystic theism\.
