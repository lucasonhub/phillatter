# Pika Optimization Algorithm: A Novel Nature\-inspired Metaheuristic for Solving Complex Real\-world Constrained Optimization Problems

**Autores:** Zeinab Montazeri; Mohammad Dehghani; Raed Abu Zitar; Omar Almomani; Kei Eguchi

**Data:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

With the increasing complexity of real\-world constrained optimization problems, the need for metaheuristic optimization algorithms with the ability to strike a balance between exploration and exploitation has increased\.In this study, the Pika Optimization Algorithm \(PiOA\) is introduced as a new parameter\-free algorithm whose structure is designed based on two complementary Pika behaviors, including environmental vigilance and predator avoidance and food foraging and food\-patch browsing\.In the exploration mechanism, environmental vigilance and predator avoidance are modeled to control the search state; in such a way that the probability of predator presence is determined based on changes in population diversity and each population member independently decides between escaping to a safe region and continuing the environmental search\.Also, the selection of a safe region using better solutions and distance criteria strengthens the movement towards different regions of the search space\.In the exploitation mechanism, the food foraging and food\-patch browsing behaviors are modeled to focus on promising regions and precisely modify the existing positions\.The performance of PiOA is evaluated using 22 CEC 2011 test suite problems and under the same conditions including 20 independent runs and 1000 iterations and compared with 9 well\-known algorithms\.The results show that PiOA ranks first in 21 out of 22 problems and records a success rate of 95\.45%\.Also, the mean, std and boxplot results show that PiOA has good stability in addition to high solution quality\.These findings indicate that the behavioral structure of PiOA can provide an effective framework for global and local search in complex problems\.
