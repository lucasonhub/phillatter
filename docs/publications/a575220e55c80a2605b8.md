# Information\-Theoretic Foundations of Online Temperature Sampling

**Autores:** Jin Sima; Nikolaos Papagiannis; Ananth Grama; Wojciech Szpankowski

**Data:** 2026-10-10 · **Tipo:** preprint

## Resumo

Repetitive, degenerate generation is a persistent failure of LLM decoding, and the standard remedies \(temperature, nucleus, and top\-$k$ sampling\) are static heuristics\. By Kac&\#x27;s lemma and the AEP, a stationary source takes time exponential in its entropy to repeat a block\. We therefore maximize the entropy of the sampling distributions under one cross\-entropy budget that keeps the output plausible under the model\. Offline, the solution is temperature sampling, with the inverse temperature as the budget&\#x27;s Lagrange multiplier\. \\emph\{Our contribution is the online problem\}: the conditionals $p\_1, p\_2, \\dots$ are revealed one at a time, and the sampling distribution $q\_t$ must be chosen causally from $p\_1, \\dots, p\_t$ while the budget couples the entire horizon\. The optimal temperature is thus a global shadow price that must be estimated from a prefix\. We propose Entropic Online Sampling \(EOS\), which re\-solves for the temperature on each prefix\. We prove it loses $O\(R\\log n\)$ entropy against the offline optimum while overshooting the budget by $O\(R\\log n\)$, with $R$ an instance\-dependent drift parameter\. A budget\-tightened variant is exactly feasible at the same rate\. Conversely, every exactly feasible online algorithm must lose $Ω\(R\)$, so EOS is optimal up to a $\\log n$ factor\. When the conditionals are drawn from a finite mixture, $R=\\tilde\{O\}\(\\sqrt\{n\}\)$\. Experiments confirm that EOS tracks the offline temperature and beats fixed\-temperature sampling in entropy\.
