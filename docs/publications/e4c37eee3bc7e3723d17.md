# The COL4A3 c\.3418\+1G&gt;T variant causes exon 39 skipping in minigene assays: functional and exploratory structural analyses

**Autores:** Xuan Ban; Yue Wang; Yunshan Guo; Juan Wang; He Sun; Ping He; 王明秀; Xiaofang Tang; Qiuling Fan

**Data:** 2026-10-12 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Background Canonical splice\-site variants are an important cause of Alport syndrome, but the transcript consequences of many heterozygous COL4A3 variants remain experimentally unverified\. We characterized COL4A3 c\.3418 \+ 1G&gt;T in a 38\-year\-old woman with persistent isolated microscopic hematuria\. Notably, the proband’s mother also had microscopic hematuria but did not carry c\.3418 \+ 1G&gt;T, precluding supportive co\-segregation\. Methods Whole\-exome sequencing identified the variant, which was subsequently confirmed by Sanger sequencing\. Splicing effects were assessed using SpliceAI, Pangolin, and MaxEntScan\. Minigene assays were performed in HEK293T, HK\-2, and human glomerular endothelial cells\. Protein consequences were examined using AlphaFold\-derived models, PyMOL visualization, and exploratory 5\-ns molecular dynamics simulations of local α3α4α5\(IV\) trimer models, with additional independently initialized 20\-ns replicate simulations performed as a supplementary reproducibility analysis\. Results Computational tools consistently predicted loss of the canonical donor splice site\. Minigene assays demonstrated complete exon 39 skipping in all 3 cell lines, resulting in an 81\-bp in\-frame deletion of 27 amino acids within the α3\(IV\) collagenous domain\. Sequence examination showed that the resulting junction preserved the Gly\-X\-Y register, although its protein\-level structural consequences remain undetermined\. Exploratory molecular dynamics analysis showed descriptive differences between wild\-type and mutant models\. The additional 20\-ns simulations showed broadly similar RMSD ranges across independent replicates, although RMSD values varied across prespecified time blocks, indicating limited short\-timescale sampling consistency\. These findings were therefore considered hypothesis\-generating rather than evidence of full convergence, altered stability, or impaired interchain interactions\. The patient exhibited persistent isolated microscopic hematuria without proteinuria, renal dysfunction, or extrarenal manifestations, compatible with a mild autosomal dominant Alport syndrome phenotype\. Conclusion COL4A3 c\.3418 \+ 1G&gt;T causes complete exon 39 skipping in the tested minigene systems and a 27\-amino\-acid in\-frame deletion\. Under a conservative author\-level application of the ACMG/AMP framework, PVS1\_Moderate and PM2\_Supporting yield a primary classification of Variant of Uncertain Significance \(VUS\); PP4 was not applied in the primary classification because isolated microscopic hematuria has limited specificity and the maternal phenotype was discordant\. A Likely Pathogenic classification is retained only as a conditional author\-level alternative if PVS1 \(RNA\) is calibrated to Strong and PP4 is applied\.
