# Sergent, Brandon M\.: You Cannot Understand Experiential Empiricism and Disagree With It: A Challenge to Find the Missing Position

**Autores:** 

**Data de descoberta:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** unknown

## Descrição do feed

I claim that full understanding of Experiential Empiricism \(EE\) entails agreement with it\. This is not a rule for classifying dissenters after the fact\. It is a claim about the peculiar subject of EE: experience as the self\-present totality within which any attempt to question, explain, deny, or escape it must occur\. Ordinary empirical propositions can be understood and rejected; a sound demonstration cannot be seen as sound while its conclusion is coherently denied\. EE belongs to the latter class if its account of totality succeeds\. I distinguish recognizing the words and usable formalism of EE from seeing what its foundational claim identifies, test a false totality argument that imitates self\-proof, and identify what a genuine case of understanding plus disagreement would have to show\. The challenge is open: produce that case, and this paper&\#x27;s central claim fails\. Until then, I cannot construct the position it would occupy\. \(direct link \)
