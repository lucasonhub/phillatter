# Personality Traits as Predictors of Psychological Well\-Being Among Health Workers in Gboko Local Government Area, Benue State, Nigeria

**Autores:** Aondongu Iulun

**Data:** 2026-10-13 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

This study investigated personality traits as predictors of psychological well\-being among health workers in Gboko Local Government Area of Benue state, Nigeria\. The study adopted a cross\-sectional survey design where 184 participants, 34 males \(18\.5%\) and 150 females \(81\.5%\) were selected for the study through simple random sampling technique\. The Ryff’s Psychological Well\-being scale and the Big Five Personality Inventory were used for data collection\. Two research questions were raised to guide the study\. Two hypotheses were formulated and tested\. Data analysis involved the use of the Pearson Product Moment Correlation and Multiple Linear Regression\. Results revealed that, there was a significant relationship between personality traits, and psychological well\-being among health workers in Gboko Local Government Area\. Result also indicated that, there was a significant influence of personality traits on psychological well\-being among health workers in Gboko Local Government Area\. The study concluded that, personality traits are strong determinants of psychological well\-being among health workers in Gboko Local Government Area\. It was therefore recommended that; periodic assessment of personality traits of health workers should be a priority of psychologists and employers in the health institutions \(hospitals and clinics\) with a view to improving the psychological well\-being of health workers\. Also, the government and private health sector should provide adequate medical equipments, conducive working environment, and welfare packages for health workers\. They should also be employment of health workers on a quarterly basis of the year to boost manpower in the hospitals and clinics\.
