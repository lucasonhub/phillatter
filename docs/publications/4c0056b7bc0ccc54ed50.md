# Fabrication of Ag\-La\-based/Arabic gum nanocomposites via Nigella sativa : Structural, biological activity, and silver release assessment

**Autores:** Mashael M\. Alharbi; Roba M\. S\. Attar

**Data:** 2026-10-17 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Ag\-La hybrid and Arabic gum\-stabilized Ag\-La nanocomposites were prepared by a green chemistry method, where an aqueous solution of Nigella sativa seeds was used as a reducing and stabilizing agent\. During the synthesis process, silver ions were reduced to Ag 0 , while a fraction precipitated as AgCl, coexisting with La\(OH\) 3 to form a hybrid nanocomposite material\. Arabic gum further enhanced nanoparticle dispersion and stabilization\. Arabic gum acted as a biopolymeric stabilizer, enhancing nanoparticle dispersion and colloidal stability through surface functionalization\. The FTIR, ultraviolet\-visible \(UV\-Vis\), transmittance electron microscopy \(TEM\), scanning electron microscopy \(SEM\) with energy dispersive X\-ray analysis \(SEM\-EDS\), X\-ray diffraction \(XRD\), and X\-ray photoelectron spectroscopy \(XPS\) analysis of the nanocomposites suggested the presence of quasi\-spherical nanoparticles \(9\-45 nm\) with polycrystalline domains of La\(OH\) 3 , AgCl, and metallic Ag\. Antioxidant analysis of Arabic gum\-functionalized nanocomposites indicated enhanced activity, such as radical scavenging, ferric reducing power, and total antioxidant activity at IC 50 = 42 µg/mL, 96\.3 µg AAE/mL, and 90\.1 µg AAE/mL, respectively\. The controlled release of Ag \+ ions \(6\.87–13\.55%\) and the high antibacterial activity, with inhibition zones between 13 and 18 mm for Gram\-positive and Gram\-negative bacteria, together with a minimum inhibitory concentration \(MIC\) of 0\.25 mg/mL, suggest strong antibacterial performance at relatively low concentrations\. Molecular docking indicated high binding affinity of oxidized quercetin and kaempferol toward DNA gyrase B, suggesting a possible interaction mechanism; however, experimental validation is required to confirm this activity\. In conclusion, the results have suggest that the green synthesis method is an effective approach for the synthesis of Ag/La\(OH\) 3 /AgCl/Arabic gum nanocomposites with promising physicochemical and biological properties\. However, further research is needed to investigate the long\-term stability and mechanisms of the nanocomposites for their applicability\.
