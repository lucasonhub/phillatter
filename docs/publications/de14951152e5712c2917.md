# Community Engagement for Ethical Digital Justice: Practical Standards for Online Hearings and Electronic Evidence

**Autores:** Volodymyr V\. Shekhovtsov; Юрій Богданович Гофман; Юлія Олександрівна Фідря; I\. O\. Bylytsia; Інна Володимирівна Ковальчук

**Data:** 2026-10-13 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

The rapid digitalization of judicial systems has transformed legal practice by expanding the use of online hearings, electronic evidence, and digital communication, while creating new ethical challenges for judges and lawyers\. Existing ethical frameworks in Ukraine provide only general guidance and do not adequately address the complexities of digital justice\. This study aims to identify the principal ethical issues arising from digital litigation and to develop a unified framework of digital ethics for judges and lawyers based on international best practices and the Ukrainian legal context\. The research employs a qualitative methodology combining doctrinal legal analysis, comparative legal analysis, and general scientific methods, including analysis, synthesis, induction, deduction, analogy, and generalization\. National legislation, judicial ethics codes, international standards, and relevant case law were examined to assess current regulatory gaps and identify effective approaches to digital judicial ethics\. The findings reveal significant shortcomings in the regulation of online hearings, electronic evidence, lawyer–client confidentiality, digital competence, transparency, and the use of artificial intelligence in judicial proceedings\. The study proposes a unified minimum standard of digital ethics that integrates procedural safeguards, ethical principles, and comparative international practices to enhance consistency, fairness, and public confidence in digital justice\. The novelty of the research lies in developing an interdisciplinary framework that systematically links ethical challenges with practical regulatory solutions through a problem–solution–impact model, offering a comprehensive basis for the modernization of judicial and legal ethics in Ukraine\. The proposed framework supports the development of a transparent, accountable, and technologically resilient justice system\. The framework also identifies the institutions responsible for implementation and links digital\-ethics reform to access to justice, public legal education, and inclusive participation in remote proceedings\.
