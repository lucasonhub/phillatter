# Springbok Optimization Algorithm: A Nature\-inspired Metaheuristic for Global Optimization and Engineering Design Problems

**Autores:** Zeinab Montazeri; Mohammad Dehghani; Raed Abu Zitar; Omar Almomani; Kei Eguchi

**Data:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Metaheuristic algorithms are used in optimization problems due to their ability to solve very complex problems; however, striking the right balance between exploration and exploitation remains a significant challenge\.In this study, the Springbok Optimization Algorithm \(SOA\) is introduced as a swarm\-based method that is inspired by the Springbok spatial structure switching between aggregation and dispersion and adjusting the position relative to the nearby members of the group\.In SOA, the dispersion behavior is modeled to increase the diversity and coverage of the search space in the exploration phase, while the use of local promising members to refine suitable solutions constitutes the exploitation phase\.The performance of SOA is evaluated using 10 fixed\-dimensional multimodal test functions, including F1\-F10, and four engineering design problems including tension/compression spring design, welded beam design problem, speed reducer design problem, and pressure vessel design problem\.The performance of SOA is compared with 9 metaheuristic algorithms, and the results are analyzed with the mean, best, worst, std, median, and rank indices\.The results show that SOA achieves the first\-best optimizer rank in all 10 benchmark functions and provides 100% successful and competitive performance\.Also, SOA has provided 100% successful performance in all four engineering design problems by providing more optimal solutions than the compared algorithms\.The quantitative and qualitative analysis of the results, along with boxplots and convergence curves, shows the appropriate and stable quality and stability of the solutions obtained by SOA\.The findings of this study indicate that SOA, with its high ability to balance exploration and exploitation, is an effective and powerful optimizer for a wide range of optimization problems in science and real\-world applications\.
