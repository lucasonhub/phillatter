# IN THE VIEWS OF THE JADIDS, THE ROLE OF LABOR IN HUMAN LIFE AND THE DEVELOPMENT OF SOCIETY

**Autores:** Muxtarov Utkirbek Mutalibjonovich

**Data:** 2026-10-12 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

This scientific article highlights the role of labor in human life and societal development in the views of the Jadids\. It analyzes the views of Jadid enlighteners on hard work, learning, mastering a profession, honesty, and moral perfection\. It is also revealed that in the works of Jadid representatives such as Mahmudkhodja Behbudi, Abdulla Avloni, and Abdurauf Fitrat, labor is interpreted as an important factor that spiritually elevates a person and develops their abilities and capabilities\.
