# Beyond Mean Absolute Error: Ancestry\-Stratified Calibration and Explainability for Warfarin Dosing Models

**Autores:** Hassan Barmandah; Omar A\. Bawazir; Siraj Aldeen Marghalani; Muath Shaat; Abdullah Alkattan; Mariam M\. AlEissa

**Data:** 2026-10-15 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Background: Warfarin’s narrow therapeutic index requires precise dose individualization, yet published machine\-learning studies report accuracy that is inversely correlated with evaluation rigor: the field’s best\-reported mean absolute error \(MAE\) comes from an unreliably small test set\. Methods: We present a reproducible, nine\-phase ML pipeline on the public International Warfarin Pharmacogenetics Consortium \(IWPC\) dataset \(n=6037 and n=1780\) with fixed patient\-level splits, strict leakage controls, and a nested clinical/genetic ablation across nine architectures\. Results: AutoGluon with combined features achieves MAE =8\.55 mg/week, R2=0\.475, PW20 \(percentage of predictions within 20% of the true dose\) =0\.466 on the larger cohort \(MAE =7\.96, R2=0\.480, PW20 =0\.497 on the smaller\), exceeding prior rigorous benchmarks\. A leave\-one\-ancestry\-group\-out evaluation, cross\-checked across two model families, reproduces only part of an earlier analysis’s known error concentration in Black/African\-American patients: the Black label shows no penalty under either model, while Han Chinese and Caucasian show model\-dependent penalties\. A deep ensemble is badly miscalibrated \(24\.9%/21\.6% coverage of a nominal 90% interval\), while MAPIE \(Model Agnostic Prediction Interval Estimator\) conformal prediction on the same model achieves near\-nominal coverage \(89\.8%/93\.3%\); per\-ancestry coverage shows every Black/African\-American label under\-covered, a fairness gap the aggregate number hides\. On IWPC\-1780, SHAP \(SHapley Additive exPlanations\) attributes 64% of feature importance to genetics, confirming VKORC1 as the strongest predictor\. Conclusions: To our knowledge, no previously published pipeline evaluated on the IWPC dataset jointly reports accuracy, ancestry\-stratified fairness, calibration, and explainability on the same fixed splits\.
