## Descrição do feed

\_Zenodo\_ 1\. 2026This paper investigates real operational configurations beyond the search for a universally minimal form of life\. It examines how material processes emerge, persist, branch, recombine, terminate, and alter subsequent possibilities across heterogeneous environments, temporal scales, and experimental arrangements\. The analysis distinguishes the inheritance of material effects from the continuation of the mechanisms that produced them, the preservation of scientific records from the persistence of scientific operations, and successful scientific intervention from the preservation of its original conditions of observation\. Drawing on minimal\-cell research, protocell chemistry, Xenobots, Anthrobots, and extraterrestrial investigation, the paper develops a realist account of observation, generation, feedback, and reconfiguration without presupposing a universal life–nonlife boundary\. It further examines how scientific operations may transform their own conditions of access, intervention, and continuation\. The argument connects these investigations to Xenoscience, the Ziran System, and a locally typed minimal notation for scientific operational shapes\. It preserves the distinction between real processes and their experimental registration while identifying the conditions under which operational dependencies, scientific access, and subsequent trajectories change\. The paper presents these analyses as a concrete development of an existing operational\-realist research programme rather than claiming an unprecedented universal theory of life\. \(direct link \)
