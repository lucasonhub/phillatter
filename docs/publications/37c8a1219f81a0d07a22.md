# Spintronic and thermoelectric functionalities in transition\-metal\-based K₂MCl₆ double perovskites \(M = Mn, Mo, Ta, Tc\)

**Autores:** Mohamed El Amine El Goutni; Sahar Abdalla; Mohammed Batouche; Mohamed Houcine Dhaou; Abdulrahman Alsawi; Hela Ferjani; Sami M\. Alharbi

**Data:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

The vacancy\-ordered double perovskites K₂MCl₆ \(M = Mn, Mo, Ta, Tc\) were systematically investigated using first\-principles calculations to explore their structural, electronic, magnetic, and thermoelectric properties\. Calculations based on the FP\-LAPW \+ lo method within the WIEN2k framework, supported by phonon dispersion and AIMD analyses, confirm the thermodynamic and dynamical stability of all compounds in the cubic Fm\-3 m phase\. The transition\-metal substitution strongly influences lattice parameters, elastic behavior, and electronic characteristics\. Magnetic calculations reveal stable ferromagnetic ground states for all systems\. K₂MnCl₆, K₂TaCl₆, and K₂TcCl₆ exhibit half\-metallic behavior with complete spin polarization, whereas K₂MoCl₆ behaves as a spin\-polarized semiconductor\. Strong exchange interactions and spin–orbit coupling effects significantly govern the electronic structure\. Thermoelectric analysis demonstrates remarkable performance, including Seebeck coefficients up to − 1150 µV/K and electronic figures of merit approaching unity over 300–1000 K, highlighting K₂MCl₆ compounds as promising materials for spintronic and thermoelectric energy applications\.
