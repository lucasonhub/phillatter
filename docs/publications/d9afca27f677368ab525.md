# Current status and future outlook of artificial intelligence and automation in intravenous infusion systems

**Autores:** Islam M\. Ibrahim; Rahma A\. Elkomy; Merna A\. Mohamed; Fatemah A\. Farok; Roaa E\. Emam; Salma A\. kamel; Reham M\. Asem

**Data:** 2026-10-14 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Abstract The combination of the Internet of Things \(IoT\), artificial intelligence \(AI\), and wearable biosensors is transforming healthcare by allowing continuous, real\-time monitoring and more intelligent clinical decision\-making\. This review explores AI\-driven monitoring, smart intravenous \(IV\) infusion automation, and wearable biosensors, and proposes Intelligent IV Automation and Smart Patient Synchronization \(INFUSYNC\), a conceptual framework synchronizing monitoring, automated fluid delivery, and predictive analytics\. The literature shows meaningful progress: wearables increasingly support non\-contact monitoring with near clinical\-grade accuracy for select parameters, while infusion pumps with dose\-error\-reduction and AI\-assisted decision support are reported to improve delivery precision and safety\. Predictive models further support early detection of physiological decline\. Progress remains uneven, however: most AI\-enabled infusion systems are prototype\-stage, closed\-loop control remains rare, and interoperability, data security, and privacy remain underdeveloped\. Future systems will need compatible, security\-by\-design, clinically validated, feedback\-controlled architectures\. Overall, this review outlines a clear but early\-stage trajectory toward integrated smart\-health environments for IV therapy\.
