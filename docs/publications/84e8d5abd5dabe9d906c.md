# Development And Optimization Of Hesperidin Decorated Multiwalled Carbon Nanotubes Using An ANN Approach

**Autores:** 

**Data:** 2026-10-09 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Multiwalled carbon nanotubes serve as advanced nanocarriers that protect and precisely deliver these active plant compounds\.Further loading phytochemical actives leads to enhanced bioavailability, controlled and targeted release, stability and protection and synergizing therapeutic effects\.Hesperidin \(HPD\) is a bioactive flavonoid derived from citrus fruits that exhibits a wide spectrum of biological and pharmacological properties, such as antioxidant, anti\-inflammatory and anti\-apoptotic properties\.The present investigation aimed to load MWCNTs with HPD, by applying and ANN approach\.Initially the MWCNTs were purified by acid refluxing method, involving two steps, acylation and amination, followed by HPD loading\.Independent parameters investigated were concentration of MWCNTs, stirring time and centrifugation speed\.Drug loaded MWCNTs were investigated for Fe\-SEM, XRD, FTIR, DSC, particle size, zeta potential and in\-vitro drug release\.Fe\-SEM results proved that the MWCNTs were successfully loaded with drug\.The chemical groups attached to the non\-covalent functionalized MWCNTs were identified in FT\-IR study\.Crystallinity nature and thermal stability was characterized using DSC\.Batch B7 having concentration of MWCNTs 30 mg, stirring time 30 hrs and centrifugation rpm 16000 was selected as optimized batch due to its higher entrapment efficiency 82\.01 %, in\-vitro drug release 90\.13 % in 24 hrs and percent yield 70 %\.Particle size of optimized batch was 118\.5 nm and zeta potential was 54\.7 mV\.The methodology adopted proved to be highly efficient in development and optimization of MWCNTs loaded with model drug HPD\.
