**Autores:** 

**Data de descoberta:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** unknown

## Descrição do feed

This Reader&\#x27;s Guide is a free companion to The Building Blocks of Thought: A Rationalist Account of the Origins of Concepts \(Laurence and Margolis, Oxford University Press, 2024\)\. It outlines different paths through the book to suit readers with different interests\. It also provides suggestions for reading schedules for graduate and undergraduate courses, a glossary of key terminology, and abstracts for every chapter\. The book itself is open access and free to download\.
