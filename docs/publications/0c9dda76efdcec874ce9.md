# Эффективность применения технологий искусственного интеллекта в совершенствовании физической подготовки студентов

**Autores:** Ернур Курмангалиев; Жасканат Сесерхан; Ернур Курмангалиев; Ернур Курмангалиев

**Data:** 2026-10-10 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

The digital transformation of the modern education system is creating new opportunities in the field of physical education and sports\. In particular, the application of artificial intelligence technologies expands the possibilities for monitoring students’ physical fitness, individualizing the training process, analyzing motor activity, and providing timely feedback\. The purpose of this article is to theoretically analyze the main areas and pedagogical potential of using artificial intelligence technologies to improve the physical fitness of university students\. The study employed methods of analysis, comparison, systematization, and generalization of scientific literature\. The analysis of scientific studies indicates that artificial intelligence\-based tools can be used to assess motor activity, develop individualized training programs, monitor physical activity, and provide feedback during the educational and training process\. In addition, the article addresses issues related to maintaining the professional role of the instructor, protecting personal data, ensuring the reliability of algorithmic assessment, and providing access to digital tools when integrating artificial intelligence into physical education classes\. Artificial intelligence is considered not as a replacement for traditional methods of physical education, but as an additional tool that can help take students’ individual characteristics into account and support the instructor’s pedagogical activities\.
