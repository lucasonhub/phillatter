# Hybrid Blockchain and Zero\-Knowledge Proof Model for Privacy\-First SSI Verification

**Autores:** Waleed A\. Alrodhan

**Data:** 2026-10-13 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Self\-sovereign\-identity systems grant users control over their digital identities, thereby eliminating the need for centralized authorities\. Conventional blockchain\-based identity management systems can expose identity\-related metadata while imposing significant storage and computational overhead, and this raises issues with maintaining privacy and scalability\. A hybrid architecture of “blockchain \+ zero\-knowledge proof” was proposed by this study for privacy\-first self\-sovereign\-identity verification, where the layers of public and private blockchains were integrated with zero\-knowledge proofs to assure a secure, scalable, and privacy\-preserving identity verification while protecting sensitive information from exposure\. The proposed system had less data exposure, 0\.2% against 10% in traditional systems\. It also had a higher verification speed that could reach a one identity verification in 0\.0051 s at low loads against 0\.0068 s in traditional settings\. Hence, the proposed system might double the performance, 98 transactions processed per second against 49 transactions processed per second, with improved efficiency hovering between 20% and 40%\.
