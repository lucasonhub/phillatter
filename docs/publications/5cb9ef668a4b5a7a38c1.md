# A Case Study of Business–Engineering Integrated Dual Carbon Postgraduate Training at Chongqing Technology and Business University

**Autores:** yongli chen; Keyu Chen; Yixuan Zhou

**Data:** 2026-10-14 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

To achieve China’s “dual carbon” goals, interdisciplinary talent is urgently needed\. Taking Chongqing Technology and Business University as a case, this paper examines a “business–engineering integration” model for dual\-carbon postgraduate training\. Launched in 2024, the program offers carbon economy, carbon finance, carbon management, carbon technology, and digital intelligence directions\. It builds a “policy–technology–market” curriculum with compulsory, elective, and practical modules, supported by interdisciplinary mentors, industry mentors, and joint training bases\. The paper also reviews relevant literature and proposes recommendations on differentiated positioning, double\-qualified faculty teams, and industry\-education integration to improve local university talent training and regional service capacity\.
