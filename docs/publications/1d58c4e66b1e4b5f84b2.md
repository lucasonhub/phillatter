# استكشاف استخدام أدوات الذكاء الاصطناعي في تعلم اللغة الإنجليزية والأدب واللغة الإنجليزية لأغراض محددة

**Autores:** Arwa Harith Hasan

**Data:** 2026-10-15 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

This study aims to reveal the difference in the use of artificial intelligence \(AI\) tools between students studying English in English departments and students in non\-English departments\. The researcher adopted a qualitative approach\. The study sample consisted of 5 students from the Department of English Language and Literature, 5 students from the Department of Translation, 5 students from the Department of Arabic Language, 5 students from the Department of History, and 5 students from the Department of Anthropology and Sociology at the College of Arts, Al\-Mustansiriya University, during the second semester of the 2025–2026 academic year
