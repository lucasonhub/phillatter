# Intelligent Inverse Design of Meta‐Absorbers Based on Reversible Deep Learning

**Autores:** Mingyong Zhuang; Yiming Yan; Yuan Gao; Wei Chen; Jiaqing Shen; Fajun Li; Zhaogang Dong; Jianyang Zhou; Jinfeng Zhu

**Data:** 2026-10-14 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

ABSTRACT Optical meta‐absorbers are artificially engineered materials capable of trapping light in a highly controllable manner, rendering them attractive for a wide range of applications\. Artificial intelligence provides powerful tools for the efficient design of such complex structures\. Among current methods, tandem neural networks \(TNNs\) have emerged as a mainstream solution for forward‐inverse modeling\. However, TNNs require two separate networks, leading to high computational costs and limited accuracy—particularly in inverse design\. In this work, we propose a novel and highly precise design paradigm based on reversible deep learning \(RDL\), which employs a single, bidirectionally trainable neural network\. The RDL framework performs simultaneous training of the inverse and forward models within a unified architecture—addressing structural generation and spectral prediction, and significantly reducing computational costs\. As a proof of concept, we demonstrate the design of near‐infrared meta‐absorbers using RDL, achieving an order‐of‐magnitude improvement in inverse prediction accuracy compared to TNNs, while using only 66\.35% of the training parameters and requiring minimal memory and storage\. This study introduces a flexible and efficient platform for the rapid and accurate design of meta‐absorbers tailored to specific absorption spectra, facilitating the intelligent development of advanced optical metamaterials and metadevices\.
