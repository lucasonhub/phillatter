# Robust collapse prediction for corroded subsea pipelines under multiaxial loading: A physics\-informed Bayesian framework constrained by symbolic regression

**Autores:** Chuan Wang; Peng Zhang; Yunfei Huang; Siming Liu; Shangru Wu; Tian Xu; Wei Liu

**Data:** 2026-10-10 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Subsea pipeline collapse under multiaxial loads and corrosion severely threatens offshore structural integrity\. This study introduces an integrated FE\-SR\-PINN\-BNN framework, combining detailed 3D finite element \(FE\) numerical modeling in ABAQUS with a physics\-informed Bayesian neural network \(PINN\-BNN\) constrained by symbolic regression \(SR\)\. Explicit algebraic expressions of underlying mechanical mechanisms derived via SR are embedded into the PINN\-BNN loss function as structural physics constraints\. Numerical results show that the proposed model achieves a high coefficient of determination \(R 2 = 0\.9940\) with prediction residuals within ±1\.5 MPa, significantly outperforming purely data\-driven machine learning models and empirical formula\-constrained physics\-informed baselines\. In robustness evaluations with 5%\-20% added noise, its R 2 performance degrades by only 33\.49%, substantially lower than benchmark model drops\. Under an extreme small\-sample evaluation using only 10% training data, its R 2 reaches 0\.8464, outperforming four purely data\-driven baselines by 97\.99%, 42\.18%, 39\.44%, and 8\.35%, respectively\. Beyond precise collapse pressure predictions, the framework incorporates uncertainty quantification based on 95% prediction intervals, achieving a mean prediction interval width of 1\.88 MPa and a coverage probability of 0\.952, exceptionally close to the theoretical 0\.95 target\. This study provides reliable support for structural integrity and safety assessment of subsea pipelines in complex service environments\.
