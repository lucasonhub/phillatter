# Optimizing metro and BRT transfer design in Isfahan using passenger generalized cost and ant colony optimization

**Autores:** Navid Nakhaie; Ali Naderan; Shahriar Afandizadeh Zargari; Hooman Rahimi

**Data:** 2026-10-13 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Abstract This study presents a revised framework for screening and optimizing local Metro and BRT transfer connections in Isfahan using a state\-separated passenger network, fixed\-OD evaluation, explicit transfer screening, and ant\-colony\-based search\. The formulation prevents implicit intermodal switching and holds the OD matrix constant between baseline and intervention networks, thereby separating modeled network\-design effects from demand regeneration\. Because the available empirical demand information does not provide a complete observed spatial OD matrix, the gravity deterrence coefficient is treated as a sensitivity parameter rather than as an empirically calibrated behavioral coefficient\. Coordinate and network screening retained 29 feasible transfer candidates\. Candidate 22, a 249\.2 m pedestrian connection, produced the largest modeled improvement in the central case\. System passenger generalized cost decreased from 6,265,519\.636 to 6,258,241\.797 passenger\-min, a reduction of 7,277\.839 passenger\-min \(0\.116157%\)\. Exhaustive evaluation of all 406 candidate pairs and targeted triple screening found no additional benefit beyond the best single link\. I\-ACO, standard ACO, and greedy search identified the same one\-link solution, although the fast benchmark does not establish statistical superiority of any stochastic algorithm\. Given the small system\-wide effect and limitations in observed OD validation, route\-choice behavior, and service\-parameter calibration, the result is interpreted as a localized model\-based screening outcome that requires empirical and site\-level validation before implementation\.
