# Factors Influencing Consumer Adoption And Willingness\-To\-Pay for Assistive Technologies: A Case Study of Voice\-Controlled Devices for the Elderly in Vietnam

**Autores:** Nguyen Ha Bao; Quan Nguyen

**Data:** 2026-10-15 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

The Viet Nam is experiencing rapid population ageing, creating an increasing need for technologies that support older adults’ independence and ageing in place\. This study examines the factors influencing older Vietnamese consumers’ intention to adopt voice\-controlled devices \(VCDs\) and investigates whether adoption intention contributes to willingness to pay \(WTP\)\. Drawing on the Unified Theory of Acceptance and Use of Technology 2 \(UTAUT2\) and gerontechnology research, the study develops a model incorporating performance expectancy, effort expectancy, social influence, facilitating conditions, trust, privacy concern, and technology anxiety as determinants of adoption intention\. The model further proposes adoption intention as a mediator between these antecedents and WTP\. Data were collected through a cross\-sectional survey of 225 Vietnamese adults aged 60 years and above in Hanoi and Ho Chi Minh City\. The measurement and structural models were assessed using partial least squares structural equation modelling \(PLS\-SEM\)\. The results show that facilitating conditions exert the strongest positive effect on adoption intention \(β = 0\.711, p &lt; \.001\), followed by effort expectancy \(β = 0\.235, p &lt; \.001\), trust \(β = 0\.145, p &lt; \.001\), social influence \(β = 0\.113, p = \.016\), and performance expectancy \(β = 0\.108, p = \.002\)\. Privacy concern negatively affects adoption intention \(β = −0\.230, p &lt; \.001\), while technology anxiety has a smaller but significant negative effect \(β = −0\.043, p = \.027\)\. Adoption intention strongly predicts WTP \(β = 0\.787, p &lt; \.001\)\. Furthermore, adoption intention significantly mediates the relationships between all seven antecedents and WTP\. The findings demonstrate that older consumers’ willingness to pay is closely connected to their readiness to adopt VCDs, while practical support and ease of use are particularly important for adoption in the Vietnamese context\. The study contributes to technology\-acceptance and assistive\-technology research by integrating enabling and inhibiting factors with WTP and provides implications for developers, retailers, families, caregivers, and policymakers seeking to promote age\-friendly voice technologies in Viet Nam\.
