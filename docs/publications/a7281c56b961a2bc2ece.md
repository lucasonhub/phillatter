# Effectiveness of the SEFT teaching model on the clinical practice competence of surgical residents: a retrospective cohort study

**Autores:** Xiang Jin; Weihong Zhao; Xinye Lin; Xiaomei Xie; Xinli Mao; 李卫林

**Data:** 2026-10-15 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Background This study aims to evaluate the effectiveness of the SEFT teaching model in improving surgical residents’ clinical practice competencies, so as to provide a basis for optimizing training strategies\.Methods This single\-center, non\-randomized retrospective cohort study included 38 residents\. The 2019 cohort \(n = 19\) was the control group, receiving traditional demonstration and practice\-based training, while the 2020 cohort \(n = 19\) was the intervention group, trained via the SEFT teaching model\. Scores across five core competencies were compared using the Wilcoxon rank\-sum test\.Results The intervention group scored significantly higher than the control group across all five assessment domains \(all p &lt; 0\.05\): medical history taking \(90\.43 ± 3\.83 vs\. 84\.29 ± 10\.63, p = 0\.039\), physical examination \(91\.20 ± 4\.88 vs\. 83\.76 ± 11\.51, p = 0\.017\), initial medical record documentation \(90\.61 ± 4\.98 vs\. 84\.20 ± 4\.97, p &lt; 0\.001\), and clinical reasoning and decision\-making \(87\.00 ± 3\.64 vs\. 80\.76 ± 9\.24, p = 0\.039\)\.Discussion The SEFT teaching model, with its closed\-loop structure and targeted feedback, showed promising potential to enhance surgical residents’ clinical practice competencies\.
