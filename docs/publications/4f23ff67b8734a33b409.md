# Sergent, Brandon M\.: Ontology Spam: How Explanatory Hunger Turns Abstractions Into Axioms

**Autores:** 

**Data de descoberta:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** unknown

## Descrição do feed

Externalist frameworks repeatedly perform the same epistemic operation\. Inquiry begins with experiential patterns, abstracts those patterns into models, encounters the explanatory limits of those models, and then responds to the remaining dissatisfaction by promoting some element of the model into an independently existing foundation\. The explanatory arrow is subsequently reversed: what was epistemically derived from experience becomes the thing invoked to explain experience\. This paper calls that transition ontological promotion and argues that it produces explanatory inversion\. The mechanism is examined across block\-universe metaphysics, scientific realism, ontic structural realism, mathematical Platonism, informational structural realism, ontic pancomputationalism, primitive\-law theories, and causal\-powers metaphysics\. These positions are not identical, and their proponents do not describe their own arguments using the genealogy proposed here\. The narrower claim is that each assigns independent or fundamental ontological status to structures whose evidential warrant enters inquiry through experienced regularities\. The resulting ontology then inherits the credibility legitimately earned by the abstraction before promotion\. Totality analysis reveals why the process is self\-renewing\. Once a promoted foundation is treated as ultimate, explanatory hunger immediately asks what explains, grounds, selects, validates, or causes it\. If the proposed answer lies outside the supposed totality, the totality was never total; if it lies inside, no external ground has been supplied\. Externalism repeatedly responds by manufacturing another outside anyway\. This explains both the proliferation of rival ontologies and two previously identified phenomena in Experiential Empiricism: the Free Rider problem and Axiom Camouflage\. EE responds differently\. Rather than manufacturing additional ontology when explanation bottoms out, it applies skeptical subtraction and retains only what survives burden of proof\. Explanatory dissatisfaction does not license ontological promotion, an unexplained pattern is not evidence for a hidden substrate, and an abstraction does not become axiomatic merely because explanation has nowhere further to go\. \(direct link \)
