# A METHODOLOGICAL FRAMEWORK FOR DEVELOPING FUTURE ENGLISH LANGUAGE TEACHERS&\#x27; PROFESSIONAL COMPETENCE IN AI\-ENHANCED LEARNING ENVIRONMENTS

**Autores:** Sharifa Jo&\#x27;rayevna Qarshiyeva

**Data:** 2026-10-14 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

The integration of artificial intelligence \(AI\) into education has created new opportunities for improving teacher preparation and developing professional competence among future English language teachers\. AI\-enhanced learning environments provide innovative tools for personalized instruction, adaptive learning, automated feedback, language assessment, and the development of digital pedagogical skills\. However, the effective use of these technologies requires a systematic methodological framework that combines linguistic knowledge, pedagogical expertise, digital literacy, and ethical awareness\. This thesis aims to examine the key methodological principles and structural components of a framework for developing the professional competence of future English language teachers in AI\-enhanced learning environments\. Particular attention is given to the integration of AI\-based educational tools, learner\-centered approaches, reflective practice, and competency\-based instruction\. The proposed framework emphasizes the importance of preparing prospective teachers to use AI critically, responsibly, and effectively in English language teaching\. The study concludes that the purposeful integration of AI technologies into teacher education can support the development of professional autonomy, instructional creativity, and readiness for digital transformation in education\.
