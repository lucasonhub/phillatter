# AI in Non\-Free and Autocratizing Contexts: Trends, Theoretical Frameworks, and the Minimum Digital Security Package

**Autores:** Andrej Kallaur

**Data:** 2026-10-13 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Drawing on a systematic synthesis of academic literature and reports from leading research institutions, this article conceptualizes contemporary trends in AI deployment within non\-democratic regimes\. I argue that while AI empowers autocrats and helps establish a baseline toolkit to deter regime transition, digital capabilities alone remain insufficient without traditional hard power\. Nevertheless, global technological diffusion and cross\-border collaboration within the “authoritarian international” facilitate a minimum digital security package\. This mechanism enables less technologically advanced non\-democracies to mitigate risks through AI\-driven surveillance, censorship, and information manipulation\.
