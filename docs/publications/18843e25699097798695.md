# Climate policy uncertainty and domestic–international grain\-market risk spillovers: a TVP\-VAR\-BK analysis with machine learning early warning

**Autores:** Jia You

**Data:** 2026-10-13 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Background Climate\-policy transition uncertainty may affect grain\-market risk transmission\. Methods We use 2010–2025 daily data for Chinese and US corn and wheat, US soybean landed cost, and GCPU in a TVP\-VAR\-BK connectedness model and machine\-learning warning exercise\. Results TCI averages 15\.29% and peaks at 57\.32%; short\-horizon transmission dominates; XGBoost achieves PR\-AUC of 0\.986/0\.949/0\.781 at 1/5/20\-period horizons\. Conclusion Climate\-policy uncertainty is dynamically associated with grain\-market risk and can support market\-stability monitoring, but the evidence is associational rather than causal\.
