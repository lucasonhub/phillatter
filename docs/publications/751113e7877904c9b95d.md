# Eureiandra orientalis \(Cucurbitaceae\), a new species from Kenya and Tanzania

**Autores:** Ruo\-Zhou Wang; Zhaohui Ran; Hanno Schaefer; Quentin Luke; Neduvoto P\. Mollel; Paul Muigai Kirika; Christopher Chapano; Xue Yan; Shengwei Wang; Neng Wei; Qing\-Feng Wang

**Data:** 2026-10-15 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Eureiandra orientalis , a new species of Cucurbitaceae from Kenya and Tanzania, is described and illustrated\. It was previously treated as Eureiandra sp\. A by Charles Jeffrey in the &quot;Flora of Tropical East Africa&quot;, and was later referred to as Eureiandra orientalis Q\.Luke ined\., but it has never been formally described and named\. Morphologically, the new species is most similar to E\. fasciculata , but is readily distinguished from other members of Eureiandra by its nearly glabrous habit, with hairs mainly restricted to the nodes, leaf veins and margins, dark\-green glossy leaves, denser shortly pedunculate male inflorescences, ovate petals, larger fruits, and oblong\-elliptic blackish brown seeds with indistinct margins and fibrous testa\. In addition, the two species differ in phenology: in E\. orientalis , the leaves are fully developed at flowering, whereas in E\. fasciculata , the stems are leafless at anthesis\. The new species is mostly known from coastal forests of Eastern Africa in Kenya and Tanzania, one of the globally recognized biodiversity hotspots\.
