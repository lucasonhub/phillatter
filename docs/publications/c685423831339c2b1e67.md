# Touch Localization with a Resistive Position Sensor\-Gated Oxide Neuromorphic Transistor

**Autores:** Bo Li; Si yuan Zhou; Wei  Sheng Wang; Lin Feng Wu; Wan Lin Zhang; Yu Fan Hu; Ya Rong Yuan; Chen Rui Zhai; Li Qiang Zhu

**Data:** 2026-10-15 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Abstract The ability to localize external mechanical stimuli is fundamental to human tactile perception, making its hardware implementation of significant interest for advanced human–machine interaction\. Here, a resistive position sensor \(RPS\) is proposed for touch location recognition, exhibiting a high sensitivity of ∼55 kΩ/cm and excellent stability\. A tactile perceptual system \(TPS\) is proposed by integrating the RPS with a chitosan\-gated oxide ionotronic neuromorphic transistor\. Benefiting from the strong proton relaxation effects of the chitosan\-based electrolyte, the neuromorphic transistor can effectively process electrical signals generated from the RPS\. The TPS can emulate various synaptic behaviors under mechanical stimuli\. As a proof of concept, it demonstrates Braille code recognition\. Furthermore, the TPS enables touch intention recognition through machine learning, demonstrating a high recognition accuracy of ∼99\.2%\. The results demonstrate great promise in human−machine interaction\.
