# Reference\-Guided Turn\-Taking Style Transfer for Full\-Duplex Spoken Language Models

**Autores:** Yuxuan Chen; Yang Xiao; Jack Sun; Chak Sun; Brick Li;  Sam

**Data:** 2026-10-14 · **Tipo:** preprint

## Resumo

Full\-duplex spoken language models decide at every frame whether to take the floor, defining a conversational personality \-\- yet each set of weights encodes exactly one, which human speakers bracket rather than share\. Existing controls mostly act from outside; none identifies the quantity that sets how much the model speaks\. We identify it: a single late\-layer residual\-stream direction, the initiative vector, extracted from the model&\#x27;s own conversations without training or annotation\. The same direction reads the decision, anticipating onsets up to 800 ms ahead, and shifts it: scaled during generation, it moves speech ratio from 6\-12% to 42\-61% \(no steering: 21\-30%\), consistently across conversations, weights, and corpora\. A short reference recording suffices to transfer a speaker&\#x27;s turn\-taking style to the model, and the transfer is reference\-specific: swapping in another speaker&\#x27;s reference worsens the match in 93\-98% of cases, whereas the best uniform dose is indistinguishable from no steering\. Turn\-taking style is not frozen into the weights but a direction that can be read, shifted, and calibrated per person\. Audio examples are available at https://elpsykongloo\.github\.io/initiative\-vector/\.
