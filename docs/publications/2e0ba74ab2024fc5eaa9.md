# Projected Wasserstein\-Fisher\-Rao Flows for Hawkes Background\-Intensity Estimation

**Autores:** Isaias Banales; Arturo Jaramillo

**Data:** 2026-10-14 · **Tipo:** preprint

## Resumo

We consider the problem of estimating the background intensity of a Hawkes process when the triggering mechanism is known, treating the background as an unknown finite positive measure with a regular density\. We propose a likelihood\-based variational approach based on the Wasserstein\-Fisher\-Rao geometry\. In this setting, the relevant first variations are naturally distribution\-valued and therefore fall outside the classical gradient\-flow formulation\. We develop a distributional framework for the resulting dynamics and give them a finite\-dimensional meaning through projections onto statistical manifolds\. We identify the true background as the unique zero of a limiting generalized gradient and, under suitable stability, compactness, integrability and approximation conditions, establish a conditional consistency theorem for measurable estimators satisfying the empirical projected equations on events whose probabilities tend to one\. Finally, we specialize the construction to Gaussian\-mixture manifolds and obtain an explicit finite\-dimensional scheme\.
