# Effectiveness of Business English Training Programs in Corporate Organizations

**Autores:** Varsha Palsule

**Data:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Effectiveness of Business English training programs is one of the crucial issues in improving the communication skills, professional performance, and workplace productivity of the personnel in corporate enterprises\. This study looks into the effectiveness of Business English training, which includes the following aspects of employees’ development: language proficiency, communication skills, confidence level, presentation skills, and overall workplace performance\. This study takes into consideration several aspects related to training including duration of training program, number of training hours, teaching methods, participation of employees, and business communication exercises\. Quantitative data is processed through statistical and machine learning analysis techniques to establish correlations between training variables and performance results of employees\. The results have shown that Business English training can help employees become effective communicators in their meetings, negotiations, and presentations and also while writing professional emails\. Regular practice and context\-based learning activities will help employees become more confident and proficient in language\.
