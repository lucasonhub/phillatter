# A Study on the Relationship Between the Digital Economy, Industrial Relocation, and Labor Migration

**Autores:** Ziyang Cai

**Data:** 2026-10-13 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Digitalized economy has been revamping the world industrial division of labor together with driving industrial relocation by geographical relocation of industries into digital restructuring of industries chains\. Being one of the main factors influencing the process of industrial relocation, labor migration will undergo significant change in the patterns and determinants of migration\. It is evident that digital tech alters the spatial logic of labor migration by reducing the cost of information and transactions as well as industrial relocations keep moving the regional labor force overtime due to the creation of job opportunities and the shifting nature of skill demands\. The research suggests that the results of this study would help to enhance the digital infrastructure in the interior areas and improve the policies on talents in order to bring in skilled employees\. Meanwhile, firms should embrace remote\-work models to reduce unnecessary geographic moves\. Crucially, targeted digital training is needed for low\-skilled migrants, so they are not left behind\. The present analysis offers empirical support to the concept that there are new features and processes of migration in a digital economy environment, offering a reference for formulating regional coordinated development strategies and labor market policies\.
