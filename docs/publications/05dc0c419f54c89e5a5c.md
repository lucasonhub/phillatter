# TD\-interpreter: enhancing the understanding of timing diagrams with visual\-language learning

**Autores:** Jie He; Vincent Theo Willem Kenbeek; Zhantao Yang; Meixun Qu; Ezio Bartocci; Dejan Ničković; Radu Grosu

**Data:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Abstract We introduce TD\-Interpreter, a specialised machine learning \(ML\) workflow that assists engineers in understanding complex timing diagrams \(TDs\), originating from a third party, during their design and verification process\. The outcome of this workflow is a visual question\-answer environment which allows engineers to input a set of TDs and ask design and verification queries regarding these TDs\. To address limited training data availability, we developed a synthetic data generation pipeline that aligns visual information with its textual interpretation\. We then implemented TD\-Interpreter using a lightweight 7B Multimodal Large Language Model \(MLLM\), guided by both supervised fine\-tuning \(SFT\) and reinforcement learning \(RL\)\. We also developed extensive TD\-reasoning benchmarks from simple retrieval tasks to complex reasoning problems in the real world\. Our experimental results show that TD\-Interpreter substantially outperformed both the untuned base model, and commercial MLLMs \(GPT\-5\.2\), on the evaluated benchmarks, demonstrating the effectiveness of the proposed workflow\.
