# AMPK activation stabilizes HIF\-2α by disrupting VHL binding and promotes erythroid differentiation under anemic stress via the mTOR pathway

**Autores:** Yun Cen; Pingjing Cai; 蒋瑾瑾; 黄丽华; Yiwei Hua

**Data:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Objective Anemia affects over two billion people globally, and ESA resistance poses major clinical hurdles\. HIF\-2α governs erythropoiesis, yet how AMPK/mTOR energy sensors regulate HIF\-2α in erythroid cells remains unclear\. This study explored whether AMPK activation stabilizes HIF\-2α via impaired VHL binding to boost erythroid differentiation under anemic stress through mTOR signaling\. Methods UT\-7/EPO cells were cultured under 1% O 2 with low EPO to mimic anemic stress, treated with AMPK/mTOR pharmacological modulators\. CHX chase and Co\-IP detected HIF\-2α stability and VHL interaction; Ser672 phosphorylation, erythroid and iron\-related genes, hepcidin and intracellular iron were measured\. Results Anemic stress suppressed cell viability, stabilized HIF\-2α, activated AMPK and inhibited mTOR, upregulated erythroid markers and triggered apoptosis\. AICAR extended HIF\-2α half\-life, disrupted HIF\-2α\-VHL binding and elevated Ser672 phosphorylation, while mTOR activator MHY1485 reversed these effects\. AMPK activation elevated EPOR, DMT1, FPN1, lowered hepcidin and ferritin, and restored intracellular iron; these effects vanished upon HIF\-2α knockdown or mTOR overactivation\. Conclusions AMPK stabilizes HIF\-2α by blocking VHL binding to facilitate erythroid maturation and iron homeostasis under anemic stress via mTOR\. The AMPK/mTOR/HIF\-2α cascade controls stress erythropoiesis, and targeting this axis may treat ESA\-insensitive anemia\.
