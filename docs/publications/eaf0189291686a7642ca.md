# The Application of Modern Technologies in Fine Arts as a Means of Artistic Expression

**Autores:** Murad Babayev

**Data:** 2026-10-09 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

The rapid development of digital technologies has significantly transformed the production, presentation, and interpretation of fine arts\. Technologies such as digital painting, computer graphics, three\-dimensional modelling, artificial intelligence \(AI\), virtual reality \(VR\), augmented reality \(AR\), interactive installations, and digital fabrication have expanded the conventional boundaries of artistic expression\. The purpose of this study is to examine the role of modern technologies in contemporary fine arts and to identify the ways in which technological tools contribute to new forms of artistic creativity and audience engagement\. The study employs a qualitative research methodology based on a review and comparative analysis of recent academic literature concerning digital art, AI\-generated art, immersive technologies, and technology\-mediated artistic practices\. The findings demonstrate that modern technologies function not merely as technical instruments but increasingly as artistic media that influence composition, aesthetics, spatial organization, authorship, interaction, and the relationship between artist and audience\. Digital technologies enable artists to experiment with forms that would be difficult or impossible to achieve through traditional media, while VR and AR create immersive and interactive artistic environments\. At the same time, AI introduces new forms of human–machine collaboration and challenges conventional concepts of originality, creativity, and authorship\. The study concludes that technology should be understood as an expanding medium of artistic expression rather than a replacement for human creativity\. Its effective application requires a balance between technological innovation, artistic intention, cultural identity, and ethical responsibility\.
