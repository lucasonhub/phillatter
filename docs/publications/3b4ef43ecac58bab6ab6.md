## Descrição do feed

\_Behavioral and Brain Sciences\_ 18 \(2\):251\-\-2\. 1995To demonstrate that a fallacy is committed, Block needs to convince us of two things: first, that the concept of phenomenal consciousness is distinct from that of access consciousness, and second, that it picks out a different property from that of access consciousness\. I raise doubt about both of these claims, suggesting that the concept of a phenomenal property is the concept of a property to which we have a special sort of access\. \(direct link \)
