# Can We Believe AI Peer Review?

**Autores:** Zongrui Yang; Haoyuan Li; Zhongsheng Wang; Gillian Dobbie; Jiamou Liu

**Data:** 2026-10-14 · **Tipo:** preprint

## Resumo

As large language models become widely used in scientific review and manuscript revision, AI scores are increasingly serving as feedback for assessing paper quality and guiding edits\. Yet whether a single AI score reliably reflects the reviewer&\#x27;s judgment, and whether revision gains based on that score are endorsed by other reviewers, remain understudied\. Using 200 papers from ICLR 2025 and 2026 and approximately 14,000 AI reviews, we study score reliability, score\-distribution estimation, and cross\-model agreement on revision gains\. Repeated reviews fluctuate even under identical conditions\. Finer\-grained scoring protocols reduce score variance but also systematically shift mean scores, while the gains assigned to manuscript revisions depend strongly on the reviewer\.
