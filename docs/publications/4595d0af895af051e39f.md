# Context\-aware Multimodal Classification of Coffee Leaf Diseases Using Vision Transformer and Agro\-environmental Sensor Data

**Autores:** Alfis Arif; Desi Puspita; Siti Aminah

**Data:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Coffee leaf diseases may show overlapping visual symptoms and are also associated with surrounding agro environmental conditions\.This study develops a context\-aware multimodal framework integrating coffee leaf images with agro\-environmental information for four\-class disease classification\.A pretrained Vision Transformer \(ViT\-Base\) extracts visual features, while an MLP encodes relative humidity, soil moisture, and soil pH, followed by feature\-level fusion\.The dataset contains 840 paired observations, divided into 672 training and 168 source\-separated validation observations\.Six configurations were evaluated: Sensor\-only MLP, EfficientNet\-B0, ViT\-Base, MaxViT\-Tiny, ViT \+ Sensor, and ViT \+ Shuffled Sensor\.The proposed ViT \+ Sensor achieved 100% validation accuracy, 100% macro F1score, balanced accuracy, Cohen&\#x27;s kappa of 1\.0000, and MCC of 1\.0000\.The Sensor\-only MLP achieved 71\.43%, confirming predictive information in the environmental variables\.However, ViT\-only and shuffled\-sensor models also achieved 100%\.Thus, multimodal integration is feasible, but no incremental accuracy benefit from image\-sensor correspondence was demonstrated\.
