# A laboratory\-based selected logistic model for early prediction of 28\-day mortality in patients with septic shock

**Autores:** Liying Zhang; Pu Chen; Jie Li; 肖维雷; Wentao Zhou

**Data:** 2026-10-13 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Background Early risk stratification in septic shock requires transparent models based on variables available soon after clinical diagnosis of septic shock\. We reanalysed a retrospective cohort using the original screening strategy: candidate predictors with univariable P &lt; 0\.05 were entered into multivariable logistic regression, and variables with multivariable P &lt; 0\.05 were retained in the final model\. Methods The analysis included 250 patients with complete 28\-day outcome and laboratory data\. The outcome was 28\-day mortality\. Candidate predictors comprised demographics, comorbidities, complete blood count, chemistry, coagulation, inflammatory markers, arterial pH, and PaO2/FiO2\. Laboratory values were treated as the first available values within 24 h after clinical diagnosis of septic shock\. The final fixed model and the complete model\-development pipeline were internally validated using 1,000 bootstrap resamples\. Discrimination, calibration, Brier score, ROC\-derived cutoff, decision curve analysis, LAR comparison, restricted cubic spline analysis, VIF, and LASSO sensitivity analysis were reported\. Results Among 250 patients, 73 died by day 28 \(29\.2%\)\. Univariable screening identified 12 candidate predictors, and multivariable screening retained lactate, D\-dimer, and PaO2/FiO2\. The final equation was logit\( P \) = 0\.412145 \+ 0\.250015 × Lactate \+ 0\.046852 × D\-dimer \- 0\.016820 × PaO2/FiO2\. The apparent AUC was 0\.902 \(95% CI 0\.864–0\.940\), and the optimism\-corrected AUC for the fixed final model was 0\.897\. When the complete model\-development pipeline was repeated within 1,000 bootstrap resamples, the optimism\-corrected AUC was 0\.875\. The final model had higher discrimination than LAR alone \[0\.902 \(0\.864–0\.940\) vs\. 0\.719 \(0\.645–0\.793\); DeLong P &lt; 0\.001\]\. Conclusions A selected logistic model using lactate, D\-dimer, and PaO2/FiO2 provided internally validated early risk estimates for 28\-day mortality\. External validation is required before routine clinical use\.
