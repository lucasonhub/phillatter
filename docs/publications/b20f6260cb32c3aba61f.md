# Rcc, Inja: The Aesthetic Pleasure of Deviation

**Autores:** 

**Data de descoberta:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** unknown

## Descrição do feed

Inja is not merely a raccoon, but the result of many generations of selective training\. She graduated with honors from the secret raccoon university, where she completed her dissertation on the beats and morals of animal societies, as well as on the emothematics hidden behind their thoughts and movements\. In this book, she follows the course of life from the simplest mechanisms to the most complex emothematics, examining how the past is preserved within organisms as memory, emotion, and aesthetic pleasure\.
