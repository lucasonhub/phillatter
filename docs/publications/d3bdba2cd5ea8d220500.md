# Research on the Value\-Added Evaluation System for Talent Cultivation Through Accounting Practice Based on AI Technology: A Case Study of Kashgar Chengxin Tourism Resource Development and Operation Co\., Ltd\.

**Autores:** Zhong Yuan; Ma Xiaoting

**Data:** 2026-10-15 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Accounting practice constitutes a critical component in accounting talent cultivation\. However, traditional practice evaluations tend to prioritize outcomes while neglecting baseline differences, and often suffer from fragmented data between academic institutions and enterprises, making it difficult to scientifically assess the effectiveness of talent cultivation\. In accordance with the requirements of educational evaluation reform—specifically the “exploration of value\-added evaluation”—this paper defines the connotation of value\-added evaluation in accounting practice\-based talent cultivation\. By leveraging technologies such as AI\-assisted data collection, digital profiling, and learning analytics, this study constructs a value\-added evaluation indicator system encompassing three dimensions: professional competence, professional ethics, and digital\-intelligent literacy\. Furthermore, the paper designs a technical architecture comprising a data layer, a model layer, and an application layer, along with a value\-added calculation model based on the “baseline–process–outcome” framework\. The application process is illustrated through a practical case study conducted at Kashgar Chengxin Tourism Resource Development and Operation Co\., Ltd\. This evaluation system measures talent cultivation value through the magnitude of improvement, thereby facilitating a shift in evaluation focus from mere identification and ranking toward fostering development and refinement\. This approach provides a valuable reference for industry\-academia collaboration in accounting practice\-based talent cultivation\.
