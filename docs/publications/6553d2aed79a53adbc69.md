## Descrição do feed

\_Zenodo\_ 1\. 2026This monograph reconstructs political reality through the generation, propagation, retention, transformation, and re\-entry of differences across heterogeneous material, institutional, computational, and historical configurations\. Beginning with Marx’s analysis of capitalist valorization and state machinery and Brassier’s account of real abstraction and the opacity of practice, it develops a continuous account of political operational shapes and their reality\-penetrating effects\. Credit scoring is examined as a potentially useful mechanism of prediction, coordination, and expanded financial access, whose institutional connections may also generate exclusion, cumulative disadvantage, and irreversible losses of opportunity\. The analysis distinguishes prediction, physical implementation, institutional efficacy, and justification\. Bonapartism, hyperpolitics, techno\-feudalism, platform capitalism, surveillance capitalism, and contemporary theories of domination are critically examined through their capacity to reconstruct concrete transformations in institutional reproduction and political intervention\. The monograph further investigates heterogeneous philosophical operations, autonomous machine politics, Philosophical AI, and the Ziran System\. It examines how operational conditions may be modified through interference, historical reinsertion, and the reconfiguration of observation and intervention\. Finally, it develops the hypothesis that the termination of trickster\-like political operations may occur when disruptive effects cease to depend on their original personal agents and become reproducible through institutional, capitalist, and machine configurations\. Such termination does not itself establish Xenoscience, whose emergence requires the independent ignition of heterogeneous scientific operations\. The resulting account treats political reality as a historically variable field of operative relations whose effects can persist beyond the disappearance of their originating authorities, classifications, and practices\. \(direct link \)
