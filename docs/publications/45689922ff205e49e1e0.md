# Impactos do Consumo de Conteúdos Curtos \(Reels\) na Atenção e Concentração de Adolescentes

**Autores:** Vanusa Batista da Silva; George Felinto da Silva

**Data:** 2026-10-09 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

The intensive use of social media by adolescents has led to significant changes in behavioral patterns, especially regarding attention and concentration\. In this context, this study aims to analyze the impacts of frequent consumption of short\-form content, such as Reels, on adolescents&\#x27; cognitive processes\. The research problem is guided by the following question: what are the effects of frequent use of such content on attention and concentration in this population? The methodology is characterized as a bibliographic research with a qualitative approach, based on scientific articles, books, and recent studies available in databases such as SciELO and PubMed\. The results indicate that continuous exposure to fast and highly stimulating content may contribute to reduced attention span, increased distraction, and difficulties in maintaining focus during prolonged activities\. Furthermore, a relationship was observed between excessive use of these platforms and changes in brain reward mechanisms\. It is concluded that frequent consumption of short\-form content may negatively impact adolescents&\#x27; cognitive development, highlighting the need for conscious use strategies\.
