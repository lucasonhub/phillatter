# Role of Artificial Intelligence in Advancing Biotechnology: A Comprehensive Review

**Autores:** Aman; Virender Kumar; Jyoti Mundlia

**Data:** 2026-10-13 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Abstract \- The convergence of Artificial Intelligence \(AI\) and biotechnology is revolutionizing scientific research, healthcare, and industrial applications\. This paper examines how AI techniques, like computer vision, machine learning, deep learning, and NLP, are revolutionizing important biotechnology fields like drug discovery, genomics, personalized medicine, agricultural innovation, and industrial bioprocessing\. AI enables faster data analysis, accurate prediction of biological behaviors, and automation of complex tasks, significantly accelerating the pace of innovation\. We also discuss commonly used AI tools in biotechnology and highlight the growth of AI\-driven biotech startups\. The incorporation of AI presents a number of obstacles despite its transformative potential, such as the requirement for transparent algorithms, ethical issues, and data privacy concerns\. The goal of the article is to provide a thorough review of the state of affairs, recent developments, and potential future progress in the nexus of biotechnology and artificial intelligence\.
