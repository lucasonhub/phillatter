# Vision\-spectroscopy cooperative sensing and cold atmospheric plasma inactivation for early warning and management of agricultural product diseases

**Autores:** BaoJing Sun; Wei Zhang; Xin Hu

**Data:** 2026-10-14 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Abstract Pathogen\-induced disease causes heavy post\-harvest losses and threatens food safety, yet routine inspection rarely identifies an infection before it becomes visible\. This study evaluates multimodal sensing, threshold\-based early warning and plasma\-based decontamination for post\-harvest disease management in strawberry \(Fragaria × ananassa cv\. ‘Hongyan’\)\. A dual\-branch network, pairing a ResNet\-34 visual encoder with a one\-dimensional convolutional spectral encoder through bidirectional cross\-modal attention, fuses RGB imagery with near\-infrared hyperspectral reflectance\. Across five severity grades the fused model reached 93\.5 ± 0\.7% accuracy over fifteen cross\-validation runs and 94\.2% on a fruit\-level held\-out test set of 260 samples \(95% CI 91\.4 to 97\.1\), with a weighted F1 score of 0\.938 and a Cohen’s Kappa of 0\.927\. Its 2\.7\-point margin over an unweighted late\-fusion baseline did not reach significance after correction for eleven held\-out comparisons \(continuity\-corrected McNemar $$\\:\{\\chi\\:\}^\{2\}$$ = 4\.00, Holm\-adjusted $$\\:p$$ = 0\.140\)\. Alarms preceded the expert\-confirmed transition to a visible lesion by 52\.0 ± 9\.6 h in the 80\-fruit monitoring cohort\. Cold atmospheric plasma parameters were screened systematically: 1\.5 W/cm 2 in Ar/O 2 for 180 s delivered more than a 3\.5\-log reduction for Botrytis cinerea , Colletotrichum gloeosporioides and Aspergillus niger ; the measured quality attributes did not differ significantly from untreated controls under the tested conditions\. Leakage assays and a sigmoidal dose\-response model support membrane damage associated with oxidative stress\. The proposed progression from membrane peroxidation through enzyme disruption to DNA damage remains a mechanistic hypothesis\. Sensing and treatment were evaluated separately; the present evidence is limited to one commodity, laboratory conditions and no integrated pilot\-scale trial\.
