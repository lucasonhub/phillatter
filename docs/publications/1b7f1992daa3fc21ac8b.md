# Dablemont, Paul: La Cosmologie de l&\#x27;esprit d&\#x27;Evald Ilyenkov et la philosophie de la réalité chez John Searle

**Autores:** 

**Data de descoberta:** 2026-10-19 · **Tipo:** unknown

## Descrição do feed

This paper explores the unexpected conceptual bridges between Evald Ilyenkov&\#x27;s Cosmology of Spirit and John Searle&\#x27;s philosophy of reality\. While Ilyenkov develops a dialectical cosmology in which the ideal emerges as a latent potentiality of matter on a cosmic scale, Searle articulates a biological naturalism in which consciousness is an emergent property of the brain\. Despite these ontological differences, both philosophers reject Cartesian dualism and reductive materialism, and both share the same fundamental question: how can meaning and significance arise from brute physical reality? The paper proposes a &quot;weak&quot; argument \(grounded in collective practices – praxis in Ilyenkov, collective intentionality in Searle\) and a &quot;strong&quot; argument \(reading Searle&\#x27;s Background as a local biological instance of Ilyenkov&\#x27;s latent ideal\)\. It concludes that these two frameworks can be read as complementary scales of a single stratified process, opening a fruitful dialogue between analytic philosophy and dialectical materialism\. \(direct link \)
