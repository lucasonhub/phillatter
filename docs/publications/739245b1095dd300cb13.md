# Rabern, Brian: Knowledge and Action in LLM Systems

**Autores:** 

**Data de descoberta:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** unknown

## Descrição do feed

Terms like &quot;tool&quot;, &quot;harness&quot;, &quot;skill&quot;, &quot;memory&quot;, &quot;RAG&quot;, &quot;MCP&quot;, and &quot;agent&quot; are used fluently but rarely defined\. This paper defines them\. One fact organizes the account: a language model is a stateless function from tokens to a distribution over tokens, and everything else is a surrounding program deciding what goes in \(knowledge\) and what to do with what comes out \(action\)\. Once that line is drawn, the domain turns out to be much smaller than its vocabulary\.
