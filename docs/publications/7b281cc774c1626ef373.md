# Case Report: Neonatal ultra\-severe hemolytic anemia complicated by pulmonary hypertension induced by compound heterozygous variants in the KLF1 gene and literature review

**Autores:** Yunzhi Liu; Dejian Yuan; Yanxing Lv; Biwen Huang; Lanqiu Hu; Yongjiang Jiang

**Data:** 2026-10-13 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Objective To investigate the clinical characteristics, genotypic profiles, and diagnostic/therapeutic strategies for congenital hemolytic anemia induced by compound heterozygous variants in the KLF1 gene\. Methods We retrospectively analyzed the clinical data of a neonate who presented primarily with ultra\-severe anemia, elevated direct bilirubin, and pulmonary hypertension\. Whole\-exome sequencing \(WES\) was utilized for variant identification, followed by a comprehensive review of literature\. Results The patient, a female neonate, developed severe anemia within 10 h of birth, accompanied by rapidly progressing hyperbilirubinemia \(predominantly direct hyperbilirubinemia\)\. Bedside echocardiography revealed a patent ductus arteriosus \(PDA\) and pulmonary hypertension\. Trio\-WES identified pathogenic compound heterozygous variants in the KLF1 gene: c\.519\_525dup \(p\.Gly176ArgfsTer179\) and c\.1012C &gt; A \(p\.Pro338Thr\)\. Following multiple red blood cell transfusions and mechanical ventilatory support, the infant&\#x27;s condition stabilized\. During a 24\-month follow\-up, the patient exhibited transfusion\-dependent anemia\. Conclusion For neonates presenting with unexplained \(Coombs/DAT\-negative\) severe hemolytic anemia, particularly when complicated by pulmonary hypertension and atypical cholestasis, early genetic testing should be performed to rule out KLF1 \-related diseases\.
