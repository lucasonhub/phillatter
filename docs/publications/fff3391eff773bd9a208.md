# Role of Internet of Things and Artificial Intelligence for electronic waste management: A Comprehensive Review and Recommendations

**Autores:** Parvathi Sindhuja; Shaik Vaseem Akram; L\.M\.I\. Leo Joseph

**Data:** 2026-10-13 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Electronic waste \(e\-waste\) is one of the fastest\-growing waste streams worldwide, driven by rapid technological change and increased consumption of electronic products\. Improper management and disposal of e\-waste lead to the release of hazardous materials, including heavy metals and toxic chemicals, posing significant challenges to environmental sustainability and human health\. Recently, Industry 4\.0\-enabled technologies such as the Internet of Things \(IoT\), machine learning \(ML\), and deep learning \(DL\) have emerged as promising technologies for e\-waste monitoring, classification, and environmental risk assessment\. Although, recent studies have increasingly integrated multiple sensing modalities, where most existing methods are application\-specific and rely on low\-level integration of data from heterogeneous sources, without adopting a unified feature\-level multimodal fusion approach for systematic real\-time e\-waste hazard assessment\. This review presents a comparative analysis of IoT and AI implementation in e\-waste management and identified key research gaps and the performance of these two technologies\. The analysis shows that both technologies enhance waste monitoring, routing efficiency, and intelligent decision\-making in e\-waste management\. Furthermore, AI\-enabled methods have been shown to be successful at e\-waste classification, hazard identification, and automated disassembly\. Though recent research has begun to incorporate multiple sensing modalities, most AI–IoT studies have been application\-specific, and a unimodal framework for full\-scale real\-time hazard assessment is still lacking\. The gaps in the existing literature led to the conceptualization of an AI–IoT system that combines image\-based waste classification, environmental sensing, and multimodal data fusion for real\-time, intelligent detection of hazardous waste\.
