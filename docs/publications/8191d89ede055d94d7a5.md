# The Construction of College Counselor Team based on Professional Competence from the Perspective of Building a Leading Country in Education: Logical Implications, Realistic Challenges and Practical Approaches

**Autores:** Lingling Wang; Youmai Li

**Data:** 2026-10-13 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

With the building China into a leading country in education shaping a new pattern of fostering moral character, the college counselor team, along with teaching, research, and management, is referred to as the &quot;fourth team&quot;\. The new strategic positioning has raised higher requirements for the construction of the college counselor team\. This article, based on the theory of competence and the strategy of building China into a leading country in education, explores from three logical perspectives \(the theoretical alignment basis, the value\-oriented goals, and the evolving trends in reality\) how professional competence can be integrated into the construction of the college counselor team\. It analyzes the current practical challenges, such as blurred identity boundaries, unbalanced ability structure, weakened professional identity, and lagging evaluation mechanisms, and proposes using the competence model as a guiding principle to establish a complete chain mechanism of &quot;selection\-training\-utilization\-assessment,&quot; empower the upgrade of competence through stratified and categorized training, build an evaluation and incentive system through collaborative and coordinated linkage, optimize the competence structure through digital means, and promote the high\-quality development of the &quot;fourth team&quot;\.
