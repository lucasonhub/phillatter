# Predictive modeling of cardiovascular injury in gestational diabetes mellitus incorporating deep learning

**Autores:** Xuanzi Liu; Yingdi Liu; Xinyu Guo

**Data:** 2026-10-09 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Aiming at the difficulty in recognizing the risk of cardiovascular injury in gestational diabetes mellitus patients, this paper proposes a deep prediction model that integrates convolution, temporal coding and attention mechanism\. The model is based on LSTM and Transformer structure, constructed on the basis of multi\-dimensional clinical features and dynamic monitoring data, and improves the recognition ability of high\-risk individuals through multi\-channel feature extraction and weighted cross\-entropy loss optimization\. The experimental results show that the proposed model achieves an accuracy of 89\.5%, an F1\-score of 87\.6%, and an AUC value of 0\.927 on the test set, which are 2\.3%, 3\.3%, and 2\.3% higher than the traditional LSTM model, respectively\. In terms of inference efficiency, the single\-sample prediction takes 14\.1ms, which balances the accuracy and deployment performance, and has good prospects for engineering applications\.
