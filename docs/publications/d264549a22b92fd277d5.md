# Physically Active Learning and Primary Students&\#x27; Outcomes: A Bibliometric Science Mapping Study \(2000–2026\)

**Autores:** Jeni Arianti Pa Nyola; Mubiar Agustin; Idat Muqodas

**Data:** 2026-10-13 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

This study presents a bibliometric analysis of Physically Active Learning \(PAL\) and its relationship with primary school students&\#x27; learning outcomes\. To the best of the authors&\#x27; knowledge, no prior bibliometric study has exclusively focused on PAL as a pedagogical construct\. Using the Scopus database \(2000–2026\), a modified PRISMA approach, and VOSviewer v\.1\.6\.20, 1,001 articles from 374 journals were analyzed\. The dataset yielded 21,897 citations, an h\-index of 68, and a CAGR of 17\.1% \(2010–2025\)\. Three temporal phases were identified: health\-oriented foundation \(2000–2010\), pedagogical diversification \(2011–2017\), and expansion–consolidation \(2018–2026\)\. Thematic analyses suggest a gradual paradigm shift from PAL as a health intervention toward a pedagogically oriented strategy, reflected in the increasing prominence of embodied learning and movement integration\. The United States dominates global production \(45\.1%\), while Indonesia remains underrepresented \(1\.5%\)\. This study proposes research directions for Indonesian scholars, including traditional game\-based PAL interventions and Merdeka Belajar curriculum integration\. Keywords: Efficiency, Knowledge Management, Tax Administration, Tax Officer, Training
