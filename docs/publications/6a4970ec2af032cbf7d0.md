# Mapping smart technologies in sustainable tourism: Knowledge structure, research fronts, and future research directions

**Autores:** Nguyen Tuong Lai; Cao Thi Sen

**Data:** 2026-10-14 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

This study maps the development, knowledge structure, research fronts, and emerging directions of research on smart technologies in sustainable tourism\. Bibliographic data were retrieved from Scopus on August 31, 2026\. The initial search identified 645 documents, of which 220 articles and reviews were retained after applying subject\-area, document\-type, language, and relevance criteria\. The study combines performance analysis with science mapping techniques, including reference co\-citation analysis, keyword co\-occurrence analysis, and document\-level bibliographic coupling, using VOSviewer\. The results show that research at the intersection of smart technologies and sustainable tourism has expanded rapidly, particularly since 2024\. The intellectual structure of the field is primarily rooted in smart tourism, smart destinations, Big Data, technology acceptance, and sustainability\-oriented applications of digital technologies\. Keyword co\-occurrence analysis identifies five major thematic clusters: technology\-enabled sustainable tourism and digital transformation; data\-driven tourism management and destination development; smart tourism, machine learning, and sustainability analytics; artificial intelligence and generative artificial intelligence for sustainable destination management; and digital innovation in smart ecotourism experiences\. Bibliographic coupling further reveals seven contemporary research fronts: digital platforms and sustainable consumption, artificial intelligence\-enabled governance, smart destinations, generative artificial intelligence, data\-driven planning and mobility, Big Data analytics, and smart\-green integration\. Overall, the findings indicate a transition from technology\-centred research towards a broader perspective that emphasises governance capacity, sustainability outcomes, stakeholder coordination, and technological trade\-offs\. The study contributes to the literature by integrating fragmented technological and sustainability\-oriented research streams and identifying future directions concerning artificial intelligence governance, smart\-green integration, destination resilience, responsible tourist behaviour, and evidence\-based destination management\.
