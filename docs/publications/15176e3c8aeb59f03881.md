# AN INTELLIGENT FRAMEWORK FOR CUSTOMER\-CENTRIC PACKAGING OPTIMIZATION AND OPERATIONAL EFFICIENCY ACROSS INDUSTRIES

**Autores:** Daniel Perez Chayka

**Data:** 2026-10-13 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

The research introduces a smart customer packaging optimization and operationefficiency framework in industries, with packaging and development of packaging automation solutions as a focus\.The secondary research approach and the qualitative analytical bargain is applied to investigate the customer demand, product features, packaging aspects, operational limitations and the possibility of automation\.The suggested framework comprises of six phases, including customer needs assessment, solution development, evaluation and quoting, sampling and testing, production and implementation, and performance follow\-up\.The Sales, Design, Pricing, Production, Quality and Engineering would work together as cross\-functional to develop solutions that are tailored\.The analysis suggests that product protection, customer requirements, compatibility in operation, cost efficiency, optimization of materials and sustainability are good packaging decision factors\.The automation of packaging such as case erectors, case packers, tapers and palletizers have the ability to aid high throughput, capacity, consistency, productivity and labor application\.The framework offers a feasible methodology of matching the packaging solutions to customer requirements besides sustaining perpetual enhancement and operational worth\.
