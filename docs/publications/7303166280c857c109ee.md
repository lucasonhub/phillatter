# Improved RT\-DETR\-based detection and counting of shrimp in EK80 acoustic echogram images

**Autores:** Yanrui Qu; Sheng Chen; 薛瑞超; Siyuan Zheng

**Data:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Accurately detecting shrimp targets in EK80 acoustic echogram images is challenging due to their small size, high\-density aggregation, and strong background clutter, which collectively hinder conventional object\-detection models\. To address these issues, we propose an enhanced detection framework based on RT\-DETR\. Our method introduces three key improvements: the integration of an Efficient Multi\-Scale Attention \(EMA\) mechanism to strengthen feature representation, the addition of a dedicated P2 detection layer to better capture fine\-grained information for small objects, and the adoption of a combined Focal Loss and Wise\-IoU \(WIoU\) loss function to mitigate class imbalance and refine bounding\-box regression\. Experimental evaluations demonstrate that the improved model achieves a mean mAP@50 of 0\.844 and a mean mAP@50–95 of 0\.437, outperforming the baseline RT\-DETR\. Furthermore, by incorporating DeepSORT for multi\-object tracking and identity association, the proposed system enables automated shrimp counting with an average accuracy of 83\.32% across 10 video clips\. These results demonstrate the effectiveness of the proposed approach in enhancing detection, tracking, and enumeration performance in complex acoustic imagery, offering a promising technical solution for real\-world applications such as intake safety monitoring and early warning systems at nuclear power plants\.
