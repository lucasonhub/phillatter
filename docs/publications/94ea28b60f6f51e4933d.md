# From challenge to solution: Perspectives on AI\-powered multi\-omics in tackling CDK4/6 inhibitor resistance in breast cancer

**Autores:** Jia Wu; Xinran Liu; Guoyu Wu

**Data:** 2026-10-15 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Currently, three CDK4/6 inhibitors \(CDK4/6i\) have been approved globally in clinical practice for combination with endocrine therapy as first\-line treatment for advanced or metastatic hormone receptor\-positive/human epidermal growth factor receptor 2\-negative \(HR \+ /HER2 − \) breast cancer\. The advent of CDK4/6i has significantly improved the survival rates of patients with breast cancer\. However, once resistance to CDK4/6i emerges, follow\-up treatment options are limited\. Therefore, there is a growing demand for biomarkers to detect intrinsic or acquired resistance and for effective subsequent therapies\. Recent advances in artificial intelligence \(AI\) and multi\-omics data have enabled significant breakthroughs in biomarker prediction and drug development, advancing personalized medicine\. This review synthesizes the primary mechanisms of CDK4/6i resistance in breast cancer and explores the current applications and prospects of integrating AI with multi\-omics to overcome these challenges and advance personalized therapeutic strategies\.
