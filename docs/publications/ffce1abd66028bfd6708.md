# Communication\-efficient collaborative 4D mapping via federated temporal Gaussian pruning and knowledge\-distilled neural radiance fields for post\-disaster rescue robotics

**Autores:** Lei Ding; Zhiying Hu; Jin Yue; Dong Li

**Data:** 2026-10-17 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Post\-disaster rescue operations demand real\-time, collaborative 4D mapping of dynamic ruins, yet severely damaged communication infrastructure and limited edge computing resources make conventional neural radiance fields and 3D Gaussian splatting impractical: they lack temporal representation, incur high transmission latency, and do not address the risks of centralizing raw sensor observations\. To bridge this gap, we propose Fed\-TGP\-KD\-NeRF, a federated collaborative mapping framework that integrates a learnable temporal latent code with multi\-dimensional positional hashing and Fourier feature mapping to construct a continuous 4D dynamic Gaussian field\. Adaptive pruning based on Gaussian opacity and gradient sensitivity removes redundant primitives, while a teacher\-student knowledge distillation paradigm with intermediate\-layer feature alignment and KL divergence compresses the model to approximately 10% of its original size\. An improved federated averaging mechanism with dynamic weight adaptation \(bandwidth and battery status\) and periodic synchronization enables data\-local collaboration by keeping raw observations on the originating robot\. Experiments on the 3DAeroRelief dataset show that the proposed method achieves a PSNR of 32\.58 dB, SSIM of 0\.912, and real\-time rendering at 35\.26 FPS, while reducing per\-round communication overhead to only 13\.45 MB and converging in 42 rounds, outperforming the evaluated baseline methods\. The framework balances reconstruction fidelity, communication efficiency, and raw\-data locality; it does not claim a formal differential\-privacy guarantee\.
