# Research on the Cultivation Path of Students&\#x27; Critical Thinking in Management Consulting Courses under AI Empowerment

**Autores:** Fan Yang

**Data:** 2026-10-09 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

The widespread adoption of generative artificial intelligence has restructured the working models and talent demand standards of the management consulting industry\. As an essential advanced competency for management consulting professionals, critical thinking equips practitioners and students to analyze AI\-generated consulting results, address complex managerial issues\. Based on AI technology application scenarios, this paper analyzes the current dilemmas in cultivating critical thinking in undergraduate management consulting courses, constructs a cultivation system covering four dimensions—teaching objectives, teaching content, teaching models, and evaluation mechanisms—and explores curriculum education pathways adapted to the AI era\. This study seeks to develop students’ capabilities in consulting critical thinking, problem analysis, and intelligent tool mastery, providing references for the intelligent teaching reform of economics and management majors and the cultivation of higher\-order thinking talents\.
