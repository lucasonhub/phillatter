# TraceRank: configurable review support for requirements traceability recovery

**Autores:** Hamad Iqab Alsawalqah; Ahmad Abadleh; Yazn Alshamaileh; Marwan Al\-Tawil; Ibrahim Aljarah; Mohammed Saad Alaydaa

**Data:** 2026-10-14 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Introduction Requirements traceability recovery is commonly evaluated as a single ranking task, although practical review settings differ in the evidence available before review, the inspection budget, and the possibility of analyst feedback\. We present TraceRank, a configurable review\-support framework that makes recovery assumptions explicit and enables only the recovery modes that are valid for the available evidence and review process\. Methods TraceRank separates cold\-start recovery, partial\-trace recovery, selective validation, fixed\-budget review, and interactive feedback\. It organizes retrieval, semantic\-structural, dependency, hypergraph, validated\-link, and feedback evidence within a common recovery pipeline\. We evaluate TraceRank on five traceability datasets: eTour, iTrust, SMOS, eANCI, and LibEST\. Results In the cold\-start setting, the best retained configuration recovers 404 of 501 true links at Top\-30 \(LinkRecall@30 = 80\.6%\) and completes 33 of 60 held\-out requirements\. Under the partial\-trace assumption, the Validated Trace Responsibility Graph \(VTRG\) recovers 465 links \(92\.8%\), completes 43 requirements, and reduces severe misses to 12\. Across the same five requirement\-level split seeds used for the split\-robustness analysis, VTRG achieves mean LinkRecall@30 of 91\.3% \(SD 4\.0%\) and mean CompleteRate@30 of 76\.5% \(SD 8\.1%\)\. Archived fixed\-budget and simulated\-feedback outputs are retained only as exploratory review\-process sensitivity evidence because the originating row\-level priority queue and sequential update history are not preserved in the clean archive; they are therefore not treated as confirmatory evidence in the primary conclusions\. Discussion The results support a configuration\-aware interpretation of trace recovery: cold\-start, partial\-trace, fixed\-budget, and feedback\-based recovery are different operating settings and should not be interpreted as one universal static ranking problem\.
