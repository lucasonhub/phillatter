# A COMPARATIVE ANALYSIS OF THRESHOLD, REFERENCE AND MACHINE\-BASED APPROACHES TO ASSESSING THE CORRECTNESS OF REHABILITATION EXERCISES

**Autores:** Mariia Korostylova; Oleg Kobylin; Ilya Kobylin

**Data:** 2026-10-15 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

The task of automatically assessing the correctness of rehabilitation exercise performance based on key body points extracted from video is considered\. Three approaches — threshold\-based, reference\-based \(dynamic time warping, DTW\) and machine\-based \(Random Forest\) — are compared on the REHAB24\-6 dataset for three exercises \(arm abduction, leg abduction, squats\)\. Group cross\-validation by participant and McNemar&\#x27;s exact test are applied; the results are compared against trivial baseline classification levels\. A useful predictive signal is shown not to be present for all exercises: for arm abduction the machine\-based approach achieved an accuracy of 82\.1 % against a baseline of 56\.2 %, whereas for squats no approach exceeded the baseline\. The advantages and limitations of each approach for use in home telerehabilitation systems are identified\.
