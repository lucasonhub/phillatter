# Promises and Problems of Paediatric Digital Radiography in Nigeria: A Commentary

**Autores:** Flavious Bobuin Nkubli

**Data:** 2026-10-15 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Abstract Background: Advances in paediatric digital radiography promise improved image quality and enhanced patient radiation protection\. However, clinical challenges including overexposures, underexposures, and suboptimal image quality persist despite the availability of proven technical interventions\. Consequently, a critical gap remains in translating these safety standards into routine clinical practice within developing healthcare systems\. Objective: To explore the dual realities of promises and operational challenges in paediatric digital radiography within the Nigerian context, and to articulate the imperative for adopting implementation research frameworks\. Methods and Materials: A comprehensive review of current literature, quality improvement guidelines, and diagnostic reference paradigms was synthesized alongside anecdotal clinical evidence from the Nigerian radiographic setting to identify systemic execution bottlenecks\. Results: While efficacious interventions and safety guidelines exist internationally to ensure paediatric radiation protection, contemporary evidence in Nigeria reveals a rising burden of unintentional radiation exposure and &quot;dose creep\.&quot; Implementation research offers the necessary methodologies to design, test, and sustain context\-specific implementation strategies, directly bridging the gap between established radiation protocols and real\-world clinical compliance\. Conclusion: Promising paediatric radiation protection interventions fail to deliver intended outcomes in Nigeria due to contextual implementation barriers\. Embracing implementation research is imperative to design practical strategies that safeguard vulnerable paediatric populations\. Keywords: paediatric radiography; digital radiography; implementation research; radiation protection; dose creep\.
