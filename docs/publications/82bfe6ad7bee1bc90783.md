# A Study on Youth’s Awareness and Understanding of Data Privacy While Using AI Chatbots in Maharashtra\.

**Autores:** Bhakti Anil Rindhe; Samruddhi Santosh Takalkar; Shweta LahuMohandule; Vaishnavi Namdev Kate; Vijay Dhawale

**Data:** 2026-10-11 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

The extensive usage of AI chatbots, such as ChatGPT and Google Gemini, has led to a complete transformation of the ways young people acquire information, conduct their academic assignments, and perform technical tasks\. However, with the rise in popularity of these platforms, worries emerge regarding the protection of users&\#x27; information while exchanging it on these sites\. The purpose of this study is to identify the degree of awareness regarding the problem of data privacy among young people who use AI chatbots in the state of Maharashtra\. The descriptive approach was applied in the research process, and primary data were collected through the use of Google Forms surveys\. The sample consisted of 151 respondents aged 15\-29 who utilized AI chatbots and exchanged personal information, privacy measures, risks, knowledge about the storage and training of the model, and data protection laws\. Some of the AI systems that were in common usage include ChatGPT and Google Gemini, while the type of information that was in widespread sharing included academic information\. However, there is one other important observation on the lack of privacy settings, the lack of awareness of storing conversations, and also the limited self\-reported awareness of the Indian Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 or similar data laws\. The findings of this research suggest the need for proper digital privacy education, clear privacy information in different languages, proper data sharing, and awareness programs\.
