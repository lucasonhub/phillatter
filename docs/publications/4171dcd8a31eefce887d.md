# Johnson, Jamison: On Seeing \- The Hypothetical Perspective of an Individual Aligned in Time

**Autores:** 

**Data de descoberta:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** unknown

## Descrição do feed

Do you know what you look like? Starting from the fact that no one has seen their own face except by mirror, photograph, or report, this lyric\-philosophical essay imagines a person whose Jays are aligned: someone who sleeps little, eats little, and receives every signal at once, time among them\. Such a person would not see the future, which is open, but would move through the present with a timeless vantage close to Boethius&\#x27;s eternity and Spinoza&\#x27;s view under the aspect of eternity, and close to the Dzogchen rigpa of Tenzin Wangyal\. The essay then widens the lens to the Transfiguration, seen whole by no one present, and to the Miracle of the Sun at Fátima in 1917, where tens of thousands gave tens of thousands of accounts and the cameras caught only the crowd\. A thought experiment with a glass of water traces the positive and negative positions a single moment holds, and Jung&\#x27;s transcendent function names the third way out\. The answer to the opening question: not alone\. \(direct link \)
