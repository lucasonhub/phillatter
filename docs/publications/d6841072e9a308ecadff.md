# СУЧАСНИЙ ІНСТРУМЕНТАРІЙ СТРАТЕГІЧНОГО АНАЛІЗУ ДІЯЛЬНОСТІ ПІДПРИЄМСТВА В КРИЗОВИХ УМОВАХ

**Autores:** М\.А\. Скрипник

**Data:** 2026-10-13 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

The article examines modern tools for strategic analysis of enterprise activities under crisis conditions characterized by a high level of uncertainty, a dynamic external environment, increasing economic, financial, political, technological, and operational risks, disruptions in established value chains, and the need for prompt adjustment of managerial decisions\. The relevance of the study is determined by the fact that traditional approaches to strategic analysis, which are primarily based on periodic assessment of the internal and external environment of an enterprise, are becoming less capable of ensuring the required speed of response to changes under conditions of crisis transformations\. In the contemporary business environment, strategic analysis should evolve from an instrument for assessing the current state of an enterprise into a continuous system of analytical and informational support for strategic management aimed at the early identification of threats, forecasting possible development scenarios, assessing strategic resilience, and developing adaptive managerial decisions\. The purpose of the article is to substantiate modern tools for strategic analysis of enterprise activities under crisis conditions and to develop an integrated approach to their application in order to improve the soundness, timeliness, and adaptability of strategic managerial decisions\. Achieving this purpose involves systematizing modern methods of strategic analysis, determining their functional relevance under crisis conditions, and substantiating the need to integrate traditional analytical tools with digital business analytics, risk analysis, scenario modelling, stress testing, and enterprise resilience assessment\. he methodological framework of the study is based on general scientific and specialized research methods\. The methods of analysis and synthesis were used to decompose modern strategic analysis tools and identify their principal components; the systems approach was applied to examine strategic analysis as an integrated subsystem of enterprise strategic management; the comparative method was used to identify the advantages and limitations of traditional and modern analytical tools; the structural and logical method was employed to establish relationships between strategic diagnostics, risk assessment, scenario forecasting, and the development of strategic alternatives; expert assessment methods were applied to substantiate the possibility of determining weighting coefficients for the components of strategic resilience; the scenario method was used to model alternative enterprise development trajectories under different crisis\-related conditions; stress testing was applied to identify critical values of key influencing factors; and the integral assessment method was used to provide a comprehensive evaluation of enterprise strategic resilience\. The theoretical and methodological provisions of the study are based on contemporary approaches to strategic, crisis, and adaptive enterprise management\. The scientific novelty of the research lies in the development of theoretical and methodological provisions for establishing a modern system of strategic analysis of enterprise activities under crisis conditions\. In contrast to the traditional approach, strategic analysis is proposed to be viewed as a dynamic, continuous, and integrated system combining external environment monitoring, early warning of crisis threats, internal resource and competence diagnostics, financial and economic analysis, competitive analysis, risk assessment, scenario modelling, stress testing, digital business analytics, and strategic resilience assessment\. The approach to organizing strategic analysis has been improved by shifting from a static assessment of the enterprise&\#x27;s actual condition to a cyclical model of “monitoring – diagnostics – forecasting – selection of a strategic alternative – implementation – control – adjustment\.” An integrated model of strategic analysis is proposed, aimed at ensuring a direct link between analytical findings and specific managerial decisions\. The approach to assessing enterprise strategic resilience has been further developed by distinguishing its financial, operational, market, organizational, innovative, and digital dimensions\. The main results of the study include the systematization of modern strategic analysis tools and the identification of ways to adapt them to crisis conditions\. It is substantiated that PESTEL analysis should be transformed into a dynamic system for monitoring external factors by determining the probability and magnitude of their impact on the enterprise\. SWOT analysis is proposed to be applied in a dynamic form, taking into account the projected changes in the significance of internal and external factors\. The study substantiates the expediency of combining competitive analysis with benchmarking, resource analysis with the assessment of enterprise dynamic capabilities, and financial diagnostics with cash\-flow analysis and an early warning system\. A system of key indicators of an enterprise&\#x27;s crisis condition is proposed, covering financial, market, operational, logistics, human\-resource, innovation, digital, and risk\-related indicators\. The expediency of establishing three zones for each indicator – normal, warning, and critical – is substantiated\. Such an approach enables the development of an early warning system and facilitates the transition from reactive to proactive crisis management\. It is demonstrated that scenario analysis and stress testing should be considered essential elements of strategic analysis under conditions of uncertainty, as they make it possible to assess the potential consequences of changes in key parameters of the external and internal environment\. Particular attention is paid to the digitalization of strategic analysis\. The study substantiates that the use of business intelligence, predictive analytics, automated KPI monitoring, time\-series analysis, anomaly detection algorithms, and machine learning tools creates the prerequisites for improving the timeliness and quality of analytical support for strategic decision\-making\. Digital tools make it possible to reduce the time lag between the emergence of a crisis signal and managerial response, while also enabling the simultaneous analysis of large volumes of financial, marketing, production, human\-resource, and external information\. Recent studies also confirm the importance of business analytics for strategic planning and decision\-making, as well as the role of digital capabilities in building enterprise resilience\. Based on the research findings, an integrated sequence of strategic analysis of enterprise activities under crisis conditions is proposed\. It comprises ten interrelated stages: external environment monitoring; early warning; internal diagnostics; strategic positioning assessment; risk analysis; scenario modelling; stress testing; strategic resilience assessment; development of strategic alternatives; and monitoring of implementation with subsequent adjustment of strategic decisions\. The application of this model ensures comprehensive strategic diagnostics, improves the quality of management information, and creates conditions for the timely adaptation of an enterprise to crisis\-related changes\.
