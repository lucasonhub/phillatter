# Arbejdslivet i utrygge tider

**Autores:** Thomas Bredgaard; Stine Rasmussen; Laust Høgedahl; Mads Peter Klindt; Anders Albin Esbjerg

**Data:** 2026-10-09 · **Tipo:** book

## Resumo

Arbejdsliv i utrygge tider \(Working Life in Times of Insecurity\) is the second publication in the Labour Market Security Barometer series and presents the results of the 2025 Labour Market Security Barometer\. Based on a representative survey of the Danish workforce, the report examines experiences of security in working life and explores a range of current issues in the Danish labour market, including young people&\#x27;s working lives, working time, atypical employment, foreign labour, disability, artificial intelligence, job mobility, and the Danish flexicurity model\.
