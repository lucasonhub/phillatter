# Data, Artificial Intelligence and Content as Levers of Sales Growth in Digital Marketing

**Autores:** Tais Fraile; Thiago Anderson Lira Clemente

**Data:** 2026-10-18 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Abstract: The availability of customer data, the advance of artificial intelligence \(AI\) and the growing importance of digital content and influencers have changed the tools that firms can use to increase sales\. This narrative review examines how these three levers—data and analytics, AI\-enabled personalization, and content and influencer marketing—contribute to sales growth, and under which conditions they generate results\. Peer\-reviewed studies and reference works published up to 2023 were analyzed qualitatively\. The literature indicates that marketing analytics improves decisions on targeting, pricing and resource allocation; that AI supports personalization at scale, from mechanical automation to analytical and feeling\-oriented applications; and that content and influencer marketing build engagement, trust and purchase intention when they are relevant and credible\. Generative AI, which gained prominence in 2023, expands content production and customer interaction capabilities but also raises concerns regarding accuracy, transparency and ethics\. The review concludes that these levers produce the greatest effect when combined in an integrated system in which data informs content and personalization, and results are measured and fed back into decisions\. It also emphasizes that privacy, trust and human judgment remain essential conditions for sustainable sales growth\.
