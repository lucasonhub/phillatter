# Lehotay, Michael: Informational Experiential Realism: Experience as a Continuing Physical Operation

**Autores:** 

**Data de descoberta:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** unknown

## Descrição do feed

Informational Experiential Realism \(IER\) proposes that a token physical operation under coherent intrinsic constraint is numerically identical with one total experience and its experiential subject\. The subject is the continuing operation through changes in its contents and material participants, rather than an enduring owner receiving separately produced experiences\. This paper presents the framework&\#x27;s core: physically admissible continuation, differentiated unity, ownership, constitutive membership, and numerical persistence\. Synchronic unity requires non\-factorizable successor support across every independently declared physically meaningful partition\. Ownership concerns where that joint continuation problem is internally borne; membership requires contribution, bearing, joint resolution, later uptake, and common physical typing\. Persistence requires continuous closure, operational successor production, effective consequence inheritance, operation\-mediated change, and unique continuation in both directions\. Failure of any requirement ends the token; neither resemblance nor reconstruction restores it\. Strawson supplies the direct process\-subject identity precedent, Papineau makes system\-level experience available without experiential constituents, and cybernetics supplies resources for possibility, constraint, and continuing organization\. IER develops these resources into an explicit physicalist framework without inferring experience from feedback, self\-maintenance, or closure alone\. Its identity proposal remains abductive\. The account allows turnover, changing qualitative organization, and compatible multiscale operations, while rejecting numerical identity by branching or fusion\. It avoids experiential constituent inheritance rather than solving combination on panpsychist terms\. Operational individuation, worked physical instantiation, empirical attribution, particular qualitative correspondence, and comparison with one\-event rivals remain unresolved\. The contribution is the framework&\#x27;s formulation, organization, arguments, and implications; no historical first priority or empirical demonstration is claimed\.
