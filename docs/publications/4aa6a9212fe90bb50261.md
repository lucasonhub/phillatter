# Pembinaan dan pengesahan instrumen untuk penilaian keberkesanan program dan aktiviti ilmiah jabatan dalam konteks pendidikan TVET

**Autores:** Geetha Subramaniam; Siti Norzailina Md Som

**Data:** 2026-10-11 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

In the context of polytechnic education and academic management, particularly within the Department of Mathematics, Science and Computer \(JMSK\), various academic programmes and activities are organised to enhance students’ knowledge, skills, competencies and learning experiences\. To ensure that these programmes and activities achieve their intended objectives, participant feedback must be collected systematically using a valid and well\-designed questionnaire\. Therefore, this study aimed to develop a questionnaire and establish its content validity for evaluating the effectiveness of JMSK’s academic programmes and activities\. The instrument’s content validity was evaluated by eight experts based on the relevance of each item\. The Content Validity Index \(CVI\) was employed to assess the content relevance of the developed items\. The results revealed a Scale\-Level Content Validity Index based on Average \(S\-CVI/Ave\) of 0\.97, indicating a high level of content validity\. Furthermore, the Scale\-Level Content Validity Index based on Universal Agreement \(S\-CVI/UA\) was 0\.75, indicating that 75% of the items achieved universal agreement among the eight experts\. These findings demonstrate that the developed instrument possesses strong content validity and has the potential to systematically collect participant feedback on the perceived effectiveness of JMSK’s academic programmes and activities\. The instrument may consequently support programme improvement, departmental activity planning and the enhancement of educational quality in Technical and Vocational Education and Training \(TVET\)\. Dalam konteks pendidikan dan pengurusan akademik di politeknik, khususnya di Jabatan Matematik, Sains dan Komputer \(JMSK\), pelbagai program dan aktiviti ilmiah dilaksanakan bagi menyokong pembangunan pengetahuan, kemahiran, kompetensi serta pengalaman pembelajaran pelajar\. Bagi memastikan program dan aktiviti tersebut mencapai objektif yang ditetapkan, maklum balas peserta perlu diperoleh secara sistematik melalui instrumen soal selidik yang sah dan berkualiti\. Oleh itu, kajian ini bertujuan untuk membina dan mengesahkan kandungan instrumen soal selidik bagi menilai keberkesanan program dan aktiviti ilmiah JMSK\. Kesahan kandungan instrumen dinilai oleh lapan orang pakar berdasarkan tahap kerelevanan setiap item\. Indeks Kesahan Kandungan \(Content Validity Index, CVI\) digunakan untuk menentukan tahap kesesuaian dan kerelevanan item yang dibangunkan\. Hasil penilaian menunjukkan bahawa nilai purata Indeks Kesahan Kandungan peringkat skala \(Scale\-Level Content Validity Index based on Average, S\-CVI/Ave\) bagi keseluruhan instrumen ialah 0\.97, yang menunjukkan tahap kesahan kandungan yang tinggi\. Selain itu, nilai Indeks Kesahan Kandungan peringkat skala berdasarkan persetujuan sejagat \(Scale\-Level Content Validity Index based on Universal Agreement, S\-CVI/UA\) ialah 0\.75, yang menunjukkan bahawa 75% daripada keseluruhan item mencapai persetujuan sejagat dalam kalangan lapan orang pakar\. Dapatan ini menunjukkan bahawa instrumen yang dibangunkan mempunyai kesahan kandungan yang baik dan berpotensi digunakan untuk mendapatkan maklum balas peserta secara sistematik berkaitan keberkesanan program dan aktiviti ilmiah JMSK\. Instrumen ini seterusnya berpotensi menyokong usaha penambahbaikan program, perancangan aktiviti jabatan serta peningkatan kualiti pelaksanaan Pendidikan dan Latihan Teknikal dan Vokasional \(Technical and Vocational Education and Training, TVET\)\.
