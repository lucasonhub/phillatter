# Link Both Views: Cross\-Image Routing Attention for Dual\-Endpoint V2I Beam\-Pair Power Prediction

**Autores:** Shiliang Lu; Lu Bai; Ziwei Huang; Xiang Cheng

**Data:** 2026-10-13 · **Tipo:** preprint

## Resumo

For sensing\-aided communications, single\-endpoint sensing offers only a partial view of propagation, motivating link\-centric sensing across both endpoints\. We propose cross\-image routing attention \(CIRA\) for sparse association and mutual enhancement of propagation\-relevant features from dual\-endpoint RGB images\. Building on CIRA, CiFormer predicts millimeter\-wave \(mmWave\) beam\-pair power matrices for vehicle\-to\-infrastructure \(V2I\) communications\. Experiments show that CiFormer reduces mean absolute error and improves top\-1 beam\-pair selection accuracy by 3\.15 percentage points over the strongest baseline\. Visualizations and ablation studies support dual\-endpoint sensing and selective cross\-view interaction in capturing propagation\-relevant dependencies, suggesting broader applications in wireless tasks beyond beam prediction\.
