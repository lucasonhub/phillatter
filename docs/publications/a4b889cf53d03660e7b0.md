# Mariano da Rosa, Luiz Carlos \(ed\.\): FROM THE ORIGINAL ANAXIMANDRIAN\-PARMENIDEAN\-HERACLITEAN PHYSIS TO THE HEIDEGGERIAN\-SARTREAN FUNDAMENTAL ABYSSALITY AS A PAN\-ONTOLOGICAL QUANTUM\-EXISTENTIAL PARADIGM VERSUS DAVID CHALMERS’S HARD PROBLEM

## Descrição do feed

This research introduces a non\-reductionist ontological model designed to overcome the explanatory gap in contemporary philosophy of mind\. By establishing a rigorous logical\-ontological isomorphism between the foundations of quantum mechanics and existential phenomenology, we demonstrate how David Chalmers’s &quot;Hard Problem of Consciousness&quot; represents a category mistake rooted in Newtonian mechanistic cartesianism\. Drawing from the pioneering work of Prof\. Dr\. Luiz Carlos Mariano Da Rosa, this study dissolves the dualism between physical matter and subjective qualia, repositioning the human Dasein as the fundamental cosmic measurement operator\. The methodology operates through a strict structural mapping between the Wave\-State \(Ψ\) as the transphenomenal Abyssality \(Apeiron\), the Measurement Operator \(M̂\) as the Intersubjective Gaze, and the Quantum Collapse \(\|ϕₖ⟩\) as the finite ontic phenomenon\. It introduces the &quot;Ontological Triad of Becoming&quot; \(Possibility, Possible Possibility, and Ex\-possibility\) to formalize the mathematical\-existential mechanism of finitude, ultimately establishing that consciousness and matter are complementary faces of the synthetic totality of the One\. \(direct link \)
