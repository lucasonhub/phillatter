# Learnable coarsening for two\-phase flow in porous media

**Autores:** Viacheslav Naumov; Nikolay B\. Yavich; Alexander A\. Ryabov; Sayan Ranu; N\. M\. Anoop Krishnan; Evgeny Vladimirovich Burnaev; Vladimir Vanovskiy

**Data:** 2026-10-13 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Abstract Hydrodynamic simulations of multi\-phase flow in porous media are essential for oil production forecasting but require significant computational resources\. Traditional approaches lead to high memory and time demands, restricting multi\- scenario analysis\. We propose a learnable coarsening algorithm based on graph neural networks \(GNNs\) with node aggregation for 2D two\-phase flow modelling\. The coarsened grid adapts to the reservoir geometry, its properties, and production data\. Our algorithm is integrated with a graph\-based finite volume method \(FVM\) solver, differentiable Voronoi tessellation, and a fully implicit upwind scheme\. The unstructured computational grid is modeled as a graph with cell centers, adjacency edges, and rich a set of node features: coordinates, permeability, masks, distances to wells, permeability gradients, and wells influence potential\. GNN produces soft assignments for probabilistic clustering, preserving wells and sensors\. Training employs an FVM solver with physics\-informed loss matching saturation and production rate time series at wells, promoting grid uniformity\. Extending our prior works, our method enables coarsening by 10 times with relative error below 1%, accurately reproducing saturation fields, oil production, and water breakthrough on synthetic and realistic permeability fields
