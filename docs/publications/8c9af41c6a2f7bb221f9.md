# Dopamine

**Autores:** Kaushik Mitra

**Data:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Dopamine is a chemical messenger \(neurotransmitter\) in the brain and a circulating hormone in the blood\.While popular culture commonly describes it as the &quot;feel\-good&quot; chemical, neuroscientists categorize it differently\.Rather than producing the sensation of pleasure, dopamine generates an overwhelming urge to seek out things that provide pleasure or assure survival\.
