# KONSULTASI KEAGAMAAN DI ERA AI: PERBANDINGAN RESPONS CHATGPT DAN GEMINI TERHADAP KASUS\-KASUS MODERASI BERAGAMA

**Autores:** Nazwa Niswatusshofiyah; Raudlatul Intan Nurul Balqies; Iksan Kamil Sahri; Muhammad Bachrul Ulum

**Data:** 2026-10-14 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

The shift of religious consultation from traditional authorities toward artificial intelligence \(AI\) chatbots such as ChatGPT and Gemini raises new challenges for Islamic Religious Education, particularly regarding the accuracy, consistency, and level of moderation in the responses generated\. This study aims to compare the capabilities of ChatGPT and Gemini in providing religious consultation based on four religious moderation indicators established by the Ministry of Religious Affairs, namely tolerance, national commitment, anti violence, and acceptance of local traditions, as well as to examine the sensitivity of responses to framing and their consistency across repeated prompts\. The study employs the Comparative AI Religious Consultation Audit \(CARCA\) method with a mixed\-method approach combining quantitative content analysis and qualitative comparative analysis\. The results show a mean score of 92\.07 for ChatGPT and 90\.90 for Gemini, with high and relatively comparable performance on the three normative\-social criteria, but markedly lower performance on religious accuracy \(around 71% for both models\)\. ChatGPT outperformed Gemini in context sensitivity, while provocative framing produced opposite effects across models, raising ChatGPT&\#x27;s score slightly while lowering Gemini&\#x27;s score\. The study concludes that both AI models are able to translate some principles of religious moderation into moderate practical advice, but cannot yet be fully relied upon as a source of religious authority, given their weaknesses in accuracy and religious textual grounding\. These findings underscore the importance of positioning AI as a companion to, rather than a replacement for, traditional religious authority\.
