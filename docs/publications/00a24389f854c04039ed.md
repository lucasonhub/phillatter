# High\-speed 850 and 1060 nm VCSELs with ring\-shaped Zn\-diffusion apertures for energy\-efficient single\-mode performance

**Autores:** Po\-Jui Lai; Yu‐Hsiang Sung; Jie\-Yau Ho; Zhang\-Wei Wang; Zhe\-Quan Liang; Lu Tien\-chang; Jinwei Shi

**Data:** 2026-10-13 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Abstract High\-speed vertical\-cavity surface\-emitting lasers \(VCSELs\) and their arrays are essential for enhancing bandwidth density while minimizing energy consumption per bit in near\-packaged or co\-packaged optics \(NPO or CPO\) modules for modern AI networks\. In this paper, we demonstrate that incorporating optimized Zn\-diffusion ring\-shaped apertures with 850 and 1064 nm VCSEL cavities for single\-mode \(SM\) output significantly improves slope and modulation efficiencies while preserving low RIN\. Compared with multi\-mode reference devices without Zn\-diffusion apertures, these VCSELs achieve clear eye\-opening at much lower pre\-bias currents and smaller peak\-to\-peak RF driving\-voltages \(Vpp\)\. For the 850 nm VCSEL, clear eye\-opening can be achieved at 100 Gbit/s with the PAM\-4 modulation format under a low bias current of 4 mA, a small EDR \(100 fJ/bit\), and a record low Vpp as 0\.35 V\. The 1060 nm VCSEL exhibits superior high\-temperature performance compared to the SM reference device using the conventional circular Zn\-diffusion aperture\. It obtains error\-free 32 Gbit/sec on\-off\-keying \(OOK\) transmission at 125 ℃ with a Vpp as low as 0\.28 V, without requiring power\-consuming retiming DSP ICs\.
