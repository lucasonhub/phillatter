# A Multidimensional Assessment of Coaching Pedagogy in Malaysian Football: Implications for Coach Education and Athlete Development

**Autores:** Habeeb Yonis Alani; Shahrulfadly Rustam; Mohammad Firdaus Mohd Israj; Mohar Kassim; Ahmad Bisyri Husin Musawi Maliki

**Data:** 2026-10-11 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Effective coaching is central to athlete development, balancing performance outcomes with the long\-term welfare of players\. Although global literature emphasizes the importance of pedagogy, reflective practice, and interdisciplinary support, empirical evidence regarding coaches&\#x27; perceptions in developing football nations like Malaysia remains scarce\. This study addresses this gap by analyzing Malaysian football coaches&\#x27; perceptions of coach education effectiveness across four key domains: coaching modalities, infrastructure and resources, reflective practices, and external expertise\. A cross\-sectional quantitative approach was utilized, and 40 coaches, who were certified and held professional as well as academy coaching licenses, were selected through purposive sampling\. A structured questionnaire, which was validated by experts, was used to collect data\. A 5\-point Likert scale was used in the questionnaire\. Cronbach&\#x27;s alpha assessed the reliability and internal consistency, and coaches&\#x27; responses were summarized using descriptive statistics\. A one\-sample t\-test and a Friedman non\-parametric test were employed to assess whether the domain scores surpassed the neutral benchmark, as well as to assess the domain construct and measure differences between items\. Of the items measured, coaches&\#x27; response means ranged from 3\.35 to 4\.18 \(SD = 0\.50 to 0\.81\), suggesting that coaches generally perceived the pedagogical practices as effective to a great extent\. The reflective practices domain had some variability, as the implementation of reflective practices was inconsistent in some coaching contexts\. The measure had a high level of internal consistency \(α = 0\.958\), indicating high reliability\. The Friedman test highlighted significant differences among items \(χ2 = 8\.05, p &lt; 0\.001\)\. This shows that coaches made distinctions among pedagogical dimensions, pinpointing bottom\-level NFDP\-focused practice engagement\. Malaysian football coaches demonstrate bottom\-level engagement with integrative coaching pedagogy and pedagogical breakdowns within reflective, infrastructural, and interdisciplinary dimensions\. The proof\-of\-concept tool exhibits good psychometric and evaluative capacity\. The findings support the NFDP&\#x27;s grassroots and youth development pathways rationale, especially the reflective practitioner role with curriculum advocacy and coaching pathway reconstruction\. NFDP integrative pedagogy is likely to promote the athletes&\#x27; learning and retention for sustained improvement of the nation&\#x27;s football\.
