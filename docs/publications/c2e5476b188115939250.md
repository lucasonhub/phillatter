# From Bare to Informed Looking: An Art\-Historical Proposition on Mindful Art Engagement, Emotion Regulation, and Cognitive Enrichment in the Age of AI

**Autores:** Ewelina Chwiejda

**Data:** 2026-10-15 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

The rapid diffusion of artificial intelligence has unsettled more than the labour market\. It has given rise to two related anxieties: an existential unease about human meaning, agency, and uniqueness—“AI anxiety”—and a quieter abdication of critical effort to machines, which recent works call “cognitive surrender\.” The remedies most often proposed for these conditions, among them psychoeducation, regulation, engineering ethics, and therapy, leave a conspicuous gap, since none of them draws on aesthetic experience or on the making of meaning\. This article, written from an engaged, applied art\-history position, proceeds as a conceptual analysis rather than an empirical study\. It defines “mindful art engagement” as a receptive, two\-phase practice that moves from bare to informed looking, an open and contemplative looking followed by art\-historically informed interpretation, sets out its boundaries, distinguishes the three constructs on which it bears \(felt meaningfulness, art\-historical understanding, and cognitive enrichment\), and maps the existing evidence onto its components\. On this basis, it advances a hypothesis that such a practice could contribute to emotion regulation and to cognitive enrichment in the age of AI, and that meaning is the point at which the two pathways meet\.
