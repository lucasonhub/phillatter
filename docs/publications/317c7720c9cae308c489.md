# Dyer, Miroslav: Refutation of Chalmer&\#x27;s view of meaning and the architecture of consciousness

**Autores:** 

**Data de descoberta:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** unknown

## Descrição do feed

We begin with Saul Kripke’s provable assertion that mental states cannot be cleanly reduced to, or strictly identified with, purely physical brain states\. We then dismantle David Chalmers&\#x27; belief in the ‘hard’ problem, revealing it to be a category error driven by a failure to recognise subjectivity in its semantic guise—specifically, as a system of recognising type\-hierarchical feature combinations that represent objective reality through subjective semantic atoms, termed &\#x27;semes
