# Do Financing and Investment\- Based Courses Influence the Gen\-Z Investment?

**Autores:** Eneng Nur Hasanah; Sudarso Kaderi Wiryono; Deddy Priatmodjo Koesrindartoto

**Data:** 2026-10-15 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

The number of young investors in Indonesia has grown significantly in recent years, marking a notable shift in the country’s investment landscape\. Although the total assets of younger investors are smaller compared to those of older investors, their increasing participation has positively influenced the development of the investment sector and improved financial literacy among younger generations\. Based on this phenomenon, the purpose of this study is to examine the attitudes of Generation Z as the young investors towards business and management programs, the use of investment applications, and robo\-advisor services\. The research focuses on two distinct groups: students who have had prior exposure to finance and investment\-related courses, and those who have not had such courses\. The method used was structured focus group discussions to explore students’ knowledge and experiences, then supplemented with a survey questionnaire to validate findings and provide deeper insights\. The research concluded that there were no significant differences between the two cohorts regarding their interest in understanding and the importance of investments\. Some participants had already started investing without formal financial education, while others explored unconventional investments such as collectible hobby items\. Moreover, participants generally trusted risk assessments provided by robo\-advisors, but many of them were hesitant to follow the recommendations\. They expressed a greater confidence in their ability to make independent investment decisions\.
