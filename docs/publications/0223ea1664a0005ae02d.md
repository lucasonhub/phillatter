# Orthogonal Reversible Chemistries Affording Multistate Photoswitching in Polymers for Recyclable Intelligent Material Systems

**Autores:** Dachuan Zhang; Chenrui Yuan; Jingning Cao; Shuofeng Liang; Zhulu Xie; Wenyong Jiang; Jie Yan; Si Wu

**Data:** 2026-10-13 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

The wide applications of intelligent material systems in robotics, flexible devices, AI\-driven techniques, and other high\-tech areas call for the development of recyclable intelligent material systems toward a sustainable future\. However, intelligent material systems typically comprise multiple components with heterogeneous structures that usually interfere with or cross\-contaminate each other during operation or recycling\. Here, we design recyclable intelligent material systems based on orthogonal reversible chemistries, specifically photoisomerization of azobenzene and metal\-ligand coordination of ruthenium complexes\. We create a material, P1\-Ru1, by crosslinking an azobenzene\-containing polymer with a ruthenium complex\. P1\-Ru1 switches among multiple states that serve as structural elements, shape\-changing modules, adhesives, and other components in intelligent material systems such as soft machines\. Crucially, multicomponent, heterogeneous intelligent material systems constructed using different states of P1\-Ru1 can be recycled via a one\-pot, cross\-contamination\-free process because all components share the same chemical origin\. This work demonstrates that orthogonal reversible chemistries make intelligent responses and recyclability compatible in complex systems\. Orthogonal reversible chemistries offer a new strategy for designing recyclable intelligent material systems with multiple components and heterogeneous structures for sustainable high\-tech applications\.
