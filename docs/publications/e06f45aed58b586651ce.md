# Geometrie

**Autores:** Eleonora Di Nezza; Hans‐Joachim Hein; Aaron Naber; André Neves

**Data:** 2026-10-12 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

The workshop Geometrie was well attended with 47 in\-person and two virtual participants whose interests covered a considerable part of current research in Differential Geometry\. We had talks in such diverse subjects as geometric evolution equations, Kähler geometry, minimal surfaces, RCD spaces and positive scalar curvature metrics\.
