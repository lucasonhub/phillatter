# O discuție despre parlamente, proceduri și Inteligența Artificială

**Autores:** Marius Văcărelu

**Data:** 2026-10-13 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

An honest discussion about the future of democracy and the rule of law in the new world that Artificial Intelligence technology is beginning to shape should start with parliaments, with their role in a society that does not fully know constitutional texts, but desires better governance and a better balance between citizens and state powers\. As a long\-standing legal institution, with complex roles and different ways of selecting its members throughout history, parliament enjoys a presumption of representativeness that should be augmented by the capabilities of new electronic technologies\. However, not every change brought by new forms of data analysis and processing will prove useful, as one of their effects is a cognitive decline in the minds of those who are meant to legislate\. The text we propose is an introduction toward an analysis that should be carried out with great depth by parties and citizens, because the relationship between them will inevitably be reshaped by contemporary technological developments, and states cannot afford an imbalance in society\. It therefore remains to be discussed where the limits of applying new technologies in the sphere of national representations might lie, because constitutions will soon have to adapt to the unique transformations that have emerged and are growing before our eyes\.
