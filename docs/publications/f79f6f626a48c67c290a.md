# Computational design and translational development of TCR\-mimic scFv antibodies for peptide\-HLA neoantigen targeting

**Autores:** Sarfaraz K\. Niazi; Matthias Magoola; Zamara Mariam

**Data:** 2026-10-12 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Single\-chain variable fragments \(scFvs\) penetrate tissue more effectively than full antibodies and are simpler to produce, but the mutant proteins that define a tumor are predominantly intracellular and therefore inaccessible to conventional antibodies\. What is accessible is the mutation\-derived peptide displayed on cell\-surface HLA\. This review concerns TCR\-mimic scFvs directed at those peptide\-HLA complexes\. It separates three questions that are frequently conflated: which mutations are predicted to be presented, which presented complexes are genuinely targetable given surface density and cross\-reactivity risk, and how immunogenic the resulting therapeutic molecule will itself be\. We review computational strategies spanning target identification, AI\-driven structure prediction, de novo design, affinity and specificity engineering, half\-life extension and deimmunization, and distinguish throughout between the scFv as a recognition module and the effector architecture, such as a CD3 bispecific, a chimeric antigen receptor binding domain or an Fc\-containing format, in which it must ultimately be deployed\.
