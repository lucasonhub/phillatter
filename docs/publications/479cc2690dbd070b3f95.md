# EgoTokens: Egocentric 3D human reconstruction and language\-guided shape editing via quantized position tokens in vision\-language models

**Autores:** Gulraiz Khan; Maneeha Rani; Waqas Ahmed; Kevin Pimbblet; Kenneth Y\. Wertheim

**Data:** 2026-10-13 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

3D human body reconstruction from egocentric images is a challenging task because of environmental and self\-occlusion\. Downward viewpoints and scale ambiguity can also hinder precise 3D estimation\. Current methods mainly rely on specialized regression networks to reconstruct 3D meshes that often lack structural guidance from high\-dimensional SMPL\-X parameters\. In the proposed work, we introduce EgoTokens, a framework that estimate egocentric SMPL\-X parameters as a discrete sequence\-generation task\. Our end\-to\-end system employs vision\-language models \(VLMs\) to predict SMPL\-X body parameters directly from a single image\. Each of the 122 SMPL\-X parameters is quantized into 1000 bins and represented as an extended vocabulary for training the VLM\. To reflect the extended vocabulary in the network, the corresponding embedding and output\-projection layers are redefined and trained with multiple learning rates to accelerate convergence of new quantized tokens\. The system is trained with a distance\-aware objective that intuitively combines the original cross\-entropy with an L2 regression loss to capture actual numerical closeness between predicted bins\. Standard VLMs with cross\-entropy lack the capability to capture this numerical closeness, and they treat close numbers the same as far numbers\. A language\-instructed semantic shape editing pipeline is also constructed, extending the EgoBody dataset from 150,000 \(visible\) to 1\.3 million instruction\-image\-parameter samples\. This language\-instructed reconstruction allows editing without extra region segmentation\. On the EgoBody dataset, the proposed system achieves a PA\-MPJPE of 29\.49 mm and PA\-MPVPE of 28\.20 mm for fine\-tuning InternVL2\-8B, and a PA\-MPJPE of 33\.43 mm for Qwen2\.5\-VL\-7B\. The results show that the proposed system outperforms the specialized regression and transformer\-based baselines for both InternVL2\-8B and Qwen2\.5\-VL\-7B\. Zero\-shot evaluation on the EHF dataset achieved a PA\-MPVPE of 33\.85 mm, showing the trained model’s generalization from egocentric to third\-person view with complex poses\. The results show that tuned VLMs with distance awareness is a promising direction for 3D human reconstruction and shape control within a single framework\.
