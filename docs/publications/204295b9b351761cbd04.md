# AUTOMATIC GENERATION OF DIDACTIC CARDS WITH ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE IN HIGHER EDUCATION: A METHODOLOGICAL MODEL AND SOFTWARE TOOL FOR ENHANCING CLASSROOM MOTIVATION

**Autores:** Masharipov Madiyor; Gulchehra Abdusamatova

**Data:** 2026-10-13 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

This article substantiates a methodological model, and a software tool built on it, for the automatic generation of didactic cards with artificial intelligence \(large language models\) in order to increase learner motivation in higher education classes\. The study aims to develop a solution that, from a teacher&\#x27;s input on the subject, level of education, group and topic, quickly produces a set of questions of gradually increasing difficulty and allows these questions to be used as a game on an interactive whiteboard\. A design\-based approach was applied, combining the revised Bloom&\#x27;s taxonomy, formative assessment and gamification principles\. As a result, a mechanism for distributing cards across four difficulty levels, a structured prompt for question generation, and an algorithm for hiding questions and answers behind numbered cards and revealing them only under the teacher&\#x27;s control were developed\. The model was implemented as a web application that runs on computers and mobile devices\. The article discusses the didactic potential and limitations of the model and outlines a protocol for its empirical validation in pedagogical practice\. The findings may be useful for university teachers, instructional designers and researchers in educational technology\.
