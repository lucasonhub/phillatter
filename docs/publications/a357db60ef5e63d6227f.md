# Evaluating the Effectiveness of an AI\-Assisted Personalized Learning Pathway in Mobile\-Supported Online Physical Education

**Autores:** Xia Wan; Libing Fu

**Data:** 2026-10-15 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

This study evaluates the effectiveness of an AI\-assisted personalized learning pathway in mobile\-supported online physical education\. To overcome issues of homogeneous content and delayed feedback, a system integrating cognitive knowledge, physical fitness, and motor skills was developed\. Reinforcement learning dynamically adapted learning pathways, while edge\-based computer vision enabled real\-time feedback on low\-end mobile devices\. A randomized controlled trial with 120 students over eight weeks compared the proposed system to conventional video\-based instruction\. Results showed low inference latency \(34\.8 ms\) and a 61\.2% reduction in movement variability\. NASA\-TLX scores revealed lower mental demand and frustration, with high learner engagement\. The findings highlight the effectiveness of AI\-assisted personalization and mobile feedback in improving learning outcomes and experiences in online physical education\.
