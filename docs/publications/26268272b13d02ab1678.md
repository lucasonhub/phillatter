# Preliminary clinical changes during use of a personalized video\-based mobile intervention for community\-dwelling individuals with severe mental illness: An exploratory randomized crossover trial

**Autores:** Yong Hyuk Cho; Anna You; Sooin Jeong; Ga Eun Kim; Sun Hwa Hong; Sang Joon Son; Bumhee Park; Joung Hwan Back; Chang Hyung Hong

**Data:** 2026-10-09 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Severe mental illness \(SMI\) often limits participation in traditional therapy due to motivational and cognitive deficits\. We examined short\-term clinical changes during use of a personalized video\-based mobile app \( MARO \)—providing psychoeducation, emotion regulation, cognitive restructuring, and behavioral activation—in community\-dwelling individuals with SMI and explored whether the timing of minimal human coaching was associated with these outcomes\. This 8\-week exploratory randomized crossover trial compared an Immediate\-Coaching group \(app \+ coaching → app only\) and a Delayed\-Coaching group \(app only → app \+ coaching\); both groups used MARO throughout the study\. Among 40 randomized participants, 27 completed all assessments\. Depression \(Patient Health Questionnaire\-9\), anxiety \(Generalized Anxiety Disorder\-7\), and positive automatic thoughts \(Automatic Thoughts Questionnaire–Positive\) were assessed at baseline and after 4 and 8 weeks\. Both groups showed decreases in PHQ\-9 and GAD\-7 scores over the 8\-week intervention period, while ATQ\-P scores increased\. Overall PHQ\-9 scores were lower and ATQ\-P scores were higher in the Immediate\-Coaching group than in the Delayed\-Coaching group\. These preliminary findings support further evaluation in larger, adequately powered controlled studies of personalized video\-based mobile interventions as a potentially scalable digital–human approach to community\-based care for individuals with severe mental illness\. Trial Registration: CRIS, KCT0011492\.
