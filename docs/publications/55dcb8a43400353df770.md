# THE INFLUENCE OF SELENIUM ON OSTEOGENESIS: MORPHOLOGICAL FEATURES

**Autores:** P\. Ye\. Kovalchuk; S\. V\. Tulyulyuk

**Data:** 2026-10-14 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Objective: to investigate the effect of selenium deficiency on physiological and reparative osteogenesis\. To determine the features of reparative osteogenesis in experimental animals with metadiaphyseal defects of the femur under conditions of selenium deficiency\. Material and methods: the study was conducted on 63 randomly selected white male rats of three months of age\. The features of reparative osteogenesis were studied on a single type of traumatic injury model constructed in the proximal metadiaphysis of the femur regardless of the experimental conditions, which represented a perforated defect in the frontal region\. Results: the presented data indicate the negative impact of selenium deficiency on reparative and physiological osteogenesis, which is manifested by the inhibition of these processes and is accompanied by disorders of the formation of bone regenerator, deterioration of the structural and functional state of bone tissue, the development of degenerative\-necrotic changes in bone tissue and epiphyseal cartilage\.
