# Multicentre validation of BRCAura, an AI\-based histology model predicting germline BRCA1/2 mutations in HR\+/HER2− breast cancer

**Autores:** Oussama Tchita; Elin Samuelsson; Damien Jacobs; Axel Boulet; Katharina von Loga; Estelle Hocquet; Ross J\. Hill; T\. Di Maio; Greg Rossi; Jack Rawson; Paraic A\. Kenny; Thomas Holdbrook; Javier Hernández‐Losa; Sheeba Irshad; Ferroudja Daidj; Andrew Whittum; Cécile Badoual; Séphora Campoy; Christine Lasset; Catherine Chassagne‐Clément; Fabrice André; Ingrid Garberis; Olivier Caron; Magali Lacroix‐Triki

**Data:** 2026-10-12 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Germline BRCA1/2 alterations \(gBRCAm\) are actionable drivers of breast cancer management, yet testing strategies based on clinical and family\-history criteria miss many carriers\. We developed BRCAura, a deep learning model that predicts gBRCAm status directly from routine histology whole\-slide images\. The model was externally validated in a retrospective multicentre cohort of 1025 patients with early\-stage hormone receptor\-positive \(HR\+\), Human epidermal growth factor receptor 2\-negative \(HER2−\) breast cancer across six centres\. BRCAura achieved area under the curve values of 0\.81 for resections and 0\.82 for biopsies, maintaining &gt;93% sensitivity at a specificity of approximately 40%, consistent with its intended high\-sensitivity rule\-out design, while ruling out approximately one third of wild\-type cases\. Performance was robust across staining protocols and scanners and significantly exceeded established clinical and family\-history models\. Phenotype analysis revealed enrichment of immune infiltration and increased tumour cellularity in mutation\-associated regions\. These findings suggest BRCAura could help streamline germline testing pathways by optimising sequencing allocation and enabling efficient histology\-based pre\-screening\.
