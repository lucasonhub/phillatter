# Analysis of Maiquer&\#x27;s Debt Repayment Ability Under the New Economic Situation

**Autores:** Xujuan Zhao; Jizhong Wu\*

**Data:** 2026-10-09 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

This article mainly provides an example of Maiqu&\#x27;er Group Co\., Ltd\. to discuss the current situation and improvement strategies of Maiqu&\#x27;er&\#x27;s debt paying ability in the context of the new economy\. Of particular note is that debt paying ability is an important measure for evaluating the normal operation of a company\. So this article attempts to analyze the financial situation of Maiqu&\#x27;er and concludes that Maiqu&\#x27;er&\#x27;s debt paying ability is showing signs of fatigue, and the company&\#x27;s situation is very dangerous\. This paper mainly proposes two solutions: firstly, with the goal of improving asset operation efficiency, through structural adjustment and management innovation, to maximize the utilization efficiency of distributable funds; Secondly, formulate a reasonable debt repayment plan, clarify goals, and optimize fund allocation; These measures will help enhance Maiqu&\#x27;er&\#x27;s debt paying ability, minimize potential risks, and safeguard the stability and long\-term development of the enterprise\. This study provides practical reference for Maiqu&\#x27;er and other similar enterprises\.
