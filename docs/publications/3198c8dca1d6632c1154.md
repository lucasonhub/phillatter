## Descrição do feed

Artificial intelligence is frequently discussed in terms of productivity, employment displacement, dependency, and the changing distribution of human and machine labour\. Less attention has been given to a longer\-term possibility: that increasingly capable cognitive automation may alter not merely which tasks humans perform, but which cognitive capabilities remain broadly practised across a population\. \(direct link \)
