# Comparative study of thermoelectricity in cotton fabrics coated by immersion with Pyrograf® III carbon nanofibers\-based inks

**Autores:** Antonio J\. Paleo; Jose Abreu; Beate Krause; Sergio González\-Casal; Louis Thomas; Maria F\. Cerqueira; Enrique Muñoz; Petra Pötschke

**Data:** 2026-10-15 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Herein, the thermoelectric \(TE\) properties of cotton woven fabrics \(CWFs\) modified by dip coating in aqueous inks made from two Pyrograf® III carbon nanofibers \(CNFs\), PR 24 LHT XT and PR 25 PS XT, are compared\. Transmission Electron Microscopy \(TEM\) and Raman spectroscopy analyses of CNFs reveal similar morphologies and structures, although PR 24 LHT XT have smaller diameters and higher grade of crystallinity than PR 25 PS XT\. Despite these differences, CWFs made from inks with them are quite similar in terms of their electrical conductivity \( σ \), Seebeck coefficient \( S \), and thermal conductivity \( k \)\. Although, overall, textiles produced using PR 24 are slightly superior to those obtained using the PR 25 PS XT CNFs, achieving σ of ∼35 S m −1 and negative Seebeck coefficients around −6 μVK −1 \. These values correspond to a Power Factor \(PF\) of ∼1 × 10 −3 μW mK \-1 , and a figure of merit \(zT\) of ∼6 × 10 −6 at 30 °C\. Notably, between 30 °C and 100 °C, the S of the conductive textiles \(CTs\) is slightly higher, in absolute terms, than that of the corresponding CNFs\. In addition, in this same temperature range, the CTs exhibit negative temperature coefficient \(NTC\) behavior, which according to the 3D variable range hopping \(VRH\) model, can be interpreted as resulting from the thermal activation of charge carriers capable of crossing several potential wells, and a simultaneous increase in their S \. All of these results are discussed in order to clarify the role played by these two types of Pyrograf® III CNFs on the TE properties of their conductive textiles\.
