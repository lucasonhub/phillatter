# Posterior reversible encephalopathy syndrome \(PRES\) associated with imatinib: A case report and literature review of PRES associated with tyrosine kinase inhibitors

**Autores:** Prachi Mohapatra; Priyanka Vasist; Ajay Kumar Garg; Divyani Garg; Achal Kumar Srivastava; Shailesh  B Gaikwad; Ayush Agarwal

**Data:** 2026-10-14 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Early recognition of drug\-induced posterior reversible encephalopathy syndrome \(PRES\) is crucial, as timely discontinuation of the offending agent and supportive management typically result in rapid and complete recovery\. We have presented a case of imatinib induced PRES after 5 weeks of therapy for a gastrointestinal stromal tumour in a 38\-year\-old woman highlighting the importance of maintaining a high index of suspicion for new\-onset neurological symptoms in patients receiving tyrosine kinase inhibitors \(TKIs\), even those not primarily targeting vascular endothelial growth factor \(VEGF\) pathways\.
