# The Universal Information Matrix, Cosmic Frequency Loops, and the Natural Geometry of Quranic Texts

**Autores:** muhammed haşim gümrah

**Data:** 2026-10-17 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

The universe is analyzed as a dynamic operating system driven by real\-time frequency transformations rather than static matter\. This study presents a pioneering architectural hypothesis that bridges modern theoretical physics—including Quantum Mechanics, String Theory, and Gödel&\#x27;s Incompleteness Theorems—with Quranic semantic geometry\. Grounded in mathematical Set Theory, the Creator is defined as the absolute Superset, while the physical simulation exists as a limited Subset\. This paradigm redefines the materialization process, explores non\-material cosmic data loops, and proposes a fundamental framework to understand and resolve logical fractures \(hallucinations\) in artificial intelligence within local boundaries\.
