# Evaluation of Early Outcomes of Trapeziectomy With Suture\-Button Suspensionplasty and Palmaris Longus Zig\-Zag Augmentation for Thumb Carpometacarpal Osteoarthritis: A Prospective Case Series

**Autores:** Malkesh D\. Shah; Chirag S\. Kapoor; Prateek Randhawa; Devanshu Garg; Harsh S; Sankalp S Jain; Aadesh Gandhi

**Data:** 2026-10-13 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

AimThe study aimed to evaluate the early \(two\-month\) clinical, functional, and radiographic outcomes of trapeziectomy with suture\-button suspensionplasty \(SBS\) augmented by a palmaris longus \(PL\) zig\-zag ligament reconstruction in thumb carpometacarpal osteoarthritis\. ObjectivesThe objectives of the study were: to measure change in pain, grip strength, pinch strength, and thumb range of motion; to assess functional recovery using the QuickDASH score; and to determine maintenance of the trapezial space ratio at two months after surgery\.
