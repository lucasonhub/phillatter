# Spatially Decoupled Cascade Oxygen Reduction on Bi Single‐Atom and Cluster‐Hybrid Sites for Hydroxyl Radical Generation

**Autores:** Lixu Wu; Zhiming Wang; Chao Miao; Yanguang Yang; Nan Hu; Haiyu Wang; Yan Li; Yongxin Zhou; Guohua Zhao

**Data:** 2026-10-12 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

ABSTRACT The two‐electron oxygen reduction reaction \(2e − ORR\) for H 2 O 2 production and the subsequent in situ activation to ·OH are constrained by linear scaling relationships, making it difficult for a single active site to simultaneously optimize O 2 activation and H 2 O 2 conversion\. This work overcomes the trade‐off by spatially decoupling the cascade reaction onto single‐atom and cluster‐hybrid sites and employing photoelectrocatalysis \(PEC\)\. Through a ligand‐exchange and pyrolysis route derived from a metal–organic framework precursor, Bi single atoms and clusters are co‐anchored on B‐doped TiO 2 \. The hybrid interface thermodynamically optimizes the intermediate adsorption energies to ensure cascade selectivity, whereas PEC kinetically accelerates the H 2 O 2 ‐to‐·OH conversion by supplying photogenerated electrons\. Density functional theory and free‐energy diagrams indicate that H 2 O 2 forms at the single atoms and dissociates into ·OH at the clusters, with the relationship between the p‐band center and ·OH yield following the Sabatier principle\. The catalyst delivers a ·OH production rate of 250 µM h −1 at neutral pH and remains active over a broad pH range \(3–9\)\. Moreover, it enables broad‐spectrum removal of recalcitrant organic pollutants and retains stable performance over 50 h of operation in a flow‐cell reactor\. This work offers guidance for designing cascade catalysts at single‐atom and cluster‐hybrid interfaces\.
