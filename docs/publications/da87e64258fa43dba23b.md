# Multiplexed single\-molecule analysis of barcoded protein variants

**Autores:** Stefan H\. Mueller; David M\. Grimson; Holden Paz; Aidan C\. Fitch; Haibo B\. Yu; Marco Ribezzi‐Crivellari; Andrew D\. Griffiths; Antoine M\. van Oijen; Lisanne M\. Spenkelink

**Data:** 2026-10-12 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Abstract Single\-molecule techniques provide exceptional resolution of biomolecular dynamics but are limited by low throughput\. Here, we present a DNA barcoding strategy that enables parallel kinetic profiling of multiple protein variants in a single experiment\. Each protein is covalently linked to a unique DNA barcode, decoded through transient hybridisation of fluorescent probes distinguished by colour and binding duration\. Applied to a library of 16 ClpS variants, this approach revealed \~5\-fold variation in dissociation kinetics and uncovered previously unrecognised bimodal binding behaviour arising from distinct structural subpopulations\. By enabling scalable, multiplexed functional characterisation at the single\-molecule level, this platform provides a general framework for protein engineering, directed evolution and the development of next\-generation single\-molecule technologies\.
