# DIGITAL STORYTELLING DALAM PEMBELAJARAN PENDIDIKAN AGAMA ISLAM: KAJIAN LITERATUR TERHADAP MOTIVASI BELAJAR GENERASI Z

**Autores:** Lutfiana Nazmaatun

**Data:** 2026-10-12 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

The development of digital transformation in the world of education has also encouraged a shift in learning strategies towards a more interactive, contextual, and student\-oriented direction\. One of the approaches that has begun to be looked at in this shift is digital storytelling, a strategy that combines storytelling activities with multimedia elements to present a more meaningful learning experience\. Departing from this phenomenon, this study seeks to examine the role of digital storytelling in Islamic Religious Education \(PAI\) learning and its relationship with the learning motivation of Generation Z, through a literature review approach with narrative synthesis techniques based on a number of previous studies\. In order to deepen the analysis, this study relies on Self\-Determination Theory \(SDT\) and Cognitive Theory of Multimedia Learning \(CTML\) to examine the motivational and cognitive dimensions in digital\-based learning\. The results of the study indicate that digital storytelling has the potential to support Generation Z&\#x27;s learning motivation, especially through strengthening active participation, creativity, and a deeper understanding of teaching materials\. Especially in PAI learning, this approach has been proven to be able to bridge religious materials with students&\#x27; personal experiences through the process of reflection and internalization of values\. However, the success of its implementation still depends on a number of factors, including the validity of religious sources, the readiness of educators, the level of digital literacy, and the accuracy of the learning design used\. Keywords: Digital storytelling; Learning motivation; Generation Z; Religious Education
