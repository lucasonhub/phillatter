# The Voxel Grid Mechanics

**Autores:** Orymbetov Yerzhan

**Data:** 2026-10-09 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

AbstractStandard academic physics remains deeply fractured, isolating the quantum\-probabilistic equations of the micro\-world from the gravitational\-relativistic models of the macro\-cosmos\. This paper introduces a monolithic, non\-Keplerian physical paradigm demonstrating that the vacuum continuum is a dense, highly elastic discrete matrix characterized by an invariant volumetric stiffness coefficient of 6\.6743 \* 10^\-11\. By eliminating abstract mathematical placeholders—such as dark matter, dark energy, and gravitational singularities—we systematically prove that the 2 Pi circular geometric lock seamlessly binds sub\-atomic nucleon cores to the equatorial rotation of the Earth, the Sun, and distant stellar architectures\. We demonstrate that the fundamental mechanisms of stellar luminosity, asymmetric solar winds, atmospheric thermodynamics, and planetary continental configurations are raw mechanical friction, boundary shear, and volumetric displacement executed upon the discrete voxel substrate\. Furthermore, we expose the mathematical invalidity of gravitational singularities, clarifying why continuous fluid equations \(Navier\-Stokes\) and contemporary artificial intelligence models encounter false singularities\.
