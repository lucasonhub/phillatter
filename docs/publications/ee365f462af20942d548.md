# Jovanovic, Vladisav: After the Conversation: How Repeated AI Interaction May Reshape Human Contact and Inner Life

**Autores:** 

**Data de descoberta:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** unknown

## Descrição do feed

Conversational AI, generative AI, large language models, AI companions, and chatbots are usually evaluated at the level of the immediate interaction: whether an answer is accurate, helpful, persuasive, supportive, sycophantic, or emotionally meaningful\. This paper shifts attention to what happens after repeated human\-AI interaction\. It develops the concept of post\-conversation carryover to organize emerging research on AI\-mediated communication, social interaction, loneliness, companionship, emotional dependence, support\-seeking, self\-reflection, reflective agency, cognitive offloading, identity, and human relationships\. Three destinations of carryover are distinguished: interpersonal carryover, in which habits and expectations acquired through AI interaction appear in later human communication; behavioral carryover, in which AI conversation changes verification, decision\-making, repair, avoidance, or support\-seeking; and intrapersonal carryover, in which repeated AI use alters the user&\#x27;s relation to uncertainty, unfinished thought, judgment, imagination, and self\-interpretation\. Four recurring functional directions—rehearsal, extension, bridge, and substitution—are used as a descriptive synthesis rather than as exclusive mechanisms\. Particular attention is given to reciprocity, social presence, low\-friction disclosure, norm leakage, AI companionship, relational substitution, and the possibility that AI can either scaffold human contact or increasingly replace independent judgment and reciprocal interaction\. The central question is not whether conversational AI is inherently beneficial or harmful, but what repeated use makes easier to practice, what it makes less necessary to practice, and what remains when the system is no longer responding\.
