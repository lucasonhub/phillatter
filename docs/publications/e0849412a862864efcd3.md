# Humanizing Technology, Vol\. III

**Autores:** Silvia Lima Gonçalves Araújo; Micaela Aguiar; Dalila Durães

**Data:** 2026-10-13 · **Tipo:** book

## Resumo

This third volume of the Humanizing Technology collection explores the intersections between artificial intelligence \(AI\) and the humanities, addressing issues ranging from language processing, ethics, and data practices to digital heritage and computational creativity\. By combining contributions from multiple disciplines, it offers diverse perspectives on how AI is reshaping research, creativity, and culture\. Each chapter stands alone while also contributing to a broader reflection on the evolving role of AI in scholarly and artistic practices\. This volume is an essential reference for researchers, educators, and practitioners interested in the transformative impact of AI on the humanities\.
