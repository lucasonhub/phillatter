# Performance of convolutional neural networks in the histopathological grading of canine cutaneous mast cell tumors: a pilot study

**Autores:** Dyessica Ferreira Maia; Dyonnatan Ferreira Maia; Alice Assunção dos Santos; Ana Maria de Souza Almeida; Estela Vieira de Souza Silva; Veridiana Maria Brianezi Dignani de Moura; Eduardo de Paula Nascente

**Data:** 2026-10-15 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Canine cutaneous mast cell tumor is one of the most prevalent skin neoplasms in dogs, and histological grading is essential for prognosis and therapeutic decision‑making\. However, histological classification according to the Patnaik and Kiupel systems presents limitations related to subjectivity and low reproducibility among pathologists\. This study evaluated the performance of convolutional neural networks \(CNNs\) in the histopathological classification of canine cutaneous mast cell tumors\. Eighteen histological slides labeled by specialists were used and processed into image patches\. The AlexNet, InceptionV3, ResNet50, and YOLO11 architectures were trained using cross‑validation and evaluated based on accuracy, sensitivity, precision, F1‑score, and area under the curve \(AUC\)\. Grad‑CAM techniques and feature maps were also applied for interpretative analysis of the models’ decisions\. The YOLO11 and ResNet50 models showed the best performance, achieving AUC values of up to 93\.5%, but did not reach the arbitrarily defined minimum clinical threshold of 95%\. Visual analysis demonstrated that the models recognized relevant morphological patterns, such as granules and pleomorphism, but failed to differentiate between grades II and III\. It is concluded that, although promising as auxiliary tools, CNNs still require methodological improvements, such as refined curation, semantic segmentation, and cellular structure identification, for clinical application\.
