# Research on the innovation path of retail business models based on the cognitive profiling of Generation Alpha

**Autores:** Qianyi Liu

**Data:** 2026-10-10 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Generation Alpha, the first cohort to grow up with artificial intelligence, is expected to become the largest consumer segment in history\. However, academic research on their cognitive characteristics and consumption behaviour remains limited\. This study aims to construct a multi‑dimensional cognitive profile of Generation Alpha and to examine how this profile drives retail business model innovation\. A structured questionnaire survey was conducted with 316 parents of Generation Alpha children in China, covering four cognitive dimensions: behavioural patterns, product preferences, social concerns, and brand acceptance\. Descriptive statistics, correlation analysis, and multiple regression analysis were used to analyse the data\. The results show that Generation Alpha children exhibit moderate levels across all four cognitive dimensions\. Product preference emerged as the strongest predictor of brand acceptance \(β = 0\.318, p &lt; 0\.001\), followed by social concern \(β = 0\.218\) and behavioural profile \(β = 0\.213\)\. The model explained 31\.8% of the variance in brand acceptance\. These findings provide empirical support for three retail innovation pathways: product creativity enhancement, channel transformation, and communication upgrading\. The study offers both theoretical contributions to generational consumer behaviour research and practical guidance for retailers seeking to engage Generation Alpha\.
