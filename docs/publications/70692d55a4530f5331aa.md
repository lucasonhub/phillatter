# A Stability\-aware Computational Intelligence Framework for Risk\-guided Corrective Decision Support in Execution\-stage Routing Systems

**Autores:** Ayub Prasetyo; Sarifuddin Madenda; Ernastuti; Murni

**Data:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Route disruptions require corrective decisions that respect what has already been served\.This study develops a stability\-aware framework that locks the executed route prefix and re\-sequences only the remaining suffix, combining quantile travel\-time risk, an adaptive metaheuristic search, and a route\-stability term with an admissible unchanged\-route option\.Sixty\-three daily instances were split into calibration and held\-out subsets, the latter used only for evaluation\.Daily\-instance statistical analysis confirmed significant differences among the compared methods\.The proposed method significantly reduced lateness relative to no re\-planning while accepting higher lateness than free and risk\-guided re\-sequencing in exchange for greater route stability and fewer route changes\.An adapted external adaptive\-large\-neighborhood\-search comparator achieved lower lateness but with more than twice as many moved stops and lower route stability, and higher computational cost\.A complementary fuzzy decision\-activation gate, calibrated only on the calibration subset, retained most of the always\-SA lateness\-reduction benefit as a post\-review robustness check\.
