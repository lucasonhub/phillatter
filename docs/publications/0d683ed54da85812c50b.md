# THE EL\-RAKHAWI CAUSAL ENTROPIC BOUND FOR AUTONOMOUS LEARNING SYSTEMS \(ECB\)

**Autores:** m el\-rakhawi

**Data:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** book

## Resumo

The El\-Rakhawi Causal Entropic Bound \(ECB\) introduces a fundamental thermodynamic law governing autonomous learning, transcending statistical machine learning\. It addresses a critical physical reality: discovering true causality requires breaking time\-reversal symmetry\. By integrating the Crooks Fluctuation Theorem with Causal Algorithmic Complexity, the ECB proves that extracting pure causal information necessitates minimum entropy production strictly exceeding the classical Landauer limit\. The framework establishes an absolute, dimensionally consistent lower bound on energy dissipation for any system to distinguish causal mechanisms from spurious correlations\. Formulated as S\_dot &gt;= \(k\_B / tau\) \* \(I\_causal \+ beta \* K\_c\(G\)\), it defines the average entropy production rate required to acquire a total causal information load over timescale tau\. This paradigm redefines intelligence as a thermodynamically constrained ability to uncover reality&\#x27;s causal fabric, setting the ultimate theoretical benchmark for next\-generation neuromorphic hardware, quantum learning, and Artificial General Intelligence\.
