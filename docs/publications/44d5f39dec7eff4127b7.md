# Unveiling the heterogeneity: PD\-L1 expression and metabolic activity in primary vs\. metastatic NSCLC, a retrospective single\-center analysis

**Autores:** 胡冰心; Huibin Jin; Yue Yu; Jie Zhang; Yang You; Chang Fu; Ang Xuan; Xinyu Wu; 高永举

**Data:** 2026-10-13 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Introduction Programmed death\-ligand 1 \(PD\-L1\) expression was different in primary tumors \(PT\) and metastatic tumors \(MT\)\. Total\-body positron\-emission tomography/computed tomography \(PET/CT\) comprehensively visualizes primary lesions and metastases\. We aimed to explore the heterogeneity of PD\-L1 high expression and metabolic activity between primary and metastatic lesions in patients with non\-small cell lung cancer \(NSCLC\)\. Methods Immunohistochemistry analysis \(IHC\) was conducted for PD\-L1 expression on tumor cells \(TC\) and tumor\-infiltrating immune cells \(IC\) with 22C3 antibody\. PD\-L1 positive scores on TC no less than 50% or IC no less than 10% was defined as high expression\. Total 598 \(456 adenocarcinoma \(ADC\) and 142 squamous cell lung carcinoma \(SCC\)\) primary diagnosed NSCLC patients who underwent 18 F\-FDG\-PET/CT imaging were collected retrospectively\. The statistic methods included chi\-square test, Mann Whitney test and multivariate regression\. Results 598 patients included 441 with PD\-L1 low expression and 157 high expression\. In all subtypes and ADC, MT had higher PD\-L1 high expression \(P = 0\.023 and P = 0\.010, respectively\)\. The maximum standard uptake value \(SUVmax\) of PD\-L1 high expression was significantly higher than that of PD\-L1 low expression, no matter in PT or MT \(15\.23 \(8\.85\) vs 9\.42 \(8\.34\), P&lt;0\.001; 13\.36 \(5\.55\) vs 8\.52 \(6\.93\), P&lt;0\.001\)\. The squamous\-cell\-carcinoma metastatic\-lesion analyses were exploratory owing to the very small sample size and risk of type\-II statistical error\. Multivariate regression indicated that SUVmax was independently associated with PD\-L1 high expression\. In all metastatic sites, we compared the SUVmax of MT and paired PT and found SUVmax was higher in PT than MT in PD\-L1 low expression subgroup \(11\.39 \(8\.12\) vs 8\.52 \(6\.93\), P = 0\.002\)\. For lymph node metastases, SUVmax was significantly elevated in PT compared to MT in PD\-L1 low expression \(13\.71 \(7\.95\) vs 8\.54 \(7\.30\), P = 0\.002\), whereas no correlation was observed in PD\-L1 high expression\. Discussion Both PD\-L1 expression and metabolism exhibited heterogeneity between primary tumors and their metastases\. Compared to the primary tumors, different types of metastatic tumors showed variations not only in PD\-L1 expression but also in their metabolic parameter\. Utilizing whole\-body PET/CT imaging facilitates the selection of optimal biopsy sites by providing metabolic reference data pertinent to clinical practice\.
