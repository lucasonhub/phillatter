# Evaluation of Soil Microbial Biomass Carbon, Nitrogen, and Enzymatic Activity in Degraded Soils Amended With Organic and Inorganic Fertilizers

**Autores:** 

**Data:** 2026-10-17 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Soil microbial biomass represents the living engine of the soil nutrient cycling system, and its restoration in degraded agricultural soils is a prerequisite for sustainable productivity in Pakistan&\#x27;s intensively managed irrigated plains\.The present greenhouse Completely Randomised Design \(CRD\) study, evaluated five amendment treatments \-unamended control, NPK chemical fertilizer, farmyard manure \(FYM, 10 t ha⁻¹\), biochar \(2% w/w\), and FYM\+biochar combination \-for their effects on twelve soil biological, chemical, and crop performance parameters in a severely degraded sandy loam soil \(initial MBC 84\.4 mg kg⁻¹, SOM 0\.42%, pH 8\.02\)\.Parameters assessed included microbial biomass carbon \(MBC\), microbial biomass nitrogen \(MBN\), basal soil respiration, metabolic quotient \(qCO₂\), MBC:MBN ratio, soil organic matter \(SOM\), available nitrogen \(N\), available phosphorus \(P\), dehydrogenase activity, urease activity, soil pH, and wheat crop yield\.The FYM\+biochar combination produced the highest values for all biological and yield parameters: MBC 212\.4 mg kg⁻¹ \(\+151\.6%over control\), MBN 28\.6 mg kg⁻¹ \(\+130\.6%\),basal respiration 22\.4 mg CO₂ 100 g⁻¹ d⁻¹ \(\+166\.7%\),dehydrogenase activity 38\.4 µg TPF g⁻¹ 24h⁻¹ \(\+209\.7%\),urease activity 62\.4 µg NH₄⁺\-N g⁻¹ h⁻¹ \(\+153\.7%\),and crop yield 5\.24 g pot⁻¹ \(\+84\.5% over unamended control\)\.Principal component analysis \(PCA\) explained 77\.2% of total variance across PC1 \(52\.4%\) and PC2 \(24\.8%\), with the FYM\+biochar treatment positioned at the extreme positive end of PC1 along with all biological health variables\.The metabolic quotient \(qCO₂\) was lowest under FYM\+biochar \(0\.106\), indicating the most efficient microbial community metabolism and the least stressed microbial biomass\.These results confirm that FYM combined with biochar is the superior amendment strategy for restoring soil microbial fertility, enzymatic activity, and crop productivity in degraded irrigated soils of Punjab, Pakistan, and provide a mechanistic understanding of the complementary contributions of labile organic substrate \(FYM\) and structured microhabitat \(biochar\) to soil biological recovery\.
