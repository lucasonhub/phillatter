# PROJETONÓS PROPOMOS\! E O ENSINO DEGEOGRAFIA: CONSTRUINDO CIDADESSUSTENTÁVEIS COM PARTICIPAÇÃO,INOVAÇÃO E COMPROMISSO SOCIAL

**Autores:** Cecília Silva; Jairo Rodrigues Silva; Talita Valadares

**Data:** 2026-10-09 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

This work, developed by students from the Federal Institute of Minas Gerais \(IFMG\), Ouro Preto campus, aimed to analyze the main problems existing in the Bauxita and Lagoa neighborhoods\. To this end, bibliographic research was conducted to understand the historical context, the formation process, and the main problems of these localities\. In the subsequent stage, a structured questionnaire was developed and applied through fieldwork\. The data obtained were systematized and analyzed, allowing the identification of the most recurring demands pointed out by the community\. Among the main problems identified, the poor condition of the sidewalks, the insufficiency of green areas, and the lack of spaces for leisure stand out\. Based on the results obtained, interventions aimed at improving urban quality were proposed, including the implementation of green areas using the Miyawaki method, the revitalization of sidewalks based on the principles of tactical urbanization, and the recovery of the lagoon using artificial intelligence tools, considering the needs indicated by the residents of the Lagoa neighborhood\. Furthermore, the development of the project enabled the strengthening of the interaction between students and the local community, contributing to the academic training of the students and to the construction of proposals aligned with the identified social demands\.
