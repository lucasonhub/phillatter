# ML\-Based Smart Appliance Energy Monitoring System

**Autores:** Divya Dongre; Astha Bavistale; Atharva Wasnik; Rohan Hedau; Sanchit Nardelwar; Krishnakumar Bhabhda; Ms\. Priya Meshram

**Data:** 2026-10-15 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Modern homes only get a combined electricity consumption value\. In other words, they have no information about which of their appliances have used the electricity a house has consumed\. This paper introduces the design of an ‘ML\-Based Smart Appliance Energy Monitoring’ system for recognizing the electricity consumption of a smart home\. The Non\-Intrusive Load Monitoring system uses an ESP32 development board and a PZEM\-004T V3\.0 energy meter and current transformer \(CT\) to measure the voltage, current, power, frequency, and power\-factor data of a particular circuit of the home’s electrical circuit\. These are then transmitted over Wi\-Fi/HTTP to a Flask\-based inference server where a 50\-feature input \(constructed in identical fashion between training and inference from a 13,439\-row labeled dataset\) is classified by a joint ensemble \(HistGradientBoosting \(45%\), ExtraTrees \(35%\), and Random Forest \(20%\)\) of regressors into one of eight appliancecombination classes representing three target devices \(a phone charger, a laptop charger, and a hot glue gun\)\. An additional individual\-device model enables ON\-time tracking, per\-device energy estimation, and electricity cost calculation for the devices\. Classification results are then locally visualized on a 16 × 2 I2C LCD and remotely on the Blynk IoT platform while storing relevant device statistics in the ESP32 Preferences\. In contrast to the recent deep\-learning\-based approaches, which typically require heavy computation and a large amount of annotated data to train on, we propose an explainable ensemble pipeline for low\-end single\-household appliances\.
