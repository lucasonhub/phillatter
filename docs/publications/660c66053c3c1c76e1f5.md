# Collective kernel EFT for pre\-activation ResNets

**Autores:** Hidetoshi Kawase; Toshihiro Ota

**Data:** 2026-10-14 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Abstract In finite\-width deep neural networks, the empirical kernel G evolves stochastically across layers\. We develop a collective kernel effective field theory \(EFT\) for a simplified pre\-activation residual model\. Exact conditional Gaussianity of the residual increment gives finite\-width discrete identities for G and its sigma\-kernel source\. A projected G\-only closure and a continuous\-depth approximation define reference equations for the mean kernel K0, the kernel covariance V4, and the next\-to\-leading mean correction K1\. Numerical tests show that this closure captures the leading mean\-kernel evolution, whereas its covariance and next\-to\-leading accuracy depend on sigma\-kernel modes omitted from the G\-only state\. For the scalar \(N=1\) model with tanh activation, a Hermite\-\-Galerkin extension agrees with the extrapolated K1 value and reduces the V4 endpoint error by 85\-93% relative to the G\-only reference\. These results clarify the range of validity of the equal\-time Markov G\-only description and identify the additional collective information required beyond it\.
