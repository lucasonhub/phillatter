# Aloysia deserticola \(rica\-rica\) essential oil impairs inhibitory synaptic transmission in hippocampal CA1 pyramidal neurons

**Autores:** Juan Ahumada; Pablo Morel\-Alonso; Nicole Vidal\-Herrera; Koyam Morales\-Weil; Waleska Vera; Marco Fuenzalida

**Data:** 2026-10-14 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Aloysia deserticola is an aromatic plant native to the Chilean Andean highlands that has traditionally been used in herbal preparations\. Although a previous behavioral study reported the anxiolytic and antidepressant\-like effects of rica\-rica essential oil in an animal model, its neurophysiological actions at cellular and synaptic levels remain largely unexplored\. In this study, we characterized A\. deserticola essential oil \(AD\-EO\) using gas chromatography–mass spectrometry \(GC–MS\) and examined its acute effects on GABAergic synaptic transmission via whole\-cell patch\-clamp recordings from CA1 pyramidal neurons in acute dorsal hippocampal slices obtained from C57BL/6 J mice\. GC–MS analysis revealed an enriched thujone chemical profile, with peaks tentatively identified as α\- and β\-thujone accounting for 89\.06% and 3\.38% of the total chromatographic area, respectively\. Application of AD\-EO to the bath reduced evoked inhibitory postsynaptic current \(eIPSC\) amplitude in a concentration\-dependent manner and shortened eIPSC decay kinetics\. This is consistent with a postsynaptic contribution to the suppression of inhibitory signaling\. At intermediate\-to\-high estimated thujone\-equivalent concentrations, AD\-EO increased the paired\-pulse ratio\. Coefficient\-of\-variation analysis supported the presence of an additional presynaptic component\. Consistent with these findings, AD\-EO reduced the frequency and amplitude of miniature inhibitory postsynaptic currents\. Furthermore, the effects on eIPSC amplitude and the paired\-pulse ratio were reversible following washout\. Together, these results demonstrate that AD\-EO suppresses inhibitory synapses in CA1 pyramidal neurons via effects consistent with pre\- and postsynaptic locus \. These results provide electrophysiological evidence that AD\-EO modulates hippocampal inhibitory transmission and support further investigation of the mechanisms underlying the neurophysiological actions of this complex natural preparation\.
