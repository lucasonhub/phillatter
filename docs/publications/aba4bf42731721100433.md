# Sustainable Development of Multifunctional Jute Fiber\-Reinforced Epoxy Composites Enhanced with Agricultural Bran Fillers for Sustainable Structural and Antimicrobial Applications

**Autores:** Raja Thandavamoorthy; Yuvarajan Devarajan; Ashwini Kumar; Alok Kumar Tiwari; Sankar Narayan Das; Naveen Kumar Rajendran; T\. Aravinda; Yogeesha Pai

**Data:** 2026-10-12 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

This study investigates multifunctional jute fiber\-reinforced epoxy composites incorporating agricultural bran filler \(0–4 wt\.%\) fabricated by hand lay\-up\. The composite containing 3 wt\.% bran exhibited optimum performance, achieving tensile, flexural, and impact strengths of 76\.16 MPa, 81\.27 MPa, and 17\.19 kJ/m2, respectively\. Shore D hardness increased from 58 to 69, while thermal conductivity and coefficient of linear thermal expansion decreased from 0\.86 to 0\.62 W/mK and 1\.16 × 10−5 °C−1 to 0\.96 × 10−5 °C−1, respectively\. Heat deflection temperature increased from 79°C to 104 °C, while thermogravimetric analysis indicated improved thermal stability and char formation\. Under cyclic loading initiated at 70% of the respective ultimate tensile strength, the 3 wt\.% composite retained a fatigue strength of 42\.86 MPa at 20,000 cycles, 15\.4% higher than the unfilled composite\. SEM examination of fatigue\-fractured specimens revealed matrix cracking, fiber pull\-out, fiber fracture, and interfacial debonding, indicating progressive cyclic damage mechanisms\. Antibacterial evaluation against Klebsiella pneumoniae showed a concentration\-dependent response, with the 3 wt\.% bran composite extract producing inhibition zones of 16 mm and 24 mm at extract dosages of 50 μg and 100 μg, respectively\. Therefore, 3 wt\.% bran provided the most favorable balance of mechanical, fatigue, thermal, and antibacterial properties\.
