# ИССЛЕДОВАНИЕ ПРИМЕНЕНИЯ ТЕХНОЛОГИЙ ИСКУССТВЕННОГО ИНТЕЛЛЕКТА В ОБУЧЕНИИ ЗАДАЧАМ НА ПОСТРОЕНИЕ С ПОМОЩЬЮ ЦИРКУЛЯ И ЛИНЕЙКИ В ШКОЛЬНОЙ ГЕОМЕТРИИ

**Autores:** Ж\.Н\. Әбжан; Ж\.У\. Байдуллаева; Н\.Н\. Турдиева

**Data:** 2026-10-15 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

The study examines the methodology for solving geometric construction problems, which is one of the most complex and important sections of school geometry\. In the modern education system, primary attention is paid to developing students’ logical thinking and spatial imagination\. Geometric constructions enhance students’ cognitive abilities and open the way to a deep mastery of fundamental knowledge\. The distinctive feature of construction problems is that they are based not on ready\-made formulas and memorized algorithms, but on students’ creative exploration and critical thinking\. While performing such tasks, students sharpen their skills in working with drawing instruments, from graphic literacy, and see first\-hand the practical proof of theoretical theorems\. At the same time, during the analysis of geometry textbooks for grades 7\-9 in the course of the research, a limited number of sample construction tasks was noticed\. In our view, students encounter numerous difficulties in independently understanding construction problems using a compass and straightedge\. The goal of the study is to systematize effective methods for solving construction problems and reveal their practical significance\. According to the main hypothesis of the work, in\-depth teaching of geometric constructions significantly improves students’ mathematical literacy\. Along with traditional methods, modern digital tools such as GeoGebra Classic were used during the research\. Modeling problems in an interactive environment allows students to visually grasp construction algorithms\. The results of the study are recommended as a methodological guide for teachers in school geometry classes and elective courses\.
