# A Self‐Sustained System for Producing Green Hydrogen via Moisture Power

**Autores:** Zhenglin Wang; Jianning Lv; Xiaojun He; Xiangyang Li; Zifeng Jin; Lanmin Qiao; Hui Zhang; Nan Chen

**Data:** 2026-10-15 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

ABSTRACT We report a self‐sustaining system that directly converts atmospheric moisture into green hydrogen, eliminating reliance on intermittent renewables and liquid water sources\. The core of the system is a multifunctional hydrogel device that integrates moisture‐enabled electricity generation, atmospheric water harvesting, and electrolysis into a seamless operation\. Concerted ion diffusion and active electrode chemistry in the gel yield a stable power output of 1\.87 V and 450 μA cm −2 , while its hygroscopic components capture water at 1\.318 g g −1 \. The harvested water and electricity are used in situ to drive efficient overall water splitting at 1\.50 V, catalyzed by a Mo‐FeNi 3 /NiFe 2 O 4 heterostructure catalyst, producing hydrogen at a rate of 2\.6 μmol g PPM −1 per cycle\. This integrated “moisture‐to‐hydrogen” paradigm offers a scalable, off‐grid solution for continuous and carbon‐neutral hydrogen production\.
