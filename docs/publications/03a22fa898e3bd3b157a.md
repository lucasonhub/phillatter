# Reasoning for Propaganda Detection via Label\-Guided CoT Optimisation

**Autores:** Zeba Afroz; Harsh Vardhan; Md\. Shad Akhtar

**Data:** 2026-10-12 · **Tipo:** preprint

## Resumo

Propaganda detection systems usually operate as black boxes, lacking explanations\. To address this, we hypothesise that persuasive techniques contain definitional characteristics with respect to their social\-political setting\. We introduce ReCoT, a reasoning\-conditioned detection framework that captures definitional cues of persuasive techniques via population\-level refinement and performance\-guided resampling, without requiring additional explanation annotations\. On INDI\-PROPspan, ReCoT achieves a macro F1\_LCS of 0\.364, surpassing baselines and reaching 88% of the SFT upper bound on Avg F1\_LCS\. Similarly, on PTC, ReCoT reaches 85% of the SFT upper bound on Avg F1\_LCS, closing much of this gap while remaining interpretable\. Importantly, the analysis of the reasoning pool&\#x27;s convergence and update pattern for ReCoT shows effective refinement of reasoning CoTs\.
