# ACTUALIZATION OF THEORETICAL PRINCIPLES AND PRACTICAL TOOLS OF SELF\-MANAGEMENT IN MODERN CONDITIONS

**Autores:** Yolana Shashovec

**Data:** 2026-10-15 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

The article examines problems in the field of self\-management of individual activities and outlines new vectors of research in this field, which are formed under the influence of modern realities\. The author summarizes the most common approaches to interpreting the essence and content of self\-management, and makes a conditional division of these definitions into two groups: the first involves considering self\-management exclusively in the context of the employee&\#x27;s professional development, and the second considers the possibility of using self\-management methods and techniques to achieve various goals in different areas of life\. The set of factors whose influence on the activities and professional development of a modern person contributes to the actualization of the principles and techniques of self\-management is outlined\. Attention is paid to the issues of the effectiveness of personal activity planning, the choice of methods for developing one&\#x27;s own work plans, the selection of working tools for optimizing labor efforts and time costs\. Also, a set of issues and problems that traditionally belong to the subject of scientific research in the field of self\-management is identified, and they are compared with modern issues, which are determined by the new conditions of activity formed by the information society\. The existence of a close relationship between the classical and modern issues of the science of self\-management is emphasized, which is manifested in changing the aspects of considering traditional issues, and not in the formation of fundamentally new questions for scientific research\. It is emphasized that the uncertainty and instability of the external environment puts the development of stress resistance of the employee as a key quality at the forefront among the issues of modern self\-management\. The author of the article also touches on such an issue as the integration of digital tools and artificial intelligence technologies into the self\-management system, characterizes specific practical problems that arise for employees who introduce such technologies to improve the planning and organization of personal activities\. The need for further scientific research in the field of self\-management and strengthening the applied value of such research to ensure the comprehensive development of each person as an individual is emphasized\.
