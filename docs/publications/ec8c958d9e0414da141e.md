# Johnson, Jamison: On Questioning \- Nature Exposed to Our Method of Questioning

## Descrição do feed

Does the answer you get depend on how you asked? Starting from Heisenberg&\#x27;s remark that what we observe is not nature in itself but nature exposed to our method of questioning, this lyric\-philosophical essay sets the per\-person reading of the author&\#x27;s coherent\-existence functional beside the interference term of two\-beam optics\. Both pair a bounded measure of agreement — fringe visibility, the size of the shared average — with the cosine of a relative angle\. The essay follows the correspondences outward to superposition, collapse, and interference, and centers on phase: a quantity that cannot be read in any single measurement yet writes every fringe, which the framework identifies with Intent\. It then marks the limit of the resemblance at length\. The shared form is generic, optics is measured and the framework is not, and a structural fidelity shows only that the framework is well formed, not that it is true\. It closes on the observer&\#x27;s responsibility for the question asked\. \(direct link \)
