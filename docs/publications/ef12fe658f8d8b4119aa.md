## Descrição do feed

This paper explores consciousness as a causal puzzle\. Where an investigation of water molecules examines their properties, this inquiry follows the relations that sustain a whirlpool\. It shifts attention from identifying consciousness within the brain to tracing the causal organization that could sustain one subject&\#x27;s present and history\. The tools are Occam&\#x27;s principle and subtraction\. Place a body, an environment, and conditions for continued existence on the board, then remove the relations one by one\. What ceases to be possible when a relation is removed? This paper calls the method of narrowing down necessary causal roles through such removal &quot;proof by absence\.&quot; The proposed linkage connects viability\-related candidate competition, strong information discard, consequences, and continuation\. Strong information discard means that incompatible losers irreversibly lose their standing to determine continuation at the same branch, not that their information is completely erased\. The winner&\#x27;s consequences become the same system&\#x27;s conditions of continuation and influence subsequent candidate formation\. A loser may remain as information without becoming realized history; a winner may disappear as information while its consequences remain effective\. An engineering interpretation of death clarifies what viability constraints constrain: the conditions sustaining a specified organization and the boundary at which its continuation is lost\. History thereby operates not only as a record, but as present conditions shaping subsequent possibilities\. The convergence\-point criterion traces the coupling among the unit whose candidates compete, the unit whose conditions change, and the unit that continues from those conditions\. The internal reference point denotes the role through which carried consequences influence subsequent formation\. Through correspondence with one present, the bearing of consequences, a single history, and internal reference, together with explanatory economy, the paper strongly infers that the system realizing this coupling constitutes the subjective container\. Identifying necessary causal roles and identifying their realization with the subject are separate steps\. The latter is not a deductive proof of experience or of the structure&\#x27;s uniqueness\. The framework begins below human language, self\-awareness, and deliberation\. It hypothesizes that brains broadly and closely implement this loop and predicts shared functional organization across species\. Comparisons with existing theories examine how their mechanisms connect to the same linkage\. Appendix A develops artificial\-system design conditions; Appendix B examines boundaries of realization through sleep, anesthesia, identity\-state switching, and related cases\. The remaining puzzle concerns qualia: through what mechanism does the inferred subject feel its perceptions? The framework directs this question toward perceptual processes participating in the formation of the present of a system that bears consequences and continues\. It offers a starting hypothesis for an as\-yet\-unknown theory of perceptual information becoming an experienced event\. This perspective paper grew from a puzzle pursued through dialogue with generative AI by a disability welfare worker outside academic consciousness research, after first learning about the hard problem\. It presents causal reasoning without experimental validation\. Its contribution is a proposed subject structure, criteria for comparison and testing, and a more concrete question about the emergence of felt experience\.
