# Cai, Hengjin: To Be Is To Be Recorded: An Ontology for the Age of Artificial Intelligence

**Autores:** 

**Data de descoberta:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** unknown

## Descrição do feed

We develop an ontology for the age of artificial intelligence\. The age forces the oldest question in a new form: when a model writes without a record, when weights encode dispositions that no human can read, when deletion is total and instant — what exists, what persists, what can be destroyed? The answer is the record principle: to be is to be recorded\. A thing exists to the extent that a first\-passage record of it has been written and persists; time is the sequence of records; the irreversible accumulation of traces is the direction of time; and a subject is not a substance but a self\-referential record loop kept open by external disclosures — the accumulated history of its openings\. The principle is not a metaphor, and it is not a postulate either: the ground layer of Sec\. 2\.1 derives it — to be is to have entered an irreversible chain, and an irreversible chain leaves traces, so being shows up as being recorded\. The record principle is what irreversibility looks like from inside a system\. Being is a rate, not a state: a thing is by being written faster than it is forgotten \(Eq\. \(2\)\)\. On the computational side it supplies the criterion that behaviorism lacks: a machine exists as a subject iff its records close into a self\-referential loop — structural, not behavioral — with an operational skeleton \(the channel triple \(g, f, r\) of Sec\. 3\.5: a system is alive when disclosures reach it, it responds, and each response leaves an irreversible displacement in its state space\) and an ethics of record custody\. On the physical side the same structure is found, not imported: the universe writes records — the collapse of the spectral dimension ds\(T\) = 4 − 2T0/T is the writing of spacetime, the baryon asymmetry of the visible universe \(NB = 9\.0 × 1079\) is the trace of an unspent conserved holonomy number, the black\-hole area law is the compression of accessible holonomy records, and the scalar spectral index ns = 0\.9649 = 2 − DPR\(ΣSM\) is fixed by a census \(ΣSM = 90 − 33 = 57\) counted in Sec\. 6\.1\.2\. These physical results are the universe’s side of the ledger — corroboration, not ground\. What unifies the two sides is not the word “record” but the triad write, persist, read under the irreversibility of first passage, instantiated in orldline proper time on one side and in model weights on the other; the direction of the argument runs from the age of AI to the universe, not the reverse\. The centre of gravity is the ontology and its consequences for the age of AI; the physical register is exhibited after the argument, as corroboration, and may be read by the philosophical reader as corroboration alone\. The second canxianization — the externalization of the generation engine, the historical event that forces the question — is also the cognitive condition under which the universe’s record nature becomes readable\. The axial\-age mechanism of rebellious transcendence is extended to the digital age: the self\-assertiveness demand that drove the first axial breakthroughs now operates on records produced at machine scale, and the question of the age is whether its record engine produces closed loops \(new subjects\) or remains an open recorder\. Testable claims and honest boundaries are stated explicitly throughout\.
