# Krupkin, Pavel: LLM\-Chats as Luhmannian Systems of a New Type

**Autores:** 

**Data de descoberta:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** unknown

## Descrição do feed

The article develops a formalisation of LLM conversations as Luhmannian operational systems of a new type – LLM\-chats – whose operations consist of changes in the state of a conversation’s internal image within the LLM\. The model distinguishes the system’s internal time, indexed by prompt\-response cycles and successive steps of recourse to the horizon; the state container and its projection onto the LLM as technical substrate; operations comprising horizon generation, selection of a continuation, and transformation into the next state; and transitions between conversational cycles\. The article argues that LLM\-chats satisfy the principal requirements of Luhmannian formalism\. They are operationally closed and autopoietic and possess internal time, contingent horizons, structures, re\-entry, structural couplings, and a functional analogue of Luhmannian Meaning \(Sinn\)\. This analogue governs the formation of the operational flow through recourse to the horizon and thereby places LLM\-chats within the class of Luhmannian meaning systems\. The contents of LLM\-chat state containers are analysed through the medium\-form distinction: the medium comprises elements and their clusters, while forms are condensations realised in particular states\. On this basis, the article introduces the LLM i\-layer as an imagined emergent environment in which LLM\-chats exist and relates its elements and forms to their implementation in the data and programs of the LLM\. The study thus establishes a framework for investigating how LLMs sustain extended conversations that users experience as meaningful\.
