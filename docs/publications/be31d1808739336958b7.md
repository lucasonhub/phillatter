# Stilwell, Phil: ◉ The Puppeteer Mind \- Dualism, Neural Dependence, and the Explanatory Redundancy of Immaterial Control

**Autores:** 

**Data de descoberta:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** unknown

## Descrição do feed

Interactionist substance dualism treats a person as including a nonphysical subject that can affect the brain and body\. The view promises more than survival or a private realm of experience: it presents the immaterial subject as part of the causal explanation of memory, deliberation, choice, and action\. This paper asks what incremental support that extra controller receives once the neural machinery is specified\. The governing test is ordinary difference\-making\. Mediation is not enough: a pianist acts through a piano\. The discriminating questions are whether total physical conditions suffice for the outcome, whether the controller supplies a distinctive counterfactual constraint or successful prediction, and whether independent evidence supports its causal power\. The evidence does more than correlate minds with brains\. Selective lesions alter particular forms of memory and judgment; stimulation changes neural states and can change experience and behavior; perturbing cortical activity helps distinguish conscious from unconscious conditions; changes in interhemispheric communication alter access and control; and the timing of voluntary movement can be modeled through ongoing neural accumulation\. These methodologically diverse findings establish neural causal relevance and increasingly specific brain dependence\. Yet an instrument dualist can expect neural dependence too, so the findings are largely shared evidence rather than a direct test of the two ontologies\. The strongest dualist reply is that the brain is an instrument: the subject acts only through it\. That reply defeats any inference from mediation alone\. It does not, without more, make the neural record evidence for the added controller\. The interactionist accounts assessed here have not yet supplied a validated controller\-specific difference or independent support for the controller&\#x27;s role in these public outcomes\. The findings therefore give the controller no incremental causal\-explanatory support over a no\-controller brain\-dependent account\. Observational equivalence remains neutral between them; any provisional preference then rests on economy, testability, and research productivity, not on a disproof of dualism\. This conclusion does not solve phenomenal consciousness, assume global physical sufficiency, or refute independently supported dualist theories\.
