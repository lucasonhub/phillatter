# Buckling behavior and interpretable machine\-learning of CFRP\-strengthened circular steel tubes under axial compression

**Autores:** Wenyi Yang; Xu Jiang; Qiang Xu

**Data:** 2026-10-17 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

This study develops a physics\-informed and interpretable data\-driven framework for predicting the axial buckling capacity of circular steel tubes strengthened with longitudinal CFRP strips\. A validated finite element \(FE\) model against test results was used to generate 196 parametric samples, and these data were combined with 190 experimental results from literature to establish a 386\-sample database for machine\-learning modeling\. The FE analysis shows that CFRP strengthening increases the sectional flexural stiffness and can improve the ultimate load by up to 49\.57% for slender members\. For medium\-length columns, a distinct capacity jump of up to 30\.12% is observed, which is attributed to a transition from global flexural buckling to localized instability induced by the longitudinal stiffness constraint of high\-modulus CFRP strips\. Four prediction models \(XGBoost, BPNN, SVR and Stacking\) were optimized using four swarm\-intelligence algorithms to overcome the sensitivity of model performance to hyperparameter selection\. Pearson correlation analysis identifies the slenderness ratio as the dominant capacity\-reducing variable \( r = \-0\.81\), whereas the number of CFRP layers, the diameter\-to\-thickness ratio and the steel yield strength show positive correlations with buckling resistance \( r = 0\.25, 0\.25 and 0\.13, respectively\)\. Among all optimized models, AVOA\-XGBoost provides the best overall performance, with a testing R 2 of 0\.925, a MAE of 18\.40 kN and a RMSE of 30\.11 kN\. SHAP analysis further confirms that the learned feature effects are consistent with structural stability theory\. The proposed framework offers both mechanistic insight into CFRP\-assisted instability control and a practical surrogate model for the rehabilitation design of steel tubular members\.
