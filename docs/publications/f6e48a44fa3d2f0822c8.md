# Enhancing immunogenicity: Evaluating the polio antigen immune response in hexavalent vaccine formulations

**Autores:** Tiza Wuri Mawaddah; Elisa D\. Pratiwi; Arif Rahman Sadjuri; Dimas Tri Nugroho; Arip Hidayat; Acep Riza Wijayadikusumah; Astria Novitasari Nidom; Zakiyyan I\. Ayyuba; Kadek Rachmawati; Suwarno Suwarno; Jola Rahmahani; Setyarina Indrasari; Reviany V\. Nidom; Chairul Anwar Nidom

**Data:** 2026-10-15 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Hexavalent vaccines simplify childhood immunization by combining antigens against six diseases in a single formulation; however, the immunogenicity of each component must be evaluated\. This study assessed poliovirus\-specific immunoglobulin G \(IgG\) responses elicited by candidate hexavalent vaccines containing different doses of Sabin strain\-derived inactivated poliovirus vaccine \(sIPV\)\. A preclinical study was conducted using 40 Wistar rats allocated to five groups: hexavalent vaccine containing low\-, medium\-, or high\-dose sIPV; pentavalent vaccine combined with sIPV; or adjuvant control\. Each group comprised four male and four female rats\. The animals received primary immunization at week 0 and booster doses at weeks 4 and 8\. Serotype\-specific IgG titers were measured at weeks 0, 4, 8, and 12 using indirect enzyme\-linked immunosorbent assay\. Antibody responses were analyzed using repeated\-measures analysis of variance\. Titers against poliovirus serotypes 1, 2, and 3 were negligible at baseline and remained low at week 4\. Marked responses were observed in all vaccinated groups at week 8, followed by lower titers at week 12\. Significant between\-group differences were detected at weeks 8 and 12 for serotype 1 \(p&lt;0\.001 and p=0\.010\), serotype 2 \(both p&lt;0\.001\), and serotype 3 \(both p=0\.010\)\. However, no consistent dose–response pattern was observed across serotypes, time points, or sexes\. All animals survived and gained body weight during follow\-up\. The candidate hexavalent formulations elicited marked poliovirus\-specific binding\-IgG responses, supporting further preclinical evaluation using validated serological and serotype\-specific neutralization assays\.
