# Deep learning\-based forecasting and model comparison of caisson tilt using numerical simulation–derived time\-series data

**Autores:** Saeyon Kim; Jihoon Kim; Heejung Youn

**Data:** 2026-10-13 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Abstract Accurate forecasting of caisson tilt during construction is important for ensuring structural stability and effective construction management\. However, reliable prediction remains challenging when only limited early\-stage observations are available\. In this study, the caisson tilt forecasting problem was formulated as an early\-stage limited observation forecasting task using time\-series data generated from numerical simulations\. A staged construction model was developed using PLAXIS2D, and construction\-related uncertainties in deep cement mixing \(DCM\)\-improved ground, riprap, and heaving soil were incorporated through Latin Hypercube Sampling, resulting in a total of 1,000 analysis cases\. Stage\-wise tilt time series over 28 construction stages were extracted\. A masking strategy, in which only the initial observations were provided as input was applied to compare the forecasting performance of a statistical model \(ARIMA\) and deep learning models \(LSTM, TCN, and Transformer\)\. Model performance was evaluated over the forecast horizon using MAE, RMSE, and $$\{R\}^\{2\}$$ \. The deep learning models consistently outperformed ARIMA under all conditions, and the TCN model showed stable forecasting performance even under highly limited early\-stage observations\. The LSTM model demonstrated improved accuracy as more observations became available\.
