# Mass as topological charge: an axiomatic framework for the equivalence principle and topological phase transitions in spiral spacetime

**Autores:** liwei ji

**Data:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

This paper establishes an axiomatic differential\-geometric framework for spiral spacetime\. Static mass is defined as a conserved flux charge of spacetime singularities\. Under explicitly stated divergence\-free conditions, its invariance under divergence\-preserving diffeomorphisms yields a conservation\-law interpretation of mass conservation with geometric\-flux structure\. Within this framework, gravitational and inertial mass are represented as two response projections of the same topological charge\. The equivalence principle is not derived from the axioms; rather, the framework provides a common mathematical carrier for it and yields a falsifiable corollary: gravitational and inertial mass must modulate synchronously and in equal proportion under external driving\. Parametric\-resonance\-driven topological phase transitions are investigated using Floquet stability theory\. Under testable phenomenological hypotheses H1–H3, two quantitative residual\-mass predictions are presented, with residual mass fractions ranging from 2\.58 % to 3\.93 % \. A four\-level rescaling\-gain principle is formulated as an efficiency optimization constrained by global conservation laws\. The mass\-energy balance requires an open\-system flux\-exchange channel, stated explicitly as an open validity postulate\. Five falsifiable experimental predictions are provided for future verification\.
