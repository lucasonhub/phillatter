# Balanced Nutrition Education to Prevent Heart Disease at SMPN 1 Kampar

**Autores:** Yessi Marlina; Falinda Oktariani

**Data:** 2026-10-10 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Background: Adolescence is a critical period in establishing healthy lifestyle patterns that will determine health status in adulthood\. Coronary heart disease can be prevented early through proper nutrition education\. Aims: This community service activity aims to improve nutrition literacy and form healthy eating behaviors among SMPN 1 Kampar students through education on the My Plate \(Isi Piringku\) concept as an effort to prevent heart disease from adolescence\. Methods: Implementation methods include observation, preparation of My Plate module, pre\-test and post\-test questionnaires, balanced nutrition education through interactive counseling, and poster design assignments\. The activity was attended by 26 students of SMPN 1 Kampar\.Results: Pre\-test results showed an average student knowledge score of 51\.30 \(±17\.89\), which increased to 55\.25 \(±9\.74\) in the post\-test\. The decrease in standard deviation indicates a more homogeneous distribution of knowledge among students following the intervention\. Post\-test improvements were observed across key indicators, particularly in understanding balanced nutrition principles, identifying protein sources, and knowing daily water intake recommendations\. Conclusion: This activity successfully increased students&\#x27; knowledge about My Plate principles and is expected to form healthy eating behaviors to prevent heart disease in the future\.
