# Integrity\-gated Multimodal Attention\-capsule Fusion for Vehicle Coordination and Infrastructure Health Monitoring

**Autores:** Sunkavilli Vijaya Nirmala; Subhani Shaik; Yalla Venkat

**Data:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Current vehicle\-to\-infrastructure \(V2I\) research increasingly relies on synchronized cooperative sensing, but no existing public benchmark was identified that simultaneously provides co\-referential vehicle targets and infrastructure\-health annotations for the joint problem studied here\.The proposed V2I\-MACF\-IG \(Integrity\-Gated Multimodal Attention\-Capsule Fusion for V2I coordination and infrastructure health monitoring\) is therefore evaluated as a proof\-of\-concept scenario\-paired learning framework rather than as a physically synchronized V2I validation\.Its methodological contribution is an integrity\-gated scenario\-pairing approach that partitions source scenes and sensor recordings before pairing, preserves source\-record independence, audits operating\-state compatibility, and prevents adjacent frames or windows from crossing data splits\.Vehicle\-side colour imagery, point clouds, trajectories, and inertial features are combined with infrastructure\-side vibration, acoustic, and environmental descriptors\.Scale\-Invariant Feature Transform \(SIFT\), Speeded\-Up Robust Features \(SURF\), PointNet, Fast Fourier Transform \(FFT\), and Autoregressive Integrated Moving Average \(ARIMA\) representations are projected to a common embedding, refined by dense\-residual channel/spatial attention, and routed through capsule layers to separate coordination and infrastructure\-health heads\.Across seeds 42, 123, and 2025, coordination accuracy/F1 was 0\.992 \+/\-0\.002/0\.988\+/\-0\.002 and maintenance accuracy/F1 was 0\.988 \+/\-0\.003/0\.983\+/\-0\.003, with a maintenance false\-alarm rate of 0\.037 \+/\-0\.002\.Missing\-modality, noise, delay, ablation, and vehicle\-side transfer tests show measurable degradation\.Recent V2X benchmarks from 2025\-2026 are used only for scope\-aware context because their datasets, tasks, labels, and metrics are not directly comparable\.The reported results support leakage\-controlled task\-coupled learning within the constructed scenario\-pairing protocol; pairing\-disruption controls \(random\-label, shuffled\-partner, cross\-class\) collapse to near\-chance performance and an independent infrastructure\-holdout evaluation shows only limited degradation, together supporting genuine cross\-modal dependence rather than label leakage\.The findings still do not establish physically synchronized or field\-deployed V2I cooperation, and a head\-to\-head comparison against a post\-2024 cooperative\-fusion baseline under an identical protocol remains outstanding\.
