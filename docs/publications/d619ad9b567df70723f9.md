# Emodin attenuates CFA\-induced inflammatory pain in association with suppression of the Cx3cl1/Cx3cr1/p38 MAPK signaling axis

**Autores:** Xiang Zheng; Jiabao Dai; Changyuan Mao; Weidong Zhang; Jing Huang; Shuqin Hu

**Data:** 2026-10-14 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Objectives To investigate the analgesic effect of emodin on Complete Freund’s Adjuvant \(CFA\)\-induced inflammatory pain and to clarify the underlying molecular mechanism\. Methods Emodin was administered intraperitoneally or prophylactically to attenuate CFA\-induced inflammatory pain\. RNA\-seq was performed to identify the biological functions of differentially expressed genes in mouse dorsal root ganglion \(DRG\) tissues\. The expression levels of Cx3cl1/Cx3cr1 were evaluated by RT\-qPCR, Western blotting, and immunofluorescence\. Pharmacological and genetic approaches were employed to block Cx3cr1 and assess its effect on pain thresholds in CFA\-treated mice\. Hematoxylin and eosin \(H&amp;E\) staining was used to examine the infiltration of immune and inflammatory cells in dorsal root ganglion \(DRG\) tissues before and after emodin treatment\. Following emodin treatment, the levels of p\-p38/p38 in DRG tissues were determined by Western blotting, and the mRNA levels of the pro\-inflammatory cytokines Tnf\-α , Il\-1β , and Il\-6 in DRG tissues were measured by RT\-qPCR\. Results Both therapeutic and prophylactic administration of emodin effectively alleviated CFA\-induced mechanical and thermal hypersensitivity\. Emodin treatment suppressed the CFA\-induced upregulation of Cx3cl1/Cx3cr1 mRNA and protein levels in the DRG\. Pharmacological and genetic blockade of Cx3cr1 effectively attenuated CFA\-induced hyperalgesia\. In addition, emodin markedly inhibited p\-p38/p38 protein expression in the DRG of mice with CFA\-induced inflammatory pain, reduced immune cell infiltration in DRG tissues, and downregulated the levels of the pro\-inflammatory cytokines Tnf\-α , Il\-1β , and Il\-6 \. Conclusion Emodin attenuated CFA\-induced inflammatory pain in mice, an effect associated with reduced Cx3cl1/Cx3cr1 signaling, decreased p38 MAPK activation, diminished immune\-cell infiltration in the DRG, and lower expression of the proinflammatory cytokines Tnf\-α , Il\-1β , and Il\-6 in DRG tissue\.
