# RESULTS OF SURGICAL TREATMENT OF PRIMARY MALIGNANT AND METASTATIC TUMORS OF THE HUMERAL DIAPHYSIS

**Autores:** Volodymyr V\. Protsenko; V\.V\. Voloshin A\.E\. Volkov; Yuriy L\. Sobolevskiy; Andrii S\. Kukharuk; Yevgen Solonitsyn

**Data:** 2026-10-17 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Introduction\. The view on the surgical treatment of primary malignant and metastatic tumors of the diaphyseal zone of the humerus after resection of the malignant tumor is ambiguous, the optimal choice of reconstructive method remains debatable due to the risk of complications and recurrence\. Aim\. To evaluate the results of treatment of patients with tumors of the humerus diaphysis in order to select methods of surgical treatment of tumors of this location\.Materials and methods\. We present 29 patients from three clinical groups who underwent different surgical treatment methods for primary malignant and metastatic bone tumors with damage to the diaphysis of the humerus\. The functional outcome of the limb was determined by the MSTS scale, the degree of pain syndrome was determined by the R\.G\. Watkins scale, the quality of life of patients was determined by the EORTC QLQ–C30 questionnaire, and patient survival was determined by the Kaplan\-Meier method\.Results\. The functional outcome of the operated upper limb \(according to the MSTS score\) after endoprosthetic replacement of the humeral shaft ranged from 84% to 91\.2%; after reinforced metal osteosynthesis, from 70% to 86%; and after transosseous extrafocal osteosynthesis using a rod external fixation device, from 66% to 72\.4%\. Patients’ quality of life \(according to the EORTC QLQ\-C30\) improved after endoprosthetic replacement of the humeral shaft from 30 to 80 points, after reinforced osteosynthesis from 40 to 72 points, and after transosseous extrafocal osteosynthesis from 30 to 66 points\. The three\-year overall survival rate of patients in the group after endoprosthetics was 81\.8±13\.6%, in the group after reinforced osteosynthesis – 62\.7±15\.8%, in the group after the use of external fixation devices – 12\.2±11\.1% \(p&gt;0\.05\)\.Conclusions\. Segmental resection of the humeral shaft followed by reconstruction using an individual segmental endoprosthesis is indicated for primary malignant tumors\. Metal osteosynthesis using a rod external fixation device is indicated for multiple bone metastases when a pathological fracture has occurred, in order to allow radiotherapy to the metastatic lesion in the humerus and subsequent limb\-sparing reconstructive surgery\.
