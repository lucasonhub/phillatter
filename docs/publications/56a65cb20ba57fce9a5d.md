# ON ART, CULTURES, AND REEXISTENCE: VIEWS ON THE MOVIMENTO NEGRO EDUCADOR \(MNE\) IN CONTEMPORARY TIMES

**Autores:** María Laura Pardo; Ana Lucia Silva Souza; Paulo Paulo Melgaço da Silva Júnior

**Data:** 2026-10-15 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

In this issue, we present an interview with Professors Ana Lúcia Silva Souza\(Federal University of Bahia\), Professor Paulo Melgaco da Silva Júnior \(MunicipalTheatre of Rio de Janeiro\), and Professor María Laura Pardo \(CONICET‑FFyL‑UBA\)\.In this interview, the professors discuss themes linked to Professor Nilma Lino Gomes’swork on the Movimento Negro Educador, which lends its name to the research team towhich the interviewees belong\. Topics such as art as a vehicle of liberation and identity,the importance of body and discourse as manifestations of subjectivity, vulnerable groupswho share struggles with the Black movement, and South–South relations are exploredfrom a critical, intersectional, and decolonial perspective\.
