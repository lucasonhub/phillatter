# Retracing the olive \(Olea europaea L\. subsp\. europaea\) origin and its early history diffusion among the civilizations overlooking the Mediterranean coasts: an integrated approach

**Autores:** Luca Lombardo; G\. Godino; Samanta Zelasco

**Data:** 2026-10-12 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

The olive tree is one of the most important crops in the world, but even more, one of the most identitarian plant for the countries of the Mediterranean basin\. In this article, the vast consultation of scientific literature, archaeological, historical and anecdotal sources, allowed to outline a global reasoned vision on the olive origin, its spread during early history and how olive fruit fly may represent as proxy for tracing its early diffusion\. The reconstruction of the first forms of exploitation, propagation and trade routes yielded a chronologically coherent westward emergence of integrated olive\-oil economies and cultures throughout the Mediterranean Basin, with the exception of some independent cultivation events tied to the widespread diffusion of the wild olive tree from the end of the last Glacial Period\. Written sources, iconography, and archaeological installations further anchor this spatio\-temporal narrative, leading to the transition from \-wild\- olives gatherers to olive growers\.
