# WHEN THE BOTTLE DECIDES THE DOSE: DELIVERYCONSTRAINED INTRAVENOUS IMMUNOGLOBULIN ADMINISTRATION IN A CHILD WITH ANTI\-N\-METHYL\-DASPARTATE RECEPTOR ENCEPHALITIS

**Autores:** 

**Data:** 2026-10-12 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Intravenous immunoglobulin sits awkwardly between prescribing and pharmaceutics\.The dose is calculated from body weight, yet the product reaches the bedside in fixed\-volume glass bottles and is given through a rate\-limited infusion protocol\.We describe a 10\-year\-old boy weighing 30\.65 kg, already known to have anti\-N\-methyl\-D\-aspartate receptor \(NMDAR\) encephalitis, readmitted to a tertiary teaching hospital in northern Karnataka three months after his index event for a planned course of immunoglobulin\.Cerebrospinal fluid was positive for NMDAR antibody while paired serum was only weakly positive, and plain magnetic resonance imaging of the brain showed no neuroparenchymal abnormality; serological testing alone would have left the diagnosis unproven\.He received a 10% glycine\-stabilised human normal immunoglobulin supplied as 10 g in 100 mL, infused at a fixed 20 mL/h over five hours daily\.Each infusion therefore delivered 0\.33 g/kg, and five consecutive infusions delivered 50 g in total, or 1\.63 g/kg \-precisely what had been prescribed, and well short of the international consensus target of 2 g/kg, which would have required about 61 g in this child\.Structured review using the Pharmaceutical Care Network Europe classification version 9\.1 identified six drugrelated problems\.The dominant one was a dosing deviation produced by bottle size, the twenty\-four\-hour discard rule after puncture and a conservative fixed infusion rate rather than by any deliberate clinical decision\.Dual atypical antipsychotic exposure in a disorder associated with neuroleptic intolerance, prophylactic anticholinergic co\-prescription, absent corticosteroid\-sparing measures, missing live\-vaccine deferral advice and a recorded serum potassium of 6\.4 mEq/L that was never repeated, never investigated electrocardiographically and never commented upon accounted for the remainder\.Immunoglobulin presentations and paediatric infusion protocols deserve to be designed around the child rather than the container\.
