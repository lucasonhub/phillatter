# Trepp, Tenzin C\.: Harmonized Mind and Perception to Coherence, Sensory Integration and Conscious Awareness

**Autores:** 

**Data de descoberta:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** unknown

## Descrição do feed

Conscious cognition is better understood as active integration than as passive registration\. From a cognitive\-constructivist perspective, the mind organizes sensory signals, bodily states, memory, affect, and attention into a coherent world\-model that supports perception and action\. This construction does not imply that reality itself is cognitively produced: perceptual models remain constrained by mind\-independent sensory input and are continuously adjusted through interaction with the environment\. This paper develops a coherence\-based account of conscious awareness across four domains\. First, it characterizes consciousness as selective integration rather than simple filtering\. Second, it examines multisensory coordination and the role of cross\-modal information in stabilizing perception\. Third, it argues that extensive nonconscious processing prepares and selects contents for conscious access\. Fourth, it considers the adaptive value of integrated cognition for flexible action, social coordination, and planning\. The resulting picture treats conscious experience as an organized and embodied synthesis: a constructed but world\-constrained representation that converts heterogeneous inputs into a unified, meaningful, and actionable perspective\. \(direct link \)
