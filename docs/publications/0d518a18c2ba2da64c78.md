# Deep Learning Sandbox for Next\-Generation of Artists

**Autores:** Selcuk Cara

**Data:** 2026-10-10 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

This document introduces the &quot;Deep Learning Sandbox,&quot; an innovative, interdisciplinary stagecraft methodology developed and formulated by Selcuk Zvi Cara that bridges artificial intelligence principles with contemporary performing arts\. Conceived by Selcuk Zvi Cara, this framework conceptually evolves the historic foundations of the Actors Studio \(Strasberg/Stanislavski\) into a modern paradigm named &quot;The Global Performer Sandbox\.&quot; It completely restructures traditional performance training across cinema, theater, music theater, and all performing arts by replacing rigid classic models with a fluid, multi\-disciplinary, and cost\-effective incubation environment driven by an active, omnipresent camera lens for the next generation of artists\.
