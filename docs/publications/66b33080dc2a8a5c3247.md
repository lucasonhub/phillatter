# PUBLIC ADMINISTRATION MECHANISMS FOR ENSURING HIGHER EDUCATION QUALITY: RESULTS OF APPLICATION AND PROSPECTS FOR DEVELOPMENT

**Autores:** Д\. К\. Шутов

**Data:** 2026-10-15 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

The article examines the mechanisms of public administration for ensuring the quality of higher education in Ukraine through the prism of the classifications established in the scholarly discourse, as well as through the practices of their functioning and their actual impact on the international competitiveness of domestic higher education institutions\. The methodological basis of the research combines the analysis of regulatory and legal acts, programme documents of the international and national levels, statistical data of the National Agency for Higher Education Quality Assurance, and a comparative analysis of the dynamics of the positioning of Ukrainian universities in the leading international rankings during 2022–2026\. A contradiction has been identified between the architectonics of building the higher education quality assurance system and the comparatively low positioning of Ukrainian universities in international rankings \(Quacquarelli Symonds World University Rankings; Times Higher Education World University Rankings; Academic Ranking of World Universities, ShanghaiRanking\)\. In particular, it has been established that the number of Ukrainian universities represented in the QS World University Rankings decreased from 11 in 2023 to 4 in 2026, while the highest position of domestic institutions in the Times Higher Education ranking has consistently remained within the 801–1000 group despite the quantitative growth of their representation\. The exhaustion of the potential of the traditional regulatory\-legal, organisational\- administrative and economic mechanisms of public administration in the higher education sphere has been substantiated, along with the need to search for new mechanisms of public administration for ensuring quality\. The article also offers proposals on the necessity of applying innovative mechanisms for ensuring the quality of higher education, namely those grounded in the toolkit of artificial intelligence, big data analytics, and robotic process automation, the practical significance of which is confirmed by international experience and the analytical priorities of the national quality assurance system\.
