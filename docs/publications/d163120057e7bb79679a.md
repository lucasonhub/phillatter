# BO‘LAJAK ELEKTR MUHANDISLARINI INTEGRATIV O‘QITISH SAMARADORLIGI: IKKI YILLIK, UCH UNIVERSITETDA O‘TKAZILGAN PEDAGOGIK TAJRIBA

**Autores:** Xoliyor Sayyid Safar o‘g‘li Safarov

**Data:** 2026-10-10 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Background\. Education–industry integration is widely advocated in engineering education, but quantitative evidence for specific power\-engineering courses is scarce\. Aim\. To test whether integrative teaching of the course “Electric Power Systems and Networks” in a university–industry integration environment, based on three author’s methodologies \(Apospasmatika, InterAkt, Spiral Regressus–Progressus\), raises students’ level of professional competence compared with traditional teaching\. A parallel pedagogical experiment was conducted in 2023/24 and 2024/25 at three universities in Uzbekistan \(Karshi, Bukhara, Navoi\); 856 students \(experimental group, n = 432; control group, n = 424\) and 141 enterprise specialists as experts took part\. Preparedness was assessed on a four\-level scale \(low, medium, good, high\) by four criteria and 13 indicators; InterAkt cards were scored by three sources \(self 0\.2, peer 0\.3, expert 0\.5\)\. Groups were compared with Pearson’s χ² test \(df = 3\), Cramér’s V and a relative growth coefficient K; a Mann–Whitney test served as a robustness check for the ordinal scale\. The groups were equivalent at entry \(χ² = 0\.065, p &gt; 0\.05\)\. At the final test the distributions differed significantly \(χ² = 51\.282, p &lt; 0\.001; V = 0\.245; Mann–Whitney p &lt; 0\.001; Cliff’s δ = 0\.235\)\. The mean score was 3\.713 vs 3\.321 \(K = 11\.81 %\)\. The share of students at the good and high levels rose from 40\.7 % to 60\.2 % in the experimental groups and stayed at 40\.8 % in the control groups; the share at the low level fell from 23\.6 % to 6\.7 % \(control: from 23\.6 % to 21\.7 %\)\. The effect replicated in both years \(K = 11\.66 %; 12\.02 %\) and at all three universities \(11\.49– 12\.03 %\)\. Integrative teaching based on the three methodologies was associated with a statistically significant, replicated improvement in competence levels, with a small\-to\-medium effect size\.
