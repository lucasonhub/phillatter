# Usage of Fintech Adoption and Behavioural Consequences

**Autores:** Ms\. Soumya M and Dr\. P Radha

**Data:** 2026-10-13 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

The rapid advancement of financial technology \(FinTech\) has transformed the delivery and consumption of financial services, creating significant changes in the way individuals conduct transactions, manage money, save, invest, and make financial decisions\. The present study examines the usage of FinTech adoption and its behavioural consequences among users, with particular emphasis on the technological, financial, and behavioural factors influencing adoption\. The study aims, first, to examine the level and determinants of FinTech adoption with reference to perceived usefulness, ease of use, convenience, trust, security, financial literacy, and social influence\. Second, it analyses the behavioural consequences of FinTech adoption in terms of spending behaviour, saving behaviour, investment behaviour, financial decision\-making, transaction frequency, and dependence on digital financial services\. The study adopts a descriptive and analytical research design and is primarily based on primary data collected through a structured questionnaire\. A sample of 200 respondents is considered for the study using a convenient sampling method\. The questionnaire uses a five\-point Likert scale to measure respondents&\#x27; perceptions regarding FinTech adoption and its behavioural outcomes\. Descriptive statistics, including frequency, percentage, mean, and standard deviation, are used to analyse the demographic characteristics and response patterns\. Cronbach&\#x27;s Alpha is applied to examine the reliability of the measurement scales\. Pearson correlation is used to determine the association between FinTech adoption and its major determinants, while multiple regression analysis is employed to identify the significant factors influencing FinTech adoption\. Simple regression analysis is used to examine the relationship between FinTech adoption and behavioural consequences\. The findings indicate a relatively high level of FinTech adoption among respondents\. Convenience, perceived usefulness, and ease of use emerge as important determinants of adoption, while trust, security, and financial literacy also demonstrate significant relationships with FinTech usage\. The study further indicates that FinTech adoption is associated with important changes in financial behaviour, including increased transaction frequency, reduced dependence on cash, faster financial decision\-making, and changes in spending, saving, and investment practices\. Respondents also demonstrate considerable dependence on digital financial services for routine financial activities\. However, the influence of FinTech on impulsive financial decisions is comparatively moderate\. The study concludes that FinTech adoption represents more than technological acceptance; it is closely associated with changing pattern\.
