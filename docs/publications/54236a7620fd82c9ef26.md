# Radiologic recurrence after minimally invasive ventral hernia repair in patients with class III obesity: a single\-center retrospective cohort study

**Autores:** Ahsan Ahmed; Faizan Khalid; Ali Sadeghi; Marlon Rodriguez Valladares; Marlen Flores; Valeria Loja Pauta; Cesar Alas; Cosman Camilo Mandujano

**Data:** 2026-10-12 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Background Obesity is a known risk factor for complications and recurrence after ventral hernia repair \(VHR\)\. Many surgeons defer elective repair in patients with class III obesity based on body mass index \(BMI\) thresholds\. We evaluated postoperative outcomes and factors associated with recurrence after minimally invasive VHR in this high\-risk population\. Methods This single\-center retrospective cohort study included consecutive adult patients with BMI ≥40 kg/m 2 who underwent predominantly minimally invasive VHR between January 1, 2012, and December 31, 2024, and had postoperative radiologic follow\-up by computed tomography \(CT\)\. The primary outcome was radiologic hernia recurrence\. Secondary outcomes included 30\-day complications and postoperative weight trajectory\. Between\-group comparisons used Fisher’s exact or χ 2 tests for categorical variables and Welch t\-test or Mann\-Whitney U test for continuous variables, as appropriate; two\-sided p &lt; 0\.05 was considered significant\. Results Fifty\-two patients \(mean BMI 45\.0 ± 5\.4 kg/m 2 \), 80\.8% female, met the inclusion criteria\. Radiologic recurrence occurred in 9 patients \(17\.3%; CI 9\.4%–29\.7%\) at a median imaging follow\-up of 793 days \(IQR 412–1381\) in the overall cohort\. Recurrence was observed exclusively in incisional \(2/16, 12\.5%\) or recurrent incisional hernias \(7/21, 33\.3%\) \(p = 0\.028\), with no recurrences in primary hernias \(0/15\)\. 30\-day complications occurred in 11\.5% of patients\. Patients with recurrence experienced significantly greater postoperative weight loss than those without \(15\.6% vs\. 4\.6% of total body weight; p = 0\.015\)\. Operative technique and mesh\-related variables were not significantly associated with recurrence \(all p &gt; 0\.25\), although the study was underpowered to detect such associations\. Conclusion Minimally invasive VHR in patients with class III obesity was associated with low short\-term morbidity, but the mid\-term radiologic recurrence rate of 17\.3% is on the higher end of published estimates and should not be characterized as low\. Recurrence occurred exclusively in incisional and recurrent incisional hernias, reaching 24\.3% when primary hernias were excluded; associations with technical factors and BMI were not statistically significant in this small sample\. Greater postoperative weight loss was associated with recurrence, though reverse causation and ascertainment bias cannot be excluded\. Given the small sample size, these findings are hypothesis\-generating and warrant confirmation in larger, prospective studies\.
