# Time‐Resolved Physically Unclonable Functions Enabled by Solvent‐Free Scalable Boron‐Based Ultralong Phosphorescent Systems for Machine‐Learning‐Resilient Optical Encryption

**Autores:** Changsong Xu; Daxiang Shi; Guoyi Pan; Ru‐Jia Wang; Jiankang Qin; Jin Peng; Peng Chen; Yingjuan Sun; Wenjing Lin; Wen‐Cheng Chen; Xiaofeng Lin; Dingshan Yu; Guobin Yi

**Data:** 2026-10-12 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Anti\-counterfeiting technologies are increasingly challenged by advances in imaging and machine learning, exposing the fundamental limitations of conventional static optical security systems\. Here, a time\-resolved physically unclonable function \(TR\-PUF\) system is established, transforming optical authentication from static pattern recognition to high\-dimensional temporal encoding\. Central to this approach, boron\-based room\-temperature phosphorescent \(RTP\) materials are developed via covalent coupling with coumarin derivatives using a solvent\-free strategy that is readily scalable to kilogram\-level production, exhibiting a maximum phosphorescence lifetime of over 2 s, an ultralong afterglow visible for up to 30 s, and color\-tunable emission\. The formation of rigid B\-O\-C linkages spatially isolates emissive centers, suppressing energy transfer and preserving temporally independent decay channels\-an essential prerequisite for high\-entropy time\-resolved encoding\. Randomly embedding these multi\-emissive RTP units into a polymer matrix forms a complex encryption structure that enables a dual\-entropy encoding mechanism with both spectral and temporal dimensions, generating dynamic time keys for authentication\. Moreover, we propose a matched time\-gated authentication framework that achieves &gt;99% identification accuracy within 1\.2 s while exhibiting resistance against the tested generative machine\-learning attacks\. This work proposes a practical materials platform and a time\-gated authentication strategy, offering a scalable technological path for the development of dynamic optical encryption tags\.
