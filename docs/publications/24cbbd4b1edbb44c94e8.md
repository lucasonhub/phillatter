# Area\-optimized crossover\-free n\-bit coplanar ripple carry adder in quantum\-dot cellular automata

**Autores:** Md\. Abdullah\-Al\-Shafi

**Data:** 2026-10-12 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Abstract Quantum\-Dot Cellular Automata \(QCA\) provides a potential post\-CMOS platform for compact and low\-power arithmetic circuits\. This paper presents compact and area\-conscious coplanar n\-bit Ripple Carry Adder \(RCA\) architectures based on a modified and structurally optimized QCA full adder cell derived from a previously reported design\. The modified full adder cell is used as a reusable building block to develop and systematically evaluate crossover\-free coplanar 2\-bit, 4\-bit, and 8\-bit RCA architectures\. The full adder cell requires 14 cells, occupies an extent of 0\.014 μm², and achieves a quantum cost of 0\.0078 with a latency of 0\.75 clock cycles\. The resulting 2\-bit, 4\-bit, and 8\-bit RCAs require 64, 118, and 376 cells, respectively, with occupied extents of 0\.09, 0\.15, and 0\.56 μm² and latencies of 1\.50, 2\.00, and 3\.75 clock cycles, respectively, without crossover structures\. Relative to the evaluated state\-of\-the\-art baselines, the 4\-bit and 8\-bit RCAs achieve average reductions of 85\.40% and 64\.53% in cell intricacy and 96\.83% and 76\.64% in quantum cost, respectively\. Energy\-dissipation analysis using QCADesigner\-E further characterizes the proposed architectures, while direct comparison with literature energy values is treated cautiously because of differences in estimation methodologies\. The crossover\-free organization supports compact and regular arithmetic realization; however, the present study is limited to QCA simulation, physical and energy estimation, and architectural comparison\. Potential relevance to ML\-oriented nano\-computing is discussed conceptually and is not validated through direct ML workload implementation or physical fabrication\.
