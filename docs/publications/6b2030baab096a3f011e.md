# Associations of Dopamine Agonist Dose and Anxiety Symptoms with Impulse Control Behaviors in Parkinson’s Disease

**Autores:** Xin Shu; Pinjun Li; Xin Zou; Lei Lei

**Data:** 2026-10-10 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Background: This cross\-sectional study examined the association of impulse control disorder \(ICD\) screening positivity with anxiety symptom severity and dopamine agonist \(DA\) dosage in patients with Parkinson’s disease \(PD\)\.Methods: We consecutively studied 112 patients with PD admitted from July 2024 to June 2025\. Impulse control behaviors were screened with the QUIP\-RS; patients meeting the standard cut\-off for at least one behavior formed the ICD group and the remainder the nICD group\. Levodopa equivalent daily doses \(LEDD, DA\-LEDD and Total\-LEDD\) were calculated, and anxiety, depression, motor severity, apathy and probable RBD were assessed with validated scales \(HAMA, HAMD, UPDRS\-III, AES\-C and RBDSQ\)\.Results: Statistically significant differences were observed between groups in terms of DA\-LEDD, Total\-LEDD, HAMA and H\-Y staging \(P &lt; \.001, P = \.04, P &lt; \.001, P = \.02\)\. Spearman correlation confirmed positive associations of HAMA with ICD status and with the dose variables \(all P &lt; \.05\)\. In the final multivariable model \(DA\-LEDD and HAMA\), DA\-LEDD \(OR=1\.03, 95% CI 1\.01–1\.06, P = \.01; equivalently OR≈1\.36, 95% CI 1\.08–1\.72 per 10 mg/day increase\) and HAMA \(OR=1\.19, 95% CI 1\.07–1\.31, P = \.001\) were independently associated with ICD screening positivity\. The four ICD subtypes were too few for inferential comparison and are described descriptively\.Conclusion: ICD screening positivity was positively associated with anxiety severity and higher DA\-LEDD\. Because the design is cross\-sectional and screening\-based, these associations are hypothesis\-generating and not causal\.
