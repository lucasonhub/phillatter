# Artificial Intelligence and Corporate Energy Efficiency: A Pathway to Sustainable Manufacturing—Evidence from Chinese Firms

**Autores:** Yahan Shi; Yinchao Zhang; Xizhe Xu; Xiaofeng Xu; Da Tong

**Data:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

In the context of global efforts to achieve sustainable development and carbon neutrality, improving industrial energy efficiency has become a cornerstone of green transformation\. This study examines how corporate AI\-related disclosure intensity is associated with sustainable energy efficiency in manufacturing firms\. Using panel data on Chinese A\-share listed manufacturing firms from 2012 to 2024, we measure corporate energy efficiency using energy intensity based on standard coal\. AI\-related disclosure intensity is constructed from the frequency of AI\-related terms in firms’ annual reports and logarithmically transformed\. Based on panel regression, process innovation association analysis, moderation analysis, and ownership\-based subgroup analysis, this study finds that AI\-related disclosure intensity is negatively associated with energy intensity\. Higher AI\-related disclosure intensity is also linked to a higher share of process innovation, indicating enhanced attention to production process improvement\. Data element utilization weakens the negative association between AI\-related disclosure intensity and energy intensity, suggesting a moderating effect\. The negative relationship persists across both state\-owned and non\-state\-owned firms\. These findings contribute to the understanding of how AI\-related disclosure intensity reflects corporate energy management and promotes sustainable manufacturing\. They offer actionable implications for firms and policymakers seeking to advance green innovation and achieve a sustainable economy\.
