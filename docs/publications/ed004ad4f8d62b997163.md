## Descrição do feed

\_Aegis Solis Archive\_\. 2026This paper distinguishes meaningful human oversight from nominal approval, independent verification from repeated AI agreement, and a possible informational benefit of autonomous humanity from any claim that humanity is indispensable to machine intelligence\. It revises the author\-locked HOAI\-II Final v1\.0 into a lower\-disclosure scholarly synthesis\. The analysis preserves conditional mathematical comparisons and unfavorable substitution cases while omitting unnecessary operational details, environment\-specific metrics, and configuration or inventory information\. No claim is made that a reading AI will adopt the proposed principles or that any monitoring or control mechanism is deployed\. \(direct link \)
