# Leveraging Meaningful Intrusion Detection Mechanism for Voice Traffic Patterns

**Autores:** Sin\-Ban Ho; Aizam Alinoordin; Liana Ameerah Rosli; Said Suleiman Naseer Al Harthy; Ian Chai

**Data:** 2026-10-14 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Voice over IP \(VoIP\) communication has emerged as an important way of communicating as a modern digital medium but it has been facing a lot of cyber threats because of the protocols involved and the real\-time aspect associated with it\. conventional Intrusion Detection Systems \(IDS\) do not cope well with VoIP traffic, as signature\-based solutions fail to handle various types of intrusions, while anomaly\-based approaches tend to produce a high false positive rate\. In this research, a hybrid intrusion detection mechanism is integrated, a lightweight and application\-based system built around signature matching and anomaly detection to offer advanced protection to VoIP traffic\. The system permits traffic analysis offline, based on Packet Capture \(PCAP\) or Comma Separated Values \(CSV\) files, as well as active traffic observation, which is provided using two python\-based user interfaces, a desktop Tkinter interface and a web interface based on Flask\. The predefined Session Initiation Protocol \(SIP\) or Real\-time Transport Protocol \(RTP\) signature rules namely SIP Flood, RTP Injection, and SIP Brute\-force are used as part of hybrid detection engine together with anomaly discovery based on one\-class SVM\. Other options available are a SIP honeypot listener, email alert notifications and there is a complete logging of incidents\. The historical inspection are facilitated by such visualizations as protocol distribution or traffic trend analysis\. Through testing, the system is effective in the detection of the VoIP attacks without tuning of the thresholds, and at low false alarm rates\. This hybrid IDS demonstrates the feasibility of the signature and anomaly\-based detection integration as a way of ensuring VoIP traffic monitoring and as a baseline of any further enhancement with the help of more advanced machine learning or enterprise use\.
