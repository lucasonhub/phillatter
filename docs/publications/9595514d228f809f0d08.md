# Pulsed Oxidative–Reductive Electrolysis Over Dual Cu and Co Single‐Atom Sites for Urea Electrosynthesis

**Autores:** Putri Ramadhany; Peiqi Qiu; Thành Trần‐Phú; Denny Gunawan; Bernt Johannessen; Rosalie K\. Hocking; Yan Jiao; Rose Amal; Rahman Daiyan

**Data:** 2026-10-13 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

ABSTRACT Electrochemical C─N coupling of CO 2 with nitrogen species offers a sustainable route to urea and related chemicals but is constrained under steady‐state electrolysis by mismatched reaction kinetics\. Here, pulsed oxidative–reductive electrolysis temporally coordinates CO 2 reduction and NH 3 oxidation, regulating the generation and coexistence of carbon‐ and nitrogen‐derived intermediates\. Co/CuNC, containing atomically dispersed Co and Cu sites within a nitrogen‐doped carbon \(NC\), achieves a total C─N faradaic efficiency of 42 ± 4%, dominated by urea \(20 ± 2%\) and ethyl urea \(16 ± 2%\), with respective yield rates \(YR\) of 295 ± 25 and 50 ± 5 mmol h −1 g cat −1 \. In situ synchrotron x‐ray absorption spectroscopy \(XAS\) reveals potential‐dependent changes in Cu and Co oxidation states and coordination environments, while in situ infrared spectroscopy identifies pulse‐regulated \*CO and \*NH x intermediates and suggests concurrent electrochemical and Wöhler‐type pathways\. Density functional theory calculations reveal complementary site functions: Cu favors \*NH 2 formation, whereas Co more strongly stabilizes \*NH x and \*CO intermediates, with \*CO also accessible at Cu sites\. These findings establish temporal coordination of reactant activation, intermediate populations, and active‐site states as a mode‐enabled strategy for accessing higher‐order C─N products\.
