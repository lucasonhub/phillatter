# ALEX\-GWO: Reinforcement\-learning\-driven Adaptive Grey Wolf Optimization with Landscape\-aware Local\-optima Escape for Large\-scale Global Optimization

**Autores:** Rajpreet Kaur; Rupali Dhir

**Data:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Large\-scale global optimization exposes a structural weakness of the grey wolf optimizer: its convergence coefficient follows a schedule fixed in advance, the three leading wolves collapse into a single attraction basin long before the budget is spent, and that loss of diversity is neither detected nor repaired\.Published variants answer the symptoms with fixed remedies applied on a schedule rather than on evidence, so their cost is paid whether or not the population is trapped\.This paper presents ALEX\-GWO \(Adaptive Learning\-driven EXploration Grey Wolf Optimizer\), which closes the loop between observation and control\.A six\-component search\-state vector summarises progress, diversity, success rate, improvement, stagnation and leader dispersion; population\-level parameter control, per\-wolf success memory and an epsilon\-greedy Q\-learning selector consume it at three time scales; and a composite landscapetrapping score releases a targeted escape only when several independent indicators agree\.On CEC&\#x27;2013 and CEC&\#x27;2010 at 1000 dimensions under an equal budget of 3\.0 x 10^6 evaluations, ALEX\-GWO obtains the best average Friedman rank of the ten algorithms compared on the first suite \(2\.00\) and of the nine on the second \(1\.85\)a set including ADSL\-GWO, RL\-LGWO and IAGWO re\-implemented under an identical protocoland improves on canonical GWO by a median of four orders of magnitude\.It reaches a lower mean error than SHADE\-ILS on 13 of the 15 CEC&\#x27;2013 functions \(Wilcoxon p = 0\.048\) but is not separated from MOS, CC\-RDG3 or IAGWO after Holm correction, so the evidence supports competitive consistency rather than dominance\.ALEX\-GWO costs 11\.3% more runtime than canonical GWO, and removing landscape detection alone drops the escape success rate from 0\.68 to 0\.31\.A binary variant reaches 90\.32\-93\.75%accuracy under nested cross\-validation on four feature\-selection datasets while discarding 65\-97% of the features\.Code and raw results: https://github\.com/Rajpreet\-kaur1989/ALEXGWO\.
