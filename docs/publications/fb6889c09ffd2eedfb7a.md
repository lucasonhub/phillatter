# THE NETWORKING OF AI\-DRIVEN IMPROVED SANITATION ARCHITECTURE INNOVATIONS, STRATEGIES AND SOLUTIONS TOWARDS ODF IN BAUCHI BUILT ENVIRONMENT

**Autores:** Osunkunle Abdulmageed Abiodun; BELLO MUSBAU ADEWUMI; ALIYU MUNKAILA ABUBAKAR; FAITH GAYE MICHAEL; Idris Abdullahi Mohammed

**Data:** 2026-10-10 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

The quest for the pursuit of Open Defecation Free \(ODF\) status in Nigeria, particularly in states like Bauchi, has historically relied on conventional Community\-Led Total Sanitation \(CLTS\) approaches\. However, the sustainability of these interventions is often compromised by inadequate facility maintenance, poor design aesthetics, and lack of economic incentives\. This paper explores the integration of Artificial Intelligence \(AI\) and improved toilet architecture innovations as a transformative strategy for achieving and sustaining ODF in the Bauchi built environment\. By leveraging AI\-driven generative design, parametric modeling, and Internet of Things \(IoT\) predictive maintenance, architects and urban planners can develop culturally resonant, climate\-resilient, and economically viable sanitation facilities\. This research proposes a framework where AI algorithms analyze local topographical, climatic, and socio\-cultural data to generate optimized architectural designs for public and communal toilets\. Furthermore, the integration of AI\-powered digital twins and smart sensors facilitates real\-time monitoring of facility usage, structural integrity, and environmental impact\. The findings suggest that AI\-driven architectural innovations can significantly enhance community acceptance, reduce slippage from ODF status, and foster sustainable sanitation practices in Bauchi State\.
