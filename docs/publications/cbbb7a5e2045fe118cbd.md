# MEDIATA’LIM MUHITIDA BO‘LAJAK O‘QITUVCHILARNING MONO VA POLIPARADIGMAL TAFAKKURINI RIVOJLANTIRISHNING DIDAKTIK KONSEPSIYASINI ISHLAB CHIQISH

**Autores:** Turayeva Gulnoza Ergashevna

**Data:** 2026-10-13 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

This article develops a didactic concept for the development of mono\- and polyparadigmatic thinking of future teachers in the media education environment\. The relevance of the research, the main goals and objectives, and the impact of the modern media space on teachers&\#x27; thinking are analyzed\. The essence of the concepts of mono\- and polyparadigmatic thinking is revealed\. The main principles of the concept and ways of its implementation are outlined\.
