# THE EVOLUTION AND PARADIGMS OF DIAGNOSTIC MICROBIOLOGY

**Autores:** Juan Pablo Domínguez Enríquez; Cristian David Cusco Cuzco

**Data:** 2026-10-10 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Diagnostic microbiology has evolved from early observational microscopy to advanced molecular, biochemical, and immunodiagnostic methodologies\. This article traces the historical evolution and development of microbiological diagnostic techniques\. Key methods examined include light microscopy, differential staining \(such as Gram and acid\-fast stains\), solid culture media, colorimetric metabolic profiling \(API 20E\), and immunodiagnostic serotyping\. Finally, we analyze the role of modern genomic tools specifically 16S ribosomal RNA \(rRNA\) gene sequencing and phylogenetic reconstruction in establishing a definitive microbial taxonomy\. Together, these complementary paradigms form the foundation of contemporary clinical diagnosis\.
