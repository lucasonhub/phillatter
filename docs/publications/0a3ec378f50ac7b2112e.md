# Hybrid transformer and CNN for hyperspectral image classification with spectral band selection

**Autores:** Eman N\. Abdelhafez; Ahmed Hagag; Tamer Ahmed Abassy

**Data:** 2026-10-12 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Abstract Hyperspectral images \(HSI\) play a vital role in remote sensing applications such as surveillance and precision agriculture, yet they face challenges including limited labeled samples and class imbalance\. This paper presents a new method for HSI classification by integrating spectral band selection with advanced deep learning techniques\. First, we propose an optimized band selection method alongside data augmentation using crop\-and\-resize transformations to enhance feature diversity\. Second, we introduce a hybrid convolution\-transformer framework that combines Vision Transformer \(ViT\) and 2D\-CNN architectures, supplemented with a sequence aggregation layer to mitigate overfitting on small datasets\. This hybrid model effectively captures both spatial and spectral features while preserving critical spectral details\. Finally, we employ a support vector machine for robust classification\. Extensive experiments on three widely used HSI datasets demonstrate that our proposed ViT\-CNN\-BS framework achieves superior classification accuracy compared to state\-of\-the\-art few\-shot learning methods and traditional approaches\. Beyond achieving higher accuracy, our approach also reduces computational complexity, making it suitable for resource\-constrained applications\. The findings highlight the potential of hybrid deep learning frameworks for advancing remote sensing analytics, with possible extensions to environmental monitoring, disaster management, and precision agriculture\.
