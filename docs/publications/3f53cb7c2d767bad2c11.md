# Drug repurposing in hereditary ataxia

**Autores:** Lorenzo Cipriano; Matteo Rossi Sebastiano; Filippo Maria Santorelli

**Data:** 2026-10-12 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Hereditary ataxias are rare disorders, and a context where small cohorts and high development costs make drug repurposing a pragmatic route\. Technological advances are creating opportunities to complement pathway\-guided plausibility with more target\-supported and AI\-nominated mechanistically grounded candidates\. Also, digital twin and The Virtual Brain frameworks should be viewed in hereditary ataxias as future\-oriented research tools to integrate multimodal data, support stratification, and generate testable system\-level predictions in rare cohorts\.
