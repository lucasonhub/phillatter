# A SMOTE enhanced multi stream deep learning ensemble for credit card fraud detection

**Autores:** Lubna Abdulaziz Alharbi; Faizah Hameed Alsulami

**Data:** 2026-10-15 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Credit card fraud detection is highly imbalanced, making minority\-class metrics and deployment efficiency more informative than accuracy alone\. This study proposes SMOTE\-EnsembleNet \(SENet\), combining CNN, BiLSTM, and Transformer streams through learnable cross\-attention fusion\. Among 100%, 200%, and 500% SMOTE ratios with focal loss, 200% SMOTE achieved the best overall balance\. SENet obtained AUC\-ROC values of 0\.9967 \(95% CI 0\.9955–0\.9979\), 0\.9567 \(95% CI 0\.9542–0\.9592\), and 0\.9923 \(95% CI 0\.9911–0\.9935\) on ULB, IEEE\-CIS, and PaySim, respectively\. Compared with no SMOTE, AUC\-ROC improved by 1\.55, 1\.55, and 1\.34 percentage points, while cross\-attention improved AUC\-ROC by 0\.22 percentage points over concatenation\. Fold\-level significance testing \(\(p &lt; 0\.05\)\) was conducted against all tuned baselines\. SENet achieved 12\.4 ms inference latency per transaction on an NVIDIA A100 GPU while supporting interpretable error and feature\-importance analyses\. AUC\-ROC and AUC\-PR are threshold\-independent, whereas precision, recall, and F1\-score are reported separately in the experiments\. The 12\.4 ms latency applies only to the stated NVIDIA A100 configuration\. Cross\-attention additionally improved AUC\-PR by 1\.3 percentage points over concatenation, and the small differences between 200% and 500% SMOTE are interpreted together with fold\-level statistical tests\.
