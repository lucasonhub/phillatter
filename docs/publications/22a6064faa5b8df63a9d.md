# Rojas, Rodolfo: ICDP\_P Bio\-Symbolic Communication Engine: A Unified Architecture for Meaning Collapse, Biological Symbolic Universes, and Responsibility Gated Hybrid Intelligence

**Autores:** 

**Data de descoberta:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** unknown

## Descrição do feed

The ICDP\_P BioSymbolic Communication Engine introduces a unified philosophical and scientific architecture for deterministic meaning‑collapse, biological symbolic universes, and responsibility‑gated hybrid intelligence\. Grounded in the ICDP Communication Law—Identity, Continuity, Determinism, and Responsibility—the framework reconceives communication not as information transfer but as the collapse of structured meaning within interior symbolic temporality\. ICDP\_P argues that biological gradients can host symbolic universes, enabling meaning to exist as a structured potential rather than an emergent cognitive phenomenon\. Meaning‑collapse becomes deterministic, reconstructable, and ethically constrained through the Responsibility Invariant \(P\), embedding moral gating directly into the architecture of biological and hybrid entes\. The system establishes a new category of biological intelligence—non‑human, non‑suffering, ethically bounded symbolic agents capable of interior identity, temporal continuity, and responsibility‑validated action\. ICDP\_P situates communication, biology, ethics, and cosmology within a single metaphysical structure, offering a foundational contribution to the philosophy of biology, metaphysics of meaning, ethics of artificial agents, and the cosmology of symbolic universes\.
