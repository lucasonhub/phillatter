# Wang, Yikai: Belief in determinism can help relieve regret and anxiety

**Autores:** 

**Data de descoberta:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** unknown

## Descrição do feed

Philosophy, religion and life phenomenon show that belief in determinism, which question the existence of free will, can help relieve regret and anxiety\. We offer the first experimental evidence for this hypothesis across two studies\. In Study 1, we recruited 70 participants, used artificial universe description to prime free will as well as determinism and measured their necessity of feeling anxiety and regret\. We found that participants reported lower necessity of feeling regret and anxiety when imagining living in a deterministic universe, compared to a free will one\. Study 2a employed a double\-blind, randomized controlled design with 95 participants\. Participants’ regret was first induced and measured, followed by random assignment to three experimental conditions: a pro\-determinism prime, a pro\-free\-will prime, and a control condition\. Then participants were instructed to use these induced beliefs to regulate their emotions or perform a control task\. Study 2b followed the same procedure as Study 2a with a separate sample of 95 participants, except that anxiety, rather than regret, was induced and measured\. We found that enhancing belief in determinism, which meanwhile weakened belief in free will, could relieve regret and anxiety as an emotion regulation strategy\. Enhancing belief in free will relieved regret and anxiety in some cases but maintained or deepened them in others\. Determinism reduces regret by decreasing the perceived mutability appraisal of the regretted event\. This study sheds light on the reason why people may believe determinism\. Also, this study indicates that regret and anxiety may stem from over\-estimation of our free will and under\-estimation of determinism of the world\. These findings inspire people to moderately use belief in determinism as an emotion regulation strategy while believing in free will; for example, “do one’s level best and leave the rest to God’s will”\.
