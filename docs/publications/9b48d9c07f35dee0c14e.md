# Generative AI Literacy and Social Innovation Intention Among University Students: The Dual Mediating Roles of Social Opportunity Recognition and Social Entrepreneurial Self\-Efficacy

**Autores:** Jigar Nagvadia; Virendra N\. Chavda; Ms\. Supriya Vyas; Ms\. Jagruti Patni

**Data:** 2026-10-13 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

The growing global grand challenges and the prevalence of socioeconomic disparities have increased the demand for business practices that create economic and social value at the same time\. Although Generative Artificial Intelligence \(GenAI\) is being widely used, no studies have been conducted to assess how GenAI capabilities lead to social innovation intentions through cognitive and motivation process\. Based on the Social Cognitive Theory and the Information Processing Theory, the current study constructs and tests a dual mediation model explaining how Generative AI Literacy \(GAIL\) impacts Social Innovation Intention \(SII\) through Social Opportunity Recognition \(SOR\) and Social Entrepreneurial Self\-Efficacy \(SESE\)\. The quantitative cross\-sectional study involved a sample of 550 university students and participants in incubators of technology entrepreneurs and innovations from educational institutions and incubators in India\. The covariance\-based structural equation model \(CB\-SEM\) was analyzed in IBM SPSS AMOS version 28, with the calculation of indirect effects done using bias\-corrected bootstrapping based on 5,000 resamples\. Results indicate that GAIL has a significant impact on SII \(β= 0\.185, p &lt; 0\.001\)\. Indirect paths found to be significant includes SOR with a 0\.128 effect \(CI at 95% is \[0\.085, 0\.180\]\) and a stronger effect through SESE at 0\.188 \(CI at 95% is \[0\.139, 0\.245\]\)\. The significant direct relation along with both indirect routes confirm complementary parallel mediation\. The results put GenAI Literacy as a technology usability notion using different cognitive and agentive means for support of Social Innovation intention\. The research is contributing to the field of digital entrepreneurship and providing useful insights for universities, policymakers, and incubators to create responsible GenAI\-supported entrepreneurial learning and social innovation systems\.
