# STATISTICAL MODELING OF THE REPRODUCTION OF THE LABOR POTENTIAL OF UKRAINE’S TOURISM AND HOTEL SECTOR UNDER DEMOGRAPHIC TRANSFORMATIONS

**Autores:** Сергій Іванович Пирожков

**Data:** 2026-10-09 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

The article examines the theoretical and methodological foundations of statistical modeling of labor potential reproduction processes in Ukraine’s tourism and hotel sector under contemporary demographic transformations\. The relevance of the study is determined by the growing impact of depopulation, population aging, migration losses, and structural changes in human capital on the functioning of the labor market and staffing of the tourism and hotel sector\. Existing studies mainly focus either on demographic processes or labor market issues separately, while insufficient attention has been paid to integrated statistical modeling of labor potential reproduction in the tourism and hotel sector under demographic transformations\. In the context of full\-scale war and increasing labor market instability, there is a growing need for comprehensive statistical support for managerial decision\-making aimed at reducing staffing risks and ensuring sustainable sectoral development\. This study aims to substantiate the theoretical and methodological foundations of statistical modeling of labor potential reproduction processes in Ukraine’s tourism and hotel sector and to identify directions for improving statistical support for managerial decision\-making under demographic transformations\.The study applies a system approach, methods of scientific generalization, content analysis, structural\-logical analysis, comparison, and theoretical modeling\. The research is based on international recommendations on tourism statistics and tourism satellite accounts methodology, scientific publications of Ukrainian and foreign researchers, and materials of international organizations\.The findings demonstrate that the key factors affecting labor potential reproduction in the tourism and hotel sector include depopulation, population aging, migration processes, changes in the professional structure, and transformations in the qualitative characteristics of human capital\. It has been substantiated that traditional approaches focused primarily on quantitative labor market indicators are insufficient under current conditions\. The study proposes a conceptual model of statistical modeling integrating demographic, labor, and sectoral characteristics into a unified analytical framework\. In addition, a system of statistical indicators for assessing staffing risks, structural imbalances, and labor potential development prospects in the tourism and hotel sector has been developed\.The scientific novelty of the study lies in the development of a conceptual approach to statistical modeling of labor potential reproduction processes in the tourism and hotel sector based on the integration of demographic, labor, and sectoral indicators within a unified system of statistical support for managerial decision\-making\. The practical significance of the obtained results consists in the possibility of applying the proposed approaches by public authorities, regional institutions, and tourism and hotel sector entities for monitoring staffing imbalances, assessing labor market risks, and developing strategies for labor potential development under contemporary demographic challenges\.
