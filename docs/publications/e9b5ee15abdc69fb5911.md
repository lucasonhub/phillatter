# Lange, Daniel H\.: Who Has the Indicators? A Same\-Bearer Constraint

**Autores:** 

**Data de descoberta:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** unknown

## Descrição do feed

Indicator evidence is beginning to accrue piecemeal across models, applications, and increasingly complex agentic architectures\. Butlin et al\.’s preference for internal\-processing properties over behavioral indicators makes the inside/outside boundary methodologically consequential, since what counts as internal depends on the candidate system\. This Letter asks a question the established boundary problem does not answer: when indicator claims are established at different boundaries or timescales, what licenses combining them as evidence about one bearer? A worked GWT\-3/AE\-2 example shows each indicator defensibly supported at a different candidate boundary while neither candidate has been shown to possess both; a HOT\-2 example gives the temporal analogue\. Combining such evidence converts a union into an apparent conjunction of properties, and Bayesian weighting cannot supply the missing inferential step\. The stakes are practical: allowing indicator evidence to migrate between candidate bearers may misidentify which artificial entity, if any, warrants further investigation as a possible conscious system\.
