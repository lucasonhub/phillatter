# ТРАНСФОРМАЦІЯ РИНКУ РЕКЛАМНИХ ПОСЛУГ В ЕКОСИСТЕМІ ЕЛЕКТРОННОГО БІЗНЕСУ ПІД ВПЛИВОМ ФРАГМЕНТАЦІЇ СВІТОВОЇ ЕКОНОМІКИ ТА ІМПЕРАТИВІВ СТАЛОГО РОЗВИТКУ

**Autores:** М\.А\. Живко

**Data:** 2026-10-12 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

The article examines the transformation of the advertising services market within the e\-business ecosystem, emphasizing the contradictory influence of global economic fragmentation and sustainable development imperatives\. It is shown that the digitalization of advertising, the platformization of e\-business, the diffusion of algorithmic technologies and artificial intelligence are reshaping interactions among advertisers, digital platforms and e\-consumers and changing the mechanisms of creating, distributing and evaluating advertising value\. The study argues that the technological globalization of advertising increasingly coexists with institutional, regulatory, technological and data fragmentation of the digital environment\. This combination creates a fundamentally new configuration of the global advertising market, in which the growing technological interconnectedness of market participants is accompanied by increasing differentiation of regulatory regimes, digital ecosystems and conditions for accessing consumer data\. Particular attention is paid to the growing concentration of platforms and investment resources, the regional differentiation of data governance rules, and the increasing role of AI\-driven advertising, retail media and programmatic technologies\. It is emphasized that these processes strengthen the role of data, algorithms and digital infrastructure as strategic resources of advertising activities and simultaneously increase the dependence of e\-business entities on technological platforms and their ecosystems\. Sustainable development is considered not only as a communication agenda but as a criterion for organizing advertising production and delivery, including responsible data use, carbon efficiency, algorithmic transparency, inclusiveness and consumer trust\. In this context, the transformation of advertising efficiency criteria is substantiated, as economic performance is increasingly complemented by environmental, technological, ethical and social dimensions\. The article develops an original transformation matrix and a conceptual model linking global fragmentation, digital platforms, data, algorithms, sustainable development and the transformation of e\-business\. The proposed approach makes it possible to interpret the contemporary advertising services market as a complex platform\-algorithmic ecosystem whose development is determined by the interaction of technological, economic, institutional, environmental and behavioral factors\. Four scenarios for the advertising services market up to 2030 are outlined: inertial, fragmentation\-based, platform\-algorithmic and sustainable\-adaptive\. The sustainable\-adaptive scenario is identified as the most balanced trajectory for the long\-term development of the advertising services market and e\-business ecosystem\. It is concluded that the advertising market is moving from an economy of advertising reach toward an economy of algorithmically managed, adaptive and responsible interaction with consumers, in which technological efficiency, strategic adaptability and responsible sustainability become interconnected determinants of long\-term competitiveness\.
