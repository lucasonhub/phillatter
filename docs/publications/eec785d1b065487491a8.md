# Ética y moral en los procesos jurídicos en tiempos de inteligencia artificial: caso Ecuador

**Autores:** Luis Fernando Piñas\-Piñas; Paúl Orlando Piray\-Rodríguez; Guido Javier Silva\-Andrade; Julio Juvenal Aldana\-Zavala

**Data:** 2026-10-15 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

The study analysed the ethical and legal challenges of applying artificial intelligence in Ecuadorian judicial proceedings, the so\-called digital trials, and examined their risks, limits and opportunities\. The research was qualitative and descriptive, with a non\-experimental design, documentary review and comparison of international regulatory frameworks\. The results indicate that artificial intelligence can improve efficiency and access to justice, but in Ecuador its use lacks a specialised framework, so the validity, transparency and oversight of automated decisions remain in doubt\. Compared with European regulation, Latin America advances with less precision\. As a contribution, a scheme of ethical\-legal audits prior to the adoption of any algorithm\-based judicial system is proposed, and it is concluded that technology must complement, not replace, human judgment\.
