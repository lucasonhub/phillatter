# Rickettsial Illness: A Diagnostic Challenge

**Autores:** Ruchi Gajendra Agrawal; Sujag Gokhroo; Kena Shah; Dipika Bhil

**Data:** 2026-10-12 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

SPO 2 : 96% on room air\.• Blood pressure: 110/70 mm Hg\.• Respiratory system: Retractions present, nasal flaring positive, grunting absent, chest clear\.• Per abdomen: Hepatomegaly \-liver palpable 5\-6 cm below the right costal margin in the midclavicular line, soft in consistency with a smooth surface; no splenomegaly\. Investigations• Hemogram: Progressive fall in hemoglobin, with a decrease of 3\.5 g/dL from the admission value; leukopenia \(total leukocyte count 3,590/mm³\); and thrombocytopenia \(platelet count 25,000/mm³\)\.• Widal test: Negative\.• Dengue serology: Negative\.• Malarial parasite: Negative\.• Ultrasound: Bilateral mild pleural effusion and mild\-to\-moderate ascites\.• Cerebrospinal fluid analysis: Normal\.• MRI brain: Normal\.• Rathi\-Goodman\-Aghai \(RGA\) clinical score: 23/35, suggestive of rickettsial infection\.A score of ≥14 has been reported to have a sensitivity of 96\.15% and a specificity of 98\.84% for spotted fever group rickettsial disease\.3,5 • Weil\-Felix test: Negative\.
