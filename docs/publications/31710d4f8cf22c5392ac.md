# Economic evaluation of six immune checkpoint inhibitor\-based first\-line therapies for advanced squamous non\-small\-cell lung cancer: a cost\-effectiveness analysis based on network meta\-analysis

**Autores:** Zijian Qiu; Guodong Wang; Ying Lou; Xuan Feng

**Data:** 2026-10-13 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Background Advanced squamous non\-small\-cell lung cancer \(sq\-NSCLC\) imposes a substantial clinical and economic burden\. Immune checkpoint inhibitors \(sugemalimab, tislelizumab, penpulimab, serplulimab, camrelizumab, and pembrolizumab\) combined with chemotherapy improve survival, but their comparative cost\-effectiveness remains uncertain\. Methods A three\-state partitioned survival model with 3\-week cycles and a lifetime horizon was developed from the Chinese healthcare\-system perspective\. Clinical efficacy was derived from six phase 3 trials and synthesized using network meta\-analysis\. Costs, life\-years \(LYs\), quality\-adjusted life\-years \(QALYs\), and incremental cost\-effectiveness ratios \(ICERs\) for each ICI\-based regimen versus chemotherapy were estimated\. The willingness‐to‐pay \(WTP\) threshold was set at $27,906/QALY\. Deterministic, probabilistic, scenario, and full incremental analyses were performed\. Results In the base\-case analysis, chemotherapy alone yielded 0\.71 QALYs at $12,227\.47\. All ICI\-based regimens increased QALYs to between 1\.24 and 2\.07\. Pembrolizumab plus chemotherapy provided the greatest health benefit, yielding 2\.07 QALYs, but had an ICER of $70,913\.04 per QALY versus chemotherapy alone\. Penpulimab plus chemotherapy had the lowest ICER versus chemotherapy alone \($13,064\.23 per QALY\), followed by camrelizumab \($13,361\.50/QALY\), and tislelizumab plus chemotherapy \($14,022\.95/QALY\), all below the WTP threshold\. Sugemalimab plus chemotherapy and serplulimab plus chemotherapy exceeded the threshold\. Probabilistic sensitivity analysis indicated that camrelizumab plus chemotherapy had the highest probability of being cost\-effective \(95%\) at $27,906/QALY\. In the full incremental analysis, the efficiency frontier comprised chemotherapy alone, penpulimab plus chemotherapy, camrelizumab plus chemotherapy, and pembrolizumab plus chemotherapy; camrelizumab plus chemotherapy was the preferred strategy after dominated options were excluded\. Conclusion At the selected WTP threshold, camrelizumab plus chemotherapy may be the preferred first\-line strategy after strictly and extendedly dominated alternatives were excluded\. This finding depends on the indirect comparisons, chemotherapy baseline, drug prices, survival extrapolation, and post\-progression assumptions\.
