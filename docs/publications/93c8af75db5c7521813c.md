# Hickey, Samuel: Will computers ever be conscious?

**Autores:** 

**Data de descoberta:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** unknown

## Descrição do feed

Exploring the possibility of computer consciousness, the sole question of what is consciousness arises\. As the definition is subjective, it is difficult to state whether computers are conscious or whether we will ever be able to tell if they are conscious at all\. Exploring key arguments such as the &\#x27;Chinese room argument&\#x27; and &\#x27;Mary&\#x27;s room&\#x27;, alongside key philosophical ideas such as functionalisms and the works and ideas of influential philosophers, the plausibility of computers holding conscious can be evaluated\.
