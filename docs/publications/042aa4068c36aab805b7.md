# Bulgaria, Jamel &amp; Xǔ, Chénglán: The Structural\-Constructivist Architecture of Nurture: A Comprehensive Model of Parental Attunement and Boundary Regulation within the Core Emotion Framework

**Autores:** 

**Data de descoberta:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** unknown

## Descrição do feed

This paper presents the first structural constructivist model of parenting grounded in the Core Emotion Framework \(CEF\), offering a mechanistic account of how caregivers regulate attunement, boundaries, and developmental vision through ten functional emotional operators\. As the document states, caregiving is best understood as “an active, self regulating feedback system governed by the foundational polarity of self assertion \(Agency\) and connection \(Yielding\)\.” By mapping parental behavior onto the Head \(Sensing–Calculating–Deciding\), Heart \(Expanding–Constricting–Achieving\), and Gut \(Arranging–Appreciating–Boosting\) centers, the model reveals how real time operator sequencing shapes child co regulation, emotional safety, and long term developmental scaffolding\. The paper further demonstrates how pathological fusions—such as Sensing–Calculating hypervigilance—disrupt parental flexibility, producing instability in the caregiver child affective loop\. A mathematical formulation using coupled state vectors and cross individual influence matrices formalizes the dynamics of family co regulation, while the Detangling Protocol provides a clinically actionable method for restoring operator independence\. As summarized in the text, “Accepting represents the capacity to accept the child exactly how they are,” functioning as the global baseline that recalibrates the entire system\. Together, these contributions establish a unified computational, somatic, and clinical architecture for understanding and improving parental emotional agility\.
