# Auditing Audiovisual Correspondence in Generative AI Coursework with Frozen Multimodal Encoders

**Autores:** Gang Xu; Xiaojing Qin; Weitao Liu

**Data:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Computational evaluation of generative artificial intelligence coursework requires distinguishing audiovisual correspondence from creative achievement\. We conducted a retrospective computational measurement audit of 49 archived student submissions containing 58 videos\. Frozen CLIP and CLAP representations were queried with paired event and affect descriptions\. Five fixed anchor configurations and a pretrained AudioCLIP comparator were evaluated through original\-pair retrieval, temporal and coordinate controls, blur and noise transformations, teacher judgments, and selected cases\. The primary configuration achieved a mean reciprocal rank of 0\.179, compared with 0\.093 for the designated AudioCLIP comparator; its advantage over other temporal anchor configurations remained uncertain\. Temporal and anchor\-index disruptions reduced discrimination, while perturbations could increase matched scores despite poorer retrieval\. During revision, two teachers independently rerated all submissions after calibration on separate examples and rubric freezing\. Total\-score agreement was ICC\(A,1\) = 0\.970 and ICC\(A,2\) = 0\.985, but the primary diagnostic’s association with their mean total was near zero \(Spearman ρ = 0\.020\)\. A few adjusted dimension\-level associations did not retain significance in the non\-silent sensitivity analysis\. Selected cases exposed unverified semantic responses and nonzero outputs from silent audio\. The workflow supports inspectable correspondence diagnostics; artistic grading and educational usefulness require further validation\.
