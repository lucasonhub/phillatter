# Instability\-Aware Digital Twins for Robust Co\-Optimization of Operational Carbon Emissions, Thermal Comfort, and Structural Serviceability Reliability under Uncertainty in Smart Buildings

**Autores:** Seyed Reza Samaei; Jarek Kurnitski; James Riffat

**Data:** 2026-10-15 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Modern smart buildings operate under continuously varying climatic, occupancy, equipment, grid\-carbon, material, degradation, and sensing conditions, making nominal optimization insufficient for reliable operational decision\-making\. This study develops and numerically evaluates an instability\-aware digital twin for robust co\-optimization of operational carbon emissions, occupied\-zone thermal discomfort, and component\-level structural serviceability reliability under uncertainty\. The framework combines a multi\-rate thermal–energy–moisture–IAQ–structural model, carbon\-aware CO₂\-based ventilation control, extended Kalman filtering, Monte Carlo uncertainty propagation, FORM and design\-point importance sampling, Gaussian\-process surrogate modeling, NSGA\-II, and dimensionless objective\- and decision\-instability metrics\. A controlled six\-zone benchmark represents a six\-story office building with a gross floor area of 10,200 m² over a one\-year operational horizon and a 20\-year reliability horizon\. Re\-evaluation of the nominally selected P0 policy under the full modeled uncertainty set increased carbon emissions from 42\.6 to 50\.4 kgCO₂e m⁻² year⁻¹, occupied zone\-time discomfort from 115 to 128 K h year⁻¹, and the serviceability limit\-state exceedance probability from 0\.021 to 0\.031\. The corresponding nominal\-input underestimations were 15\.5% for carbon and 32\.3% for the exceedance probability\. The selected robust policy produced 48\.8 kgCO₂e m⁻² year⁻¹, 98 K h year⁻¹, and PSLS = 0\.012, with a reliability index of 2\.257\. Relative to the uncertainty\-aware P0 evaluation, it reduced carbon by 3\.2%, discomfort by 23\.4%, PSLS by 61\.3%, and decision instability by 65\.7%\. The robust policy maintained a mean occupied zone\-time CO₂ concentration of 684 ppm, a 99th percentile of 821 ppm, and zero occupied zone\-hours above 1,000 ppm\. Within the controlled numerical benchmark, the results show that Pareto efficiency alone does not establish operational robustness and that explicit instability quantification materially changes policy selection\.
