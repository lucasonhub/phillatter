# ARMG Scheduling Optimization Study for Automated Terminals with Sender Selection

**Autores:** Yu Wang; Yifan Zhai; Caimao Tan; Yue Wang

**Data:** 2026-10-15 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

This paper focuses on the study of the non\-cantilever ARMG operates on export container tasks, the senders are the bracket buffers located on both sides of the yard\. Compare and analyze the total travelling energy consumption of ARMG and AGV in completing all specified export container tasks in the container yard between the operation mode with sender selection and the traditional operation mode without sender selection\. Establish an integer programming model with the goal of minimizing the total travelling energy consumption of ARMG and AGV when sending all export container tasks to the sea side of the automated container terminal yard, and design a genetic algorithm for solution\. Such kind of operation mode fully utilizes the AGV bracket buffers on both sides of the automated container terminal yard, making ARMG more flexible in handling export container tasks\. The results indicate that in this operation mode, when all export container tasks are sent to the sea side of the yard, the total travelling energy consumption of ARMG and AGV, will be effectively reduced compared to traditional operation mode which rely solely on ARMG to send all export containers to the sea side of the yard\.
