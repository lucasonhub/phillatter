# Dynamic pricing for improved parking space utilization: a data\-driven Bayesian optimization approach

**Autores:** Prakash Ranjan; Fatemeh Fakhrmoosavi; Krishna Murthy Gurumurthy; Abdelrahman Ismael; James Cook

**Data:** 2026-10-15 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Parking scarcity in downtown areas presents a major challenge for commuters seeking convenient spaces near their destinations\. This study proposes a dynamic pricing optimization framework for garage parking in downtown Chicago, balancing the often\-conflicting objectives of garage operators and users\. The framework formulates garage pricing as a multi\-objective optimization problem that combines normalized garage revenue and occupancy, with the weight factors calibrated through a sensitivity analysis to identify the trade\-off that maximizes occupancy gains while approximately preserving baseline garage revenue\. Using simulated POLARIS data, we applied a Bayesian optimization framework to determine the optimal garage prices for both peak and off\-peak periods, then evaluated key performance metrics, including the temporal and spatial distribution of garage fees, occupancy rates, and revenues over a 24\-hour period\. Results showed that the optimized pricing strategy substantially reduced off\-peak garage fees, improving accessibility during off\-peak periods, while peak\-period fees remained close to existing baseline levels, avoiding disadvantage to peak\-period travelers\. Peak\-hour occupancy increased from approximately 50% to over 70%, with improved overall parking space utilization and limited revenue trade\-off\. The study also highlights the influence of allocating a portion of parking spaces to other road users\.
