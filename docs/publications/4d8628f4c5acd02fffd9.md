# Online comprehensive identification algorithm for seafloor hydrothermal plumes based on anomaly detection

**Autores:** Tingyi Zhang; Wei Cai; Yuan Wang; Xu Yao; Yiming Yu; Shangqi Diao; Dan Li; Li Huang; Songqing Lin; Yongshun Chen; Chunhui Tao

**Data:** 2026-10-09 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

The detection of hydrothermal plumes is crucial for discovering active seafloor hydrothermal fields and exploring seafloor resources\. To enable autonomous underwater vehicles \(AUVs\) to adapt their survey strategies in real time, this study proposes an integrated online identification algorithm that couples real\-time anomaly detection with fluid\-type classification using deep learning models trained on historical data from the Qianlong\-II AUV\. The algorithm combines sliding\-window statistical features, gated recurrent unit networks for sensor\-specific anomaly detection, and a multi\-layer perceptron that fuses the anomaly probabilities to classify the fluid types of the plumes\. Training labels are generated by offline algorithms tailored to different anomaly types, K\-medoids clustering for point anomalies \(temperature, turbidity\) and adaptive iterative fitting for subsequence anomalies \(methane, oxidation\-reduction potential\)\. Experimental evaluation under a leave\-one\-field\-out protocol demonstrates that the online algorithm replicates the offline labeling logic with 98\.67% fidelity, with ablation studies confirming the critical contribution of the anomaly detection stage\. The proposed algorithm offers an effective, real\-time deployable solution for autonomous hydrothermal exploration, significantly enhancing AUVs’ operational autonomy\.
