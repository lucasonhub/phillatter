# Negotiating Authorship with Generative AI in Cinema Education: Attribution and Creative Decision\-Making in Screenwriting

**Autores:** Ufuk Kucukcan

**Data:** 2026-10-15 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

This study examines how cinema students attribute authorship during AI\-assisted screenwriting and how those judgments relate to creative decisions\. Drawing on a fourteen\-week, design\-based course, it compares logbooks with 436 student turns across twenty generative\-AI conversations\. In four of six co\-writing cases, students selected and retained system\-originated material\. The clearest tension appeared when students claimed the idea as their own but assigned the text entirely to AI while repeatedly rejecting generated material, a response the instrument’s own categories leave ambiguous\. Read against Livingston, Gaut and Sellors, these cases show that textual generation, creative decision\-making and authorship attribution can diverge within one process\. Cinema education should ask not only who authored a text, but on what basis\.
