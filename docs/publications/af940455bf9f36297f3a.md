# T echnical P ath and E ngineering A pplication of L ow C arbonization P reparation of C oncrete

**Autores:** Chenxi Wang

**Data:** 2026-10-13 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

The carbon emissions of concrete run through its entire lifecycle, and reducing the carbon footprint of concrete has become an important issue in the field of building materials\. Although various research systems for low\-carbon concrete have gradually improved, there is still a lack of systematic comparison of the economic and carbon reduction potential of different technological paths in practical engineering\. This article divides the sources of carbon emissions into three stages: clinker production, aggregate mining and processing, and mixing and construction\. Based on the core principles of optimizing the cementitious material system and coordinating mineral admixtures, a systematic review is conducted on the technical paths of high\-dosage mineral compounding, alkali\-activated cementitious materials, and solid waste synergistic utilization\. The carbon emission reduction and engineering application conditions of each scheme are compared horizontally\. The carbon reduction range of the high\-dosage compound scheme is 20% to 45%, and the engineering acceptance is the highest\. The carbon reduction potential of alkali\-activated materials can reach about 70%, but it is limited by material shrinkage cracking and construction adaptability\. The carbon reduction rate of the solid waste collaborative utilization plan is between 10% and 40%\. The promotion of the three types of schemes is constrained by fluctuations in the quality of raw materials\.
