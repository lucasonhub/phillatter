# Sauchelli, Andrea: Cosmic Significance and Artificial Intelligence

**Autores:**

## Descrição do feed

\_American Philosophical Quarterly\_\. forthcomingI examine how the development of artificial intelligence might affect humanity’s cosmic significance\. I first outline a theory of cosmic significance that links it to both decisive value contributions and certain forms of uniqueness value\. This provides a framework for analysing how AI systems might affect our positive cosmic significance in two distinct ways: by matching or exceeding our contributions to some kinds of value, and by diminishing our uniqueness\. I then argue that AI development need not threaten our positive cosmic significance\. First, some AI systems might become ‘some of us ’ – whether through incorporating relevant biological structures or achieving appropriate forms of mental capacities\. Second, even if AI systems remain distinct from us, they might still secure our positive cosmic significance through what I call creation origin value – the finally valuable relationship between conscious creators and their valuable creations\. The idea is that our positive cosmic significance may also depend on our success in creating successors who can continue and expand upon our most valuable trajectories\.
