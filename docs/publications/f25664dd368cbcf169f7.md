# Investigar a inteligência artificial generativa na construção do conhecimento socioambiental: desafios metodológicos e éticos

**Autores:** Carolina Sabugueiro Suzano

**Data:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

The growing presence of generative artificial intelligence in the production, organisation and interpretation of information raises methodological and ethical challenges for research in the Social and Health Sciences, particularly when dealing with complex socio\-environmental problems marked by uncertainty, value conflicts, territorial inequalities and impacts on health, the environment and citizen participation\. This article discusses how generative artificial intelligence participates in the construction of socio\-environmental knowledge, identifying its contributions, limitations and risks\. Through a critical theoretical\-methodological essay supported by a narrative and non\-systematic review of the literature, it brings together work on language models, science and technology studies, situated knowledge, environmental justice and citizenship, critical discourse analysis and artificial intelligence ethics\. Drawing on recent uses of the notion of epistemic compression, the article extends it to the socio\-environmental domain through four mechanisms: linearisation, pacification of conflict, decontextualisation and individualisation of responsibility\. It argues that, by transforming uncertain, situated and politically contested problems into fluent, ordered and apparently neutral responses, these systems make information more accessible but may reduce context, plurality of knowledge, controversy and collective responsibility\. It concludes that their use in research requires human oversight, critical validation, transparency and methodological reflexivity, as well as systematic attention to the frames, omissions and shifts in responsibility they produce\.
