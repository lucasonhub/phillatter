# BOLT: Multi\-Bottleneck Bound\-Guided Learning for Flexible Job\-Shop Scheduling with Time Lags and Resource\-Coupled Transport

**Autores:** Ziheng Zhang; Wei Zhang; Yunyi Zhao; Zuming Liu; Wei Zhou; Boon Ping Gan

**Data:** 2026-10-15 · **Tipo:** preprint

## Resumo

Flexible job\-shop scheduling becomes resource\-coupled when a scarce vehicle fleet moves jobs between stations, because the schedule sets the transport waits\. BOLT \(bound\-oriented learning for transport\-coupled scheduling\) builds a lower bound from chain, machine\-capacity, and fleet\-capacity relaxations\. The bound equals the makespan at termination, so its per\-step changes form a reward that sums to a constant minus the makespan\. Because it never exceeds the attainable makespan, each candidate&\#x27;s bound increase is a proven rise of the makespan floor, which the policy reads as a price\. The root bound certifies a completed schedule&\#x27;s suboptimality gap without a solver\. On industry\-motivated 10\-module prefabricated\-construction instances, explicit transport coupling outperforms a transport\-penalty policy in every tested cell where loaded moves occupy at least 66% of fleet time and in none at or below 56%\. Across five zero\-shot 50\- and 80\-module production batches, BOLT outperforms the best dispatching\-rule pair on four and the penalty policy on the three with long moves\. In the three\-vehicle 80\-module batch, its mean makespan is 13\.0% below the best pair and 4\.2% below an hour\-budget constraint\-programming solver whose proven gap averages 34%\. BOLT decides in 2\.3\-5\.9 ms per event on one processor core and re\-plans a disruption in seconds\.
