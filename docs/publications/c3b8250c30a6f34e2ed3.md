# A multi\-channel ratio\-type self\-powered photoelectrochemical sensor for simultaneous detection of carbendazim and imidacloprid

**Autores:** Yunyi Liang; Gang Xiang; Liu Fengping; Qing Huang; 张翠忠; Yingying Huang; Jing Sun; Jinyun Peng

**Data:** 2026-10-15 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Herein, we report a multi\-channel ratiometric self\-powered photoelectrochemical \(SP\-PEC\) sensor for the simultaneous detection of two common pesticides, carbendazim \(Cbd\) and imidacloprid \(Imp\)\. A conjugated microporous polymer\-grafted reduced graphene oxide \(CMP\-rGO\) composite was employed as the photoanode material, while an electrodeposited CuSCN/indium tin oxide \(ITO\) layer served as the photocathode\. Using a spatially resolved design strategy, the photoanode and photocathode components were integrated onto a single ITO electrode, which was patterned into three photoanode regions and one photocathode region\. Cbd and Imp aptamers were selectively immobilized on two of the photoanode regions, with the third acting as an internal reference\. Instead of a conventional electrochemical workstation, a multimeter was adopted as the readout device, allowing for simultaneous detection of the two analytes via ratiometric signal readouts between the reference and working photoanodes\. The resulting multi\-channel SP\-PEC sensor exhibited excellent selectivity and stability\. Under optimal conditions, the sensor achieved linear responses over the range of 0\.001–1000 ng/mL for both pesticides, with detection limits \(LOD\) of 0\.000208 ng/mL for Cbd and 0\.000341 ng/mL, respectively\. The practical applicability of the sensor was further validated by its successful use in the simultaneous determination of Cbd and Imp in real lettuce and cucumber samples\.
