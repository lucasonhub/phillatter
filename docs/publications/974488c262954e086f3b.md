# First Report of Biallelic NPPC Variants Causing Acromesomelic Type of Short Stature

**Autores:** S\. Deepak Amalnath; Shreya Rajagopal; Chandan Akkadka

**Data:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

C\-type natriuretic peptide \(CNP\) increases chondrocyte maturation and growth of long bones by acting on its receptor NPR\-B\. Increased activity of CNP and NPR\-B has been reported to cause tall stature while decreased activity of NPR\-B causes short stature\. Monoallelic loss\-of\-function variants in NPPC, which codes for CNP, have been reported in patients with idiopathic short stature\. We report a patient with acromesomelic type of short stature due to biallelic missense variants in NPPC which codes for CNP\.
