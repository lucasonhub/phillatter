# Penyusunan Modul Kepemimpinan Gembala di Era Digital: Integritas, Batas Teologis Teknologi, dan Regenerasi Kepemimpinan bagi Gereja Lokal

**Autores:** Veronicha Nussa; Darmawan Darmawan; Satya Mega Ywana; Ferry Simanjuntak

**Data:** 2026-10-14 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Demands for organisational professionalism and the acceleration of digital technology can easily reduce church leadership to a purely managerial affair, even though the heart of Christian leadership is shepherding\. This community service programme took the form of module development rather than seminar delivery\. Its aim was to provide teaching material that pastors, elders, and ministry coordinators in local churches can use to re\-examine their leadership practice through the shepherd leadership paradigm, while also addressing questions of integrity, the use of artificial intelligence, and leadership regeneration\. The module was developed through a literature review of Christian leadership and ecclesiology scholarship, framework formulation by four contributors, and cross\-checking among contributors to maintain theological consistency\. It comprises four complementary parts: five pillars of shepherd leadership as a corrective framework for church governance, a biblical\-theology model of pastoral integrity, an ecclesiological\-digital church leadership model that positions technology as an instrument under theological limits, and the strengthening of four dimensions of shepherd leadership in digital culture\. The outputs are a module manuscript and a leadership self\-assessment instrument that a local church can use repeatedly without a facilitator present\.
