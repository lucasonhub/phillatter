# RECONSTRUCTING PRODUCTIVITY\-BASED WAGE REGULATION IN INDONESIAN EMPLOYMENT CONTRACTS: LESSONS FROM SINGAPORE&\#x27;S PROGRESSIVE WAGE MODEL

**Autores:** Bobby Christian Halim; Ida Hanifah; Ramlan Ramlan

**Data:** 2026-10-17 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

This study examines the normative gap in productivity\-based wage indicators under Government Regulation No\. 49 of 2025 in Indonesia\. It compares Indonesia&\#x27;s wage regulation framework with Singapore&\#x27;s Progressive Wage Model \(PWM\) and proposes a legal reconstruction adapted to Indonesia&\#x27;s context\. Using normative legal research, the study employs statutory, conceptual, functional comparative, and case approaches\. The findings indicate that Indonesia&\#x27;s abstract regulatory provisions contribute to wage compression and evidentiary difficulties in litigation, whereas Singapore links productivity to sector\-specific competency standards and tripartite enforcement mechanisms\. Accordingly, this study proposes a three\-layer reconstruction model integrating mandatory contractual clauses, sectoral productivity standards, and national competency certification\. To address institutional capacity constraints, implementation should proceed in phases, beginning with sectors that have established certification infrastructure\.
