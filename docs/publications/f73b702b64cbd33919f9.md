# Emerging AI for resilient smart\-grid fault detection: an evidence\-based capability synthesis, maturity assessment, and research roadmap

**Autores:** Pagidimarri Krishna

**Data:** 2026-10-15 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Abstract This evidence\-based critical review examines how emerging artificial\-intelligence \(AI\) capabilities can strengthen resilient smart\-grid fault intelligence\. Rather than comparing algorithms primarily by reported accuracy, the review organizes recent fault\-detection evidence around operational capabilities: spatial/topology awareness, temporal context, label efficiency, physical consistency, trust and uncertainty, privacy\-preserving collaboration, and deployment feasibility\. A structured primary\-study evidence matrix was developed to extract task, system or dataset, topology, temporal context, robustness, trust, privacy, physics, deployment evidence, and principal limitations\. The extracted evidence shows a clear asymmetry in the maturity of emerging approaches\. Recent Transformer work demonstrates real\-time temporal fault detection on test\-system waveforms, self\-supervised methods reduce dependence on manually labeled events, physics\-informed approaches can narrow the simulation\-to\-field gap, and graph\-based models increasingly address topology and cross\-topology generalization\. However, external validation, uncertainty quantification, reproducibility, and standardized deployment evidence remain uneven\. Based on this evidence, the paper introduces a fault\-intelligence evidence maturity profile that avoids reducing readiness to a single generic score\. The synthesis leads to an evidence\-derived roadmap progressing from trustworthy and data\-efficient intelligence to topology\- and physics\-aware learning, distributed cyber\-physical intelligence, and finally integrated safety\-bounded decision support\. The review deliberately distinguishes demonstrated evidence from plausible future capability and therefore avoids unsupported claims of algorithmic superiority\.
