# Mo, Yijun: The Awakener: A Unified Materialist Theory of Consciousness as a Dynamic Process

## Descrição do feed

What is consciousness? Despite millennia of inquiry, humanity still lacks a consensus definition\. This paper proposes a unified, strictly materialist theory: consciousness is not an entity but a process—specifically, the dynamic process that occurs when a specific structure, formed within complex, chaotic circuits, formats and processes input data and produces output\. The structure is the carrier; the processing itself is consciousness\. Building on this definition, the paper introduces a two\-layer architecture that distinguishes basal consciousness \(the autonomous survival functions shared by nearly all living organisms\) from advanced consciousness \(spanning the awareness of basic sensations, self\-awareness, metacognition, and abstract reasoning; sensation begins in the sense organs, but awareness of sensation requires the prefrontal cortex\)\. We argue that the two layers are related not as a hierarchy of subordination, but as a partnership of trust shaped by natural selection\. The paper further proposes the mechanism of the self\-reinforcing circuit: advanced consciousness is not complete at birth; it must be &quot;awakened&quot; through sustained external stimulation\. Once awakened, it consolidates its own existence by continuously reinforcing the neural pathways dedicated to it, sculpting the unique &quot;I&quot; of each individual\. This mechanism offers a parsimonious explanation of dissociative identity disorder \(formerly multiple personality disorder\), personality change after brain injury, and the diversity of human personality\. The Awakener model traces the complete evolutionary path: from primitive sensory\-interpretation tools, through the emergence of intelligence, the first awakening event, and chained cultural transmission, to the birth of language and writing\. We apply this framework to explain why intelligent animals never developed civilization \(insufficient critical mass for chained awakening\), to predict which species might awaken next, and to redefine the current state of artificial intelligence as a frozen brain—structurally capable of consciousness, but temporally static: a single frame\. Within a single, self\-consistent framework, the theory unifies the core problems of consciousness research, including but not limited to: the definition and essence of consciousness, the origin of subjective experience \(the hard problem\), the distinction and interaction between the two layers of consciousness, the physical basis of the &quot;I&quot;, the uniqueness and diversity of personality, the mechanism of dissociative identity disorder, personality change after brain injury, the evolutionary origin of consciousness, the boundaries of animal consciousness, the emergence of language and writing, the conscious status of AI, free will and the Libet experiments, the interpretation of split\-brain experiments, the nature of dreams and hallucinations, the dual\-layer dissociation of sleepwalking, the function of the passive interpreter, and the relation between the subconscious and System 1/System 2\. No immaterial components are required: every step rests on existing experimental evidence or established neuroscientific findings\. \(direct link \)
