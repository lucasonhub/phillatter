# Engineering the FK228 biosynthetic gene clusters for high\-yield production

**Autores:** Maoqin Wang; Xuexin Jia; Yunling Zhang; Junchi Ma; Youming Zhang; Jun Fu; Ruijuan Li

**Data:** 2026-10-15 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

FK228 \(romidepsin\) is a potent HDAC inhibitor; however, its clinical application is severely constrained by low natural yields and the genetic intractability of its native producer\. Here, we leveraged the established Burkholderia chassis KOGC1 to develop a high\-performance platform for FK228 production\. Through systematic fermentation optimization, we addressed major production constraints including potential end\-product toxicity and precursor supply\. Specifically, the integration of XAD\-16 resin adsorption together with the combined feeding of key amino acids \(cysteine, valine, and threonine\) elevated the titer to 743\.5 mg/L\. Subsequent refactoring of the biosynthetic gene cluster and promoter engineering further increased the titer to 1109\.4 mg/L\. Furthermore, the engineered strain exhibited remarkable genetic stability over five generations without antibiotic selection, which may help address a key barrier to future scale\-up\. Overall, this study establishes a highly productive laboratory\-scale FK228 biosynthetic platform, providing a foundation for future process development of this important therapeutic agent\.
