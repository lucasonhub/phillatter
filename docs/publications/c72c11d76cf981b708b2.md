# Case Report: Talonavicular joint replacement in congenital talonavicular coalition with individualized 3D\-printed prosthesis

**Autores:** Guimiao Li; Shixin Liu; Chenchen Yuan; Leilei Zhao; Hecheng Zhou; 刘德华; Dehang Liu; Gang Ji; Guobin Liu; Zhenhui Sun

**Data:** 2026-10-13 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Background Talonavicular coalition is a rare type of tarsal coalition that may lead to painful flatfoot and progressive functional impairment\. Conventional talonavicular arthrodesis provides symptom relief but sacrifices joint motion\. Motion\-preserving reconstruction strategies remain largely unexplored\. Case presentation We report a case of congenital talonavicular coalition accompanied by flatfoot treated with individualized 3D\-printed talonavicular joint replacement \(TNJR\) in a 12\-year\-old patient\. At 24\-month follow\-up, the patient achieved complete pain relief \(VAS 0\) and significant functional improvement \(AOFAS 98\)\. Talonavicular joint motion was preserved with 15° inversion/eversion and 15° pronation/supination\. Radiographic evaluation demonstrated restoration of the medial longitudinal arch and correction of hindfoot valgus\. Progressive improvement was observed in the calcaneal pitch, talonavicular coverage, and hindfoot alignment angles, whereas Meary&\#x27;s angle showed a slight increase\. Conclusion This case demonstrates the feasibility of individualized 3D\-printed TNJR as a potential motion\-preserving option for selected patients with extensive congenital talonavicular coalition\.
