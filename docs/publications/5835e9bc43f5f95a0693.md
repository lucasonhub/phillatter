# Modeling mini electric vehicle purchase using SOR and task technology fit: evidence from the third\-tier city of Liuzhou, China

**Autores:** Jialin Li; Fowokemi Alaba Ogedengbe; Ammar Bani Doumi

**Data:** 2026-10-15 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Technology has been revolutionizing the transportation sector through electric vehicles \(EVs\)\. Statistics and data have revealed that EV usage is currently growing globally\. This is due to its many benefits, such as its contribution to achieving the Sustainable Development Goals \(SDGs\)\. Hence, this study examines people&\#x27;s intentions to purchase Mini EVs\. Based on positivist epistemology, this research is quantitative in nature\. A framework, grounded in Stimulus\-Organism\-Response \(SOR\) and Task\-Technology Fit \(TTF\), was proposed with three research objectives and 7 hypotheses\. The framework was tested using survey data from 308 respondents in a third\-tier region in China\. Analysis was conducted using structural equation modeling, and the result revealed that the proposed framework was validated\. This research shows that factors such as the perceived price and design aesthetics influence the TTF and purchase intention\. The novelty of this paper lies in its focus on Mini EVs \(specifically the Wuling Hongguang\), the third tier \(developing economy\) in China, the integration of SOR and TTF into the framework, and the mediating impact of TTF on the postulated hypotheses\. All of which are current gaps in EV academics\. The major limitation is the examination of only a few variables, which could be expanded upon in future research\.
