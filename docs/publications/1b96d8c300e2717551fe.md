# ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE ETHICS AND ITS LEGAL REGULATION

**Autores:** Turg&\#x27;unboyev Abrorjon

**Data:** 2026-10-11 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

This paper provides an accessible overview of the essence of artificial intelligence \(AI\) ethics and its relationship with legal regulation\. Drawing on international experience and new regulatory instruments adopted in Uzbekistan in 2026, it puts forward proposals for translating ethical principles into effective legal mechanisms that ensure their practical implementation\.
