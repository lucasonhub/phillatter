# STREGO: A Scalable Implementation of a Trust\-Region Framework for Noisy Bayesian Optimization

**Autores:** Youssef Diouane; Issmail El Hallaoui; Meisseme Kadri; Amina Lamghari

**Data:** 2026-10-09 · **Tipo:** preprint

## Resumo

TREGO, a trust\-region Bayesian optimization \(BO\) method for expensive blackbox problems, has convergence guarantees, but only for exact function values\. In high dimension, its performance tends to deteriorate\. This paper asks whether a trust\-region BO method can be extended to handle noisy evaluations while being competitive in high dimension\. We first generalize TREGO into a trust\-region framework for noisy BO and extend its convergence analysis to noisy function estimates that are sufficiently accurate with sufficiently high probability\. We then propose STREGO, a scalable implementation of this framework\. Its global phase selects a batch of candidates by solving a bi\-objective problem on the posterior mean of a Gaussian process and the integrated variance reduction\. Unlike the predictive variance, this second objective does not favor boundary points merely because they are far from the data\. Its local phase minimizes the posterior mean of a second Gaussian process, trained only on points close to the incumbent\. STREGO is compared with TREGO, TURBO, BAXUS and NSMA\-BO on synthetic and real\-world problems of dimension $4$ to $124$, with and without noise\. It gives the best average results in every synthetic setting tested and the best or tied\-best solution on four of the six real\-world problems\. An implementation of STREGO is available at https://github\.com/Mayousk/STREGO\.
