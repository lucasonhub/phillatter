# Practical competencies required for professional practice in industrial engineering

**Autores:** Zdenka Gyurak Babelova; Augustín Stareček; Natalia Zazova

**Data:** 2026-10-14 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

The main task of process engineers is to optimize processes, whether in terms of efficiency, quality, or costs\. For this important role, it is necessary that industrial engineers and managers possess the required skills that consider all aspects of the work in industrial engineering\. In addition to the ability to design and manage technical solutions, ensure production, and maintain the standard course of processes taking place in manufacturing, the skills of industrial engineers should also reflect the demands of implementing changes, project management, leadership, team management, ensuring occupational fire protection and health and safety requirements, as well as innovation and improvement\. The diversity of requirements placed on industrial engineers leads to the need for an interdisciplinary set of skills\. Therefore, in the present study, we focused on identifying which competencies, in addition to technical ones, are required of professionals in the field of industrial engineering and industrial management\. The analysis revealed differences in competency profiles across the analyzed positions, with managerial positions emphasizing resource optimization and project management, while technical positions placed greater emphasis on proficiency in industrial engineering methods and process optimization\. These findings provide valuable information for employers, higher education institutions, and human resources professionals who seek to align talent development and educational programs with the changing demands of modern industrial practice\. Keywords: competencies; engineering; industry; management; requirements
