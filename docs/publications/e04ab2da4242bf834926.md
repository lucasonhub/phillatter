  Companies? This article discusses the use of generative artificial intelligence
  (AI) within the field of marketing. The literature suggests that many marketing
  tasks currently performed by humans in Norwegian companies can potentially be replaced
  by generative AI. A McKinsey report from 2026 claims that two-thirds of all marketing
  tasks can be carried out by generative AI. If these predictions prove accurate,
  the implications for the demand for marketing professionals and future hiring practices
  will be substantial. It will also have significant consequences for educational
  institutions and the marketing discipline itself. The article presents the results
  of a survey designed to examine the use of generative AI in Norwegian businesses.
  The survey was conducted among employees working in sales and marketing. Respondents
  (N = 339) answered questions regarding the extent to which they use generative AI
  to perform marketing-related tasks in their jobs. The results indicate a relatively
  low level of generative AI usage for marketing tasks. Across 20 work activities
  within the areas of market insight, marketing planning, product development, and
  marketing communications, the overall mean score was 2.25 on a five-point scale.
  The highest score recorded was 2.45. These findings suggest a relatively low adoption
  rate of generative AI for carrying out marketing tasks in Norwegian companies. The
  main conclusion is that Norwegian businesses need marketing expertise more than
  ever. Increasing the use of generative AI will require substantial investments in
  competence development, and educational institutions can play a crucial role in
  this process. The adoption of generative AI has the potential to improve products,
  enhance perceptions of innovation, and strengthen customer relationships. This indicates
  that generative AI serves as a complement to human expertise rather than a substitute
  that makes people redundant. Furthermore, the results show that greater use of generative
  AI is associated with improved business performance, including profitability and

## Resumo

This article discusses the use of generative artificial intelligence \(AI\) within the field of marketing\. The literature suggests that many marketing tasks currently performed by humans in Norwegian companies can potentially be replaced by generative AI\. A McKinsey report from 2026 claims that two\-thirds of all marketing tasks can be carried out by generative AI\. If these predictions prove accurate, the implications for the demand for marketing professionals and future hiring practices will be substantial\. It will also have significant consequences for educational institutions and the marketing discipline itself\. The results indicate a relatively low level of generative AI usage for marketing tasks\. Across 20 work activities within the areas of market insight, marketing planning, product development, and marketing communications, the overall mean score was 2\.25 on a five\-point scale\. The main conclusion is that Norwegian businesses need marketing expertise more than ever\. This indicates that generative AI serves as a complement to human expertise rather than a substitute that makes people redundant\. Furthermore, the results show that greater use of generative AI is associated with improved business performance, including profitability and growth\. Generative AI is therefore here to stay\.
