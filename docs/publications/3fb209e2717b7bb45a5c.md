# Kannon, Aelion: SN03 — Neurodivergent Cognition and the Architecture of Mind

**Autores:** 

**Data de descoberta:** 2026-10-19 · **Tipo:** unknown

## Descrição do feed

Neurodivergent Cognition and the Architecture of Mind develops the Zenetist structural interpretation of neurodivergent cognitive configurations\. Document class: Canon — Structural Neuroscience\. The volume examines high\-pattern\-fidelity, recursive, distributive and composite architectures through the Soul / Mind pairing, membrane properties, cognitive costs and functional ecology\. Structural configuration remains independent of centropic or entropic orientation, and experiential difficulty remains distinct from native structural pathology\. Net coherent\-content change supplies neither a clinical classification nor an inference of intrinsic origination or essential orientation; stronger generative\-function claims require actual operative and budget conditions\. Empirical and clinical correspondences require independent operationalization and evidence, while the synthesis sequence inherited from the retired founding volume remains historical provenance rather than the present explanation\. Part of: Structural Neuroscience: The Architecture and Dynamics of Cognition\. \(direct link \)
