# Subtype\-Specific Efficacy of Adrenoceptor Agonists in Inhibiting Lens\-Induced Myopia in Chicks

**Autores:** Mezbah Uddin; Fengjuan Yu; Rachel Ka Man Chun; Bing Zuo; King Kit Li; Chi Ho To; Dennis Y\. Tse

**Data:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

PURPOSE: Alpha\-adrenergic receptors \(ARs\) may potentially play a critical role in controlling myopia, with subtype\-specific signalling pathways mediating distinct effects\. This study systematically investigated the efficacy of three subtype\-specific pharmacological agents in myopia control and dose\-response relationships in lens\-induced myopia \(LIM\) chick models\. METHODS: White Leghorn chicks were randomised to a phosphate\-buffered saline \(PBS\) control group or treated groups receiving metaraminol \(α1\-AR agonist\), lofexidine \(α2\-AR agonist\) or cirazoline \(a dual α1‑AR agonist and α2‑AR antagonist\) at low \(3 nmol\), medium \(30 nmol\) or high \(300 nmol\) doses\. A \-10 D single\-vision lens was fitted to the right eye for 7 days to induce myopia, with intravitreal injections administered at baseline and on days 2, 4 and 6\. Natural ocular recovery was allowed for another 7 days\. Interocular differences \(IOD\) in spherical equivalent refraction \(SER\) and ocular biometrics were compared\. RESULTS: After 7 days of lens wear, all drug groups exhibited lower myopia progression than the control group\. Lofexidine showed potent and consistent myopia\-inhibiting effects\. The IOD in SER, vitreous chamber depth \(VCD\) and axial length \(AL\) were significantly reduced at three doses compared with PBS control \(all p &lt; 0\.05\)\. Metaraminol and cirazoline exhibited significant efficacy mainly at medium and high doses\. All three α\-AR modulators alleviated choroidal thinning at both 30 and 300 nmol doses\. During recovery, all treatment groups trended toward emmetropia, without significant differences from the PBS group\. CONCLUSIONS: This first head\-to\-head in vivo comparison of three subtypes of α\-AR modulators in the chick LIM model demonstrates the potent myopia\-suppressive effects of α2\-AR agonist lofexidine at low dose\. α1\-AR agonist metaraminol required a distinct threshold dose to achieve efficacy and blockade of α2\-AR with cirazoline may counteract anti\-myopic protection\. These findings provide crucial subtype\-specific preclinical insights for the rational development of future drugs for myopia\.
