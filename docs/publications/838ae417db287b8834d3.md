# Research on Visual Communication Design Based on Communication Theory

**Autores:** Xuelian Lv; Ju Li

**Data:** 2026-10-14 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

In the era of information overload and rapid digital transformation, visual communication design faces challenges in accurately and attractively transmitting information\. This study conducts a case\-driven analysis of two Information Technology \(IT\)\-integrated projects: Dunhuang Mogao Grottoes&\#x27; digital initiative \(using TikTok short videos and Unity 3D/Augmented Reality \(AR\) with &gt;1m Location\-Based Service\) and Suzhou Wenlv&\#x27;s brand modernization \(via interactive HTML5 \(H5\) and semiotics\-based symbol optimization\)\. Empirical results show that short\-video channels achieved 300% user growth with 87% longer user engagement, while AR\-based channels delivered 500% user growth with 82% longer engagement\. Meanwhile, AI\-driven visual symbol optimization improved accuracy of information memory by 63% and cross\-cultural recognition rate by 62% \(A/B test, N=200\)\. Challenges include theoretical complexity and creativity constraints\. The study offers a reusable framework for integrating communication theory with AR/dynamic media to optimize visual communication in cultural/commercial contexts\.
