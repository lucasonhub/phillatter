# Using the Geriatric Core Dataset \(G\-CODE\) to predict mortality in older patients with cancer: development and external validation

**Autores:** Anne‐Laure Couderc; Claudia Martinez‐Tapia; Simone Mathoulin\-Pélissier; Marie Laurent; Michaël Bringuier; Pierre Gay; Philippe Caillet; Jeanne Bertona; Laurent Greillier; Johanna Canovas; Pierre Soubeyran; Siri Rostoft; Florence Canouï‐Poitrine; Éléna Paillaud

**Data:** 2026-10-12 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

BACKGROUND: The Geriatric Core Dataset \(G\-CODE\) was developed to standardise geriatric assessment \(GA\) in older adults with cancer\. This study aimed to develop and externally validate a mortality prediction score based on the G\-CODE at 6\-month \(main endpoint\), 12\- and 24\-month \(secondary endpoints\), in older patients with cancer\. METHODS: Data came from two prospective French cohorts of cancer patients aged ≥70 referred for GA: the Elderly Cancer Patients \(ELCAPA\) cohort \(training set\) and the Geriatric Oncology Marseille \(GOM\) cohort \(validation set\)\. G\-CODE items were evaluated using multivariate Cox regression analyses, with Akaike information criterion selection\. Discrimination was assessed using time\-dependent area under the receiver operator curve \(tAUC\) for 6\-, 12\-, and 24\-month mortality\. RESULTS: We included 950 and 766 patients in the training and validation sets \(mean ± sd age: 83 ± 6 years/81 ± 6 years; men: 55%/59%; 6\-month mortality: 27%/22%\)\. The final G\-CODE model highlighted five strong predictors of 6\-month mortality: not having a caregiver, weight loss, comorbidities, an impaired 4\-instrumental activities of daily living score, and an impaired timed\-up\-and\-go test\. The tAUCs of the model, including the G\-CODE, age, cancer type, metastases, and treatment, were 82%, 78% and 80%\. CONCLUSIONS: The final G\-CODE score \(range: 0\-17\) accurately predicts 6\-, 12\-, and 24\-month mortality\. CLINICAL TRIAL REGISTRATION: NCT02884375 \(ClinicalTrials\.gov\)\.
