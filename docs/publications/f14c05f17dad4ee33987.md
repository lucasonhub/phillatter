# Niami, Morteza: Ideology, Subjective Opacity, and the Erosion of Reconfigurative Capacity From Marx&\#x27;s Camera Obscura to an Ontology of Finite Revisability

**Autores:** 

**Data de descoberta:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** unknown

## Descrição do feed

This article argues that the persistence of ideology cannot be adequately explained by the persistence of false representations\. A subject may recognize a contradiction, possess accurate information, and understand the structural conditions producing an experience while remaining unable to reorganize the practical and interpretive configuration through which that experience continues to be lived\. I call this condition the knowledge–capacity gap: the interval between recognition and the reconfigurative capacity required to convert recognition into sustained alteration of a configuration\. The argument proceeds through critical engagements with Marx, Lacan, Althusser, Žižek, Heidegger, and Hegel, none of which is reduced to the others\. Marx establishes the problem of socially organized appearance; Lacan and Althusser specify the divided and interpellated structure of the subject; Žižek formulates the persistence of attachment after conscious recognition; Heidegger supplies an ontological account of everyday falling and thrownness; Hegel clarifies the productivity of contradiction\. I then introduce reconfigurative capacity as a distinct analytical variable and redefine ideology as provisional stabilization within an erosive field\. Such stabilization acquires durability when the temporal, attentional, affective, interpretive, and reflective capacities required for reconfiguration are progressively depleted\. I conclude by proposing responsible suspension as a temporal practice that preserves the interval in which recognition can become reconfiguration, and I defend the framework against objections concerning individualism, unfalsifiability, and the relation between the psychic and the social\. Keywords: ideology; Marx; Žižek; Althusser; Heidegger; Hegel; subjective opacity; reconfigurative capacity; erosive field; responsible suspension \(direct link \)
