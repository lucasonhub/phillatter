# Pace Giannotta, Andrea: Panqualityism as a critical metaphysics for neurophenomenology

**Autores:** 

**Data de descoberta:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** unknown

## Descrição do feed

\_Constructivist Foundations\_ 16 \(2\):163\-166\. 2021I examine Michel Bitbol’s proposal of a metaphysical counterpart of neurophenomenology, arguing that such a metaphysics should address the issue of the origin of consciousness\. This can be accomplished through panqualityism, which conceives of the subject and object of experience as grounded in a flow of pre\-phenomenal qualities\. I conclude by framing this view in terms of a critical metaphysics that is consistent with the pragmatic and existential dimension of neurophenomenology\. \(direct link \)
