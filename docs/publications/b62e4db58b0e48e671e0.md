# RNA base\-modification and A\-to\-I editing landscapes of oxidatively stressed human retinal pigment epithelium under Quantum Molecular Resonance electrotherapy and blood\-derived secretome

**Autores:** Domenico Mordà; Simona Alibrandi; Concetta Scimone; Angela D’Ascola; Federica Aliquò; Dr Giorgia Abate; Gianantonio Pozzato; Sergio Zaccaria Scalinci; Rosalia D’Angelo; Antonina Sidoti; Luigi Donato

**Data:** 2026-10-15 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Chemical modifications of RNA bases and adenosine\-to\-inosine \(A\-to\-I\) editing are regulatory layers essentially unexplored in the retinal pigment epithelium \(RPE\), whose oxidative dysfunction underlies age\-related macular degeneration\. We mapped both layers in ARPE\-19 cells exposed to tert\-butyl hydroperoxide under two non\-pharmacological interventions, Quantum Molecular Resonance \(QMR\) electrotherapy and a patient blood\-derived secretome, across 56 libraries spanning four arms, three time points and matched stressed and stress\-free conditions \(GEO: GSE328981\)\. Base modifications were called with HARMLNC and A\-to\-I editing screened with a transition\-signal caller, then re\-analysed with REDItools3, JACUSA2 and LoDEI\. The high\-confidence set comprised 241 calls at 15 sites, all of classes canonical to tRNA and rRNA\. The most prevalent was an adenosine modification at chrM:2617 in mitochondrial 16S rRNA \(MT\-RNR2\), detected in 51 of 56 libraries and in every arm\. Two positions 1 bp apart lie in an EZR/SYTL3 overlap region, whereas a third chromosome 6 site maps to HLA\-C, 127\.5 Mb away\. No position of either layer differed between treatment arms: 7 testable modification sites \(minimum p = 0\.19\) and, at single\-nucleotide resolution under dedicated callers agreeing to r = 0\.975–0\.992, 15 curated editing positions \(minimum q = 0\.169\)\. Two one\-sided tests bound any change in substitution composition within 1\.4% and in mean editing frequency within 10% for secretome and combination and 20% for QMR\. Against a stress\-free baseline internal to the cohort, only chrM:13710 \(MT\-ND5\) changed detectably \(q = 0\.03\)\. These layers are therefore small, largely constitutive and quantifiably undisturbed by either intervention at the resolution available\.
