# Usability Evaluation of a Web\-Based Rabbit Reproduction Recording System for Smallholder Farmers: An ISO 9241\-11:2018 Approach

**Autores:** Ivan Fahd Abidalbar Humaidi; Sri Minarti; Muhammad Halim Natsir; Osfar Sjofjan; Herman Tolle

**Data:** 2026-10-15 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Reproductive recording is essential for effective rabbit breeding management, yet manual systems often hinder breeding stock selection at the smallholder level\. This study developed a rabbit livestock recording website using The Five Planes framework and evaluated its usability under ISO 9241\-11:2018 across effectiveness \(success rate and error rate\), efficiency \(task completion time\), and satisfaction \(System Usability Scale/SUS\)\. Testing was conducted in two iterative phases involving animal science students and active rabbit farmers as respondents\. Phase I \(5 respondents\) yielded a success rate of 86\.25%, an error rate of 11%, an efficiency of 53\.05 seconds per task, and a SUS score of 63\.50 \(Marginal High\)\. Following interface improvements, Phase II \(20 respondents\) recorded a success rate of 74\.38%, an error rate of 12%, an efficiency of 66\.91 seconds per task, and a SUS score of 66\.13 \(Marginal High\)\. This decline is primarily attributable to the inclusion of farmer respondents with more heterogeneous digital literacy in Phase II, rather than a deterioration in system design\. These findings indicate that while The Five Planes framework produced a functionally robust recording website, further refinement is needed to improve usability for users with lower digital literacy before broader implementation in smallholder rabbit farming\.
