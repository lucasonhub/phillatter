# Design and Experimental Characterization of a Solar\-Powered Multi\-Sensor Instrumentation System for Smart Dumpster Monitoring and GPS Tracking

**Autores:** Abdul\-wasiu Alani Olowosebioba; Fatai Sunday Akingbade; Isaiah Olaoluwapo Andrew

**Data:** 2026-10-13 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

This study presents the design and experimental characterization of a solar\-powered multi\-sensor instrumentation system for smart waste monitoring and GPS tracking\. The developed system integrates an ultrasonic sensor for waste\-level measurement, a GPS module for location tracking, a GSM communication unit for remote notification, and a photovoltaic power subsystem for autonomous operation\. An Arduino\-based controller was employed for data acquisition, processing, and communication\. Experimental evaluation was conducted to determine the performance of the sensing, communication, positioning, and power\-management subsystems\. Results showed a waste\-level measurement accuracy of 99\.1% with an average sensor error of 0\.28 cm, demonstrating high reliability for real\-time monitoring applications\. The GPS subsystem achieved an average positioning accuracy of 3\.3 m, enabling effective location identification and tracking of the waste bin\. The solar\-energy subsystem delivered a maximum output power of 40 W, while the battery backup system sustained continuous operation for approximately 15\.6 h under test conditions\. The servo\-actuated lid mechanism exhibited an average response time of 0\.81 s, ensuring prompt system operation\. Furthermore, the GSM communication module achieved a 100% message transmission success rate during the experimental trials\. The results demonstrate that the prototype instrumentation system provides reliable sensing, communication, and energy autonomy for intelligent waste\-management applications\. The integration of renewable energy, multi\-sensor monitoring, and wireless communication offers a practical and sustainable solution for smart\-city dumpster\-monitoring infrastructure\.
