# ZAMONAVIY MUTAXASSIS KASBIY MA’NAVIYATINING TARKIBIY KOMPONENTLARI

**Autores:** UMIDJON DONIYOROVICH ORTIQOV

**Data:** 2026-10-10 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

This article theoretically and practically examines the essence and structural components of the professional morality of a modern specialist\. Professional morality is considered a complex phenomenon that includes not only professional knowledge and skills but also ethical values, professional responsibility, integrity, professional identity, communicative culture, reflective practice, and commitment to continuous professional development\. Based on the analysis, a model of professional morality is proposed, comprising value\-ethical, cognitive, activity\-oriented, communicative, reflective, and digital\-ethical components\. The study also Zamonaviy jamiyat va innovatsiyalar ilmiy nazariy jurnali 2026\-yil, 9\-Son, 2\-Qism\. ISSN 3060\-544X identifies pedagogical opportunities for developing professional morality in the contemporary professional and educational environment\. The findings can be used to improve specialist preparation and systems of continuous professional development\.
