# Developing Criteria and Indicators for Assessing Students’ Knowledge in a Digital Educational Environment

**Autores:** Urmat Moldosanov

**Data:** 2026-10-15 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

The digital transformation of higher education requires the development of assessment systems capable of measuring not only the amount of knowledge acquired by students but also their ability to understand, apply, analyze, evaluate, and create information in technology\-mediated contexts\. This article examines the theoretical and methodological foundations for developing criteria and indicators for assessing students’ knowledge in a digital educational environment\. The study employs systematic analysis, comparison, classification, synthesis, and pedagogical modelling\. The proposed framework includes six assessment criteria: conceptual knowledge, comprehension and interpretation, practical application, analytical and problem\-solving ability, knowledge integration and creation, and reflective and self\-regulated learning\. Each criterion is operationalised through measurable qualitative and quantitative indicators\. A four\-level assessment scale — advanced, proficient, developing, and insufficient — is introduced to improve transparency and consistency\. The article also proposes a weighted formula for calculating an integrated student knowledge score\. Particular attention is given to validity, reliability, fairness, transparency, accessibility, academic integrity, and the pedagogically responsible use of learning analytics and artificial intelligence\. The findings indicate that digital assessment becomes educationally meaningful when assessment tasks are aligned with learning outcomes, indicators are observable and measurable, students receive timely feedback, and automated data are combined with professional teacher judgement\. The proposed framework may be adapted to different university disciplines, learning management systems, and blended or fully online educational programmes\.
