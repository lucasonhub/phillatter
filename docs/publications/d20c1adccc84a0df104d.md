# Artificial Intelligence and Copyright: Authorship, Ownership, and Originality

**Autores:** N\. Sudarshan; Deepika Nallabelli

**Data:** 2026-10-17 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

This study critically examines the relationship between Artificial Intelligence and copyright law by focusing on the three foundational concepts of authorship, ownership, and originality\. It evaluates existing legal frameworks, judicial decisions, international developments, and scholarly opinions to determine whether current copyright principles are capable of addressing AI\-generated works or whether legislative reform is required, and proposes recommendations aimed at balancing technological advancement with the protection of human creativity\.
