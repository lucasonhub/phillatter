# Luz invisible que revela moléculas: el papel de la radiación infrarroja en la química

**Autores:** Fernando Ortega‐Jiménez

**Data:** 2026-10-12 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Infrared \(IR\) radiation is a part of the electromagnetic spectrum that is invisible to the human eye but very important for the chemical sciences\. Using IR, particularly IR spectroscopy, chemists can identify compounds, verify product quality, or monitor the progress of a chemical reaction without destroying samples\. This article provides a clear explanation of the operating principles of IR, as well as its applications in modern chemistry and its potential future developments, with examples ranging from the food industry to space exploration\.
