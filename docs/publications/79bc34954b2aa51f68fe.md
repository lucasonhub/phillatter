# Naoto, Fujie: Where Does Law Ignite Before the Subject? — Legal Individuation, Operational Address, ReachProfile, and Operational\-Shape Rate Analysis across AI, Biophysical Dynamics, and Xeno\-Capital

**Autores:**

## Descrição do feed

\_Zenodo\_ 1\. 2026This paper asks where law becomes operational before a fully individuated legal subject is already available\. Rather than presupposing persons, agents, corporations, organisms, or artificial systems as fixed bearers of legal status, it examines the conditions under which legally consequential differences become sufficiently localized, addressable, persistent, and causally connected to downstream effects for legal individuation to become non\-redundant\. Operational address identifies the configuration to which a legal intervention, obligation, permission, restriction, or consequence can effectively attach without assuming that this address must already constitute a person\. ReachProfile describes the configuration\-relative set of actions, resources, jurisdictions, interfaces, and downstream states that an addressed system can access or alter\. Operational\-shape rate analysis compares the speed at which legally relevant capacities and effects change with the speed at which legal classifications, responsibilities, and institutional responses can be revised\. The framework is tested across artificial intelligence, biophysical dynamics, and Xeno\-Capital, where causal organization and practical reach may cross conventional boundaries of body, ownership, agency, and legal personality\. The paper distinguishes causal contribution, operational addressability, legal individuation, and responsibility rather than collapsing them into a single concept of subjecthood\. Its broader claim is that legal subjecthood can be reconstructed as a later stabilization within legal operations: law may first require an addressable locus of consequential difference and only subsequently determine whether that locus should be individuated as a rights\-holder, duty\-bearer, responsible agent, legal person, or some other juridical form\. \(direct link \)
