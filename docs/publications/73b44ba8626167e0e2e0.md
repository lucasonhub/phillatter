## Descrição do feed

\_Zenodo\_ 1\. 2026This paper develops Transcendental Operational\-Shape Metaphysics as a general philosophical framework for investigating the generation, transformation, maintenance, and dissolution of conditions of possibility\. Rather than presupposing transcendental conditions as invariant structures, it examines how operational configurations enable, discriminate, retain, transform, and reorganize subsequent possibilities across different scales, temporal horizons, boundaries, and media\. The framework distinguishes enabling conditions, discriminative conditions, and validity conditions without reducing transcendental questions to empirical changes in scientific instruments\. It develops Operational\-Shape Realism, Operational\-Shape Naturalism, Non\-Closure Realism, and Xenoscientific Realism as interconnected but non\-identical philosophical positions\. Through critical engagement with Kant, Hegel, Sellars, Brandom, Brassier, Simondon, Hacking, and Rheinberger, the paper situates operational\-shape analysis within existing debates concerning transcendental conditions, scientific realism, conceptual mediation, and historical transformations of scientific practice\. The framework is further developed through Xenoscience, the Ziran System, Thing\-in\-Itself Engineering, autonomous physics, embodied operational configurations, life and death, institutional transformations, and formal identification systems\. Computational findings from Ziran Runtime are discussed as bounded evidence of configuration\-dependent operational differences, without treating them as direct demonstrations of transcendental transformation\. The central proposal is that conditions of possibility should themselves be investigated as historically and materially reconfigurable operational formations, while no locally constituted description is identified with reality\-as\-a\-whole\. \(direct link \)
