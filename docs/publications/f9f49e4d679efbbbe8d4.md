# A Survey Study on Brain Tumor Segmentation Techniques Using MRI

**Autores:** Haneen Ahmed Abd Almunaim; Waleed Abdullah Araheem

**Data:** 2026-10-12 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Brain tumor segmentation is a crucial task for accurate diagnosis and treatment planning in medical image analysis\. Even with the advantages of magnetic resonance in magnetic resonance imaging \(MRI\), automated segmentation becomes difficult owing to the presence of heterogeneity in tumors, irregular edges, and imaging artifacts\. Recently, there has been a paradigm shift from classical image processing techniques to more advanced machine learning and deep learning approaches to enhance the accuracy of segmentation\. Approaches based on U\-Net architecture variations, three\-dimensional CNN, and attention mechanism\-based approaches have proven successful on benchmarking datasets\. In this survey, we present a review of brain tumor segmentation studies conducted between the years 2020 to 2025, classify the existing methods, and highlight the major challenges
