# Annealed Lévy Diffusion via Gaussian Surrogate Reverse Dynamics

**Autores:** Yuling Jiao; Lican Kang; Jerry Zhijian Yang; Cheng Yuan; Ruizhe Zhang

**Data:** 2026-10-14 · **Tipo:** preprint

## Resumo

Gaussian diffusion models offer a practical framework for generative modeling\. Denoising Lévy Probabilistic Models \(DLPM\) introduce heavy\-tailed perturbations with a fixed tail index throughout denoising\. We introduce Annealed Lévy Diffusion \(ALD\)\. ALD varies the stability index $α\(t\)$ to combine heavy\-tailed exploration with Gaussian refinement\. Our marginal\-first formulation represents stable noise as a scalar Gaussian scale mixture\. This formulation enables standard Gaussian noise\-prediction training with one additional scale input\. We study two sampling processes\. ALD\-renoise estimates the clean sample and injects fresh marginal noise\. ALD\-posterior preserves dependence between successive states through an accumulated conditional variance and a Gaussian bridge update\. Both samplers reuse the same trained predictor with different scalar inputs\. We evaluate ALD on four 2D benchmarks and an imbalanced nine\-mode mixture\. We also examine training efficiency and the effects of prediction perturbations on clipped Holtsmark data\. Additional experiments evaluate MNIST and CelebA64 generation and CIFAR\-10LT augmentation\. ALD preserves local geometry on the evaluated light\-tailed and manifold\-structured targets\.
