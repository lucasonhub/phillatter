AI\-generated catalogue description of a self\-published conceptual paper by Andreas Ehstand\. The paper proposes AUGMANITAI as an internalised, world\-coupled multi\-model mode of thinking\. It distinguishes external dialogue with several AI models from thinking routines that may persist without device use, and discusses planned fading of external support, human judgement and feedback from real\-world consequences\.
