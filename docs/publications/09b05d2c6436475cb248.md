# Aspiration\-line pressure stability in Venturi and peristaltic pump configurations during phacoemulsification: an in vitro study

**Autores:** Tomoyuki Kunishige; Hisaharu Suzuki; Fumiki Okamoto

**Data:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

This in vitro study descriptively evaluated aspiration\-line pressure waveforms between Venturi and peristaltic pump configurations and their associated tubing systems using two Stellaris systems and a KITARO artificial anterior chamber model\. VAC200 and VAC400 represented 200\- and 400\-mm Hg vacuum settings in the Venturi configuration and vacuum limits in the peristaltic configuration, with peristaltic flow settings of 20 and 35 mL/min, respectively\. Pressure was recorded at 0\.1\-s intervals and analyzed during the stable 30\- to 50\-s period after activation\. Only one continuous run was performed per condition\. Reported standard deviations \(SDs\) represent within\-run temporal fluctuation, not between\-run variability\. Mean pressure, SD, and transient fluctuations of 10 mm Hg or greater were evaluated\. At VAC200, mean pressure was − 10\.40 ± 0\.53 mm Hg in the Venturi configuration and 11\.02 ± 2\.99 mm Hg in the peristaltic configuration\. At VAC400, values were − 70\.90 ± 1\.06 and − 50\.97 ± 2\.54 mm Hg, respectively\. Descriptive analysis suggested greater pressure fluctuation in the peristaltic configuration at both settings\. Intermittent transient fluctuations of 10 mm Hg or greater occurred only in P\-VAC400\. These findings describe aspiration\-line pressure waveforms under the tested in vitro conditions\.
