# The Influence of Digital Promotion and Information Overload on Gen Z&\#x27;s Consumptive Behavior Mediated by Sharia Financial Management \(A Study of Shopee Users\)

**Autores:** Gustina Angreani; Anzu Elvia Zahara; Beid Fitrianova Andriani

**Data:** 2026-10-14 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

This study aims to analyze the influence of digital promotion and information overload on the consumptive behavior of Generation Z Shopee users, mediated by Sharia\-compliant financial management\. A quantitative approach utilizing Partial Least Squares\-Structural Equation Modeling \(PLS\-SEM\) was employed\. The results indicate that information overload and digital promotion have a positive and significant effect on consumptive behavior\. Conversely, neither information overload nor digital promotion significantly affects Sharia\-compliant financial management, nor does Sharia\-compliant financial management significantly influence consumptive behavior\. Mediation analysis reveals that Sharia\-compliant financial management does not mediate the impact of information overload or digital promotion on consumptive behavior\. These findings suggest that the consumptive behavior of Generation Z Shopee users is driven more directly by informational stimuli and digital promotions than through the mechanism of Sharia\-compliant financial management\.
