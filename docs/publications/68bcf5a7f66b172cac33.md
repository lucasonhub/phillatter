# FlowSep\-RF: Rectified Flow Matching for Single\-Channel Radio\-Frequency Signal Separation

**Autores:** Mohamed Kamel; Tamer Nadeem

**Data:** 2026-10-14 · **Tipo:** preprint

## Resumo

Single\-channel radio\-frequency \(RF\) signal separation is important for interference mitigation when a signal of interest overlaps with a co\-channel interferer in time and frequency\. Classical matched filtering and LMMSE receivers are limited under structured non\-Gaussian interference, while diffusion and score\-based methods often require iterative sampling or optimization\. We propose FlowSep\-RF, a rectified flow matching framework that learns a clean\-endpoint transport from the received mixture to the signal of interest\. The model combines time\-domain IQ samples with frequency\-domain features and recovers the separated waveform using one endpoint velocity evaluation\. We evaluate FlowSep\-RF on QPSK mixtures with OFDM\-BPSK, OFDM\-QPSK, and RFChallenge CommSignal2 interference against classical, discriminative, and generative baselines\. FlowSep\-RF achieves the lowest average BER and MSE among evaluated baselines across all three settings with one\-step inference\.
