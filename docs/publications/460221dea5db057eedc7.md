# Adaptive federated ensemble learning with hyperparameter optimization for distributed healthcare IoT

**Autores:** Abrar Almjally; Mudassir Khan; Alaa Mohammad Menshawi; Tahani Alsubait; Riaz Ahmad Ziar

**Data:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

The rapid evolution of IoT\-enabled healthcare systems has resulted in large\-scale distributed data generation across various institutions\. While federated learning enables collaborative model training without centralized data, it degrades non\-identical data distributions and statistical imbalance across clients\. Moreover, the convergence instability and hyperparameter sensitivity remain critical challenges in heterogeneous healthcare environments\. To address these limitations, this paper proposes a Stability\-Aware Secure Federated Ensemble Framework integrated with LampyraCetacea Optimization \(ASEFE\-LCO\)\. The platform integrates FedAvg and FedProx algorithms with an adaptive multi\-model ensemble mechanism to ensure convergence stability under non\-IID conditions\. To enhance predictive robustness, LampyraCetacea Optimization is employed for dynamic hyperparameter adaptation in non\-convex environments\. To offer formal privacy tamper\-proof protection, an integrated blockchain layer is applied to ensure decentralized auditability\. The proposed architecture offers an end\-to\-end intelligent platform that combines optimization stability, security assurance, scalability, and distributed knowledge integration\. As a core tenet of the proposed model, the experimental evaluation of the IoT healthcare security benchmark dataset achieves 99\.94% accuracy, indicating a robust, secure, and clinically interpretable solution for an intelligent healthcare system\.
