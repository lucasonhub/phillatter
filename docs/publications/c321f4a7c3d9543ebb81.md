# Sentinel\-2 NDVI as an indicator of apple orchard yield

**Autores:** N\. N\. Gasimzade; A\. Radzhabov; I\. Savin

**Data:** 2026-10-09 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Satellite data is increasingly being used as a basis for operational crop monitoring\. However, satellite technologies for monitoring perennial crops remain underdeveloped\. The aim of this study was to assess the relationship between apple yield and NDVI calculated using all available Sentinel\-2 data for the growing seasons \(March\-November\) from 2017 to 2024\. Totally 81 cloud\-free images were analyzed\. The seasonal absolute maximum and area\-weighted average NDVI were calculated based on analysis of all images for each growing season for each plot\. Intensively irrigated, mixed\-age apple orchards located in northern Azerbaijan served as the research site\. Totally 30 plots with 6 apple tree varieties \(Arian, Gala, Golden, Golden Parsi, Granny Smith, and Fuji\) planted from 2007 to 2017 were analyzed\. Apple yield for each plot for 2018–2024 were collected\. The linear regression models between NDVI \(the seasonal absolute maximum and area\-weighted average\) and apple yield was used as the relationship criterion\. An analysis of the relationship was conducted for individual apple varieties, for orchard plots, and for plots of different ages\. It was found that reliable relationships between apple yield and NDVI are only observed for specific plots with a single variety of the same age \(R2cv higher than 0,8 with RPIQ more than 2,1\)\. Grouping plots of the same variety or age does not demonstrate a reliable relationship between NDVI and yield\. Only for groups of middle\-aged orchards does such a relationship become stronger\. These results should be considered when developing methods for satellite monitoring of apple orchards\.
