# The Development Role of College English Teachers&\#x27; Digital Competence under AI Formative Evaluation

**Autores:** Yumei Zou

**Data:** 2026-10-13 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

The pressure to implement formative evaluation of English in colleges and universities is increasing day by day, and the massive and complex learning data increases the burden of teachers&\#x27; evaluation\. Artificial intelligence can run through the whole evaluation process, improve evaluation efficiency, and promote the transformation of digital teaching for teachers\. This paper takes college English teachers as the object, relies on multi\-source data to build an analysis framework, and explores the relationship between AI\-empowered formative evaluation and teachers&\#x27; digital competence\. The research shows that teachers&\#x27; AI application has the characteristics of staged evolution, digital ability differentiates with the degree of AI integration, and the overall competency improvement effect is obviously stratified\. This study improves the relevant theoretical system, and also provides practical reference for teacher training, intelligent teaching platform optimization and evaluation reform\. AI has become a key boost to reshape the evaluation model and enhance teachers&\#x27; digital literacy\.
