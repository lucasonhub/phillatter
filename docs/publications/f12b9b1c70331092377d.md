# Kluge Corrêa, Nicholas Kluge &amp; Mönig, Julia: Catalog of Field\-Specific Ethical Requirements for AI Certification

## Descrição do feed

\_Certified Ai Project\_\. 2025How to deal with ethical challenges when using artificial intelligence \(AI\) applications in the fields of arts, biometric profiling, industry and critical infrastructure, law enforcement, medicine and care and social media? This white paper offers guidance and provides a basis for ethical reflection on this question\. It continues the effort tied to the &quot;Certified AI&quot; project, one of the KI\-NRW flagship projects, financed by the Ministry of Economic Affairs, Innovation, Digitalization, and Energy of the State of North Rhine\-Westphalia\. The project seeks to develop certification methods for trustworthy artificial intelligence \(AI\)\. While in our Use Cases Catalog we documented 18 potential fields of application for AI systems, in our Catalog of General Ethical Requirements for AI Certification, we sought to propose general principles, evaluation criteria, normative recommendations, and practical implementations that can help developers and other types of stakeholders in making trustworthy AI development a pragmatic endeavor\. Building upon the above mentioned works, this study explores the ethical challenges of applying the general principles and practices we mapped onto specific fields of application\.
