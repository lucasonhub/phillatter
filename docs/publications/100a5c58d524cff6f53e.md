# Adaptive Randomized Neural Networks for PDEs: Structure\-Aware Feature Redistribution and Residual\-Driven Block Enrichment

**Autores:** You Yang; Fei Wang

**Data:** 2026-10-15 · **Tipo:** preprint

## Resumo

Randomized neural networks retain an output\-linear least\-squares solve, but globally sampled random features may fail to capture the spatial and scale structure of PDE solutions\. We propose SFRBE\-\-RaNN, a two\-stage adaptive trial\-space construction\. SFR redistributes a fixed feature budget according to regional indicator masses and adapts regional weight scales through low\-dimensional residual\-based optimization\. RBE constructs nonredundant enrichments by screening multiple activation families, removing represented response directions, and selecting the smallest admissible prefix\. For solution\-dependent operators, candidates are screened with a frozen operator and accepted only after nonlinear residual verification\. For fixed linear problems, we establish an exact discrete residual\-reduction identity and a conditional solution\-error estimate\. Building on this identity, we combine safeguarded SFR with RBE capture factors to derive a finite\-horizon error bound involving baseline best approximation and empirical consistency, together with a conditional random\-search success\-probability bound\. Numerical experiments on localized, multiscale, and nonlinear space\-\-time problems demonstrate the method&\#x27;s effectiveness\.
