# Mathematical model of the oil primary processing furnace

**Autores:** V\. A\. Dmitriev; S\. A\. Milyushenko; K\. V\. Belyaev

**Data:** 2026-10-11 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Improving the precision of automatic control systems for process heaters is a critical factor in optimizing crude oil primary distillation units\. The article introduces a dynamic mathematical model designed to maintain tight control over the outlet temperature, a vital parameter for subsequent high\-quality fractionation\. The object of the research i s a gable barrel\-type oil refinery furnace within a primary crude oil processing unit, featuring a temperature control loop for the petroleum product at the coil outlet based on fuel gas flow rate\. The modeling is based on the synthesis of transfer functions of system elements: the temperature sensor is represented as a first\-order lag element, the actuator — as an integrating element, the control valve — as an aperiodic link and the controlled process — as an aperiodic link\. A distinctive feature of the proposed approach is the comprehensive consideration of physicochemical factors: the variable calorific value of the fuel gas calculated via the Wobbe index; the heat accumulation capacity of the fire tube walls; and the dependence of crude oil specific heat capacity on its moisture content\. The simulation model is implemented in the SimInTech environment; the research is conducted using computational experiments involving the sequential application of step disturbances\. Therefore, the authors conclude that with an increase in the feedstock inlet temperature by 50 °C, a 30 % rise in the mass flow rate of oil and a fivefold increase in moisture content, the controlled variable reaches extreme values of 410, 350 and 340 °C relative to the setpoint of 370 °C, with a regulation time not exceeding 15 000 seconds\. The developed model enables minimization of technological regime deviations providing conditions for precise fractionation\.
