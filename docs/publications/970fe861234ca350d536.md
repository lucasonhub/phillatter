# A GEFS\-ensemble\-driven dual\-gate machine\-learning system for 3\-hourly quantitative precipitation forecasting over Brazil: independent dual\-reference verification and calibration for flood early warning

**Autores:** Guanru Lyu; Yue Jiang; Jiandan Zhong; Qi Wang; Linyan Luo; Yu Luo; Qunfu Huang; Tongzhe Liu

**Data:** 2026-10-14 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Abstract Sub\-daily quantitative precipitation forecasts \(QPF\) are a primary input to flood early warning, yet over Brazil they are hampered by convective, intermittent rainfall and a sparse observing network\. We present a machine\-learning post\-processing system \(V2\.0C\) producing 3\-hourly QPF on a 0\.25° grid by coupling a LightGBM precipitation\-occurrence classifier with a Tweedie amount regressor and a dual\-gate routing scheme with a physically guarded heavy\-rain \(≥5 mm\) hard route\. Its 71\-dimensional feature set is dominated by GEFS ensemble statistics\. The system is verified over two independent windows — April–May 2026 and the complete austral\-summer convective season \(October 2025– March 2026\) — against the IMERG satellite product \(areal\) and the INMET automatic\-station network \(point\)\. Against IMERG, V2\.0C attains the highest correlation \(0\.324 and 0\.286\) and a heavy\-rain CSI \(≥10 mm\) of 0\.063–0\.071, 3\.3–4\.4 times raw GFS, with stable month\-to\-month skill through the SACZ peak\. A common mask analysis localizes the 60–65% ensemble\-driven correlation gain to the southern third of the domain, where GEFS statistics are the only direct precipitation predictors available\. Controls show that the guarded routing makes a high\-recall heavy\-rain expert safe to deploy: it adds 6–10% heavy\-rain detection over a no\-expert control, whereas unguarded use halves heavy\-rain CSI\. Gauge verification reproduces the heavy\-rain advantage \(CSI ≥10 mm an order of magnitude above raw GFS\) and the wet bias, and the point–area correlation gap is explained by sub\-grid representativeness\. The detection\-oriented bias is removable a posteriori: leakage\-free quantile mapping eliminates it \(bias \+0\.02 mm, MAE −69%\) without reducing detection, supporting a dual\-product strategy for flood\-warning and hydrological applications\.
