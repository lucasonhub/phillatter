# A pictorial key to Southeast Asian species of Collotheca \(Rotifera, Collothecidae\), with a new species from Thailand

**Autores:** Phuripong Meksuwan; Rapeepan Jaturapruek; Thanida Saetang; Yurachat Meksuwan; Wijitra Choedchim; Phannee Sa\-ardrit; Diego Fontaneto; Supiyanit Maiphae

**Data:** 2026-10-13 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

A new species is described from Thailand, Collotheca laddae sp\. nov \., and a pictorial key to all Collotheca species and subspecies recorded in Southeast Asia is provided\. The new species was discovered during a freshwater zooplankton identification workshop\. This species is characterized by a five\-lobed infundibulum with very small lateral lobes and short vibratile setae\. The pictorial key was developed from a review of original descriptions and illustrations of all valid species of Collotheca and is designed to facilitate species\-level identification based on live specimens\. The discovery of C\. laddae sp\. nov \., together with the absence of new regional records for more than a decade, indicates that sessile rotifer diversity in Southeast Asia remains insufficiently explored\.
