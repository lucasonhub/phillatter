**Autores:** Sarmiati; Baili Kurniawan; Sugeng Kurniawan; Rugaiyah; Muhammad Sayuti

**Data:** 2026-10-13 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

This study aims to identify opportunities and challenges of AI utilization by elementary teachers in Bungo Regency, Jambi Province, and to map adaptation strategies developed within resource\-constrained contexts\. Employing a qualitative phenomenological approach, the research involved 15 purposively selected teachers with varied age, teaching experience, and school locations\. Data were collected through in\-depth interviews, classroom observations, and pedagogical document analysis, then analyzed thematically using Braun &amp; Clarke’s framework assisted by NVivo 14\. Findings reveal four main opportunities: \(1\) personalized learning pathways, \(2\) administrative efficiency and formative assessment, \(3\) interactive multimodal material development, and \(4\) expanded access to learning references\. Conversely, structural and pedagogical challenges emerged, including digital infrastructure gaps, TPACK competency variation, ethical concerns regarding data privacy and technological dependency, and lack of continuous mentoring\. Grounded in TPACK and UTAUT theoretical frameworks, the findings explain how perceived usefulness, ease of use, and institutional support shape AI adoption\. The study recommends practice\-based training enhancement, hybrid infrastructure provision, and responsive school policies for technological innovation\. This research contributes to elementary education literature in non\-metropolitan regions and offers a contextual, ethical, and sustainable AI implementation framework\.
