# XORIJIY MAMLAKATLAR BOSHLANG‘ICH TA’LIMIDA &quot;SCAFFOLDING&quot; TAJRIBASI \(FINLYANDIYA, SINGAPUR, AQSH\) VA UNI O‘ZBEKISTONGA MOSLASHTIRISH IMKONIYATLARI

**Autores:** NIGORA UMAR QIZI BO&\#x27;RONOVA

**Data:** 2026-10-12 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

The article presents a comparative analysis of scaffolding practices in the primary education of Finland, Singapore and the United States, focusing on early literacy and reading literacy instruction in Grades 1–4\. The role of the primary class teacher, early support systems, key instructional models and mechanisms for fading support are examined\. Taking into account the psychological characteristics of young learners, the agglutinative nature of Uzbek and the content of the subject &quot;Native Language and Reading Literacy&quot;, the article proposes directions for adapting this experience to Uzbek primary schools and a trajectory for reducing support from Grade 1 to Grade 4\.
