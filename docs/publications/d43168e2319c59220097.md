# Morphologic and Morphometric Differences between Cenostigma pyramidale and Cenostigma microphyllum Seeds

**Autores:** Thiago Costa Ferreira; E\. T\. Silva

**Data:** 2026-10-13 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

The characterization of Cenostigma pyramidale and Cenostigma microphyllum seeds for image measurements is important for improving knowledge of Caatinga areas\. Accordingly, this research aimed to compare the seeds of C\. pyramidale and C\. microphyllum in terms of morphological and morphometric traits, using digital image analysis as a method for measuring and classifying the seeds\. C\. pyramidale and C\. microphyllum seeds were analysed for morphological and morphometric characters\. The results obtained were analysed for descriptive statistics and non\-parametric methods\. C\. pyramidale and C\. microphyllum exhibit similar morphological characteristics, but show significant differences in seed shape and size variability\. Morphometric indices, combined with multivariate statistical analysis, allowed for a clear distinction between them\. The findings support the use of morphometric parameters as consistent tools for species discrimination, with direct applications in ecological restoration, seed technology, and biodiversity conservation in the Caatinga areas\.
