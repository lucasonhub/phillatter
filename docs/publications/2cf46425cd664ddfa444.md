# Comparative analysis of multi\-objective optimization algorithms for sustainable flyover design framework

**Autores:** Jaya Rajkumar Ramchandani; Suddhasheel Ghosh

**Data:** 2026-10-14 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Introduction Urban flyover infrastructure requires balancing multiple conflicting objectives, including structural efficiency, economic viability, and environmental sustainability\. Methods This study presents a comparative analysis of nine optimization algorithms applied to flyover design, integrating Radial Basis Function \(RBF\) surrogate models with explicit construction\-related carbon emission metrics\. The framework simultaneously minimizes flyover length, construction cost, and environmental impact\. A real\-world case study from Aurangabad, India was used to benchmark algorithm performance in terms of Pareto front diversity, convergence quality, computational efficiency, and solution feasibility\. Results Results highlight the superiority of NSGA\-II and NSGA\-III in generating diverse Pareto fronts, while methods such as Nelder\-Mead provide rapid convergence for targeted design refinements\. Sensitivity analysis further demonstrates the robustness of multi\-objective evolutionary algorithms under parameter uncertainty\. Discussion The novelty lies in integrating surrogate modeling with multi\-objective optimization for flyover design, with explicit environmental objectives\. This work contributes methodological innovation, practical guidelines for algorithm selection, and sustainability advancement in civil engineering\. By providing evidence\-based recommendations, the study supports engineers and planners in achieving resilient, cost\-effective, and environmentally responsible flyover designs\.
