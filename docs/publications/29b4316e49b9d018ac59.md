# Evaluación de sistemas de riego agrícola mediante tecnologías IoT para mejorar la eficiencia hídrica en viveros de balsa en la provincia de los Ríos

**Autores:** Jhon David Simba Moran

**Data:** 2026-10-15 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

The inefficiency of an agricultural irrigation system in raft nurseries \(Ochroma pyramidale\) is a problem faced by farmers in the Los Ríos province, due to limited automation and a heavy reliance on manual procedures\. The main objective of the research is to evaluate existing agricultural irrigation systems and identify critical areas where the implementation of IoT technologies can improve efficiency\. The methodology employed was descriptive, field\-based, and experimental, and involved the application of the deductive method to general principles regarding IoT technologies, smart controllers, and irrigation systems, relating them to the specific reality of the nurseries in the Los Ríos Province\. The research findings concluded that an automated micro\-sprinkler system using PID control improves the efficiency of manual irrigation at the El Moral forest nursery, as water consumption decreased from 14 L/day with manual irrigation to 7\.5 L/day\. This translates to a reduction in water consumption from 210 L to 112\.5 L over the experimental period, representing a savings of 97\.5 L, or 46\.43%\.
