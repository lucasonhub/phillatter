# Samir, Mohamed: Universal Resonance Theory \(URT\): A 12\.8 Hz Wave Unifying Dark Matter, Dark Energy, and Consciousness

**Autores:** 

**Data de descoberta:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** unknown

## Descrição do feed

The Universal Resonance Theory \(URT\) proposes a unified wave\-based framework in which the large\-scale structure and dynamics of the universe emerge from a single periodic resonance\. The theory is centered on a universal wave equation that models cosmic composition as an eternal oscillatory process rather than a collection of independent components\. Within this framework, phenomena traditionally attributed to dark matter, dark energy, and visible matter arise naturally from different regimes of the same resonance: wave peaks, temporal averages, and troughs, respectively\. The model provides a structural interpretation of flat galactic rotation curves observed in the SPARC dataset through resonance propulsion peaks \(RPP\), without introducing additional matter components\. URT further suggests that cosmic expansion and large\-scale stability follow from the long\-term behavior of the resonance itself, eliminating the need for singular origin assumptions\. Computational analyses are provided to demonstrate internal consistency and qualitative agreement with observational constraints\. This work is presented as a theoretical and computational framework intended to stimulate further investigation into resonance\-based cosmology and its potential observational consequences\.
