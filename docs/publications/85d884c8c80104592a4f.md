# Inaugural editorial for Discover Applied Mathematics

**Autores:** Chris G\. Antonopoulos; Majid Forghani\-elahabad; Kira Yue\-Ran Shi

**Data:** 2026-10-12 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

We are delighted to introduce Discover Applied Mathematics , a peer\-reviewed, fully open access journal published by Springer Nature\. The journal supports multidisciplinary research and policy developments that advance mathematical methods, computational techniques, and quantitative approaches to address challenges arising across science, engineering, industry, society, and public policy\.
