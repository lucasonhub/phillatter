# Cryogenic distortion compensation in guided ultrasonic waves using a Physics\-Informed Generative Adversarial Network

**Autores:** Shuozhi Wang; Jinyu Wang; Changxian Zheng; Tao Liu; Wenfeng Zhang; Jianqiang Li; Yishou Wang

**Data:** 2026-10-14 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Reusable launch vehicles undergo severe thermal cycling, and cryogenic storage conditions introduce strong, nonlinear distortions in guided ultrasonic wave signals used for structural health monitoring\. These temperature\-induced effects obscure damage sensitive features and limit the robustness of both conventional compensation techniques and purely data driven learning models\. This paper proposes a physics informed machine learning framework, termed the Guided Wave Physics\-Informed Generative Adversarial Network, to compensate cryogenic guided ultrasonic wave distortions in a physically consistent manner\. The artificial intelligence component is realized through generative adversarial learning and is applied to cryogenic guided\-wave temperature compensation The model adopts a residual generative formulation in the Hilbert\-envelope domain and integrates adversarial sequence learning with wave\-physics priors through curvature based regularization and peak morphology constraints\. These physics\-informed components explicitly suppress nonphysical temporal artifacts and preserve the modal structure of guided\-wave responses, enabling stable compensation across varying cryogenic conditions\. Experiments conducted on an aluminum alloy plate under liquid\-nitrogen cooling–heating cycles demonstrate that GW\-PIGAN achieves high\-fidelity reconstruction while maintaining consistent performance across varying reference temperatures\. Compared with purely data\-driven baselines, the proposed physics\-informed formulation exhibits improved stability, physical plausibility, and generalization under extreme thermal conditions\. By embedding guided\-wave physics into generative learning, the proposed framework provides a practical and interpretable physics\-informed machine learning approach for cryogenic structural health monitoring\. It also establishes a methodological basis for future monitoring applications in reusable aerospace structures under extreme cryogenic conditions\.
