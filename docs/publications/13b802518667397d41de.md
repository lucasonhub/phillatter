# Geopolitical Risk Prediction and Robust Investment Decision\-Making for Transnational Photovoltaic Projects in Africa via Generative AI and Spatiotemporal GNN

**Autores:** Jiayu Sun; Guan Yang; Zhenguo Cao; Wei Zhao

**Data:** 2026-10-18 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Geopolitical risks associated with transnational photovoltaic \(PV\) projects in Africa are dynamic, interconnected, and difficult to assess using conventional investment models\. Specifically, existing approaches often struggle to characterize cross\-border risk transmission and incorporate evolving geopolitical information into investment decisions\. To address these challenges, this study proposes EG\-GeoInvest, a framework that integrates generative artificial intelligence \(AI\) with spatiotemporal graph neural networks for geopolitical risk assessment and risk\-aware investment decision\-making\. First, geopolitical event records, multilingual news, national governance indicators, and PV investment data are combined within an evidence\-constrained, multisource heterogeneous information system to support the identification and semantic representation of risk events\. Second, a Spatiotemporal Geopolitical Risk Encoding Module \(ST\-GREM\) integrates a temporal convolutional network with relationship\-aware graph convolution to represent the evolution of risks and their interdependencies across transnational investment, energy\-trade, and financing networks\. Third, cross\-modal semantic projection and Low\-Rank Adaptation \(LoRA\) enable graph\-structured information and generative language models to contribute jointly to forecasts of geopolitical risks, project delays, and economic loss distributions\. Finally, the predicted risk distributions inform a Conditional Value\-at\-Risk \(CVaR\) and distributionally robust optimization framework for investment allocation under return and tail\-risk constraints\. A proposed evaluation design encompassing comparative risk prediction, cross\-country generalization, and investment scenario analysis provides a basis for assessing predictive performance and potential decision\-making value\.
