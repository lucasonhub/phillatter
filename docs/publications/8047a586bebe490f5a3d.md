# Singh, Keshav: Should Love Make Us Crazy?

## Descrição do feed

In Lindsay Brainard, Ryan W\. Davis &amp; Jessica Flanigan, The Philosophy of Taylor Swift\. New York: Oxford University Press\. 2026As Taylor Swift explores in much of her music, love can make us crazy\. It can make us act and feel in ways that are beyond the grasp of reason\. But should it make us crazy in these ways? In other words, is it part of a legitimate ideal of love that the person in love acts and feels in ways that cannot be rationally justified? To answer such questions, we must tease apart two philosophical issues they raise regarding the connection between love and reason\. The first issue is whether loving itself – especially whom we should love – is subject to the standards of reason\. The second issue is whether being in love makes it the case that we should do things out of love that are in some sense contrary to reason\. For each of these issues, we can identify, roughly speaking, a rationalist and an anti\-rationalist position\. Considering each position reveals that both extremes have problems, which suggests that the relationship between love and reason is fraught in rich and interesting ways\. As such, the correct position is likely to be a sort of middle ground in the form of a limited and qualified form of rationalism\.
