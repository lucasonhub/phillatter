# Cultural identity in rapidly urbanizing cities: the role of public art, urban design, and digital engagement

**Autores:** Khaula Alkaabi; Fatmaelzahraa Hussein; Salieu Senghore; Nour Agiel; Sarah Aljaberi

**Data:** 2026-10-09 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Introduction This study investigates the complementary roles of public art \(PA\), culturally responsive urban design \(UD\), and digital public art platforms \(PADP\) in preserving cultural identity and strengthening national identity in the UAE\. Methods Using a quantitative approach informed by postpositivist and constructivist perspectives, a survey of 543 residents \(72% under 35 years\) examined perceptions of national identity and cultural motifs\. Results Structural Equation Modeling revealed that 81% viewed PA as enhancing pride, while 65% valued PADP for promoting culture\. Arabic calligraphy \(68%\) and natural motifs \(74%\) were key identity anchors\. Discussion Public art serves as the primary visual medium, urban design provides the physical setting, and digital platforms extend cultural engagement beyond the built environment\. Integrating cultural elements into physical urban landscapes and digital public art platforms can strengthen community belonging\.
