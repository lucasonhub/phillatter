# Enhancing the residual strength of clay bricks exposed to elevated temperatures using metal oxides, carbon black, and sodium hydroxide activation

**Autores:** Hebah Mohammad Al\-jabali; Walid Fouad Edris; Amr Abdelkhalik; Abd Al\-Kader A\. Al Sayed; Shady Khairy

**Data:** 2026-10-12 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Clay bricks are widely used in construction because they are strong, durable and fire\-resistant, but their thermal insulation is still not good enough, which leads to a big loss of energy\. The combined effect of alkali activation or addition of metal oxides on residual mechanical properties of clay bricks at elevated temperature has not been systematically addressed although these treatments have been studied separately\. This paper presents the enhancing effects of metal oxides \(Fe 2 O 3 , Al 2 O 3 and Carbon Black N550\) and sodium hydroxide \(NaOH\) activation on residual strength of clay bricks exposed to elevated temperatures up to 800°C\. Clay bricks were prepared using 82% clay, 13% fine sand, 5% fly ash and different replacement/additive dosages of Fe 2 O 3 \(2–6 wt\.%\), Al 2 O 3 \(3–7 wt\.%\), and Carbon Black N550 \(0\.25–1 wt\.%\) activated with 8 M NaOH\. Compressive strength, bulk density, water absorption, TGA and accelerated aging tests were conducted following the international standards\. The Al\-7A mixture exhibited the highest compressive strength of 19\.5 MPa at ambient temperature \(57\.3% increase over control\) and retained 14\.8 MPa \(75\.9%\) after exposure to 800°C\. Fe\-4A and CB\-0\.5A achieved 16\.8 MPa and 16\.0 MPa at ambient with residual strengths of 11\.5 MPa and 10\.5 MPa at 800°C\. The water absorption of Al\-7A was 38\.0% lower \(8\.8%\) than that of the control \(14\.2%\)\. The bulk density was increased to 1980 kg/m 3 \. TGA revealed Al\-7A exhibited the lowest weight loss \(4\.8%\) and highest residual mass \(95\.2%\)\. After seven accelerated aging cycles, Al\-7A retained 82\.1% of initial strength, outperforming Fe\-4A \(75\.0%\) and CB\-0\.5A \(70\.6%\)\. The research demonstrates that combining alumina addition with NaOH activation offers a viable pathway for producing high\-performance clay bricks with enhanced thermal stability and durability for fire\-prone construction applications\.
