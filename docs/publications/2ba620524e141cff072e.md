# Construction of personal health knowledge graphs for clinical data harmonization in breast cancer

## Resumo

Integrating heterogeneous personal health data and maintaining clinical registries for breast cancer management is labor\-intensive\. This study presents an AI\-powered virtual assistant transforming identifiable breast cancer patient data into interoperable personal health knowledge graphs for secondary use\. The first\-generation prototype was deployed across three hospitals to extract and ingest patient data under informed consent, mapped to ontology\-compliant knowledge graphs, with SHACL\-based structural validation and human\-in\-the\-loop review\. Curated data covered demographics, diagnosis, pathology, laboratory results, surgery, therapy, and follow\-up, from which local SPARQL queries generated per\-site standardized registries\. Ingestion and curation results were completed at two sites: ingestion success of 51–94%, curation success of 59–64% and automated curation of 41–47%\. Overall, SHACL\-based structural data quality was 94\.5–96\.1% \(validity: &gt;98%; completeness: 76–81%\)\. These results demonstrate the feasibility of semi\-automated health data harmonization and knowledge graph construction, representing an initial step toward scalable, interoperable registry infrastructure in breast cancer\.
