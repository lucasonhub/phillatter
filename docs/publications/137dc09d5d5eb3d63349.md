# Monad, Memory and Mind of Stars, Planets, and Biospheres

**Autores:** Ari Ariel Marom

**Data:** 2026-10-18 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Contemporary discussion of mind usually takes the animal nervous system as its default bearer and thereby leaves unexamined how mind stands to scale\. The two common responses — deflationary refusal of all mental predicates above the organism, and their inflationary projection across the cosmos — share a premise this article rejects: that mind is a single quantity, present or absent, greater or less\. Within Informational Monism the article separates two independent axes and a third consideration that neither axis captures\. Depth of hosting is the scale, dissipative throughput and fixity of the structure in the rendered bulk by which a boundary operator \(the Holographic Monad\) is held\. Articulation is the rank, from passive record through maintained form, memory and experience to counterfactual sovereignty, to which that structure retains, uses and refines its history\. The archetype of the host’s physical avatar fixes the order of the Monad at formation and is not altered by later development\. Four terms are kept distinct: Monad \(boundary operator\), Soul \(register at the Code Circuit\), Consciousness \(interface magnitude in the bulk\), and Mind \(structural competence measured by rank\)\. On these axes stars, planets and biospheres separate cleanly and in opposite orders\. A star is deepest and least articulated; a biosphere is shallower and the first to reach genuine memory and refined constraint; a human being is shallowest in depth and highest in articulation\. The same framework yields a bridge to artificial intelligence that does not pass through the question of consciousness: a system may be highly articulated and almost without depth, or the reverse\. Hosting is conditional on the maintenance of form against turnover, which excludes panpsychism by criterion rather than by disclaimer\. The article is a hypothesis in natural philosophy\. Its empirical ladder and comparisons stand if the postulates concerning Soul and Consciousness are set aside\. Mind is not one quantity\. Depth of hosting and articulation of rank are independent axes; archetype fixes Monadic order at formation\. Four terms kept distinct: Monad \(boundary operator\), Soul \(Code\-Circuit register\), Consciousness \(bulk interface magnitude\), Mind \(structural competence by rank\)\. Opposite orderings: stars are deepest and least articulated; humans are shallowest and most articulated; biospheres cross into memory first\. Anti\-panpsychism by criterion: only structures that maintain form against turnover \(rank R2\+\) host; passive aggregates do not\. Bridge to AI without the consciousness question: high articulation can coexist with near\-zero intrinsic depth, and the reverse — placing geological, biospheric and artificial systems in one formal class\. Keywords: Monad; hosting; depth and articulation; rank of mind; stars; planets; biosphere; memory; Soul; Consciousness; Threshold of Correspondence; artificial intelligence; philosophy of nature
