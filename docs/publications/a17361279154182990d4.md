# An Analytical Study of a Governed Lakehouse Framework in Multi\-State Medicare Claims and Provider Data for Fraud, Waste, and Abuse Detection

**Autores:** Chandra Obul Reddy Nagi Reddy

**Data:** 2026-10-17 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Medicare fraud detection, waste, and abuse is hampered by the high volume of claims, highly fragmented data\-analysis systems, limited visibility into data quality, and significant class imbalance\. This study assessed the potential of an Original Medicare Part B \(OMB\) Bronze\-Silver\-Gold lakehouse to improve data quality, traceability, analytical efficiency, and provider\-level risk identification across OMB\-covered populations in California, New York, Florida, Texas, and Pennsylvania in 2024\. Using Apache Spark/PySpark with Delta\-format tables, the study sampled 1,500,000 provider\-service records in proportion to state Traditional Medicare enrollment, representing 214,873 unique NPIs, consolidated them with CMS provider files and HHS\-OIG provider\-exclusion data, interpreted 30 features, and used four models: Logistic Regression, Random Forest, XGBoost, and Isolation Forest\. During Silver\-layer screening, 27,362 records \(1\.82%\) triggered one or more quality rules and were quarantined; NPI enrichment had 99\.7% compliance, and Gold\-layer data quality had 99\.2% compliance\. This reduced query time from a median of 18\.4 seconds to 5\.7 seconds \(approximately 69%\)\. XGBoost performed best among the supervised models, with ROC\-AUC = 0\.91, PR\-AUC = 0\.34, and F1 = 0\.66\. State elevated/high\-risk flags were not confirmed as fraud; they were a priority for investigation and ranged from 2\.2%–3\.4% of unique providers within each state\. Manually managed, repeatable pipelines provide defensible Medicare FWA prioritization, and repeatable, source\-to\-feature, auditable pipelines can support multi\-state, analytic workflows where needed\. Areas to explore include longitudinal claims, Part D, DMEPOS, enforcement outcomes, graph analytics, and prospective investigator validation\.
