# Lightweight multimodal fusion for UAV classification with explicit missing\-modality modeling

**Autores:** Yibo Feng; Ning Li; Di Wu; Ximing Ma; Xiangyue Yu; Haiyang Bao

**Data:** 2026-10-14 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Abstract Fine\-grained unmanned aerial vehicle \(UAV\) type classification benefits from complementary sensors, yet real deployments face incomplete observations and heterogeneous stream reliability\. We present a missing\-aware fusion framework for RGB detections \(YOLO11\-S\), Livox Avia LiDAR, 360° LiDAR, and radar\-enhanced point clouds on MMAUD\. Confidence–density scores are masked and renormalized so fusion weight is assigned only to observed streams \(Eq\. \(2\)\); each absent stream still occupies a classifier slot via a learnable missing embedding that is not multiplied by the zero weight \(Eq\. \(1\)\), keeping absence as a trainable signal rather than a numerical zero\. On a time\-stratified split, Tiny MLP \(≈ 0\.1 M fusion parameters\) reaches 96\.12% test accuracy in a representative seed\-42 run\. Over seeds 42–46, final test accuracy is 95\.29 ± 0\.60% without training\-time modality dropout and 95\.77 ± 0\.90% with 30% training ModDrop \(mean ± sample s\.d\.\)\. Under matched 30% test missingness, accuracy remains 83\.68% \(seed 42\)\. Protocol\-aligned baselines and orthogonal ablations support the slot rule\. Fusion\-stage inference is about 2\.1 ms per sample with pre\-extracted features on an RTX 4090\. Under our fixed split and frozen features—not as a challenge\-leaderboard claim—compact missing\-aware fusion is practical for heterogeneous UAV sensing under partial sensor availability\.
