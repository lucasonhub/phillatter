# Lane, Elek: Slurs, Words, and Articulations: Critical Notice on \_Inflammatory Language\_ by Una Stojnić and Ernie Lepore

**Autores:** 

**Data de descoberta:** 2026-10-19 · **Tipo:** unknown

## Descrição do feed

\_Analytic Philosophy\_\. forthcomingThe Articulation Account provides a novel explanation of how slurs shock and offend their audiences, and is defended by Stojnić and Lepore \(2025\)\. The account is defined by two primary commitments: to being articulation\-based and to being associationist\. I look at these commitments and consider some reasons to be hesitant about adopting them\. Along the way, I discuss the inheritance phenomenon, one of the major pieces of evidence given in support of the Articulation Account, and question its scope\.
