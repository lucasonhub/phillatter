# Desafíos jurídicos de las exposiciones inmersivas y gestión responsable del patrimonio cultural

**Autores:** Ana\. E Quintana Jiménez

**Data:** 2026-10-13 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

The integration of immersive technologies into museum exhibitions has profoundly transformed the ways in which cultural heritage is accessed, interpreted and experienced\. Through the use of virtual reality, augmented reality, artificial intelligence and blockchain, cultural institutions have developed new ways of engaging with artefacts and historical narratives\. However, these innovations raise significant legal and ethical challenges, particularly at the intersection of intellectual property law and cultural heritage protection frameworks, including issues such as the legal status of digital surrogates, the commercialisation of works in the public domain, the rights of communities of origin, and the regulatory implications of emerging technologies\. This paper argues that the sustainable future of immersive exhibitions lies not only in technological advancement, but also in the cultivation of ethical, legal and social frameworks that prioritise cultural rights, historical integrity and public participation\.
