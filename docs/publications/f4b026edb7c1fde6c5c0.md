# AMS\-LPP: adaptive model selection for lightfastness property prediction in textile materials using similarity\-guided transfer learning and AutoML

**Autores:** Murad Ali Khan; Muhammad Faseeh; Syed Shehryar Ali Naqvi; Do\-Hyeun Kim

**Data:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Abstract Accurate material property prediction can accelerate materials discovery, but conventional experimental methods remain costly and time\-intensive, and machine learning alternatives are often degraded by domain shifts across heterogeneous datasets\. Transfer Learning \(TL\) is effective only when source and target domains are similar, while AutoML ignores inter\-domain relationships entirely, so neither approach alone adapts to varying domain similarity\. This paper proposes AMS\-LPP, a similarity\-guided framework that dynamically switches between TL and AutoML based on domain similarity quantified via Mahalanobis distance: a pretrained TabNet model is fine\-tuned with Bayesian hyperparameter optimization under high similarity, while AutoML is invoked under low similarity to ensure robust generalization\. Experiments on multi\-domain textile datasets for lightfastness prediction show that TL achieves $$R^2 &gt; 0\.99$$ with up to 35% less training time under high and moderate similarity, while AutoML attains the best generalization \( $$R^2 = 0\.998$$ \) under low similarity\. SHAP\-based explainability further shows predictions are driven by physically meaningful optical and color\-related features\. These results demonstrate that AMS\-LPP provides a scalable, efficient, and interpretable solution for similarity\-aware model selection in heterogeneous material property prediction\.
