# Experimental Investigation of Chilled Water Flowrate Due to Temperature Change in Cooling Coil

**Autores:** Sekiteri Patience Sametu; Umar Adam Isah

**Data:** 2026-10-13 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

The experiment conducted shows that repeated cooling capacity of 18\.00W has steady buoyancy\. At this set point air densities, convective heat transfer coefficients and volumetric mass flowrate tends to compensate objective constraints of heat loss within air handling units\. When forced heat transfer convection occurs, differential pressure and water flow rate varies haptic relief valve was set at 40\.20W cooling capacity\. This research estimated the pressure drop due to piping using counter flow to attain 0\.90 effectiveness\. When the inlet and outlet temperature decreases at 0\.30W at 3 percent of effectiveness\.
