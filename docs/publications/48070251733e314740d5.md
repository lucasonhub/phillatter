# INFRARED THERMOGRAPHY OF SUBSTATION EQUIPMENT IN HOT CLIMATES: A REVIEW OF ERROR SOURCES AND A FIELD PROTOCOL

**Autores:** Dilafruz Abdullabekova

**Data:** 2026-10-13 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

The article reviews error sources of infrared inspection of substation equipment in hot climates\. A radiometric model shows that default camera settings underestimate bare\-metal temperatures by tens of degrees and compress phase\-to\-phase differences\. A seven\-step field protocol for surveys is proposed\.
