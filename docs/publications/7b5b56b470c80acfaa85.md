# Cott, Nicholas: The Empty Occupant: Answerability, Testimony, and the Limits of Machine\-Mediated Authority

**Autores:** 

**Data de descoberta:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** unknown

## Descrição do feed

Institutional issuance and the capacity to answer for it can scale differently\. This monograph develops a substrate\-neutral Discursive Ontology connecting commitment bearership, warranted adoption, and sustainable authority\. Answerability is the conjunction of attribution continuity, challenge\-responsive reconsideration, repair competence, and standing\-consequence within reciprocal normative standing\. Actual, Residual, Prospective, and Empty Occupancy distinguish a role’s present, historical, developing, and missing bearership\. Empty Occupancy is a conditional diagnosis requiring presentation as an independent bearer and evidence that the relevant relations are absent; the documented cases do not establish it categorically\. The governing axiom is that missing bearership is not defective ownership\. The two\-axis model separates epistemic uptake from commitment ownership\. On assurance views, a machine\-mediated invitation to rely can exercise testimonial force without constituting testimony\. Normative Sponsorship specifies five conditions of warranted adoption: epistemic access, authority, review bandwidth, independent rejection, and repair power\. Its opacity threshold requires institutional reconstructibility at contestation grain, while organizational capture concerns reasons that cannot bind\. These distinctions extend to model succession and agentic chains\. The central argument derives a service requirement from defeasible practical authority: reliance beyond the recipient’s own grasp must remain effectively revisable within consequence\-sensitive intervals\. Deontic Debt records matured obligations deficiently serviced; scale\-indexed Institutional Insolvency identifies issuance exceeding the serviceable capacity of a specified architecture and horizon\. Its consequence is targeted loss of presumptive authority, with ownership and repair duties surviving\. Robodebt, Air Canada, TennCare, Horizon, the Dutch childcare\-benefits affair, and a bounded pressbased generative\-interface episode discipline these distinctions\. A worked obligation ledger makes missing evidence explicit\. Discursive Stewardship connects permissible use, protective review, legacy discharge, and staged resumption\. Chapter 8 addresses artificial agency, status conferral, intentionality, human comparison, scarcity, strategic challenge, and revision conditions\. \(direct link \)
