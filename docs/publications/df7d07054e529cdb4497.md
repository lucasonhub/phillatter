# From prediction to mechanism: rethinking PTSD treatment non\-response through an iron–inflammation framework

**Autores:** Mayuresh S\. Korgaonkar; Richard Allan Bryant

**Data:** 2026-10-15 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Precision psychiatry has improved prediction of treatment response across psychiatry, but translating biomarkers into treatment strategy remains difficult\. Using PTSD as a model, we outline an iron\-inflammation framework linking treatment\-predictive neural circuitry to an upstream biological mechanism, illustrating how neuroimaging may support biologically informed stratification and targeted adjunctive treatments\.
