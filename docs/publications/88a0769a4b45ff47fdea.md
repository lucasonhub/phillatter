# Short\-Term Earnings Forecast of CSI 300etf — Based on the Monte Carlo Simulation Method

**Autores:** Zixi Huang

**Data:** 2026-10-13 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

This paper takes Huatai\-Pine Bridge Fund Management Co\., Ltd&\#x27;s CSI 300ETF\(510300\) as the research object, selects the real trading data of 300 trading days from June 1, 2025, to March 24, 2026, and uses the Monte Carlo simulation method to research short\-term income prediction\. Firstly, the core parameters \(average daily rate of return \-0\.007%, standard deviation of daily rate of return 0\.662%\) are calculated based on historical data, and then 10000 price fluctuation paths are generated through geometric Brownian motion to quantitatively analyze the distribution characteristics of returns in the next 30 days\. The empirical results show that the expected total return rate of CSI 300etf in the next 30 days is \-0\.215%, and the average daily return rate is \-0\.007%, which is highly consistent with the historical return trend\. The 95% confidence interval is \[\-7\.356%, 6\.892%\], with a rise probability of 47\.23% and a fall probability of 52\.77%\. The gap between the two is small, reflecting that the subject matter has a narrow range of shocks and slight falls in the short term, and the fluctuation risk is controllable\. The historical back test verification shows that the prediction error of the model is in a reasonable range, with good adaptability and effectiveness\. The research conclusion can provide a quantitative reference for the asset allocation of robust investors, and enrich the empirical research results of broad\-based ETF earnings prediction\.
