# Stable core rhizosphere microbiota across the entire wheat growth cycle mitigates cadmium accumulation via reducing rhizosphere cadmium Bioavailiability

**Autores:** Wanqi Qiao; Zheng Lei; Yin Wang; Yonghui Zhu; Zhifang Zhang; Zhen Wang; Jiliang Lü; Jiandong Sheng; Xiaohu Zhao

**Data:** 2026-10-11 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Reducing crop cadmium \(Cd\) uptake via beneficial rhizosphere microbes offers a low\-cost, sustainable strategy for ensuring agricultural product safety\. Most prior investigations have largely focused on mechanisms examined at single time points or individual functional strains, leaving the stable core microbial taxa that persist throughout the plant growth cycle and the synergistic potential of multi\-strain consortia in alleviating Cd stress largely underexplored\. In this study, we conducted pot experiments using wheat \( Triticum aestivum L\.\) spiked with three Cd concentration gradients \(0, 1\.00, and 3\.00 mg/kg\)\. We integrated 16S rRNA gene amplicon sequencing with machine learning models to identify core microbial taxa and construct rationally designed synthetic microbial communities \(SynComs\)\. The results revealed that a subset of microorganisms stably colonized the wheat rhizosphere across all developmental stages and exhibited dynamic correlations with bioavailable Cd concentrations in the rhizosphere\. The optimal SynCom, designed around the key strain Pontibacter salisaro \(OTU1093\), significantly promoted wheat growth and development compared with the non\-inoculated control\. Specifically, it reduced shoot Cd content by 9\.7%\-24\.9% and inhibited Cd translocation from roots to aboveground tissues\. Further mechanistic investigation showed that the optimal SynCom treatment decreased bioavailable Cd in the rhizosphere by 64\.89% and promoted the transformation of Cd into less bioavailable chemical fractions\. These findings demonstrate that mining plant developmental stage\-informed core microbiomes, coupled with cultivation\-based synthetic community assembly, provides an effective framework for the microbial remediation of Cd\-contaminated farmland and the safe production of staple crops\.
