# Exercise dampens microglia synaptic engulfment in Alzheimer’s disease

**Autores:** Juan Zu

**Data:** 2026-10-13 · **Tipo:** dissertation

## Resumo

Emerging evidence substantiates the beneficial effects of exercise in mitigating cognitive impairment in Alzheimer&\#x27;s disease \(AD\)\. However, the underlying mechanisms by which exercise exerts its effects in AD remain poorly understood\. Microglia play a central role in AD\-associated dementia, particularly through the pruning of synapses\. This study demonstrates that long\-term voluntary exercise upregulates cholinergic signaling, increases the expression of microglial α7 nicotinic acetylcholine receptors \(α7 nAchRs\), and reduces excessive microglial synaptic engulfment in a mouse model of AD\. Furthermore, primary microglia exposed to acetylcholine exhibited enhanced α7 nAchRs expression and significantly decreased engulfment of synaptosomes, an effect that was reversed by the α7 nAChRs antagonist methyllycaconitine \(MLA\)\. These findings provide evidence that long\-term exercise attenuates microglial excessive phagocytosis of synaptic components via modulation of α7 nAchRs, highlighting a potential therapeutic target in AD\-associated cognitive decline\.
