# Bulgaria, Jamel: Psychometric Viability, Empirical Applications, and Strategic Future of the Core Emotion Framework

**Autores:** 

**Data de descoberta:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** unknown

## Descrição do feed

The Core Emotion Framework \(CEF\) is a structural constructivist model that formalizes human emotional functioning through ten somatic–cognitive “operators” distributed across the Head, Heart, and Gut centers\. The present manuscript evaluates the psychometric viability, empirical foundations, and strategic future of the CEF by integrating quantitative reliability analyses, confirmatory and exploratory factor modeling, qualitative participant reflections, and emerging clinical–biometric applications\. Phase 1 validation data \(N = 826\) demonstrate strong internal consistency across nine of ten operators \(α =\.75–\.86\), with Sensing marginally acceptable \(α =\.69\)\. However, strict Independent Clusters CFA produced a severe incremental fit paradox \(CFI =\.684, TLI =\.674\) despite excellent absolute fit \(RMSEA =\.0499\)\. As the document notes, this divergence arises because “the latent space of the CEF is highly integrated, and a strict CFA model is mathematically inappropriate for capturing its construct relevant multidimensionality\.” Exploratory Factor Analysis confirms meaningful cross loadings and dynamic realignment across operators, supporting the adoption of Exploratory Structural Equation Modeling \(ESEM\) as the appropriate psychometric framework\. Qualitative data reveal high ecological validity: participants reported immediate insight into their somatic patterns, with one noting they “struggle to just sit with and accept uncertainty,” mapping directly onto predicted Sensing–Calculating fusion\. Applied sections outline testable neuroendocrine mechanisms—particularly the proposed Expand–Accept fusion phenotype in obesity, hypothesized to predict mPFC–DMN hyperconnectivity, blunted cortisol awakening responses, and visceral adiposity\. The manuscript concludes with a strategic roadmap emphasizing methodological modernization \(ESEM\), decentralized open science replication, and execution of psychometric–biometric studies to secure the CEF’s long term scientific viability\.
