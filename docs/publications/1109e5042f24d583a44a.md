# Sueyoshi, Makoto: Flovien Theory: Applied Edition

**Autores:** 

**Data de descoberta:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** unknown

## Descrição do feed

This paper is the Applied Edition of Flovien Theory, a companion to the Principles Edition \(“Being Is the Stabilization of Translation,” 2026\)\. The Principles Edition established that being is the perturbation\-robust stabilization of a directed, resistance\-bearing translational pattern in the productive interval 0 &lt; R &lt; 1; it showed that translation precedes difference, description, and the subject, each of which is a late\-arriving derivative\. The Applied Edition asks what follows, within this framework, for the specific phenomena of consciousness, knowledge, value, love, ethics, and practice\. Each domain is treated by identifying what the Principles Edition already implies, adding one explicitly argued Bridge Principle, and deriving structural consequences\. The paper is careful throughout to distinguish formally derived results from philosophically argued claims\. Chapter One argues that consciousness requires directed translational self\-closure \(Ψ\) above a threshold, together with further structural conditions, and proves formally that any system with sufficient self\-closure resists lossless recovery from external inputs alone — a structural account of the explanatory gap that is neither eliminativist nor dualist\. Chapter Two argues that knowledge is robust low\-resistance translation from world to knower, and derives that epistemic humility is a theorem rather than a philosophical choice\. Chapter Three argues that normative value arises when a conscious system takes stabilization as a structural commitment, with anti\-anthropocentric consequences\. Chapter Four proposes that love is directed co\- stabilization — maintenance of the beloved’s productive interval as a component of the lover’s own\. Chapter Five derives a translational ethical principle: the duty not to foreclose another entity’s productive interval, grounded without appeal to rationality or sentience\. Chapter Six situates practical wisdom as navigation of the productive interval in action\. The Conclusion applies the framework to itself\. \(direct link \)
