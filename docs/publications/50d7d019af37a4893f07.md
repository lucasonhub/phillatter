# Multidrug\-resistant monophasic Salmonella Typhimurium ST34 strain producing blaNDM\-5 carbapenemase isolated from a patient with rectal cancer in Hainan, China: comprehensive whole genome dissection of antibiotic resistance and virulence determinants

**Autores:** Shan Zhong; Lei Xing; Licheng Wang; Chongzhen Wang; Xiaoying Fu; Lixia Xu; Yuanli Li; Huaxiong Zhu; Lvfen He; Xiong Zhu; Huan Li

**Data:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Background Here, we report the identification and comprehensive whole\-genome characterization of a New Delhi metallo\-β\-lactamase 5 \( bla NDM\-5 \)\-producing multidrug\-resistant \(MDR\) monophasic Salmonella Typhimurium ST34 strain SYA001 from an immunocompromised rectal cancer patient\. Monophasic Salmonella Typhimurium \( Salmonella enterica subsp\. enterica serovar 1,4,\[5\],12:i:\-\) is one of the leading causative agents of foodborne gastroenteritis and hospitalization worldwide, and the rapid emergence and spread of carbapenem\-resistant strains have severely compromised clinical treatment and posed a major threat to public health\. Methods In this study, a series of standardized phenotypic assays were performed to characterize the microbiological, biochemical, and antimicrobial resistance profiles of strain SYA001\. Matrix\-assisted laser desorption/ionization time\-of\-flight mass spectrometry \(MALDI\-TOF MS\), biochemical identification, and slide agglutination serotyping were used for species and serotype confirmation\. Antimicrobial susceptibility testing was conducted using the VITEK 2 compact system, and six types of carbapenemases were screened via a colloidal gold immunochromatographic assay\. Whole\-genome sequencing \(WGS\) was performed on the DNBSEQ\-T7 platform, and a comprehensive bioinformatics pipeline was applied to dissect the genomic structure, phylogenetic characteristics, antibiotic resistance determinants, plasmid profiles, prophage regions, Salmonella pathogenicity islands \(SPIs\), and protein\-protein interaction \(PPI\) network of the strain\. Results Our results confirmed that SYA001 is a serovar 1,4,\[5\],12:i:\- monophasic Salmonella Typhimurium belonging to the globally epidemic ST34 clone\. The strain exhibited an MDR phenotype, with resistance to 7 classes of antibiotics including carbapenems, cephalosporins, and aminoglycosides, and was only susceptible to aztreonam, amikacin, tigecycline, and colistin\. Genomic analysis identified the core carbapenem resistance gene bla NDM\-5 , along with multiple coexisting resistance genes including bla OXA\-10 , qnrS 1, tet \(A\), sul 2, and sul 3 and so on\. Four plasmid replicons \(IncHI2, IncHI2A, IncQ1, p0111\), 15 prophage regions, and a complete set of virulence\-related SPIs \(SPI\-1 to SPI\-5,SPI\-9, SPI\-13, SPI\-14\) were detected\. PPI network analysis revealed AcrA as the core hub of the efflux\-mediated resistance regulatory network\. Conclusion This study provides genomic insights into bla NDM\-5 \-producing MDR monophasic Salmonella\. Typhimurium from an immunocompromised rectal cancer patient, supplements clinical surveillance data of carbapenem\-resistant Salmonella in Hainan\.
