# Teaching Evidence in the Age of AI: Strategies for Engaging Students Without Discouraging Innovation

**Autores:** Whitney Sproul

**Data:** 2026-10-12 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

As artificial intelligence \(AI\) becomes increasingly integrated into student learning and research practices, librarians face the challenge of promoting evidence\-based resource use without discouraging the adoption of emerging technologies\. This poster explores strategies for encouraging students to engage with evidence\-based information sources while recognizing AI as a valuable starting point for inquiry\. Rather than framing AI and evidence\-based resources as competing tools, this approach emphasizes their complementary roles: AI can facilitate brainstorming, topic exploration, and question development while evidence\-based resources provide the authoritative, verifiable information required for clinical decision\-making and professional practice\. Strategies include using AI\-generated responses as examples for source verification exercises; comparing AI\-generated information with evidence retrieved from evidence\-based practice resources; incorporating AI into question development and search strategy design;
