# Allen, Colin: Real Traits, Real Functions?

**Autores:**

## Descrição do feed

In Andre Ariew, Robert Cummins &amp; Mark Perlman, Functions: New Essays in the Philosophy of Psychology and Biology\. New York: Oxford University Press\. pp\. 373\. 2002Discussions of the functions of biological traits generally take the notion of a trait for granted\. Defining this notion is a non\-trivial problem\. Different approaches to function place different constraints on adequate accounts of the notion of a trait\. Accounts of function based on engineering\-style analyses allow trait boundaries to be a matter of human interest\. Accounts of function based on natural selection have typically been taken to require trait boundaries that are objectively real\. After canvassing problems raised by each approach, I conclude with some facts that satisfactory notions of trait must respect\. \(direct link \)
