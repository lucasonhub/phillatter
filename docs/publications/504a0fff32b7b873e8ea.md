# A Conversational AI Framework for Teacher Training

**Autores:** Sergio Miranda; Antonio Calvani

**Data:** 2026-10-15 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

This paper introduces ETQ\-AI \(Effective Teaching Questionnaire—Artificial Intelligence\), a conversational AI framework designed to support teacher professional development by identifying pedagogical misconceptions and fostering critical reflection\. Building on Evidence\-Informed Education \(EIE\) principles, the system integrates a 50\-item diagnostic questionnaire with an automated, Gemini\-driven Explainable AI \(XAI\) feedback layer\. When teachers complete the diagnostic form, they receive personalized feedback and can engage in dialogic interactions with Google Gemini to explore the underlying meta\-analytic evidence \(e\.g\., Hattie’s effect sizes, Cognitive Load Theory\) or argue personal objections\. We evaluated system fidelity against an expert panel benchmark \(N=64 items\) and conducted a pilot evaluation with 123 educators \(70 primary teachers, 40 secondary teachers, 13 school leaders\) recruited at an EIE conference\. Gemini demonstrated high alignment with expert evaluations \(97%\), matching or exceeding static baseline feedback in user satisfaction \(Mean = 4\.1/5 vs\. 3\.8/5\)\. Qualitative thematic analysis of 34 participant objections revealed key areas of tension, particularly around contextual constraints and pedagogical subjectivity\. While the findings validate the technical proof\-of\-concept, system usability, and user engagement of ETQ\-AI as an interactive scaffolding interface, we emphasize that this pilot study measures user perceptions and conversational patterns rather than direct pre/post shifts in critical reflection or classroom instructional practice\.
