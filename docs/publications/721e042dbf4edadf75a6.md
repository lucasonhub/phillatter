# EL\-RAKHAWI GLOBAL INDEX OF CONTEMPORARY HUMAN SCIENCES A Multidisciplinary Framework for the Cognitive Age: Philosophy, Politics, Society, and Psychology

**Autores:** mohamed kamal arafa el\-rakhawi

**Data:** 2026-10-17 · **Tipo:** book

## Resumo

The El\-Rakhawi Global Index of Contemporary Human Sciences presents a revolutionary multidisciplinary framework for navigating the ontological rupture of the cognitive age\. Authored by Dr\. Mohamed Kamal Arafa El\-Rakhawi, this monumental treatise synthesizes philosophy, political science, social psychology, and technology studies into a unified intellectual architecture grounded in the principle of Cognitive Sovereignty\. Across four volumes and fifty\-two chapters, the work addresses humanity&\#x27;s most pressing challenges: artificial consciousness, democratic decline, climate crisis, identity politics, and contemporary nihilism\. The framework introduces groundbreaking metrics including the Topological\-Phenomenal Consciousness Metric, the Socio\-Biological Field Theory, and the Master Equation of Cognitive Reality, providing rigorous analytical tools for understanding algorithmic governance, platform capitalism, and geopolitical transformation\. From bioethics and digital existentialism to multipolarity and decolonial histories, this treatise establishes a new epistemic foundation for the twenty\-first century, demonstrating that the fragmentation of knowledge across disciplines reflects not reality&\#x27;s complexity but our limited perspective\. By integrating mathematical physics, philosophy, and social theory, the work affirms that human dignity, democratic governance, and collective meaning\-making depend on protecting cognitive sovereignty against the entropic forces of the digital age\. This is not merely an academic contribution but a civilizational imperative for building societies that honor the irreducible complexity of human consciousness while enabling effective global cooperation on existential challenges\.
