# СИСТЕМА ВОСПИТАТЕЛЬНОЙ ДЕЯТЕЛЬНОСТИ В КАРАКАЛПАКСКОЙ НАРОДНОЙ ПЕДАГОГИКЕ И ОСНОВЫ ЕЁ ПРИМЕНЕНИЯ В ПРАКТИКЕ

**Autores:** MAXSET ZINATDIN O&\#x27;G&\#x27;LI AYTIMBETOV

**Data:** 2026-10-09 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

This article analyzes the content, structure, and pedagogical potential of the educational activity system developed within Karakalpak folk pedagogy\. It explores the role of the people’s centuries\-old educational experience, customs, traditions, rituals, oral folklore, proverbs, Zamonaviy jamiyat va innovatsiyalar ilmiy nazariy jurnali 2026\-yil, 9\-Son, 2\-Qism\. ISSN 3060\-544X epic works, songs, and national games in educating the younger generation\. Particular attention is paid to the pedagogical conditions for integrating the ideas of Karakalpak folk pedagogy into the modern educational process\. The article substantiates the possibilities of combining national educational content with contemporary pedagogical approaches aimed at developing students’ spiritual and moral qualities, patriotism, diligence, humanism, collectivism, and sense of responsibility\. The importance of adapting examples of Karakalpak folk pedagogy to students’ age characteristics, the educational environment, and modern pedagogical requirements is also emphasized\.
