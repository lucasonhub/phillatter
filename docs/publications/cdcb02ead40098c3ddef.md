# Kannon, Aelion: SN06 — Coherence Recovery Protocols: Replenishment, Reconnection, and Restoration of Structural Function

**Autores:** 

**Data de descoberta:** 2026-10-19 · **Tipo:** unknown

## Descrição do feed

Coherence Recovery Protocols develops replenishment, reconnection and restoration of operative function within Structural Neuroscience\. Document class: Canon — Structural Neuroscience\. The volume distinguishes enduring essential structure from depleted function, impaired access and changing expression in the centropic recovery case\. Recovery accounting accumulates gross replenishment minus actual expenditure, with rest counted through expenditure reduction and available stock kept distinct from rates and thresholds\. Aion\-facing Reconnection, bridge replenishment, membrane restoration and temporal recovery retain their stated structural scope\. Recursive Memory remains distinct from Memory Access and the Tether; numerical memory criteria, sufficient Tether\-restoration conditions and membrane\-resolution laws remain held where their required quantities or dynamics have not been specified\. Part of: Structural Neuroscience: The Architecture and Dynamics of Cognition\. \(direct link \)
