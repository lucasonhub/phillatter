# Temporal trends in semen quality at a single reproductive center in Shanghai, China, during 2021–2025 amid the COVID\-19 pandemic

**Autores:** Shiyi Zhu; Jin Dong; 吴正沐; Zhengquan Wang; Jun Yang; Chao Feng; Chunhua Tang; Yuchuan Zhou

**Data:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Background Accumulating epidemiological and andrological evidence has raised concerns about potential long\-term declines in semen quality worldwide, highlighting the need for sustained, population\-specific surveillance\. In this study, we retrospectively analyzed temporal changes in semen quality during the period of 2021–2025 amid the COVID\-19 pandemic\. This work extends our previous report on annual trends in semen quality at the same center from 2015 to 2021\. Methods This retrospective study evaluated temporal changes in semen quality parameters among 21,932 men who attended a reproductive center in Shanghai between 2021 and 2025, a period characterized by widespread COVID\-19 pandemic\. After 2–7 days of sexual abstinence, semen samples were analyzed using computer\-assisted sperm analysis to assess conventional semen parameters, including semen volume, sperm count, sperm concentration, and sperm motility, as well as sperm kinematic parameters, including curvilinear velocity, straight\-line velocity, average path velocity, and beat\-cross frequency\. To better characterize temporal changes across different baseline semen\-quality strata, participants were classified according to the WHO 2010 reference criteria into above\-reference\-limit and below\-reference\-limit groups\. Differences among groups were assessed using one\-way analysis of variance, the Kruskal–Wallis test, independent\-samples t\-test, and analysis of covariance, as appropriate\. In the above\-reference\-limit group, multiple linear regression models including year, age, and abstinence duration were used to adjust for potential confounders and evaluate temporal trends in semen quality\. Results In the overall cohort and in both the above\-reference\-limit and below\-reference\-limit subgroups, sperm count, sperm concentration, and several CASA\-derived kinematic parameters, including curvilinear velocity, straight\-line velocity, average path velocity, and beat\-cross frequency, generally showed increasing temporal trends from 2021 to 2025\. After adjustment for age and abstinence duration, multiple linear regression analyses in the above\-reference\-limit group supported these associations\. Semen volume, sperm count, sperm concentration, curvilinear velocity, straight\-line velocity, average path velocity, and beat\-cross frequency increased significantly over the study period, with regression coefficients of 0\.0876, 4\.815, 0\.6929, 0\.9423, 0\.3553, 0\.5150, and 0\.1172, respectively\. In contrast, sperm motility decreased significantly over time, with a regression coefficient of −0\.7112\. Conclusion The observed increases in semen volume, sperm count, and sperm concentration contrast with the declining trends previously reported at the same reproductive center before the COVID\-19 pandemic\. These findings suggest widespread COVID\-19 pandemic have reversed the annual changes in parameters related to semen quality\.
