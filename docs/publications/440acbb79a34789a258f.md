# Regulatory Strategy for Market Authorization of Generic Antibiotics: Integrating Pharmaceutical Quality, Bioequivalence, Biowaivers and Lifecycle Management

**Autores:** Hasanath M V, Junise V\*, Kadeeja A V

**Data:** 2026-10-14 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

The gut microbiome plays an important role in determining individual variations in drug disposition and response\. Changes in drug disposition that are not adequately addressed by conventional drug delivery techniques may be induced by alterations in microbial composition and function\. Thus, pharmacomicrobiomics has emerged as a vital field for understanding drug–microbiome interactions and developing more personalised therapies\. In this review, the effect of inter\-individual variation in the gut microbiome on drugs and drug response is addressed, and novel microbiome\-guided drug delivery approaches are discussed\. Colon\-specific delivery, microbiome\-guided delivery approaches, novel nanocarriers, and microbiota\-responsive delivery platforms are considered advanced systems that future drug delivery formulations could target\. Moreover, the value of artificial intelligence, multi\-omics, systems pharmacology, and precision dosing in anticipating microbiome\-based drug responses is considered\. Despite these advances, the translation of microbiome science into clinical practice has yet to be widely achieved due to inter\-individual variability, lack of standardisation, limited validation of microbiome biomarkers, regulatory limitations, and formulation challenges\. The integration of microbiome science with cutting\-edge pharmaceutical technologies may ultimately support more individualised, safer, and more efficacious drug delivery approaches, thereby facilitating the increased utilisation of precision medicine\.
