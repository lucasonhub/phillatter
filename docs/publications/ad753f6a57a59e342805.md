# Q\($γ\_H$\): Entropy\-Guided Horizon Mixing in Multi\-Discount Q\-Learning

**Autores:** Arwa Albelaihi; Abdulrahman Altahhan

**Data:** 2026-10-15 · **Tipo:** preprint

## Resumo

A fixed discount factor in Q\-learning imposes a single planning horizon throughout training, despite the differing demands of early exploration and long\-term credit assignment\. We propose Q\($γ\_H$\), a multi\-horizon Q\-learning framework that maintains multiple discount\-specific heads and adaptively combines them at control time using an entropy\-derived confidence signal\. The resulting state\-dependent mixing weights shift control from shorter\- to longer\-horizon heads as policy confidence increases, enabling adaptive horizon mixing without modifying individual Bellman updates\. Across classical control and deep reinforcement\-learning benchmarks, Q\($γ\_H$\) shows its strongest improvements on delayed\-credit tasks relative to strong fixed\-discount baselines\. By decoupling horizon adaptation from the underlying learning updates, Q\($γ\_H$\) provides a lightweight framework for adaptive planning\-horizon control in reinforcement learning, avoiding the need for manual discount\-factor selection while remaining compatible with standard value\-learning methods\.
