# The AI Divide in Academic Libraries: A Comparative Bibliometric Analysis of Technological Determinism vs Information Equity \(2010–2025\)

**Autores:** Sudhanshu Shekhar Mishra

**Data:** 2026-10-09 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Background: While the exponential growth of artificial intelligence \(AI\) in academic libraries is extensively documented, existing bibliometric scholarship has largely treated the global research landscape as a monolith\. This oversight has led to a saturation of &quot;generalist&quot; reports that fail to address critical regional and thematic disparities\.  Methods: This study analyzes 512 Scopus\-indexed publications \(2010–2025\) using a dual\-methodological approach: quantitative science mapping via VOSviewer \(1\.6\.20\) and qualitative thematic critique\. We specifically contrast the research priorities of dominant geopolitical producers \(China and the USA\) against the emerging narrative from the Global South, with a primary focus on India\.  Results: The analysis reveals a fundamental &quot;two\-speed&quot; adoption model\. The Global North prioritizes high\-tech interventions—including robotics, advanced machine learning for metadata automation, and generative models—aimed at enhancing operational efficiency\. In contrast, the Global South, led by pivotal recent insights from India \(Mishra, 2026\), emphasizes AI as a strategic survival mechanism for bridging information literacy gaps and democratizing access in resource\- constrained environments\.
