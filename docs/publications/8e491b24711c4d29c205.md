# Construction and content validation of an OBE\-AI\-CED integrated teaching framework for the liquor factory design course in local undergraduate universities: a modified Delphi study

**Autores:** Keliang Wang; Chenglin Zhou; Yueming Leng; Jing Li; Huajie Xu; Xiangui Wang; Ju Guo

**Data:** 2026-10-13 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

The digital, intelligent and green transformation of the Chinese baijiu industry has introduced new competency demands for liquor engineering education\. The Liquor Factory Design course is a comprehensive upper\-level engineering design course for students in the liquor engineering program that combines knowledge of brewing technology, chemical engineering, engineering drawing, brewing equipment, energy\-saving and environmental protection, and production management\. However, the course in local undergraduate universities still has several problems, such as an unclear design task chain, a lack of authentic engineering contexts, ambiguous boundaries for the use of artificial intelligence, and an assessment approach that focuses too much on the final product rather than the learning process\. The above problems may limit the all\-round development of students’ engineering design abilities\. Given the above problems, this paper builds an OBE\-AI\-CED integrated teaching system for the Liquor Factory Design course by integrating Outcome\-Based Education, Artificial Intelligence and Complex Engineering Design\. A modified Delphi method was employed to examine the content validity and refine the framework\. Two rounds of expert consultations were conducted with university teachers, engineering technicians from Baijiu Enterprise, and experts from an engineering design institution\. The first round of results showed that, although most experts agreed that a framework was needed, they raised concerns about the limits of AI application, the viability of sustained cooperation between enterprises and mentors, a lack of genuine engineering resources, and an unfinished chain of evaluation evidence\. Based on expert feedback, AI was repositioned as a tool for learning support and design reasoning rather than automatic design generation\. A complex engineering design task chain was established, including production scale determination, process flow design, material balance, equipment selection, workshop layout, energy conservation and environmental protection, drawing expression, and design defense\. In addition, industrial case libraries, equipment parameter databases, engineering drawing resources, and an evidence\-based assessment mechanism were incorporated into the framework\. The second\-round results showed increased mean scores, reduced coefficients of variation, and greater concordance among participating experts\. These results reflect expert consensus on the revised framework, while student learning effectiveness was not evaluated in this study\. The proposed framework may support the transformation of the Liquor Factory Design course from a report\-oriented model to a competence\-oriented model and provide a reference for cultivating complex engineering design competence in liquor engineering programs in local undergraduate universities in China\.
