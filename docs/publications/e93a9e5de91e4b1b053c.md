# Brain\-Computer Interface for Disability Assistance: A Study on Factors Influencing Disabilities&\#x27; Purchase Intention and Price Acceptance for Mind\-Writing Pens

**Autores:** Mengya Li; Hu Bing

**Data:** 2026-10-14 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

With the practical application of brain\-computer interface \(BCI\) technology, mind\-writing pens have progressed from the conceptual stage into an iterative product development phase\. Currently, leveraging the trends of population aging and advancements in medical rehabilitation, these products are expected to enter a golden period of growth within the next eight to ten years\. Existing research primarily focuses on BCI risk management and lacks in\-depth exploration of market demand and the factors influencing medical rehabilitation\-related products\. This gap provides a critical research focus for this study\. Using individuals with disabilities as subjects, this study employs a sequential Probit model and KHB mediation analysis to examine factors affecting purchase intention and price acceptance for mind\-writing pens\. Results indicate that age has no significant impact on purchase intention or price acceptance, whereas income shows a significant positive effect\. Battery life and head\-mounted design significantly enhance purchase intention, while weight and recognition accuracy positively influence price acceptance\. Cross\-platform compatibility mediates the effects of income\. Heterogeneity analysis reveals that demand among those under fifty is primarily driven by income, whereas users aged fifty\-one and older prioritize battery life and recognition accuracy; low\- to middle\-income groups emphasize basic usability, whereas high\-income groups value functional enhancement\. Price acceptance decreases progressively as prices rise, peaking below one thousand yuan\. This study provides empirical evidence for the development, pricing, and market promotion of BCI\-based assistive products for individuals with disabilities\.
