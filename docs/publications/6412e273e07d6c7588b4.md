# A wearable ultrasonic system monitoring skeletal muscle activities

**Autores:** Shuo Wang

**Data:** 2026-10-12 · **Tipo:** dissertation

## Resumo

The skeletal\-muscular system plays a vital role in human physiology, facilitating movement and locomotion essential for interaction with the environment\. Ultrasound can be a proper way to help monitor the status of skeletal\-muscular system\. Compared to traditional bulky and complicated ultrasound monitoring system, wearable ultrasound sensor with customized control and signal reception circuit with tailored algorithm is capable of implementing the same monitoring function easily and quickly\. For the development of ultrasound sensor, a completely rigid ultrasound sensor is not suitable for use during wearing\. Flexible ultrasound sensors usually involve complex manufacturing processes and require tedious steps to achieve the desired performance\. Moreover, they are typically unfavorable for wearing due to the non\-breathable substrates and materials used\. Additionally, for B\-mode flexible sensor arrays, deformation compensation is also an urgent issue that needs to be addressed\. For the control and signal reception circuit, typical commercial devices are quite bulky or complicated\. Some proposed hardware usually lacks customization and scalability in frequency, number of channels, or algorithm logic for specific applications\. For the algorithm, algorithms are developed according to different monitoring objectives\. In the field of gesture classification using ultrasound signals, few studies focus on recognizing specific gestures based on a single transducer unit\. In existing research, the proposed machine learning methods are often complicated, with limited recognition accuracy\. Furthermore, the possibility of deploying the algorithms on edge devices is not considered, which limits their scope of application\. Therefore, in this study, we propose to develop a fabric\-based wearable ultrasound sensor with customized circuit and algorithms to monitor skeletal\-muscular status\. We investigate to develop ultrasound sensors with good performance on space cotton substrates using a minimalist manufacturing process \(mainly based on hot pressing\), which can fully leverage the advantages of fabric in terms of comfort and wearability\. And we explore the possibility of using strain sensors with deep learning method for shape calibration for B\-mode sensor array in the future with an error less than 2\.5 mm\. Then, a customized hardware for ultrasound signal control and reception has been developed based on Zynq, which can help customize the control logic, the excitation frequencies and deploy or accelerate algorithms because of the ARM and FPGA cores\. The design demonstrates the potential for further multi\-channel expansion in the future\. Finally, experiments have been carried out for monitoring extensor digitorum \(ED\) muscle&\#x27;s morphology changes with angles and strength variations\. The results show a strong positive correlation between the thickness change of the ED muscle and the applied force under different angles\. On the other hand, a lightweight framework for gestures classification based on the proposed system with a single transduce has been proposed, which combines virtual channels, multi\-scale, and transformer modules to achieve an average accuracy rate of over 98\.5%\. The distilled model can achieve over 95% accuracy while significantly reducing parameters and computational complexity, which can help with the future deployment of the model onto the proposed hardware\.
