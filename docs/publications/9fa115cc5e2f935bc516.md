\_Constructivist Foundations\_ 18 \(3\):411\-415\. 2023I explain the world of screen creatures by virtue of our familiar environment and the notion of the experiential world\. My explanation is meant to strengthen the argument for the view that the constructivist world model does not need the perceptual relation\.
