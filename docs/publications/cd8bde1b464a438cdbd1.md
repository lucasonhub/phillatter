# Generative AI — Applications, Limitations, and Ethical Challenges

**Autores:** Kamna choudhary Choudhary; Yash Choudhary

**Data:** 2026-10-14 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Generative Artificial Intelligence \(AI\) is changing how people learn, work, and create\. It can write text, generate images, assist with programming, and help users turn ideas into useful content\. These capabilities offer opportunities in education, healthcare, software development, business, and creative industries\. However, the technology also raises important questions about accuracy, privacy, fairness, ownership, and accountability\. An AI\-generated response may sound convincing while containing incorrect or misleading information\. This paper reviews the main applications of generative AI, explains its limitations, and examines the ethical challenges associated with its use\. Drawing on research studies and guidance from UNESCO, the World Health Organization \(WHO\), and the National Institute of Standards and Technology \(NIST\), it argues that successful adoption requires more than powerful models\. It also requires careful evaluation, clear rules, and responsible human oversight\. This paper is based on a literature review and does not report original experimental results\.
