# Research on the application of a CNN–transformer model fused with convolutional attention mechanisms in music\-genre recognition

**Autores:** Yuru Liu

**Data:** 2026-10-12 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Music\-genre recognition concerns timbre, tonality, and musical emotion; accurate identification of genre can markedly enhance creative performance in music production\. Music\-genre recognition is one of the core tasks in Music Information Retrieval \(MIR\)\. Aiming at the deficiencies of existing methods, such as the confusion of audio and MIDI genre classification, inadequate modeling of spatiotemporal features, and poor reproducibility, this paper proposes CCTAF\-MSR, a compact CNN\-Transformer music\-genre recognition model integrated with convolutional attention\. Taking log\-mel spectrograms as input, it constructs a lightweight hybrid architecture combining CNN for local feature extraction, CBAM for attention enhancement, and Transformer for global temporal modeling\. Training and testing are conducted on the standard GTZAN and ASAP audio datasets\. Experimental results show that CCTAF\-MSR achieves the highest precision \(86\.40%\) and F1\-score \(87\.67%\) among comparable models, while its recall reaches 88\.99%\. All metrics lie within a 3% margin of the best\-reported values in the literature\. The model can accurately capture rhythmic, timbral and melodic features\. Meanwhile, this paper provides complete and reproducible experimental settings, including dataset partitioning, spectrogram preprocessing, optimizer configuration and early stopping strategy, which offers a robust solution for practical music \-genre classification\.
