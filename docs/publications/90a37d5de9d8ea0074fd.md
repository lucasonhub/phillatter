# Development and evaluation of eduvate CBT as an integrated artificial intelligence powered assessment ecosystem

**Autores:** Peace Awofesobi; Obiseye Obiyemi

**Data:** 2026-10-17 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

The growth of digital assessment has intensified concerns about examination integrity, grading efficiency, fairness, and responsible artificial intelligence \(AI\)\. This study evaluates Eduvate CBT, an integrated role\-based assessment ecosystem combining examination administration, AI\-assisted proctoring, automated objective grading, reviewable violation evidence, and learning analytics\. A four\-week quantitative evaluation covered 250 undergraduate examination sessions across four computing courses, with usability data from 15 instructors and 5 administrators\. At the session level, proctoring achieved 94\.8% accuracy \(95% CI 91\.3–96\.9\), 94\.6% precision, 96\.6% recall, and an F1\-score of 95\.6%\. Automated grading agreed with instructor consensus for 742 of 750 sampled responses \(98\.9%; 95% CI 97\.9–99\.5\) and processed the cohort in under 10 s, compared with an estimated 23\.1 h of manual grading\. The aggregate System Usability Scale score was 87\.4 \(SD = 5\.8; 95% CI 84\.7–90\.1\)\. Analytics identified six low\-performing items in algorithmic complexity, demonstrating how assessment evidence can prompt instructional review\. The findings support the feasibility of the integrated workflow, although generalisability is limited by the single\-institution design, absence of a competing\-system control, and unavailable formal inter\-rater reliability estimates\.
