# Decoding Drought: The CBL9–CIPK11 Phosphoswitch in Guard Cells

**Autores:** Md\. Mahadi Hasan; Nadiyah M\. Alabdallah

**Data:** 2026-10-14 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Summary statement The study identifies a phosphorylation‐dependent calcium signalling module, PuCBL9–PuCIPK11, that remodels the guard‐cell actin cytoskeleton in Populus ussuriensis to accelerate stomatal closure\. This work bridges a long‐standing gap between calcium decoding and cytoskeletal dynamics in trees, and opens a new translational avenue for forest improvement\.
