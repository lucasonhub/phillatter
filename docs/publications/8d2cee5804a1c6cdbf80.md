# Juvenile Huntington Disease With Cerebellar‐Predominant Atrophy Identified by Genome Sequencing

**Autores:** Enrique Gonzalez Saez‐Diez; Amy Tam; Siofra Carty; Monica Ferrer; Kathryn Yang; Darius Ebrahimi‐Fakhari

**Data:** 2026-10-12 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Juvenile Huntington disease accounts for a small fraction of Huntington disease cases and is driven by the largest trinucleotide repeat expansions\. We describe a 6\-year\-old boy with progressive global regression, generalized dystonia, and epilepsy, in whom serial imaging showed striking cerebellar\-predominant atrophy with an initially normal\-appearing striatum\. Extensive metabolic testing, exome sequencing, and an ataxia repeat expansion panel were nondiagnostic\. Research genome sequencing identified a large pathogenic huntingtin CAG repeat expansion that confirmatory testing sized beyond 180 repeats\. This case illustrates a cerebellar\-predominant imaging signature of extreme repeat expansions and the limitations of exome sequencing for detecting them\.
