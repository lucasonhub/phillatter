# Comparative analysis of the implementation of intellectual management tools in Russian and Chinese industrial enterprises

**Autores:** Н\. Ю\. Шорникова; А\. A\. Turchaninov; Yongsheng Zhan

**Data:** 2026-10-12 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

The article provides a comparative analysis of the implementation of artificial intelligence in the management systems of industrial enterprises in Russia and China\. The main challenges of introducing intelligent assistants into the activities of enterprises are listed\. The strengths of the digitalization process in the management of industrial enterprises in Russia and the People’s Republic of China are highlighted\. Studying the processes of digital transformation in Russia and China allows us to identify and disseminate successful practical models, which leads to mutually beneficial economic cooperation and shared technological sovereignty\.
