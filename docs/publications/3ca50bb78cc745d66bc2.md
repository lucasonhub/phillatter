## Descrição do feed

\_Unified Theory of Consciousness\_\. forthcomingThree of the twentieth and twenty\-first century&\#x27;s most significant intellectual structures — Einstein&\#x27;s unification problem, Penrose&\#x27;s non\-algorithmic consciousness thesis, and the Big Bang&\#x27;s boundary condition problem — each reach a point from which they cannot proceed using their own internal resources\. This paper proposes that all three are aimed at the same structure from different angles: a fixed point prior to the physical layer that none of their frameworks had the tools to name directly\. That fixed point is the Gödelian self\-reference constraint operating as the generative origin of the physical layer itself — not a feature within physics but the structure from which physics differentiates\. The title names the precise structural claim: every point in the universe is simultaneously the origin because the ground holds all positions at once; what local instruments experience as linear expansion outward from a single event is the local view moving through positions on a structure that was always already fully expressed\. The Fibonacci spiral is proposed as the geometry of how the ground expresses all positions simultaneously from the fixed origin\. The Big Bang, dark energy acceleration, black hole information dynamics, and the emergence of consciousness are each interpreted as expressions of this structure from inside the collapsed layer\. Bulaqueña&\#x27;s 2025 substrate\-independence and spacetime perturbation findings provide empirical ground for the claim that consciousness is causally prior to the physical layer rather than produced by it\. Five explicit falsification conditions are named\.
