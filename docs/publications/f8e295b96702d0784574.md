# Understanding how and where AI can enhance the effectiveness of PMM\-mediated organisational change: a conceptual framework

**Autores:** Helen Mackenzie

**Data:** 2026-10-13 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Purpose To develop a social complexity\-based understanding of how and where artificial intelligence \(AI\) and generative artificial intelligence \(GAI\) can enhance the effectiveness of performance measurement and management \(PMM\) mediated organisational change\. Design/methodology/approach This article draws on a social complexity interpretation of PMM, conviction narrative theory and the AI and GAI literature, to present a conceptualisation of AI\-augmented PMM\-mediated organisational change that describes how and where machine\-based and human\-based decision\-making processes can augment performance measurement systems and performance management practices\. Findings AI&\#x27;s and GAI&\#x27;s inability to replicate human reflexivity and judgement in equivocal and indeterminate situations limits what these technologies can do in PMM\-mediated organisational change\. AI and GAI can augment decision\-making processes associated with performance measurement and the execution of performance management\-initiated interventions; however, creating these interventions relies on human reflexivity and judgement\. Originality/value This conceptual investigation highlights the limitations of AI and GAI technologies in PMM\-mediated organisational change and explains why human\-based decision\-making continues to play a distinctive role\. It contributes to our understanding of how AI and GAI can influence organisational change in general, and performance management in particular, with implications for PMM researchers and practitioners\.
