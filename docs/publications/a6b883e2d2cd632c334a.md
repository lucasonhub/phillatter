# Optimality of Quasi\-Monte Carlo Methods and Suboptimality of the Sparse\-Grid Gauss–Hermite Rule in Gaussian Sobolev Spaces

**Autores:** Yoshihito Kazashi; Yuya Suzuki; Takashi Goda

**Data:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Abstract\. Optimality of several quasi\-Monte Carlo methods and suboptimality of the sparse\-grid quadrature based on the univariate Gauss–Hermite rule are proved in the Sobolev spaces of mixed dominating smoothness of order \[Formula: see text\], where the optimality is in the sense of worst\-case convergence rate\. For sparse\-grid Gauss–Hermite quadrature, lower and upper bounds are established, with rates coinciding up to a logarithmic factor\. This common algebraic rate is found to be only \[Formula: see text\] with \[Formula: see text\] function evaluations, although the optimal rate is known to be \[Formula: see text\]\. The lower bound is obtained by exploiting the structure of the Gauss–Hermite nodes and is independent of the quadrature weights; consequently, no modification of the weights can improve the rate \[Formula: see text\]\. In contrast, several quasi\-Monte Carlo methods with a change of variables are shown to achieve the optimal rate, some up to, and one including, the logarithmic factor\.
