## Resumo

Neuro\-behavioral model for risk perception improvement in chemical, petrochemical and oil &amp; gas industries\. Based on 629 industrial assets tracked with 99\.34% HRN \(Health &amp; Risk Normalization\) compliance\. 80% of near\-miss incidents analyzed are related to FISPQ/MSDS misinterpretation, GHS classification failure and Flash Point \(11°C to 52°C\) misunderstanding\. Model integrates: Stimulus → Sensation → Perception → Behavior, 5 senses as perceptual PPE, Prochaska behavioral stages, 4 perception domains \(Physical, Biological, Chemical, Emotional\)\. Real cases from chemical plants, binational applicability Brazil NR\-20 / USA OSHA PSM 1910\.119 &amp; HazCom 1910\.1200\.
