# Sequent\-Prover: Training Agents for Formal, Checkable SMT\-based Reasoning

**Autores:** Debargha Ganguly; Weicong Chen; Mohsen Hariri; Vikash Singh; Sreehari Sankar; Shouren Wang; Wang Yang; Biyao Zhang; Chaoda Song; Alexander Nemecek; Kai Ye; Osama Zafar; Erman Ayday; Joe Stubbs; Srinivasan Iyengar; Vipin Chaudhary

**Data:** 2026-10-11 · **Tipo:** preprint

## Resumo

We introduce Sequent\-Prover, a family of verifier\-using language\-model agents, and the post\-training framework that produces them\. Agents reason in an executable SMT\-LIB scratchpad: they write complete SMT\-LIB programs, query Z3 across turns, inspect solver feedback, repair formalizations, and terminate with an explicit answer\. Our recipe, verifier\-conditioned trajectory distillation, bootstraps from a noisy teacher proposal policy by sampling multi\-turn trajectories, retaining the successful tail that is correct, non\-trivial, grounded, and executable, then applying supervised fine\-tuning and trajectory\-level preference optimization\. Because solver success alone does not guarantee semantic faithfulness, we evaluate task accuracy, k\-sample reliability, and SMT program quality\. Across 37 benchmarks and 7,254 problems per model family, post\-training raises the 4B model&\#x27;s per\-attempt accuracy from 85\.6% to 97\.0% and four\-sample all\-correct reliability \(pass^4\}\) from 64\.1% to 91\.7%\. It also reduces average SMT calls from 4\.10 to 2\.52 and tokens per attempt from 18\.97k to 12\.72k\. Sequent\-Prover agents give answers backed by executable formal artifacts\.
