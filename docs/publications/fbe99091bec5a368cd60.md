# Stilwell, Phil: Cø as N\*: A Minimal Network\-Dynamics Model of Phenomenal Consciousness

**Autores:** 

**Data de descoberta:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** unknown

## Descrição do feed

This paper develops an expanded version of the N\* model of phenomenal consciousness\. The target is minimal phenomenal presence, denoted Cø: the condition in which there is something it is like for a system to be in a state, without requiring self\-reflection, overt report, introspective judgment, or metacognitive access\. The central hypothesis is a deliberately risky biconditional: under an independently assessed viability condition V, Cø obtains if and only if a system instantiates a minimal network\-dynamics bundle, formally represented as N\* = N1 ∧ N2 ∧ N3\. The three conjuncts are integration/synergy \(N1\), broadcast availability \(N2\), and recurrent stability \(N3\)\. The paper supplies the formal notation, plain\-language annotations, a conceptual transition guide for readers arriving from rival frameworks, measurement policy, surrogate\-conflict rules, edge\-case predictions, a landscape map across major consciousness theories, and objection\-handling needed to make the model accessible without weakening its empirical commitments\. The proposal is substrate\-independent only in a constrained sense: a non\-biological or alternative biological substrate qualifies only if it realizes the relevant causal dynamics, at the relevant temporal grain, within pre\-registered tolerance bands\. The model is not presented as a completed solution to the hard problem or as a full theory of phenomenal quality\. It is proposed as a candidate theory of phenomenal presence and as a candidate C\-test framework whose value lies in being clear enough to test, constrained enough to fail, and modular enough to be revised by adversarial evidence\. \(direct link \)
