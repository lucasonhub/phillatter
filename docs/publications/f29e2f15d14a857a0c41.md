# Grimm, Petra ; Trost, Kai Erik &amp; Zöllner, Oliver \(eds\.\): Mensch – Maschine revisited\. Roboter und KI\-Systeme im öffentlichen Raum

**Autores:**

## Descrição do feed

Artificial Intelligence and robotics have long been part of everyday life\. But what does their rise mean for human self\-understanding, social practices, and ethical orientations? This edited volume revisits this question against the backdrop of current AI developments and robotic applications in public spaces\. From an interdisciplinary perspective, the contributions bring together philosophical, ethical, legal, empirical, and design\-based approaches to the human\-machine relationship\. At the core are fundamental questions: How are AI systems and robots transforming everyday actions, perceptions, and expectations? What hopes, fears, and value conflicts shape societal acceptance? And how can human\-robot interactions be designed in a legally sound, ethically reflective, and human\-centered way? The authors analyze narratives between utopia and dystopia, discuss concepts such as co\-intelligence, social robotics, and non\-anthropocentric ethics, and develop practical guidelines for design, regulation, and responsibility\. \(direct link \)
