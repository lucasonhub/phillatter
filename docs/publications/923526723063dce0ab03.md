# Naude, Bianca: States as distributed persons: A mycorrhizal approach to political collectivity \(Public lecture, University of Johannesburg, September 2025\)

**Autores:** 

**Data de descoberta:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** unknown

## Descrição do feed

International Relations has long deployed state personhood as interpretive shorthand to explain phenomena as broad as collective emotions, collective psychology, collective consciousness, and even collective defence mechanisms\. Yet, with a few notable exceptions, scholars have largely bracketed the question of what kind of entity the state really is, and how it can experience phenomena ordinarily reserved for human persons\. Drawing on Alfred Gell&\#x27;s and Roy Wagner&\#x27;s anthropology, I introduce the framework of distributed personhood, recasting the state not as a bounded organism but as a living, &quot;breathing&quot; flow of institutions, practices, memories, and the emotions and impulses that move through them\. Collective political life, I argue, is located in a corporeal connection that runs between bodies, interwoven with the symbolic rather than beneath it; as a “mycorrhizal ecosystem” in which every sensation reverberates through an invisible and uninterrupted mesh\.
