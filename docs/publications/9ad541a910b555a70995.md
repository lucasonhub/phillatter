# AI\-DRIVEN PERSONALIZATION AND AUTOMATED ASSESSMENT IN LEARNING MANAGEMENT SYSTEMS

**Autores:** Xujanov Elbek

**Data:** 2026-10-13 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Learning Management Systems \(LMSs\) are widely used to organize learning materials, communication, assessment, and progress tracking\. The integration of artificial intelligence \(AI\) creates opportunities to make these systems more responsive to learners’ needs\. This thesis examines two related applications: personalized learning support and automated assessment\. It outlines how AI can use learning activity data to recommend suitable resources, identify topics that may require additional practice, and provide timely feedback\. The paper also discusses limitations associated with data privacy, algorithmic bias, transparency, and the need for teacher oversight\. The study is conceptual and based on a review of relevant literature; it does not report original experimental measurements\. It concludes that AI can support teaching and learning when it is implemented as an assistive tool within a transparent, privacy\-aware, and educator\-supervised LMS\.
