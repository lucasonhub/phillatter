# Zhao, Xiangbin: The Group Form of Law: The Determination Form of Law

**Autores:** 

**Data de descoberta:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** unknown

## Descrição do feed

How can observable data determine whether a candidate internal organization qualifies as a formal solution for a law rather than merely correlating with an output? This paper converts previously established formal criteria into an executable adjudication protocol\. Its current domain is explicitly restricted to frozen, single\-office, single\-entry, measure\-type candidates\. Structural observations, exhaustively evaluated path data, and observations under permitted interventions are used to test current bearing, realization, non\-instantaneous formation, dynamical contribution, stable\-recurrence support, and coupling\. The procedure returns PASS, FAIL, or indeterminate and is executed on one positive case together with negative controls including a single strong impulse, pure correlation, an abandoned old groove, and a sub\-threshold weak trace\.
