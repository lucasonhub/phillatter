# The dynamics of psychomotor development in children from the Rostov region during the first year of life with borderline TREC and KREC marker values based on neonatal screening results

**Autores:** Gyuzyal Sh\. Gafiyatullina; Galina B\. Evdokimova; Пузикова Олеся Зиновьевна; Elena Kizhevatova; Karina S\. Karakhanyan; V\. P\. Evdokimov

**Data:** 2026-10-15 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

The aim of the study: To study the timing of psychomotor development indicators in the first year of life among clinically healthy children from the Rostov region with borderline\-low TREC and KREC values based on neonatal advanced screening\. Research methodology: A retrospective assessment of outpatient records of 294 children who underwent advanced neonatal screening at the Medical and Genetic Center of Rostov State Medical University was carried out\. In all children TREC and KREC values exceeded the thresholds for the diagnosis of primary immunodeficiency, while 55 people had both of these indicators below the reference values \(group 1\), 76 children had only a decrease in TREC levels \(group 2\), 73 subjects had only a decrease in KREC levels \(group 3\), and in 90 children, both of these markers were within the reference values \(control group\)\. The timing of psychomotor development assessment was carried out at 1, 3, 6, 9, and 12 months\. Statistical analysis included calculation of Odds Ratio \(OR\) and 95 % Confidence Intervals \(CI\)\. The results of the research: Specific characteristics of psychomotor development rates depending on the initial TREC and KREC indicators have been detected\. Children in Group 1 showed statistically significant advanced motor functions \(gaze fixation at 1 mo: OR = 0\.045; 95 % CI: 0\.003–0\.798; independent walking at 12 month: OR = 0\.22; 95 % CI: 0\.05–0\.97\) and delayed formation of neuropsychic functions \(a delay in the formation of a revival complex at 1 month and active speech at 12 mo: OR = 3\.78; 95 % CI: 1\.22–11\.7\) compared to the control group \(p 0\.05\)\. In groups 2 and 3 the indicators occupied an intermediate position \(p 0\.05\)\. Conclusions: Borderline decreased values of TREC and KREC at birth are associated with the peculiarities of psychomotor development of children in the first year of life\. Consequently these markers of functional maturity of the lymphocytic link can serve as predictors of individual developmental trajectories\.
