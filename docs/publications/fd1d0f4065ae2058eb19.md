# STEM teacher leadership profiles: Connecting innovative teaching and leadership role enactment

**Autores:** Seoyeon Park; Peter D\. Wiens

**Data:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

This study identified teacher leadership profiles among 229 mathematics and science teachers and examined how these profiles relate to innovative teaching and role enactment\. Using k \-means clustering, four distinct profiles emerged: High\-Engaged Leaders \( n = 25\) showed consistently high involvement, particularly in equity and shared leadership\. Moderate\-High Leaders \( n = 78\) displayed moderately high engagement across all five teacher leadership dimensions\. Developing Leaders \( n = 40\) showed relatively higher engagement in professional growth and collaboration but lower engagement in professional development leadership\. Low\-Engaged Leaders \( n = 86\), the largest cluster, reported the lowest engagement across all dimensions\. Analysis of covariance \(ANCOVA\) revealed significant cluster effects for innovative teaching, F \(3, 216\) = 9\.65, P &lt; \.001, η 2 = \.12\. Negative binomial regression showed significant differences in role enactment, χ 2 \(3\) = 23\.53, P &lt; \.001, with High\-Engaged Leaders emphasizing mentoring and administrative roles\. Findings reveal the layered nature of science, technology, engineering, and mathematics \(STEM\) teacher leadership and suggest the need for targeted support\.
