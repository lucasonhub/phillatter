# Social work Artificial Intelligence \(AI\) Counselling Services And Family Poor Health Reduction in Southern Senatorial Abstract District, Cross River State

**Autores:** Mary  Ala Nandi

**Data:** 2026-10-12 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

The main purpose of this study was to examine the relationship between social work AI counselling interventions services and family health outcome\. To achieve this purpose of the study, related literatures were reviewed in line with the sub variable\. Under discussion, four null hypotheses were formulated to guide the study\. A survey research design was the appropriate design for the study with a population of 420,000 household across the seven Local Government Areas in the Southern Senatorial District\. The purposive sampling technique was employed for the study with a sample size of 400 household drawn from the population across seven Local Government Areas in the Southern Senatorial District\. Pearson moment correlation coefficient analysis was used for the analysis of hypothesis at 0\.05 significance level\. Findings from the study revealed thus: that Al counselling services significantly predict reduction in poor health\. Families who accessed Al counselling intervention reported improved emotional resilience, adherence to preventive healthcare practices and enhanced intra\-family communication\. Challenges identified include limited availability of trained counselors, cultural barriers and infrastructural shortage in rural communities\. Five categories of family Al counselling services were also highlighted\. Recommendation includes capacity building for social workers, extension of community\-based Al counselling, public awareness campaigns and social development agencies\. The findings provide evidence for policy\-makers, social workers and community stakeholders\. Seeking to reduce poor health outcomes and promote family well\-being in low\-resource setting\. Keywords: social work Artificial Intelligence \(AI\) counselling, poor health reduction\.
