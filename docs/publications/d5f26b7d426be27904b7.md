# Genomics and metabolomics analyses reveal the endophytic fungus Neocucurbitaria sp\. VM\-36 as a potential source of bioactive metabolites against methicillin\-resistant Staphylococcus aureus

**Autores:** Xiao Li; Rosario del Carmen Flores\-Vallejo; Ting He; Jan Maarten van Dijl; Kristina Haslinger

**Data:** 2026-10-15 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Introduction Endophytic fungi associated with medicinal plants represent valuable sources of specialized metabolites with potential biological activities\. Methods Here, we investigated Neocucurbitaria sp\. VM\-36, an understudied endophyte isolated from the medicinal plant Vinca minor , using an integrated genomic, metabolomic, and antibacterial approach\. Results Whole genome analysis suggests substantial biosynthetic potential for a range of secondary metabolites based on the presence of various biosynthetic gene clusters\. Metabolomic profiling revealed a broad chemical repertoire, including metabolites putatively assigned as decalin\-containing tetramic acids \(DTAs\)\. Among the compounds examined experimentally, compound 1 was structurally identified and exhibited potent antibacterial activity against Gram\-positive pathogens, particularly methicillin\-resistant Staphylococcus aureus \(MRSA\), with MIC and MBC values of 0\.5–1 and 2–4 μg/mL, respectively\. Compound 1 displayed rapid bactericidal activity and showed an additive effect in combination with daptomycin against a clinical MRSA isolate \(FICI = 0\.625\)\. These findings provide evidence for the occurrence of DTA\-related metabolites in Neocucurbitaria and experimentally demonstrate the antibacterial activity of compound 1 \. Discussion Collectively, this study provides integrated genomic and metabolomic insights into an endophytic Neocucurbitaria strain, expands the known occurrence of DTA\-related metabolites in fungi, and supports the potential of Neocucurbitaria as a source of antibacterial hit compounds\.
