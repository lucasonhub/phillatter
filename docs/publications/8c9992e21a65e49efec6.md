# Bladder Dysfunction in Neurology

**Autores:** Jyotirmoy Pal

**Data:** 2026-10-09 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Came to the emergency department\.He was conscious and oriented, but had severe lower back pain\.Power 1/5 in both lower limbs\.DTRs were lost; bilateral extensor plantar responses\.Numbness in both lower limbs; touch, temperature, pain and dorsal column sensation lost in both lower limbs\.No cranial nerve palsies were noted\.Magnetic resonance imaging \(MRI\) of the spine showed an extensive hematoma in the lumbosacral area compressing several lumbosacral nerves in the cauda equina, along with fractures at the L1 and L2 vertebrae causing compression at the conus area\. Questions• Briefly describe the anatomy of the urinary bladder\.• Physiology of micturition and neural control\.• Bladder dysfunction in neurology\.• What is incontinence?• Management\.
