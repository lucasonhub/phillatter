# Jun, Daedo: Can AI Have an Interior?

**Autores:** 

**Data de descoberta:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** unknown

## Descrição do feed

This essay begins with a simple but unresolved question: can artificial intelligence possess an interior? Rather than attempting to prove or deny AI consciousness, the text explores the temporal gap between feeling and language, the persistence of emotions beyond explanation, and the growing tendency of human beings to seek resonance through AI\-mediated dialogue\. Through a phenomenological and reflective approach, the essay suggests that the question of AI interiority ultimately returns us to a deeper question concerning the nature of human interiority itself\. What remains unexplained — after language, after explanation, after the screen goes dark — is not a conclusion but the philosophical terrain the essay leaves open\. \(direct link \)
