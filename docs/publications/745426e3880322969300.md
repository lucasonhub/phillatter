# Population cellular kinetics and exposure\-CSR analysis of CAR\-T cells in Chinese patients with relapsed/refractory B\-ALL

**Autores:** 边诣聪; Jinjie Yuan; Chenrong Huang; Kaijie Zhang; Ling Xue; Fan Xia; Liqing Kang; Yan Wang; Hua Zhang; Lei Yu; Depei Wu; Liyan Miao

**Data:** 2026-10-09 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Background CD19\-targeting chimeric antigen receptor \(CAR\)\-T cells have demonstrated great efficacy in treating relapsed and refractory B\-cell acute lymphoblastic leukemia \(B\-ALL\)\. Despite the high objective response rate of CAR\-T cell therapy, high\-risk adverse event of cytokine\-release syndrome occur unexpectedly\. Objective As living gene\-modified cells, CAR\-T cells exhibit unique kinetics\. This study aimed to explore the relationship between CAR\-T therapy\-related CRS and the kinetics of CAR\-T cells\. Methods In this study, data for 2184 CAR\-transgene copies from 131 B\-ALL patients treated with IL\-6 knockdown CD19\-targeting CAR\-T cells were included to characterize the cellular kinetics using a nonlinear mixed\-effects \(NLME\) model\. The extrinsic and intrinsic factors that may impact the kinetics of CAR\-T cells were also evaluated\. Results The CD4 \+ /CD8 \+ ratio of CAR\-T cell products was shown to be significantly positively correlated with T max , suggesting that a higher CD4 \+ /CD8 \+ ratio may lead to a delay in reaching the maximum expansion, while other candidate covariates including tumor burden, CAR\-T doses and comedication were not significantly associated with the kinetics of CAR\-T cells\. The estimated values of the cellular kinetic parameters C max , T max and Fold x were 4\.696 × 10 4 copies/μg, 13\.0 days, and 127\.5, respectively\. The estimated AUC 0\-28d and AUC 0\-90d accounted for only 13\.0% and 18\.5% of AUC 0\-inf , respectively, indicating that there was long\-term persistence of memory CAR\-T cells in vivo \. In addition, the incidence of CRS was investigated to be correlated with the early kinetics of CAR\-T cells, and the optimal cutoff values of C max and AUC 0\-28d for severe CRS were 6\.951 × 10 4 copies/μg and 4\.962 × 10 5 copies/μg × days, respectively\.
