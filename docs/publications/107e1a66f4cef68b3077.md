# JUSTIFICATION AND IMPROVEMENT OF THE PARAMETERS OF A TECHNICAL FACILITY FOR ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE\-BASED CONTROL OF THE SELF\-HEATING STATE OF COTTON HEAPS

**Autores:** Isayev Bohodirjon Makhamatshoevich

**Data:** 2026-10-10 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Abstract\. The article analyzes the problem of self\-heating of raw material stacks in the cotton ginning industry of Uzbekistan\. The parameters of the control tool based on a sensor system that measures temperature, humidity, and gas content in real time, as well as artificial intelligence \(AI\) algorithms, are substantiated\. Experimental calculation methods determined the sensor placement step, measurement frequency, warning limits, and forecasting horizon\. The proposed LSTM \+ Random Forest ensemble model allows for the early detection of hotspots compared to traditional threshold control: the average detection time in the model assessment is reduced to 2\.9 times, and the proportion of false signals does not exceed 6\.5%\. The results can be used to reduce losses in cotton storage enterprises\. Key words: cotton stacks, self\-heating, artificial intelligence, temperature sensor, LSTM, early warning, cotton ginning plant, monitoring\.
