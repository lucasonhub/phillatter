# Enhancing pre\-service teachers&\#x27; GenAI literacy and teaching self\-efficacy through teacher\-guided GenAI use: a quasi\-experimental study

**Autores:** Xinying Wang; Changjuan Chen; Shuping Li

**Data:** 2026-10-13 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Generative artificial intelligence \(GenAI\) is increasingly integrated into teacher education, requiring pre\-service teachers to develop GenAI literacy and teaching confidence in its pedagogical use\. This study examined whether teacher\-guided GenAI use was associated with higher posttest GenAI literacy and Teaching Self\-efficacy than self\-directed use and traditional instruction after accounting for pretest scores\. A quasi\-experimental pretest–posttest design involved 102 pre\-service teachers at a Chinese teacher education college\. Data came from a GenAI literacy test, a Teaching Self\-efficacy scale, and semi\-structured interviews\. ANCOVA showed significant overall effects of instructional condition on both outcomes after controlling for pretest scores \(partial η 2 = 0\.17 and 0\.08\) No significant differences emerged between the self\-directed and traditional groups\. Interviews highlighted prompt design, output evaluation, and teaching\-related confidence\. By combining a test\-based literacy measure with a three\-group comparison, this study provides preliminary quantitative evidence relevant to distinguishing structured teacher guidance from GenAI tool access alone\.
