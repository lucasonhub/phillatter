# Navigating the Digital Frontier: The Impact of Artificial Intelligence on Accounting and Financial Reporting in Adamawa State, Nigeria

**Autores:** E\. Y\. Enoch; H\. I\. Wamanyi

**Data:** 2026-10-09 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

The advent of Artificial Intelligence \(AI\) is precipitating a paradigm shift in global accounting and financial reporting practices\. However, its adoption and impact in developing economies, particularly at the sub\-national level, remain underexplored\. This study investigates the impact of AI on accounting efficiency, financial reporting quality, and the operational dynamics of accounting firms in Adamawa State, Nigeria\. Anchored on the Technology Organization Environment \(TOE\) framework, the study synthesizes existing literature to conceptualize the dual impact of AI, as a catalyst for automated reporting and fraud detection, and as a challenge concerning infrastructural deficits and skill gaps\. The study proposes that while AI significantly enhances the timeliness and accuracy of financial reports in Adamawa State, its optimal adoption is severely constrained by erratic power supply, inadequate digital infrastructure, and a deficit in AI\-relevant competencies among practitioners\. The paper concludes that AI’s impact in Adamawa State is currently at a nascent but transformative stage, requiring deliberate policy and educational interventions\. Recommendations include the integration of AI modules into the curriculum of tertiary institutions in Adamawa State, public\-private partnerships for infrastructural development, and phased AI adoption strategies by accounting firms\.
