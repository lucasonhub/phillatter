# Language adaptation among linguistically diverse learners in monolingual schools

**Autores:** Yuliia Vasylieva; Konovalenko T\.V\.; Maija Ročāne; Gatis Lāma; Santa Meļķe

**Data:** 2026-10-09 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

A common language of instruction does not create a common experience of language adaptation\. This study investigated students’ perceptions of the effectiveness of instructional, social, and digital practices related to language adaptation\. A quantitative cross\-sectional survey involved 403 students in Grades 4–12 from seven Latvian public schools\. The analysis compared the responses of Latvian\-, Russian\-, and Ukrainian\-speaking learners across nine practices\. The social domain received the highest descriptive ratings\. Real\-life and daily language use were among the most highly rated practices across the three linguistic groups\. Ratings varied across the selected practices and linguistic groups\. The findings informed a theory\-based ecological framework of language adaptation\. The framework provides a conceptual interpretation of the observed patterns and intergroup differences\.
