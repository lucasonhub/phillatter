# Workplace Welfare Services in Promoting Employee’s Work\-Life Balance; Ecological System Perspective at PT\. USSI Pinbuk Prima Software

**Autores:** Arsy Nafisa Beladiena; Muhammad Fedryansyah; Budi Muhammad Taftazani

**Data:** 2026-10-13 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Background: Digital work patterns can blur the boundaries between work and personal life, particularly in project\-based information\-technology work\. Objective: This study aims to analyze how work welfare services are implemented at PT\. USSI Pinbuk Prima Software contributes to shaping employee work\-life balance through a social work perspective, especially the Person in Environment approach\. Methods: The research uses a descriptive qualitative method with data collection techniques in the form of in\-depth interviews, observations, and documentation studies of employees and company management\. Results: Prior to the policy adjustments, informants reported unpredictable working hours, work pressure, strained family interactions, and limited opportunities to participate in personal and social life\. These accounts indicated difficulties in social functioning, particularly in fulfilling family roles, maintaining interpersonal relationships, and managing daily responsibilities\. Employees subsequently perceived clearer work schedules, conditional WFH/WFA arrangements, health services, counseling, family gatherings, and EAP as support for managing competing roles and sustaining social relationships\. From a chronosystem perspective, the changes in company policy over time and employees&\#x27; transitions in family responsibilities show that social functioning and work\-life balance are dynamic and context\-dependent; the qualitative findings do not establish causal effects\. Conclusion: Work welfare services are perceived as environmental resources supporting employees&\#x27; work\-life balance and social functioning across occupational, family, and interpersonal roles\. An ecological system perspective highlights the importance of responsive organizational support as working conditions and family responsibilities change over time\.
