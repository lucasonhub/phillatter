# Beyond Expert Rankings: Contrast Calibration for Mixed\-Precision MoE Quantization

**Autores:** Renjie Xie; Juncheng Yang; Aoting Hu; Yan Liang; Mingxi Zhang; Zheheng Hong; Wei Xu; Yanzhi Chen

**Data:** 2026-10-14 · **Tipo:** preprint

## Resumo

Mixture\-of\-experts \(MoE\) language models activate only a few experts per token, yet all expert weights must be stored\. Mixed\-precision quantization reduces this storage cost by assigning higher bit\-widths to sensitive experts and lower bit\-widths to resilient ones, but expert rankings alone do not determine how strongly to weight their errors\. To address this gap, we propose NatScale, a contrast\-calibrated allocation framework for mixed\-precision MoE quantization\. It uses a model\-specific power transformation to calibrate gradient\-based expert weights to an information\-theoretic contrast target while preserving rankings\. We jointly evaluate each expert&\#x27;s gate, up and down configurations to estimate its output error\. We then combine these errors and calibrated weights with an existing prior based on weight spectra, variance and precision decay to select configurations under a storage budget\. Across three models, NatScale achieves the lowest native\-to\-quantized KL divergence and the highest top\-token agreement in all nine settings against five allocation baselines\. It also yields the lowest perplexity in seven settings\. These findings highlight the importance of objective\-aware precision allocation\.
