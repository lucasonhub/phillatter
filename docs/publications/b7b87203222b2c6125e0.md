# AI\-Native Network with Clean–Impaired Semantic Preservation for Robust Semantic Communications in Space

**Autores:** Yulin Peng; Hong Wen; Liang Chen; Mo Yang; Yuhao Shi; Yongfeng Wang

**Data:** 2026-10-14 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Semantic communication offers a method for task\-oriented information delivery in artificial intelligence \(AI\)\-enabled space networks, but compact semantic representations remain vulnerable to fading, noise, and structured corruption during transmission\. This work proposes Clean–Impaired Semantic Preservation for Robust Semantic Communications \(CISP\-SemCom\), a link\-level semantic communication framework that explicitly learns from the clean latent representation and its communication\-impaired counterpart generated from the same source sample\. In a shared projection space, class\-aware Clean–Impaired Contrastive Alignment \(CI\-Con\) preserves cross\-sample discrimination while excluding same\-class non\-matched samples from the negative set, and Semantic Preservation \(SP\) directly promotes consistency within each matched clean–impaired pair\. In a ten\-seed controlled same\-backbone evaluation on CIFAR\-10 under Rician fading, CISP\-SemCom increases the normalized area under the robustness curve \(AURC\) evaluated over the signal\-to\-noise ratio \(SNR\) \(SNR\-AURC\) from 66\.85 ± 2\.36 to 72\.48 ± 1\.24, corresponding to a paired gain of 5\.63 AURC points with a 95% confidence interval of \[3\.75, 7\.51\]\. Mechanism diagnostics show that SP\-only optimization admits projector collapse, and that class\-agnostic contrastive learning introduces same\-class false\-negative pressure\. A direct class\-aware ablation further indicates that most of the measured task\-robustness gain comes from class\-aware CI\-Con, while SP acts as an explicit matched\-pair consistency regularizer\. On EuroSAT RGB, SNR\-AURC increases from 65\.53 ± 1\.80 to 75\.35 ± 1\.58, showing that the robustness improvement is also observed on a second dataset\. Decoder\-independent latent probes further show improved accessibility of task\-relevant class information, while the measured batch\-1 inference latency remains similar to Backbone\. These results support clean–impaired semantic preservation as an effective strategy for robust task\-oriented transmission under satellite\-inspired link impairments\.
