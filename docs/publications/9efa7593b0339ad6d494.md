# Comparing corpus\-based and GenAI\-based tools: The analysis of specialised discourse using Sketch Engine and ChatGPT\-5\.1

**Autores:** Filip Kalaš

**Data:** 2026-10-09 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

This study examines the performance of Sketch Engine and ChatGPT\-5\.1 in identifying specialised lexical phenomena within a medical subcorpus of the MuchMore Springer Bilingual Corpus\. It examines three analytically distinct layers of specialised discourse – multiword terms, adjectival collocates, and four\-word lexical bundles – to assess how corpus\-driven and generative approaches capture terminological, collocational, and formulaic patterns in anaesthesiology\. Results show substantial convergence, particularly in lexical bundles, where 84% of units overlapped and ranked similarly\. Term extraction and collocation analysis also revealed a stable conceptual core shared by both tools, while divergences reflected either corpus\-specific distribution \(Sketch Engine\) or conceptual salience \(ChatGPT\-5\.1\)\. The findings suggest that the complementary use of corpus tools and generative artificial intelligence \(GenAI\) may offer potential applications for terminology\-oriented teaching contexts, such as seminars in corpus linguistics or terminology studies, where students can critically compare corpus\-based evidence with model\-generated linguistic interpretations\.
