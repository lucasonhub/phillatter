# The Influence of Robotics Technology on Library Services Delivery in Academic Libraries in Lagos State, Nigeria

**Autores:** Isiaka Mashood Ishola

**Data:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

This study examined the influence of robotics technology for library services delivery in selected academic libraries in Lagos state, Nigeria\. It investigate the types of robot currently in use, the perception of librarians’ on it relationship to users satisfaction ans services efficiency, and the obstacles facing the implementation\. The study adopted descriptive survey research design with a population of sixty \(60\) librarians from the three selected academic libraries in Lagos state\. All 60 librarians were included through total enumeration\. Data were collected through a structured questionnaire and analysed using frequencies, percentages, means, and standard deviations\. Humanoid or interactive robots were the most commonly reported robot identified by 90% of respondents and other technologies, including telepresence, shelf\-reading, and autonomous delivery robots, were less widely available\. Respondents generally perceived robotics as improving service accessibility, user experience, and operational accuracy\. However, perceived benefits in reducing waiting times and enabling staff to focus on complex user needs were comparatively modest\. Major implementation challenges included acquisition costs, difficulties integrating robotic systems with existing library infrastructure, maintenance requirements, unreliable electricity or internet connectivity, and limited technical support\. The findings suggest that robotics can support more accessible and efficient library services, but wider adoption will depend on sustained funding, reliable infrastructure, system integration, and staff training\. Implementation should also preserve access to human assistance\.
