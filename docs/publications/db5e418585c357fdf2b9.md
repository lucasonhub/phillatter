# Project Sovereign Sentinel : The Analog Foundation for an Eternal Civilization A Joint Declaration on Early Education and the Preservation of Human Cognition

**Autores:** David Michael Seagal

**Data:** 2026-10-13 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

The CERN Manifesto on Early Education outlines a radical pedagogical shift within the post\-scarcity, AI\-guided society of Project Sentinel\. Designed to prevent cognitive atrophy and tech\-dependency, this educational architecture establishes a strict analog\-first foundation for children\. By combining early training in physical, non\-digital tools \(such as slide rules, topographic paper maps, and mechanical instruments\) with a strict zero\-sugar, moderately flavoured nutritional baseline, the manifesto aims to safeguard human intelligence, spatial reasoning, and individual drive\. Central to the framework is choice architecture, allowing young learners to autonomously select their own physical tools and whole foods, cultivating the independent mindset required to become the future stewards of an eternal civilization\.
