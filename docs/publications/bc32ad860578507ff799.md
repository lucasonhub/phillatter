# A Rule\-Based Knowledge Mapping Framework to Enhance Fundamental Programming Education in TVET

**Autores:** Dwi Fitria Al Husaeni; Budi Mulyanti; Ade Gafar Abdullah; Nofouz Mafarja; Lala Septem Riza; Amay Suherman

**Data:** 2026-10-09 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Programming learning in vocational education requires linking competency achievement to national work standards, but knowledge representation in programming education generally does not provide rule mapping that explicitly links programming concepts to work competency standards and curriculum achievement indicators\. This study aims to develop and validate rule\-based knowledge mapping for algorithms and basic programming that aligns with the Indonesian National Work Competency Standards and competency indicators of the Indonesian National Curriculum for Vocational High Schools\. The research method used is multi\-method qualitative and quantitative research\. This study used design and development \(DDR\) type 1, which focuses on the development and evaluation of a product with a knowledge engineering approach to develop a rule\-based knowledge mapping framework in the context of algorithms and basic programming\. This study produces 28 rules with 13 indicators of the Rule\-Based Knowledge Mapping Framework for Basic Programming Education in TVET\. The rule\-based mapping from the view of algorithms and basic programming identifies materials, basic data types, strings, and outputs as pure prerequisites \(basic nodes\)\. Meanwhile, Functions/Procedures are positioned as advanced competencies that rely on Sequential Control and Variables\. This research yields a conceptually validated knowledge base with the potential to serve as a foundation for the further development of competency\-based adaptive assessment and learning technologies, such as Intelligent Tutoring Systems\. Although limited to alignment with the Indonesian Work Competency Standards and Education Curriculum, this research makes a global contribution in mapping the graph of fundamental concepts of algorithms and programming\.
