# PENDIDIKAN MULTIKULTURAL DI ERA DIGITAL: PERAN LITERASI DIGITAL DALAM PENGUATAN MODERASI BERAGAMA PADA PESERTA DIDIK

**Autores:** Andreo Diwa Arsenta; Muhammad Rapono; M\. Dava Aidil Fitra Harahap; Nabila Syahpitri; Alicia Windy Fransisca; Naila Putri Siregar; Nurhalimah Manik; Sopiyatul Bahana Harahap; Fauziyah Nur Ramadhani; Dinda Dwi Alriska

**Data:** 2026-10-09 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

The digital era brings complex ideological challenges to the education sector, one of which is the threat of self\-radicalization among students through social media\. Instilling tolerance through conventional multicultural education is no longer sufficient without adequate technological skills\. This study aims to describe the central role of digital literacy in strengthening religious moderation among students within the framework of multicultural education\. The method used is qualitative research with a library research approach\. Data were collected from various relevant literatures and analyzed using a descriptive\-analytical technique\. The results show that multicultural education serves as a fundamental moral defense, but its application in cyberspace absolutely requires digital literacy\. Digital literacy acts as a catalyst that equips students with critical thinking skills to filter information, validate sources, and reject provocative contents\. The integration of multicultural education and digital literacy is proven to form a resilient character of religious moderation\. The pinnacle of this educational success is the students&\#x27; proactive ability to produce inclusive counter\-narratives, ensuring that cyberspace remains a harmonious ecosystem free from ideological polarization\.
