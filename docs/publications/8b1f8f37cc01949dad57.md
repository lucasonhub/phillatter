# YOLOv8\-P2\-CBAM\-NWD: High\-Precision Small\-Target Drone Detection in Complex Visual Airspace via Shallow Attention Routing and Normalized Wasserstein Distance

**Autores:** Layeq Ali; Iftikhar Ahmed Khan; Shahid Yousaf

**Data:** 2026-10-15 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Optical ground\-to\-air detection of small unmanned aerial vehicles \(UAVs\) supports perimeter security and airspace safety, but a distant UAV may occupy fewer than 16 × 16 pixels, which is 0\.0625% of a 640 × 640 detector input\. Strided downsampling makes such objects harder to detect, and Intersection over Union \(IoU\) based box losses are highly sensitive to pixel\-level shifts\. This paper evaluates YOLOv8\-P2\-CBAM\-NWD, a nano\-scale version of YOLOv8 with a stride\-4 P2 detection head, a Convolutional Block Attention Module \(CBAM\) on the shallow P2 branch, and a box loss that combines Complete IoU \(CIoU\) with the Normalized Gaussian Wasserstein Distance \(NWD\)\. Each model was trained only once on 800 single\-class drone images and compared against a YOLOv8n baseline\. On 200 validation images, the proposed model reached a mean average precision at an IoU of 0\.5 \(mAP@0\.5\) of 89\.8%, which is below the 92\.5% of the baseline\. On a sentry test set of 2,500 frames \(1,800 drone frames and 700 cloud or clear\-sky frames\) at a confidence threshold of 0\.1, it increased frame\-level precision from 96\.94% to 98\.35% and specificity from 92\.14% to 95\.86%, and it decreased false alarms from 78\.57 to 41\.43 per 1,000 negative frames, a 47\.3% relative reduction\. Frame\-level recall went down from 96\.78% to 96\.11%\. Thus, the main advantage is fewer false alarms rather than higher object\-level accuracy\. Since the threshold was selected on the test set and the frames were not grouped by video, these results are preliminary\.
