# Wideband absorption and dual\-band biosensing in a reconfigurable VO 2 –graphene metamaterial assisted by explainable Artificial Intelligence

**Autores:** Manjusha Puliyampetta; Anand Margabandu; Jerald Francis; Vinothkumar Arumugam; Midhunraj Punnaikkal Kumaran

**Data:** 2026-10-13 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

This study presents a reconfigurable VO2\-graphene metamaterial absorber for terahertz applications, comprising interconnected VO2 square rings and a central graphene ring on a SiO2 substrate backed by gold\. The optimized design achieves over 90% broadband absorption from 0\.9075 to 6\.6375 THz, yielding a 5\.73\-THz bandwidth\. Removing the interconnection produces dual\-band absorption with near\-unity peaks at 2\.3789 and 5\.624 THz\. Refractive\-index sensing achieves a maximum sensitivity of 1\.875 THz/RIU and a figure of merit \(FOM\) of approximately 98\.17 RIU−1\. Polarization response, angular stability, and electromagnetic shielding performance are also investigated\. An equivalent circuit model and electric\-field analysis elucidate the absorption mechanism\. Furthermore, XGBoost\-based machine learning predicts absorber performance, while SHAP analysis identifies influential design parameters\. These results demonstrate the potential of the proposed absorber for multifunctional terahertz applications\.
