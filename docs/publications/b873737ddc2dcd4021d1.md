# Convergent Digital, Quantum, Spectral Frameworks for Resilient Supply Chains and Sustainable Mobility

**Autores:** Padma B

**Data:** 2026-10-12 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

This paper synthesizes a coherent research program spanning digital twins, artificial intelligence \(AI\), blockchain, quantum optimization, Industry 5\.0, electric and solar\-assisted vehicles, and a family of Fourier–Karhunen–Fourier \(FKF\) spectral transforms\. Drawing on twenty\-eight prior studies, the paper argues that resilience is no longer a purely organizational property; it is a coupled cyber\-physical\-spectral property that can be measured, optimized, and secured\. Digital twins provide a living model of multi\-echelon networks; AI and blockchain jointly raise transparency and green performance,; quantum annealing and related methods attack combinatorial bottlenecks in unit\-load and disruption recovery problems,; and the FKF/FKL transform family supplies a shift\-invariant spectral calculus for lead\-time, inventory, and resonance phenomena –\. Mobility work on battery sizing, materials, solar hybrids, and intelligent transport, closes the loop between energy, vehicles, and freight\. The contribution is an integrated agenda: sense with IoT, twin the network, optimize with AI and quantum, certify with blockchain, and diagnose dynamics with FKF spectra\.
