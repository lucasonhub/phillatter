# Dablemont, Paul: Le réel du sujet – Désir, inconscient et subjectivité différentielle

**Autores:** 

**Data de descoberta:** 2026-10-19 · **Tipo:** unknown

## Descrição do feed

This paper argues that the subject is not a stable substance, but a fragile fold stretched over the void, produced by a subjective meta\-machine that constantly territorializes the flow of desire\. By weaving together Deleuze \(surface effect\), Lacan \(lack\-of\-being\), Adorno \(social wound\), and Searle \(Background\), it shows that the subject is traversed by operators – desire, the unconscious, memory, identity – that give consistency to the void\. The cure does not aim for illusory healing, but for an ethics of &quot;sober lucidity&quot;: it deconstructs fixations through negative deterritorialization, then invents new forms of life through creative\-critical reterritorialization, thus learning to &quot;dance on the abyss&quot; in the face of threats from war, authoritarian language, and digital colonization\. \(direct link \)
