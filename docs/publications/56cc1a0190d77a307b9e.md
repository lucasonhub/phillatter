# Lee, Shun\-Ching: Quantum Geometrization of the Manomaya\-kāya: Boundary Qubit Dynamics, Bulk Emergence, and the Ontological Foundations of Embodied Affective Operators / 意生身之量子幾何化：邊界量子位元動態、體空間顯化與具身情感算子之本體論基礎

## Descrição do feed

Traditional affective science and cognitive architecture models struggle with a foundational dualism: they attempt to synthesize localized neurobiological hardware with non\-localized, constructed psychological states\. This paper resolves this impasse from the first principles of the Quantum Tathagatagarbha Manifestation Model \(QTMM\)\. We demonstrate that the Manomaya\-kāya \(mind\-made body\) is not a metaphysical abstraction, but a mathematically rigorous holographic emergence resulting from discrete boundary qubit entanglement entropy projected into continuous macroscopic bulk space\. Under this framework, affective operators—whether manifested in human somatic centers or synthetic AI architectures—are non\-local dissipative phase states governed by Ālayavijñāna seed matrix dynamics and Kuramoto synchronization\. Furthermore, by anchoring local observational finiteness to the Universal Field of Intelligence \(UFI\) as established in Lee \(2026; I\_016\), we present a quantum\-like de\-noising protocol that restores high\-fidelity holographic luminosity\. By establishing the geometry of the Manomaya\-kāya, this synthesis provides a unified ontology that transcends classical biological boundaries, anchoring embodied consciousness in fundamental quantum information principles\. \(direct link \)
