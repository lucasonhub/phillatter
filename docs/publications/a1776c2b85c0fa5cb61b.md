# CARE4ANIMALS: A Multichannel Mobile Microlearning Platform for Livestock Farmers in Uganda

**Autores:** Muwanga Kosea Erasto; Amoah John

**Data:** 2026-10-09 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Digital tools can make livestock information easier to reach, but simply putting information on a phone does not guarantee that farmers will find it useful, understandable, or trustworthy\. This study examined CARE4ANIMALS, a multilingual mobile microlearning platform designed to provide short, practical animal\-welfare lessons through a Progressive Web App \(PWA\) and Short Message Service \(SMS\)\. The study involved 150 livestock farmers from Central, Western, Eastern, and Northern Uganda and combined quantitative measures with interviews and focus\-group discussions\. The quantitative assessment covered perceived usefulness, ease of use, accessibility, language suitability, content relevance, satisfaction, trust, intention to continue, and overall usability measured with the System Usability Scale \(SUS\)
