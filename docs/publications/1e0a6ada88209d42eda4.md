# IMKONIYATI CHEKLANGAN O‘QUVCHILARNI IJTIMOIY HAYOTGA TAYYORLASHDA ZAMONAVIY PEDAGOGIK YONDASHUVLAR

**Autores:** Yakubova Umida Sharifovna

**Data:** 2026-10-12 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

This article analyzes the modern pedagogical and methodological foundations of preparing students with disabilities for social life\. It explores the essence and pedagogical potential of person\-centered, competency\-based, systemic, activity\-based, social\-integrative, and acmeological approaches, as well as their influence on the socialization of students with disabilities\. The theoretical and methodological foundations of the study are based on the concepts of personal development, activity, competence, humanism, inclusive education, and social integration\. The research employs theoretical analysis, comparative analysis, systematization, generalization, and pedagogical modeling\. The findings substantiate that integrating different pedagogical approaches is more effective in preparing students with disabilities for social life than applying a single approach\. Furthermore, communicative, social and civic, daily living, digital, legal, and professional competencies are identified as the key components of social readiness\. The article presents an integrative methodological model for preparing students with disabilities for social life\.
