# Applicability and chemical mechanism of alkali\-activated fly ash and blast furnace slag stabilized soil for sustainable embankment

**Autores:** Chen Zhang; Wei Wang; Zhiduo Zhu; Na Li; Ping Jang; Borui Ma

**Data:** 2026-10-09 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

The unreasonable disposal of waste soil in engineering not only occupies a large number of land resources, but also fails to save cost and protect environment\. This study aims to develop an eco\-friendly cementitious material \(AFS\) by using fly ash \(FA\) and blast furnace slag \(BFS\) under alkali\-activated condition, achieving the stabilization and resource recovery of waste soil\. A series of laboratory tests were conducted to investigate the applicability of AFS stabilized waste soil \(AFSS\), evaluating its carbon emission and environmental impact in terms of life cycle assessment, as well as revealing its chemical mechanism and micro\-structural characteristics\. The results showed that the comprehensive mechanical strength of AFS reached the optimal value when the FA:BFS, NaOH:Na 2 SiO 3 , alkali content and water to solid ratio were 5:5, 1:7, 10% and 0\.35, respectively\. It is feasible to stabilize waste soil by using AFS, such as compared with AFSS containing 0%AFS, the enhancement range for compressive strength, toughness index, tensile strength and water stability coefficient of AFSS containing 10%AFS were 110%, 106%, 243% and 85%, respectively\. The AFSS showed lower global warming potential, acidification potential, eutrophication potential, photochemical oxidation potential and resource consumption potential\. The AFSS underwent polymerization reaction under alkaline condition, forming C\-\(A\)\-S\-H and N\-A\-S\-H gels to bond soil particles and form the stable structure\. These results achieve the application of AFSS in embankment engineering, providing scientific importance in carbon reduction and waste management\.
