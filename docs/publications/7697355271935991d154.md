# Bulgaria, Jamel &amp; Xǔ, Chénglán: Meta\-Cognitive Mapping of Scientific Inquiry: A Structural\-Constructivist Analysis of the Core Emotion Framework Operators in Empirical Research

**Autores:** 

**Data de descoberta:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** unknown

## Descrição do feed

This paper develops a structural constructivist account of scientific reasoning by mapping the ten functional operators of the Core Emotion Framework \(CEF\) onto a unified meta cognitive lifecycle of empirical inquiry\. Drawing on the CEF’s tripartite somatic cognitive architecture—Head, Heart, and Gut centers—the analysis argues that scientific investigation is not a disembodied process of pure calculation, but a dynamically regulated sequence of somatic cognitive transitions\. Using textual evidence such as the claim that “scientific inquiry is… guided by a systematic sequence of somatic cognitive transitions” and the definition of operators as “dynamic sequences… distributed across three somatic and cognitive centers,” the paper shows how Sensing, Calculating, Deciding, Expanding, Constricting, Achieving, Arranging, Appreciating, Boosting, and Accepting jointly structure the acquisition, evaluation, expansion, discipline, execution, and integration of empirical knowledge\. The resulting model bridges affective science, embodied cognition, and the philosophy of science by demonstrating that epistemic virtues—such as disciplined boundary setting, methodological rigor, and acceptance of empirical outcomes—are grounded in somatic regulation patterns rather than purely intellectual dispositions\. The paper concludes by outlining implications for meta cognition, affective computing, and the design of synthetic agents using operator transition matrices and center activation dynamics\. \(direct link \)
