# AI\-DIGITAL HUMAN AS A NEW CLASS OF DIGITAL LAW SUBJECT: TYPOLOGY, CRITERIA OF LEGAL PERSONHOOD, AND A REGISTRATION MODEL IN DIGITAL ECOSYSTEMS

**Autores:** Наталія Миколаївна Оніщенко; Олексій Костенко

**Data:** 2026-10-10 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

This article argues for a new class of digital legal subject within AI legal\-personhood doctrine — the ‘AI\-digital human’ — largely unaddressed in domestic and international legal scholarship\. Building on the author’s earlier three\-class model of AI legal subjects and the institution of the AI\-representative, the article distinguishes two different modules of the AI\-digital human: Module 1 \(Digiperson\), a digital human as a functional reflection of a specific natural person, built on ‘red\-group’ identification attributes and digitised access credentials; and Module 2 \(autonomous Digihuman\), reflecting no real natural person, its attributes formed referentially and tied to a functional role within a digital ecosystem\. The article shows that equating a digital human with a natural person in the full scope of rights and obligations is a doctrinal error: legal status should follow an age\-based logic \(child—adolescent—adult\) rather than mirroring the prototype’s status\. Drawing on a comparative analysis of Regulation \(EU\) 2024/1689 \(AI Act\) and Regulation \(EU\) 2024/1183 \(eIDAS 2\.0\), the article substantiates the need for a Digital Humans Registry as an institutional legitimation mechanism for both modules, and formulates draft articles for the Ukrainian Law on Artificial Intelligence\.
