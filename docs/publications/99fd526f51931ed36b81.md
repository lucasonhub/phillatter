**Autores:** Qiang Wu; Weiqing Huang

## Resumo

At sub\-THz frequencies only the intensity envelope is reliably observable, and under this constraint the Fisher information matrix for joint permittivity–thickness estimation is severely ill\-conditioned at fixed incidence \(condition numbers $$10^7$$ 10 7 – $$10^9$$ 10 9 \)\.
