# Dimensionality reduction of atmospheric concentration fields using a 3D convolutional autoencoder with spatial attention mechanism

**Autores:** Stepan Polyakov; Alexey V\. Penenko

**Data:** 2026-10-15 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Abstract High\-dimensional atmospheric chemistry simulations produce volumetric concentration fields that are costly to store and repeatedly process in inverse\-modelling and data\-assimilation workflows\. This study evaluates reduced representations for CO concentration fields from WRF\-Chem over Novosibirsk and CAMS over Europe\. We propose a three\-dimensional convolutional autoencoder with a lightweight spatial attention module \(SAM3D\) and compare it with linear, spectral, tensor\-based, interpolation, manifold\-learning, and neural\-network baselines\. The evaluation includes latent dimensions from 8 to 64, temporally separated validation protocols, storage\-cost estimates, and computational\-efficiency measurements\. On the larger CAMS dataset, SAM3D achieves the lowest MSE and relative $$L\_2$$ error among methods compared at equal latent\-vector dimensions and improves over the plain 3D autoencoder\. On the smaller WRF\-Chem dataset, rankings depend more strongly on the split protocol, with fixed transforms and tensor decompositions remaining competitive\. SAM3D adds less than 1% FLOPs and only a small increase in model size while retaining a differentiable encoder–decoder mapping\. These results support SAM3D as a practical reduced\-state representation for three\-dimensional atmospheric concentration fields\.
