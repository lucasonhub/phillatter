# SUN’IY INTELLEKT VA GEYMIFIKATSIYA INTEGRATSIYASI: BO‘LAJAK BOSHLANG‘ICH SINF O‘QITUVCHILARINI TAYYORLASHDA ADAPTIV O‘YIN MUHITLARINING IMKONIYATLARI

**Autores:** ZUBAYDULLOYEVA MADINABONU KAMOL QIZI

**Data:** 2026-10-12 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

The article analyses the theoretical foundations of integrating artificial intelligence with gamification and its potential in the training of pre\-service primary school teachers\. The essence of adaptive gamification is clarified, and a three\-level integration model is proposed in which artificial intelligence acts as an adaptation mechanism, a virtual pupil simulator and a design partner\. An adaptive cycle that preserves teacher oversight, as well as the pedagogical and ethical risks of integration and the conditions for mitigating them, are substantiated\.
