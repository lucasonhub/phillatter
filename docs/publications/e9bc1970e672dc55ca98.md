# Research on the Impact of Digital Transformation on Innovation Performance in Manufacturing Enterprises

**Autores:** Wenxuan Zhao; Fa Tian

**Data:** 2026-10-09 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

This study investigates the impact of digital transformation on the innovation performance of listed manufacturing companies and explores the mediating mechanism of R&amp;D expenditure\. Utilizing a sample of Chinese firms, the empirical results demonstrate that digital transformation exerts a significantly positive impact on innovation efficiency and performance, with R&amp;D expenditure serving as a critical bridging mechanism\. This study enriches the existing theoretical framework, offering empirical evidence for digital strategies while suggesting future inquiries into firm heterogeneity\.
