# Society Under Deep Transformation: The Judiciary and Algonormative Expectations

**Autores:** Prof\. Dr\. Diógenes V\. Hassan Ribeiro

**Data:** 2026-10-15 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Observing algorithms, that is, distinguishing algorithms in today&\#x27;s technological and digital society – which is hypercomplex – is indispensable\. The emergence of artificial intelligence algorithms has surprised all scientific fields\. The education system, for example, is impacted by the possibility of perhaps superficial and certainly very rapid learning of difficult theories that previously took years to master\. The science system, on the other hand, besides being impacted by the possibilities, is above all optimistic about the advances\. The economic system also sees marked changes\. But the legal system is, in a way, apprehensive considering the ethical and normative aspects\. The first point relates to decisions\. The second point relates specifically to norms and structures of expectations\. No one can predict what the consequences will be\. But everyone can say that the systems are already revealing great changes and evolutions\. Thus, the question arises as to what evolutions have already occurred in the legal system with artificial intelligence\.
