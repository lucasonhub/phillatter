# Generative AI\-Powered Interdisciplinary Chemistry Practice in Junior High: Exploring Matter Composition and Structure

**Autores:** Zhihua Wang; Xuebing Liu; Fengxiang Chang; Cunzheng Lv

**Data:** 2026-10-12 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

To address persistent issues in junior high chemistry teaching of Composition and Structure of Substances—including fragmented disciplinary perspectives, highly abstract micro\-level concepts, and underdeveloped macro\-submicro\-symbolic triple representation thinking—this study constructs a four\-stage interdisciplinary practical teaching model integrated with generative artificial intelligence \(AI\) and virtual reality \(VR\)\. The teaching practice was implemented in a natural ninth\-grade class \(N=40\) in Zhengzhou\. To ensure in\-depth analysis of the massive multimodal data generated by AI and VR interactions, purposive sampling was employed to select five representative students as a focus group\. Following a single\-group pretest\-posttest design, data from this focus group were evaluated across four dimensions to systematically measure learning outcomes: macro cognition via concept maps, micro cognition via knowledge cards, model deepening through AI text error correction, and transfer competence and AI literacy via AI chat record coding\. Post\-intervention data revealed significant improvements across all dimensions\. In macro cognition, average concept map nodes increased by 108\.8% \(from 6\.8 to 14\.2\)\. For micro cognition, accuracy scores of micro\-level tasks surged by 170\.0% \(from 1\.0 to 2\.7\)\. Furthermore, the error correction rate reached 88\.9% during model refinement, while critical probing questions accounted for 45\.83% of total chat records, indicating enhanced transfer and AI literacy\. These findings confirm that the generative AI\-empowered interdisciplinary model effectively broadens students’ cognitive frameworks, lowers learning barriers of abstract chemical knowledge, consolidates mastery of model\-based symbolic representation, and fosters preliminary AI critical literacy\. It provides a practical, replicable reference for advancing digital interdisciplinary chemistry teaching at the junior high level\.
