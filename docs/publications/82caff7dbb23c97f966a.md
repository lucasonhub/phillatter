# Dynamic chloride frameworks enable dualistic Li\-ion transport in Li2ZrCl6: Single\-ion hopping and cooperative diffusion

**Autores:** Shisheng Wang; Yanzhao Niu; Huafeng Dong; Bingkai Zhang; Songbai Han

**Data:** 2026-10-11 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Li 2 ZrCl 6 \(LZC\) is a promising chloride fast\-ion conductor, yet the microscopic origin of its high Li \+ conductivity remains unclear\. Here we combine density functional theory, machine\-learning\-potential molecular dynamics \(MLP\-MD\) and CI\-NEB calculations to probe Li\-ion transport in crystalline LZC\. We find a critical coupling between Li migration and low\-frequency anharmonic Zr–Cl framework vibrations: dynamic chloride fluctuations intermittently open Cl\-defined migration bottlenecks\. Li transport shows a scale\-dependent cooperative–single\-ion duality\. At short times, motion proceeds mainly through intermittent single\-ion hopping rather than concerted multi\-ion jumps; over the nanosecond statistical window of our simulations, discrete hops become correlated through Li–Li interactions and framework fluctuations, yielding statistically correlated diffusion\. Static CI\-NEB barriers \(0\.15–0\.40 eV\) show that low\-barrier single\-ion paths are accessible, whereas representative two\-ion concerted paths are less favourable\. Modest sensitivity to non\-stoichiometry indicates that fast diffusion does not require one predefined Li–Li configuration\. These results establish a dualistic transport paradigm in LZC: chloride\-framework gating turns local single\-ion hops into long\-time correlated diffusion, highlighting soft anion\-framework dynamics as a useful handle for designing halide solid electrolytes\.
