# Cross‐Scale Integrated Photonic Convolver Based on Conjugate‐Symmetric Interference

**Autores:** Yixuan Zheng; 张姝然 Zhang Shuran; Jiajia Wang; Yifu Xu; Yunping Bai; Xingyuan Xu

**Data:** 2026-10-17 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

ABSTRACT Photonic hardware offers a promising route to high‐speed and energy‐efficient convolution computing beyond the bandwidth and power limitations of conventional electronic platforms\. Here, we propose and experimentally demonstrate a cross‐scale integrated photonic convolver \(CSIPC\) that enables cross‐scale feature extraction through a single training process\. Implemented on a hierarchically coupled finite‐impulse‐response \(FIR\) photonic chip, the CSIPC exploits the propagation of conjugate‐symmetric interference‐weight relations through the coupling network, together with the spatiotemporal interleaving induced by serial‐to‐parallel conversion\. A single set of trained chip weights can be mapped at different output ports into a family of equivalent convolution kernels with distinct effective tap numbers and temporal intervals\. By selecting the output port and tuning the input symbol rate, 2 × 4, 2 × 2, and 2 × 1 two‐dimensional convolution kernels can be realized on the same chip\. We experimentally validate multifunctional cross‐scale image feature extraction and achieve a maximum single‐mode equivalent computing throughput of 0\.276 TOPS\. These results provide a route toward cross‐scale photonic convolution using a single device and a single training process, while improving functional integration density and enabling the reuse of trained weights and hardware resources\.
