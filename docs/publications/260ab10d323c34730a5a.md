# Digital Transformation Management in Teaching at High Schools in Ho Chi Minh City: From Perception and Implementation Practice to Influencing Factors

**Autores:** Nguyen Thi Huong; Nguyen Minh Giam

**Data:** 2026-10-10 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Research on perception status, practices of implementation, management of School Directorates and factors affecting digital transformation in teaching at high schools in Ho Chi Minh City\. Data were collected through questionnaire administration to a sample of 200 respondents consisting of 50 educational managers and 150 teachers from five public high schools during 2023–2026 and in\-depth interviews and product analysis\. The data were analyzed with SPSS software\. The results show that the level of perception of educational managers and teachers has reached a very high level \(M = 4\.33–4\.38\)\. Practice of implementation has reached a solid baseline \(M = 3\.26–3\.51\), with considerable progress in administrative digitalization and basic digital instructions\. The future growth opportunities are advanced pedagogical formats such as flipped classroom and AI assisted individualised instruction\. For infrastructure optimisation, the most important operational priority is managing supporting conditions \(M = 3\.26\)\. The digital competencies, attitudes and readiness of teachers for innovation and the digital leadership capacity of the School Directorate were identified as key drivers of the digital transformation process\. On this basis, the study proposes a synchronized framework to assist schools in transitioning toward digital pedagogical leadership, thereby promoting digital transformation in teaching toward a substantive, high\-quality, and sustainable direction\.
