# Sustainable Business Model Innovation Research: Bibliometric Structures and the Emerging Frontier of Regenerative Business Models \(2016–2025\)

**Autores:** Merzoug Fetah; Boukhatem Lakhdar; Vincenzo Basile; Yassine Remache

**Data:** 2026-10-14 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

ABSTRACT This study presents a bibliometric mapping of sustainable business model innovation \(SBMI\) research, analysing 961 Scopus‐indexed documents \(2016–2025\) retrieved via a title‐field Boolean query and a four‐stage screening protocol\. Circular, regenerative, and resilient business models are treated as distinct, related perspectives rather than sequential stages\. The corpus shows a three‐phase growth trajectory: emergence, acceleration, and consolidation, with an overall compound annual growth rate of 24\.5%\. Knowledge production concentrates in European institutions, led by Sweden, the UK, and Italy, anchored by Lund University\. Co‐citation \(four clusters\) and keyword co‐occurrence \(five clusters\) analyses reveal a field anchored in Osterwalder and Pigneur&\#x27;s business model canvas\. The regenerative perspective remains bibliometrically marginal, appearing as an author keyword in only 2 of 961 documents\. The study clarifies the boundaries separating these perspectives and proposes a structured research agenda, positioning regeneration as an emerging, not accomplished, frontier for sustainable business model innovation scholarship\.
