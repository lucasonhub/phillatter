# Mapping the Intellectual Capital Research Landscape: A Bibliometric Analysis of Trends, Contributions, and Future Research Directions

**Autores:** Rashida Farsath

**Data:** 2026-10-17 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Intellectual capital has become an important source of value creation and competitive advantage in knowledge\-intensive economies, where organizational performance increasingly depends on the effective use of knowledge\-based and other intangible resources\. This study maps the development and structure of intellectual capital research through a bibliometric analysis of publications indexed in the Scopus database\. The search strategy used combinations of keywords relating to intellectual capital, human capital, structural capital, intangible assets, and value added\. After the application of publication\-type, subject\-area, and language filters, the final dataset comprised 787 English\-language journal articles\. Publication trends, journal distribution, institutional and country contributions, influential documents, co\-citation patterns, keyword co\-occurrence, and international collaboration were examined using Excel, VOSviewer, R, and Biblioshiny\. The findings show a marked expansion of intellectual capital scholarship, particularly from 2015 onward, with annual scientific production reaching 84 articles in 2024\. The 787 articles were distributed across 306 journals, while the Journal of Intellectual Capital was the leading publication outlet in the dataset\. India recorded the highest number of publications among the countries listed, whereas Taiwan had the highest total citations in the country\-level citation comparison\. Highly cited foundational studies and co\-citation patterns point to the continuing influence of work on intellectual capital measurement, including the Value Added Intellectual Coefficient \(VAIC\) approach\. Keyword co\- occurrence revealed interconnected themes concerning intellectual capital and firm performance, human and structural capital, relational capital, knowledge management, innovation, intangible assets, disclosure, and value creation\. The collaboration map also indicates cross\-country research links, although the distribution of contributions varies across institutions and regions\. Overall, the study provides a structured overview of the intellectual capital research landscape and identifies opportunities to extend the field through stronger cross\-country collaboration, broader measurement approaches, and greater attention to contemporary issues such as artificial intelligence, digital transformation, financial distress, and the strategic management of intangible resources\. These directions may help researchers develop a more integrated understanding of how intellectual capital is measured, deployed, and associated with organizational outcomes\.
