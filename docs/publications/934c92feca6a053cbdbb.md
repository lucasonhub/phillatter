# Integrating Multimodal Technologies and Generative Artificial Intelligence into Professionally Oriented English Language Training of Future Officers of the Armed Forces of Ukraine: Theoretical Foundations

**Autores:** Ганна Жиздюк

**Data:** 2026-10-15 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

The article aims to substantiate the theoretical foundations for integrating multimodal technologies and generative artificial intelligence \(GenAI\) into cadets&\#x27; English language training oriented towards the descriptors of Standardized Language Proficiency Level 2 under the NATO standard STANAG 6001\. The study used terminological and comparative analysis of regulatory documents and research publications, systematisation, generalisation and pedagogical design\. The conceptual framework is refined: language activity and its types, language competence, standardized language level and profile, mode, multimodal technologies, GenAI, AI\-mediated learning, the mode of permissible GenAI use, and integration\. The standardized language profile is treated as a measure of general foreign language proficiency in the four types of language activity that does not cover all components of professional communicative competence\. The Law of Ukraine &quot;On Academic Integrity&quot; links the legitimacy of auxiliary tools to the rules of a task, so the GenAI mode stated in a task instruction has legal significance\. The study substantiates seven functions of multimodal technologies and GenAI with limits of assistance, eight integration principles and four modes of GenAI use, which differ in what GenAI does with the cadet&\#x27;s language material and can be combined\. Five pedagogical conditions are defined with organisational support and indicators of implementation\. A six\-step sequence of scenario\-based learning is proposed and illustrated with the task design &quot;Reporting an Equipment Problem before a Joint Training Session&quot;: a photo shows the equipment damage, the cadet explains the circumstances orally and in writing, GenAI only asks clarifying questions, and the cadet conducts the final conversation without AI or preparation in a new situation with different equipment, addressee and constraints; the control stages without AI are performed in class under the instructor&\#x27;s supervision\. The novelty lies in the theoretical and methodological specification of known approaches for future officers&\#x27; English language training; provisions borrowed from the DARE\-HS \(Descriptor Alignment, Authentic Performance, Responsible Use, Evaluation and Reflection, Human Supervision, Security\) framework and other studies are clearly distinguished from adapted and proposed ones\. Language instructors at higher military educational institutions can use the results to design tasks for the penultimate semester, formulate GenAI rules in course syllabi and develop professional development programmes\. The effectiveness of the proposed solutions requires empirical verification\.
