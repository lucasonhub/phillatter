# Tensor train compression of molecular orbitals

**Autores:** Ruslan A\. Mallaev; Dmitry I\. Morozov; Dmitry A\. Zheltkov; Michael Romanovich Perelshtein; Roman Ellerbrock

**Data:** 2026-10-13 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

This work systematically investigates the tensor train \(TT\) compressibility of grid\-represented Hartree\-Fock \(HF\) and Density Functional Theory \(DFT\) orbitals across a range of molecular systems and TT formats \(standard, quantized, and z\-permuted quantized\)\. To overcome limitations of current TT solvers, we employ a novel orthogonal tensor train cross approximation \(TT\-Cross\-Orth\) to decompose Hartree\-Fock and DFT orbitals into TTs, enabling the study of large molecules\. We find that the approximation error decreases exponentially with TT\-rank for all systems studied and identify quantized tensor train as the most efficient TT format\. Furthermore, we identify quasi\-periodic and symmetric structures\-including graphene, graphite layers, and polyalanine helices\-as particularly favorable candidates exhibiting sublinear rank growth with system size\. The tensor train approach automatically compresses quasi\-symmetries and systems with symmetry defects, which makes it an attractive approach for materials science applications\. TT\-Cross\-Orth enforces orthogonality during the decomposition, thereby improving stability and reducing the number of function evaluations compared to standard TT\-Cross\. These findings establish an empirical foundation for the development of scalable tensor network methods for HF and DFT\.
