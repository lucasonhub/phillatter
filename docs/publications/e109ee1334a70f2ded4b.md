## Descrição do feed

The Omnibus is the definitive foundational monograph establishing the Primary Logical Infrastructure \(PLI\)framework, a radical shift in the philosophy of physics that resolves cosmological and gravitational singularities through computational monism and ontic structural realism\. Rather than modifying General Relativity by introducing arbitrary smooth metrics, the PLI framework establishes that continuous spacetime and physical matter are macroscopic emergent phenomena generated by a deep, discrete, and atemporal substrate of relational logical nodes\. Key Structural Milestones of the Omnibus: Representational Saturation: The Einsteinian singularity is analytically disarmed and defined as a category mistake—a syntactic boundary where the continuous geometric representation reaches its maximum computational processing limit \(Cmax\), yielding to an ageometric, non\-extended 2D source data code\. The Quantitative Reset Formula: By applying Landauer&\#x27;s Principle to the information content of a collapsing system, the Omnibus derives the finite, exact thermodynamic dissipation energy required by the substrate to wipe active bits and return to the Primordial Dormant State: Qreset = 1/2 M c² ln 2\. Foundational Order of Reality: The monograph formalizes the insuperable ontological hierarchy of the universe: Logical Potential → Information → Computation → Time → Geometry → Physical Universe\. Empirical Anchor: Includes the exact numerical validation of the Qreset signature calculated against current multi\-frequency astrophysical data from real macroscopic candidates \(M87\* and Sagittarius A\*\), establishing the finite and non\-divergent energy budget of cosmic horizons\. This work purges all prior external field approximations, standing as a self\-contained, axiomatically closed, and testable paradigm for the foundations of quantum computation, thermodynamics, and cosmology\. \(direct link \)
