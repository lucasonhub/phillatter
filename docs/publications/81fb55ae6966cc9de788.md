# Estimating magnetic resonance imaging proton density fat fraction from ultrasound attenuation and clinical parameters in pediatric hepatic steatosis: development of a prediction model

**Autores:** Shin Hye Hwang; Seong\-Hun Jeong; Dong Kyu Kim; Seungsoo Lee; Soo Yoon Chung; Seok‐Jae Heo; Gyeonghyun Cho; Kyungchul Song; Byung Won Yoo; Eun Byoul Lee; Hyun Joo Shin

**Data:** 2026-10-14 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Purpose: This study evaluated the diagnostic performance of the ultrasound\-guided attenuation parameter \(UGAP\) for pediatric hepatic steatosis and developed a multivariable model to estimate magnetic resonance imaging proton density fat fraction \(MRI\-PDFF\) from ultrasound and clinical variables\.Methods: This single\-center retrospective study included pediatric patients who underwent UGAP measurement and MRI\-PDFF assessment within 3 months of each other\.UGAP performance in detecting moderate\-to\-severe \(≥S2\) and severe \(S3\) steatosis was evaluated using the area under the receiver operating characteristic curve \(AUC\)\.Multivariable models were developed using linear regression and a random forest algorithm with five\-fold cross\-validation\.Results: Among 45 patients \(33 male and 12 female; median age, 12 years\), median UGAP and MRI\-PDFF values were 0\.83 dB/cm/MHz and 22%, respectively\.UGAP correlated moderately with MRI\-PDFF \(ρ=0\.596,P&lt;0\.001\)\.The AUC was 0\.74 for both ≥S2 and S3 steatosis \(95% confidence intervals \[CIs\], 0\.58 to 0\.91 and 0\.60 to 0\.89, respectively\), and two discordant cases were identified\.A model incorporating UGAP, age, height, and liver size outperformed UGAP alone in detecting ≥S2 steatosis \(optimism\-corrected AUC, 0\.882 vs\. 0\.744; P=0\.01\)\.In the machine learning analysis, the model showed stable performance during cross\-validation, with an AUC of 0\.789 \(95% CI, 0\.649 to 0\.928\) for ≥S2 steatosis and an R 2 of 0\.663 for continuous MRI\-PDFF prediction\.Conclusion: UGAP correlated moderately with MRI\-PDFF and showed fair performance in grading pediatric hepatic steatosis\.Combining UGAP with clinical parameters significantly improved MRI\-PDFF estimation, providing a practical screening tool for hepatic steatosis when MRI is unavailable\.External validation is needed\.
