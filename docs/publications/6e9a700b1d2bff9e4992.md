# FOODTECH INNOVATIONS AND ESG IN THE TRANSFORMATION OF TRADITIONAL WINEMAKING INTO SMART WINE TOURISM DESTINATIONS

**Autores:** Олена Олегівна Ахмедова; Anna Chernykhivska

**Data:** 2026-10-12 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

The paper examines the roles of FoodTech innovation and ESG in transforming contemporary wine tourism systems from traditional winemaking models to smart, sustainable destinations\. Particular attention is given to the interaction between technological modernisation in viticulture and winemaking, sustainability\-oriented management practices and the creation of competitive wine tourism experiences\. The study examines how precision viticulture, digital terroir management, traceability platforms, artificial intelligence, craft winemaking innovations and indigenous grape varieties contribute to the development of modern wine tourism ecosystems\. The research substantiates that FoodTech extends beyond technological improvements in wine production and functions as an integrated mechanism that connects vineyard management, wine quality, destination branding, visitor engagement, and sustainability objectives\. Special emphasis is placed on the role of terroir\-based differentiation, alternative vinification methods and the growing importance of craft wineries in strengthening the authenticity and attractiveness of wine\-producing territories\. The study employs a comparative analytical approach and a KPI framework comprising five dimensions\. The comparative assessment covers France, Italy, Portugal, Spain and Ukraine\. The article proposes an integrated analytical framework for evaluating FoodTech\-driven transformation in wine tourism\. It confirms that the long\-term competitiveness of wine\-producing territories increasingly depends on the ability to combine technological innovation, sustainability principles, local identity and visitor\-centred value creation\.
