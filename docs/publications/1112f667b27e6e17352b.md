# TALABALARDAGI INTELLEKTUAL\-IJODIY QOBILIYATNING O‘QUV MOTIVLARI BILAN BOG‘LIQLIGI

**Autores:** Dilsora Nodirjon qizi Abduvaliyeva

**Data:** 2026-10-12 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

This article examines the psychological relationship between students’ intellectual and creative abilities and their academic motives\. The study investigates the intelligence types and learning motives of 743 students using Howard Gardner’s Multiple Intelligences Test and the modified version of A\. A\. Rean and V\. A\. Yakunin’s Student Learning Motivation Diagnostic Method, adapted by N\. S\. Badmaeva\. Correlation analysis identifies statistically significant relationships between certain types of intelligence and communicative, professional, cognitive, social, and creative self\-realization motives\. Particular attention is given to the relationships between avoidance motives and intrapersonal intelligence, professional motives and linguistic intelligence, and creative self\-realization motives and musical intelligence\. The findings highlight Ta’limda istiqbolli izlanishlar \- xalqaro ilmiy\-metodik jurnal № 9/2026 ISSN: 3060\-527X the importance of considering students’ individual intellectual characteristics and motivational orientations when developing their intellectual and creative potential\.
