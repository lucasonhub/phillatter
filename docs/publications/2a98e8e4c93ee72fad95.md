# DUE DILIGENCE NA ERA DIGITAL: : NOVOS MECANISMOS DE PREVENÇÃO E DETECÇÃO DO TRABALHO ESCRAVO

**Autores:** LUCIANA SERAFIM DA SILVA; Saul Duarte Tibaldi

**Data:** 2026-10-12 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

This article analyzes due diligence in the digital age as a tool for preventing and mitigating risks related to labor in conditions analogous to slavery within supply chains\. Using the deductive method, through an analysis of legislation, legal doctrine, case law, and recent studies on artificial intelligence, the article examines the evolution of due diligence as a mechanism for governance and risk management\. It is demonstrated that contemporary technologies, especially artificial intelligence, expand businesses’ capacity to monitor, identify, and address risks, enabling a transition from reactive models to continuous, predictive, and data\-driven processes\. It is concluded that such tools strengthen the principles of prevention and precaution, contributing to the protection of human rights and the workplace environment\.
