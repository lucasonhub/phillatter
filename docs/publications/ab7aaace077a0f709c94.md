# La sanità del futuro: intelligenza artificiale, robotica e nuove frontiere del diritto

**Autores:** Chiara Finocchiaro

**Data:** 2026-10-14 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

This paper examines the impact of digital transformation on healthcare systems, focusing on the use of artificial intelligence and robotics\. It explores the European and national legal frameworks, highlighting the role of the Electronic Health Record, the European Health Data Space, and the protection of fundamental rights\. Particular attention is devoted to the principle of human oversight in the deployment of AI technologies in healthcare\.
