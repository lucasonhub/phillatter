# Adaptive multi\-parameter fusion for sports outcome prediction: a context\-aware intelligent platform

**Autores:** Yanxiao Zhang; 尤双从

**Data:** 2026-10-14 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Abstract Sports outcome prediction is crucial for performance assessment and strategic decision\-making\. Traditional models \(Elo\-based, regression\-based\) rely on limited historical data and fail to capture dynamic interactions between players, teams, and contexts\. We introduce an adaptive multi\-parameter fusion \(AMPF\) framework using hierarchical attention\-based neural networks to integrate team ratings, performance trends, and contextual factors\. Unlike fixed\-weight models, AMPF employs learnable attention mechanisms for adaptive, context\-specific feature weighting\. Implemented in PyTorch, AMPF is validated on FiveThirtyEight datasets covering 45,000\+ football matches \(2010–2024\) and 20,000\+ basketball games \(2012–2024\)\. AMPF achieves statistically significant improvements over all baselines: 1\.6–2\.2% higher accuracy and 3\.8–5\.6% lower Brier scores compared to deep MLPs, with larger gains \(4\.7–4\.8% accuracy, 10\.9–11\.8% Brier reduction\) over classical Elo ratings\. Ablation studies confirm the necessity of contextual embeddings and adaptive fusion\. We deploy a platform providing real\-time predictions, interpretability visualizations, and provenance tracking, bridging traditional rating systems and modern deep learning for sports analytics\.
