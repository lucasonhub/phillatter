## Descrição do feed

\_Zenodo\_ 1\. 2026This paper investigates the emergence, transformation, efficacy, and expiry of ontological operations without presupposing ontology as a universal philosophical foundation or reducing it to technical classification\. It distinguishes effective constraints, ontological operations, and ontological attribution, examining the conditions under which distinctions concerning being, identity, and dependence acquire non\-redundant roles\. Engaging Aristotle, Kant, Hegel, Heidegger, Quine, Sellars, Brandom, Brassier, and Wolfendale, the inquiry examines philosophical question\-formation, inferential justification, nonconceptual causal organization, scientific operations, and institutional reclassification\. A central component is the analysis of differential temporalities, ranging from microsecond\-scale classification updates to long\-term institutional revision\. Counterfactual comparisons distinguish detection, execution, authorization, correction, and historical retention, demonstrating how the consequences of classifications depend on situated configurations rather than temporal speed alone\. The paper further develops a nuance\-operational model in which operational distinctions may emerge, overlap, transform, or cease to remain applicable\. It connects this analysis to Xenoscience and Non\-Closure Realism, distinguishing local scientific success, failure, and measurement limitations from claims concerning reality\-as\-a\-whole\. Ontological operations are consequently examined neither as necessary preconditions of scientific inquiry nor as merely obsolete philosophical constructions, but as situated formations whose inferential significance, real effects, cross\-domain penetration, and conditions of dispensability remain open to investigation\. \(direct link \)
