# Artificial intelligence\-driven reconfiguration of customer experience: A service\-dominant logic framework

**Autores:** Dong‐Young Kim

**Data:** 2026-10-17 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

This study develops a framework explaining how artificial intelligence \(AI\) reconfigures customer experience in hospitality and tourism, drawing on service\-dominant logic\. Co\-citation and content analyses were conducted to synthesize 89 articles and identify the intellectual structure of the literature\. The findings reveal that institutional arrangements condition AI&\#x27;s legitimate participation in service ecosystems\. Three mechanisms explain how AI reconfigures customer experience: the actor role architect, the interaction mediator, and the experience orchestrator\. These mechanisms operate concurrently and recursively rather than as discrete stages within AI\-enabled service ecosystems\. The study makes three contributions to the literature\. First, it positions customer experience reconfiguration as an ecosystem\-level process\. Second, it extends service\-dominant logic from dyadic to triadic human\-AI\-human value co\-creation\. Third, it differentiates AI by functional intelligence, showing that mechanical, thinking, feeling, and multi\-functional types perform distinct roles in this reconfiguration\.
