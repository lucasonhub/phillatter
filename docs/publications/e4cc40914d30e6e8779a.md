# Cyberterrorism – a Destabilizing Factor and Threat to National Security

**Autores:** I\. V\. Sviridov

**Data:** 2026-10-15 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

The article is devoted to a comprehensive analysis of cyberterrorism as a systemic destabilizing factor in international relations and a threat to national security amid geopolitical polarization\. The author examines the evolution of the concept of cyberterrorism, its main forms and targets of attack, as well as the transformation of motivation from ideological to geopolitical\. Special attention is paid to the international legal aspects of countering cyberterrorism\.
