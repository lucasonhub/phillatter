# Fabrication of Conformal FSS Radome Based on MOPSO inverse design and mask spraying

**Autores:** Jin\-Yang Li; Jun\-Long Liu; Hao\-Bin Su; Han Ye; Xuewei Ju; Bei\-Yuan Liang; Yongzhen Liu; Xiangfeng Wang

**Data:** 2026-10-12 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Conformal electromagnetic structures play an important role in modern aerospace and communication systems by combining spatial filtering capability with aerodynamic compatibility\. However, transferring planar frequency\-selective surfaces \(FSSs\) onto complex curved profiles can induce resonant\-frequency drift and geometric distortion, creating challenges for both electromagnetic co\-design and precision fabrication\. Here, we introduce a closed\-loop framework that seamlessly integrates artificial intelligence\-driven inverse design with an automated robotic spraying process\. By employing a multi\-objective particle swarm optimization \(MOPSO\) algorithm, we achieve the global optimal geometric matching of FSS elements within a multi\-dimensional parameter space, effectively resolving the inherent conflict between in\-band high transmission and out\-of\-band rejection\. The optimized designs are rapidly translated into physical prototypes using a high\-precision, non\-contact multi\-degree\-of\-freedom robotic spraying system\. Free\-space measurements demonstrate that our fabricated X\-band conformal radome exhibits exceptional angular stability and a transmittance exceeding 0\.9 up to a 26° scanning angle\. This fully automated “design\-to\-fabrication” paradigm paves a scalable and cost\-effective pathway for the next generation of multifunctional 3D meta \-devices and stealth radomes\.
