This paradigm resolves the long\-standing paradox that chaotic systems are sources of noise rather than high\-density information carriers\. Introduction: The Dynamic Nature of Information Classical information theory, rooted in Shannon’s work, defines channel capacity as a fixed parameter constrained by bandwidth or noise floor\. Yet in complex, evolving systems — such as weather patterns, financial markets, or neural networks — this static view fails\. This is not merely a refinement\. It is a redefinition — one that repositions information not as an output of a channel, but as a property of the system’s temporal evolution\.
