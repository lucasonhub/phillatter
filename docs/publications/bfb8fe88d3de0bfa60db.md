# Interactive effect of ESG performance on the earnings management, tax avoidance and financial performance relation: A bibliometric and empirical analysis

**Autores:** Hussein Mousa Ahmad Maabreh; Mara Ridhuan Che Abdul Rahman; Ainul Huda Jamil

**Data:** 2026-10-15 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

This paper provides a bibliometric and analytical assessment of the salient works examining earnings management, tax avoidance, financial performance, and environmental, social, and governance \(ESG\) adoption\. The research is based on data obtained from the Scopus database between 2016–2026; VOSviewer was used to analyze trends in research, as well as co\-occurrence of keywords, citation patterns, and collaborations among countries\. The results indicate that previous research is quite focused on the study of earnings management related to financial performance, where few studies link tax avoidance with ESG performance \(Friede et al\., 2015; Kolsi et al\., 2023\)\. The results also suggest that while ESG performance has received increased attention in recent years as a predictor of financial performance/future cash flows or opportunistic behavior, its link to traditional accounting research streams appears weak\. Although a substantial body of research has examined earnings management, tax avoidance, financial, and ESG performance, their integrated relationships remain underexplored\. This study addresses this gap by developing a unified framework that examines ESG performance as a moderator of the relationships among the other three variables\. The conclusions of this study can provide theoretical and practical support for academic circles, enterprises, investors, regulators, and other relevant stakeholders\.
