# OPERATIONAL AND GEOTECHNICAL DYNAMICS IN WATER NETWORK REPLACEMENT USING TRENCHLESS METHODS \(MND\): AN INTEGRATED ANALYSIS OF LIFE CYCLE AND SOCIO\-ENVIRONMENTAL IMPACTS

**Autores:** Sabrina Fernandes de Mello; Acrissom Luiz Godoi Santos; Alcione Ribeiro Mendes; Analice Caetano Baltazar Tófolo; Dariel Josias Piedade; Gabriela Hyssae Suga; Luana Rocha Carlos; Wesley Duarte Correia; Sheila Leal Oliveira Loureiro

**Data:** 2026-10-15 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

This article investigates the application of Non\-Destructive Methods \(NDM\), specifically Horizontal Directional Drilling and Pipe Bursting, in the replacement and installation of urban water supply networks\. Chronic water loss due to obsolete pipelines imposes a severe economic and environmental liability, demanding the urgent renewal of underground infrastructure\. However, the traditional Open Trench method proves to be technically and socially unfeasible in densely populated urban centers, driving the adoption of trenchless technologies\. This study is characterized as applied, exploratory, and documentary research, based on the integrated analysis of technical and operational guidelines adopted in the Metropolitan Region of Belo Horizonte and the socio\-environmental mitigation strategies applied in the municipality of Joinville\. The research details critical geomechanical variables, such as the rheology of drilling fluids, the safety radius between interferences, and the maximum tensile stress\. The sequential routing of the construction works is outlined from the installation and connection of branch lines to the hydrostatic testing and final paving\. The results confirm that asset replacement via NDM optimizes the project&\#x27;s economic life cycle, significantly reduces construction waste, and mitigates operational disruptions for the population, establishing itself as the most sustainable approach for contemporary sanitary engineering\.
