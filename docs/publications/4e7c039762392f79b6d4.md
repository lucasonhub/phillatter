# Lower peaks, similar growth: Isolating the value and limits of artificial\-intelligence\-driven thermal sensation modeling for building\-grid resilience under future climates

**Autores:** Hongshan Guo; Kanxuan He

**Data:** 2026-10-13 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Evaluations of artificial intelligence \(AI\) in building energy control often conflate supervisory policy and occupant prediction\. We present a control\-isolated EnergyPlus benchmark in which three predictor\-driven arms share the same supervisory logic: analytical predicted mean vote \(PMV\), gradient\-boosted trees implemented with the Light Gradient Boosting Machine \(LightGBM\), and a physics\-informed neural\-network variational autoencoder \(PINN–VAE\); a fixed\-setpoint controller serves as a separate operational reference\. Experiments cover five American Society of Heating, Refrigerating and Air\-Conditioning Engineers \(ASHRAE\) climate archetypes, three 2001–2100 horizons, and Representative Concentration Pathway 8\.5 \(RCP8\.5\)\. The grid metric is the 95th\-percentile coincident peak \(CP95\) of 15\-minute heating, ventilation, and air\-conditioning electric demand\. City/weather realization accounts for 90\.6% of fitted main\-effect R 2 for absolute CP95 and 90\.8% for warming\-driven amplification; strategy identity accounts for 5\.0% and 1\.0%\. Against the same\-city, same\-horizon fixed\-setpoint reference, LightGBM lowers CP95 by 18–22 kilowatts \(kW\) per 10,000 square metres \(m 2 \) on average\. This is not overall superiority: its PMV\-violation rate reaches 84%–94%, PINN–VAE reaches 68%–90%, and a separate annual EnergyPlus summary reaches 2,789 discomfort hours\. The pooled stress\-index result \( p = 0\.203 \) reflects fixed design terms and hence design\-induced degeneracy rather than conceptual invalidity; archetype and pathway screens alter absolute magnitudes\. This applied\-AI benchmark separates predictor value from its engineering application\. Model comparison must combine peak and tariff effects with comfort guardrails, while long\-term planning remains conditional on building stock and climate uncertainty\.
