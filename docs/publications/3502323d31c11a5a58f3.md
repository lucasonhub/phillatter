# Myshko, Andrii: Universality of the Prohibition of Indifference

**Autores:** 

**Data de descoberta:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** unknown

## Descrição do feed

This work takes as its sole initial givenness the fact that actual Reality exists and is given as process\. The investigation does not begin with a choice of what existed &quot;in the beginning\.&quot; The question of the initial state is replaced by the question of the minimal invariant necessary for the very possibility of Reality as process\. If the processuality of Reality is accepted as givenness, then absolute identity, absolute symmetry, and absolute synchrony are incompatible with it\. The Prohibition of Indifference is thus reconstructed: Z = ¬\(Id ∨ Sym ∨ Sync\)\. Logos and Monos are distinct domains, not sequential stages\. In Logos: A = A\. In Monos, absolute identity is prohibited: A ≠ A → A₁\. Logic does not transition into Reality\. The dynamic consequences — dissipation of identity, temporality, spatiality, and recursivity — are developed in the corpus\. Recursion occupies a special place: it arises in Monos as a prerequisite of process after the exhaustion of external degrees of freedom; the Observer does not generate it but realizes this prerequisite through the means of Logos\. The work has the status of an ontological hypothesis concerning the minimally sufficient invariant of Reality\. It is not a physical theory and does not derive concrete physical equations\.
