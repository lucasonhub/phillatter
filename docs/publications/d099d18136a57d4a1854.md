# Evaluation design and reporting in machine\-learning state\-of\-health estimation on the NASA lithium\-ion battery dataset — a systematic meta\-research review of open\-access studies

**Autores:** Jeng Batacandolo

**Data:** 2026-10-14 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Pre\-registered protocol \(v1\.2, 14 October 2026\) and supporting materials for a systematic meta\-research review of evaluation design and metric reporting in machine\-learning state\-of\-health estimation studies using the NASA PCoE lithium\-ion battery dataset\. Includes the protocol, the dedicated search export \(OpenAlex, 14 October 2026\), the screening log for 3,157 records, the pre\-registered random processing order of 398 candidates and the 25% verification sample, the blank extraction form and codebook, and the screening code\. Deposited before full\-text data extraction\. A parallel OSF registration was submitted on the same day; its archiving failed because of an OSF\-side file\-copy error and is being resolved\.
