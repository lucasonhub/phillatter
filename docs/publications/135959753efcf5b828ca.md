# Effectiveness of an Artificial Intelligence–Assisted HIV Educational Video for Improving HIV Health Literacy Among Indonesian Adolescents

**Autores:** Angga Wilandika; Perla Yualita; Nur Elsa Zahrotunnaqiyah

**Data:** 2026-10-12 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Adolescents in low\- and middle\-income countries continue to face substantial barriers in accessing effective HIV education, despite global efforts in prevention\. This study evaluated the effectiveness of a culturally adapted HIV educational video developed using artificial intelligence \(AI\)\-assisted video production tools\. The intervention assessed in this study was the educational video itself rather than direct interaction with a generative AI system\. A quasi\-experimental design with pre\- and posttests and a control group was used to evaluate the intervention among 60 high school students\. Participants were randomly assigned to either a group that received a 10\-min AI\-generated educational video or a control group that received no intervention\. The video was designed to improve HIV health literacy by addressing four domains: access to information, comprehension of core concepts, evaluation of risks, and application of knowledge in real\-life decisions\. Quantitative data were collected in May 2024 using a validated scale and analyzed with nonparametric tests and analysis of covariance\. Students who received the AI\-assisted educational video demonstrated significantly higher posttest HIV health literacy scores than the control group \(Mann–Whitney U = 206\.5, p &lt; \.001\)\. After adjustment for baseline scores, analysis of covariance confirmed a significant group effect \( F \(1,57\) = 21\.70, p &lt; \.001, partial η 2 = 0\.28\), indicating a large adjusted effect size\. The findings highlight the potential of generative AI as an innovative, scalable tool for adolescent health education, particularly in resource\-limited settings\. This approach may offer new opportunities for integrating digital strategies into school\-based health promotion programs\.
