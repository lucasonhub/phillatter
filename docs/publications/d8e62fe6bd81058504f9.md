# Evolução e Estrutura da Produção Científica em Inteligência Artificial com Participação de Instituições Africanas \(2018–2025\)

**Autores:** Arieta Domingas Bartolomeu Filipe; Geraldo Vernijo Deixa; Rosalino Subtil Chicote; Verônica Angélica Freitas de Paula

**Data:** 2026-10-11 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

This article characterizes the evolution, the social and conceptual structure, and the maturity stage of research on artificial intelligence \(AI\) in Africa\. A bibliometric analysis, with document selection reported according to PRISMA 2020, covered open\-access articles and reviews indexed in Web of Science and Scopus \(2018–2025\)\. After duplicate removal and the exclusion of 2026 records, the corpus comprises 621 documents, analyzed with the bibliometrix package in R\. Output grows by 102\.99% per year and 45\.7% of the documents were published in 2025; a logistic fit \(R² = 0\.999\) places the field before its inflection point, estimated at 2027\.5\. Bradford&\#x27;s Law describes the dispersion of sources only approximately, whereas Lotka&\#x27;s Law in its classical form is not validated \(β = 4\.87\)\. South Africa accounts for 43\.3% of the documents, and the excess of country\-document mentions indicates frequent international collaboration\. The conceptual structure splits into a technical\-clinical strand and an educational strand, with no motor themes\. The field is young, concentrated, and fragmented, and consolidating recurrent authors, balanced networks, and themes that link technical performance, governance, and implementation is a priority\.
