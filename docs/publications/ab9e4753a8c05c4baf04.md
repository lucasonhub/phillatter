# Transformer\-based approach for roadside air pollutant prediction using multi\-site smart lamppost data

**Autores:** Lubing Li; Kejun Du; Faizan Ahmad Kashmiri; Yinghui Wu; Hong Kam Lo

**Data:** 2026-10-12 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Roadside air quality significantly impacts public health and the urban ecosystem\. This study develops a Transformer\-based deep learning model to predict short\-term roadside NO 2 and PM 2\.5 concentrations using data from multi\-sensor smart lampposts at three tested sites in Hong Kong\. The model integrates traffic volume, speed, and meteorological factors through a self\-attention mechanism that captures complex temporal dependencies\. The smart lamppost data, collected directly at the roadside, provide high\-fidelity measurements without the need for emission estimation algorithms\. A year\-long dataset spanning hourly, weekly, and seasonal variations is used for training and validation, with single\-site, cross\-site, and mixed\-site testing strategies\. The Transformer is compared against statistical, neural network, and tree\-based machine learning baselines, achieving promising prediction accuracy\. SHAP and sensitivity analyses reveal that NO 2 is more sensitive to traffic factors while PM 2\.5 is more influenced by meteorological conditions\.
