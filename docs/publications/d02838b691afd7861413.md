# Artificial Intelligence Approaches for Insurance Claim Analytics in Synthetic Environments

**Autores:** Zeyuan Li; Qingdao Huang

**Data:** 2026-10-10 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Insurance claim analytics is often constrained by limited access to real\-world data, which restricts the evaluation of advanced artificial intelligence approaches in practical insurance settings\. Leveraging the SynthETIC framework, this study systematically evaluates classical actuarial models, neural\-network\-based methods, generative adversarial networks, and reinforcement learning for three core components of insurance claim processes: claim severity, incremental payments, and settlement delays\. GAN\-based data augmentation is implemented using task\-specific architectures, including a tabular GAN for claim severity, an LSTM\-based sequence GAN for incremental payments, and a conditional Wasserstein GAN with gradient penalty for settlement delays, while Proximal Policy Optimization provides a sequential decision\-learning framework for claim processes\. Experimental results show that the performance of data\-driven approaches is task\-dependent, with GAN\-based data augmentation improving some predictive measures but not consistently across all tasks and evaluation metrics\. PPO\-based reinforcement learning is more sensitive to task formulation and model design, reflecting the importance of action\-space specification and reward structures\. Additional experiments and mathematical analysis further examine the role of task\-specific constraints in sequential payment allocation and delay prediction, highlighting the importance of admissible actions, state representation, and reward design\. Overall, the findings illustrate how synthetic insurance environments can support the systematic evaluation of emerging AI paradigms and provide methodological insights into the task\-dependent roles of GAN\-based data augmentation and reinforcement learning in insurance claim analytics\.
