# Telitacicept for the treatment of Sjögren’s disease complicated by unilateral vocal fold immobility, severe hypergammaglobulinemia, and diabetes: a case report

**Autores:** Kedi Zheng; Zexuan Zhou; Guangzhou Du; Qisheng Lin; Jianqun Lin; Weijin Zhang; Shaoyu Zheng; Shijian Hu; Yukai Wang

**Data:** 2026-10-14 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Sjögren’s Disease \(SjD\) involves diverse extraglandular manifestations \(EGMs\)\. Severe hypergammaglobulinemia and suspected recurrent laryngeal nerve \(RLN\) \-related unilateral vocal fold immobility represent aggressive phenotypes\. Management of such presentations may be constrained by metabolic comorbidities such as diabetes, which limit the use of conventional high\-dose glucocorticoids\. Herein, we describe a 48\-year\-old female with SjD and Type 2 Diabetes Mellitus \(T2DM\) who presented with severe hypergammaglobulinemia \(IgG 38\.9 g/L\), acute hoarseness, and sicca symptoms\. Laryngostroboscopy demonstrated left\-sided unilateral vocal fold immobility, possibly suggestive of neurogenic paralysis\. Positron emission tomography\-computed tomography \(PET\-CT\) did not identify obvious masses or extrinsic compression along the visualized RLN course, reducing the likelihood of a compressive or malignant etiology\. The EULAR Sjögren’s Syndrome Disease Activity Index \(ESSDAI\) score was 26\. A combined therapeutic regimen comprising telitacicept \(160 mg/week\), methylprednisolone \(20 mg/day\) and hydroxychloroquine \(0\.2 g, twice daily\) was initiated, both to minimize glucocorticoid exposure and to target the patient’s profound B\-cell hyperactivity manifested by severe hypergammaglobulinemia\. Marked serological and symptomatic improvements were observed during treatment\. Within 15 weeks, the IgG level decreased to 14\.6 g/L, rheumatoid factor \(RF\) decreased from 759 IU/mL to 58\.3 IU/mL, and the ESSDAI score dropped to 6\. The patient experienced symptomatic improvement of hoarseness, while methylprednisolone was successfully tapered to 4 mg/day\. Clinical stability based on patient\-reported symptoms persisted through the 12\-month follow\-up\. This case is hypothesis\-generating: A telitacicept\-containing regimen may produce marked serological improvement and symptomatic benefit for high\-activity SjD with laryngeal/vocal fold involvement, while reducing glucocorticoid\-related metabolic risks\. Definitive efficacy requires confirmation in larger controlled studies\.
