# Automotive Engineering in the Age of AI and Software\-Defined Vehicles

**Autores:** Milan Bjelica; Bogdan Pavković; Ivan Kaštelan

**Data:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Automotive engineering is undergoing a transition in which software, connectivity and artificialintelligence increasingly determine vehicle functionality throughout the complete product lifecycle\. The softwaredefinedvehicle extends development beyond the traditional point of production, as functions, operationalknowledge and customer\-facing features can continue to evolve after deployment through over\-the\-air updates\.Artificial intelligence adds another dimension to this development\. AI is becoming relevant not only for perception inadvanced driver assistance and automated driving, but increasingly for decision making and more integrated endto\-end approaches\. These developments create significant opportunities for vehicle functionality and customerexperience, while at the same time increasing the importance of systematic verification, safety engineering andlifecycle management\. This paper provides an editorial perspective on this changing automotive engineeringlandscape\. Particular attention is given to AI safety, verification pipelines, continuous engineering, software\-definedvehicle architectures, over\-the\-air updates and the emerging use of end\-to\-end AI\. The central observation is thatthese topics are closely connected\. Vehicles are becoming continuously evolving cyber\-physical products, whilesafety arguments have traditionally been developed around controlled configurations and well\-defined engineeringbaselines\. Future automotive engineering therefore requires development, verification, monitoring and updateprocesses that can preserve engineering evidence across repeated product changes\. AI further extends thisrequirement because its behaviour depends on data, operational conditions and statistical performance in additionto conventional software correctness\.
