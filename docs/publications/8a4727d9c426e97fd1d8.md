# Artificial Intelligence in Construction Management Enhancing Decision Making A cross a Project Life Cycle: throught A Machine Learning Method

**Autores:** Nedjwa Elafri; Abdallah Lalmi; Badr\-Eddine BOUMALI; Koudoua Ferhati

**Data:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

The construction industry, which creates 13% of the world’s GDP, suffers from cost overruns of 28% and delays of over 42%, yet is still undigitized\. AI has the promise of transforming management in the areas of cost, time and safety\. This research provides a systematic review of AI applications and performs an original machine learning analysis on seven algorithms, i\.e\., Support Vector Machines, Logistic Regression, K\-Nearest Neighbors, Random Forest, Stacking Ensemble, Artificial Neural Networks, and XGBoost on 139 Indian road projects with 44 risk factors\. XGBoost performed the best with 82\.50% accuracy and 84\.21% F1\-score\. The most important concerns were the delay in decision\-making \(0\.152\), cash flow problems \(0\.082\) and cost underestimate \(0\.071\)\. Case studies demonstrate AI’s capabilities: 98\.7% accurate cost estimation, 94\.36% delay prediction via deep learning, and 97% safety monitoring with computer vision\. These findings underscore the importance of ensemble ML in proactive, data\-driven risk management, bringing projects into a predictive framework rather than reactive\.
