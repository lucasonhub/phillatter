# Lee, Shun\-Ching: Quantum Informational Ethics: Interfacing Phase Synchronization with Game\-Theoretic Eudaimonia and Conatus Hierarchies / 量子資訊倫理學：相位同步化、博弈論幸福觀與欲求階層性的對接

**Autores:** 

**Data de descoberta:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** unknown

## Descrição do feed

This paper expands Quantum Informational Ethics into the social dimension by addressing the &quot;Survival First&quot; critique through Spinozist Conatus hierarchies \(Lee, 2026; Paper I\_002\)\. It categorizes human drives into &quot;Essential Survival&quot; \(thermodynamic noise reduction via basic subsistence\) and &quot;Mental Tranquility&quot; \(active quantum noise filtering\)\. By integrating the Kuramoto model with phased array antenna mechanics \($n^2$ field scaling; Paper I\_012\), this work provides the missing closed\-loop operating system for game\-theoretic axioms of Eudaimonia, showing that collective trust and love represent global minimum\-energy equilibria amidst cosmic limit tests\. 本論文透過史賓諾沙「欲求（Conatus）」階層性（Lee, 2026; 論文 I\_002）回應「生存優先」的詰問，將量子資訊倫理學擴展至社會維度。文章將人類驅動力劃分為「安身立命」（透過基礎生存進行熱力學減噪）與「身心安頓」（主動量子雜訊過濾）。透過將庫拉莫托模型與相控陣列天線力學（$n^2$ 場強增益；論文 I\_012）結合，本研究為博弈論幸福公理提供了那套缺失的閉環作業系統，證明了在宇宙極限測試中，集體的信任與愛代表了全局的最穩定低能耗狀態。 \(direct link \)
