# SIGMAR\-3D: scale\-invariant guaranteed monotone accurate registration for 3D scenes

**Autores:** Bohdan Vodianyk; Enrique Nava Baro; Anton Oleksandrovych Popov

**Data:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Abstract SIGMAR\-3D is a training\-free framework for robust pairwise 3D point\-cloud registration\. It combines a handcrafted local descriptor, SIGMA, with a scene\-adaptive coarse\-to\-fine registration stage, R\-3D\. SIGMA builds a repeatable local reference frame at each keypoint and aggregates spatial\-angular geometric cues within a spherical support, producing a compact rotation\-normalized descriptor without any learning\. R\-3D forms tentative correspondences by descriptor matching, estimates a coarse pose using robust hypothesis verification, and refines the alignment on a voxel pyramid with generalized ICP and robust losses\. Key support radii, matching gates, and refinement thresholds are tied to local spacing statistics and overlap\-related heuristic signals, enabling a single configuration to transfer across indoor RGB\-D and outdoor LiDAR data\. Monotone decrease of the trimmed\-RMSE refinement objective is enforced through an acceptance rule that accepts an update only when that objective decreases\. On standard benchmarks, SIGMAR\-3D achieves 84\.3% FMR and 79\.0% registration recall on 3DMatch, and 72\.7% FMR on ETH\. These results position SIGMAR\-3D as a practical and competitive training\-free alternative when retraining, domain\-specific supervision, or model maintenance is undesirable\.
