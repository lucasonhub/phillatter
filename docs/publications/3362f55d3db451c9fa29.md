# Big data y analítica en la toma de decisiones turísticas: estado del arte

**Autores:** Paulina Alejandra Toasa Cobo

**Data:** 2026-10-12 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Objective: Analyze the state of the art on the use of big data and analytics in tourism decision\-making, with an emphasis on documented applications, data governance, and opportunities for Ecuador\. Methodology: A structured review of the literature published between 2022, and June 2026 was conducted\. Eleven academic documents were selected, and two official statistical sources were consulted, corresponding to UN Tourism and the National Institute of Statistics and Censuses\. The academic documents were coded according to their analytical domains, data sources, and aspects of privacy and governance; the official statistics were analyzed descriptively to contextualize the sector\. Results: The examined literature focused on data sources and architecture \(7 out of 11 documents\) and on spatial and mobility analytics \(4 out of 11\)\. Privacy and governance appeared in four documents, while two addressed descriptive analytics and two focused on forecasting\. No prescriptive applications documented in the corpus were identified\. Likewise, official data showed that international tourism reached 1\.4 billion arrivals in 2024, while the entries of foreigners to Ecuador decreased by 18\.5% compared to 2023\. Conclusions: The review allowed for the identification of opportunities to develop tourism forecasting and segmentation systems in Ecuador, as well as the need to improve the availability of disaggregated data and strengthen governance\. A conceptual model was proposed that articulated data, analytics, decisions, and data protection, and the future line was the empirical evaluation of prescriptive applications with human supervision\.
