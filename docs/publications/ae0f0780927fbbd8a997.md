# Responsible Generative AI Governance in Bangladeshi Higher Education: A Risk\-Tiered Framework for Assessment Validity, Equity and Accountability

**Autores:** Sumaia Yasmin Keya; Md\. Shahed Alamm; Niger Sultana; Md\. Aminul Islam; Ummey Sumaiya; Md\. Baharul Hasan

**Data:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Purpose: Generative artificial intelligence \(GenAI\) has entered university teaching in Bangladesh faster than institutional rules\. This paper asks which risks require institutional control when GenAI is used in teaching and assessment, and which governance design can allow proportionate use while protecting learning, integrity, privacy, fairness and human accountability\.Design/methodology/approach: The study is a structured integrative review and normative policy analysis of 61 sources: peer\-reviewed research on GenAI in higher education, academic integrity, assessment design and AI literacy; Bangladeshi empirical studies; and international and national policy instruments, including Bangladesh’s draft National AI Policy 2026–2030\. Sources were coded against five governance domains and interpreted through three lenses: assessment validity, rights\-based responsible AI and virtue ethics\.Findings: Prohibition is hard to enforce and can widen inequity, while unrestricted adoption shifts educational judgment to opaque commercial systems\. Bangladeshi evidence shows near\-universal reliance on free tool versions, weak institutional guidance, unreliable Bangla\-language output and uneven device and connectivity access\.Originality/value: The paper proposes a five\-domain \(access, assessment, data, authorship, accountability\), three\-tier \(prohibited, supervised, permitted assisted\) framework and a five\-step assessment assurance cycle that treats assessment validity, rather than detection, as the central object of governance\.Implications: The framework gives universities and the University Grants Commission a low\-cost route from broad AI principles to course\-level permissions, process evidence, oral verification and appeal\. It is a conceptual proposal that requires empirical piloting\.
