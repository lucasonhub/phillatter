# Deep Learning\-Based Calligraphic Aesthetic Assessment Guided by Gestalt Psychological Features

**Autores:** Hongtao Yan

**Data:** 2026-10-13 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Calligraphic aesthetic assessment requires the information about local stroke appearance and the spatial organization of the complete character\. This paper develops a supervised regression method that combines twelve Gestalt\-inspired geometric descriptors with a ResNet50 image encoder\. The descriptors quantify the visual balance, ink distribution, reflected structure, projection rhythm, and distance\-transform smoothness\. Two feature\-wise linear modulation \(FiLM\) modules convert these measurements into channel\-wise scales and shifts for intermediate convolutional features\. A direct\-concatenation control supplies the same descriptors to the regression head\. This design allows explicit geometric information to interact with the learned visual representations during score prediction\. In the ResNet50 experiment, intermediate FiLM achieves PCC 0\.7067 and MAE 1\.3554, compared with 0\.6958 and 1\.3793 for image\-only regression\. In whole\-work assessment, combining local window\-score statistics with global layout descriptors achieves PCC 0\.6323\. These results support descriptor\-guided regression at the character level and the combination of local appearance with global composition for whole\-work assessment\.
