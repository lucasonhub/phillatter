# Time\-ordered co\-expression network analysis reveals b\-locus\-mediated transcriptional cascades underlying sugarcane resistance to smut fungus \(Sporisorium scitamineum\)

**Autores:** Xiu\-Peng Song; Xiao\-Qiu Zhang; Bing\-Qiang Lu; Dong\-Mei Huang; Yong\-Jian Liang; Bao\-Qing Zhang; Yuchi Deng; Dong\-Mei Li; Yang\-Rui Li; Qiang Liang; Krishan Kumar VERMA; Meixin Yan

**Data:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Sugarcane smut, caused by the biotrophic fungus Sporisorium scitamineum , causes serious yield and sugar losses\. Growing resistant varieties is the main control method\. The fungal b mating\-type locus \( b \-locus\) is required for filamentous growth and virulence, but how it shapes the host response is not well understood\. Here, we compared a resistant cultivar \(GT29\) and a susceptible cultivar \(GT11\-80\) after inoculation with a wild\-type \(WT\) strain or a b \-locus deletion mutant, with sampling from 0 to 7 days postinoculation \(dpi\)\. We measured bE gene copy number by quantitative PCR \(qPCR\), assayed defense enzymes and phytohormones, and performed RNA sequencing followed by time\-ordered gene co\-expression network \(TO\-GCN\) analysis\. The WT strain proliferated rapidly in GT11\-80 but was strongly restricted in GT29, and the b \-locus mutant reached much lower levels than the WT strain in GT11\-80 by 7 dpi\. GT29 showed a stronger and more sustained oxidative burst \(O 2 − and H 2 O 2 \) than GT11\-80 and maintained higher antioxidant enzyme activities\. For hormones, WT infection induced more jasmonic acid \(JA\) and 1\-aminocyclopropane\-1\-carboxylic acid \(ACC; the ethylene precursor\) than the mutant, with the highest levels in GT29 at 5 dpi, whereas salicylic acid \(SA\) accumulated late \(7 dpi\) in GT11\-80; abscisic acid \(ABA\) stayed high under WT infection but dropped under the mutant\. TO\-GCN analysis identified a WT\-specific, time\-ordered transcription factor \(TF\) cascade in GT11\-80 that was lost under mutant infection, whereas the GT29 network kept its temporal order under both conditions\. In the jasmonic acid/ethylene \(JA/ET\) subnetwork, ERF1 was a central hub in GT11\-80, while in GT29 ERF1 was much less connected and EIN3 was the main hub\. Overall, the b \-locus is required for the time\-ordered transcriptional response during infection, and the resistant and susceptible cultivars differ in their hormone dynamics and JA/ET network structure\. These findings provide candidate genes and network features for breeding smut\-resistant sugarcane\.
