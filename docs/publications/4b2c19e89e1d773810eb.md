# THE IMPACT OF ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE\-ASSISTED LEARNING ON EFL LEARNERS&\#x27; SPEAKING PROFICIENCY: EVIDENCE FROM UZBEK UNIVERSITY STUDENTS

**Autores:** Asadbek Xabibullayev; Diyora Toirxo&\#x27;jayeva

**Data:** 2026-10-13 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Abstract\. The rapid development of artificial intelligence \(AI\) has created new opportunities for improving English as a Foreign Language \(EFL\) learning, particularly in the development of speaking proficiency\. AI\-powered chatbots, speech recognition systems, conversational agents, and generative AI applications enable learners to practise English, receive immediate feedback, and engage in interactive communication beyond the traditional classroom\. This article examines the potential impact of AI\-assisted learning on EFL learners’ speaking proficiency, with particular attention to the educational context of Uzbek universities\. It explores the ways in which AI\-based tools may support speaking fluency, pronunciation, grammatical accuracy, vocabulary development, learner confidence, and willingness to communicate\. Drawing on existing research and pedagogical perspectives, the article discusses the opportunities and limitations of integrating AI into higher education\. Particular attention is given to speaking anxiety, unequal access to digital resources, feedback reliability, learner autonomy, and the importance of teacher guidance\. The analysis suggests that AI can serve as a valuable supplementary resource when integrated into structured pedagogical activities\. However, its effectiveness depends on learners’ engagement, instructional design, and the combination of technological practice with authentic human interaction\. Further empirical research involving Uzbek university students is necessary to establish the extent and long\-term effects of AI\-assisted speaking instruction\. Keywords: artificial intelligence, EFL learners, speaking proficiency, AI\-assisted learning, university students, conversational AI, language anxiety, Uzbekistan, digital education\.
