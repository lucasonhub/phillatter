# A primer on heart rate variability: use in neuroscience and psychiatry

**Autores:** Courtney C\. Louis; Antonia V\. Seligowski

**Data:** 2026-10-15 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Heart rate variability \(HRV\) is widely recognized as a key mechanism that contributes to several health outcomes\. We provide a broad overview of HRV, its clinical and translational relevance, and important factors to consider when measuring HRV in research endeavors\.
