## Resumo

This thesis explores the legitimacy of political representation in deliberative democracies\.More specifically, I investigate \(i\) how a system of proportional representation should be designed in a deliberative democracy, and \(ii\) what conditions should be met for deliberations among representatives to be democratically legitimate\.Chapters 2, 3, and 4 deal with the first sub\-question: how a system of proportional representation should be designed in a deliberative democracy\.Chapter 5 addresses the second sub\-question: what conditions should be met for deliberations among decisions of the committee are as close as possible to deliberations and decisions in the ideal direct democracy where all are able to participate\.This is an individual\-based justification for
