# Spatiotemporal differentiation and determinants of abandoned farmland utilization in the Yangtze River Economic Belt

**Autores:** Zhe Zhu; Shiwen Chen; Leyi Shao; Ping Li; Heran Zou; Yilong Sheng

**Data:** 2026-10-15 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

As a stabilizer safeguarding China’s food security, the Yangtze River Economic Belt is troubled by farmland abandonment and inefficient utilization of abandoned farmland\. Therefore, clarifying the spatiotemporal differentiation and determinants of abandoned farmland utilization is critical to addressing challenges in cultivated land protection\. Based on the China Land Cover Dataset \(CLCD\), this study adopts land\-use trajectory analysis, kernel density analysis, and spatial autocorrelation analysis to explore core issues of abandoned farmland utilization from three dimensions: scale, efficiency and stability\. The results show that: \(1\) Since 2022, abandoned farmland utilization policies have raised the annual utilization rate by an average of 2\.5 percentage points\. Historical abandoned land stocks are absorbed evenly each year, shifting governance from clearing accumulated abandoned land arrears to regularly controlling newly abandoned farmland\. \(2\) Abandoned farmland utilization shows a striking west\-high and east\-low spatial agglomeration gradient: HH clusters distribute in the upper reaches and LL clusters in the lower reaches, with the upper and middle reaches achieving a 10\-percentage\-point 4\-year growth in utilization rates\. Utilization stability follows the middle reaches \(91\.12%\), upper reaches \(90\.28%\), and lower reaches \(85\.36%\)\. \(3\) Natural geography, economic levels, labor composition and institutional policies cause these disparities\. This research provides theoretical and practical support for differentiated regional governance of abandoned farmland and consolidated national food security\.
