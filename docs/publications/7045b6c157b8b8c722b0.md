# DIGITAL TRANSFORMATION AND SMART GOVERNANCE FRAMEWORKS

**Autores:** Dr\. V\.P\. Amuthanayaki

**Data:** 2026-10-09 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Abstract Digital transformation in the public sector increasingly requires frameworks that connect technological infrastructure, institutional readiness, and citizen\-centric design rather than examining these dimensions in isolation\. This article develops an integrated smart governance research framework linking digital literacy, data interoperability, regulatory agility, institutional readiness, and citizen engagement to smart governance effectiveness\. The framework is designed for empirical survey research and combines descriptive statistics, reliability analysis, exploratory factor analysis, correlation, multiple regression, mediation\-oriented reasoning, partial least squares structural equation modelling and machine\-learning validation\. A dataset of 250 responses is used solely to demonstrate the structure and presentation of statistical analysis; it is not claimed to represent field observations\. The results show acceptable internal consistency across constructs, positive inter\-construct associations, and statistically significant structural paths\. The framework contributes a reproducible research architecture for public administration studies in which theoretical explanation and predictive accuracy are evaluated together\. Keywords: digital transformation; smart governance; digital literacy; institutional readiness; citizen engagement; PLS\-SEM; regression; machine learning; predictive analytics; public administration\.
