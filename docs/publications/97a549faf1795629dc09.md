# Hybrid co\-simulation and real\-time validation techniques for power electronics\-dominated grids

**Autores:** Seonri Hong; Hyejin Kim; Jonghoon Kim; Jongbok Baek

**Data:** 2026-10-12 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

The rapid integration of inverter\-based resources is transforming power systems into power electronics\-dominated grids, where dynamic phenomena cannot be fully captured by conventional phasor\-domain analysis\. This paper reviews hybrid co\-simulation and real\-time validation techniques for high\-IBR\-penetration grids, covering electromagnetic transient modeling, multi\-rate EMT\-RMS coupling, network equivalencing, and hardware acceleration\. GPU, FPGA, and heterogeneous real\-time platforms are compared in terms of model fidelity, latency, scalability, and implementation burden\. Standardized co\-simulation interfaces including HELICS and FMI are discussed as mechanisms for integrating electrical, communication, and control domains\. Hardware\-in\-the\-loop methods are positioned as staged validation tools that complement software simulation by incorporating actual controllers, power hardware, interface delays, and measurement nonidealities\. This review also addresses weak\-grid stability, grid\-forming converter validation, multi\-vendor model interoperability, and requirements from IEEE 2800−2022, NERC EMT guidelines, IEC standards, and CIGRE technical guidance\. By organizing these topics into a unified validation workflow, this paper identifies open challenges in scalable EMT simulation, model portability, cyber\-physical validation, and digital\-twin\-based model refinement\.
