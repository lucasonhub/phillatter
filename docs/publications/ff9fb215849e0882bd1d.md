# IMPLEMENTASI PENDEKATAN DEEP LEARNING PADA PEMBELAJARAN PENDIDIKAN AGAMA ISLAM DALAM MENUMBUHKEMBANGKAN KREATIVITAS DAN KEMAMPUAN BERPIKIR KRITIS MURID DI SMA NEGERI 1 PACET KABUPATEN MOJOKERTO

**Autores:** Farel Alhafiz; Rahmat

**Data:** 2026-10-14 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Islamic Religious Education \(PAI\) in the era of the Merdeka Curriculum is required not only to focus on the mastery of textual material but also to be able to develop students&\#x27; critical thinking skills and creativity through meaningful, conscious, and enjoyable learning\. The deep learning approach emerges as one of the learning innovations that emphasizes deep understanding, the connection of concepts to real life, and the active involvement of students in the learning process\. However, the implementation of this approach in PAI learning still faces various challenges, both in terms of teacher readiness, learning time, and supporting facilities\. This research aims to: \(1\) analyze the implementation of the deep learning approach in PAI learning at SMA Negeri 1 Pacet, Mojokerto Regency; \(2\) analyze the implications of the deep learning approach in developing students&\#x27; creativity and critical thinking skills; and \(3\) analyze the supporting and inhibiting factors in the implementation of the deep learning approach in PAI learning\. This research uses a qualitative approach with a case study type of research\. Data collection techniques were carried out through observation, in\-depth interviews, and documentation\. The research subjects include the principal, PAI teachers, and students of SMA Negeri 1 Pacet, Mojokerto Regency\. Data analysis used the Miles and Huberman model, which includes data reduction, data presentation, and conclusion drawing, while data validity was ensured through source, technique, and time triangulation\. The research results show that the implementation of the deep learning approach in PAI learning is carried out through the stages of planning, implementation, and evaluation of learning, integrating the principles of meaningful learning, mindful learning, and joyful learning\. Teachers apply discussion methods, question and answer sessions, role\-playing, project\-based learning, and case studies to encourage active student engagement\. This approach has a positive impact on the improvement of students&\#x27; creativity and critical thinking skills, as evidenced by their ability to produce original works, relate the material to everyday life, and analyze and solve problems critically\. Supporting factors for implementation include school support, teacher collaboration, training, and student motivation, while hindering factors include limited learning time and the need for adaptation by teachers and students\.
