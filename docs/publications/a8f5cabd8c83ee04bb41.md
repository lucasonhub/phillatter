# Local Coordination Asymmetry Engineering Activates Oxide Path Mechanism in Dopant‐Free RuO 2 for Ultra‐Durable Acidic Water Oxidation

**Autores:** Ke Wang; Jindou Shi; Fulai Qi; Wen‐Gang Cui; Xinqiang Wang; Guoqiang Zhao; Fan Gao; Dingsheng S\. Wang; Hongge Pan

**Data:** 2026-10-14 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

ABSTRACT Developing ruthenium‐based anode electrocatalysts to replace commercial iridium is crucial for reducing the cost of proton exchange membrane \(PEM\) water electrolysis\. However, conventional RuO 2 suffers from limited long‐term stability and severe overoxidation drawbacks\. Herein, we employ dynamic H 2 bubble template \(DHBT\)‐assisted electrodeposition combined with calcination to fabricate disorderly connected regular/irregular Ru‐O 6 octahedra\. In situ experiments and theoretical calculations reveal that this local coordination asymmetry favors an oxide path mechanism \(OPM\) involving direct O‐O coupling while suppressing \*OOH formation and substantial lattice‐oxygen participation\. The optimized d‐RuO 2‐x catalyst requires an overpotential of only 154 mV to reach 10 mA cm −2 and maintains stable operation for over 3200 h at this current density\. Crucially, in a PEM water electrolyzer, the catalyst operates robustly for over 2100 h at 200 mA cm −2 under continuous and start‐shutdown conditions\. These results demonstrate that local coordination asymmetry engineering can tune OER pathway selection in dopant‐free RuO 2 ‐based catalysts and provide a promising route toward durable acidic OER anodes\.
