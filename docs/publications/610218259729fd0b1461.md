# HOUSEHOLD EXPENDITURE STRUCTURES BY MAIN PROVIDER SEX: EVIDENCE FROM MEXICO

**Autores:** Nancy Ivonne Muller Durán; Martha Gabriela Alatriste Contreras

**Data:** 2026-10-14 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

This article examines whether Mexican household expenditure structures differ by the sex of the main provider and whether these differences identify potential channels of exposure to inflationary shocks\. Drawing on the cost\-of\-living approach, we combine ENIGH 2024 data with Mexican Consumer Price Index expenditure categories to analyze observed spending patterns through non\-parametric statistics and predictive machine learning algorithms\. The results show highly skewed expenditure structures\. The Beta distribution provides the best fit for the full sample and both household groups, suggesting bounded and asymmetric behavior\. Food and transport remain central across groups, while personal care carries greater weight among households where a woman is the main provider\. Overall, the evidence points to heterogeneous expenditure structures that may shape how common price changes affect household budgets, without estimating realized inflation exposure or implying a separate price index by sex\.
