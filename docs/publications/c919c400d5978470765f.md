# BOLALARDA KOGNITIV RIVOJLANISHNING KLINIK\-PSIXOLOGIK DETERMINANTLARI: DIAGNOSTIK MEZONLAR VA O‘ZARO BOG‘LIQLIKLAR TAHLILI

**Autores:** Ostanaqulov Alijon Dadajon o&\#x27;g&\#x27;li

**Data:** 2026-10-14 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

This article examines the clinical\-psychological determinants of cognitive development in children aged 8–11, focusing on diagnostic criteria and the relationships between cognitive functioning and psycho\-emotional well\-being\. The main components of cognitive development, including voluntary attention, working memory, executive functions, inhibitory control, and non\-verbal reasoning, are considered from neuropsychological and developmental perspectives\. The diagnostic framework described in the research material includes Raven’s Coloured Progressive Matrices, the Bourdon Cancellation Test, the Digit Span subtest of the Wechsler Intelligence Scale for Children, the Stroop Color–Word Interference Test, and Goodman’s Strengths and Difficulties Questionnaire \(SDQ\)\. The empirical data presented in the source material are used to examine associations among cognitive performance, emotional symptoms, hyperactivity, and inattention\. The reported findings suggest that emotional distress and difficulties in attentional regulation may be associated with working\-memory performance and cognitive control\. The article emphasizes that learning difficulties should not be interpreted solely in terms of intellectual ability\. A comprehensive clinical\-psychological assessment should also consider emotional, behavioral, and social factors\. Practical recommendations are proposed for the integrated assessment of cognitive development, early identification of potential risk factors, and the development of individualized psycho\-corrective interventions\. The reported statistical results should be interpreted in accordance with the quality and verifiability of the underlying empirical data\.
