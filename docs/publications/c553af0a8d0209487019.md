# Limitations of Probabilistic Governance and Design of Deterministic AI Control Protocols: Analysis of Mathematical Models via Reproducible Code and Proposal of a Governance Architecture

**Autores:** Hideo Kajino

**Data:** 2026-10-11 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Abstract This paper critiques the structural limitations of the &quot;probabilistic and statistical model\-dependent approach,&quot; which currently dominates modern data\-driven societies and artificial intelligence \(AI\) governance, and proposes an alternative mathematical model: the deterministic control protocol \(&quot;Casino Protocol&quot;\) and &quot;Governance AI&quot; \(AI Constitutionalism\)\. Rather than completely rejecting probabilistic forecasting, this proposal adopts a decoupled architecture separating probabilistic predictive/suggestive functions from explicit, constraint\-based deterministic decision\-making\. By introducing a non\-linear penalty function \(y = x^2 feedback\) corresponding to the Democratic Renewal Gap \(E\_D\), we derive the analytical break\-even point of the mathematical model \(E\_D^\* \\approx 3\.8616 years\) and organize its numerical behavior through discrete simulation\. Furthermore, we clarify that this model does not unconditionally guarantee system\-wide safety, systematically categorizing the scope of responsibilities and limitations across the predictive, normative, execution, and audit layers\.
