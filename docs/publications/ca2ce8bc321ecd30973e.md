# MTDANet: lightweight multi\-scale temporal hierarchy and distance\-regularized linear attention network for motor imagery brain\-computer interfaces

**Autores:** Binbin Chen; Chen Deng; Huanmin Ge; Xinhua Su; Yanfei Shen

**Data:** 2026-10-12 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

OBJECTIVE: Motor\-imagery \(MI\) electroencephalography \(EEG\) offers a non\-invasive route to brain\-computer interaction, yet reliable single\-trial decoding remains difficult because task\-related patterns are weak, nonstationary, and distributed over multiple temporal scales\. This study investigates whether a staged local\-to\-global model can integrate fine temporal\-spatial structure with longer\-range context without relying on unrestricted self\-attention\. APPROACH: We propose MTDANet, an architecture that combines multi\-scale temporal\-spatial feature extraction, factorized temporal consolidation, and distance\-regularized recurrent attention\. The model was evaluated on three public MI datasets under subject\-specific and leave\-one\-subject\-out \(LOSO\) protocols\. Separately initialized models were also trained on P300 and steady\-state visual evoked potential \(SSVEP\) datasets to examine broader architectural applicability\. Participant\-level paired analyses used two\-sided paired t tests with within\-setting Holm correction\. MAIN RESULTS: MTDANet achieved subject\-specific accuracies of 85\.61 ± 8\.32%, 90\.85 ± 7\.62%, and 87\.79 ± 13\.93% on BCI Competition IV 2a, BCI Competition IV 2b, and BNCI2014\_002, respectively\. Relative to the highest\-mean baseline, the paired improvements were supported after within\-setting Holm correction on BCI Competition IV 2a \(3\.56 percentage points, adjusted p = 0\.0097\) and 2b \(1\.62 points, adjusted p = 0\.0168\)\. Under LOSO evaluation, the corresponding accuracies were 63\.31 ± 15\.74%, 79\.77 ± 7\.57%, and 79\.96 ± 11\.95%\. The 2\.08\-point BNCI2014\_002 difference had a 95% confidence interval of 0\.42\-3\.73 points and an adjusted p value of 0\.0387\. These three comparisons did not remain significant in a broader 56\-comparison Holm sensitivity analysis\. The P300 and SSVEP models attained the highest observed mean accuracies\. SIGNIFICANCE: The results provide within\-setting evidence for staged local\-to\-global temporal modeling in offline MI\-EEG evaluations\.
