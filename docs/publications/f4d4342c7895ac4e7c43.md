# RESEARCH INTO MODERN APPROACHES TO BRIDGE CONSTRUCTION

**Autores:** G\.E\. Zinnatov; I\.A\. Leonova

**Data:** 2026-10-12 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

The article analyzes modern approaches to bridge construction in Russia as a key element of transport infrastructure\. The use of innovative materials – polymer composites, aluminum alloys, and carbon fiber – to increase the strength and reduce the weight of structures is examined\. The introduction of digital BIM technologies, robotics, and unmanned aerial systems at the design and construction stages of facilities is considered\. Continuous monitoring systems using fiber‑optic sensors and artificial intelligence to ensure the safety of structures are described\. The strategic importance of modernizing aging infrastructure, ensuring environmental safety, and providing state support for the industry is emphasized\.
