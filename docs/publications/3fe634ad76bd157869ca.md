# Flexible paper\-based self\-sensing biomechanical ergonomics monitoring system for early warning of carpal tunnel syndrome risks

**Autores:** Preethi Sudha Sarva; Govardhan Karunanidhi

**Data:** 2026-10-13 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

One of the most prevalent workplace\-related musculoskeletal disorders is Carpal Tunnel Syndrome \(CTS\), which is caused by compression of the median nerve at the wrist due to prolonged improper wrist posture\. Continuous monitoring of wrist posture is essential to prevent serious complications, including the need for surgery\. Current monitoring methods are often limited by high cost and poor portability because they rely on expensive electronics, rigid sensors, or vision\-based systems\. Wearable, flexible sensing technologies have emerged as an attractive solution for early detection and prevention of musculoskeletal disorders, as well as for continuous health monitoring\. This work presents a low\-cost, wearable, flexible, silver\-conductive ink\-patterned paper patch for continuous wrist posture monitoring and identification of ergonomic factors associated with CTS\. Silver nanoparticles were synthesised by a chemical reduction method owing to their high electrical conductivity and superior oxidation resistance compared with copper, and the silver conductive ink was formulated using suitable binders\. The conductive ink pattern printed on a flexible paper substrate undergoes mechanical deformation during wrist movement, resulting in detectable variations in resistance that correlate with changes in wrist bending angle\. An Arduino\-based system continuously monitored the resistance variations using a threshold\-based algorithm, while an HC\-05 Bluetooth module and a custom MIT App Inventor application wirelessly transmitted the wrist posture status to a smartphone\. Experimental evaluation demonstrated accurate real\-time detection of wrist posture with successful wireless ergonomic feedback\. The proposed paper\-based wearable system provides a lightweight, flexible, and cost\-effective solution for the early detection and warning of potentially risky wrist position associated with CTS in industrial workers and computer users\.
