# Paterson, Anthony: The Vanishing Middle: Developmental Co\-Building in the Age of Autonomous AI Agents

**Autores:** 

**Data de descoberta:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** unknown

## Descrição do feed

Rapid advances in agentic artificial intelligence increasingly make it possible to delegate substantial software\-development tasks from an initial specification through implementation, testing, and revision\. This capability offers significant benefits, particularly where the objective is efficient artifact production\. However, the transition from manual software development to autonomous agentic construction risks obscuring an intermediate mode of practice: sustained human\-AI co\-building\. \(direct link \)
