# Spatio\-temporal Characterization of Reference Evapotranspiration in Supaul District, Bihar, India, using the Fao\-56 Penman–monteith Method

**Autores:** Vivek Kumar; Pusplata Puja

**Data:** 2026-10-14 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Reference evapotranspiration \(ET₀\) represents atmospheric evaporative demand from a standardized reference surface and is widely used in irrigation scheduling, crop\-water assessment, and hydroclimatic analysis\. This study estimated monthly and annual ET₀ for 11 locations in Supaul District, Bihar, India, using the FAO\-56 Penman–Monteith method and monthly meteorological inputs for 1990–2025\. The dataset comprised 4,752 location\-month records\. Mean daily ET₀ averaged across locations and years ranged from 2\.33 mm day⁻¹ in December to 7\.76 mm day⁻¹ in April\. The average of the location\-level annual means was 1,724\.2 mm year⁻¹; Basantpur recorded the lowest mean \(1,664\.5 mm year⁻¹\), while Marauna recorded the highest \(1,811\.4 mm year⁻¹\)\. The annual location\-averaged series showed a negative ordinary least\-squares slope of −13\.72 mm year⁻¹\. This trend is interpreted as an exploratory pattern because the annual series also exhibits strong lag\-1 autocorrelation and requires autocorrelation\-aware trend testing\. The findings provide a quantitative baseline for regional irrigation planning, crop\-water assessment, and hydroclimatic analysis\.
