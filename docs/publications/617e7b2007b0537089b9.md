# LLM\-Augmented Bayesian Optimization with Adaptive Policies for Chiplet Design Space Exploration

**Autores:** Hang Liu; Zhengfei Chen; Zhengrui Chen; Hao Geng; Yu Li; Qi Sun

**Data:** 2026-10-10 · **Tipo:** preprint

## Resumo

Chiplet technology is a key approach for extending Moore&\#x27;s Law\. However, its design process is constrained by two primary challenges: the scarcity of early\-stage platforms for rapidly evaluating chiplet\-based heterogeneous systems, and the complex relationships between design parameters and optimization metrics\. To address these challenges, we propose a synergistic framework that integrates a Large Language Model \(LLM\) with Bayesian Optimization \(BO\), enabling architectural\-level simulation and automated optimization for the 2\.5D/3D CPU\-GPU heterogeneous chiplets\. We extend the open\-source chiplet simulator to evaluate 3D designs by supporting graph topologies\. Further, to enhance the exploration efficiency of BO, we introduce an LLM that generates high\-quality policies to guide the optimization process, enabled by our proposed adaptive directional policy reasoning \(ADPR\) and an exploration gain\-aware fine\-tuning method\. Finally, we use the policy\-guided BO to perform an automated and efficient exploration of the complex chiplet design space\. Experiments on 2\.5D and 3D chiplet reveal that our framework improves design space exploration efficiency\.
