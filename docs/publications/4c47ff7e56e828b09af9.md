# Case Report: Prenatal diagnosis and genetic counseling for a homozygous segment spanning chromosome 6q25\.3q27 with normal phenotype

**Autores:** Dandan Li; Jie Hu; Ji Wu; Xuna Bian

**Data:** 2026-10-15 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Background Runs of Homozygosity \(ROH\) are long, uninterrupted stretches of the genome in which an individual is homozygous at virtually every assayed variant\. Uniparental disomy \(UPD\) is an epigenetic anomaly in which both copies, or a portion of a pair of homologous chromosomes, are inherited from one parent\. Materials and Methods A 29\-year\-old gravida 2, para 1 woman underwent amniocentesis at 18 weeks’ gestation because non\-invasive prenatal testing \(NIPT\) indicated a high probability of chromosome 11 gain\. GTG\-banding karyotype analysis was performed on cultured amniocytes\. Chromosomal microarray analysis \(CMA\), whole\-exome sequencing \(WES\) on uncultured amniocytes were performed\. Results CMA detected a copy\-neutral 10\.42 Mb ROH of chromosome 6, arr \[GRCh37\] 6q25\.3q27 \(157,776,570\_168,194,615\)x2 hmz\. Trio\-WES confirmed that the homozygous 6q25\.3q27 segment resulted from biparental inheritance\. Across the 10\.42 Mb interval, multiple informative SNPs showed that the fetal homozygous genotypes were compatible with the contribution of one allele from each parent, and no informative single\-nucleotide polymorphism \(SNP\) showed fetal homozygosity with one parent being wild\-type for the fetal allele, thus excluding segmental uniparental disomy\. At the 6\-month checkup, the baby was developing normally\. Conclusion We provide a detailed description of a homozygous segment spanning chromosome 6q25\.3q27 with normal phenotype\. Combination of NIPT, prenatal ultrasound, karyotype analysis, CMA, WES and genetic counseling will prove a more accurate risk assessment for the prenatal diagnosis of ROH and UPD\.
