# Efficient Safety Guardrails through Latent Dual\-Hypothesis Reasoning with On\-Demand Trace Reconstruction

**Autores:** Md Asiful Islam; Fahmida Alam; Mihai Surdeanu

**Data:** 2026-10-13 · **Tipo:** preprint

## Resumo

Guardrail approaches that rely on reasoning\-based large language models \(LLM\) improve safety decisions, but come with substantial inference overhead due to the explicit reasoning\. We introduce LR\-Guard \(Latent Reasoning Guardrail\), an efficient and explainable guardrail that jointly learns structured latent reasoning and on\-demand reasoning reconstruction\. Our method distills a frozen teacher&\#x27;s analysis of safe and unsafe prompt interpretations and their comparison into compact latent representations\. Joint training optimizes these representations for both safety prediction and reconstruction of the teacher&\#x27;s explicit reasoning traces, enabling human\-readable explanations without requiring intermediate text generation for every decision\. Experiments show that LR\-Guard outperforms its explicit\-reasoning teacher in most in\-domain and out\-of\-domain settings on safety classification and explanation extraction, while achieving a 19\.4x inference speedup at batch size one\. Further, reconstructed reasoning traces from the latent information exhibit high semantic similarity to the teacher&\#x27;s explicit reasoning\.
