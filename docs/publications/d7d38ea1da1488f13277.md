# Development and initial evaluation of a urine DNA methylation assay for pan\-urinary cancer detection

**Autores:** Haohao Li; Junyao Guo; Bo Hong; Chen Chen; Xiaoliang Chen; Ruifang Sun; Jinfu Nie

**Data:** 2026-10-09 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Cancers affecting the urinary system, encompassing malignancies of the bladder, ureter, renal pelvis, kidney, and prostate, represent a major global health challenge\. DNA methylation biomarkers for multi\-cancer detection in the urinary tract have been evaluated in limited contexts\. This study aimed to develop a non\-invasive urine\-based assay utilizing DNA methylation markers for the simultaneous detection of multiple urinary system cancers\. In this retrospective case–control study of 609 participants, a four\-gene urine DNA methylation model \(H4C6, SIX6, SEPTIN9, and SHOX2\) was developed in a training set \(n = 425\) and evaluated in a held\-out split\-sample internal validation set \(n = 184\)\. The pan\-urinary cancer detection \(PUCD\) model achieved an area under the curve of 0\.95 \(95% confidence interval \(CI\) 0\.913–0\.987\) in the validation set\. Notably, it demonstrated high sensitivity for early\-stage \(Stage I/II\) cancers \(88\.2%, 95% CI 78\.5–93\.9%\) alongside the caveat that performance varies by cancer type\. The assay provided discrimination between cancer and non\-cancer samples even within subgroups positive for urine occult blood or protein\. These findings support further prospective evaluation of the model as a potential non\-invasive adjunctive triage tool for patients with hematuria or other suspicious urinary symptoms\.
