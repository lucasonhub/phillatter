## Descrição do feed

\_Adaptive Behavior\_\. 2026Enactivism, as a radical framework for understanding cognition based on the flexible dynamics of living systems, developed in the late 20th century in response to problems faced by the prevailing model of cognitivism\. It aimed to be all\-encompassing, accounting for the different ontological levels that cognition is instantiated on, but despite its ambition enactivism did not achieve the paradigmatic shift once wished for\. The motivation behind the special issue ‘Prospects for the Science of Enaction’, therefore, is to consider both critically and hopefully what the future of the framework might look like and, as a consequence, stimulate new thought both within enactivism and wider cognitive science\. \(direct link \)
