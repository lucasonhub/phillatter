# Digital Revolution, AI and the Future of Human Translation: From Translator to Cultural Guarantor

**Autores:** Ferial FILALI\-FELLAH

**Data:** 2026-10-15 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

The rapid development of Artificial Intelligence \(AI\) is transforming Translation Studies \(TS\)\. This article adopts a historical and critical approach, based on a literature review from 1954 to 2024, from Statistical Machine Translation to Generative AI\. While Generative Translation \(GT\) achieves unprecedented fluency and terminological consistency, it still fails to render cultural depth, ethical nuance and spiritual meaning\. Through examples such as the translation of 仁 \(Ren\) and رحمة \(Rahma\), the study argues that the translator&\#x27;s role is evolving from a linguistic transferer to a cultural guarantor and strategic curator of meaning\. The paper concludes that Human Translation is not doomed, but its survival depends on integrating AI mastery with intercultural mediation, in line with the Global Civilization Initiative\. Article submitted to Frontiers \(ID 2030041\) on 15\-10\-2026\. Deposited on Zenodo in Restricted Access for author copyright protection\.
