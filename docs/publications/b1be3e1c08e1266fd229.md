# EFFECTIVENESS OF VARIOUS FORMS OF ERECTILE DYSFUNCTION TREATMENT IN MEN WHO HAVE SUFFERED FROM THE CONSEQUENCES OF COMBAT

**Autores:** Mykola Z\. Vorobets; Dmytro Z\. Vorobets; Р\. В\. Фафула; Oksana V\. Melnyk; Mariia Kushynska; Zinoviy D\. Vorobets; Орест Мирославович Чемерис

**Data:** 2026-10-17 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Introduction\. The long\-term consequences of severe injuries sustained by combatants have a significant impact on quality of life and sexual function\. Post\-traumatic stress disorder, which develops as a result of injuries, is associated with the development of erectile dysfunction, decreased sexual desire, and premature ejaculation\.Aim\. Analysis of erectile function and quality of life indicators in men of different age groups injured as a result of combat operations before and after the end of treatment\.Materials and methods\. The study included the results of treatment and a questionnaire survey of 136 men with combat injuries and concomitant sexual dysfunction before and after treatment of the dysfunction\. To determine the health\-related quality of life, a questionnaire survey was conducted using the SF\-36 questionnaire\. The second criterion in differentiating the severity of ED was the sum of the scores of the answers to the questions of the International Index of Erectile Function\-5 \(IIEF\-5\) questionnaire\. According to the form of erectile dysfunction, patients were divided into two groups: Group 1 – patients with psychogenic erectile dysfunction after combat trauma; Group 2 – patients with erectile dysfunction of mixed genesis\.Results\. In patients of group 1 with a psychogenic form of ED, after treatment, a significant increase in the average score of the domains of general health, role\-physical functioning and role\-emotional functioning was observed\. With a mixed form of ED, as a result of treatment, patients observed significant improvements in role\-physical functioning and pain sensation\. After comprehensive treatment, in men with psychogenic ED, the average scores of the IIEF\-5 questionnaire domain regarding satisfaction with sexual life significantly increased from 2\.8 to 5\.5 and erectile function from 7\.2 to 16\.7\. In men with a mixed form of erectile dysfunction, erectile function increased the most during treatment, by 7\.3 scores\.Conclusions\. In men, after suffering combat injuries, both psychogenic and mixed forms of erectile dysfunction develop\. As a result of the treatment of erectile dysfunction of both psychogenic and mixed forms, general health, role\-physical and role\-emotional functioning improve\. After comprehensive treatment, men with psychogenic erectile dysfunction of both age groups significantly increased their indicators of satisfaction with sexual life and the erectile function domain\.
