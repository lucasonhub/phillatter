# Controlled Evidence Mapping of Digitally Enabled Climate\-Smart Tourism: From Fragmented Applications to Integrated Destination Climate Intelligence

**Autores:** Eren Gönül

**Data:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

This study maps the knowledge structure of AI\-enabled climate\-smart tourism and examines how artificial intelligence, digital twins, the Internet of Things, remote sensing, predictive analytics, and climate services support adaptation, mitigation, and destination resilience\. A systematic evidence\-mapping design was combined with controlled bibliometric science mapping\. Following PRISMA 2020 and SPAR\-4\-SLR, 77 records were screened, yielding 50 eligible publications available through June 2026\. Analyses included concept co\-occurrence, temporal overlay, technology–function cross\-tabulation, climate\-relevance coding, and evidence\-maturity assessment\. Publication activity increased after 2023, with planning and governance dominating the literature\. Only 11 studies explicitly addressed climate risk, adaptation, weather, or resilience, while outcome\-level evaluation remained limited\. The findings reveal a sensing\-to\-action gap, as observation, prediction, simulation, implementation, and learning are rarely integrated\. The study conceptualizes AI\-enabled climate\-smart tourism as an outcome\-oriented destination capability and proposes a six\-stage climate\-intelligence cycle connecting sensing, integration, prediction, simulation, decision\-making, adaptation, and learning\.
