# Music Therapy and community participation: Selfhood and artistic citizenship within and beyond the clinic

**Autores:** Brynjulf Stige

**Data:** 2026-10-14 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Music therapy practices evolve in specific contexts, which suggests that the conceptual and theoretical claims we make should be continuously challenged by practical realities\. In this paper, I challenge tendencies to polarize clinical music therapy and community music therapy, by elaborating on two main themes: a\) Possibilities and limitations of connecting community music therapy and cultural participation in music therapy within mental health clinics; b\) Ways of relating to the argument that, as professionals, music therapists must heed established models of thinking in the clinic\. This theoretical article takes inspiration from two integrative metatheoretical traditions; the bioecological and sociocultural tradition and contemporary enactivist perspectives\. The examples that I present in this paper are based in practice contexts within mental healthcare in Bergen, Norway\. A “Three\-Stage Model” is outlined, with emphasis on the ecological transitions from i\) clinical music therapy, to ii\) municipal follow\-up services, to iii\) independent cultural participation in the local community\. The affordances and limitations of the notion of artistic citizenship in relation to the Three\-Stage Model are explored and related to the notions of personhood and selfhood\. The relationships between the Three\-Stage Model and organisational culture of mental health clinics in Norway are discussed, and areas of future research indicated\.
