# Real\-world discrimination and incremental value of first\-trimester placental growth factor for hypertensive disorders of pregnancy: a retrospective Chinese cohort

**Autores:** Lingling Jiang; Minxia Chai; Yuan Xia

**Data:** 2026-10-15 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Background Placental growth factor \(PlGF\) contributes to first\-trimester screening algorithms for preeclampsia, but its prognostic performance as an isolated biomarker in routine Chinese care is uncertain\. Methods We performed a retrospective, single\-centre cohort study among pregnancy episodes recorded in a hospital PlGF workbook\. Pregnancy episodes were deduplicated, PlGF values were traced to source laboratory cells, and hypertensive disorders of pregnancy \(HDP\) were ascertained using unvalidated free\-text discharge\-diagnosis keywords\. The primary operational window was 9 \+ 0 to 13 \+ 6 weeks; 11 \+ 0 to 13 \+ 6 weeks was evaluated separately\. We compared PlGF with a parsimonious maternal\-characteristics model containing age, pre\-pregnancy body mass index \(BMI\), and nulliparity\. Results From 405 source rows, 380 pregnancy episodes were resolved; 310 met the primary criteria, including 27 HDP events \(11 cases of preeclampsia and 16 of gestational hypertension\)\. PlGF alone had an area under the curve \(AUC\) of 0\.633 \(95% bootstrap CI 0\.523–0\.738\), and internally derived PlGF multiple of the median \(MoM\) had an AUC of 0\.636 \(0\.533–0\.740\)\. In 308 complete records, the maternal\-characteristics model had an apparent AUC of 0\.740 \(0\.635–0\.837\), while adding log10\(PlGF\) yielded an AUC of 0\.748 \(0\.638–0\.849\); paired ΔAUC was 0\.009 \(95% CI −0\.019 to 0\.037\)\. Conclusion The findings are consistent with a modest association between lower first\-trimester PlGF and subsequent HDP, but they do not support isolated clinical use or a deployable prediction model\.
