# Adaptive Radiotherapy: Principles, Technologies, Clinical Applications, and Future Directions — A Comprehensive Review

**Autores:** 

**Data:** 2026-10-10 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Background: Adaptive radiotherapy \(ART\) represents a paradigm shift in radiation oncology, enabling real\-time or iterative modification of treatment plans in response to anatomical, physiological, and biological changes occurring throughout the course of treatment\.Unlike conventional radiotherapy, which relies on a single pre\-treatment plan, ART dynamically adjusts beam delivery to account for inter\-and intrafraction variations in tumor volume, shape, and position, as well as changes in surrounding normal tissues and organs at risk \(OARs\) \.Methods: A comprehensive literature search was performed across PubMed, MEDLINE, Embase, and the Cochrane Library from January 2000 to December 2024, using the MeSH terms &\#x27;adaptive radiotherapy,&\#x27;&\#x27;online adaptation,&\#x27;&\#x27;offline adaptation,&\#x27;&\#x27;image\-guided radiotherapy,&\#x27;&\#x27;MR\-linac,&\#x27; and &\#x27;cone\-beam CT\.&\#x27; A total of 148 peerreviewed articles, systematic reviews, meta\-analyses, and clinical guidelines were screened; 102 met the inclusion criteria and are cited herein\.Results: Evidence from randomized and observational studies demonstrates that ART reduces radiation dose to OARs by 10\-35% compared with conventional radiotherapy while maintaining equivalent or superior tumor control rates\.Emerging magnetic resonance\-guided linac \(MR\-linac\) technologies and artificial intelligence \(AI\)\-driven automated re\-planning systems have substantially reduced online adaptation workflow times to under 30 minutes, enabling clinical scalability\.Conclusion: ART is rapidly becoming the standard of care for anatomically complex and motion\-sensitive malignancies including head and neck, prostate, cervical, and lung cancers\.Continued innovation in imaging, automation, and biological adaptation promises to further enhance therapeutic ratios and patient outcomes\.
