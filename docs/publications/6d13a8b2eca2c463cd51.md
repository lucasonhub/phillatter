# Gerenuk Optimization Algorithm: A Novel Nature\-inspired Metaheuristic for Solving Optimization Problems

**Autores:** Zeinab Montazeri; Mohammad Dehghani; Raed Abu Zitar; Omar Almomani; Kei Eguchi

**Data:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Complex optimization problems in many engineering and scientific applications require optimization methods that can achieve an effective balance between broad search and precise local search\.In this study, the Gerenuk Optimization Algorithm \(GOA\) is introduced as a new metaheuristic algorithm inspired by the foraging behavior of the Gerenuk\.The main idea of GOA is based on two complementary behaviors of this animal: exploring different areas to identify suitable food sources and intensively exploiting promising food sources\.These behaviors are modeled through two phases: exploration and exploitation\.The exploration phase enhances global search by moving towards better food sources while simultaneously exploring new regions of the search space, whereas the exploitation phase performs local search and refinement around promising solutions\.To evaluate the performance of GOA, 23 benchmark functions, including unimodal test functions \(F1\-F7\), high\-dimensional multimodal test functions \(F8\-F13\), and fixed\-dimensional multimodal test functions \(F14\-F23\), are considered\.GOA and nine competing algorithms are evaluated over 20 independent runs with 1000 iterations, and the mean, standard deviation \(std\), and rank are used as evaluation criteria\.The results show that GOA ranked first among the compared optimizers on 19 out of 23 benchmark functions, corresponding to 82\.61% of the test functions\.The convergence curves and boxplots further demonstrate the solution quality, convergence behavior, and acceptable stability of GOA across a large proportion of the benchmark functions\.Overall, the findings indicate that GOA, with its effective exploration and exploitation mechanisms and appropriate search balance, can serve as an effective optimization tool for a wide range of optimization problems and real\-world applications\.
