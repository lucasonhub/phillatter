# The Data\-Sharing Bottleneck and Siloed Competencies: An Empirical Analysis of Data Literacy in the Circular Economy

**Autores:** Julia Beatrix Reinhard; Helen Brüggmann; J\. Deich; Andre Bröcker; Lara Baumanns; Juliano Bezerra de Araújo; Annalisa Karin Nolte; Ali Nazeri; Christiane Plociennik; André Pomp; Holger Berg

**Data:** 2026-10-10 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

The Circular Economy \(CE\) depends fundamentally on data, yet turning this data into actionable circular practice requires domain\-specific data literacy\. Moving beyond narrow technical views, this study conceptualizes CE data literacy as a multidimensional meta\-competence structured across six competency fields along the data lifecycle: \(1\) Data Culture, \(2\) Acquisition and Collection, \(3\) Management, \(4\) Analysis, \(5\) Application, and \(6\) Sharing and Publication\. Utilizing a triangulated mixed\-methods design combining a scoping literature review, two surveys, and 18 expert interviews, this research consolidates the fragmented literature into a CE\-specific competency framework and provides empirical evidence on data literacy across various stakeholder groups, thereby evaluating which competencies require targeted support\. The results reveal three conceptual maturity profiles of CE data literacy, identifying Data Sharing and Publication as the field with the lowest maturity and as the systemic bottleneck\. Crucially, the findings demonstrate that individual competency fields are currently treated as isolated silos rather than an integrated continuum\.
