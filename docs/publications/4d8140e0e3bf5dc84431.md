# FORMATIV BAHOLASH SHAROITIDA OʻQUVCHILARDA CHOLGʻU ASBOBLARINI CHALISHGA OID KREATIV QOBILIYATLARINI RIVOJLANTIRISH MAZMUNI VA TASHKILIY\-FUNKSIONAL MODELI

**Autores:** SABRINA ALLABERDI QIZI NAZAROVA

**Data:** 2026-10-13 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

The article addresses the development of students&\#x27; creative abilities in playing musical instruments under formative assessment\. It argues that judging instrumental performance only by the final result fails to reveal students&\#x27; exploration, learning from errors and independent musical decisions\. The study defines the content of creative\-ability development: criteria of creative performance, diagnostic materials, differentiated creative tasks and a feedback mechanism\. The novelty lies in an organizational\-functional model that integrates the formative assessment cycle \(diagnosis – formative feedback – reflection – planning the next performance\) with the content of instrumental performance\. The model combines a developmental environment, the project method and individual feedback into a single pedagogical technology and moves students from external grading towards conscious self\-regulation of their performance\. A rubric for assessing creative performance is also proposed\. The model is recommended for music lessons and instrumental club activities\.
