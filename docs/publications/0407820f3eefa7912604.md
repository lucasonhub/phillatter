# More is not necessarily better: A comparison of 2\-mA and 4\-mA prefrontal cortex anodal tDCS on endurance performance, head oxygenation, cognition, and psychophysiological responses in cyclists

**Autores:** Everton Borges da Silva; Mércia Jaynne Leandro de Melo; Matheus Alves Mendonça; Daniel de Medeiros Leiros; Ehsan Amiri; Heloiana Faro; Daniel Gomes da Silva Machado

**Data:** 2026-10-12 · **Tipo:** article

A single session of 2 mA anodal tDCS produced small, practically relevant improvements in endurance performance relative to sham alongside intensity\-dependent changes in prefrontal head oxygenation, with the largest performance benefit associated with poorer baseline cognitive performance; 4 mA did not differ from sham on either outcome\. These findings suggest a non\-monotonic intensity\-response relationship, where higher intensity does not necessarily produce a larger effect\.
