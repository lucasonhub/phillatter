# Racial Discrimination, School Climate, and Black Adolescents&\#x27; Internalizing and Externalizing Behaviors: A Longitudinal Moderation

**Autores:** Emily Green; Jia Julia Yan; Jasmine N\. Coleman; Elizabeth I\. Johnson

**Data:** 2026-10-13 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

ABSTRACT Using data from Black adolescents in the Adolescent Brain Cognitive Development \(ABCD\) study \( n = 1,316\), we examined the moderating effect of school climate on the relationship between racial discrimination and later internalizing and externalizing behaviors during adolescence \( M age = 12\.00; SD = 0\.65\)\. Longitudinal regression analyses showed that positive perceptions of school climate were linked to a weaker association between racial discrimination and subsequent internalizing and externalizing behaviors\. Findings highlight the need to reduce discrimination that Black youth face while simultaneously building safe and equitable school climates\.
