# Association of miR‐130a‐5p rs731384 With the Susceptibility of Peri‐Implantitis and the Function of miR‐130a‐5p in Peri‐Implantitis

**Autores:** Yong Li; Jianan Kang; Yan Zhen; Ying Cen

**Data:** 2026-10-13 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Peri\-implantitis \(PI\) is a common inflammatory disease that threatens the stability of dental implants\. This study investigated whether the promoter polymorphism rs731384 of the miR\-130a gene is linked to PI susceptibility and further explored the functional role of miR\-130a\-5p in inflammatory responses and osteoblast viability\. A total of 143 patients without PI and 127 patients with PI were gathered in this study\. MassARRAY was used to genotype rs731384\. Saliva samples were collected to measure miR\-130a\-5p expression \(via reverse transcriptase\-polymerase chain reaction \[RT\-PCR\]\) and inflammatory cytokine levels \(including interleukin \[IL\]\-1β, IL\-6 and tumour necrosis factor \[TNF\]\-α, via enzyme\-linked immunosorbent assay \[ELISA\]\)\. In vitro inflammation model was established by treating hFOB1\.19 human osteoblasts with lipopolysaccharide \(LPS\)\. The functional role of miR\-130a\-5p was assessed by transfecting cells with a miR\-130a\-5p inhibitor and evaluating its effects on cell viability, apoptosis and cytokine release in the supernatants\. Bioinformatic databases \(miRDB, ENCORI and TargetScan\) were utilized to predict downstream target genes and pathways\. A Positive association has been discovered between rs731384 and PI susceptibility under GA versus GG \(odds ratio \[OR\] = 1\.758, 95% confidence interval \[CI\] = 1\.017\-3\.036\), A versus G \(OR = 1\.779, 95% CI = 1\.140\-2\.778\), and GA \+ AA versus GG \(OR = 1\.867, 95% CI = 1\.110\-3\.140\) models\. Upregulated miR\-130a\-5p showed promising diagnostic value for distinguishing PI from healthy controls \(area under the curve \[AUC\] = 0\.858, sensitivity = 80\.31%, specificity = 79\.02%\)\. Spearman analysis revealed a positive correlation between miR\-130a\-5p and periodontal parameters as well as inflammation factors \(IL\-1β, IL\-6, TNF\-α and C\-reactive protein \[CRP\]\)\. The miR\-130a inhibitor reversed the LPS\-induced reduction in cell viability, the increase of apoptosis and the enhancement of IL\-1β, IL\-6 and TNF\-α concentrations\. Bioinformatics predicted that miR\-130a\-5p targets genes enriched in TGF\-β, Hippo and FoxO signalling pathways\. The mir\-130a\-5p rs731384 polymorphism is positively correlated with PI susceptibility\. High miR\-130a\-5p may contribute to the diagnosis of PI from healthy controls\. The inflammation and metabolism of bone tissue in PI may be significantly influenced by miR\-130a\.
