# Tadeusz, Ciecierski: Signs, Transparency, and Semantic Paradoxes

**Autores:** 

**Data de descoberta:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** unknown

## Descrição do feed

\_Studia Semiotyczne\_ 40 \(1\):79\-92\. 2026The paper revisits an intriguing idea from Husserl and the tradition of Polish logical semiotics: when we use something as a sign, our attention tends to “pass through” the sign to what it signifies\. Leon Koj developed this idea into a principle intended, among other things, to rule out semantic self\-reference and thereby avoid semantic paradoxes\. I take a closer look at this proposal and argue that, fascinating as the idea is, it cannot quite do the job Koj wanted it to do\. Instead, I suggest that semiotic transparency is better understood in terms of the pragmatic and attentional features of how we actually use signs\.
