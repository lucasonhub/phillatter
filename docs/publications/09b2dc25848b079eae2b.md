\_Zenodo\_ 1\. 2026This paper develops a realist account of the relations among poetic, trickster, philosophical, and scientific operations\. Its central thesis distinguishes an antecedent operative interval from the operational shape retrospectively observed and reconstructed through subsequent reflexive operations\. Such reconstructions can modify the segmentation of earlier operations and, when reintroduced into later interventions, produce further real effects\. The paper examines how the local termination of poetic and trickster operations may intersect with the ignition of heterogeneous autonomous science\. These events possess independent conditions of occurrence and need not coincide\. Xenoscience requires autonomous scientific operations capable of revising their observational, experimental, variable\-generating, and historical\-reentry conditions\. The argument is developed through concrete operational configurations, engagement with philosophical and literary research, and two computational studies\. An independent 128\-seed PyTorch experiment examines observation\-dependent reconstruction, while an exploratory Xeno–Ziran Simulator experiment investigates historical re\-entry\. The simulator experiment did not distinguish history\-specific effects from trajectory noise\. Neither experiment establishes the ignition of Xenoscience\. The paper advances a framework for analyzing retrospective operational\-shape formation, local termination, historical reinjection, and the conditions under which heterogeneous scientific operations may emerge\. \(direct link \)
