# MRI‐Based 2\.5D Multi‐Instance Deep Learning for Differentiating Pathological Subtypes of Brain Metastases From Lung Cancer

**Autores:** Jinling Zhu; Cairui Liu; Li Zou; Ruizhe Xu; Xin Xie; Tian Ye; Long Jiang Zhang; Bo Zhang

**Data:** 2026-10-13 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

BACKGROUND: Distinct treatment strategies for small\-cell lung cancer \(SCLC\) and non\-small\-cell lung cancer \(NSCLC\) brain metastases require accurate noninvasive subtyping\. PURPOSE: To develop and externally validate a biparametric MRI\-based 2\.5D multi\-instance learning \(MIL\) deep learning \(DL\) model for discriminating SCLC from NSCLC brain metastases, with performance compared against clinical, radiomics, and 3D\-DL models\. STUDY TYPE: Retrospective\. POPULATION: This study included 366 patients from four institutions\. Center 1 provided the training set \(n = 199; mean age, 63\.88 ± 10\.79 years; 72\.36% male\) and an internal tuning set \(n = 85; mean age, 64\.73 ± 9\.53 years; 67\.06% male\)\. Centers 2\-4 were pooled into a single external test set \(n = 82; mean age, 62\.26 ± 10\.60 years; 63\.41% male\)\. FIELD STRENGTH/SEQUENCE: 1\.5 T and 3\.0 T; contrast\-enhanced T1\-weighted imaging and fluid\-attenuated inversion\-recovery imaging\. ASSESSMENT: The 2\.5D\-MIL model was benchmarked against clinical, radiomics, and 3D\-DL models and evaluated on internal tuning and pooled external test sets\. STATISTICAL TESTS: Shapiro\-Wilk, Student&\#x27;s t\-test, Mann\-Whitney U, chi\-square, univariable and multivariable logistic regression, and DeLong tests were used\. Performance measures included the area under the receiver operating characteristic curve \(AUC\) with 95% confidence intervals and classification and calibration metrics\. Statistical significance was defined as two\-sided p &lt; 0\.05\. RESULTS: In the internal tuning set, AUCs were 0\.842 \(2\.5D\-MIL\), 0\.740 \(clinical\), 0\.710 \(radiomics\), and 0\.747 \(3D\-DL\)\. In the external test set, AUCs were 0\.826 \(2\.5D\-MIL\), 0\.826 \(clinical\), 0\.696 \(radiomics\), and 0\.664 \(3D\-DL\)\. Differences between 2\.5D\-MIL and the clinical \(p = 1\.000\), radiomics \(p = 0\.139\), and 3D\-DL \(p = 0\.119\) models were not significant\. DATA CONCLUSION: The MRI\-based 2\.5D\-MIL model showed potential for noninvasive differentiation of SCLC from NSCLC brain metastases\. Larger prospective studies are needed before clinical use\. EVIDENCE LEVEL: 3\. TECHNICAL EFFICACY: Stage 2\.
