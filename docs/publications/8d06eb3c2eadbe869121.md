# Howell, Robert J\.: Phenomenal Knowledge and Acquaintance

**Autores:** 

**Data de descoberta:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** unknown

## Descrição do feed

In Consciousness and the Limits of Objectivity: The Case for Subjective Physicalism\. Oxford: Oxford University Press\. pp\. 57\-89\. 2013This chapter provides a clear presentation and defence of a version of Frank Jackson’s “Knowledge Argument\.” It argues that despite the apparent diversity of responses to this argument, all responses must either resort to a hard\-line which denies the basic phenomenal data, or they must embrace the existence of an odd “acquaintance” relation which provides a subject with a grasp of her states which no other way of knowing can provide\. The phenomenal concepts strategy and the indexical strategy are considered in particular depth, but also considered are the ability hypothesis and the “ignorance hypothesis\.”
