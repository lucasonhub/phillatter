# Imbert, Cyrille &amp; Waszek, David: Draining the Data Pool\. Why Larger Samples Need Not Always Be Better

**Autores:** 

**Data de descoberta:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** unknown

## Descrição do feed

\_Philosophy of Science\_\. forthcomingIt is commonplace to bemoan small studies in empirical fields, and to call for larger sample sizes, for example by pooling data within large\-scale collaborations\. However, while higher\-powered studies are individually more reliable, we show that, under conditions of data scarcity, they can make scientific communities less reliable by reducing theoretical diversity\. Our results build on a formal model introduced for science by Kevin Zollman, which we adapt, stringently interpret, and partially de\-idealize to address worries about its real\-life significance\. We conclude that collaborative pooling is only beneficial up to some \(context\-dependent\) point and argue against one\-size\-fits\-all norms about sample size\.
