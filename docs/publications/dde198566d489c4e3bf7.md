# TRP channels in cerebral vascular disorders

**Autores:** Giovanna Paone; Josiane F\. Silva; Paulo Wagner Pires

**Data:** 2026-10-13 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

The cerebral circulation is a unique and highly specialized vascular bed, and its proper function is essential to maintain cognitive health\. Cerebral arteries, arterioles and capillaries exert specific functions to control homeostasis of the cerebral environment, including autoregulation, localized functional hyperemia, and formation of a virtually impermeable endothelial cell barrier \(the blood\-brain\-barrier\)\. Pathological alterations in these functions are involved in the pathophysiology of many neurological disorders, including strokes, Alzheimer’s disease and related dementias, and vascular cognitive impairment and dementia\. TRP channels are important regulators of cerebral vascular function due to their central role in the control of vascular reactivity \(contraction and dilation\), regulation of vascular permeability and EC proliferation/apoptosis\. This narrative review summarizes the literature on the role of TRP channels in the pathophysiology of neurological disorders with a strong vascular component\. Although we focus on TRP channels in the vasculature, we provide a brief discussion on TRP channels in other cells of the neurovascular unit\. Lastly, we identify gaps in our knowledge and paths for future research\.
