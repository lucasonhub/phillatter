# SUN’IY INTELLEKTNING AXBOROT XAVFSIZLIGI SOHASIGA TA’SIRI VA XALQARO\-HUQUQIY TARTIBGA SOLISHNING ASOSIY MUAMMOLARI

**Autores:** Сайфуллаева Венера Ниматжоновна

**Data:** 2026-10-13 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

In the realm of information security, artificial intelligence is utilized not only for real\-time operations, attack prediction, threat prevention, and the automation of authentication processes but also serves as a primary tool for cybercriminals\. The development of artificial intelligence has significantly altered the information security landscape; the use of AI for international criminal purposes such as deepfake\-enabled fraud, online human trafficking, blackmail and extortion, cracking encrypted passwords via malware, orchestrating virtual attacks through automated hacking, cyberterrorism, phishing, and more is rapidly expanding, creating an urgent need to strengthen information security measures\. This article analyzes the global international legal challenges arising from the impact of artificial intelligence on the sphere of information security\.
