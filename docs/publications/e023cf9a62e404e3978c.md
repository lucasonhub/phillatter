# Logic\-Guided Graph Denoising Diffusion Models

**Autores:** Samuel Cognolato; Luciano Serafini; Alessandro Sperduti; Andrea Cavallaro

**Data:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** preprint

## Resumo

Graph diffusion models are competitive approaches for generating complex graph\-structured data\. However, when such distributions have strict structural constraints, diffusion models may fail to capture them exactly, resulting in the generation of invalid graphs\. In this paper, we propose a method for grounding graph diffusion models in domain\-specific knowledge via First Order Logic \(FOL\), leveraging its expressive and interpretable formalism to impose structural constraints on the generative process\. We incorporate domain knowledge using classifier guidance, a technique that conditions the diffusion model&\#x27;s distribution on external variables to exert control over the sampled data\. During the generative process, classifier guidance steers the denoising trajectory to optimize an objective function, which we define as a fuzzy and differentiable relaxation of the logical rules\. Our methodology requires only the specification of constraints in FOL, whose satisfaction is encouraged at inference time to guide a pre\-trained generative model\. We evaluate our approach by comparing the unconditional generation of knowledge graphs with our logic\-guided framework, which incorporates domain ontologies as structural constraints during inference\. This approach significantly increases the proportion of generated graphs that satisfy the ontological constraints\.
