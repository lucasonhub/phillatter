# Non\-engineered housing construction and earthquakes: A global review of structural performance and vulnerability drivers

**Autores:** Angga Sabaruliman; Aaron Opdyke; Hao Zhang

**Data:** 2026-10-09 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Non\-engineered housing accounts for a disproportionate share of earthquake\-related casualties worldwide, yet remains under\-served by integrated research that bridges its structural, social, and regulatory dimensions\. Through a systematic review of more than 200 studies, we synthesise the global literature on the seismic performance of non\-engineered construction, the factors driving its vulnerability, and the ways in which housing safety has been conceptualised in this sector\. Across the principal typologies — unreinforced load\-bearing wall structures, confined masonry, reinforced concrete with masonry infill, timber and wood construction, and hybrid mixed systems — seismic behaviour is shown to depend less on the strength of individual elements than on the integration of those elements into a coherent structural system, a framing that accounts for both the persistent vulnerability of the most prevalent typologies and the unexpected resilience of certain vernacular hybrid systems\. The physical vulnerabilities documented across the literature are themselves the surface expression of deeper socio\-economic constraints — most notably economic limitation and limited technical capacity within the informal construction workforce — operating within a governance environment whose institutional absences from regulation, oversight, technical guidance, and capacity\-building constitute the enabling conditions for vulnerability to emerge and persist\. We propose a coordinated research and policy agenda spanning uncertainty\-aware risk assessment, context\-specific structural modelling, locally grounded design criteria and guidelines, and post\-earthquake recovery pathways, framing non\-engineered housing as a substantive research domain requiring integrated technical, socio\-economic, and governance attention\.
