# Singh, Mayank: There Is No Ultimate Foundation: There Is Only Depth to Dive \(A Phenomenological Inquiry into Seeing, Existence, Process, and the Imagination of Separation\)

**Autores:** 

**Data de descoberta:** 2026-10-19 · **Tipo:** unknown

## Descrição do feed

Human beings continually search for foundations that can explain existence, reality, consciousness, and being\. This search often assumes that whatever exists must ultimately depend on an internal principle, an external cause, an absolute beginning, or a final reality\. This paper questions that assumption through a phenomenological inquiry into seeing, experience, thought, and the continuity of existence\. Its central proposition is that there is no ultimate foundation to be established; there is only the depth of what is, and without seeing, there is no diving into that depth\. Existence is approached not as a completed object requiring a final explanation, but as what is already happening through connection, separation, creation, dissolution, and transformation\. These processes are neither denied nor reduced to illusion\. Instead, the paper questions whether their occurrence requires an ultimate ground outside or beneath them\. It further examines how thought transforms distinctions into ideas of absolute separation, how the observer imagines a position outside what is observed, and how concepts such as zero, infinity, oneness, and ultimate reality can become imagined foundations when treated as final metaphysical answers\. The inquiry also distinguishes direct seeing from the recognition and interpretation shaped by prior knowledge, social conditioning, and inherited beliefs\. Seeing is not presented as a technique for attaining an ultimate state or as another philosophical doctrine\. Rather, it concerns the encounter with what is happening before an explanation is mistaken for the whole of what is\. The paper concludes that the absence of an ultimate foundation does not end inquiry\. It leaves inquiry open to depth without a predetermined destination\. \(direct link \)
