# Bulgaria, Jamel: The 7\-Step Detangling Protocol within the Core Emotion Framework: Architectural Mechanics and Observational Foundations

**Autores:** 

**Data de descoberta:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** unknown

## Descrição do feed

The landscape of modern affective science has increasingly moved toward structural\-constructivist models that prioritize the functional mechanics of the human psychological operating system over categorical, low\-dimensional theories\. At the forefront of this shift is the Core Emotion Framework, a canonical, architecture\-level model developed by Jamel Bulgaria that reframes subjective experience not as a collection of fixed biological categories or culturally assembled narratives, but as the output of ten discrete functional operators organized within a 3×3\+1 architectural system\. While traditional dimensional models compress affective states into low\-dimensional valence\-arousal summaries, the framework demonstrates that these summaries systematically obscure critical components of emotional meaning\. By conceptualizing emotional episodes as dynamic sequences of functional operators, the framework integrates interoceptive physiology, computational operations, and clinical mechanisms of change into a single testable ontology\. Within this structural paradigm, clinical psychopathology is redefined not by the narrative content of a thought or the subjective intensity of a feeling, but by a loss of operator agility\. Psychological distress emerges when the natural boundaries between operators collapse, resulting in &quot;fusion&quot;—a state of pathological co\-activation and coupling where the activation values of two operators become linked, preventing independent modulation\. The 7\-Step Detangling Protocol serves as the primary clinical methodology designed to resolve these fusions, decouple co\-activation patterns, and restore structural integrity to the human operating system\. \(direct link \)
