# Liquid\-Phase Electrochemical Determination of Methyl Ethyl Ketone Using CuO/HAp\-like Modified PGE

**Autores:** Selin Işık; Filiz Kara; Selda Güney; Emir Baki Denkbaş

**Data:** 2026-10-15 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Monitoring of Methyl Ethyl Ketone \(MEK\) is essential for industrial safety and biomedical diagnostics, serving as a reliable biomarker for metabolic disorders\. Conventional detection techniques require bulky, expensive instrumentation, limiting portable use\. In this study, a low\-cost and disposable electrochemical sensor was fabricated using a Pencil Graphite Electrode \(PGE\) modified with a porous Hydroxyapatite\-like \(HAp\-like\) interlayer and Copper Oxide \(CuO\)\. The HAp\-like scaffold minimized the copper agglomeration and increased the electroactive surface area, with SEM and EDX confirming homogeneous CuO distribution\. Toward MEK, the sensor exhibited a “signal\-off” response, systematically suppressing the anodic peak current\. This is consistent with a steric\-hindrance mechanism where analyte adsorption blocks interfacial electron transfer\. Evaluated via square\-wave voltammetry \(SWV\) in phosphate buffer \(PBS, pH 10\.0\), the sensor demonstrated dual linear ranges \(0\.1\-5\.0 mM and 5\.0\-50 mM\) with a 1\.04 mM limit of detection\. The sensor demonstrated consistent reproducibility with an overall relative standard deviation \(RSD\) of 2\.83% \(n=15\) and an inter\-day RSD of 1\.94%, while retaining 107\.2% of its initial response after a 14\-day storage period\. Validated in PBS pH 10\.0, this CuO/HAp\-like/PGE platform is presented as a proof\-of\-concept for liquid\-phase MEK detection, establishing a foundation for future monitoring under simulated physiological conditions\.
