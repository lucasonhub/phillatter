# GENERATIVE AI AND STARTUP PERFORMANCE: THE ROLE OF ENTREPRENEURIAL CAPABILITIES

**Autores:** Dr\. V\. Renuka

**Data:** 2026-10-13 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Abstract: The ongoing development of technology is essential to the progress of entrepreneurship in today’s fast\-paced commercial environment\. Artificial Intelligence \(AI\) is one such technology that has the ability to completely transform how businesses and lone proprietors operate by automating and expediting a number of laborious operations\. The function of AI in entrepreneurship and how it might greatly boost individual productivity will be examined in this essay\. Recent research suggests that AI can improve opportunity recognition and entrepreneurial decision processes, but may also create problems relating to novelty, contextual sensitivity, and judgment\.
