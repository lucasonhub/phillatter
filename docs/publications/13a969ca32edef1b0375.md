# Efectividad del modelo croata en la gestión de las cargas de entrenamiento en futbolistas sub\-19 de un Club de Guayaquil

**Autores:** Alex Humberto Aparicio\-Torres; Alex Arturo Perlaza\-Estupiñan

**Data:** 2026-10-11 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Introduction: The management of training loads is a fundamental component for optimizing physical performance and adjusting preparation to the competitive demands of youth football\. Objective: To evaluate the effectiveness of a Croatian model for managing training loads in under\-19 football players from a club in Guayaquil, Ecuador\. Method: A pre\-experimental design was employed\. There were 25 male football players \(18\.08 ± 0\.64 years\) who participated, selected through a census sampling method\. The intervention lasted for 12 weeks, with two weekly sessions of 90 minutes each\. Both the &quot;30\-15 Intermittent Fitness Test&quot; and the &quot;Yo\-Yo Intermittent Recovery Test Level 2&quot; were conducted, and data were collected using GPS/EPTS at a sampling frequency of 10 Hz\. Results: Statistically significant improvements were found in all studied variables \(p &lt; 0\.001\)\. The final speed of the intermittent test \(VIFT\) improved from 18\.14 ± 0\.48 to 19\.26 ± 0\.57 km/h, and the distance in the Yo\-Yo IR2 increased from 894\.80 ± 124\.90 to 1103\.20 ± 145\.31 meters\. The total distance increased from 5370\.00 ± 502\.73 to 5854\.00 ± 497\.44 m, and the high\-speed running \(HSR\) distance increased from 472\.00 ± 86\.41 to 613\.60 ± 97\.51 m\. Discussion: The results show improvements in intermittent capacity and high\-intensity locomotor demands after the intervention\. Conclusions: The Croatian model proved to be effective in the studied variables, and it is a practical option for optimizing the management of training loads in football players\.
