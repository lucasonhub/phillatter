# AI\-Based Recommendation System for Indian Judiciary

**Autores:** Prof\. Nidhi Shah, Mitanshu Panchal, Hardik Vasava, Harsh Patel, Zaki Patel

**Data:** 2026-10-09 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Abstract The Indian Judiciary handles a vast number of cases, resulting in a significant backlog and increasing the workload of judges, advocates, and other legal professionals\. One of the major challenges in the legal system is identifying relevant past judgments and precedents from large collections of judicial documents\. Traditional keyword\-based search methods often require considerable time and may fail to retrieve important cases when different legal terms or expressions are used\. To address this problem, this project proposes an AI\-Based Recommendation System for the Indian Judiciary that uses Artificial Intelligence \(AI\), Natural Language Processing \(NLP\), and Machine Learning \(ML\) techniques to improve legal case retrieval and accessibility\. The proposed system is a web\-based platform that allows users to enter case descriptions, legal issues, or natural\-language queries and receive a ranked list of relevant past judgments\. The recommendation engine uses Term Frequency–Inverse Document Frequency \(TF\-IDF\) vectorization to convert legal documents into numerical representations and cosine similarity to measure the relevance between user queries and stored judgments\. Text preprocessing techniques, including lowercasing, tokenization, stop\-word removal, punctuation removal, and stemming, are applied to improve retrieval quality\. The platform is designed to support different user roles, including citizens, advocates, judges, court staff, and administrators, through a role\-based interface\. In addition to case recommendations, the system incorporates supporting features such as user authentication, case management, document handling, hearing scheduling, a virtual court interface, a legal resource library, and a chatbot that assists users with procedural information and system navigation\. The recommendation engine is evaluated using a manually prepared dataset of query\-relevance pairs\. The experimental results presented in the project report show that the proposed TF\-IDF and cosine similarity approach achieves a Mean Average Precision \(MAP\) of 0\.41 at K=10, compared with 0\.34 for BM25 and 0\.08 for the random retrieval baseline\. These results indicate that the proposed approach can improve the retrieval of relevant legal documents compared with the evaluated alternatives\. Overall, the project aims to make legal research more efficient, accessible, and systematic by reducing the effort required to locate relevant judicial precedents\. Future enhancements may include transformer\-based retrieval models, multilingual support, expansion of the legal document corpus, and integration with existing judicial portals\. The system is intended to assist legal professionals and citizens in accessing legal information while keeping final legal interpretation and decision\-making under human responsibility\.
