# Tan, Kwan Hong: Generative Uncertainty: Embracing Epistemic Friction as a Philosophical Virtue

**Autores:** 

**Data de descoberta:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** unknown

## Descrição do feed

This thesis critically examines the question of whether uncertainty can be considered a philosophical virtue\. Moving beyond the conventional understanding of uncertainty as a negative state to be merely tolerated, this work proposes a novel dialectical virtue\-theoretic framework\. This framework re\-conceptualizes uncertainty as a productive force, or epistemic friction, that is essential for intellectual flourishing\. The virtue of uncertainty, it is argued, lies not in a static disposition of tolerance, but in the agent’s dynamic and skillful engagement with the inherent tensions of the unknown\. The thesis introduces three core dialectical pairs of virtues—intellectual humility vs\. intellectual courage, open\-mindedness vs\. critical scrutiny, and cognitive flexibility vs\. focused persistence—that are central to this process\. Through a synthesis of philosophical analysis and empirical evidence from psychology and cognitive science, this work demonstrates that the virtuous agent is one who can navigate the creative tension between these opposing, yet complementary, virtues\. The framework is then applied to various domains, including scientific inquiry, medical diagnosis, and artistic creation, to illustrate its explanatory power\. This thesis concludes that uncertainty, when approached through a dialectical lens, is not only a philosophical virtue but a fundamental component of a flourishing intellectual life in an increasingly complex world\. \(direct link \)
