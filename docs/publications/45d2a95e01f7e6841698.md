# The Role of Artificial Intelligence in Modern Translation: Opportunities, Challenges, and Implications for Future Translators

**Autores:** Muhabbat Alisher kizi Safarova

**Data:** 2026-10-12 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

In today’s world, Artificial Intelligence \(AI\) has become an increasingly influential technology in the field of translation, transforming both professional translation practices and translator education\. The development of neural machine translation, large language models, and generative AI systems has provided translators with new opportunities to improve productivity, manage terminology, generate initial translations, and perform post\-editing tasks\. At the same time, the growing use of AI raises important concerns related to translation accuracy, cultural and contextual interpretation, ethical responsibility, data privacy, and the changing role of human translators\. Recent research indicates that AI\-assisted translation should not be viewed simply as a replacement for human translation\. Instead, it increasingly requires translators to develop technological literacy, critical evaluation skills, post\-editing competence, and the ability to make context\-sensitive decisions\. This study investigates the role of artificial intelligence in modern translation, focusing particularly on its opportunities and challenges for future translators\.
