# Bulgaria, Jamel &amp; Xǔ, Chénglán: Harmonic Architecture of Affect: A Structural Mapping of Core Emotion Framework Operators to the Diatonic Scale

**Autores:** 

**Data de descoberta:** 2026-10-19 · **Tipo:** unknown

## Descrição do feed

Abstract\. This paper proposes an exact structural isomorphism between the seven non\-cognitive operators of the Core Emotion Framework \(CEF\)—Accepting, Boosting, Appreciating, Arranging, Achieving, Constricting, and Expanding—and the seven functional degrees of the Western diatonic scale\. Grounded in the CEF&\#x27;s structural\-constructivist account of affect and in the psychoacoustics of tonal music, the mapping aligns each operator\-tone pair across center polarity, harmonic ratio, functional role, somatic signature, and neurobiological substrate\. Cadential progressions are then read as models of affective cycling, yielding testable hypotheses for emotional rigidity and burnout\. Implications are drawn for sound\-based clinical protocols, autonomic neuromodulation, and the engineering of synthetic affect in artificial intelligence\.
