# Cai, Hengjin: Self\-opacity requires a hidden layer

**Autores:** 

**Data de descoberta:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** unknown

## Descrição do feed

The first\-person problem begins from a residue: a complete third\-person description of a system can still leave a “for\-it” remainder\. This paper does not attempt to derive phenomenal experience from physics\. It constructs the smallest physical object about which the word perspective can be made operationally meaningful: a readout layer whose standard of consistency is held by its own generator and cannot be transferred, without loss, to an external readout\. The proposed minimal readout self is neither a private experience nor a complex informational system\. It is a directed, non\-absorbable, structurally self\-external readout layer\. Direction is supplied by a cycle affinity; non\-absorbability means that pooling layers yields a generator belonging to no layer; structural self\-externality means that the coordinate fixing the layer’s rates is not a degree of freedom transported by that layer\. Two exact carrier constructions determine the limits of these conditions\.
