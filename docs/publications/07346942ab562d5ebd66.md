# Institutional setting and vaccine sector capabilities\. The cases of Japan, Indonesia, and Vietnam

**Autores:** Michiko Iizuka; Rahmi Lestari Helmi; Bertha Vallejo; K\. Mita; Tran Son Tung

**Data:** 2026-10-14 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

The COVID\-19 pandemic exposed the urgent need for stable vaccine provision worldwide\. This paper examines how three Asian countries developed technological, regulatory, market\-enabling, and coordination capabilities for vaccine provision during the COVID\-19 crisis, with particular attention to the catalytic role of institutional capacity in reshaping vaccine\-sector incentive mechanisms\. Using a comparative institutional analysis, the study compares Japan, Indonesia, and Vietnam before, during, and after the COVID\-19 pandemic\. Drawing on semi\-structured interviews and secondary sources, the analysis shows that while COVID\-19 acted as a catalyst, outcomes varied significantly across the selected countries because of differences in pre\-existing capabilities\. The study argues that even during a crisis, institutional transformation remains strongly path\-dependent and incremental: crisis arrangements reverted once pressure receded, while those bound by contract or market instruments remained\.
