# Bayesian Optimization of CVaR with Unknown Hyperparameters

**Autores:** Valentin Margraf; Jonas Hanselle; Viktor Bengs; Serafima Lebedeva; Eyke Hüllermeier; Marcel Wever

**Data:** 2026-10-13 · **Tipo:** preprint

## Resumo

We study Bayesian optimization of the Conditional Value\-at\-Risk \(CVaR\) of an expensive black\-box function that depends on a decision and an environmental variable\. Existing no\-regret algorithms assume that the correct Gaussian process \(GP\) kernel and its hyperparameters are known\. In practice, however, they must be estimated from limited data, often yielding a misspecified surrogate\. This is especially damaging for CVaR because misspecification can distort the estimated tail\-risk and thus lead to catastrophic decisions\. We propose HE\-CV\-UCB, the first no\-regret algorithm for CVaR Bayesian optimization with unknown GP hyperparameters\. Given a finite candidate set containing a well\-specified model, HE\-CV\-UCB adaptively eliminates those inconsistent with the observed data, actively queries where the survivors disagree, and returns a design that is robust across the surviving candidates\. We prove that it attains cumulative regret matching the oracle rate of CV\-UCB up to a factor depending only on the candidate set&\#x27;s size\. Across synthetic benchmarks and three real\-world applications, HE\-CV\-UCB achieves competitive performance and frequently outperforms existing CVaR Bayesian optimization methods\.
