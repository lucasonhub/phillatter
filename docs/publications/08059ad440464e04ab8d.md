# AI\-Based Personalized Travel Planning and Recommendation System: A Unified Framework for Intelligent Trip Planning

**Autores:** Shubham Pandey; Satyam Anand; Sujal Das; Raushan Kumar Tiwari

**Data:** 2026-10-12 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

A growing number of travel planning activities involve managing multiple pieces of often competing data about destinations, budget, weather, attractions&\#x27; availability, transportation options and personal preferences that are typically distributed across a number of isolated online services\. Traditional travel\-planning tools tackle these issues one at a time: choosing or navigating, and rarely include personalization, predictive analytics, and multi\-constraint reasoning within a single interface\. We propose a unified framework for personalized travel planning that integrates personal preferences, context\-based destination recommendation, budget\- and weather\- aware filtering, and route and distance analysis, and AI\-assisted itinerary generation\. The framework is inspired, and to some extent generalized, from the design of the ongoing academic research prototype TripCraft, an AI\-powered agent for trip planning\. We build upon the state of the art of recommender\-systems theory, context\-aware recommendation, tourist trip design \(route optimization\) literature, and recent large language model\-based agents for travel\-planning to develop a system\-level description, a hybrid AI/ML approach, and a step\-by\-step algorithmic process for creating personalized itineraries that take preference, budget, weather and route constraints into account\. We provide preliminary proof of concept based on a synthetic evaluation on fabricated records using baseline comparisons and constraint\-compliance metrics\. The findings are only indicative of the possible effectiveness, but do not constitute proof of concept\. The expected contribution is the conceptual and methodological foundation for unifying preference\-, budget\-, weather\- and route awareness in intelligent travel planning\.
