# Generative AI and Literary Ambiguity: A Prompt\-Based Study of Henry James’s The Turn of the Screw

**Autores:** Meena A\. Mosa; Ghada Kamil Mustafa; \. Ali Adel Hashem

**Data:** 2026-10-15 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

This exploratory paper investigates the influence of prompt framing on AI\-generated analyses of literary ambiguity in Henry James’s The Turn of the Screw\. It examines nine responses across three chosen episodes and under the conditions of open\-ended, supernatural, and psychological/narratological prompts\. The study is guided by Wolfgang Iser’s reader\-response theory to contemplate textual accuracy, competing interpretations, uncertainty and explanation versus evidence\. These responses generally adjust to the perspective requested but once in a while mistakenly credit a fundamental statement in a final scene\. The case study demonstrates the interpretive advantage of generative AI as well as its need for textual verification, whilst acknowledging its limited data set
