# TD3\-Based Scheduling of Energy and Carbon Flows in Integrated Energy Systems of Oil and Gas Stations

**Autores:** Shasha Dong; Qian Li; Chang Su; Fengyuan Liu; Xiaoyu Yang; Luyao Hu; Shengnan Deng; Yuqing Ren

**Data:** 2026-10-13 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

This study examines intraday scheduling of energy and carbon flows in integrated energy systems for multiple oil and gas stations\. A mixed\-integer linear programming model with fair\-grid carbon accounting is used as a deterministic benchmark, while the same problem is recast as a finite\-horizon Markov decision process and solved using a Twin Delayed Deep Deterministic Gradient\-based continuous\-control policy\. The workflow is also mapped to modular service components from a service\-oriented computing perspective\. In a representative three\-station daily case, the trained policy achieves a mean operating cost of 572,309 RMB and mean fair\-grid carbon emissions of 57\.59 tCO2 over five random seeds\. Relative to the zero\-control baseline, it reduces mean cost by 18\.67% and emissions by 76\.52%, while its mean cost remains 5\.59% above the fair\-grid mixed\-integer linear programming benchmark\. Runtime, carbon\-emission reward\-weight sensitivity, and renewable\-profile perturbation tests suggest that the learned policy has potential for fast online scheduling, although its performance is still evaluated under a limited representative\-day setting\.
