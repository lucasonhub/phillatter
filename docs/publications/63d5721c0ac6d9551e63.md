# Hypoxic conditioning of neuroblastoma cells promotes CD8\+ T\-cell dysfunction through the HIF\-1α/PD\-L1 axis

**Autores:** Yujin Chao; Guili Zheng

**Data:** 2026-10-14 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Objective To investigate whether hypoxic conditioning of neuroblastoma \(NB\) cells induces CD8 \+ T cell dysfunction/exhaustion\-like phenotype via the HIF\-1 α /PD\-L1 axis and whether targeting this axis reverses immune suppression\. Methods Human NB cell lines SK\-N\-SH, SK\-N\-BE\(2\), and SK\-N\-AS were exposed to 1% O₂ hypoxia\. HIF\-1 α and PD\-L1 expression were assessed by Western blot, qRT\-PCR, and flow cytometry\. ChIP\-qPCR and dual\-luciferase assays were used to verify HIF\-1 α binding and transcriptional activation of the PD\-L1 promoter\. SK\-N\-SH cells pre\-conditioned under normoxia or hypoxia were co\-cultured with primary human CD8 \+ T cells\. CD8 \+ T cell dysfunction/exhaustion\-like phenotype \(PD\-1 \+ TIM\-3 \+ \), apoptosis, IFN\- γ , Granzyme B, CD107a, and specific cytotoxicity were evaluated\. HIF\-1 α siRNA, YC\-1, and PD\-L1 neutralizing antibody were applied\. The direct effect of YC\-1 on T cells was also assessed\. Results Hypoxia increased HIF\-1 α and PD\-L1 expression in a time\-dependent manner and also upregulated PD\-L1 in additional NB cell lines\. ChIP\-qPCR and dual\-luciferase assays confirmed direct HIF\-1 α binding to the HRE \(5’\-AGGTGC\-3’\) and transcriptional activation\. PD\-L1 elevation declined after reoxygenation\. Hypoxia\-preconditioned NB cells increased PD\-1 \+ TIM\-3 \+ CD8 \+ T cells \(5\.49%–32\.24%\) and apoptosis \(10\.61%–26\.89%\), while reducing IFN\- γ \+ , Granzyme B \+ , CD107a \+ cells and specific cytotoxicity \(79\.94%–38\.40%\)\. HIF\-1 α knockdown or PD\-L1 blockade alleviated these effects\. YC\-1 at 25 μM did not significantly affect T cell viability or IFN\- γ secretion\. Conclusion Hypoxic conditioning of NB cells stabilizes HIF\-1 α , enhances its binding to the PD\-L1 promoter HRE, upregulates PD\-L1, and induces CD8 \+ T cell dysfunction/exhaustion\-like phenotype and impaired antitumor function\. Targeting HIF\-1 α or PD\-L1 partially restores T cell function in this co\-culture model\. The hypoxia\-driven HIF\-1 α /PD\-L1 axis contributes to immune suppression in NB and may be a potential therapeutic target\.
