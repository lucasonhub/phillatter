# Bulut, Levent: Why AI Cannot Write Emotional Scenes: Objective Projection as a Framework for Auditable Narrative Generation

**Autores:** 

**Data de descoberta:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** unknown

## Descrição do feed

\_Zenodo\_\. 2026Large language models consistently produce emotionally labeled prose when prompted to generate affective narrative content\. This paper argues that the failure is not technical but architectural: current AI systems optimize for statistical pattern reproduction, reproducing the most frequent emotional label constructions in their training corpora rather than encoding the physical conditions that produce physiological responses in readers\. We introduce Objective Projection \(Nesnel İzdüşüm\), a narrative engineering methodology that replaces emotional labels with measurable physical parameters derived from the Autonomic Activation Window \(AAW\) of target physiological states\. The methodology&\#x27;s central claim — that physical parameter specification produces more consistent, more cross\-culturally stable, and more auditable narrative output than emotional label specification — is formalized through the concept of Narrative Entropy \(Sₙ = ∫\(If × Cb\) dt\), where Information Friction \(If\) and Causal Branching \(Cb\) function as measurable proxies for reader engagement\. We document the Output Layer principle, which specifies that physical parameters govern narrative generation without appearing in the generated text, and present the Objective Projection Calibration Test \(OPCT v2\.0, pre\-registered OSF as an empirical validation framework\. The paper concludes that Objective Projection offers the digital humanities a reproducible, parameter\-based approach to narrative generation that is amenable to systematic evaluation and independent replication\. Keywords: narrative engineering, large language models, emotional generation, physical parameters, Objective Projection, Narrative Entropy, digital humanities, prompt engineering, computational narratology \(direct link \)
