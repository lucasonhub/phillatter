# Montoya, Martín &amp; Montoya Camacho, Luis: Artificial intelligence and person\-centered education

## Descrição do feed

\_Apuntes de Bioética\_ 9 \(2\)\. 2026Artificial intelligence \(AI\) currently occupies a central place in the debate on the use of technology within person\-centered educational ecosystems\. Its capacity to manage information and support complex cognitive tasks calls for a critical evaluation of its integration into contexts whose purpose is formative rather than merely instrumental\. This article proposes a conceptual framework for such integration, grounded in a robust understanding of human nature, the ambivalent power of technique, and the role of justice and friendship in Aristotle, together with the virtue ethics developed by the philosopher Alasdair MacIntyre\. \(direct link \)
