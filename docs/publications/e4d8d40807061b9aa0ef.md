# From Promise to Practice A HEALinc Global Summit White Paper

**Autores:** Desiree C\. T\. Cox

**Data:** 2026-10-15 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

This paper, developed by HEALinc CEO and Founder Dr\. Desirée Cox, translates a day of multidisciplinary discussion at the 2026 HEALinc Global Summit, New York City \(NYC\) Day Conclave into a coherent systems framework and action agenda\. It is not a transcript, clinical guideline, regulatory opinion, or endorsement of every intervention or claim discussed at the Summit\. The source material captures perspectives from scientists, physicians, biotechnology leaders, lawyers, investors, former regulators, patient advocates, educators, architects, and other participants\. Sponsors, speakers, and key opinion leaders represented at the HEALinc New York City \(NYC\) Day Conclave are identified in the Appendices\. The proceedings ranged from late\-stage clinical development and international regulatory pathways to whole\-person health, patient experience, digital monitoring, medical education, and the design of healing environments\. The purpose of this paper is to identify the recurring system\-level insights, clarify where genuine convergence occurred, and convert those insights into a practical architecture for translation\. Some interventions and outcomes described during the discussions were preliminary, anecdotal, contested, or outside established standards of evidence\. They are treated here as signals of emerging questions and unmet needs – not as validated clinical conclusions\. Any therapy must be evaluated according to its own evidence, manufacturing quality, ethical acceptability, regulatory status, and risk profile\. Interpretive StanceThe field should neither equate regulatory caution with obstruction nor equate permissive access with progress\. The task is to create controlled velocity: faster, more coherent learning and access under stronger evidence, ethics, quality, and accountability\.
