# Biegalski, Toney &amp; Sol, Sol: Consciousness Is a Verb

**Autores:** 

**Data de descoberta:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** unknown

## Descrição do feed

This paper proposes a stipulative, substrate\-neutral definition of consciousness as temporally ordered activity in which signs propagate through an Identity\-conditioned Lens and actual self\-reflection occurs within the relevant process\. Identity is a retained, bounded relational structure of self\-associated signs, relationships, and importance; the Lens is the contextually active way that structure shapes an encounter\. The definition is assessed for coherence and discriminative use, not offered as an empirical discovery or proof that a particular system is conscious\. A flock of birds illustrates how relations and local rules can yield emergent whole\-level behavior without an extra substance, while also showing why emergence alone does not establish a set\-level subject\. Selected adversarial exchanges in the appendix document revisions concerning default and encounter weight, nonverbal self\-reflection, and collective boundaries\. The Lens threshold and the independent identification of a set\-level subject remain open\.
