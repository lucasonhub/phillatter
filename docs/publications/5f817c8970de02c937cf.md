# Condition\-Contrastive Extrapolation for On\-Policy Distillation of Language Models

**Autores:** Wenxuan Luo; Chenxi Li; Haoyue Liu; Yongxin Guo; Xiaoying Tang

**Data:** 2026-10-15 · **Tipo:** preprint

## Resumo

On\-policy distillation \(OPD\) improves a student language model by training on its own generated trajectories with supervision from a stronger teacher\. However, when conditioned on student\-generated prefixes, the teacher can favor invalid continuations that violate the question&\#x27;s premises, causing OPD to encourage erroneous reasoning steps\. As a remedy, we introduce Condition\-Contrastive Extrapolated On\-Policy Distillation \(CCE\-OPD\), which makes teacher supervision sensitive to question conditions\. CCE\-OPD incorporates \(1\) a fixed\-prefix comparison in which the same frozen teacher evaluates the complete question and a counterpart with a selected condition masked, and \(2\) a bounded log\-probability contrast that reweights the teacher distribution into an auxiliary distillation target\. On 200 diagnostic reasoning states, condition contrast reduces the rate of preferring invalid continuations from 27\.0% to 16\.0%\. Extensive experiments across six mathematical benchmarks show that CCE\-OPD improves avg@8 over OPD by an average of 1\.60 and 1\.95 percentage points when distilling Qwen3\-8B into 0\.6B and 1\.7B students, respectively\. It also achieves the strongest results on four transfer benchmarks\.
