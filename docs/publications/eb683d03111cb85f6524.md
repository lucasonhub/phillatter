# A multi\-dimensional resilience assessment framework for indoor aging\-friendly environments: LLM\-assisted indicator construction using Google Gemini Pro

**Autores:** Quan Wen; Mazran Ismail; Muhammad Hafeez Abdul Nasir

**Data:** 2026-10-15 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Assessment of aging\-friendly housing commonly separates accessibility, functional support, indoor environmental quality, and safety technology, making it difficult to examine how dwellings accommodate changing occupant and environmental conditions\. This framework\-development study introduces SAFER \(Spatial, Adaptive, Functional, Environmental, Responsive\), a five\-dimensional resilience\-oriented indicator system\. A four\-stage workflow combined a systematic review that retained 186 publications and yielded 312 candidate indicators, Gemini Pro\-assisted first\-pass screening, expert content\-validity assessment, and a preliminary application to six dwellings\. Eighteen resilience\-framework indicators were added to the review\-derived pool, and 43 multidisciplinary experts assessed indicator importance and content validity under research\-team oversight\. The resulting expert\-reviewed framework contains 47 indicators\. All 43 final AHP matrices satisfied the prespecified consistency criterion \(CR ≤ 0\.10\) and were included in the group aggregation; the dimension\-level AHP weights were Spatial \(0\.162\), Adaptive \(0\.231\), Functional \(0\.145\), Environmental \(0\.264\), and Responsive \(0\.198\), with Environmental and Adaptive highest\. A preliminary application to six purposively selected dwellings \(n = 6\) produced primary AHP\-weighted scores from 42\.8 to 80\.9\. Equal, case\-derived entropy, and 0\.5 AHP–entropy hybrid weighting were used only as secondary sensitivity analyses; the case ordering was unchanged under these alternatives, although the small, non\-independent case set means that this sensitivity result does not establish external validity\. SAFER should therefore be understood as an expert\-reviewed framework with aggregate content\-validity evidence and preliminary feasibility results, not as a fully validated assessment instrument\. Larger independent samples, preserved assessor\-level records, and cross\-model LLM comparisons are required before normative thresholds or broad policy use\.
