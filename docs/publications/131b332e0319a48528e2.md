# ORGANIZATIONAL AND METHODOLOGICAL APPROACH TO THE USE OF ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE TOOLS IN THE SYSTEM ANALYST WORK LIFECYCLE

**Autores:** Y\.V\. Dubrovsky

**Data:** 2026-10-10 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

The article examines the use of artificial intelligence tools in the work lifecycle of a system analyst when preparing requirements, specifications, and related project documents\. It is shown that the main practical effect of AI is determined not by the mere use of a language model, but by the quality of context preparation, templates, rules, and result verification procedures\. It is argued that high initial requirements for AI prompts and the cost of transferring broad context may lead analysts to skepticism after their first unsuccessful attempts to use AI\. An organizational and methodological approach to building auxiliary support tools for AI\-assisted work is proposed, including artifact templates, query scenarios, style and content rules, and acceptance control criteria\. It is shown that this approach reduces result uncertainty, improves the predictability of document structure and quality, and creates conditions for the gradual development of analyst skills in working with AI\. The conclusion is drawn that it is advisable to treat AI not as a standalone replacement for analytical work, but as a managed tool for supporting the lifecycle of analytical artifacts\.
