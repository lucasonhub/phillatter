# A Generalized McDiarmid Inequality: Sample Reuse and Non\-Uniform Unbounded Differences

**Autores:** Jeremiah Birrell

**Data:** 2026-10-12 · **Tipo:** preprint

## Resumo

We develop a novel generalization of McDiarmid&\#x27;s concentration inequality and use it to obtain a uniform law of large numbers result and error bounds on stochastic optimization problems which simultaneously incorporate two key features: 1\) They capture the benefit of training sample reuse in algorithms that also employ easily\-sampled auxiliary random variables\. 2\) They allow for one\-component mean\-difference bounding functions which are both unbounded in the component of interest and have non\-uniform dependence on another component\. We demonstrate the utility of our method by deriving concentration inequalities for score\-based generative modeling\. This application naturally leverages both key features of our new tools: 1\) It utilizes auxiliary Gaussian random variables in conjunction with the training samples; accounting for training sample reuse results in tighter error bounds by effectively integrating out the dependence on the auxiliary random variables\. 2\) The ability to handle non\-uniform mean\-difference bounding functions allows us to treat unbounded score models and data distributions with unbounded support\.
