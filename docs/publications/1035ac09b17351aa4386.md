# ROC curve analysis for functional markers

**Autores:** Ana M\. Bianco; Graciela Boente; Juan Carlos Pardo–Fernández

**Data:** 2026-10-09 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Functional markers have become a frequent tool in medical diagnosis\. In this paper, we aim to define a discriminating index to distinguish two populations when the observations are functional data belonging to a Hilbert space\. We discuss some of the problems arising when estimating optimal directions defined to maximize the area under the curve of a projection index and we construct the corresponding ROC curve\. We also go one step further and consider the case of possibly different covariance operators, for which we recommend the ROC curve based on a quadratic discrimination rule\. Under mild conditions, consistency results for the ROC estimators are derived for general indexes, including both linear and quadratic ones\. The results of our numerical experiments allow to see the advantages of the quadratic rule when the populations have different covariance operators\. We also illustrate the considered methods on a real data set on cardiotoxicity related to therapies on breast cancer patients\.
