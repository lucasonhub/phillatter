# Inoculum\-dependent activity of contemporary β\-lactam agents against carbapenemase\- producing Enterobacterales

**Autores:** Tokeshwar Kumar Sahu; Pravin Gadkari; Jaishriram Rathored

**Data:** 2026-10-12 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Carbapenemase\-producing Enterobacterales \(CPE\) pose a major therapeutic challenge because antimicrobial susceptibility measured at standard inoculum may not reflect activity at higher bacterial burdens\. This Mini Review examines the biological basis, carbapenemase dependence, and agent\-specific expression of inoculum effects among contemporary β\-lactams\. Increasing bacterial density can enhance β\-lactamase burden and extracellular carbapenemase activity, alter the effective drug\-to\-enzyme relationship, and interact with permeability defects, bacterial physiology, and antimicrobial exposure\. Available evidence shows substantial heterogeneity across carbapenemases and agents\. Ceftazidime\-avibactam appears comparatively stable in several CPE studies, whereas cefiderocol, aztreonam\-avibactam, and other β\-lactam/β\-lactamase inhibitor combinations can show marked density\-dependent reductions in activity under specific conditions\. However, in vitro MIC increases do not consistently translate into reduced in vivo efficacy\. Standardized testing approaches, mechanism\-informed susceptibility assessment, and clinically relevant pharmacokinetic and pharmacodynamic studies are needed to clarify the therapeutic significance of inoculum\-dependent β\-lactam activity\.
