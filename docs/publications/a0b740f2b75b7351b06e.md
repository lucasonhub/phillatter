# Johnson, Jamison: On Observing \- The Power of Timeless Art and Artifacts

**Autores:** 

**Data de descoberta:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** unknown

## Descrição do feed

When was the last time looking at something ancient changed you, and did you realize what you were looking at? This lyric\-philosophical essay stands three observers before the same bronze Buddha and the same Christ the Redeemer: a scientist who traces the metal back to dying stars, a believer who receives the message whole, and a visitor who swears the thing hums\. Taking its shape from relational quantum mechanics and QBism, and its warrant from the older philosophy of perspective \(Nietzsche, Goodman, Putnam, Nagel\), it holds that the power of an artifact lives in the relation between object and observer, which Walter Benjamin called aura\. The singing Colossus of Memnon gives the hum a precedent: a stone that sounded at dawn on many mornings for two centuries, heard as physics by some and as a king greeting his mother by others\. An observer whose Jays were aligned would let every true relation sound at once\. If something ancient has changed you, you were looking at the relation\. \(direct link \)
