# The effects of artificial intelligence feedback on the writing proficiency of English majors in a Chinese context

**Autores:** Jie Li; Supei Wang

**Data:** 2026-10-14 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Artificial intelligence feedback has become a hot topic in second language writing research\. Although previous studies have yielded fruitful findings, most of them have adopted a cross\-sectional research design, failing to demonstrate the long\-term effects of AI feedback\. In view of this, the current study adopts a longitudinal design to study the effects of AI feedback on the writing proficiency of 72 English majors at a comprehensive university in northwest China\. The writing proficiency was evaluated via lexical complexity, syntactic complexity and accuracy\. Findings revealed that AI feedback had a significant positive effect on English majors&\#x27; language accuracy and lexical complexity, but had no effect on their syntactic complexity\. This study finally highlights a few implications for the use of AI in second language writing\.
