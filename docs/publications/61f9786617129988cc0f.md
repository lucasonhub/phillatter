# Deep Learning\-Based Japanese Speech Recognition for Robust Organizational Knowledge Capture

**Autores:** Zhiyuan Li

**Data:** 2026-10-15 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Online Japanese teaching and organizational knowledge management depend on reliable Japanese automatic speech recognition, while existing conformer models perform poorly amid regional dialects, complex phonetics, and background noise\. This paper proposes an improved conformer connectionist temporal classification framework with multi\-scale acoustic feature enhancement, dialect adaptation layers, bidirectional acoustic\-language model fusion, and multi\-task training\. Tests on 800 hours of standard, mixed\-dialect, and noisy speech data and three months of real\-scene deployment show the model improves dialect recognition accuracy by nearly 15% and reduces word error rate by 12% under heavy noise, with only 5 milliseconds longer inference latency than the baseline\. Separate module tests show each design strengthens noise resistance, dialect adaptability, and long\-sentence modeling\. The framework balances accuracy and efficiency, supporting real\-time oral teaching feedback and structured extraction of implicit enterprise knowledge from voice recordings\.
