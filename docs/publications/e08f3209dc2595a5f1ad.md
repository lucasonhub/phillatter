# Mass cytometry of early immune remodeling after mRNA booster vaccination reveals exploratory CD4 naive T\-cell associations with documented breakthrough infection

**Autores:** Xuanjing Yu; Xinyu Jiang; Yupeng Xue; Yuqi Ji; Chunmei Li; Zijie Scott Zhang; Rui Cheng

**Data:** 2026-10-15 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Background Messenger RNA \(mRNA\) vaccines rapidly engage innate and adaptive immunity, but the systemic immune changes occurring within 24 h of vaccination remain incompletely understood\. We investigated whether early cellular and global H3K27ac/m6A changes were associated with subsequent documented breakthrough infection \(BTI\)\. Methods We performed an exploratory longitudinal systems\-immunology analysis of an outcome\-balanced subset of 124 participants from a randomized trial of COVID\-19 mRNA booster vaccination \(62 BTI and 62 non\-BTI\)\. Peripheral blood mononuclear cells collected at Day 0 and Day 1 were profiled by high\-dimensional mass cytometry\. Seventeen major immune\-cell populations were assessed for changes in relative abundance, marker profiles, and global H3K27ac/m6A signals\. Neutralizing\-antibody responses were measured through Day 180\. Results Within 24 h, booster vaccination induced rapid lineage\-specific remodeling of circulating immune composition\. Classical and intermediate monocytes showed positive shifts in CLR\-transformed relative abundance, whereas pDCs, NK cells, and selected CD8 and unconventional T\-cell populations showed negative shifts\. CD4 T\-cell populations remained comparatively stable on this relative\-composition scale\. Early H3K27ac and m6A changes correlated with abundance changes in selected innate and T\-cell populations\. Associations with later humoral immunity were limited: after exclusion of antibody measurements obtained on or after documented BTI, no association between early H3K27ac/m6A changes and Day 14 or Day 180 neutralizing\-antibody responses remained significant after multiple\-testing correction\. Major\-cell abundance trajectories did not differ significantly by subsequent BTI status\. In contrast, BTI was associated with smaller Day 0\-to\-Day 1 changes in CD4\_Tn IFN–Th1 chemokine, cytotoxic–Th1\-associated, and CCL2–CCR2\-associated marker scores and in the m6A readout\. Conclusions mRNA booster vaccination induces rapid, cell\-type\-specific immune remodeling within 24 h\. Major\-cell abundance changes were broadly shared across BTI groups, whereas early CD4\_Tn marker\-associated and m6A signal changes were associated with subsequent documented BTI\. These findings support further evaluation of dynamic early immune states as correlates of heterogeneity in vaccine responses\.
