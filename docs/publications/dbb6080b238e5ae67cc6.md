# Leveraging Automation and Machine Learning To Bridge the Sustainability Data Gap

**Autores:** Eric T\. Sletten; Lenz Fiedler; Pooja Dwivedi; Ilia Dorokhov; Peter H\. Seeberger; Theodore Tyrikos‐Ergas

**Data:** 2026-10-14 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Abstract Materials with minimal environmental impact are urgently needed if we want to meet climate goals\. The chemical industry, responsible for approximately 5% of global greenhouse gas emissions, is a critical sector for reducing or eliminating human\-caused carbon dioxide\. Designing inherently sustainable products and processes, emphasizing renewable feedstocks, greener solvents, and energy\-efficient methods are at the core of “sustainable chemistry”\. However, robust metrics and comprehensive chemical data are needed to measure the progress toward sustainability\. Life cycle sustainability assessment is a key framework for fully quantifying potential sustainability impact but has several limitations that need to be overcome including a disconnect between the data demands of AI for predictive sustainability metrics and the data that exist\. Advanced data\-driven methods, coupled with standardized data workflows, can help bridge these gaps\. Integrating ML with automated experimentation enables closed\-loop optimization, embedding sustainability directly into chemical process design while acquiring high fidelity data, which can be later used\.
