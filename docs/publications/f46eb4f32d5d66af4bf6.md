# AWARE\-RG: Abstraction Aware Explanation of Resiliency Graphs for Cyber Physical Systems

**Autores:** Shadaab Kawnain Bashir; Rakesh Podder; Philip D\. Hopkins; Sarath Sreedharan; Indrakshi Ray; Indrajit Ray

**Data:** 2026-10-14 · **Tipo:** preprint

## Resumo

Understanding the security posture of cyber physical systems \(CPS\) requires reasoning across both information technology \(IT\) and operational technology \(OT\)\. However, a security analyst often holds an incomplete, role\-specific view of the underlying system which has most likely resulted from their knowledge limited by a need\-to\-know framework\. Such an incomplete view can potentially lead them to incorrectly assess if a cyber attack produces an untoward physical consequence resulting in making uninformed decisions about system resilience\. In this work, we present AWARE\-RG \(Abstraction Aware Explanation of Resiliency Graphs\), a semi\-automated, human\-in\-the\-loop framework that provides an analyst with a role\-bounded, need\-to\-know explanations of resiliency paths in a CPS\. The AWARE\-RG abstraction structure is derived using Formal Concept Analysis over MITRE ATT&amp;CK for ICS tactics, ordered by the ICS Cyber Kill Chain\. For valid attack\-fault queries, plan repair and backward means\-end analysis identify the missing dependencies, explain why the physical hazard is reachable, and suggest defensive strategies\. For invalid attack\-fault queries, AWARE\-RG localizes the first abstraction level at which the path fails, and reports confirmed blocking conditions as hardening pointers\.
