# AAOIFI Protocols, the EPI/EVI Dual\-Core Framework, and the Conditions Artificial Intelligence Creates for Islamic Model Deployment

**Autores:** Syed Muntasir Mamun

**Data:** 2026-10-11 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

This paper examines three linked elements: the principal protocols of the Accounting and Auditing Organization for Islamic Financial Institutions \(AAOIFI\); the author&\#x27;s prior writings on the EPI/EVI indices and the Dual\-Core framework for mapping and filling institutional voids; and the specific conditions under which artificial intelligence creates a favourable environment for the deployment of authentic Islamic financial models\. The argument is that AAOIFI supplies the substance standard, the EPI/EVI Dual\-Core apparatus supplies the institutional\-diagnostic and instrument\-mapping method, and AI reduces the monitoring, information and screening frictions that have historically constrained pure risk\-sharing and asset\-backed deployment\. The paper is a working synthesis\. It is not a fatwa and does not represent the position of any government or institution\.
