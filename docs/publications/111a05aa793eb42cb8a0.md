# YO‘L\-QURILISH TA’LIMIDA ZAMONAVIY RAQAMLI TEXNOLOGIYALARDAN FOYDALANISHNING PEDAGOGIK ASOSLARI

**Autores:** JAHONGIR AKBAR O&\#x27;G&\#x27;LI YANGIBOYEV

**Data:** 2026-10-10 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

This article analyzes the pedagogical foundations of using modern digital technologies in road construction education\. It examines the didactic and methodological potential of digital technologies in preparing future road construction specialists for their professional activities\. The role of GIS, BIM, AutoCAD, Civil 3D, QGIS, 3D modeling, drones, artificial intelligence, the Internet of Things \(IoT\), GPS, and digital geodesy technologies in the educational process is identified\. The study substantiates the potential of digital technologies to develop students’ spatial visualization, independent thinking, design skills, problem\-solving abilities, information management skills, and professional competencies\.
