# From cubic semen profiles to body form: linking seminal dynamics with LAS\-derived zoometrics in Murciano\-Granadina bucks

**Autores:** María Pía Peláez Caro; Ander Arando Arbulu; Juan Vicente Delgado Bermejo; José Manuel León Jurado; Javier Fernández Álvarez; Francisco Javier Navas González

**Data:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Introduction Selection of Murciano\-Granadina goats has traditionally relied on Linear Appraisal Systems \(LAS\) to evaluate morphometric traits associated with dairy productivity\. However, the relationship between LAS\-derived traits and male reproductive performance remains insufficiently explored\. This study aimed to assess the association between morphometric characteristics and semen quality parameters in bucks under standardized breeding conditions\. Methods A total of 6,868 ejaculates were recorded, of which 1,901 ejaculates from 77 bucks with complete LAS data were included in the analysis\. The sample was balanced by age \(≤2 years: 53\.25%; &gt;2 years: 46\.75%\), allowing age\-stratified inference\. Ten morphometric traits were evaluated alongside semen parameters, including ejaculate volume, sperm concentration, total motility, progressive motility, acrosome integrity, and endosmosis\. Multicollinearity diagnostics confirmed independence among explanatory variables \(all VIF &lt; 5\)\. Associations were assessed using cubic regression models and correlation analyses\. Results Cubic regression models explained up to 72% of the variance in ejaculate volume, which showed strong positive correlations with chest width \(r = 0\.61, p &lt; 0\.01\) and rump width \(r = 0\.58, p &lt; 0\.01\)\. Sperm concentration was positively associated with stature \(r = 0\.55, p &lt; 0\.05\) and chest width \(r = 0\.49, p &lt; 0\.05\)\. Motility parameters exhibited dynamic patterns, with progressive motility primarily explained by quadratic effects and total motility by cubic trends, particularly influenced by chest width and rump depth \(r = 0\.46 \-0\.52, p &lt; 0\.05\)\. Acrosome integrity showed strong baseline and cubic effects, while endosmosis followed a linear trend \(R² = 0\.41\)\. Processed semen traits reduced variability, whereas real added volume preserved biologically meaningful differences\. Discussion LAS\-derived morphometric traits \-\-especially chest width, rump width, rump angularity, general angularity, and stature \-\-demonstrated consistent and biologically meaningful associations with semen quality, although effect sizes were moderate\. These findings highlight the potential of integrating morphometric evaluation with reproductive assessment to enhance both dairy and reproductive performance in Murciano\-Granadina goats\. Incorporating reproductive indicators into LAS\-based selection frameworks could improve breeding efficiency and genetic progress\.
