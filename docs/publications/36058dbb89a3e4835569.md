# Knowledge and Implementation of Perioperative Safety Standards Among Perioperative Nurses in a Nigerian Teaching Hospital: An Observational Study

**Autores:** The Operating Room Global \(TORG\); Dorcas Iyanuoluwa Odunlade; Myada A\. Ibrahim

**Data:** 2026-10-14 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Introduction: Surgical safety remains a critical concern globally due to the significant risk of adverse events during operative procedures and perioperative nurses play an important role in ensuring safe surgical care through adherence to these established safety standards\. Objective: The study assessed knowledge, and level of implementation of perioperative safety standards in the operating theatre of UNIOSUN Teaching Hospital, Osogbo, Osun State\. Method: An observational study design using a quantitative research method was used\. Data was collected using a self\-designed questionnaire developed by the World Health Organization \(WHO\) safety checklist and Association of Perioperative Nurses \(AORN\) guidelines\. Data was collected from 14 participants that represented both the sample size and target population\. Data was analyzed using descriptive statistics\. Results were presented in frequency tables, percentages and charts\. Results: Findings revealed that all participants \(14, 100%\) had good knowledge of perioperative safety standards\. 12\(85\.7%\) participants demonstrated a high level of implementation while 2\(14\.3%\) demonstrated low level\. Although high implementation was reported for several safety practices, the WHO surgical safety checklist and surgical smoke evacuation showed areas requiring improvement\. Conclusion: This study suggests that perioperative nurses had high formal exposure, strong knowledge of perioperative safety standards, and high self\-reported implementation, indicating a favorable foundation for safe surgical nursing practice\. However, published literature consistently shows that knowledge alone does not guarantee sustained compliance, and that stronger outcomes usually require continuing education, supportive leadership, teamwork, adequate resources, and audit\-based monitoring\. Keywords: Perioperative Nurses, Perioperative Safety Standards, Knowledge, Implementation\.
