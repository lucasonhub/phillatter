# Analisis Dinamika Sosial\-Ekologis dan Keadilan Lingkungan: Keresahan dan Harapan Masyarakat terhadap Pembangunan Fasilitas Pergudangan di Desa Liang Anggang

**Autores:** Rijali Noor; Nova Annisa; Karisadini Karisadini

**Data:** 2026-10-13 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

The establishment of a 25,234 m² warehousing complex in Liang Anggang Village, Tanah Laut Regency, creates complex socio\-ecological dynamics alongside local economic opportunities\. This study evaluates community expectations and environmental concerns during the Environmental Impact Assessment \(EIA/AMDAL\) public consultation\. Primary data were collected from 16 key representatives and analyzed through thematic frequency mapping and qualitative evaluation\. Findings reveal a pronounced disparity: socio\-economic demands accounted for 82\.8% of total inputs, prioritizing a 70% local labor quota, flexible educational requirements, vocational training, and BUMDes partnerships\. Conversely, environmental issues comprised only 17\.2%, addressing hazardous waste, wastewater treatment, traffic, and noise\. This low environmental concern reflects structural socio\-economic vulnerability and technical literacy barriers rather than minimal ecological risks\. Consequently, this study outlines collaborative multi\-stakeholder governance, transparent recruitment mechanisms, grievance channels, and participatory environmental monitoring to ensure environmental justice\.
