# Three\-state method for radio\-channel occupancy assessment using energy detection

**Autores:** Bohdan Kovalchuk; Serhii Ivko

**Data:** 2026-10-17 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Dynamic spectrum access in cognitive radio systems depends on a reliable estimate of whether a radio channel is occupied\. Energy detection is computationally simple, but at low signal\-to\-noise ratios its single\-threshold binary output becomes unreliable when the noise floor is estimated inaccurately, fading occurs, or the observation is too short\. This study develops a three\-state method for radio\-channel occupancy assessment that combines the energy statistic with the measurement context, identifies an uncertain state, links it to a flagged cause and a corrective action, and leaves the access decision to a separate stage\.
