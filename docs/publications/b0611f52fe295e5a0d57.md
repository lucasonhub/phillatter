# Axtell, Guy: Counter\-Inductive Ideational Immunity and the Maintenance of Religious Belief: Introducing the CICI Project

**Autores:** 

**Data de descoberta:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** unknown

## Descrição do feed

Sincere, thoughtful, and etiologically similar people persist in mutually contrary religious commitments across generations, without converging and without obvious irrationality\. In Problems of Religious Luck: Assessing the Limits of Reasonable Religious Disagreement \(PRL, or Axtell 2020a\) I argued that this stability is best understood through Counter\-Inductive Ideational Immunity \(CICI\): a coordinated battery of cognitive and affective strategies that treat recognized disagreement and etiological symmetry not as reasons to reduce confidence or revise beliefs and attitudes but as pressure to be neutralized so that the commitment is preserved\. This paper introduces the CICI Project — a program that carries forward that taxonomy of strategies and mechanisms from philosophical description into a measurable model\. \[Collaborators are sought to help carry through the study or a future iteration of it, collect, analyze and publish the data; contact author\]
