# The Academic Operating System

**Autores:** Susan L\. Stansberry

**Data:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** book

## Resumo

The Academic Operating System: Successfully Navigating Doctoral Education&\#x27;s Hidden Curriculum is an interactive open educational resource designed to support current and prospective doctoral students by making the implicit expectations of graduate education explicit\. Recognizing that success in doctoral programs depends on more than coursework and research, the resource illuminates the often\-unspoken norms, practices, and expectations that shape the doctoral experience while providing practical guidance for navigating common challenges along the way\.Developed with students in PhD and EdD programs in education and human sciences in mind, the resource draws on experiences from learning, design, and technology programs while offering strategies and insights applicable across a wide range of academic disciplines\. Interactive activities, reflective prompts, and practical resources encourage readers to develop the skills, habits, and confidence needed to thrive throughout their doctoral journey\.
