# Machine\-learning\-assisted integration of single\-cell and bulk transcriptomics characterizes glioblastoma ecosystem states and immune\-associated programs

**Autores:** Jun Cao; Hao Cheng; Yu Wang; Yu Wang; Bing Zhao; Yang Wang; Yang Wang; Xiaochun Jiang

**Data:** 2026-10-14 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Background Glioblastoma \(GBM\) is characterized by marked malignant\-cell plasticity and a complex tumor ecosystem composed of heterogeneous malignant and microenvironmental cell populations\. We integrated single\-cell and bulk transcriptomic data to characterize reproducible GBM cell and ecosystem\-associated states, evaluate cross\-platform concordance, and examine immune\-associated transcriptional programs while distinguishing descriptive associations from mechanistic inference\. Methods The deposited Smart\-seq2 \(7,930 cells\) and 10x \(16,201 cells\) expression matrices from GSE131928/SCP393 were analyzed on their verified expression scales\. Official annotations and malignant\-state modules were audited, four broad malignant states were recomputed, and a tumor\-held\-out machine\-learning label\-transfer framework was applied to the 10x dataset\. Public TCGA\-GBM RNA\-seq data were used to evaluate state\- and microenvironment\-associated transcriptional programs and to perform nested prediction benchmarking\. Selected ecosystem\-associated genes were additionally examined by RT\-qPCR in glioma cell lines and normal human astrocytes\. Results Among Smart\-seq2 cells, 6,576 malignant cells had valid state scores, whereas 287 alias\-affected records were excluded from state\-score denominators\. The machine\-learning label\-transfer model achieved an out\-of\-fold accuracy of 0\.998, and malignant\-state fractions showed moderate\-to\-strong concordance across six shared tumor labels\. At the tumor\-label level, MES\-like fraction was not significantly associated with macrophage fraction \(Spearman rho = 0\.171, P = 0\.395\), indicating that ecosystem\-level transcriptional association should not be interpreted as a simple directional immune\-to\-state mechanism\. In TCGA\-GBM, MES and myeloid\-associated programs correlated with immune and stromal expression signatures, whereas survival and 12\-month prediction performance remained modest and specification\-sensitive\. RT\-qPCR further demonstrated increased STAT3, CHI3L1, SPP1, and VEGFA expression in glioma cell lines relative to normal human astrocytes\. Conclusion Integrated single\-cell and bulk transcriptomic analysis supports reproducible characterization of malignant\-cell heterogeneity and immune\-associated ecosystem features in IDH\-wildtype GBM\. Machine\-learning\-assisted cross\-platform analysis provides a reproducible framework for evaluating tumor ecosystem\-associated states, while the observed immune\-mesenchymal associations remain descriptive rather than causal\. Experimental RT\-qPCR provides expression\-level support for selected ecosystem\-associated genes and highlights testable hypotheses for future functional and spatial studies\.
