# From wetting\-front coalescence to urban road collapse: interpretable machine learning of hydro\-mechanical failure under coupled rainfall and pipeline leakage

**Autores:** Liming Xue; Qiangbing Huang; Jiawei Liang; Yuxuan Gou; Jun Dong

**Data:** 2026-10-10 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Abstract Urban ground collapse poses a severe threat to public safety, road operation and underground infrastructure in densely developed cities\. This study investigates the hydro\-mechanical failure mechanism of road collapse induced by coupled rainfall infiltration and pipeline leakage through physical model testing, finite element simulation and interpretable surrogate modelling\. A validated numerical model incorporating saturation\-dependent strength and stiffness degradation was used to generate 120 cases with different pipe burial depths, rainfall intensities and leakage pressures\.The results indicate that collapse develops through shallow\-soil pre\-wetting, leakage\-induced wetting\-zone expansion, convergence of the upper and lower wetting fronts, and upward propagation of deformation\. Leakage pressure is the dominant factor, contributing 86\.42%, 87\.79% and 92\.77% to the variations in collapse depth, near\-saturated zone area and wetting\-front depth, respectively\. Rainfall mainly dissipates matric suction and lowers the failure threshold, whereas burial depth regulates the wetting\-front convergence and deformation\-transfer path\. GPR/Kriging achieved test\-set \(R 2 \) values of 0\.997–0\.998, while XGBoost\-SHAP confirmed the mechanism of leakage\-pressure domination, rainfall amplification and burial\-depth regulation\. Risk zoning further shows that increasing rainfall shifts high\-risk conditions towards lower leakage pressures, particularly at intermediate burial depths\. The proposed framework provides an efficient tool for identifying vulnerable pipeline sections and supporting targeted inspection and collapse prevention\.
