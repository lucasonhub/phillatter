# A review of multi\-objective optimization on industrial architectural heritage for conservation and sustainable development

**Autores:** Tongtong Liu; Yanhui Wang; Xiangrui Xiong

**Data:** 2026-10-12 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Abstract The conservation and sustainable development of industrial architectural heritage \(IAH\) have become growing priorities under “Dual Carbon” goals\. This review synthesizes literature around three interrelated objectives: multi\-dimensional value conservation, environmental performance enhancement, and low\-carbon transition\. Research has maintained steady growth, with 4\.8% publications in 2006–2009 \(Initial stage\), 34\.6% in 2010–2018 \(Fluctuating ascent stage\), and 60\.6% in 2019–2025 \(Rapid development stage\)\. Seven hotspot clusters indicate shift towards comprehensive and technological heritage regeneration\. Four thematic dimensions reveal trajectories about hierarchical intervention, simulation\-based optimization of physical environment, low\-carbon strategy under different logics, and multi\-objective paradigm in heritage context\. After identifying major limitations, it outlines future directions for IAH and provides integrated framework for multi\-objective optimization in broader heritage buildings\.
