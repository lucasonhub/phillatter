# Settegast, Sascha: Leben und Vernunft: Zum Naturbegriff in der neo\-aristotelischen Tugendethik

**Autores:** 

**Data de descoberta:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** unknown

## Descrição do feed

Neo\-Aristotelians such as Philippa Foot, Michael Thompson, and Rosalind Hursthouse attempt to render the objective validity of ethical norms intelligible in recourse to human nature; an attempt that has been met with considerable criticism\. In particular, the concept of life\-form central to their project is often rejected as incompatible with modern biology and natural science\. Both critics and proponents of neo\-Aristotelian Naturalism however operate with significantly different conceptions of nature, viz\. a scientistic and an idealist one, and therefore tend to talk past each other\. This book undertakes to explicate both conceptions of nature and their respective ontological and epistemological presuppositions\. Its goal is to advance a defense of neo\-Aristotelian Naturalism inspired by the philosophy of John McDowell\. \(direct link \)
