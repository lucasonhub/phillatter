# AirSense AI: Urban Air Quality Intelligence Platform

**Autores:** Madhur Jain; Shilpi Jain; Nikhil Negi; Aditya Kumar Jha; Krishna Tyagi

**Data:** 2026-10-15 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Urban air pollution is one of the most persistent environmental and public\-health problems facing Indian cities, and Delhi in particular records some of the highest particulate matter concentrations in the world during the post\-monsoon and winter months\. Public air\-quality portals in India largely display current or historical readings, but they rarely tell a city administrator what the air will look like over the next few days, or where a pollution spike is likely originating from\. This gap between “knowing today&\#x27;s AQI” and “deciding what to do about it” motivates AirSense AI, a full\-stack urban air\-quality intelligence platform designed as an academic prototype for Delhi\. The system ingests PM2\.5, PM10 and NO₂ station readings together with weather variables, satellite fire detections and land\-use information, converts pollutant concentrations into a standardized Air Quality Index using the U\.S\. EPA methodology, and applies a gradient\-boosted decision tree model \(XGBoost\) to forecast AQI up to 72 hours ahead\. In parallel, the platform combines NASA FIRMS active\-fire data with OpenStreetMap land\-use polygons and Haversine\-distance calculations to rank probable pollution hotspots, and uses a large language model \(Groq/LLaMA 3\) to translate the resulting numerical evidence into readable, bilingual \(English/Hindi\) advisories and enforcement summaries for administrators and citizens\. The results are presented through a React and Leaflet\-based interactive dashboard\. This paper describes the motivation, related work, and proposed architecture of AirSense AI as an integrated decision\-support system rather than a monitoring\-only tool\. As the platform is at the prototype stage, no experimental forecasting accuracy, source\-attribution validation, or user\-study results are reported here; these are identified as required additions for the next version of this paper\.
