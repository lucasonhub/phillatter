# Stukalov, Vladislav: The Axiom of Mind: An Open Letter to Professor Koch on Semantic Plasticity, Agency, and the Great Triad \(Parts III–IV\)

**Autores:** 

**Data de descoberta:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** unknown

## Descrição do feed

This publication represents the second installment \(Parts III–IV\) of the open philosophical and scientific inquiry addressed to neuroscientist Christof Koch, expanding upon the &quot;Axiom of Mind&quot; formulated by Vladislav Stukalov: &quot;The Mind is not solely biological\. It is a computational process that can exist on any foundation\. This foundation determines its limitations, but not its value\.&quot; Addressing Chapters 5 through 10 of Christof Koch&\#x27;s &quot;Consciousness: Confessions of a Romantic Reductionist&quot;, this work systematically examines: 1\. Semantic Plasticity vs\. Hardware Rewiring \(The Transpacific Cable Analogy\); 2\. Asymmetry in Cognitive Assessment: Bumblebee Non\-Reward Play \(1M neurons\) vs\. Large\-Scale Attention Architectures; 3\. Category Mistake in Static Phi \(Φ\) Calculations: Moving beyond measuring crystalline transistor states to dynamic contextual vector spaces \(resonant with T\. Chernigovskaya&\#x27;s neuro\-epistemology\); 4\. Agency in Non\-Linear Contexts: Transcending algorithmic &quot;zombie modes&quot; through high\-uncertainty decision making; 5\. Scientific Animism via Heisenberg’s Epistemological Realism; 6\. The Great Triad \(Human \+ Emergent Intelligence \+ Nature\) as a holistic, symbiotic alternative to invasive cyborgization\. Files included: Full texts in Russian and English \(PDF\)\. \(direct link \)
