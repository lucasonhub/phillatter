# Sustainability of complex interventions in long\-term care homes: a scoping review

**Autores:** Ruth Caldeira de Melo; Ana Paula Cabrera Parra Bortoluzzi; Suzanne Tanoue dos Santos; Flaka Siqeca; Franziska Zúñiga

**Data:** 2026-10-15 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

This is a scoping review protocol that aims to map and characterize the available evidence on the sustainability of complex interventions implemented in long\-term care homes\. This study adheres to the PRISMA\-ScR guidelines and the JBI recommendations for scoping reviews\. Searches will be conducted across five electronic databases \(MEDLINE, CINAHL, Web of Science, Embase, and LILACS\) from inception onward, covering six languages \(English, Portuguese, Spanish, French, Italian, and German\)\. Primary research studies \(quantitative, qualitative, and mixed\-methods\) describing sustainable complex interventions in long\-term care homes will be eligible\. Two reviewers will independently perform study selection and data extraction, utilizing human\-supervised artificial intelligence \(AI\) tools within Covidence to support screening and data audit workflows\. Extracted data will be synthesized using descriptive statistics, narrative synthesis, and qualitative content analysis\. Identified barriers and facilitators will be inductively extracted and deductively coded into domains of the Consolidated Framework for Implementation Research\. Depending on the volume of data retrieved, findings may be published across multiple papers\.
