# Prognostic Implication of a CT\-FFR\-Based Functional SYNTAX Score in Coronary Artery Disease Patients Undergoing Percutaneous Coronary Intervention

**Autores:** Qiaoyu Shao; Uwe Joseph Schoepf; Xiaoteng Ma; Lei Xu; Yujie Zhou; Fei Gao; Zhijian Wang

**Data:** 2026-10-10 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Background: This study aims to investigate the value of a SYNTAX score \(SS\) based on coronary computed tomographic angiography \(cCTA\)\-derived fractional flow reserve \(CT\-FFR\), termed “Functional SYNTAX score” \(FSSCTA\), to predict major adverse cardiovascular events \(MACEs\) in patients undergoing percutaneous coronary intervention \(PCI\)\. Methods: This cohort study included 876 patients who underwent cCTA and underwent PCI within 30 days during 2016–2020\. CT\-FFR was calculated by a machine learning algorithm\. All 876 patients were divided into a low\-risk group \(≤22\) and an intermediate\-to\-high\-risk group \(&gt;22\) with cCTA\-based SS \(SSCTA\) as the control\. Results: Based on the FSSCTA, 10\.4% \(91/876\) of patients were reclassified to the low\-risk group, and 2\.9% \(25/876\) of patients were reclassified to the intermediate\-to\-high\-risk group\. MACEs occurred in 16\.6% \(145/876\) of patients over a median follow\-up period of 19\.4 months\. FSSCTA and invasive coronary angiography\-based SS \(SSICA\) were independently associated with the risk of MACEs regardless of whether they were in continuous or categorical form \(FSSCTA: c continuous HR 1\.082, 95% CI 1\.038–1\.128; categorical HR 1\.865, 95% CI 1\.138–2\.305\. SSICA: continuous HR 1\.045, 95% CI 1\.012–1\.079; categorical HR 1\.520, 95% CI 1\.128–2\.048\)\. FSSCTA showed the best predictive power for MACEs compared with SSCTA and SSICA \(all comparisons with FSSCTAp &lt; 0\.05\)\. Conclusions: FSSCTA, SSCTA and SSICA were all independent predictors of MACEs in patients undergoing PCI, and FSSCTA showed a significantly better predictive value for MACEs than SSCTA and SSICA\.
