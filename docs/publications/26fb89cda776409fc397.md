# Identificación de índices de vegetación relacionados con componentes de rendimiento de frijol común \(Phaseolus vulgaris L\.\) mediante el uso de sensores multiespectrales

**Autores:** Guillermo Vargas\-Hernández; Eddison José Araya\-Morales; Juan Carlos Hernández; Ignacio Mora\-Cordero

**Data:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Bean production in Costa Rica supplies only 20 % of domestic consumption, requiring the importation of the remaining amount; this situation highlights the need to know in advance the actual productive capacity of local farmers to improve planning, agronomic management, and import regulation\. Therefore, the objective of this study was to evaluate the use of multispectral data and vegetation indices to analyze their relationship with yield components in bean varieties cultivated in different production areas of Costa Rica and to identify the yield component with the greatest spectral association\. The research was conducted at three sites located in Alajuela, Costa Rica\. Multispectral reflectance data were collected during the R5 phenological stage using an airborne multispectral sensor\. Subsequently, yield components were quantified per meter of crop row, including 100\-grain weight, number of pods per meter, number of plants, number of grains, and total grain weight\. The number of pods showed the highest association with spectral variables and was therefore identified as the yield component with the greatest potential for analysis through remote sensing\. The SR \(Simple Ratio\) and GCI \(Green Chlorophyll Index\) indices showed correlations greater than 0\.7 with this variable, being identified as the vegetation indices with the highest spectral association during the R5 stage of the crop\.
