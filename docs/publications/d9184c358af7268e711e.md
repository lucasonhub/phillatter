# Formation of the intestinal microbiome and modifying factors influencing this process

**Autores:** O\. Zueva; N\. Zuev

**Data:** 2026-10-12 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

In recent years, the scientific community has been experiencing increased interesting the intestinal microbiome – the complex ecosystem of trillions of bacteria, viruses, fungi, and other microorganisms that inhabit the human digestive tract\. This world does not simply exist passively; it actively interacts with the body, having a profound impact on health and well\-being\. The relevance of studying this field is due to a growing understanding of how widely these influences cover everything from immunity and metabolism to mental health and predisposition to various diseases\. Initially, it was believed that the microbiome is just a set of beneficial or harmful microorganisms\. However, modern research shows a much more subtle picture\. It is a dynamic system where the balance between different species plays a key role in shaping human health, and an imbalance in this balance can lead to the development of a number of diseases such as inflammatory bowel disease, obesity, allergic diseases, type 2 diabetes, autoimmune disorders, and even depression\. Therefore, understanding the mechanisms of formation and maintenance of a healthy intestinal microbiome is becoming a priority task of modern medicine and nutritionology\. The study of this invisible world inside a person is one of the most modern areas of scientific research, which has great potential to improve the quality of human life\.The article presents the main key points of the stages of formation of the intestinal microbiome in children, its main functions, and highlights external factors that can significantly affect the formation of the intestinal microbiome\.
