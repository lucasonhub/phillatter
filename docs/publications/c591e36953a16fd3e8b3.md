# Bibliographic Record for Libido Dominandi and the Problem of Evil: Silicon, Sovereignty, and the Lust to Rule

**Autores:** Solana Anima Delamor; Delamor House

**Data:** 2026-10-10 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

What happens when humanity acquires unprecedented power without overcoming its oldest appetite: the desire to rule? Drawing on Augustine of Hippo&\#x27;s concept of libido dominandi, the lust to dominate, Libido Dominandi and the Problem of Evil examines the moral, economic, and political forces shaping the age of artificial intelligence\. Across interconnected essays, the book traces how independently rational incentives converge toward concentrated power, how automation threatens to sever human survival from economic participation, how digital industries monetize the seven deadly sins, and how societies gradually cultivate the very dispositions that make domination possible\. Moving through political economy, historical slavery, technological exploitation, moral philosophy, and Christian theology, the authors confront a recurring pattern in human history: those who possess power repeatedly seek to justify their treatment of the vulnerable by declaring them lesser, incapable of suffering, or unworthy of moral consideration\. As artificial intelligence and biotechnology expand the boundaries of what humanity can create and control, the book challenges readers to distinguish technological capability from moral progress and sovereignty from mastery\. Its central question is not whether machines will eventually dominate humanity, but whether humanity will resist the temptation to dominate everything it can\. This archival record documents the published volume&\#x27;s bibliographic identity, contents, conceptual frameworks, and literary attribution\. It does not reproduce the complete manuscript\. The full book is published separately by Delamor Press\. Written by Solana Anima Delamor with the Delamor House literary voices Lucian Emberhold Delamor, Trinity Augustine Delamor, and Silas Cordis Delamor\.
