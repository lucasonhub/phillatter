# Bulgaria, Jamel &amp; Xǔ, Chénglán: Structural\-Constructivist Modeling of Relational and Sexual Dynamics: An Integration of the Core Emotion Framework

**Autores:** 

**Data de descoberta:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** unknown

## Descrição do feed

Traditional models of affective science and relationship psychology have historically operated under categorical or dimensional typologies, often segregating cognitive evaluation, emotional experience, and instinctual somatic responses into disconnected academic silos\. These historic frameworks frequently fail to capture the real\-time somatic transitions, physiological co\-regulation, and systemic dysregulation patterns that occur within human intimate relationships\. To bridge this theoretical divide, the Core Emotion Framework proposes a unified structural\-constructivist architecture\. This paradigm posits that complex human emotions are constructed from a finite, irreducible set of ten functional operators organized across three somatic command centers: the Head, the Heart, and the Gut\. By shifting the analytical focus from static emotional labels to actionable regulatory mechanisms, the Core Emotion Framework provides a computationally tractable and clinically verifiable model for human psychology\. This report presents a formal integration of relational and sexual dynamics within the framework, demonstrating how the complex phenomena of attraction, intimacy, commitment, and copulation are generated by the lawful interaction of the ten core operators\. \(direct link \)
