# SUN’IY INTELLEKT VOSITALARI ASOSIDA IT TALABALARNING INGLIZ TILIDAGI KOMMUNIKATIV KOMPETENSIYASINI RIVOJLANTIRISH METODIKASI

**Autores:** Sherdor Ne’Matjonovich Po‘Latov

**Data:** 2026-10-12 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

The article provides theoretical and practical substantiation of a methodology for developing the English communicative competence of information technology students based on artificial intelligence \(AI\) tools\. The linguistic, sociolinguistic, discourse, strategic and professional\-digital components of communicative competence are specified\. A methodological model for integrating AI tools \(generative chatbots, speech recognition systems, automated writing evaluation tools\) into the learning process is developed, comprising the stages Ta’limda istiqbolli izlanishlar \- xalqaro ilmiy\-metodik jurnal № 9/2026 ISSN: 3060\-527X “diagnosis – modelling – professional simulation – reflection”\. A programme of experimental work to verify the effectiveness of the methodology is proposed\.
