# Neutrosophic evidence: Exact bridges between probability, logic, belief functions and learning, extended to refined probability, refined logic and refined belief functions

**Autores:** Florentin Smarandache; Maikel Yelandi Leyva\-Vázquez

**Data:** 2026-10-11 · **Tipo:** book

## Resumo

This book develops exact mathematical connections between neutrosophic evidence, probability, logic, belief functions, and machine learning\. Taking truth, indeterminacy, and falsity as separately assessed components, it establishes correspondences with imprecise probabilities, credal sets, Subjective Logic, and Belnap–Dunn logic, specifying the assumptions and information preserved by each representation\. It extends these connections to contradictory evidence, dependence\-sensitive aggregation, plithogenic models, and signed measures for values outside the unit interval\. Refined representations distinguish contradiction, undetermined evidence, ill\-posed questions, and missing data, supporting statistical estimation and evidence fusion\. Further developments address conflict redistribution, sound and complete probability logics, typed evidential learning, multi\-view clustering, calibrated conflict scores, and conformal tests with bounded false\-alarm rates\. The framework is extended to refined probability, refined logic, and refined belief functions\. Proofs, worked examples, computational tools, and empirical studies clarify both the capabilities and limitations of the proposed connections, providing a foundation for reasoning and learning with uncertain, incomplete, and conflicting evidence\.
