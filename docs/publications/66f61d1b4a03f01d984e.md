# Angular‐Multiplexed Photoluminescence Meta‐Display via Unidirectional Emission Control

**Autores:** Zejing Wang; Qing Huang; Xinglong Li; Yangyang Shi; Cuiting Wang; Zhongyang Li; Shuai Wan

**Data:** 2026-10-12 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

ABSTRACT Metasurfaces have revolutionized the optical field due to their exceptional wavefront shaping capabilities, boosting the multi‐dimensional light‐field manipulation and emission control\. However, the incoherent, omnidirectional, unpolarized, and phase‐random nature of fluorescence emission limits the applicability of conventional metasurface multiplexing strategies, posing significant challenges for photoluminescence \(PL\) encoding and multi‐dimensional light‐emitting metasurface \(LEM\)‐based display technology\. Here, the PL angular‐multiplexing is proposed and experimentally demonstrated based on unidirectional emission control for multi‐dimensional meta‐display\. By exploiting the inherent numerical aperture limitation of the detection system and coupling PL into the guided mode via an on‐chip metasurface, the LEM‐empowered precise emission angle control is transferred into the angular‐multiplexed intensity encoding degree of freedom, enabling a dual‐channel PL meta‐display\. Furthermore, by incorporating directional multiplexing, the multi‐dimensional LEM achieves four independent PL images corresponding to distinct observation angles and illumination directions\. This PL angular‐multiplexing approach, together with the multi‐dimensional meta‐display architecture, significantly expands the multiplexing encoding capability of fluorescence light‐field manipulation, offering promising opportunities for diverse applications, including next‐generation optical display/encryption techniques, biological sensing, and incoherent illumination engineering\.
