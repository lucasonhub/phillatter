# Development of pH\-Responsive Polymeric Nanoparticles for Targeted Doxorubicin Delivery in Breast Cancer: Advances, Design Strategies, and Future Perspectives

**Autores:** Deepika Gupta; Sourabh Jain; Rohit Rohit; Shelly Shelly; Nivesh P\. Singh Gurjar; Ravindra Balappa Malabadi

**Data:** 2026-10-15 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Breast cancer remains a major cause of cancer\-related mortality among women worldwide\. Although doxorubicin is widely used in chemotherapy, its clinical application is limited by cardiotoxicity, systemic toxicity, and non\-specific drug distribution\. pH\-responsive polymeric nanoparticles offer a promising approach for targeted drug delivery by releasing doxorubicin in the acidic tumor microenvironment while maintaining stability under physiological conditions\. Biodegradable polymers, including PLGA, PEG, chitosan, and poly\(β\-amino esters\), have been investigated for developing these nanocarriers\. Preclinical studies indicate improved intracellular drug delivery, enhanced antitumor activity, and potential reductions in systemic toxicity\. This review highlights recent advances in the design, development, and therapeutic applications of pH\-responsive polymeric nanoparticles for doxorubicin delivery in breast cancer, along with challenges in safety, clinical translation, and regulatory approval\. These systems hold considerable potential for improving targeted cancer therapy\.
