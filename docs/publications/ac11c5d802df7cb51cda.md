# STUDENTS’ PERSPECTIVES AND SIGNIFICANCE OF “WRITE AND IMPROVE” WEB APP

**Autores:** Sainzaya Sumiya; Oyun\-Erdene Tumurbat

**Data:** 2026-10-09 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

This research study focuses on the pedagogical importance and student perspectives of the AI\-driven “Write &amp; Improve” web app in developing English writing skills\. Using a mixed\-methods research design, the study followed 24 students from the 12th grade at Mandakh Lyceum high school during a five\-week experimental period\. The quantitative results, gathered from a 5\-point Likert scale survey, showed that 75% of the participants highly valued the web app because it provides immediate feedback on their work\. However, there was a noticeable gap in how students understood this feedback, as 50\-75% of them felt neutral about whether the automated suggestions were clear or detailed enough\. At the same time, qualitative findings from focus group interviews and teacher observations showed a clear shift toward self\-regulated learning\. These results suggest that while the W&amp;I web app effectively helps students practice a “Plan\-Draft\-Revise” cycle and reduces writing anxiety, its educational impact is most effective when combined with traditional teaching\. The study concludes that the web app should not be seen as a standalone replacement for a teacher, but rather as a powerful supporting tool within a structured classroom environment\. Ultimately, integrating this AI technology allows for a more active and repetitive approach to writing, helping students bridge the gap between receiving basic corrections and achieving true academic progress\.
