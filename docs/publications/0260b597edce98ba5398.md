# Where Polanyi Meets Galois: Tacit Knowing as an Algebro\-Deterministic Operation, Toward Re\-rooting AI\-Generated Science

**Autores:** Hiroyuki Chuma; Kanji Otsuka; Yoichi Sato; Kodo Yamauchi

**Data:** 2026-10-12 · **Tipo:** preprint

## Resumo

We argue that tacit knowing is better modelled as an operating regime of one memory substrate than as a kind of content\. The substrate is VaCoAl, an algebro\-deterministic XOR\-and\-shift hyperdimensional memory over GF\(2\), realized as CASRAM \(commercial SRAM\-CAM\) and PyVaCoAl \(DRAM\-CAM\)\.In VaCoAl/PyVaCoAl a directed acyclic graph \(DAG\) reads two ways\. Repairing every collision \(Rescue mode\) makes the trace exactly auditable but ranks no path higher; tolerating collisions \(Don&\#x27;t Care mode\) yields a path\-integrated confidence, CR$\_2$, that silently favours direct routes\. This audit\-valuation complementarity is a formal counterpart of Polanyi&\#x27;s claim that tacit and explicit knowledge are not sharply divided\. It separates contingent tacitness, which a new query brings out \(AlphaGo&\#x27;s game tree\), from regime\-bound tacitness, statable only in a regime where it is gone \(AlphaGo&\#x27;s hunches\)\. \(1\) Polanyi&\#x27;s from\-to structure reads as non\-commutative binding, the Identikit as role\-filler composition, recognition as majority\-vote coincidence\. \(2\) Explicating the valuation destroys it, as attending to his fingers can make Polanyi&\#x27;s pianist falter, yet a closed\-form law states it\. Polanyi, Nonaka, Tsoukas and Collins each capture part of this but narrowly; even Nonaka and von Krogh&\#x27;s continuum cannot tell contingent from regime\-bound tacitness\. \(3\) Collins&\#x27;s three tacit knowledges become strata, one outside the substrate\. \(4\) Our mentor\-student DAG is a tacit\-transmission graph\. \(5\) Terence Tao&\#x27;s remark that AI\-solved problems spawn few follow\-ups is diagnosed as rootless explicit knowledge: checkable results whose route no one can follow and that no one yet understands well enough to extend, teach or judge\. To re\-root them, LLMs generate, VaCoAl/PyVaCoAl keeps an auditable trace with its confidence, and people value, a role virtue epistemology shows cannot be offloaded\. Testable predictions follow\.
