# TF\-former: A concentration regression model for toxic and harmful mixed gases based on time\-frequency analysis

**Autores:** Xinyu Zhang; 高颂琪; Xiaoran Huang; Peter Feng; Jin Chu

**Data:** 2026-10-13 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Abstract Accurate quantification of individual components in mixed gases remains challenging for metal oxide \(MOX\) electronic noses because cross\-sensitivity can produce highly overlapping sensor responses\. In this study, we propose TF\-Former, a time–frequency Transformer framework for the simultaneous regression of carbon monoxide \(CO\) and ethylene \(C2H4\) concentrations from multichannel MOX sensor signals\. For the public UCI dynamic gas\-mixture dataset, electrical responses from 16 chemiresistive sensors were acquired at 100 Hz under a constant heater voltage of 5 V\. The signals were downsampled to 1 Hz, standardized using statistics estimated from the training data only, and grouped by continuous constant\-concentration exposure intervals to reduce information leakage across the training, validation, and test subsets\. TF\-Former jointly encodes the original time\-domain sequence and single\-level Haar discrete wavelet transform coefficients, and integrates the two representations through bidirectional cross\-domain attention before concentration regression\. Across five random seeds, TF\-Former achieved R2 values of 0\.9960±0\.0019 for CO and 0\.9931±0\.0021 for C2H4 on the grouped UCI evaluation\. On a second MOX\-array dataset evaluated using held\-out concentration combinations, TF\-Former achieved R2 values of 0\.9877±0\.0101 for CO and 0\.9886±0\.0093 for C2H4\. Additional Gaussian\-noise perturbation experiments were conducted to assess prediction stability under corrupted inputs\. These results demonstrate the potential of joint time\-frequency representation learning for concentration estimation of known CO/C2H4 mixtures under the investigated conditions\.
