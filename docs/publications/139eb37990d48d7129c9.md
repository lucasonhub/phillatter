# Guarino, Brian: Consciousness as Observational Coupling: A Defense of the Fourth Dimension Thesis

**Autores:** 

**Data de descoberta:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** unknown

## Descrição do feed

I argue that the thesis &quot;consciousness is the fourth dimension&quot; fails geometrically but succeeds when reframed: consciousness is not a fourth axis but the dimension along which the spatial and temporal axes become observable\. I formalize this as a dimensionless coupling coefficient, Γ\_consciousness, where Ψ\_observed = Ψ\_manifold × Γ\_consciousness, constructed by Buckingham π dimensional analysis on the same pattern as Carnot efficiency, optical mode coupling, and the Born rule\. Drawing variables from integrated information theory and the phenomenology of time consciousness, I derive a bounded composite form and check its limits\. I am explicit that the structure is dimensionally rigorous but not yet empirically rigorous, and mark where empirical anchoring is required\.
