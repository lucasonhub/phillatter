# Generative AI Feedback and Learning Engagement in Distributed Learning Environments: The Roles of Feedback Literacy and Self\-Regulated Learning

**Autores:** Ye Huo

**Data:** 2026-10-11 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Distributed learning environments address the constraints of fixed locations and schedules in traditional education, offering diverse teaching approaches, including online, blended, and lifelong learning\. Learners can make the most of fragmented learning opportunities by engaging in a range of learning activities\. At the same time, generative artificial intelligence \(GenAI\) offers real\-time interaction, personalized adaptation, and dynamic learning opportunities, potentially creating more flexible learning pathways in distributed learning environments\. AI\-generated feedback may also support learners&\#x27; engagement in the learning process\. Feedback literacy and self\-regulated learning may play important roles in supporting learners&\#x27; sustained engagement\. This paper explores how feedback literacy and self\-regulated learning may shape the relationship between generative AI feedback and learner engagement in distributed learning environments\. It also proposes an integrated educational framework that combines AI\-enhanced feedback, competency development, and instructional monitoring and support to promote sustained learning engagement\.
