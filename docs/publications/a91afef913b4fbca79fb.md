# Inclusive Education and Digital Skills Development for Learners with Disabilities in Africa’s Emerging Global Economy

**Autores:** Dr\. Akhogbai Emmanuel Monday Ph\.D

**Data:** 2026-10-15 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

The transition to a digital and knowledge\-driven global economy has increased the demand for advanced technological competencies and adaptable workforce skills\. However, many learners with disabilities in Africa continue to face significant barriers to quality education, digital inclusion, and meaningful participation in the labour market\. This paper examines the role of inclusive education in promoting digital skills development and enhancing workforce readiness among learners with disabilities\. Drawing on existing literature, policy documents, and global best practices, the study explores how accessible learning environments, assistive technologies, and inclusive pedagogical approaches can improve educational outcomes and employability prospects\. The paper argues that inclusive education is not merely a social justice imperative but a strategic pathway for harnessing the talents and contributions of persons with disabilities in Africa&\#x27;s emerging digital economy\. It further highlights the need for coordinated policy reforms, investment in digital infrastructure, teacher capacity development, and stakeholder collaboration to ensure equitable access to 21st\-century skills\. The study concludes that strengthening inclusive education systems will enhance social inclusion, economic participation, and global workforce competitiveness across the continent\.
