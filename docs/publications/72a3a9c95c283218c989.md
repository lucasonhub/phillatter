# Demographic subgroup\-aware analysis of blood pressure predictive models

**Autores:** Blessing Kolawole; Sinuo Fan; Yingjie Lao; Valencia Joyner Koomson

**Data:** 2026-10-15 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Blood pressure \(BP\) is an important cardiovascular health indicator, usually monitored non\-invasively with a sphygmomanometer, which can be bulky and inconvenient for real\-time monitoring\. Blood pressure estimation using photoplethysmography \(PPG\) waveform analysis offers a promising alternative for intermittent or continuous cuff\-less BP monitoring\. However, changes in vascularization due to individual physiology and age can directly affect the morphology of the PPG waveform\. Thus, prediction accuracy requires a demographic\-aware approach\. To address these challenges, we trained three conventional ML models for BP prediction and conducted a subgroup\-aware analysis to evaluate model performance across individual and intersectional demographic groups\. Additionally, we show that signal quality assessment is a pivotal step in the signal\-processing pipeline before feature extraction to mitigate external perturbations \(e\.g\., motion artifacts\) in the PPG waveform\. We achieve a mean absolute error \(MAE\) of 3\.36 mmHg for systolic blood pressure \(SBP\) and 1\.76 mmHg for diastolic BP \(DBP\) with models trained when signal quality assessment is incorporated\.
