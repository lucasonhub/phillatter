# MODELS AND ALGORITHMS FOR FORECASTING TRAFFIC LOAD AND ADAPTIVELY ALLOCATING NETWORK RESOURCES IN TELECOMMUNICATION NETWORKS

**Autores:** Normamatova Yulduz Ravshan qizi

**Data:** 2026-10-10 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

The rapid growth of mobile broadband, cloud services, video streaming, and Internet of Things \(IoT\) applications has increased the complexity of traffic management in modern telecommunication networks\. Static resource allocation approaches may fail to respond efficiently to rapidly changing demand, resulting in congestion, inefficient bandwidth utilization, increased latency, and deterioration in quality of service \(QoS\)\. This article proposes a conceptual framework that integrates traffic forecasting with adaptive network resource allocation\. The framework combines statistical forecasting models, machine learning algorithms, and deep learning techniques to estimate short\-term traffic demand and guide the dynamic allocation of bandwidth, radio resources, computing capacity, and network paths\. A hybrid forecasting approach incorporating temporal prediction, spatial traffic dependencies, and uncertainty estimation is presented\. An optimization model is formulated to balance resource utilization, service quality, and operational costs while respecting capacity constraints and service\-level requirements\. The proposed workflow includes data collection, preprocessing, traffic prediction, resource optimization, implementation, and continuous feedback\. The article also establishes an evaluation framework using mean absolute error, root mean square error, resource utilization, latency, packet loss, and service\-level agreement violations\. The framework provides a foundation for developing intelligent, proactive, and scalable network management systems\.
