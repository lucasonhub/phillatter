# AI and Machine Learning: Concepts, Models and Real\-World Applications

**Autores:** Goldi Soni; Dr\. Kranti Kumar Dewangan; Omprakash Dewangan

**Data:** 2026-10-17 · **Tipo:** book

## Resumo

AI and Machine Learning: Concepts, Models and Real\-World Applications provides a structured introduction to the fundamental concepts, models, techniques, and practical applications of Artificial Intelligence \(AI\) and Machine Learning \(ML\)\. The book covers AI foundations, machine learning fundamentals, data collection and preprocessing, feature engineering, supervised and unsupervised learning, regression, classification, decision trees, ensemble models, artificial neural networks, and deep learning\. It also explores natural language processing, computer vision, reinforcement learning, model evaluation, validation, explainable AI, ethical considerations, and responsible AI practices\. The book discusses real\-world applications across healthcare, finance, manufacturing, retail, transportation, and technology\. It also addresses practical challenges involving data quality, computational resources, model performance, privacy, security, and regulatory compliance\. This book is intended for students, researchers, educators, and professionals seeking to understand AI and machine learning concepts, models, workflows, and real\-world implementations\. Publisher: Geh Press Edition: 2026 ISBN: 978\-81\-68406\-60\-5
