# Effects of Combined Drought and Salinity Stress on Oxidative Damage and the Antioxidant Enzyme System in Aronia \(Aronia melanocarpa L\.\) ‘Nero’ under In Vitro Conditions

**Autores:** Çağlar Kaya

**Data:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

This study investigated the effects of single and combined drought \(10% PEG\-6000\) and salinity \(100 mM NaCl\) stress on oxidative injury, antioxidant defense, and physiological responses of aronia \(Aronia melanocarpa L\.\) ‘Nero’ under in\-vitro conditions\. After 14 days, combined stress induced markedly stronger biochemical disruption than individual treatments\. Malondialdehyde \(MDA\) increased from 8\.4 nmol g\-1 FW in the control to 14\.7 \(PEG\) and 16\.2 \(NaCl\), reaching 24\.8 nmol g\-1 FW under combined stress\. Hydrogen peroxide \(H2O2\) rose from 3\.2 to 11\.6 µmol g\-1 FW in the dual treatment, indicating intensified reactive oxygen species accumulation\. Antioxidant enzymes were strongly upregulated in response to stress\. SOD activity increased from 62\.4 to 138\.6 U mg\-1 protein under combined stress, while CAT \(21\.8 U mg\-1 protein\), APX \(13\.2 U mg\-1 protein\), GR \(9\.7 U mg\-1 protein\), and POD \(29\.4 U mg\-1 protein\) also showed their highest activities in the dual treatment\. Non\-enzymatic antioxidants followed a similar pattern: proline increased nearly fivefold \(1\.8 to 8\.7 µmol g\-1 FW\), total phenolics rose from 5\.4 to 13\.8 mg GAE g\-1 FW, flavonoids reached 5\.2 mg CE g\-1 FW, and DPPH scavenging activity increased from 38\.6% to 64\.3%\. Despite enhanced antioxidant responses, oxidative markers remained elevated under combined stress\. Relative water content declined from 91\.4% to 61\.3%, and SPAD values decreased from 42\.5 to 28\.9, indicating impaired water status and chlorophyll stability\. Simultaneous osmotic and ionic stress, therefore, imposes a substantially greater redox and physiological burden than single stress exposure in aronia ‘Nero’\. Future studies should evaluate different stress intensities and exposure durations across various aronia cultivars and under field conditions, while also investigating the molecular and metabolic mechanisms underlying tolerance to combined stress\.
