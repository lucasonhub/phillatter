# Neural network\-based piezoelectric haptic interaction system with integrated perception and feedback

**Autores:** Cansen Huang; Yajun Zheng; Zhaohan Gong; Haoyu Xu; Jiahao Hu; Xin Fu; Changyu Deng; Hongping Hu

**Data:** 2026-10-12 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Abstract Discrete tactile sensors and haptic feedback devices with various architectures and novel functionalities have attracted increasing interest across multiple research areas\. However, developing a highly integrated haptic system with low control complexity that can simultaneously detect external tactile signals and generate programmable feedback signals remains challenging\. Herein, an integrated perception and feedback haptic system \(IPFHS\), which combines accurate detection of external pressure and programmable intelligent feedback within a single compact unit, is proposed to achieve the combination of sensing and actuation\. Enabled by a lightweight neural network, the system composed of four piezoelectric sensing\-feedback units and a rigid plate achieved high spatial resolution in perceiving pressing locations across the entire interaction plane with 96\.46% accuracy, and realized accurate magnitude identification of external force with 98\.75% accuracy\. In addition, the IPFHS provided personalized feedback based on different characteristics of the identified pressing, such as amplitude, frequency, and duration\. The IPFHS is further successfully applied to representative Braille typing scenarios\. Due to the advantages of simple structure, compact integration, low power consumption, and fast response, the IPFHS has great potential for implementation in human\-machine interaction interfaces\. This work provided a novel design strategy for highly integrated tactile interaction systems and offered a reference technical framework for the development of intelligent tactile interfaces, virtual interactions, remote operations, and rehabilitation assistive devices\.
