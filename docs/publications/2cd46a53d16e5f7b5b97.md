# СТРАТЕГІЧНІ ПРІОРИТЕТИ РОЗВИТКУ КАДРОВОГО ПОТЕНЦІАЛУ ПІДПРИЄМСТВ В УМОВАХ ЦИФРОВИХ ТРАНСФОРМАЦІЙ

**Autores:** О\.В\. Станіславик; О\.М\. Коваленко

**Data:** 2026-10-13 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

The article examines the strategic development of enterprise human resource potential in the context of digital transformations\. The impact of digitalization, automation and artificial intelligence on competency requirements and human resource management is considered\. It is substantiated that technological changes require enterprises to shift from responding mainly to current staffing needs towards the proactive development of human resource capabilities aligned with strategic objectives and prospective competency needs\. The strategic priorities for human resource potential development are systematized, including the development of professional, digital and managerial competencies, employee upskilling and reskilling, internal mobility, talent pool formation, recruitment of specialists with scarce competencies, and digitalization of HR processes\. It is determined that the choice and combination of these priorities should depend on strategic competency gaps and the prospective needs of a particular enterprise\. A conceptual model for the strategic management of human resource potential development is proposed\. It includes defining strategic objectives, considering changes in the external environment, identifying prospective competency needs, assessing existing human resource potential, identifying competency gaps, selecting and implementing priority HR decisions, evaluating their effectiveness, and subsequent adjustment based on feedback\. Digital HR tools are considered a cross\-cutting means of information and analytical support for this management cycle\. They facilitate competency assessment, HR analytics, employee learning and managerial decision\-making\. It is substantiated that digital technologies should not be regarded as an independent objective of HR policy, but as an instrument for improving the validity of HR decisions and aligning human resource potential with the strategic needs of the enterprise\. The proposed approach supports the choice between employee development, reskilling, internal mobility and external recruitment based on identified competency gaps\.
