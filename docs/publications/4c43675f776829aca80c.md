# OLIY О‘QUV YURTLARI TALABALARINING KASBIY KOMPETENSIYASINI KROSSVORD VOSITASIDA RIVOJLANTIRISH METODIKASI\.

**Autores:** Raxmanov Farxodjon Gabbarovich

**Data:** 2026-10-13 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

This article examines the pedagogical potential and methodological foundations of using crosswords to develop the professional competence of university students\. It analyzes how the use of crosswords in the educational process helps consolidate students&\#x27; professional knowledge, master field\-specific terms and concepts, and develop skills in logical thinking, independent inquiry, and problem\-solving\. Furthermore, the article substantiates the importance of purposefully integrating crosswords into academic sessions, tailoring them to students&\#x27; knowledge levels and professional specializations, and enhancing educational effectiveness\. Zamonaviy jamiyat va innovatsiyalar ilmiy nazariy jurnali 2026\-yil, 9\-Son, 2\-Qism\. ISSN 3060\-544X Special attention is paid to the methodology of using crosswords, the stages of their development, and assessment criteria\. Research results indicate that the systematic and purposeful use of crosswords fosters the development of professional competence, increases student interest in independent learning, and helps create an interactive educational environment\.
