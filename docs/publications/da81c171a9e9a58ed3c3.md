**Autores:** 

**Data:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Earthquakes are among the most destructive natural hazards\. As populations in seismically active regions grow, the need for effective risk mitigation strategies is more urgent than ever\. In that regard, earthquake early warning systems are a key component to reduce the impact of seismic shaking\.
