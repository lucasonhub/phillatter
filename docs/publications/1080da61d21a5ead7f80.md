# THE EL\-RAKHAWI CIVIL CODE FOR THE DIGITAL\-BIOLOGICAL AGE From Smart Contracts to the Rights of Future Generations A Comprehensive Theoretical Framework for Modernizing Civil Law in the Era of Artificial Intelligence, Genomic Engineering, Digital Assets, and Intergenerational Justice

**Autores:** mohamed kamal arafa el\-rakhawi

**Data:** 2026-10-11 · **Tipo:** book

## Resumo

The El\-Rakhawi Civil Code for the Digital\-Biological Age \(DOI: 10\.5281/zenodo\.23293387\) proposes a comprehensive theoretical framework across six volumes addressing 21st\-century legal gaps: \(1\) General Theory of Rights covering AI legal personality \(4 graduated levels\), personal data as sui generis category, digital assets, and rights of nature; \(2\) Contracts in the Digital Age including smart contracts, electronic consent, and AI\-concluded agreements; \(3\) Property covering generative AI copyright, metaverse assets, biological property, and environmental limitations; \(4\) Civil Liability addressing autonomous systems, self\-driving vehicles, genomic errors, climate change, and data breaches; \(5\) Family and Personal Status in the biological age; \(6\) Rights of Future Generations including intergenerational trusts and specialized judicial chambers\. Four surgical refinements ensure doctrinal defensibility: \(1\) Climate liability framed as &quot;Continuous Nuisance&quot; rather than retroactive punishment, preserving non\-retroactivity; \(2\) Smart contract judicial intervention via Multi\-Signature Escrow and Judicial Oracle Protocols respecting blockchain immutability; \(3\) Differentiated liability—fault\-based for somatic therapeutic editing, strict for germline/enhancement editing; \(4\) Chamber for Future Generations as specialized division within existing Supreme Courts, enabling immediate implementation without constitutional amendment\. Protected by Sovereign Legal Framework with AI\-training bans, Defensive Publication, and Justice Ethics Clause, released under the Ferial Zayed Humanitarian License—establishing a blueprint for civil law that protects the vulnerable, the voiceless, and the unborn in the digital\-biological age\.
