# Three\-dimensional progressive damage and geohazard assessment of an open\-pit slope affected by subsurface voids and staged excavation: a case study of the Nanfen mine

**Autores:** Hua Li; Ruijie Gong; Shihui Jiao; Yong Zhao; Yilin Tian; Tianhong Yang; Wenxue Deng; Yawei Li

**Data:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Historical underground voids and surface excavation can affect the three\-dimensional response of open\-pit slopes\. This study applies geological modeling and an elasto\-damage constitutive model to the east slope of the Nanfen open\-pit mine\. UAV photogrammetry and geological exploration data represent the topography, lithology, and underground voids\. A continuous excavation sequence uses the actual 2024 and end\-of\-2025 mine geometries, followed by the planned end\-of\-2026 and end\-of\-2027 design geometries\. Stress, damage, and historical strain variables are inherited on the retained mesh\. Reported settlement decreases from a baseline maximum of 27\.5 mm to sub\-millimeter values in the final configuration\. The settlement statistics describe the remaining slope surface in each configuration and incorporate changes in the sampled domain\. The damage plots suggest spatial separation between void\-associated and slope\-associated regions\. The simulated sequence captures the combined response to slope cutting and pit excavation\. These results characterize the Nanfen slope under the modeled excavation stages and loading conditions\.
