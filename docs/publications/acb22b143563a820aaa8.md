# REGULARITIES OF GROWTH AND MORPHOGENESIS OF HUMAN METACARPAL BONES DURING THE PERIOD OF OSTEOGENESIS COMPLETION

**Autores:** I\. H\. Biriuk

**Data:** 2026-10-14 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

The study of morphometric parameters of hand bones in adolescents and young adults is an important direction in modern anatomy, pediatric orthopedics, and forensic medicine\. The age period of 17\-21 years is characterized by the final phases of osteogenesis, when the rate of linear growth slows down, yet individual and sex\-specific features of skeletal formation persist\. Establishing clear age\- and sex\-related norms for metacarpal bone lengths allows for increasing the accuracy of biological age assessment, predicting growth potential, and evaluating skeletal maturity\. Objective\. To investigate the anatomical and morphometric standards and sex differences in the dimensions of the metacarpal bones of the right and left hands in young adults aged 17\-21 years\. Material and Methods\. The study of the morphometric parameters of the metacarpal bones of the right and left hands in young adults was conducted through a retrospective analysis of 224 hand radiographs obtained from patients aged 17 to 21 years who received inpatient or outpatient treatment at the Department of Traumatology of the Emergency Hospital in Chernivtsi\. The total sample included 112 young men and 112 young women without signs of skeletal system pathology that could affect the growth or morphometric parameters of the metacarpal bones\. The study was carried out within the framework of cooperation with the medical institution in accordance with a formal agreement\. Inclusion criteria: age range of 17\-21 years, presence of clear radiographs of both hands in a direct anteroposterior projection without distortion, absence of radiological signs of trauma \(fractures, cracks, bone calluses\), congenital developmental anomalies, bone and joint pathologies, or systemic skeletal diseases\. Exclusion criteria: presence of artifacts on radiographs that complicated osteometry; destructive, inflammatory, or neoplastic changes in bone tissue; traces of surgical interventions or metal osteosynthesis; and structural deformities of the metacarpal bones\. Since the study was retrospective and based on the analysis of a de\-identified radiological archive, additional informed consent from patients was not required, which complies with the ethical standards of the Declaration of Helsinki of the World Medical Association \(1964, amended in 2013\) and the Council of Europe Convention on Human Rights and Biomedicine \(04\.04\.1997\)\. The study was conducted in compliance with bioethical norms and was approved by the Bioethics Committee of Bukovinian State Medical University \(Protocol No\. 2 dated October 16, 2025\), which revealed no violations of moral and legal standards\.The study is a fragment of the department&\#x27;s comprehensive research project of the Department of Anatomy, Clinical Anatomy, and Operative Surgery: &quot;Sex and age patterns of ontogenetic transformations and morphometric parameters of organs and structures in normal and experimental conditions\. Morpho\-functional and anthropometric characteristics of the musculoskeletal system of athletes&quot; \(State Registration Number: 0125U001531\)\. Statistical processing of the data was performed using Microsoft Excel\. Results are presented as mean values \(M\) and standard deviations \(SD\)\. Relative annual growth values \(percentages\) and bilateral asymmetry indices were also calculated\. Results\. Analysis of the radiographs revealed that the length of all metacarpal bones increases continuously during young adulthood; however, growth dynamics are characterized by heterochrony and marked sexual dimorphism\. Throughout the entire observation period \(17\-21 years\), the absolute dimensions of all metacarpal bones in young men significantly exceeded the corresponding values in young women\. Growth processes in young men exhibited a wave\-like pattern with two distinct peaks in intensity\. The first spurt was recorded at 17\-18 years of age, with maximum growth observed in the 1st metacarpal bone \(\+2\.33% on the right hand\) and the 2nd metacarpal bone \(\+1\.71%\)\. The second growth spurt occurred at 20\-21 years, showing peak values for the 3rd, 4th, and 5th metacarpal bones\. In young women, growth rates generally slowed down after the age of 17\. The maximum growth increment of the 1st metacarpal bone in young women occurred at 18\-19 years \(\+0\.96 ±1\.21%\), the 2nd bone at 17\-18 years \(\+0\.16 ±0\.32%\), and the 3rd bone at 19\-20 years \(\+0\.50%\)\. At the same time, at 20\-21 years of age, a significant peak growth increment was observed in the 5th metacarpal bone \(\+3\.37%\) and the 4th metacarpal bone \(\+0\.56 ±0\.74%\)\. The least intensive period of bone length increase for both sexes was between 19 and 20 years of age, during which the annual increment of most bones decreased to 0\.18 ±0\.67% in males and 0\.16 ±0\.40% in females, corresponding to a phase of adaptive structural and mineral remodeling of bone tissue\. Analysis of bilateral differences showed that asymmetry between the right and left hands in young men and women was minimal \(ranging within 0\.00 ±0\.93% or 0\.01 ±0\.04 cm\) and lacked a systematic direction\. Conclusions\.The process of metacarpal bone osteogenesis during young adulthood \(17\-21 years\) is incomplete and is characterized by pronounced sexual dimorphism: the absolute sizes and growth rates of all metacarpal bones in young men significantly exceed the corresponding values in young women throughout the entire period\.In young men, two growth spurts are observed\- at 17\-18 years \(predominantly the 1st and 2nd metacarpals\) and at 20\-21 years \(the 3rd, 4th, and 5th metacarpals\)\. In young women, growth processes generally decelerate after the age of 17, but demonstrate a local peak growth increment in the 5th metacarpal bone at 20\-21 years \(\+3\.37%\)\.The period of minimal growth activity for both sexes is the age interval of 19\-20 years \(increment of 0\.16 ±0\.67%\), which corresponds to a phase of relative quiescence and organotypic remodeling of compact bone prior to the final closure of meta\-epiphyseal growth plates\.Bilateral asymmetry between the right and left hands is minimal\.
