# Statistical Analysis and Predictive Modelling of Urban Traffic Flow for Route Generation: Evidence from Padua and Port Harcourt

**Autores:** Captain Gospel Otto; Fun\-Akpo Pere Kormane; Thankgod Ode; Kemejika Ichechi Amadi\- Oparaeli

**Data:** 2026-10-12 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Urban route generation depends on traffic statistics that are persistent in time, uneven in space and only partly predictable\. This study applies descriptive, temporal, concentration, clustering, regression and sensitivity statistics to two complementary datasets: 7,908 ten\-minute junction\-volume periods and hourly travel times from Padua, Italy, and aggregate morning and evening peak observations from seven Port Harcourt, Nigeria, locations linked to a directed OpenStreetMap network of 48,504 nodes and 111,839 edges\.
