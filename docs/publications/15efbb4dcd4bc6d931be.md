# A INFORMAÇÃO E A BOA\-FÉ OBJETIVA NA PROTEÇÃO DO CONSUMIDOR NA SOCIEDADE DA INFORMAÇÃO

**Autores:** Jorge Shiguemitsu Fujita; Thiago Henrique Rodrigues da Silva

**Data:** 2026-10-12 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

With the advent of the internet, consumer behavior has been radically transformed; it has become possible to examine products and obtain detailed information with just a few taps\. However, this convenience has been exploited for improper data collection and monitoring, as well as to foster abusive practices on e\-commerce platforms\. In this context, one must ask: how do dark pattern practices impact the duty to inform as a manifestation of objective good faith on e\-commerce platforms? This study seeks to review historical aspects of consumer law, understand what constitutes good faith and its application in consumer relations, and analyze how abusive practices—especially dark patterns—correlate with objective good faith in digital consumer relations\. The research method employed is a literature review, based on the consultation of scientific materials relevant to the topic\.
