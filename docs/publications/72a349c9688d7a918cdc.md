# IMPLEMENTASI PEMBELAJARAN MENDALAM MELALUI PROJECT\-BASED LEARNING YANG DIDUKUNG AI\-BASED MICROLEARNING TERHADAP HASIL BELAJAR TEKNIK KOMPUTER DAN JARINGAN PADA MATERI SERANGAN SIBER KELAS XI SMK MUHAMMADIYAH 1 PEKANBARU

**Autores:** SYANTIA ARTINDA; Melly Novalia; Edi Ismanto

**Data:** 2026-10-13 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

This study was motivated by the low learning outcomes of students in the cyberattack topic in the 11th\-grade Computer and Network Engineering \(TKJ\) class at SMK Muhammadiyah 1 Pekanbaru\. This study aimed to describe the implementation of Deep Learning through the Project\-Based Learning \(PjBL\) model supported by AI\-Based Microlearning and to analyze changes in students’ learning outcomes after the implementation of the learning approach\. The study employed a quantitative approach using a pre\-experimental method with a One Group Pretest\-Posttest Design\. The participants consisted of 25 students from the 11th\-grade TKJ class\. Data were collected through learning outcome tests, product assessments, presentation assessments, and documentation\. The data were analyzed using normality tests, the Paired Sample t\-Test, N\-Gain, and effect size analysis using Cohen’s d\. AI\-Based Microlearning was used as conceptual reinforcement by presenting concise learning materials on the types, characteristics, impacts, and prevention of cyberattacks before students conducted case analyses, participated in group discussions, and completed projects in the form of educational posters and presentations\. The product assessment score for the posters was 88\.3%, while the presentation assessment score was 90\.3%, both categorized as very good\. These scores reflected students’ achievement in project activities and were not used as the sole indicators of the overall implementation of the learning process\. The mean learning outcome score increased from 61\.46 in the pretest to 83\.39 in the posttest\. The Paired Sample t\-Test showed a significant difference between the pretest and posttest scores \(p &lt; 0\.001\)\. The mean N\-Gain score was 0\.569, categorized as moderate, while Cohen’s d of 1\.43 indicated a large effect size\. These findings indicate that the implementation of Deep Learning through PjBL supported by AI\-Based Microlearning was associated with improved student learning outcomes; however, the improvement cannot be fully attributed to the intervention because the study did not employ a control group\. The integration of these three approaches may serve as an alternative strategy for contextual and problem\-solving\-oriented learning in Computer and Network Engineering\.
