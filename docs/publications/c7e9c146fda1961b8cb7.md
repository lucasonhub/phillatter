# RecurIndex\-derived molecular features associated with total pathological complete response in triple\-negative breast cancer: An exploratory prospective cohort study with single\-cell transcriptomic contextualization

**Autores:** Meilin Chen; Xiwen Yan; 陈琦凡; Jiayi Bei; Zheng Pan; Tian Zhang; Weihao Cheng; Lu Xu; Feng Jin; Yan Gao; Zining Jin; Ang Zheng

**Data:** 2026-10-10 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

BACKGROUND: Reliable biomarkers for predicting neoadjuvant chemotherapy \(NAC\) response in triple\-negative breast cancer \(TNBC\) remain limited\. We explored whether RecurIndex\-derived molecular features were associated with total pathological complete response \(tpCR\)\. METHODS: Seventy\-four patients with TNBC treated with dose\-dense NAC were analyzed\. Associations between RecurIndex risk scores, 18 core genes, and tpCR were assessed using logistic regression and ROC analyses\. Clinical\-molecular models were internally validated by bootstrap resampling\. Public single\-cell RNA\-seq data were used for biological contextualization\. RESULTS: Twenty\-nine patients \(39\.2%\) achieved tpCR\. The distant recurrence score, was significantly associated with tpCR\. OBSL1, BLM, and CCR1 were identified as candidate tpCR\-associated genes\. The model combining OBSL1, BLM, and clinical nodal stage achieved an AUC of 0\.838 and an optimism\-corrected AUC of 0\.799\. CONCLUSIONS: RecurIndex\-derived molecular features were associated with tpCR in TNBC\. These exploratory findings warrant validation in independent cohorts\.
