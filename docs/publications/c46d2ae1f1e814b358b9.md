# A Comparative Review of Soilless Precision Agriculture Techniques and Emerging Smart Farming Technologies

**Autores:** Monica Dutta; Deepali Gupta; Walaa Jamal Alharthi; Mohammad Zubair Khan; Adnan Nadeem; Sapna Juneja

**Data:** 2026-10-14 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

The rapidly diminishing arable land, uncertain climatic conditions, and lack of ample natural resources are worsening the urgency for adopting sustainable agricultural systems\. Soilless cultivation methods promote enhanced resource utilization and better productivity\. These methods, like aeroponics, hydroponics, and aquaponics, are evolving as smart farming techniques, providing notable advantages over traditional farming methods, enhancing water conservation, resource utilization, and enabling cultivation in places with reduced area and less water\. The concept of soilless cultivation provides multifold advantages over traditional cultivation methods by further making provision for cultivating in the closest proximity to urban farmers, like residential rooftops\. A bibliometric analysis is also conducted to highlight the recent trends in smart precision farming technologies and the choice of substrate for each technique\. In this article, various methods of soilless smart precision agriculture are identified and compared with traditional cultivation methods\. Moreover, aspects like economic sustainability and recent research trends are analyzed to obtain insight into the three basic classes of soilless cultivation, namely aeroponics, aquaponics, and hydroponics\. Furthermore, the study also focuses on briefly analyzing the economics of soilless techniques\. The study concludes by outlining the major challenges and possible future enhancements in this domain that can lead to the evolution of smart, efficient, and resilient agricultural systems\.
