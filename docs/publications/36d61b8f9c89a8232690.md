# Attention\-based and contrastive deep learning models for real\-time anomaly detection in Edge\-IIoT networks

**Autores:** Ahmad Reda Alzighaibi; Adam Marks; Abdullateef I\. Almudaifer; Hossam Magdy Balaha; Nadhir Ben Halima; Mostafa A\. Elhosseini

**Data:** 2026-10-14 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

The rapid expansion of Industrial Internet of Things \(IIoT\) systems has increased the cyber\-attack surface of critical infrastructure, requiring intrusion detection systems \(IDS\) that are accurate, robust, and efficient under resource\-constrained edge environments\. Traditional approaches suffer from limited generalization, poor handling of class imbalance, and lack of statistical reliability and interpretability\. This paper proposes a modular deep learning framework integrating TabTransformer, Variational Autoencoder, and contrastive learning, combined through multi\-trial training \(N = 10\), statistical aggregation, and F1\-weighted ensemble fusion\. Experiments on the Edge\-IIoTset dataset \( $$\\approx $$ 1\.9M samples, 15 classes\) achieve 97\.60% accuracy and 83\.42% F1\-score, with multi\-trial aggregation reducing the uncertainty associated with single\-run reporting\. From a computational perspective, training is performed using large\-batch optimization \(batch size = 16,384\) with early stopping \(patience = 50\), while inference requires a single forward pass per model followed by constant\-time fusion\. The framework operates on compact tabular inputs \( $$\\approx $$ 96 features\), resulting in a lightweight memory footprint \(on the order of a few megabytes per model\) and enabling efficient deployment\. The inference pipeline processes batches in a few milliseconds on GPU\-class hardware, ensuring real\-time responsiveness for high\-throughput IIoT traffic streams while maintaining low computational overhead and energy demand\. Ablation analysis shows that removing ensemble fusion reduces F1\-score by approximately 1%:2%, while eliminating multi\-trial aggregation significantly increases variance, highlighting the importance of statistical robustness\. Furthermore, SHAP\-based explainable AI \(XAI\) provides insight into feature\-level contributions, enhancing transparency and supporting human\-in\-the\-loop security operations\. Overall, the proposed framework achieves a balanced trade\-off between predictive performance and computational efficiency, delivering a reproducible, interpretable, and deployment\-oriented IDS solution for real\-time IIoT environments\. Practical deployment considerations, including model export to ONNX format and quantization for edge hardware, are outlined as immediate next steps\.
