# Dentists’ Attitudes Toward Artificial Intelligence in Dental Practice

**Autores:** Anshuman Swami; Astha Chauhan; Bhuvnesh Airen; Ranjan Mani Tripathi; Chirag Vyas; Surbhi Gendar

**Data:** 2026-10-12 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Background: Dentists’ readiness to use artificial intelligence \(AI\) remains an important concern in dental practice\.Aim: This study explored attitudes, perceived barriers, and willingness to use AI\.Materials and Methods: A survey of 220 dentists was analysed using descriptive statistics and Pearson’s chi\-square tests\.Results: Willingness was 70\.0%, while 75\.0% reported privacy concerns and 90\.0% reported a demand for training\.Conclusion: This analysis provides a framework for studying responsible AI adoption and testing educational needs in future empirical research\.
