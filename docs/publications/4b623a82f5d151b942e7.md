# PENGARUH ALOKASI DANA DESA DAN JUMLAH PENDUDUK USIA PRODUKTIF TERHADAP PENDAPATAN PERKAPITA MASYARAKAT DI PROVINSI ACEH

**Autores:** Amara Imanika; Hamdani Hamdani; Julia Alfianti

**Data:** 2026-10-14 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Abstract: This research aims to determine the effect of village fund allocation and the number of productive age population on the per capita income of the community in Aceh Province, both partially and simultaneously\. This research uses a quantitative approach with a panel data regression analysis method, namely a combination of time series and cross section data, with the help of EViews 13 software\. The data used is secondary data obtained from the Central Statistics Agency \(BPS\) and the Directorate General of Fiscal Balance \(DJPK\), which covers 23 districts/cities in Aceh Province during the 2020–2024 period\. Based on the results of model selection, it was found that the best model used was the Fixed Effect Model \(FEM\)\. The research results show that village fund allocation and the number of productive age population partially have a positive and significant effect on the per capita income of the community in Aceh Province\. Simultaneously, these two variables have a significant effect on the per capita income of people in Aceh Province\. These findings show that optimizing the management of village funds and utilizing the productive age population can encourage increased community welfare and equal distribution of income in Aceh Province\.Keywords: Village Funds, Productive\-Age Population, Per Capita Income\.
