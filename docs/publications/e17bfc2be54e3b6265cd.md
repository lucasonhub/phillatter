# Li, Yuchen ; Li, Xintong &amp; Fan, Zongqi: Functional Selfhood and First\-Person Experience: Self\-Indexing, Localized Presentation, and Constructed Continuity

**Autores:** 

**Data de descoberta:** 2026-10-19 · **Tipo:** unknown

## Descrição do feed

Brain activity changes constantly, yet we experience an ordered life lived by one &quot;I&quot;\. In our three\-layer account, the basis is the physical medium that receives projected encodings and in which experience occurs; the encoding, each moment&\#x27;s organized activity, fixes what is experienced, including what is marked as one&\#x27;s own; the hardware, the persisting brain, stores traces and generates the next encoding\. First, the three layers answer different questions and can fail separately\. Second, encodings appear and disappear, so nothing in them guarantees that t follows t\-1: if experience were a property of each encoding alone, nothing would fix the order in which a first\-person perspective passes through them, yet within a specious present we directly experience the transition\. Experience is thus ordered in itself\. Since causation and time are two sides of one structure and projection is causal, its basis is a field in spacetime, or spacetime itself, that receives the projections in causal order\. As it cannot be decided whether the observer fixes time or the reverse, we identify the subjective observer with this local carrier of time, distinct from the projector, the functional encoding of self\-consciousness\. Third, each encoding leaves a trace from which the next is generated and which the next reads back as its own past, so an &quot;I&quot; is rebuilt at every moment\. Recognizing something as one&\#x27;s own is a second\-order readout of this mark, and language models show that it can be absent while the mark is present\. Copying duplicates the projector and its feeling of continuation, not the observer: that feeling cannot single out the original person, but continuity along a world line can\.
