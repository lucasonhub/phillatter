# Noise\-robust fault diagnosis of elastic ring\-supported rolling bearings using multi\-scale dilated convolution and multi\-head self\-attention

**Autores:** Dawei Gu; Jia Li; Zhuo Xu; Kai Sun; Juan Wen; Guang Zhang; Baisong Pan; Bangchun Wen

**Data:** 2026-10-15 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Elastic ring\-supported rolling bearings are critical components in high\-end rotating machinery such as aero\-engines and gas turbines\. However, the vibration responses of such bearings are often affected by complex transmission paths, elastic\-support\-induced dynamic coupling, and strong background noise, which makes weak fault feature extraction and reliable diagnosis difficult\. To address these challenges, this paper proposes a noise\-robust fault diagnosis method termed the multi\-scale dilated convolution and multi\-head self\-attention convolutional neural network \(MSDA\-CNN\)\. In the proposed framework, Z\-score normalization and variational mode decomposition \(VMD\) are used to preprocess raw vibration signals and construct multi\-channel inputs that retain fault\-related components in different frequency bands\. A multi\-scale dilated convolution module with parallel branches is then employed to extract complementary local and long\-range temporal features under noisy conditions\. Finally, a multi\-head self\-attention \(MHSA\) layer is introduced to enhance global feature interaction and improve class\-discriminative representation learning\. Experiments on a self\-built rotor\-bearing fault diagnosis platform and the public Paderborn University bearing dataset demonstrate that the proposed method achieves average diagnostic accuracies of 99\.86% and 98\.52%, respectively\. Under different Gaussian noise levels, MSDA\-CNN consistently outperforms MLP, 1D\-CNN, WD\-CNN, and MCL\-DRL\.
