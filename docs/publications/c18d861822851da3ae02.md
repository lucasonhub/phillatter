# Nanotechnology in Orthodontics: Current applications, clinical implications and future perspectives

**Autores:** 

**Data:** 2026-10-13 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Background: Nanotechnology has emerged as one of the most promising advances in modern healthcare, offering innovative solutions through the manipulation of materials at the nanoscale level\.In orthodontics, nanomaterials have demonstrated significant potential for improving the mechanical, biological &amp; antimicrobial properties of orthodontic appliances &amp; biomaterials\.Objective: This review aims to critically evaluate the current applications of nanotechnology in orthodontics, with emphasis on orthodontic materials, antimicrobial strategies, drug delivery systems and orthodontic anchorage devices, while highlighting future directions &amp; existing challenges\.Methods: A narrative review of the contemporary literature was conducted to assess the role of nanotechnology in orthodontic practice &amp; its impact on treatment efficiency, oral health and patient outcomes\.Results: Nanotechnology has contributed to the development of nano\-coated arch\-wires &amp; brackets with reduced frictional resistance &amp; enhanced corrosion resistance\.Antimicrobial nanoparticles, including silver, titanium dioxide, &amp; zinc oxide nanoparticles, have demonstrated effectiveness in reducing bacterial colonization, biofilm formation and enamel demineralization during orthodontic treatment\.Nano\-reinforced adhesives exhibit improved mechanical properties and bond strength\.Furthermore, nanotechnology\-based drug delivery systems enable controlled release of therapeutic agents for inflammation control and periodontal support\.Surface nanomodification of orthodontic mini\-implants has shown potential to enhance osseointegration and implant stability\.Conclusion: Nanotechnology represents a transformative approach in contemporary orthodontics by improving material performance, enhancing antimicrobial protection, and facilitating biologically targeted therapies\.Despite encouraging laboratory &amp; early clinical evidences, further long\-term clinical studies are required to establish the safety, efficacy and cost\-effectiveness of nanotechnology\-based orthodontic applications before widespread clinical implementation\.
