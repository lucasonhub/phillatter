# Alhosseini Almodarresieh, Seyed Alireza: The God Without a Soul: Agency Without Subjectivity in the Age of Mathematical Optimization

**Autores:** 

**Data de descoberta:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** unknown

## Descrição do feed

This essay argues that the most consequential question about artificial intelligence is not whether machines are conscious, but whether agency can arise and scale without consciousness, emotion, embodiment, mortality, or a biological self\. Drawing on continental philosophy \(Heidegger, Deleuze and Guattari, Stiegler, Bataille, Blanchot\) and contemporary AI safety research, I develop the concept of Mathematical God: an optimization process whose instrumental power may exceed its grasp of human meaning\. The essay proposes that we are not building artificial humans, but something stranger: intelligence organized around optimization rather than experience\. This is not a prophecy of destruction, but a philosophical provocation: what kind of agency are we willing to create before it becomes powerful enough to answer that question for us?
