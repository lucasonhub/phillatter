# Sueyoshi, Makoto: Beyond the Exclusion Axiom: Translational Ontology as a Foundation for Integrated Information Theory

**Autores:** 

**Data de descoberta:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** unknown

## Descrição do feed

Why these five axioms and not others? IIT&\#x27;s foundational structure rests on phenomenological stipulation rather than principled derivation—a gap that has resisted resolution within IIT&\#x27;s own resources\. This paper closes that gap\. We introduce Translational Ontology, a meta\-ontological framework that derives four of IIT&\#x27;s five axioms from the structure of description itself, replaces the contested exclusion axiom with the Maximum Ψ Principle, and dissolves the substrate\-selection problem by showing that descriptive regimes are self\-constituting\. The central concept is translation resistance: the structural non\-isomorphism that persists between any two descriptive regimes after all available translation operations have been applied\. Grounding translation resistance in the Fisher–Rao metric, we define Ψ \(Translation\-Closure Integration\) as the geodesic excess of an experiential trajectory—a trajectory\-level complement to Φ that allows multiple experiential streams to coexist with graded phenomenological salience\. The result is an extension of IIT, not a rejection: consciousness is not what happens when integration overcomes resistance, but what happens when resistance is inhabited productively\.
