# SiAman EduBot: Inovasi AI untuk Pelaporan Insiden dan Edukasi Keselamatan Pasien Dalam Satu Platform Digital\.

**Autores:** Ronald Winardi Kartika; Sarwoko SITANALA

**Data:** 2026-10-15 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

RSUP dr\. Sitanala is committed to strengthening patient safety culture toward a smart hospital paradigm through an artificial intelligence \(AI\)\-driven innovation named Si Aman EduBot\. This interactive chatbot, integrated via WhatsApp and the hospital&\#x27;s proprietary application, was developed to provide real\-time education on safety standards, emergency procedures, and digital incident reporting systems for both staff and patients\. This study aims to evaluate the impact of Si Aman EduBot implementation on enhancing safety culture within the hospital environment\. The evaluation results over a three\-month period demonstrated significant achievements, including over 5,000 user interactions, a 35% increase in incident reporting volume, and a 20% improvement in patient safety culture scores from the baseline\. The rapid and responsive nature of the chatbot proved effective in increasing staff awareness and fostering an open, blame\-free reporting culture\. Through its interactive educational features and digital system integration, Si Aman EduBot holds the potential to serve as a national model for building a sustainable patient safety culture\. Future developments will focus on integrating voice assistant capabilities and electronic medical records\. Keywords: Patient Safety, Educational Chatbot, Digital Incident Reporting, Blame\-Free Culture\.
