# A Combined Molecular Dynamics and Experimental Study on the Processing and Property Modification of UHMWPE / HDPE Blends by CaSt 2

**Autores:** Jie Liu; Ning Han; Gonghao Wang; Shengxue Qin

**Data:** 2026-10-14 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

ABSTRACT High melt viscosity limits the processability of ultra‐high‐molecular‐weight polyethylene \(UHMWPE\)\. Here, UHMWPE/high‐density polyethylene \(HDPE\) blends modified with calcium stearate \(CaSt 2 \) were studied by combined molecular dynamics \(MD\) simulation and experiment\. MD results show that 0\.5 wt% CaSt 2 maximizes the fractional free volume and diffusion coefficient, with increases of 8\.06% and 27\.17%, respectively\. Interaction strength increases monotonically with CaSt 2 content, while chain mobility peaks at 0\.5 wt%, indicating that excessive interactions restrict segmental motion at higher loading\. Experimentally, CaSt 2 increases the melt flow rate by 23\.53%, reduces processing torque, improves extrusion stability, and increases tensile strength and crystallinity by 4\.01% and 5\.18%, respectively\. These results show that appropriate CaSt 2 incorporation improves melt processability while maintaining mechanical performance\.
