# DIGITAL\-INTELLIGENT MODELS FOR FINANCING RENEWABLE ENERGY PROJECTS

**Autores:** Xamidov Bekzod Xolmirzayevich

**Data:** 2026-10-14 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

This paper examines how digital\-intelligent models can support the financing of renewable energy projects in Uzbekistan\. Using World Bank data on two solar projects, it reviews the role of international lending, guarantees and blended finance, proposes a conceptual framework combining AI\-based generation forecasting, cash flow forecasting and risk assessment, and outlines key risks and recommendations\.
