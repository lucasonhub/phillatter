# The rise of agentic AI in drug discovery from static tools to autonomous virtual scientists

**Autores:** Donghai Zhao; Meijing Fang; Lexiao Mao; Yuchen Zhu; Qun Su; Hui Shi; Hongxia Xu; Lanqing Li; Tingjun Hou; Chang\-Yu Hsieh

**Data:** 2026-10-12 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Driven by large language models, AI agents are transforming drug discovery, enabling AI to break free from task\-specific tools and advance toward the vision of autonomous virtual scientists\. This perspective reviews applications across the R&amp;D lifecycle\. To evaluate system maturity, we introduce a 6\-level &quot;Spectrum of Agentic Autonomy\.&quot; We advocate for novel evaluation paradigms centered on full\-chain traceability and outline adoption barriers, positioning AI agents as pragmatic scientist\-in\-the\-loop co\-pilots\.
