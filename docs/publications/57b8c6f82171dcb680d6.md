# India and Latin America and the Caribbean: emerging partners in a shifting global economy

**Autores:** 

**Data:** 2026-10-15 · **Tipo:** book

## Resumo

This document presents the proceedings of the international seminar entitled “India–Latin America and the Caribbean: emerging partners in a shifting global economy”, convened by the Economic Commission for Latin America and the Caribbean \(ECLAC\)\. The seminar examined the growing economic links between India and the region and their significance amid changes in trade, investment, production and technology\. The discussions highlighted the considerable scope for expanding and diversifying economic relations between the two partners, while also identifying persistent constraints related to market knowledge, connectivity, institutional presence and limited productive linkages\. The contributions in this volume seek to provide policymakers, business leaders and other stakeholders with analytical inputs to better understand these opportunities and constraints, identify areas of mutual interest and advance trade diversification, investment, productive and technological linkages, and institutional engagement between India and Latin America and the Caribbean\.
