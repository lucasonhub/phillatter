# STRATEGI GURU DALAM MENINGKATKAN MOTIVASI BELAJAR ANAK USIA DINI MELALUI AKTIVITAS BERMAIN DI TAMAN KANAK\-KANAK AL\-BARRA KELURAHAN BANDAR RATU KECAMATAN KOTA MUKOMUKO

**Autores:** Sri Wahyuni; Achmad Fadlan; Eka Mei Ratnasari

**Data:** 2026-10-12 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Early childhood education requires learning strategies that can create meaningful, enjoyable, and developmentally appropriate learning experiences\. This study aims to analyze teacher strategies in improving early childhood learning motivation through play activities at TK Al\-Barra, Bandar Ratu Village, Kota Mukomuko District\. This research employed a descriptive qualitative approach involving three kindergarten teachers as research subjects\. Data were collected through observation, interviews, and documentation, then analyzed using the Miles and Huberman interactive analysis model consisting of data reduction, data display, verification, and conclusion drawing\. The findings revealed that teachers implemented various strategies through educational games, group play activities, and creative play activities adapted to children’s needs and developmental characteristics\. Play\-based activities effectively enhanced children’s engagement, enthusiasm, curiosity, and active participation during the learning process\. However, the implementation of play\-based learning still encountered several challenges, including limited learning facilities, time constraints, and diverse characteristics of children\. This study highlights that teacher creativity and pedagogical competence are essential factors in optimizing play\-based learning implementation in early childhood education\.
