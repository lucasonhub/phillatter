# Payne, Chris: The Residue That Never Closes A Reflection on Finitude, Hiddenness, and the Limits of Approach

**Autores:** 

**Data de descoberta:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** unknown

## Descrição do feed

This essay offers a philosophical reflection built around a single tautological identity, 1 − 10⁻ᴴ \+ 10⁻ᴴ = 1, used not as a proof but as a lens\. The equation guarantees that a whole remains intact regardless of the size of a residue subtracted from and returned to it — even as that residue grows without bound\. Three readings of that residue are explored: the structural ceiling on a finite mind&\#x27;s comprehension, the theological motif of hiddenness found in the Elijah narrative at Horeb \(1 Kings 19\), and the relationship between computational systems and conscious choice\. The essay makes no claim that mathematics proves anything about metaphysics\. It claims only that the equation is a fertile object to think with, in a spirit related to apophatic or negative theology, which has long described the transcendent by naming what escapes finite predicates rather than what is captured by them\.
