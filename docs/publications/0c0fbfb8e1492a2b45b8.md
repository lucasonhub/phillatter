# CNN\-Based Target Detection Under Domain\-Mismatched and Low\-SNR Sub\-THz Radar Conditions

**Autores:** Subhankar Shome; Suman Das; Vandana Yadav; Saumya Das; Sushmita Chaudhari

**Data:** 2026-10-15 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Abstract Sub\-THz radar systems enable high\-resolution sensing, which is critical for next\-generation intelligent sensing applications, but can suffer from poor performance in low\-SNR conditions, waveform distortion, phase instability, and non\-stationary clutter conditions\. A lightweight CNN framework is proposed in this article based on the domain of spectrogram to detect targets in sub\-THz sensing environments with degraded and mismatching environment\. In the framework proposed here, realistic sensing errors due to the stochastic variability of the clutter, impulsive interference, spreading of the spectrum and distortion of the phase and mismatch of the waveform when the data sets are generated are taken into account\. To enable the CNN to learn the distributed statistical target features in a cluttered environment, received radar signals are mapped into time\-frequency spectrogram representations using Short\-Time Fourier Transform \(STFT\)\. The robustness of operation can be tested by deliberately introducing a mismatch between training and testing environments, such as changes in the waveforms and noise background\. The proposed detector is compared to the conventional Matched Filter and spectral CA\-CFAR baselines in terms of ROC\-AUC, probability of detection and low\-false alarm\. The experimental results show that a CNN\-based framework can provide better robustness in low SNR and distorted sensing conditions, and it also showed that the proposed CNN framework outperforms conventional model\-driven detectors in terms of detection performance\. The results demonstrate the practicability of deep learning for the spectrogram space in a realistic non\-ideal sensing scenario for practical target detection in sub\-THz radar\.
