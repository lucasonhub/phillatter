# Ferrari, Edson Fernando: Consciousness as Computable Projections of State

**Autores:** 

**Data de descoberta:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** unknown

## Descrição do feed

We propose an analysis of consciousness stated in terms of systems, states, canonical projections, and computability\. Let S have state x = \(s₁,\.\.\., sₙ\), and let Projᵢ\(x\) = sᵢ be the canonical projection of its ith component\. A component sᵢ is conscious for S if and only if Projᵢ is computable by S\. The consciousness of S is therefore the family Cₛ = \(Projᵢ₁,\.\.\., Projᵢₖ\) of its internally computable state projections, while Cₛ\(x\) = \(sᵢ₁,\.\.\., sᵢₖ\) is its current state of consciousness\. The analysis distinguishes a component&\#x27;s existence and causal efficacy from its presence for the system; distinguishes the capacity to compute projections from their current values and actual executions; and makes system boundaries and state specification logically prior to consciousness\. An elementary C program supplies the derivation rather than a metaphor\. Reports and answers provide operational evidence of computability but do not constitute consciousness or its elementary occurrences\. An elementary conscious phenomenon is an execution by the system of a computable canonical state projection\. The same criterion applies to organisms, programs, and AI agents; hence simple programs can be conscious of components of their own states\. This is a consequence of the analysis, not an attribution of subjective experience\. We compare the proposal with higher\-order, self\-representational, global\-workspace, recurrent, attention\-schema, and computational\-reflection accounts, and state the conditions for a genuine counterexample\.
