# A Hybrid FT\-Transformer\-Mamba Framework with SBSA Optimization and Blockchain\-ECC Cyber Resiliency for Advanced Persistent Threat Detection

**Autores:** Diana Arulkumar; K\. Kartheeban

**Data:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Advanced Persistent Threats \(APTs\) constitute one of the most sophisticated cybersecurity threats, as attackers execute prolonged, multi\-stage intrusions involving reconnaissance, lateral movement, foothold establishment, and data exfiltration while evading conventional intrusion detection mechanisms through stealthy and low\-signature attack behaviors\.Existing APT detection approaches, including signature\-based techniques, machine learning methods, and deep learning frameworks, have significantly improved cyberattack detection; however, many existing solutions rely on single\-architecture models that provide limited capability to jointly capture contextual feature interactions and long\-range temporal dependencies across the complete APT kill chain, while also lacking secure mechanisms for authenticated post\-detection cyber incident management\.To overcome these limitations, this paper presents a hybrid FT\-Transformer\-Mamba framework with Self\-Adaptive Backtracking Search Algorithm \(SBSA\)based hyperparameter optimization and a Blockchain\-ECC Cyber Resiliency architecture for post\-detection security management\.The proposed framework employs FT\-Transformer to learn contextual relationships among network flow features, Mamba to efficiently model long\-range temporal dependencies associated with multistage attack progression, SBSA to determine optimal hyperparameter configurations, and a Blockchain\-ECC component designed to support ECC\-based authentication, integrity verification, and traceable management of detected attack alerts within a permissioned ledger architecture\.To assess the performance of the proposed framework, evaluation was conducted using the publicly accessible DAPT2020 dataset comprising five target classes: Normal, Reconnaissance, Establish Foothold, Lateral Movement, and Data Exfiltration\.Experimental outcomes demonstrate that the proposed FT\-Transformer\-Mamba\-SBSA classification framework attained an accuracy of 98\.70%, weighted precision of 98\.73%, weighted recall of 98\.70%, weighted F1\-score of 98\.71%, specificity of 99\.41%, Matthews Correlation Coefficient \(MCC\) of 0\.982, and a macro\-average Area Under the Receiver Operating Characteristic Curve \(AUC\) of 0\.9940, demonstrating robust classification performance under the adopted five\-class DAPT2020 sequence\-classification protocol\.These findings demonstrate the effectiveness of the proposed FT\-Transformer\-Mamba model for APT killchain stage detection, while the Blockchain\-ECC component is presented as a complementary post\-detection security architecture for supporting authenticated, integrity\-oriented, and traceable alert management\.
