# Donor\-aware multi\-cohort single\-cell analysis identifies an LXR\-linked tubular cholesterol\-handling state in membranous nephropathy with a coordinated five\-gene lipid\-remodeling signature across renal tumors

**Autores:** Yalan Mao; Ran Li; Bin Li; Hongfen Pan

**Data:** 2026-10-14 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Background Membranous nephropathy \(MN\) is an immune\-mediated glomerular disease in which sustained proteinuria exposes renal tubular epithelial cells to an abnormal filtered\-protein load\. The tubular metabolic response to this exposure remains incompletely characterized\. We investigated whether MN is associated with a reproducible tubular cholesterol\-handling transcriptional state and assessed its relationship to protein overload in vitro \. We further evaluated whether an experimentally anchored five\-gene subset showed a coordinated tumor\-associated transcriptional shift across major renal cancer histologies\. Methods Three kidney single\-cell RNA\-sequencing datasets and one glomerular bulk\-transcriptomic cohort were analyzed using the donor as the biological replicate\. GSE241302 was used for complete\-case exploratory discovery, OEP004186 for formal donor\-aware support, GSE171458 for directional support, and GSE200828 for tissue\-level disease context\. A frozen 47\-gene LXR\-linked cholesterol\-handling program and its nested 37\-gene cholesterol transport/efflux component were evaluated by donor\-level pseudobulk analysis, leave\-one\-donor\-out and leave\-one\-gene\-out sensitivity analyses\. HK\-2 cells were exposed to protein overload, followed by quantitative reverse\-transcription PCR for representative transport and lipid\-remodeling genes\. The same experimentally anchored five\-gene panel was then evaluated in 129 paired tumor–adjacent\-normal samples across TCGA\-KIRC, TCGA\-KIRP, and TCGA\-KICH to define a renal tumor\-associated lipid\-remodeling context\. Results Across all 3 MN donors in GSE241302, program scores were higher than the internal reference in proximal tubule \(PT\) and loop\-of\-Henle/thick ascending limb \(loop/TAL\) compartments\. In OEP004186, the program was increased in PT \(effect \+0\.480, false\-discovery rate \[FDR\] 0\.0128\), PT\-VCAM1 \(\+0\.348, FDR 0\.0165\), and loop/distal epithelial cells \(\+0\.310, FDR 0\.0242\), with positive effects in every leave\-one\-MN\-donor\-out analysis\. GSE171458 provided concordant directional support in PT and loop/TAL\. The PT\-VCAM1 fraction was not increased in MN, whereas the program remained higher within both PT and PT\-VCAM1, favoring a within\-state transcriptional change\. Sixteen program genes showed positive average effects across the three single\-cell resources\. In GSE200828, the overall program was higher in MN than in living donors \(\+0\.226, FDR 0\.0349\) but not higher than in other glomerular diseases\. Protein overload increased ABCA1, ABCG1, SREBF1, FASN, and SCD expression in HK\-2 cells\. Across the three renal cancer histologies, the five\-gene panel showed a concordant paired increase \(REML\-pooled effect \+0\.967, 95% CI 0\.843–1\.092\), and 85\.3% of patients exhibited simultaneous increases in the cholesterol\-efflux and lipid\-synthesis/remodeling arms\. Conclusion MN is associated with a donor\-supported LXR\-linked cholesterol\-handling transcriptional state concentrated in PT and loop/TAL\-related epithelial compartments\. The state is responsive to protein overload in HK\-2 cells and may reflect a broader tubular lipid\-homeostasis response to proteinuric kidney injury\. The data do not establish direct LXR activation, increased cholesterol\-efflux flux, or MN specificity\. The paired renal cancer analysis further identifies a coordinated tumor\-associated five\-gene lipid\-remodeling signature across KIRC, KIRP, and KICH, while not establishing tumor\-cell\-intrinsic LXR activation, immune effects, or a causal tumor mechanism\.
