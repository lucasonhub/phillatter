# Machine Learning\-Based Characteristic Survey of High Blood Pressure in Young Adults

**Autores:** Nne Rena Saturday; Igbudu Ezebunwo Kingsley; Vivian Onyinyechi Anthony

**Data:** 2026-10-09 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Amongst the six major constituent of cardiovascular ailment worldwide, hypertension \(high blood pressure\) is the third in rank\. High blood pressure \(HBP\) serves as a preceding event for many other illnesses, its subtle way of affecting patients makes it important to monitor and find a way of early detection before it becomes a serious illness in the lives of patients\. A growing percentage of young adults and teenagers are suffering from hypertension \(HBP\) in recent times and poses a problem of high mortality rate\. This study is focused on predicting the occurrence of HBP in a young adult based on young adults’ responses in order to assist in curbing the problem\. A logistic predictive model \(binary logistic regression\) was used to predict the possibility of a young adult being hypertensive\.
