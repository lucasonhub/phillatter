# Mediación asistida por inteligencia artificial: implicaciones éticas en la resolución de conflictos

**Autores:** Martha Cecilia Rosero\-Puetate; Jhon Sebastián Fuertes\-Realpe; Hamilton Fernando Lucero\-Arellano; Angie Michelle Velasco\-Campaña

**Data:** 2026-10-15 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Artificial intelligence \(AI\) is entering alternative dispute resolution and raises ethical questions about party autonomy, impartiality and transparency\. The study analysed the ethical implications of AI\-assisted mediation, with emphasis on its possible use in Ecuador\. The research was qualitative and documentary, reviewing scholarship, domestic rules, international instruments and studies on online dispute resolution\. The results indicate that AI can improve efficiency and widen access, but it carries risks of dehumanisation, algorithmic opacity and reproduction of bias, and that Ecuadorian law does not regulate its use in mediation\. It is concluded that adoption requires ethical principles, specific regulation and constant human oversight\.
