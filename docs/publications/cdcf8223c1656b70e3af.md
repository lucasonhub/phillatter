# Comparison of the Melbourne rapid fields\-web device\-independent online perimeter and the octopus 600 perimeter in glaucoma patients

**Autores:** George Y\. X\. Kong; Algis Jonas Vingrys; Manisha Ambawat; Md Mahboob; Shakira Bano; Syril Kumar Dorairaj; Shibal Bhartiya

**Data:** 2026-10-12 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Introduction The purpose of this study is to evaluate the agreement, repeatability, and testing efficiency of Melbourne Rapid Fields\-Web \(MRF\-Web\), a browser\-based, device\-independent perimeter, compared with the Octopus 600 perimeter in glaucoma patients\. Methods This retrospective, cross\-sectional, single\-center observational study included 113 patients undergoing routine visual field assessment for glaucoma or glaucoma suspicion at Marengo Asia Hospitals, Gurgaon, India\. MRF\-Web testing was performed on a calibrated 14\-inch laptop with Hindi voice guidance\. Agreement between MRF\-Web and Octopus 600 for Mean Deviation \(MD\) and pattern\-based global indices was assessed using intraclass correlation coefficients \(ICCs\) and Bland–Altman analysis\. Test\-retest repeatability was evaluated in 51 patients who underwent repeat MRF\-Web testing\. Results MRF\-Web showed strong group\-level concordance with Octopus 600 for MD, with an ICC of 0\.88 \(95% CI, 0\.81–0\.92\), Bland–Altman bias of \-0\.78 dB, and 95% limits of agreement from \-4\.72 to 3\.17 dB\. Concordance for the pattern\-based indices was moderate, with an ICC of 0\.72 \(95% CI, 0\.58–0\.81\), bias of \-0\.59 dB, and 95% limits of agreement from \-4\.41 to 3\.24 dB\. MRF\-Web demonstrated good test\-retest concordance, with an ICC of 0\.86 \(95% CI, 0\.69–0\.94\)\. Mean per\-eye test duration was significantly shorter with MRF\-Web than with Octopus 600 \(232\.9 ± 49\.8 versus 342\.4 ± 94\.4 seconds\)\. Conclusions MRF\-Web demonstrated strong group\-level concordance with Octopus 600 MD measurements and shorter per\-eye testing times in this predominantly mild\-disease cohort\. The limits of agreement indicate that measurements from the two platforms should not be regarded as directly interchangeable, and further study is required in moderate and advanced glaucoma\.
