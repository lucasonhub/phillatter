# Nyholm, Sven: The Ethics of Defining Artificial Intelligence

**Autores:** 

**Data de descoberta:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** unknown

## Descrição do feed

In Manuel Gustavo Isaac, Lily E\. Frank &amp; Jeroen Hopster, Reshaping the world: Conceptual engineering for emerging science and technologies\. Routledge\. forthcomingThe history of the concept of artificial intelligence \(AI\) has partly been a history of evolving definitions of what AI is or should be defined as being\. This chapter reflects on this observation partly with an eye to the normative question of what definition of AI might be the best or right definition to use and partly with an eye to the meta\-normative question of whether it matters — and if so, why — how we define AI\. In addition to reviewing some classic definitions of AI, the chapter also considers more recent suggestions\. Ultimately, the chapter argues that it makes sense to have a broad and inclusive definition of what qualifies as artificial intelligence, and that this definition should leave it open whether or not AI is always a force for good\. The chapter also argues that we should distinguish between weak and strong forms of artificial general intelligence\.
