## Resumo

Background Catheter ablation for symptomatic atrial fibrillation \(AF\) is an established treatment, with cryoballoon \(CBA\) and radiofrequency ablation \(RFA\) showing similar success rates\. However, comparative data on health\-related quality of life \(HRQoL\) outcomes between these methods are limited\. This study compares HRQoL changes following CBA vs\. RFA in symptomatic AF patients\. Methods This sub\-analysis of the FREEZE Cohort Study \(NCT01360008\) compared HRQoL outcomes between CBA \(group A\) and RFA \(group B\) using the EQ\-5D\-3L questionnaire and EHRA symptom scores at baseline and follow\-up\. Changes in mobility, self\-care, usual activities, pain/discomfort, anxiety/depression, EQ\-VAS, and general physical condition \(GPC\) were assessed\. Results Between 2011 and 2016, 4,189 patients were included, with 2,110 \(50\.4%\) completing all HRQoL questionnaires\. The mean age was 61\.8 ± 10\.4 years, with 39\.2% female patients\. Group A had a higher proportion of patients with paroxysmal AF \(72\.8% vs\. 65\.4%, p &lt; 0\.01\)\. Overall complication rates were similar \(7\.6% vs\. 10\.1%, p = 0\.07\)\. The median follow\-up was 434 \(375, 517\) days\. Atrial arrhythmia recurrence was more frequent in group B \(45\.0% vs\. 56\.9%, p &lt; 0\.0001\), as were repeat ablations \(7\.9% vs\. 22\.3%, p &lt; 0\.001\) and rehospitalizations \(26\.0% vs\. 46\.7%, p &lt; 0\.0001\)\. Both groups experienced significant improvements in HRQoL\. After multivariable adjustment, group A remained associated with greater improvement in pain/discomfort \(aOR 1\.62, 95% CI 1\.26–2\.08\), EQ\-VAS \(aOR 1\.60, 95% CI 1\.27–2\.02\), and the EuroQol 5D\-Score \(aOR 1\.39, 95% CI 1\.10–1\.76\)\. Conclusion Both CBA and RFA significantly improved HRQoL\. However, CBA was associated with greater improvements in pain/discomfort, EQ\-VAS, and the EuroQol 5D\-Score, indicating potential differences in selected HRQoL outcomes between ablation modalities\. Clinical Trial Registration: NCT01360008\.
