## Descrição do feed

\_Minds and Machines\_ 36 \(4\):47\. 2026This study investigates whether large language models \(LLMs\) can replicate the human abductive reasoning patterns reported in Douven and Mirabile \( 2018 \)\. Three experiments with 16 models from seven developers test sensitivity to explanation quality, the suppression effect of alternative explanations, and the link between quality difference and metacognitive confidence\. To address concerns about data contamination \(the materials from Douven and Mirabile’s paper may have appeared in models’ training corpora\), we created new materials with the same evidential structure and conducted all experiments with both the old and the new materials\. Our findings, supported by model\-level inferential tests and mixed\-effects analyses, indicate that LLMs replicate the key human patterns on both sets of materials\. Moreover, when models are compared with individual human participants rather than with the average human response, their quality\-sensitivity falls within the range of individual human variation\. \(direct link \)
