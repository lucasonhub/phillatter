# Front\-resolved mode\-II–III nominal driving\-force evolution and group\-validated machine\-learning prediction for high\-speed railway wheel\-tread cracks

**Autores:** Qian Xiao; Zeda Wang; Zhongxu Xu

**Data:** 2026-10-17 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Closed wheel\-tread cracks can remain compression\-dominated during rolling contact while sustaining substantial in\-plane and out\-of\-plane shear\. A sequential ABAQUS–FRANC3D–Python framework resolves this response over ten crack\-geometry updates\. A closure\-aware nominal driving force combines event\-paired differences in the mode\-II and mode\-III stress\-intensity factors\. Four arc\-length descriptors summarise its front distribution: mean, peak, peak coordinate and coefficient of variation\. Controlled comparisons evaluate initial crack size and orientation, axle load, speed and wheel–rail friction\. Thirty Latin\-hypercube finite\-element conditions provide 4,272 front points for condition\-grouped machine\-learning validation\. ExtraTrees achieves a case\-macro cross\-validation RMSE of 16\.9587 MPa·mm1/2\. Four held\-out numerical conditions, comprising 579 front points, give a pointwise RMSE of 8\.5794 MPa·mm1/2\. Front coordinate and initial orientation have the largest mean absolute SHAP contributions\. The predicted terminal profiles quantify differences in driving\-force magnitude and localisation across conditions within the sampled domain\.
