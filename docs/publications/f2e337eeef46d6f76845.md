# Beyond battered baby syndrome: Neonatal case of osteogenesis imperfecta

**Autores:** Sanchit Singla; Mohit Kumar; Neha Kc; Jaivinder Yadav

**Data:** 2026-10-09 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo
