# Prediction equations for nutrient intake and piglet performance

**Autores:** Camilla Mendonça Silva; Tamires Arimatéa Leal; Maria Clara Moura Oliveira de Souza; Priscila Antão dos Santos; Ana Paula Del Vesco; Valdir Ribeiro Junior; Silas B\. Félix; R\. C\. S\. Silva; Júlio Cézar dos Santos Nascimento

**Data:** 2026-10-13 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

The objective of this study was to develop linear regression equations incorporating lactose intake and supplementation duration to predict nutrient intake and performance of weaned piglets\. Additionally, a bibliometric analysis based on author keywords and science mapping techniques was conducted to identify thematic structure and research trends related to lactose use in piglet diets\. A meta\-analysis was performed using studies published between 2002 and 2021 evaluating lactose\-containing diets and defined supplementation periods\. The database comprised 20 publications, 24 diets, and 749 piglets \(initial body weight 6\.11 ± 0\.69 kg; age 20\.79 ± 0\.72 days\)\. Lactose intake ranged from 7\.62 to 90\.58 g/day \(mean 42\.86 g/day\), and supplementation lasted 15\.25 ± 2\.80 days\. Lactose intake and supplementation duration were identified as significant predictors of performance\. The models estimated nutrient intake \(R² = 0\.59; P = 0\.001\), lysine intake \(R² = 0\.57; P = 0\.002\), metabolizable energy intake \(R² = 0\.57; P = 0\.002\), and weight gain \(R² = 0\.37; P = 0\.0104\)\. These equations predict nutrient intake, energy consumption, and growth performance of weaned piglets based on lactose intake and supplementation duration, providing a practical tool for diet formulation\.
