# AIEdTechism\. Revolutionising and Characterising a New Learning Theory in the Age of AI\-Ed\.

**Autores:** McGregor Alison

**Data:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

As artificial intelligence reshapes classrooms, do our existing learning theories still explain how people learn? This presentation introduces AIEdTechism, a proposed learning theory for the age of AI in education, and sets out what characterizes it and how it could change the way teachers, designers and researchers think about teaching with AI\. Presented at the Twentieth International Conference on Technology, Knowledge, and Society\.
