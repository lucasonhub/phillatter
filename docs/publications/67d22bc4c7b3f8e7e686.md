# How will AI affect theorizing in management research?

**Autores:** Stephan Davys Manning

**Data:** 2026-10-14 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Abstract As the use of artificial intelligence \(AI\) tools is becoming increasingly common in all aspects of research, there is a growing debate as to how and how much such tools will influence theorizing as well\. In this viewpoint article, I discuss three plausible scenarios that could arise from a combination of efficiency pressures, practice shifts and collective choices: \(i\) theorizing will remain human‐centric while empirical research will be AI‐centric; \(ii\) theorizing will become more data‐driven and evidence‐based thanks to AI, crowding out paradigm‐based theorizing; and \(iii\) theorizing will be professionalized as an AI‐enhanced workflow\. I compare characteristics and major implications of each scenario and reflect on skills we scholars need to develop to effectively theorize with and without AI going forward\.
