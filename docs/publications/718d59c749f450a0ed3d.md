# EFEKTIVITAS PENGGUNAAN MEDIA PICTURE SERIES TERHADAP PENINGKATAN KETERAMPILAN BERBICARA BAHASA INGGRIS SISWA KELAS IV SDN TAMAN BARU 2

**Autores:** Rizka Hidayati; Ratna Dewi; Kurniati Rahmadani

**Data:** 2026-10-10 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

This study addresses the problem of low English speaking skills among fourth\-grade students at SDN Taman Baru 2, characterized by low self\-confidence, limited vocabulary, and difficulty constructing simple oral sentences\. This research aims to determine students&\#x27; English speaking skills before and after the implementation of picture series media and to examine the effectiveness of this media in improving students&\#x27; speaking skills\. This study employed an experimental method with a one\-group pretest\-posttest design\. Data were collected through speaking tests administered as a pretest and posttest to 30 fourth\-grade students \(class IV\-A\) selected using the purposive sampling technique\. Data analysis was conducted through prerequisite tests \(normality and homogeneity tests\) and hypothesis testing using the Wilcoxon signed\-rank test, along with the N\-Gain test to measure the level of improvement\. The results revealed that the students&\#x27; average speaking skill score increased from 53\.00 in the pretest to 83\.50 in the posttest, with a significant hypothesis test result \(Z = \-4\.851; Asymp\. Sig\. \(2\-tailed\) = 0\.000 &lt; 0\.05\)\. The mean N\-Gain score was 0\.6944 \(69\.44%\), categorized as moderate\. These findings indicate that picture series media is effective in improving the English speaking skills of fourth\-grade students at SDN Taman Baru 2, as it provides visual support that helps students generate ideas, expand vocabulary, and build confidence in oral expression\. Teachers are recommended to integrate picture series media as an alternative strategy for teaching speaking skills at the elementary school level\.
