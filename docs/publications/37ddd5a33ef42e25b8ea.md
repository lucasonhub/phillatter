# Beyond Technology Adoption: The I\-TRACK Model of AI\-TPACK Strategy Formation in Chinese Culture Teaching in Thailand

**Autores:** Kanokporn Numtong; Keran Wang; Kewalee Petcharatip; Ming Li

**Data:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

This study develops a practice\-grounded model explaining how high school Chinese language teachers in Thailand integrate artificial intelligence, pedagogy, and cultural content knowledge when forming and revising instructional strategies\. Semi\-structured interviews with 30 teachers were analyzed using grounded theory procedures\. Six categories were identified: institutional and personal conditions, teacher judgment and strategic mediation, learner response and strategy revision, pedagogical adaptation for Thai learners, contextual challenges in teaching Chinese culture, and knowledge\-driven content transformation\. These categories form the I\-TRACK model, in which teacher judgment connects contextual and institutional conditions with AI\-supported content transformation, localized pedagogy, and feedback\-based revision\. The model distinguishes AI\-TPACK as the professional knowledge base from I\-TRACK as the process through which that knowledge is enacted in culturally responsive practice\. The study contributes a process\-oriented explanation of AI integration and offers implications for teacher development, curriculum design, and educational AI tools in TCSOL\.
