# The Role of Micro\-Ultrasound in Prostate Cancer Detection

**Autores:** Navid Jabalameli; Feres Camargo Maluf; Fatema Bhinderwala; Thomas James Polascik; Soroush Rais‐Bahrami

**Data:** 2026-10-09 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

PURPOSE OF REVIEW: To synthesize current evidence on the role of 29\-MHz micro\-ultrasound \(microUS\) in prostate cancer \(PCa\) detection and to define its position relative to multiparametric MRI \(mpMRI\) across the diagnostic pathway\. We conducted a narrative review of prospective, comparative, and randomized studies, with clinically significant prostate cancer \(csPCa\) detection as the primary endpoint\. RECENT FINDINGS: MicroUS provides \~ 70\-µm axial resolution and enables real\-time PRI\-MUS\-based risk stratification\. Across head\-to\-head studies, microUS achieves diagnostic performance comparable to mpMRI\. Evidence supports utility in biopsy\-naïve patients, selected MRI\-negative men with persistent clinical suspicion, repeat\-biopsy settings, active surveillance, and local tumor staging, while combined mpMRI\-microUS approaches have been shown to maximize yield of csPCa detection\. Key limitations include operator dependence, interobserver variability, lack of validated biopsy\-avoidance thresholds, and reduced sensitivity in anterior lesions\. MicroUS is a practical, high\-resolution platform that complements mpMRI and may enhance accessibility, efficiency, and precision in prostate cancer care\.
