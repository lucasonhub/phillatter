# Behavioral Intention Towards Artificial Intelligence Integration and Teachers’ Self\-Efficacy

**Autores:** Joan S\. Baricuatro; Pepa V\. Pontillas; Gerlinda G\. Corpuz; Jovit D\. Comon

**Data:** 2026-10-17 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

The rapid integration of artificial intelligence \(AI\) in education has reshaped instructional practices, making teachers’ readiness and confidence critical considerations\. This study determined the level of Grade 7 teachers’ behavioral intention towards AI integration and self\-efficacy\. Specifically, it described the respondents’ profile in terms of age, sex, position, educational attainment, and AI\-related training; their behavioral intention based on attitude, subjective norm, and perceived behavioral control; their self\-efficacy concerning student engagement, instructional strategy, and classroom management; the relationship between the two variables; and whether respondents’ profile moderates this relationship\. A quantitative descriptive\-correlational design was employed, involving 185 Grade 7 teachers from the districts of Kadingilan, Damulog, Kibawe, and Dangcagan in the Division of Bukidnon, selected through universal sampling\. Data were gathered using a researcher\-made questionnaire\. Mean, standard deviation, Pearson Product\-Moment Correlation, and multiple linear regression were used for statistical treatment\. Findings revealed a very high level of behavioral intention and self\-efficacy among teachers, with a significant positive relationship between the two variables, further moderated by respondents’ profile, particularly position and trainings attended\. The study concludes that teachers’ favorable behavioral intention toward AI strengthens their self\-efficacy\. It is recommended that school heads may enhance teachers’ readiness and confidence in integrating AI into instruction\.
