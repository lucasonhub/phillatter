# Bending\-dominated miniature creep testing: A synthesis of theory and practice

**Autores:** Wei Sun; Guo\-yan Zhou; Ming Li; Renfan Luo; Zhixun Wen; Zhufeng Yue; Shantung Tu

**Data:** 2026-10-09 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

This paper presents a unified framework for interpreting miniature bending creep test data, encompassing simply supported beams, fixed beams, cantilever beams, and bending plates, through the systematic synthesis of previously developed formulations within a common reference\-stress framework\. Material\-independent but geometry\- and loading\-mode\-dependent conversion parameters \( η , β \) relate measured global displacement to equivalent uniaxial creep response, with the Equivalent Gauge Length \( L EGL \) providing a useful geometry\-dependent metric for test sensitivity\. Analytical solutions are compiled for plane\-stress, plane\-strain, and finite\-width 3D conditions, together with corresponding load\-line displacement\-rate formulations\. The framework is further extended to consider creep damage, large\-deformation effects, and multilayer systems\. Experimental comparisons support the applicability of the reference\-stress conversion approach for the specific configurations and materials considered, while analytical and numerical extensions identify areas requiring further validation\. A comparative selection matrix highlights the characteristics and practical considerations of different miniature bending configurations\. The resulting framework provides a consistent basis for extracting creep properties from limited material volumes and supports high\-temperature structural integrity assessment and life management\.
