# MASLD, cardiometabolic and inflammatory disorders

**Autores:** Alina N\. Saidi

**Data:** 2026-10-09 · **Tipo:** dissertation

## Resumo

This thesis addresses the underrecognized burden of MASLD and liver fibrosis in high\-risk patient groups\. Focusing primarily on individuals with cardiometabolic conditions, including T2DM, obesity, and arterial hypertension, as well as patients with rheumatoid arthritis \(RA\), a chronic inflammatory disease linked to elevated CV risk, the studies aim to fill critical gaps in understanding MASLD prevalence, fibrosis severity, and associated CV risk\. Additionally, the thesis evaluates the impact of combined pharmacological and lifestyle interventions on liver health in patients with T2DM and overweight or obesity\. Together, these investigations seek to improve identification, risk stratification, and management of MASLD in clinical practice, ultimately contributing to better patient outcomes\.
