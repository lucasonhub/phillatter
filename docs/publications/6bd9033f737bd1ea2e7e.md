# Add\-on sirolimus in refractory iMCD\-TAFRO: a TAFRO\-enriched 8\-patient case series with paired single\-cell profiling

**Autores:** Remi Sumiyoshi; Tomohiro Koga; Toru Michitsuji; Masataka Umeda; Ayuko Takatani; Yoshito Nishimura; David C\. Fajgenbaum; Frits van Rhee; Atsushi Kawakami

**Data:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Introduction Anti–IL\-6 therapy with tocilizumab \(TCZ\) is ineffective in a proportion of patients with idiopathic multicentric Castleman disease \(iMCD\), particularly the TAFRO subtype, and rescue options remain limited\. Evidence for mTOR inhibition comes mainly from cohorts in which iMCD\-TAFRO was underrepresented\. Methods Eight Japanese patients with refractory iMCD \(iMCD\-TAFRO, n=6; iMCD\-NOS, n=1\) or TAFRO syndrome without iMCD \(n=1\) received add\-on sirolimus \(1–2 mg/day\)\. Five had active refractory iMCD\-TAFRO at initiation and formed the efficacy set; Case 5 \(sirolimus for glucocorticoid sparing\), Case 1 \(iMCD\-NOS\) and Case 3 \(TAFRO syndrome without iMCD\) are reported descriptively\. Response was defined as improvement in at least one refractory feature \(CRP &lt;0\.3 mg/dL, platelets &gt;15×10 4 /µL, improvement of anasarca, or a ≥50% rise in eGFR\) at 3 and 12 months\. Results In the efficacy set, 4/5 responded at 3 months \(80%; 95% CI 28–99\) and 3/4 evaluable at 12 months \(75%; 95% CI 19–99\); a prespecified TAFRO\-phenotype analysis \(n=6, including Case 3\) gave 5/6 \(83%; 95% CI 36–100\) and 4/5 evaluable \(80%; 95% CI 28–99\)\. Under a prespecified stringent definition additionally requiring CRP normalization and no escalation of concomitant immunosuppression, proportions fell to 3/5 \(60%\) and 2/4 evaluable \(50%\), each reclassification resting on a single criterion\. All six responders eventually discontinued sirolimus without relapse during available follow\-up\. Glucocorticoids were withdrawn in all four responders receiving prednisolone, TCZ intervals were extended to ≥5 weeks in three patients, and both patients with dialysis\-dependent active disease stopped hemodialysis\. Exploratory paired single\-cell RNA sequencing in one TAFRO\-phenotype patient showed reductions in inflammatory monocytes and B cells with lower cytotoxic and proliferative programs\. Discussion Given the very small sample, wide confidence intervals and the sensitivity of the estimates to the response definition, add\-on sirolimus should be regarded as a hypothesis\-generating adjunctive rescue option on top of standard treatment, not a substitute, in refractory iMCD\-TAFRO\.
