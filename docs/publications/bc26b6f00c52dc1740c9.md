# M\., Evoluit: EVOLUISM: A Brief Introduction

**Autores:** 

**Data de descoberta:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** unknown

## Descrição do feed

The continuing growth of scientific knowledge, formal theory, and artificial intelligence raises a question that concerns more than the amount of what remains unknown: can increasing theoretical power culminate in a final determinate account of Reality? Evoluism approaches this question through the structural relation among distinction, condition, determinacy, warranted scope, and limit\. Its central result, the Strong Evoluist Theorem, holds that no finite theory can become the final theory of Reality while remaining semantically determinate\. This philosophical result has subsequently generated an eight\-study formal research programme establishing exact corresponding limitations within broad but explicitly defined classes of theoretical architectures and interfaces\. This article provides a compact statement of the resulting philosophical architecture\. It develops the distinction between World and Reality, introduces Evoluism as a philosophy of post\-totalising orientation, clarifies the status of the formal programme, and outlines its developing consequences for psychology, freedom, ethics, religion, artificial intelligence, and institutions\. These domain developments retain their own evidential and argumentative requirements while sharing a common discipline: the force of a finite form remains proportionate to the grounds that warrant it\. The broader shift is from the search for final theoretical possession to an architecture in which knowledge, commitment, and action can remain strong without requiring final closure\.
