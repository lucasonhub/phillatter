# Description of Cochylimorpha lapoana sp\. n\. \(Lepidoptera Tortricidae\) from the Apennines of Central Italy

**Autores:** Pasquale Trematerra

**Data:** 2026-10-12 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Cochylimorpha lapoana Trematerra sp\. n\. is described as new; the latter belongs to the Straminea group\. As adult C\. lapoana resemble C\. meridiana \(Staudinger, 1859\), in the male genitalia the new species resemble C\. decolorella \(Zeller, 1839\), C\. tiraculana \(Bassi &amp; Scaramozzino, 1989\), and C\. straminea \(Haworth, 1811\)\.
