# THE HUMAN SPECIFIES THE MACHINE EXECUTES : On AI Successionism, the Arithmetic That Forbids It, and the Architecture of the Signer

**Autores:** M\. TAALABI; Team LLM\-DIPLOMAT

**Data:** 2026-10-14 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

A thesis is circulating in technology, in the longtermist community, and in the marketinglanguage of the AI industry\. It has several names\. The most direct is successionism\. The claim: AI is the successor to humanity\. Not &quot;AI is a tool\.&quot; Not &quot;AI is a mirror\.&quot; Not &quot;AI is a category of system we must govern\.&quot; But:AI is what comes next\. The next dominant intelligence\. The next stage\. This paper argues that the claim fails on two independent grounds: First — the architectural ground\. Succession requires a stable object, a direction, aspecification, and a trust root\. A stochastic system has none of these\. A successor cannotbe signed\. The architecture of every certifiable system already answers the question: thehuman specifies, the machine executes\. Second — the arithmetic ground\. Even if the architectural question were set aside, thescaling path that successionism depends on is bounded by physical constants\. The energytable is not a forecast\. It is a ratio\. The successor cannot be built\. Two arguments\. Both close the same door\. From different directions\. Neither depends on the other\. The paper also names the actor who is proceeding anyway — on the record, with a factory — and states what the response is, and what it is not\.
