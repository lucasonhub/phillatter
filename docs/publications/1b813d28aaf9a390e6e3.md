# Segmentation of ovarian cancer tumors from CT images based on a two\-stage neural network fusion system

**Autores:** Miruna\-Cristina Andone; Loretta Ichim; Dan Popescu

**Data:** 2026-10-14 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

The detection of ovarian cancer using computed tomography images is making rapid progress, combining the strengths of different neural networks within a complex ensemble, a more effective model can be created to support medical decision\-making in ovarian cancer cases\. Various neural networks and ensemble models were tested on the TCGA OV database\. Tumor regions were segmented using computer\-assisted annotation masks, while molecular subtype labels were assigned at the patient level based on TCGA genomic classifications and mapped to tumors\. As the main novelty, the proposed method involves a two\-stage fusion system\. The first stage combines several neural networks into four ensembles to capture diverse features, and the second stage integrates these results into a global system to enhance segmentation accuracy\. The selected models demonstrated strong segmentation performance within the investigated experimental setting and were subsequently integrated into the proposed two\-stage fusion framework\. The neural networks for the first stage are Attention U\-Net, DeepLabV3\+, and ViT\. The results show that the proposed two\-stage fusion system achieved higher observed segmentation performance than the evaluated individual neural networks and first\-stage ensemble configurations within the present TCGA\-OV experimental setting\. Across the five evaluated classes, the Global System achieved an average F1 score of 93\.53%, recall of 93\.38%, and precision of 93\.68%, supporting the technical feasibility of the proposed two\-stage fusion approach for ovarian tumor segmentation within the investigated dataset\.
