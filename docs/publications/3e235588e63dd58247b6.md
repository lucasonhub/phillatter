# The Mediating Role of Fall Risk in the Relationship Between Loneliness Levels and Quality of Life Among Older Adults Living Alone

**Autores:** Ayla Hendekçi; Hacer Gök Uğur

**Data:** 2026-10-09 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Objective: This study was conducted to determine the mediating role of fall risk in the relationship between loneliness levels and quality of life among older adults living alone\.Materials and Methods: The research was a correlational descriptive study\. It was carried out with older adults individuals living alone in a provincial center in the Eastern Black Sea Region\. The study sample consisted of 131 older adults\. Data were collected using the Information Form, Loneliness Scale for the Older Adults, Morse Fall Risk Scale, and Quality of Life Scale for the Older Adults\.Results: The study determined a moderate negative correlation between loneliness levels and quality of life, a moderate positive correlation between loneliness levels and fall risk, and a moderate negative correlation between fall risk and quality of life among older adults individuals living alone \(p&lt;0\.001\)\. Fall risk was found to have a partial mediating role in the relationship between loneliness and quality of life\. When the mediating variable, fall risk, was included in the model, loneliness and fall risk together explained 68\.5% \(R²=0\.685\) of the variance in quality of life\.Conclusion: It was determined that loneliness increases fall risk, loneliness and fall risk reduce quality of life, and fall risk plays a partial mediating role in the relationship between loneliness and quality of life among older adults individuals living alone\. Interventions should be developed to prevent loneliness and reduce the risk of falls in older adults\.
