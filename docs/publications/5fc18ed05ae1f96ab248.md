# Artificial Intelligence and China’s Cross\-Border E\-Commerce Development: Threshold Effects and Transmission Mechanisms

**Autores:** Meina Zhao; Lu Zhou

**Data:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

The integration of artificial intelligence and search technology is transforming traditional online shopping models, particularly cross\-border e\-commerce \(CBEC\), which involves complex customer multi\-attribute decision\-making with numerous considerations\. This study systematically examines the influence of artificial intelligence on the high\-quality advancement of CBEC and its underlying mechanisms using panel data from 30 Chinese provinces covering the period 2013–2023\. The study found a significant positive correlation between artificial intelligence and cross\-border e\-commerce\. This conclusion remains valid after a series of robustness tests \(including removing the interference of pandemic\-related data, substituting core explanatory variables, substituting the dependent variable, and lagging explanatory variables by one period\)\. Mechanism analysis indicates that the positive impact of AI on the high\-quality development of cross\-border e\-commerce is realized through improvements in logistics capabilities and the unlocking of consumer potential, with mediation effects accounting for 40\.27% and 17\.02%, respectively\. Threshold model results show that the positive impact of AI on the high\-quality development of cross\-border e\-commerce exhibits a single threshold effect based on the level of human capital; once human capital exceeds the threshold value, the coefficient further increases\. Heterogeneity analysis reveals that AI has the most significant empowering effect on the development of cross\-border e\-commerce in the eastern regions; however, its impact on the central and western regions is relatively minor\. It is worth noting that AI exerts its empowering effect more effectively in regions with high e\-commerce procurement activity, whereas the relationship is not significant in regions with low activity\.
