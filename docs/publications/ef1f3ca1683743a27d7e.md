# Artificial Intelligence in Healthcare: From Innovation to Clinical Applications and Regulatory Frameworks

**Autores:** Amisha Saini, Jasmine Sagwal, Tamanna Sirohi, Neha Saini, Rupinder Kaur

**Data:** 2026-10-17 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

ABSTRACTThis thorough analysis examines the revolutionary significance of artificial intelligence \(AI\)in contemporary healthcare, stressing its development, uses, difficulties, and legalconsiderations\. From its initial limits, artificial intelligence has quickly advanced to providesophisticated features like deep learning and predictive analytics\. It is transformingmedication discovery, rehabilitation, treatment planning, diagnostics, and healthcaremanagement\. Patient care is becoming more accurate, efficient, and personalized thanks toapplications like data\-driven decision support systems, virtual care, and AI\-enhancedimaging\.
