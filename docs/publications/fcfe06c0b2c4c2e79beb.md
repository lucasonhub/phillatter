# THE USE OF ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE TECHNOLOGIES IN TEACHING INFORMATICS

**Autores:** Ibaydullayev Dostonbek

**Data:** 2026-10-14 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

This article explores the opportunities for using artificial intelligence technologies in teaching computer science and examines their potential impact on educational effectiveness\. AI tools can support personalized learning, explain complex concepts, improve programming skills, provide automated feedback, and reduce teachers’ routine workload\. The article also discusses important challenges, including data privacy, information security, academic integrity, and the reliability of AI\-generated content\. Practical recommendations are proposed for integrating artificial intelligence into computer science lessons in a responsible and pedagogically effective manner\.
