# A Bidirectional Molecular Barrier for Efficient and Stable Inverted Perovskite Solar Cells

**Autores:** Yue Wei; Yiqi Fu; Zhiguo Zhu; 蔡彭麟; Yao Huang; Xin Xie; Yafeng Li; Mingdeng Wei

**Data:** 2026-10-14 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

ABSTRACT The inward diffusion of Ag electrodes and outward migration of perovskite constituents trigger a vicious degradation cycle in inverted perovskite solar cells \(PSCs\), severely limiting device longevity\. Herein, 2,5‐dimercapto‐1,3,4‐thiadiazole \(DMTD\) is introduced as a bidirectional ionic barrier layer to simultaneously stabilize the Ag electrode and the perovskite lattice\. The ‐SH groups strongly coordinate with Ag \+ to establish a molecular barrier against Ag migration, while the ‐N‐N‐ groups bind Pb 2\+ to passivate defects and reinforce the crystal structure\. This synergistic bidirectional inhibition effectively prevents interfacial reactions between perovskite species and Ag \+ , thereby breaking the chain of mutually reinforced degradation\. As a result, DMTD‐modified devices achieve a power conversion efficiency of 26\.13% and exhibit outstanding stability, retaining 87\.68% of their initial PCE after 3000 h of storage at 25°C \(15% ± 5% RH\), and 80\.87% after 1000 h under continuous heating at 85°C\.
