# Comprehensive study on the Mn–ZnO/SiO2 nanocomposites: from the digital colorimetry to the visible\-light photocatalysis

**Autores:** Michael Nazarkovsky; Mariia V\. Galaburda; Artur Sokołowski; Bożena Czech; Enrique Roy Dionisio Calderon; João F\. Chaves; Olena Artiushenko; Danian A\. Dugato; R\.J\. **Data:** 2026-10-13 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

A series of manganese\-doped ZnO/SiO 2 nanocomposites was studied to establish the structure\-property relationships governing visible\-light photocatalysis and to assess whether digital colorimetry, chemometrics and machine learning can provide rapid, nondestructive composition control for doped semiconductor/support systems, using the antiviral drug lopinavir as a model pollutant\. The novelty lies in coupling machine learning, chemometrics\-supported spectroscopy, and digital colorimetry with conventional characterization to enable rapid, nondestructive compositional quality control of this doped oxide/support system\.Nanocomposites containing 3, 6, and 9 mmol of Mn–ZnO per gram of SiO 2 were prepared by a wet chemical route followed by thermal treatment at 600 °C\. They were characterized by X\-ray diffraction, Raman spectroscopy, scanning electron microscopy with AI\-assisted image segmentation, nitrogen adsorption–desorption, atomic force microscopy, UV\-visible spectroscopy and photoluminescence\. Digital images were classified using convolutional neural network \(Inception v3\) embeddings combined with a support vector machine, and infrared spectra \(FTIR and IR\-ATR\) were modeled by principal component analysis and NIPALS regression\.Hexagonal wurtzite ZnO formed, with crystallite sizes of 27\.7–31\.0 nm\. Changes in the Raman A 1 \(LO\) mode were consistent with defect\-related lattice distortion, including contributions from oxygen vacancy\- and zinc\-interstitial\-related states\. The predominantly mesoporous solids exhibited specific surface areas of 110–160 m 2 g −1 and enhanced visible\-light absorption with defect\-mediated photoluminescence\. The classification pipeline accurately discriminated the samples, and IR\-ATR provided improved compositional predictions compared with conventional FTIR\. The most active composite, 9\-Mn–ZnO/SiO 2 , removed 96 % of lopinavir within 1 h under visible light \( k 1 = 0\.0465 min −1 \), with • OH and O 2 −• implicated as the dominant reactive species and stable performance over four reuse cycles\.This integrated approach provides mechanistic insight into structure\-property relationships and practical, non\-destructive quality\-control tools for visible\-light\-active doped metal oxide/support photocatalysts\.
