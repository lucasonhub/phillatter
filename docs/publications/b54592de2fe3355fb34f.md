# Bongo Optimization Algorithm: A Novel Nature\-inspired Metaheuristic Approach for Efficient Optimization

**Autores:** Zeinab Montazeri; Mohammad Dehghani; Raed Abu Zitar; Omar Almomani; Kei Eguchi

**Data:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Metaheuristic algorithms have attracted much attention in recent years due to their ability to solve complex optimization problems\.However, striking a good balance between exploration and exploitation remains a major challenge in designing these algorithms\.In this paper, a new algorithm, the Bongo Optimization Algorithm \(BOA\), is introduced, which is inspired by the natural behavior of Bongo in searching for food resources and selecting highquality resources\.In BOA, the behavior of Bongo&\#x27;s extensive movement in the habitat to explore different areas is used as the basis for designing the exploration phase, while the behavior of selective browsing of high\-quality food is used to focus on promising areas and improve existing solutions, which forms the basis for the exploitation phase\.This structure allows for an effective balance between global search and local refinement\.In order to evaluate the performance of BOA, a set of 23 standard benchmark functions including unimodal test functions \(F1\-F7\), highdimensional multimodal test functions \(F8\-F13\), and fixed\-dimensional multimodal test functions \(F14\-F23\) has been investigated\.The performance of BOA has been compared with 9 well\-known metaheuristic algorithms and the statistical measures of mean, standard deviation \(std\), and rank have been used to analyze the results\.In addition, the search behavior of BOA has been investigated through visual analysis, convergence curve, population diversity, exploration, and exploitation percentage\.The simulation results show that BOA has obtained the first\-best optimizer rank in all 23 benchmark functions F1\-F23 and has provided 100% successful and competitive performance compared to the investigated algorithms\.Also, the analyses have confirmed the ability of BOA to maintain appropriate diversity in the early stages and increase exploitation in the final stages of optimization\.The findings of this study show that BOA has been introduced as a powerful metaheuristic optimizer with high power in balancing exploration and exploitation and has the potential to be used in a wide range of optimization problems in science and real\-world applications\.Future studies can focus on developing multi\-objective, binary versions and applying BOA to complex engineering problems\.
