# Comparing Global and Segment\-Aware Machine Learning Models for Used Car Price Prediction

**Autores:** Rukiye Nur Kaçmaz; Hakan Onay

**Data:** 2026-10-14 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Background: Used car prices are influenced by model year, mileage, fuel type, transmission, engine size, engine power and market position\. This study compares global and segment\-aware machine learning approaches for used car price prediction using the CarDekho dataset from Kaggle\. Methods: After 167 exact duplicate extras were removed, 15,244 records were evaluated\. The primary analysis used ten sealed 80/20 outer splits\. Segment topology was selected by three\-fold cross\-validation within each outer\-training set, and fixed CatBoost/XGBoost expert profiles were combined through an XGBoost routing classifier\. Global, hard\-routing, soft\-gating and oracle variants were compared\. All target models used log1p prices and a three\-fold out\-of\-fold Duan retransformation correction\. Results: Across the full\-market splits, global XGBoost achieved R²=0\.8523±0\.1120, RMSE=349,460±180,834 INR and MAE=104,138±5,274 INR\. Soft gating achieved R²=0\.8733±0\.1007, RMSE=321,661±173,497 INR and MAE=99,196±5,479 INR\. Paired Wilcoxon p values were 0\.0039 for R² and RMSE and 0\.0020 for MAE\. In the seed\-42 paired bootstrap, the R² and RMSE intervals included zero, whereas the MAE reduction remained supported\. The secondary IQR\-restricted analysis yielded almost identical mean R² and RMSE for global and soft\-gated models, with a lower MAE for soft gating\. Conclusion: Segment\-aware modeling reduced average full\-market error, but the size of the advantage varied across splits\. The contribution is a controlled empirical comparison rather than a new learning algorithm\.
