# Sandal divination: A Little\-Noticed Areal Practice Across Eastern Africa

**Autores:** Peter Unseth

**Data:** 2026-10-13 · **Tipo:** dissertation

## Resumo

Authors have noted that various ethnic groups in eastern Africa have practiced a form of divination based on throwing sandals and then interpreting the way that they fell\. Evidence is presented here that it is found in South Sudan, Uganda, Ethiopia, Kenya, Tanzania, and Namibia, a much wider area than has been previously noted\.
