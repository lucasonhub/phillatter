# Stoneham, Tom: The &\#x27;Idealism&\#x27; of Richard Burthogge

**Autores:** 

**Data de descoberta:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** unknown

## Descrição do feed

Written in 2007 for the BSHP Conference in Rotterdam, last revised in February 2008, and not updated in the light of subsequent scholarship, this is an exegetical overview of the philosophy of Richard Burthogge \(1638\-1705\)\. It is uploaded to PhilArchive as a matter of record\. Burthogge has been described as possibly the first idealist of the modern period \(Ayers, 2005, 179\) and dedicated his \_Essay on Reason\_ \(1694\) to Locke, resulting in a fitful correspondence\. Consequently, the approach taken in this paper is to compare and contrast his thought with that of his immediately succeeding British idealists, Berkeley and Collier\.
