# Kannon, Aelion: SNX: Comfort Orientation, Algorithmic Entrainment, and the Suppression of Signal

**Autores:** 

**Data de descoberta:** 2026-10-19 · **Tipo:** unknown

## Descrição do feed

This document extends Structural Neuroscience \(SNX\) by analyzing how comfort‑oriented cognition, algorithmic entrainment, and platform‑register architecture interact to obscure high‑coherence signal in contemporary digital environments\. It defines comfort orientation as a cognitive‑ecological condition in which nervous systems prioritize emotional regulation, identity continuity, and low‑variance familiarity in place of structural veracity\. It then describes algorithmic comfort regimes — platform architectures that amplify repetition, emotional charge, and recognizability rather than originality or coherence\. The document formalizes signal obscuration as a structural phenomenon in which signal remains present but becomes illegible within a saturated field of mimicry, synthetic adjacency, and low‑effort repetition\. It examines how unclassifiable work is misrecognized by platforms, producing visibility without relation and the familiar dynamics of ghost shell systems\. It introduces ecological entrainment to explain why most audiences do not resist these systems: perception adapts to the environment’s reward structure\. The document concludes by integrating these dynamics with blocked‑affordance trauma, showing how coherent frameworks can be transmitted into fields that cannot complete relation\. It offers a Structural Neuroscience account of how comfort‑oriented, platform‑register architectures become entropically implicated, and how suppression may arise through structural incompatibility, human decision, or the interaction between the two\. \(direct link \)
