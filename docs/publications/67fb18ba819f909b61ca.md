# Nanocrystalline SrTi1\-x Fex O3\-δ perovskites as high\-performance electrodes for intermediate\-temperature symmetric SOFCs

**Autores:** Esteban Asto\-Ramos; María  Belén Arcentales\-Vera; Loreto Troncoso; Faramarz S\. Gard; Cristián Huck‐Iriart; Diego German Lamas

**Data:** 2026-10-12 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Nanocrystalline SrTi 1 − x Fe x O 3 − δ perovskites \(x = 0\.20–0\.50\) represent a compositionally tunable platform for cobalt\-free symmetric electrodes in intermediate\-temperature solid oxide fuel cells\. Synthesized via a gel\-combustion route, all as\-prepared compositions form single\-phase cubic perovskites with nanometric domains, while the Fe/Ti ratio governs lattice contraction, mixed Fe \+ 3 / F e \+ 4 surface chemistry, and redox responsiveness\. Electrochemical impedance spectroscopy reveals that Fe\-rich materials \(x = 0\.35 and x = 0\.50\) exhibit markedly lower polarization resistances than the x = 0\.20 composition under both oxidizing and reducing atmospheres\. Distribution of relaxation times analysis reveals that Fe incorporation markedly attenuates the polarization contributions across the intermediate\- and high\-frequency ranges, confirming accelerated charge\-transfer and surface\-reaction kinetics under both oxidizing and reducing conditions\. Under reducing conditions, STF50 undergoes adaptive structural evolution, including Fe exsolution and the emergence of Ruddlesden–Popper\-type domains, highlighting a dynamic interplay between high electrochemical activity and redox\-induced restructuring\. Preliminary button\-cell tests confirm the functional viability of STF50 as a symmetric electrode within the intermediate\-temperature range\. Overall, nanocrystalline SrTi 1 − x Fe x O 3 − δ emerges as a promising and structurally adaptive electrode family, in which controlled Fe substitution modulates defect chemistry, redox behavior, and electrochemical performance\. These findings demonstrate the potential of Fe\-rich titanate perovskites as next\-generation cobalt\-free electrodes for efficient and adaptable IT\-SOFC architectures\.
