# Thermal radiation metamaterials for on\-demand control of thermal emission: principles, designs, and applications

**Autores:** Bo Xu; Chengcong Li; Ping Jin; Hongjie Luo; Xun Cao; Bo Xu; Chengcong Li; Ping Jin; Hongjie Luo; Xun Cao

**Data:** 2026-10-10 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Thermal radiation is a fundamental channel for energy transfer and dissipation, playing a critical role in energy technologies, electronic thermal management, and defense\-related applications\. However, the intrinsically incoherent, broadband, and omnidirectional nature of conventional thermal radiation, typified by blackbody emission, makes it difficult to meet the growing demands of high\-performance and actively tunable applications\. The emergence of thermal radiation metamaterials has provided a route to overcome this limitation\. By incorporating artificially engineered structural elements, thermal radiation metamaterials enable control over the spectrum, direction, and polarization of thermal emission, thereby establishing a new paradigm for on\-demand tailoring of thermal emission\. We provide a comprehensive analysis of the control mechanisms and micro\- and nanostructures of thermal radiation metamaterials, as well as their applications in radiative cooling, thermal camouflage, and thermophotovoltaics\. Finally, we identify key challenges, including scalable fabrication, broadband performance and dynamic tunability, environmental durability, and system\-level integration, and highlight future opportunities enabled by artificial intelligence, inverse design, and data\-driven materials discovery\. We aim to provide a coherent framework for understanding, designing, and applying thermal radiation metamaterials and to inspire further advances in programmable and intelligent thermal\-radiation control\.
