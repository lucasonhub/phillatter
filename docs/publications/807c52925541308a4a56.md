# Edwards, J\.: Tychokinesis: Rethinking Mind–Matter Interaction as Distribution\-Level Influence

**Autores:** 

**Data de descoberta:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** unknown

## Descrição do feed

This paper chooses the term, Tychokinesis for a narrowly defined hypothesis concerning whether conscious intention is associated with small, statistically detectable deviations in stochastic physical systems\. Unlike deterministic psychokinesis, Tychokinesis concerns shifts in outcome frequencies across many trials rather than control of individual events\. The term overlaps with micro\-psychokinesis \(micro\-PK\) but is used here more narrowly to distinguish an operationally testable pattern\-systematic deviations under intention conditions from claims about the mechanism or metaphysical interpretation of any such effect\. Tychokinesis is not presented as the established phenomenon, but as the falsifiable framework for investigating whether intention reliably covaries with departures from expected statistical distributions\. A reproducible frequency deviation would constitute an empirical anomaly; although by itself, it would not necessarily demonstrate that objective probability had been altered\.
