# The Role of Artificial Intelligence in Administrative Decision\-Making in Iraq: Digital Transformation as a Model

**Autores:** Zahraa Alhalfi

**Data:** 2026-10-15 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

This research aims to examine the role of artificial intelligence \(AI\) in enhancing the quality and efficiency of administrative decision\-making in Iraq, adopting digital transformation as both an analytical model and an integrated practical framework\. The study focuses on the readiness of Iraqi government institutions to adopt intelligent systems and the impact of these technologies on improving decision\-making speed and transparency, reducing human error, and fostering a work environment grounded in data and intelligent analysis\. The research employs a descriptive\-analytical approach to review concepts related to AI and digital transformation, an inductive approach to infer relationships between variables, and a field\-based approach involving the analysis of government documents and reports\. Content analysis tools were also utilized to assess the extent to which AI applications—such as predictive systems, decision\-support algorithms, chatbots, and big data analytics tools—are being employed\. The findings indicate that the implementation of AI within Iraqi public administration remains in its early stages, despite official moves toward digitalization\. Key opportunities identified include faster decision\-making, increased transparency, reduced human intervention in routine tasks, and the creation of reliable databases to support planning and policymaking\. Challenges include weak digital infrastructure, limited technical skills among staff, the absence of a legal framework regulating artificial intelligence applications, and poor integration between government systems\. The research recommends formulating a comprehensive national strategy for artificial intelligence, developing digital infrastructure, upskilling government personnel, strengthening partnerships with the private sector and academic institutions, and updating legislation to align with digital governance and privacy protection standards, thereby ensuring a secure and effective environment for the adoption of smart technologies\.
