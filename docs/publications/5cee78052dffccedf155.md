# Robust DeepLearning\-Based FaceRecognition Under Unseen Combinations of Real\-World Image Distortions

**Autores:** Pradarsha Kolapudi; Sheshnag Degadwala; Malini Joshi

**Data:** 2026-10-09 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

While more recent work evaluates larger OOD situations and realistic degradations, deep face recognition can suffer when capture conditions deviate from training data; blur, noise, and occlusion have negatively impacted performance in face\-recognition evaluations\.\[1\-3\] Although staged or adaptive training and distortion augmentation are viable approaches, the research addressed here does not prove generalization to systematically held\-out combinations of natural distortions\.\[3, 4\] This paper makes the case that robustness evaluation should report task\-appropriate, subgroup, and clean\-image performance; differentiate distortion types, severities, and combinations; and use naturally acquired data\. The main finding is that deployment robustness cannot be fully demonstrated by single\-corruption outcomes\.
