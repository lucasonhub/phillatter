# Digital Agriculture and Sector Transformation in South Africa

**Autores:** Mokgadi Julia Ngoepe\-Ntsoane

**Data:** 2026-10-09 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Digitalisation is increasingly promoted as a catalyst for agricultural productivity, climate resilience, and market integration\. However, there is limited systematic analysis of how digital agricultural transformation unfolds within South Africa’s institutional and socio\-economic context\. This study examines the trajectory, drivers, and governance implications of digitalisation in South African agriculture\. Anchored in an integrated Socio\-Technical Systems and Digital Agriculture Ecosystem framework, the study applies a structured qualitative document analysis of 82 academic and policy documents published between 2013 and 2025\. The findings reveal highly uneven digital adoption patterns that reflect a structural dualism between capital\-intensive commercial agriculture and resource\-constrained smallholder systems\. While South Africa has developed a relatively advanced digital policy architecture, implementation fragmentation, infrastructure constraints, and limited capacity for extension impede inclusive digital outcomes\. The study contributes by integrating socio\-technical and ecosystem perspectives to analyse digital transitions in emerging economies and by providing a transparent analytical protocol for document\-based research\. Policy implications highlight the need for coordinated governance, investment in rural connectivity, and targeted support mechanisms to ensure equitable digital transformation\.
