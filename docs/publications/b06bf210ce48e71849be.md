# NITRIC OXIDE SYNTHASE ACTIVITY AND PLASMA CORTISOL DURING COMPREHENSIVE THERAPY FOR NON\-Q\-WAVE MYOCARDIAL INFARCTION

**Autores:** Olha Honchar; Ігор Миколайович Скрипник; O\. Ye\. Akimov

**Data:** 2026-10-17 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Introduction\. Non\-Q\-wave myocardial infarction is accompanied by changes in nitric oxide metabolism and the stress response\. The biochemical effects of adding temgicoluril to standard treatment require clarification\.Aim\. To assess changes in nitric oxide synthase isoform activity, nitrite, peroxynitrite and cortisol concentrations during standard therapy with or without temgicoluril\.Materials and methods\. The prospective study included 40 patients \(mean age 62\.9±11\.7 years, mean±standard deviation\), divided into two groups of 20\. Both groups received standard therapy and multidisciplinary rehabilitation\. Group 2 additionally received temgicoluril, 500 mg three times daily for one month\. Blood biochemical parameters were assessed before and after treatment and compared with those of 20 control participants\.Results\. Biochemical values are means ± standard errors\. Before treatment, both patient groups had lower endothelial enzyme activity and higher neuronal enzyme activity, nitrite, peroxynitrite and cortisol concentrations than controls\. Baseline values did not differ significantly between treatment groups\. Endothelial nitric oxide synthase activity increased in both groups during treatment, while nitrite, peroxynitrite and cortisol concentrations decreased\. After treatment, nitrite concentrations were 1\.25±0\.04 nmol/L in group 1 and 1\.00±0\.04 nmol/L in group 2\. Endothelial enzyme activity was lower in group 2 than in group 1 \(0\.34±0\.005 versus 0\.41±0\.001 µmol/min per g protein\)\. Inducible and neuronal enzyme activities were higher in group 2, whereas peroxynitrite concentrations did not differ significantly between groups\. Cortisol decreased from 252\.2±9\.2 to 67\.2±1\.2 nmol/L in group 1 and from 278\.5±10\.8 to 126\.2±3\.4 nmol/L in group 2\. Endothelial enzyme activity remained below the control value in both groups\.Conclusions\. Both regimens were associated with changes in nitric oxide metabolism and a decline in cortisol\. Temgicoluril use was associated with lower post\-treatment nitrite but higher cortisol concentrations than standard therapy alone\. These biochemical differences do not establish superior endothelial recovery or a clinical advantage of the additional treatment\.
