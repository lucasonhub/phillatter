# INTEGRATIV YONDASHUV ASOSIDA TALABALARNING TAHLILIY FIKRLASH KOMPETENSIYASINI RIVOJLANTIRISH METODIKASINI TAKOMILLASHTIRISH

**Autores:** Nilufar Matyakubova Karimberganovna

**Data:** 2026-10-12 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

This article examines the improvement of a methodology for developing students’ analytical thinking competence through an integrative approach\. The aim of the study is to substantiate methodological approaches that promote interdisciplinary integration in the educational process, connect theoretical knowledge with practical situations, and develop students’ abilities to analyze information, compare and synthesize data, draw logical conclusions, and make independent decisions\. The research methodology is based on systems and integrative approaches, a review of scientific and pedagogical literature, comparative analysis, synthesis, and the design of pedagogical methods\. The article explores the content and structural components of analytical thinking competence, as well as the pedagogical factors influencing its development\. It also substantiates the potential for improving educational effectiveness through the integration of interdisciplinary tasks, problem\-based situations, case studies, discussions, and reflective analysis\. As an expected outcome, the study presents an improved methodological model aimed at fostering students’ independent thinking, evidence\-based reasoning, and ability to apply knowledge in new contexts\. The proposed approach may contribute to enhancing students’ intellectual engagement and developing their analytical thinking competence in higher education\.
