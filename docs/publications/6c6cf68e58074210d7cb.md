# MONITORING STUDENTS&\#x27; LEARNING OUTCOMES AND ASSESSING INDIVIDUAL DEVELOPMENT WITH ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE IN A DIGITAL EDUCATIONAL ENVIRONMENT

**Autores:** Shakhnoza Mingyasharovna Ravshanova

**Data:** 2026-10-13 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

This paper provides a scientific and methodological analysis of monitoring students&\#x27; learning outcomes and assessing their individual development with artificial intelligence \(AI\) in a digital educational environment\. It examines the content of learning outcome monitoring, the components on which it relies \(data sources, indicators, analytical methods and feedback\), and the opportunities offered by AI\-based learning analytics\. It also defines three groups of criteria for assessing individual development: the dynamics of results, the achievement of learning goals, and independent work and reflection\. As a result, a six\-stage methodology is proposed in which the teacher keeps the final pedagogical decision\. The methodology is intended to support the timely and individualised assessment of students in higher education institutions\.
