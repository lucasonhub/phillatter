# ResQNet: A Crowdsourced Disaster Preparedness and Real\-Time SOS Mesh Network Using Wi\-Fi Direct MANET, Delay Tolerant Networking, and Artificial Intelligence

**Autores:** Hitha Sundar P S; Huma Anjum; Karan Vivek; Roshan Kumar B K; Divyashree

**Data:** 2026-10-15 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Natural disasters such as earthquakes, floods, cyclones, landslides, and wildfires frequently damage cellular towers and internet infrastructure, leaving victims unable to contact rescue teams and depriving responders of real\-time situational information\. This paper presents ResQNet, a crowdsourced, infrastructure\-independent disaster communication system that uses Wi\-Fi Direct\-based Mobile Ad Hoc Networking \(MANET\) and Delay Tolerant Networking \(DTN\) to relay SOS alerts between nearby smartphones without relying on cellular or internet connectivity\. Each smartphone in the network functions simultaneously as a sender, relay, and store\-and\-forward node: SOS packets, tagged with a unique identifier, timestamp, GPS coordinates, hop count, and time\-to\-live \(TTL\), are propagated hop by hop until they reach an internet\-enabled gateway device or a rescue authority\. Duplicate packet suppression and TTL\-controlled flooding are used to keep the network efficient under heavy load\. ResQNet further incorporates artificial intelligence for emergency severity classification, location\-based clustering of victims, accessibility analysis, and hotspot identification, together with voice\-triggered SOS activation for hands\-free operation\. A web\-based dashboard visualizes live SOS alerts, victim clusters, and priority levels on a map to support rescue coordination\. Simulation\-based evaluation of packet delivery ratio, latency, and message loss indicates that the proposed architecture can sustain reliable emergency communication even when conventional networks fail, offering a scalable and low\-cost foundation for future disaster\-response systems\.
