# The Role of the Multidisciplinary Team in Professional Identity Formation

**Autores:** Jenny Houston; Jocelyne Velupillai

**Data:** 2026-10-15 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

for their scoping review exploring professional identity formation \(PIF\) during the transition from undergraduate to postgraduate medical education\.They identified four themes influencing PIF: navigating the self, the system, clinical practice and collaboration\.They highlighted that PIF does not occur in isolation but is socially and organisationally situated\.Collaboration can promote belonging, strengthen supervisory relationships and create social learning opportunities\.
