# Applying ICH E9\(R1\) Sensitivity Analysis to an FDA Substantial\-Evidence Standard: An Allogeneic Cell Therapy Case Study in Duchenne Muscular Dystrophy

**Autores:** Anushree Siddharth Sheladia; Peivand Pirouzi

**Data:** 2026-10-15 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

The statutory standard of substantial evidence of effectiveness is often discussed in general terms, but its practical meaning is best understood through the specific sensitivity analyses a regulator applies to a contested result\. This article examines a representative allogeneic cardiosphere\-derived cell therapy for Duchenne muscular dystrophy \(DMD\), whose pivotal trial produced a sponsor\-reported, statistically significant functional benefit that collapsed toward non\-significance once a regulator applied its prespecified missing\-data handling rules to the sponsor&\#x27;s own revised analytic model, a robustness failure the article argues is the clearest practical illustration available of what the substantial\-evidence standard actually demands for a mutation\-agnostic therapy relying on a clinical functional endpoint rather than a validated surrogate\. The article develops the mechanism, the full analytic dispute, and the regulatory history this produced, situates the case within ICH estimand methodology, and proposes a real\-world evidence framework designed specifically around robustness and prespecification\. The article closes with recommendations for regulatory affairs professionals designing statistical strategy for functional\-endpoint programs in rare disease\.Keywords: substantial evidence of effectiveness; sensitivity analysis; estimands; Duchenne muscular dystrophy; allogeneic cell therapy; regulatory artificial intelligence
