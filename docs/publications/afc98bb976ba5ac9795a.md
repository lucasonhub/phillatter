# Raneses, Andaran: NARRATIO DE EGO \- CERITA TENTANG &quot;AKU&quot; \(A Book\)

**Autores:** 

**Data de descoberta:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** unknown

## Descrição do feed

BOOK DESCRIPTION NARRATIO DE EGO is a work of existential philosophy by Andaran Raneses that examines the fundamental problems of human existence: the fear of non\-being and the need to be recognized\. Across 27 chapters, the book traces the journey of the &quot;I&quot; — from the primordial condition of suffering, through moral structures, social systems, and the economy of recognition, to cosmic absurdity and existential alienation\. The Author does not offer final answers\. Instead, this book presents a testimony of how consciousness endures in the midst of emptiness\. Written in a reflective and argumentative style, NARRATIO DE EGO engages with the traditions of existentialism and absurdism, while maintaining an original voice\. It is not intended as doctrine, but as a personal\-philosophical account of what it means to be human in a world that provides no inherent meaning\. Intended for readers interested in philosophy, existential psychology, and contemporary reflective literature\. ABOUT THE AUTHOR Andaran Raneses an independent writer and thinker from Surabaya, Indonesia\. His work addresses questions of existential philosophy, ethics, and the condition of modern humanity\. His primary concern lies in consciousness, freedom, and the individual’s struggle with meaning and void\. NARRATIO DE EGO is a philosophical reflection on the fear of nothingness and the drive to be seen in a world that offers no certainty\.
