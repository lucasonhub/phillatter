# Causes of Brain Death and Medical Contraindications in Donors from Northwest Rio Grande do Sul \(2019\-2024\)

**Autores:** Giovanna Carolina Girotto; Guilherme Krahl

**Data:** 2026-10-09 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Objective: To analyze the causes of brain death and the medical conditions that prevent characterizing a potential donor as eligible, from 2019 to 2024 in the state of Rio Grande do Sul\. Methods: A retrospective, quantitative, descriptive study based on data collected from the Organ Procurement Organization of the 4th region of Rio Grande do Sul, located at Hospital São Vicente de Paulo in the city of Passo Fundo, between August and October of 2025\. Data collection involved reviewing of spreadsheets \(Microsoft Excel®\) from 2019 to 2024, as well as the organization of extracted information into graphs \(Google Drive®\)\. Records included both adults and children, excluding cases outside the established study period and contraindications due to family refusal\. The variables analyzed were the cause of brain death, sex, and medical contraindications\. Results: Between 2019 and 2024, approximately 583 brain death protocols were recorded, among which 68 contraindications were documented\. Conclusion: The most common causes of brain death observed were cerebrovascular accident, traumatic brain injury, and subarachnoid hemorrhage\. The main medical contraindications preventing a potential donor from becoming effective were neoplasms, active infections, and positive HIV serology\. Additionally, female sex showed higher rates of medical contraindications
