# Anterior\-to\-Posterior vs Direct Posterior Fixation of the Posterior Malleolus in Weber Type C Trimalleolar Ankle Fractures

**Autores:** Ahmet Polat; Mustafa Polat; Enes Başer; Yasin Kılıc; Osman Yüksel YAVUZ

**Data:** 2026-10-15 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Objective: To compare early and 1\-y outcomes after anterior\-to\-posterior screw fixation and direct posterior fixation of the posterior malleolus in Weber type C trimalleolar ankle fractures\. Methods: This retrospective cohort study included 183 patients: 78 treated with anterior\-to\-posterior fixation and 105 with direct posterior fixation\. The visual analog scale \(VAS\), Olerud\-Molander Ankle Score \(OMAS\), and American Orthopaedic Foot and Ankle Society \(AOFAS\) ankle\-hindfoot score were assessed at 3 mo and 1 y\. Radiological alignment, residual posterior step\-off, ankle motion, and return to work were also compared\. Results: At 3 mo, posterior fixation was associated with lower VAS scores \(mean \[SD\], 0\.99 \[1\.07\] vs 2\.09 \[1\.14\]\), higher OMAS \(mean \[SD\], 82\.11 \[6\.68\] vs 72\.76 \[8\.78\]\), and higher AOFAS scores \(mean \[SD\], 85\.19 \[6\.82\] vs 76\.78 \[7\.87\]; all P &lt; \.001\)\. These scores did not differ significantly at 1 y\. Syndesmotic screw fixation was less frequent after posterior fixation \(4\.8% vs 94\.9%; P &lt; \.001\)\. Residual posterior step\-off was slightly lower after posterior fixation \(mean \[SD\], 0\.06 \[0\.14\] mm vs 0\.09 \[0\.13\] mm; P = \.01\), with all values below 2 mm\. Mortise alignment, final ankle motion, and return\-to\-work time were comparable between groups\. Conclusions: Direct posterior fixation was associated with better early pain and function and less frequent syndesmotic screw fixation\. Clinical outcomes converged and were similar at 1 y\. Prospective studies are needed to determine whether these associations are causal\.
