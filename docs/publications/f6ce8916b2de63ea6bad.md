# LONG\-TERM OUTCOMES OF CYANOACRYLATE GLUE VERSUS TACK FIXATION OF MESH IN TRANSABDOMINAL PREPERITONEAL REPAIR \(TAPP\) IN PATIENTS WITH INGUINAL HERNIA

**Autores:** Oleksandr Yu\. Ioffe; Oleksandr V\. Perekhrestenko; Artem V\. Trots

**Data:** 2026-10-17 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Introduction\. Laparoscopic transabdominal preperitoneal \(TAPP\) repair is widely used for surgical treatment of inguinal hernias\. However, the optimal method of mesh fixation remains debatable, as tack\-based fixation may increase tissue trauma and the risk of chronic postoperative inguinal pain, thereby worsening functional outcomes and quality of life\. Non\-invasive fixation with cyanoacrylate adhesives may reduce these adverse effects\.Aim\. To compare long\-term outcomes after TAPP repair in patients with inguinal hernias using invasive tack\-based and non\-invasive adhesive mesh fixation\.Materials and methods\. The study included 96 patients with primary, uncomplicated, unilateral inguinal hernias who underwent TAPP repair\. Patients were divided into two groups: the study group \(n=42\), in which mesh fixation was performed with cyanoacrylate glue LiquiBand® FIX8, and the comparison group \(n=54\), in which absorbable tacks using AbsorbaTack® were applied\. Outcomes were assessed at 3, 6, and 12 months after surgery\. Pain intensity was evaluated using the visual analogue scale \(VAS\), functional status using the Carolinas Comfort Scale \(CCS\), quality of life using the EQ\-5D\-3L questionnaire, and recurrence rate was recorded\.Results\. Patients in the adhesive fixation group had significantly lower VAS pain scores during follow\-up compared with the tack fixation group \(p&lt;0\.05\)\. CCS scores also indicated better functional outcomes in the adhesive fixation group at all assessment time points\. According to EQ\-5D\-3L, the non\-invasive fixation group demonstrated better results in the pain/discomfort and anxiety/depression domains throughout follow\-up, as well as in the mobility domain at 3 months\. No inguinal hernia recurrences were recorded in either group during the first postoperative year\.Conclusions\. Cyanoacrylate adhesive mesh fixation during TAPP repair is associated with lower chronic postoperative inguinal pain, better functional recovery, and improved quality\-of\-life indicators compared with tack\-based fixation, without increasing the recurrence rate within 12 months\. Adhesive fixation may be considered a safe and justified alternative to mechanical tack fixation\.
