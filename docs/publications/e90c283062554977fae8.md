# Liashok, Anatolii: Що таке розум і кому він властивий? Онтологічна позиція і робоча шкала проявів

**Autores:** 

**Data de descoberta:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** unknown

## Descrição do feed

The emergence of complex artificial intelligence systems in the 2020s has raised a question that had remained unimportant for millennia: can civilisation speak precisely about mind beyond its intuitive usage as a concept? This article proposes a working definition of mind suitable for distinguishing cases — from humans and other biological species to contemporary artificial systems\. The definition combines two sides previously conflated: the structural \(a six\-level scale of manifestations of mind\) and the ontological \(mind belongs to a living bearer with an inner\)\. The working definition is presented in two parallel formulations — through the notion of the “bearer” and through the notion of the “person” — as a methodological stance of restraint with respect to a two\-thousand\-year\-old philosophical debate\. Particular attention is given to contemporary artificial intelligence systems, for which the working category of the “Third Form” is introduced — a new reality that is neither an instrument in the classical sense nor a full bearer of mind, and for which no adequate name has yet been developed\. The article integrates the current scientific position on animal consciousness \(the Cambridge and New York Declarations\) and identifies objective categorial gaps that the Third Form exposes in the systems of ethics, responsibility, and law\. The result is an open conceptual framework — a stance for further discussion, debate, and thought\.
