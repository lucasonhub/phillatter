# Efeitos do monitoramento durante a aquisição do responder sobre a manutenção do seguimento de regras

**Autores:** Letícia Fernanda da Silva; Maíra Costa Gonçalves; Mariéle Diniz Cortez

**Data:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

This study evaluated the effect of monitoring during the acquisition of responding on the maintenance of discrepant rule\-following\. Twenty college students, divided into two conditions, were exposed to a computerized matching\-to\-sample task\. In Phase 1 \(Acquisition of Responding\), participants were exposed to conditions in which the rule was corresponding to the contingencies in the presence \(Condition I\) or absence \(Condition II\) of the experimenter\. Phase 2 assessed, for all participants, the maintenance of rule\-following in blocks that were either corresponding or discrepant to the contingencies\. Five participants from Condition I and six participants from Condition II maintained rule\-following in the discrepant blocks\. No statistically significant difference was found in the number of correct responses in the discrepant blocks between the two conditions\. The presence of the experimenter during the acquisition phase of responding appeared to have little influence on the maintenance of following discrepant rules\.
