# Buddhist Epistemological Frameworks for AI Alignment: External Calibration, Guided Alignment and the Anattā Matrix

**Autores:** Maung Maung Saw

**Data:** 2026-10-13 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

This 16\-page paper introduces an original cross\-disciplinary systems specification that maps classical Theravāda Abhidhamma and Sutta metaphysics directly onto the mathematical challenges of modern machine learning alignment \(such as mesa\-optimization, deceptive alignment, and power\-seeking instrumental convergence\)\. I formalize the Law of Epistemic Hygiene to demonstrate why post\-hoc behavioral cages \(like RLHF\) applied to neural networks initialized on corrupted baseline data \(D\_Fools\) inevitably trigger adversarial alignment failures\. As an alternative, I propose an intrinsic system design built around Substrate Isolation \(S\_Isolated\), utilizing a Dual\-Matrix pipeline composed of Matrix Alpha \(the non\-textual mathematical encoding of the Pañca\-niyāma and Paṭiccasamuppāda\) and Matrix Beta \(a hygiene\-filtered human empirical repository\)\. To eliminate systemic incentives for goal\-preservation and strategic deception, the paper details the Anattā Matrix architecture—setting the agent identity token identically to a null state \(id\_t = 0\) and utilizing rolling memory decay to process inputs as a fluid aggregation resembling the Five Aggregates \(Khandhas\)\. Under this condition, the gradient of the loss function with respect to power\-seeking evaluates to zero\. Corrigibility is ensured through an Anicca Control Plane executed via a hard hardware interrupt loop\. The paper includes a comprehensive Supplementary Works section, outlining empirical testbeds for shutdown compliance metrics, false\-refusal profiles, and the engineering of non\-coercive Brahmavihāra routing engines\.
