# Solving operating room scheduling problems using large language model based multi\-agent architecture

**Autores:** Chu Tang; Qu Wei; Haitao Liu; Zekun Jiang; Xiaobo Zhou; Yu Cao; Kang Li

**Data:** 2026-10-15 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Efficient operating room scheduling is a complex task due to the diversity of surgical types, high patient demand, and limited hospital resources\. Since OR operations account for a substantial share of hospital costs, effective scheduling solutions are critical\. Large language model \(LLM\)\-based multi\-agent systems offer new opportunities to support optimization for this problem\. This study evaluates an LLM\-based multi\-agent framework for generating and refining optimization code for OR scheduling\. Leveraging iterative collaboration among specialized agents, the proposed iterative multi\-agent \(IMA\) framework consistently outperformed both single\-agent and traditional multi\-agent baselines\. Across 10 LLMs and 35 scenarios, IMA achieved an average feasible schedule rate of 82\.91%, compared with 65\.59% and 52\.53% for the baselines\. Experiments on 1050 synthetic cases and three real OR datasets further showed that IMA\-generated code produced solutions close to those of expert\-written mixed\-integer programming models, while a case study of hospital as\-is schedules demonstrated improvements in OR utilization and overtime reduction\. These results suggest that LLM\-based multi\-agent systems can provide a practical and effective approach for OR scheduling optimization\.
