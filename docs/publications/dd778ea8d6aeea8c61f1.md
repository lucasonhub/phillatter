# An enhanced model\-based EEG source imaging method captures dynamic changes during motor skill learning

**Autores:** Melisa Daryayi; Zakieh Hassanzadeh; Ashkan Mahdavian; Fariba Bahrami; Ahmad Kalhor; Behzad Moshiri

**Data:** 2026-10-12 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Understanding brain reorganization during motor learning requires methods with both high temporal and spatial resolution\. We developed an enhanced model\-based approach to solve the EEG inverse problem that combines a multi\-layer perceptron neural network with finite element modeling for subject\-specific head conductivity estimation\. The framework resolves up to six simultaneously active sources across 116 cortical and subcortical regions and achieves 85\.98% source localization accuracy, outperforming existing methods \(67\.1% accuracy, 1 even under varying noise conditions\. A dedicated leakage analysis using single\- and multi\-region simulations confirmed minimal cross\-talk between cortical motor and deep subcortical/cerebellar sources, supporting reliable separation of activity across brain depths\. As a proof\-of\-concept application, we used this framework to analyze EEG data from five healthy adults performing a novel foot\-drawing motor learning task across multiple sessions, with behavioral performance improving after the fourth session\. Model\-based source estimates revealed a broad reorganization of cortical and subcortical activity across sessions, consistent with functional refinement of motor networks with practice; given the small cohort \( n = 5\), these findings are preliminary and offered as suggestive of learning\-related dynamics rather than definitive evidence\. This enhanced model\-based EEG source localization approach offers a practical, non\-invasive method for combining computational modeling with neuroimaging of motor behavior\.
