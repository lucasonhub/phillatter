# Flood Relief Management System for Multi\-Agency Disaster Response

**Autores:** Muhamad Alif Ikmal Abdul Halim; Nor Kamaliah Khamis; Ahgalya Subbiah

**Data:** 2026-10-10 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Flooding places recurrent pressure on disaster\-response agencies in Malaysia, where fragmented communication, delayed resource distribution, and limited operational visibility can reduce the effectiveness of relief activities\. This study develops and validates a Flood Relief Management System \(FRMS\) that integrates real\-time water\-level monitoring with centralized coordination of humanitarian resources and rescue activities\. The system combines an ESP32 microcontroller, a non\-contact ultrasonic water\-level sensor, a local liquid\-crystal display and traffic\-light alert module with a web dashboard supported by MySQL and Firebase databases\. Development followed an Agile, stakeholder\-informed process and was validated through user acceptance testing involving 15 stakeholders from government, non\-governmental organizations, emergency services, community leadership, and flood\-affected groups\. Three operational workflows were assessed\. Live telemetry and threshold configuration achieved a 100\.0% task\-completion rate with a median execution time of 4\.5 s and a usability rating of 4\.8/5\. Donation and resource tracking achieved 93\.3% completion in 8\.2 s with a rating of 4\.6/5, while rescue dispatch routing achieved 86\.7% completion in 10\.5 s with a rating of 4\.5/5\. Page loading remained below 1\.2 s in the tested configuration\. The findings indicate that a lightweight, shared digital platform can improve situational visibility and support more coordinated multi\-agency flood relief operations\.
