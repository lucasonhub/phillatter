# CFRD\-Net: Calibrated Residual Latent Diffusion Network for Pomegranate Fruit Quality and Disease Classification

**Autores:** Harish Bommenahalli Mallikarjunaiah; Balaji Prabhu Baluvaneralu Veeranna

**Data:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Fruits are essential components of the global diet which plays a major role in agricultural growth and the economy\.However, high\-value fruits like pomegranates frequently lose the market\-value due to quality deterioration, ripeness variations, surface defects, and diseases\.Therefore, this research introduces the Calibrated Residual Latent Diffusion Network \(CFRD\-Net\) for pomegranate fruit quality classification\.The CFRD\-Net architecture integrates Latent Conditional Diffusion learning, Residual Feature Calibration \(RFC\), and Residual Latent Refinement \(RLR\)\.This integration enables the learning of robust, discriminative latent representations, which captures local fine\-grained texture irregularities, surface defects, and global ripeness\-related color distributions\.The model requires 62\.34 million parameters and 11\.86 GFLOPs, demonstrating a trade\-off between accuracy and computational cost\.CFRD\-Net achieved 99\.33% accuracy on the Pomegranate Fruit Quality Grading Dataset and 98\.75% accuracy on the Pomegranate Fruit Diseases Dataset\.These results indicate that CFRD\-Net performs effectively under the evaluated benchmark settings for automated pomegranate image classification\.
