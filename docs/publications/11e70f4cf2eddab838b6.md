# Immune cell\-derived exosomes in the tumor microenvironment: mechanisms of immune evasion and emerging therapeutic strategies in cancer immunotherapy

**Autores:** Rashid Mir; Mohammad Fahad Ullah; Mamdoh S\. Moawadh; Faisal H\. Altemani; Jameel Barnawi; Naseh A\. Algehainy; Mohammed M\. Jalal; Malik A\. Altayar; Reema Almotairi; Faris Jamal Tayeb; Ruqaiah I\. Bedaiwi; Kholoud S\. Almasoudi; Ibrahim Altedlawi Albalawi; Aeshah A\. Awaji

**Data:** 2026-10-14 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Tumor\-released exosomes have emerged from the shadows of “cellular dust” to be recognized as pivotal mediators of intercellular communication within the tumor microenvironment \(TME\)\. These nano\-sized vesicles \(30–150 nm\) carry a complex cargo of proteins, lipids, and nucleic acids that profoundly reshape anti\-tumor immunity\. This review comprehensively examines the dualistic nature of immune cell\-derived exosomes in cancer progression, with particular focus on exosomes originating from B lymphoma cells, T lymphocytes, natural killer \(NK\) cells, and myeloid\-derived suppressor cell lineages including tumor\-associated macrophages \(TAMs\), mast cells, and neutrophils\. We delineate the mechanistic pathways through which these exosomes disrupt the cancer immunity cycle suppressing CD8\+ T cell activation via PD\-L1/PD\-1 engagement, inducing lymphocyte apoptosis through FasL and TRAIL, promoting regulatory T cell expansion, polarizing macrophages toward the M2 immunosuppressive phenotype, and establishing pre\-metastatic niches through MMP\-mediated extracellular matrix remodeling\. The molecular machinery governing exosome biogenesis including ESCRT\-dependent and ceramide\-mediated pathways is critically reviewed, alongside the selective packaging mechanisms that concentrate immunosuppressive miRNAs \(miR\-155, miR\-21, miR\-23a, miR\-223\) into exosomal compartments\. Importantly, we highlight the emerging therapeutic premises targeting these vesicles: pharmacological inhibition of exosome secretion \(GW4869, Rab27a knockdown\), checkpoint blockade reversing exosomal PD\-L1\-mediated T cell exhaustion, and the engineering of immunostimulatory exosomes as cell\-free cancer vaccines establishing exosomes not merely as biomarkers of disease progression but as actionable therapeutic targets\. The clinical translation of exosome\-based diagnostics including liquid biopsy platforms detecting circulating tumor\-derived exosomes and therapeutics that include engineered exosomes delivering immunomodulatory cargo represents a frontier in precision cancer immunotherapy\.
