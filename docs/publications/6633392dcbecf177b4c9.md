# Agroecologia e animais: reflexões sobre invisibilidade e relações interespecíficas nos sistemas alimentares

**Autores:** Juliana Vieira Paz; Anna Cristina Souza; Beatriz S\. Souza; Paulo Eduardo Moruzzi Marques; Carolina Refinetti Schiesari

**Data:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Agroecological transition is necessary in the face of socio\-environmental crises associated with agrifood chains\. However, non\-human animals are frequently excluded from discussions, even though they are part of most agricultural systems\. When included, the motivations are centered on anthropocentric interests, keeping them invisible\. This marginalization reproduces the concept of animal welfare and the interspecific relationships developed from the industrial logic, preventing a full rupture with this model of exploitation\. This Agroecological Note argues that discussions on agroecology must urgently include non\-human animals in a meaningful way, effectively breaking with the industrial, anthropocentric, and exploitative model and logic that have been perpetuated in animal husbandry, even where agroecology has been the guiding principle\.
