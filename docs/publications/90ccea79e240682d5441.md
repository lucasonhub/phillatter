**Autores:** Alexander C\. Poltzer; Kiana N\. Jones; Alexandra J\. Sheridan; Nikolay Sirakov

## Resumo

• New vector fields from linear combinations of PDE solution functions studied\. • The new VFs produce non\-isolated singularities with a novel pair of eigenvalues\. • The vector field singularities exhibit invariance under affine transformations\. • Vector fields can be embedded into databases to improve image distinguishability\. • Convolutional neural network shows improvements in medical image classification\. This research is a continuation of several studies that developed the vector fields \(VFs\) ∇ ϕ ^ , ∇ ‾ ϕ ^ , ∇ ψ ^ , and ∇ ‾ ψ ^ generated from the solution of the Poisson Image Equation\. In the present study, we develop new VFs generated from linear combinations of the above VFs after proving they are not constant multiples of each other\. Further, we classify their singular points \(SPs\), determine their locations, and formulate mappings between critical points \(CPs\) and corresponding VF SPs\. We also prove the conditions for SPR existence and explore SP shape invariance under affine transformations\. The paper concludes with a discussion on the advantages of using the new VFs and SPRs and outlines directions for future work\.
