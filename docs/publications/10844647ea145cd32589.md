# Strategic Management in AI\-Orchestrated Organizations: A Dynamic Capabilities Perspective

**Autores:** Christian Estay Ojeda Ojeda

**Data:** 2026-10-14 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Artificial intelligence \(AI\) is increasingly influencing organizational strategy, decision\-making, resource allocation, and operational coordination\. As AI moves from task automation and analytical support toward generative systems and intelligent agents, its role is expanding across organizational functions\. AI\-orchestrated organizations integrate AI capabilities with human expertise to support information analysis, opportunity identification, decision support, workflow coordination, and learning\. This creates opportunities for greater strategic responsiveness, while also raising issues of decision authority, accountability, governance, and organizational control\. This study examines AI orchestration through a dynamic capabilities perspective and develops a conceptual framework linking AI\-enabled sensing, strategic seizing, resource reconfiguration, human–AI coordination, governance, and continuous learning\. The framework positions AI as an enabling organizational capability rather than an isolated technological resource and emphasizes the interaction between technological capabilities, human judgement, organizational resources, and governance\. It further explains how these elements can support strategic responsiveness, organizational agility, and continuous adaptation as organizations respond to changing environments\. The study develops seven conceptual propositions to provide a structured basis for future empirical examination of AI\-orchestrated strategic management\.
