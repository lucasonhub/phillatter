# Hypothesis\-generating network toxicology analysis of potential molecular convergence between a polystyrene oligomer proxy and papillary thyroid carcinoma

**Autores:** 谢伟东; Jian Tang; Xiaoyu Qian

**Data:** 2026-10-14 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Background Polystyrene microplastics \(PS\-MPs\) are emerging environmental contaminants with endocrine\-disrupting and pro\-inflammatory effects, but whether PS\-MPs\-associated molecular targets converge with papillary thyroid carcinoma \(PTC\) alterations remains unclear\. This hypothesis\-generating study aimed to identify candidate molecular networks and cellular contexts shared between a simplified polystyrene computational proxy and PTC\. Methods Putative targets from CTD and from TargetNet/SwissTargetPrediction using a five\-styrene\-unit oligomer proxy were intersected with thyroid cancer\-related genes from OMIM and GeneCards\. Protein functional\-association and enrichment analyses were followed by differential expression analysis in GSE33630 \(45 normal and 49 PTC samples\)\. LASSO and SVM\-RFE identified core genes; stability was examined by repeated nested resampling conditional on the seven\-gene candidate panel\. Individual\-gene tumor\-normal discrimination was assessed in GSE33630 and GSE60542\. Protein patterns were evaluated qualitatively in the Human Protein Atlas and preliminarily in 15 matched PTC\-adjacent tissue pairs\. CIBERSORT\-estimated immune fractions and two single\-cell datasets were analyzed as exploratory context\. Results Among 144 putative proxy\-associated targets and 2,139 thyroid cancer\-related genes, 75 were shared\. Seven candidates overlapped with 1,306 PTC differentially expressed genes, and BCL2, BAX, and FN1 were selected by both feature\-selection methods and remained stable across the tested GeneCards thresholds and CTD\-only sensitivity analysis\. BCL2 was downregulated, whereas BAX and FN1 were upregulated in PTC; individual\-gene AUCs were 0\.963, 0\.941, and 0\.965 in GSE33630 and 0\.942, 0\.907, and 0\.955 in GSE60542\. HPA images and clinical IHC supported the same protein\-level directions\. After multiple\-testing correction, only the CIBERSORT\-estimated neutrophil fraction differed between groups \(higher in PTC; q=0\.0021\)\. Single\-cell analysis localized the three genes across epithelial, stromal, and immune compartments\. Conclusion BCL2, BAX, and FN1 are candidate molecular nodes at the convergence of polystyrene\-proxy annotations and PTC\-associated alterations\. The findings do not demonstrate patient PS\-MPs exposure, intact\-particle effects, or causality, and require validation in material\-characterized exposure experiments and exposure\-aware clinical studies\.
