# Data Privacy Challenges in the Age of Artificial Intelligence: A Study of Privacy Awareness and AI Regulation

**Autores:** Isha Singadiya; Harpreet Kaur

**Data:** 2026-10-15 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Abstract: The rapid adoption of Artificial Intelligence \(AI\) has increased the collection, processing and automated use of personal data\. While AI supports innovation, personalization and efficiency, data\-intensive AI systems create concerns relating to privacy, consent, transparency, profiling, security, surveillance and individual control\. In India, the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 provides a statutory framework for digital personal data, while the Digital Personal Data Protection Rules, 2025 provide the implementation framework\. This study examines privacy awareness and stakeholder perceptions of data\-protection regulation in an AI\-driven environment using secondary empirical evidence obtained from an existing published research/data source\. The analysis focuses on privacy awareness, awareness of selected rights under the DPDP Act, perceived regulatory effectiveness, privacy\-business balance and grievance redressal\. Reported statistical evidence indicates higher privacy\-awareness scores for professional stakeholders than laypersons, while organizational communication is associated with stronger awareness of privacy rights\. Keywords: Artificial Intelligence, Data Privacy, Privacy Awareness, AI Regulation, DPDP Act 2023, AI Governance, Data Protection, Ethics\.
