# Theoretical and Methodological Foundations for Preparing Prospective Primary School Teachers to Use Generative Artificial Intelligence in Teaching the Russian and Kyrgyz Languages

**Autores:** A\. Iusupova; Zh\. Algozhoeva; Asylgul Sarinjievna Jumataeva

**Data:** 2026-10-15 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

The article addresses the pressing scholarly problem of preparing prospective primary school teachers to use generative artificial intelligence \(GenAI\) in teaching the Russian and Kyrgyz languages within the context of the digital transformation of education and the transition of the Kyrgyz Republic to a new, modern twelve\-year model of school education\. The relevance of the study stems from the shared need to update the content of teacher education, the rapid development of artificial intelligence technologies, and the increasing requirements for the digital and methodological competence of modern teachers\. The analysis of contemporary international research, regulatory documents, and national and international pedagogical experience demonstrates the need to develop a new component of prospective teachers’ professional preparation — AI\-based linguodidactic competence\. This competence is understood as an integrative ability of a teacher to use generative artificial intelligence in a pedagogically justified, ethically responsible, and safe manner when designing, organizing, and assessing the process of teaching the Russian and Kyrgyz languages in primary school\. For the first time, the article proposes an original concept for preparing prospective primary school teachers to use generative artificial intelligence in language education\. It outlines the methodological foundations, structural components, and pedagogical conditions for implementing this concept, as well as the practical possibilities of integrating advanced intelligent digital technologies into language\-teaching methodology\. The proposed author\-developed model comprises motivational and value\-oriented, cognitive, technological, methodological, communicative, and reflective\-evaluative components\. It is aimed at developing prospective teachers’ professional readiness to work in a modern digital educational environment\. The practical significance of the study lies in the possibility of using the proposed model to improve teacher\-education programmes at higher educational institutions in the Kyrgyz Republic, introduce new academic disciplines, develop professional development programmes for teachers, and support the practical implementation of the Concept for the Transition to a Twelve\-Year General Education System\.
