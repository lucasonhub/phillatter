# Sustainable agro\-waste ash reinforced aluminium matrix composites with emerging potential of nano\-banana peel ash in Al7075

**Autores:** Ch\. V S R Satya Prasad; N\. Rao

**Data:** 2026-10-15 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Abstract The development of sustainable aluminium matrix composites using agricultural waste\-derived reinforcements provides an alternative approach to utilizing biomass residues in advanced engineering materials\. In this study, nanoscale banana peel ash \(Nano\-BPA\) was synthesized through dehydration, calcination, and high\-energy ball milling and incorporated into an Al7075 alloy by stir casting\. Nano\-BPA was characterized using particle size analysis \(PSA\), scanning electron microscopy \(SEM\), energy\-dispersive spectroscopy \(EDS\), and thermogravimetric analysis \(TGA\)\. The Al7075 base alloy and composites containing 2 and 4 wt% Nano\-BPA were fabricated and evaluated under dry sliding wear, Charpy impact, and immersion corrosion conditions\. Particle size analysis yielded a D50 value of 20\.2 nm, with D10 and D90 values of 12\.4 and 33\.8 nm, respectively\. The specific wear rate decreased from 1\.160 × 10⁻⁴ mm³/\(N·m\) for Al7075 to 8\.456 × 10⁻⁵ and 3\.315 × 10⁻⁵ mm³/\(N·m\) for the 2 and 4 wt% Nano\-BPA composites, respectively\. The absorbed impact energy increased from 5\.5 ± 0\.20 J for Al7075 to 7\.0 ± 0\.25 and 8\.0 ± 0\.30 J for the 2 and 4 wt% Nano\-BPA composites, respectively\. After 72 h of immersion in a 3\.5 wt% NaCl solution, the measured corrosion rate decreased from 0\.649 ± 0\.027 mm/year for Al7075 to 0\.460 ± 0\.027 and 0\.203 ± 0\.014 mm/year for the 2 and 4 wt% Nano\-BPA composites, respectively\. Among the investigated compositions, the 4 wt% Nano\-BPA composite exhibited the best overall measured performance\. These results demonstrate the feasibility of Nano\-BPA as a sustainable reinforcement for improving the experimentally evaluated wear, impact, and corrosion performance of Al7075 composites\.
