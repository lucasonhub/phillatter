# Career Development and Job Selection Using Fuzzy TOPSIS: A Multi\-Criteria Decision\-Making Model

**Autores:** R\. Chandrasekar; M Ramachandran; Arunambigai Ramesh

**Data:** 2026-10-17 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Career Making decisions is a difficult process that entails weighing several variables to determine which suitable job path\. The Fuzzy Technique for Order Preference by Similarity to Ideal Solution \(Fuzzy TOPSIS\) is used in this study to assess and rank career alternatives based on key job attributes\. The research focuses on four primary evaluation parameters: employment prospects, job stability, job adaptability, and job advantages and perks\. Research Significance: This study provides a structured and data\-driven approach to career selection, enabling individuals and policymakers to make well\-informed decisions by prioritizing job attributes based on their importance\. The Fuzzy TOPSIS methodology helps in handling uncertainty and subjectivity in career evaluations, offering a reliable decision\-making model\. Methodology: The Fuzzy TOPSIS method is used to evaluate and prioritize various career alternatives\. The process involves normalizing decision matrices, determining fuzzy weights, calculating ideal solutions, both positive and negative, and calculating closeness coefficients to rank career choices\. Alternative Career Options: The study evaluates different career paths based on the following parameters: Career opportunities \(growth potential and advancement prospects\), Job security \(stability and long\-term sustainability\), Job flexibility \(work\-life balance and adaptability\), Job benefits and perks \(compensation, incentives, and additional benefits\) Results: The analysis ranks career options based on their alignment with the ideal job conditions\. The findings suggest that job security and career opportunities are the most influential factors, while job benefits and perks have relatively lower weight\. The results provide valuable insights for career planning by highlighting the most favorable career paths based on individual preferences and priorities\.
