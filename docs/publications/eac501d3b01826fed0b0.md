# PROSPECTS FOR THE APPLICATION OF HYBRID ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE ALGORITHMS IN INVENTORY OPTIMIZATION

**Autores:** Dilfuzakhon Abdullayeva

**Data:** 2026-10-12 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

This conference paper examines a hybrid artificial intelligence framework that connects demand forecasting with practical inventory decisions under variable and uncertain market conditions\. The approach integrates a forecasting module, a probabilistic uncertainty assessment module, and a constrained optimization module to support replenishment planning, safety\-stock calculation, and service\-level management\. Particular attention is given to intermittent demand, lead\-time variability, cost\-sensitive decisions, and the use of forecasting combinations\. A structured methodology for future empirical validation is outlined using chronological evaluation and inventory\-related performance measures\. The proposed framework provides a conceptual basis for developing adaptive decision\-support systems in retail and logistics\.
