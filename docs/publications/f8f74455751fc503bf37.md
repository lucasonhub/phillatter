# Applying Generative Artificial Intelligence to Personalized Theatre Productions and Film

**Autores:** Kira Mineeva

**Data:** 2026-10-11 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

This study explores the potential of generative artificial intelligence in theatre and film, focusing on personalized audience experiences, creative production processes, and emerging business models\. It examines the opportunities offered by AI\-driven content generation and adaptation, as well as the associated risks, including ethical concerns, intellectual property issues, and the potential impact on creative professionals\. The research also considers how personalization may influence audience engagement and consumer behavior\. Finally, it proposes practical approaches to responsible AI implementation in the creative industries, emphasizing the importance of balancing technological innovation with artistic integrity, transparency, and human creativity\.
