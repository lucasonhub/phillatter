# PRIME: Pre\-Retrieval Index Modeling Enhancement for Structured Document Retrieval\-Real

**Autores:** Cheuk Hei Chung; Li Liu

**Data:** 2026-10-09 · **Tipo:** preprint

## Resumo

High\-precision retrieval over external knowledge bases is essential for LLM agents to operate effectively\. However, existing Information Retrieval \(IR\) research primarily optimizes query\-side retrievers \(e\.g\., ColBERT, BGE\) and generators \(e\.g\., Self\-RAG, GraphRAG\), while reducing corpus\-side representation to chunking\. This leaves index\-side quality an underexplored lever\. We propose Pre\-Retrieval Index Modeling &amp; Enhancement \(PRIME\), an index\-side optimization framework built on two complementary tracks: an LLM\-driven Semantic Restructuring \(SR\) track that transforms long documents into structured, information\-dense sections, and a deterministic Metadata Synthesis \(MS\) and Hierarchical Retrieval \(HR\) track that extracts semantic anchors and heading structure for coarse\-to\-fine section retrieval\. Our evaluations show that deterministic metadata matches structure\-aware chunking&\#x27;s retrieval quality while cutting embedding computation by 45\.8 $\\times$ and index vector count by 22\.4 $\\times$\. On top of these gains, Semantic Restructuring \(SR\) further improves Answer\-F1 by 13\.7% in full deployment, with characterized quality\-fidelity\-cost trade\-offs\. Across QASPER, BRIGHT, and NFCorpus, we further characterize three qualitative regimes that determine whether index\-side optimization helps or degrades retrieval, while showing that these index\-side effects are neutralized under a strong reranker\.
