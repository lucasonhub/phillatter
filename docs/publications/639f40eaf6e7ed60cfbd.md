# Evaluating ONOS as a complementary literature retrieval platform using neonatal sepsis mortality as a test case

**Autores:** Melissa Glenda Lewis; Saheli Samanta; M\. A\. Lone; Oindrila Roy Chowdhury; Diplina Barman; Dipti Yadav; Sudipta Pati; Pramit Ghosh

**Data:** 2026-10-10 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Abstract The One Nation One Subscription \(ONOS\) initiative was launched in India to improve institutional access to subscription\-based scientific literature\. This methodological study compared the retrieval performance of ONOS with PubMed using neonatal sepsis mortality literature from India as a test case\. Identical search terms were applied across both platforms for studies published between 2000 and 2025\. Retrieval performance was evaluated using precision, recall, F\-measure, number needed to screen \(NNS\), and relative retrieval efficiency \(RRE\)\. PubMed retrieved 751 records and ONOS 39, with minimal overlap between platforms\. ONOS demonstrated higher precision \(15\.4% vs\. 9\.1%\) and lower screening burden, whereas PubMed showed substantially higher recall \(91\.9% vs\. 8\.1%\)\. ONOS\-indexed journals were fewer but more concentrated in Q1 journals\. Overall methodological quality of included studies was comparable between platforms\. ONOS may serve as a complementary platform for focused rapid retrieval and improved access to biomedical literature in India\.
