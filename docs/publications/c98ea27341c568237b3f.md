# Development of a Natural Psyllium Husk Enabled Orodispersible Tablet of Mebeverine Hydrochloride: Formulation Optimization, In Vitro Performance, and Analytical Characterization

**Autores:** 

**Data:** 2026-10-12 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Background: Irritable bowel syndrome \(IBS\) is characterized by recurrent abdominal pain and altered bowel habits and can impair daily functioning and quality of life\.Conventional mebeverine hydrochloride tablets may be inconvenient for patients with swallowing difficulties\.This study developed an orodispersible tablet using natural psyllium husk as a superdisintegrant\.Its high water uptake and swelling capacity, together with biodegradability and low cost, support its use as a natural excipient\.The formulation was optimized using Response Surface Methodology\.Methods: Nine formulations were prepared by direct compression using a 3 × 3 response surface design with psyllium husk and microcrystalline cellulose concentrations as independent variables\.Powder blends and tablets were evaluated for flow properties, hardness, friability, weight variation, drug content, disintegration time, dissolution, and stability\.Statistical analysis included ANOVA, regression modelling, response surface analysis, and optimization\.Results: Both formulation variables significantly influenced disintegration time and hardness \(p &lt; 0\.0001\)\.Increasing psyllium husk shortened disintegration, whereas higher binder levels improved hardness\.The optimized formulation \(F3\) showed rapid disintegration in 24\.3 s, hardness of 21 N, friability of 0\.63%, drug content of 100\.12%, and 99\.49% drug release within 30 min\.Powder blends showed good flow after glidant addition, and accelerated stability testing showed preservation of tablet quality and dissolution performance for six months\.Conclusion: Psyllium husk functioned as an effective natural superdisintegrant, producing rapidly disintegrating tablets with satisfactory mechanical strength, high drug release, and acceptable stability\.The optimized formulation provides a promising patient friendly dosage form for patients who have difficulty swallowing conventional tablets\.
