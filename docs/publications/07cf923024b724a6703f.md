# The Effect of Visual Information Chunking\-Based Marketing on Customer Engagement With Kompas One

**Autores:** Anggun Aulia Putri; Achmad Fadillah

**Data:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Background: Kompas One, a premium content bundle by KG Media anchored on Kompas\.id, faced a wide engagement gap in its email marketing: open rates well above the media\-industry average were not followed by comparable click\-through activity\. Purpose: This study identifies the root causes of low interaction with text\-heavy email design and empirically tests whether a Visual Information Chunking \(VIC\) layout improves customer engagement\. Design/methodology/approach: A mixed\-methods sequential exploratory design was applied\. Fishbone Diagram and 5 Whys analysis, validated through interviews with three Growth and UI/UX informants, identified root causes\. Thirty baseline text\-heavy email campaigns \(September–October 2025\) and 30 treatment Visual Information Chunking email campaigns \(November–December 2025\), representing a four\-month dataset across randomized, non\-overlapping cohorts of prospective, at\-risk, and churned subscribers, were compared using descriptive statistics, Shapiro\-Wilk normality tests, and Mann\-Whitney U tests on Open Rate, Click\-Through Rate \(CTR\), and Click\-to\-Open Rate \(CTOR\)\. Findings/Results: Root causes centered on a mismatch between dense narrative layouts and audiences&\#x27; need for scannable content, compounded by clipped\-message risk\. VIC significantly increased CTR \(\+71\.43%, p = 0\.007, effect size r = 0\.347\) and CTOR \(\+61\.54%, p = 0\.008, effect size r = 0\.345\), while Open Rate showed no statistically significant change \(p = 0\.802, r = 0\.032\)\. Conclusion: Reducing text density and strengthening visual hierarchy lowers cognitive load and drives post\-open engagement\. Originality/value \(State of the art\): The study extends Cognitive Load Theory and the Stimulus\-Organism\-Response framework to bundled\-product email marketing within the Southeast Asian digital news sector\. Keywords: cognitive load, customer engagement, email marketing, product bundling, visual hierarchy
