# Highly efficient second\-harmonic generation in TFLN via compact\-footprint spiral PPLN waveguides

**Autores:** Shixin Wang; 郜定山; Ruzhao Chen; Jianji Dong; Xinliang Zhang

**Data:** 2026-10-11 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Extending the interaction length in thin\-film lithium niobate waveguides is a conventional approach to enhancing nonlinear conversion efficiency\. However, this strategy is severely limited by phase mismatch induced by intra\-wafer thickness nonuniformity\. Here, we demonstrate a compact spiral periodically poled lithium niobate \(PPLN\) waveguide that overcomes this bottleneck\. This device tightly folds a long nonlinear interaction path within a sub\-millimeter localized space, fundamentally circumventing the constraints imposed by the waveguide length and large\-scale intra\-wafer thickness gradients\. Without the need for complex passive structural compensation or active phase tuning, this design achieves highly consistent quasi\-phase matching \(QPM\) globally\. Experimental results show that under low\-power and high\-power pumping conditions, the device achieves a high normalized conversion efficiency of 3723%/W and an absolute conversion efficiency of 26\.75%, respectively\. This work realizes ultrahigh nonlinear conversion efficiency within a minimal device footprint, not only overcoming the performance degradation typical of traditional long straight waveguides, but more importantly, offering a highly fabrication\-tolerant geometry suitable for scalable wafer\-level manufacturing\. It provides a scalable and highly manufacturable solution for future high\-density on\-chip photonic integration and quantum light source networks\.
