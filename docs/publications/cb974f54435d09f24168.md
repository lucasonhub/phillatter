# Performance and eco\-efficiency of high\-strength concrete incorporating a screened quaternary low\-carbon binder

**Autores:** Prathibha P\. Shetty; Asha Udaya Rao

**Data:** 2026-10-14 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Abstract This study investigates the development and performance of high\-strength concrete designed for an M65 target grade incorporating an optimized quaternary low\-carbon binder system comprising Ordinary Portland Cement \(50%\), Class F fly ash \(20%\), ground granulated blast furnace slag \(15–25%\), and calcined seashell powder \(5–15%\)\. Binder proportions were systematically investigated using an S/N\-ratio\-based comparative screening approach to rank the predefined mixtures based on compressive strength\. A comprehensive experimental evaluation was conducted to assess mechanical properties, durability characteristics, and eco\-efficiency\. Results indicate that the incorporation of industrial and aqua\-industrial waste materials significantly reduces carbon footprint and embodied energy compared to conventional OPC\-based concrete, while demonstrating continued strength development at later ages and enhancing long\-term durability\. The developed quaternary binder system demonstrates strong potential as a sustainable and practically viable solution for high\-performance concrete, promoting effective waste valorisation and contributing to the objectives of the United Nations Sustainable Development Goals \(SDGs 9, 12, and 13\)\.
