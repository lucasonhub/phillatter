# Serviciile publice, între agonie și extaz

**Autores:** Verginia Vedinaș; Ioan Laurentiu Vedinas

**Data:** 2026-10-13 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

The present study aims to examine the theory of public service, a fundamental institution of administrative law, with which the latter was, for a long time, virtually identified\. Public service is one of the forms through which public administration carries out its activities, and its significance derives from the very mission of public administration, namely to meet social needs of public interest\. The title of the article is intended to suggest that the study addresses the transformations undergone by public services in the contemporary world, in light of the evolution of societies, the development of artificial intelligence, and the changes affecting the relationship between needs and expectations\. Last but not least, it also considers the various methods of delivering public services, where, alongside public and private forms of provision, public\-private partnerships have emerged as an additional model\.
