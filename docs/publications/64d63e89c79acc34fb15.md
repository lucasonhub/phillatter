# Conformational Locking With Built‐in Recognition: A Generalizable Strategy for Reversible SO 2 Detection

**Autores:** Leyi Lu; Dingfang Hu; Zhouyu Chen; Jiancheng Zhou; Ruijuan Wen; Lingya Peng; Rongrong Huang; Xin Li; Yu Fang

**Data:** 2026-10-13 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

ABSTRACT The development of fluorescent sensors capable of simultaneous ultrafast response and high reversibility poses a significant challenge, as these two performance metrics are typically constrained by a trade‐off\. Here, we present a conformational locking strategy with built‐in recognition, realized through a tetracoordinate boron‐bridged fluorophore \(Py‐BF 2 \)\. In this design, the B←N Lewis pair rigidifies the molecular framework while extending π‐conjugation, yielding an electronic structure that promotes a pronounced intramolecular charge transfer \(ICT\) state with a substantially enlarged Stokes shift relative to the unlocked analogue\. This integrated architecture not only enhances fluorescence efficiency but also exploits the ICT state for sensitive SO 2 detection\. The resulting film‐based sensor achieves a response time of under 2 s, a detection limit of 1 ppm, and remarkable cycling stability exceeding 100 cycles without performance degradation, effectively overcoming the conventional speed‐reversibility trade‐off\. Practical applicability is validated through rapid screening of SO 2 residues in grapes and raisins, with results corroborated against the Chinese national standard method \(GB 5009\.34–2022\) and enabling compliance determinations within minutes\. This work establishes a generalizable design paradigm that unifies signal transduction and analyte recognition within a single molecular scaffold for reversible gas sensing applications\.
