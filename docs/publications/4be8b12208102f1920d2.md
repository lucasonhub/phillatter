# ONTOLOGICAL MODELING OF UZBEK GRAMMAR: An OWL Model of Morphological and Syntactic Knowledge in Pro\-tégé

**Autores:** Sobirova Madina Mahmudjon qizi

**Data:** 2026-10-12 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

This study addresses the formal representation of Uzbek grammatical knowledge in a machine\-readable form\. Due to the agglutinative nature of Uzbek, several morphological meanings and grammatical categories may be ex\-pressed sequentially within a single word form\. Consequently, a conventional terminology list or simple taxonomy is insufficient for representing complex relationships among grammatical concepts\. The study identifies funda\-mental concepts of Uzbek morphology and syntax and models them as ontology classes and object properties\. The model was implemented in the WebProtégé/Protégé environment using OWL/RDF\. The morphological layer rep\-resents morphemes, roots/stems, affixes, parts of speech, and grammatical categories, while the syntactic layer represents syntactic relations, sentence constituents, and sentence types\. In addition to hierarchical relations, se\-mantic relations are formalized through properties such as takesCase, hasTense, and modifies\. The resulting pre\-liminary ontology provides a machine\-readable representation of selected Uzbek grammatical knowledge\. The model can serve as a knowledge layer for future morphological analysis, syntactic parsing, grammar error detec\-tion, semantic search, and ontology\-guided machine learning for Uzbek NLP\.
