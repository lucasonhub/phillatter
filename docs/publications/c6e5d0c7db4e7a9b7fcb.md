# Scalable Cloud Solutions Through Artificial Intelligence Governance: Applications in Healthcare and Financial Systems

**Autores:** Godavari Modalavalasa

**Data:** 2026-10-10 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

This research develops and evaluates artificial intelligence governance frameworks for scalable cloud solutions in healthcare and financial systems\. It focuses on regulatory compliance, algorithmic fairness, security, transparency, and accountability in regulated industries\. The proposed framework integrates policy definition, real\-time monitoring and detection, automated policy enforcement, and audit reporting\. It examines applications across healthcare cloud platforms and financial systems, addressing privacy protection, AI bias mitigation, anomaly detection, access control, and continuous compliance monitoring\. The study reports 94\.7% regulatory violation detection, a 71% reduction in manual audit overhead, an 82% reduction in discriminatory outcomes after bias mitigation, and prevention of 96% of attempted policy violations\. It also evaluates workload scalability, governance latency, healthcare privacy outcomes, and financial fraud detection performance\. The paper provides an engineering\-oriented framework for organizations seeking to deploy AI\-enabled cloud services responsibly while balancing security, fairness, compliance, and operational scalability\. Keywords: AI Governance, Cloud Computing, Healthcare Systems, Financial Services, Regulatory Compliance, Algorithmic Fairness, Cloud Security\.
