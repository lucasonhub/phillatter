# Teymuri, Parsa: On Causal Role of Consciousness

**Autores:** 

**Data de descoberta:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** unknown

## Descrição do feed

I argue that any grounded realist account of consciousness must attribute to conscious states causal efficacy, specifically in a content\-sensitive form\. Standard objections to epiphenomenalism leave open the possibility of a causally inert yet systematically correlated domain\. I develop an epistemic argument against this view: if conscious states are causally inefficacious, beliefs about them cannot be non\-accidentally true and so fail to constitute knowledge\. Moreover, successful reference to phenomenal properties requires an appropriate causal connection between those properties and the concepts that purport to track them\. Appeals to phenomenal concepts do not resolve this difficulty\.
