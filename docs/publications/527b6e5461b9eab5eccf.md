# METHODOLOGY FOR DEVELOPING THE CRITICAL THINKING SKILLS OF FUTURE ENGLISH LANGUAGE TEACHERS THROUGH LINGUOPRAGMATIC APPROACHES

**Autores:** Haytanova Yulduzkhon Zafarjon qizi

**Data:** 2026-10-10 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

In the modern education system, developing innovative strategies for preparing future philologist specialists for professional activity under the conditions of digital technologies and artificial intelligence is recognized as a priority direction\. Developing skills in critically analyzing texts, identifying pragmatic goals, and logically substantiating one&\#x27;s own opinion is of particular relevance for enhancing the professional preparedness of students majoring in foreign philology\. Introducing intensive teaching models aimed not only at future specialists mastering language norms but also at forming the ability to make independent and well\-founded decisions in complex discursive\-pragmatic situations is of great importance\.
