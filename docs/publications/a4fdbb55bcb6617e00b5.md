# Farias, Sergio: The Fractal Co\-Creative Universe

**Autores:** 

**Data de descoberta:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** unknown

## Descrição do feed

This paper proposes a speculative metaphysical model in which reality is understood not primarily as a fixed object but as an evolving, recursively creative process\. The central proposition is that conscious beings may participate in the formation of reality while simultaneously being products of the reality in which they participate\. The framework explores consciousness, imagination, perception, autonomy, collective reality, consent, and the possibility of multiple or nested realities\. It draws conceptual connections to process philosophy, phenomenology, social constructionism, and John Archibald Wheeler&\#x27;s participatory universe, while distinguishing those precedents from the paper&\#x27;s original speculative extensions concerning creative sovereignty, branching realities, recursive creators, and reality rewriting\. The model does not claim that contemporary science has established direct consciousness\-based reality creation\. Its purpose is to ask whether these possibilities can be formulated coherently, what philosophical consequences follow from them, and what would be required to distinguish them from competing accounts of reality\.
