# THE USE OF ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE AND BIG DATA TECHNOLOGIES IN PARLIAMENTARY JOURNALISM

**Autores:** Mukhiddinova Aziza Fazliyevna; Worldly Knowledge Publishing Centre

**Data:** 2026-10-11 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

This article analyzes the opportunities, practical directions and risks of applying artificial intelligence and Big Data technologies in parliamentary journalism\. The study is based on a qualitative review of academic literature, reports of international organizations, documented practical cases and relevant legal acts of the Republic of Uzbekistan\. Seven main directions of use were identified, and for each of them the practical benefit and the ethical risk are described\. The article proposes a three layer model that allows newsrooms to introduce these technologies under human editorial control\.
