# A 2\-year multimodal prospective cohort study to predict sustained progression independent of relapse activity in multiple sclerosis

**Autores:** Luis Rafael Solís Tarazona; Salma Reddam; Ana Merchan\-Cabanillas; Silvia Navarro\-Quevedo; Lars Lau Rakêt; Sara Gil‐Perotín

**Data:** 2026-10-13 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Background Disability accrual in multiple sclerosis \(MS\) is heterogeneous\. We assessed whether a 2\-year multimodal evaluation could identify patients who were developing progression independent of relapse activity \(PIRA\) or relapse\-associated worsening \(RAW\)\. Methods Among the 81 MS patients with a 2\-year follow\-up and outcomes determined through 5 years, a composite for PIRA was developed using Firth’s penalized logistic regression analysis; discrimination was calculated as the area under the curve \(AUC\) using repeated stratified nested cross\-validation\. Results In total, 21 patients \(25\.9%\) met the PIRA criteria, 9 \(11\.1%\) met the RAW criteria, and 1 met both criteria\. A multivariate Firth regression analysis selected two variables associated with PIRA after adjustment: Modified Fatigue Impact Scale \(MFIS\) total score \(OR 2\.04, 95% CI 1\.17–3\.83; p = 0\.011\) and cerebrospinal fluid \(CSF\) chitinase\-3\-like protein 1 \(CHI3L1\) \(OR 2\.00, 95% CI 1\.11–5\.26; p = 0\.018\)\. Notably, the Symbol Digit Modalities Test \(ΔSDMT\) showed an association in the univariate analysis \(OR 0\.47, 95% CI 0\.23–0\.82; p = 0\.006\) but was attenuated in the multivariate analysis \[odds ratio \(OR\) 0\.64; p = 0\.141\]\. Oligoclonal immunoglobulin G band \(OCB\) positivity was universal in PIRA, but the findings are inconclusive due to sparse OCB\-negative observations and incomplete OCB data\. Worsening gait \[ΔTimed 25\-Foot Walk \(ΔT25FW\)\] and new T2 lesions showed associations in the univariate analysis that were not retained in the multivariate analysis\. None of the variables had a p \-value of less than 0\.10 for RAW\. The median out\-of\-sample AUC was 0\.74 \(95% CI 0\.68–0\.78\)\. Conclusion A two\-year multimodal assessment identified a biologically coherent PIRA composite driven by intrathecal neuroinflammation and fatigue\. No reliable RAW predictor was found, and external validation is warranted\.
