# A Comprehensive Survey of Deep Learning Applications for Liver Disease Diagnosis and Treatment

**Autores:** B\. Sujatha; M\. Swapna

**Data:** 2026-10-09 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Liver illness represents a critical health issue worldwide, impacting countless individuals and leading to significant health complications and deaths\. Timely and precise identification is essential for proper treatment and enhanced patient results\. This research investigates the role of artificial intelligence \(AI\) in transforming the processes of diagnosing, forecasting, and treating liver disorders\. We analyse the shortcomings of conventional diagnostic approaches, including blood analyses and imaging methods, and emphasize how AI technologies can address these obstacles by examining extensive collections of varied data to detect subtle trends\. The document addresses the necessity for AI\-driven assessment to improve early diagnosis, customize treatment strategies, and anticipate disease advancement\. An in\-depth review of existing literature is provided, outlining different AI and machine learning strategies used for categorizing fatty liver ultrasound images, diagnosing liver ailments, and delineating liver tumours from medical imaging\. We pinpoint crucial research deficiencies, such as the requirement for enhanced accuracy and clarity in models\. The driving force behind this study is to utilize AI to tackle the intricate issues associated with fatty liver, cirrhosis, and liver neoplasms\. The challenge is framed as the necessity to use AI to boost diagnostic precision and productivity\. The goals of the survey include developing robust AI systems for image classification, disease identification, and tumour delineation\. The results indicate that ongoing investigation and partnership are crucial for effectively incorporating AI into medical practices, ultimately resulting in improved patient outcomes and more effective healthcare services\.
