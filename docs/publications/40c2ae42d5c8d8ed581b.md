# USING CONTEMPORARY ENGLISH\-LANGUAGE SONGS IN TEACHING ENGLISH AT SENIOR SECONDARY SCHOOL

**Autores:** Taisiia Gotthard; Oksana Sierhieieva

**Data:** 2026-10-15 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

The article substantiates the systematic rather than occasional use of contemporary English\-language songs as authentic material in teaching English to senior secondary school learners \(grades 10\-11\)\. Cognitive, affective, and sociocultural evidence on the role of music in second language acquisition is reviewed, and nine criteria for selecting songs for this age group are formulated\. The core of the study is a mapping of song material onto the thematic units of the Ukrainian senior\-school foreign language syllabus, accompanied by an indication of the language focus that each song can service and of a corresponding productive task\. A three\-phase methodological model \(pre\-listening, while\-listening, post\-listening\) is described, together with a typology of tasks in which every deletion, transformation and discussion prompt is tied to an explicit language objective instead of to the song as entertainment\. A sample forty\-five\-minute lesson framework and guidance on formative assessment are supplied, and the practical limitations of the approach are examined — non\-standard grammar in lyrics, content vetting, copyright compliance, and the risk of overuse\. The material is addressed to practising teachers of English and to students of pedagogical institutions\.
