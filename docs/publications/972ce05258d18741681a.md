# COMPARATIVE ANALYSIS OF TRADITIONAL ENDOSCOPY AND AI BASED SYSTEMS

**Autores:** Jalgasbaeva Aziza Artiqbaevna

**Data:** 2026-10-15 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Reliable gastric ulcer detection depends on both systematic endoscopic inspection and the quality of the recorded images\. Motion blur, specular reflections, bubbles and mucosal debris can obscure lesion margins and compromise automated analysis\. This paper compares conventional endoscopic evaluation with artificial intelligence assisted image interpretation through a focused literature based analysis and a proposed evaluation framework\. It examines artifact mechanisms, preprocessing options, model selection and clinical oversight\. The central conclusion is that automated detection should be coupled with image quality assessment and transparent reporting\. Enhancement cannot recover tissue information that was never captured, and performance on selected clean images cannot establish clinical reliability\. A patient separated evaluation, artifact stratification and external validation are therefore necessary before claims of diagnostic superiority can be made\. No new clinical experiment or validated accuracy estimate is reported in this paper\.
