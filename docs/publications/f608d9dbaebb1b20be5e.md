# Synergistic Effects of Boron‐Based Derivatives via Triple Char‐Forming Effects: Simultaneously Conferring High Flame Retardancy and Mechanical Toughening to Epoxy Resins

**Autores:** Antai Bian; Ye Liu; Penglun Zheng; Yuhao Zhou; Lingfeng Zhang; Jikang Qi; Yawei Meng; Weilin Liu

**Data:** 2026-10-12 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

ABSTRACT To overcome the flammability, poor charring, and brittleness of epoxy resin \(EP\), a flame‐retardant additive, BPD, was synthesized from N‐aminoethylpiperazine, 4‐acetylphenylboronic acid, and DOPO, and then incorporated into EP to obtain EP/BPD composites\. With 3 wt% BPD, the LOI increased from 25\.7% to 28\.3%, and the UL‐94 rating improved from NR to V‐0\. At 5 wt% BPD loading, pHRR, THR, SPR, TSR, COP, and CO 2 P decreased by 39\.1%, 10\.2%, 42\.8%, 74\.3%, 30\.0%, and 39\.3%, respectively, versus pure EP, demonstrating excellent flame retardancy, smoke suppression, and fire safety\. Moreover, 1 wt% BPD raised impact strength to 28\.79 kJ/m 2 \(65\.9% increase\), while tensile \(51\.98 MPa\) and flexural strengths \(69\.78 MPa\) remained nearly unchanged, achieving a synergistic balance of flame retardancy and mechanical toughness\.
