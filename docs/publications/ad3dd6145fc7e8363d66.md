# Development and validation of a deep learning\-based multitask model for multidimensional quality assessment of colonic endocytoscopic images

**Autores:** Jia\-Wei Fan; Ming\-Qing Liu; Ke Tao; Fengming Ni; Dong Yang; Guo\-Hua Jin; Jun Gao; He Zhu; Hong Xu

**Data:** 2026-10-13 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

BACKGROUNDThe clinical application of endocytoscopy \(EC\) is hindered by the subjective nature and low efficiency of manual image quality assessment\. AIMTo develop and validate an automated and reliable artificial intelligence system for standardized assessment and quality control of EC images\. METHODSIn this retrospective multicenter study, we developed Endocytoscopic Image Quality Assessment via Multitask Learning, an automated framework for EC image quality assessment\.A Swin Transformer Large encoder underwent self\-supervised pre\-training on 7990 unlabeled images and multitask fine\-tuning on 10242 annotated images\.Overall quality grading and four auxiliary dimensions were evaluated internally and externally using discrimination, calibration, and patient\-clustered confidence intervals\.Exploratory analyses examined four lesion characteristics\.Three junior endoscopists participated in a sequential paired reader study\. RESULTSInternal and external accuracy was 0\.8597 \(95% confidence interval: 0\.8441\-0\.8780\)and 0\.8476 \(95% confidence interval: 0\.8313\-0\.8638\),respectively; corresponding macro\-F1 scores were 0\.8638 and 0\.8495\.Overall top\-label expected calibration error was 0\.0802 internally and 0\.1019 externally\.Auxiliary performance varied by dimension and score\.Accuracy and macro\-F1 point estimates exceeded those of all five benchmark models, although not all paired differences remained significant after multiplicity adjustment\.During the subsequent artificial intelligence\-assisted session, reader accuracy was 8\.28\-9\.28percentage points higher than during the unaided session\. CONCLUSION Endocytoscopic Image Quality Assessment via Multitask Learning provides an accurateand interpretable method for EC image quality assessment\.It may facilitate standardized quality control and support future intelligent clinical diagnostic workflows\.
