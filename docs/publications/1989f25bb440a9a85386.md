# Machine Learning Explicable \(XAI\) en Sistemas de Software Inteligentes: Un Enfoque para Mejorar la Confiabilidad

**Autores:** Maria Teodolinda Ortega Ovalle

**Data:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

The increasing integration of Machine Learning models into intelligent software systems has driven significant advances in automation, prediction, and decision\-making\. However, the inherent complexity of many algorithms, particularly those based on deep learning, has raised concerns regarding reliability, transparency, and accountability in critical contexts\. Explainable Artificial Intelligence \(XAI\) is an essential response to these limitations, providing mechanisms to interpret, justify, and audit model behavior\. This article examines the main XAI approaches applied to intelligent systems, including local and global explanation methods, surrogate models, feature decomposition techniques, and visualization tools\. It also analyzes current challenges in explanation evaluation, integrating XAI into software architectures, and balancing interpretability with predictive performance\. The results show that systematically incorporating XAI enhances system reliability, strengthens decision\-making, and facilitates the adoption of ML models in regulated sectors such as healthcare, finance, and security\. Finally, the paper proposes a conceptual framework to guide the implementation of XAI in intelligent systems, ensuring transparency, robustness, and algorithmic responsibility\.
