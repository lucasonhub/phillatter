# STRESSGA CHIDAMLILIK KIBERBULLING OQIBATLARIDAN HIMOYA QILUVCHI OMIL SIFATIDA

**Autores:** NARGIZA RAJABBOYEVNA BEKKIYEVA

**Data:** 2026-10-12 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

The article provides a theoretical rationale for stress resistance as a protective factor that mitigates the negative psychological consequences of cybervictimisation\. Drawing on the work of N\.Garmezy, M\.Rutter, A\.Masten and M\.Zimmerman, the compensatory, buffering \(moderating\) and &quot;inoculation&quot; models of protective factors are applied to cyberbullying\. Using the transactional model of R\.Lazarus and S\.Folkman, the article systematises at which stages of the stress process – appraisal, coping and recovery – and through which mechanisms stress resistance exerts its protective effect\. The components of stress resistance \(hardiness, resilience, active coping, social support\) and their protective functions are described\. A research design, Ta’limda istiqbolli izlanishlar \- xalqaro ilmiy\-metodik jurnal № 9/2026 ISSN: 3060\-527X hypotheses and a set of diagnostic instruments are proposed for empirically testing the moderating role of stress resistance in adolescents and young people aged 15–22, and practical recommendations are offered\.
