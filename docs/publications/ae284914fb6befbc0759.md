# Analysis of the driving mechanisms of atmospheric particulate matter and carbon emissions under the influence of landscape patterns in southern Shanxi, China

**Autores:** Wenjing Ren; Jingyuan Zhao; Fangtong Hu

**Data:** 2026-10-14 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Rapid urbanization has intensified the synergistic environmental pressures of carbon emissions and atmospheric particulate matter pollution\. Landscape patterns play an important role in regulating atmospheric pollutant diffusion and regional ecological processes\. This study focuses on the southern Shanxi region, a resource\-based region in the middle reaches of the Yellow River\. By integrating remote sensing data and geographic information system technologies with spatial statistical methods, geographically weighted regression, and multi\-buffer gradient analysis, this study explored the driving mechanisms and spatial characteristics of landscape patterns on carbon emissions and atmospheric inhalable particulate matter concentrations during the winter heating season\. The results indicated that high carbon emissions in southern Shanxi were concentrated in industrially dense areas such as Yaodu District and Yanhu District, while atmospheric particulate matter concentrations exhibited a distribution pattern of being higher in the southwest and lower in the northeast, with local spatial correspondence and overall misalignment characteristics between the two\. Intensive development of architectural landscapes aggravated carbon emissions and atmospheric particulate matter pollution, whereas green landscapes with high coverage and connectivity exhibited beneficial effects on both atmospheric particulate pollution mitigation and carbon emissions reduction\. Moreover, the driving effects of landscape patterns exhibited significant spatial heterogeneity, and the spatial clustering effect of atmospheric particulate matter pollution was stronger than that of carbon emissions\. There was a positive correlation between architectural landscapes and both carbon emissions and atmospheric particulate matter concentrations, while the negative regulatory effect of green landscapes increased with distance from Yuncheng Salt Lake\. These findings provide a scientific basis and practical guidance for implementing differentiated landscape planning strategies and promoting coordinated atmospheric particulate pollution control and carbon emissions reduction in similar resource\-based regions within the Yellow River Basin\.
