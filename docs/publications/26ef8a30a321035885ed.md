# Artificial Intelligence for Caesarean Surgical Site Infection Surveillance: A Scoping Review of Risk Prediction, Detection, and Clinical Translation

**Autores:** Maiga Ayub Hussein; Cahya Tri Purnami; Bagoes Widjanarko; Julius Nteziyaremye; Lintang Dian Saraswati

**Data:** 2026-10-17 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Background: Caesarean surgical site infection can develop after hospital discharge, when routine surveillance may miss early warning signs\. Globally, the estimated infection rate after caesarean birth is 5\.63%, increasing to 11\.91% in the African region\. Artificial intelligence may support earlier identification and remote wound monitoring, but its clinical reliability is unclear\. Objective: To map artificial intelligence applications for caesarean surgical site infection surveillance, examine their performance and validation, and identify barriers to clinical translation\.
