# Multi\-Criteria Decision\-Making in Construction Management: A Bibliometric Analysis of the Integration of MCDM, BIM, and Artificial Intelligence \(2015–2025\)

**Autores:** Zakaria Ghedjati; Imane F\.I Fantazi; Choubeila Koreichi

**Data:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

The construction industry faces growing challenges that require better decision\-making frameworks\. Combining Multi\-Criteria Decision\-Making \(MCDM\) with digital tools like Building Information Modeling \(BIM\) and Artificial Intelligence \(AI\) offers a promising way forward\. Yet their integration remains fragmented across research efforts, revealing a significant gap in theory and practice\. As a result, their potential remains underutilized, limiting the development of flexible, data\-driven decision\-making tools for complex construction projects\. This study conducts a systematic bibliometric analysis to map the development and structure of the MCDM\-BIM\-AI connection in construction management from 2015 to 2025\. Using a PRISMA\-compliant protocol, we analyzed 253 peer\-reviewed articles from the Scopus database\. Our approach combines performance analysis with science mapping through keyword co\-occurrence networks and thematic evolution analysis across three time periods: 2015\-2018, 2019\-2021, 2022\-2025, along with a country\-level co\-authorship network analysis\. Our findings identify five main thematic clusters and show a significant increase in publications since 2019, mainly driven by the Asia\-Pacific region\. The analysis highlights a notable knowledge gap in the deep connection between BIM and AI within MCDM frameworks\. The temporal mapping reveals three phases of evolution: pre\-integration \(2015\-2018\), Integration \(2019\-2021\), and Innovation \(2022\-present\), with AI and Machine Learning becoming key focus areas in the latest phase\. The study suggests promising future directions for combining MCDM, BIM, and AI to promote more sustainable, data\-driven, and resilient construction practices\. It proposes a conceptual outline for the Intelligent Cognitive Decision Framework \(ICDF\), where BIM serves as a semantic data backbone, MCDM acts as the evaluation engine, and AI provides cognitive support for flexible, data\-driven decision\-making in construction 4\.0 applications\. This intellectual mapping offers researchers data\-driven guidance for prioritizing impactful investigations, practitioners with technology investment plans that match research developments, and policymakers with informed priorities for research and development investment in underrepresented regions\.
