# Beyond Physical Performance: Integrating the Psychology of Religion, Embodied Cognition, and Psychospiritual Development in Athletic Achievement

**Autores:** Karadağ Erdal

**Data:** 2026-10-18 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Athletic achievement is commonly evaluated through competition results, yet the processes that sustain participation, learning, bodily adaptation, identity, and psychological well\-being extend beyond physical performance\. This critical integrative narrative review develops a research framework linking psychology of religion, grounded and embodied cognition, and psychospiritual development in sport\. It differentiates religious affiliation from situation\-specific coping, bodily signal detection from interpretation, and developmental change from short\-term performance fluctuations\. Published scholarship on religion and spirituality in sport, meaning\-making, self\-determination, interoception, resilience, and athlete mental health provides the conceptual foundation\. The proposed context\-meaning\-embodiment\-development framework describes conditional paths from personal values and social environments through bodily appraisal, motivated action, reflection, and longer\-term adaptation\. Its potential benefits include coherent purpose, flexible coping, and sustained participation; adverse pathways include spiritual struggle, externally imposed identity, compulsive ritual, and delayed health care\. No new participants, original empirical results, meta\-analytic estimates, or validated assessment instrument are presented\. The article proposes preregistered longitudinal and mixed\-method investigations incorporating repeated observations, comparisons with secular meaning systems, carefully defined behavioral and performance outcomes, measurement\-equivalence checks, and athlete autonomy safeguards\. The current evidence supports examination of these interacting domains but cannot establish a unique causal contribution of religious belief to sporting success\. A scientifically useful account must demonstrate incremental explanatory value, remain open to null findings, and preserve the distinction between excellence, well\-being, and personal meaning\. psychology of religion; embodied cognition; psychospiritual development; athletic achievement; interoception; athlete well\-being
