# Interpretable Deep Multimodal Fusion of ECG and Facial Expressions for Robust Emotion Detection

**Autores:** Sankar Ganesh Karuppasamy; Rajeev Kumar M

**Data:** 2026-10-17 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

In healthcare, emotional computing, and dynamic human\-computer interaction, emotion recognition is a key component of human\-centric Artificial Intelligence \(AI\)\. However, existing approaches depend on either physiological or visual signals and often show limited robustness or accuracy in noisy or inadequate data environments\. This study proposes a task\-specific bidirectional cross\-modal fusion framework in which physiological ECG representations and facial\-expression representations mutually attend to each other, while an auxiliary embedding\-alignment objective constrains the two modalities to a shared latent space\. While the facial images are processed using EfficientNet\-B0 to extract discriminative visual features, the ECG signals are transformed using a one\-dimensional Convolutional Neural Network \(1D\-CNN\) in conjunction with a Bidirectional Long Short\-Term Memory \(Bi\-LSTM\) network to extract local morphological features and long\-range temporal inference\. A cross\-attention fusion technique is then used to fuse the trained ECG models with facial expression data\. The inter\-modal dependencies between physiological and behavioral signals are represented by a cross\-attention fusion module, which improves the model&\#x27;s dependability and broadens its capacity to identify a variety of emotional classes, specifically focusing on six fundamental emotions: happy, sad, angry, fearful, surprised, and neutral\. The acquired representation is classified using a fully connected attention\-based decoder\. Model interpretability is attained through SHAP analysis of ECG embeddings and Grad\-CAM visualization of the facial areas\. When compared to current and baseline fusion techniques, the evaluation conducted using the MAHNOB\-HCI and AMIGOS datasets demonstrated that the proposed model achieved higher performance, including an accuracy of 97%, precision of 96%, recall of 97%, F1\-score of 96%, and improved adaptability\.
