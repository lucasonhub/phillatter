# DIALOGICAL APPROACH: INTELLECTUAL INTERACTION OF LITERACY IN TEACHING AND LEARNING WITH A COHESIVE STANDARD OF EDUCATION

**Autores:** Sifat Jahan Chowdhury

**Data:** 2026-10-12 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

This paper introduces the Dialogic Education Theory, a transformative framework inspired by Mikhail Bakhtin’s dialogism, designed to reimagine education as a vibrant, inclusive process of co\-creating knowledge\. Rooted in the belief that every student’s voice matters, the theory promotes classrooms where diverse perspectives drive learning through dialogue\. It offers a practical model that weaves dialogic principles into pedagogy, curriculum, assessment, and peer evaluation, fostering critical thinking, empathy, and cultural understanding\. By celebrating diversity as a strength, the framework supports multicultural and equitable education, empowering teachers and students as partners in a shared journey\. The paper explores the theory’s conceptual foundations, practical applications, strengths, and challenges, positioning it as a powerful tool for 21st\-century classrooms that prepare students to thrive in a connected, diverse world\. In this article, my contribution is to develop a new structure of the Dialogical Approach with a cohesive standard in education in teaching and learning through the implementation in a more effective way, where intellectual interaction will enlighten the students&\#x27; efficacy\. My restructuring of the dialogical approach added new ideas with peer evaluation, which encloses intellectual interaction through dialogue from both ends of teaching and learning\. I added a positive approach, creativity analysis, perpetual instruction, and authentic exposition to improve the dialogical approach with parent involvement\. My exploration includes a new concept with peer observation and peer assistance, where multicultural and multi\-ethnographic essence will get equal emphasis to ensure the involvement of all students of all nations\.
