# Undergraduate students’ mastering of fragmentary technical translation using artificial intelligence

**Autores:** Roman Konstantinovich Chalov

**Data:** 2026-10-12 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

The purpose of this study is to provide a theoretical foundation for and develop a technology for the integrative training of future translators – undergraduate students – in fragmentary technical translation \(FTT\) using artificial intelligence \(AI\) tools\. The main content of the article includes an analysis of the specifics of FTT, a classification of AI tools, and a description of four conceptual levels of integration \(discursive, speech\-cognitive, speech\-activity, and subject\-linguistic\)\. The scientific novelty lies in providing a theoretical justification for the didactic functions of the four levels of AI integration in FTT activities, developing a matrix for the distribution of functions between the student translator and AI as a pedagogical support tool \(“scaffolding”\), as well as in the didactic specification of the principle of “verifiable assistiveness”, which defines the boundaries of delegating translation tasks to AI in order to preserve the learner’s regulatory function\. The results of theoretical modeling suggest that the integration of AI into the learning process, provided that the student exercises reflective control, creates conditions for reducing cognitive load, stimulating speech\-thinking activity, and developing the strategic flexibility necessary for translation work within modern information flows in a corporate setting\.
