# Molecular Genetic Analysis and Prenatal Diagnosis of a Family With MECP2 Duplication Syndrome

**Autores:** FengQiu Lin; DingGuo Chen; Zhenglin Lu; Yu Huang; Chun Wang; Juhua Liang; Xuejuan Nong; ZhengYi Chang; FuBo Wei; Guidan Xu

**Data:** 2026-10-15 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

OBJECTIVE: This study aimed to conduct a molecular genetic analysis and prenatal diagnosis for a family with MECP2 duplication syndrome \(MDS\), with the goal of improving diagnostic accuracy and enabling informed reproductive decisions to reduce the incidence of this condition\. METHODS: The study involved a proband \(a 15\-week\-old fetus\), his symptomatic older brother, and their parents\. Genomic DNA from chorionic villus and peripheral blood samples was analyzed using high\-throughput sequencing to detect chromosomal copy number variations \(CNVs\)\. To confirm the findings, multiplex ligation\-dependent probe amplification \(MLPA\) was specifically employed\. RESULTS: High\-throughput sequencing identified a 0\.35 Mb duplication at Xq28 in the fetus and his mother, and a 0\.4 Mb duplication in the brother\. The duplicated region contained the critical MECP2 gene\. MLPA analysis confirmed the MECP2 gene duplication in the fetus, leading to a definitive diagnosis of MDS\. The mother was identified as an asymptomatic carrier\. CONCLUSION: Both the proband and his brother were diagnosed with MDS, having inherited the duplication from their asymptomatic mother\. The combined use of high\-throughput sequencing and MLPA proved to be an effective strategy for accurate pedigree analysis and prenatal diagnosis of MDS, providing a crucial basis for genetic counseling\.
