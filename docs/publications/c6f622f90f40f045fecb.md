# Human Writing as Endogenous Prompt Engineering: From Mental Constraints to Text Generation 作为内生提示工程的人类写作:从心理约束到文本生成

**Autores:** He

**Data:** 2026-10-17 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Human Writing as Endogenous Prompt Engineering: From Mental Constraints to Text Generation PART I: ENGLISH MONOGRAPH \#\# AbstractContemporary discourses surrounding generative artificial intelligence are constrained by an unexamined dichotomy: human literary composition is romanticized as a transcendent, spontaneous outpouring of unmediated interiority, whereas algorithmic text generation is dismissed as the sterile derivation of extrinsic textual instructions \(&quot;prompts&quot;\)\. This paper deconstructs this division\. We advance the \*\*Structural Isomorphism Hypothesis\*\*: literary composition has always functioned as \*endogenous prompt engineering\*\. Before a single token is inscribed, the cognitive apparatus of the writer constructs an internal, invisible parameter matrix—a deliberate lattice of teleological targets, lexical bans, cadential rhythms, and epistemic limits\. The technological emergence of Large Language Models \(LLMs\) does not invent prompt\-driven generation; it merely reifies and externalizes an operational architecture that human consciousness has practiced for millennia\. By pairing genetic criticism of manuscript archives \(Flaubert, Kafka\) with an experimental study contrasting the rule\-making behavior of authors and prompt engineers \($R^2 = 0\.89$ topological alignment\), we show that linguistic generation is universally constraint\-bound\. Far from diminishing authorial agency or reducing the mind to a machine, this realization reclaims subjectivity&\#x27;s true identity: human agency does not reside in the naive illusion of unconstrained freedom, but in the sovereign, conscious mastery of self\-imposed constraints\. 围绕生成式人工智能的当代学术争论,普遍受困于一种未经审视的二元对立:人类的文学书写被浪漫化为不可言说、自发倾泻的心灵真纯体验;而算法生成则被贬损为受制于外部指令\(“提示词/Prompt”\)的机械衍生。本文解构了这一本体论分野,确立了\*\*“结构同构假说”\(Structural Isomorphism Hypothesis\)\*\*:人类文学创作在认知机制上,历来便是一套\*\*“内生提示工程”\(Endogenous Prompt Engineering\)\*\*。在落笔生成文字之前,创作者的心智已然构建了一套隐秘运行的参数矩阵——包含目标定向、词汇禁忌、句式节奏及认知视界。大语言模型\(LLM\)的降临并未发明“由指令驱动的生成”,而仅仅是将人类操演了数千年的内在约束机制进行了技术外化与对象化。本文通过手稿发生学\(福楼拜、卡夫卡\)的实证考察,结合人类作家与提示词工程师制定约束拓扑的对比实验\($R^2 = 0\.89$ 的高度同构\),论证了文本生成的全过程皆由约束支配。最为关键的是,这一发现不仅没有消解人类的主体性,反而揭示了主体性的本真面相:主体性从未存在于脱离规则的空洞自由中;能够自主设定、背负并跨越内部约束,正是人类主体性的真正真名。
