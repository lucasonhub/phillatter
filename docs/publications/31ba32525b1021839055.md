# Mapping students&\#x27; 4C competency profiles in steam\-oriented intelligent systems learning using k\-means clustering

**Autores:** Hotmaida Lestari Siregar; Rahma Hidayanthi; Ahmad Rosyadi Nasution; Rahmad Wahid; Aqil Muflih Dahlan Hutasuhut

**Data:** 2026-10-13 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Background\-Competency assessment in higher education often produces class\-level averages that can conceal meaningful differences among students\. Objectives\-This study maps Critical Thinking, Creativity, Collaboration, and Communication \(4C\) profiles in a STEAM\-oriented Intelligent Systems course using an exploratory Educational Data Mining \(EDM\)\. Methods\-The study involved 66 Vocational Informatics Education students in Indonesia, comprising 31 students in Class A and 35 in Class B, organized into 12 project groups\. The analysis used 20 indicators, five for each 4C dimension, measured on a 1–5 scale\. Self\-, peer\-, and lecturer assessment were compared before clustering\. Observations and reflections contextualized the quantitative profiles\. Results\-Critical Thinking obtained the highest overall mean \(3\.84\), whereas Creativity obtained the lowest \(3\.78\); Collaboration and Communication were both 3\.80\. The Elbow pattern supported k=3, and the reported Silhouette Score was approximately 0\.34\. The three\-cluster solution was retained as exploratory rather than statistically definitive\. K\-Means produced relatively high \(22 students; mean 4C = 4\.34\), moderate \(21 students; mean 4C = 3\.78\), and low \(23 students; mean 4C = 3\.33\) profile\. Centroid patterns were broadly parallel, indicating that clusters primarily reflect overall competency level rather than distinct 4C configurations\. Conclusions\- The study combines multi\-source assessment with EDM to inform non\-stigmatizing instructional considerations\.
