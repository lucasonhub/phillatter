# Multi\-objective Parametric Optimization of Chemical Mechanical Polishing for BK7 Optical Glass Using RSM\-NSWOA\-TOPSIS

**Autores:** Xincheng Yin; Zikai Wang; Huijun Han; Youliang Wang; Yufei Chen; Yanjun Shen

**Data:** 2026-10-15 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Abstract This study investigates the effects of chemical mechanical polishing \(CMP\) process parameters on the surface quality, polishing efficiency, and material removal mechanism of BK7 optical glass\. The primary objectives are to optimize parameters to enhance polishing performance and to accurately predict surface roughness \(Ra\) and material removal rate \(MRR\)\. Preliminary single\-factor experiments were conducted to determine the appropriate parameter ranges for polishing time, polishing pad rotational speed, CeO2 concentration, and slurry pH\. Based on these ranges, a response surface methodology \(RSM\) model was established using the four process parameters as input variables\. With the objectives of minimizing Ra and maximizing MRR, multi\-objective optimization was performed using the Non\-dominated Sorting Whale Optimization Algorithm \(NSWOA\), and the optimal parameter combination was identified via the TOPSIS method\. The RSM models for Ra and MRR yielded R² values of 0\.9840 and 0\.9878, respectively, indicating good agreement with the experimental data used for model development\. The hybrid RSM\-NSWOA\-TOPSIS approach yielded the optimal parameters: polishing time of 51 min, polishing pad rotational speed of 59 r/min, CeO2 concentration of 0\.62 wt%, and slurry pH of 5\.6, with predicted values of Ra=2\.30 nm and MRR=1\.74 µm/min\. Experimental validation under these optimal conditions yielded a Ra of 2\.10 ± 0\.07 nm and an MRR of 1\.92 ± 0\.06 µm/min, with deviations between predicted and measured values within 10%\. XPS results revealed surface chemical changes during CMP, while nanoindentation measurements showed a lower mean indentation modulus after polishing and load\-dependent changes in hardness, suggesting that surface chemical interactions contributed to the mechanical removal process\. The proposed hybrid strategy RSM\-NSWOA\-TOPSIS effectively improves both polishing efficiency and surface quality for BK7 optical glass, providing guidance for balancing surface quality and material removal efficiency under the investigated conditions\.
