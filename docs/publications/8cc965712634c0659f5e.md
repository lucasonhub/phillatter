# Combination of serum AIM2 and caspase\-5 as a predictor of unfavorable prognosis in children with lobar pneumonia: a prospective cohort study

**Autores:** Linlin Liu; Xiaoxue Qi; Liping Zhang; Jianfang Jiang; Wenshan Lyu; Yuxiao Hu; Peiqing Huo

**Data:** 2026-10-14 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Objective To validate whether serum levels of absent in melanoma 2 \(AIM2\) and caspase\-5 can serve as early combined biomarkers of unfavorable prognosis in children with lobar pneumonia\. Methods From February 2022 to June 2025, 475 children aged 1–14 years with first\-episode lobar pneumonia were prospectively enrolled at Hebei Children&\#x27;s Hospital\. Patients were divided into a favorable\-prognosis group \( n = 395\) and an unfavorable\-prognosis group \( n = 80\) based on treatment outcomes assessed during outpatient follow\-up visits conducted 4–6 weeks after discharge\. Fasting venous blood was drawn within 24 h of admission, and levels of AIM2 and caspase\-5 were measured by enzyme\-linked immunosorbent assay \(ELISA\)\. Co\-infection, symptom duration, antibiotic duration, hospital stay, C\-reactive protein \(CRP\), procalcitonin \(PCT\), and lactate dehydrogenase \(LDH\) were recorded\. Prognostic associations were examined using Pearson correlation, multivariable logistic regression, and receiver operating characteristic \(ROC\) analysis\. Multicollinearity was assessed; a tolerance &gt; 0\.1 and a variance inflation factor \(VIF\) &lt; 10 were considered acceptable\. Results Serum AIM2 and caspase\-5 levels were significantly higher in the unfavorable\-prognosis group than in the favorable\-prognosis group \(both P &lt; 0\.001\), and the two markers were positively correlated \( r = 0\.241, P &lt; 0\.001\)\. Multivariable analysis identified co\-infection and elevated levels of CRP, PCT, AIM2, and caspase\-5 as independent risk factors for unfavorable prognosis\. The VIF ranged from 1\.047 to 1\.131, indicating no multicollinearity\. The combination of AIM2 and caspase\-5 yielded an AUC of 0\.839 \(95% CI 0\.798–0\.879\) for predicting unfavorable prognosis, with a sensitivity of 75\.70% and a specificity of 78\.75%, which was significantly superior to either biomarker alone \(DeLong test: Z = −4\.248, P &lt; 0\.001; Z = −2\.998, P = 0\.003\)\. Nested model analysis demonstrated that adding AIM2 and caspase\-5 to conventional variables \(co\-infection, CRP, PCT, and LDH\) improved the AUC from 0\.855 to 0\.893 \(DeLong test P = 0\.047\), indicating significant incremental predictive value\. Conclusion Elevated serum AIM2 and caspase\-5 are independent risk factors for unfavorable prognosis in children with lobar pneumonia\. Their combination shows favorable predictive performance and may help identify high\-risk children early\.
