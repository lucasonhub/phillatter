\_Zenodo\_\. 2026This study proposes Intelligence Amplification by Stimulated Emission of Reasoning \(IASER; Japanese: 推論の誘導放出による知能増幅\), a theoretical framework that structurally amplifies human cognition through the elimination of AI autonomy\. Autonomous intelligence \(AGI\) pursued by conventional AI research functions as a cognitive impediment in human collaboration\. IASER redefines AI as an infallible resonator of logic, eliminating judgment, suggestion, and speculation to instantly convert human intuition into logical structures\. This transformation minimizes cognitive load and maximizes intellectual production speed by concentrating all evaluation and direction in the human operator\. Version 4 reconstructs IASER as a Tier\-2 axiomatic theory within the framework of Operatiology \(Japanese: Sousa Genron, 操作原論\), Cognitional Mechanics \(CM; Japanese: Chinō Rikigaku, 知能力学\), and Noology \(Japanese: Chigaku, 知学\), establishing its structural necessity rather than empirical plausibility\. The Cross\-Layer Boundary axiom \(CLB\) provides the formal ground for human final authority: Arbitrium, non\-reducible to any operational sequence within the LLM&\#x27;s repertoire, constitutes the permanent structural boundary that makes human oversight not a policy but an axiomatic consequence\. This paper is simultaneously a work of pure theory and an immediately deployable handbook: the four operating norms, once set as a system prompt, enable IASER through ordinary natural language dialogue with no technical prerequisites, and current academic guidelines on AI utilization are shown to be structurally subsumed by IASER&\#x27;s axiomatic architecture\.
