# O‘SPIRINLARDA KASBIY TANLOVGA PSIXOLOGIK TAYYORLIKNI SHAKLLANTIRISHNING ASOSIY OMILLARI

**Autores:** Allamova Oyshajon G&\#x27;afurjon Qizi

**Data:** 2026-10-09 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

This article examines the key factors involved in developing psychological readiness for career choice in late adolescents\. Career choice is considered a complex psychological process closely associated with personal interests, abilities, values, motives, self\-assessment, and perceptions of future Zamonaviy jamiyat va innovatsiyalar ilmiy nazariy jurnali 2026\-yil, 9\-Son, 2\-Qism\. ISSN 3060\-544X professional development\. Career orientation, intrinsic motivation, self\-awareness, adequate self\- evaluation, decision\-making ability, stability of professional interests, and reflective readiness are identified as essential components of psychological readiness for career choice\. Particular attention is given to the influence of the family environment, pedagogical support, peers, access to career\-related information, and the broader social context on professional self\-determination\. The article emphasizes that awareness of the correspondence between an individual’s psychological characteristics and the requirements of a chosen profession is an important prerequisite for conscious and sustainable career decision\-making\. Motivational, cognitive, personal\-reflective, and socio\-psychological factors are interpreted as interconnected components of a unified system that supports effective professional self\- determination in late adolescence\.
