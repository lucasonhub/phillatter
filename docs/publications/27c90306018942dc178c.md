# MATNNI TUSHUNIB O‘QISH KO‘NIKMASINI RIVOJLANTIRISHDA &quot;SCAFFOLDING&quot; STRATEGIYALARI \(PIRLS TALABLARI ASOSIDA\)

**Autores:** NIGORA UMAR QIZI BO&\#x27;RONOVA

**Data:** 2026-10-12 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

The article examines the use of scaffolding strategies for developing reading comprehension skills in primary school pupils on the basis of the requirements of the PIRLS international study\. The four comprehension processes of the PIRLS 2021 assessment framework are analysed, the difficulties pupils encounter in each process are identified, and matching support strategies are proposed\. An author’s definition of &quot;scaffolding in reading comprehension&quot; is given, and a correspondence table linking the PIRLS processes with Question–Answer Relationships \(QAR\), reciprocal teaching, the K\-W\-L chart and graphic organisers is developed\. A three\-level procedure for the gradual fading of support is illustrated with an informational text composed by the author\.
