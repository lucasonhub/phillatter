# Knitted NiTi shape memory alloy layer enabling adaptive air thickness for enhanced thermal protection and thermo\-wet comfort in firefighting clothing

**Autores:** Die Zhu; Tianlei Xu; Pibo Ma; Xinli Wang; Ailan Wan

**Data:** 2026-10-09 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Abstract The complex firefighting environment poses significant challenges to firefighter&\#x27;s safety and rescue activities, driving the increasing demand for superior thermal protection and comfortable firefighting protective clothing \(FPC\)\. This study investigated the optimization of NiTi shape memory alloy monofilament \(SMM\) parameters and their application in FPC\. The influence of NiTi alloy monofilament diameter \(ranging from 0\.6 mm to 1\.0 mm\) and annealing temperature \(400℃ to 550℃\) on austenite\-martensite phase transformation temperatures was systematically investigated\. Four types of shape memory fabric were fabricated by inserting SMM into knitted fabric\. The weft knitted spacer fabric was also made to replace two layers of traditional FPC\. These materials were subsequently incorporated into a four\-layered smart firefighting clothing \(SFC\) system\. Comparative evaluation was conducted with conventional FPC in terms of thermal protective performance and thermo\-wet comfort characteristics\. The results demonstrated that the austenite start temperature \(As\) of SMM exhibited a negative correlation with annealing temperature\. Thicker SMM exhibited superior shape stability in the austenitic state and enhanced mechanical properties\. In the non\-activated state, the SFC exhibited significantly improved thermo\-wet comfort compared to conventional FPC\. The second\-degree burn time of the SFC in the activated state exceeded 33 seconds\. Notably, the SFC incorporating 1\.0 mm SMM achieved a stabilized surface temperature of 39℃ under constant exposure to 200℃, demonstrating exceptional heat insulating property\. In addition, the SFC had dynamic comfort regulation capability and mechanical impact resistance\. This fabrication strategy showed promising applications in thermal protection and comfort firefighting clothing\.
