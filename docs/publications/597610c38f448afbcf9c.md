# Exploring nursing students’ communication experiences with artificial intelligence–supported virtual patients: a qualitative study

**Autores:** Ayşegül Korkmaz Doğdu; Ayşe Dağıstan Akgöz

**Data:** 2026-10-09 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Effective communication is essential in nursing, and artificial intelligence \(AI\) tools such as ChatGPT are increasingly integrated into nursing education\. This phenomenological study explores how ChatGPT\-supported virtual patient scenarios influence nursing students’ empathy and communication skills\. Third\-year nursing students enrolled in a Family Health Nursing elective interacted with a virtual patient through predefined ChatGPT prompts simulating a counseling session\. Data were collected through focus group interviews and online reflective diaries\. Thematic analysis revealed three themes: “Unrealistic empathy and communication experience”, “Solution\-oriented and effective communication experience”, “Communication and empathy awareness: Future communication strategies and professional preparation” Although participants perceived ChatGPT\-supported virtual patient communication as providing a less realistic communication and empathy experience than real patient interactions, they also reported that ChatGPT’s interaction style offered a structured and solution\-oriented communication experience\. Furthermore, the ChatGPT\-supported virtual patient communication experience was perceived as helping students make reflection and self\-assessment about communication and empathy, and identify areas for improvement before interacting with real patients\. These findings suggest that ChatGPT\-supported virtual patient communication cannot replace real patient encounters, but it can serve as a complementary educational tool that encourages reflective learning and prepares nursing students for subsequent clinical communication experiences\.
