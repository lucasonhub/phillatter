# Feedback mit Haken: Was Schüler:innen aus dem KI\-Netz fischen

**Autores:** Lena Bukmakowski; Carolyn Blume

**Data:** 2026-10-15 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Artificial intelligence \(AI\) promises to make it possible to get immediate and personalized formative feedback and correction for grammar and structure in written texts\. In this contribution, an exploratory case study examines to what extent the coordination between the technology and the learners fosters a strategic development of the writing process\. After first describing the principles of feedback delivered by generative AI, the ways in which learners utilize an illustrative AI\-based tool to revise foreign language texts will be analyzed\. It becomes clear that the writers intermittently use the feedback to apply metacognitive strategies\. At the same time, opaque and vague feedback leads to a rejection of some suggestions\. Finally, these findings will be examined in the context of their implications for supporting process\-oriented writing in foreign language instruction\.
