# Artificial Intelligence And The Future Of Geography

**Autores:** Geeta; Suresh Awase

**Data:** 2026-10-15 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

ABSTRACT Artificial Intelligence \(AI\) is increasingly transforming the nature, methods, and applications of geographical research\. The integration of AI with Geographic Information Systems \(GIS\), Remote Sensing, Global Positioning Systems \(GPS\), and geospatial big data has created new opportunities for analyzing complex spatial and temporal processes\. This study examines the evolving relationship between Artificial Intelligence and Geography, with particular emphasis on its applications, transformative potential, limitations, and future possibilities\. The study adopts a descriptive, analytical, and exploratory approach based primarily on secondary data obtained from research articles, academic publications, institutional reports, and relevant scholarly literature\. It examines the application of AI across major geographical domains, including Physical Geography, Human Geography, Urban Geography, and Economic Geography\. The findings indicate that AI enhances geographical research through automated spatial analysis, image classification, predictive modelling, environmental monitoring, disaster prediction, urban planning, and data\-driven decision\-making\. However, challenges related to data quality, algorithmic bias, transparency, privacy, technological dependence, and unequal access to digital infrastructure remain significant\. The study argues that AI should not be viewed as a replacement for geographical expertise but as a complementary technological capability\. The future of Geography is therefore likely to be shaped by human–AI collaboration, integrating computational intelligence with geographical reasoning, field knowledge, contextual understanding, and ethical judgement to address emerging spatial and environmental challenges
