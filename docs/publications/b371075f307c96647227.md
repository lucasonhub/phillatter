# p\-adic geometry of finite and infinite type

**Autores:** Jack Jerry Garzella

**Data:** 2026-10-12 · **Tipo:** dissertation

## Resumo

In this thesis, we present four results: in the first, we give a fast algorithms for calculating the quasi\-F\-split height of Calabi\-Yau hypersurfaces in characteristic p\. In the second, we give a fast algorithm for calculating the zeta functions of projective hypersurfaces in characteristic p\. In the third, we show that Berkovich’s affinoid algebras and dagger algebras are F\-instersection flat \(and thus contain big test elements\)\. In the fourth, we consider the problem of the nullstellensatz of perfectoid Tate algebras\. We give a result and a counterexample; we establish a limitation on what the possible quotient fields, and we generalize a counterexample due to Gleason\.
