# Entrepreneurial Opportunity Landscape in Wealth Management – Technology\-Driven Entrepreneurship

**Autores:** Suyu Zheng

**Data:** 2026-10-13 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

With the rapid advancement of digital technologies, such as artificial intelligence, big data, blockchain, and cloud computing, the traditional wealth management industry is undergoing profound transformation\. While technology\-driven innovation is reshaping wealth management service models, it has also given rise to a multitude of entrepreneurial opportunities\. From a technology\-driven entrepreneurship perspective, this paper integrates entrepreneurial opportunity theory to construct a three\-dimensional analytical framework encompassing &quot;Technology – Value Chain – Customer Base\.&quot; It presents the entrepreneurial opportunity landscape within the wealth management sector as a three\-tiered structure consisting of the Technology Infrastructure Layer, Service Platform Layer, and Scenario Application Layer\. The study reveals that technology\-enabled wealth management is characterized by three major trends, including intelligence, inclusivity, and ecosystem integration, which entrepreneurs can capture differentiated opportunities across these three layers\. Using four representative cases of Ant Wealth \(China\), Betterment \(USA\), Syfe \(Singapore\), and Licai Magic Cube \(China\), this paper examines the success pathways and limitations of four distinct business models: platform\-based ecosystem models, technology\-native models, regionally focused models, and locally rooted entrepreneurial models\. The study further proposes a five\-dimensional framework for opportunity assessment and entry strategies tailored to three types of teams, and identifies unique Chinese market opportunities such as individual pension schemes, county\-level emerging markets, and silver\-haired wealth management services\. This paper provides entrepreneurs, investors, and policymakers in the wealth management sector with a systematic framework for identifying opportunities and strategic references\. Due to limitations in the research materials, this paper conducts an analysis based on publicly available secondary data and case studies; The opportunities identified in the dataset require further validation through subsequent primary data collection and empirical research\.
