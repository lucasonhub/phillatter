# An accuracy prediction method based on Monte Carlo simulation for inertial integrated structure alignment measurement in confined spaces

**Autores:** Shiwang Lv; Zhipeng Chen; Wang Xia; Bin Zhang; Jinhua Wu; Liang Fei

**Data:** 2026-10-09 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Abstract Inertial integration is an effective method for alignment measurement in confined spaces\. Due to factors such as the large volume of data and the complexity of the calculations, there is currently no effective quantitative accuracy prediction method for inertial integrated alignment measurement in confined spaces in advance\. This paper proposes an accuracy prediction method for the inertial/odometer/control point integrated measurement method\. This method is based on Monte Carlo simulation\. It generates true sensor measurements according to the designed alignment and generates error values based on the sensor error model\. After introducing the error values into the true measurements, the simulated trajectory is solved\. By repeating this process and conducting statistical analysis, the prediction accuracy of the alignment can be obtained\. The experimental results indicate that this method achieves a resolution accuracy of better than 5 ppm, with an average relative accuracy of over 80% in real\-world scenarios, demonstrating good validity and practicality\.
