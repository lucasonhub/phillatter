# THE QUANTITY\-TO\-QUALITY PARADOX: How Language Models Turn Prediction into Intelligence

**Autores:** Michael Aksman

**Data:** 2026-10-09 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

The large\-language\-model revolution demonstrates that quantitative scale alters the computational nature of a learning system:Scaled Data \+ Scaled Parameters \+ Scaled Optimization⇓Dense, Hierarchical Internal Representations⇓Invariant\-Preserving Compositional Operators⇓Qualitatively Distinct Computational Regimes Prediction is an engine of algorithmic compression\. A learning machine cannot predict a complex symbolic world without encoding the invariants that structure it\. When that symbolic world contains physics, mathematics, algorithms, and logic, the internal representations mirror human reason\.The central research question of artificial intelligence has moved beyond empirical benchmark fitting to the underlying science of representation:At what point does an internal representation cease to be a model of language and become an invariant model of reality?
