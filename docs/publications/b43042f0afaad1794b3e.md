# Artificial Intelligence Machining Based Axes Detection for Precise Depth of Cut in CNC Processes

**Autores:** Sekiteri Patience Sametu; Ogunobo Romanus; Umar Adam Isah

**Data:** 2026-10-15 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

This research uses theoretical and machine learning approach to evaluate depth of cut\. Comparison shows that when heat is high it increases axial stresses and percentage error increment value from 2\.20 \(µm\) to predicted surface at 36\.50 \(µm\)\.
