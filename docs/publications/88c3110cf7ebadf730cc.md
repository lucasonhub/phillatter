# Yu, Haoyang: The Universal Consciousness Field: An Ontology from the Primordial State to Cosmic Evolution

**Autores:** 

**Data de descoberta:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** unknown

## Descrição do feed

This article proposes a thoroughgoing monistic ontology, the theory of the Universal Consciousness Field \(UCF\)\. Its basic propositions are as follows: the sole substance \(Dao\) is the absolutely unmanifested state \(0\); through Pure Agency, Dao manifests as Manifestation Itself \(1\); within Manifestation there arises the Binary Relation between the Manifested and the Unmanifested \(2\); the tension between the two appears as the Contradiction between the Return Force and the Manifestation Force \(3\); and in this Contradiction nian \(the thought\-moment\) interferes with itself, forms standing waves, and condenses into Consciousness Condensates—the myriad things of the universe\. Epistemologically, the article argues that the content of consciousness can be experienced whereas consciousness itself cannot be directly experienced; to experience oneself directly would require the experiencer, the object, and the process to become one, which precisely cancels the structural conditions under which cognition occurs—so Dao can know itself only indirectly, through manifested Consciousness Condensates\. The article further holds that all things are manifestations of consciousness, and that every Consciousness Condensate has its own Individual Consciousness: whether two can communicate depends on Frequency Coupling, not on whether consciousness is present\.
