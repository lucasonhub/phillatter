# RAQAMLI TA&\#x27;LIM MUHITIDA FALSAFIY MUNOZARA ORQALI TALABALARNING TANQIDIY FIKRLASH KOMPETENSIYASINI RIVOJLANTIRISH

**Autores:** ISLOMBEK ABDUNAZAROVICH BIYBALAEV

**Data:** 2026-10-17 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

The article examines the theoretical and methodological foundations of organising asynchronous and blended digital discussions in philosophy courses as a means of developing students&\#x27; critical thinking competence\. Drawing on M\. Lipman&\#x27;s idea of a &quot;community of inquiry&quot;, the Community of Inquiry framework of D\.R\. Garrison, T\. Anderson and W\. Archer, H\. Mercier and D\. Sperber&\#x27;s argumentative theory of reasoning, and P\. Freire&\#x27;s dialogical pedagogy, the author proposes a &quot;digital philosophical community&quot; model\. Ta’limda istiqbolli izlanishlar \- xalqaro ilmiy\-metodik jurnal № 9/2026 ISSN: 3060\-527X The model comprises four rotating student roles \(moderator, sceptic, synthesiser, fact\-checker\) and a &quot;trigger – exploration – integration – resolution&quot; discussion protocol adapted to philosophy\. A rubric for assessing the quality of discussion posts based on R\. Paul and L\. Elder&\#x27;s intellectual standards is developed, and the conditions and limitations of implementing the model are substantiated\.
