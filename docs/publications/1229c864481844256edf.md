# Memory on Trial: Neuroscience, Eyewitness Evidence and the Reliability of Human Recollection

**Autores:** Kumari Tanya

**Data:** 2026-10-15 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

The eyewitness has always held near\-sacred authority in court\. &quot;I saw it with my own eyes&quot; is treated as stronger proof than fingerprints or forensic reports\. Yet four decades of cognitive neuroscience tell a different story: memory is not a recording that can be paused and replayed\. It is reconstructed anew at every stage encoding, consolidation, and retrieval and is vulnerable to distortion at each one\.
