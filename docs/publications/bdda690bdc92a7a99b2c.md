# The Role of Artificial Intelligence in Enhancing English Language Learning

**Autores:** Ergasheva E’zoza Ozod qizi

**Data:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

This article analyzes the role of artificial intelligence \(AI\) in enhancing the process of learning English\. Specifically, it examines the capabilities of AI\-powered tools in expanding vocabulary, strengthening grammatical knowledge, developing writing skills, improving pronunciation, and shaping oral speech\. Furthermore, the advantages and challenges of utilizing artificial intelligence are highlighted, including personalized education, instant feedback, over\-reliance on technology, misinformation, and academic integrity issues\. The study is based on an analysis of existing academic literature\. The findings indicate that AI tools create additional opportunities for independent language learning and skill development\. However, the effectiveness of these tools depends on their proper application, teacher guidance, and students&\#x27; critical thinking\. The article recommends employing artificial intelligence not as a replacement for the teacher, but as a supporting technology in the educational process\.
