# From duality to triadic structuration: Coupling Agile agency, generative technology, and regulatory structure

**Autores:** Xiaofeng Wang; Nan Yang; Zheying Zhang; Ali Mehraj; Pietro Scola; Tomas Herda; Sami Hyrynsalmi

**Data:** 2026-10-13 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

As generative artificial intelligence \(GenAI\) increasingly permeates software development, Agile organizations face growing pressure to achieve continuous lifecycle compliance amid rapidly evolving regulatory demands\. Classical Structuration Theory and its derivatives account for technology as either an instantiation of social structure or a medium for human agency; neither pole adequately captures GenAI’s quasi\-autonomous capacity to reshape organizational practices and give rise to new regulatory obligations\. This study addresses this gap by developing Triadic Structuration Theory\. Following an incremental Design Science Research \(DSR\) paradigm, we develop this abstract design artifact through cycle\-level intermediate artifacts, with cross\-cycle knowledge accumulation\. Cycle 1 derives a problem backlog of GenAI\-induced tensions in Agile practice\. Cycle 2 conceptualizes RegOps, showing how Agile agency adapts under regulatory pressure\. Cycle 3 designs an EU AI Act\-aligned model card template, showing how regulatory structure becomes embedded in generative technology\. Cycle 4 explores GenAI\-enabled solutions to ISO\-related compliance barriers in an Agile company\. Together, the evidence and knowledge accumulated within and across the cycles position generative technology as a third construct rather than a medium between agency and structure\. Regulatory structure is embedded directly into it, it reshapes Agile agency through quasi\-autonomous action, and its use to meet existing obligations may recursively generate new ones\. The study extends Structuration Theory as a middle\-range theory to Agile and GenAI contexts, and demonstrates how nested DSR with intermediate artifacts can support theory development\. For practitioners, it offers guidance on leveraging GenAI for compliance, cautioning that it is a structural intervention rather than a one\-off fix\.
