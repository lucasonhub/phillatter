# A Video\-Based Artificial Intelligence Platform for Measuring Esophagogastroduodenoscopy Duration and Suggesting Real\-Time Landmark Documentation

**Autores:** Zohar Levi; Shani Brosh; Inbar Segol; Naim Abu‐Freha; Manor Shpriz; Ziv Varsano; Daniel Khodyrker; Dotan Asselman; Sari Maril; Keren Hod; Hadar Edelman\-Klapper; Sapir Eizenstein; Ian Gralnek M

**Data:** 2026-10-13 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Abstract Background and Study Aims The European Society of Gastrointestinal Endoscopy \(ESGE\) sets key quality indicators for esophagogastroduodenoscopy \(EGD\), including a minimum procedural time of 7 min and photodocumentation of landmarks\. We evaluated AI\-derived procedural times, physician variations, and the impact of AI suggestions on landmark documentation rates\. Methods This retrospective multicenter study included 78,336 EGDs performed by 213 physicians \(01\.01\.2024–31\.12\.2025\)\. A video\-based AI platform \(Theator Inc\., Palo Alto, USA\) measured procedural time, provided real\-time landmark suggestions, and measured landmark documentation\. Two blinded experts performed confirmatory validation of 500 procedures, with concordance defined as agreement within 2 s\. Results Median procedural time was 3\.30 min \(IQR: 2\.27–4\.65\)\. AI timestamps were concordant with expert review in 98\.5% of cases \(95% CI: 97\.1–99\.3%\)\. Among 203 physicians performing at least 50 procedures, procedural time varied from 1\.35 to 7\.56 min \(a 5\.6\-fold difference\), with only 6\.1% of procedures meeting the ESGE\-recommended 7\-min threshold\. AI landmark suggestions reduced zero\-landmark procedures by 42\.6% \(15\.8 vs\. 27\.5%; P &lt; 0\.001\), with improvements across all seven anatomical sites \(P &lt; 0\.001 for each\)\. Physicians in the slowest total EGD time quintile had fewer normal examinations than those in the fastest quintile: 19\.8 versus 31\.7% \(P = 0\.002\), an association that persisted when physicians were restratified by biopsy\-free procedural time\. Conclusions Video\-based AI accurately measured EGD procedure times and improved landmark documentation rate with AI\-generated suggestions\. A median procedural time of 3\.3 min reveals a significant gap in quality standards\. Automated, unattended measurement offers a scalable route to auditing upper endoscopy quality\.
