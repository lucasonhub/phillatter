# Implementing AI and Virtual Reality in Elite Sports Psychology

**Autores:** Jian Cao; Weimin Shen

**Data:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

In team sports, psychological regulation is crucial, yet traditional methods lack personalization, rely on subjective assessments, and offer low realism\. This study examined the integration of computer\-assisted technologies into psychological training\. A six\-month trial involved 90 professional football, basketball, and ice hockey players, randomly assigned to an experimental group or control group\. Data on physiology, psychology, performance, and team communication were analyzed using t\-tests\. Per the results, the experimental group showed 21\.3% lower anxiety, 25\.4% higher cohesion, 21\.2% faster decision response, 14\.3% higher clutch success, 38\.7% better communication efficiency; clutch error rate was also reduced from 27% to 11%\. However, \~15% experienced virtual reality discomfort, and artificial intelligence overreliance reduced tactical creativity by 19%\. Computer\-assisted technologies enhance psychological resilience and team performance\. Balancing data\-driven precision with human\-centered coaching is vital\. Future work should optimize training models and establish data ethics\.
