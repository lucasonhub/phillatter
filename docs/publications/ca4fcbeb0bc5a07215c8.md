# Bernini, Marco: &quot;Dreams and Heightened Narrowed Immersivity: Combining Saturation, Permeability, Presentationality, and Presence&quot;

**Autores:** 

**Data de descoberta:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** unknown

## Descrição do feed

In Marco Bernini &amp; Ben Alderson\-Day, Dreams, Narrative, and Liminal Cognition: An Interdisciplinary Framework\. New York: Oxford University Press\. pp\. 70\-92\. 2026This chapter proposes an interdisciplinary model for oneiric experientiality, which makes the case for dreams being immersive, and for immersive worlds\. It compares dream and waking lives, and it provides conceptual, phenomenological, and ontological contrast by adding also our experience of fictional worlds to the mix \(via the concepts of fictional immersion and ‘saturation’\)\. The chapter argues that dreamworlds share some ontological and phenomenological properties with fictional worlds that are missing in our waking reality \(or, vice versa, they lack properties that the real world displays\)\. To capture, explore, and partly explain dreams’ phenomenological and ontological specificity, the chapter proposes a new model for dreams’ immersive experiences that it calls ‘heightened narrowed immersivity’ \(HNI\)\. The chapter concludes by suggesting that HNI supports recent theories proposing an evolutionary value for dreams against the brain’s overfitting—a function that HNI extends to fictional indeterminacy too, thus linking dreamworlds and fictional storyworlds even more\. \(direct link \)
