# The Effectiveness of the 5Ps Model as an Active Learning Approach for Enhancing Learning Outcomes and Knowledge Construction among Thai Secondary Students

**Autores:** Sangrung Poolsuwan; Rossarin Jermtaisong; Sujin Nukaew

**Data:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

To move beyond passive knowledge transfer, instructional design must enable learners to construct knowledge autonomously while addressing individual differences\. This study aimed to: 1\) develop an instructional model integrating Social Constructivism with Learning Styles to enhance learning outcomes and students&\#x27; knowledge construction abilities, and 2\) evaluate its systemic effectiveness\. Using a research and development \(R&amp;D\) framework, the investigation involved 102 Grade 8 students from four regions of Thailand, selected via multi\-stage random sampling\. Research instruments comprised the 5Ps Model, lesson plans, an achievement test, and a knowledge construction assessment\. Data analyses utilized means, standard deviations, paired\-samples t\-tests, one\-sample t\-tests, one\-way ANOVA, Wilcoxon signed\-rank tests, Cohen’s d, and Cohen’s dz\. Results indicated that the developed model was structurally valid and content\-consistent\. Students&\#x27; post\-test scores surpassed their pre\-test scores significantly \(p &lt; \.05\), demonstrating a large effect size\. Additionally, students&\#x27; knowledge construction scores significantly exceeded the 70% proficiency threshold, with no significant regional variations observed in post\-test outcomes\. Qualitative interview data indicated that the model promoted active learning, knowledge co\-construction, and the translation of concepts into real\-world situations\. The findings demonstrate that integrating Social Constructivism with flexible learning activity design systematically supports deep learning in diverse classroom contexts\.
