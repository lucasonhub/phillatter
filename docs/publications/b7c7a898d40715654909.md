# EFFECT OF HUMANITARIAN LOGISTICS AND LEADERSHIP FACTORS ON HUMANITARIAN OPERATIONS PERFORMANCE IN BORNO STATE, NIGERIA

**Autores:** Usman Usman

**Data:** 2026-10-13 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Humanitarian organizations operating in crisis\-affected environments face persistent challenges in coordinating actors, procuring and delivering relief supplies, managing logistics operations, and maintaining transparency\. These challenges can undermine leadership effectiveness and organizational performance\. Despite the strategic importance of these capabilities, limited empirical evidence explains how humanitarian logistics factors influence leadership and, consequently, logistics performance
