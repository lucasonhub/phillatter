# A Framework for Identifying and Addressing Bottlenecks in Government Administrative Approval Processes

**Autores:** Yaddehi Arachchilage Oshani Ayeshika Kalapriya

**Data:** 2026-10-17 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Many government offices in Sri Lanka now handle files through digital workflow systems, yet the record of how files move is rarely used to study delay\. This paper presents a simple and reusable framework for identifying and addressing bottlenecks in government administrative approval processes\. The framework was developed and tested on one six\-stage file approval process carried out in five government offices\. Because system event logs were not available, a structured questionnaire was used: Subject Officers copied the stage\-by\-stage dates from files they had personally closed, giving 150 file records \(30 per office\)\.
