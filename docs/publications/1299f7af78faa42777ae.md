# Comment on “Cardiometabolic Multimorbidity Increases the Risk of Hip Fracture: A Longitudinal Cohort Study Based on CHARLS ”

**Autores:** Harun Sağlıcak

**Data:** 2026-10-14 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

In the CHARLS cohort of Tian et al\., the abstract&\#x27;s 13% versus 8% are column percentages from Table 1: the prevalence of cardiometabolic multimorbidity \(CMM\) among participants with and without hip fracture\. Read across rows, the same counts give a nine\-year hip fracture incidence of 6\.6% \(36/544\) with CMM and 4\.4% \(251/5770\) without, an absolute difference of 2\.3 percentage points \(95% CI 0\.4\-4\.7\) rather than 5\.0\. The adjusted hazard ratio of 1\.70 is unaffected; its lower confidence limit reads 1\.318 in the abstract and 1\.18 in Table 2\. STROBE item 16\(c\) asks for such absolute risks\.
