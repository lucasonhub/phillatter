# Ranking Prompt–Plateau Correlations in Gamma\-Ray Bursts with Symbolic Regression

**Autores:** F\. Rastegarnia; R\. Moradi; Brwa Shukur Ismael; Mohsen Asaadi; Yu Wang

**Data:** 2026-10-09 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Abstract Gamma\-ray burst \(GRB\) correlations probe central engines, relativistic outflows, and radiation processes\. The Dainotti relation is an anticorrelation between the X\-ray plateau luminosity L a and rest\-frame plateau duration T a rest \. We use symbolic regression to compare it with other low\-dimensional correlations in 251 GRBs with Swift\-XRT plateau properties and Swift\-BAT prompt\-emission parameters\. We perform a Dainotti\-guided search, in which log T a rest is always present, and an unrestricted search over subsets of at most three variables\. The guided search recovers the Dainotti dependence; a D’Agostini fit gives log L a = \( 51\.92 ± 0\.30 \) − \( 1\.31 ± 0\.09 \) log T a rest , with intrinsic scatter σ int = 0\.82 ± 0\.05 dex\. In the unrestricted ranking of 377 feature subsets, the classical Dainotti relation remains competitive, within the top ∼25% of the tested search spaces\. The highest\-ranked screened expression is log L a ≈ 0\.855 log L peak − 0\.782 log T a rest , with RMSE ≈ 0\.46 and R 2 ≈ 0\.88\. The highest\-ranked runs repeatedly reduce to this prompt–plateau structure, without retaining additional spectral, absorption, or afterglow\-shape variables\. A direct regression gives log L a = 4\.718 \+ 0\.776 log L peak − 0\.805 log T a rest , with R P = 0\.91 for 246 bursts\. An errors\-in\-variables refit gives consistent coefficients\. After removing shared luminosity–distance dependence, the relation retains nonnegligible explanatory power \( R 2 ≈ 0\.62\), consistent with a possible intrinsic component\. Restricted Monte Carlo null tests give p ≈ 0\.01\. Plateau luminosity therefore shows a coupled dependence on prompt luminosity and plateau duration\.
