# Prevalence of Sleep\-Related Problems and Their Link to Stress Triggers: Potential Role of Yoga Interventions

**Autores:** Unni Raja B; Rahul R K; Aswathy H\. V

**Data:** 2026-10-17 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Background: Sleep problems are a growing concern among young adults, with psychological stress strongly linked to poor sleep\. While pharmaceuticals are common, yoga offers a promising non\-pharmacological option\.  Objective: To assess the prevalence of sleep problems among Indian college students and examine their association with stress triggers, supporting the design of targeted yoga interventions\.
