# How Siemens Evolved Its AI Lab for Impact

**Autores:** Tobias Gutmann; Steffen Wagner; Johan Frishammar; Benno Blumoser

**Data:** 2026-10-12 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Corporate innovation hubs \(CIHs\) are established to help large firms explore emerging technologies, yet most fail to create a lasting impact\. Siemens’s AI Lab is a rare exception\. In a longitudinal study \(2017–2025\), we show how Siemens avoided “innovation theater” by operating its AI Lab as a dynamic platform that continuously recalibrated its goals, value proposition, and operating model\. A three\-phase evolution model—from exploring AI use cases, to establishing itself as an AI pioneer, to facilitating AI\-driven transformation—illustrates how CIHs can remain relevant over time\. Above all, the Lab created value by enabling others to innovate, not by owning innovation\.
