# APPLICATION OF SENSORS IN THE ADAPTIVE CONTROL OF A MECHATRONIC MODULE ACTUATOR

**Autores:** Nodira Alimova; Madina Makhamadtoirova

**Data:** 2026-10-13 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Modern gripper systems used in mechatronic modules still face significant challenges when handling objects of unknown mass in unstructured working environments\. Conventional rigid grippers typically operate through simple opening and closing mechanisms and lack adaptive tactile feedback\. Consequently, object slippage, excessive gripping force, and instability during manipulation may occur\. Therefore, there is a growing demand for intelligent, adaptive, and cost\-effective gripping elements\. This paper addresses the development of a relatively low\-cost adaptive robotic gripper capable of detecting object slippage in real time and intelligently adjusting the gripping force through sensor integration and machine\-learning\-based control algorithms\. This study proposes a practical solution to the challenges associated with handling and transferring objects in manufacturing, warehousing, and logistics by integrating FSR force sensors with an embedded AI model deployed on a microcontroller\.
