# BOSHLANG‘ICH SINF O‘QUVCHILARINING MANTIQIY FIKRLASHINI RAQAMLI DIDAKTIK O‘YINLAR VOSITASIDA RIVOJLANTIRISH METODIKASINI TAKOMILLASHTIRISH

**Autores:** Qobilova Shahrizoda Yorqinjon qizi

**Data:** 2026-10-13 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

This article examines the improvement of methodologies for developing primary school students’ logical thinking through digital didactic games\. In contemporary education, the integration of digital technologies into the learning process and the development of students’ independent thinking, analytical abilities, and problem\-solving skills have become important pedagogical priorities\. In primary mathematics education, interactive and age\-appropriate learning activities play a significant role in fostering logical reasoning and enhancing students’ cognitive engagement\. The article analyzes the educational potential of digital didactic games, the methodological foundations of their application in mathematics lessons, and their contribution to the development of primary school students’ cognitive abilities\. Particular attention is given to approaches that help students develop comparison, classification, generalization, pattern recognition, logical reasoning, and problem\-solving skills\. The article also discusses the selection of appropriate digital games, the organization of tasks according to their levels of difficulty, the consideration of individual learning needs, and the assessment of educational outcomes\. The proposed methodological recommendations aim to improve the effectiveness of primary mathematics instruction, increase students’ interest in learning, and support the systematic development of logical thinking\. The findings and recommendations presented in the article may serve as a practical resource for primary school teachers seeking to integrate digital educational resources into classroom instruction\.
