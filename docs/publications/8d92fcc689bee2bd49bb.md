# AI Project Management

**Autores:** Muhammad Awais

**Data:** 2026-10-17 · **Tipo:** book

## Resumo

Most AI projects do not fail because the algorithm was wrong\. They fail becausethe wrong problem was chosen, the data was not what anyone assumed, the prototype never found a path into production, or the people who were supposed to usethe system quietly kept doing things the old way\. These are project managementfailures, and they are preventable\.This book exists because the discipline of project management has not yet fullycaught up with the realities of artificial intelligence\. Classic project managementassumes that requirements can be specified, work can be estimated, and “done” canbe defined before you start\. AI work routinely violates all three assumptions\. Youoften cannot know whether a model will reach the accuracy the business needs untilyou have tried\. The data you need may not exist in the form you expected\. And amodel that works on launch day starts to decay the moment the world it learnedfrom begins to change\.At the same time, AI projects are not so exotic that the hard\-won lessons of projectmanagement stop applying\. Clear goals, honest stakeholder communication, disciplined risk management, staged investment, and relentless attention to value stillseparate the projects that deliver from the ones that drift\. The task is not to throwout what we know\. It is to adapt it
