# Semantic Communications and Edge Intelligence in 6G Wireless Networks: Task\-Oriented Joint Source\-Channel Coding and Latency Bounds

**Autores:** Rohit Rana; Tanishk Jangid; Dr\. Vishal Shrivastava; Deepmala Sharma; Sangeeta Sharma; Rahul Sharma; Aarti Sharma

**Data:** 2026-10-13 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Abstract—As cellular architectures transition from 5G toward Sixth\-Generation \(6G\) wireless networks, the classical Shannon\-Nyquist paradigm of bit\-level data transmission is rapidly approaching its theoretical physical channel capacity limits\. Ultra\-dense Internet of Things \(IoT\) ecosystems, autonomous vehicular swarms, and extended reality \(XR\) telepresence demand terabit\-per\-second \(Tbps\) data rates, sub\-millisecond latencies, and continuous real\-time edge artificial intelligence \(AI\) inference over severely bandwidth\-constrained wireless channels\. Classical communications optimize bit error rates \(BER\), transmitting massive streams of uncompressed binary representations regardless of the underlying computational task\. Semantic Communications and Task\-Oriented Edge Intelligence establish a transformative paradigm shift: shifting the transmission objective from error\-free bit reconstruction to the faithful transmission of actionable semantic meaning and task\-relevant latent features\. By uniting Deep Joint Source\-Channel Coding \(Deep JSCC\) with transformer\-based feature extractors, semantic communication frameworks filter out redundant sensory data at the transmitter, communicating only task\-critical representations to edge compute nodes\. However, deploying semantic communications over stochastic wireless channels introduces acute foundational challenges: semantic noise induced by channel fading, joint training instability across non\-differentiable wireless channels, and catastrophic performance degradation under out\-of\-distribution physical channel shifts\. This paper presents an exhaustive, mathematically formal technical investigation and empirical benchmark of semantic communications for 6G edge intelligence\. We formalize the rate\-distortion\-perception tradeoff under Weaver\-Shannon semantic communication tiers, model continuous\-time Deep JSCC over Rayleigh and Rician fading channels, and establish Lyapunov\-guided end\-to\-end latency bounds for distributed edge inference\. Furthermore, we construct an empirical software\-defined radio \(SDR\) and GPU testbed evaluating multimodal perception and edge autonomous vehicle telemetry under severe signal\-to\-noise ratio \(SNR\) degradation\. Our empirical findings demonstrate that Deep JSCC semantic transmission achieves a 78\.4% reduction in required channel bandwidth while preserving 94\.6% downstream edge classification accuracy under extreme channel noise \(SNR = \-6 dB\), where classical JPEG/264 paired with LDPC coding completely collapses\. Finally, we formulate open research directions in semantic knowledge base synchronization, neuro\-symbolic semantic verification, and zero\-trust semantic cybersecurity\. Index Terms—Semantic Communications, 6G Wireless Networks, Edge Intelligence, Deep Joint Source\-Channel Coding \(Deep JSCC\), Task\-Oriented Communications, Shannon\-Weaver Levels, Physical Layer AI\.
