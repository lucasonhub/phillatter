# Immediate effect of Rosario’s shoulder elevator and abductor chain release on cervicocephalic kinaesthesia and craniovertebral angle in forward head posture: a randomized placebo\-controlled trial

**Autores:** 

**Data:** 2026-10-09 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Background:Forward head posture \(FHP\) is associated with altered cervical biomechanics and sensorimotor changes, including impaired cervicocephalic kinaesthesia \(CCK\)\.Contemporary postural science recognizes that postural variability exists in asymptomatic individuals, and FHP does not necessarily indicate dysfunction\.Myofascial chain\-based interventions such as Rosario&\#x27;s Shoulder Elevator and Abductor \(SEA\) chain release may influence sensorimotor performance through neuro\-fascial mechanisms\.Methods:Thirty participants with FHP were randomly allocated into an experimental group \(n = 15\) and a control group \(n = 15\) using a simple randomization \(lottery\) method\.The experimental group received Rosario&\#x27;s SEA chain release, while the control group received a sham intervention\.Outcomes included cervicocephalic kinaesthesia \(measured using repositioning error\) and craniovertebral angle \(CVA\)\.Assessments were conducted at baseline and after 24 hours\.Data were analyzed using the Wilcoxon signed\-rank test for within\-group comparisons and the Mann\-Whitney U test for between\-group comparisons\.Results:The experimental group demonstrated significant improvements in CVA \(p = 0\.0012\) and reduction in CCK error across all cervical movements \(p &lt; 0\.05\)\.The control group showed no significant changes \(p &gt; 0\.05\)\.Between\-group analysis revealed significant differences favoring the experimental group\.Conclusion:Rosario&\#x27;s SEA chain release may demonstrate short\-term improvements in cervical alignment and kinaesthetic accuracy in asymptomatic individuals with FHP\.These findings are exploratory, limited to immediate effects, and should be interpreted with caution\.Trial registration: Clinical Trials Registry of India \(CTRI/2024/12/077882\)\.\.
