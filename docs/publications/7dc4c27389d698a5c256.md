# Mental Physics\. Neurocybernetics and Psychoacoustics of the Liquid Cooling Environment in a Data Center: The Impact of mCDU Infrastructure Vibration and Noise on Cognitive Processes and Thermodynamic Isomorphism\.

**Autores:** Rajmund Olszewski

**Data:** 2026-10-17 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Abstract\. This paper investigates the impact of the unique vibroacoustic environment in modern high\-density data centers\-shaped by high\-speed server fans and mCDU/CDU circulation pumps\-on the human nervous system and cognitive processes\. This phenomenon is analyzed at the intersection of acoustic physics and neurocybernetics\. The analysis demonstrates that overlapping frequency bands \(broadband aerodynamic noise &gt;250 Hz and structural hydraulic pulsations 50\-100 Hz\) induce effects of sensory isolation, stochastic resonance, and vibroacoustic entrainment within the cognitive apparatus\. Furthermore, the study highlights a strict isomorphism between predictive algorithms controlling mCDU circuits and the allostatic hemodynamics of the human brain\. Within the framework of Embodied Cognition theory, it is shown that long\-term engineering exposure to thermodynamic parameters, noise variance, and fluid dynamics realigns the operator&\#x27;s cognitive schema from a reactive to an anticipatory model, fostering advanced Systems Thinking\.
