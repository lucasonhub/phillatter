# Phenotypic and metabolic characterization of an ADH2 deletion mutant associated with reduced fluconazole susceptibility in Candida albicans

**Autores:** Chunjiao Zheng; Wenjing He; Chang Su; Lulu An; Jingwen Tan; Lianjuan Yang

**Data:** 2026-10-09 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Introduction Alcohol dehydrogenases \(ADHs\) participate in fungal carbon metabolism and redox homeostasis, but the contribution of ADH2 to virulence\-associated phenotypes and fluconazole response in Candida albicans \( C\. albicans \) remains incompletely defined\. Methods An ADH2 deletion mutant, adh2Δ/Δ , was generated in a SC5314 genetic background\. Growth kinetics, adhesion, filamentous development, biofilm metabolic activity, cell surface hydrophobicity, intracellular ATP, reactive oxygen species, mitochondrial membrane potential, NAD \+ /NADH ratio, selected glucose\-metabolism\-related transcript levels, fluconazole response, and in vivo infection burden were evaluated\. Results The adh2Δ/Δ mutant displayed modestly altered growth kinetics and showed a shorter doubling time than SC5314\. However, qualitative assays showed reduced adhesion and filamentous development, accompanied by decreased expression of ALS1 , ALS3 , and HWP1 \. The mutant displayed increased biofilm metabolic activity but decreased cell surface hydrophobicity, together with increased CSH1 expression\. Metabolic analyses showed reduced ATP levels and basal intracellular ROS\-sensitive fluorescence, together with an increased NAD \+ /NADH ratio and elevated mitochondrial membrane potential\. Among selected glucose\-metabolism\-related genes, HXK1 was significantly upregulated after correction for multiple testing, whereas CDC19 , PGI1 , and PGK1 were not significantly altered\. The adh2Δ/Δ mutant showed a two\-fold increase in fluconazole MIC and stronger growth under FLCZ stress, accompanied by increased ERG11 transcript levels, whereas MDR1 , CDR1 , and CDR2 were not significantly altered after correction for multiple testing; ADH1 was also unchanged\. In mice, adh2Δ/Δ infection resulted in higher renal fungal burden and higher residual renal fungal burden after fluconazole treatment\. Discussion These findings define an ADH2 deletion\-associated phenotypic and metabolic/redox state and provide a hypothesis\-generating framework for exploring its association with reduced fluconazole susceptibility in C\. albicans \.
