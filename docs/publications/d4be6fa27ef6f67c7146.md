# Nuclear mechanomics beyond stiffness: toward a genuine mechanical state space

**Autores:** Diego Herráez‐Aguilar; Carlos del Pozo; Lucía Benito\-Barca; Sonia Contera; Manuel R\. Ramírez; Francisco Monroy

**Data:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Mechanical measurements of the nucleus remain fragmented across modalities and are often reduced to scalar “stiffness” proxies\. We define the Genuine Nuclear Mechanome as a regime\-aware, physically interpretable representation of intrinsic nuclear mechanical state\. Readouts from non\-equivalent techniques are converted into model\-dependent descriptors and mapped onto experimentally supported chromatin contexts\. A living\-HeLa reconstruction illustrates the framework, which requires state preservation, identifiability, uncertainty propagation and cross\-regime validation for multimodal systems biology\.
