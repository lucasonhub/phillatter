# Adversarial Detection for Pre\-trained Classifiers via Lipschitz\-Margin Analysis with Certification

**Autores:** Rui Zhang; Xinle Cao; Huazhen Yu; Jian Liu; Youwen Zhu

**Data:** 2026-10-11 · **Tipo:** preprint

## Resumo

Deep classifiers deployed in safety\-critical services like autonomous\-driving perception remain vulnerable to small adversarial perturbations\. Certified $\\ell\_2$ defenses today face a costly trade\-off: randomized smoothing \(RS\) requires hundreds of noisy queries per input, while Lipschitz networks \(LN\) constrain the architecture and sacrifice clean accuracy\. We take a detection\-oriented approach instead: rather than certifying that predictions are invariant inside an $\\ell\_2$ ball, we certify that adversarial inputs within an $\\ell\_2$ ball are \\emph\{detected\} \-\-\- a guarantee aligned with security\-critical deployments where missing an attack is costlier than flagging a clean input\. To realize this, we prove a Lipschitz\-margin reduction from certified $\\ell\_2$ robustness to certified detection, which reduces the problem to handling a single Lipschitz constant $L$\. We introduce two detector families: $\\mathbb\{D\}\_\{Adv\}$, which estimates $L$ from a single defender\-side perturbed pair, and $\\mathbb\{D\}\_\{lc\}$, which augments that pair with a local margin centroid to keep the estimate above what the certificate requires\. Both deploy on any pretrained classifier without retraining\. With a valid global bound, the detector is fully certified at that radius; with a local estimate $L&\#x27;$, certification is sample\-wise and applies only when our certified condition holds\. Across MNIST, CIFAR\-10, and ImageNet\-1k on four backbones \(LeNet\-5, VGG16, ResNet\-50, ViT\-B/16\), our detectors match or outperform five baselines \(CTT, SortNet, CRM, ContraNet, A$^2$D\)\. For CIFAR\-10 with 1\-Lipschitz SortNet, our reduction delivers $100\\%$ certified detection at the lowest FPR among compared certified detectors; on the standard CIFAR\-10 VGG16, our detectors reject all evaluated in\-budget adversarial examples, with 62\-82\\% both rejected and formally certified\.
