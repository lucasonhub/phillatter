# TECHNICAL EVOLUTION AND INFRASTRUCTURE CHALLENGES IN THE IEEE 802\.11 STANDARD: FROM LEGACY TO THE WI\-FI 10 HORIZON

**Autores:** Haroldo Benedito Tadeu Zattar; João Pedro Kiedzerski Silva; Jhoni Soares Raymundo

**Data:** 2026-10-09 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

The evolution of wireless local area networks, from the IEEE 802\.11 Legacy standard to the Wi\-Fi 10 horizon, represents a significant technological leap in telecommunications engineering\. The study is justified by the accelerated pace of this evolution, marked by the continuous growth in the number of connected devices and by the demand for higher throughput and lower latency\. This article aims to analyse the historical trajectory of wireless connectivity, to systematically identify the technical and infrastructure challenges that condition the feasibility of the next generations of the standard and, based on this evolutionary study, to estimate the characteristics of Wi\-Fi 10\. The research, of a quali\-quantitative nature, started from a bibliographic review of articles, books and technical standards, followed by predictive modelling and the technical analysis of the authors\. The results show the transition from incremental advances to exponential throughput growth, with estimated rates exceeding 400 Gbps through the exploitation of Terahertz \(THz\) bands, a projection subject to the uncertainty margin inherent to long\-term predictive models\. The rebalancing of Key Performance Indicators \(KPIs\) is discussed, highlighting the era of Ultra High Reliability \(UHR\) to be implemented by Wi\-Fi 8, as well as the evolution of cryptographic security, which still demands research effort\. The systemic challenges imposed by the last\-mile bottleneck are also presented\. It is concluded that the full feasibility of Wi\-Fi 10 depends on the mandatory convergence between the air interface, ultra\-high\-capacity optical backhaul infrastructures and the new satellite internet access systems\.
