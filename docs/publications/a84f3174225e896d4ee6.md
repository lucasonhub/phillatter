# IA e protezione dei dati personali: complementarietà e criticità nel quadro di tutela dei diritti fondamentali

**Autores:** Francesca Mollo

**Data:** 2026-10-12 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

AI and Personal Data Protection: complementary andcritical aspects within the framework of fundamental rightsprotection The article reconstructs the intersections between European AI regulation and personal data protection legislation, in terms of complementarities and critical points\. To this end, it considers the processing of special categories of data as a potential risk to fundamental rights, especially with regard to the risk of algorithmic bias with discriminatory effects\.
