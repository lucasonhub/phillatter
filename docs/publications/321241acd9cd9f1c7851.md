# Thermo\-Mechanical Analysis of Laminated and Sandwich Plates—Bending and Buckling Behavior

**Autores:** Mohini Khade; Keshav Kashinath Sangle

**Data:** 2026-10-14 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

This paper presents a comprehensive thermo\-mechanical analysis of laminated composite sandwich plates, focusing on bending deformations and buckling stability under combined thermal and mechanical loading\.A First\-Order Shear Deformation Theory \(FSDT\)\-based formulation is employed, and a finite element model is developed in ABAQUS to simulate plate response\.A symmetric sandwich plate with carbon/epoxy face sheets and a lightweight core is analyzed under a uniform transverse pressure and a through\-thickness temperature gradient\.Results show that thermal effects substantially increase the bending deflection \(over 100% rise for a 100°C gradient\) and alter stress distributions, with face sheets carrying most of the load while the core bears minimal stress\.Parametric studies indicate that a higher temperature gradient notably reduces the plate&\#x27;s effective bending stiffness \(by \~18%\) and consequently lowers the critical buckling load and natural frequencies\.The numerical predictions are validated against an analytical FSDT solution, showing close agreement \(&lt;5% difference in deflection\)\.These findings highlight the importance of including thermal loads in the design of composite sandwich structures and demonstrate the accuracy of the FSDT\-based finite element approach for predicting their structural behavior\.
