# Intuitionistic Fuzzy Multi\-Attribute Group Decision Making Using CoCoSo Technique

**Autores:** Mingyue Zhang; Dan Zeng; Wei Ji

**Data:** 2026-10-14 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

The evaluation of the quality of integrating traditional Chinese culture into multicultural classroom teaching can help standardize pathways for cultural integration, mitigate cross\-cultural teaching conflicts, support the inheritance of Chinese cultural heritage and the cultivation of intercultural competence, improve the multicultural classroom evaluation system, and provide a foundation for enhancing teaching effectiveness and quality of culture\-based education\. The teaching quality evaluation in rural multicultural classrooms through Chinese traditional culture incorporation represents a significant MAGDM\. In this research, we propose a method termed Intuitionistic Fuzzy CoCoSo, grounded in the DSM \(IF\-DSM\-CoCoSo\), designed to efficiently address MAGDM tasks that involve IFSs\. Then, the IF\-DSM\-CoCoSo framework provides a robust scheme for assessing teaching quality in rural multicultural classrooms, incorporating traditional Chinese culture\. In addition, a numerical evaluation is performed to examine teaching quality and to confirm the validity of proposed model in practice\.
