# Bounds on Treewidth via Excluding Disjoint Unions of Cycles

**Autores:** Meike Hatzel; Chun‐Hung Liu; Bruce Reed; Sebastian Wiederrecht

**Data:** 2026-10-09 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

One of the fundamental results in graph minor theory is that for every planar graph $H$, there is a minimum integer $f\(H\)$ such that graphs with no minor isomorphic to\~$H$ have treewidth at most $f\(H\)$\. The best bound known for an arbitrary planar $H$ is $\{O\(\|V\(H\)\|^9\\operatorname\{poly\~log\}\|V\(H\)\|\)\}$\. We show that if $H$ is the disjoint union of cycles, then $f\(H\)$ is $O\(\|V\(H\)\|\\log^2 \|V\(H\)\|\)$, which is a $\\log\|V\(H\)\|$ factor away from being optimal\.
