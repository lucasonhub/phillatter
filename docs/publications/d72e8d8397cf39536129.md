# APOE ε4 carriership status enhances age\-related changes in Alzheimer’s disease plasma biomarkers concentrations

**Autores:** Rebecca Z\. Rousset; Anouk den Braber; Bram Bongers; Lynn Boonkamp; David John Wilson; Lannie Ligthart; René Pool; Pieter Jelle Visser; Sophie M\. van der Landen; Eco J\. C\. de Geus; Charlotte Elisabeth Teunissen

**Data:** 2026-10-17 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Alzheimer’s disease \(AD\) plasma biomarkers are cost\-effective tools to detect early stage amyloid pathology\. We investigated how interactions between AD genetic risk \(apolipoprotein E \(APOE\) ε4, AD polygenic risk score \(excluding APOE; PRSAD\)\), age, and sex affected AD plasma biomarkers concentrations in a population\-based cohort\. We included 5913 participants from the Netherlands Twin Register age 30 – 80 \(64\.1% females, 29\.8% ε4 carriers\) and measured plasma phosphorylated\-tau\-217 \(p\-tau217\), amyloid\-β−42/40 \(Aβ−42/40\), neurofilament light \(NfL\), and glial fibrillary acidic protein \(GFAP\)\. We showed that the PRSAD did not associate with biomarker concentrations over age\. In ε4 carriers, decreases in Aβ−42/40 became steeper after age 50, and increases in p\-tau217 and GFAP became steeper after age 55 and 60 compared to non\-carriers, respectively\. Analyses in a separate longitudinal cohort \(EMIF\-AD\) confirmed that p\-tau217 concentration changes in ε4 carriers reflect AD pathology\. These results highlight the potential of plasma biomarkers, especially p\-tau217, for early detection of preclinical AD pathology\. In cognitively normal individuals, the rate of change in concentrations of plasma ptau217 and Aβ−42/40 over age differs between APOE ε4 carriers and non\-carriers\. For p\-tau217, the effect of APOE ε4 carriership is stronger in males than females\.
