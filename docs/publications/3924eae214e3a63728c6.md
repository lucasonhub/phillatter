# Integrated Case Studies, Reviews, Chapters and Technical Papers in Veterinary and Animal Sciences \(Volume\-1\)

**Autores:** Editors: Dr\. Srinivas Sathapathy Dr\. Smrutirekha Mallick Dr\. G\. Ambica Dr\. Santwana Palai Initiated and managed by Vidadala Rajendra

**Data:** 2026-10-13 · **Tipo:** book

## Resumo

Veterinary and animal sciences have grown far beyond the traditional boundaries of anatomy, physiology, and clinical practice\. Today, understanding animal health demands an integrated approach—one that connects structure with function, disease with mechanism, and treatment with toxicology\. This book, Integrated Case Studies, Reviews, and Technical Papers in Veterinary and Animal Sciences, is born from that conviction\. The volume brings together four essential pillars: Anatomy, Physiology, Clinical Complex, and Pharmacology and Toxicology\. Rather than treating these as isolated subjects, the chapters here weave them into a single narrative\. A case of hydrocephalus in a calf, for instance, is not just an anatomical curiosity—it reflects developmental physiology, clinical diagnosis, and the broader implications for animal welfare\. Similarly, a case of paraquat toxicity or NSAID poisoning demands knowledge of pharmacology, pathology, and emergency medicine all at once\.
