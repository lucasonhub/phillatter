# Frequency of Cervical Intraepithelial Neoplasia in Symptomatic Women with Clinically Unhealthy Cervix Based on Colposcopic Assessment

**Autores:** 

**Data:** 2026-10-09 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Background: Cervical intraepithelial neoplasia \(CIN\) represents a precursor to invasive cervical cancer and remains a major public health concern, particularly in low\-and middle\-income countries where screening programs are limited\.Aim: To determine the frequency of cervical intraepithelial neoplasia in symptomatic women with a clinically unhealthy cervix using colposcopic assessment and Reid scoring\.Methods: This descriptive cross\-sectional study was conducted at the Department of Obstetrics and Gynecology, Gulab Devi Hospital, Lahore, over a period of six months, from October 2025 to March 2026\.A total of 223 symptomatic women aged 20\-70 years with clinically unhealthy cervix were included using non\-probability consecutive sampling\.Data on demographic, clinical, and reproductive characteristics were collected\.Colposcopic examination was performed using 5% acetic acid and Lugol&\#x27;s iodine, and CIN was diagnosed based on Reid Colposcopic Index scores\.Data were analyzed using SPSS version 26\.0, with appropriate descriptive and inferential statistics applied\.Results: The mean age of participants was 38\.6 ± 9\.4 years\.CIN was identified in 61\.4% \(n=137\) of women, with 57\.7% having low\-grade and 42\.3% having high\-grade lesions\.Abnormal vaginal discharge \(36\.8%\) was the most common presenting symptom\.Significant associations were observed between CIN and age \(p=0\.003\),age at first pregnancy \(p=0\.001\),duration of symptoms \(p=0\.002\),contact bleeding \(p=0\.001\), and visible cervical lesions \(p&lt;0\.001\)\.Logistic regression analysis identified age \(OR 1\.04\), symptom duration \(OR 1\.09\), contact bleeding \(OR 2\.41\), and visible lesions \(OR 3\.76\) as independent predictors of CIN\.Conclusion: There is a high burden of CIN among symptomatic women with clinically unhealthy cervix, with significant clinical predictors identifiable through colposcopic assessment\.Early detection using colposcopy can play a crucial role in preventing progression to invasive cervical cancer\.
