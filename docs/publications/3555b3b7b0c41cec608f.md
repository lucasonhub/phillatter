# Early Detection of Pension\-Sponsor Distress from Public Filings: A Sponsor Vulnerability Index, Stress\-Augmented VaR, and Dynamic Self\-Investment Caps

**Autores:** Ravil Akhtyamov

**Data:** 2026-10-09 · **Tipo:** preprint

## Resumo

A defined\-benefit pension is only as safe as its sponsor, yet sponsor distress is under\-instrumented relative to plan\-side modelling\. We present a transparent, reproducible early\-warning pipeline built entirely on public data\. A Sponsor Vulnerability Index \(SVI\), a gradient\-boosted classifier over point\-in\-time SEC EDGAR balance\-sheet fundamentals and two macro series, predicts a filings\-based distress state one to two quarters ahead\. On 36 U\.S\. sponsors in five sector groups \(1,418 firm\-quarters, 2015Q1\-2025Q3\) it reaches an out\-of\-fold AUC\-ROC of 0\.887 and PR\-AUC of 0\.711 \(base rate 11\.9%\) under leave\-one\-firm\-out cross\-validation\. A Monte Carlo VaR with a stylized transition\-stress overlay shows that climate\-type stress is negligible for a diversified plan but adds 20\-43% to one\-year 95% VaR when 22\.5% of assets are held in sponsor securities\. A dynamic self\-investment cap maps the SVI to a limit that falls from 8% to below 1% in acute distress and recovers with the sponsor, which we illustrate on PG&amp;E&\#x27;s 2019 bankruptcy and Boeing&\#x27;s persistent fragility\. We report the method&\#x27;s failure modes alongside its results \(book\-equity labels that also fire on non\-credit events such as spin\-offs, positives concentrated in one firm, and stylized stress parameters\) and release code, data and results under a concept DOI\.
