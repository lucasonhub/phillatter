# Pengembangan Media Pembelajaran Android Berbasis MIT App Inventor pada Materi Komponen Elektronika \(Development of Android\-Based Learning Media Using MIT App Inventor for Teaching Electronic Components\)

**Autores:** Putri Shabira Rahman Rahman; Mohammad Fatkhurrohman; Siswo Wardoyo

**Data:** 2026-10-12 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

This study aims to develop an Android\-based learning medium covering electronic components using MIT App Inventor and to determine its feasibility based on expert validation and user feedback\. The study employs the Research and Development \(R&amp;D\) method using the ADDIE model, comprising the stages of Analyze, Design, Development, Implementation, and Evaluation\. The developed product is a learning application featuring content on electronic components, animated videos, practice questions, a 4\-band resistor calculator, and resistor circuit calculations\. Feasibility was assessed through validation by media and subject matter experts, while user testing involved a teacher and 65 Grade X students from the Electrical Power Installation Engineering program at SMKN 4 Serang City\. Feasibility was determined based on assessment percentages using a four\-point Likert scale\. The results indicate that media expert validation achieved a score of 90% and subject matter expert validation achieved 94%, both falling into the &quot;highly feasible&quot; category\. Teacher feedback yielded a score of 95%, while student feedback was 85\.28%, also placing the medium in the &quot;highly feasible&quot; category\. These results demonstrate that the developed Android\-based learning medium is suitable for supporting instruction on electronic components\. A limitation of this study is that testing did not cover the medium&\#x27;s effectiveness in improving learning outcomes\. Therefore, future research could examine the effectiveness of the medium on student learning outcomes across a broader range of subjects and locations\.
