# SmolWAM: A Latent World\-Action Model for Robotic Manipulation on Edge Devices

**Autores:** Timi Owolabi; Hikmah Olawore; Frances Adelakun; Ayotomiwa Oyewumi

**Data:** 2026-10-09 · **Tipo:** preprint

## Resumo

World\-action models \(WAMs\) predict a robot&\#x27;s actions together with the observations those actions will produce\. However, the strongest models rely on video diffusion transformers that are far too computationally expensive for resource\-constrained edge devices\. We present SmolWAM, a latent WAM that we derive from the 2B\-parameter Cosmos Policy by keeping its pretrained trunk and rebuilding its inference interface: single\-frame visual latents, cached text features that preserve the trunk&\#x27;s attention sinks, 12 of 28 transformer blocks, and 1,008 tokens from which actions and future visual latents are read out in one rectified\-flow step\. Trained in 3\.6 GPU\-hours, SmolWAM succeeds in 95\.5% of 400 LIBERO episodes at 57 ms and 2\.3 TFLOP per 16\-action chunk with 2\.4 GiB of GPU memory, 11x faster than its teacher with 18\.5x fewer FLOPs and 3\.2x less memory, and its world head still forecasts scene motion\.
