# High\-dose colchicine and clinical outcomes in COVID\-19 patients with surgical conditions: A single\-center retrospective cohort study

**Autores:** Elena Arabadzhieva; Atanas S\. YONKOV; D Bulanov; Georgi Korukov; Zhivko A\. SHAVALOV; Vanyo Mitev

**Data:** 2026-10-15 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

This single\-center retrospective observational study included 28 patients with surgical conditions and confirmed COVID\-19\. The small sample size limited the statistical power to detect a significant reduction in mortality among patients treated with higher\-dose colchicine; nevertheless, a trend toward lower mortality was observed\. Colchicine treatment was associated with a statistically significant reduction in serum ferritin levels, accompanied by trends toward lower CRP and D\-dimer levels\. These findings are consistent with the proposed anti\-inflammatory effects of colchicine and with previous reports suggesting that earlier administration and higher therapeutic doses may be associated with improved clinical outcomes in patients with COVID\-19\.
