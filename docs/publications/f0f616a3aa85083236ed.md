# Lee, Jinho: Art of Consciousness v1\.0 — Operating System for Human Creativity and Collective Evolution

**Autores:** 

**Data de descoberta:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** unknown

## Descrição do feed

This book proposes a creative operating system that treats art as core infrastructure for a consciousness\-based civilization, rather than as a luxury or representation\. It develops new metrics of consciousness and creative resonance, links them to economic and governance structures, and sketches protocols for AI–human co\-creation that avoid extraction and collapse dynamics\. The result is a speculative but technically framed blueprint for replacing labor\-driven societies with creativity\-centered, consciousness\-indexed institutions capable of coordinating humans and advanced AI without sacrificing freedom, depth, or diversity \(direct link \)
