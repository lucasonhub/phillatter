# Let’s talk money: The financial situation and capability of people with psychotic disorders

**Autores:** Josephien; id\_orcid 0000\-0002\-8741\-8316 Jansen

**Data:** 2026-10-13 · **Tipo:** dissertation

## Resumo

My PhD thesis comprises six studies \(chapters\) focusing on the financial functioning of people with psychosis\.These chapters form a coherent body of work, supplemented by a general introduction and discussion\.All studies have been published in peer\-reviewed scientific journals, co\-authored with collaborating researchers\.The research ideas, conceptualization, and methodologies of each study were developed collaboratively within a research team of which I was an active member\.I played a leading role throughout all stages of the research process, including data processing, analysis, and drafting the initial manuscripts\.Additionally, I was responsible for incorporating feedback from coauthors, refining the manuscripts, and managing the submission process\.Furthermore, during the peer\-review phase, I took the initiative in addressing reviewers&\#x27; comments and revising the manuscripts as discussed within the research group\.Importantly, I was the primary and leading contributor of each study, but I was supervised and/or assisted by an amazing team of researchers, healthcare professionals, financial professionals, and individuals with lived experience, all of whom played key roles in the project&\#x27;s development and implementation\.
