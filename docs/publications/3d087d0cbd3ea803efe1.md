# Mussaenda saxatilis \(Rubiaceae\), a new limestone species from Guangxi, China

**Autores:** Jin Li; Xiao\-Yu Wei; Tingting Duan

**Data:** 2026-10-14 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

A new species of Mussaenda \(Rubiaceae\), M\. saxatilis , is described from the limestone karst regions of Guangxi, southern China\. Morphologically, the new species most closely resembles M\. hainanensis but differs from that species in having shallowly or moderately 2\-lobed stipules, subsessile inflorescences, rotundate\-ovate corolla lobes, smaller berries, and a pericarp that lacks lenticels\. A detailed description, habitat information, phenology, distribution, and comparisons with morphologically similar species are provided\.
