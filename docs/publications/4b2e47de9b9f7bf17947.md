# Optimizing Marine Current Energy Systems: Application of the MOORA Method for Sustainable Development

**Autores:** Sathiyaraj Chinnasamy; M Ramachandran; Srividhya Velmurugan

**Data:** 2026-10-17 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Marine current energy refers to the harnessing of kinetic energy from ocean currents to generate electricity\. This renewable energy source utilizes underwater turbines, similar to wind turbines, placed in areas with strong, consistent currents\. Marine currents are predictable and can potentially generate substantial power, helping to reduce reliance on fossil fuels and combat climate change\. Marine current energy harnesses the kinetic energy produced by ocean currents to generate electricity\. The methodology involves installing underwater turbines that capture the movement of water, converting kinetic energy into mechanical energy, and then into electrical energy\. Ongoing monitoring and maintenance are crucial to optimize performance and address any ecological concerns, ensuring sustainable energy production from marine currents\.
