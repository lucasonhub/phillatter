# Residual Strength Prediction of Composite Cylindrical Shells With Cutouts Using a Machine Learning Method

**Autores:** Shiyu Li; Qiubo Li; Hua Rong; Kai Xiao; Wenhao She; Chuanzhe Kang; Boxu Zhu; Shigang Ai

**Data:** 2026-10-11 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

ABSTRACT The assessment of residual performance for damaged composite cylindrical shells is critical and challenging in practical engineering applications\. Traditional numerical methods suffer from low computational efficiency and cannot support rapid on‐site evaluation\. Meanwhile, complex coupling behavior among multiple cutouts increases the difficulty of identifying key degradation‐controlling parameters\. To address this issue, this study proposes a hybrid decision tree–artificial neural network \(DT‐ANN\) method for residual performance prediction of damaged composite structures\. The proposed framework firstly adopts a decision tree to establish a damage interaction model, which realizes effective decoupling and dimensionality reduction for multi‐cutout characteristic parameters\. Subsequently, an ANN is employed to achieve rapid prediction of residual strength retention\. Experimental and numerical results show that the prediction errors are limited to 7\.31% for single‐cutout cases and 6\.71% for triple‐cutout cases, verifying good engineering applicability\. Comparative analysis under various cutout configurations demonstrates that the presented method possesses high prediction accuracy and superior computational efficiency compared with traditional numerical approaches\. This work provides a theoretical basis and technical reference for performance evaluation and decision‐making of composite cylindrical shells in engineering practice\.
