# Quality of recovery \(QoR\-15\) after patient\-controlled sedation during venous port implantation: A prespecified substudy of the PACSPI\-2 randomised clinical trial

**Autores:** Stefanie Seifert; Andreas Nilsson; Josip Ažman; Olle Eriksson; Michelle S\. Chew; Fredrik Hammarskjöld; Knut Taxbro

**Data:** 2026-10-14 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

BACKGROUND: Postoperative recovery after subcutaneous venous port implantation is generally considered uncomplicated; however, patient\-reported recovery outcomes in patients with cancer remain poorly characterized\. The impact of patient\-controlled sedation \(PCS\) on postoperative recovery has not been established\. OBJECTIVE: To assess early postoperative recovery using the Quality of Recovery\-15 \(QoR\-15\) questionnaire in a prespecified substudy of the PACSPI\-2 randomised clinical trial and to compare recovery between patients receiving local anaesthesia alone and those receiving local anaesthesia combined with patient\-controlled sedation\. DESIGN AND SETTING: Prespecified substudy of a randomised, open\-label clinical trial conducted at two Swedish anaesthesia departments\. PATIENTS: Three hundred thirty\-four adult patients with cancer\. INTERVENTIONS: Local anaesthesia \(LA\) alone or LA combined with propofol\-alfentanil PCS during SVP implantation\. MAIN OUTCOME MEASURES: Primary outcome was difference of postoperative total QoR\-15 scores between groups\. The primary endpoint of the parent PACSPI\-2 trial was maximal intraprocedural pain\. RESULTS: There was no overall difference in total postoperative QoR\-15 scores between the treatment groups \(P = 0\.61\)\. Total QoR\-15 scores changed significantly over time \(P &lt; 0\.001\) in both groups\. By postoperative day 1 \(POD 1\), mean QoR\-15 scores had increased from baseline in both groups \(mean difference 2\.9 and 3\.1, respectively; P = 0\.02 for both\), although these changes were below the published minimal clinically important difference \(MCID\) of 6\. Before discharge, the LA \+ PCS group showed its largest within\-group improvement in QoR\-15 \(mean difference 5\.6, P &lt; 0\.001\), primarily driven by the &quot;Emotional state&quot; and &quot;Psychological support&quot; dimensions, however no significant between group differences were observed\. Median pain scores on POD 1 were 3 \(IQR 2\-5\) in both groups, with NRS ≥ 4 reported by 49% in the LA and 48% in the LA \+ PCS group\. CONCLUSIONS: Early postoperative recovery after subcutaneous venous port implantation was generally good and did not differ between the LA and LA \+ PCS group\. A transient within\-group improvement in emotional and psychological recovery domains was observed before discharge in the LA \+ PCS group\. Pain on POD 1 remained common in both groups\. These findings were based on secondary analyses and should therefore be considered exploratory\. TRIAL REGISTRATION: European Union Clinical Trials Registry: EudraCT: 2021\-003821\-31, ClinicalTrials\.gov: NCT05688384\.
