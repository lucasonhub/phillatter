# Modern Educational Technologies in Teaching Foreign Languages in the Context of Inclusive and Multilingual Education \(Based on Materials from Kyrgyzstan\)

**Autores:** Zh\.T\. Kadenova; D\. Egamkulov

**Data:** 2026-10-15 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

The article examines modern educational technologies used in teaching foreign languages in the context of inclusive and multilingual education based on the material of Kyrgyzstan\. Special attention is paid to the use of innovative pedagogical approaches aimed at improving the effectiveness of education and ensuring equal access to education for all students, including students with special educational needs\. Technologies such as a communicative approach, digital educational resources, interactive teaching methods, as well as differentiated and individualized learning are analyzed\. It is noted that the use of modern technologies contributes to the development of linguistic competence, the formation of intercultural communication and the creation of a favorable educational environment in the multilingual society of Kyrgyzstan\. The study concludes that it is necessary to integrate innovative technologies and inclusive practices into the system of teaching foreign languages\.
