## Resumo

In a distance learning environment, a student&\#x27;s competence is not limited to subject\-specific knowledge and practical skills; rather, it encompasses complex attributes such as planning independent learning activities, self\-management, purposeful use of digital tools, critical Ta’limda istiqbolli izlanishlar \- xalqaro ilmiy\-metodik jurnal № 8/2026 ISSN: 3060\-527X selection of information, effective virtual communication, and reflective assessment of one&\#x27;s own learning outcomes\.
