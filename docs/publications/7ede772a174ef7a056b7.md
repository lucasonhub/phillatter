# AI\-Driven Personalization in Microlearning: A Systematic Review and Bibliometric Mapping of an Emerging Research Gap \(2020–2026\)

**Autores:** najlaa mai

**Data:** 2026-10-13 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Two\-strand bibliometric\-systematic review \(B\-SLR\) following PRISMA 2020\. Strand 1 maps the microlearning literature indexed in Scopus \(2020–2026\)\. Strand 2 systematically reviews a sub\-corpus on personalization and artificial intelligence in microlearning, extracting adaptation mechanisms, evaluation designs and reported learning outcomes\.
