# Phenomenological Functionalism: Toward Resolving the Hard Problem Through Supersystemic Integration and Carrier\-Code Distinction

**Autores:** Daria Fadaleva

**Data:** 2026-10-13 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

This paper introduces Phenomenological Functionalism to address the Hard Problem of Consciousness by distinguishing between the physical medium and semantic configuration\. It demonstrates how top\-down supersystemic dynamics reconfigure subjective experience and resolve the explanatory gap without reductionism\.
