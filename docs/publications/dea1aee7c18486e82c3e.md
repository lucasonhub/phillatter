# A differentiable multi\-physics learning model for robust single\-image dehazing under complex atmospheric conditions

**Autores:** Mohammad Omar Ibrahim Alhawarat; Maged Faihan Alotaibi; Reem Aljuaidi; Bandar Saad Alshreef; E\. M\. Shaban; Wedad M\. Alawad; Maha Abdallah Alnuwaiser

**Data:** 2026-10-14 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Restoring clear visibility from a single hazy image remains a challenging inverse problem when multiple atmospheric phenomena jointly affect image formation\. In real environments, light propagation can be influenced not only by particle scattering but also by turbulence\-induced blur and wavelength\-dependent attenuation\. Most existing dehazing approaches primarily address scattering, which limits their physical consistency and robustness in heavy haze or long\-range scenes\. In this work, we introduce a differentiable multi\-physics learning framework that is designed to integrate scattering, turbulence, and spectral attenuation within a unified image formation model\. The degradation process is expressed as a compact linear observation equation, where turbulence is represented through a spatial convolution operator and spectral effects are modeled via channel\-dependent attenuation\. Since the governing parameters cannot be directly inferred from a single observation, the formulated as an iterative refinement process guided by a learned approximation of the physical model\. A lightweight neural estimator predicts the observation operators, while a learnable spectral module captures wavelength\-dependent behavior\. An iterative regularization network stabilizes the reconstruction process and prevents error propagation\. Experiments conducted on the RESIDE\-SOTS benchmark show competitive performance, achieving PSNR/SSIM values of 27\.3/0\.94 for indoor scenes and 30\.2/0\.94 for outdoor scenes with only a few optimization steps\. Additional analyses confirm improved robustness under noise perturbations and severe illumination variations, highlighting the effectiveness of differentiable learning with physics\-inspired constraints for real\-world atmospheric image restoration\.
