# High\-Quality, Low\-Cost Quasi\-Bulk Hexagonal Boron Nitride Crystals by LP\-HVPE: Machine Learning\-Assisted Process Optimization via XRD and Raman Characterization

**Autores:** Xi Chen; Ziting Han; Weibo Xu; Yalei Zhou; Xiaohang Liu; Lixin Hou; Yanjun Gao; Zhanguo Chen

**Data:** 2026-10-18 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Abstract Highly ordered, quasi\-bulk hexagonal boron nitride \(hBN\) films were grown on c\-plane sapphire \(α\-Al2O3\) substrates via low\-pressure hydride vapor\-phase epitaxy \(LP\-HVPE\), using industrial\-grade boron trichloride \(BCl3\) and ammonia \(NH3\) as low\-cost boron and nitrogen sources, respectively\. The effects of four key growth parameters \(growth temperature, III/V source flow ratio, growth ambient, and growth pressure\) on the crystalline quality of hBN films were systematically investigated via the single\-variable method\. A gradient boosting regression machine learning \(ML\) model was constructed based on 24 groups of experimental datasets to achieve high\-precision prediction of hBN crystalline quality, with determination coefficients \(R2\) of 0\.96 and 0\.94 for the prediction of X\-ray diffraction and Raman characteristic peak full width at half maximum, respectively\. The optimized growth condition was determined as follows: a growth temperature of 1350 °C, a III/V ratio of 1:3, N2 ambient, and a growth pressure of 100 Pa, which is consistent with the global optimal parameters obtained by Bayesian optimization combined with the ML model\. This work provides a low\-cost, high\-efficiency route for the controllable preparation of high\-quality quasi\-bulk hBN crystals via LP\-HVPE with ML\-assisted optimization\.
