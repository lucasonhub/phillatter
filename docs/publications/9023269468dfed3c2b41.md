# Keppel Hesselink, Jan: Authentic Spirituality and the Colonisation of Inner Space: Closed Symbolic Systems, Dark Magick, and Yoga as Liberation

**Autores:** 

**Data de descoberta:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** unknown

## Descrição do feed

Series Note This paper belongs to the Authentic Spirituality series, a sequence of essays developed from the author&\#x27;s wider work on contemplative phenomenology, inner light, yoga, medical critique, and embodied practice\. Across these domains, the central question remains the same: what remains real when hype, projection, ideology, and spiritual theatre are stripped away? Abstract This paper contributes to the series Authentic Spirituality by examining how closed symbolic systems colonise inner space\. Through a comparative reading of Jean Lorédan&\#x27;s account of the Gaufridy sorcery trial and Jules Bois&\#x27; Le Satanisme et la magie \(1895\), the paper argues that spiritual misdirection does not depend only on belief, coercion, or historical superstition\. It operates wherever images, doctrines, rituals, and interpretations occupy the field of experience so completely that no open inner space remains\. Lorédan shows the mechanism from the side of the accused, whose lived reality is absorbed by an inquisitorial template already prepared in advance\. Bois shows the same mechanism from the side of the investigator, whose prolonged immersion in dark magick gradually draws the observer into the phenomenological atmosphere of the material studied\. Together, these cases reveal a single structure, the occupation of consciousness by a closed interpretive field\. Against this background, the paper develops a phenomenological account of yoga, not as posture, belief, or spiritual identity, but as the capacity to sustain open, non\-interpretive attention\. This account is brought into dialogue with Gurdjieff&\#x27;s concept of identification and Jeanne de Salzmann&\#x27;s elaboration of presence as its counterforce\. Identification, in de Salzmann&\#x27;s precise formulation, is the structural default of attention without inner ground: consciousness merges with whatever it attends to, leaving no witnessing space\. This corresponds directly to the mechanism of closed\-system absorption described here, and de Salzmann&\#x27;s practice of presence corresponds directly to what this paper calls inner emptiness\. In this convergence, yoga and the Gurdjieff Work point toward the same prior capacity\. The paper concludes that authentic spirituality is not defined by exotic imagery, ritual intensity, or metaphysical claims, but by its ability to open inner space rather than occupy it\.
