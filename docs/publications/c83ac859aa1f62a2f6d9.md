# Arqueología cognitiva: historia, debates y perspectivas

**Autores:** Diana Platas\-Neri; Luis Alberto Arau Ruiz

**Data:** 2026-10-09 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

This article critically reviews cognitive archaeology, a discipline that aims to reconstruct mental processes and cognitive abilities from the archaeological record\. Its origins in the critique of the processual paradigm and its links with post\-processual approaches are examined, as well as the intersections with cognitive science, whose theories and methods have enriched the interpretation of material culture and fossil evidence\. The main contemporary theoretical perspectives —evolutionary, psychobiological, materialistic and phenomenological— are also presented, highlighting their contributions and tensions\. Overall, the article offers an integrated view of the current debates and challenges facing this emerging discipline\.
