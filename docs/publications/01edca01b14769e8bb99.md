# Towards consumer\-oriented smart grids : investigating consumer perspectives on the adoption of smart technologies and services

**Autores:** Petteri Siitonen

**Data:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** dissertation

## Resumo

Consumers are expected to play a central role in smart grids, and therefore, it is becoming increasingly important to identify and understand factors that influence consumer engagement in them through various smart grid technologies and services\. This dissertation focuses on investigating consumer opinions, awareness, and experiences of key smart grid technologies and services, as well as on analysing factors and variables that influence their adoption\. By providing insights for both theory and practice, the research carried out in this dissertation aims to support the development of consumer\-oriented smart grids and to inform efforts to promote the adoption of smart grid technologies and services\. The research conducted in the dissertation adopts both qualitative and quantitative methods\. First, consumer perspectives towards demand response are investigated using a systematic review and thematic synthesis of previous European demand response projects\. Secondly, to improve the understanding of the roles of trust and technological knowledge in affecting technology adoption in the smart grid context, a cross\-sectional online questionnaire survey of Finnish consumers is carried out, and the data are analysed using path analysis\. Lastly, a machine learning\-based social media sentiment analysis is conducted to examine how consumer opinions of various smart grid technologies and behaviours have changed in response to the 2022 energy crisis\. This dissertation contributes to the smart grid literature in several ways\. Firstly, by highlighting the importance of incorporating consumer perspectives into the design of demand response programs, this work provides insights that can inform the development of smart grid services that are compatible with consumers’ preferences\. Secondly, the research elucidates the mechanisms by which trust and technological knowledge may influence the adoption of smart grid technologies and discusses the potential implications for future research and practice\. Lastly, to shed light on how times of crisis can change public opinion on smart grids, it is investigated how the 2022 energy crisis affected Finnish consumers’ awareness and opinions of smart grid technologies and services\. The results of this dissertation can serve both researchers and practitioners in the development of consumer\-oriented smart grids and in informing efforts to increase the adoption of smart grid technologies and services\.
