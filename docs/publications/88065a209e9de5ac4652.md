# Charting dark matter down to the neutrino floor/fog in the 2HD\+a scenario

**Autores:** Giorgio Arcadi; Abdelhak Djouadi A; Stefano Profumo

**Data:** 2026-10-13 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Introduction Next\-generation direct detection experiments will probe dark matter \(DM\) scattering cross\-sections deep into the neutrino fog, the regime where coherent neutrino scattering becomes an irreducible background\. We investigate whether thermally produced weakly interacting massive particles \(WIMPs\) can naturally populate this regime while satisfying relic density and indirect detection constraints\. Adopting as a case study the 2HD\+a, a two\-Higgs doublet model with h,H,A,H ± scalar states supplemented by a light singlet pseudoscalar Higgs state a\. Methodology The study of the model has been performed through extensive parameter scans using a combination of publicly available packages, referenced in the main text, and codes realized in purpose, as, for example, in the case of the loop\-induced DM scattering cross\-section\. Three main scenarios have been depicted: high singlet\-doublet mixing \(sin θ ≥ 10 −3 \), suppressed singlet\-doublet mixing \(sin θ &lt; 10 −3 \) and two component DM scenario \(sin θ = 0\)\. In each scenario the scan has been repeated for the Type\-I and Type\-II configurations\. Results We evaluate all the viable parameter space compared to current LZ and FERMI\-LAT bounds and projected XLZD and CTA sensitivities\. We find that the single component setup lies naturally below the neutrino floor for DM masses above 100 GeV while, on the contrary, most of the parameter space of the two component DM scenario is strongly disfavored already considering current limits alone\. The parameter space of both single and two component DM scenario can be nevertheless broadened by considering specific relations among the model parameters to suppress the coupling between the 125 GeV bosons and two a states\. Discussion Overall, our results provide strong motivation to fully exploit future ton\-scale detectors\.
