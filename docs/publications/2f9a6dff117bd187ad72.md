# Estabilización y control de un sistema de levitación magnética de segundo orden utilizando diseñosde control basados en Lyapunov

**Autores:** Oscar Danilo Montoya; Walter Gil\-González; Luis Fernando Grisales Noreña; Oscar Danilo Montoya

**Data:** 2026-10-17 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

This paper presents a Lyapunov\-based control framework for stabilizing a second\-order magnetic levitation system \(MLS\)\. The study explores three distinct candidate Lyapunov functions—exponential, logarithmic, and trigonometric\-quadratic—to derive nonlinear controllers ensuring asymptotic stability\. The proposed control laws are designed to regulate the levitating mass position while minimizing control effort\. A systematic tuning process using particle swarm optimization \(PSO\) is employed to optimize control parameters based on performance indices, including ITAE, IAE, ITSE, and total control energy\. Numerical simulations validate the effectiveness of the control strategies, showing that all controllers successfully stabilize the MLS while exhibiting different transient responses and energy consumption levels\. The logarithmic Lyapunov\-based controller demonstrates superior performance in terms of precision, response speed, and energy efficiency\. Future work aims to extend these methods to handle parameter uncertainties and disturbances while exploring machine learning\-based control optimization\.
