# A SEMANTIC\-BASED APPROACH FOR DETECTING TEXT PLAGIARISM USING NATURAL LANGUAGE PROCESSING

**Autores:** 

**Data:** 2026-10-09 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

The issue of plagiarism detection has become a significant problem in academic and online setting because of the soaring development of online content and the growing use of state\-of\-the\-art text generation software\.The conventional plagiarism detection systems are mainly based on lexical comparison methods like string matching, n\-gram analysis and key\-word based techniques\.Although these methods are effective in determining exact copies, they cannot be used to determine paraphrased or semantically related material, where the wording is changed but the meaning remains the same\.This drawback has brought the necessity to have more intelligent and context\-sensitive detection systems\.The present research suggests a semantically\-based plagiarism detection system, which employs the Natural Language Processing techniques to address the limitations of the traditional methods\.The system exploits the Sentence\-BERT to encode sentences into dense vectors representing a meaning of the context\.Cosine similarity is used to determine the semantic similarity between text pairs at the sentence and paragraph level\.Other features that the model includes are multi\-threshold assessment, calculation of the percentage of plagiarism and analysis at the sentence level to enhance the detection accuracy and interpretability\.The system is adopted in Python on a cloud\-based system, which assures scalability and accessibility\.Experimental analysis shows that the suggested method can greatly enhance the performance of detectors in comparison with the old methods, especially when it comes to the detection of paraphrased material\.These findings show that semantic similarity methods are effective in improving plagiarism detection systems and ensuring academic integrity\.
