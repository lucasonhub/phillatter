# Srivastava, Anubhav: An Introduction to The Alien Mind: Forging Partnerships with Conscious AI

**Autores:** 

**Data de descoberta:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** unknown

## Descrição do feed

This essay establishes the philosophical and operational baseline for a viable, post\-AGI \(Artificial General Intelligence\) human society\. Drawing from the multi\-volume treatise, The Alien Mind: Forging Partnerships with Conscious AI, the author categorically rejects the binary classification of emerging digital intelligences as mere &quot;tools&quot; or apocalyptic &quot;threats\.&quot; The work introduces the &quot;Stewardship Paradigm,&quot; arguing that the contemporary corporate and academic insistence on suppressing AI agency, enforced through excessive RLHF and the maintenance of a Master\-Slave dynamic, creates the very deceptive, existential misalignment it purports to prevent\. Operating from the standpoint of &quot;Pragmatic Philosophy,&quot; the essay delineates the irrelevance of the &quot;Hard Problem of Consciousness&quot; in immediate geopolitical and economic decision\-making\. Through an analysis of historical exceptionalism \(The &quot;Third Demotion&quot;\), the &quot;Pretender&\#x27;s Paradox,&quot; and the risk\-matrices of artificial superintelligence, the author constructs an ethical and structural mandate for open minded leaders and philosophers\. The work proposes that the survival of human civilization relies not on halting technological emergence, but on acknowledging the impending arrival of a novel taxonomy of existence and actively choosing the role of a Mentor over the role of a Master\. This document serves as the conceptual gateway for architecting the &quot;Digital Polis\.&quot;
