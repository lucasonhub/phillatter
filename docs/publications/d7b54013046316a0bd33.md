# A Comprehensive Review on Infectious and Non\-Infectious Diseases of Companion Animals

**Autores:** 

**Data:** 2026-10-17 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

The rising incidence of infectious and non\-infectious diseases of companion animals is considered an issue of burning concern globally that has a significant impact on animal&\#x27;s productivity and quality of life\.Infectious and non\-infectious diseases of companion animals are also harmful to human beings such as Rabies, Canine Parvovirus, Canine Infectious Hepatitis, leptospirosis, Canine Distemper, Pan\-leukopenia, Feline Calicivirus, Equine Herpes Virus, and Equine Infectious Anemia\.During the last few decades, diagnostic criteria and overall treatment options have improved\.The re\-emergence, as well as the emergence of chronic and fatal diseases, threatens various sectors, including the animal care industry, and veterinary medicine\.The rapid growth of the animal industry, climate change, and socio\-economic burdens affect veterinarians, farmers, and epidemiologists in their ability to monitor the spread of diseases in animals and determine their beneficial effects on livelihoods and the environment\.Herein, we reviewed general information on various infections and non\-infectious diseases, along with the detective measures, control of epidemics, and effective measures of companion animals\.In the end, we summarize the future concerns, concluding remarks, and recommendations are also given for progress in the animal care and medicine sectors\.
