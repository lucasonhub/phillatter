# Initial Developments in AI\-Informed Quantum Device Engineering

**Autores:** Dragica Vasileska; Gil Speyer; Adit Shah

**Data:** 2026-10-18 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Identifying energy states in quantum dot arrays that are suitable for quantum computing applications requires computationally intensive simulations\. This work explores the use of an image classifier trained on simulated electron\-density plots to evaluate different quantum dot geometries\. As array configurations increase in complexity, extending to longer and two\-dimensional arrays, classification accuracy decreases as expected\. Nevertheless, the results demonstrate promising performance and highlight the potential of image\-based machine learning to accelerate the analysis of quantum dot systems\. The approach is also readily adaptable to other scientific computing workflows in which simulation outputs can be represented and analyzed as images\.
