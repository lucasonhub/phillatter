# Klasifikasi Arah Tren Harga Bitcoin Menggunakan Metode Ensemble Learning Random Forest

**Autores:** Fadhlan Subhan Fadilah; Indra Yustiana; Nugraha

**Data:** 2026-10-15 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Bitcoin&\#x27;s extreme price volatility poses significant challenges for investors and traders in making informed investment decisions\. Conventional technical analysis approaches are often subjective and unable to capture complex market dynamics accurately\. This study aims to develop a Bitcoin price trend direction classification system as a more objective technical analysis tool using the Random Forest ensemble learning method\. Historical Bitcoin price data from January 2017 to March 2026 with an H1 \(1\-hour\) timeframe was obtained from MetaTrader 5 and Binance\. Raw price data \(Open, High, Low, Close, Volume\) were processed through preprocessing stages and converted into 106 technical indicator features grouped into nine categories, including Trend Indicators, Momentum Oscillators, Volatility Features, and Rolling Statistics\. The Random Forest Classifier model was built and optimized through hyperparameter tuning with time\-series cross\-validation, then evaluated using standard classification metrics as well as trading metrics under scenarios employing a trailing stop mechanism\. The results show that the model achieved an accuracy of 58\.65%, precision of 41\.38%, recall of 99\.50%, and F1\-score of 0\.5856\. Although the low AUC\-ROC value \(0\.5106\) indicates the model can hardly distinguish between the two classes individually, the backtest generated significant trading performance with a net profit of 8,491 USD from an initial capital of 10,000 USD, a win rate of 45\.08%, and a profit factor of 1\.30\. Feature importance analysis identified RSI\_14\_std\_168 \(3\.77%\), SMA\_200 \(3\.03%\), and ATR\_pct\_14\_mean\_168 \(2\.92%\) as the three most influential technical indicators\. This study concludes that although standard classification accuracy is limited, the Random Forest model combined with probability threshold optimization and risk management strategies can serve as a beneficial technical analysis tool\. The system has been implemented as an interactive web dashboard providing real\-time prediction features\.
