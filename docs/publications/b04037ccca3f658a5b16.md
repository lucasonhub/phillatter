# Do Syntax Heads Influence Model Behavior?

**Autores:** William Timkey; Tal Linzen

**Data:** 2026-10-13 · **Tipo:** preprint

## Resumo

Transformer language models develop attention heads that specialize in syntactic relations, for example, attending from verbs to their subjects\. Do these heads play a causal role in the model&\#x27;s syntactic predictions? Evidence for such a role is mixed to negative: even when we force these heads to attend to a distractor noun other than the subject, the model&\#x27;s syntactic predictions are still more consistent with the subject than with the distractor\. Interventions on specialized syntax heads had a significantly stronger effect on syntactic predictions than interventions on random heads, but only in one of the two measures we propose\.
