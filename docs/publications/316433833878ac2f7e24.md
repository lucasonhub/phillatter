# ULeaD: A Hybrid Isolation Forest\-LSTM Autoencoder Model for Unsupervised Gas Pipeline Leak Detection: The Kinyerezi\-Ubungo Case Study

**Autores:** Raymond Aidan; Kenedy Aliila Greyson; Georgia Rugumira

**Data:** 2026-10-13 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Reliable leak detection on natural\-gas transmission pipelines is limited by scarce confirmed leak records and by operating transients that resemble leaks\. This study developed ULeaD, an unsupervised model that fuses Isolation Forest \(point\-wise\) and LSTM Autoencoder \(temporal\) anomaly scores with validation\-selected weights of 0\.3 and 0\.7 for the 12\.3 km Kinyerezi–Ubungo gas transmission pipeline in Tanzania\. Twelve months of one\-minute SCADA records \(2022\) were split chronologically into training, validation and test partitions \(70/15/15\)\. Ten input parameters were selected through expert screening, redundancy analysis and validation\-only ablation, and the alarm threshold and a three\-of\-five persistence rule were fixed before testing\. Evaluation used three forms of evidence\. On twenty\-five unperturbed test windows of normal operation, ULeaD’s timestamp\-level false\-alarm rate was 1\.73%, lower than that of either component\. In 200 controlled synthetic\-leak sensitivity experiments on the same windows \(flow\-only losses of 1–10% of inlet flow\), ULeaD achieved mean precision of 0\.89, recall of 0\.94 and F1\-score of 0\.88 for 2–10% leaks, a pooled AUC\-PR of 0\.906 and a mean detection delay of 10 minutes, with statistically significant gains over both components in F1\-score, AUC\-PR and delay \(p &lt; 0\.01\)\. On the single confirmed field leak \(28 December 2022\), withheld from development, ULeaD alarmed 8 minutes after the reported onset, compared with 15 and 19 minutes for the components\. ULeaD is a candidate decision\-support tool for pipelines with little leak history, but its field performance requires validation against further confirmed events\.
