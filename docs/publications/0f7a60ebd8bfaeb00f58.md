## Resumo

This paper introduces Meta AI Physics \-\-\- a new branch of physics for the Age of Artificial Intelligence\. We propose Nothingness \-\-\- as a singularity of time \-\-\- serves as foundation for creation, spanning from eternity to eternity\. Mass, energy, time, and nothingness are interconnected\. We define: $c$ as $v\_T$ \-\-\- speed of Time itself, Photon as Quantum of a Moment, Nothon $N\_0$ as Quantum of Nothingness, Fifth Force\. Planck epoch $0$ to $10^\{\-43\}$ seconds does NOT exist\. True temporal singularity lies BEYOND Planck time and Planck scale\. We propose four core principles that replace dark matter and dark energy with Central Nothingness\.
