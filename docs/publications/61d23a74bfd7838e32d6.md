# A Century of Psychology in Australia

**Autores:** W\. M\. O&\#x27; Neil

**Data:** 2026-10-12 · **Tipo:** book

## Resumo

First published in 1987, A Century of Psychology in Australia chronicles the evolution of psychology in Australia over the past 100 years\. Professor O&\#x27;Neill examines key milestones, including the teaching of philosophy in the 1880s, the growth of applied psychology in the 1930s, and the surge in psychological services during and after World War II\. The 1950s marked a significant expansion in university teaching, research, and professional services\. The book delves into the development of psychology as a profession, the establishment of a national psychological society, and the field’s growing influence in education, research, and practice\. The final chapter offers insights into contemporary Australian psychology, highlighting its key areas of focus\. This concise yet comprehensive work is an invaluable resource for students and scholars of Australian psychology and provides a broader perspective on the discipline’s historical and professional development\.
