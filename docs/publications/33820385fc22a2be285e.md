# Stilwell, Phil: Learning Cø / N\*: A Student Textbook of Phenomenal Presence

**Autores:** 

**Data de descoberta:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** unknown

## Descrição do feed

Learning Cø / N\*: A Student Textbook of Phenomenal Presence presents a fourteen\-chapter, graduate\-accessible introduction to the Cø / N\* network\-dynamics theory of minimal phenomenal consciousness\. It integrates the core model with the research program on subject boundaries, report\-independent system\-wide availability, scientific indeterminacy, component ablation, phenomenal character, and structural legibility\. The book locates the model among integrated\-information, global\-workspace, recurrent\-processing, higher\-order, predictive\-processing, embodied, biological, and illusionist approaches without treating the new theory as settled\. Plain\-language annotations, formal guardrails, worked examples, analogies and their limits, thought experiments, collaborative exercises, counterexample challenges, and a complete adversarial capstone workbook help students reconstruct, differentiate, test, and revise the theory\. Synthetic examples are pedagogical rather than empirical estimates, and the book repeatedly separates component evidence, phenomenal attribution, constitutive interpretation, and practical action\.
