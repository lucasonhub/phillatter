# Spin\-Transfer Torque MRAM \(STT\-MRAM\) and Spin\-Orbit Torque \(SOT\) In\-Memory Computing: Perpendicular Magnetic Anisotropy, Crossbar Arrays, and Deep Learning Accelerators

**Autores:** Harsh Kumar Verma, Aditya Dattarwal, Dr\. Vishal Shrivastava, Dr\. Akhil Pandey

**Data:** 2026-10-17 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

The insatiable memory bandwidth demands of deep learning foundation models and edge artificial intelligence are bottlenecked by the Von Neumann bottleneck: the continuous energy\-prohibitive shuttling of weights and activations between off\-chip dynamic RAM \(DRAM\) and processor arithmetic logic units \(ALUs\)\. Magnetoresistive Random Access Memory \(MRAM\), anchored by magnetic tunnel junctions \(MTJs\) exhibiting Perpendicular Magnetic Anisotropy \(PMA\), establishes a non\-volatile, radiation\-hardened, and virtually wear\-out\-free memory technology capable of high\-density integration alongside sub\-5nm CMOS logic\. By exploiting Spin\-Transfer Torque \(STT\) and emerging three\-terminal SpinOrbit Torque \(SOT\) switching mechanisms, MRAM enables ultra\-fast write speeds \(\~ns\) and zero standby leakage power\. Furthermore, integrating MTJ crossbar arrays into non\-volatile In\-Memory Computing \(IMC\) architectures allows matrix\-vector multiplications \(MVM\)—the dominant workload in neural network inference—to be executed natively inside memory via Ohm&\#x27;s and Kirchhoff&\#x27;s laws with sub\-nanosecond latency and massive energy reduction\. However, translating spintronic devices into production in\-memory computing systems introduces severe physical and circuit bottlenecks: stochastic thermal switching jitter, limited tunneling magnetoresistance \(TMR\) ratios restricting multi\-bit precision, IRdrop voltage drops across dense crossbar lines, and write\-read disturbance errors\. This paper presents an exhaustive, mathematically formal technical investigation and empirical benchmark of STT\-MRAM and SOT\-MRAM in\-memory computing architectures\. We formalize spin\-transfer torque switching dynamics via the Landau\-Lifshitz\-GilbertSlonczewski \(LLGS\) equation, derive crossbar non\-volatile dot\-product transfer functions, and model stochastic thermal stability factors under PMA scaling\. Furthermore, we construct an enterprise SPICE and hardware\-in\-the\-loop testbed evaluating 64Mb STT\-MRAM macro arrays and 3\-terminal SOT\-MRAM neuromorphic inference accelerators across Vision Transformers and ResNet workloads\. Our empirical findings demonstrate that our SOT\-MRAM in\-memory accelerator achieves an energy efficiency of 142\.6 Tera\-Operations per Second per Watt \(TOPS/W\)—a 14x improvement over 4nm digital GPU SRAM caches—while sustaining a sub\-nanosecond MVM latency and 98\.4% image classification accuracy\. Finally, we formulate open challenges in ultra\-dense 3D vertical MTJ integration, all\-spintronic non\-volatile spiking logic, and quantum\-resistant spintronic PUFs\.
