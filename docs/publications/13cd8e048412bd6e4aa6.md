# Teacher Readiness for Human\-Centred AI Integration in Higher Education: Evidence from Assam, India

**Autores:** Karshika Boruah

**Data:** 2026-10-15 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Generative artificial intelligence \(GenAI\) is transforming higher education, making teacher readiness essential for responsible integration\. This paper proposes an empirical framework to examine higher education teachers’ readiness for human\-centred AI integration in Assam\. Based on UNESCO’s AI Competency Framework for Teachers, the study focuses on five dimensions: human\-centred mindset, AI ethics, AI foundations and applications, AI pedagogy, and AI\-supported professional learning, alongside institutional support\. A quantitative, cross\-sectional survey design is proposed, employing a structured questionnaire and statistical analyses to assess readiness and its influencing factors\. Addressing a regional research gap, the study emphasises ethical judgment, pedagogical competence, and human accountability rather than technological adoption alone\. As primary data collection is pending, the framework provides a basis for future empirical investigation\. The anticipated findings may inform faculty development, institutional AI policies, and responsible AI integration in higher education\.
