# Interface Rectification via Roll‐to‐Roll Dual‐Confinement Nanocoatings for High‐Temperature Polymer Capacitors

**Autores:** Ning Guo; Marina Dabaghian; Jierui Zhou; Josh N\. Bodin; Chong Xiang; Fei Wang; Antigoni C\. Konstantinou; Dingxin Wei; Yifei Wang; Chao Jun Wu; R\. Casalini; Bryan D\. Huey; Priya D\. Vashishta; Luyi Sun; Yang Cao

**Data:** 2026-10-14 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Polymer film capacitors are indispensable for energy storage and power conversion, yet their energy density and efficiency deteriorate rapidly at elevated temperatures\. At temperatures relevant to wide\-bandgap semiconductor electronics \(&gt;150°C\), heat\-resistant polymer films must be heavily derated to avoid premature aging due to intensive hot\-carrier injection\. Inspired by multilayered barrier structures in nature, we developed a scalable roll\-to\-roll self\-assembled organic\-inorganic nanolaminate coating that significantly enhances the high\-temperature dielectric performance of polymer films\. The nanolaminate architecture introduces dual confinement mechanisms: nanoconfinement of the polymer binder suppresses chain motion and enhances thermal stability, while anisotropic carrier relaxation within two\-dimensional nanoclay nanosheets impedes through\-plane hot\-carrier transport\. Together, these effects synergistically establish an interface\-rectification mechanism that promotes in\-plane energy dissipation and suppresses carrier injection at electrode\-polymer interfaces\. At 150°C, the coating significantly reduces the pre\-breakdown conduction current of polyetherimide \(PEI\), increases the breakdown strength from 450 to 501 MV/m, and improves the discharged energy density at 95% efficiency by 105%\. Furthermore, roll\-to\-roll\-coated PEI capacitors exhibit a 45\-fold lifetime enhancement at 150°C and 180 MV/m\-a design field previously sustained only by BOPP below 60°C\. This work provides a scalable and technology\-ready strategy for high\-temperature, high\-density polymer capacitive energy storage\.
