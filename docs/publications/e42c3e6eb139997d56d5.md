# Donor\-wise metabolomic prediction of platelet storage duration: a proof\-of\-concept study: Donor\-wise metabolomic prediction in platelets

**Autores:** Bin Zhang; Ming Gao

**Data:** 2026-10-15 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Background \- Whether the metabolomic profile of stored platelets can predict storage duration across donors under donor\-wise validation is unknown\. We assessed a multi\-task learning framework\. Materials and methods \- Utilizing the public metabolomic dataset ST003937 \(239 samples, 24 donors, 111 metabolites\), a dual\-task machine learning framework was constructed to predict storage duration \(Task A\) and biotinylation status \(Task B\)\. Models were evaluated by donor\-wise 5\-fold cross\-validation with random\-split comparison\. Inter\-donor distances were quantified and SHAP \(SHapley Additive exPlanations\) identified key metabolites\. Multi\-task learning was also performed\. Results \- Task A failed to generalize, with a mean area under the curve \(AUC\) of 0\.46, improving to 0\.59 under random split, whereas Task B was robust \(AUC 0\.77\)\. Median inter\-donor distance \(6\.35\) far exceeded the storage group distance \(1\.73\)\. Multi\-task learning marginally improved Task A by 0\.03\. SHAP and Gini importance identified metabolites related to energy metabolism and oxidative stress as key features for Task B\. Discussion \- Inter\-donor metabolic variability exceeded storage\-induced changes in magnitude, explaining why cross\-donor prediction of storage duration was unsuccessful despite documented group\-level metabolic differences\. The robust AUC gap between validation strategies confirms a &quot;donor fingerprint&quot; effect\. Donor\-specific baselines may be useful for future assessment of platelet quality\. Multi\-task learning provided marginal improvement\. Task B&\#x27;s robust performance validates the analytical framework\.
