# Sunset long shadows: daylight and safety perception

**Autores:** Pavel Jelnov

**Data:** 2026-10-09 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

This study is among the first to examine the long\-run effects of daylight on fear of crime and related behaviors\. The empirical strategy exploits a natural experiment created by forty years of reforms that shifted the boundaries of Russia&\#x27;s eleven time zones\. Unlike daylight saving time transitions, these changes are persistent, allowing for identification of their long\-run impacts\. Using longitudinal survey data, I show that the daylight regime—even after it changes—has a lasting effect on women&\#x27;s feelings of safety, whereas the effect on men is considerably shorter\-lived\. I further document behavioral effects—an immediate shift in utilitarian walking time and a medium\-run adjustment in sleep patterns\.
