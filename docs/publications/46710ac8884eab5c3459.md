# Ai\-powered Chess Analysis System for Skill Development: a Case Study Aligned with the Sustainable Development Goals

**Autores:** DINESH MUNIRETHINAM; DURGA P

**Data:** 2026-10-11 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Chess players often face difficulties in identifying their mistakes, understanding positional weaknesses, and improving their playing skills through traditional training methods\. This research proposes an AI\-powered chess analysis system that uses chess engine evaluations and game analysis to identify errors, assess player performance, and recommend personalised training activities\. The study also explores how AI\-based chess learning can support Sustainable Development Goal 4 \(Quality Education\) by promoting accessible, personalised, and technology\-driven learning\. The proposed system aims to provide a structured approach to chess skill development through data\-driven feedback and targeted practice\.
