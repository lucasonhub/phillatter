# Context\-Dependent Complex Sentiment Analysis of Social Media Brand Design and Marketing Comments Based on Natural Language Processing

**Autores:** Huijuan Wang; Xinghao Fu; Siyuan Qin; Cheng Zhao

**Data:** 2026-10-13 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Social media comments provide timely and publicly observable feedback for brand marketing, yet their sentiment expressions are often short, informal, context\-dependent, and semantically complex\. Conventional polarity classification is therefore insufficient for detecting hidden dissatisfaction, sarcasm, and aspect\-specific brand problems\. To address this limitation, this study proposes LACoS\-BERT\-CRF, a large language model\-assisted framework for complex sentiment analysis in social media brand comments\. The framework integrates contextual sentiment classification, complex sentiment modeling, and CRF\-based aspect\-opinion extraction to support both affective judgment and interpretable brand feedback diagnosis\. Experiments were conducted on four public datasets: Weibo Senti 100K, ToSarcasm, DiaASQ, and Amazon Dog Food Reviews\. On Weibo Senti 100K, Chinese RoBERTa\-wwm\-ext achieved the best overall sentiment classification performance, with an Accuracy of 0\.9781 and a Macro\-F1 of 0\.9781\.
