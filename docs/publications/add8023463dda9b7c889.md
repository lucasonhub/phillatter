# EQD2\-adjusted mean lung dose and symptomatic pneumonitis after thoracic radiotherapy following immune checkpoint inhibitors in lung cancer: a single\-center retrospective cohort study

**Autores:** Xiqing Li; Libin Liu; Nuo Zheng; Nila Wang; Tingxiong Xiao; Xiaolong Lin; Haidun Chen; Qin Shi; Yuyin Le

**Data:** 2026-10-09 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Background Evidence on pneumonitis after thoracic radiotherapy following immune checkpoint inhibitor \(ICI\) exposure remains limited\. We examined associations with voxel\-wise EQD2 mean lung dose \(MLD EQD2 \) and the ICI–radiotherapy interval\. Methods This single\-center retrospective cohort included 98 patients in a 90\-day competing\-risk analysis of grade ≥2 radiation pneumonitis \(CTCAE\), comprising 26 pneumonitis events, 70 event\-free observations, and two deaths before pneumonitis\. Archived DICOM doses were converted voxel\-wise using α/β = 3 Gy\. Cumulative incidence was estimated using the Aalen–Johansen method, and Fine–Gray regression included continuous MLD EQD2 and treatment interval\. Supplementary logistic analyses included 96 patients\. Results The 90\-day pneumonitis cumulative incidence was 26\.5% \(95% CI 17\.7%–35\.3%\)\. The adjusted subdistribution hazard ratio \(SHR\) for MLD EQD2 was 1\.339 per Gy \(95% CI 1\.113–1\.612; P = 0\.002\)\. The interval SHR was 0\.784 per 10 days \(95% CI 0\.639–0\.963; P = 0\.020\)\. Physical MLD showed a consistent association \(SHR 1\.197 per Gy, 95% CI 1\.036–1\.382; P = 0\.015\)\. Conclusion Higher MLD EQD2 and shorter treatment intervals were associated with grade ≥2 symptomatic pneumonitis in this treatment sequence\. Clinical ascertainment and dose\-reconstruction uncertainties limit interpretation\. The absence of an ICI\-naive comparator precludes conclusions about ICI\-specific radiosensitization\. Prospective validation is required before these associations can inform dose constraints or treatment scheduling\.
