# INFORMATION AND ANALYTICAL SUPPORT OF INNOVATIVE MANAGEMENT: DATA ANALYSIS METHODS AND MODERN DIGITAL TECHNOLOGIES

**Autores:** Тамара Качала; Sergiy Dotsenko; Mykhailo Maksymov

**Data:** 2026-10-09 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

The article addresses the development of information and analytical support for innovation management under conditions of digital transformation, rapid technological change and growing uncertainty in managerial decision\-making\. The relevance of the study is determined by the need to move from fragmented use of data and separate digital tools toward an integrated system capable of transforming heterogeneous information into timely analytical results for innovation decisions\. The purpose of the article is to substantiate the theoretical and methodological foundations of information and analytical support for innovation management, systematize modern data analysis methods and digital technologies, and determine their applicability at different stages of the innovation process\. The study applies analysis and synthesis, comparison, grouping and classification, structural and functional analysis, scientific abstraction and modeling\. These methods made it possible to structure the components of information and analytical support, classify analytical approaches and identify the role of digital technologies in decision\-making\. The results show that information and analytical support should be considered as an integrated management system combining information needs, relevant data, descriptive, diagnostic, predictive and prescriptive analytics, digital technologies, analytical outputs, managerial decisions and feedback\. The capabilities of Business Intelligence, Big Data, machine learning, artificial intelligence, generative artificial intelligence, cloud technologies and data visualization tools are systematized in relation to innovation management tasks\. A matrix matching data analysis methods and digital technologies to the main stages of the innovation process is proposed\. The study also substantiates a sequential logic in which an information need determines the required data, the data determine the analytical method, the method determines the digital technology, and the analytical result is transformed into a managerial decision and updated through feedback\.
