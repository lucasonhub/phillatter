# Fujie, Naoto: Is Operational\-Shape Theory a Transcendental Nuance\-Operational Vocabulary? What Kind of Philosophical Inquiry Is It? Heterogeneous Operational Rearticulation, the Generation and Dissolution of Conditions, Real Constraints, and Non\-Closure Realism

## Descrição do feed

\_Zenodo\_ 1\. 2026This paper examines whether Operational\-Shape Theory can be characterized as a transcendental nuance\-operational vocabulary and what kind of philosophical inquiry it constitutes\. It distinguishes nuance\-operational vocabulary, the Nuance\-Operational Model, and Operational\-Shape Realism without treating operational shapes as fundamental constituents of reality\. The inquiry reconstructs heterogeneous transformations across real changes, conditions of discrimination, and descriptive rearticulation, including their ignition, coupling, division, modulation, retention, dissolution, and historical re\-entry\. Drawing on Kant, Husserl, Sellars, Brassier, Bergson, Simondon, and Foucault, the paper examines the conditions under which transcendental terminology performs non\-redundant explanatory work\. Particular attention is given to Sellarsian picturing, nonconceptual causal organization, conceptual justification, scientific realism, and rational revision\. A hypothetical autonomous scientific arrangement illustrates the distinctions among minimal signs, configuration\-dependent ignition conditions, real effects, real penetration, and changes in subsequent possibilities\. The paper argues that Operational\-Shape Theory can function as a transcendental inquiry in restricted contexts, but transcendental terminology is neither universally necessary nor constitutive of the theory\. Its philosophical role consists in examining the formation, transformation, and possible dissolution of its own conceptual distinctions while remaining constrained by reality\. Neither philosophical mediation nor operational vocabulary is a necessary passage point for scientific operations, and reality\-as\-a\-whole is not exhausted by any such description\. \(direct link \)
