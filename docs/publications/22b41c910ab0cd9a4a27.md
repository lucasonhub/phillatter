# A Protocol for Developing Q\-SOFT Instructional Design: Process, Components, and Implementation Guidelines

**Autores:** Hanife Esen Aygün; Şerife Gonca Zeren; Mehmet Aşıkcan; Tuğçe Suskan

**Data:** 2026-10-14 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Soft skills refer to the skills that support individuals’ functioning in interpersonal, emotional, and cognitive domains\. They play a determining role in both academic achievement and social adjustment, as well as subjective well\-being and life satisfaction\. This study presents the process of determining, preparing, and creating the implementation guideline of the components of the Q\-SOFT instructional design, which was developed based on the ADDIE instructional design model and the Quantum Learning approach to improve the soft skills of primary school students\. Accordingly, the process consists of the stages of analysis, design, development, implementation, and evaluation\. The Q\-SOFT instructional design aims to holistically support primary school students’ interpersonal, emotional, and cognitive skills\. In the instructional design, the six\-stage cycle of the quantum\-based learning model, namely enroll, experience, label, demonstrate, review, and celebrate, was followed\. A structured implementation process consisting of 10 weeks and 20 hours was developed within the framework of a protocol approach including different teaching methods and techniques, as well as teacher and family training\. Within the scope of this study, the Q\-SOFT instructional design is planned to support primary school students&\#x27; soft skills in a systematic and planned manner\.
