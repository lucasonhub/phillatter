# Lvs, D\.: Metabolic Cognition: The Principle of Metabolic Energy

**Autores:** 

**Data de descoberta:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** unknown

## Descrição do feed

The second volume of the Metabolic Weather series develops the concept of metabolic climate into a cognitive framework, describing cognition as an energetic exchange between attention and environment\. The work introduces the principle of metabolic energy, in which energy is not consumed but sustained through phase coherence\. The book presents the Calibrated Generator of Metabolic Climate \(CGMC\) — a prototype applying ΦR and λₐ equations at an engineering level — with pilot experiments showing up to 65% efficiency improvement through dynamic phase alignment\. The final part outlines the A₀Module, a self\-regulating architecture of synthetic consciousness demonstrating equilibrium recovery within ≤3 cycles\. This work transforms theoretical metaphors into reproducible engineering protocols, bridging cognition, thermodynamics, and system stability\. \(c\) LvsD, Independent Researcher, 2025\. \(direct link \)
