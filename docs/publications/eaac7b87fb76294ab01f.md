# Reinforcement Learning for Energy\-Constrained Edge IoT in Smart Cities: A Systematic Review and Research Roadmap

**Autores:** Dan\-Abia Dan; Udofia Kingsley; Udofia Dr\. Kufre

**Data:** 2026-10-14 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Smart city infrastructure relies on battery\-backed, energy\-harvesting edge IoT nodes for applications such as street lighting, environmental sensing, and traffic monitoring\. Managing energy on such nodes is a sequential decision problem under stochastic harvesting, time\-varying demand, and strict hardware limits\. Reinforcement learning \(RL\) has become a promising alternative to hand\-coded and common model\-based power management systems, yet the literature remains fragmented across sensor networks, communications, energy systems, and embedded machine learning\. This paper presents a structured review of RL for energy\-constrained edge IoT control in smart cities\. A review protocol is defined, a taxonomy of RL methods organized by edge deployability is proposed, and applications are synthesized across energy\-harvesting power management, smart lighting, traffic and environmental sensing, and communication control\. A layered reference architecture for on\-device RL control is then proposed, combining compact feature engineering, resource\-matched policies, a safety supervisor, and a fleet\-level learning loop, and is instantiated for a solar street\-lighting node\. Guidance is given on environment modeling, training methodology, hardware deployment, and security, and RL is compared with non\-RL control\. Finally, open challenges are identified, including sample efficiency, nonstationarity, on\-device learning cost, safety, and benchmarking, and a prioritized research roadmap is outlined\.
