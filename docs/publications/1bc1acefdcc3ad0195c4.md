# Enhancing clinical insight through business intelligence over OpenEHR\-structured data

**Autores:** João Cunha; Ricardo Duarte; Paulo Henriques; Tiago André Saraiva Guimarães; Manuel Filipe Santos

**Data:** 2026-10-12 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Purpose This study presents a modular and scalable functional architecture for the standardisation, processing and analysis of clinical data in hospital environments\. The aim is to bridge the gap between OpenEHR modelling and its near\-real\-time analytical usability for clinical decision support and institutional auditing\. Design/methodology/approach The Design Science Research methodology was employed in the design and validation of the proposed artefact\. The solution is organised into three integrated layers: \(1\) Clinical Data, in which records are captured in the hospital information system and stored in Oracle as the authoritative transactional source, \(2\) Data Processing, supported by a Python\-based ETL micro\-batch pipeline that extracts, validates, and semantically transforms new or updated OpenEHR\-structured records and \(3\) Data Analysis, with scalable NoSQL storage \(MongoDB\) serving as the analytical store, and a Flask\-based presentation layer that provides interactive dashboards and multidimensional visualisations\. Findings The architecture supported end\-to\-end semantic consistency, preserving data integrity through near\-real\-time micro\-batch processing and providing useful analytical artefacts to support decision\-making and auditing\. The semantic middleware helped mitigate the effects of OpenEHR model versioning, while the combination of Python\-based ETL processes with MongoDB favoured scalability and flexibility\. Although evaluated within a single\-site context, the findings suggest that the proposed architecture may be transferable to other institutional scenarios\. Originality/value This work provides an integrated perspective combining semantic rigour, programmatic processing, and analytical capacity within a modular and scalable framework\. It contributes to data\-driven digital transformation in healthcare by reinforcing interoperability, near\-real\-time analytical refresh, and alignment between semantic models and analytical dimensions\.
