# Freeman, N\. Will: Discursive Mindedness and the Symbolic Triad: Warrant for Treating Systems as Reason Participants

**Autores:** 

**Data de descoberta:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** unknown

## Descrição do feed

This essay develops criteria for warranting the treatment of an artificial system as a participant in the space of reasons, with public answerability as its central target, while bracketing phenomenal consciousness as a separate issue\. Drawing on Block’s distinction between phenomenal and access consciousness, and on Sellarsian and Brandomian inferentialism, I define discursive mindedness as the publicly attributable profile of a reason\-responsive system whose commitments, inferences, and revisions can be followed across time\. I develop and defend a symbolic triad as a defeasible necessary profile for warranted attribution of machine discursive mindedness: interpretive embedding, by which inferential content remains stable across meaning\-preserving variation and defeat; reflexive situatedness, by which present outputs are located within a diachronic history of commitments; and internal normativity, by which revision proceeds under constraints that retain authority under conflict and pressure\. These capacities support robust warrant under adversarial, open\-ended interaction\. The essay develops operational anchors and minimal falsifiers for each dimension, and addresses worries concerning conceptual circularity, the public character of normativity, and shallow compliance under optimization pressure\. The resulting framework makes the attribution of artificial reason\-participation explicit, testable, and defeasible, while reframing the AI debate as a question of when public practices of challenge, uptake, repair, and scorekeeping entitle us to treat a machine as a discursive interlocutor\. \(direct link \)
