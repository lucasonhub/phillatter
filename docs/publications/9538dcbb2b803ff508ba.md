# Analysis of the spatiotemporal evolution, convergence trends, and influencing factors of ecological efficiency in Chinese cities

**Autores:** Tonghui Li; Wei Gao; Qiang Li; Xiangjin Li

**Data:** 2026-10-14 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Understanding the spatiotemporal evolution, convergence trends, and influencing factors of ecological efficiency in Chinese cities is crucial for achieving coordinated development between socioeconomic progress and the ecological environment\. This paper assesses the ecological efficiency level of 279 prefecture\-level cities in China by adopting the super\-efficient EBM model\. This research explores the convergence pattern of urban ecological efficiency across China from 2010 to 2023 using a spatial convergence model and examines factors associated with urban ecological efficiency using OLS and GTWR models\. \(1\) This study reveals that between 2010 and 2023, urban ecological efficiency generally increased with fluctuations, accelerating post\-2016\. From 2020 to 2023, there was a significant improvement in overall efficiency; however, provinces such as Shandong, Henan, and Shanxi exhibited efficiency collapse zones\. \(2\) The results provide no evidence of overall σ\-convergence in China’s urban ecological efficiency during the study period\. indicating obvious spatial absolute β convergence and spatial conditional β convergence, with corresponding convergence speeds of 0\.0410 and 0\.0419\. Urban ecological efficiency converges toward a steady state when accounting for Level of Foreign Openness, Industrial Structure, Local Government Economic Influence, and Environmental Regulation Level\. \(3\) Between 2010 and 2023, the factors associated with urban ecological efficiency became increasingly differentiated and multi\-dimensional over time\. In terms of spatial distribution, it developed toward regional balance, and the approach shifted from arough model to a precise model\. By measuring regional disparities in urban ecological efficiency, this study delineates the convergence characteristics of urban ecological efficiency and identifies its influencing factors, providing a basis for analyzing the spatial patterns and determinants of urban ecological efficiency\. It offers policy insights for promoting coordinated high\-quality development of cities in China\.
