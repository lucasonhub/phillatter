# PEAS\-DeiT: Multi\-Scale Spatial Attention Enhanced DeiT for Pea Leaf Disease Classification

**Autores:** Fatma Ozge Ozkok

**Data:** 2026-10-13 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Early and accurate detection of pea diseases is essential for reducing crop losses and supporting precision agriculture\. Although deep learning methods have shown good performance in plant disease classification, CNN\-based models may have limitations in learning long\-range relationships and detailed disease features\. In this study, an explainable transformer\-based framework, named PEAS\-DeiT, is proposed for automated pea leaf disease classification\. The proposed approach integrates a multi\-scale spatial attention mechanism into the Data\-efficient Image Transformer \(DeiT\) architecture to enhance the representation of disease\-related visual features\. A unified pea leaf disease dataset \(UPLDD\) was constructed by combining multiple publicly available datasets, covering six classes, including five disease categories and healthy samples\. The proposed model was evaluated against CNN\-based and transformer\-based architectures using five\-fold cross\-validation\. Furthermore, explainable artificial intelligence \(XAI\) analysis was performed to investigate the regions contributing to model predictions\. Experimental results demonstrate that PEAS\-DeiT achieved an accuracy of 93\.11%, showing competitive performance compared with the evaluated CNN and transformer\-based models\. The gradient\-based saliency analysis also revealed localized response patterns in several evaluated samples, providing qualitative insight into the image regions contributing to model predictions\. Statistical analyses were conducted to evaluate the performance differences among the compared models\. These findings indicate that PEAS\-DeiT provides a competitive approach for pea leaf disease classification within the evaluated experimental setting\.
