# AI Readiness and Logistics Timeliness: A Multiple Linear Regression Analysis with a Time\-Lag Model in 114 Countries

**Autores:** Valijon Xudoyberdiyev

**Data:** 2026-10-14 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Research design and empirical approach\. The study investigates artificial intelligence technologies through quantitative measurements which determine their effects on logistics processes across entire systems\. The research design used to achieve these objective required researchers to analyze data from a specific time period\. The Multiple Linear Regression method of econometric analysis was used to study 114 countries at different stages of economic development and their relationship with macroeconomic indicators\.
