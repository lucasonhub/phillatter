# BEYOND AUTOMATION: REFRAMING ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE IN EDUCATION THROUGH COGNITIVE AUTONOMY, ADAPTIVE LEARNING, AND HUMAN–AI COLLABORATION

**Autores:** Rafiqova Sanobar

**Data:** 2026-10-12 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

The integration of artificial intelligence \(AI\) into education is transforming instructional practices, assessment methods, and the relationship between teachers and learners\. However, the educational value of AI cannot be determined solely by its ability to automate tasks or generate information\. This paper examines AI\-supported education through three interconnected dimensions: cognitive autonomy, adaptive learning pathways, and human–AI collaboration\. Drawing on international research and educational policy frameworks, the study adopts a conceptual and analytical approach to identify opportunities, challenges, and principles for responsible AI integration\. Particular attention is given to students&\#x27; independent reasoning, critical evaluation of AI\-generated content, personalized instruction, and teachers&\#x27; professional judgement\. The paper proposes a conceptual model, termed the Attempt–Assist–Evaluate–Transfer model, which encourages learners to attempt tasks independently, receive targeted AI support, evaluate generated information, and transfer their knowledge to unfamiliar situations without assistance\. The analysis suggests that effective AI integration requires more than technological access; it requires pedagogical planning, ethical safeguards, and assessment strategies that prioritize genuine learning\. The paper concludes that AI should function as a supportive educational resource rather than a substitute for human reasoning or professional teaching\.
