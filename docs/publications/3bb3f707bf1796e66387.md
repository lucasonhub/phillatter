# Global Leadership in the World of AI:

**Autores:** Danielle Badgett; Michael King

**Data:** 2026-10-14 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Background: This poster offers a practical approach to global leadership when introducing artificial intelligence \(AI\) into culturally diverse organizations\. Methods/Approach: The approach draws from leadership theory, emerging research on AI in the workplace, and cross\-cultural communication theory\. Through this research, The Ethical AI Competency Model and AI Implementation Protocol were developed\. Objectives: The Ethical AI Competency Model highlights six interconnected competencies: Cultural intelligence, Ethical AI Stewardship, Sensemaking and Strategy, Human\-Oriented AI Application, Cross\-Cultural Communication, and AI Literacy \(Chee et al\., 2025; Hagerty &amp; Rubnov, 2019; Oh et al\., 2026; Passlack et al\., 2026; Yang et al\., 2025\)\. Results: Grounded in this competency model, the AI Implementation Protocol offers a seven\-step protocol to support responsible and culturally inclusive AI adoption\. Conclusions: At its core, the framework argues that successful AI implementation within global leadership necessitates human\-centered values, organizational transparency, and the willingness and knowledge to adapt across cultures \(Pillai, 2024; Rezaei et al\., 2024\)\.
