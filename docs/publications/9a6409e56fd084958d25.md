# Sami, Zawwar: What Development Evidence Can and Cannot Show\. AAG, EMALS and model\-dependent engineering tests\. W2

**Autores:** 

**Data de descoberta:** 2026-10-19 · **Tipo:** unknown

## Descrição do feed

A successful demonstration does not establish dependable service\. A failed test does not establish a limit of physics\. I examine this distinction through documented AAG aircraft\-recovery and EMALS launch\-system milestones\. Four worked analyses show why operation counts alone cannot establish peak loads, reliability confidence, failure mechanisms or performance after configuration changes\. The most instructive example concerns test design: a contrast that separates two explanations can leave others indistinguishable when interactions are allowed\. Exact calculations also show how assumed count variability changes the repetitions needed for a specified decision\. These are case\-based analytical results and synthetic computations, not carrier measurements\. The practical contribution is a traceable assessment sequence for choosing the next useful record, measurement or test\. \(direct link \)
