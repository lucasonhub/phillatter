# DEMOCRATIC ACCOUNTABILITY ON PAPER AND IN PRACTICE: A SCOPING REVIEW OF EU AND US ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE POLICY, WITH A CONCLUDING WORD FOR INDIA

**Autores:** Mangal Deo; Medhavi Gupta

**Data:** 2026-10-17 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

The artificial intelligence policy secures democratic accountability has become almost a ritual opening line in both Brussels and Washington, and it is a claim this paper has set out to test rather than repeat\. Through a scoping review of the primary legal instruments in force between 2023 and 2026, together with the peer\-reviewed and specialist\-professional commentary written on them, democratic accountability has been operationalised along three variables: binding regulatory red lines, transparency in AI\-enabled political campaigning, and the robustness of public oversight institutions once a system is already in the field\. The European Union, it is found, has legislated all three dimensions in explicit statutory language — and has, in the same breath, generated a literature preoccupied with the gap between what Parliament authorised and what administration has actually delivered\. The United States has moved the other way, from a voluntary risk\-management posture toward a strategy of executive\-led federal preemption that several scholars read not as deregulation but as a redistribution of who gets to regulate\. Neither trajectory, the paper argues, offers India a template that can simply be imported; each offers a caution worth absorbing before, rather than after, India&\#x27;s own institutions harden into their final shape\.
