# Molecular Conformation as a Determinant of Self\-Assembly in Sequence\-Isomeric Tripeptides

**Autores:** Zhuojun Li; Rein V\. Ulijn; Hamish W\. A\. Swanson

**Data:** 2026-10-13 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Abstract In this work, we evaluate whether computed conformational ensembles of seven tripeptides─the six C\-terminal amidated D/Y/F sequence isomers and the closely related sequence FFD─are informative of their self\-assembly propensity\. Using all\-atom molecular dynamics \(AA\-MD\) simulations and intramolecular side\-chain dihedral angles as a collective variable, we calculate conformational free\-energy landscapes \(FELs\) for these sequences\. We then introduce a novel method to numerically characterize the computed FELs and find a correlation between thermal conformational accessibility and a sequence’s tendency to self\-assemble or remain in solution\. In addition, we evaluate and rationalize the impact of water model selection on conformational ensembles in terms of peptide\-water interaction strengths, and also investigate the influence of ionization state on conformation\. The presented methodology implicitly accounts for amino acid sequence context, which is a major determinant of divergent properties for sequence isomers\. It also has a low associated computational cost, making use of single\-molecule simulations to predict supramolecular assembly outcomes\. These features are an important advancement toward a general scheme for the theory\-informed design of peptide materials\.
