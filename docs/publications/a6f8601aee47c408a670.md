# Democratizing 3D indoor data capture: Smartphone\-based ToF and structure from motion integration using iterative closest point

**Autores:** mohammad amooshahi; asghar milan; Alireza Sharifi

**Data:** 2026-10-13 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

This study presents a workflow that integrates an iPhone’s Time\-of\-Flight \(ToF\) data with Structure from Motion \(SfM\) photogrammetry through ICP \(Iterative Closest Point\)‑based scale transfer, enabling accurate, without control point 3D interior scanning\. Validation in four rooms showed ToF\-only errors exceeded 40 cm and were reduced to 10–20 cm with a gimbal\. The integrated ToF\-SfM approach further improved accuracy, achieving deviations of under 5 cm and a 2D floor plan RMSE \(Root Mean Square Error\) of 2–2\.5 cm\. This portable, cost\-effective solution enhances ToF accuracy using SfM geometry, enabling rapid, accurate 3D capture in the tested residential environment \(72 m2, four rooms\) without control points\.
