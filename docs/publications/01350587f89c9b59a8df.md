# The Expanding Role of Artificial Intelligence in the Justice Delivery System of India

**Autores:** Priyadarshini Rawat

**Data:** 2026-10-14 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Artificial Intelligence \(AI\) is rapidly transforming justice delivery systems worldwide\. Traditionally used to streamline case management and legal research, AI’s role now extends to complex decision\-making, dispute resolution, and improving access to justice\. This paper explores the novel and diverse applications of AI in the justice sector, examines its benefits and challenges, and discusses ethical considerations and future prospects, with a particular focus on lessons for India and other developing countries\.
