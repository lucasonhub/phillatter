# Development of a Web\-Based Management Information System for Customer Complaints and Feedback

**Autores:** Yana Suryana; Verdi Yasin; Hidayatul Ichwan

**Data:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Small distributors often handle customer complaints through instant messaging, where complaint information is produced but not retained\. Prior complaint\-management systems are reported after the transition, for institutional users or structured datasets, without a measured account of the process they replace\. This single\-case study at a heavy equipment spare part distributor in Central Jakarta measures that pre\-formalisation baseline and derives the system design and its evaluation indicators from it\. Observation, WhatsApp history review and interviews with five staff and eight customers were triangulated into a case register with fixed counting rules: from January to March 2026 it held 70 complaints, of which 24 \(34\.3%\) took more than three working days to resolve and 18 \(25\.7%\) had histories administrators could not retrieve\. A web\-based system was developed through Rapid Application Development and tested with eight black box scenarios derived by equivalence partitioning\. All scenarios produced the expected output, and each baseline problem has a corresponding capability at handover\. These are functional and process\-level results; no post\-implementation data were collected and security, boundary, concurrency and volume testing were outside scope, so no improvement in service quality is claimed\. The contribution is a measured baseline, a low\-cost design traceable from each baseline problem to a tested function, and indicators with baseline values for later evaluation\.
