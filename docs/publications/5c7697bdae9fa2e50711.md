# A cross\-domain review of artificial intelligence techniques for iot security, computer networks, image processing and smart agriculture in connected environments

**Autores:** Bashir Olaniyi Sadiq; Mohammed Dahiru Buhari; IBRAHIM ADABARA; Enerst Edozie; Asaba Shabiluh

**Data:** 2026-10-17 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

The rapid digitization of modern systems has increased the demand for intelligent, adaptive, and data\-driven solutions across connected systems\. However, these domains face persistent challenges such as heterogeneous datasets, limited labeled data, real\-time processing constraints, privacy concerns, model interpretability, and poor generalization across environments\. This review examines the application of major Artificial Intelligence \(AI\) techniques for solving classification, prediction, optimization, anomaly detection, routing, image analysis, and decision\-support problems\. The empirical patterns across the literature are summarized cautiously across studies\. General lessons that emerged across the reviewed domains were also presented\. Overall, the review provides researchers with a concise foundation for understanding current AI methods, their practical limitations, and future research directions in the aspect of connected environments\. This article has tried to clarify various aspects and overall issues related to the use of AI techniques in computer networks, image processing and computer vision, smart agriculture and related intelligent systems\. The authors believe that this is a comprehensive and integrated survey of these AI techniques in a number of application domains, with an emphasis on their practical suitability, their current empirical limitations, and future research directions\. Thus, the article is informative to researchers in the field and may be a guide to future research on the development of robust, interpretable, scalable, and context\-aware AI\-based connected systems\.
