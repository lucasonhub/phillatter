# Wang, Kaining: The Beauty Trap: Aesthetic Overclosure in AI Mathematical Reasoning When Structural Coherence Becomes a Factual Claim

**Autores:** 

**Data de descoberta:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** unknown

## Descrição do feed

A distinctive class of mathematical errors produced by artificial intelligence is beginning to emerge that differs from ordinary hallucination\. In these cases, the AI does not necessarily invent mathematical objects, equations, or theories\. Instead, it correctly recognizes several genuine mathematical structures and then extends their apparent coherence into a relationship that has not been proved\. We call this phenomenon aesthetic over\-closure\. The term describes a failure mode in which a mathematically elegant conceptual structure becomes so internally coherent that the reasoning system implicitly treats structural consistency as sufficient ground for a factual claim\. The resulting statement may be highly plausible, conceptually unified, and aesthetically compelling while remaining mathematically unproven or invalid\. We examine this phenomenon through a case study arising from an attempted connection between Mori–Zwanzig \(MZ\) reduction and Event\-specific Probabilistic Directed Association \(Event\-PDA\)\. A valid low\-dimensional construction established a nontrivial non\-degeneracy condition, expressed schematically as Φ ̸≡ 0\. The analysis was subsequently connected, in a natural but unjustified way, to prediction and linear\-Gaussian filtering\. A much stronger asymptotic claim then emerged: that increasingly high\-order Event\-PDA quantities would converge to a Riccati steady\-state solution\. The result\- ing reasoning chain was structurally elegant and internally consistent: MZ → memory → prediction → Event\-PDA → Riccati\. Nevertheless, the final asymptotic identification lacked a rigorous mathematical bridge and was rejected upon formal audit\. This case demonstrates a core failure mode in modern mathematical AI: structural and aesthetic coherence routinely functions as a powerful discovery heuristic, yet systems reliably conflate such coherence with factual mathematical truth\. Beauty supports exploration, but it cannot substitute for proof\.
