# SMART SURGICAL SCHEDULING: AN AI\-ENABLED FRAMEWORK FOR OPERATING ROOM EFFICIENCY AND QUALITY OF CARE

**Autores:** Mohammed Mudassir; Bater Ibrahim khan; Mudassir Uddin Ahmed; Mohammed Mushtaq

**Data:** 2026-10-15 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

The scheduling of surgical procedures is a vital and intricate aspect of hospital operations, significantly impacting patient outcomes, financial success, and clinician welfare\.Traditional manual scheduling techniques are inadequate for managing the dynamic and unpredictable characteristics of the operating room \(OR\) environment, resulting in staff fatigue, increased overtime expenses, and a decline in care quality\.This study presents a comprehensive artificial intelligence \(AI\) framework that utilises predictive analytics, multi\-objective optimisation, and natural language processing to create dynamic, efficient, and equitable surgical schedules\.The technology utilises a continuous feedback loop, assimilating historical and real\-time data to optimise operational efficiency while considering human aspects\.Conceptual analysis indicates Smart Surgical Scheduling: An AI\-Enabled Framework for Operating Room Efficiency and Quality of Care https://iaeme\.com/Home/journal/IJCET
