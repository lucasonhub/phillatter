# SimOPD: Relational Knowledge Transfer for On\-Policy Distillation

**Autores:** Kai Tang; Xing Zheng; Haobo Wang; Guangcheng Zhu; Shenzhi Yang; Zhengqing Zang; Yingfan Ma; Bowen Song; Weiqiang Wang

**Data:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** preprint

## Resumo

A key challenge in on\-policy distillation \(OPD\) is extracting effective supervision from teacher knowledge\. Supervision from token probabilities may underutilize internal knowledge, while direct hidden\-state matching forces the student to shift to the teacher&\#x27;s feature space and complicates cross\-architecture transfer\. Inspired by contrastive learning&\#x27;s use of relationships between instances, we propose \\textbf\{SimOPD\} to transfer teacher internal knowledge through response similarities\. Specifically, SimOPD uses paired memory banks of student\-generated responses to extract supervision from the teacher&\#x27;s internal representations as similarity distributions\. The student then matches these distributions within its own representation space, retaining representational freedom and removing the need for cross\-model projection\. Extensive experiments show that SimOPD improves the overall average over the strongest baselines by up to 2\.6 and 3\.1 percentage points in same\- and cross\-architecture settings\.
