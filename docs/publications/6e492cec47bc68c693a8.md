# Adaptive Leader Development by Black Women Students in Medical School

**Autores:** Mark Anthony Torrez; Darren E\. Pierre; Antonio Durán; Sydney Torres; Ashley F\. Curtis

**Data:** 2026-10-15 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Black women continue to be underrepresented in leadership theory, including within scholarship on leadership in higher education\. What does exist typically centers on undergraduate student leaders, overlooking the realities that Black women students in graduate/professional programs face\. In response, this project placed critical perspectives on leadership theory in conversation with the testimonies of eight Black women medical students to understand how they define, manage, and perform their leadership identities\. Findings revealed how these participants viewed environmental threats that framed their leader development, how they managed identity perceptions through their performance, and how they committed to continual learning regarding their leadership\. Informed by their perspectives, we offer implications for practice, research, and leadership theory\.
