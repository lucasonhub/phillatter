# Generation of a CRISPR/Cas9\-corrected isogenic control iPSC line \(NIMHi001\-A\-1\) from a Parkinson’s disease Patient\-Derived iPSC line carrying the LRRK2 I1371V mutation

**Autores:** Roon Banerjee; Khushboo Singh; Ujaswini Gogoi; Vedam Ramprasad; Gunisha Pasricha; Jiny Nair; Riyanka Kumari; Ravi Yadav; Nitish L\. Kamble; VikramVenkayappa Holla; Pramod Kumar Pal; Indrani Datta

**Data:** 2026-10-09 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

We report NIMHi001\-A\-1, a CRISPR/Cas9\-corrected isogenic iPSC line derived from a patient line carrying a heterozygous LRRK2 missense mutation \(chr12:40702420A&gt;G; p\.Ile1371Val\) associated with familial Parkinson&\#x27;s disease\. Sanger sequencing confirmed successful correction\. The line retained iPSC morphology and expressed pluripotency markers \(OCT4, NANOG, SOX2, TRA\-1\-60, SSEA4\) by immunofluorescence, gene expression analysis, and flow cytometry\. Normal karyotype \(46, XY\) and complete genetic identity with the parental line were established by STR profiling\. No off\-target modifications were detected\. The line demonstrated trilineage differentiation potential and tested mycoplasma\-negative, providing an isogenic platform to investigate LRRK2 I1371V\-specific pathomechanisms and evaluate therapeutic strategies\.
