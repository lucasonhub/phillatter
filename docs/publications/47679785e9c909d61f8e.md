# Application of Artificial Intelligence in Agriculture

**Autores:** Shailendra Bhalawe; Rishikesh Thakur; Ajay Singh Lodhi; Atul Shrivastav; Surendra Rai

**Data:** 2026-10-15 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Abstract: Climate change, population growth, and increasing pressure on natural resources pose major challenges to global agricultural production\. Artificial Intelligence \(AI\) has emerged as a promising technology for addressing these challenges through precision farming, machine learning, robotic automation, and data\-driven decision\-making\. This study examines the applications of AI in crop management, real\-time monitoring, yield prediction, resource optimization, and sustainable agricultural production\. The integration of AI with advanced genetic technologies can improve productivity, resource\-use efficiency, and climate resilience\. However, challenges related to technology, infrastructure, farmer awareness, data availability, affordability, and regulatory frameworks limit its widespread adoption\. The study highlights the need for supportive policies, capacity building, digital infrastructure, and collaboration among farmers, researchers, industries, and policymakers\. Overall, AI has significant potential to transform modern agriculture, enhance food security, and promote sustainable and climate\-resilient agricultural development\.
