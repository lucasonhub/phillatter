# Bulgaria, Jamel &amp; Xǔ, Chénglán: The Decalogue of Affective Action: Comparing the Philosopher&\#x27;s Vision with the Structural\-Constructivist Architecture of the Core Emotion Framework

**Autores:** 

**Data de descoberta:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** unknown

## Descrição do feed

This paper argues that the philosopher’s ten step sequence of human action is not simply a conceptual parallel to the Core Emotion Framework \(CEF\), but an intuitive deployment of the very cognitive tools and structural operators that the CEF formalizes\. As the document states, “the CEF asserts that human emotional experiences and psychological traits are constructed from a finite, universal architecture of exactly ten irreducible primal powers,” and the philosopher’s progression—sensing, calculating, deciding, expanding, constricting, achieving, arranging, appreciating, boosting, and accepting—constitutes a direct phenomenological enactment of these operators\. The analysis shows that the philosopher is not theorizing from the outside; he is using the same computational primitives that govern the CEF’s Tri Axial System across the Head, Heart, and Gut centers\. The document further situates these operators within a ten dimensional Hilbert space, enabling the modeling of entangled emotional states such as the GoodPerson Anxiety Pattern, where “the co activation of Calculating and Constricting exhibits quantum like contextuality\.” Clinical implications are explored through the 7 Step Detangling Protocol, which restores operator independence through somatic choreography\. Empirical foundations—including pilot studies and the CFA/SEM paradox—support the framework’s structural validity\. Overall, the document demonstrates that the philosopher’s vision is not merely descriptive but is an active instantiation of the same ten operator emotional operating system that the CEF formalizes mathematically, somatically, and clinically\. \(direct link \)
