# DEEP LEARNING ENSEMBLES FOR THE DIAGNOSIS OF ENDOSCOPIC LESIONS

**Autores:** Breno Augusto Tamanini; Danilo Maia de Oliveira; Marcos Raffael Almeida Amorim; Felipe da Costa Mota; José Leal Rodrigues; Genilson Vieira Martins; João Otávio Bandeira Diniz; Luiz Otávio de Oliveira Souza Júnior

**Data:** 2026-10-15 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Gastrointestinal lesions represent a significant public health concern, where early detection reduces mortality despite the subjectivity of manual endoscopic image analysis\. This work proposes DeepEnsemble\-Endo, a weighted soft voting ensemble with weights optimized via grid search\. After evaluating 11 pre\-trained CNNs via transfer learning and selecting the top\-5 \(DenseNet121, ResNet50, InceptionV3, MobileNetV2, EfficientNetB2\), weights were systematically optimized, achieving 96\.63% accuracy and 97\.25% F1\-score \- outperforming simple ensembles from the literature and demonstrating potential as automated clinical decision support\.
