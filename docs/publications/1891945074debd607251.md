# Thickness\-dependent structural and hysteretic properties of pld\-grown VO2 thin films on glass substrate

**Autores:** Amina Zouina Ait Djafer; Slimane LAFANE; Mustapha Zaghrioui; Abdel Yazid Khereddine; Samira Abdelli\-Messaci

**Data:** 2026-10-09 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Abstract Vanadium dioxide \(VO2\), known for its insulator to metal transition \(IMT\) at approximately 68°C, has sparked technological interest owing to its potential in several applications including sensors, electro\-optic devices, and memory technologies\. This study investigates the effect of thin film thickness on the structural properties and hysteresis behavior of VO2 thin films deposited by pulsed laser deposition on corning glass substrates\. The results revealed a significant correlation between transition properties and film thickness throughout structural properties\. Crystalline VO2 begins to form at thickness of 9 nm\. With the increase of the film thickness, particularly above 83 nm, grain coalescence takes place and leads to continuous coverage of the substrate\. Structural analysis reveals that crystallinity and crystallite size improve with increasing thickness up to approximately 125 nm\. These improvements are directly reflected in the electrical properties, leading to a sharper and more symmetric IMT, a reduced hysteresis width \(down to 6 °C\), and enhanced transition sharpness \(3 °C\), together with a transition amplitude of approximately 5\.3 × 10²\. Further increasing the thickness does not produce significant additional improvements, except for changes in preferential texturing and hysteresis asymmetry\. Temperature\-dependent Raman measurements are in agreement with electrical characterization, confirming both the transition temperatures and the asymmetric behavior during cooling for 83 nm thick film, whereas this feature is no longer observed for the 280 nm suggesting that structural effects contribute to the double transition and hysteresis asymmetry in thinner films, while other mechanisms dominate as the film thickness increases\. Overall, PLD enables the growth of high\-quality VO2 films on Corning glass with excellent transition characteristics over a broad thickness range\. These results demonstrate that optimizing film thickness is a key factor for tailoring the structural quality and switching characteristics of VO2 films, providing useful guidelines for the development of high\-performance phase\-transition\-based devices\.
