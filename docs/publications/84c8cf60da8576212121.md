# Coaching for Wellbeing in Entrepreneurial Education

**Autores:** Elona N\. Ndlovu\-Hlatshwayo; Patrick Ebong Ebewo; Tendai Makwara

**Data:** 2026-10-15 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Entrepreneurship education in emerging economies often emphasises venture creation and technical competencies, with comparatively less attention to the psychological, relational, physical, environmental, and economic conditions that may shape students&\#x27; entrepreneurial learning and well\-being\. This paper develops the Coaching for Well\-being \(C4W\) conceptual framework to integrate coaching\-informed pedagogy and student well\-being within entrepreneurship education\. The framework was developed through a conceptual synthesis of relevant literature, informed in part by findings from an earlier systematic review of entrepreneurial coaching, complementary scholarship on coaching, positive psychology, well\-being and entrepreneurship education, and the authors&\#x27; practice\-informed experience in African higher education\. C4W uses the nine dimensions of PERMA\+4, Positive Emotion, Engagement, Relationships, Meaning, Accomplishment, Physical Health, Mindset, Environment, and Economic Security as its well\-being architecture, and conceptually maps these dimensions to entrepreneurship teaching and coaching\-informed activities\. These include reflection, peer coaching, goal setting, networking, business simulations, values clarification, ecosystem mapping, and financial\-resource activities\. The framework articulates the pedagogical rationale that links these activities to the PERMA\+4 dimensions and identifies the intended entrepreneurial learning outcomes\. C4W contributes a context\-sensitive framework that positions well\-being as integral to entrepreneurial learning rather than as a separate student\-support concern, with relevance to resource\-constrained higher education environments\. As a theoretically informed conceptual framework rather than an empirically validated intervention, its proposed relationships require testing and refinement across different institutional, cultural, and socioeconomic contexts\.
