# Analyzing the Effects of GDP per Working\-Age Capita on AI Usage

**Autores:** Deepali Khandelwal

**Data:** 2026-10-15 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

This paper investigates how income levels shape the adoption of artificial intelligence across countries\. Using newly available data from Anthropic&\#x27;s Economic Index alongside a range of econometric techniques, the paper explores the complex relationship between national income, labour market composition, and technological adoption\. As AI continues to transform economies worldwide, this research offers timely insights into the structural factors influencing its diffusion\.
