# THE DIGITAL EYE FOR DENTAL IMPLANTS: ENHANCING IDENTIFICATION ACCURACY USING SPOTIMPLANT SOFTWARE\-A RETROSPECTIVE STUDY

**Autores:** Dr\. Sudha rani\. P, Dr\. Lakshmana Rao\. B, Dr\. Praveen\. N, Dr\. Anupama\.CH, Dr\. Namratha\. S

**Data:** 2026-10-10 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Abstract Background: Identification of a previously placed dental implant can be challenging because of the wide variety of implant systems, designs, dimensions, and manufacturers\. Similar radiographic appearances, incomplete clinical records, and difficulty in retrieving previous treatment information may further complicate identification\. Recent advances in digital dentistry and artificial intelligence \(AI\) have introduced software\-based approaches to assist clinicians in recognizing implant systems from radiographic images\. Aim: This article aims to evaluate the accuracy and efficiency of Spotimplant software in identifying dental implants from radiographic images and to compare software\-generated identification with information available from clinical records\. Materials and Methods: A retrospective study was conducted using 20 blinded dental implant radiographs obtained from 10 dental colleges, with two radiographs from each institution\. Each radiograph was uploaded to Spotimplant software, which generated an identification report containing information regarding the implant type, trade name, and model\. These findings were compared with the actual implant details obtained from clinical records\. Statistical analysis was performed using IBM SPSS Statistics version 26, with a t\-test used to assess reliability\. Results: Spotimplant demonstrated an overall identification accuracy of 92%, with a sensitivity of 90% and specificity of 95%\. The presentation reported an accuracy of 75% for traditional identification methods\. Conclusion: Spotimplant demonstrated a high level of effectiveness in identifying dental implants from radiographic images and may facilitate faster and more reliable implant recognition, particularly when previous records are unavailable or incomplete\. Further studies with larger samples and a wider range of implant systems are required\. Keywords: Dental implants; implant identification; artificial intelligence; digital dentistry; radiography; Spotimplant\.
