# DIDACTIC POSSIBILITIES OF USING ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE TECHNOLOGIES IN MOTHER TONGUE LESSONS

**Autores:** Qurolova Mohira Adxamovna

**Data:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

This article examines the didactic possibilities of using artificial intelligence technologies in the organization of mother tongue lessons, their role in the educational process, and their influence on the development of students&\#x27; knowledge\. It analyzes the opportunities offered by artificial intelligence tools for taking individual learning needs into account, differentiating language\-related tasks, developing written and oral communication skills, and assessing learning\. It also addresses pedagogical appropriateness, the reliability of information, and the need to maintain teacher oversight when introducing these technologies into mother tongue education\. The article substantiates that the effective use of artificial intelligence can increase students&\#x27; interest in language learning and help accommodate individual differences in the learning process\.
