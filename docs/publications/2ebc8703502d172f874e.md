# Analysis of the excitation degree of J/ψ emission sources from squared momentum transfer spectra in high\-energy collisions

**Autores:** Qi Wang; Jia\-Rong Shi; You\-Long Wang

**Data:** 2026-10-14 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

This study analyzes squared momentum transfer spectra of J / ψ produced in high\-energy photon–proton \( γ p → J / ψ \+ p \) processes within electron–proton \( e p \) collisions, as measured by the GlueX, J / ψ \-007, and ZEUS Collaborations\. Monte Carlo calculations are employed in the analysis of these spectra, where the momentum transfer occurs from the incident γ to the emitted J / ψ or, equivalently, from the target proton to the emitted proton\. In the calculations, the Erlang distribution and Tsallis–Lévy function are used to describe the transverse momentum spectra of the emitted particles\. Our results indicate that, except for the first point, the average transverse momentum of the J / ψ , along with the initial\- and final\-state excitation degrees of the J / ψ emission source, roughly decrease from lower to higher photon energies and photon–proton center\-of\-mass energies\. The photon energy is approximately 10 GeV, and the photon–proton center\-of\-mass energy ranges from 50 GeV to 290 GeV\. This indicates that the excitation functions of the related parameters in e p collisions differ from those in heavy\-ion collisions within similar energy ranges\.
