# Zhang, Sulin: The Sense of Understanding: A Metacognitive Theory of Semantic Stability and Calibrated Trust

**Autores:** 

**Data de descoberta:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** unknown

## Descrição do feed

A learner can translate a sentence correctly in a language she has not fully mastered and yet feel that she does not understand it\. A confident reasoner can feel that he understands a mechanism perfectly and be demonstrably wrong\. The theory developed here takes this pair of failures as its central explanandum and argues that epistemology has largely overlooked a distinct object of theory: the sense of understanding, the metacognitive signal by which a subject registers her own grasp\. Drawing on the epistemology of understanding and on research on metacognition, fluency, and subjective confidence, the paper distinguishes understanding as an epistemic achievement from the sense of understanding as a monitoring signal, and analyses that signal along two orthogonal dimensions: the stability of the semantic space in which content is embedded, and the calibration of the trust placed in that space\. A four\-cell topology distinguishes genuine understanding, contextual guessing, untrusted mediation, and illusory understanding, and a minimal formal regimentation expresses the account in terms of posterior sharpness, an estimated channel\-reliability hyperprior, and cross\-context invariance\. The model explains insight experiences, the growth of expertise, and suspended understanding in a weakly mastered second language, and it yields a graded rather than principled verdict on machine understanding\. The contribution is a metacognitive theory of the sense of understanding, not a reductive definition of understanding itself\.
