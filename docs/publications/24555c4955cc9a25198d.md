# ICT Engagement and Knowledge Acquisition among Undergraduate Students in Lagos State University: The Impact of 4th Industrial Revolution

**Autores:** Adebola Oluwamayokun Adegoroye; Sarah Akpomiemie Gbenu; Faith Ibukunoluwa Amosun

**Data:** 2026-10-13 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

The Fourth Industrial Revolution \(4IR\) is characterised by the convergence of new technologies encompassing artificial intelligence \(AI\), robotics and the Internet of Things \(IoT\), and these innovations have permeated the everyday lives of young people globally, including in Nigeria\. This study explored the level and factors of ICT engagement and knowledge acquisition among undergraduate students in Lagos State University \(LASU\)\. A descriptive survey design was adopted\. Quantitative data was collected from 1,009 undergraduate students in the Faculties of Science and Social Sciences through an online questionnaire which was distributed through social media platforms, and the data collection spanned three weeks\. Data were analysed using descriptive statistics \(frequencies and percentages\) with IBM SPSS Statistics version 26\. The findings revealed a high level of ICT engagement in academic activities: most respondents actively used digital devices for academic purposes, expressed confidence in using diverse ICT tools, and participated in online discussions and forums\. The primary channels of knowledge acquisition combined traditional sources, notably lecture notes and print textbooks, with digital resources such as educational websites, e\-books and online databases, while reliance on the university library showed signs of decline\. Respondents overwhelmingly perceived that ICT engagement enhances their understanding of course content, broadens their knowledge beyond classroom instruction and contributes to critical thinking skills\. The study recommends comprehensive digital literacy programmes, equitable ICT infrastructure and the modernisation of library services to consolidate these gains
