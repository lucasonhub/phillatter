# Zhang, Sulin: The Unconscious Development of Thought and Art: On the Shared Genesis of Theoretical Innovation and Creative Narrative

**Autores:** 

**Data de descoberta:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** unknown

## Descrição do feed

This paper argues that both genuine theoretical innovation and enduring works of narrative art commonly arise not from forward\-looking plans but from a form of unconscious development: an internal, self\-organizing process in which dispersed elements—images, intuitions, problems, stylistic gestures—accrete over time and only later reveal themselves as a coherent system\. After defining the central terms and distinguishing this account from the engineering model of creativity, I offer conceptual tools \(autopoiesis of thought, unconscious unfolding, retrospective organic unity\), illustrative case readings from philosophy, science, and literature, and a compact three\-layer model of generation\. I then consider implications for how we evaluate creativity, teach craft, and live with creative work\. The central claim is simple but radical: to understand originality we must listen less for the heroism of rational design and more for the slow grammar of an organismal mind that composes itself from within\.
