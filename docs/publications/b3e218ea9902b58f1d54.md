# Yaratıcı işler üretken yapay zekâya devredilirken: Reklamcılıkta mesleki muhakeme

**Autores:** Mustafa Okay

**Data:** 2026-10-14 · **Tipo:** dissertation

## Resumo

This thesis examines how practitioners in advertising and related creative\-content work describe the reorganization of creative work through delegation to generative artificial intelligence \(AI\) and considers the implications for professional skill and judgment\. The interpretive qualitative study draws on fifteen semi\-structured interviews with sixteen practitioners in 2025\. Thematic analysis used a codebook informed by the literature and interviews\. Participants used AI for production, search, consultation, comparison, and preliminary checking\. They continued to interpret assignments, set direction, revise material, and decide what to deliver\. AI enabled some participants to attempt or complete work requiring more time, resources, or technical knowledge and to understand unfamiliar terms, workflows, and problems\. Preparing inputs, correcting outputs, and integrating material added work\. Using each participant’s earlier workflow for comparison, the study distinguishes what AI did, what it replaced, and what it added\. It then examines opportunities for practice, correction, guidance, and feedback in the reorganized work\. This connects research on AI\-assisted creative work with research on practice, learning, and assisted performance\. Opportunities for exploration sometimes narrowed, while other forms of practice continued and participants reported learning from AI\-generated examples\. Reported changes concerned particular capacities and varied in direction within individual interviews\. Interviews did not independently measure capacity or establish how wider professional judgment changed over time\. Most participants developed expertise before using generative AI, leaving limited evidence on newcomers’ professional formation\. Policy implications concern occupation\-specific capacity, the conditions for its use, process\-wide evaluation of AI adoption, and clear project responsibility\.
