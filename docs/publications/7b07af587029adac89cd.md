# A multimetric clustering approach for unbiased categorization of computer science journals: the framework proposed

**Autores:** Abdul Hameed; Muhammad Omar; Rahat Hussain Bokhari; Han Woo Park

**Data:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Purpose The rank of a journal is usually quantified by a single metric like Impact Factor \(IF\), CiteScore or SCImago Journal Rank \(SJR\)\. To avoid bias due to the use of a single metric, a multimetric\-based data\-centric unsupervised machine learning framework is proposed for journal categorization\. Design/methodology/approach This study integrates six bibliometric indicators as features to map the latent structure of journal impact\. The proposed framework incorporates two state\-of\-the\-art dimensionality reduction algorithms \(PCA and t\-SNE\), which also contribute to feature engineering\. Using PCA, the correlated metrics are transformed into reduced dimensions \(2D/3D\) that serve as input for k\-means clustering\. The optimal clusters \(k = 4\), aligned with IF, Scopus and SJR quartiles, are validated through internal and external evaluation measures\. Findings Multimetric\-based clustering of 4,146 computer science \(CS\) journals is compared with established single\-metric approaches \(SJR, IF and Scopus quartiles\) and with HJRS&\#x27;s proprietary multimetric approach, adopted in Pakistan\. The average metric\-based results provide interpretable outcomes against SJR, IF and Scopus quartiles \(single\-metric\) as well as HJRS \(multi\-metric\)\. The proposed journal categorization method is fully automated and offers improved classification of journals compared to well\-known traditional metrics\. Originality/value The contribution of this study is evident in the proposed framework for categorizing journals using multiple metrics\. The novelty of our work lies in the integration and domain\-specific adaptation of unsupervised machine learning to categorize CS journals\.
