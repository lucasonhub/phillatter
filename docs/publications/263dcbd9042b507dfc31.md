# IMPACT OF CYBER\-SECURE BLOCK CHAIN SYSTEMS ON TRUST, REPAYMENT BEHAVIOUR, AND GOVERNANCE IN MICROFINANCE INSTITUTIONS

**Autores:** Surekha Nadikuda

**Data:** 2026-10-17 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

The study examines the impact of cyber\-secure Block chain systems on borrower trust, loan repayment behaviour, and institutional governance in microfinance institutions\. Using primary data collected from 425 respondents through purposive sampling, the study applied Chi\-square, Pearson correlation, ANOVA, and multiple regression analyses to test the proposed hypotheses\. The findings reveal that cyber security and Block chain transparency significantly enhance borrower trust, improve repayment behaviour, strengthen data protection and user confidence, and promote institutional governance and accountability\. Borrower trust was also found to play a significant mediating role between Block chain adoption and repayment performance\. The study concludes that integrating Block chain with robust cyber security mechanisms can improve transparency, operational efficiency, and the long\-term sustainability of digital microfinance services
