# Severe capillary leak syndrome caused by postnatal cytomegalovirus infection in a very preterm infant: a case report

**Autores:** Junchen Fang; Xin Chen; Tingting Li; Liyuan Cao; Li Ma

**Data:** 2026-10-15 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Postnatal cytomegalovirus \(pCMV\) infection in very preterm infants may lead to severe adverse outcomes; however, capillary leak syndrome as a complication of CMV infection has rarely been reported in this population\. This article reports a case of severe capillary leak syndrome \(CLS\) in a very preterm infant following postnatal CMV infection\. Despite conventional treatments, including antiviral therapy, fluid management, and cardiopulmonary support, the infant&\#x27;s condition progressively worsened, with signs of progressive fluid overload and acute kidney injury \(AKI\)\. Following the initiation of continuous renal replacement therapy \(CRRT\), the infant&\#x27;s clinical status improved, leading to eventual cure and discharge\. This case highlights severe CLS can be a major manifestation of pCMV infection in very preterm infants\. In addition to traditional therapy, CRRT can serve as an effective treatment option\.
