# McRitchie, Kenneth D\.: Environmental Cosmology

**Autores:** 

**Data de descoberta:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** unknown

## Descrição do feed

Written in response to the need for a theory of astrology, Environmental Cosmology attempts to find common ground that can realign the discourse between science and astrology\. Skeptical views of astrology are critically examined for faulty assumptions and misunderstandings\. Theory is developed through axioms and rational concepts based upon the physical structures of cycles\. The relevant cosmology refers to the properties of the things and places that come to our conscious awareness of what it is like for each of us to observe something from our own microcosmic center of the universe\. This necessarily entails a consideration of astrology and correlative worlds\. In contrast to how astrology is typically described as &quot;influences,&quot; this approach uses a framework of alternating equality and extremes that relates mind to the cosmological order\. For astrological &\#x27;natives&\#x27; environmental interactions and properties are modeled as categories of urges, values, intentions, and skills\.
