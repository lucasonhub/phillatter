# Anticipatory Commonsense with Moving Objects: An ASP\-Based Framework for Predictive Reasoning with Interacting Trajectories

**Autores:** Julius Monsen; Jakob Suchan; Mehul Bhatt

**Data:** 2026-10-14 · **Tipo:** preprint

## Resumo

We present a general declarative framework for anticipatory reasoning about interactive motion in complex dynamic environments\. Our focus is on \(explainable\) anticipatory space\-time mental model formation at a semantic level to heuristically guide low\-level motion computation\. Our method is hybrid, modular and elaboration tolerant integrating: 1\) An ASP model characterising the dynamics of interactive motion trajectories; 2\) Semantically\-guided prediction for deriving \`branching trajectory futures&\#x27;; and 3\) An energy\-based \{optimisation\} model to evaluate, rank and filter anticipations\. We evaluate with the Argoverse 2 benchmark, where each anticipated trajectory carries an explicit, inspectable event\-level hypothesis, unlike end\-to\-end stochastic prediction\.
