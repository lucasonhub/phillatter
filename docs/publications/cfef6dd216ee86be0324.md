# Among rural older adults, an apparent physical\-activity contrast in nonblank medication\-field presence was markedly attenuated when chronic\-disease management registration was incorporated into the analysis

**Autores:** Kun Xu; Yiming Zhang; Yongheng Zhao; Xuefeng Xi; Gaixia Hou; Zhuangzhuang Guo; Limeng Liu; Yajuan Wang; Dehui Zhang

**Data:** 2026-10-15 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Background Medication fields created for chronic\-disease management may mislead when reused as medication\-exposure measures in broader older\-adult populations\. Using routine exercise\-frequency as routine\-recorded physical activity \(PA\), we compared PA \+ records \(daily, at least weekly, or occasional exercise\) with PA− records \(no exercise\) and examined how nonblank medication\-field presence changed after management\-registration context was incorporated\. Methods This cross\-sectional study used 2,270 examination records from a 2025 rural primary\-care program in northeastern China; PA was available for 2,226 records\. The outcome was any nonblank text in the main\-medication field\. Modified Poisson regression estimated prevalence ratios \(PRs\) with small\-sample\-adjusted cluster\-robust inference across 20 sites\. Sequential models incorporated demographics, site, and four hypertension/diabetes management\-registration categories\. Logistic regression estimated standardized proportions and absolute differences\. Medication text underwent secondary audit, including 200 randomly sampled retained entries independently reviewed by two pharmacists\. Results Nonblank medication fields occurred in 1,114/1,157 \(96\.3%\) records with hypertension and/or diabetes management registration versus 27/1,113 \(2\.4%\) with neither registration\. Field presence was low without registration and near\-complete with hypertension\-only or dual registration; the diabetes\-only stratum was small \(n = 37\)\. Overall field presence was 56\.4% in PA\+ and 42\.8% in PA− records \(PR 1\.317, 95% CI 1\.076–1\.612\)\. After registration context was incorporated, the PR was 1\.001 \(95% CI 0\.960–1\.043\); full adjustment yielded 1\.002 \(95% CI 0\.959–1\.046\)\. Standardized proportions were 51\.26% and 51\.14%, a difference of 0\.12 percentage points \(95% CI −2\.07 to 2\.30\)\. Among 1,368 retained entries, archived labels classified 75\.9% as antihypertensive, 14\.5% as glucose\-lowering, 0\.5% as other cardiovascular, 1\.7% as other medications, and 7\.4% as unresolved\. Inter\-reviewer agreement for broad therapeutic class was 99\.5% \(κ = 0\.982\), and parser–manual\-reference concordance was 99\.0%; these assessments did not verify medication exposure\. Conclusion Nonblank main\-medication\-field recording was strongly patterned by chronic\-disease management registration, and the apparent overall PA\-related contrast was markedly attenuated after this context was incorporated\. Blank fields cannot establish medication nonuse, and nonblank or consistently parsed text cannot establish complete, current medication exposure\.
