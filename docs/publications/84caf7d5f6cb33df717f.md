# Urinary biomarkers in non\-muscle invasive bladder cancer: translating multiplex and genomic liquid biopsies into clinical practice

**Autores:** Yuanyuan Zhang; Colton Walker; Adham Bassen; Tracy Criswell; Navid Jabalameli; Michael Patrick Feloney; Liang Cheng; Anthony Atala; Soroush Rais‐Bahrami

**Data:** 2026-10-15 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Urinary biomarkers have long promised to reduce the cystoscopy burden in bladder cancer, but a persistent gap separates assay performance from clinical practice\. This review synthesizes the shift from protein\-based tests to multiplex, genomic, and extracellular\-vesicle liquid biopsies, including minimal residual disease detection and therapy selection, and sets out an implementation roadmap spanning pre\-analytical standardization, decision thresholds, and pragmatic trial design\.
