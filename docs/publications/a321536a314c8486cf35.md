# Relación entre uso académico de herramientas digitales y aprendizaje autónomo en estudiantes de Medicina y Enfermería

**Autores:** Nahomy Yulizeth Carrion Lopez; Carlos Eduardo León Reyes; Deilis Yohana Posligua Muentes; Jennifer Lilibeth Rodríguez Vélez; Hortencia Manuelita Solórzano

**Data:** 2026-10-11 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Introduction: Academic work in higher education increasingly relies on digital tools and Artificial Intelligence \(AI\)\. For medical and nursing students, making safe, critical, and responsible use of these technologies is essential to develop self\-regulated learning in the face of changing practice scenarios\. Objective: To analyze the relationship between the academic use of digital tools and autonomous learning in students of medicine and nursing programs at a higher education institution\. Methodology: Quantitative, non\-experimental, descriptive\-correlational, and cross\-sectional study\. 80 students were surveyed using a validated instrument\. Results: Both variables reported high levels: the academic use of digital tools had an average of 3\.71 out of 5, and autonomous learning an average of 3\.78\. A positive, moderate, and statistically significant association was found between both variables\. The ethical and critical use of AI was the technological dimension with the lowest score\. Conclusions: There is a favorable relationship between the critical mastery of digital tools and the ability for independent learning\. However, institutions should focus their pedagogical efforts on improving information verification, the transparent use of AI, and student self\-assessment strategies\.
