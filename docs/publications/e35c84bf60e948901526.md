# The Relationship between the Level of Speech Culture and the Success of Using AI in Pedagogical Activities: Results of the Experiment

**Autores:** Kun Li; Elvira Gabdelbarovna Galimova; Irina V\. Khairova

**Data:** 2026-10-18 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

The active integration of artificial intelligence \(AI\) systems into educational practice places higher demands on the professional training of teaching staff\. Within the structure of this training, speech culture becomes of key importance, since it is this that ensures the accuracy of task formulation and the adequate interpretation of digital results\. The aim of the study is to identify the nature of the relationship between the level of speech culture formation in future teachers and the effectiveness of AI use in the educational process\. In the experimental work \(sample of undergraduates, N=68\), the following were used: an author\-developed questionnaire for assessing the normative, communicative, and ethical components of speech culture, a pedagogical experiment involving problem\-solving tasks with the use of AI tools, and methods of statistical analysis\. The diagnostics revealed the absence of a low level of speech culture, but revealed a differentiation between high and medium levels\. Statistically significant positive correlations were obtained \(p &lt; 0\.05\)\. Participants with a high level of speech culture formulated queries more accurately, analyzed AI responses more critically and deeply, and interpreted results with higher quality\. The conclusion is drawn about the need to integrate speech and digital training of future teachers, since the effectiveness of AI use depends on speech culture, critical thinking, and professional reflection, and not only on technical skills\.
