# Conclusion: Synthesis and a research agenda for nature\-based tourism

**Autores:** Marjan Kamyabi; Aaron Tham

**Data:** 2026-10-09 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Purpose This paper concludes the special issue “Reimagining Nature\-Based Tourism: Innovations, Experiences and Sustainability in Tourism Destinations” by synthesising the 11 contributions through a thematic synthesis approach and outlining an agenda for future research\. Design/methodology/approach The paper adopts a thematic synthesis approach\. The eleven articles included in this special issue were coded against the issue&\#x27;s guiding research question and three derived sub\-questions, and grouped into six cross\-cutting themes\. The contributions span qualitative interview\-based case studies, analysis of visitor\-generated online content and bibliometric analysis\. Findings The synthesis shows that nature\-based tourism destinations convert natural resources into sustainable visitor value only when aesthetic appeal is matched by interpretive depth and infrastructural investment; that human intermediaries, community enterprises and certification bodies jointly, and sometimes unevenly, mediate conservation outcomes; and that digital methods and artificial intelligence are reshaping both how sustainability discourse is understood and how destinations might be managed, while introducing new risks of standardisation and dehumanisation\. Originality/value This original concluding article brings together eleven geographically and methodologically diverse studies through a structured, research\-question\-driven synthesis, translating individual contributions into a shared reference framework and research agenda for nature\-based tourism scholarship and destination practice\.
