# Bifunctional applications of Laurus nobilis\-derived silver nanoparticles: From catalytic synthesis of aminoindolizines to cytotoxicity against breast cancer cells

**Autores:** Shuai Wu; Hongyan Jia

**Data:** 2026-10-15 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

We report the green synthesis and characterization of silver nanoparticles \(AgNPs\) mediated by Laurus nobilis leaf extract\. Comprehensive structural analysis \(UV\-Vis, transmission electron microscopy \(TEM\), field emission scanning electron microscopy \(FESEM\), energy dispersive X\-ray spectroscopy \(EDX\) confirmed the successful formation of a stable AgNPs/ L\. nobilis composite\. TEM analysis revealed the particle size being 25\-30 nm\. As a heterogeneous catalyst, the nanocomposite facilitated the efficient, high\-yield synthesis of aminoindolizines via A 3 \-coupling \(yield 90\-96%\), exhibiting excellent stability across 8 recycling runs\. Furthermore, the biomedical potential of the Ag NPs was evaluated against breast cancer models\. The nanoparticles demonstrated significant, dose\-dependent antiproliferative activity against Michigan cancer foundation \- 7 \(MCF\-7\) and M D Anderson \- metastatic breast – 231 \(MDA\-MB\-231\) cell lines \(IC50​: 197 and 205 µg/mL, respectively\) with negligible toxicity toward normal cells\. Complemented by potent antioxidant activity \(1,1\-diphenyl\-2\-picrylhydrazyl \(DPPH\) IC50​: 190 µg/mL\), these results highlight the dual utility of L\. nobilis \-derived AgNPs as efficient catalysts and promising anticancer agents\.
