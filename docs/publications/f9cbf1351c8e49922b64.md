# Reflections in Resin: Three\-dimensional Profilometric Insights into Composite Polishing Techniques

**Autores:** Gowtham Kumar Kolagani; Sravya G Duggireddy

**Data:** 2026-10-09 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Aim: This study aims to evaluate the surface roughness of composite resin restorations subjected to finishing and polishing using two different polishing systems, using three\-dimensional \(3D\) profilometry\.Materials and methods: Forty\-two composite resin samples will be prepared using a silicone mold and randomly divided into three groups: Group I\-control \(cured under Mylar strips; no polishing system will be used\), group II\-polishing using the Shofu Super\-Snap kit, and group III\-polishing using Ivoclar Vivadent Optragloss\.After finishing the samples with a fluted tungsten carbide bur, polishing with the said polishing kits will be done\.Samples will be evaluated for surface roughness using a 3D profilometer\.Results: Surface roughness values \(Ra\) were highest for the Ivoclar Vivadent Optragloss group and least for the control group\.The surface roughness values of the Shofu Super\-Snap group were almost similar to the control group\.Conclusion: Within the limitations of the present in vitro study, it can be concluded that the control group and the Shofu Super\-Snap polishing group show lesser surface alterations when compared to the Ivoclar Vivadent Optragloss polishing group\.Clinical significance: A smooth polished surface improves the longevity of a restoration by reducing plaque retention, gingival inflammation, and recurrent caries\.Proper techniques for finishing and polishing play a significant role in improving the esthetics and longevity of restorations\.
