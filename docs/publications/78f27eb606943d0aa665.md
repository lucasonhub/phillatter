# Blockchain\-enabled value generation induces cooperative tipping points in geriatric care networks

**Autores:** Guan Huang; Jiqian Ma; Jingmiao Wang

**Data:** 2026-10-12 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

The global demographic shift threatens the fiscal sustainability of state\-sponsored geriatric care\. While community mutual aid offers an alternative aligned with Sustainable Development Goals \(SDGs 3 and 11\), traditional systems collapse due to free\-rider exploitation and deflationary hoarding\. This study proposes a permissioned consortium blockchain to transform informal volunteerism into a robust care economy\. Utilizing a high\-fidelity Agent\-Based Model \(ABM\) tracking 1000 heterogeneous agents over 60 months, we computationally validate an algorithmic “Credit Production” mechanism\. By treating the token issuance rate \( α \) as an independent variable, simulations identify a strict macroeconomic “Viability Corridor\.” An optimal algorithmic subsidy of α = 1\.15 successfully overcomes labor friction without triggering hyperinflation, inducing a critical\-mass tipping point that achieves 90% network participation and a sustainable 68\.3% satisfaction equilibrium\. Furthermore, adversarial stress\-testing confirms the architecture’s sybil\-resistance, algorithmically depleting and isolating free\-riders by Month 20\. Economically, the mechanism safely decouples elderly well\-being from initial fiat wealth, dynamically redistributing social capital to active labor providers\. This validated blueprint offers policymakers a deployable, mechanism\-designed infrastructure to resolve collective action failures and address the global caregiver shortage\.
