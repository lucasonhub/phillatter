# Unlocking Quantitative Skills: A Serious Game for University\-Level Social Science

**Autores:** María M\. Serrano\-Baena; Sonia Navajas\-Torrente; Rosa María Muñoz\-Millán; José Ríos‐Madrid; José Ángel Roldán Casas

**Data:** 2026-10-17 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

The deficiencies in basic mathematical skills and emotional factors such as maths anxiety hinder learning in quantitative subjects in non\-STEM university degrees, so it becomes essential to reconsider the teaching methodologies being used\. In this context, both serious games and gamification are considered as innovative pedagogical tools that strengthen student commitment, autonomy, and collaboration\. Thus, this study presents the design and implementation of a serious game developed for undergraduate students enrolled in Business Administration and Management degree programs, aimed at fostering deeper engagement and understanding in quantitative subjects\. It takes shape in an educational breakout, based on a business narrative and focused on the collaborative resolution of quantitative challenges related to mathematics, statistics and accounting\. The pilot project implemented in October 2024 with second\-year undergraduate students, not only validates their effectiveness but also offers a replicable and scalable model adaptable to different disciplines and educational contexts\. In conclusion, the presented project demonstrates that serious games, and particularly educational breakouts, can be powerful tools for transforming the teaching of quantitative content in non\-STEM university degrees\.
