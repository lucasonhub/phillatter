# An Optimized Hybrid Deep Learning Framework for Short\-Range Rainfall Forecasting in Mumbai’s Monsoon Regime

**Autores:** Manpreet Kaur Aiden; Sanjeev Thakur; Kasimahanthi Amar Jyothi; Manmeet Singh; Deepti Mehrotra

**Data:** 2026-10-14 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Abstract Short\-range rainfall forecasting in tropical coastal megacities is a formidable challenge owing to the nonlinear dynamics, strong seasonality, and pronounced interannual variability that characterise monsoon precipitation regimes\. This study develops and evaluates deep learning frameworks for multi\-lead\-time \(D\+1 to D\+4\) daily rainfall forecasting over Mumbai \(72\.88°E, 19\.07°N\), India, a densely populated coastal metropolis where accurate precipitation prediction is directly consequential for flood early warning\. Six forecasting systems namely Random Forest, XGBoost, a one\-dimensional CNN, a Transformer, and two metaheuristically tuned variants of a novel Conv1D–CBAM–BiLSTM–attention Enhanced Hybrid architecture \(GA\-Hybrid and PSO\-Enhanced\) are benchmarked against persistence and climatology references\. The PSO\-Enhanced ensemble is the strongest system at every lead, attaining R² = 0\.636 \(RMSE = 8\.97 mm day⁻¹\) at D\+1 and 0\.454, 0\.373 and 0\.399 at D\+2–D\+4, ahead of the GA\-Hybrid \(0\.626, 0\.428, 0\.352, 0\.385\), the strongest gradient\-boosting benchmark XGBoost \(R² ≤ 0\.561\), persistence \(R² = 0\.572 at D\+1, declining to 0\.049 by D\+4\), and climatology \(R² = 0\.327, lead\-independent\)\. Recurrent\-memory architectures outperform the memoryless CNN and Transformer at every lead, indicating that sequential dependence, rather than attention alone, carries most of the exploitable predictive signal\. For detection of rainfall exceeding the 95th percentile, the two optimised hybrids achieve the highest critical success index at every lead \(up to 0\.403 at D\+1\), considerably exceeding persistence, whose detection skill collapses beyond D\+2\. SHAP attribution shows that D\+1 skill rests chiefly on rainfall persistence and 850–500\-hPa moisture, whose combined importance nearly halves by D\+2\. Benchmarked against GraphCast and NOAA GFS for June–September 2019, the PSO\-Enhanced framework trails the global systems at short lead but attains higher correlation with observations by D\+4 \(r = 0\.61 versus 0\.32 and 0\.25, respectively\), indicating a comparative advantage of the localised, predictor\-rich statistical approach at longer point\-forecast horizons\.
