# Nomenclatural Notes on the Genus Meyna \(Rubiaceae, Vanguerieae\) With Lectotypification of Five Names

**Autores:** Pallavi B\. Dhal; Anishya; Sachchidanand Tripathi; Rajkumari Sanayaima Devi

**Data:** 2026-10-12 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

ABSTRACT The lectotypification of five names in the genus Meyna \( M\. spinosa , M\. tetraphylla , M\. velutina , M\. grisea , and M\. pubescens \) is presented\. Further, we clarified the nomenclature of the genus name, originally published by Robyns in his monograph Tentamen Monographiae Vangueriae Generumque Affinium \. A comprehensive taxonomic treatment of the genus Meyna , along with a diagnostic key at species rank, is also provided\.
