# VARIABILITY OF ANXIETY IN THE PRE\-COMPETITIVE SPORTS PERIOD

**Autores:** Leonardo Pinheiro da Silva; Thiago Alberto da Silva; Juliana Vilas Bôas Carpi; Alexandre Rocha Augusto; Rodrigo Paiva

**Data:** 2026-10-09 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Anxiety is a psychological and physiological phenomenon frequently present in the sports context, especially in the period leading up to competitions\. Its manifestation may vary according to individual characteristics, previous experiences, sport modality, gender, perception of threat, self\-confidence and importance attributed to the competitive event\. This study aimed to analyze, through a literature review, the main manifestations and variabilities of anxiety in the pre\-competition period, considering psychological, physiological and neuroscience\-related aspects, as well as differences associated with gender, sports experience and participation in individual or collective modalities\. A literature review was carried out based on national and international articles and books related to sports psychology, anxiety and sports performance, located in databases and sources such as SciELO, Google Scholar, scientific journals and printed books\. The literature analyzed indicates that pre\-competition anxiety does not have uniform effects on performance, and may assume different manifestations according to the characteristics of the athlete and the competitive situation\. Higher levels of anxiety are observed in certain groups, especially in women, younger athletes and practitioners of individual modalities\. Strategies such as relaxation, goal setting, mentalization and self\-talk are pointed out as resources to assist in emotional self\-regulation\. It is concluded that the individualized understanding of anxiety is fundamental for the planning of sports and psychological preparation\.
