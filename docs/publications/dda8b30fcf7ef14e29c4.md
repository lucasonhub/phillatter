# Emerging Trends in AI, Data Science and Cloud\-Based Technologies

**Autores:** Thoomula Kavya Sri

**Data:** 2026-10-12 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

The fusion of artificial intelligence \(AI\), data science, and cloud computing technologies is transforming the nature of the digital economy from disconnected technological silos to interdependent ecosystems that complement each other\. This paper explores current trends within these interrelated fields such as world models, agentic AI systems, cloud\-native AI integration, and industrialization of data science\. Mixed methods of research including the systematic literature review combined with the quantitative analysis of industry data is suggested\. Convergence patterns analysis shows that cloud platforms transform from mere infrastructure into smart services ecosystems, and data science changes from disconnected modeling into engineering practice\. Comparative analysis shows that companies embracing integrated approach to cloud\-AI\-data management enjoy considerably lower training costs and reduced deployment timeframes\. The conclusion is made about the challenges related to governance, skill development, and infrastructure sustainability that need addressing\.
