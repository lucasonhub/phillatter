# HistoBreastCAD: an integrated deep learning framework for segmentation and multiclass classification of whole\-slide breast histopathology images

**Autores:** Oswaldo Celi\-Vega; Yuliana Jiménez\-Gaona; Patricio Corral\-Dominguez; Ángel Gutiérrez; Zaida Socola\-Barzallo

**Data:** 2026-10-13 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Breast cancer remains a major cause of cancer\-related morbidity and mortality worldwide, and histopathological examination remains the diagnostic gold standard\. This study presents HistoBreastCAD, an integrated deep learning framework that combines tissue\-region segmentation, four\-class histopathology classification, spatial mapping of predictions, and interactive whole\-slide image \(WSI\) review\. Segmentation was evaluated using WSI\-grouped five\-fold cross\-validation, while classification used source\-image\-grouped five\-fold cross\-validation to prevent source\-level data leakage\. The final DeepLabV3\+ L0 segmentation configuration achieved an out\-of\-fold micro\-Dice of 0\.8390 , IoU of 0\.7226 , precision of 0\.8278 , and recall of 0\.8505 \. For classification, EfficientNetB0 was the strongest individual model, while a frozen equal\-weight soft\-voting ensemble of EfficientNetB0 and ResNet18\-GAP\-attention achieved an accuracy of 0\.8283 ± 0\.0320 and macro\-F1 of 0\.8282 ± 0\.0316 , outperforming EfficientNetB0 in all five validation folds\. An exploratory reader study using the 100 microscopy images from the held\-out BACH test set showed moderate interobserver agreement between two pathologists \(Cohen’s κ = 0\.5105; n = 95\), whereas model\-to\-reader concordance was lower\. The ensemble did not increase reader concordance relative to EfficientNetB0\. HistoBreastCAD therefore demonstrates the feasibility of integrating established deep learning components into a source\-separated WSI\-oriented digital pathology workflow\. However, external multi\-institutional validation, adjudicated reader studies, and formal explainability assessment are required before claims of clinical generalizability or utility can be supported\.
