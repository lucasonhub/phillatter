# UMUMTA’LIM MAKTABLARIDA XOR SAN’ATINI O‘QITISHNING PEDAGOGIK AHAMIYATI VA ZAMONAVIY YONDASHUVLAR

**Autores:** SEVARAXON RAZZOQOVA

**Data:** 2026-10-12 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

The article thoroughly examines the stages of historical formation of choral art, its pedagogical significance in Uzbekistan&\#x27;s general education schools, modern methods \(Kodály and Orff elements, innovative technologies\), and the impact of President Sh\.M\. Mirziyoyev&\#x27;s cultural policy\. The results section presents empirical data on the effectiveness of choral education, while the discussion covers advantages, practical challenges \(teacher qualifications, resource shortages\), and development paths\. Findings confirm the need for broader integration of choral art and teacher professional development\.
