# MODERN APPROACHES TO THE APPLICATION OF ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE TECHNOLOGIES IN PUBLIC ADMINISTRATION

**Autores:** Maratova Shaxsanem

**Data:** 2026-10-10 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

This article examines modern approaches to the application of artificial intelligence technologies in public administration, focusing on their potential to automate public services and improve administrative efficiency\. Based on scientific studies by international researchers, the advantages and challenges of implementing artificial intelligence in the public sector are analyzed\. The article also explores the legal framework for the development of digital public administration in Uzbekistan and provides recommendations for the effective application of artificial intelligence technologies\.
