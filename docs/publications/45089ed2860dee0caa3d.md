# Electronic health record developer adherence to federal AI risk transparency requirements

**Autores:** Nidhi Manikkoth; Ngan Vo; Jessica L\. Handley; Joshua Biro; Jordan Everson; Raj M\. Ratwani; Kristen Miller

**Data:** 2026-10-14 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

We evaluated electronic health record developer compliance with federal transparency requirements for AI\-enabled predictive decision support interventions\. Developers are required to publicly report on risk analysis, mitigation, governance, and on eight other attributes\. Across 35 developers, reporting was inconsistent and often incomplete, with substantial variation in structure and detail, yet all developers were certified as meeting the requirements\. Results suggest a need for standardized reporting and more rigorous certification review\.
