# Howell, Robert J\.: Acquaintance and Objectivity

**Autores:**

## Descrição do feed

In Consciousness and the Limits of Objectivity: The Case for Subjective Physicalism\. Oxford: Oxford University Press\. pp\. 90\-97\. 2013This chapter argues that a view that depends upon a relation of acquaintance must thereby deny that any objective theory of the world can be complete\. The chapter develops accounts of objectivity and completeness, and defends them as the accounts relevant for these debates\. The operative notion of objectivity is epistemic, in that an objective theory is one that can be fully grasped without a subject’s entering into a privileged epistemic relation to one of her own states\. The notion of completeness is similarly epistemic, in that a complete theory is one that if fully understood would leave no doubt about which of many epistemically possible worlds it described\. The upshot, when combined with the results of the previous chapter, is that no theory can be complete unless it relies on the relation of acquaintance, in which case it is not objective\.
