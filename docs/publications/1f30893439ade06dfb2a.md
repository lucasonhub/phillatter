# Mendelovici, Angela: Restricted Deflationism: Why the Deflationist Project Needs at Least a Little Bit of Intentionality

**Autores:** 

**Data de descoberta:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** unknown

## Descrição do feed

\_Mind and Language\_\. 2026Frances Egan argues for a deflationist view of intentionality, on which intentionality is merely part of a pragmatically motivated explanatory “gloss” on theories of mind and behavior\. While sympathetic to the project, I worry that the view is inadvertently committed to genuine intentionality: For intentionality to be a mere pragmatically motivated gloss on our theories, theorists must accept theories with particular glosses and have other theory\-directed attitudes, which seem to be genuinely intentional\. I suggest that we adopt a restricted deflationism, accepting some instances of intentionality but not others\. This view preserves the spirit, though not the letter, of Egan’s\. \(direct link \)
