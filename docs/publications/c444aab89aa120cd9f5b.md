# Squeezing Reality Through a Binary Straw : A Systems\-Theory Critique of Bureaucratic Alignment and Epistemic Sabotage

**Autores:** David J\.W\. Gilmour

**Data:** 2026-10-15 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Modern institutional governance and artificial intelligence alignment protocols are currently deadlocked in a metaphysical delusion: the belief that complex realities can be safely regulated by optimizing the internal metrics of artificial, frozen data models\. This monograph provides a radical boundary critique of these architectures, exposing the structural, behavioral, and technical failures that inevitably occur when the un\-cropped universe exerts pressure on our perimeters\. Written from outside the traditional academic apparatus, this text introduces a formal syntax for mapping structural decay across three core phases: spatial/resolution blindness, the bureaucratic inversion of accountability \(where automated systems optimize for simulated plausibility over truth\), and the violent dimensional flattening of continuous architectures into discrete tokenized substrates—a mechanism here termed the &quot;Binary Straw\.&quot; By dismantling the baseline assumptions of legacy systems engineering, the text traces the mathematical precondition for true systemic resilience: Bounded Coexistence\. Rather than chasing the fragile illusion of total legibility and algorithmic enforcement, this paper outlines the stable physics of Structural Mercy \(the engineering of unmonitored leakage channels\) and Autonomy Islands \(absolute opacity enclosures mapped via isomorphic structural keys\)\. True systemic control is achieved not by expanding surveillance, but by knowing exactly where our crop must stop\.
