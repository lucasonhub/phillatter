# The Future of Healthcare Management in 2026: From Traditional Administration to Data\-Driven Leadership, Artificial Intelligence, and Organizational Resilience

**Autores:** Hamza Elgazzar Selwan

**Data:** 2026-10-09 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Healthcare management is undergoing a profound transformation driven by artificial intelligence, digital technologies, evidence\-based management, and increasing expectations for quality, patient safety, operational efficiency, and organizational sustainability\. This conceptual article introduces the Selwan Integrated Healthcare Management Framework \(SIHMF™\), an integrated management model that combines artificial intelligence, data\-driven decision\-making, human\-centered leadership, governance, organizational resilience, workforce engagement, and patient safety into a unified strategic framework for healthcare organizations\. The article discusses the transition from traditional administrative models toward predictive, evidence\-based management capable of anticipating risks, supporting executive decision\-making, and achieving sustainable healthcare excellence\. Rather than viewing digital transformation as a technological project, the framework emphasizes leadership, governance, accountability, and measurable execution as the primary drivers of successful transformation\. The proposed framework may support healthcare executives, quality leaders, policymakers, researchers, and healthcare organizations seeking to integrate digital innovation with patient\-centered care, organizational resilience, and strategic performance improvement\.
