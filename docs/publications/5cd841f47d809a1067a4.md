# Reproducibility and relative validity of a food frequency questionnaire developed among reproductive\-aged women in Northeast China

**Autores:** Fanghua Liu; Qijun Wu; Tingting Gong

**Data:** 2026-10-12 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Background: To evaluate the reproducibility and relative validity of a newly developed 118\-item food frequency questionnaire \(FFQ\) for assessing habitual dietary intake among reproductive\-aged women in Northeast China\. Methods: A total of 207 eligible reproductive\-aged women were enrolled\. After exclusions for incomplete records or implausible energy intakes, 192 completed two FFQs \(FFQ1 \[first FFQ administration\] and FFQ2 \[second FFQ administration\]\) 9 months apart, 182 completed the FFQ and four planned 4\-day weighed diet records \(WDRs\)\. Reproducibility was assessed using crude and energy\-adjusted Spearman correlation coefficients \(SCC\), intraclass correlation coefficients \(ICC\), weighted kappa \(κw\), and quartile misclassification\. Relative validity was assessed against WDRs using crude, energy\-adjusted, and de\-attenuated SCC, κw, and quartile misclassification\. Results: For reproducibility, energy\-adjusted SCC ranged from 0\.32\-0\.60 \(median 0\.48\) for food groups and 0\.30\-0\.58 \(median 0\.44\) for nutrients; κw ranged from 0\.24\-0\.56 \(median 0\.43\) and 0\.26\-0\.53 \(median 0\.37\), respectively\. For validity, de\-attenuated SCC ranged from 0\.32\-0\.75 \(median 0\.57\) for food groups and 0\.29\-0\.58 \(median 0\.48\) for nutrients\. Same\-or\-adjacent quartile classification ranged from 76\.15%\-92\.25% \(median 85\.72%\) for reproducibility and 66\.94%\-85\.48% \(median 78\.23%\) for validity, with low extreme misclassification \(median &lt; 5% for both\)\. Conclusions: The FFQ demonstrated acceptable reproducibility and relative validity for ranking the habitual intake of most foods and nutrients among reproductive\-aged women in Northeast China, supporting its use in epidemiological studies of diet and reproductive health\.
