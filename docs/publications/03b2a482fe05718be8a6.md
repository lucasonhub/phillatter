# The Pemberdayaan Komunitas Kreatif Kampung Batik Semarang Melalui Edukasi AI Motif Batik Berbasis Kearifan Lokal

**Autores:** Bernadeta Lenny Setyowati; Peter Ardhianto; Ekawati Marhaenny Dukut; Krismalita Sekar Diasti

**Data:** 2026-10-15 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

This community service program aims to empower batik artisans, particularly female MSMEs in Kemijen and Kampoeng Batik Semarang, by educating them on utilizing Artificial Intelligence \(AI\) as an assistive technology to design Semarangan batik motifs based on local wisdom\. An interdisciplinary team spanning Communication Science, Cultural Studies, English, and Visual Communication Design implemented this program\. The execution methods included conceptual counseling, practical training on generative AI, project\-based creative mentoring, and design evaluations\. The results demonstrated a significant increase in digital literacy: 100% of participants understood AI&\#x27;s role as an assistive design tool, and practical AI operational skills rose to 86%\. The program successfully produced more than 5 collaborative batik motif prototypes \(e\.g\., Warak Ngendog, blekok, and tamarind tree\) and digital promotion copywriting in English\. Program outputs include an ISBN\-registered interdisciplinary educational module, an Intellectual Property Rights \(IPR\) registration draft for the educational model, and a scientific article draft submitted to a national Sinta 5 accredited journal\. In conclusion, utilizing AI as an assistive technology accelerates visual design exploration without diluting local cultural philosophy and authenticity, thereby strengthening the economic resilience and digital capacity of female batik MSMEs in the creative industry\. Keywords\-\-\- Artificial Intelligence \(AI\), Semarangan Batik, female MSMEs, local wisdom, community empowerment\.
