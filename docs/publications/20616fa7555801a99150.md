# A Review of Web\-Based Society Maintenance Management Systems for Residential Communities

**Autores:** Sonali Nagargoje; Vaishnavi Alandkar; Sonam Kamble; Anjali Bhande; Prof\. Nandini Umap

**Data:** 2026-10-13 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Residential housing societies coordinate recurring activities such as maintenance fee collection, complaint resolution, worker coordination, event communication, resident records, and security\-related tasks\. When these activities are handled through paper registers, spreadsheets, phone calls, and separate messaging groups, information can become fragmented and follow\-up may be delayed\. This review examines literature relevant to digital society administration, property management, and facility management\. It considers web\-based management portals as a practical approach for centralizing records, supporting role\-based access, tracking complaints and payments, recording worker details, and communicating events and notices\. Existing society\-management work identifies resident, secretary, and security\-guard workflows as important parts of a unified system, while wider facility\-management research emphasizes service monitoring, data integration, asset maintenance, privacy, and interoperability\. The review identifies common design requirements: clear role separation, reliable record keeping, status tracking, secure handling of personal and financial data, accessible interfaces, and transparent administrative processes\. It also highlights limitations in the literature, including a lack of comparable evaluation metrics for small and medium residential societies, limited evidence on long\-term adoption, and challenges related to data quality and user training\. The paper concludes that a simple, secure, and maintainable web\-based system can support more organized residential community operations when accompanied by appropriate governance and regular evaluation\. Keywords— Society maintenance; residential community; facility management; web\-based system; complaint management; maintenance payments; role\-based access; digitalization\.
