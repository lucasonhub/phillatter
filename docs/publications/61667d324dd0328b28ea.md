# Priority aware demand curtailment in smart grid reliability assessment

**Autores:** Doğan Urgun

**Data:** 2026-10-09 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

This study proposes a priority aware demand curtailment formulation for smart grid reliability assessment\. The proposed formulation is designed to evaluate both the amount of unserved demand and the priority level of the interrupted demand\. Each bus demand is divided into interruptible, ordinary, and protected portions, and higher interruption weights are assigned to more important portions\. The formulation is embedded in a sequential Monte Carlo simulation in which curtailment decisions are obtained by optimization under network constraints\. The simulation is applied to an electricity network based on the IEEE Reliability Test System Grid Modernization Lab Consortium\. The proposed formulation is compared with a uniform curtailment objective that minimizes total unserved demand\. The results show that conventional reliability indices remain in a similar range, while protected expected unserved energy and weighted expected unserved energy are strongly reduced\. Thus, priority aware curtailment can support more informative reliability assessment for modern smart grids with renewable generation and demand flexibility\.
