# Repetition Representation and Semantic Framing Across English Discourse

**Autores:** Birgul Asadova

**Data:** 2026-10-10 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Critical discourse analysis requires an explicit connection between linguistic description and claims about social representation\. This article examines how repetition, lexical choice, grammatical patterning, and semantic framing interact in selected English\-language political, media, and literary texts\. It presents a comparative qualitative close reading of passages from six political speeches, one news report, and four novels\. The analysis distinguishes directly observable textual features from interpretations of their discourse functions and from audience effects that would require separate investigation\. Political examples show how parallelism, participant reference, modality, metaphor, and contrast organize commitments and collective identities\. The media example demonstrates the importance of distinguishing journalistic narration from attributed commentary when analyzing social actor labels\. Literary examples show how modified propositions, imposed naming, and perspective\-taking represent institutional hierarchy and interpersonal relations\. Across the cases, similar forms support different functions according to speaker position, genre, and surrounding discourse\. The article proposes a transparent procedure linking textual evidence, contextual interpretation, alternative readings, and the limits of each claim\. Its contribution is a methodologically bounded account of multilevel textual analysis; it does not report acoustic measurements, genre\-wide frequencies, or experimentally established effects on audiences\.
