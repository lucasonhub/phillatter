# Importancia de la predicción del perfil ADMET en bases de datos de productos naturales

**Autores:** Alejandro Gómez‐García; José L\. Medina‐Franco

**Data:** 2026-10-15 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Natural products \(NPs\) are the largest source of bioactive molecules with therapeutic properties\. Currently, there are compound databases containing thousands of NPs, from which it is possible to select those with the best absorption, distribution, metabolism, excretion, and toxicity \(ADMET\) profiles, which greatly increases their chances of success in clinical trials\. This article discusses the importance of predicting the ADMET profile in early stages of drug development, especially using NP databases\. Nineteen freely accessible web servers that allow these predictions are presented, and some of the most relevant ADMET parameters that can be evaluated using these tools are discussed\.
