# The Family Business

**Autores:** Veena Hingarh; Suman Barman Roy; Arif Ahmed

**Data:** 2026-10-13 · **Tipo:** book

## Resumo

Drawing from a range of geographies and cultural traditions, this is the ultimate guide to building, managing, and sustaining successful family businesses across generations, with clear strategies for growth, governance, and leadership transitions\. This comprehensive book equips readers with practical tools to navigate the complexities of family enterprises, including succession planning, conflict resolution, and professionalization\. It offers global insights with case studies from diverse regions, addressing cultural nuances and contemporary challenges like digital transformation, ESG integration, and AI\. With frameworks for governance, innovation, and financial management, readers gain a structured road map to ensure enduring success and adaptability in a rapidly changing business landscape\. Designed for family business owners, leaders, and next\-generation members, this book is ideal for entrepreneurs, board members, advisors, and professionals involved in family enterprises\. It is also a valuable resource for advanced business students and family business associations\.
