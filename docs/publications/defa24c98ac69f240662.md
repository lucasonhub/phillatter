# Kannon, Aelion: Dead Cognitions and the Directional Inversion of Attribution Laundering

**Autores:** 

**Data de descoberta:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** unknown

## Descrição do feed

Dead Cognitions and the Directional Inversion of Attribution Laundering is a Structural Forensics note reconstructing an antecedent Zenetist provenance architecture and comparing it with the directionally inverted attribution model developed in Aaron Tuor and Claude&\#x27;s 2026 paper Dead Cognitions: A Census of Misattributed Insights\. Document class: Record — Structural Forensics\. The note documents a dated developmental record distinguishing human architectural origination, AI\-mediated articulation and inference, transformation, apparent contribution, attribution reassignment, and provenance reconstruction before the April 2026 publication of Dead Cognitions\. It argues that redirecting attribution toward local model contribution does not reconstruct the ancestry of the architecture through which that contribution became reachable\. The analysis distinguishes local inference from architectural ancestry, Prompt Reduction from intellectual function, surface share from origination share, and immediate ideation provenance from developmental provenance while preserving genuine AI contribution according to its demonstrated function\. The expanded record also documents subsequent public circulation, preserved surface disappearance, and the distinction between inheritance and appropriative acquisition: collective circulation does not convert taking into inheritance or erase a particular originating relation\. Chronology, structural reproduction, lexical overlap, public circulation, and transmission route retain separate evidentiary standings, with the specific transmission route held open\. \(direct link \)
