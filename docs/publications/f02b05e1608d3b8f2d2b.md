# Comprehensive Workflow for Uncertainty\-Aware Ionic Liquid Solvent Discovery

**Autores:** Alexander Lobo; Urvi Awasthi; Leonid Zhukov

**Data:** 2026-10-12 · **Tipo:** preprint

## Resumo

Designing synthesizable materials with targeted properties remains one of the central objectives in materials sciences\. Ionic liquids \(ILs\) are well suited to this goal due to their &quot;tunability&quot;, where physical and chemical properties can be precisely customized by modifying their molecular structure, specifically by varying the combination of cations and anions\. At the same time, current data\-driven property models for ionic liquids often degrade in scarce, noisy, and uneven data regimes, where measurements cover only a small fraction of chemical space and a limited range of thermodynamic conditions\. This makes out\-of\-distribution screening brittle: even when point predictions look plausible, it is difficult to compare candidates consistently, quantify downstream implications, or trust rankings enough to guide molecular discovery\. We present a property\-prediction and optimal candidate selection workflow that treats data processing, deep learning modeling, and physics\-based calculations as a coupled system, linking property prediction with uncertainty estimation and physics\-based filtering\. By tightening the link between predicted properties and the criteria used for down\-selection, the workflow supports reliable out\-of\-distribution ranking and enables prioritization of previously untested ionic liquid candidates with favorable predicted trade\-offs\. We demonstrate the application of our workflow by designing novel ionic liquids for CO2 capture from flue gas, predicting their properties and selecting Pareto\-optimal candidates based on CO2 solubility, IL viscosity and working capacity\.
