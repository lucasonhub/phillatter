# Decoding Tunable Ring\-Specific Adaptive Aromaticity in 1,3\-Diaza\-5,6\-Diborabicyclo\[3\.1\.0\]hexatriene: Insights from a Combined DFT and Machine Learning Study

**Autores:** Sajid Imran; Muhammad Nouman Khan; Jun Zhu

**Data:** 2026-10-15 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Aromaticity, a cornerstone of chemical stability, is governed by Hückel’s and Baird’s rules for the ground $\(S\_0\)$ and triplet excited $\(T\_1\)$ states, respectively, yet these canonical rules are challenged by two\-state aromaticity \(adaptive aromaticity\)—a burgeoning frontier in aromaticity research\. Herein, density functional theory \(DFT\) investigations reveal that 1,3\-diaza\-5,6\-diborabicyclo\[3\.1\.0\]hexatriene \(1\) and its all\-carbon congener both exhibit $S\_0$ entire\-fused system aromaticity, but show striking $T\_1$ divergence: the carbon analog loses aromaticity entirely, while compound 1 displays ring\-specific adaptive aromaticity \(5MR antiaromatic, 3MR aromatic\)\. Nitroso/nitro substituents act as potent spin modulators, enabling switching between localized and whole\-system $T\_1$ aromaticity\. A multiple linear regression model identifies spin localization and charge distribution as the principal contributors to $T\_1$\-state electron delocalization within the present dataset, while an Extreme Learning Machine \(ELM\) classifier reproduces the DFT\-derived aromaticity categories with 93% accuracy on the held\-out entry\-level test set\. This combined DFT–ML strategy provides a complementary data\-driven framework for analyzing adaptive aromaticity in BN\-doped heterocyclic systems\.
