# Design and Control of an Intelligent Mobile Gardening Robot Supported by Deep Learning\-Based Image Processing

**Autores:** Mutez Rahal; Abdurrahman Elhac Abdullah; Hakan Çelik; Mehmet Kaya

**Data:** 2026-10-12 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

In this study, the design and control of a mobile gardening robot based on image processing and artificial intelligence were implemented\. The system, running on Raspberry Pi 4B, can distinguish different plant species and weeds using a YOLOv5\-based object detection and classification model; it performs tasks such as cutting weeds, watering useful plants, and moving autonomously while avoiding obstacles\. Experiments conducted in a pot environment demonstrated that the robot can successfully perform plant detection, classification, and maintenance functions\. With these features, the developed robot offers a low\-cost and sustainable solution for small and medium\-scale agricultural businesses\. The mobile gardening robot designed within the scope of the study was experimentally tested for image processing\-based plant recognition, autonomous plant orientation, plant cutting, and watering operations for different scenarios\. When the results obtained were examined, it was seen that the developed deep learning\-based plant detection model achieved an 82% mAP@50 success rate, while the plant classification model achieved a 92\.9% mAP@50 success rate\.
