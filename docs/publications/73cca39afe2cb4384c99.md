# Kajian Yuridis Penyebaran Konten Deepfake Pornografi Melalui Media Sosial di Indonesia

**Autores:** Ketut Wirya; Abdul Wahid; Fidyah Faramita Utami

**Data:** 2026-10-11 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

The dissemination of deepfake pornographic content through social media in Indonesia presents a serious legal challenge in the digital era\. Artificial intelligence technology can produce visual manipulations that are difficult to distinguish from authentic recordings, creating complexities in evidentiary procedures and law enforcement\. This study aims to examine the legal framework governing the dissemination of deepfake pornographic content through social media in Indonesia, evaluate the effectiveness of legal protection for victims, and formulate a construction of criminal liability for perpetrators\. The method employed is normative legal research using statutory, conceptual, and case approaches\. The findings indicate that Law Number 44 of 2008 concerning Pornography, the Electronic Information and Transactions Law, and the new Criminal Code can normatively encompass deepfake pornography due to their broad formulation and consequence\-oriented approach\. However, the absence of explicit provisions regarding deepfake renders law enforcement heavily dependent on judicial interpretation, potentially leading to inconsistent decisions\. This study recommends the formulation of specific provisions explicitly recognizing deepfake as a form of AI\-based pornography, the strengthening of digital forensic capacity, and the establishment of more responsive content detection and removal mechanisms by social media platforms\.
