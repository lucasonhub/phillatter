# Assessment\-guided Multi\-horizon LSTM for Dissolved Oxygen Risk Prediction in an AIoT Fish Nursery Decision Support System

**Autores:** Firda Amalia; Dewi Agushinta R\.; Sulistyo Puspitodjati; Syarifuddin Nasution

**Data:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Fish nursery water\-quality management requires interpretable assessment and decision\-oriented risk information beyond sensor monitoring\.This study develops an assessment\-guided multi\-horizon dissolved oxygen \(DO\) prediction approach within a Decision\-Ready AIoT DSS\.Primary IoT data \(5,376 observations, four pond/species contexts\) were transformed using suitability, excursion, and stability features to evaluate eleven configurationsmatched LSTM/GRU pairs, two reimplemented 2024\-style baselines, and a PatchTST\-lite Transformer comparatorfor 15\-60 minute DO forecasting\.Repeated across five seeds, Raw LSTM attained the lowest RMSE \(0\.335±0\.015 mg/L\), while Full Assessment\-Guided LSTM attained the highest decision accuracy among the LSTM configurations \(0\.929±0\.010\)\.PatchTST\-lite achieved RMSE 0\.369±0\.033mg/L, recall 0\.864±0\.060,and decision accuracy 0\.926±0\.006acompetitive Transformer benchmark without universal dominance\.The proposed DSS translates assessment and forecast outputs into risk status, recommendations, and NLPbased explanations for human\-in\-the\-loop nursery management\.
