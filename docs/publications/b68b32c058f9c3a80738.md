# Gugak\-VocalSet: A Multi\-Genre Studio\-Quality Dataset of Korean Traditional Singing with Sigimsae Annotations

**Autores:** Danbinaerin Han; Wonil Kim; Seah Hong; Dongjoo Moon; Jaeok Lee; Jongpil Lee; Chaewon Kim; Juhan Nam

**Data:** 2026-10-11 · **Tipo:** preprint

## Resumo

We present Gugak\-VocalSet, the first publicly available audio dataset for Korean traditional singing with dense annotations of sigimsae, a structured pitch contour system of vocal expressions\. Gugak\-VocalSet contains 24\.5 hours of unaccompanied solo vocal recordings across 305 tracks of representative repertoire curated by the National Gugak Center, performed by 20 professional singers in a controlled studio environment with fixed key and tempo\. In addition to genre, lyrics, and mood/timbre tags, the dataset provides a unified sigimsae ontology and approximately 68,000 segments \(71,700 instances including multi\-label\) of audio\-grounded sigimsae annotations; the ontology adopts a functional\-position\-based hierarchy designed for consistent application across genres, and 14 expert annotators specializing in Korean traditional music performed segment\-level labeling accordingly\. Baseline experiments on sigimsae classification and event detection show that domain\-informed F0 representations remain competitive with, and on classification surpass, frozen probing of a general\-purpose music foundation model, while pitch and spectral cues play complementary roles in event\-level detection\.
