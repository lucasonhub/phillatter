# DSTG\-Former: A Graph\-Transformer for Intrusion Detection in DNP3 Smart Grids

**Autores:** Shruthi Madhugiri Venugopal; A\. Kailas; Sumit Gupta

**Data:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

The Distributed Network Protocol 3 \(DNP3\) remains the dominant communication standard in modern smart grids, yet it lacks intrinsic security, exposing supervisory control and data acquisition \(SCADA\) infrastructures to protocol\-specific cyberattacks\.Conventional intrusion detection systems \(IDSs\) rely on flow\-level statistics, discarding protocol semantics, physical topology and temporal evolution\.This paper proposes the Domain\-informed Spatial\-Temporal Graph\-transFormer \(DSTG\-Former\), operating directly on raw DNP3 payloads extracted through deep packet inspection \(DPI\)\.Packets are mapped onto a topology\-aware communication graph whose vertices are the devices identified from DNP3 link\-layer addresses and whose edges carry standardized 64\-byte payloads; an edgeconditioned graph neural network \(EC\-GNN\) learns spatial dependencies per snapshot and a transformer encoder with attentive readout models dependencies across twenty\-snapshot sequences\.On 725,708 window sequences from the public DNP3 Intrusion Detection Dataset, spanning nine attack categories and normal polling, DSTG\-Former attains 98\.71% accuracy, 94\.59% macro F1\-score and 0\.9992 macro one\-versus\-rest area under the receiver operating characteristic curve \(AUC\) under the stratified window\-level partition, and 97\.84% accuracy and 89\.19% macro F1score under a stricter partition whose training and test packets are provably disjoint\.An ablation identifies the recovered topology and the edge\-conditioned branch as decisive, and controls against a rule\-based detector, masked inputs and four baselines retrained on the same partitions isolate the contribution of the learned representation\.
