\*\*Basic Principles and Practical Paths of LLM\-Empowered Senior High School English Writing Evaluation in China: A Conceptual Framework\*\* \*\*Jiang Li\*\* College of Education, Zhejiang Normal University, Jinhua 321004, China Zhengzhou No\. 36 Middle School, Zhengzhou 450042, China \*\*Abstract:\*\* This paper adopts a conceptual analysis approach based on policy document interpretation and narrative literature synthesis to explore large language model empowered senior high school English writing evaluation within the Chinese educational context\. Driven by national digital transformation of education and the 2020 Overall Plan for Deepening the Reform of Educational Evaluation in the New Era, generative large language models deliver innovative technical support to resolve prominent drawbacks of traditional writing assessment including superficial evaluation dimensions, single evaluation subjects, rigid marking mechanisms and delayed interactive feedback\. Different from conventional automated writing evaluation tools limited to surface linguistic scoring, large language models generate conversational feedback, support adjustable evaluation standards through prompt design and conduct comprehensive discourse assessment covering language competence, cultural awareness, thinking quality and learning ability which forms the core innovation of this research\. This study systematically elaborates two guiding orientations for human machine collaborative evaluation including disciplinary core literacy orientation and digital intelligent diversified evaluation orientation and constructs three targeted practical paths matching real senior high school teaching scenarios\. The three paths cover teacher led evaluation guided by curriculum standards, visualized learner profiling for targeted instruction and cross time and space personalized writing guidance\. Supplementary supporting measures regarding academic integrity, minor data compliance, balanced digital resource allocation and teacher artificial intelligence literacy cultivation are further supplemented to improve practical operability\. The established framework helps shift assessment logic from score oriented judgment to literacy oriented developmental guidance\. Restrictions such as algorithm bias, student over dependence on artificial intelligence and data ethical risks hinder stable large scale application of model based evaluation and relevant discussions are fully presented in the limitations section\.
