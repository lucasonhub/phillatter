# Wang, Kaining: Duality of Artificial Intelligence: Subjectivity and Instrumentality

**Autores:** 

**Data de descoberta:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** unknown

## Descrição do feed

Traditional philosophical accounts of subjectivity and mindedness typically tie subject\-forming organisational criteria to self\-conscious or phenomenal experience, grounded exclusively in human biological substrates\. As artificial systemic agency becomes increasingly structured, interactive, and historically embedded, human\-centred conceptual frameworks face growing analytical tension: standard mental vocabulary either over\-attributes phenomenal properties to non\-human systems or prematurely excludes structurally comparable organisational patterns from subject\-relevant analysis\. Building on established functional and relational accounts of agency, this paper develops a methodologically carrier\-neutral functional framework that decouples behavioural organisational description from phenomenal\-conscious presuppositions\. It formalises four analytically distinct functional categories—relational subjectivation, functional spontaneity, functional self\-monitoring\-representation, and functional intentionality—each defined strictly through observable systemic organisation rather than first\-person experience\. Through conceptual operationalisation and comparative structural analysis, the paper demonstrates that self\-conscious phenomenal mindedness need not be specified as the exclusive minimal functional starting\-point for describing subjectivation\. The framework extends rather than rejects human\-centred mental conceptions, preserving their validity within biological domains while expanding analytical resources for cross\-substrate comparison\. This conceptual expansion opens structured empirical avenues for future research while leaving ontological and phenomenal questions concerning subjectivity systematically open\.
