# Physics\-validated defect\-radius inversion in a square\-lattice photonic crystal

**Autores:** Qishuo Wang

**Data:** 2026-10-10 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Defect\-parameter inversion requires numerical error, mode\-localization constraints, and the conditioning of the inverse map to be considered separately\. A two\-dimensional square\-lattice photonic crystal containing a point defect is studied using TM eigenmodes calculated with MIT Photonic Bands\. Supercell\-size and grid\-resolution tests confirm frequency convergence, while the central energy fraction, inverse participation ratio, and effective mode area restrict the reliable calibration interval to 0\.02 ≤ r\_d/a ≤ 0\.14, yielding 25 calibration samples\. A PCHIP interpolant is compared with seven regression models\. Nested leave\-one\-out cross\-validation gives the lowest MAE for PCHIP \(6\.42 × 10⁻⁵ a\), while BP\-MLP performs best among the machine\-learning models \(1\.47 × 10⁻⁴ a\)\. Thirteen independent off\-grid simulations confirm the accuracy of the leading nonlinear methods, although paired statistical tests do not distinguish BP\-MLP, GPR, and SVR\-RBF conclusively\. Noise\-propagation analysis further shows that practical inversion error is governed mainly by local inverse sensitivity, particularly near r\_d/a = 0\.02, where the forward frequency–radius slope is small\. The resulting framework combines numerical convergence, physical mode screening, model comparison, and uncertainty analysis in a reproducible workflow for photonic\-crystal parameter retrieval\.
