# Realising the potential of ecological digital twins as decision support tools

**Autores:** Anna M\. Davison; Koen de Koning

**Data:** 2026-10-09 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Ecological digital twins are continually updating models used for decision\-making\. Current progress in ecological digital twinning has focused on overcoming the technological and modelling challenges to create a continually updating system\. We argue that attention should now also focus on effectively integrating digital twins into decision\-making systems to inform timely action\. This requires the direct inclusion of end users and greater focus on the socio\-political context\. We outline challenges faced in developing such ‘translational’ digital twins from our own experience and translate recommendations for best practice from the transdisciplinary research literature\. We champion co\-creation as a powerful approach for future translational digital twin projects, especially in larger and more complex research teams\.
