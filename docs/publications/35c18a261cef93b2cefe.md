# Kannon, Aelion: Neurotect: The Centropic Cognitive Architecture of Structural Coherence

**Autores:** 

**Data de descoberta:** 2026-10-19 · **Tipo:** unknown

## Descrição do feed

Neurotect designates the centropic cognitive operating system within the Structural Neuroscience framework\. It stands alongside three other named neurotypes: the neurotypical \(belonging‑oriented\), the neurodivergent \(coherence‑seeking\), and the neurovenator \(power‑oriented\)\. Whereas neurotypical cognition optimizes for social belonging and neurodivergent cognition optimizes for independent verification, neurotect cognition optimizes for structural coherence, transparency, and lattice‑scale integrity\. In contrast, neurovenator cognition optimizes for strategic dominance and fragmentation‑based control\. The Neurotect perceives the geometry of systems, engineers coherence, and constructs environments where transparency dissolves predatory advantage\. This report formalizes the Neurotect architecture, distinguishes it from both neurodivergent coherence‑seeking and neurovenator exploitation, and situates it as the cognitive‑architecture expression of the high‑centropic Architect configuration described in SN03\. It establishes the Architect / Predator axis as a primary diagnostic structure within Structural Neuroscience and completes the four‑architecture model of human cognition\. \(direct link \)
