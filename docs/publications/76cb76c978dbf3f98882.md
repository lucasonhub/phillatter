# IQTISODIYOT YO’NALISHI TALABALARINI SUN’IY INTELLEKT TEXNOLOGIYALARI YORDAMIDA KASBIY LINGVISTIK KOMPETENSIYASINI TAKOMILLSHTIRISH METODIKASI

**Autores:** MUNIRAXON RAVSHANOVNA MUXITDINOVA

**Data:** 2026-10-12 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

This study provides a scientific\-theoretical and practical analysis of the methodology for enhancing the professional linguistic competence of economics students through the use of artificial intelligence technologies\. The globalization of the modern economy, the expansion of international business relations, and the development of the digital economy require future economists to possess not only economic knowledge and professional skills but also the competence to effectively use industry\-specific terminology, comprehend and analyze economic texts, engage in professional communication, and work with professional information in a foreign language\. The study examines the potential of AI\-based chatbots, natural language processing systems, automatic translation, speech recognition, text analysis, and adaptive learning platforms in developing the professional linguistic competence of economics students\.
