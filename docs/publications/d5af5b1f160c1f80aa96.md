# Limits and being unlimited by the reading format: a phenomenological study on emotional experiences of readers of audiobooks and physical books

**Autores:** Henri Nerg

**Data:** 2026-10-15 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Introduction\. This is a qualitative study of the reading habits and experiences of readers of audiobooks and physical fiction books, with a focus on their emotional reactions to the reading process and the format of reading\. Method\. Qualitative interviews were carried on for Finnish participants \(N=6\) and transcribed\. Analysis\. The analysis was interpretative phenomenological analysis, which focuses on the personal interpretations of the participants’ reading experiences\. The transcribed interviews were analysed in multiple rounds and the process of coding was used to map out themes\. Results\. The results suggest that both formats of reading create strong affective and emotional connections in the readers, but in various and often polarizing and dichotomic ways\. Feeling of being limited by the format and having control over the environment were two major themes regarding the reading and listening experience\. Conclusion\. All participants formed strong emotional connections with the medium and felt that the one that they did not prefer had negative connotations and limitations to the reading experience, whereas their preferred medium enhanced their experience and liberated them from the limitations of the not preferred medium\.
