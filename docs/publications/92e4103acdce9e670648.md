# A responsabilidade civil médica no Brasil sobre erro de diagnóstico assistido por Inteligência Artificial

**Autores:** Luísa de Castro Bezerra

**Data:** 2026-10-14 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

This study analyzes medical civil liability in Brazil for diagnostic errors assisted by Artificial Intelligence \(AI\)\. There is a growing use of AI in the healthcare sector, where,through databases and pattern recognition, it is possible to reach diagnoses within minutes\. Although the benefits of innovation are widely recognized, concerns about regulating its use—especially in high\-risk activities such as healthcare—are increasing\. This research adopts an exploratory and qualitative approach, aiming to provide aninitial understanding of a still underdeveloped topic\. The analysis is based on relevant national sources, with emphasis on Bill No\. 2,338/2023, which proposes a regulatory framework for Artificial Intelligence in Brazil, as well as international references such as European Union legislation and recommendations from the World HealthOrganization \(WHO\)\. The examined documents highlight challenges to traditional civil liability\. These include the control problem, as AI systems operate autonomously andmay evolve unpredictably; opacity, which hinders understanding of the decisionmaking process; and the multiplicity of agents involved, which dilutes the attribution of responsibility\. Such factors render the application of classical civil liability models insufficient\. The Brazilian proposal establishes strict liability for high\-risk AI systems,distributed according to each party’s contribution to the harm\. However, proving such contribution may be excessively burdensome\. Therefore, although there is a tendency for legal systems to adapt, definitive answers remain uncertain, and this study aims to support further reflection and future research\.
