# Design Implications for Teacher\-Configurable Generative AI Writing Tools: A Participatory Design Study with StoryBot

**Autores:** Ariel Han; Shenshen Han; Zhenyao Cai; Joey Huang; Franceli L\. Cibrian; Kylie Peppler

**Data:** 2026-10-13 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

As generative AI \(GenAI\) tools become accessible, their integration into K–12 writing presents opportunities and challenges\. This study partnered with 21 primary school teachers in a participatory co\-design process to develop StoryBot, a teacher\-configurable GenAI story\-authoring platform\. Teachers identified instructional needs and learning outcomes that informed system features, including genre\-specific writing scaffolds, configurable prompts, and constraints on full\-text generation to preserve student voice\. The platform positions educators as co\-designers of AI behavior rather than passive users\. We conducted a pilot writing workshop with 13 youth \(ages 8–12\) and a focus group with six educators to examine how the platform influenced students’ writing behaviors and classroom integration\. Findings demonstrate the value of teacher co\-design and configurable AI systems in aligning GenAI tools with pedagogical priorities and practices\. We present design implications for teacher\-configurable GenAI writing systems that support student agency, ethical safeguards, and instructional alignment in human–AI coauthoring environments\.
