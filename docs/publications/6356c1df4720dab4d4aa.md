# A Review on Pharmaceutical Marketing and Management

**Autores:** Omkar Narayan Musale; Dr\. Nitin Gawai; Ms\. Rekha Kolape

**Data:** 2026-10-10 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Pharmaceutical marketing and management occupy a distinct position among commercial disciplines because they operate at the intersection of profit\-driven business objectives and public health responsibility\. Thisreview examinesthe strategic, operational, and ethical dimensions of pharma marketing and management, drawing on recent literature covering promotional practices, digital transformation, supply chain operations, and regulatory oversight\. The review finds that traditional promotional tools such as medical representative \(MR\) visits, free drug samples, and continuing medical education \(CME\) sponsorships remain influential in shaping physician prescribing behaviour, even as digital channels, artificial intelligence \(AI\), and data analytics increasingly reshape how companies engage healthcare professionals \(HCPs\) and patients\. On the management side, the Indian pharmaceutical supply chain continues to face structural challenges including fragmentation, import dependence on active pharmaceutical ingredients \(APIs\), cold chain limitations, and counterfeit products, even as digital tools and regulatory reforms attempt to close these gaps\. Ethical concerns persist around inducements, exaggerated promotional claims, and inconsistent enforcement of codes such as the Uniform Code for Pharmaceutical Marketing Practices \(UCPMP\), which became legally binding in 2024\. The review concludes that a balanced, ethically anchored, and technologically integrated approach to marketing and supply chain management is essential for the pharmaceutical sector to serve both commercial and public health goals sustainably\.
