# Mitigating Volatility in P2E through Allowance Verification in Decentralized Platform

**Autores:** Md Shariful Islam; Hye\-Young Kim

**Data:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Play\-to\-Earn \(P2E\) game economies integrated with Automated Market Maker \(AMM\) protocols face persistent sustainability challenges driven by whale\-induced price volatility and the absence of effective token\-burning mechanisms\. These issues erode economic fairness and discourage long\-term participation within web\-based blockchain gaming platforms\. This paper proposes EG\-AMM \(Epoch\-Guarded Automated Market Maker\), a software engineering framework that adds a governance\-aware volatility protection layer to existing AMM\-based P2E infrastructures without modifying the underlying pricing model\. Inspired by circuit breaker mechanisms in traditional finance, EG\-AMM enforces per\-epoch dynamic swap allowances for individual players, computed from price impact tolerance and configurable through Decentralized Autonomous Organization \(DAO\) parameters\. The framework employs Merkle\-proof\-based cryptographic verification and on\-chain accounting to ensure allowance limits are transparent and tamper\-resistant\. Unlike existing pool\-level approaches such as static transaction caps or dynamic fee schedules, EG\-AMM operates at the individual player level, curbing whale dominance while preserving equitable market access for ordinary players and newcomers\. Preliminary analysis and game\-theoretic simulation confirm improvements in price stability, participation fairness, and economic resilience, offering a practical and deployable path toward sustainable token ecosystems in blockchain\-integrated gaming applications\.
