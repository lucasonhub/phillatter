# Competitive Intelligence in the Age of Digital Transformation: Challenges and Opportunities: A Nigerian Perspective

**Autores:** BLESSING OMOTOSHO ONI; YACOB HALISO

**Data:** 2026-10-17 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

This study analyses the impact of digital tools such as Artificial Intelligence \(AI\) and Big Data on the activities of Nigerian companies\. This process is called Competitive Intelligence \(CI\)\. The study also finds that though these tools offer great opportunities, there is an “Intelligence Paradox” in Nigeria\. It indicates that firms have a lot of data at hand but tend to grapple with leveraging it into proper business advice\. This is largely caused by bad infrastructure like poor power supply and expensive internet, plus new privacy laws, including the Nigeria Data Protection Act \(NDPA\) 2023\. To tackle these problems, the research recommends low\-cost methods such as Open\-Source Intelligence \(OSINT\) the use of free, public information, while ensuring humans are in charge of machines’ work\. To succeed in business in Nigeria, Nigerian firms must develop their own &quot;Compliant Intelligence&quot; using smart technologies that are compliant with the law, the paper concludes\. Mixing global technology with local knowledge helps businesses stay ahead in a tough economy\.
