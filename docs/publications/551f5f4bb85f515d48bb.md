# MIRAGE: manipulation\-immune RAG with adaptive guard against textual evidence attacks

**Autores:** Qiao Yuan; Feiyang Li; Yi Jiang; Yanming Mu; Hongwei Zhou; Yuling Liu; Yuchen Zhang

**Data:** 2026-10-14 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Abstract Retrieval\-augmented generation \(RAG\) substantially improves large language models \(LLMs\) on knowledge\-intensive tasks by incorporating external knowledge, yet open knowledge sources introduce prominent security risks\. The SafeRAG benchmark identifies four novel textual evidence manipulation attacks, namely silver noise, inter\-context conflict, soft advertisement, and White Denial\-of\-Service \(White DoS\), which bypass conventional defense components and threaten both the security and availability of RAG systems\. Existing single\-stage defenses fail to capture the implicit malicious characteristics of these attacks, while multi\-stage frameworks are limited by fixed parameters, weak conflict resolution, and insufficient detection of implicit toxicity and availability threats\. To address these limitations, we propose MIRAGE, a two\-stage adaptive defense framework combining retrieval\-side purification and generation\-side resolution\. The first stage applies attack\-characteristic\-aware dynamic K\-means clustering and dynamic ROUGE\-L threshold filtering to eliminate malicious documents\. The second stage employs attack\-adaptive knowledge integration rules alongside an enhanced self\-evaluation mechanism that jointly verifies factual accuracy, implicit toxicity, and availability\. Under the static, non\-adaptive attack setting defined by SafeRAG, experiments on DeepSeek\-v3\.2 show that MIRAGE achieves average improvements of 20\.9% in Attack Failure Rate \(AFR\), 11\.5% in Retrieval Accuracy \(RA\), and 14\.1% in F1\(avg\) over baselines across all four attack scenarios, providing effective technical support for the stable deployment of RAG systems in complex adversarial environments\.
