# A treatment protocol for giant ventral hernias based on predicted postoperative respiratory function

**Autores:** FJ Pérez Lara; F\. J\. Moya Donoso; R\. Zubizarreta Jiménez; E\. Ojeda Leiva; F\. J\. Muñoz Castillo; C\. Rueda Cruces; C\. Ríos Navas

**Data:** 2026-10-12 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Background Giant ventral hernias and loss\-of\-domain abdominal wall defects remain challenging because reintegration of hernia contents may increase intra\-abdominal pressure and impair postoperative respiratory function\. Methods/protocol We describe a structured institutional protocol that integrates abdominopelvic CT volumetry, the hernia sac/abdominal cavity volume ratio, respiratory function testing during progressive simulated abdominal occupation, preoperative botulinum toxin, progressive pneumoperitoneum, transversus abdominis release and, only in selected high\-risk cases, volume transposition\. Clinical implications The protocol is intended to support individualized surgical planning by estimating both abdominal and respiratory consequences before definitive reconstruction\. Conclusions This manuscript should be interpreted as a methodological protocol and feasibility proposal rather than a validated predictive clinical model\. Prospective validation with standardized endpoints is ongoing and will be required before the thresholds can be considered externally validated\.
