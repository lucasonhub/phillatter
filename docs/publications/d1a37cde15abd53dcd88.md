# Beuria, Jyotiranjan ; Chembrolu, Venkatesh H\. &amp; Kumar, Nitin: A Category\-Theoretic Foundation for the Unity of Phenomenal Experience in a Consciousness\-First Framework

**Autores:** 

**Data de descoberta:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** unknown

## Descrição do feed

We propose a consciousness\-first framework to coherently explain the contextual diversity of local experiences and the global unity of the phenomenal field\. Utilizing category theory, we model experience as a coherent structure arising from the integration of context\-dependent content\. We introduce a category of Raw states \(representing pre\-semantic relational data\) and a category of Sem states \(representing meaningful experiential content\)\. A faithful translation between these domains is established via an integrator functor, which maps raw relational structures to stable semantic categories\. These local semantic states are organized into a diagram indexed by a category of contexts, capturing the relationships and overlaps between distinct perspectives\. We demonstrate that phenomenal unity can be rigorously defined as the colimit of this semantic diagram\. Furthermore, a universal construction glues local contents together along their shared relational constraints, yielding a unified global structure that preserves perspectival differentiation without collapsing into a flat, undifferentiated whole\. Ontologically, this formalism results in the following: rather than assuming a pre\-existing physical spacetime reality that generates experience, it is possible to model the objective world as the invariant semantic core emerging from the coherence relations between plural phenomenal worlds\.
