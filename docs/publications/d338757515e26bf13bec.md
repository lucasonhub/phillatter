# ENHANCING INTELLIGIBILITY AND LEARNER AUTONOMY IN EFL PRONUNCIATION INSTRUCTION THROUGH AI\-DRIVEN PEDAGOGICAL FEEDBACK

**Autores:** Nurmamatova Nodira Shuhrat qizi Orental unversititi Pasixologiya yo&\#x27;nalish 2 kurs magstir talabasi

**Data:** 2026-10-15 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Modern English as a Foreign Language \(EFL\) pronunciation instruction has shifted its primary goal from native\-like accent imitation to functional intelligibility and comprehensibility\. Despite this paradigm shift, traditional classroom settings often struggle to provide personalized, real\-time diagnostic feedback due to large class sizes and time constraints\. This study investigates the pedagogical integration of Artificial Intelligence \(AI\)\-driven speech analysis and automated acoustic feedback in EFL pronunciation teaching\. Synthesizing contemporary literature on Computer\-Assisted Pronunciation Training \(CAPT\) and feedback literacy, this paper examines how AI feedback impacts learner autonomy, segmental clarity, and suprasegmental awareness\. The review reveals that AI tools provide consistent, low\-anxiety practice environments that facilitate cognitive noticing and self\-correction\. However, over\-reliance on automated metrics without explicit pedagogical framing can result in mechanical practice detached from communicative contexts\. To reconcile these dynamics, this article introduces a conceptual Scaffolded Pedagogical Feedback Model \(SPFM\) that integrates AI\-generated acoustic diagnostic data, structured peer dialogue, and teacher\-mediated instructional intervention\. The framework highlights the necessity of developing student feedback literacy to transform automated output into actionable learning strategies\. Keywords: EFL pronunciation instruction, Computer\-Assisted Pronunciation Training \(CAPT\), artificial intelligence, intelligibility, learner autonomy, feedback literacy\.
