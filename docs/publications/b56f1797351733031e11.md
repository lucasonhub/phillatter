# Kravetskyy, Aleksandr: Introduction for Specialists: Ontological Foundations of the Clinical Approach

**Autores:** 

**Data de descoberta:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** unknown

## Descrição do feed

Classical cognitive\-behavioral therapy \(CBT\) and third\-wave therapies \(ACT, MBCT\) interpret depressive and anxiety states through the lens of maladaptive thought content or deficits in attentional regulation\. This paper proposes a fundamentally different, ontophysiological diagnosis within the Time Density \(TD\) paradigm: psychopathology is not a problem of thought content but a structural consequence of chronic reduction of temporal density and temporal displacement of the patient&\#x27;s consciousness relative to their own axis of presence\. Drawing on Libet&\#x27;s experimental evidence for temporal delay in conscious perception \(1983\), Friston&\#x27;s free\-energy principle \(2010\), and the Daoist concept of wu wei, the paper introduces a dual\-mode model of consciousness — Original Mode \(−1\), characterized by maximal temporal density and minimal interpretive noise, and Reflection Mode \(\+1\), a state of consciousness fully captured by processed post\-simulation reconstructions\. Anxiety disorders are reinterpreted as chronic forward displacement along the \+1 axis, depressive states as fixation on retrospective \+1 models, and burnout as complete collapse of the internal time axis\. The paper presents a practical neurocognitive recalibration technique — the Entry Protocol \(Stop → Return → Proceed\) — designed to interrupt default\-mode network mind\-wandering, minimize Libet&\#x27;s delay, and restore the patient&\#x27;s capacity for action from the Original Mode\. A therapeutic ethics code, clinical assessment scale, and self\-observation diary template are provided for practitioner use\.
