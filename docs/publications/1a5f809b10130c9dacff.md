# Laboratory Diagnosis of Cancer: Integration of Conventional Methods, Precision Medicine, and Their Impact on Public Health Systems

**Autores:** 

**Data:** 2026-10-13 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Laboratory diagnosis of cancer is a key component of early detection, prognosis assessment, and therapeutic decisionmaking in oncology\.In middle\-income countries such as Ecuador, limited access to screening programs and advanced diagnostic technologies remains a significant challenge for cancer control, particularly affecting early detection\.This study critically reviewed both traditional methods and the most recent tools applied to the diagnosis of breast, prostate, and cervical cancer\-neoplasms that account for a significant proportion of national morbidity and mortality\.To this end, studies published in both international and national databases were analyzed, including original research, reviews, and clinical guidelines focused on diagnostic performance\.The findings show that widely used tests such as mammography, prostate markers, and cervical cytology remain fundamental in screening, although they have limitations in the early stages; in contrast, the use of molecular biomarkers, more advanced imaging techniques, and computational tools has improved the accuracy and identification of clinically relevant lesions\.Rather than relying on a single approach, the strengthening of cancer diagnosis in the Ecuadorian context is based on the interaction and complementarity of these tools\.
