# MODERN MEDICAL EDUCATION: PEDAGOGICAL TRENDS AND HEALTH OF PARTICIPANTS OF THE EDUCATIONAL PROCESS

**Autores:** N\. I\. Zavgorodnia; Anastasiia Horetska; Igor Zavgorodnii; S\.P\. Hlabets

**Data:** 2026-10-17 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Introduction\. The modern system of medical education is undergoing active transformation under the influence of globalization, rapid development of medical technologies, and the digitalization of the educational process\. The implementation of innovative approaches, including simulation\-based learning, artificial intelligence technologies, as well as virtual and augmented reality, contributes to improving the effectiveness of training medical professionals\. At the same time, the intensification of the educational process and the digitalization of the learning environment may affect the psycho\-emotional state and health of participants in the educational process, which determines the need to analyze current pedagogical trends in medical education\. Aim\. To analyze modern pedagogical trends in medical education and determine their impact on learning effectiveness and the health of participants in the educational process\. Materials and methods\. An analytical review of scientific publications by domestic and foreign authors devoted to the development of medical education, the implementation of digital technologies in the educational process, and studies examining the influence of the educational environment on the psycho\-emotional state of students and teachers was conducted\. Methods of system analysis, generalization, and comparison of the results of contemporary scientific research were used\. Results\. It was established that the integration of innovative educational technologies contributes to improving the quality of training of medical professionals, the development of clinical thinking, and the formation of practical skills\. At the same time, the increasing academic workload, the digital intensity of the educational process, and the need for continuous professional development may lead to higher levels of stress, emotional exhaustion, and professional burnout among participants in the educational process\. Conclusions\. Modern medical education requires a balanced combination of innovative pedagogical technologies and approaches aimed at preserving the mental and physical health of participants in the educational process\. A promising direction is the development of health\-preserving educational strategies, optimization of academic workload, and the implementation of trauma\-informed approaches in the system of medical education\.
