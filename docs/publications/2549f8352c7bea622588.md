# Use of Computers and Internet as Correlates of Managers’ Empowerment in Public Tertiary Institutions in Benue State, Nigeria

**Autores:** OJonoka Victoria Attah; Genesis Onah Agbudu; Donatus Peter Ochai

**Data:** 2026-10-12 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

This study investigated the use of computers and the internet as correlates of managers’ empowerment in public tertiary institutions in Benue State, Nigeria\. Two objectives and corresponding null hypotheses guided the study\. A correlational research design was adopted\. The population comprised 4,887 academic staff from eight public tertiary institutions in Benue State, from which a sample of 489 was selected using a multistage sampling procedure\. Of the 489 questionnaires administered, 480 were retrieved and found usable for analysis\. Data were collected using a researcher\-developed 10\-item questionnaire structured on a four\-point Likert scale: Strongly Agreed \(SA\) = 4, Agreed \(A\) = 3, Disagreed \(D\) = 2, and Strongly Disagreed \(SD\) = 1\. The instrument was validated by experts and yielded a Cronbach’s alpha reliability coefficient of 0\.93\. Data were analyzed using Pearson Product\-Moment Correlation \(PPMC\) at the 0\.05 level of significance\. The findings revealed a very strong, positive, and significant relationship between computer use and managers’ empowerment \(r = 0\.954, p &lt; \.001\)\. Similarly, a very strong, positive, and significant relationship was found between internet use and managers’ empowerment \(r = 0\.831, p &lt; \.001\)\. The study concluded that computer and internet use are significantly associated with managers’ empowerment in public tertiary institutions in Benue State\. It recommended that government and relevant stakeholders provide adequate computer facilities, improve internet connectivity, and organize regular training programmes to strengthen managers’ digital competencies for effective institutional administration\.
