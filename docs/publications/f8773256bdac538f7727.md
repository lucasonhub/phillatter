# Cross\-lineage anomaly detection of microRNA expression: a framework for deriving exploratory candidate biomarkers in sarcoma

**Autores:** Mritunjoy Dey; Piotr Remiszewski; Ewelina Perdas; Paweł Golik; Jakub Piątkowski; Anna M\. Czarnecka

**Data:** 2026-10-11 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Introduction Sarcomas are rare, heterogeneous malignancies of mesenchymal origin whose diagnosis and molecular classification remain challenging\. We introduced a oneclass anomaly detection framework to microRNA expression profiles to test whether sarcoma\-associated miRNA features are distinguishable from those of epithelial, haematological, and neurological cancers and if these features carry any biological relevance\. Methods Candidate miRNAs meeting cross\-lineage significance criteria were further evaluated through differential expression analysis against subtype\-matched normal mesenchymal reference tissue, pathway enrichment, and survival analysis\. A five\-miRNA index \(“Index 5”\), derived by Fisher’s linear discriminant analysis with leave\-one\-out cross\-validation in a TCGA\-SARC training cohort, was applied without refitting to an independent external cohort \(GSE36982\)\. Results The five\-miRNA index \(”Index 5”\) achieved an AUC of 0\.93 and, applied without refitting to an independent external cohort \(GSE36982\), an AUC of 0\.97\. After Benjamini–Hochberg correction, one candidate miRNA \(miR\-1305\) remained significantly associated with overall survival\. Discussion These findings identify a computationally derived, externally tested candidate miRNA signature associated with sarcoma\. While this study lacks experimental and clinical validation, the broader aim of this study was to come up with a framework that would assist and speedup the exploratory candidate biomarker discovery process in rare malignancies\.
