# OILA BOSHLIQLARI O‘RTASIDA QO‘LLANILADIGAN “KECHIRIMLILIK” KONSEPTI

**Autores:** Мўминов Шерозбек Сиддиқжонович

**Data:** 2026-10-12 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

This article analyzes the linguo\-cognitive, psychological, and communicative characteristics of the concept of &quot;forgiveness&quot; as applied between the heads of the family\-the husband and wife\. Forgiveness is viewed as a crucial conceptual element that serves to mitigate the hurt, disagreements, grievances, and conflicts that arise during family interaction\. The study elucidates the semantic content of the &quot;forgiveness&quot; concept, as well as its emotional, cognitive, motivational, and communicative components\. It also explores the relationship between forgiveness and factors such as empathy, mutual trust, respect, responsibility, and constructive communication within the marital relationship\. Based on an analysis of scholarly sources, forgiveness is assessed as a mechanism that aids in managing family conflicts, Ta’limda istiqbolli izlanishlar \- xalqaro ilmiy\-metodik jurnal № 9/2026 ISSN: 3060\-527X reducing negative emotions, and restoring relationships\. The article also focuses on the linguistic and pragmatic manifestations of the &quot;forgiveness&quot; concept within family discourse\.
