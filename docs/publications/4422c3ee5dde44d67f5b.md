## Descrição do feed

\_Linguistics and Philosophy\_ 49 \(3\)\. 2026Holton \(2017\) draws on the observation that no natural language has a contrafactive to argue that the object of knowledge is a fact\. I fill in Holton’s argument sketch by asking how the truth and falsity presuppositions of, respectively, &\#x27;know&\#x27; and a contrafactive could arise\. I defuse previous objections to his argument sketch, but, in the final section, suggest that Holton’s argument fails because presupposition triggering requires less than his argument assumes\. \(direct link \)
