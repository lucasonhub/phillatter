## Descrição do feed

\_Philosophy of Science\_\. forthcomingA widely held approach characterizes the notion of representation in cognitive science in terms of a selected effect notion of biological function\. I argue that these theories of representation have trouble saying there are representations where cognitive scientists say there are in at least some, and likely many, cases\. I spell out the difficulties this raises for selected effect theories\.
