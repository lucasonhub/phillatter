# Quantitative analysis of China’s selenium\-enriched policy texts: Based on a three\-dimensional framework of “theme–instrument–effectiveness”

**Autores:** Pengfei Xu; Xiongchao Xu; Qing Wang; Wenrong Ye; Jianli Luo

**Data:** 2026-10-13 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Selenium, as an essential trace element for human health, plays a critical role in antioxidant defense, immune regulation, and disease prevention\. With approximately 72% of China&\#x27;s soil area being selenium\-deficient, developing the selenium\-enriched industry has become a strategic priority for local governments under the &quot;Healthy China&quot; and Rural Revitalization initiatives\. While existing research has extensively explored selenium&\#x27;s scientific mechanisms and industrial development pathways, a critical research gap remains in the systematic quantitative evaluation of selenium\-enriched policy texts\. This study addressed this gap by conducting a comprehensive quantitative analysis of 42 selenium\-enriched policy documents issued in China from 2009 to 2025\. Employing a three\-dimensional analytical framework of &quot;policy theme\-policy instrument\-policy effectiveness&quot; integrated with Latent Dirichlet Allocation \(LDA\) topic modeling, content analysis, and the Policy Modeling Consistency \(PMC\) index model, this study reveals three key findings: \(1\) Current policies heavily emphasize industrial cultivation and brand building while paying insufficient attention to long\-term selenium resource management and cross\-regional coordination; \(2\) Policy instruments predominantly rely on supply\-side inputs and indirect market cultivation, with weak direct stimulation of consumer health demand; \(3\) While policy design demonstrates good operationality \(average PMC index = 6\.9\), it exhibits deficiencies in long\-term strategic planning and national\-level top\-level design\. This study provides actionable insights for policymakers to strengthen national top\-level design, balance short\-term industrial growth with long\-term resilience construction, and optimize policy tool configurations to stimulate endogenous market vitality for the high\-quality development of the selenium\-enriched industry\.
