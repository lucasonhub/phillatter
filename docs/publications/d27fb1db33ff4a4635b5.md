# Barkalle, W\. Hajinur: The Mirroring Theory: Kinetics of Knowledge, Perception, and Quantum Reflection

**Autores:** 

**Data de descoberta:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** unknown

## Descrição do feed

The Mirroring Theory, proposed by Barkalle Wersha Hajinur, presents a conceptual and mathematical framework for understanding knowledge, perception, and comprehension as dynamic and kinetic processes\. Using a three\-dimensional cubic mirror configuration, the theory models information as reflections that interact, converge, and generate increasing depth of understanding around an observer\. The framework connects reflection dynamics with probabilistic descriptions, uncertainty, wavefunction\-like representations, and a centripetal knowledge potential that drives information toward a point of convergence\. Mathematically, it formulates reflection coefficients through path sums, establishes convergence conditions, constructs a normalized probability field, introduces an uncertainty inequality, and models the temporal evolution of knowledge through a transport–reaction partial differential equation\. The theory further proposes that maximal integration occurs at the center of the reflective system, where multiple perspectives converge\. By bringing together geometry, mathematical modeling, epistemology, cognition, and physics\-inspired concepts, the Mirroring Theory offers an alternative lens for examining how information becomes integrated into understanding and insight\.
