# Bulgaria, Jamel: The Architecture of Emotional Stability: A Comprehensive Integration Strategy for the Core Emotion Framework in High\-Constraint Institutional Ecosystems

**Autores:** 

**Data de descoberta:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** unknown

## Descrição do feed

The scientific investigation into the architecture of human affect has historically been defined by a fundamental theoretical schism, often characterized as a &quot;100\-year war&quot; between discrete emotion models and dimensional frameworks\. Discrete models, such as those proposed by Izard and Ekman, argue for biologically hardwired, universal emotional categories, whereas dimensional models prioritize a continuous spectrum of valence and arousal\. The Core Emotion Framework \(CEF\) emerges as a sophisticated structural\-constructivist resolution to this dichotomy, proposing a modular system that accounts for the complexity of human experience while maintaining a rigorous, repeatable structural foundation rooted in ten universal emotional operators or powers\. Within the CEF, emotions are not viewed as static states or feelings but as functional operators within a complex human operating system \(Human OS\), suggesting that emotional states are predictable computational events rather than subjective mysteries\. This paradigm shift operationalizes affective phenomena into a small, non\-overlapping set of ten operators serving as the emotional equivalent of CPU instructions\. As institutions such as schools, mental health facilities, and prisons face escalating crises of emotional dysregulation and staff burnout, the refinement and expansion of the CEF for deployment in these settings offers a non\-clinical, skills\-based pathway to stability\. \(direct link \)
