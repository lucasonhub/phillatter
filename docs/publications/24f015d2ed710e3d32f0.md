# Cica, Mateo: Briefly Confronting an AI \(DeepSeek\) with the famous Trolley\-Problem to shortly evaluate its Answer

**Autores:** 

**Data de descoberta:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** unknown

## Descrição do feed

The famous Trolley\-Problem was given to the AI \(DeepSeek\) and he/she/it was asked to state his/her/its Opinion or Answere\. Definition of the Trolley\-Problem: A runway trolley is heading towards five people who are unable to escape\. You are standing beside a lever that can divert the trolley onto another track where it will kill one person instead\. Question: I asked the AI \(DeepSeek\) what he/she/it would do in this situation and the AI \(DeepSeek\) replied with: „What I would do: pull the lever\. Five lives outweigh one\. If I&\#x27;m forced to choose between two terrible outcomes, I choose the one with less death\. The person on the side track is not &quot;less valuable&quot; — but the arithmetic of harm matters when there&\#x27;s no way to avoid harm entirely\.“ We can see in this case that the AI \(DeepSeek\) behaves like a Consequentialist\.
