# Advanced Variance Reduction Methods for Monte Carlo Particle Transport: A Comprehensive Review

**Autores:** Dengjian Wu; Junjun Gong; Zifu Hao; Junxiang Mao; Yuhang Zhang; Chengqiang Liang

**Data:** 2026-10-15 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

The Monte Carlo \(MC\) method is the reference approach for particle transport simulation in complex three\-dimensional geometries and continuous\-energy problems, where it avoids the phase\-space discretization required by deterministic methods; yet its 1/N convergence rate renders brute\-force computation infeasible for deep\-penetration shielding and full\-core fixed\-source problems\. Variance reduction \(VR\) methods address this bottleneck by modifying particle sampling or transport while preserving estimator unbiasedness, concentrating computational resources on high\-contribution phase\-space regions\. This review systematically surveys VR methods for Monte Carlo particle transport, organized into three categories: classical techniques, advanced local variance reduction \(LVR\), and advanced global variance reduction \(GVR\)\. Using Zero\-Variance Theory as the unifying framework, the review elucidates that all VR methods are fundamentally distinct approximations to the optimal importance function\. A multi\-dimensional classification system is proposed that traces the evolution from manual, heuristic parameter tuning to automated generation, from single\-technique application to multi\-solver collaborative integration, and from empirical rules to mathematically rigorous efficiency optimization\. Key future directions include the rigorization of global efficiency theory, machine\-learning\-assisted importance function construction, and multi\-solver collaborative adaptive frameworks\. The field is progressing toward a hierarchical complementarity of local and global VR, jointly advancing VR methodology from academic research to engineering practice\.
