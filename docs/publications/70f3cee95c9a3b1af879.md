# Current applications of artificial intelligence for advancing agriculture, sustainability and climate mitigation

**Autores:** Mostafa Farajpour

**Data:** 2026-10-17 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Artificial intelligence \(AI\) is reshaping agriculture by enabling data\-driven decision\-making across crop production systems and supporting the transition toward more sustainable, resilient, and climate\-smart farming\. This review critically synthesizes advances in AI applications spanning precision irrigation, nutrient and soil management, yield prediction, pest and disease monitoring, computer vision, autonomous agricultural robotics, and large language model \(LLM\)\-based decision support systems\. Beyond summarizing technological developments, the review evaluates their practical deployment, emphasizing that successful agricultural AI depends not only on predictive accuracy but also on model generalizability, explainability, economic feasibility, interoperability, and integration with existing agronomic knowledge\. Emerging technologies including Vision Transformers, foundation models, Digital Twins, multimodal AI, Retrieval\-Augmented Generation, and federated and continual learning—are discussed as important directions for overcoming limitations related to data scarcity, environmental variability, and scalable field implementation\. The review highlights that responsible AI adoption requires transparent governance frameworks addressing data ownership, privacy, algorithmic fairness, cybersecurity, and equitable access, particularly for smallholder farming systems\. Although numerous AI models report outstanding performance, widespread agricultural adoption remains constrained by cross\-region validation, fragmented datasets, infrastructure requirements, and insufficient real\-world deployment\. Future progress will depend on multidisciplinary collaboration integrating advances in artificial intelligence, sensing technologies, robotics, crop science, and agricultural engineering to develop robust, trustworthy, and farmer\-centered decision\-support systems\. By critically evaluating technological innovations and implementation challenges, this review provides a framework for understanding the current state of AI in agriculture while identifying research priorities that will shape the next generation of sustainable and climate\-resilient agricultural systems\.
