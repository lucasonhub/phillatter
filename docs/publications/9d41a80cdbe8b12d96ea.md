# Evolution of Diagnosis, Treatment, and Global Elimination Strategies for Pneumoconiosis

**Autores:** Nabi Ummatovich Chamsutdinov; N\. R\. Mollaeva; A\. A\. Ramazanov; А\. Г\. Тайгибова; Djariyat N\. Abdulmanapova; Ахмедова Пахай Набиевна

**Data:** 2026-10-09 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Pneumoconioses remain the leading cause of occupational disability and mortality worldwide\. Occupational risks associated with the inhalation and accumulation of inorganic dust particles in the lungs contribute substantially to the global burden of pneumoconioses\. This review analyzes approaches to the diagnosis, treatment, and prevention of pneumoconioses based on international and national criteria, guidelines, and recommendations, including the 2014 Helsinki Criteria; documents from the World Health Organization, the International Labour Organization, the American Thoracic Society, and the European Respiratory Society; current clinical guidelines from the Russian Ministry of Health; and data from key original studies and systematic reviews of recent years\. The evolution of diagnostic imaging for pneumoconioses is considered, with particular attention to the role of high\-resolution computed tomography and the 2022 digital classification of radiographs of the International Labour Organization\. The 2019 spirometry standardization by the American Thoracic Society and the European Respiratory Society is presented, which allows optimization of the interpretation of spirometric abnormalities in patients with pneumoconioses\. The role of ethnic\-specific spirometry reference equations from the 2012 Global Lung Function Initiative is analyzed; these equations improve the accuracy of spirometric measurements in individuals of various ethnic groups\. The diagnostic potential of serum biomarkers in the early diagnosis of pneumoconioses \(Krebs von den Lungen 6\) and malignant pleural mesothelioma \(fibulin\-3, soluble mesothelin\-related peptides, and cyclin\-dependent kinase inhibitor 2A\) is presented\. Lung cancer screening in asbestos\-exposed individuals is covered in detail\. The outcome of progressive pneumoconiosis with the development of a usual interstitial pneumonia pattern and progressive pulmonary fibrosis is described\. Particular attention is paid to the concept of progressive pulmonary fibrosis, which justifies the use of the antifibrotic drugs nintedanib and pirfenidone in patients with a progressive phenotype of pneumoconiosis\. The potential of artificial intelligence and radiomics, which open new horizons in the diagnosis of pneumoconioses, is explored\. Global and Russian programs for the elimination of pneumoconioses are analyzed\. Based on the data obtained, recommendations for optimizing Russian clinical guidelines are proposed\.
