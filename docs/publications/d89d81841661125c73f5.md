# Assessment of land\-use and land\-cover changes impacts on land surface temperature using remote sensing and GIS: a case study of Kohat District, Pakistan \(2015–2025\)

**Autores:** Waheed Ali Khoso; Rukhshanda Rehman; Muhammad Junaid; Aqib Khattak; Abdul Basit; Muhammad Lutfur Rehman; Mussa Umali

**Data:** 2026-10-14 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

ABSTRACT Graphical abstract showing LULC changes and their impacts on LST in Kohat District, Pakistan \(2015–2025\), including increased built\-up and barren areas, decreased vegetation and water bodies, and associated spatial\-temporal LST variations\. Kohat District, located in the semi\-arid region of Pakistan, has experienced rapid urbanization and environmental change driven by climate variability and land\-use transformation\. This study investigates the spatiotemporal dynamics of land\-use/land cover \(LULC\) and their relationship with land surface temperature \(LST\) from 2015 to 2025 using remote sensing and geographic information system techniques\. Multitemporal Landsat 8 and Landsat 9 images were classified into five LULC classes using the maximum likelihood classification method\. Results showed substantial land\-cover changes, with barren land increasing from 1,171\.90 to 1,573\.76 km2 and built\-up areas expanding from 110\.60 to 290\.53 km2\. In contrast, forest cover declined from 717\.59 to 314\.53 km2, agricultural land decreased from 945\.65 to 771\.79 km2, and water bodies slightly reduced from 31\.30 to 26\.42 km2\. Correspondingly, maximum LST increased from 38\.66 °C in 2015 to 45\.38 °C in 2025\. Vegetation and water bodies consistently exhibited lower surface temperatures, highlighting their cooling effect\. The strong positive relationship between urban expansion, barren land growth, and increasing LST demonstrates that land\-cover change is a major driver of local warming\. These findings emphasize the need for sustainable urban planning, afforestation, and effective natural resource management to enhance climate resilience in Kohat District\.
