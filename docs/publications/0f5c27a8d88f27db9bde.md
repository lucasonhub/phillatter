# THE NATURE OF THE SOUL \(Volume 3\)

**Autores:** Majid Karimi Baghmaleki

**Data:** 2026-10-13 · **Tipo:** book

## Resumo

The third volume examines the emergence of scientific approaches to mind\. Physiology, psychophysics, psychology, neuroscience, behaviorism, cognitive science, information processing, artificial intelligence, and contemporary theories of consciousness progressively replaced older metaphysical vocabularies with empirical and computational frameworks\. Yet scientific progress generated new philosophical problems\. If consciousness depends upon the brain, what exactly is consciousness? If cognition is information processing, where does subjectivity enter? If behavior can be explained externally, is an inner mind still necessary? If neural correlates of consciousness can be identified, does identifying them explain experience itself? This volume argues that the scientific investigation of mind does not eliminate the philosophical problem of the soul\. It makes the problem more precise
