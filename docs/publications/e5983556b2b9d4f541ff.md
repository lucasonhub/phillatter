# AI 4 Translatology \- A short deep dive in large language models

**Autores:** Oliver Welz; Robert Haase

**Data:** 2026-10-14 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

In this slide deck we dive into applications and best\-practices of large language models for natural language processing for the purpose of information retrieval and text translation\. The target audience are scientists in linguistics and translatology who want to work more deeply with artificial intelligence\. These slides are relate to the hands\-on exercise materials published here: http://scads\.github\.io/ai4translatology\-2026 The English slides were presented during the training session\. The German slides were auto\-translated using Microsoft Copilot and translate\-pptx an open source alternative using sovereign IT infrastructure at the Compute Center ZIH at TU Dresden\.
