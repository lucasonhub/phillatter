# Federated causal inference for effect estimation in healthcare: methods, challenges, and applications

**Autores:** Haoyang Li; Zheqi Lyu; Meihan Wei; Simai Cheng; Wenhan Zheng; Fuying Ding; Jie Xu; Kyra Gan; Max Sunog; Colin Magdamo; Mark W\. Albers; Fei Wang; Chengxi Zang

**Data:** 2026-10-17 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Federated causal inference enables treatment\-effect estimation across healthcare systems without centralizing patient\-level data\. We review methods for binary, continuous, and time\-to\-event outcomes through three lenses: estimand, distributed\-computation protocol, and privacy mechanism\. We compare major strategies, clarify when federated analyses can approximate pooled results, and highlight unresolved challenges in confounding, heterogeneity, survival estimands, privacy–utility trade\-offs, validation, and software\. This framework supports rigorous evaluation of federated causal methods in healthcare\.
