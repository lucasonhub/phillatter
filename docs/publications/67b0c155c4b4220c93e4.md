# Saka, Hakan: Organizational Phenomenology: A Primer

**Autores:** 

**Data de descoberta:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** unknown

## Descrição do feed

A Short Guide to the OP Research Programme\. This short document is a companion to the Organizational Phenomenology \(OP\) research programme&\#x27;s Series Overview\. Where the Series Overview is a bibliographic map of the programme&\#x27;s five papers and companion computational works, this Primer is a conceptual entry point: a plain\-language account of what OP claims, using minimal formalism and concrete illustrative cases \(the thermal grill illusion, blindsight, and memory reconsolidation\) in place of the technical apparatus developed in the full papers\. It introduces the F1/F2 architecture, the compression singularity, Positioning and Organizational Bonds, the programme&\#x27;s qualia taxonomy, its relation to established theories of consciousness \(IIT, Global Workspace Theory, Enactivism, Attention Schema Theory, and Predictive Processing\), and its testable\-predictions programme\. It closes with an explicit statement of what the programme does not claim, and a glossary\. The Primer is not a substitute for the series&\#x27; formal definitions, proofs, or citation record; where it simplifies, the source papers are authoritative\.
