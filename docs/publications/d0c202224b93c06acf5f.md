# DEVELOPING STUDENTS&\#x27; METACOGNITIVE COMPETENCE THROUGH GENERATIVE ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE TOOLS

**Autores:** Yodgora Zayriddinova

**Data:** 2026-10-10 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Modern higher education requires graduates who are able to manage their own learning independently, critically and responsibly\. This ability is described by the concept of metacognitive competence, which includes knowledge about one&\#x27;s own cognition and the regulation of learning through planning, monitoring and evaluation\. The growing availability of generative artificial intelligence \(GenAI\) tools offers new means of supporting these processes, but it also raises the concern that learners may delegate their thinking to the machine\. This paper examines how GenAI tools can be used to develop students&\#x27; metacognitive competence\. Drawing on Flavell&\#x27;s theory of metacognition, Zimmerman&\#x27;s cyclical model of self\-regulated learning and the principle of scaffolding, the study identifies the functions that GenAI can perform in relation to each component of metacognition, describes a sequence of pedagogical techniques for their implementation, and discusses the main risks and ways of reducing them\. The paper concludes with recommendations for teachers and higher education institutions\.
