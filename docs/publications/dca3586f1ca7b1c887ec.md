# Epileptic seizure onset early warning using a lightweight multimodal self\-explainable neural network

**Autores:** Daraje Kaba Gurmessa; Kula Kekeba Tune; Worku Jimma

**Data:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

BackgroundCutting\-edge deep learning algorithms are applied to epileptic seizures to uncover hidden patterns and achieve high accuracy\. Post hoc algorithms explain black\-box models but do not reveal the detailed workings of the algorithms\. Therefore, intrinsic methods overcome these issues; however, they suffer from improper use of indicative metrics and performance\-to\-accuracy trade\-offs\.ObjectiveThis study implements a lightweight multimodal explainable Neural Network for real\-time early warning of epileptic seizure onset\.MethodsPeople with drug\-resistant epilepsy across all age groups who had regular follow\-up at Emanuel Mental Specialized Hospital in Ethiopia during the data collection period were the study population\. EEG recordings were performed using the 10/20 system\. A pretest was conducted two weeks before actual data collection\. EDFbrowser was used for preparing the dataset in CSV format\. Learned and identity self\-explainable neural network algorithms were used to develop a model\. In addition to classical evaluation metrics, explicitness, faithfulness, stability, and clear patterns behind predictions were used\. Ethical approval was also obtained from the institutional review board \(IRB\) with reference number No: AM/146/2/36\.ResultsLearned and identity self\-explainable neural networks were developed and compared\. The models&\#x27; accuracies are 96\.524% and 95\.328%, and their stability mean values are 99\.88% and 99\.88%, respectively\. Additionally, explicitness values of 0\.0055 and 0\.01197 were achieved, respectively\.ConclusionThe results demonstrate that using a self\-explainable neural network is a promising step toward responsible and transparent artificial intelligence\. Comparing the two models shows that the learned self\-explainable neural network is slightly more optimistic than the identity self\-explainable neural network\.
