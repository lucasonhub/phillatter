# Deeply Supervised 3D U\-Net with Auxiliary Classification for Cerebral Aneurysms Segmentation

**Autores:** Mario Mata\-Castillo; Nelly Gordillo; Boris Mederos; Dora‐Luz Flores; Vicente García; José Díaz

**Data:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Cerebral aneurysms are dilations of intracranial arteries whose early detection is essential for treatment planning and rupture prevention\. Automated aneurysm segmentation remains challenging because of severe class imbalance and the small size of many lesions\. This study presents a 3D U\-Net incorporating deep supervision and an auxiliary classification branch, trained on the public ADAM dataset using skeleton\-guided patch extraction\. Fixed\-size patches \(64 × 64 × 64\) were extracted from volumes resampled to 0\.4 mm isotropic resolution, and performance was evaluated using stratified five\-fold cross\-validation\. The proposed model achieved a Dice coefficient of 0\.425 \(95% CI: 0\.362–0\.488\) and a false\-positive \(FP\) patch rate of 0\.026 \(95% CI: 0\.017–0\.035\) on the test set\. For aneurysms larger than 2\.5 mm, the Dice coefficient increased to 0\.548 \(95% CI: 0\.485–0\.612\), while the FP patch rate was 0\.030 \(95% CI: 0\.018–0\.042\), indicating improved performance on clinically relevant lesions\. Statistical analysis confirmed that the proposed model was significantly superior to the compared models in both the Dice coefficient and FP patch rate\. Although the framework produces patch\-level rather than whole\-volume segmentations, visual analysis demonstrates accurate localization of aneurysm regions\. These results indicate that combining skeleton\-guided patch extraction with deep supervision and auxiliary classification improves segmentation performance while providing reliable localization cues for automated aneurysm detection\.
