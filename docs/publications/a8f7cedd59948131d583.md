# Biased AI in Recruitment as a Socio\-Legal Issue of Right to Equality under Article 14 and Emerging Legal Responses in India

**Autores:** Kavita Jagannath Garad

**Data:** 2026-10-15 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Examining a problem through the law and society framework is one way to test whether modern legal structures can cope with the changes that new technologies bring\. Recently, AI\-based recruitment methods, considered fairer and more efficient, have introduced inequality in India\. This paper addresses AI recruitment bias as a present socio\-legal issue that undermines the equality guaranteed by Article 14 and the prohibition of discrimination in Articles 15 and 16 of the Constitution of India\. Because AI tools are trained on past data carrying inherent biases against women, Scheduled Castes, Scheduled Tribes, persons with disabilities and persons from particular regions, that inequality is carried forward into the future\. Existing laws such as the Information Technology Act, 2000, the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, and the Equal Remuneration Act, 1976 \(since replaced by the Code on Wages, 2019\) are silent on algorithmic audit and on a right to explanation\. An analysis of constitutional and statutory provisions, of important judicial pronouncements such as K\.S\. Puttaswamy and Anwar Ali Sarkar, and of global instruments such as the EU AI Act \(2024\) brings out substantive equality, non\-arbitrariness, transparency and accountability as emerging legal answers\. The study proposes bias audits, impact assessments and a right to human review\. The research is purely doctrinal and fits the seminar theme of identifying socio\-legal challenges and emerging legal responses\.
