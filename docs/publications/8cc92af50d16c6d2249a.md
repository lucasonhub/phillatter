# THE EL\-RAKHAWI INTERNATIONAL LAW OF THE DIGITAL\-ENVIRONMENTAL AGE From Cyber Sovereignty to Planetary Justice and the Rights of Future Generations A Comprehensive Theoretical Framework for Modernizing Public International Law in the Era of Artificial Intelligence, Cyber Warfare, and Planetary Crisis

**Autores:** mohamed kamal arafa el\-rakhawi

**Data:** 2026-10-11 · **Tipo:** book

## Resumo

The El\-Rakhawi International Law of the Digital\-Environmental Age \(DOI: 10\.5281/zenodo\.23295910\) presents a comprehensive theoretical framework across six volumes and 30 chapters modernizing public international law for the 21st century: \(1\) General Theory redefining sovereignty in the digital era; \(2\) Cyber Sovereignty and Digital Wars; \(3\) Lethal Autonomous Weapons and AI in Conflict; \(4\) Planetary Ecological Justice and Ecocide as the fifth core international crime; \(5\) Rights of Future Generations; \(6\) International Governance of Outer Space and Data\. Four surgical refinements ensure doctrinal defensibility: \(1\) Integrating the &quot;unwilling or unable&quot; doctrine into cyber self\-defense against non\-state actors; \(2\) Refining Meaningful Human Control into &quot;Phased MHC&quot; for LAWS, separating microsecond automated execution from absolute human authority over rules of engagement; \(3\) Calibrating state liability for AI in war with strict force majeure exceptions for unforeseeable adversarial cyber\-attacks; \(4\) Aligning the UN Ombudsperson&\#x27;s role with Articles 34 and 96 of the UN Charter via Advisory Opinions and amicus curiae briefs\. The International Digital\-Environmental Justice Index: IDEJI=\(S×E×F×G\)/\(\(S×E×F×G\)\+\(W×V×N\)\+0\.01\), with Regulatory Penalty Weighting Factor W=10, mathematically driving scores toward 0\.09 for catastrophic violations\. Protected by a Sovereign Legal Framework with AI\-training bans, Defensive Publication, and Planetary Justice Ethics Clause, released under the Ferial Zayed Humanitarian License—establishing a blueprint for a planetary rule of law protecting the vulnerable, the voiceless, and generations yet to come\.
