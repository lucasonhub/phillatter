# PEDAGOGIK PARADIGMALARNING O‘ZGARISHI VA O‘ZARO MUNOSABATI: NAZARIY\-METODOLOGIK TAHLIL

**Autores:** Sokina To‘xtayeva

**Data:** 2026-10-14 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

The article analyzes the scientific and philosophical content of the concept of “pedagogical paradigm”, the internal regularities of the historical change of pedagogical paradigms, and their interrelation\. The aim of the study is to substantiate the concepts of “paradigmatic pluralism” and “pedagogical synthesis” through a conceptual analysis of this change and interrelation\. Comparative\-historical, conceptual, systemic, and hermeneutic methods were applied\. The possibilities and limitations of transferring T\. Kuhn’s theory of scientific paradigms to pedagogy are substantiated, and the stages of transition from the epistemological paradigm to the humanistic and then the competence\-based \(pragmatic\) paradigm are described\. Three regularities of paradigm change in pedagogy are identified \- gradual change, paradigmatic multilayeredness, and selective synthesis \- and five types of interrelation between paradigms are classified\. It is concluded that pedagogical paradigms do not completely replace one another but develop through integrative harmonization, while pedagogical synthesis, unlike eclecticism, is a theoretically grounded integration\.
