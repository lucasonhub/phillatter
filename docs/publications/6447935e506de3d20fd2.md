# UCEC: A Knowledge Graph\-Assisted Machine Learning for Molecular\-Mechanism\-Interpretable Natural Product\-Disease Association Prioritization

**Autores:** Wenyu Lu; Yihui Cai; Xiaoqian Peng; Shulan Mei; Xunkai Yin; Zhe Zheng; Zhenzhen Zhu; Songyun Xu; Hanyuan Zhang; Yue Wang; Weijiang Lin; Xia Zhang; Lihong Hu; Jian Liu

**Data:** 2026-10-15 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Abstract Natural products \(NPs\) have been considered a critical source of therapeutic drugs, but prioritizing NP\-disease associations remain challenging because supporting evidence is distributed across multiple ingredients, targets, protein interactions, and pathways\. Herein, we propose a knowledge graph\-assisted machine learning model: Uncertainty\-Calibrated Evidence\-Chains \(UCEC\) for prioritizing NP\-disease associations\. UCEC separates predicted association strength from the evidence\-conditioned stability of local mechanistic support\. On a proxy benchmark designed to recover graph\-supported indirect associations, evidence\-chain integration increased Hits@50 from 0\.392 to 0\.509, with 68\.8% of NPs indicating improved per\-NP AUPRC\. Source\-separated validation using HERB 2\.0 clinical trial records further supported candidate prioritization\. Thus, UCEC provides a reliability\-aware approach for prioritizing graph\-supported NP\-disease candidates and generating traceable mechanistic hypotheses for subsequent validation\.
