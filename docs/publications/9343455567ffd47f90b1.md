# Analysis of the evolution of dominant attributes and composition reconstruction of flood risk from dam failures in parallel reservoir groups

**Autores:** Chunxin Sun; Jinjun Zhou; Jiahong Liu; Ruirui Sun; Cheng Qin; Songyun Jin; Hao Wang; Yingdong Yu

**Data:** 2026-10-09 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Study region The parallel reservoir groups in Zhaotong City, Yunnan Province, China\. Study focus Based on Monte Carlo simulation, identify Local Failure Scenarios \(LFS\) and construct Global Failure Scenarios \(GCS\); simulate dam\-break flood processes using DB\-IWHR–HEC\-RAS; and, combining improved projection pursuit, Attribute Pattern Dominance Identification, and compositional data analysis methods, analyze the risk organizational characteristics and their evolution for different scenarios and risk areas from the perspective of relative composition of Hazard \(H\), Exposure \(E\), and Vulnerability \(V\)\. New hydrological insights for the region Multi\-source dam\-break flood convergence triggered by overall failure leads to scale\-dependent reconstruction of the risk structure and reorganizes the relative compositional relationships of H–E–V\. At the regional scale, newly inundated areas shift the overall risk composition in a hazard\-dominated direction; within the commonly inundated areas, as flood hazards tend to saturate, the risk composition mainly shows relatively increased vulnerability\. The Aitchison distances at the regional and inundation zone scales are 1\.514 and 7\.807, respectively, indicating that risk reconstruction is mainly concentrated within the flood\-affected areas\. The evolution path of risk\-dominant attributes changes from EH\_mix–EHV\_mix–EHV\_mix under LFS conditions to H–EHV\_mix–E under GCS conditions, and risk organization evolves from a multi\-attribute coordination pattern to a single dominant attribute pattern\.
