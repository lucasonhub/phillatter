# TIBBIYOT BO‘YICHA BAKALAVRIAT TA’LIMIDA MOSLASHTIRILGAN EKRANLI SIMULYATOR ASOSIDA MODULLI MASHG‘ULOTLAR MAJMUASINI ISHLAB CHIQISH VA JORIY ETISH

**Autores:** QOBILJON NIGMATILLAEVICH TURSUNBAEV

**Data:** 2026-10-09 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

This article reports on the adaptation of the Filatov screen\-based simulator \(GEOTAR\-Media\) to the healthcare system of the Republic of Uzbekistan and on the design and implementation of a three\-module set of simulation\-based classes built on it\. The adaptation comprised translation of the interface into Uzbek, nosological revision of the scenario library, alignment of diagnostic and treatment algorithms with national clinical protocols, and technical modification of the software\. The resulting set comprises 36 classes \(108 academic hours\) and 136 clinical scenarios distributed across basic, core and advanced modules\. It was implemented with 285 students in their 3rd to 6th years of study\. In all modules, task performance improved significantly from the first to the final class \(p&lt;0\.001\)\. The basic module yielded the largest knowledge gain, 34\.1±7\.8 points or 80\.6% \(p&lt;0\.001\)\. The advanced module produced the highest final score \(79\.6±9\.8\) and the largest proportion of excellent grades \(52\.1%; p&lt;0\.05\)\. Overall, 90\.2% of students rated the training positively\. Experts gave the adapted simulator a mean score of 8\.5 out of 10, with high inter\-rater agreement \(Kendall’s W=0\.78; p&lt;0\.001\)\. These findings support the integration of the adapted screen\-based simulator and the modular set of classes into undergraduate medical training in Uzbekistan\.
