# CNN\-Transformer with Sigmoid Label for High\-Resolution Sub\-Degree DOA Estimation

**Autores:** Lifeng Wang; Xintong Li; Aifei Liu

**Data:** 2026-10-09 · **Tipo:** preprint

## Resumo

This paper addresses the grid quantization error of deep\-learning \(DL\)\-based direction\-of\-arrival \(DOA\) estimation\. Classification\- based DOA methods discretize the continuous angular space into a grid, so that with a peak\-based read\-out, the accuracy and resolution are limited by the grid step and by the width of the label peak\. To address this issue, we propose a sigmoid label that encodes the sources&\#x27; DOAs as the sum of angle\-shifted monotonic sigmoid functions\. Differentiating the output of a CNN\-Transformer network trained with the sigmoid label produces sharp spectral peaks at the source DOAs\. Combined with a moderately refined grid step of 0\.25 degrees, the CNN\-Transformer with the proposed sigmoid label attains sub\-degree accuracy and a 1\-degree resolution, breaking the limit of the grid quantization error\. Under the unified CNN\-Transformer backbone, we compare the proposed Sigmoid label with the Gaussian label, the 0\-1 hard label, and the binary cross\-entropy \(BCE\) baseline\. Numerical results demonstrate that the Sigmoid label attains the highest accuracy and superior resolution compared to other labels\.
