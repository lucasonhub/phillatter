# The Impact of Cyberworthiness on Corporate Organisations and Defence Organizations

**Autores:** Saad Almarri

**Data:** 2026-10-12 · **Tipo:** dissertation

## Resumo

The growth of interconnected digital systems and the Internet of Things \(IoT\) has enlarged the cyber\-attack surface of modern corporate and defence organisations\. In this setting, cyberworthiness denotes a system&\#x27;s capacity to operate securely, reliably, and continuously under adversarial conditions\. This thesis treats cyberworthiness as a multidimensional construct that extends beyond conventional cybersecurity by combining security and reliability with resilience, adaptability, and mission assurance across complex cyber\-physical systems that support corporate operations and defence\-relevant deployments\. ​The thesis makes four contributions, each responding to a distinct organisational challenge\. First, it presents a structured review of cyberworthiness standards, governance frameworks, and software assurance models, identifying fragmentation and the lack of automated, end\-to\-end assessment mechanisms\. Second, it proposes a federated deep learning intrusion detection framework for privacy\-preserving cyberworthiness assessment in distributed IoT environments; a four\-layer architecture yields strong detection performance, cross\-dataset generalisation, and improved communication efficiency\. Third, it introduces CyberFND\-Net, a dual\-stream convolutional network for fake news detection that combines semantic and structural features with a Cyberworthiness\-Aware Loss to reflect asymmetric operational risks, and reports superior results across multiple datasets\. Fourth, it presents a six\-dimensional Digital Twin architecture for IoT\-enhanced combat systems that embeds cybersecurity as an intrinsic design dimension and demonstrates improved detection, lower latency, and substantial reductions in mean time to mitigation\. Collectively, these contributions advance a coherent cyberworthiness agenda and provide empirically supported methods for improving security, resilience, and mission assurance in corporate and defence digital ecosystems\.
