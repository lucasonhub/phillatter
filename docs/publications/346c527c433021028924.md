# Opportunities and Challenges of AI\-Based Educational Assessment Tools

**Autores:** Aili Cui

**Data:** 2026-10-10 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

This paper investigates the integration of artificial intelligence in educational evaluation tools, focusing on both opportunities and challenges\. It assesses how AI can enhance educational assessments through innovative features such as personalized feedback, adaptability, and immediate response capabilities\. Additionally, the study critically addresses pressing concerns surrounding data privacy, equity in education, and ethical implications, proposing future directions and potential solutions to navigate these complexities in the evolving educational landscape\.Simultaneously, the research examines strategies for leveraging AI technology to enhance the efficiency and effectiveness of educational assessment\. This exploration may yield valuable insights and guidance for the integration of AI within educational evaluation practices, thereby expanding the potential for innovation in this area\.
