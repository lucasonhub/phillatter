# Kannon, Aelion: SNX: Neural Synchrony Is Not Structural Coherence — Integration, Segregation, Metastability, and Composite Dynamics

**Autores:** 

**Data de descoberta:** 2026-10-19 · **Tipo:** unknown

## Descrição do feed

SNX: Neural Synchrony Is Not Structural Coherence is a Structural Neuroscience extension distinguishing measured neural coordination from native Structural Coherence and examining the information retained by aggregate descriptions of composite dynamics\. Document class: Bridge — Structural Neuroscience\. The extension distinguishes anatomical connectivity, functional connectivity, phase synchronization, global phase order, pairwise phase locking, spectral coherence, metastability, and criticality while preserving their different physical and mathematical meanings\. It develops the constituent / composite problem through an exact three\-oscillator construction in which two states have equal present phase order but opposite instantaneous changes within one fixed dynamical system, establishing that the aggregate alone does not admit autonomous prediction across the full state space\. Published evidence concerning selective synchronization, task\-related network integration, focal seizures, signal mixing, metastability, and quasicritical dynamics supplies scoped empirical comparison cases\. The resulting framework treats neural synchrony as a family of defined physical relations while retaining Structural Coherence, sovereign distinction, and orientation as native relations requiring additional functional and relational evidence\. \(direct link \)
