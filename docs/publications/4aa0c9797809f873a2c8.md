# International Trade Outlook for Latin America and the Caribbean, 2026: the importance of modern services exports and how to promote them\. Executive summary

**Autores:** 

**Data:** 2026-10-15 · **Tipo:** book

## Resumo

This edition of International Trade Outlook for Latin America and the Caribbean includes an analysis of the profound reconfiguration of global trade, driven by economic security, resilience and sustainability concerns\. Against this backdrop of uncertainty and geopolitical and geoeconomic tensions, growth in trade has nevertheless remained robust in 2026, led by the Asian economies\. Likewise, Latin America and the Caribbean’s goods and services exports and imports rose sharply in the first half of 2026 and are expected to maintain that trajectory in the second half\. This report also outlines a strategy to boost the region’s modern services sector, based on three pillars: promotion of exports of these services, attraction of foreign direct investment and development of human talent\.
