# Research on Generative AI\-Enabled Emotional Brand Design Based on the S\-O\-R Model and Combined Weighting Methods

**Autores:** Zekuang Li; Jing Liu

**Data:** 2026-10-11 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

With the rapid development of generative AI technology, artificial intelligence is transitioning from a supporting tool to a creative design partner\. This study draws upon the S\-O\-R \(Stimulus\-Organism\-Response\) model and Norman’s three\-level theory of emotional design to construct a theoretical framework and an operational evaluation indicator system for assessing how generative AI\-driven brand emotional design may shape consumer responses\. The study employs the Analytic Hierarchy Process, entropy weight method, and game theory\-based combined weighting to derive subjective and objective weights for nine evaluation indicators\. Ten respondents \(five brand designers and five consumers\) participated in the weight determination stage, while twelve evaluators \(six designers and six consumers\) assessed the design proposals using fuzzy comprehensive evaluation\.
