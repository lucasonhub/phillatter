# Искусственный интеллект и философия науки: возможности и проблемы научного познания

**Autores:** Ақбота Наурызбекова; Айзат Мұқатаева; Дана Зейнолла; Аяжан Альдибекова

**Data:** 2026-10-10 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

The article examines the influence of artificial intelligence on contemporary scientific inquiry from the perspective of the philosophy of science\. The relevance of the study is associated with the expanding use of artificial intelligence systems at different stages of research, including information retrieval, data processing, hypothesis generation, data analysis and preparation of scientific texts\. The aim of the study is to identify the main opportunities and problems that arise when artificial intelligence becomes part of the production of scientific knowledge\. The research uses theoretical analysis, comparative analysis and synthesis of scholarly publications and normative materials concerning the use of artificial intelligence in science\. The historical relationship between scientific inquiry and the development of new research tools is also considered\. The results lead to a three\-level analytical framework for evaluating AI\-assisted scientific results: factual verification, assessment of scientific justification, and preservation of human responsibility for final scientific claims\. The article argues that artificial intelligence can expand the cognitive capabilities of researchers, but scientific knowledge cannot be reduced to automatic generation of information\. The study concludes that AI should be treated as a research instrument that requires critical verification and human oversight\.
