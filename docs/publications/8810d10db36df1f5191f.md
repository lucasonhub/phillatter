# A double inertial method for non\-monotone variational inequality problem

**Autores:** Olayinka Martins Onifade; Austine Efut Ofem; S\. A\. Kajola; Chinedu Izuchukwu; Ojen Kumar Narain

**Data:** 2026-10-17 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Abstract In this paper, we propose a viscosity\-type double inertial algorithm with self\-adaptive stepsizes for solving non\-monotone variational inequality problems involving uniformly continuous operators in real Hilbert spaces\. The proposed method combines viscosity approximation and double inertial extrapolation techniques with a self\-adaptive stepsize rule that does not require prior knowledge of the Lipschitz constant\. Under suitable conditions, we establish a strong convergence of the generated sequence without assuming monotonicity, Lipschitz continuity, line\-search procedures, or additional projection techniques\. The obtained results extend several existing inertial and viscosity methods to a broader class of non\-monotone and non\-Lipschitz variational inequality problems\. Numerical examples are presented to illustrate the effectiveness of the proposed algorithm\.
