# A Mixed\-Initiative Procedural Workflow for Fulldome Scene Assembly from Layered 2D Illustrations

**Autores:** Halim Yentür

**Data:** 2026-10-10 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Transferring layered 2D illustrations to immersive fulldome environments is challenging: manual 3D modeling does not scale to scenes containing several hundred elements, while AI\-based monocular depth estimation \(MDE\) suffers from domain shift on stylized and panoramic content and lacks deterministic artist control\. Existing approaches address procedural automation, artist\-driven calibration, and dome\-specific layout separately rather than in one mixed\-initiative pipeline\. This study proposes a mixed\-initiative procedural workflow that transforms layered 2D illustrations into 3D fulldome scenes with stable placement and billboard orientation under a fixed dome camera, requiring no recomputation under camera rotation\. The system uses a horizon\-parameterized depth mapping heuristic converting pictorial height into radial depth, cylindrical mapping for 360° layout, and figure\-derived terrain fitting via implicit surface reconstruction\. Hierarchical parametric calibration preserves artist control over depth and scale at global and category levels\. The workflow was tested on a 400\-element historical visualization project\. It achieved full procedural placement coverage across eligible scene elements with no per\-element manual correction, completed in three parameter\-adjustment iterations\. Across 400 automatically identified overlapping figure pairs, determined by pixel\-level alpha\-mask intersection without manual selection, occlusion\-order consistency with source\-layer relations was 357/400 \(89\.25%\)\. The mean ground\-contact correction after raycasting was 0\.62 m, with 78% of corrections within ±1 m\. Production time was estimated to improve approximately 30\-fold\. Rule\-based depth mapping with mixed\-initiative calibration thus offers a viable, deterministic, artist\-controllable approach for fulldome scene assembly from stylized panoramic content\.
