# Digital Twin\-Based Modeling and Optimization of Continuous Biomanufacturing Processes: A Data\-Driven Simulation Study

**Autores:** ijbes

**Data:** 2026-10-15 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Continuous biomanufacturing, particularly perfusion\-based monoclonal\-antibody production, offers highproductivity and consistent quality but requires faster, more reliable monitoring and control thanconventional fed\-batch operation\. This paper reviews digital\-twin\-based optimisation of continuousbiomanufacturing and presents an illustrative, reproducible simulation informed by publicly availablebioprocess datasets\. A three\-tier digital\-model, digital\-shadow, and digital\-twin architecture is examinedalongside mechanistic, data\-driven, and hybrid modelling paradigms\. The simulation demonstrates modelpredictive control of glucose set\-point tracking in a perfusion bioreactor\. Across the reviewed studies andillustrative results, hybrid mechanistic\-machine\-learning twins provide stronger predictive performancethan purely mechanistic or data\-driven alternatives, while digital\-twin\-based control reduces glucosedeviation relative to conventional control and open\-loop feeding\. Key barriers include fragmenteddatasets, model validation, regulatory acceptance, cybersecurity, and cross\-scale transferability\. The studytherefore recommends open, standardised bioprocess datasets and rigorous deployment validation tosupport reproducible, industrially credible digital\-twin research\.
