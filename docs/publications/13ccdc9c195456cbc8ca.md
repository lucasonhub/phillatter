# ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE IN THE MODERN ERA: YOUTH EDUCATION AND THE CULTURE OF INDEPENDENT THINKING

**Autores:** Mardonova Marjona Erkinovna4

**Data:** 2026-10-15 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

This article examines the role of artificial intelligence \(AI\) in modern education, its potential to improve young people&\#x27;s learning experiences, and its influence on the development of independent thinking\. It also discusses challenges associated with AI use, including critical evaluation of information, academic integrity, and digital literacy\. Furthermore, the article proposes an original three\-step approach designed to encourage students to use artificial intelligence responsibly while developing their analytical and creative thinking skills\.
