# ASCPT News

**Autores:** 

**Data:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

As Giving Tuesday approaches, we are reminded of the real impact your generosity has on the ASCPT Low\-to\-Middle\-Income \(LMIC\) Accelerator Program\-a program made possible entirely through charitable contributions from donors like you\.For researchers in low\-and\-middle\-income countries, the program offers more than funding\-it offers validation\.Participants have described the Accelerator as a powerful affirmation of the work being done in underrepresented regions, raising the visibility of critical health challenges on the global stage\.The LMIC Accelerator program provides structured mentorship and hosts networking sessions where members of the cohort can share experiences, triumphs, and frustrations\.Through the program, they gain confidence and skills to drive meaningful change in resource\-limited regions, but they also learn that they are not alone\.They are able to find their voice within the global clinical pharmacology community\.Many alumni point to the same turning point: the moment they realized their work\-and their perspective\-belonged in the global conversations\.That shift in confidence ripples outward, shaping careers and, ultimately, the future of patient care worldwide\.To our past donors: thank you\.Your generosity continues to open doors for scientific leaders\.To those considering a gift: your support will make that same opportunity possible for future cohorts participants\.Visit our website and donate to the ASCPT initiatives you care about most \(https:// www\. ascpt\. org/ Resou rces/ Donat ions\)\.
