# Thomas, Emily: C592The Genesis of J\. M\. E\. McTaggart’s “The Unreality of Time” 1893–1908

**Autores:** 

**Data de descoberta:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** unknown

## Descrição do feed

In Real Time: A Reinvention in British Metaphysics 1883\-1928\. Oxford University Press\. 2026McTaggart’s 1908 “The Unreality of Time” has long vexed philosophers\. Despite the fascination with this argument for time’s unreality, there is very little work on its origins\. This chapter investigates and poses a puzzle\. In his 1893 to 1901 work on Hegel, McTaggart pronounced himself unable to explain how the appearance of time arises from a timeless reality\. Although McTaggart wrestles with time in this early work, there are no indications he is reaching a solution\. Yet, in 1908, McTaggart bursts forth with a novel argument for the unreality of time and a novel explanation for why we misperceive time as real\. What happened between 1901 and 1908? The author argues he developed a clutch of fresh views, sparked by engagement with a source outside Hegel: new work on the specious present\.
