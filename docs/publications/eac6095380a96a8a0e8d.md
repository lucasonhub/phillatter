# Physics\-informed acoustic–vibration fusion for all\-ceramic bearing fault diagnosis under complex operating conditions

**Autores:** Xiaotong Li; Ke Zhang; Xu Duo Bai; Tianhao Gao; Song Liang; Lixiu Zhang; Basem Ibrahim Esawy Selim; Yang Hong

**Data:** 2026-10-14 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Abstract Complex noise and variable operating conditions substantially reduce the observability of fault\-induced impact and modulation features in acoustic–vibration measurements of all\-ceramic bearings and create modality\-dependent differences in signal reliability\. To address this problem, this study proposes PH\-FusionNet, a physics\-informed network for acoustic–vibration signal fusion and fault diagnosis\. The proposed method follows a progressive framework consisting of local physics\-prior enhancement, global representation stabilization, and reliability\-aware fusion\. First, a Laplace–Morlet physics\-prior feature extraction layer is developed based on the impact–resonance mechanism to enhance fault\-related features, including vibration impact responses and acoustic modulation wave packets\. Subsequently, a long\-range global competitive representation enhancement module is designed to aggregate long\-range contextual information and recalibrate global responses, thereby strengthening fault\-related representations while suppressing non\-stationary noise interference\. Finally, an impulse\-guided gated fusion mechanism is introduced, in which vibration impact responses serve as structural references for selective acoustic\-feature injection and vibration\-conditioned fusion\. Experimental results show that, across five representative noise conditions, PH\-FusionNet achieved an average diagnostic accuracy of 86\.84%, outperforming the second\-best model by 3\.48 percentage points, while attaining an average FNR of 13\.16%\. The model also retained comparatively robust diagnostic performance under asymmetric modality degradation and simulated speed\-transition conditions, thereby demonstrating the effectiveness and robustness of the proposed method\.
