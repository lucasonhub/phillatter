# DS\-InSAR combined with dual\-attention enhanced CNN\-BiGRU for deformation prediction of typical landslides in the Jinsha River Basin

**Autores:** Boyu An; Yanxia WANG; Jun Yan; Benhao Wang; Xiaoyu Ni; Xiaoxiao Jiang; Puchen Zhao

**Data:** 2026-10-12 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Accurate prediction of step\-like abrupt landslide deformation is critical for disaster early warning but remains hindered by the over\-smoothing bias of conventional models\. This study proposes an end\-to\-end prediction framework integrating Distributed Scatterer InSAR \(DS\-InSAR\) monitoring data with a dual\-attention enhanced CNN\-BiGRU network\. Two architectures—direct\-output and Sequence\-to\-Sequence \(Seq2Seq\)—were validated on the Guoba Landslide, a large landslide in the Jinsha River Basin\. The dual attention mechanism adaptively isolates critical precursor features, enhancing both precision and interpretability\. A global evaluation across all measurement points delineates their domains of applicability\. The direct\-output DA\-CNN\-BiGRU acts as a robust spatial filter, achieving a global R2 of 0\.92 for ubiquitous continuous creep\. Conversely, the Seq2Seq\-DA\-CNN\-BiGRU is specifically optimised to mitigate this over\-smoothing bias and capture severe step\-like deformations, achieving an R2 of 0\.636 at a representative critical point where baselines fail\. Across representative kinematic points, it reduces average RMSE and MAE by 24\.02% and 30\.04%, respectively, compared to standard CNN\-BiGRU\. This study provides a targeted, interpretable early\-warning scheme for complex landslides in high\-altitude canyon regions\.
