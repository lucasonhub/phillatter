# Multi\-method NLP analysis of stakeholder discourse and coordination challenges in the industrial hemp value chain

**Autores:** Yihan Huang; Md Mayedul Islam; Md Abdul Quddus; Andre J\. West; A\.M\.M\. Nazmul Ahsan; Sudipta Chowdhury

**Data:** 2026-10-11 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Industrial hemp is re\-emerging as a strategically important crop in the United States, yet prior studies suggest that its development is hampered by coordination challenges across agriculture, processing, textiles, composites, and the broader value chain\. While prior studies identify technical, economic, and regulatory barriers, less attention has been paid to how stakeholders across sectors perceive and articulate these challenges\. To address this gap, this study applies natural language processing \(NLP\) and large language model \(LLM\)\-assisted analysis to secondary data from structured roundtable discussions at the 2025 Global Industrial Hemp Fiber Summit\. The summit brought together approximately 200 participants across five thematic areas, providing a multi\-stakeholder corpus that captures perspectives from different segments of the hemp fiber industry\. Using TF\-IDF keyword extraction, comparative topic modeling, and multi\-method sentiment analysis, we explore latent thematic groupings that suggest potential cross\-sector coordination barriers and perceived opportunities within the hemp industry\. The findings provide a methodological framework for specialized discourse analysis and practical insights for policy, investment, and value\-chain coordination in emerging hemp industries\.
