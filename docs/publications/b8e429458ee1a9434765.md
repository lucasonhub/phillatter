# Comments on “Global Terminal Sliding‐Mode Control for the Synchronization Problem of Uncertain Bilateral Teleoperation System With Time‐Varying Delays”

**Autores:** Yuxin Su

**Data:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

ABSTRACT Assumption 4 and the claims that finite‐time convergence of tracking errors in Theorems 1 and 2 of \[A\. Ounissi, et\., Global terminal sliding‐mode control for the synchronization problem of uncertain bilateral teleoperation system with time‐varying delays, Asian J\. Control 26 \(2024\), 2180–2205\] are invalid, making the proposed controls therein untenable\. The circular argument problem of the proposed controls and other significant flaws are also pointed out\. The correction of Assumption 4 on the upper bound of the lumped uncertainties is presented\.
