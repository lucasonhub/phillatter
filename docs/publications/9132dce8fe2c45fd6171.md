# PARTISIPATIV BOSHQARUV KOMPETENSIYASI SHAKLLANISHINING EMOTSIONAL VA IRODAVIY\-REGULYATIV DETERMINANTLARI

**Autores:** NASIBA MUMINOVNA IBRAGIMOVA

**Data:** 2026-10-12 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

This article empirically explores the emotional and volitional\-regulatory determinants of participatory management competence among university students\. The diagnostic toolkit included methods by J\. Kuhl, Y\. Zharikov, T\.V\. Kornilova, and Y\. Krushelnitsky\. Statistical analysis using Pearson&\#x27;s correlation coefficient revealed significant relationships between volitional regulation metrics, decision\-making strategies \(vigilance, procrastination, avoidance\), and leadership orientation \(action vs\. state orientation\)\. The findings provide a methodological foundation for psychological and pedagogical programs aimed at enhancing managerial competence and emotional\-volitional resources in students\.
