# Post\-stroke sequelae risk score \(PSSRS\): a 16\-year retrospective study developing an internally validated multi\-algorithm model for post\-stroke sequelae

**Autores:** Yiqun Dong; Rongji Lu; Yuying Zhang; Yuteng Huang; Ziyi Wang; 孫吉滿; Siyi Li; Yuxin Jia; Ying Tian; Haodong Hu; XiFeng Li; Yanchao Liu; Chuan‐Zhi Duan

**Data:** 2026-10-15 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Introduction Post\-stroke sequelae \(PSS\) encompass persistent functional impairments after stroke and may benefit from early risk stratification\. We developed and internally validated a time\-to\-event prediction model based on routinely available clinical and biochemical variables\. Method This retrospective single\-center cohort included 2,095 patients hospitalized with acute stroke at Zhujiang Hospital between 2008 and 2024\. Thirty\-seven candidate variables were assessed\. The cohort was split 7:3 into training \( n = 1,467\) and internal test \( n = 628\) sets before preprocessing, feature selection, tuning, and model comparison\. Univariate Cox, multivariable Cox, least absolute shrinkage and selection operator \(LASSO\), and random survival forest \(RSF\) analyses identified eight intersection variables\. A total of 101 survival\-model pipelines were compared using training\-set resampling; the selected model was then evaluated in the internal test set\. Result The StepCox \(forward\) \+ RSF pipeline showed the highest prespecified training\-resampling performance\. Its Harrell C\-index was 0\.87 in the training set and 0\.85 in the internal test set\. Test\-set time\-dependent AUROCs at 1, 3, and 5 years were 0\.800 \(95% CI, 0\.760–0\.840\), 0\.830 \(0\.790–0\.870\), and 0\.820 \(0\.780–0\.860\), respectively; corresponding AUPRCs were 0\.748, 0\.772, and 0\.761, and Brier scores were 0\.140, 0\.170, and 0\.180\. Calibration slopes were 0\.96, 0\.93, and 0\.90\. LDH, CK\-MB, and DBIL were among the leading contributors in the model\-interpretation analyses\. Conclusion PSSRS provides an internally validated framework for estimating individualized risk of post\-stroke sequelae\. External temporal and multicenter validation is required before clinical implementation\.
