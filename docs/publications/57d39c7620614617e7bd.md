# QD\-Rec: Open\-Ended Evolution of Recommendations via Quality\-Diversity

**Autores:** Takashi Ishida; Taro Makino; Issa Sugiura

**Data:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** preprint

## Resumo

Open\-ended search combined with foundation models has produced advances such as faster algorithms, new results on open mathematical problems, and self\-improving agents\. We extend this paradigm to recommendation for decision\-making, focusing on decisions where the set of possible choices is fixed but the reasons for choosing them are not\. In high\-stakes decisions, e\.g\., which company to acquire or which customer to target, comparing diverse, well\-reasoned options can be more useful than a single ranked list\. We formalize open\-ended recommendation as building a diverse, high\-quality set of recommendations, each an item together with a hypothesis, a natural\-language reason why the item fits the needs of a decision maker\. We propose QD\-Rec, a quality\-\-diversity method that selects items with a contextual bandit and generates hypotheses with a foundation model from earlier ones kept in an archive as stepping stones\. A new recommendation enters the archive if it is novel or better than its nearest neighbor\. Across M&amp;A, customer targeting, and paper and book recommendation, QD\-Rec gives the highest mean quality and builds the largest archive in most settings\.
