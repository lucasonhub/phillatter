# Hospitality in the Digital Age: Innovation, Workforce Transformation, and Guest Experience

**Autores:** Amrik Singh

**Data:** 2026-10-15 · **Tipo:** book

## Resumo

Rapid technological progress, shifting guest expectations, and an increased focus on sustainable and personalized services are fueling a transformation in the hospitality industry\. Hospitality in the Digital Age: Innovation, Workforce Transformation, and Guest Experience investigates the synergy between technology, human resources, and hospitality service excellence management strategies in today&\#x27;s hotel sector\.This book explores the impact of new technologies such as artificial intelligence, automation, robotics, Internet of Things, virtual and augmented reality, data analytics, and smart hospitality systems for hotel operations and guest experiences\. The key role of workforce transformation, digital skills, employee engagement, leadership development and the human–technology interactions as core pillars to building resilient and future\-ready hospitality companies are also identified\. In addition, the book covers the potential positive and negative aspects of transitioning to a digital environment, such as cybersecurity, data privacy, ethical use of technology, sustainability and keeping the human touch in hospitality service\. Learning About new technologies and new theoretical research in real\-world terms and human dimensions will be a key aim of it\.The publication will be useful for academics, researchers, the hospitality industry, students and policy makers in this field as a way to gaining insights into the changing digital hospitality world\. Whether you&\#x27;re looking for a book about coding for the web or a refresher on basic sacred hospitality fundamentals, it is hoped the contributions are herein will inspire innovative thinking, encourage responsible technological adoption, and support the creation sustainable and competitive hospitality businesses geared for the digital age\.
