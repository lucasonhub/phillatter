# METHODOLOGY FOR DEVELOPING STUDENTS&\#x27; METACOGNITIVE COMPETENCE THROUGH GENERATIVE ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE TOOLS IN TEACHING ENGLISH

**Autores:** Yodgora Zayriddinova

**Data:** 2026-10-10 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Metacognitive competence, the ability to plan, monitor and evaluate one&\#x27;s own learning, is a key condition for successful and autonomous English language learning\. Generative artificial intelligence \(GenAI\) tools such as ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini and Copilot are widely used by students, but when they are used simply to obtain ready\-made answers they may weaken rather than develop learners&\#x27; self\-regulation\. This article presents a methodology for improving students&\#x27; metacognitive competence through GenAI based on a five\-phase cycle \(Plan, Prompt, Monitor, Evaluate, Reflect\) and a three\-stage model of gradually fading scaffolding\.
