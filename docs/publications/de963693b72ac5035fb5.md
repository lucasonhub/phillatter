# METHODOLOGICAL FOUNDATIONS OF USING ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE TOOLS IN PROGRAMMING EDUCATION

**Autores:** Farog&\#x27;at Fayzullayeva

**Data:** 2026-10-13 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

The article analyzes the penetration of artificial intelligence \(AI\) tools into programming education, their didactic opportunities and risks\. A four\-component methodological model is proposed for using AI tools while preserving students&\#x27; independent programming skills and algorithmic thinking: staged use of AI, developing AI\-collaboration competence, renewing the assessment system, and project\-based learning\. The model can be applied in teaching programming and computer science at higher education institutions\.
