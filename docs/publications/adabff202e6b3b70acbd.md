# SWORCA™: A Unified Digital and Physical Institution for Technological Invention, Interoperability, Commerce, and the Intelligent Economy

**Autores:** Rashon Rahming

**Data:** 2026-10-11 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

SWORCA™ \(Seattle Worldwide Organization for Regulated Capital &amp; Automation\) is a proposed global technological institution designed to advance the discovery, development, evaluation, interoperability, commercialization, and economically productive utilization of emerging and established technologies\. The institutional framework integrates two complementary foundations: SWORCA DIGITAL, a proposed globally accessible technological commerce and innovation platform, and SWORCA SEATTLE, the envisioned permanent physical headquarters and institutional landmark in Seattle, Washington, United States\. SWORCA DIGITAL proposes an integrated environment through which eligible consumers, independent inventors, developers, researchers, manufacturers, startups, and established organizations may discover, create, evaluate, acquire, license, exchange, and commercialize technological products, components, systems, services, and capabilities\. Its proposed scope encompasses artificial intelligence, autonomous agents, computational systems, semiconductors, computer hardware and software, robotics, cybersecurity, blockchain infrastructure, digital identity, data storage, data centers, and related technological industries\. The framework incorporates technological interoperability, collaborative invention, requirements\-based discovery, intellectual property and commercial rights management, evidence\-based technical evaluation, simulation, advertising, educational participation, and customer loyalty services\. These capabilities are organized through complementary functional domains intended to reduce fragmentation, improve technological accessibility, support informed purchasing decisions, and connect independent innovation with qualified commercial demand\. The proposed permanent Seattle institution would complement the digital infrastructure through physical technology experiences, professional collaboration, research, education, demonstrations, and institutional activities\. This white paper establishes the conceptual foundations, proposed architecture, commercial objectives, governance principles, regulatory boundaries, and phased development strategy of SWORCA\. It distinguishes proposed capabilities from implemented technologies and identifies technical validation, intellectual property review, commercial feasibility, and regulatory compliance as prerequisites for operational advancement\. SWORCA&\#x27;s central research proposition is that integrating technological discovery, interoperability, verifiable evidence, authorized commerce, and measurable outcomes within a unified institutional framework may improve participation, accountability, and economic efficiency across the evolving technological economy\.
