# Robust Navigation Corridor Extraction from Water Segmentation for Inland Autonomous Surface Vehicles

**Autores:** Bareq W\. Fathi; Ahmed N\. Rasheed

**Data:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Vision\-based perception is essential for autonomous surface vehicles operating in inland rivers, canals, and semi\-structured waterways\.This study presents an open\-loop, navigation\-oriented evaluation framework that transforms water\-segmentation masks into left and right boundaries, a centerline, corridor width, and exploratory plausibility and robustness indices\.All experiments were conducted on the same low\-resolution USVInland split of 293 training, 52 validation, and 173 test images at 640 × 320 resolution, using identical preprocessing, mask cleaning, corridor extraction, and per\-image evaluation procedures\.The evaluation includes a seed\-based heuristic, U\-Net, the proposed Edge U\-Net, and protocol\-controlled independent reproductions of two recent models, Fuse\-ResNet and ASSA\-ResNet\.Edge U\-Net achieved the strongest clean performance in the controlled comparison, with an IoU of 0\.964 \(95% CI: 0\.960\-0\.968\),a Boundary\-F1 score of 0\.685, and an average symmetric surface distance of 4\.18 pixels\.Paired image\-level analysis further showed that similar pixel\-overlap scores can coexist with meaningful differences in boundary displacement, centerline error, corridor\-width error, and failure behavior\.Weight\-sensitivity and failuretrigger analyses were therefore used to complement conventional segmentation metrics\.The proposed NCCS and Navigation Robustness Score are treated as heuristic descriptive indices rather than calibrated safety measures\.Overall, the framework provides a reproducible bridge between water segmentation and corridor\-oriented geometric evaluation, while not establishing obstacle\-aware or closed\-loop navigation safety\.
