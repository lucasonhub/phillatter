# INFLUENCE OF POLYPROPYLENE IMPLANTS COATED WITH TANTALUM AND ITS DERIVATIVES ON INFLAMMATION AND REGENERATION PROCESSES

**Autores:** O\. V\. Kyslov; Оксана Анатоліївна Наконечна; Liudmyla D Popova; С\. А\. Денисенко; Ievgen Ivanov

**Data:** 2026-10-17 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Introduction\. Polypropylene implants are widely used in surgical practice; however, some authors point to numerous postoperative complications after their use, the main cause of which is the development of aseptic inflammation\. This necessitates the search for effective anti\-inflammatory coatings\.Aim\. To evaluate the influence of polypropylene implants coated with tantalum and its derivatives on inflammation and regeneration processes\.Materials and methods\. The study used 40 male rats, divided into 6 groups: intact \(1\); with surgical intervention without an implant \(2\); with an uncoated polypropylene implant \(3\); with a tantalum\-coated implant \(4\); with a tantalum pentoxide\-coated implant \(5\); with a tantalum nitride\-coated implant \(6\)\. Morphological and immunohistochemical studies were performed\.Results\. In rats of groups 3 and 6, pronounced neutrophilic infiltration, purulent exudate, and a thin layer of immature granulation tissue were detected\. Multinucleated foreign body giant cells were often observed in the granulation tissue\. In rats of groups 4 and 5, neutrophilic infiltration was absent, granulation tissue was mature, and its layer thickness was one\-third greater compared to groups 3 and 6\. In rats of groups 4 and 5, a greater number of Ki\-67 positive and a smaller number of p53 positive cells were detected, and a higher proliferative\-apoptotic ratio \(Ki\-67/p53\) was found compared to rats of groups 3 and 6\.Conclusions\. Polypropylene implant induces aseptic inflammation, leading to increased apoptosis and decreased regeneration\. Coating the implant with tantalum or tantalum pentoxide prevents inflammation, reduces apoptosis, and increases cell proliferation\. Coating the implant with tantalum nitride does not change it, while coating with tantalum or tantalum pentoxide improves its biointegration\.
