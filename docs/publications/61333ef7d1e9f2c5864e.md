# FDD Massive MIMO Channel State Information Compression and Reporting Technology Based on AI/ML—Take Reducing Feedback Overhead as the Main Line

**Autores:** Yi Jiang; Xianmeng Wang

**Data:** 2026-10-13 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

FDD Massive MIMO is an important enabling technology for 5G\-Advanced and 6G, but its downlink channel state information \(CSI\) feedback overhead increases significantly with the increase in the number of antenna array elements and has become the main obstacle affecting system performance\. The traditional schemes based on codebooks and compressed sensing rely on preset constraints and a step\-by\-step processing approach, and their compression performance has approached the optimal compression ratio\. In recent years, AI/ML methods represented by deep learning have provided a new idea of &quot;Data\-driven end\-to\-end joint optimization&quot; for CSI compression and feedback\. However, these AI/ML methods have several problems such as complex plans, inconsistent assessment criteria, and difficulties in implementing standardization\. This article takes reducing feedback overhead as the main line, classifies the existing schemes into two categories: autoencoders and Transformer architectures, and comprehensive overview of methodological principles, strengths, and weaknesses of each type of method\. In addition, the article links the latest research results to the standard progress of 3GPP Rel\-18/19/20 and points out the gap between the existing work and the standard\. Also, this article offers multi\-objective optimization suggestions and a deployment plan by combining parameter size, inference latency, energy consumption\. Finally, the article proposes generalization ability‌, lightweight design, and the capability boundaries of large models as future research directions, with the aim of promoting the early practical application of AI/ML\-based CSI feedback technology\.
