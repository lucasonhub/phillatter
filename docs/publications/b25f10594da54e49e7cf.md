# SPORTCHILARDA PSIXOLOGIK REZILIENTLIK VA EMOTSIONAL O‘ZINI O‘ZI BOSHQARISHNING MUSOBAQA FAOLIYATI SAMARADORLIGIGA TA’SIRI

**Autores:** HUSNIDDIN SULFIDINOVICH SATTOROV

**Data:** 2026-10-14 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

This article examines the role of psychological resilience and emotional self\- regulation in enhancing athletes’ competitive performance\. In sporting environments characterized by psychological pressure, pre\-competition anxiety, unexpected setbacks, and high performance expectations, the ability to maintain psychological balance becomes particularly important\. The article explores psychological resilience in relation to adapting to challenging situations, recovering from defeat, and sustaining intrinsic motivation\. It also discusses the contribution of emotional self\-regulation to attentional control, the management of impulsive reactions, and effective decision\-making during competitions\. Particular emphasis is placed on approaches to strengthening athletes’ psychological stability through specialized training exercises, individualized psychological support, and the development of self\-regulation skills\. These approaches may help improve athletes’ psychological preparedness and enable them to demonstrate their abilities more effectively under competitive conditions\.
