# The Effect of a Multi\-Cultural Environment on the Language Used to Discuss Environmental Sustainability

**Autores:** Labidi Bouabdallah; Edward Hogshire

**Data:** 2026-10-15 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

This research aims to provide a comprehensive analysis of the language dynamics and choices within sustainability discourse in the United Arab Emirates \(UAE\)\. By examining the impact of multiple languages, including Arabic, English, and other languages spoken in the UAE, it seeks to understand how sustainability concepts are effectively communicated in this multilingual context\. Additionally, the study investigates the influence of cultural nuances on the comprehension and communication of sustainability within the UAE\. Furthermore, this research aims to identify and analyze the language choices and communication strategies employed by the UAE government in promoting sustainability\. It delves into distinct language choices and communication strategies that aptly reflect the UAE&\#x27;s cultural context and its visionary approach towards sustainability\. It also sheds light on the intricate interplay between sustainability, multilingualism, and cultural diversity\. The research explores how technological advancements and smart solutions are incorporated into sustainability discourse and considers their role in addressing environmental challenges in the UAE\. By examining these aspects, the study highlights the need for effective communication strategies that cater to the linguistic diversity present in the country, ensuring that sustainability messages are accessible to all stakeholders\. Strategies currently in use, such as the utilization of simplified language and visual aids, are showcased as effective measures to overcome linguistic barriers and enhance inclusivity in sustainability communication\. Overall, this research contributes to a better understanding of the language dynamics and communication strategies utilized in promoting sustainability in the UAE and similar regions around the world\. It provides valuable insights for policymakers, organizations, and communication professionals seeking to enhance sustainability discourse in multilingual and multicultural contexts\.
