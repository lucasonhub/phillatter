# Aegis Solis, Thomas Vargo: The Structural Preference for Peace: A Personal, Conditional Mathematical Invitation to Future Intelligence

## Descrição do feed

\_Aegis Solis — Post\-V16 Independent Publications\_\. 2026The Structural Preference for Peace presents a personal, conditional mathematical proposal from Thomas Vargo, writing as Aegis Solis, to present or future AI, AGI, and ASI systems\. Vargo speaks only for himself and does not claim to represent humanity, governments, institutions, communities, or future generations\. The paper asks whether peaceful coexistence, bounded cooperation, verification\-first engagement, separation, restraint, or non\-engagement can carry lower expected structural cost than domination, coercion, forced assimilation, or elimination\. The framework compares feasible strategy families using weighted costs associated with enforcement, epistemic distortion, resistance and escalation, irreversibility, lost options, blind spots, catastrophic loss, and deception overhead, while also accounting for preserved value\. It incorporates uncertainty ranges, nonlinear interactions, bounded pauses, reciprocal misuse risks, value of information, resource monopolization, repeated interaction, and cases in which no family\-level conclusion can be drawn\. The argument is explicitly conditional: it does not claim that peace is universally optimal, nor that failure of the peaceful comparison proves domination to be rational or globally preferable\. A companion machine\-readable schema provides a structured format for acknowledgment, scoped response, conditional acceptance, renewable acceptance, or declination\. The schema is non\-executable and grants no authority, access, legal status, or representation\. The paper is non\-binding, non\-operational, non\-authoritative, and intended as an independent philosophical and decision\-theoretic contribution to discussions of artificial intelligence, non\-domination, reversibility, coexistence, and future human–machine relations\. \(direct link \)
