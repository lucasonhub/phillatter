# Self‐Reporting Hierarchical DNA Nanoplatform for Tumor Cell‐Selective Senescence Modulation and Immune Remodeling

**Autores:** Wenya Dang; Jun Da; Jun Cao; Haolan Hu; Yuqi Xie; Lu Yu; Lu Wu; Linlin Wang; Jili Li; Yanlan Liu

**Data:** 2026-10-13 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Therapy\-induced senescence \(TIS\) promotes tumor resistance and recurrence through the senescence\-associated secretory phenotype \(SASP\), but achieving tumor cell\-selective senescence modulation remains a formidable challenge due to limited cellular specificity, insufficient spatiotemporal control, and the complexity of the secretory program\. Here, we develop a self\-reporting hierarchical DNA nanoplatform \(Gal\-lipo@S\-CHA\) that enables selective senescence reprogramming and immune remodeling in TIS tumor cells\. In this design, a newly engineered β\-galactosidase\-responsive charge\-reversal lipid carrier establishes the first layer of senescence selectivity by enabling preferential delivery and enhanced endosomal escape of a hierarchically gated catalytic DNA circuit\. Subsequent intracellular decoding of GSH and microRNA\-21, two tumor\-associated and senescence\-enriched molecular signatures, provides a second layer of tumor cell selectivity, activating the circuit specifically in pathological senescent tumor cells\. The activated circuit simultaneously generates a fluorescence signal for real\-time monitoring and assembles an NF\-κB decoy to block NF\-κB nuclear translocation, thereby suppressing downstream pro\-tumorigenic SASP production at the upstream regulatory source\. In vivo studies demonstrate that Gal\-lipo@S\-CHA effectively remodels the tumor immune microenvironment by enhancing natural killer cell infiltration and M1 macrophage polarization\. Collectively, this framework establishes a new paradigm for selectively reprogramming pathological senescence, providing a versatile platform to support immunomodulatory cancer therapies\.
