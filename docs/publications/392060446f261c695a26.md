# Machine learning\-driven adsorption science: A review of intelligent modeling, adsorbent discovery, and process optimization

**Autores:** Meshal Shutaywi; Zahir Shah; Narcisa Vrînceanu; Wejdan Deeban

**Data:** 2026-10-15 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Adsorption techniques play quite important roles in various domains such as emission control, gas separation, and wastewater treatment\. Computational methods have been applied in process modeling, material research, and process optimization\. In recent years, machine learning \(ML\) has emerged as a powerful alternative for accelerating computational research in process modeling, optimization, and adsorbent discovery\. This review presents recent advancements in ML applications for adsorption processes\. A wide range of ML techniques including artificial neural networks \(ANN\), support vector machines \(SVM\), random forests \(RF\), gradient boosting methods, convolutional neural networks \(CNN\), graph neural networks \(GNN\), recurrent neural networks \(RNN\), adaptive neuro\-fuzzy inference systems \(ANFIS\), and hybrid optimization frameworks are critically discussed\. The review helps identify improvement in prediction performance, reduction in computational cost, speed of material selection, discovery of the best adsorber, and robust optimization techniques\. Furthermore, the integration of explainable ANN techniques such as SHAP and LIME increases the intuitiveness of the method\. Despite substantial progress, challenges remain regarding dataset standardization, model generalization, experimental validation, and industrial\-scale implementation\. In this study, it is identified that hybrid physics\-informed data\-driven models, digital twins, scalable AI, and autonomous models will support the development of sustainable and efficient adsorption technologies\.
