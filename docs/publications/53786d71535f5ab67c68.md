# Scarcity of Opportunity and Educational Resources: Analyzing Systemic Barriers Among Secondary Students in Balochistan, Pakistan

**Autores:** Maham Shah

**Data:** 2026-10-13 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

This research paper investigates the severe educational resource scarcity in Balochistan, and its impact on student learning outcomes and career Preparation\. Regardless of growing youth populations, public and private educational institutions in the region face widespread systemic challenges\. Based on survey data his study shows how local education falls short in basic school facilities, modern technology subjects, practical career training, global exam access, college degree tracks, and extracurricular activities\. Specifically, over 69% of local students rely on government schools plagued by broken facilities, missing basic utilities, and unmonitored teacher absenteeism\. Furthermore, institutions suffer from a complete absence of modern fields like Artificial Intelligence \(AI\) and Robotics, poor quality in practical computer\-based training, extreme financial barriers in advanced schooling \(A\-Levels\), a total lack of international test centers \(SAT/TOEFL\), and a major shortage of sports, public speaking, and student development programs\. The paper also highlights severe gender disparities caused by unsafe transport options and domestic burdens\. To bridge the gap between classroom instruction and modern market requirements, this study proposes key policy recommendations, including targeted infrastructure investment, institutional accountability, curriculum modernization, fully equipped science and computer laboratories, structured career counseling, extracurricular programs \(sports and Model United Nations\), and safe, dedicated transportation for female students\.
