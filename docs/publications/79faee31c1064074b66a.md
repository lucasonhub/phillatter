# Biological possibility: a mechanistic account

**Autores:** Ziren Yang; Jiyuan Li

**Data:** 2026-10-10 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Abstract Current projects in synthetic biology, universal biology, and the origin\-of\-life study have posed a pressing issue regarding the nature of biological possibility\. This essay proposes a novel account of biological possibility, which highlights the role of mechanism: X is biologically possible just in case there is a possible life\-defining or life\-participating mechanism that actualizes X, and such mechanisms determine the features that individuate X\. This mechanistic account captures a broad scope of biological possibilities while affording sufficient constraints in delivering them, thus being superior to three previous accounts \(the “Library of Mendel,” combinatorial, and law\-based accounts\)\.
