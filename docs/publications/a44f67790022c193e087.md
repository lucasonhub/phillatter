# AI IN DRUG THERAPY OPTIMIZATION: A COMPARATIVE STUDY OF MACHINE LEARNING MODELS FOR GUIDELINE ADHERANT THERAPY PERSONALIZATION IN HYPERLIPIDEMIA

**Autores:** Sean Kurian

**Data:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Hyperlipidemia is a key contributor to cardiovascular diseases\. While guideline\-based treatment strategies exist, real\-world implementation varies due to patient\-specific complexities, clinician subjectivity, and evolving clinical evidence\. This study evaluates Random Forest \(RF\) Classifier and XG\-Boost models for personalizing hyperlipidemia treatment based on AHA and ACC guidelines with factors like eGFR, Baseline\_LDL\_C, Age, ASCVD\_Risk\_Score, Cost\_Sensitivity, ASCVD, Diabetes, Triglycerides, BMI, and Smoking\_Status, with an emphasis on guideline adherence and performance metrics\. This E\-Poster was presented at Kerala Pharmaceutical Congress 2025, held at Caritas College of Pharmacy, Kottayam, Kerala, India and won the &quot;Best Paper Award&quot; in the General stream\.
