# CORRELATION SIGNS OF CLINICAL COURSE AND DIAGNOSTIC FEATURES OF CRANIAL POLYNEUROPATHIES AFTER COVID\-19 INFECTION

**Autores:** E’tibor Nuriddinova

**Data:** 2026-10-13 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

This study focuses on the clinical course and diagnostic features of cranial polyneuropathy in patients following COVID\-19 infection\. A total of 30 patients aged 18–60 years were examined\. Clinical, neurological, and laboratory investigations were performed\. Changes in leukocyte, lymphocyte, platelet, thrombocrit, and D\-dimer levels were observed in the studied patients\. These changes were associated with a prolonged course of the disease, delayed recovery, and persistent focal neurological symptoms\. The results emphasize the importance of clinical and laboratory assessment in patients with post\-COVID cranial polyneuropathy\.
