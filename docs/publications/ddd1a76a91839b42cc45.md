# Design and Implementation of a lightweight evasion\-aware brute\-force detection and response system for SMEs

**Autores:** Khalid Muhammad Maina; Bello Musa Yakubu; Samson Adeyinka; Abdulrahman Tunde Alabelewe

**Data:** 2026-10-10 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Authentication endpoints are a primary target for brute\-force and credential\-stuffing attacks, and attackers evade per\-IP thresholds by pacing requests and rotating across many source addresses, while deep learning detectors need infrastructure that Small and Medium Enterprises \(SMEs\) rarely have\. This paper presents a lightweight detection and response gateway built with FastAPI and Redis\. Each request is scored with a time\-decaying Reputation Score and an Evasion Score combining IP diversity, username diversity and timing regularity over failed attempts\. Because per\-source scoring cannot see attacks spread across rotating IPs, two target\-keyed signals are added: Account Pressure, raised when many sources fail against one account, and Global Pressure, raised when failures dominate traffic from IPs with no login history\. Both respond with a server\-verified CAPTCHA rather than a lockout, and IPs that previously authenticated to an account are exempt\. In a controlled evaluation of three seeds of about 860 labelled requests, covering six attack and six benign traffic families, the system detected all 60 attack campaigns, including every rotating\-IP campaign, with 95\.0% per\-request recall and 2\.3% of benign requests denied\. On identical traffic, Fail2Ban reached 50\.0% recall and missed all rotating\-IP and honeypot campaigns, while per\-account lockout denied 74\.4% of benign requests\. The cost is friction: during attacks reaching every account, new\-device users were challenged on 60\.7% of requests, while returning users on known IPs were never challenged\. Seven attacks on the gateway itself, all successful against the initial implementation, are mitigated, and load tests up to 500 concurrent clients completed without server errors\. These are laboratory results on synthetic traffic\.
