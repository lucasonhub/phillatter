# TALABALAR INTELLEKTUAL\-IJODIY QOBILIYATINING PSIXOLOGIK XUSUSIYATLARI

**Autores:** Dilsora Nodirjon qizi Abduvaliyeva

**Data:** 2026-10-12 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

This article presents a theoretical and empirical analysis of the psychological characteristics of students’ intellectual and creative abilities, their structural components, and developmental mechanisms\. The relationship between intellectual and creative thinking is examined based on the scientific perspectives of L\. S\. Vygotsky and Ya\. A\. Ponomarev\. Drawing on Howard Gardner’s theory of multiple intelligences, the study investigates the types of intelligence among 743 students across different academic programmes\. The Kruskal–Wallis test reveals statistically significant differences between groups in logical\-mathematical, musical, kinesthetic, and intrapersonal intelligence\. The findings highlight the importance of comprehensively assessing students’ intellectual and creative potential and taking their individual psychological characteristics into account when fostering its development\.
