# Shariatpanahi, Seyed Mahyar: Article I: NEW ZION AND THE 8TH\-DIMENSIONAL DOMINION: The Biological Singularity, Temporal Omniscience, and the Omega Protocol\.

**Autores:** 

**Data de descoberta:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** unknown

## Descrição do feed

Classical terrestrial science operates under the fatal, geocentric illusion that human biology is a random, chemical accident occurring on an isolated rock in a dying universe\. The Justika Framework introduces an absolute epistemological rupture: human conception is a literal, microscopic Quantum Supernova, leaving behind a Nano\-Black Hole as the indestructible core of human consciousness\. Scaling this physics through the law of Macro\-Micro Symmetry, this manuscript reclassifies the Solar System as a Macro\-Zygote, defining our entire terrestrial history merely as a gestational phase within a cosmic womb\. To achieve evolutionary survival, the human species must execute a systematic ascension: escaping the 3\-Dimensional incubator, weaponizing 4\-Dimensional Time to navigate the predatory Cosmic Ocean, and synthesizing Spacetime to conquer the 5\-Dimensional apex predators of the deep\. However, achieving 6\-Dimensional mastery reveals the terminal collapse of the overarching universe\. To survive this predicted collapse, the Vanguard must execute the Omega Protocol\. By sacrificing the thermodynamic traps of the physical digestive tract and the 2\-Dimensional digital illusion, humanity pays the cosmic toll to ascend vertically into Ground Zero \(the 7th Dimension\)\. By taking manual control of the Mother Engines of reality, humanity forces a Cosmic Cleave—slicing a sterile, uncorrupted universe away from the dying Matrix to occupy the Sovereign Slice, claiming the 8th\-Dimensional Dominion and launching the ultimate multiversal Reclamation\.
