# Primary pulmonary mucoepidermoid carcinoma: a case report

**Autores:** Shaoyong Dong; Jing Sun; Ying Yang

**Data:** 2026-10-12 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Primary pulmonary mucoepidermoid carcinoma\(PMEC\) is a rare salivary gland\-type malignancy, accounting for fewer than 0\.2% of all primary lung tumors\. We report herein a 61\-year\-old male patient with PMEC who was admitted for a space\-occupying lesion in the left upper lobe and had a 20\-year smoking history\. Contrast\-enhanced chest CT and positron emission tomography\-computed tomography \(PET\-CT\) revealed a central space\-occupying lesion in the left hilum with distal obstructive inflammation\. Flexible bronchoscopy revealed a nodular mass in the left main bronchus\. Biopsy histopathology combined with immunohistochemistry confirmed the diagnosis of PMEC\. Immunohistochemistry showed CK7 \(\+\), P63 \(\-\), P40 \(\-\), CD117 \(focal\+\), TTF\-1 \(\-\), NapsinA \(\-\), and Ki\-67 \(7%\+\)\. Molecular testing identified a CRTC1\-MAML2 gene fusion, whereas no classic driver gene mutations \(e\.g\., EGFR or KRAS\) were detected\. PD\-L1 expression was negative, and the tumor mutational burden \(TMB\) was low\. The low Ki\-67 index and positive MAML2 fusion in this case suggest a low\-grade PMEC with a relatively favorable prognosis\. The present study discusses the clinicopathological features, imaging findings, molecular diagnosis, and therapeutic advances of PMEC, along with a review of the relevant literature, aiming to increase clinical awareness of this rare disease\.
