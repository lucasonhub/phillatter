# BIOLOGIYA TA’LIMIDA O‘QUV MAQSADLARI TAKSONOMIYASIDAN FOYDALANISH ASOSIDA TALABALARNING KASBIY KOMPETENSIYALARINI SHAKLLANTIRISH METODIKASINI TAKOMILLASHTIRISH

**Autores:** NODIRA NIZAMOVNA MUSAYEVA; Хакимова Мушарраф Фозиловна

**Data:** 2026-10-09 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

This article addresses theoretical and empirical aspects of improving the methodology for developing students’ professional competencies through the taxonomy of educational objectives in biology education\. The empirical section uses a model survey of 32 second\-year students in the Biology Education program at Asia International University, consisting of 10 statements rated on a five\-point Likert scale\. The overall mean was 3\.67±0\.72, the normalized index was 73\.4%, and 59\.1% of all responses were positive, 25\.6% neutral, and 15\.3% negative\. The internal consistency of the scale was high \(Cronbach’s α=0\.879\)\. The strongest result concerned applying theoretical knowledge in laboratory and practical tasks, whereas the comparatively lowest score was observed for reflective activity\. The modeled statistical results indicate that a taxonomy\-based approach is a promising methodological basis for strengthening students’ practical, analytical, research, and professional competencies in biology education\.
