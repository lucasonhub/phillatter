# YOSHLARDA OILAVIY QADRIYATLAR VA ROLLI MUNOSABATLARNI EMPIRIK O‘RGANISH KO‘RSATKICHLARI

**Autores:** Yuldoshev Tojiddin Abdukarimovich

**Data:** 2026-10-13 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

This article examines the empirical study of the formation of family values among young people, their attitudes toward family, perceptions of marriage and family, and indicators of role relationships in family life\. The influence of family values on young people’s worldviews, interpersonal relationships, sense of responsibility, and preparedness for future family life is scientifically analyzed\. Particular attention is also paid to examining attitudes toward marital, parental, and filial roles, identifying perceptions of the distribution of family responsibilities, and assessing the harmony between traditional and modern values\. The study substantiates the potential use of questionnaires, interviews, observation, and psychodiagnostic methods\. The empirical indicators obtained may help identify young people’s family value systems, their readiness for role relationships, and their socio\-psychological views on family life\.
