# Quantitative CT in the real\-world evaluation of progressive pulmonary fibrosis; the REVISE PPF multicentre observational study

**Autores:** Ayodeji Adegunsoye; Anna J\. Podolanczuk; Teja Kulkarni; Christian Rennison\-Jones; Steven Rothenberg; Kristin Berger; YANA RABKOVA; Christine Wu; Lydia Chelala; Cathryn T\. Lee; Iazsmin Bauer Ventura; Mary E\. Strek; Finja A\. Ottink; Wassim Bou\-Zeid; Olivier Joly; Eric White; George WJ Harston; Claire Fernandez; Anand Devaraj; Peter Myles George

**Data:** 2026-10-14 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

RATIONALE AND OBJECTIVE: We assessed whether an FDA\-cleared quantitative CT \(qCT\) tool \(Brainomix, e\-Lung\) identifies progressive pulmonary fibrosis \(PPF\) earlier than routine clinical diagnosis and evaluated the prognostic value of baseline and serial imaging biomarkers\. METHODS: Multicentre retrospective study involving University of Alabama \(UAB\), Weill Cornell Medicine \(WCM\) and University of Chicago \(UChicago\); n = 509\. HRCTs were analysed with e\-Lung to derive Weighted Reticulovascular Score \(WRVS, %\), Total Disease Extent \(TDE, %\), and e\-Lung Volume \(mL\)\. Prespecified progression thresholds were an absolute WRVS increase of ≥ 3 percentage points, an absolute TDE increase of ≥ 1\.5 percentage points, and a relative e\-Lung Volume decline of ≥ 10%, over less than 2 years\. We compared the earliest qCT progression date with recorded clinical PPF diagnoses, assessed clinically stable controls, and modelled associations with future PPF and mortality using adjusted time\-varying Cox models\. RESULTS: Among 200 patients with PPF and serial HRCTs meeting the prespecified timing criterion, qCT identified progression earlier in 78% \(UAB\), 83% \(WCM\), and 47% \(UChicago\), with mean lead times of 434, 847, and 220 days, respectively\. Among 187 clinically stable patients meeting the same serial\-imaging criterion, progression thresholds were met in 67%, 56%, and 42%, respectively\. High\-risk baseline WRVS \(≥15%\) predicted future PPF \(OR 4\.09, 2\.48, and 2\.30\) and mortality \(HR 2\.03 pooled UAB/WCM; 2\.75 UChicago\)\. Per 1\-percentage\-point increase, adjusted mortality HRs were 1\.15 and 1\.19 for WRVS and 1\.05 and 1\.04 for TDE in pooled UAB/WCM and UChicago, respectively\. Threshold crossings were also associated with mortality \(WRVS: HR 6\.50 pooled UAB/WCM, 4\.05 UChicago; TDE: 7\.60 and 3\.26; e\-Lung Volume: 8\.20 and 2\.59\)\. CONCLUSION: qCT detects radiological progression months to years before clinical PPF diagnoses\. Integrating e\-Lung into ILD pathways could accelerate treatment decisions and personalize follow\-up\.
