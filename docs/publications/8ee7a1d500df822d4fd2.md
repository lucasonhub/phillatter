# Predictive role of demographics, maths anxiety, and enjoyment in pupils’ standardised maths achievement: a machine learning approach

**Autores:** Daniel Doz

**Data:** 2026-10-11 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

This study seeks to determine the factors affecting the achievements of Grade 5 primary school pupils in the Italian national assessment, INVALSI, using Machine Learning \(ML\) regression algorithms\. Four ML algorithms were applied to data from 25,827 Grade 5 Italian primary school pupils who participated in the 2017–2018 INVALSI mathematics test\. The Boosting model achieved the highest explanatory power \(R2 = \.152\), with mathematics anxiety \(relative importance 40\.2\), ESCS \(37\.0\), and enjoyment \(17\.0\) emerging as the dominant predictors\. Random Forest \(R2 = \.142\) and Decision Tree \(R2 = \.097\) models confirmed this pattern, consistently ranking anxiety \(46\.1%\), ESCS \(33\.5%\), and enjoyment \(14\.0%\) as the three most important factors\. Regularised Linear Regression \(R2 = \.127\) revealed directional effects: ESCS \(β = \.213\) and enjoyment \(β = \.130\) positively predicted achievement, while anxiety \(β = −\.180\) was negatively associated\. Across all four algorithms, gender and macroregion demonstrated minimal predictive importance \(≤ 6\.4% and ≤ 2\.3%, respectively\)\.
