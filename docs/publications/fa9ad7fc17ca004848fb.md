# Student Career Guidance System Using Machine Learning

**Autores:** Sultana Hasan Sayyad and Ms\. Asha Mane

**Data:** 2026-10-14 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Career selection is an important decision for students, as their academic performance, skills, and employability attributes can influence their future career opportunities\. This research presents a Student Career Guidance System using Machine Learning for predicting suitable career domains from student\-related information\. A secondary dataset containing 50,000 student records was used for the study, including academic, skill, and employability\-related attributes\. The dataset was preprocessed and different Machine Learning classification techniques, namely Logistic Regression, Decision Tree, Random Forest, and Gradient Boosting, were applied and compared\. The performance of the models was evaluated using accuracy, precision, recall, and F1\-score\. Among the evaluated models, Random Forest achieved the highest accuracy of 70\.49% and was selected as the final model\. The proposed system can provide data\-driven career guidance by predicting suitable career domains and can support students in making informed career\-related decisions based on their available information\.
