# Neotypification of Three Names in Myristica \(Myristicaceae\)

**Autores:** Anand Kumar; Tapas Chakrabarty; Subir K\. Bandyopadhyay

**Data:** 2026-10-12 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

ABSTRACT In absence of the original materials, neotypes are proposed for three names\. These are Myristica cagayanensis, Myristica hellwigii , and Myristica sulcata\. These neotypifcations will be helpful in correct application of these three names\.
