# From AI Tools to Learner\-Centered Human–AI Collaboration: Developing and Evaluating the Learner\-Centered Human–AI Pedagogical Framework \(LCHAP\)

**Autores:** Myagmarsuren Orosoo; Namjildagva Raash; Tumentsetseg Baljinnyam; Munkhtsetseg Narantsetseg

**Data:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Generative artificial intelligence \(AI\) is reshaping higher education, yet practical guidance for integrating it into meaningful learning remains limited\. This paper introduces the Learner\-Centered Human–AI Pedagogical Framework \(LCHAP\), which positions AI as a collaborative cognitive partner while preserving learner agency and responsibility\. LCHAP was implemented through the Human–AI Learning Cycle \(HALC\) in a 15\-week quasi\-experimental mixed\-methods study involving 80 first\-year undergraduates at the Mongolian National University of Education\. At post\-intervention, the experimental group showed higher mean scores than the control group in English writing, learner engagement, learner autonomy, AI literacy, reflective learning, and critical thinking\. Qualitative evidence indicated a shift from answer generation toward idea exploration, comparison, revision, and reflection\. Because the reported quantitative evidence is descriptive, the findings should be interpreted as preliminary rather than causal\. LCHAP offers a practical design framework for responsible, learner\-centered Human–AI collaboration in higher education\.
