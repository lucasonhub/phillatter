# Constructive Bounds for the Topological Expressivity of Neural Networks

**Autores:** Kosio Beshkov

**Data:** 2026-10-09 · **Tipo:** preprint

## Resumo

The expressivity of a neural network $Φ$ describes the class of functions that a network can express\. The term topological expressivity refers to a family of measures particularly suited to understand binary classification and is often formulated in terms of the Betti numbers of a decision boundary $β\(Φ^\{\-1\}\(0\)\)$\. Despite its popularity, this measure is not as useful for regression and other problems that do not involve a decision boundary\. Instead, we propose to study topological expressivity in terms of the Betti numbers of the output of a neural network $β\(Φ\(X\)\)$\. We formally prove that a ReLU network with architecture $\(d,\\sum\\limits\_\{k=1\}^\{d \} β\_k\(3k\+2\),\\sum\\limits\_\{k=1\}^\{d\}β\_k\(k\+1\)\)$ can express spaces homotopy equivalent to wedge sums of spheres with arbitrary Betti numbers\. This gives upper bounds for any arbitrary connected \($β\_0=1$\) sequence of Betti numbers, but by constructing spaces homotopy equivalent to products of $N$ spheres of dimension $d\_1,\.\.\.,d\_N$ we achieve much better bounds $\(\\sum\\limits\_\{n=1\} d\_n, 3\\sum\\limits\_\{n=1\}\\limits^N d\_n \+ 2N, \\sum\\limits\_\{n=1\}\\limits^N d\_n \+ N\) $ for Betti numbers given by $\\prod\_n \(1\+x^\{d\_n\}\)$\. Finally we conjecture a much stronger tight bound which gives the architectural bound $\(d,d\+1,d\+1\)$ for a space homeomorphic to the sphere\.
