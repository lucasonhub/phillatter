# Thomas, Emily: G\. E\. Moore’s Common Sense Realism

**Autores:** 

**Data de descoberta:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** unknown

## Descrição do feed

In Real Time: A Reinvention in British Metaphysics 1883\-1928\. Oxford University Press\. 2026Early analytic philosopher G\. E\. Moore is not known for his metaphysics of time\. Within Moore scholarship, his metaphysical views on time are under\-studied; within the philosophy of time, his views are omitted\. This chapter focuses on Moore’s most substantial, realist discussion of time, advanced in a set of 1910–1911 lectures, later published as Some Main Problems of Philosophy \(hereafter, Main Problems\)\. This chapter shows these lectures defend a Common Sense realism about time, presentism, and A\-theory\. Moore is not currently recognised as a presentist or an A\-theorist, yet appreciating that he held both positions is important\. This chapter argues that Moore offers the earliest sustained defence of presentism in English\-language philosophy\. And, if Bertrand Russell is the ‘father’ of B\-theory, Moore is the ‘father’ of A\-theory\.
