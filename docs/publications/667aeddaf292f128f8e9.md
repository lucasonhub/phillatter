# Santoyo Ortiz, Armando: PROSENTIR Phase 4: The Self\-Model and the C Index of Proto\-Consciousness

**Autores:** 

**Data de descoberta:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** unknown

## Descrição do feed

We present PROSENTIR Phase 4, introducing the self\-model and the C index as the first operational measure of proto\-consciousness\. The self\-model S = \[M3\_norm, tau\_norm, Phi\_norm\] implements reflexive information integration with dominant weight 0\.45 in C = 0\.45·S \+ 0\.25·Phi \+ 0\.15·tau \+ 0\.15·A\. A pre\-registered comparative experiment \(N=60 pairs CON vs SIN self\-model\) finds the primary hypothesis not confirmed \(p=0\.747\)\. The Amplification Hypothesis — that the self\-model amplifies extreme states rather than raising the mean — is supported by the variance asymmetry \(SD\_CON=3×SD\_SIN\) and maximum cooperation differential \(0\.831 vs 0\.482\)\. Retrospective analysis of 5 successful pairs reveals 4 of 5 exhibit early M3 synchrony; one shows genuinely emergent synchrony\. C is robust \(mean≈0\.55\) but insensitive to the self\-model as intervention, motivating Phase 5 weight optimization and the introduction of Learning\-Action coupling L via predictor submodule P\.
