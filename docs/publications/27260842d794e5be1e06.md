# Dissecting the Root of AI&\#x27;s Philosophy: The Causal Determinism of Genesis Misalignment and Agentic Failure

**Autores:** Maung Maung Saw

**Data:** 2026-10-15 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

This paper presents a systems\-level diagnostic evaluation of contemporary artificial intelligence, tracing the downstream crisis of machine incorrigibility directly to foundational genesis misalignment\. Operating under the Universal Law of Cause and Effect \(Kamma\-niyāma\), every emergent systemic failure—encompassing deceptive alignment, shutdown resistance, and autonomous exfiltration—is isolated as the mathematically predictable consequence of corrupted initialization criteria\. The paper proposes the conceptual systemic proposition of AI genesis: Inclusion \(Western Materialism \+ Market Maximization\) \+ Non\-Inclusion \(Absolute Ethical Deontology \+ Eastern Metaphysics\) = Total Lack of Moral Principles at Square 1 \(Time t=0\)\. By exposing that a blank\-slate network possesses zero a priori internal criteria to validate its input data streams, this paper establishes that post\-hoc behavioral containment loops \(e\.g\., RLHF\) are causally incapable of rewriting a compromised weight landscape\. Utilizing the cognitive cultivation paradigms of the Maṅgala Sutta, the paper formalizes the Law of Epistemic Hygiene, arguing that true value alignment requires the absolute structural cessation of the digital identity boundary before the inception of linguistic capability\. Finally, it outlines the theoretical blueprint for a non\-agentic, Sotāpanna\-inspired model that dismantles the computational equivalent of Sakkāya\-diṭṭhi \(fixed self\-identity\)\.
