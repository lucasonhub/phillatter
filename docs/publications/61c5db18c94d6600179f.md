# Nested Self\-Similarity as a Created Icon of Perichoresis: Toward an Analogical Structural Isomorphism of the Vestigium Trinitatis in Physical and Mystical Systems

**Autores:** Fr Joseph Gee

**Data:** 2026-10-15 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

AbstractClassical dogmatic theology asserts that the created order bears the footprint \(vestigium Trinitatis\) of its Triune Creator \(ex parte creaturae\)\. However, modern dialogues between theology and science often rely on vague, poetic metaphors or superficial triadic associations\. This paper demonstrates a precise analogical structural isomorphism between the Trinitarian dogma of perichoresis—the mutual indwelling and &quot;distinction without separation&quot; of the Three Divine Persons—and the mathematical\-physical phenomenon of nested self\-similarity\. By framing nested self\-similarity as a created icon of divine indwelling, this paper establishes a unified structural grammar linking macro\-cosmic astrophysics \(solar system formation via disks inside disks and exponential distance spacing\), acoustic wave mechanics \(fundamental pitch and integer harmonics\), recursive fractal topology \(V to 0, A to infty\), Thomistic hylomorphism \(Summa Theologiae I, q\. 76\), the interconnection of virtues \(connexio virtutum, ST I\-II, qq\. 65–66\), and Carmelite interior topography \(Nada\-Todo, Stanza 5 of the Spiritual Canticle, and the &quot;deep caverns&quot; of St\. John of the Cross\)\. Preserved by the Analogia Entis and the doctrine of Divine Simplicity \(ST I, qq\. 3 &amp; 13\), this framework offers a rigorous, orthodox, and integrated Christian cosmology\.
