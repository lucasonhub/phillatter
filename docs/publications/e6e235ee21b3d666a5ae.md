# Stepping toward the edge

**Autores:** Rony Hirschhorn; Niccolò Negro; Liad Mudrik

**Data:** 2026-10-11 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

The Edge of Sentience asks us to shift from asking whether an entity is sentient to whether it is a sentience candidate\. Candidacy then motivates a debate about proportionate precautions, which Birch argues should be settled by citizens’ panels\. This raises the question of what citizens bring to such a debate: what they attribute to entities, how they treat those entities, and how these vary depending on both the attributor and the entity in question\. We report survey findings bearing on each question and ask what follows for the composition of the panels entrusted with these judgments\.
