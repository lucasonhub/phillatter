# Danielle, Williams: Not every model in cognitive science is \(or should be\) mechanistic

**Autores:** 

**Data de descoberta:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** unknown

## Descrição do feed

\_Behavioral and Brain Sciences\_\. forthcomingHaueis and Colaço label all models as cognitive\. However, just because cognitive science is so\-called doesn’t mean that all models are\. When type differences are ignored, an argument runs the risk of prescribing a monist modeling strategy\. In their case: mechanistic\. Such a prescription is empirically inadequate and overly restrictive, as modeling in cognitive science relies on many modeling types\.
