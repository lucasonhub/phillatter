# Safeguarding Childhood: A Comprehensive Review of Child Abuse in Pakistan

**Autores:** Zohra Asad; Khadija Khaja

**Data:** 2026-10-17 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Child abuse is a phenomenon that has affected children from every country, culture, or socio\-economic class\. Children who are abused or neglected may be more likely to repeat the cycle of violence by entering violent relationships as teens and adults, or with some abusing their children\. This paper aims to explore child abuse in Pakistan, the incidence of child abuse, and ways to address it\. This academic article seeks to investigate and examine the patterns of child abuse in Pakistan\. This report offers insights into the prevalence, types, risk factors, and effects of child abuse in Pakistan by reviewing the relevant literature, statistical data, and research studies\. The current judicial system, societal attitudes, and difficulties in resolving child abuse are also addressed\. The results of this review highlight the urgent need for all\-encompassing initiatives to prevent child abuse and protect children&\#x27;s rights and welfare in Pakistan, including awareness campaigns, regulatory changes, and multi\-sectoral partnerships that help reduce child abuse\.
