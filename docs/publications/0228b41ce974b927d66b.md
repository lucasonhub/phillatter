# Gratitude and happiness among university students: a cross\-sectional study in teacher education

**Autores:** Hernán Vicente Rojas\-Moscoso; Adrián Orlando Manrique\-Rojas; Naylea Nayeli Velasquez Cutipa; Pilar Valeria Zapana\-Aguilar; Kevin Mario Laura\-De La Cruz

**Data:** 2026-10-15 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Introduction This study examined the association between gratitude and happiness among education students at a public university in Tacna, Peru, including four dimensions of happiness: positive sense of life, life satisfaction, personal fulfillment, and joy of living\. Methods A quantitative, non\-experimental, cross\-sectional correlational design was used\. Participants were 507 students \(313 women and 194 men\) aged 18–37 years\. Gratitude and happiness were assessed using the Lima Gratitude Scale and the Lima Happiness Scale\. Internal consistency and associations were examined using Cronbach&\#x27;s alpha, model\-based omega, Spearman&\#x27;s rho, bootstrap confidence intervals, and Holm\-adjusted p \-values\. Results Internal consistency was good for gratitude \(α = 0\.807, 95% bootstrap CI \[0\.777, 0\.831\]; ω = 0\.823\) and excellent for happiness \(α = 0\.942, 95% bootstrap CI \[0\.933, 0\.950\]; ω = 0\.943\)\. Gratitude was positively associated with overall happiness, Spearman&\#x27;s ρ = 0\.447, 95% bootstrap CI \[0\.368, 0\.519\], p &lt; 0\.001\. Dimension\-specific correlations ranged from ρ = 0\.351 for personal fulfillment to ρ = 0\.420 for joy of living; all remained significant after Holm adjustment\. Excluding the participant with one reconstructed item response produced a similar overall correlation \(ρ = 0\.448\)\. Discussion Gratitude showed a robust, moderately sized positive association with happiness and its four dimensions among teacher education students\. However, the cross\-sectional design precludes causal inference, and the single\-institution convenience sample limits generalizability\. Longitudinal and multi\-institutional studies are needed to determine the temporal direction and broader applicability of these associations\.
