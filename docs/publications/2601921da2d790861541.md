# SBCA: cross\-modal gated actor\-critic with frozen BERT\-based sentiment features for multi\-asset portfolio optimization

**Autores:** Jinfeng Pan; Mi Liu; 晓萍; Jiahao Chen

**Data:** 2026-10-14 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Portfolio optimization is constrained by linear assumptions and limited integration of multi\-modal information\. Throughout this study, BERT serves only as a text encoder producing a scalar sentiment score from headlines; it is fine\-tuned offline once and then held fixed, so its parameters are neither updated nor jointly optimized with the policy, and its scores are pre\-computed static inputs\. We propose a cross\-modal gated Actor\-Critic framework \(SBCA\) for multi\-asset portfolio optimization, addressing two gaps in deep reinforcement learning: reliance on a single affine concatenation layer for price\-text fusion, and the absence of downside\-risk and turnover penalties in reward designs\. SBCA learns gated cross\-modal interactions and embeds both penalties in its reward\. On 11 years of U\.S\. equity data, SBCA outperforms Equal Weight, Buy &amp; Hold, the Dow Jones index, MVO and PPO on all metrics in the 2\- and 4\-asset groups, and in the 6\-asset group trails only Equal Weight and MVO in absolute return\. Block\-bootstrap tests give exploratory, uncorrected evidence for the Actor\-Critic component over gated fusion in the 2\-asset group, though no component\-level comparison survives correction; cost sensitivity gives descriptive evidence of robustness\. SBCA thus combines offline text preprocessing with a jointly trained policy for dynamic portfolio decision\-making\.
