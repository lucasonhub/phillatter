In Kristof Nyiri, K\. Nyiri \(ed\.\), Towards a Philosophy of Artificial Intelligence\. Budapest: Hungarian Academy of Sciences\. pp\. 63\-71\. 2026Extraordinarily optimistic claims are made on behalf of OpenAI, Anthropic and their supporters as concerns the powers and potential of Large Language Models\. These claims include the idea that the apparent ever expanding powers of LLMs will soon lead to an Arti\-ficial General Intelligence comparable in its power to the intelligence of human beings\. We move from there to address the consequences of the widespread assumption that AI already comes close to achieving this goal coupled with the underlying facts of the matter, which point to major limitations on what AI can achieve\.
