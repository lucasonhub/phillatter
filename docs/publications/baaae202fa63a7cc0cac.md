# Changes in Eating Disorder and General Psychopathology During Olanzapine Treatment in Young People with Anorexia Nervosa: Exploratory Results from OPEN

**Autores:** R\. Crawford Morris; Dominic Stringer; Olena Said; Ece Sengun Filiz; Ben Carter; Jessica Bentley; Johanna Louise Keeler; Vanessa C\. Lawrence; Janet Treasure; Ulrike Schmidt; Dasha Nicholls; Hubertus Himmerich

**Data:** 2026-10-10 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Background: Olanzapine is in clinical use for weight recovery in people with anorexia nervosa \(AN\), but evidence of its effect on psychopathology is contradictory\. Individuals with lived experience have emphasized the importance of targeting general and eating disorder\-specific psychopathology in treatment\.Methods: Data from 15 participants who adhered to oral olanzapine \(1\.25\-10 mg per day\) for at least 16 weeks in the OPEN feasibility study of olanzapine in young people \(12\-24 years\) with AN were used for post\-hoc exploratory analyses of olanzapine on general and eating disorder\-specific psychopathology over time\.Results: In post\-hoc exploratory linear mixed models of psychopathology, depression \(\-0\.410, 95% CI: \[\-0\.651, \-0\.180\]\), and anxiety \(\-0\.414, 95% CI: \[\-0\.649, \-0\.179\]\) decreased, and voices were perceived as less central and emotionally charged, whereas there was no clear evidence of change in obsessive\-compulsive \(0\.049, 95% CI: \[\-0\.282, 0\.380\]\), sleepiness \(0\.046, 95% CI: \[\-0\.178, 0\.269\]\), and self\-regulation of eating behaviors \(\-0\.0007, 95% CI: \[\-0\.013, 0\.011\]\)\.Conclusion: We observed improvements in depression and anxiety following adherence to olanzapine\. These findings are notable considering the inconsistent evidence base for olanzapine on psychopathology in AN, although interpretation is limited by the underpowered nature of the study and missing data\.
