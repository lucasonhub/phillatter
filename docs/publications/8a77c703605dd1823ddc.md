# Deflating bank transaction data for GDP nowcasting: whether and how to use inflation lags

**Autores:** Kris Boudt; Arno De Block; Feliciaan De Palmenaer; Elsa Laura Verbeken

**Data:** 2026-10-12 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Bank transaction data are increasingly used to nowcast economic activity, but their nominal nature raises the question of whether and how they should be adjusted for inflation\. We develop a measurement framework showing why, when prices are determined before payments are observed, lagged inflation may provide a more suitable deflator than contemporaneous inflation\. Using a rich dataset of Belgian corporate bank transactions, we compare alternative price indices and lags in an out\-of\-sample GDP nowcasting exercise\. The one\-quarter\-lagged GDP deflator performs well across most specifications\. The result generally persists with broader macroeconomic controls and alternative estimation methods, although the gains vary across samples and sectors\. These findings show that both the choice and timing of the deflator matter when using transaction data to nowcast real economic activity\.
