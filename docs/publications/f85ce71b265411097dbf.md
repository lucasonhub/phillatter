# Artificial intelligence for performance prediction and optimization of hybrid photovoltaic\-thermal systems: a comprehensive critical review

**Autores:** Omer K\. Ahmed; Khaoula Hidouri; Ali Benhmidène; Sameer Saadoon Algburi; Zaid H\. Ali

**Data:** 2026-10-09 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Photovoltaic\-thermal \(PV/T\) systems simultaneously produce electricity and useful heat, but their coupled thermo\-electrical behavior is highly nonlinear and sensitive to environmental and operating conditions\. This comprehensive critical review evaluates artificial intelligence \(AI\) applications for prediction, optimization, control, and diagnosis of PV/T systems, focusing on phase\-change materials, nanofluids, building\-integrated systems, heat pipes, and electrical\-output prediction\. The unique contribution of this review is a dual function\- and evidence\-based classification framework\. AI applications are classified by their primary function—prediction, optimization, control, fault diagnosis, and thermal management—and by the strength of supporting evidence, from conceptual or simulation\-based studies to experimentally validated applications\. This classification distinguishes high model\-fitting accuracy from experimentally demonstrated operational benefits and deployment readiness\. Reviewed ANN\-, ML\-, and deep\-learning models frequently report R 2 values above 0\.98, while selected experimentally supported systems achieve overall efficiencies of 44\.69–49\.07% with prediction errors below 1%\. However, direct comparison remains difficult because of differences in datasets, validation procedures, climates, system scales, and performance metrics\. Major limitations include small datasets, simulation\-dominated validation, limited external testing, weak uncertainty assessment, and insufficient evaluation of computational cost and transferability\. Future progress requires standardized datasets, multi\-site experimental validation, physics\-informed and uncertainty\-aware learning, adaptive control, explainable AI, edge/IoT implementation, and techno\-economic assessment\. This framework provides an evidence\-based roadmap toward reliable and scalable AI\-enabled PV/T systems\.
