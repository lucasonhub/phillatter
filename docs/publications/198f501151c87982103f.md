# Retrospective discriminative performance of an exploratory modified early obstetric warning score for identifying critical obstetric conditions in pregnant and puerperal women

**Autores:** Jiali Shen; 沈利萍; Qiong Sun

**Data:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Objective: To compare the retrospective discriminative performance of the modified early warning score \(MEWS\), modified early obstetric warning score \(MEOWS\), and an exploratory modified MEOWS \(MMEOWS\) for identifying critical obstetric conditions in pregnant and puerperal women\. Methodology: This single\-center retrospective study included 5,867 women hospitalized from January 2023 to December 2024 at Huzhou Maternal and Child Health Hospital\. Participants were classified according to the occurrence of critical obstetric conditions during hospitalization\. MEWS, MEOWS, and MMEOWS were calculated using the most abnormal values recorded during hospitalization\. Receiver operating characteristic analysis was used to assess area under the curve \(AUC\), optimal cutoff values, sensitivity, and specificity\. Results: Of 5,867 participants, 1,050 \(17\.9%\) met the composite definition of critical obstetric conditions\. MEWS, MEOWS, and MMEOWS scores were significantly higher in the critical group \(all P&lt;0\.001\)\. The AUCs were 0\.817 \(95% CI, 0\.803–0\.830\), 0\.882 \(0\.870–0\.894\), and 0\.911 \(0\.901–0\.922\), respectively\. At the optimal cutoff, MMEOWS showed 80\.76% sensitivity and 88\.15% specificity\. At predefined clinical thresholds, MEOWS had the highest sensitivity but low specificity, whereas MMEOWS showed a more balanced sensitivity\-specificity profile\. Conclusion: All three scores discriminated critical from non\-critical obstetric cases in this retrospective cohort\. MMEOWS showed the highest retrospective discriminative performance, but its prospective early\-warning value requires validation using predefined early assessment time points\.
