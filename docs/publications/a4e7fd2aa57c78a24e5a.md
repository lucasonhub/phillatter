# A Predictive\-Correlational Study of Error Patterns in the Four Fundamental Operations as Predictors of Mathematical Problem\-Solving Performance Among Grade 6 Learners in Portlamon Elementary School, Division of Surigao del Sur: Basis for a Targeted Mathematics Intervention Program

**Autores:** Marje Sargado

**Data:** 2026-10-09 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

This study examined error patterns in the four fundamental operations as predictors of mathematical problem\-solving performance among Grade 6 learners at Portlamon Elementary School, Division of Surigao del Sur, as a basis for developing a targeted mathematics intervention program\. A quantitative predictive\-correlational research design was employed involving 31 Grade 6 learners with complete and valid responses\. Researcher\-made tests measured learners’ performance and error patterns in addition, subtraction, multiplication, and division, as well as problem\-solving performance across Grade 6 mathematics domains\. Data were analyzed using weighted mean, standard deviation, rank ordering, Pearson product\-moment correlation, and multiple linear regression\. Findings revealed very low overall performance in the four fundamental operations, with 58\.55% accuracy, and low mathematical problem\-solving performance at 38\.39%\. Multiplication obtained the highest accuracy, while division showed the weakest performance\. The overall relationship between fundamental\-operation performance and mathematical problem\-solving was positive, moderate, and statistically significant \(r = \.5972, p = \.00039\)\. Subtraction, multiplication, and division were significantly related to problem\-solving performance, whereas addition was not\. Multiple regression further showed that the four operation\-based indicators collectively significantly predicted mathematical problem\-solving performance, F\(4, 26\) = 3\.790, p = \.0148, explaining 36\.8% of the variance, although no individual operation emerged as a significant unique predictor\. Based on these findings, a targeted mathematics intervention program was proposed, emphasizing division, subtraction, fraction and decimal computation, proportional reasoning, percentage applications, and multi\-step problem solving to strengthen learners’ foundational computational skills and mathematical problem\-solving performance\.
