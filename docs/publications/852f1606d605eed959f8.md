# Seyyed Jafar, Hosseini: The Structure of Existence and Sequential Realms: A Novel Theory on Existential Justice, Free Will, and the Gradual Actualization of the Soul’s Capacities

**Autores:** 

**Data de descoberta:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** unknown

## Descrição do feed

Human beings possess a diverse set of capacities, inclinations, and possibilities, some of which cannot simultaneously emerge within a single Context / Realm⁵\. On the other hand, existential justice requires that the structure of existence provide an appropriate field for the realization of humanity’s essential capacities, while free will necessitates that human choices be formed under real and conscious conditions\. A single world, due to the limitations of its framework, linear time, the conflict of certain circumstances, and the irreversible nature of many choices, cannot fully reveal all of the existential possibilities of the human being\. This study, employing an analytical\-deductive method and drawing upon the foundations of Transcendent Philosophy \(al\-Ḥikmah al\-Mutaʿāliyah\), particularly the principles of substantial Motion⁷ of the Soul \(Nafs\)² and existential intensification, presents the theory of “Sequential Realms\.” This model proposes that independent and successive contexts provide the conditions for the soul’s encounter with diverse and sometimes opposing circumstances\. Within this framework, a “turn” is considered a unit of the soul’s existential experience within a specific context; “existential Intensity⁴” refers to the degree of prominence and prioritization of certain possibilities within the soul’s cognitive horizon; and “existential Weight³” expresses the quality of the soul’s stabilized transformation resulting from experiences, awareness, and choices\. Existential weight is neither something transferred between contexts nor an entity circulating among realms; rather, it represents a genuine transformation in the cognitive and existential structure of the soul\. In this theory, the continuity of the soul is preserved, while each context possesses its own independent experience\. Thus, the qualitative effects of experiences and choices remain within the trajectory of the soul’s existential development, without requiring the direct transfer of narrative memory between realms\. Within this framework, Sequential Realms are not a substitute for Barzakh or Resurrection, but rather an explanation of how the capacities of the soul may be realized throughout the continuation of the worldly stage\. This structure differs from reincarnation, since it does not involve the transfer of the soul among multiple bodies; instead, it concerns the gradual realization of existential experiences within successive contexts\. Accordingly, divine justice does not mean the equalization of outcomes, but rather the provision of opportunities for the realization of capacities\. Human perfection is the process of intensifying awareness and stabilizing existential dispositions through freely chosen actions
