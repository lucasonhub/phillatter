# Flexible Integration of Structurally Diverse Spatial Multi‐Omics With AgaeSMO

**Autores:** Kunpeng Liu; Jinpo Su; Haotian Zhou; Yilin Shen; Mengke Qin; Yanyan Luo; Tiancai Wang; Zhenglin Chen; Yuanyuan Wang; Xiaokai Ma

**Data:** 2026-10-12 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Spatial multi\-omics \(transcriptome, epigenome, proteome, metabolome, morphomics\), provide near\-panoramic omics profiles across tissues\. However, cross\-integration of structurally unpaired and underutilized morphomic datasets \(e\.g\., Hematoxylin and eosin \(H&amp;E\)\-stained images\), remains a bottleneck due to inherent differences in spot structures, feature dimensions, and cellular heterogeneities\. Here, we present AgaeSMO, an adaptive graph auto\-encoder for versatile cross\-modality integration of both structurally paired and unpaired spatial multi\-omics data\. AgaeSMO encodes each omics modality from a cell type\-aware KNN \(k\-nearest neighbor\) feature graph and a spatial adjacency graph, while leverages GATs \(Graph Attention Networks\) as the backbone to fuse modalities by learning joint representations through an attention\-weighted module\. It specially fuses structurally unpaired modalities, which applies a morphological transformation strategy by designing a cross\-guidance alignment among modalities through graph cross\-attention learning\. H&amp;E images are treated as a standalone morphomic modality \(rather than auxiliary features\) is specially fused with other modalities through image patch embeddings to fully utilize cellular morphological heterogeneities\. AgaeSMO is a powerful tool can effectively integrate diverse omics modalities with both paired and unpaired spot architectures\. These unique capabilities enable precise spatial domain delineation, micro\-environmental heterogeneity detection, compatible across broad platforms, that outperforming pre\-existing tools\. AgaeSMO offers a versatile, robust framework for integrative spatial multi\-omics analysis and facilitates multimodal, biologically interpretable mining\.
