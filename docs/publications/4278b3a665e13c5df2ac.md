# Physically inspired geometry\-to\-photometry rendering and lightweight convolutional neural network for real\-time weld defect detection

**Autores:** Ling Cao; Jiajun Qiu; Yunzhi Zhang; Daquan Feng; Wei Jun Pan

**Data:** 2026-10-10 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Welding quality inspection of battery top covers is critical to lithium\-ion battery safety and reliability\. The height deviation between weld defects and normal areas is extremely small; on specular, weakly textured surfaces it produces no stable brightness contrast, so two\-dimensional \(2D\) images carry almost no usable defect signal\. Three\-dimensional \(3D\) sensing captures such deviations but incurs computational costs prohibitive for production lines\. Raw depth maps, as scalar grayscale fields, cause 2D convolutional networks to overlook tiny\-defect features\. Addressing all three limitations simultaneously remains a key challenge for artificial intelligence \(AI\) in industrial inspection\. To address this challenge, we propose a geometry\-to\-photometry defect detection framework that converts 3D weld geometry into discriminative 2D photometric representations via physically inspired rendering\. Depth data are first mapped to pseudo\-color images, and surface normals then modulate lighting under a Phong\-based model to transform subtle geometric discontinuities into high\-contrast photometric cues\. Building on this strategy, we develop YOLO\-WaveletTiny \(YOLO\-WT\), a lightweight You Only Look Once \(YOLO\) detector integrating wavelet depthwise separable convolution and inverse wavelet upsampling for efficient feature extraction and reconstruction\. Experiments on a real industrial dataset with six defect categories show the rendered representation improves the baseline by 5\.9 percentage points in mean average precision at 50% intersection over union \(mAP@50\), and the full framework achieves 93\.8% mAP@50 with only 2\.47 million parameters and 5\.7 GFLOPs, outperforming mainstream detectors\. These results demonstrate the framework as an effective, practical AI solution for low\-cost, high\-precision weld defect inspection\.
