# Immune\-checkpoint blockade and candidate predictive biomarkers in HIV\-negative Kaposi sarcoma: current evidence and a practical research framework

**Autores:** Yang Song; Yan Fang; Lin Zeng; Hua Guan

**Data:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Kaposi sarcoma \(KS\) is a KSHV/HHV\-8\-driven vascular neoplasm with heterogeneous HIV\-negative epidemiological settings\. Immune\-checkpoint blockade is biologically plausible, but direct clinical evidence remains limited to a small, non\-comparative phase II cohort of pembrolizumab in classic or endemic KS\. Its 71% best objective response rate is a signal that warrants confirmation, not comparative evidence or a basis for biomarker\-guided treatment\. This narrative review distinguishes established clinical observations from candidate translational hypotheses, appraises analytical and biological limitations of proposed markers, and outlines requirements for prospective subtype\-stratified validation\. PD\-L1 expression, tumor mutational burden, and KSHV DNA should not be used as stand\-alone selection or exclusion tests in routine care\.
