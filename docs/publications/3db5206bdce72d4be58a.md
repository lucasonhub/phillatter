# PROFILE OF PATIENTS WITH COLORECTAL CANCER IN A CITY IN THE NORTHWEST OF SÃO PAULO STATE

**Autores:** Laura Brunelli Ducatti; Lara Gonçalves dos Santos; LARISSA ANDRADE TERAO; Lívia Isso Batistela; Henrique Mendes Soares; Leandro de Moura Centurion

**Data:** 2026-10-13 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Introduction: Colorectal cancer is one of the leading causes of morbidity and mortality in Brazil, with an expected increase in the number of cases in the coming years\. Despite being widely studied, regional gaps remain regarding patients&\#x27; clinical profiles and risk factors\. Objective: To describe the clinical, histological, and epidemiological profile of patients diagnosed with colorectal cancer in a city in the northwest region of São Paulo State, Brazil\. Methods: A cross\-sectional observational study was conducted using hospital medical records from 2022 to 2023\. Sociodemographic, clinical, histological, and lifestyle\-related variables were collected\. Statistical analysis included the t\-test with bootstrap resampling and the chi\-square test\. Results: The final sample comprised 195 patients, of whom 51\.8% were male, with a mean age of 65\.4 years\. Stage II colorectal cancer was the most frequent stage, followed by stage III\. Moderately differentiated adenocarcinoma was the predominant histological type\. Lifestyle\-related risk factors were identified in 71\.8% of patients and were more common among males\. No significant differences were found between sexes regarding comorbidities or family history of cancer\. Conclusion: The study revealed a high prevalence of risk factors and a predominance of intermediate and advanced stages at the time of colorectal cancer diagnosis, particularly among patients older than 60 years\. These findings reinforce the need for regional strategies focused on early screening and prevention in at\-risk populations\.
