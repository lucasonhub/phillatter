# Pengembangan Multimedia Pembelajaran Interaktif Berbantuan Artificial Intelligence pada Materi Cahaya untuk Murid Kelas V Sekolah Dasar

**Autores:** Yunike widianti; Mixghan Norman Antono

**Data:** 2026-10-10 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

This study aimed to develop interactive learning multimedia assisted by Artificial Intelligence on light material for fifth\-grade elementary school students and to determine its validity, practicality, and effectiveness\. This research employed a Research and Development method using the ADDIE model, consisting of Analysis, Design, Development, Implementation, and Evaluation stages\. The product was validated by material and media experts and tested through a small\-group trial involving 10 sixth\-grade students and a large\-group trial involving 15 fifth\-grade students at SDN Tanjung Jati 2\. Data were collected through validation sheets, teacher and student response questionnaires, teacher and student activity observation sheets, and learning outcome tests\. The results showed that the multimedia obtained a validity percentage of 96\.25% from the material expert and 86\.7% from the media expert, both categorized as very valid\. The practicality results showed 95\.7% for the teacher response in the small\-group trial, 94% for the student response in the small\-group trial, 97\.1% for the teacher response in the large\-group trial, and 94\.4% for the student response in the large\-group trial, all categorized as very practical\. The effectiveness results in the large\-group trial showed teacher activity of 97\.5%, student activity of 96\.25%, and classical learning mastery of 86\.7%, all categorized as very effective\. Therefore, the developed interactive learning multimedia assisted by Artificial Intelligence is valid, practical, and effective for supporting IPAS learning on light material for fifth\-grade elementary school students\.
