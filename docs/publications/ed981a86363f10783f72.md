# Content\-adaptive Multi\-channel Hybrid Quantum\-classical Block\-wise Optimization for Robust Image Watermarking

**Autores:** Nova Rijati; Aris Marjuni; Sudipta Kr Ghosal; Aditya Kumar Sahu; Deshinta Arrova Dewi; De Rosal Ignatius Moses Setiadi

**Data:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

The rapid growth of digital image distribution necessitates robust watermarking techniques to protect ownership and ensure data integrity\.This paper proposes a content\-adaptive hybrid quantum\-classical watermarking framework that integrates local adaptive modeling and global optimization\.The method employs Discrete Wavelet Transform \(DWT\) and Discrete Cosine Transform \(DCT\) to provide a stable embedding domain\.Local texture features are extracted from image blocks and mapped using a trained Parameterized Quantum Circuit \(PQC\) to determine adaptive embedding strength\.In parallel, Quantum\-Inspired Annealing \(QIA\) is used to optimize the selection of DCT coefficient positions globally\.A multi\-channel embedding strategy is further incorporated to enhance robustness\.Experimental results demonstrate that the proposed method achieves high imperceptibility \(PSNR &gt; 47 dB, SSIM ≈ 0\.99\) and strong robustness against the evaluated attacks, while remaining competitive with existing methods\.The proposed framework provides a hybrid quantum\-classical approach to image watermarking and represents a process innovation by integrating adaptive quantum learning with global coefficient\-position optimization for robust multimedia copyright protection\.
