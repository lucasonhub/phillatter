# Human uniqueness and vulnerability to mental disorder

**Autores:** Riadh Abed; Benjamin Griffin; Paul St John‐Smith

**Data:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Summary Among all animals, humans stand out as uniquely vulnerable to mental disorder\. Explaining this vulnerability constitutes a central challenge for psychiatry that requires an explicitly evolutionary approach\. A necessary first step is a rigorous scientific account of human uniqueness: species\-specific psychological/behavioural traits distinguishing Homo sapiens from other animals\.
