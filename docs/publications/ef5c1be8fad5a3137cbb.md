# Network pharmacology\-guided investigation and experimental validation of the anti\-arthritic potential of Alpinia galanga root extract in complete Freund&\#x27;s adjuvant\-induced arthritis in rats

**Autores:** Sakshi R\. Bachhav; Manoj Mahajan; Aman Babanrao Upaganlawar; Chandrashekhar Devidas Upasani

**Data:** 2026-10-12 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Introduction: Rheumatoid arthritis \(RA\) is a chronic autoimmune inflammatory disorder in which multi\-target interventions may complement existing pharmacotherapy\.Aim and Objectives: To investigate the anti\-arthritic potential of Alpinia galanga root extract \(AGE\) using network pharmacology, molecular docking/simulation and experimental validation in Complete Freund&\#x27;s Adjuvant \(CFA\)\-induced arthritis\.Materials and Methods: Galangin\-related targets were intersected with RA\-associated genes, followed by protein\-protein interaction and pathway\-enrichment analyses\. Galangin was docked with SRC, EGFR, PTGS2 and PARP1 using SwissDock, and protein\-ligand flexibility was evaluated using CABS\-flex\. Male Wistar rats received AGE 200 or 400 mg/kg or indomethacin 10 mg/kg for 21 days after CFA induction\.Results: Fifty\-seven targets were common to galangin and RA\. PTGS2 showed the strongest docking affinity \(\-8\.577 kcal/mol\), followed by PARP1 \(\-7\.203 kcal/mol\); SRC and EGFR each showed \-4\.154 kcal/mol\. RMSF analysis indicated overall stable EGFR\-, PTGS2\- and PARP1\-galangin complexes\. AGE dose\-dependently reduced paw oedema, anti\-CCP, RF\-IgM, CRP, TNF\-alpha and IL\-6, improved biochemical indices, reduced lipid peroxidation and restored SOD, CAT and GSH\.Conclusion: AGE demonstrated significant anti\-arthritic activity supported by convergent network\-pharmacology, docking/simulation and in vivo findings\.
