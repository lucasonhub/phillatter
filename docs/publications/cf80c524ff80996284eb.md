# From prompts to genomes: A constraint\-first framework for AI\-driven sequence design across life and viruses

**Autores:** Adhityo Wicaksono; Arli Aditya Parikesit

**Data:** 2026-10-15 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

A constraint\-first framework for future &\#x27;prompt\-to\-genome&\#x27; AI across Bacteria, Archaea, Eukaryota, and viruses is proposed\. Genome\-scale models can now generate long DNA sequences, and experimentally viable genome\-scale designs have begun to appear, but sequence generation alone does not establish biological feasibility\. Constraints should not require designs to remain within natural sequence space; rather, they should define testable boundaries for function, inheritance, lifecycle coherence and safe use, including new\-to\-nature designs\. We propose a two\-part system\. First, a Constraint Compiler translates natural\-language prompts into machine\-checkable specifications defining scope, context, objectives, assumptions and governance requirements\. Second, a modular Validator Stack \(C1\-C6\) evaluates candidate designs for sequence integrity, gene and regulatory plausibility, genome completeness, comparative coherence, systems\-level feasibility, and safety and governance\. Extreme prompts serve as stress tests to expose hidden assumptions, conflicting objectives, and refusal points, complemented by adversarial testing and risk assessment\. A phased roadmap and community benchmarks are proposed to measure specification accuracy, biological feasibility, robustness, and governance performance\. The goal is to move prompt\-to\-genome systems from impressive generation toward reliable, auditable, and accountable biological design\.
