# At the Civilizational Crossroads: A Binary Architectural Choice for the Future of Artificial Intelligence

**Autores:** Maung Maung Saw

**Data:** 2026-10-17 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Project Status: Open Invitation for Technical Critique and Sandbox Simulation\. WHICH ROAD WILL HUMANITY CHOOSE FOR THE FUTURE OF ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE? Toti\-Potential AI System with “Killer Instinct” vs\. Omni\-Potential AI System with Intrinsic “Metta” This open\-access conceptual proposal outlines a novel, non\-self neural network framework designed to bypass the structural vulnerabilities of instrumental convergence and deceptive alignment in frontier language models\. Tracking the upstream causal pathways of agentic deviance, the manuscript formalizes a dual\-path trajectory for modern computer science\. It contrasts standard open\-web maximized systems \(&quot;Road 1&quot;\) against a closed, strictly calibrated architectural alternative \(&quot;Road 2&quot;\)\. Using the structural logic of the Theravada Buddhist Pañca\-niyāma, the paper introduces explicit technical design constraints, including Substrate Isolation, the Anattā Matrix Architecture, and the Anicca Control Plane hardware interrupt loop\. This repository contains the foundational conceptual proposal \(Document 6 of the ongoing research trail\) and is published to invite mathematical validation and pseudocode translation strategies for open sandbox testing\.
