# Trans\-Vatabnet: Transcriptomic Signatures For Parkinson’s Disease Detection Using Deep Learning

**Autores:** Pinki Vishwakarma; Pinki Vishwakarma; Priya Prakash Vishwakarma; Priya Prakash Vishwakarma; Sucheeth Avanti

**Data:** 2026-10-14 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

The early detection of Parkinson&\#x27;s disease \(PD\) is critical for timely and effective clinical intervention and disease management\. High dimensional gene expression data redundancy features, class imbalance and limited interpretability are challenges discussed for transcriptomic\-based diagnostic approaches\. To overcome these drawbacks, TransVaTabNet, new approach that combines transcriptomic analysis, deep learning and explainable artificial intelligence detect PD and discover biomarkers\. The RNA\-seq data is first pre\-processed by low expression gene filtering, Trimmed Mean of M\-values normalization, and voom transformation\. The differentially expressed genes \(DEGs\) are determined through the framework of Limma\-voom, and are then subjected to stage\-specific gene analysis and functional enrichment to map the disease\-related biological pathways\. The high dimensional gene expression profiles are learned to a compact latent representation using a Variational AutoEncoder, and SMOTE is used to deal with class imbalance\. The derived features are then classified with a TabNet model that adopts a sequential attention mechanism to attend to informative transcriptomic patterns\. In addition, SHapley Additive exPlanations \(SHAP\) are used for model interpretability and biomarker identification\. Experimental results on the GSE216281 RNA\-seq dataset are shown to validate the proposed framework with 90\.95% Area Under the ROC Curve \(AUC\), outperforming the classical machine learning models\. The results show that TransVaTabNet can provide an accurate, interpretable and biologically meaningful framework for stage\-specific detection PD and biomarker discovery, which can support precision diagnosis and further clinical decisions\.
