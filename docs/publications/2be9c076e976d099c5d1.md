# Bustamante, Cirilo Oscar: Quantum Nora: A game about physical randomness and conversation

**Autores:** 

**Data de descoberta:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** unknown

## Descrição do feed

Quantum Nora is a small experiment in which a conversational system receives its initial dispositions and thematic affinities from a quantum\-generated random sample\. A state machine carries those dispositions through the conversation, translating them into changing degrees of closeness, interest, availability, candor, and conversational initiative\. The language model itself remains conventional: it sees only prompts and instructions, without knowing where they came from\. From the observer’s point of view, however, the system’s trajectory begins with an event that, according to standard quantum mechanics, was not determined in advance\. Nora does not claim to be a quantum intelligence, nor to be conscious or free\. She is a deliberately simple machine built to make a philosophical question visible\.
