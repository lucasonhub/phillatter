# Comparative Analysis of Innovative Preparedness of Library Schools in Nigeria and Abroad for the Fourth Industrial Revolution \(4IR\)

**Autores:** Anuoluwa Awodoyin; Temitope Adeleke OSISANWO; Ahmed Olakunle Simisaye; Olabode Thomas Fajiwe

**Data:** 2026-10-13 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Library schools or ischools especially in developed nations have responded to the challenges and opportunities of the 4IR era by upgrading their curricula with courses to make their graduates relevant\. This study out a comparative content analysis of curricula of selected Nigerian library schools \(NLS\) and best library schools in the world with the view of examining how prepared are the curricula of NLS in training library and information professionals for 4IR libraries\. The study selected fourteen \(14\) library schools out of 37 library schools offering Bachelor’s Degree in LIS spread across the six geopolitical zones in Nigeria and ten best library schools in the world\. NLS were purposively selected using the quota sampling technique based on their year of establishment while the ten best leading library schools in the world were selected based on their positions on the QS world ranking of universities\. The findings revealed some innovative courses in the curricula of NLS in preparing its undergraduates for the 4IR era:Information retrieval, Database Management, Knowledge Management, Web design and Multimedia Application\. Whereas leading library schools had more advanced courses such as Databases &amp; Data Modeling, Data Structures &amp; Algorithms, information architecture, Data science, machine learning for advanced analytics among others\. The study concluded that innovative courses that will prepare graduates of NLS to be 4IR compliant are grossly inadequate in the curricula when compared to leading library schools globally\. The study recommended the redesign of curricula of library schools in Nigeria to meet 4IR libraries globally\.
