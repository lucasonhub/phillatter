# Influence of milking methods on microplastic contamination of milk from cows and buffaloes

**Autores:** Hamza Shafiq; Mamoona Amiir; Sadaf Yaqoob; Muhammad Riaz; Muhammad Sameem Javed; Joana C\. Prata; Matteo Bordiga; Hind A\. Abu\-Hiamed; Tawfiq S\. Alsulami; Muhammad Zubair Khalid

**Data:** 2026-10-09 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Microplastics \(particles of &lt;5 mm\) are synthetic polymer contaminants with growing environmental concern\. Raw milk samples \(n = 52\) were collected from hand\- and machine\-milked buffaloes and cows from dairy farms in Multan, Pakistan\. Samples were examined visually and subsequently analyzed by Fourier Transform Infrared spectroscopy\. Fibers \(59%\), fragments \(24%\), and films \(17%\) were the dominant microplastic particles identified\. A total of 3,380 particles were visually identified; of 845 particles analyzed by ATR\-FTIR, 756 \(89\.5%\) were confirmed as polymers, and visual counts were corrected for this rate \(about 3,025 estimated microplastics\)\. Estimated polymer shares, extrapolated from FTIR analysis, were PE \(57%\), PP \(23%\), PET \(13%\), and PS \(7%\)\. Machine\-milked cow milk showed maximum particle counts \(PE: 68–84, PP: 12–16, PET: 8–14, PS: 8–12 particles/100 mL\); however, this group comprised only three animals \(n = 3\); hence, these findings should be interpreted with caution\. Semi\-quantitative estimated daily intake \(EDI\) calculations indicated that children aged 1–3 years represented the highest estimated exposure group\. The microplastic pollution load index provisionally categorized machine\-milked cow and buffalo milk as well as hand\-milked cow milk in category IV and hand\-milked buffalo milk in category III\. However, universally accepted toxicological thresholds for microplastics in food are still not established, and these semi\-quantitative indices \(EDI, MPLI, PRI\) should be regarded as exploratory rather than definitive\. The findings highlight the microplastic contamination of dairy milk and indicate that further investigation and policy consideration are warranted\.
