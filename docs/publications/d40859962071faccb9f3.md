# Rapid planning of deposition strategies for wire arc additive manufacturing: from target\-geometry design to dimensional\-accuracy control

**Autores:** Ke Wu; Hao Wang; Chao Chen; Chunguo Liu; Chuanhai Chen; Xiaohui Zhao

**Data:** 2026-10-09 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

The accuracy and efficiency of wire arc additive manufacturing \(WAAM\) depend on the geometrically consistent translation of a target geometry into multi\-layer multi\-pass bead layouts and robot\-executable toolpaths under prescribed process parameters\. To bridge this design\-to\-deposition gap, this study introduces a target\-geometry\-driven framework that integrates four capabilities: weld bead geometry prediction, primitive\-based deposition geometry modelling, layout parameter optimisation, and toolpath generation\. The weighted mean of vectors \(INFO\) algorithm was integrated with a cascade\-forward backpropagation \(CFBP\) neural network to predict bead height, width, and cross\-sectional area, yielding RMSE values of 0\.08914 mm, 0\.1160 mm, and 0\.3874 mm², respectively\. Based on these predictions, a multi\-layer multi\-pass deposition geometry model incorporating constraint\-state decomposition and an overlap\-ratio criterion was developed to rapidly determine the number of layers, beads per layer, inter\-bead overlap relationships, and deposition toolpaths\. Validation experiments on four component types yielded height and width forming errors relative to the target geometry of \-4\.3527% to 4\.7484% and \-6\.1409% to 7\.9370%, respectively\. The manufacturing execution errors relative to model\-predicted dimensions were \-3\.9984% to 5\.1799% and \-4\.0145% to 4\.6739%\. The proposed framework can rapidly generate deposition layouts and toolpaths from prescribed process parameters and specified target geometries within the investigated process, material, and geometric ranges\.
