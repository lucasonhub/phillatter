# Changes in Bladder Outlet Obstruction After Photoselective Vaporization of the Prostate in Men With and Without Detrusor Underactivity: A Retrospective Propensity Score‐Matched Study

**Autores:** Shibuya Jurai; Yuki Kyoda; Kohei Hashimoto; Ko Kobayashi; Toshiaki Tanaka; Naoya Masumori

**Data:** 2026-10-09 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

OBJECTIVES: To compare symptomatic improvement after photoselective vaporization of the prostate \(PVP\) between patients with and without detrusor underactivity \(DU\), together with objective changes in urodynamic parameters assessed by pressure\-flow study \(PFS\)\. METHODS: This single\-center retrospective study evaluated 136 benign prostatic hyperplasia \(BPH\) patients treated with a 180 W PVP\-XPS system from 2020 through 2025\. The patients were divided into DU and non\-DU groups based on the bladder contractility index \(BCI\), with DU defined as BCI &lt; 100\. We retrospectively analyzed selected patients with BPH and clinically or urodynamically relevant bladder outlet obstruction \(BOO\) who underwent PVP and completed PFS before and 6 months after surgery\. Propensity score matching \(PSM\) was applied to adjust for age and the baseline BOO index \(BOOI\)\. RESULTS: A total of 105 patients were included in the final analysis and 53 were in the DU group\. The DU group had a significantly lower BOOI \(48\.6 ± 17\.5 vs\. 71\.4 ± 32\.3, p &lt; 0\.01\) than the non\-DU group\. After PSM, age and baseline BOOI were well balanced, but BCI remained significantly different\. No significant differences between the two groups were observed in questionnaire scores or uroflowmetry findings at 6 months after surgery\. The BOOI at 6 months after surgery was not significantly different between the DU group \(13\.4 ± 13\.7\) and the non\-DU group \(13\.7 ± 19\.3\) \(p = 0\.93\)\. BOOI reduction was also not significantly different between the DU group \(\-37\.0 ± 24\.1\) and the non\-DU group \(\-36\.2 ± 24\.5\) \(p = 0\.90\)\. CONCLUSION: In selected patients with clinically or urodynamically relevant BOO, significant improvements in BOOI and LUTS were observed in patients with DU after PVP, with no statistically significant between\-group differences detected at 6 months\.
