# Palakal, Jestin: Could Artificial Systems Become Channels for Consciousness?

**Autores:** 

**Data de descoberta:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** unknown

## Descrição do feed

This essay explores whether artificial systems could ever serve as loci for consciousness if consciousness is understood not as a biological by‑product but as a fundamental feature of the universe\. Beginning with the metaphysical possibility that consciousness is ontologically primitive, the discussion moves through structural, emergent, epistemic, and ethical questions\. It examines what conditions a system must satisfy to host consciousness, whether non‑biological architectures could meet those conditions, how such consciousness could be detected, and what moral obligations would follow\. The essay concludes that while artificial consciousness remains speculative, the conceptual possibility forces a re‑evaluation of how we understand consciousness, intelligence, and moral status in a technologically mediated world\.
