# Leadership in the Age of Artificial Intelligence

**Autores:** Joshua N\. Longmire

**Data:** 2026-10-13 · **Tipo:** book

## Resumo

In today&\#x27;s fast\-paced, technology\-driven environment, leaders in every sector must grapple with the realities of artificial intelligence \(AI\), from how to harness AI to gain competitive advantage, to how to preserve ethics and human\-centric values in the process\. Leadership in the Age of Artificial Intelligence gives leaders a clear, durable framework for that shift\. The text offers an accessible yet rigorous exploration of these challenges, demonstrating how AI can be leveraged to enhance decision\-making, streamline routine tasks, and create transformative organizational cultures\. Bridging the gap between theory and practice, this textbook combines foundational leadership principles, such as transformational, servant, and adaptive leadership, with the evolving landscape of AI\-driven tools\. Readers will learn how to build an AI\-ready workforce, develop ethical governance frameworks, and cultivate resilience in the face of rapid technological change\. Leadership in the Age of Artificial Intelligence treats AI not as a destination but as a test of the qualities leadership has always required\. Each chapter features clear learning objectives, real\-world case studies built around composite cases drawn from real organizational life, reflective questions, self\-assessment audits and decision frameworks, and a concise summary for quick review\. Written for both undergraduate and graduate students, as well as practitioners seeking to elevate their leadership skills, this book emphasizes responsible innovation and empathetic leadership\. By weaving together examples from diverse industries and geographies, it provides a global perspective on AI adoption, underscoring the vital role leaders play in shaping a future that balances technological progress with humanity&\#x27;s best interests\.
