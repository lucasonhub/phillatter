# COMPARATIVE GENOMIC ANALYSIS OF PROBIOTIC STRAINS FOR SAFETY AND FUNCTIONAL ATTRIBUTES

**Autores:** Manochitra A S; Inchara K S; Monishkumar R; Kowshika S; Reshma A S; Balakrishnaraja Rengaraju

**Data:** 2026-10-17 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Probiotics are good bacteria that help keep people healthy by balancing the microbes in the gut, improving the immune system, and supporting important body processes\. As they are used more in special foods, supplements, and treatments, it&\#x27;s important to check how safe they are and how well they work, looking at their genetic makeup\. This study looked at four common probiotic strains: Lactobacillus rhamnosus, Lactobacillus plantarum, Bifidobacterium bifidum, and Bifidobacterium longum\. The full genetic codes of these bacteria were taken from public databases and checked for quality using tools like QUAST and CheckM\. The genes in each genome were studied using RASTtk to find out which ones help the bacteria have probiotic benefits\. To make sure the probiotics are safe, they checked for harmful genes and genes that make bacteria resistant to antibiotics\. For their useful functions, they looked at genes connected to breaking down sugars, using amino acids, making vitamins, and responding to stress\. By comparing the genes of these strains, the study found out which parts of the genome are shared and which are unique to each strain\. The results showed that all the selected probiotics had high\-quality genomes, were safe, and had different kinds of useful genes\. Lactobacillus plantarum had the most variety in its functions, while Lactobacillus rhamnosus was better at handling stress\. This research shows that using genome comparisons is a good way to check the safety and effectiveness of probiotics\. The findings help find better probiotics and support making safe and effective products for food, health supplements, and medical treatments\.
