# Evaluating the implementation of electronic\-based government systems in improving public service efficiency

**Autores:** Erni Qomariyah; Marsia Sumule Genggong; Rahmawati Rahmawati; Ashmarita Ashmarita; Nadzirin Nadzirin; Deprianus Sarlis; Marlina Rajab

**Data:** 2026-10-13 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

This study evaluates the implementation of Electronic\-Based Government Systems \(locally known as Sistem Pemerintahan Berbasis Elektronik or SPBE\) in South Konawe Regency and its contribution to public service efficiency\. A descriptive mixed\-methods approach was employed, combining quantitative and qualitative data collection conducted in April 2025\. The study involved 50 respondents, consisting of 33 government officials and 17 community members\. Quantitative data were analysed using descriptive statistics, while qualitative data were analysed thematically\. A strengths, weaknesses, opportunities, and threats \(SWOT\) analysis formulation was applied\. The findings indicate that leadership commitment, information and communication technology \(ICT\) infrastructure development, supportive regulations, and competent human resources support SPBE implementation\. However, uneven internet infrastructure, limited digital literacy, and budget constraints remain obstacles\. Quantitative findings show that 40 per cent of government respondents use SPBE applications daily, while 33 per cent do not\. Among community respondents, 76 per cent have internet access, but only 12 per cent are aware of SPBE services\. The study concludes that SPBE implementation has contributed to bureaucratic efficiency; however, sustainability depends on continued investment in infrastructure, institutional capacity, and public engagement\.
