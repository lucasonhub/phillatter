# Paired biopsy\-surface and tissue microbiome profiling reveals tissue\-associated bacterial signatures in nasopharyngeal carcinoma

**Autores:** Nan Shi; Zongjian Huang; Xuesong Wang; Ying Xie; 唐安洲; Wei Xia

**Data:** 2026-10-14 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Introduction Tumor\-associated microbiota have been implicated in cancer biology, but the relationship between bacteria detected on the biopsy surface and those detected within tumor tissue remains poorly defined\. Methods In this study, we profiled paired biopsy\-surface swabs and tissue biopsies from patients with nasopharyngeal carcinoma \(NPC\) and benign nasopharyngeal lymphoid hyperplasia \(NLH\) controls using five\-region 16S rRNA gene sequencing\. Matched bulk RNA\-seq data were analyzed to explore associations between microbial features, Epstein\-Barr virus \(EBV\) transcription, host pathway activity, and inferred immune\-cell composition\. Results Tissue\-associated microbial profiles showed stronger separation between NPC and NLH than biopsy\-surface profiles in this discovery cohort\. Several NPC\-enriched species\-level taxonomic assignments were associated with higher EBV transcript levels, proliferative and cell\-cycle pathway activity, and lower inferred CD8 T\-cell and plasmacytoid dendritic\-cell signatures\. Discussion These findings indicate that NPC tissue contains microbial signatures that differ from biopsy\-surface communities and support further validation of tissue\-associated microbe\-EBV\-host relationships\.
