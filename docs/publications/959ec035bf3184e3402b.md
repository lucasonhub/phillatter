# PEDAGOGLARNING EMOTSIONAL KUYISHINING OLDINI OLISH ZAMONAVIY TA’LIM MENEJMENTINING VAZIFASI SIFATIDA

**Autores:** ДИЛЬБАР ДМИТРИЕВНА БОЙМИРЗАЕВА

**Data:** 2026-10-13 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

The article examines the prevention of teacher burnout as one of the priority areas of modern educational management\. The relevance of the study is обусловлена the increasing intensity of teachers’ professional activities, the growing administrative workload, the need for Ta’limda istiqbolli izlanishlar \- xalqaro ilmiy\-metodik jurnal № 9/2026 ISSN: 3060\-527X continuous adaptation to the digital learning environment, and rising demands for the quality of educational outcomes\. The aim of the study is to provide a theoretical justification for organizational and managerial mechanisms aimed at preventing teacher burnout and to develop a comprehensive prevention model at the level of an educational institution\. The methodological framework includes the analysis and synthesis of scientific literature, a comparative analysis of existing approaches to preventing occupational burnout, and the conceptual modeling of managerial mechanisms for supporting teaching staff\. The theoretical framework draws on the occupational burnout model, the Job Demands–Resources \(JD\-R\) approach, and contemporary research on teachers’ psychological well\-being\.
