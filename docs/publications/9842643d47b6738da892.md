# INTELIGÊNCIA ARTIFICIAL, DOCUMENTAÇÃO PEDAGÓGICA E EDUCAÇÃO ESPECIAL INCLUSIVA

**Autores:** Luiz Fernando Marques dos Santos; Marcilene Moreira Donadoni

**Data:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

This article analyzes the possibilities and limits of Artificial Intelligence in organizing pedagogical evidence aimed at monitoring the learning pathways of students who are the target audience of Special Education\. It develops a bibliographic and theoretical\-conceptual study grounded in research on Artificial Intelligence and education, pedagogical documentation, mediating assessment, and Inclusive Special Education\. It argues that Artificial Intelligence can contribute to systematizing records, integrating different sources of information, and expanding institutional pedagogical memory, but it cannot replace the ethical, relational, and interpretive processes carried out by teachers and pedagogical coordinators\. The analysis indicates that data become evidence only when contextualized and interpreted in light of school experiences\. It concludes that the contribution of Artificial Intelligence to democratic and inclusive education depends on its articulation with pedagogical documentation and human decisions committed to students’ singularities and to ensuring the right to learn\.
