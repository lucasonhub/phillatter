# Managing a Disputed Statistical Analysis Plan in an FDA Biologics License Application: An Allogeneic Cell Therapy Case Study in Duchenne Muscular Dystrophy

**Autores:** Kaushik Barad; Peivand Pirouzi

**Data:** 2026-10-15 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Allogeneic cardiosphere\-derived cell therapy for Duchenne muscular dystrophy \(DMD\) illustrates a regulatory lesson that extends well beyond this single product category: in a disease area without a validated pharmacodynamic biomarker, the integrity of the prespecified statistical analysis plan can determine a regulatory outcome as decisively as the underlying biology does\. This article examines a representative case in which a sponsor&\#x27;s Biologics License Application for a mutation\-agnostic cell therapy, built on an immunomodulatory and antifibrotic mechanism rather than dystrophin restoration, produced materially different effect estimates depending on which statistical analysis plan was applied to the same pivotal trial data, prompting a Complete Response Letter and a subsequent advisory committee vote against approval\. The article develops the regulatory and statistical lessons this case offers, situates them against the field&\#x27;s existing mutation\-specific accelerated\-approval precedent, and proposes a real\-world evidence framework built specifically to prevent the analysis\-plan dispute from recurring post\-approval\. The article closes with recommendations for regulatory affairs professionals managing evidentiary strategy for mutation\-agnostic cell and gene therapies in rare pediatric disease\.Keywords: allogeneic cell therapy; Duchenne muscular dystrophy; statistical analysis plan; Biologics License Application; real\-world evidence; regulatory artificial intelligence
