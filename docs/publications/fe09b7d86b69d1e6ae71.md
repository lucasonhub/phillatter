# LLM\-based User\-Product Relevance Evaluation for Dynamic Product Advertising

**Autores:** Wen Zhang; Xiao Bai; Hsiang\-wei Chao; Mengfan Zhu; Tong Zhao; Mingxuan Ju; Xiaodong Liu; Congfei Zhang; Yunzhi Zhou; Yajun Wang; Jinchao Li; Yu Zhang

**Data:** 2026-10-10 · **Tipo:** preprint

## Resumo

Dynamic Product Advertising \(DPA\) retrieval systems are a core component of modern e\-commerce advertising; however, their evaluation remains challenging\. Conventional offline engagement metrics are limited by sparse, biased feedback, while online evaluation is costly and time\-consuming\. In this work, we propose a user\-\-product relevance metric for offline evaluation of DPA retrieval systems\. The metric first compresses the engagement history into a compact set of intent representatives via Semantic\-ID\-grouped, event\-stratified segmentation, then assesses the relevance of a recommended product to each individual intent and aggregates them as a user\-level relevance score, yielding a stable and interpretable measure of retrieval quality\. To compute this metric at scale, we leverage large language models \(LLMs\) as semantic relevance judges, capturing nuanced functional and semantic relationships that heuristic methods often miss\. We propose an agentic, human\-aligned prompting framework that generates multi\-category relevance guidelines and distills teacher\-model judgments into an efficient student model for scalable evaluation\. The resulting metric strongly correlates with offline and online business metrics, enabling faster and more reliable DPA retrieval iteration before A/B testing\.
