# Finite element analysis driven optimization of an electrodynamic levitation system with permanent magnet Halbach arrays

**Autores:** Erkan Caner Ozkat; Murat Abdioğlu; K\. Öztürk

**Data:** 2026-10-11 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

This study presents a finite element analysis \(FEA\)\-driven optimization for the design of an electrodynamic levitation \(EDL\) system incorporating permanent magnet Halbach arrays \(PM\-HAs\)\. The primary objective is to maximize the net force difference between lift \( F y \) and drag \( F x \) forces under realistic physical and manufacturing constraints\. The nonlinear electromagnetic behavior of the EDL system is modeled using high\-fidelity FEA, capturing the complex interaction between the PM\-HA and a conductive plate\. At each iteration of the population\-based optimization algorithm, full magnetoquasistatic simulations are performed to ensure a physically accurate evaluation of key performance metrics\. A comprehensive parametric study involving 256 distinct PM\-HA configurations reveals a non\-monotonic relationship between the number of magnets and levitation performance, highlighting the critical role of magnetic field distribution\. Configurations with 9 to 11 magnets achieve the highest net levitation force\. The optimization results indicate that the optimal configuration includes four horizontally aligned permanent magnets \(PMs\) with a width of 74\.00 mm and three vertically aligned PMs with a width of 74\.67 mm, along with a 7 mm thick conductive plate having a conductivity of 5 × 10 7 S/m\. This design delivers a maximum lift force of 3550 N and a minimum drag force of 279 N at 100 m/s\. The findings demonstrate that effective EDL system design requires a carefully arranged magnet configuration rather than a simple maximization of magnet count or size\. The proposed FEA\-guided framework offers a robust and scalable methodology for the optimization of high\-performance Maglev systems\.
