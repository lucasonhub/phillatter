# KASBIY IDENTIFIKATSIYA VA PEDAGOGIK ADAPTATSIYA O‘RTASIDAGI O‘ZARO BOG‘LIQLIK

**Autores:** Keldiyorova Manzura Gaibullayevna

**Data:** 2026-10-12 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

The article provides a theoretical and methodological analysis of the relationship between professional identity and pedagogical adaptation\. Professional identity is conceptualized as a dynamic system of teachers’ perceptions of themselves as professionals, professional values, pedagogical beliefs, professional roles, self\-evaluation, and sense of belonging to the profession\. Pedagogical adaptation is understood as a constructive process through which teachers adjust to educational environments, professional requirements, learners’ needs, pedagogical technologies, institutional norms, and changing socio\-cultural conditions\.
