# LANGUAGE CODE OF COMMUNICATION: TERMINOLOGICAL COMPONENT

**Autores:** Tetiana Koliada\-Berezovska; Olga Novak

**Data:** 2026-10-14 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

In the context of rethinking approaches to teaching and learning the terminology system of a particular field of science, the authors propose an algorithmic model for developing learners’ terminological competence\. The model makes it possible to foreground the terminological component of the language code of professional and business communication\. The study confirms the hypothesis that the application of the algorithmic model for developing terminological competence enhances the analytical potential of the intellect and makes it possible to combine rational and creative modes at the textual and discursive level of language\. Consequently, learning based on this methodological framework enables linguistic knowledge and skills to be cognitively transformed into competencies\. The authors’ concept of an adaptive technology for developing terminological competence as a core component of communicative competence is supported both by learners’ conscious engagement with this competence and by its correlation with the didactic principle of rethinking the status of the term within the contemporary linguistic environment\. Particular emphasis is placed on the synergy between analytical and cognitive approaches, which provides grounds for interpreting adaptive technology as a “resonant system” in which the learner becomes both a subject of new knowledge and an agent of a discursive mode involving the realization of personal intentions\. It is through this process that the language code of communication is formed\. The potential of the proposed algorithmic model lies in its representation of an interdisciplinary analytical strategy, which highlights the interdisciplinary nature of terminology studies\. The latter synthesizes achievements in language theory, communication studies, and cognitive science, while also integrating such parameters as registration, systematization, and diagnostics into professional language training\.
