# BUXDU ZOOLOGIYA MUZEYI KOLLEKSIYALARINING SISTEMATIK TAHLILI: TURLARI, TAYYORLASH VA FIKSATSIYALASH USLUBLARI

**Autores:** MADINABONU MIZROF QIZI GADOYEVA

**Data:** 2026-10-15 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Aim \- to determine the relationship between the composition, preparation technology and fixation regimes of 1185 storage units in the BuxDU zoological museum fund, and to build an orderly scheme for collection passporting\. Methods \- fund records were reviewed, inventory work was carried out, storage units underwent morphological inspection, and descriptive statistics were applied; the units were divided into four main groups, and for each group the preparation mode, fixation medium and label quality were compared\. Results \- 1185 storage units were fully re\-registered and assigned to the four groups in 100\.0 % of cases\. The vertebrate group occupied the largest share, while invertebrates, insects and other zoological objects formed the next levels of the structure\. Materials prepared along three directions showed uneven preservation: in alcohol specimens, morphological traits remained more stable, whereas dry preparations had a higher risk of mechanical damage\. The diversity of fixation regimes also affected the completeness of passport data\. Conclusion \- standardized passporting, unified naming rules and harmonized fixation protocols are required; the scientific contribution lies in a repeatable scheme for structural assessment of the museum fund and criteria for comparing preservation quality across groups\.
