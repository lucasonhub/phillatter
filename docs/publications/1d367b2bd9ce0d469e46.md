# Federated digital twin framework for spectrum handover management in vehicular THz ORAN

**Autores:** Akanksha Gupta; Amira Nisar; Mumtaz Ahmad Khan

**Data:** 2026-10-15 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Vehicular Terahertz \(THz\) Open Radio Access Networks \(ORAN\) face severe challenges due to ultra\-high mobility, rapid channel dynamics, spectrum scarcity, and frequent handovers, which limit the effectiveness of existing reactive and centralized management strategies\. This paper proposes a Federated Blockchain\-Assisted Digital Twin framework for proactive spectrum sharing and seamless handover in vehicular THz ORAN systems\. The framework employs a Contrastive Denoising Spatio\-Temporal Autoencoder \(CD\-STAE\) to refine multi\-source telemetry while preserving transient anomalies, followed by a Federated Multi\-View Neural State\-Space Twin \(FMNSS\) that constructs a privacy\-preserving digital replica of the network\. A Spatio\-Temporal Transformer with Heteroscedastic Uncertainty \(ST\-TrH\) enables confidence\-aware forecasting of load, interference, and mobility\-driven congestion, while On\-Chain Verifiable Reward Shaping \(OVRS\) integrates blockchain\-derived trust scores into federated aggregation and proactive spectrum leasing\. Building on these predictions, a Reinforced Graph Trajectory Encoded DiffNet \(R\-GTEDiffNet\), comprising EM\-ST\-GAT, Causal Trajectory Encoder \(CTE\), and Differentiable Handover Cost Layer \(DHCL\), is introduced to perform mobility\-aware spectrum slicing and predictive handover optimization\. Simulation results demonstrate superior performance over state\-of\-the\-art models, achieving up to 94\.1% spectrum utilization, 6\.2 ms leasing delay, 10\.5 ms handover latency, 1786 Mbps throughput, and an average classification accuracy of 98\.68%, showing the framework’s strength in high\-speed vehicular THz ORAN scenarios\.
