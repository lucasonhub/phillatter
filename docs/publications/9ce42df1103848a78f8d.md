# A multivariable investigation of the critical state line in iron mine tailings

**Autores:** Leonardo Alberto do Nascimento; Magnos Baroni; Dêreck Hummel Becher; ‪Fernando Schnaid

**Data:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

The geotechnical behavior of non\-plastic tailings is governed by a unique critical state line \(CSL\) that, unlike those of many natural soils, may exhibit pronounced non linearity associated with state transitions from strain\-softening at low mean effective stresses to strain\-hardening at higher stress levels\. This study investigates the material properties and state variables controlling this behavior, with particular emphasis on their correlation with index properties and their role in defining inflection points associated with transitions from flow instability and liquefaction to stable soil response\. A data driven meta\-analysis is performed using a global database of laboratory test results on iron tailings\. The methodology integrates statistical characterization of physical attributes, followed by exploratory analysis using Spearman correlation matrices to identify the variables governing the shape and position of the CSL\. Results demonstrate that CSL parameters are primarily controlled by minimum and maximum void ratios and specific gravity, while fines content and mineralogy exert secondary influences\. The established correlations enable prediction of the CSL geometry, providing a practical screening framework for preliminary assessment of the geomechanical behavior of iron tailings and their susceptibility to liquefaction\.
