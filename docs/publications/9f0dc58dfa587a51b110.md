# Caputo\-inspired Adaptive Genetic Algorithm and Taguchi Design of Experiment to Solve Capacitated Ambulance Routing Problem

**Autores:** Marissa Marcella Daulip Lakkui; Jackel Vui Lung Chew

**Data:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

This study proposes an adaptive genetic algorithm based on a nabla Caputo fractional difference with the Taguchi design of experiment for solving the capacitated ambulance routing problem\.The optimization problem considered aims to minimize the total ambulance routing cost while satisfying ambulance availability and capacity\.In this study, a nabla Caputo fractional difference\-based adaptive mutation mechanism is designed and incorporated into the genetic algorithm evolutionary process\.This so\-called memory\-aware mechanism captures historical fitness behavior and is used as an adaptive control signal for dynamically adjusting the mutation rate during optimization\.To obtain appropriate parameter settings systematically, the Taguchi design of experiment is employed in two stages\.The first stage tunes the genetic algorithm parameters, such as the maximum number of generations, the population size, and the fixed mutation rate\.While the second stage tunes the nabla Caputo fractional\-memory parameters, such as the fractional order, the memory window, the scaling coefficient, and the maximum adaptive mutation rate\.The proposed algorithm is interpreted through the lens of nonlinear dynamical systems\.For the performance evaluation, this study uses Augerat benchmark instances\.The results show that the proposed algorithm is competitive against established hybrid metaheuristics\.These findings suggest that integrating the nabla Caputo fractional\-memory dynamics with statistically guided parameter tuning offers a promising solution for the capacitated ambulance routing problem\.
