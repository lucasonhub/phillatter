# Smyth, Daniel Reid: The Affective Forms of Acute Fatigue: Antagonistic, Acquiescent, Hesitative

**Autores:** 

**Data de descoberta:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** unknown

## Descrição do feed

\_Phänomenologische Forschungen\_\. forthcomingThis article argues that acute fatigue resists classification within standard phenomenological affective categories \(emotion, mood, existential feeling, vital feeling, atmosphere, and bodily stirring\)\. Although categorization attempts initially distinguish acute fatigue from chronic fatigue and exhaustion, the diverse cases within acute fatigue do not easily unify under a single affective type\. To go beyond this limit, the article describes how acute fatigue manifests in three structurally distinct affective forms: antagonistic fatigue, in which one finds oneself counteracting a felt sense of inability; acquiescent fatigue, in which felt inability aligns with and confirms one’s evaluative orientation; and hesitative fatigue, an internally divided form that renders transitions between the other two intelligible\. It closes by examining how the social world both exacerbates and partially counteracts the negative affectivity of acute fatigue\.
