# PEDAGOGICAL CONDITIONS FOR USING ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE TECHNOLOGIES IN PREPARING FUTURE TEACHERS FOR PROFESSIONAL ACTIVITY

**Autores:** Sharipova Mashhura Gʻolib qizi

**Data:** 2026-10-10 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

This thesis investigates the structured integration of artificial intelligence \(AI\) tools within higher pedagogical education under specialty 13\.00\.01\. As educational paradigms shift globally towards digital environments, future educators must develop comprehensive digital\-pedagogical competencies\. The study highlights the key technical, methodological, and psychological conditions necessary to successfully adapt generative AI systems like large language models and smart tutoring agents in training workflows\. Ultimately, the paper proposes a cohesive pedagogical framework aimed at boosting critical thinking and lesson planning efficiency among upcoming school teachers\.
