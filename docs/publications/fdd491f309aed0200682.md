# Surgical Approaches for Complex Tibial Plateau Fractures Involving the Posterolateral Quadrant: A Comparative Analysis

**Autores:** Boris A\. Maiorov; Igor G\. Belenkiy; Gennadii D\. Sergeev; Сергеева Мария Александровна

**Data:** 2026-10-15 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

In complex tibial plateau fractures involving the posterolateral quadrants, the standard anterolateral approach has limited capabilities for visualization and stable fixation, which necessitates the search for alternative surgical approaches\. The aim of the study was to conduct a comparative analysis of outcomes following various surgical approaches in patients with tibial plateau fractures involving the posterolateral quadrant\. Materials and Methods\. This prospective multicenter cohort non\-randomized study included 100 patients with intra\-articular tibial plateau fractures extending to the posterolateral quadrant\. Based on the surgical approach used, patients were divided into four groups: direct posterolateral approach or the K\. Frosch approach \(n=25\); fibular head osteotomy approach \(n=22\); combined posteromedial and extended anterolateral approach \(n=26\); and standard anterolateral approach \(ALA\) \(n=27\)\. Reduction quality, complication rates, and functional outcomes \(assessed using the KSS and Lysholm scores\) were evaluated at 6, 12, and 24 months postoperatively\. Results\. The proportion of anatomical reduction was significantly higher in the groups with direct or extended approaches \(60\.0\-76\.9%\) compared to the ALA group \(25\.9%\)\. The rate of unsatisfactory reduction in the ALA group was 40\.7%, versus 3\.8\-9\.1% in the other groups \(p0\.05\)\. The complication rate in the ALA group was 55\.6%, significantly exceeding that of the other groups \(16\.0\-19\.2%; p0\.05\)\. Functional outcomes in the ALA group were inferior at all follow\-up time points: at 24 months, the median Lysholm score was 68\.0 versus 87\.0\-88\.0 in the alternative approach groups \(p=0\.015\), and the median KSS score was 66\.0 versus 86\.0\-87\.0, respectively \(p=0\.019\)\. Multivariate analysis confirmed that outcomes were dependent on the combination of fracture type and the chosen surgical approach\. Conclusion\. The use of extended surgical approaches provided superior reduction quality, lower complication rates, and better functional outcomes compared to the standard anterolateral approach in tibial plateau fractures involving the posterolateral fragment\. Keywords: tibial plateau, posterolateral fragment, surgical approach, fibular head osteotomy, Frosch approach, four\-column theory
