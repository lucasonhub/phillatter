# The Paradox of Ossified Rationality: From the Iron Cage of Bureaucracy to Systemic Alienation in the Post\-Industrial State

**Autores:** Marcos Rafael Berrozpe de Luna

**Data:** 2026-10-11 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

This conceptual paper analyzes the structural frictional losses within the institutional framework of the Federal Republic of Germany at the intersection of sociological theories of domination, political party sociology, and technological disruption\. Building upon Max Weber’s model of legal\-rational domination, the study demonstrates how the historical superiority of this model over feudal arbitrariness has inverted into its opposite\. Integrating perspectives from Michel Foucault, Ulrich Beck, Robert Michels, Manuel Castells, and Mariana Mazzucato, the research argues that the deliberate exclusion of technological tools—such as the prohibition of artificial intelligence in academic examinations—is not an isolated deficit, but rather a systemic defensive reaction and an epistemological survival mechanism of an overburdened, intellectually hollowed\-out bureaucratic apparatus that prioritizes procedural justiciability over the future viability of society\.
