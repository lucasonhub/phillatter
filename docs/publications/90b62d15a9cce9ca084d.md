# Evaluating Educational Policy and Administration: A Weighted Scoring Method Analysis of Performance Dimensions

**Autores:** Mani Devipriya; M Ramachandran; Saravanan Libiya

**Data:** 2026-10-17 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

This study evaluates education policy and management using the Weighted Scoring Method \(WSM\) across five domains: education leadership, management, administration, planning, and public education policy\. Four equally weighted dimensions—policy effectiveness, equity, collaboration, and student allocation—were considered\. The findings indicate that education planning ranked first with a priority score of 0\.72991, demonstrating excellent collaboration and equity performance\. Education management secured second place \(0\.70486\), reflecting balanced performance across the dimensions\. Education leadership ranked third \(0\.56527\), achieving the highest equity score but requiring improved collaboration\. Public education policy placed fourth \(0\.55337\), demonstrating strong policy effectiveness but weaker student allocation\. Educational administration ranked last \(0\.40102\), highlighting the need for comprehensive improvements\. The normalized data and weighted decision matrix identified the relative strengths and weaknesses of each domain\. the study emphasizes strengthening collaboration, promoting equitable resource distribution, and integrating policy effectiveness with efficient student allocation to improve educational governance and management\.
