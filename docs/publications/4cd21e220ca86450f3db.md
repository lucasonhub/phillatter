# AI &amp; ETHICS LITERACY for Teachers

**Autores:** a cura di Aldo Pisano, Ines Crispini, Tiziana Catarci, Maria Cristina Carrisi, Piergiorgio Donatelli, Colin De La Higuera

**Data:** 2026-10-09 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

How do we educate a generation that will grow up making decisions alongside Artificial Intelligence? And how do we develop citizens who are capable not only of using AI, but also of critically understanding how it works, recognizing its limitations, and evaluating its ethical implications? \*AI &amp; Ethics Literacy for Teachers\* was created to address this challenge, proposing an educational model that integrates technological skills, philosophical reflection, and teaching practice\. This volume presents the AI &amp; Ethics Literacy framework through a genuinely interdisciplinary approach, in which moral philosophy, the ethics of Artificial Intelligence, computer engineering, mathematics education, special education, educational sciences, and the methodology of Philosophical Inquiry come together to offer teachers a unified perspective for understanding the complexity of the contemporary digital ecosystem\. Designed primarily for high school, but easily adaptable to middle school as well, the textbook combines scientific rigor, clarity of presentation, and practical applicability\. Alongside the theoretical foundations—from the Large Ethics Model to the PAIA taxonomy, from normative ethics to the principles of Artificial Intelligence—it offers workshops, lesson plans, interdisciplinary pathways, guiding questions, and activities that can be immediately applied to everyday teaching practice\. This book is based on a specific conviction: new educational needs do not necessarily have to translate into more content, additional subjects, or an increased workload for teachers, nor do they require abandoning what already works\. What is needed are tools capable of helping to reinterpret and guide what is already being taught: an agile, flexible, and scientifically grounded toolkit\. For this reason, the textbook does not propose a separate curriculum, but rather a flexible integration into existing curricula, adaptable to various disciplines, interdisciplinary design, and civic education, offering a new lens through which to interpret disciplinary content and bringing to light the ethical, social, and epistemological issues involved in the use of AI technologies\. The aim is to foster argumentative skills, critical thinking, epistemic awareness, and digital citizenship to educate citizens capable not only of using these technologies but also of understanding, discussing, and consciously shaping them\. The result of a pilot program in Italian secondary schools and validated by teachers from various disciplinary fields, this book proposes a transferable model of educational innovation aimed at building a culture of Artificial Intelligence that places the individual, dialogue, and responsibility at its core\.
