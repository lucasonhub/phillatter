# Contributions of Ibero\-American Governments to Making Public Transport Viable with Artificial Intelligence, Neurotechnology, and the Quantum Internet

**Autores:** Eduardo Rommel Rodríguez Gil; Gabriel Judá Muñoz Chávez; Josué Carlos Rojas Krugger

**Data:** 2026-10-11 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Abstract: This research is valuable because it addresses the usefulness of the futuristic internet\. Objective: to analyze what contributions Ibero\-American governments have made to make public transport viable with artificial intelligence \(AI\), neurotechnology, and the quantum internet, and what evidence is still missing for decision\-making\. Method: a qualitative documentary review, descriptive\-interpretive in scope, of 17 sources: 14 journal articles from Ibero\-American and international journals published between 2022 and 2026, and three official public norms or recommendations\. A governance matrix crossing five government functions with three technologies was applied, and the evidence in each cell was classified as sufficient, partial, or absent\. Results: the evidence is sufficient to regulate AI and protect rights in relation to neurotechnology, partial for planning and operating with AI, and scarce for the quantum internet; no source supports the evaluation function\. Contributions to regulatory frameworks and advances in public policy exist; however, no source evaluates their implementation in public transportation\. Conclusions/Contributions: Ibero\-American governments have provided general frameworks, not instruments specific to public transport\. Five alternatives subject to validation are proposed: assessing data impact, postponing neurophysiological monitoring of staff, appointing a sectoral focal point, monitoring the quantum internet without yet investing in its deployment, and establishing post\-implementation evaluation\.
