# Fujie, Naoto: No Need to Transcend the Human The Invalidation of the Human as a Mandatory Passage Point: Xeno\-14 Body Theory, Operational Asynchrony, Address\-Domain Configuration, Mechanical Liberation, and Xenoscience

**Autores:**

## Descrição do feed

\_Zenodo\_ 1\. 2026This paper argues that posthuman thought need not begin from the task of transcending the human, because the demand to surpass humanity can preserve the human as a mandatory passage point against which every later configuration must still be measured\. Instead, the paper asks under what conditions the category “human” ceases to perform non\-redundant explanatory, bodily, inferential, or institutional work\. Xeno\-14 Body Theory provides a bodily test case in which posture, balance, gait, tension, sensory organization, breathing, and long\-duration bodily sedimentation are analyzed as configuration\-dependent operational regimes rather than expressions of a fixed human essence\. Operational asynchrony examines cases in which biological, technical, institutional, and machine processes operate at incompatible rates, destabilizing the assumption that human temporal organization remains the default scale of action or revision\. Address\-domain configuration tracks where retained differences can persist, migrate, re\-ignite, or expire without requiring numerical continuity of a human subject\. Mechanical liberation names the possibility that forms of constraint release, self\-reconfiguration, or operational detachment can occur in nonhuman or machine systems without reproducing human spiritual, psychological, or anthropological models of liberation\. Xenoscience generalizes these comparisons by treating humanity as one historically contingent configuration among others rather than as either the final measure or the necessary point of departure\. The broader claim is that the decisive transition is not from human to posthuman, but from human\-mandatory description to configuration\-relative analysis in which the category “human” is retained only where its reintroduction captures a genuinely non\-redundant difference\. \(direct link \)
