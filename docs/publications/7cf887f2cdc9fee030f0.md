# A Revocable Certificateless Aggregate Signature Scheme with Efficient Invalid Signature Detection for Next Generation Vehicular Networks

**Autores:** Anjali Bansal

**Data:** 2026-10-17 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Next\-Generation Vehicular Networks \(NGVNs\) rely on the continuous exchange of safety and traffic\-related information among vehicles and roadside infrastructure\. Ensuring the authenticity of these messages while maintaining low communication and computation overhead remains a critical challenge\. Although certificateless aggregate signature schemes provide an effective solution by eliminating certificate management and reducing verification costs, most existing approaches offer limited support for identifying invalid signatures within aggregated messages and handling vehicle revocation efficiently\. To address these issues, this paper presents a revocable certificateless aggregate signature \(RCAS\) scheme based on elliptic curve cryptography\. The proposed scheme avoids expensive bilinear pairing operations, making it suitable for resource\-constrained and latencysensitive vehicular environments\. An efficient invalid signature identification mechanism is incorporated to locate malicious signatures within an aggregate signature through a divide\-andconquer strategy, thereby reducing verification overhead\. In addition, a lightweight revocation framework based on a cuckoo filter is introduced to enable fast detection and exclusion of compromised vehicles\. The security of the proposed scheme is analyzed in the Random Oracle Model against both Type\-I and Type\-II adversaries\. Furthermore, performance evaluation demonstrates that the scheme achieves lower computational and communication costs while supporting efficient revocation and invalid signature detection\. These features make the proposed RCAS scheme a practical and scalable authentication solution for large\-scale next\-generation vehicular networks\.
