# Towards Cost\-Efficient Video Dataset Construction with Large Language Models

**Autores:** Mohammed Shabot; Jonathan Adams; Jan Van den Stock; Joost Vennekens

**Data:** 2026-10-12 · **Tipo:** preprint

## Resumo

Research on video classification models has witnessed significant growth recently, accompanied by a significant increase in the demand for datasets to train such models\. Existing video dataset curation approaches share a common bottleneck: candidate videos must be downloaded before any relevance judgment is made, which results in storage, time and annotation costs\. In this work, we present a filtering step, using Large Language Models \(LLMs\) to pre\-screen videos based on their metadata, before actually downloading these videos, thereby saving on these costs\. We evaluate the LLMs against a strict human majority vote derived from four independent annotators and validate the reliability of the annotations through inter\-annotator agreement analysis \(Gwet&\#x27;s AC1\)\. We compare the LLM pre\-filter against two no\-LLM baselines, namely two keyword heuristics\. Via a Leave\-One\-Rater\-Out \(LOO\) mechanism and an Add\-One\-Rater\-In \(AORI\), we evaluate the performance of the LLMs as virtual annotators\. Finally, we report cost comparisons relative to existing approaches\. To ensure reproducability, we made our implementation available on github\.
