# Weighted Views Representation with Multi\-Head Coulomb Attention for Molecular Property Prediction

**Autores:** Muhammad Shahzeb Ali; Martin J\. Mohlenkamp

**Data:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Abstract We extend the weighted\-views molecular featurization framework \(d’Avezac, Botts, Mohlenkamp, and Zunger, 2011\) by enriching it with single\-atom properties and Coulomb pair information\. We use these enriched views within a machine\-learning architecture incorporating shared Transformers whose attention scores directly incorporate Coulomb interactions, weighted aggregation of view outputs, and a final prediction neural network\. We demonstrate performance competitive with the state\-of\-the\-art on the QM7 atomization energy benchmark \(regression\), ranking enantiomers by their docking scores \(trained as regression, tested by ranking\), lipophilicity \(regression\), and blood–brain barrier penetration \(classification\)\.
