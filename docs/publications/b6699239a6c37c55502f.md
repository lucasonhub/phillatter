# Reconceptualizing Investor Protection in Islamic Equity Crowdfunding: A Maqāṣid al\-Sharīʿah\-Based Framework for Digital Financial Governance

**Autores:** Eni Dasuki Suhardini; Dani Durahman; Ahmad Masum; Andri Herman Setiawan; Rifqi Zaeni Achmad Syam

**Data:** 2026-10-14 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Islamic Equity Crowdfunding \(IEC\) has emerged as an alternative financing mechanism for small and medium enterprises and an investment channel consistent with Islamic principles\. In Indonesia, POJK No\. 17 of 2025 regulates IEC, guided by DSN\-MUI Fatwa No\. 140/DSN\-MUI/VIII/2021\. Using digital platforms as IEC intermediaries creates interconnected investor\-protection challenges, including information asymmetry, limited post\-investment monitoring, challenges in continuing Sharia oversight, and emerging digital risks\. This study examines how regulatory and governance mechanisms address these challenges and reconceptualizes investor protection within Islamic economic law\. Using a qualitative socio\-legal approach, the study analyzes regulatory and Sharia documents alongside semi\-structured interviews with investors, platform operators, regulators, and Sharia supervisory stakeholders\. The study analyzes findings through Asymmetric Information Theory and Responsive Law Theory\. It interprets them through maqāṣid al\-sharīʿah, particularly ḥifẓ al\-māl, al\-ʿadl, al\-maṣlaḥah, and rafʿ al\-ḍarar\. The findings reveal a gap between formal compliance and substantive investor protection, particularly in ongoing verification, post\-investment monitoring, risk disclosure, continuing Sharia supervision, and responses to emerging digital risks\. The study develops an Integrated Digital Sharia Investor Protection Framework comprising regulatory oversight, platform accountability, investor risk transparency, continuous Sharia supervision, and digital monitoring\.
