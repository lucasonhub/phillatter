# Suspect and nontargeted screening for arsenic speciation: Advances, challenges, and opportunities

**Autores:** Juanjuan Fu; Mason D’Souza; Tingting Zhao; Qiming Shen; Xiufen Lu; Xing‐Fang Li; X\. Chris Le

**Data:** 2026-10-13 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Arsenic is a ubiquitous metalloid whose toxicity is dependent on chemical species\. Targeted analytical approaches have facilitated the quantification of selected arsenic species; however, structural characterization of previously unrecognized arsenic species remains challenging, particularly when reference standards are unavailable\. Suspect and nontargeted screening \(NTS\) approaches provide opportunities for the discovery of novel arsenic species\. This review discusses recent advances in NTS as applied to arsenic speciation\. Inductively coupled plasma mass spectrometry \(ICPMS\) and electrospray ionization high\-resolution mass spectrometry \(ESI\-HRMS\), in combination with high performance liquid chromatography \(HPLC\), serve complementary roles in arsenic speciation analysis\. The use of ICPMS for specific detection of arsenic complements ESI\-HRMS detection that provides molecular and structural information\. Preservation of arsenic species and efficient extraction of arsenic from real\-world samples are paramount for quantitative speciation analysis\. Challenges related to simultaneous ICPMS and ESI\-HRMS detection from the same HPLC separation include compatibility of solvents and HPLC mobile phases, limited number of authentic standards, and complexity of data acquisition and analysis\. Future research should explore new approaches, assisted by machine learning, that facilitate high\-throughput analysis of complex MS and tandem MS data, which will contribute to establishing a NTS framework for arsenic speciation\.
