# Scalable Identification of Physics\-Based and Neural Dynamics from Partial Measurements

**Autores:** Yibo Zhou; Johannes Köhler

**Data:** 2026-10-12 · **Tipo:** preprint

## Resumo

We present a scalable method for identifying nonlinear dynamics from partial measurements, supporting physics\-based, neural\-network, and gray\-box models\. We use the Prediction Error Method \(PEM\) coupled with state estimation performed by a Moving Horizon Estimation \(MHE\) scheme, which can account directly for general nonlinear dynamics\. This results in a bi\-level optimization problem that scales poorly with increasing model parameters\. To improve computational efficiency, we derive a reformulation that avoids explicit parameter\-wide sensitivity matrices and exploits the temporal structure of the underlying optimization problem\. Numerical comparisons show up to a $215\\times$ speedup in hypergradient evaluation\. Racing\-vehicle simulations demonstrate accurate prediction and physical\-parameter recovery in tire\-force identification\. Real\-world data show that the gray\-box model improves prediction accuracy with a neural residual while retaining physical interpretability\.
