# Teacher\-guided Pseudo\-supervised Dance Human Video Segmentation via Open\-vocabulary Localization and SAM2 Masklet Propagation

**Autores:** Li Dongze; G\. Barbaatar

**Data:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Dance videos combine rapid non\-rigid motion, limb crossings, deformable costumes, and changing illumination, which make human segmentation vulnerable to boundary loss, identity switches, and temporal flicker\.We present DanceMask\-SAM, a teacher\-guided pseudo\-supervised framework that constructs dance pseudo supervision without frame\-wise masks from target training videos\.Offline, open\-vocabulary localization provides global\-human and local\-structure prompts, and overlapping bidirectional SAM2 masklets provide redundant candidates\.Prompt credibility, dance\-aware reliability, and disagreement arbitration assign candidates to hard supervision, weak consistency, or rejection\.Online, a fixed MobileNetV3\-Large\-FPN student uses single RGB frames only and therefore requires no text prompt, SAM2, or pose estimator at deployment\.On the manually reviewed test protocol, the complete student obtains 0\.872 mIoU, 0\.875 J&amp;F, and 0\.061 MFR; across three seeds, the corresponding means are 0\.874, 0\.877, and 0\.061\.The final student is close to, but does not exceed, the teacher\-fused pseudo\-label quality reported for reliability plus arbitration \(0\.874 mIoU and 0\.876 J&amp;F\)\.Video\-level, Bonferroni\-corrected tests support the reported comparisons within the stated split\.External validation on Zhuang ethnic dance videos yields 0\.824 J&amp;F, confirming transfer under domain shift\.Threshold\-frozen protocol details, baseline interaction budgets, reliability sensitivity, risk\-coverage bands with video\-level confidence intervals, teacher compute cost and failure boundaries are reported in Section IV\.The contribution is limited to pseudo\-supervision construction and training decisions under teacher\-guided pseudo\-labels, rather than a new foundation model, a new student architecture, or a self\-supervised representation\-learning result\.
