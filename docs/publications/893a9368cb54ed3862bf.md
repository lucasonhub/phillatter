# Integrating Sustainability and Performance Metrics in Enterprise Architecture Management: A TOPSIS Approach

**Autores:** Vidhya Prasanth; M Ramachandran; Mani Devipriya

**Data:** 2026-10-10 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Enterprise Architecture Management \(EAM\) plays a critical role in aligning business processes, IT infrastructure, and corporate strategy to enhance organizational efficiency and drive digital transformation\. This study evaluates five architectural alternatives using the Technique for Order of Preference by Similarity to Ideal Solution \(TOPSIS\) method to support data\-driven decision\-making in EAM\. The alternatives—Mixed\-Use Development A, High\-Rise Office Building B, Residential Complex C, Educational Institution D, and Healthcare Facility E—were assessed across four key criteria: energy efficiency \(kWh/sqft\), aesthetic appeal, community impact \(rated 1\-5\), and accessibility \(miles\)\. The study employed a systematic approach using normalized data matrices and weighted criteria to determine the optimal solution\. Results revealed that High\-Rise Office Building B ranked highest with a Closeness Coefficient \(Ci\) of 0\.78759, followed by Healthcare Facility E \(Ci = 0\.63579\) and Residential Complex C \(Ci = 0\.56394\)\. Mixed\-Use Development A and Educational Institution D ranked fourth and fifth with Ci values of 0\.40765 and 0\.19466, respectively\. The analysis demonstrates that while High\-Rise Office Building B excelled in energy efficiency and accessibility, Healthcare Facility E showed strong performance in aesthetic appeal and community impact\. The study highlights the effectiveness of the TOPSIS method in evaluating complex architectural alternatives by considering multiple criteria simultaneously\. This approach provides organizations with a structured framework for making informed decisions about infrastructure development while balancing sustainability, functionality, and social impact\. The findings contribute to the growing body of knowledge on quantitative decision\-making methods in EAM and offer practical insights for enterprises seeking to optimize their architectural choices in an increasingly complex digital landscape\.
