# “Comparative Evaluation of Airway Dimensions in Different Sagittal and Vertical Skeletal Patterns: A Two\-Dimensional Cephalometric Study from Central India”

**Autores:** 

**Data:** 2026-10-12 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Background: Pharyngeal airway dimensions are influenced by craniofacial morphology and skeletal growth patterns\.Variations in sagittal skeletal relationships and vertical growth patterns may affect the dimensions of the upper and lower pharyngeal airway\.Understanding these associations may provide useful information for orthodontic diagnosis and treatment planning\.Aim: To evaluate and compare upper and lower pharyngeal airway widths among different vertical growth patterns in skeletal Class I, Class II, and Class III subjects\. Materials and Methods:The study included 150 subjects distributed equally among skeletal Class I, Class II, and Class III groups, with 50 subjects in each skeletal class\.Each skeletal class was further categorized according to vertical growth pattern into hypodivergent, normodivergent, and hyperdivergent groups\.Upper and lower pharyngeal widths were assessed using lateral cephalometric measurements\.The mean values and standard deviations were calculated for each subgroup, and comparisons were performed between growth patterns and among skeletal classes\.Pairwise comparisons were used to determine differences between individual skeletal groups\.Results: Upper pharyngeal width showed a progressive reduction from hypodivergent to hyperdivergent growth patterns in all three skeletal classes\.In skeletal Class I, the mean upper pharyngeal width was 12\.563 mm in hypodivergent, 10\.494 mm in normodivergent, and 9\.583 mm in hyperdivergent subjects\.Corresponding values for Class II were 11\.762, 9\.981, and 8\.344 mm, while Class III showed values of 15\.737, 14\.962, and 14\.366 mm, respectively\.A similar pattern was observed for lower pharyngeal width\.The overall mean upper pharyngeal widths were 10\.82 mm, 9\.96 mm, and 14\.99 mm for Classes I, II, and III, respectively\.For lower pharyngeal width, the corresponding means were 10\.49 mm, 7\.25 mm, and 14\.12 mm\.Pairwise analysis demonstrated statistically significant differences among the skeletal classes for both upper and lower pharyngeal widths\.Conclusion: Pharyngeal airway dimensions varied significantly according to skeletal classification and vertical growth pattern\.Hypodivergent subjects generally demonstrated greater pharyngeal widths than hyperdivergent subjects, while skeletal Class III subjects exhibited the greatest upper and lower pharyngeal dimensions\.These findings highlight the relevance of skeletal morphology and vertical growth pattern in the assessment of pharyngeal airway dimensions during orthodontic diagnosis and treatment planning\.
