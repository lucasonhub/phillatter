# A Literature\-calibrated Digital Twin\-inspired Framework for Secure and Predictable IoT Smart Homes

**Autores:** Ibtehal Shakir Mahmoud; H A Abdulkareem

**Data:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Smart homes are expected to be reliable, secure, and predictable at once, yet most existing systems address these goals in isolation\.This paper introduces a literature\-calibrated, digital\-twin\-inspired simulation framework that integrates network behaviour, biometric authentication, and failure prediction within one environment\.Rather than assuming independent device failures, the framework models failures as a consequence of a slowly varying networkhealth state, allowing a leakage\-controlled classifier trained only on pre\-execution signals to achieve moderate predictive discrimination\.The framework&\#x27;s automatic retry mechanism raises the full system&\#x27;s mean command success rate to 96\.2% \(mean network\-command latency 110\.9 ms, reported alongside the closest published figures for context\); a separate resource\-constrained ablation isolates the risk scores&\#x27; specific contribution, showing that risk\-guided retry allocation preserves 90\.7% success under a matched \~50% retry budget, versus 88\.4% for random allocation\.A STRIDE threat model with CVSS scoring assigns low or very low modelled residual risk to five of six identified threats, leaving traffic analysis as a medium residual risk\.Ablation experiments show retry is the dominant driver of reliability, with risk\-guided prediction adding further benefit once retry capacity is constrained\.
