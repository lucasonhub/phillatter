# Molecular Interaction Dynamic of Natural Alkaloids From Strychnos sp\. With Murine Double Minute Type 2 \(MDM\-2\) – p53 Protein

**Autores:** Arif Setiawansyah; Muhammad Ikhlas Arsul; Gita Susanti; Reza Alrayan; Ismanurrahman Hadi; Endang Kintamani; Nurul Hidayati; Susianti Susianti

**Data:** 2026-10-15 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

MDM\-2 functions as a negative regulator of the p53 tumor suppressor protein, playing a critical role in cancer development and proliferation\. Reactivation of p53 through MDM\-2 inhibition represents a promising therapeutic strategy for combating cancer progression\. This study investigated the potential inhibitory activity of Strychnos species alkaloids against MDM\-2 and assessed their ADMET profiles using in silico approaches\. Molecular docking was performed using AutoDock 4\.2, followed by molecular dynamics simulations in GROMACS to evaluate binding stability\. Computational analyses revealed that Strychnopentamine and Leuconicine A demonstrated superior binding affinity to MDM\-2 compared to the established inhibitor Nutlin\-3\. Both compounds exhibited favorable pharmacokinetic profiles, though Leuconicine A showed predicted hepatotoxicity and mutagenicity risks\. Molecular dynamics simulations confirmed the stability of the Strychnopentamine\-MDM\-2 complex over 50 nanoseconds, with binding characteristics comparable to Nutlin\-3\. These computational findings identify Strychnopentamine as a promising lead candidate for MDM\-2 inhibition, though experimental validation through in vitro and in vivo studies is required to confirm these predictions and establish therapeutic potential\.
