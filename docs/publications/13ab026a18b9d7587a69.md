# Thomas, Emily: C14313C\. D\. Broad on Eternalism and Growing Block Theory 1913–1928

**Autores:**

## Descrição do feed

In Real Time: A Reinvention in British Metaphysics 1883\-1928\. Oxford University Press\. 2026Over the course of his long career, Broad’s philosophy of time evolved, comprising three phases\. Each phase is associated with a particular text\. The first, associated with Broad’s 1921 encyclopaedia article “Time”, offers eternalism\. The second phase, associated with Broad’s 1923 Scientific Thought, offers what may be the first English\-language defence of growing block theory\. The third phase, associated with the second, 1938 volume of Broad’s Examination of McTaggart’s Philosophy, appears to offer a new theory altogether\. This chapter explores the first and second phases\. This chapter shows that Broad’s early eternalism is marked by his admiration for Russell and an antagonism towards Bergson\. Further, this chapter argues Broad was driven away from eternalism by a problem he found in Alexander: a newfound concern with the direction of time\. He likely arrived at the unreality of the future as a solution following his earlier apparent reading of Whitehead as a growing block theorist\.
