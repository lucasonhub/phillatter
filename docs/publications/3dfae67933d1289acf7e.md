# Adaptive storage optimization for deep learning workloads in converged HPC\-cloud environments: a reinforcement learning approach

**Autores:** Hovhannes Baghdasaryan; Hrachya V\. Astsatryan

**Data:** 2026-10-17 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Abstract Storage systems remain a significant bottleneck in large\-scale deep learning training\. Most evaluation studies rely on synthetic benchmarks that do not capture the I/O heterogeneity present in real training pipelines\. Automated configuration methods often treat parameter selection as stateless, overlooking the temporal structure of recurring training phases\. These challenges are addressed using a converged HPC\-cloud platform that integrates Lustre with S3\-compatible object storage\. Three workloads with distinct I/O characteristics were evaluated: ResNet\-50 on ImageNet\-1K, 3D U\-Net on the Medical Segmentation Decathlon, and Whisper Large on LibriSpeech\. Time\-to\-convergence was used as the primary metric\. We introduced a deep reinforcement learning framework that combines Proximal Policy Optimization and Soft Actor\-Critic with a Transformer\-based state encoder to predict workload phase transitions rather than merely respond to them\. The learned policies reduced checkpoint write latency by up to 74\.6% and improved GPU utilization across all three workloads\. The approach generalizes across heterogeneous I/O profiles without per\-workload reconfiguration\. The results indicate that temporally aware storage control can improve the efficiency of distributed training pipelines in heterogeneous HPC\-cloud environments\.
