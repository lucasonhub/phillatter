# Biophotonic Signaling in Energetic Biocommunication: A Conceptual Framework and Experimental Strategy for Validating Weak Optical Interactions in Biofields

**Autores:** Rick Sá

**Data:** 2026-10-15 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Ultraweak photon emissions \(UPEs\) represent a plausible yet underexplored biophysical mechanism underlying energetic biocommunication between human organisms, particularly in the context of biofield therapies \(BTs\) such as Reiki, Therapeutic Touch, and External Qigong\. Although biophotonic emission is a well\-documented property of living systems across all kingdoms of life, the present perspective focuses specifically on the human organism as a photoinformatic system\. The central methodological gap obstructing empirical validation lies in the inability to simultaneously detect UPEs from a human source and correlate them with the resulting biochemical and functional changes induced in a target biological system\. This work presents a restructured, convergent framework built around three pillars: \(i\) a theoretical and experimental basis for evaluating four interrelated hypotheses concerning selective photonic biocommunication; \(ii\) a tripartite laboratory strategy integrating multimodal multiphoton tomography, stimulated Raman spectroscopy, and polychromatic spectral analysis of UPEs; and \(iii\) an explicit discussion of limitations\. The framework anchors energetic biocommunication in measurable parameters such as photon flux, spectral distribution, coherence, and biological responses, providing operational foundations for rigorous investigation of BTs as potential modulators of endogenous photonic signaling and paving the way for standardized biophotonic measurement technologies in precision medicine\.
