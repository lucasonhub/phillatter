# Integrating Median Filtering with Deep Pattern Recognition Models for Improved Breast Ultrasound Cancer Detection

**Autores:** Zeeshan Ahmad; Yasir Aziz

**Data:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Even though ultrasound of the breast is an easy to access imaging modality and is radiation free, speckle\-like intensity fluctuations, low contrast, variability of acquisition and limited model transparency are the challenges in automated classification of ultrasound lesions\. In this paper, we introduce a controlled framework of preprocessing\-classification\-explainability that compares the aforementioned three image types \(raw, 3 × 3 median filtered, median filtered \+ contrast\-limited adaptive histogram equalization \(CLAHE\)\) using the same leakage\-safe data split, convolutional neural network \(CNN\), training budget and evaluation protocol\. To minimize the information leakage, the downloaded Kaggle archive of benign and malignant images was pre\-augmented with 9016 images; and then divided into 70/15/15 train/validation/test sets based on augmentation\-family grouping\. The 96\.97% accuracy, 98\.95% sensitivity, 95\.04% specificity, 95\.10% precision, 96\.99% F1\-score, 0\.99623 ROC\-AUC and 0\.99608 PR\-AUC of the model that was median\-filtered were the highest\. Compared to raw standardized baseline, median filtering resulted in an increase in accuracy and sensitivity by 3\.55 and 6\.15 percentage points, respectively, and in a decrease of false negative cases from 48 to 7\. Median restoration also gave the best results in terms of PSNR and SSIM for controlled synthetic\-speckle testing, which were 27\.9313 dB PSNR and 0\.8214 SSIM, respectively\. There was a significant reduction in paired errors after raw versus median filtering \(adjusted p = 0\.000008\) by Exact McNemar analysis\. Grad\-CAM, LIME and SHAP also revealed class\-specific areas and failure modes but only showed low spatial overlap, which indicated that these are complementary rather than interchangeable\. The results indicate that a very basic median filter which preserves the edges can enhance the predictive reliability and model auditing when the effects of the pre\-processing are separated in an experimental setup\.
