# From Access to Agency: A Capability\-Based Reconceptualization of Linguistic Equity in Generative AI–Mediated Language Education

**Autores:** Nazik Ahmed

**Data:** 2026-10-13 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Generative artificial intelligence \(GenAI\) has recently become adopted into language learning environments at unprecedented rates\. Though research has yet to catch up to practice, language educators and researchers are concerned with questions of equity related to GenAI tools\. The bulk of this research has been technocentric, prioritizing access to AI instead of how students convert access into actionable freedoms\. To fill this gap, the current study sought to reconceptualize linguistic equity from a capability perspective\. The researchers utilized a quasi\-experimental mixed\-methods design with 80 EFL learners in experimental and control groups\. Paired and independent sample t\-tests were used to analyze quantitative data, while thematic analysis was used to code qualitative data\. Results showed significant improvement for the experimental group \(pre\-test M = 10\.58, post\-test M = 18\.27; t\(39\) = 15\.63, p &lt; \.001\) over the control group \(pre\-test M = 10\.46, post\-test M = 13\.34; t\(39\) = 5\.42, p &lt; \.001\), with a statistically significant difference between groups at post\-test \(t\(78\) = 11\.48, p &lt; \.001\)\. Quantitative results from questionnaires show a high sense of agency \(M = 4\.18\)\. The results of this study have important implications for reconceptualizing linguistic equity as depending not just on access but on the capability to use artificial intelligence to succeed in language performance environments\. The study adds to theory by providing preliminary work on a capability framework and provides practical application toward equitable GenAI pedagogy Keywords: generative artificial intelligence, linguistic equity, capability approach, learner agency, EFL education
