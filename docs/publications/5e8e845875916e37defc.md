# INKLYUZIV TA’LIM SHAROITIDA BO‘LAJAK O‘QITUVCHILARNING XULQ\-ATVOR KORREKSIYASIGA OID KASBIY KOMPETENTLIGINI RIVOJLANTIRISH

**Autores:** Shermatova Manzura Ikromjanovna

**Data:** 2026-10-13 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

This article examines the development of preservice teachers’ professional competence in identifying challenging behavioural situations, analysing the possible functions of behaviour, and designing individualized support within inclusive educational settings\. International research on inclusive pedagogy, functional behavioural assessment, positive behavioural support, and teacher efficacy is critically reviewed alongside the contributions of Uzbek scholars P\. Po‘latova and B\. Xodjayev\. Drawing on this analysis, a conceptual framework is proposed that integrates diagnostic, functional\-analytical, intervention\-planning, collaborative, and evaluative competencies\. The article argues that professional preparedness should be assessed not only through theoretical knowledge but also through observable pedagogical decision\-making, individualized support planning, and the evaluation of educational interventions\. The study provides a theoretical foundation for competency\-based teacher education in inclusive settings while recognizing the need for subsequent empirical validation\.
