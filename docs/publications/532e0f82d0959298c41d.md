# Validation of diagnostic artificial intelligence models for prostate pathology in a Middle Eastern cohort

**Autores:** Peshawa J\. Muhammad Ali; Navin Vincent; Saman S\. Abdulla; Han N\. Mohammed Fadhl; Anders Blilie; Kelvin Szolnoky; Julia Anna Mielcarz; Xiaoyi Ji; Nita Mulliqi; Abdulbasit K\. Al‐Talabani; Kimmo Kartasalo

**Data:** 2026-10-10 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Artificial intelligence \(AI\) for pathology is accelerating cancer diagnostics\. For global adoption, studies in currently under\-represented populations \- where the potential gains from AI may also be greatest \- are needed\. We present the first external validation study in a Middle Eastern setting, focusing on diagnosis and Gleason grading of prostate cancer\. We digitised 339 prostate glass slides from Kurdistan, Iraq, representing a consecutive series of 185 patients from 2013 to 2024\. Each slide was scanned on three digital pathology scanners, generating 1,017 whole\-slide images\. Task\-specific and foundation AI models demonstrated AI\-pathologist grading concordance similar to pathologist\-pathologist concordance \(Cohen&\#x27;s quadratically weighted kappa 0\.801 vs\. 0\.799\)\. Cross\-scanner concordance was high \(kappa &gt; 0\.90\) for all AI models across three scanners\. The demonstrated robust performance on a low\-cost compact scanner could enable validation studies in non\-digitalised settings and cost\-effective AI adoption in laboratories with limited sample volumes\. The publicly shared dataset supports further research to facilitate broader adoption of AI pathology\.
