# Comprehensive multi\-omics analysis identifies BTG2 as a potential regulator of radiotherapy response in lung adenocarcinoma

**Autores:** Ming\-Hao Wang; Xiao\-Jing Wei; Yu Wang; Dilinaer Wusiman; Yi\-Tong Li; Xiao\-Kai You; Hai‐Ling Qie; Cheng\-Yi Zhang; Xuefeng Wang

**Data:** 2026-10-13 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Lung adenocarcinoma \(LUAD\) is one of the most common malignancies worldwide\. However, patients exhibit heterogeneous responses to radiotherapy, and a subset of individuals develops resistance, which severely compromises clinical outcomes\. The underlying molecular basis of this variability remains unclear\. This study aimed to investigate RSG\-associated molecular features in radiotherapy\-treated LUAD and to identify potential prognostic biomarkers\. We integrated multi\-omics data and applied multiple machine learning approaches to comprehensively analyze LUAD samples\. In addition, single\-cell data were incorporated to further characterize the tumor microenvironment\. The results revealed two distinct RSG\-associated prognostic subtypes with different survival outcomes and biological characteristics\. The poor\-prognosis subtype was associated with significant alterations in the immune microenvironment and related biological pathways\. Furthermore, we developed a predictive model with stable performance for evaluating patient survival outcomes\. Among the identified candidates, BTG2 emerged as a key gene in the RSG\-based prognostic model\. Functional experiments further showed that BTG2 overexpression inhibited tumor cell proliferation\. Bidirectional clonogenic radiation dose\-response assays showed lower post\-irradiation survival after BTG2 overexpression and higher survival after BTG2 depletion, supporting BTG2 as a cellular modulator of radiation response\. In summary, this study identified two RSG\-associated prognostic subtypes among radiotherapy\-treated patients with LUAD and highlighted BTG2 as a potential biomarker associated with prognosis and radiation\-response\-related biological features\. These findings provide a framework for further prospective validation and may contribute to future risk stratification and individualized treatment strategies\.
