# Blockchain\-Enabled IoT Tracking for a Real\-Time, Tamper\-Proof Cold\-Chain Monitoring System in India’s Vaccine Supply Network

**Autores:** 

**Data:** 2026-10-13 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

India&\#x27;s vaccine distribution network is one of the largest and most complex in the world, serving over 1\.4 billion people through its Universal Immunisation Programme \(UIP\)\.However, this vast network relies on a fragile cold\-chain that is often monitored through manual, paper\-based logs, which are susceptible to human error, data manipulation, and significant time delays in detecting temperature excursions\.Recent systematic reviews have identified persistent data gaps, limited end\-to\-end visibility, and high loss\-to\-specification rates as key bottlenecks undermining vaccine potency and public health outcomes\.This paper proposes and evaluates a hybrid system that couples low\-power Internet of Things \(IoT\) temperature sensors with a lightweight blockchain ledger\-specifically Hyperledger Besu operating under a Proof\-of\-Authority \(PoA\) consensus mechanism\-to record every temperature reading in a tamper\-proof, time\-stamped, and cryptographically verifiable format\.The proposed architecture comprises four layers: \(i\) a hardware layer featuring DS18B20 digital temperature sensors interfaced with ESP32 microcontrollers communicating over LoRaWAN /LPWAN gateways; \(ii\) an edge\-computing layer built on Raspberry Pi 4 nodes running lightweight Docker containers that aggregate sensor data, encrypt it using AES\-256 encryption, and generate Merkle proofs; \(iii\) a blockchain layer where each encrypted data hash and its corresponding timestamp are immutably recorded on the distributed ledger; and \(iv\) a public\-key\-based access layer that exposes RESTful APIs secured by OAuth 2\.0 and real\-time dashboards built on Grafana and Prometheus, allowing authorized stakeholders\-including state health departments, auditors, and vaccine manufacturers\-to monitor cold\-chain integrity in real time\.A comprehensive cost\-benefit analysis using a net present value \(NPV\) model over a five\-year horizon with an 8% discount rate projects that nationwide roll\-out across India&\#x27;s approximately 27,000 cold\-chain points would require an estimated capital expenditure of ₹300 million and annual operational expenditure of ₹50 million, while yielding net savings of approximately ₹1\.2 billion in reduced vaccine wastage within the first two years\.The study thus demonstrates that blockchain\-enabled IoT tracking not only improves auditability and reduces vaccine losses but also provides scalable, interoperable, and economically viable infrastructure for India&\#x27;s evolving vaccine logistics ecosystem\.Policy recommendations include integration with the Ministry of Health and Family Welfare&\#x27;s electronic Vaccine Intelligence Network \(eVIN\) and incentive structures for private cold\-chain operators to adopt such systems\.
