# Pedagogical conditions for forming the readiness of future teachers at the university to use modular e\-learning technology

**Autores:** Bayan Kylyshpai; I Yusupova; Raikhan Skakova; Kenzhekhan Medeubayeva; Salima Seitenova

**Data:** 2026-10-13 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

The study aims to determine the impact of the use of modular e\-technology on the level of formation of motivational\-value, cognitive and activity\-practical components of readiness of students of the pedagogical university\. The study is based on a quasi\-experimental design of “pre\-testing with a control group”\. The sample was formed by 162 students of pedagogical specialties of 2–4 years, evenly distributed between the experimental \(n = 81\) and control \(n = 81\) groups\. The diagnostic complex consisted of the author&\#x27;s questionnaire RMETQ, the knowledge test KMELT and a practical task with the analytical rubric ARMECD\. Statistical analysis consisted of MANOVA, ANCOVA, dependent and independent t\-tests, correlation analysis \(Pearson/Spearman\) and effect size estimation\. The results indicated the superiority of the experimental group in all components of readiness \(p &lt; \.001; d = 1\.77–2\.58; η²p = \.439–\.622\)\. The greatest increase was recorded for the activity\-practical component \(\+103\.8%\)\. In the experimental group, an integrated structure of readiness with strong intercomponent correlations \(r = \.61–\.72\) was formed, in contrast to weak connections in the control group \(r = \.27–\.33\)\. The results determined the feasibility of introducing modular e\-technology into the system of pedagogical university training\.
