# DeLTA 3\.0: A modern segmentation and tracking tool for microscopy images

**Autores:** Virgile Andreani; Jean‐Baptiste Lugagne; Owen M\. O’Connor; Mary J\. Dunlop

**Data:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Time\-lapse microscopy is a popular approach for quantifying single\-cell dynamics\.Analyzing image sequences requires two steps: segmentation \(locating the cells\) and tracking \(following them\)\.DeLTA \(Deep Learning for Time\-lapse Analysis\) is a Python package that performs segmentation and tracking\.Initially developed to handle images of bacteria growing within the &quot;mother machine,&quot; \(Wang et al\., 2010\) a popular microfluidic device for single\-cell analysis that constrains cells to grow in one dimension \(Lugagne et al\., 2020\), it was later extended to handle cells growing in 2D \(O&\#x27;Connor et al\., 2022\)\.After three years of experience and feedback, we are releasing version 3\.0 \-a major overhaul to achieve four objectives: enhance usability for scientists, improve modularity and adaptability for developers, integrate features like growth rate computation and lineage manipulation, and adopt modern open\-source development practices\.
