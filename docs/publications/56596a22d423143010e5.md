# DIGITAL LOBBYING MODEL OF THE DIASPORA IN THE ERA OF ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE: SOCIAL MEDIA, NARRATIVE DIPLOMACY AND TRANSNATIONAL POLITICAL COMMUNICATION

**Autores:** Kamandari A\.

**Data:** 2026-10-15 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

The article examines digital diaspora lobbying as an organizational and communicative process linking collective interests with their presentation to decision\-making institutions\. It aims to clarify the functional relationship between representation, social media, narrative diplomacy and artificial intelligence, and to propose a conceptual model of the main stages of this process\. The study is based on an analytical review of selected scientific literature, conceptual comparison and functional modeling\. Five foundations of diaspora representation are identified: defining the represented community, agreeing on common interests, granting representative authority, ensuring internal participation and maintaining accountability\. The model includes six stages: defining the mandate, specifying the problem and evidence base, preparing the narrative and institutional proposal, public communication, engagement with decision\-making institutions, and evaluation of results\. Artificial intelligence supports data classification, source search, multilingual drafting and document comparison, while collective decision\-making and public responsibility remain with the organization\. Results are assessed through communicative visibility, trust and institutional response\. The proposed framework links these levels to specific stages and traceable outputs and requires further empirical verification\.
