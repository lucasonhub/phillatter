# EFFICIENCY OF LAND RESOURCE USE BY THE REGIONAL AGRICULTURAL BUSINESS: ZONAL AND INNOVATIVE ASPECTS

**Autores:** Oleg Nikolaevich Bunchikov; Vladimir Dzhuha; Vladimir Ivanovich Gayduk; Tat&\#x27;yana Chumakova; Aleksey Vorotyncev

**Data:** 2026-10-17 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

This article examines key approaches to the efficient use of agricultural land in the Rostov Region&\#x27;s agrarian sector\. Drawing on official statistical data, scholarly publications, and other sources, the study evaluates Don\-region agribusinesses operating in the crop production sector\. The analysis focuses on indicators and aspects such as crop yields, the efficiency of crop rotation patterns across different natural\-agricultural zones, and the impact of various modern innovative technologies on the economic efficiency of crop production\.
