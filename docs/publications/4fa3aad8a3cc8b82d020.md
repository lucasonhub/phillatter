# One Health in action: regional innovations and shared challenges in addressing antimicrobial resistance

## Resumo

Antimicrobial resistance \(AMR\) disproportionately affects low\- and middle\-income countries, yet perspectives and innovations from these regions remain underrepresented\. During World Antimicrobial Awareness Week 2025, the AMR Knowledge Hub convened a four\-day webinar series on AMR challenges and One Health solutions across three regions, with a country spotlight on Nigeria\. Discussions highlight critical gaps in political commitment, sustainable financing, and locally relevant evidence, particularly on antibiotic stewardship\. Regional and country experiences demonstrate the importance of context\-specific research, locally driven innovation, and stronger governance\. Integrating these voices into global AMR governance is essential for a more equitable, effective, and enduring response\. Authors present their work on practitioner perspectives on antimicrobial resistance \(AMR\) from Africa, Asia, and the Middle East and North Africa, with a country spotlight on Nigeria, highlighting shared implementation gaps and locally driven innovations\.
