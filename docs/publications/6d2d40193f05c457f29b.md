# Psychometric\-aware Fusion of Tabular and Item\-sequence Representations Using a Stacking Ensemble Meta\-learner for Anxiety Severity Classification in Final\-year Students

**Autores:** Nur Arminarahmah; Sri Ayatina Hayati; Muhammad Subhan; Haldi Budiman; Rezka Norhafizah

**Data:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Academic, career and social challenges may heighten anxiety among final\-year students\.In this work, a psychometric\-aware stacked ensemble for anxiety\-severity classification based on Hamilton Anxiety Rating Scale \(HARS\) scores was proposed\.It does not replace the deterministic scoring rule, but rather assesses item\-response representations to approximate severity scores derived from the HARS\.Fourteen items are treated as ordinal responses via tabular descriptors and ordered sequences, and three models \(Random Forest, Support Vector Machine and Long Short\-Term Memory\) are unified through a logistic\-regression meta\-learner trained on out\-of\-fold probabilities\.After duplicate screening, 306 responses were analysed\.The proposed model attained 0\.9677 accuracy on the test split and averaged 0\.948 ± 0\.037 \(empirical 2\.5th\-97\.5thpercentile interval 0\.905\-0\.998\)across five stratified splits, the principal result\.It was contrasted with ordinal regression, calibrated LightGBM, CatBoost, monotonic gradient boosting, and a deterministic\-rule baseline, while ablation and missing\-and noisy\-input analyses corroborated itemresponse characterisation amid constrained conditions\.
