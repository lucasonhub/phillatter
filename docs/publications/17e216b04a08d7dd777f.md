# Socio\-Cultural and Spiritual\-Ideological Factors of Preserving and Strengthening National Identity Under Globalization

**Autores:** Salim Musurmonovich Doniyorov

**Data:** 2026-10-15 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

The article examines the socio\-cultural and spiritual\-ideological factors of preserving and strengthening national identity under globalization\. An analysis of 2023–2025 shows a sharp increase in the global openness of society: inbound and outbound trips grew from 11\.4 to 19\.2 million \(1\.69 times\), the share of Internet users among people aged 10 and over reached 92\.7%, and 99\.3% use mobile communications\. In response, the network of libraries and information\-library centres expanded by 36\.2% \(to 17,763\), while the number of magazines and other periodicals fell by 48\.9% and theatre performances by 8\.3%\. The need to move from a defensive model of identity preservation to an adaptive\-competitive model combining ideological immunity, the digital presence of national culture and cultural diplomacy is substantiated\.
