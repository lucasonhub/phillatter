# Bridging scales: a perspective on macro\-to\-micro transfer for microscopy foundation models

**Autores:** Yuanjie Gu; Yiqun Wang; Jun Zhang; Zhi Lü; Biqin Dong

**Data:** 2026-10-14 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Foundation models \(FMs\) are transforming artificial intelligence for science, but microscopy FMs remain constrained by limited, heterogeneous, and information\-sparse data\. We propose a Macro\-to\-Micro paradigm in which large\-scale macroscopic pretraining establishes a broad visual representation, followed by microscopy\-specific adaptation that introduces biological specificity and modality\-dependent imaging knowledge\. This staged strategy enables scarce microscopy data to specialize a mature representation rather than build one from scratch\. Combined with domain\-aware adaptation and a coordinated microscopy data ecosystem, Macro\-to\-Micro provides a data\-efficient roadmap toward foundation models that ground general visual intelligence in biological knowledge and the physics of image formation\.
