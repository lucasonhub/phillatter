# RINOLALIYALI BOLALAR NUTQINI RIVOJLANTIRISHDA RAQAMLI TA’LIM VOSITALARIDAN FOYDALANISH IMKONIYATLARI

**Autores:** SAYYORA KENJABEK QIZI UMARALIYEVA

**Data:** 2026-10-15 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

The article analyzes the theoretical foundations and practical potential of using digital educational tools to develop the speech of preschool children with open rhinolalia\. The study involved 24 children aged 5–7 in the postoperative period following cleft palate repair\. In the experimental group, conventional speech therapy sessions were combined with visual biofeedback software, interactive game platforms and remote speech therapy support\. The results showed more pronounced improvement in speech breathing, sound production, phonemic hearing and reduction of hypernasality compared with the control group\. Methodological recommendations for integrating digital tools into speech therapy are provided\.
