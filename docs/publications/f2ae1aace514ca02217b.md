# Green Card Health Insurance and Out\-of\-Pocket Health Expenditures in Türkiye: Evidence from Entropy Balancing

**Autores:** Abdullah Tirgil

**Data:** 2026-10-15 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Over the past two decades, Türkiye has significantly advanced health system reforms towards universal health coverage, notably expanding non\-contributory, government\-funded health insurance benefits \(Green Card\) for low\-income households to mitigate inequities in financing, access, and outcomes\. This research investigates the association between the Green Card and low\-income households&\#x27; out\-of\-pocket healthcare expenditures, focusing on physician and hospital costs\. Using the Entropy Balancing technique on TurkStat Household Budget Survey data \(2005\-2018\) to balance observed characteristics between Green Card holders and non\-holders, the study finds an inverse relationship between Green Card and healthcare costs, especially in regions with enhanced infrastructure\. These results are consistent with non\-contributory insurance being an important tool for addressing financing inequities for the disadvantaged, and the association between the program and out\-of\-pocket expenditures varies with the region&\#x27;s health infrastructure\.
