# Neuroderechos como respuesta a los avances de las neurotecnologías

**Autores:** Lesly Gissela Tipanguano\-Chicaiza; Bolívar David Narváez\-Montenegro; Byron Javier Chulco\-Lema

**Data:** 2026-10-15 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

The advance of neurotechnologies, capable of recording and influencing brain processes, poses the challenge of protecting personal integrity\. The paper examines neurorights as an ethical and legal response aimed at safeguarding mental privacy, identity, autonomy and psychological integrity\. With a qualitative, descriptive\-explanatory and documentary approach, international human rights instruments, scholarly proposals and the models of Chile and Spain were analyzed\. It is concluded that traditional frameworks, designed for data protection and physical integrity, are insufficient for technologies that access brain information and may interfere with decision\-making, and that Chile, with its constitutional reform, offers a reference\. It is proposed that Ecuador discuss the recognition of neurorights and a regulation of neurodata\.
