# AI in Aviation Security: Creating Policy and Regulating the Process

**Autores:** Beka Khitiri

**Data:** 2026-10-12 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Aviation security is a field that demands precise policies and tailor\-made regulations\. However, this need tends to make it harder to evolve over time\. Hence, it is particularly difficult to adapt to technological changes\. This creates a major weakness, as the threats that aviation security is created to oppose are not bound by such limitations\. Therefore, it is imperative that the field at hand is regulated in a way that makes it possible to keep up with new instruments available, such as artificial intelligence \(AI\)\. By clearly understanding the benefits, drawbacks, and limitations of AI, regulators can, by remaining in line with the above principles, ensure that this useful tool is integrated within the field, resulting in a major net positive\.
