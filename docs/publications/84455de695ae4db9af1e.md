# Legalità algoritmica e antropocentrismo formale: la deci\-sione amministrativa automatizzata alla prova dell&\#x27;art\. 14 l\. 132/2025

**Autores:** Dominique Andreoli

**Data:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

This paper analyses Article 14 of Law No\. 132/2025 on the use of artificial intelligence in public administration, within the broader framework of algorithmic legality\. It examines the relationship between administrative automation, transparency, traceability, human oversight and responsibility, highlighting the main critical issues raised by opaque systems, automation bias, discretionary power and the effectiveness of safeguards\.
