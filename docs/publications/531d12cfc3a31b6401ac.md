# Professional Development Needs of English Language Teachers in Azerbaijani Higher Education: A Conceptual and Methodological Framework

**Autores:** Afsana Mirjalal Orujlu

**Data:** 2026-10-11 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

This article develops a contextual framework for investigating the professional\-development needs of English language teachers in Azerbaijani higher education\. It distinguishes attendance at isolated training events from sustained professional learning that changes classroom reasoning and practice\. An integrative review links six dimensions: reflective practice, teacher agency, collegial learning, classroom\-based inquiry, institutional support, and equitable access across locations and career stages\. On this basis, the article proposes a convergent mixed\-methods needs assessment combining a questionnaire, semi\-structured interviews, non\-evaluative classroom observations, and document analysis\. The design examines how teachers judge the relevance of available development, apply new approaches, collaborate with colleagues, use digital resources, and experience organizational opportunities or constraints\. It also specifies sampling, instrument development, analysis, integration, ethics, and quality\-assurance procedures suitable for a future multi\-institutional study\. Because no original participant data are reported, the discussion presents evidence\-informed propositions rather than empirical findings\. The framework suggests that Azerbaijani universities should connect professional learning with classroom problems, protected time, mentoring, peer inquiry, research support, and accessible hybrid provision\. Its principal contribution is a transparent research design for identifying locally differentiated needs and converting them into institutional action\.
