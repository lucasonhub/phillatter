# Numerical investigation of valence band offset at the HTL/Cs2PdBr6 interface for high\-performance double perovskite solar cells

**Autores:** Sankarshan Arane; Piyush Kumar Patel

**Data:** 2026-10-12 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

All\-inorganic lead\-free Cs 2 PdBr 6 perovskite solar cells have attracted significant attention owing to their excellent environmental stability and promising optoelectronic properties\. In this work, the influence of HTL valence band offset on the electrostatic behaviour, carrier transport, recombination kinetics, and photovoltaic performance of Cs 2 PdBr 6 solar cells is systematically investigated using SCAPS\-1D simulation\. Different HTLs spanning cliff\-type, intermediate spike regime and excessive spike\-type alignments are analysed\. The results reveal a strong non\-monotonic dependence of device performance on the HTL valence band offset\. Cliff\-type alignment produces weak interfacial electric fields and diffusion\-limited carrier extraction, leading to increased interfacial recombination losses\. In contrast, balanced positive VBO generates an optimised depletion field that promotes efficient drift\-assisted carrier transport and suppresses interfacial recombination\. Among the investigated HTLs, V 2 O 5 with a \+ 0\.3 eV VBO achieves the best performance, delivering a power conversion efficiency of 27\.48% with V OC = 1\.26 V, J SC = 25\.1 mA cm − 2 , and FF = 87\.3%\. Large positive VBO alignment induces carrier accumulation and a transport barrier, causing performance degradation\. QE analysis further confirms that optimised VBO engineering enhances long\-wavelength carrier collection by extending the effective drift region across the absorber bulk\. These findings establish HTL VBO engineering as a key strategy for achieving high\-efficiency lead\-free Cs 2 PdBr 6 perovskite solar cells\.
