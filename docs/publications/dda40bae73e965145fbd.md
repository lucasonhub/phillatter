# ENHANCING ENGLISH LANGUAGE LEARNING THROUGH ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE: OPPORTUNITIES AND CHALLENGES FOR TEACHERS IN UZBEKISTAN

**Autores:** Juraeva Maftuna Komilovna

**Data:** 2026-10-10 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Artificial intelligence \(AI\) is creating new opportunities for English language teaching through personalized learning, interactive practice, automated feedback, and instructional material development\. However, its effective integration requires pedagogical planning, digital competence, and attention to ethical considerations\. These issues are particularly relevant to English language education in Uzbekistan, where teachers work in diverse educational settings\.
