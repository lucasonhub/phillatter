# Wang, Haoyu: Humanoid Robots and Common\-Living Compatibility: A Practice\-Based Account of Value Formation

**Autores:** 

**Data de descoberta:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** unknown

## Descrição do feed

\(This article needs improvement\)This article argues that humanoid robots have a relative advantage in forming value orientations compatible with human common life because they are more likely to enter human social practices under conditions close to those faced by human agents\. The relevant unit of formation is not isolated task performance but structured practice, in which risks, dependencies, coordinative pressures, and feedback gradually sediment evaluative priorities\. Bodily structure therefore matters less as a symbol of humanness than as a condition of access to the practices through which practical priorities are stabilized\. On this basis, the article defends a practice\-based account of humanoid signiﬁcance and identiﬁes common\-living compatibility as the relevant normative target: predictable, intelligible, cooperative, and institutionally receivable coexistence in a human\-built world\. A brief analysis of care shows why this claim matters in a role\-structured domain organized by vulnerability, restraint, and dependence\.
