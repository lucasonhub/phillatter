# PEDAGOG VA TALABANING TIL TA’LIMIDA SUN’IY INTELLEKT TEXNOLOGIYALARIDAN FOYDALANISH KOMPETENTLIGI VA UNING LINGVODIDAKTIK ASOSLARI

**Autores:** MUNIRAXON RAVSHANOVNA MUXITDINOVA

**Data:** 2026-10-12 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

This article examines the linguodidactic foundations for forming and developing the AI competence of both educators and students within the context of the effective use of artificial intelligence technologies in language education\. Amidst digital transformation, artificial intelligence\-specifically generative language models, chatbots, speech recognition systems, automated assessment tools, and adaptive learning platforms\-is becoming a new component of the foreign language teaching and learning process\. The article defines an educator&\#x27;s AI competence as a composite of technological knowledge, methodological\-pedagogical competence, critical evaluation skills, awareness of ethics and digital security, and the ability to integrate AI tools into language education\.
