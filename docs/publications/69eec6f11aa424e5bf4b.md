# Unlocking the Interfacial Activity of Insoluble Lithium Salts for Stable Lithium Batteries

**Autores:** 韩道红; Wenjie Lyu; Wenjie Lyu; Yubing Wang; Kexiang Wang; Xingchen He; Zhouzhou Yao; Mi Zhou; Siying Zhao; Junting Wang; Junting Wang; Wei Yan; Wei Lyu; Wei Lyu; Yaozu Liao; Jianan Wang; Jianan Wang

**Data:** 2026-10-12 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

ABSTRACT Employing functional lithium salts in electrolytes is an effective strategy to suppress anode instabilities and sluggish charge‐transfer kinetics in lithium batteries\. However, high solubility requirements intrinsically exclude a broad class of promising, high‐lattice‐energy inorganic salts for carbonate electrolytes\. Here, we establish an immobilized functional platform \(IFP\) by deploying insoluble lithium salts with strong Lewis‐acidic Li \+ sites directly onto an aramid‐scaffolded separator\. This design bypasses conventional dissolution–diffusion limitations, yielding a thin, ionically conductive, LiF‐rich solid electrolyte interphase \(SEI\) driven by regulated interfacial Li \+ solvation and enhanced anion‐derived decomposition\. Systematic screening and 6 Li/ 7 Li isotope tracing identify Li 2 SO 4 \(LS\) as the optimal IFP candidate \(IFP‐LS\) owing to its moderate Lewis acidity, superior solvent affinity, and industrial viability\. Remarkably, LS provides a finite sacrificial lithium contribution during continuous SEI evolution, mitigating irreversible lithium consumption\. Consequently, IFP‐LS separators quadruple the lifespan of Li\|\|LiNi 0\.9 Co 0\.05 Mn 0\.05 O 2 cells at 0\.7 C compared to bare separators, and deliver 88\.4% capacity retention over 2400 cycles in Li\|\|LiFePO 4 cells at 5 C\. Scalability is further validated through 100 m roll‐to‐roll manufacturing across diverse Ah‐level formats, adding only 0\.163 USD m −2 \. This work overcomes the lithium salt solubility barrier and unlocks a vast library of potent materials for next‐generation high‐energy‐density batteries\.
