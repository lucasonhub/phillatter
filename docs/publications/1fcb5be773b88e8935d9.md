# O potencial da Inteligência Artificial para a Gestão do Conhecimento na Petrobras

**Autores:** Arturo Cavalcanti Catunda; Tayane Cristina Mattera Souza

**Data:** 2026-10-15 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

This article discusses the growing importance of knowledge as an organizational asset and the integration between Knowledge Management \(KM\) and Artificial Intelligence \(AI\)\. This integration can enhance KM processes, while consistent KM practices provide high quality data for intelligent algorithms\. The purpose of this study is to present how Petrobras is structuring itself to apply AI in its KM processes\. This is a case study based on content analysis of documents \(internal reports, consulting reports, event materials\), interviews with the company’s KM team, and a literature review\. The study contributes to the field by highlighting Petrobras’ journey as a complex and regulated organization\. This journey involved combining agile methods, experimentation, international scouting, and other strategies to meet business requirements aligned with organizational needs, expanding the benefits of this technology\. As a result, in addition to the strong ROI potential and the intangible benefits associated with applying AI to KM processes, the study concludes that this innovation represents a strategic step for Petrobras\. However, success will depend on the combination of technology and human factors, ensuring that the established governance enables proper data curation and mitigation of risks associated with this emerging technology\.
