# Keppel Hesselink, Jan: Are You Enlightened? The Myth of Spiritual Attainment and the Phenomenology of a Once\-Seen Truth

**Autores:** 

**Data de descoberta:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** unknown

## Descrição do feed

This paper examines the question “Are you enlightened?” not as a neutral request for information but as a malformed speech act grounded in a modern folk concept of enlightenment as a permanent, trait\-like state of detachment, serenity, and spiritual completion\. Against this concept, the paper argues that enlightenment is better understood as a punctual noetic disclosure rather than as a permanent personal condition\. One is not enlightened as a special kind of person\. One is enlightened about one’s true nature\. The disclosure in question is the recognition of consciousness as a vast, non\-dimensional field within which personhood, body, thought, memory, and world appear, and in which, at the moment of disclosure, they may dissolve into all\-encompassing luminosity without center\. Once this has been seen, repetition adds no new epistemic content\. What persists is neither a continuous state nor a mere memory, but a structural reconfiguration of self\-experience: the witness becomes explicit, the ego is recognized as an instrument rather than a sovereign self, and agency is reorganized under the condition of witnessing\. The paper further examines the consequences of this shift for fear of death, spiritual training, teacher\-student dynamics, and the verification problem inherent in first\-person reports\. It concludes that the myth of enlightenment is not the belief in awakening itself, but the conversion of awakening into status, portrait, and spiritual theatre\.
