# Radiomics\-based interpretable machine learning model for identifying high\-risk patients with small bowel obstruction: a multicenter study

**Autores:** Guozheng Zhang; Xiaohui Liu; Weitao Huang; Xisong Zhu; Cong Xia; Hua Xia; Yuguo Wei; Xiaowei Han; Danqiong Wang

**Data:** 2026-10-09 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Background Early identification of high\-risk small bowel obstruction remains clinically challenging, especially when prompt intervention is needed to prevent bowel ischemia and related adverse outcomes\. Methods In this multicenter retrospective study, 274 patients with small bowel obstruction from three hospitals were included\. Patients from Hospital A were randomly divided into training and test cohorts, and patients from the other two hospitals formed an external validation cohort\. Radiomics features were extracted from venous\-phase contrast\-enhanced CT images, and logistic regression, naive Bayes, Extra Trees, Light Gradient Boosting Machine \(LightGBM\), and multilayer perceptron models were developed\. Independent clinical predictors were identified and integrated with radiomics features to build a combined model\. Results LightGBM showed the most stable internal performance, with AUC values of 0\.934 and 0\.870 in the training and test cohorts, respectively\. Lower diastolic blood pressure and bowel wall thickening were independent predictors of high\-risk status\. The combined model achieved AUC values of 0\.942, 0\.867, and 0\.803 in the training, test, and external validation cohorts, respectively, with an external validation accuracy of 0\.833 and sensitivity of 0\.946\. Conclusion CT\-based radiomics can identify patients with small bowel obstruction at high risk, and a combined model integrating radiomics and clinical predictors may support earlier risk stratification and intervention\.
