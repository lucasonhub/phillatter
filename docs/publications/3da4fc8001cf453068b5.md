# Bulgaria, Jamel: The CEF Model of Democratic Stability and Collapse: An Affective\-Computational Architecture of Institutional Resilience and Backsliding

## Descrição do feed

This paper introduces a unified affective computational architecture for understanding democratic stability, institutional resilience, and political backsliding through the Core Emotion Framework \(CEF\)\. Whereas traditional institutional theories explain national success through inclusive versus extractive political structures, and psychological models reduce political affect to coarse valence–arousal dimensions, the CEF models political behavior as structured sequences of ten functional emotional operators distributed across the Head, Heart, and Gut centers\. Using this operator calculus, the paper reframes democratic stability as a dynamic equilibrium of emotional somatic processes across leaders and citizens, and models democratic backsliding as the emergence of rigid operator fusions—such as Calculating–Sensing, Achieving–Constricting, and Boosting–Achieving—that suppress adaptive governance\. The framework further formalizes affective polarization as a set of infectious microstates \(Political Outrage, Tribal Loyalty, and the Polarization Spiral\) that co\-opt cognitive evaluation and undermine institutional coherence\. At the macro level, the paper introduces a stability failure taxonomy—Collapse, Rigidity, Drift, Lag, Overshoot, and Fragmentation—derived from operator algebra and center activation matrices\. Finally, it outlines a rigorous empirical program combining structural equation modeling, somatic kinematic tracking, and autonomic resonance analysis to validate the CEF as a mechanistic model of democratic resilience\. This work positions the CEF as a foundational paradigm for integrating emotional architecture with institutional theory in the study of democratic stability\.
