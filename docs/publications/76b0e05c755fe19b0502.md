# MEDICAL AND BIOLOGICAL PHYSICS AS A FUNDAMENTAL NATURAL DISCIPLINE: MODERN CHALLENGES AND INNOVATIONS IN PRE\-PROFESSIONAL AND PROFESSIONAL MEDICAL EDUCATION

**Autores:** Alexander V\. Chalyi; Olena Chaika; Dmytro Lukomsky; E\. G\. Zaitseva; Наталія Леонідівна Гриценко

**Data:** 2026-10-17 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Aim\. To analyze the impact of physical and technical discoveries on the development of instrumental and new, high\-tech methods in medicine\. To investigate modern educational processes in institutions of pre\-professional and professional medical education and to substantiate the effectiveness of using electronic laboratory online practical training in medical and biological physics for students of Ukrainian\-language and English\-language forms of education in the conditions of modern challenges\. Materials and methods\. The materials of the research included legislative documents and resolutions regulating educational processes at the pre\-professional and professional levels\. The research methods included the bibliosemantic method, system analysis, comparison, generalization, and statistical hypothesis testing to analyze the efficiency of electronic laboratory online practical training in medical and biological physics in online learning\.Results\. In order to provide high\-quality training for future doctors at the stage of professional education, an electronic laboratory online workshop in medical and biological physics was developed with an orientation towards the practical use of modern digital medical devices and applications\. Statistical hypothesis testing proved the efficiency of the electronic laboratory online workshop in medical and biological physics compared with offline study\. Conclusions\. An electronic laboratory online workshop in medical and biological physics for students of Ukrainian\-language and English\-language forms of education is an innovative and relevant adaptive environment that meets the modern challenges of the time and has significant methodological value for professional education\.
