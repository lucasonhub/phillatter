# Evolution of the Documents Related to Musculoskeletal Diseases and Physical Activity Research: A Bibliometric Review

**Autores:** Víctor Hernández\-Beltrán; Mário C\. Espada; Carolina A\. Cabo; José A\. Parraça; Orlando Fernandes; Cátia Ferreira; José M\. Gamonales

**Data:** 2026-10-13 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Introduction\. Musculoskeletal diseases \(MDs\) fit more than 150 disorders, mainly affecting the locomotor system\. These types of disorders can be caused by fractures, sprains, or strains, as a summary they affect the quality of life by modifying lifestyle behavior\. Through physical activity \(PA\) and exercise is possible to reduce the level of pain and improve the quality of life\. Aim\. This study aimed to carry out a trend analysis related to the documents that are focused on the study of MDs and PA\. Study Design\. The present document fits within the “Ex\-post facto retrospective” studies Methods\. A bibliometric analysis was carried out using the keywords “Musculoskeletal diseases” and “Physical activity” considering the paper published between 1995 and 2022, identifying a total of 147 documents using the Web of Science Core Collection\. Results\. The results reported an exponential growth of 79% from 2004 till 2022\. According to the authors, the journal and countries were “Estevez\-López, F\.&quot; and &quot;Gossec, L\.&quot;, “International Journal of Environmental Research and Public Health”, and Germany and England the most productive, respectively\. Regarding the keywords, a great diversity was observed, in which &quot;Musculoskeletal diseases&quot; and &quot;Physical activity&quot; presented the highest occurrence with 52 and 29 times, respectively\. Conclusions\. To reduce the consequences of MDs, it is recommended to develop PA program, to reduce the pain, and improve the quality of life and mental health of the individuals\.
