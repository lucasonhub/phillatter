# Complete Genome Sequence and Replicon\-Resolved Phosphorus\- and Potassium\-Acquisition Gene Inventory of Niallia taxi Strain Si250\_34 from Long\-Term NPK\- and Silicate\-Fertilized Soil

**Autores:** Seung\-Yeon Jeong; Daye Choi; Sook\-Min Kwon; Ikwhan Kim; Sihyun Park; Jae\-Ho Shin

**Data:** 2026-10-13 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Niallia was one of 17 genera proposed in a genomebased reclassification of Bacillus \[1\]\.Niallia taxi is a Gram\-stain\-positive, aerobic, endospore\-forming endophyte of Taxus chinensis \[2\]\.Long\-fertilized soils hold large phosphorus stocks of low plant availability \[3\], and highaffinity phosphate uptake in Bacillus subtilis runs through the pst operon \[4\]\.No phosphorus\-or potassiumacquisition gene set has been described for the species, and only two of the 20 other assemblies labeled N\. taxi are closed\.We report the closed genome of strain Si250\_34, a replicon\-resolved inventory of its phosphorus\-and potassium\-acquisition genes, and the routes for which no gene was detected\.Bulk soil was sampled in 2024 at the soybean R3 \(beginning pod\)\-R4 \(full pod\) growth stages from the Si250 plot of a 50\-year nutrient\-management field trial maintained by the Gyeongsangbuk\-do Agricultural Research and Extension Services \(GBARES\) in Chilgok, Republic of Korea\.The plot received urea, fused phosphate, and potassium chloride at rates equivalent to 12 kg N ha \-1 , 8 kg P 2 O 5 ha \-1 , and 6 kg K 2 O ha \-1 , respectively, together with silicate at 2\.5 t ha \-1 \.A soil suspension was plated on tryptic soy agar; after 48 h at 30℃ a single colony was picked, purified by repeated streaking and grown overnight in tryptic soy broth \(30℃, 200 rpm\)\.Genomic Niallia taxi strain Si250\_34, assigned to the species by average nucleotide identity, was isolated from a 49\-year NPK\-and silicate\-fertilized plot in Chilgok, Republic of Korea\.Its genome comprises a circular chromosome of 3,840,758 bp and two circular plasmids of 1,367,869 and 381,819 bp, totaling 5,590,446 bp at 37\.7% G\+C\.The pst operon occurs on both the chromosome and the 1\.37\-Mb megaplasmid; genes for a Pit\-family transporter and a phoP\-phoR\-type pair are present\.Potassium\-acquisition genes include a megaplasmid kdpFABC operon and three Ktr\-family permease genes\.No pqq biosynthetic gene, phyC\-type phytase or phnG\-phnJ/phnM C\-P lyase gene was detected\.
