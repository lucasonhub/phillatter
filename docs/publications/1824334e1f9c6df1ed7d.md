# Thermal conductivity in marble: A derivative\-based approach to sensitivity and functional response

**Autores:** Khawar Jalil; Tariq Feroze; Naseer M\. Khan; Irtaza Bashir Raja; Haihui Xin; Bekir Genc

**Data:** 2026-10-09 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Reliable interpretation of thermal conductivity in marble requires analytical approaches that extend beyond conventional correlation and predictive modelling to characterize functional response behavior\. This study develops an interpretable derivative\-based analytical framework integrating polynomial response modelling, marble\-variety\-level Group K\-fold cross\-validation, one\-standard\-error model selection, analytical differentiation, and cluster\-bootstrap stability assessment\. A dataset comprising 390 specimens representing 26 commercially recognized marble varieties was analyzed by modelling thermal conductivity independently against nine physico\-mechanical and thermophysical properties within a globally standardized analytical space\. First\- and second\-order derivatives quantified response sensitivity, functional curvature, and directional stability, while grouped validation evaluated predictive generalizability across previously unseen lithological groups\. The strengthened analytical framework consistently selected first\-degree polynomial models for all predictors demonstrating that the nonlinear curvature identified under the exploratory analytical framework was methodology\-dependent and not supported after rigorous validation\. Thermal diffusivity and volumetric heat capacity exhibited the strongest predictive performance and sensitivity because of their intrinsic relationship with thermal conductivity, whereas density, uniaxial compressive strength, rebound number, and P\-wave velocity emerged as the most informative independent engineering properties\. The proposed framework complements conventional predictive approaches by integrating predictive performance, response sensitivity, functional interpretation, and statistical robustness within a single reproducible workflow providing a robust basis for interpreting thermophysical trend in marble\.
