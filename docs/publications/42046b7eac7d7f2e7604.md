# FLOOD\-SHAB: Final Scientific Outcomes and Event\-Aware Bridge Monitoring Framework

**Autores:** Karīna Buka\-Vaivade

**Data:** 2026-10-12 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Final scientific presentation summarising the main outcomes of the FLOOD\-SHAB project \(Structural Health Assessment of Bridges during and after FLOOD events, MSCA\-PF Grant Agreement No\. 101154316\)\. The presentation introduces the event\-aware bridge SHM interpretation framework and the AI\-based debris monitoring module, including event\-triggered monitoring, bridge\-specific debris\-state logic, real\-webcam validation, structural\-response interpretation, a unified event\-tagged timeline and tiered decision\-support levels\. It provides a concise methodological synthesis of the project’s main scientific results and their integration into an event\-aware bridge assessment workflow\. Third\-party images and other externally sourced material remain subject to their original copyright and licensing conditions, as indicated in the presentation\.
