# Small\-sample ERP\-assisted screening for depression and audio multimodal fusion based on the MODMA dataset

**Autores:** Jiaxuan WAN; Zhenshen Qu

**Data:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

OBJECTIVE: Event\-related potentials \(ERPs\) may provide objective neurophysiological markers for depression\-assisted screening\. We benchmarked structured ERP features and strictly paired ERP\-audio fusion in small\-sample, subject\-level settings\. APPROACH: Two experiments on MODMA used leakage\-controlled leave\-one\-subject\-out \(LOSO\) validation, with all preprocessing, tuning and fusion restricted to training folds\. Experiment I included 52 ERP subjects retained after quality control \(QC\) \(23 with major depressive disorder \(MDD\) and 29 healthy controls \(HC\)\), each represented by a 60\-feature vector, to compare machine learning \(ML\) and deep learning \(DL\) models\. This paper additionally evaluated convolutional neural network \(CNN\)\-based architectures \(EEGNet and ShallowConvNet\) on a multichannel ERP time\-series representation \(28 channels × 129 samples\) constructed from subject\-level averaged cue\-locked and dot\-locked ERPs\. Experiment II used 37 strictly paired ERP\-audio subjects \(16 MDD, 21 HC\) with 178 audio features to evaluate unimodal and decision\-level fusion under balanced and screening objectives\. MAIN RESULTS: In Experiment I, SVM\-RBF achieved area under the ROC curve \(AUC\) 0\.859 vs 0\.588 for TinyMLP \($\\Delta$AUC = \+0\.271\)\. On ERP time\-series inputs, EEGNet achieved AUC = 0\.640 and ShallowConvNet achieved AUC = 0\.540, both inferior to the engineered\-feature SVM baseline\. In Experiment II, the best unimodal baseline was audio SVM\-RBF \(AUC = 0\.753\)\. Fusion achieved AUC to 0\.762 and increased specificity \(Spec\) to 0\.810 under the balanced objective, and increased recall \(Rec\) to 0\.813 with a false\-negative rate \(FNR\) of 0\.188 under the screening objective\. SIGNIFICANCE: This study provides a leakage\-controlled, strictly paired, subject\-level evaluation framework for depression\-assisted screening and shows that ERP\-audio fusion offers objective\-dependent complementary value\.
