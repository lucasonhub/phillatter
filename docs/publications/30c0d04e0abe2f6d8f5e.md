# AI Fitness OS: Verifiable, Multi\-Metric Fitness Guidance by Constraining a Large Language Model with Deterministically Computed Targets

**Autores:** Selvin Lopes; Perseus Bhavnagri

**Data:** 2026-10-12 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Fitness applications usually record one behaviour in isolation, and large\-language\-model \(LLM\) coaches produce fluent advice whose numbers are rarely checked\. This paper presents AI Fitness OS, a web application that keeps training, nutrition, hydration, sleep, weight, steps and goals in one per\-user record, computes every numerical target deterministically from published equations and stated heuristics, and passes those targets to Google Gemini as binding constraints; each generated meal plan is re\-checked in code against a ±5 % tolerance\. The premise was examined through secondary research on a public dataset of 2,111 records, and the artefact through tests, simulation and live measurement\. All eight lifestyle metrics that the system tracks or addresses were significantly associated with obesity level \(Bonferroni\-adjusted α = 0\.00625\), although only three remained so on 515 records resembling the original survey\. A Random Forest that combined the eight metrics reached a macro\-F1 of 0\.720 against 0\.263 for the best single metric \(Wilcoxon W = 55, p = 0\.00098\)\. Across 1,000 simulated user profiles, every constrained meal plan fell within ±5 % of its calorie target, against 75 of 1,000 unconstrained plans \(exact McNemar p &lt; 10⁻²⁰⁰\)\. The calculator agreed with the dataset&\#x27;s weight\-category labels for 98\.82 % of records, and live responses took 2\.5–6\.1 s at no cost\. The findings support computing arithmetic, generating language and checking the output against the computed numbers\.
