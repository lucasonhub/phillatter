# The Relationship Between Critical Thinking Dispositions and Barriers to Critical Thinking of Engineering Students

**Autores:** Çetin Semercı; Fatma Ünal; Emrullah Yılmaz; Nurgün Gençel

**Data:** 2026-10-15 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

This study aims to examine the relationship between critical thinking dispositions and barriers to critical thinking of engineering students\. The study is a correlational research\. The population of the study includes all the students at the faculties of engineering in universities in Turkey and the sampling consists of 628 students from five different universities; Bartın, Karabük, Bozok, Bayburt and Süleyman Demirel\. Convenience sampling was used to select the respondents\. Two data collection tools were used in the study\. The first one is the Critical Thinking Disposition Scale \(CTD\) and the second one is the Critical Thinking Barriers \(CTB\) scale\. The collected data were analysed using SPSS 24 statistical analysis programme\. The mean values were compared in terms of gender, grade, university and department\. The critical thinking disposition scores of the students are high, whereas the scores of barriers to critical thinking are low\. The mean scores in the Critical Thinking Barriers scale significantly differ in terms of gender and grade but not university and department\. Also, critical thinking dispositions of students differ significantly with respect to universities but not gender, grade and department\. Finally, a moderate, negative correlation \(\-0\.54\) is observed between CTB and CTD scores
