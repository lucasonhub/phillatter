# Soltani, Sahar Foster: Why Whale Hunting MUST END: The Whale is SENTIENT\. The Slaughter is AVOIDABLE\. Why Does Europe Still Fail to Abolish It in 2026?

**Autores:** 

**Data de descoberta:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** unknown

## Descrição do feed

What moral authority does tradition possess when the tradition requires the deliberate killing of highly intelligent and sensitive sentient beings? What remains of “necessity” when whale hunting continues after the conditions that once made it necessary have disappeared? And what does international protection mean when commercial whaling, cultural justification, and sovereign permission continue to preserve practices that modern knowledge of cetacean sentience and suffering can no longer morally excuse? This paper examines whale hunting in Europe through animal ethics, international law, moral responsibility, cultural justification, and the governance of sentient life\. It focuses on Norway, Iceland, the Faroe Islands, the International Whaling Commission, and the commercial whaling moratorium, and argues that historical necessity, inherited custom, economic interest, and national tradition cannot create a permanent moral entitlement to kill whales\. The conclusion is direct: whale hunting is no longer morally defensible as culture, necessity, or sovereign choice and should end\.
