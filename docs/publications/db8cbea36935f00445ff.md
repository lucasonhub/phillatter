# Materov, Sergej: The Generalized No\-Chaos Theorem

**Autores:** 

**Data de descoberta:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** unknown

## Descrição do feed

The Generalized No\-Chaos Theorem: Bounded Complexity Growth at Any Fixed Coarse\-Graining Within the Quantum Gravity Consistency Framework \(QGCF\) \- any finite\-dimensional, closed, unitarily evolving system satisfying \(QGCF1\)\-\(QGCF4\) \- three logically distinct results are established\. First, ontic chaos is impossible in three senses: spectral quasiperiodicity, Poincaré recurrence, and exact vanishing of the asymptotic quantum Lyapunov exponent λL \(§5\)\. Second, dynamical \(Lyapunov/0\-1\-test\) chaos at any FIXED coarse\-graining scale ℓ is also impossible, unconditionally \- a direct corollary of the same almost\-periodicity that gives λL=0 \(§7\); the one established quantum\-chaos diagnostic that survives this obstruction is level \(spectral\) statistics, checked preliminarily against a non\-integrable/integrable benchmark pair \(§7\.3\)\. Third, and logically independent of the first two: no observer realized as a proper subsystem can simultaneously reconstruct the complete global state from its own accessible data and evolve under an autonomous unitary law, once genuinely \(entangling\-ly\) coupled to the rest of the system \(§8\) \- a consequence of purification non\-uniqueness \(Hughston\-Jozsa\-Wootters\) and the non\-unitary character of open marginal dynamics, requiring no assumption beyond a generic \(non\-fine\-tuned\) boundary coupling\. The two failures named in that theorem arise from the same physical mechanism \- entanglement across the observer&\#x27;s own boundary \- but are shown not to be strictly the same fact, only generically co\-occurring\. Thermalization of coarse blocks \(Calabrese\-Cardy growth to a Page\-value plateau\) is real, established physics, distinct from all of the above, and is presented \(§9\) together with the timescale τ\*\(ℓ\) it requires, and its role as the only available justification for treating the system&\#x27;s effective Hamiltonian Ĥeff as possessing a meaningful temperature Tc, given that the axioms admit no literal external bath\. A note on generality\. QGCF does not require a literal discrete graph\. \(QGCF3\) is stated as a disjunction: a discrete, graph\-based realization \(QGCF3a\), giving the sharper results of §8\.2 and §8\.6\.2 exactly; or a continuum realization with only a locality/metric structure \(QGCF3b\), giving the standard, approximately\-causal Lieb\-Robinson bound instead\. Everything else in this document §5\-§7, §8\.3\-§8\.6\.1, §9 \- depends only on finite\-dimensionality and unitarity \(QGCF1\-QGCF2\), or on \(QGCF4\)&\#x27;s boundedness/genericity, and holds identically under either branch of \(QGCF3\)\. \(direct link \)
