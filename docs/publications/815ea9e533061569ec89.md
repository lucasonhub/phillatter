# A Systematic Benchmark of Physics\-Informed Neural NetworkStrategies for Stiff Ion Transport in Lithium Symmetric Cells

**Autores:** David Pankaczy; Conrard Giresse Tetsassi Feugmo

**Data:** 2026-10-14 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Abstract The Poisson–Nernst–Planck \(PNP\) system governs ion transport in lithium\-ion battery electrolytes and presents a canonical stiff coupled PDE challenge: extreme coefficient ratios, sharp electric double\-layer boundary layers, and nonlinear coupling between the Poisson and Nernst–Planck equations\. Physics\-informed neural networks \(PINNs\) are appealing because they require no mesh and handle forward and inverse problems in one framework, but spectral bias and multi\-task loss imbalance have limited their accuracy on stiff PNP systems\. We present the first systematic, simulation\-only benchmark of eleven PINN configurations, organised into four strategy groups \(adaptive loss weighting, spectral bias mitigation, spatio\-temporal decomposition, and physics enrichment\), on a physically parametrised one\-dimensional PNP model for a lithium symmetric cell, implemented in NVIDIA PhysicsNeMo Sym and validated against a high\-resolution finite volume method reference\. Root\-mean\-square errors span 10⁻² to 10⁻⁴: Neural Tangent Kernel \(NTK\) adaptive weighting achieves the lowest errors \(RMSE = 6\.6×10⁻⁴ for anion concentration\), while the balanced residual decay rate \(BRDR\) scheme matches NTK within 10% RMSE for concentrations while reducing wall\-clock time by 3\.2 ± 0\.4 h per run\. Loss landscape geometry corroborates the RMSE ranking\. An open\-source PhysicsNeMo Sym implementation is released for reuse in electrochemical modelling\.
