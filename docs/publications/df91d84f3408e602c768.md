# FitCoach: A Multimodal AI\-Powered Personal Fitness Coaching System using Human Pose Estimation, Nutrition Intelligence, and Large Language Models

**Autores:** Abhinav Kumar; Aamir Hamza; Aditi Jha; Ayan Abdul; C E Chandana

**Data:** 2026-10-12 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Maintaining a consistent and effective fitness routine requires more than simply following predefined exercise plans, as users often struggle with incorrect exercise form, inadequate personalization, nutritional decision\-making, and the lack of continuous feedback\. Recent advances in computer vision, deep learning, pose estimation, and artificial intelligence have enabled automated systems for exercise recognition and form assessment\. However, many existing fitness applications address these capabilities independently and provide limited integration between exercise monitoring, nutritional analysis, and personalized coaching\. This paper presents FitCoach, an AI\-assisted fitness coaching system designed to provide an integrated and personalized fitness experience using computer vision and intelligent decision\-making\. The proposed system utilizes human pose estimation to identify body landmarks and analyze exercise movements, enabling real\-time assessment of exercise form and feedback\. In addition, the system incorporates food recognition and nutritional information to support dietary monitoring, while user\-specific information is considered to generate more personalized fitness recommendations\. The system is designed to combine multimodal inputs, including visual exercise information, user activity, and nutritional data, into a unified coaching framework\. By integrating exercise\-form analysis, dietary awareness, and personalized guidance, FitCoach aims to overcome limitations of isolated fitness technologies and provide users with accessible, adaptive, and intelligent fitness support\. The proposed framework demonstrates how computer vision and AI\-based personalization can be combined to develop a more comprehensive digital fitness coaching solution\.
