# Meme as a Unit of Digital Discourse

**Autores:** A\. P\. Yakhontova

**Data:** 2026-10-14 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

The purpose of this article is to examine the Internet meme as one of the basic units of modern digital communication\. The article summarizes various approaches to studying this phenomenon, from the classical memetics theory of Richard Dawkins to more recent linguistic, semiotic, and sociocultural interpretations\. Special attention is given to how memes combine semiotic, cognitive, and pragmatic aspects\. Based on the analysis, the article proposes a model that describes a meme in several dimensions: structural, content\-based, and dynamic\. In addition, such characteristics as intertextuality and the unexpected semantic collision effect are considered\. Unlike the traditional understanding of a meme as a &quot;replicator,&quot; the article views it as a multimodal text that is closely connected to the digital environment and user communities\. The modern stage of meme development is also discussed, including the emergence of AI\-generated content, which significantly changes the nature of digital discourse\.
