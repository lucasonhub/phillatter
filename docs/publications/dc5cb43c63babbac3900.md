# Škondrić, Dragan: Intelligence as Relational Leap: A Dual\-Domain Framework for State, Impulse, Connection, and Higher\-Order Transition

**Autores:** 

**Data de descoberta:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** unknown

## Descrição do feed

This paper proposes a dual\-domain relational framework for understanding intelligence as a transition rather than primarily as a stored capacity, quantity of knowledge, or isolated property of an agent\. The model introduces two analytically neutral domains, L and R, whose relation constitutes the operative state of a system\. A change in this relation is described as an impulse\. Under appropriate conditions, the impulse enables a new functional connection, and when that connection reorganizes the system&\#x27;s space of possible states rather than merely selecting among existing responses, a relational leap occurs\. The framework distinguishes first\-order transitions, which modify states within an existing rule structure, from higher\-order transitions, which modify the rule by which future states and connections are generated\. An atemporal relational\-network representation is introduced as an analytical device for comparing repeated relational patterns across temporal sequences without treating it as a physical theory of timelessness\. The model is situated in relation to emergence, complex systems, systems theory, cognitive science, and artificial intelligence, and concludes with formal definitions and directions for empirical investigation\. \(direct link \)
