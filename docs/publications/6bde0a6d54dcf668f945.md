# Fabrication and Characterization of CL‐20@Sodium Alginate Core‐Shell Composites With Enhanced Safety

**Autores:** 陈子键; Jing Wei Hou; Bindong Li

**Data:** 2026-10-14 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

ABSTRACT To address the high mechanical sensitivity limitation of CL‐20, a core–shell‐structured CL‐20@sodium alginate composite was fabricated using a facile solution dispersion and filtration method\. Comprehensive characterization confirmed that sodium alginate was successfully coated onto the surface of ε ‐CL‐20 crystals through hydrogen‐bonding interactions without altering the crystal structure of CL‐20\. Thermal analysis revealed that the sodium alginate coating modulates the decomposition behavior, resulting in a more gradual energy release process\. Impact sensitivity testing demonstrated that the limiting impact energy significantly increased from 2\.0 J for pristine CL‐20 to 22\.5 J for the composite, corresponding to an 11‐fold reduction in impact sensitivity\. This study demonstrates that low‐cost sodium alginate serves as an effective desensitizing shell material, offering a promising strategy for developing high‐energy insensitive energetic composites with enhanced safety characteristics\.
