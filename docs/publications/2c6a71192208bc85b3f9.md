# Inclusión educativa e interculturalidad en la integración académica de estudiantes universitarios amazónicos

**Autores:** Mery Nora Atencio Rivera

**Data:** 2026-10-12 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Context: Educational inclusion and interculturality in the academic integration of Amazonian university students are examined against the mismatch between inclusive policies and Indigenous worldviews in Peru&\#x27;s central Amazon region\. Objective: To examine how inclusion strategies predict academic integration and how interculturality moderates this relationship\. Methods: An explanatory sequential mixed\-methods design \(QUAN → QUAL\) was adopted\. The quantitative phase employed partial least squares structural equation modeling \(PLS\-SEM\) with N = 380 students, while the qualitative phase used a multiple\-case study \(n = 12\)\. Results: Inclusion strategies positively and significantly predicted academic integration \(β = 0\.141, p = 0\.003\), and interculturality moderated this relationship \(β = 0\.170, p = 0\.004\)\. The effect reached a substantive magnitude only at high levels of interculturality \(β = 0\.309, p &lt; 0\.001\)\. Qualitative evidence revealed that, without epistemic recognition, these strategies operate as performative inclusion\. Conclusions: Interculturality is associated with variations in the relationship between inclusion and integration, suggesting that inclusive strategies function as bridges or barriers to academic integration in Amazonian higher education\.
