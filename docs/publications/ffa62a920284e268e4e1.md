# INTEGRATION OF DIGITAL TECHNOLOGIES INTO THE ECONOMIC MANAGEMENT SYSTEM OF CONSTRUCTION ENTERPRISES BASED ON THE INTELLECTUALIZATION OF MANAGERIAL DECISION\-MAKING

**Autores:** Iurii Chupryna

**Data:** 2026-10-13 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

The digital transformation of construction enterprises requires a transition from the fragmented automation of individual operations to an integrated economic management system capable of continuous data processing, outcome forecasting, and intelligent support for managerial decision\-making\. The integration of BIM, ERP, CDE, IoT, cloud technologies, business intelligence systems, artificial intelligence, machine learning, and predictive modeling creates a unified digital environment in which financial, resource, time\-related, production, and risk parameters are transformed into a structured basis for economic choice\. The article substantiates the principles of integrating digital technologies into the economic management of construction enterprises through the intellectualization of generating, evaluating, and selecting managerial decisions\. The study applies systemic, structural\-functional, and comparative methods alongside integral, multicriteria, nonlinear, and dynamic modeling\. The architectonics of the intellectualized management framework encompasses the operational, tactical, and strategic levels and operates as a closed\-loop cycle, in which the outcomes of implemented decisions return to the digital environment, continuously updating the information base and improving forecasting accuracy\. An index of digital\-intellectual saturation, a coefficient of intersystem digital convergence, and an integral level of intellectualization are proposed, accounting for technological compatibility, information\-flow relevance and structural fragmentation\. A multicriteria approach to evaluating managerial alternatives is developed, combining economic effect, resource efficiency, temporal sustainability, forecasting accuracy, and risk exposure; its multiplicative structure reflects the interdependence of criteria, whereby a critical deterioration of a single parameter constrains the overall performance of a decision\. The risk\-adjusted priority index and the optimization function enable the selection of a managerial alternative capable of maintaining economic performance under budgetary, resource, and time constraints\. A composite transformational\-effect index and a dynamic model of economic return confirm the nonlinear nature of this transition\.
