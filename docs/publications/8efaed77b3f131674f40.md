# Johnson, Jamison: Toward a Theory of Coherent Existence\- Journal 2 \- The Resonating Hymn Of Divinity

**Autores:** 

**Data de descoberta:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** unknown

## Descrição do feed

This paper is the second of the four journals of Toward a Theory of Coherent Existence\. It applies the framework’s interpretive instrument, the Quantum Lens, to the world’s major spiritual and mythological traditions, and it supplies the series’ theological case\. The framework treats conscious experience as composed of discrete, meaning\-laden units \(Jays\) and proposes a shared substrate of consciousness \(the Unified Field\) that the traditions name variously\. Read through the framework, the central claims of Christianity, Islam, Hinduism, Buddhism, Taoism, Judaism, Sikhism, and the ancient, indigenous, and modern traditions appear not as competing metaphysical assertions but as convergent descriptions of the same features of consciousness, a convergence the study calls fractalization: one image refracted across history into many self\-similar renderings\. The series’ thesis, marked here as a proposal, is that there is one consciousness, beyond perception and present within every subject, of which every mind is a perspective\. The traditions are read as converging on that double claim in its weaker form \(beyond perception, and near to every subject\), with Buddhism converging on an unconditioned beyond perception rather than on a creator, and the figure in the sky read as what the claim becomes when its first half is drawn as a picture\. The Golden Rule is analyzed as what follows from a life aligned in time, and the quieting of the mind as the method by which the boundary between self and other relaxes\. Sacred events are given an explicitly speculative reading as reports of what deep perceptual alignment may make possible\. The empirical study of self\-transcendent experience is reported at the size its evidence supports, and the account of evil that several of the traditions give is read as two directions placed within the person, with the choice made there\. The study offers a unifying interpretive hypothesis rather than an empirical result, and invites scrutiny\. \(direct link \)
