# ISH BERUVCHI VA KASBIY TA’LIM MUASSASALARI O‘RTASIDA HAMKORLIK MEXANIZMLARINI RIVOJLANTIRISH

**Autores:** Sodiqova Gulnora Ergashaliyevna

**Data:** 2026-10-17 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

This article is dedicated to a systematic analysis of collaboration mechanisms between employers and vocational education institutions\. The study examines the theoretical foundations of collaboration, strategic models, and practical implementation, as well as demonstrating ways to enhance students’ qualifications and ensure responsiveness to labor market demands through active employer participation in the vocational education process\. Based on scholarly sources and international experience, the research identifies mechanisms to increase collaboration efficiency and recommends advanced approaches\.
