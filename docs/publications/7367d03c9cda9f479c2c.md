# Li, Yuguo: A Witness\-Based Event Criterion for Quantum Measurement, with a Bookkeeping Re\-reading of Everettian, Relational, and Decoherence Accounts \- \*\*Author\*\*: Yuguo Li （李于国）, Independent Researcher, Hangzhou · ORCID 0009\-0002\-3166\-0880

**Autores:** 

**Data de descoberta:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** unknown

## Descrição do feed

Quantum mechanics assigns probabilities to outcomes, yet each measurement delivers exactly one, and decoherence — by its developers&\#x27; own stated position — does not by itself explain the outcome&\#x27;s unique‐ ness\. We supply the missing event criterion: a record is an event for an observer when witnessed and closed under explicit consistency rules, every term operationally defined in the text\. It occupies three gap positions usually stated separately: the Everettian branch definition, the relational interaction criterion, and the decoherence system–environment cut\. Given the squared\-amplitude share structure, the book‐ keeping weight is forced to equal it \(a representation theorem\); the measure over histories is provably un‐ derdetermined and registered as an explicit slot with a named default\. A candidate\-grade settlement dy‐ namics reproduces decoherence at ensemble level and — conditionally on its ontic reading — selects a single registered sector at trajectory level, the state vector untouched\. No spacetime background is input at this layer; its reconstruction is owed to the series and registered as debt\. Discriminating content lives at the fixation level: a fidelity step and a witness gate \(near\-term decidable\); calorimetry is feasibility\-lim‐ ited\. At the time of writing, all discriminating content is prospective — nothing framework\-specific has yet been measured anywhere, and we say so rather than imply otherwise\. Uniqueness is claimed chain\-in‐ ternally only; cross\-chain agreement \(the merge rule\) is a postulate, stated as such — the Wigner\-friend class of questions is thereby registered, not solved\. The three accounts are re\-read in one bookkeeping vocabulary, with conflicts registered\. \(direct link \)
