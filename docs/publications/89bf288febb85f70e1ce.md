## Resumo

Department of Health and Human Services and the remaining state plaintiffs in State of Texas, et al\. v\. Kennedy , No\. 5:24‐cv‐00225 and vacated several provisions of HHS&\#x27;s 2024 Section 504 regulation relating to the integration mandate\.
