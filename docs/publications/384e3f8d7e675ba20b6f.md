# The Effect of Corporate Social Responsibility on Corporate Financial Performance: The Evidence from Borsa Istanbul

**Autores:** Cennet GÜRBÜZ

**Data:** 2026-10-15 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Today, the practices implemented by companies within the scope of environmental sustainability have become the focus of attention of investors and researchers within the scope of Corporate Social Responsibility \(CSR\)\. This study was conducted to determine the impact of CSR on Corporate Financial Performance \(CFP\)\. For this purpose, data from companies traded on the Borsa Istanbul \(Istanbul Stock Exchange\) that have disclosed ESG scores between 2010 and 2020 were used\. In the study, market\-based financial measure \(TOBIN’S Q\) and accounting\-based measure \(ROA\) were used as dependent variables\. While the ESG score was used as the CSR indicator in the study, sales growth rate, property, plant, and equipment ratio, financial leverage, and dividend paid ratios were also considered control variables\. The study findings indicate that CSR practices negatively impact market\-based performance and have no significant impact on accounting\-based performance\. Using Turkey as an example, a developing country, the study emphasizes the importance of companies raising public awareness of their CSR efforts and policymakers supporting market participants through legal regulations\. These results offer implications for individuals, investors, business management, societies, and policy practitioners and outline directions for future research\.
