# The role of injectable porcine type I collagen \(Tropocollagen\) in chronic musculoskeletal disorders

**Autores:** Kamil Koszela

**Data:** 2026-10-09 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

The aim of this narrative review is to present current data on the use of porcine type I collagen \(tropocollagen\) injections in the treatment of chronic musculoskeletal disorders, with particular emphasis on its clinical application and safety profile\. A search was conducted in the Medline/PubMed, Scopus, and Directory of Open Access Journals \(DOAJ\) databases\. Based on inclusion and exclusion criteria, 14 publications were identified\. The available studies cover the use of collagen administered intra\-articularly, periarticularly, and via mesotherapy\. The analyzed publications reported improvements in clinical symptoms and function in various chronic musculoskeletal conditions, accompanied by a favorable safety profile\. However, it should be emphasized that the available clinical evidence is limited by small sample sizes, heterogeneity in treatment protocols, and a relatively short follow\-up period\. Injectable porcine type I collagen is a promising adjunctive treatment for chronic musculoskeletal disorders; however, further, well\-designed randomized clinical trials with larger patient cohorts and longer follow\-up periods are necessary\.
