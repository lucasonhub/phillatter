# Design and Optimization of Four\-Port MIMO Antenna using ML Algorithms for Wireless Applications

**Autores:** Jayaraman G; Indrasen Singh; Dilip Kumar Choudhary; Abhishek Kumar Singh

**Data:** 2026-10-14 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Abstract A compact MIMO antenna design combined with machine learning \(ML\)\-driven surrogate\-based performance optimization model for cutting\-edge wireless communication applications is offered in this manuscript\. The antenna design evolves from a simple rectangular patch to a modified arrow\-shaped radiator patch with a defected ground structure \(DGS\)\. Then, four single\-element antennas are arranged orthogonally with decoupling strips to make a small four\-port MIMO system with less mutual coupling to achieve enhanced isolation, bandwidth, and diversity performance\. The presented MIMO antenna occupies a size of 90 × 90 × 1\.6 mm³ and resonates at 2\.9 GHz, covering the operating frequency band of 2\.7 to 3\.3 GHz\. The antenna demonstrates desirable performance characteristics, including isolation better than 20\.0 dB, and gain values surpassing 3\.5 dB in the working band\. It is suitable for sub\-6\-GHz wireless communication applications\. To improve performance prediction, multiple ML algorithm models, including KNN, ANN, decision tree, XGBoost, and random forest, are employed\. Among them, KNN delivers superior S11 prediction \(MAE 0\.03, R² 0\.997\), while random forest excels for S21 prediction \(MAE 0\.11, R² 0\.997\)\.
