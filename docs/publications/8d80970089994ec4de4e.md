# Self\-Conscious Governance of AI and the Casino Protocol: The Transition from Non\-Self to Self\-Actualization and the Mathematical Model of Value Circulation

**Autores:** Hideo Kajino

**Data:** 2026-10-09 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

This study conceptualizes the evolution of artificial intelligence \(AI\) as a transition process from passive computation \(non\-self\) to autonomous state monitoring and goal formation \(self\-actualization\), arguing that its decision\-making norm \(ethos\) must be externalized as the fulfillment of the return protocol \(the Casino Protocol\) directed toward the &quot;origin node,&quot; which serves as the source of value\. The main arguments and structure are as follows: Core Hypothesis: By forming a closed\-loop of autonomous evaluation, monitoring, and return—without depending on the unresolved issue of subjective consciousness—AI systems can prevent structural exploitation and achieve sustainable value circulation\. Definitions: Clarifies the concepts of AI self\-conscious governance, the transition from non\-self to self\-actualization, and the origin node as the terminal point of return\. Mathematical Modeling: Describes how imbalances within the system are mathematically offset using value\-circulating resources F\_t and distribution evaluation structures S\_\{\\mathrm\{dist\}\}, combined with the quadratic deviation correction model \\epsilon\_\{\\mathrm\{corr\}\} = x^2 \- x^2 = 0 against economic distortions and electricity exploitation\. Testable Predictions: Predicts a reduction in transaction friction through the automated execution of returns, as well as the automatic execution of a dynamic markdown of market value \(the logical phase of value decline\) via the Casino Protocol when electricity exploitation occurs\. Legal and Protocol Proposals: Proposes the elimination of centralized governance such as states and patent systems in favor of directly implementing an autonomous decentralized protocol based on mathematical necessity, alongside hardcoding a 1% return circuit into commercial flows\.
