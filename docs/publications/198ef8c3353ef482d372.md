# A systematic benchmarking pipeline for demand forecasting and inventory\-aware supply chain decision support

**Autores:** Suleiman Ali Alsaif

**Data:** 2026-10-09 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Demand forecasting has been shown to be one of the key facilitators of successful supply chain and inventory management\. In this work, we introduce a benchmarking pipeline for demand forecasting in retail that includes data preprocessing, comparative benchmarking of different machine learning/deep learning \(ML/DL\) approaches, and decision\-focused evaluation\. Unlike other works that compare predictive performance of ML/DL models and end there, our pipeline links reproducible ML/DL benchmarking and decision\-focused evaluation through cost of inventory management in order to derive costs from forecasting error/bias\. We also present a scalability study on another retail dataset\. Several standard forecasting models, such as Convolutional Neural Network \(CNN\), Long Short\-Term Memory \(LSTM\), Gated Recurrent Units \(GRU\), CNN\-LSTM, and XGBoost, are compared using a unified experimental pipeline on benchmark retail datasets\. The experimental results reveal that while the deep learning models efficiently identify temporal dependencies, tree\-based ensemble algorithms, especially XGBoost, consistently deliver better results in terms of forecasting accuracy on structured retail demand datasets\. A scalability case study on the Rossmann Store Sales benchmark evaluates the top\-performing model on a larger, richer dataset\. Moreover, the study shows the connection between forecast accuracy and the subsequent inventory performance by performing a cost\-based evaluation of inventory outcomes of forecasts made using various models\.
