# A deep learning approach to member identification and mass estimation in galaxy clusters

**Autores:** Zicheng Wang; Liping Fu; Wei Du; Wentao Luo; Wenxiang Pei; Zhu Chen; Zhijian Luo; Yicheng Li; Jiachen Qiu; Haoran Zhao; Kaiwen Mai; Weiming Ren

**Data:** 2026-10-12 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Accurate dynamical mass estimates of galaxy clusters are essential for studies of cluster formation and evolution and for cosmological applications\. However, interloper galaxies, which are not gravitationally bound to the cluster but are incorrectly identified as members, can bias dynamical mass measurements and increase their uncertainties\. We developed a probabilistic deep\-learning framework for cluster member identification and dynamical mass estimation and tested the sensitivity of the inferred masses to cluster miscentring and radially dependent incompleteness in controlled mock experiments\. We used mock galaxy cluster catalogues to train a two\-stage model\. In this model, a long short\-term memory \(LSTM\) network assigns membership probabilities to galaxies arranged by projected phase\-space proximity\. These probabilities are then used to construct membership\-weighted projected phase\-space density maps, from which a Bayesian neural network \(BNN\) infers cluster masses and predictive uncertainties\. The LSTM membership probabilities are well calibrated and form an isoprobability envelope in the projected phase space, with the łangle p\_ ̊m mem ̊angle=0\.5 contour broadly tracing the empirical member\-\-interloper transition\.
