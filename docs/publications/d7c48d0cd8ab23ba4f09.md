# Formal\-Verification\-Guided Bayesian Optimization for Non\-Intrusive Last\-Mile Coverage Closure

**Autores:** Chengjia Liu; Priyadarsi Mishra; David Kebo Houngninou; Michael Quinn; Aakash Tyagi; Jiang Hu

**Data:** 2026-10-12 · **Tipo:** preprint

## Resumo

Last\-mile functional coverage closure for modern chip designs remains costly because a mature UVM environment exposes many controls while direct hits on the remaining bins are sparse\. We present a non\-intrusive workflow that closes pre\-defined bins in an existing, human\-authored functional coverage model; it does not synthesize verification objectives from the design specification\. The workflow first uses abstract\-syntax\-tree \(AST\) parsing and cone\-of\-influence \(COI\) tracing to map each residual target to existing testbench knobs\. Under a matched 20\-simulation budget, Bayesian optimization \(BO\) is compared with continued constrained\-random verification \(CRV\) and random search over the same AST/COI\-reduced space\. Formal reachability then removes only targets supported by complete unreachability proofs, after which retained targets are grouped by formal difficulty and processed by group\-specific BO under a second matched 20\-simulation budget\. Cleanup and grouping never add numerator bins; only simulator coverage artifacts do\. On OpenTitan AES, first\-pass reduced\-space BO reaches 241/271 bins; formal cleanup removes two denominator bins; the integrated post\-cleanup stage \- difficulty grouping, policy routing, and group\-specific BO \- adds 20 simulator\-confirmed hits, yielding 261/271 raw coverage \(96\.31%\) and 261/269 effective coverage \(97\.03%\)\. On Entropy Source, first\-pass reduced\-space BO reaches 2117/2277 bins; formal cleanup removes ten denominator bins; the integrated post\-cleanup stage \- difficulty grouping, policy routing, and group\-specific BO \- adds 93 simulator\-confirmed hits, yielding 2210/2277 raw coverage \(97\.06%\) and 2210/2267 effective coverage \(97\.49%\)\. These results characterize the integrated diagnosis\-and\-search workflow; the present evaluation does not isolate the causal contributions of difficulty classification, policy routing, and post\-cleanup BO\.
