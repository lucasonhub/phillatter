# Prediction with Expert Advice under Heavy\-Tailed Losses

**Autores:** Yuki Shibukawa; Shinji Ito

**Data:** 2026-10-10 · **Tipo:** preprint

## Resumo

We study prediction with expert advice over $T$ rounds with $K$ experts under heavy\-tailed losses whose $\(1\+ε\)$\-moment is bounded by $θ&gt;0$ for some $ε\\in\(0,1\]$\. In this setting, existing algorithms designed for the case $ε=1$ can be suboptimal when $ε&lt;1$, suffering an additional polynomial dependence on $K$\. We characterize the minimax pseudo\-regret rate as $Θ\(\(θT\)^\{\\frac\{1\}\{1\+ε\}\}\(\\log K\)^\{\\fracε\{1\+ε\}\}\)$ and establish gap\-dependent lower bounds in a self\-bounded environment\. To attain this minimax rate without prior knowledge of either $ε$ or $θ$, we propose Adaptive Heavy\-Tailed FTRL, which uses robust data\-dependent scale adaptation together with truncation\. We further develop Adaptive Heavy\-Tailed OMD, which likewise requires neither $ε$ nor $θ$ and achieves near\-optimal regret guarantees in both adversarial and self\-bounded environments\. Finally, when $ε$ and $θ$ are known, an FTRL algorithm with Shannon\-entropy regularization achieves optimal regret guarantees in both environments\.
