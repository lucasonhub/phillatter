# Krupkin, Pavel: Cognitive Mises\-en\-scène

**Autores:** 

**Data de descoberta:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** unknown

## Descrição do feed

\_Ssnr\_\. 2026This essay continues the development of the concept of cognitive mises\-en\-scène – recurrent socio\-spatial forms of cognition that define the positions of participants, the distribution of attention, the character of epistemic action, the communicative protocol, and the modes through which knowledge is recognised\. Its starting point is the well\-known distinction between Western and Eastern forms of cognition and technical creativity\. The essay proposes a two\-tier model of knowledge production: at the base tier, epistemically warranted candidates for the status of knowledge are constituted through the mises\-en\-scène of the Workshop, Journey, and Deciphering Signs; at the upper tier, they undergo social legitimation in various forms of Gathering\. The two tiers are linked by the mises\-en\-scène of Lecture and Theatre, and by their textual variant: a report on the results obtained\. Combinations of these mises\-en\-scène are instantiated in the practices of epistemic communities in modern science\. Further analysis makes it possible to distinguish elementary epistemic schemes within the mises\-en\-scène: Working with the Hands, Reading Signs, Departing and Returning, Transmitting Knowledge, and Recognising Together, as well as two limiting orientations of cognition: the distanced\-objectifying and participatory\-harmonising ones\. The proposed morphology is understood as open and capable of further reconfiguration\. \(direct link \)
