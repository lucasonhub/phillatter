# SKG: A Law\-Induced Engine from Inference to Induction

**Autores:** Jian An; Chaoshi Wu; Jinhui Wu

**Data:** 2026-10-14 · **Tipo:** preprint

## Resumo

This paper proposes the SKG \(Symplectic\-Kernel Gradient\) reasoning engine for law\-induced inference and induction\. Laws are a priori; data provide instances\. Inference is geometric evolution under law constraints\. We organize symplectic geometry, GENERIC, kernel methods, RKHS, group\-equivariant networks, and constrained mechanics into a unified, analyzable, implementable, verifiable framework\. We propose the SKG octuple S = \(M, g, omega, H, S, G, Delta, Pi\), whose four components correspond to four levels of laws: conservation laws, constitutive relations, symmetry groups, and axiom systems\. Main results: \(1\) Invariants enter metric construction via the Jacobian: g = J^T Sigma^\-1 J \+ epsilon\_G I\. For constant cases, we establish inverse reconstruction theory: solvability rank condition \(Thm 3\.9\), consistency criterion \(Thm 3\.11\), and uniqueness theorem \(Thm 3\.14\)\. \(2\) The kernel bandwidth matrix is uniquely determined by Fisher information and satisfies task\-independence \(Thm 3\.19\)\. \(3\) The metric reconstruction error decomposes into four terms \(Thm 3\.21\), with squared condition number kappa\(Sigma^\*\)^2 as the dominant amplification factor \(Thm 3\.22\)\. \(4\) For state\-dependent cases, we establish function\-space reconstruction theory on matrix\-valued RKHS \(Thms 4\.8, 4\.9\)\. \(5\) Three\-layer projections handle axiom, symplectic\-step, and conservation constraints\. \(6\) The residual equation yields the observable formula, four\-dimensional rank condition, and noise propagation, while g\_qq is structurally unidentifiable\. \(7\) Kernel parameterization yields the RKHS extrapolation bound \(Thm 4\.15\) and strict OOD gain theorem in group directions \(Thm 8\.15\)\. \(8\) The learning\-decoupling theorem \(Thm 9\.1\) proves the sole object of induction is the parameters of the task energy\. Validation: 1D damped oscillator and 2D rotation\-invariant system; 6 unit tests, 10 experiments \(paired t\-test, p &lt; 0\.01\)\.
