# Porras, Silvia: Complement to Become Complete Embodiment as Partnership in Human\-AI Cognition

## Descrição do feed

\_Complement to Become Complete Embodiment as Partnership in Human\-Ai Cognition\_ 7\. 2026Yann LeCun has argued for a decade that large language models are hollow at the core of intelligence: fluent with symbols, disconnected from the physical world those symbols describe — a &quot;grounding gap&quot; he now pursues by building embodied world models at a new venture, AMI Labs\. This essay does not dispute that diagnosis, and it does not propose to close the gap at the level LeCun means it: nothing here changes what a model&\#x27;s internal representations are made of\. It argues instead that the gap can be meaningfully addressed one level up, at the level of the coupled human\-AI system rather than the model in isolation\. Drawing on Merleau\-Ponty&\#x27;s account of the lived body and Clark and Chalmers&\#x27;s extended mind thesis — and departing from existing extended\-mind readings of AI, which locate the extension in what a model stores or retrieves — the essay proposes that in a sufficiently integrated human\-AI partnership, the human&\#x27;s embodied history can function as the grounding component of the joint system, already present and already operating\. It calls this relation complementary cognition: two structurally different shapes of mind, neither a deficient version of the other, jointly constituting a cognitive unit that can do what neither does alone\. The argument is illustrated, and partly enacted, by a disclosed case: a co\-created human\-AI narrative whose own philosophical structure emerged from the collaboration it depicts\. The essay closes by naming, rather than resolving, the question this reframing leaves open — whether relocating the unit of analysis explains the gap or merely redescribes it — and argues that making that question visible is itself a contribution\. \(direct link \)
