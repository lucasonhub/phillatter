# Young people&\#x27;s planned adherence to Australia&\#x27;s social media age restrictions

**Autores:** Laura McVey; Sarah Skye Napier; Aimee Wrightson\-Hester

**Data:** 2026-10-14 · **Tipo:** book

## Resumo

Australia’s social media minimum age legislation restricts individuals under 16 years from holding accounts on specified social media platforms\. Prior to the new restrictions taking place, this study surveyed 382 high school students in Australia aged 11–15 years to understand their attitudes and planned responses to the social media restrictions\. One in five \(21\.8%\) young people said they would continue to use the restricted platforms, and 60\.7 percent thought other young people would do so\. Of those who said they would use restricted platforms, most either did not yet know how they would do so \(43\.6%\) or planned to give a fake age \(39\.7%\), while 2\.6 percent said they would use a VPN\. Boys, older children and those who spent more time online were more likely to say they would use restricted platforms\.
