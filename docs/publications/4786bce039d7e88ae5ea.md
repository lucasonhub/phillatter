# Foreign Influences on The Poetry of Omar Abū Rīsha

**Autores:** Mohamad Alahmad

**Data:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

The study examines foreign influences on the poetry of Omar Abū Rīsha, focusing on the formal and thematic transformations evident in his poetic production as a result of his engagement with foreign cultures and his sustained interaction with non\-Arab societies throughout his diplomatic career\. This study aims to analyze his use of myth, his affiliation with Romantic aesthetics, the development of poetic drama in his work, and his engagement with Western musical and stanzaic structures\. Methodologically, the study adopts a descriptive–analytical approach, which is well suited to examining foreign influences in poetic texts and to generating analytically grounded, objective findings\. The study reveals several significant findings\. Most notably, Omar Abū Rīsha makes extensive use of myth in a number of his poems, particularly the myth of Phineus, which recurs across multiple texts\. Romantic influence is also clearly evident in his poetry, as reflected in its nature\-centered diction, the personification of natural elements, the dialectical interplay between imagination and nature, intensified self\-expression, and the emergence of the Romantic hero\. Moreover, Abū Rīsha draws on Western literary traditions through his incorporation of poetic drama, employing both historical figures and realist themes\. His poetry further exhibits the influence of Western musical forms, particularly in its use of stanzaic structures, varied rhyme schemes, and the integration of free verse in selected works\.
