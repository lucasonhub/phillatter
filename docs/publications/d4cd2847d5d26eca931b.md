# Ablation study of RGB encoding strategies for diffusion\-based dual\-view mammogram synthesis

**Autores:** Jorge Alberto Garza\-Abdala; Gerardo Alejandro Fumagal\-González; Daly Avendaño; Servando Cardona; Sadam Hussain; Eduardo de Ávila\-Armenta; Jasiel Toscano; Diana Sofia Milagros Rosales Gurmendi; Alma Pedro; José G\. Tamez‐Peña

**Data:** 2026-10-15 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

The availability of large multi\-view mammographic datasets is limited by privacy concerns and acquisition variability, restricting the development of robust artificial intelligence models\. This study investigates the impact of RGB encoding strategies on diffusion\-based simultaneous dual\-view mammogram synthesis\. A Denoising Diffusion Probabilistic Model was fine\-tuned using RGB representations that encode craniocaudal and mediolateral oblique views with different third\-channel formulations\. Three encoding strategies were evaluated using image quality metrics, distribution\-based comparisons, and anatomical consistency measurements\. Results show that zero\-channel and difference representations achieved comparable performance, while sum\-based encoding provided lower performance\. These findings highlight the importance of input representation design for generating consistent synthetic multi\-view mammograms\.
