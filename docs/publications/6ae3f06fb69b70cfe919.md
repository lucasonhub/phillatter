# Inhibitors of PIM‐1 Signaling Pathways as Anticancer Agents: Pharmacological and Structural Viewpoints

**Autores:** Banty Kumar; Ankur Vaidya; Shweta Jain; Sanjeev Kumar Sahu; Preeti Patel; Vivek Asati

**Data:** 2026-10-14 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Pim\-1, a carcinogenic threonine and serine kinase, inhibits apoptosis and advances the cell cycle\. It is overexpressed in several human cancers\. The proviral integration site of Moloney murine leukemia virus 1 is Pim\-1\. Previous studies have shown that the Pim\-1 30\-UTR regulates the stability of Pim\-1 mRNA\. Threonine and serine are identified as the proto\-oncogene Pim\-1 kinase\. Pim\-1 mediates numerous biological processes, including apoptosis, cell division, proliferation, and cancer\. Pim\-1\-mediated anticancer activity has been reported in several analogs of thiazole/bis\-thiazole, tetrazine, thiophenes, quinoline, pyrazolopyrimidine, 3\-cyanopyridine, thiazolidine\-2,4\-dione, thieno\[2,3\-b\]pyridine, triazolo\[4,3\-b\]pyridazine, pyrazolo\[1,5\-a\]pyrimidines, and pyrazolo\[3,4\-b\]pyridine derivatives\. Additionally, flavonoids and amino alcohol compounds that constitute Pim\-1\-mediated cancer therapy have also been identified thus far\. Many compounds with Pim\-1\-mediated cytotoxicity have been discovered up to this point, and scientists are currently concentrating on bringing novel compounds with anticancer potential to the market\.
