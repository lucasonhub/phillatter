# ПСИХОЛОГИЧЕСКИЕ ОСОБЕННОСТИ ПРОЯВЛЕНИЯ СОЦИАЛЬНОЙ КРЕАТИВНОСТИ У МЕНЕДЖЕРОВ ОБРАЗОВАНИЯ В УСЛОВИЯХ ВИРТУАЛЬНОГО ПЕДАГОГИЧЕСКОГО ВЗАИМОДЕЙСТВИЯ

**Autores:** ДИЛЬБАР ДМИТРИЕВНА БОЙМИРЗАЕВА

**Data:** 2026-10-13 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

This article examines the psychological characteristics of social creativity among education managers in the context of virtual pedagogical interaction\. The relevance of the study is determined by the digital transformation of the educational environment, the expansion of remote forms of professional communication, and the need to develop new approaches to managing teaching staff\. Particular attention is paid to the cognitive, emotional\-volitional, communicative, and reflective components of social creativity in educational leaders\. The article explores the role of empathy, cognitive flexibility, emotional intelligence, communicative competence, and the ability to resolve conflicts constructively in organizing virtual pedagogical interaction\. Based on a theoretical analysis of psychological and pedagogical literature, the main conditions for developing social creativity among education managers in a digital environment are identified\. The study substantiates the importance of developing educational leaders’ psychological readiness to address management challenges innovatively, maintain a favorable socio\-psychological climate, and facilitate productive professional dialogue in virtual environments\.
