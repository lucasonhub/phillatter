# From sensing to transforming: The discursive trajectory of AI and the limited prominence of human\-centered engagement themes

**Autores:** Miloš Petković; Maribel Labrado\-Antolín

**Data:** 2026-10-10 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Background Generative Artificial Intelligence \(AI\) adoption transformed work structures after 2022, raising psychological safety concerns as efficiency priorities overshadow human\-centered employee wellbeing\. Objective This longitudinal study investigates how engagement is framed as a corporate communication strategy in Standard &amp; Poor&\#x27;s \(S&amp;P\) 500 technology firms during early large\-scale AI adoption \(2022–2024\)\. Methods Using IRaMuTeQ, 150 S&amp;P 500 corporate reports \(2022–2024\) underwent Reinert&\#x27;s Descending Hierarchical Classification, Correspondence Factorial Analysis, and similarity network analysis, employing chi\-square \( χ 2 \) marker filtering\. Results Findings reveal a three\-stage trajectory mapping onto dynamic capabilities: initial sensing \(2022\), platform seizing \(2023\), and process transformation \(2024\)\. However, human\-centered engagement language \(meaningfulness, safety, vigor\) remained structurally marginalized, failing to form a stable lexical cluster and framing participation strictly through technical integration\. Conclusions Public discourse serves as a proxy for enacting dynamic capabilities\. Practically, HR leaders should prospectively audit AI communications to ensure technical efficiency narratives do not displace human\-centered and psychological safety registers\.
