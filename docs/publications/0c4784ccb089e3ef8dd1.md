# APPLICATION OF ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE AND AUTOMATION TECHNOLOGIES IN MODERN AGRICULTURAL MACHINERY

**Autores:** Rahimjonov Shoxrux Shuhratjon o&\#x27;g&\#x27;li

**Data:** 2026-10-09 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

The rapid development of artificial intelligence \(AI\), robotics, computer vision, Internet of Things \(IoT\), satellite navigation, remote sensing, and autonomous control systems is transforming the design and operation of modern agricultural machinery\. Agricultural machinery is increasingly evolving from conventional mechanical equipment into intelligent cyber\-physical systems capable of sensing field conditions, processing large volumes of data, making operational decisions, and executing agricultural tasks with limited human intervention\. This article examines the application of artificial intelligence and automation technologies in tractors, harvesters, sprayers, robotic platforms, unmanned aerial vehicles, and other agricultural machinery\. The study is based on a systematic analysis of scientific publications, international institutional reports, and documented agricultural technology adoption data\. Particular attention is given to autonomous navigation, machine vision, precision spraying, variable\-rate application, crop monitoring, yield prediction, robotic weeding, harvesting, and safety systems\. Evidence from the United States demonstrates substantial growth in precision\-agriculture adoption: automated guidance systems were used on more than half of the acreage planted to several major crops, while in 2023 guidance autosteering was used by 52 percent of midsize farms and 70 percent of large\-scale crop\-producing farms\. Scientific literature also demonstrates the growing role of machine learning in yield estimation, nitrogen management, disease detection, and robotic perception\. However, high acquisition costs, inadequate rural connectivity, data governance issues, cybersecurity, technical complexity, and safety risks remain significant barriers\. The study concludes that AI\-enabled agricultural machinery can contribute to higher operational efficiency, more precise input application, reduced operator fatigue, improved resource management, and greater agricultural sustainability, provided that technological development is accompanied by appropriate infrastructure, human\-capacity development, interoperability standards, and safety regulation\.
