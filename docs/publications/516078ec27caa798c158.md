# E Evaluasi Efisiensi Biaya dan Waktu Pembangunan Infrastruktur Transportasi Berbasis Building Information Modeling \(BIM\) Studi Kasus Jembatan Wai Ela : Evaluasi Efisiensi Biaya dan Waktu Pembangunan Infrastruktur Transportasi Berbasis Building Information Modeling \(BIM\) Studi Kasus Jembatan Wai Ela

**Autores:** Kurniasih Rahmadhani Lasinta

**Data:** 2026-10-12 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Conventional construction projects often face various challenges, including cost overruns, inaccurate quantity estimations, and delays in project completion\. This study evaluates cost and time efficiency in the construction of the Wai Ela Bridge in Maluku Province by integrating Building Information Modeling \(BIM\) using Autodesk Revit and Navisworks software with the probabilistic Program Evaluation and Review Technique \(PERT\)\. Based on the analysis results, the implementation of BIM and PERT yielded an optimized project duration of 437 days under the second trial scenario, representing a time efficiency improvement of 24\.52% compared with the actual project duration of 579 days\. In addition, the revised total estimated cost resulted in savings of IDR 1,829,862,541 \(7\.12%\) compared with the actual project cost\. An analysis of delay factors using Fisher’s exact test indicated that contractor management aspects, particularly inadequate field investigations, had a significant influence on delays in material procurement\.
