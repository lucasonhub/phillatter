# The Influence of Training Programs on Work Motivation of Indonesian National Police Officers: An Empirical Study at the South Tangerang Police Resort

**Autores:** Paringsih; Pusporini Palupi Jamaludin

**Data:** 2026-10-18 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Work motivation is a critical factor influencing the performance and professionalism of Indonesian National Police \(POLRI\) officers in carrying out their public service duties\. Within police organizations, training programs represent a key instrument for human resource development that not only aims to improve technical competence but also influences the psychological conditions of officers in performing their responsibilities\. This study aims to analyze the influence of training programs on the work motivation of POLRI officers at the South Tangerang Police Resort\. The research employed a quantitative approach using a survey research design\. The population consisted of all POLRI officers with the rank of Bintara serving in five police sectors within the South Tangerang Police jurisdiction, totaling 338 personnel\. The research sample consisted of 136 respondents selected using proportional random sampling\. Data were collected through structured questionnaires using a Likert scale and analyzed using simple linear regression analysis\. The findings indicate that training programs have a significant influence on the work motivation of police officers\. These results suggest that well\-structured and relevant training programs can contribute to strengthening officers&\#x27; professional readiness and encouraging stronger work motivation in performing policing duties and providing services to the community\.
