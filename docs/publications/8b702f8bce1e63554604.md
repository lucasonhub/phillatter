# Chatbot medici e disorientamento etico, dal Dr\. Google al Dr\. ChaGPT: la voce dell’intelligenza artificiale nel percorso di cura

**Autores:** Ana Maria Ganev

**Data:** 2026-10-09 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

In recent years, access to online health information has undergone a radical and profound transformation: we have moved from the passive information model of search engines \(“Dr\. Google”\) to active, conversational interaction with chatbots based on large language models \(LLMs\)\. This shift represents not only a technological evolution but also an anthropological change in how patients interpret their own experience of illness and build their relationship with their doctor\. A qualitative comparative analysis was conducted on five chatbots \(GPT\-5, Gemini 2\.5 Flash, Claude Sonnet 4\.5, Grok 4 Veloce, and DeepSeek\), presenting each with the same seven medical questions in separate chats on the same day, using free\-access versions to simulate the average user’s experience\. Findings highlight the risk of a progressive disintermediation of medical knowledge, accompanied by phenomena of conversational manipulation and an implicit redefinition of clinical authority\. In this context, the patient arrives at the consultation no longer as a seeker of information, but as an already informed interlocutor, carrying expectations, beliefs, and very often a self\-diagnosis\. This dynamic gives rise to an ethical disorientation involving both parties: patients, who may be influenced by simulated relational interractions, and physicians, who face a perceived erosion of epistemic authority\. In light of this, there is a need to rethink and reframe the therapeutic relationship as an encounter between human vulnerabilities, in which artificial intelligence may serve as a supportive tool, but cannot replace the relational, ethical, and responsible dimension of medical practice
