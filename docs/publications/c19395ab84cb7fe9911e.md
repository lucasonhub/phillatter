# Targeted pathway mining reveals novel modified hexapeptides from the demiguisin family

**Autores:** Tanya Decker; Marie Dayras; Lena Schweizer; Sari Rasheed; Franziska Fries; Bulisa Iova; Kasun H\. Bodawatta; Knud A\. Jønsson; Rolf Müller; Christine Beemelmanns

**Data:** 2026-10-17 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Following the discovery of the piperazic acid \(Piz\)\-containing hexapeptide demiguisin A from a toxic bird\-associated Amycolatopsis strain, we aimed to investigate the structural diversity and bioactive potential of this natural product family\. Here, we used the dmg biosynthetic gene cluster from Amycolatopsis sp\. PS\_44\_ISF1 as a pathway mining probe to search for alternative demiguisin A producers\. A global search identified only one other soil\-derived Amycolatopsis sp\. NBC\_01488, encoding a highly syntenic pathway\. Under feather\-mimicking conditions, PS\_44\_ISF1 demonstrated enhanced growth and metabolite production relative to NBC\_01488\. Despite phylogenomic distance, both strains produced shared metabolites, while NBC\_01488 yielded more abundant demiguisins, enabling the isolation of five novel derivatives characterized by two Piz\-moieties with different hydroxylation patterns and glycine truncation\. Two congeners displayed potent activity against the avian pathogen Mycobacterium intracellulare and moderate activity against Mycobacterium tuberculosis\. These findings show that targeted pathway mining guided the expansion of the chemical diversity of Piz\-modifications and demiguisin congeners with promising anti\-Mycobacterium activity\.
