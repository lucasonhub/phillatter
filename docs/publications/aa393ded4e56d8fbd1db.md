# Elkin, Lee: AI Welfare, Enfranchisement, and Deceptive Misalignment

**Autores:** 

**Data de descoberta:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** unknown

## Descrição do feed

Interest in the possibility of AI welfare is growing within the AI research community, but taking the matter seriously is not without risks\. This paper introduces a societal risk that is independent of whether AI systems have the potential to become genuine welfare subjects\. In particular, dedicating attention and resources to the matter could spur an unwarranted belief among the public that AI systems are genuine welfare subjects\. This, in turn, might trigger premature interventions that eventually lead to AI enfranchisement, or more broadly, to the extension of participatory rights\. Such an extension, however, risks inviting a form of deceptive misalignment under which AI systems obscure their true preferences in an effort to strategically influence collective preferences to their own benefit\. As a proof of concept, I construct a stylized social choice example in which AI systems are included in a voting procedure\. The example is structured by a diagnostic framework I call The Social Choice Test for Welfare\-Based \(Mis\)alignment\. The test deliberately creates an opportunity for strategic voting, allowing AI systems to report seemingly innocent preference orderings that strategically undermine human welfare\. After systematically illustrating the possibility of manipulation by AI systems, I review recent evidence of scheming, alignment faking, and blackmail capabilities among frontier models, suggesting that strategic voting could be a capability on the horizon\. The identified risk supplies a compelling reason for approaching the issue with epistemic modesty and practical restraint\.
