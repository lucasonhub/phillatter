# RCNNricePPI: leveraging deep learning to predict rice protein\-protein interactions for climate\-responsive agriculture

**Autores:** Neeraj Budhlakoti; Nitesh Kumar Sharma; Sharanbasappa D Madival; Anshuman Singh; Sudhir Srivastava; Dwijesh Chandra Mishra; Mahender Kumar Singh; Atul Kumar; Girish Kumar Jha

**Data:** 2026-10-15 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Protein\-protein interactions \(PPIs\) are fundamental to cellular regulation, metabolism and stress\-response pathways in rice, but experimentally characterized interaction networks remain incomplete\. Here, we present RCNNricePPI, a sequence\-based deep learning framework for rice PPI prediction that combines word2vec\-derived amino acid embedding with a Siamese recurrent convolutional neural network integrating convolutional and bidirectional gated recurrent units\. Rice PPIs data were curated from publicly available interaction resources, followed by removal of self\-interactions, redundant pairs and low quality sequence\. To reduce protein\-level information leakage, model development and evaluation were performed using a protein\-aware partitioning strategy, including protein disjoint five\-fold cross\-validation and an independent held\-out test set\. At a classification threshold of 0\.40, RCNNricePPI achieved an accuracy of 0\.8996, balanced accuracy of 0\.7451 and AUROC of 0\.8295, compared with a baseline logistic regression values of 0\.6459, 0\.6964, 0\.7820 respectively\. Performance was further assessed based protein novelty, with AUROC values of 0\.9259, 0\.7995 and 0\.7340 for both\-seen, one\-unseen and both\-unseen protein pairs, respectively\. Architectural ablation and conventional machine learning comparisons further supported the contribution of the integrated architecture\. RCNNricePPI is implemented as an accessible web\-based resource and provides a framework for prioritizing candidate interactions for subsequent experimental investigation\. The RCNNricePPI is freely available at riceppi\.biodb\.in \.
