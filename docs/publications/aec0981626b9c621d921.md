# Finite\-Sample Guarantees for Fully Discrete Conditional Diffusion Models

**Autores:** Qiuyun Zhu

**Data:** 2026-10-09 · **Tipo:** preprint

## Resumo

We establish finite\-sample guarantees for conditional diffusion models learned from data and implemented with finitely many denoising steps\. Our approach aligns the score\-training objective with the sampling grid, allowing statistical estimation and numerical discretization errors to be analyzed together\. Under a smooth conditional density model, we derive high\-probability bounds on the generated distribution&\#x27;s error and quantify the required number of score evaluations\. For every smoothness index $β&gt;0$, a suitable grid yields averaged conditional Kullback\-\-Leibler \(KL\) divergence $\\widetilde O\_p\(n^\{\-2β/\(d\_x\+d\_y\+2β\)\}\)$ relative to the original target with or without early stopping\. Both the full and early\-stopped samplers attain average target total\-variation \(TV\) error $\\widetilde O\_p\(n^\{\-β/\(d\_x\+d\_y\+2β\)\}\)$\. Here $n$ is the sample size, $d\_x,d\_y$ are the data and conditioning dimensions, and errors are averaged over the conditioning variable\. The total\-variation bound matches the rate established in existing conditional distribution\-estimation theory, up to logarithmic factors\.
