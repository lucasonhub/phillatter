# BOSHLANG‘ICH TA’LIMDA EKOLOGIK TAFAKKUR VA BARQAROR RIVOJLANISH KOMPETENSIYALARINI LOYIHAVIY TA’LIM ASOSIDA RIVOJLANTIRISH METODIKASI

**Autores:** Alisher Yuldashev

**Data:** 2026-10-12 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

This article examines the pedagogical and methodological potential of project\- based learning for developing environmental thinking and sustainable development competencies among primary school pupils\. Particular attention is given to fostering children’s sense of responsibility toward nature, helping them recognize environmental problems, analyze their underlying causes, and identify practical solutions\. The study highlights the educational value of project\-based activities in promoting independent inquiry, observation, basic research skills, teamwork, and the ability to evaluate learning outcomes\. It also describes methodological approaches to integrating environmental projects into the primary school curriculum, using issues related to the local natural environment as learning opportunities, and reinforcing environmentally responsible behavior through everyday practices\. The proposed approach emphasizes the integration of theoretical knowledge with practical activities that enable pupils to understand the importance of conserving natural resources, protecting the environment, and taking personal responsibility for ecological well\-being\. By connecting classroom learning with real\-life environmental challenges, project\-based learning can create meaningful opportunities for pupils to develop environmental awareness, practical problem\-solving abilities, and competencies associated with sustainable development\. The methodology is intended to support the gradual formation of environmental culture and responsible citizenship from the early stages of formal education\.
