In Beyond reduction: philosophy of mind and post\-reductionist philosophy of science\. New York: Oxford University Press\. pp\. 151\-182\. 2007This chapter argues for the plausibility of Cognitive Pluralism as a general principle of cognitive architecture, and argues further that scientific pluralism is plausibly seen as a special case of this general principle\.
