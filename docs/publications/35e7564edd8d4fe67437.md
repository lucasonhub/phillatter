# Physically constrained Mamba forecasting supports multiobjective reservoir water quality decisions

**Autores:** Peng Shu; Juan Zhu; Zhonglin Huang; Xiang Yu; Xiaoyu Li; Fangkai Cai; Xuhao Wen

**Data:** 2026-10-10 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Reservoir water\-quality management requires forecasts that remain dynamically plausible and scenario choices that balance water\-quality objectives against the magnitude of requested trajectory change\. Historical monitoring archives, however, rarely contain labelled control actions\. We develop a physics\-informed inverse\-decision framework that combines a decomposition–interaction Mamba forecaster, observation\-space oxygen\-balance diagnostics, a conditional variational autoencoder \(CVAE\) and preference\-conditioned multi\-objective search\. The forecaster separates multiscale dynamics before channel\-specific state\-space encoding and gated cross\-variable exchange\. A lumped Streeter–Phelps residual regularises dissolved\-oxygen forecasts, while the CVAE represents DO–CODMn recovery changes observed after natural events\. Evaluation on Miyun Reservoir observations from June 2021 to January 2025 gives 24\-step RMSE values of 0\.305 for dissolved oxygen and 0\.318 for CODMn\. The physical\-violation rate, defined as disagreement between predicted and Streeter–Phelps model\-implied DO\-increment signs, decreases from 8\.4% without the two output\-space terms to 0\.3% with the full model\. Fixed\-horizon latent search yields a hypervolume of 0\.824, compared with 0\.706 for NSGA\-III and 0\.487 for weighted\-sum latent search, with no diagnostic\-margin crossings in 55 evaluated decisions\. Because the archive contains no device\-action records, the method returns candidate target trajectories and dimensionless concentration\-equivalent demand\-and\-roughness values, not executable commands or calibrated costs\. Prospective use requires independent validation of the physical diagnostic, event generalisation, recursive forecasting and measured actuator response\.
