# Conceptualizing truth\-telling pedagogies in the Norwegian—Sámi context: beyond “closing the knowledge gap” in teacher education

**Autores:** Kristin Gregers Eriksen; Camilla Eline Andersen; Hege Merete Somby

**Data:** 2026-10-15 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

This article explores truth\-telling pedagogies in the Norwegian \- Sámi context in the wake of the 2023 Truth and Reconciliation Commission \(TRC\)\. While the TRC emphasizes shared knowledge as a foundation for reconciliation, it marginalizes explicit engagement with colonialism, thereby risking continued reproduction of sanctioned ignorance and colonial power structures\. Placing experiences from research and practice in the Norwegian \- Sámi context in conversation with international discussions on truth\-telling and decolonial and affective pedagogies, the authors argue that truth\-telling must move beyond addressing a perceived “knowledge gap” toward confronting coloniality as ongoing structure\. A model for a conceptual framework for truth telling in the Norwegian \- Sámi context is suggested and explored\. The model constructs truth\-telling as emerging through invitations, openings, and the cultivation of discomfort as a collective capacity\. The authors argue that while truth\-telling pedagogy holds transformative potential and can be an avenue for nurturing anti\-colonial hope, depoliticization is a real risk if truth\-telling is detached from broader structural change\.
