# Detection Pyramid Reconstruction for Fine\-grained Rice Grain Impurity Detection Using YOLO11s

**Autores:** Sunitha N V; Pradeep B S

**Data:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

The Impurity detection in rice grains plays an important role in assessing the quality and food safety as well as automation\.Unfortunately, most object detectors are built for general purposes and work badly for fine\-grained object detection since their design involves low\-resolution prediction layers\.Our work introduces the strategy of Detection Pyramid Reconstruction \(DPR\) of YOLO11s by substituting P5 prediction layer with high\-resolution P2 prediction layer to enhance detection of rice grains and impurities of small and medium sizes while preserving lightweight architecture\.The model was tested on the custom dataset with 195 images and 13,343 annotations of whole rice, broken rice, husk, and stones using five\-fold cross\-validation\.Precision of the model reached 0\.9190 ± 0\.0247, recall 0\.9115 ± 0\.0297, mAP@50 0\.9516 ± 0\.0134 and mAP@50\-95 0\.5040 ± 0\.0078\.The relative improvement of mAP@50\-95 was 4\.98% while decreasing number of trainable parameters by 41\.8% compared with YOLO11s\.Analysis of results obtained from comparisons of YOLOv8s, YOLOv10s and RT\-DETR\-L, together with ablation studies, revealed that the DPR strategy enhances detection performance and computation efficiency\.Additional evaluation on the VisDrone dataset offered supplementary validation besides the rice dataset\.
