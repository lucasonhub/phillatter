# A hybrid decision method for enhancing resilience of complex systems integrating quantitative resilience evaluation and cost trade\-offs

**Autores:** Guolei Lu; Xin Guo; Mingyue Yang; Xurui Li; Wu Zhao; Jun Li

**Data:** 2026-10-13 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Abstract High\-impact disturbances can easily cause the performance of complex systems to drop sharply or even fail, thereby leading to potential safety risks, a large amount of maintenance costs and losses\. Investing in enhancing the resilience of complex systems is crucial for mitigating the adverse consequences caused by high\-impact disturbance events and ensuring system operational safety\. However, in practical engineering decision\-making, it remains challenging to identify effective schemes from numerous potential improvement measures and quantitatively balance resilience benefits against implementation costs\. Accordingly, this study proposes a hybrid decision method integrating resilience quantification and cost trade\-offs to evaluate resilience enhancement schemes and identify a compromise solution\. First, failure analysis is performed to derive corresponding resilience enhancement measures\. Then, a Bayesian network \(BN\) is used to perform a static capability\-based quantitative evaluation of different resilience enhancement schemes\. Finally, the Non\-dominated Sorting Genetic Algorithm II\(NSGA\-II\) is used to generate the cost–resilience Pareto front, and the Knee Point method is applied to identify a compromise solution\. Application to a complex\-terrain drilling rig yields a baseline resilience score of 0\.3089\. S 9 is identified as the Knee Point of the unconstrained Pareto front, with a cost of 19\.11 × 10 4 CNY and a resilience score of 0\.5718, while under the additional M 8 \-requirement, S 21 is identified as the Knee Point, with a cost of 41\.63 × 10 4 CNY and a resilience score of 0\.6545\. These results demonstrate the capability of the proposed method to identify cost–resilience compromise solutions under different decision requirements\.
