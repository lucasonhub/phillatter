# AI\-Powered Adaptive Learning and Smart Education Platform

**Autores:** Pranav Kohad; Rahul Tiwari; Swara Paliwal; Saniya Bante; Tanvi Salve; Madhura Zade; Ms\. Shraddha Raut

**Data:** 2026-10-12 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Education is becoming increasingly digital, yet many students still face difficulties in understanding complex concepts, remembering long answers, identifying their learning gaps, and preparing effectively for examinations\. This paper presents an AI\-powered adaptive learning platform designed to provide personalized and interactive learning support\. The system uses Artificial Intelligence to explain questions in detail, generate short exam\-oriented answers and recall keywords, analyze students&\#x27; performance, identify knowledge gaps, and recommend suitable learning tasks\. The platform also provides 3D visualization for mathematical and geometric concepts, AI\-assisted learning for languages such as Marathi and Sanskrit with Hindi and English explanations, AI\-based viva examination, instant tests, PDF/notes\-based learning, progress tracking, and gamification\. The main aim is to make learning personalized, understandable, interactive, and focused on actual student improvement rather than simply providing answers
