# Generative Health Data Science for Data\-to\-Dose Precision Pharmacology: A Critical Methodological Review and Translational Framework

**Autores:** 

**Data:** 2026-10-12 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Precision pharmacology aims to select the most appropriate medicine, dose and monitoring strategy for an individual patient\.Its translation is limited by fragmented records, incomplete biological characterization, timevarying physiology and the difficulty of converting high\-dimensional evidence into a safe action\.Generative health data science extends conventional prediction by learning structured representations of molecules, cells, patients and clinical trajectories and by producing reviewable candidates such as molecular structures, treatment options, synthetic records, counterfactual courses and dose scenarios\.This critical review evaluates large language models, multimodal and biological foundation models, graph and diffusion architectures, syntheticpatient generators, digital twins and bounded agentic workflows across target discovery, repurposing, pharmacogenomics, treatment selection, individualised dosing and pharmacovigilance\.Evidence is strongest for representation learning, structure\-enabled discovery and selected pharmacogenomic pathways; autonomous patient\-level treatment and dosing remain investigational\.Recent advances include explainable drug\-repurposing foundation models, multimodal clinical foundation models and a randomised phase 2a trial of a generative\-AIdiscovered TNIK inhibitor\.We propose a Data\-to\-Dose framework linking intended\-use definition, data provenance, longitudinal patient\-state modelling, constrained generation, mechanistic and causal checks, uncertainty\-aware ranking, professional review and lifecycle monitoring\.Generation must remain distinct from clinical authorisation, and synthetic\-data fidelity must not be equated with privacy, fairness or external validity\.Generative systems may become an enabling layer for precision pharmacology only when their creative capacity is bounded by pharmacology, evidence and accountable clinical judgment\.
