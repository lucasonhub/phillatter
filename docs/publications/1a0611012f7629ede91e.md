# Norman, John Mark: The BAL\-Looping Framework in a Nutshell: A Standalone Diagram

**Autores:** 

**Data de descoberta:** 2026-10-19 · **Tipo:** unknown

## Descrição do feed

This single diagram is presented as a standalone item intended to accompany any of the papers on the BAL\-looping framework\. It shows the framework&\#x27;s bare\-bones architecture, omitting much of the terminology and detail developed in the papers, such as the brain at large \(BAL\), the proxy transfer device \(PTD\), neuronal proxies, the internal model of the environment, and other elements that flesh out the framework\. It also leaves aside important conceptual distinctions, such as the fact that gist is not linguistic in nature but is the very stuff of subjective experience, in all its modes\. Some functional relationships, such as the resonance between the output and input channels during looping, are not explicitly labeled but are readily discernible from the diagram\. While examining the diagram, the reader thus closes certain gestalts, a process that can contribute both to understanding and to memory\. \(Updated October 17, 2026 – Added a second page containing a detailed textual description of the diagram for blind and visually impaired readers, followed by the original legend\. The diagram and its explanation remain unchanged\.\)
