# Developing a resource\-based analysis of technological innovation systems: The case of digital innovation in Dutch processing industries

**Autores:** Nikhil John; Joeri Hendrik Wesseling; Koen Frenken

**Data:** 2026-10-10 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Resource\-based perspectives in Technological Innovation Systems \(TIS\) research offer new insights into innovation dynamics and address gaps in the traditional structure\-function approach\. We take stock of this perspective to develop a framework that enhances TIS in two ways\. First, we improve the conceptual linkage between TIS elements and functions by focusing on innovation resources and their dynamics\. Next, we offer a combined view of system\-building and hindering processes by identifying and characterizing meso\-level TIS mechanisms through micro\-level resource changes\. We apply our framework to a qualitative case study on the digital TIS within the Dutch processing industry\. The analysis generalizes four system\-building activities and four systemic problems, which emerge from the presence, accessibility, applicability, and scale of innovation resources\. Additionally, four case\-specific mechanisms are identified which illustrate tensions between positive and negative dynamics, revealing fundamental complexity underlying the overall TIS functioning\. Our framework provides a basis for unpacking such tensions and identifying more targeted policy interventions, particularly for innovation systems of general\-purpose technologies\.
