# INTEGRATING MODERN TECHNOLOGIES WITH BPM: WAYS TO ACHIEVE EFFICIENCY AND SUSTAINABILITY

**Autores:** Khazar Najimbayli; Said JAMALOV

**Data:** 2026-10-13 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

This article explores integrating modern technologies with BPM and how it can be a game changer for organizations\.Tools such as Power BI and Python are shown to be effective in optimizing processes, minimizing time loss, and aligning workflows with organizational goals\.The study analyzes the stages of BPM in detail \-process discovery, analysis, redesign, and implementation \-and shows how this approach creates effective decision\-making, operational agility, and long\-term sustainability\.Furthermore, it demonstrates how data\-driven automation reduces human error and provides real\-time visibility into performance bottlenecks\.The results highlight the importance of BPM in the modern business environment and the strategic role that technology plays in these processes\.
