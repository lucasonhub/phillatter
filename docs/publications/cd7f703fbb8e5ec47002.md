# Generation of Elite Ideal Rice Germplasm via CRISPR/Cas9‐Mediated Multiplex Targeted Gene Editing

**Autores:** Jiangmin Xu; Jianmin Li; Yilin Luo; Mingzhe Pan; Jing Yang; Yuchun Rao

**Data:** 2026-10-14 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Summary statement Gene editing of five key negative regulatory genes, GS3 , OsBADH2 , Xa13 , Bsr‐d1 and OsSULTR3;6 , generated an elite ideal‐type rice variety mk‐10 , which exhibits a longer grain shape, enhanced tolerance to heat and alkali stress, increased 2AP content, and simultaneous resistance to Xoo , rice blast and Xoc \.
