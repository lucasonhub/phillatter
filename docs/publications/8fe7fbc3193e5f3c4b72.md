# Myshko, Andrii: METAMONISM : Chapter 2\. Continuation of Actualization: Dissipation of Identity, the Emergence of Spatiality and Temporality, and Orthogonal Resolution

**Autores:** 

**Data de descoberta:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** unknown

## Descrição do feed

The second chapter of the monograph &quot;Metamonism \(Ex uno omnia\)&quot; examines the transition from a single act of actualization to a continuing process\. It is demonstrated that no new fundamental invariants are required for the continuation of actualization — all subsequent structures arise as necessary consequences of the sole Prohibition of Indifference\. A strict distinction is drawn between the trivial logical reflexivity of a state and its substantive, ontodynamic exhaustion; it is shown that it is substantive exhaustion, rather than formal tautology, that removes the state from the domain of the actualization operator\. Three consequences are derived from this contradiction: dissipation of identity as the essence of the continuing process; the emergence of spatiality and temporality as forms of process continuation; orthogonal resolution as the minimal transformation that resolves the contradiction of the current state\. The concept of a recursive loop and conjugate mapping is introduced\. The methodological status of equidistance and of the transition from asynchrony to periodicity is separately qualified — as economical but not strictly uniquely demonstrated solutions\. The theorem on the fundamental incompletability of actualization is formulated\. The chapter demonstrates that space, time, and all subsequent structures are not independent entities but arise as necessary consequences of a single fundamental prohibition\. \(direct link \)
