# Multi\-AI agent framework reveals the “oxide gatekeeper” in aluminum nanoparticle oxidation

**Autores:** Yiming Lu; Tingyu Lu; Di Zhang; Lili Ye; Hao Li

**Data:** 2026-10-15 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Aluminum nanoparticles \(ANPs\) are among the most energy\-dense solid fuels, yet the atomic mechanisms governing their transition from passivated particles to highly reactive states remain elusive\. This stems from a fundamental computational bottleneck: Ab initio methods offer quantum\-level fidelity but are restricted to small spatiotemporal scales \(&lt;500 atoms, picoseconds\), while empirical force fields lack the reactive fidelity required for complex combustion environments\. Here, we bridge this gap by employing a human\-audited, multiagent framework to make the development and validation of a task\-specific machine learning potential \(MLP\) more traceable and auditable\. The workflow coordinates active learning, model evaluation, validation evidence, and human expert review, while leaving scientific interpretation and model\-use decisions under researcher control\. This collaborative cycle ensures quantum mechanical accuracy while exhibiting near\-linear scalability\. Strikingly, our simulations reveal a temperature\-regulated dual\-mode oxidation mechanism: At moderate temperatures, the oxide shell acts as a dynamic &quot;gatekeeper,&quot; regulating oxidation through a &quot;breathing mode&quot; of transient nanochannels; above a critical threshold, a &quot;rupture mode&quot; leads to shell failure and rapid combustion\. Importantly, we resolve a decades\-old controversy by demonstrating that aluminum cation outward diffusion, rather than oxygen transport, dominates mass transfer in studied regimes, with diffusion coefficients consistently exceeding those of oxygen by 2 to 3 orders of magnitude\. These results provide an atomistic framework for interpreting ANP reactivity and illustrate how human\-audited agentic workflows can make domain\-specific reactive MLP development and deployment more reproducible\.
