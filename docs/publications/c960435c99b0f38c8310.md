# Physics\-guided residual inverse\-transition modeling for continuum robots with simulation and ordered experimental evaluation

**Autores:** Mohamed Razi Morakchi; Talhi Amar; Abdelhamid Ghoul; Selman Djeffal; Badreddine Bendriss; Ameur Latreche

**Data:** 2026-10-15 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Abstract This study distinguishes two levels that were conflated in the original submission: a tendon\-driven mechanical dynamics model used only in simulation, and an ordered one\-step inverse\-transition estimator used for the recorded experiment\. The experimental estimator does not identify force\-level inverse dynamics because the supplied dataset contains no verified sampling interval, velocity, acceleration, tendon tension, actuator torque, pneumatic pressure, or contact\-force channel\. Instead, it predicts the next measured six\-channel length\-state increment from the present Cartesian state, desired Cartesian increment, and a structured representation of the measured length state\. The first\-order prior is a global empirical differential map, $$\\widehat\{\{\\bf J\}\}\\in \\mathbb \{R\}^\{3\\times 6\}$$ , estimated only from the training block; it has rank 3, condition number 3\.49, and singular values 1\.425, 1\.317, and 0\.409\. Reviewer\-driven ablation further showed that the Clarke\-like $$3\\times 3$$ transform is a full\-rank coordinate change rather than a dimensionality reduction, that appending both raw channels and deterministic modes reweights Gaussian\-kernel distances, and that the one\-step history term does not improve the held\-out experimental prediction\. The revised experimental instantiation therefore retains only the four bending coordinates $$\(q\_\{\\alpha ,1\},q\_\{\\beta ,1\},q\_\{\\alpha ,2\},q\_\{\\beta ,2\}\)$$ in the nonlinear residual learner and excludes both the common mode and duplicated raw/mode features\. On the chronologically held\-out 39\-transition test block, this bending\-mode residual KRR obtains a task\-space RMSE of 0\.365 mm and a cable/length\-state RMSE of 0\.745 mm, compared with 0\.469 mm for a validation\-tuned direct KRR, 0\.596 mm for ridge regression, 0\.654 mm for the NARX\-feature model, and 0\.795 mm for the global empirical prior\. A four\-sample moving\-block bootstrap gives a within\-trajectory 95% interval of approximately 0\.282–0\.437 mm for the revised task\-space RMSE\. A paired sign\-randomization test against direct KRR gives $$p\\approx 0\.060$$ , so the small test block does not justify a claim of statistically conclusive superiority over that strongest nonlinear baseline\. The independently learned forward evaluator has a test component RMSE of 0\.139 mm and vector RMSE of 0\.240 mm, but its larger validation error also reveals chronological distribution shift\. Accordingly, the revised claims are restricted to free\-space, one\-step inverse\-transition prediction on the recorded trajectory family; no cross\-speed, cross\-platform, force/contact, or closed\-loop hardware generalization is claimed\.
