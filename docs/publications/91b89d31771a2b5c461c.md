# Workforce agility and organizational transformation: The mediating role of employee resilience and the moderating effect of change fatigue

**Autores:** Shivali M; Sindu Bharath

**Data:** 2026-10-14 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Knowledge Intensive Business Service \(KIBS\) organizations operate in rapidly evolving environment where digitalization, technological disruption and changing client expectation demand continuous organizational transformation\. Present study investigates the relationship between Workforce Agility \(WFA\), and perceived Organizational Transformation \(OT\) while examining the mediating role of Employee Resilience \(ER\) and the moderating role of Change Fatigue \(CF\)\. The study employed a three\-wave quantitative survey design, preceded by exploratory semi\-structured interviews conducted with 30 employees from leading KIBS organization in South India to assess the contextual relevance of the survey instrument\. Further, the survey was administered to 477 employees\. Data analysis was carried out using SPSS, AMOS and Hayes PROCESS Macro \(Model 14\)\. The findings revealed that WFA has positive direct effect on perceived OT and significantly enhances ER\. Furthermore, ER mediated the relationship between WFA and OT with indirect effect becoming significant only under moderate and high level of CF\. The moderated mediation results confirmed that the indirect effect \(WFA on OT via ER\) was stronger as CF increased\. The findings imply that resilience acts a critical adaptive resource when employees experience sustained change demands\. The present study contributes to dynamic capability theory and conservation of resources theory by explaining the conditional mechanism through which WFA facilitates perceived OT\. Essentially the findings emphasize the importance of developing WFA, reinforcing ER and implementing supportive change management practices for successful organizational transformation\. Keywords: Workforce Agility; Organizational Transformation; Employee Resilience; Change Fatigue; KIBS\.
