# A Project\-Based Hybrid Learning Model Using Artificial Intelligence to Enhance Students&\#x27; Self\-RegulatedLearning

**Autores:** Surya Priyambudi; Yulis Setyowati; Alven Safik Ritonga

**Data:** 2026-10-09 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Students&\#x27; SRL ability in the English Literature Study Program at Universitas Wijaya Putra remains low, as shown by a preliminary survey recording a mean SRL score of 2\.21\. This is worsened by post\-pandemic hybrid learning implemented without pedagogical design for learning independence, and by the absence of a model integrating PjBL, hybrid learning, and an AI to improve SRL\. This study develops a PjBHL model assisted by an AI through a Research and Development approach using the ADDIE model\. The model maps Zimmerman&\#x27;s three SRL phases forethought, performance, and self\-reflection into project\-based and hybrid learning syntax, with AI acting as metacognitive scaffolding and a cognitive interlocutor\. Feasibility was tested through expert validation and a practicality questionnaire, while effectiveness was tested through a quasi\-experimental non\-equivalent control group design involving experimental and control groups\. Results show the model is valid and practical based on expert and user assessments, and effective in improving students&\#x27; SRL compared with conventional learning\. This study contributes theoretically through a new, structured pedagogical model, and practically by helping lecturers foster students&\#x27; learning independence in the AI era\.
