# Die Förderung von Critical Digital Literacy im Englischunterricht am Beispiel visueller Repräsentationen von Gender durch künstliche Intelligenz

**Autores:** Subin Nijhawan

**Data:** 2026-10-15 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

This article examines learners’ use of artificial intelligence \(AI\) with both languagerelated and subject\-specific learning processes in the foreign language classroom\. The development of critical digital literacy \(\#CDL\), understood as the ability to critically assess and evaluate digital, including AI\-generated content, is focused on in the reported research data, a video\-taped double lesson of an upper\-secondary advanced English course\. In the lesson, learners used generative AI ChatGPT and DALL\-E 3\) to design their own campaigns for the promotion of gender equality, and discussed and reflected the appropriateness of AI\-generated visual representations\. The data analysis draws on video and screen recordings, group discussions, the plenary presentation and the subsequently initiated reflection\. Focusing on a critical incident, the analysis shows how learners negotiate meaning by aligning with or distancing themselves from AI\-generated artefacts, concomitantly developing, testing, evaluating and reflecting upon prompting strategies\. In particular, the data reveal the learners’ critical engagement with AI\-generated representations of gender in relation to diversity, equality, justice and intersectional equity as well as implicit and contested beauty ideals\. Processes of interpretation, appropriation, emotional engagement and the act of developing and defending a position become visible as learners distance themselves from DALL\-E 3’s stereotypical visual worlds and the underlying algorithmic biases of ChatGPT\.
