# The mediating role of social distance on the association between audience\-protagonist similarity and fear: a case of fear of COVID\-19

**Autores:** Inna Levy; Irene Razpurker‐Apfeld; Lipaz Shamoa‐Nir; Keren Cohen‐Louck

**Data:** 2026-10-14 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Objective This study examined the effects of news content on fear of COVID\-19 by examining perceived social distance and audience\-protagonist similarity\. Method Participants \( N = 78\) read a fictitious news story about a COVID\-19 patient \(similar/dissimilar protagonist\) and then rated social distance regarding this protagonist and their fear of COVID\-19\. Results The results showed that participants exposed to a similar protagonist reported significantly lower perceived social distance, and that social distance was moderately negatively correlated with fear of COVID\-19\. Social distance mediated the association between audience\-protagonist similarity and fear of COVID\-19\. Conclusion These findings provide insights into how exposure to health\-related news content can impact fear of disease\.
