# KIBERBULLINGGA UCHRAGAN O‘SMIRLARDA STRESS VA STRESSGA CHIDAMLILIK XUSUSIYATLARI

**Autores:** NARGIZA RAJABBOYEVNA BEKKIYEVA

**Data:** 2026-10-12 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

The article analyses cyberbullying as a specific traumatic stressor of adolescence\. It describes the features that distinguish cyberbullying from traditional bullying – continuity, anonymity, a wide audience and permanence of content – and the mechanisms by which they intensify the stress response\. Drawing on the stress theories of H\.Selye, R\.Lazarus and S\.Folkman, S\.Hobfoll and recent international research, the article describes how stress manifests in cyberbullied adolescents at the physiological, emotional, cognitive and behavioural levels\. Zamonaviy jamiyat va innovatsiyalar ilmiy nazariy jurnali 2026\-yil, 9\-Son, 2\-Qism\. ISSN 3060\-544X Personal resources \(self\-esteem, emotion regulation, coping strategies, digital literacy\) and social resources \(family, peers, school\) of stress resistance are systematised\. A set of diagnostic instruments for empirical study is proposed, and practical recommendations are offered for psychologists, teachers and parents\.
