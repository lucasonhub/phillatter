# DOCENTIA Lectures

**Autores:** Hernán Viguera Figueroa; Gustavo Pavez Von Martens; Patricia Ibáñez Polanco; Cintia Montenegro Villalobos; Mauricio Saavedra Oyarzún; Carlos Loyola Lobo

**Data:** 2026-10-13 · **Tipo:** book

## Resumo

DOCENTIA Lectures, Volume 3, examines the role of university teaching amid the rapid expansion of artificial intelligence and argues that the central challenge is not to compete with technology but to strengthen judgment, critical thinking, and human capacities that should not be delegated\. The volume explores the relationship between higher education and employability, educational and career pathways, ten trends reshaping higher education, and the risk of so\-called cognitive debt when AI substitutes for reasoning processes\. It also addresses the limitations of AI\-detection tools and the need to redesign assessment around observable thinking processes, the renewed role of the humanities as an ethical and critical framework, and seven skills redefining teaching in 2026, including attention management, creativity, critical use of AI, empathy, lifelong learning, translating complexity into meaningful understanding, and teacher wellbeing\. Framed within the work of DOCENTIA at Universidad Autónoma de Chile, the publication advocates a pedagogical integration of AI that preserves human agency, judgment, and intellectual autonomy\.
