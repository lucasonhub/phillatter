# ІННОВАЦІЙНІ ІНСТРУМЕНТИ РОЗВИТКУ ФОНДОВОГО РИНКУ

**Autores:** Л\.С\. Морозова; І\.С\. Любчак

**Data:** 2026-10-13 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

The article is devoted to the study of modern innovative instruments of the stock market and their definition\. The study determined the role of digital financial instruments in the transformation of the mechanisms of capital markets\. The main trends in the development of financial technologies were considered\. Their impact on increasing the efficiency of investment activities, optimizing asset management and ensuring the transparency of stock market transactions was analyzed\. The advantages of using innovative instruments compared to traditional approaches to investment were determined\. The main advantages are high speed of transactions, reduced transaction costs, expanded access to financial services, automation of decision\-making processes and increased liquidity of capital markets\. The article examines the features of the application of blockchain technologies, tokenization of financial assets, cryptocurrencies, exchange\-traded funds for digital assets and digital currencies of central banks\. Smart contracts, fintech platforms, open banking solutions and APIs are considered\. Special attention is paid to Big Data technologies, artificial intelligence, machine learning, robotic process automation, algorithmic and high\-frequency trading, robo\-advisory as an innovative tool for managing investment portfolios\. The global experience of using innovative stock market instruments is analyzed\. Current trends in their development on leading international stock exchanges are identified\. Particular attention is paid to the risks and challenges associated with the implementation of new technologies\. These include cyber risks, regulatory restrictions, technological risks, volatility of digital assets and information protection problems\. It is substantiated that the further implementation of innovative instruments will contribute to increasing the efficiency of the stock market\. This will ensure its competitiveness, expand investment opportunities and contribute to the formation of a modern digital financial ecosystem\.
