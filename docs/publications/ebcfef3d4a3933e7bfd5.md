# A Review of Solar\-Powered Automated Railway Track Fault Detection Systems with Real\-Time GPS Tracking

**Autores:** Prajyot Devidas Ingle; Saksham Kumar Almel; Shreyash Manohar Chitragar; Aaditya Prabhakar Gund; Prof\.  Waghmode M\.  N\.

**Data:** 2026-10-14 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Railway infrastructure is essential for safe and efficient transportation\. Traditional track inspection relies on manual observation, which is slow, labor\-intensive, and can expose inspection staff to operational and environmental hazards\. This review paper surveys existing approaches to automated railway track fault detection, concentrating on solar\-powered monitoring and real\-time GPS tracking\. Technologies reported in earlier studies, namely ultrasonic sensors, mechanical limit switches, embedded microcontrollers, GPS modules, GSM communication, and photovoltaic power systems, are examined for their use in track monitoring\. The paper explains how these technologies can be combined to detect track\-related abnormalities, determine where they occur, and send fault information so that maintenance can be carried out on time\. The approaches are also compared in terms of detection capability, communication, power requirements, reliability, and practical deployment challenges\. In addition, the paper points out current limitations and discusses future possibilities involving IoT, artificial intelligence, computer vision, and predictive maintenance\. Overall, the review shows that combining automated fault detection, renewable energy, and real\-time location tracking can improve the reliability, efficiency, and safety of railway track monitoring systems\.
