# Kato, Yasushi &amp; Sugimoto, Shunsuke \(eds\.\): Dignity and AI/Robotics

**Autores:** 

**Data de descoberta:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** unknown

## Descrição do feed

The rapid development of AI and robotics is compelling us to rethink the meaning of “human dignity” and, more fundamentally, to reconsider what it means to be human\. How is dignity institutionalized in law, society, and technology? How do researchers and practitioners in AI and robotics understand dignity, and how do they situate it within their research and business activities? This volume presents dignity as an indispensable framework for thinking about the ethical and social challenges of the age of AI, and explores its scope and potential from a variety of perspectives\.
