# Liderazgo adaptativo e innovador: articulación entre innovación pedagógica y gestión estratégica en la educación superior

**Autores:** Julio César Martínez Aguilar

**Data:** 2026-10-13 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

The transformation of higher education has increased the need to articulate classroom\-based innovation with institutional management and governance processes\. In this context, academics who assume academic program leadership responsibilities occupy an intermediate position between teaching practice and strategic management, although the literature frequently addresses these dimensions separately\. This article aims to analyze and integrate recent literature on academic leadership, pedagogical innovation, governance, and faculty development in higher education, with particular attention to the role of academic middle leaders as agents connecting educational practice and institutional change\. An integrative literature review was conducted based on thematic relevance, recency, scientific quality, and availability of DOI, complemented by regional evidence concerning artificial intelligence adoption and governance in Latin American and Caribbean higher education\. The thematic synthesis identified four interconnected dimensions: adaptive and innovative leadership, academic middle leadership, shared governance, and servant leadership\. The findings suggest that institutionalizing innovation depends not only on individual faculty initiative but also on organizational conditions that promote participation, trust, capacity development, and shared decision\-making\. The study concludes that academic program leaders who maintain a teaching role can become strategic actors connecting pedagogical innovation with institutional management when they have adequate autonomy, leadership development, and organizational support\.
