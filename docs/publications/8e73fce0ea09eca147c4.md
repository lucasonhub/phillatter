Del Valle ORCID ID: 0009\-0004\-5294\-7981 October 13, 2026 Abstract: This research identifies the mechanical degradation of the Superior Colliculus and Frontal Eye Fields \(FEF\) as a direct consequence of lack of aesthetics in senior residential environments\.
