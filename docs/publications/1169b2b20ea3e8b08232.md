# Rebacz, Andrzej: Philosophical Foundations of the Ontology of Operational Time: Superrhythm and the Fractal Cascade of Coherence

## Descrição do feed

This work presents an original ontology of operational time, arguing that time is not an a priori background of reality but an emergent effect of systemic organization\. The central thesis is that operational time—defined as a system&\#x27;s capacity for action, endurance, and directed change—arises exclusively when a relational system exceeds a critical coherence threshold, denoted $\\lambda\_\{\(c\)\}$\. Part I deconstructs the assumption of pre\-given time, distinguishing timeless \`\`relational happening&\#x27;&\#x27; \(subcritical noise\) from time\-generating \`\`acting\.&\#x27;&\#x27; The mechanism of phase retention and info\-structural work is introduced: a system must actively compensate for dissipation \(asymmetry $T\_1 &gt; T\_2$\) to sustain global phase\. This yields the minimal arrow of time as an internal consequence of rhythmic organization, not entropy or external physics\. Part II extends the framework cosmologically through the model of the \\textbf\{Cascade of Coherence\}—a fractal hierarchy of nested rhythms\. Local operational times are stabilized by higher levels, culminating in the \\textbf\{Superrhythm\}, the global phase of the cosmos that maintains unity, anchors physical constants as base resonances, and sustains the laws of nature\. Consciousness emerges as the highest and most costly threshold \($\\Phi$ in analogy to Integrated Information Theory\), where the universe achieves self\-reflective coherence\. Part III rigorously delineates the theory&\#x27;s boundaries: ontological \(silence of subcritical noise\), epistemic \(no knowledge without rhythm\), mathematical \(irreducibility of process to formalism\), and empirical \(explicit falsification protocol: action without rhythm, superluminal classical causality, consciousness without integration, autonomous locality\)\. Metaphysical questions \(teleology of rhythm, status of qualia, ultimate fate of cycles\) remain deliberately open\. The work concludes that reality is not given but achieved through rhythmic organization\. \`\`To be is to maintain phase&\#x27;&\#x27;—this formula bridges the physics of constants with the metaphysics of endurance, offering a unified framework for understanding cosmic unity and the place of consciousness in its fractal structure\. \\textbf\{Keywords:\} operational time, coherence threshold, Superrhythm, fractal cosmology, phase coherence, consciousness, Integrated Information Theory, arrow of time, falsifiability, ontology of rhythm \(direct link \)
