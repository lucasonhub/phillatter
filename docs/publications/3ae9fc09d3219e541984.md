# Implementation Of Internship at the Central Statistics Agency of Sidoarjo Regency Msme Mapping Process Ahead of the 2026 Economic Census

**Autores:** Davin Khurniawan; Atikha Sidhi Cahyana

**Data:** 2026-10-15 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

General Background Digitalization within public statistical institutions enables streamlined data collection during national economic mapping activities\. Specific Background The Central Statistics Agency utilizes mobile applications like Kendedes Mobile for field survey tagging and micro\-business enumeration ahead of national economic censuses\. Knowledge Gap Although mobile surveys expedite field operations, systemic evaluations measuring multi\-dimensional operational factors that dictate overall user satisfaction levels remain understudied\. Aims This research analyzes multi\-variable determinants influencing user satisfaction with the Kendedes Mobile application at the Central Statistics Agency of Sidoarjo Regency using multiple linear regression modeling\. Results Multiple linear regression modeling reveals that work efficiency \(X4, coefficient 0\.559\) and system quality \(X2, coefficient 0\.419\) exhibit strong positive correlations with user satisfaction \(Y\), whereas ease of use, information quality, and individual user satisfaction variables show negative regression coefficients\. Novelty Empirical modeling identifies work efficiency as the primary positive driver governing field enumerator satisfaction in digital statistical mapping systems\. Implications Institutional IT managers must prioritize optimizing application responsiveness and operational workflow efficiency to improve user satisfaction and data collection productivity\.
