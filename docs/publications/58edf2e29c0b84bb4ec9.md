# MassNet: billion\-scale AI\-ready mass spectral corpus empowering scalable deep learning in proteomics

**Autores:** Jun A; Xiang Zhang; Xiaofan Zhang; Jiaqi Wei; Te Zhang; Yamin Deng; Pu Liu; Zongxiang Nie; Yiwen Yu; Yi Chen; Nanqing Dong; Zhiqiang Gao; Siqi Sun; Tiannan Guo

**Data:** 2026-10-14 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Here we present MassNet, inspired by large\-scale data corpora of text and images; MassNet is an artificial intelligence \(AI\)\-ready data corpus for mass spectrometry \(MS\)\-based proteomics\. The corpus contains 1\.5 billion tandem MS spectra from high\-quality data\-dependent acquisition \(DDA\) MS data across 35 species\. Approximately 1\.7 million peptide precursors are from human samples\. We further present an AI\-ready DDA data format called Mass Spectrometry DDA Tensor \(MSDT\) for compact and standardized storage, curation and reuse of data from multiple sources, which can be optionally converted to a Lightning Memory\-Mapped Database for accelerated input/output in deep learning\. On the basis of MassNet, we develop XuanjiNovo, a nonautoregressive transformer model that leverages a curriculum learning strategy for de novo peptide sequencing\. XuanjiNovo achieves 38\.35–143\.57% improvement over state\-of\-the\-art methods on human Astral data\. MassNet also supports additional downstream tasks, including retention time prediction and PSM rescoring\. MassNet represents a foundational MS\-based proteomics data resource, marking a critical milestone for AI proteomics research\. This Article presents MassNet, a large\-scale AI\-ready corpus of 1\.5 billion MS/MS spectra spanning 35 species, paired with a standardized storage format called Mass Spectrometry DDA Tensor \(MSDT\)\. Built on this resource, XuanjiNovo is a nonautoregressive transformer for de novo peptide sequencing that substantially outperforms previous methods\.
