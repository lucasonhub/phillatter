# Johnson, Jamison: Toward a Theory of Coherent Existence \- Journal 3 \- Frequency, Resonance, and the Architecture of Reality

**Autores:** 

**Data de descoberta:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** unknown

## Descrição do feed

This companion paper develops the physical and mathematical branch of Toward a Theory of Coherent Existence\. Offered as speculative philosophy of science, it proposes that frequency and resonance may be read as a single organizing grammar running through physical, chemical, biological, cosmological, and cognitive description, and that this grammar is the measurable face of what the framework calls the Unified Field\. The same mathematics recurs across these domains: Fourier decomposition, eigenvalue problems, symmetry and conservation, and the dynamics of coupled oscillators\. The framework’s coherence functional is identified formally as an intensity\-weighted form of the Kuramoto order parameter, the weighting being the framework’s own; the form reduces to Kuramoto’s average when every intensity is equal\. The recurrence is treated as suggestive only\. It is given its strongest form in the universality of critical phenomena, and the extension to consciousness is marked as a proposal that this result does not cover\. The claim that a person’s rhythms can be moved by practice is examined against neurofeedback, the meditation EEG literature, and breath\-driven entrainment, and stated at the size the evidence allows\. The journal also supplies the first premise of the series’ argument: that the sciences, at every scale surveyed, describe the world by its structure and leave its intrinsic nature open, which is where the case for one consciousness begins\. Two further parallels are recorded at their proper size: the argument that an entangled cosmos is a whole prior to its parts, and the block reading of relativistic time\. Every claim is held to the evidentiary standard proper to its domain, most stringently in the contested territory of consciousness and noetic studies\. \(direct link \)
