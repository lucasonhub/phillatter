# Design and Implementation of an Integrated Cross\-Platform Privacy Management Architecture Using a Rule\-Based Privacy Enforcement Algorithm \(RBPEA\) for Cross\-Platform Social Media Systems

**Autores:** Sabir Umar Danillela

**Data:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

The widespread use of multiple social media platforms has increased user exposure to privacy risks due to fragmented and complex privacy controls\. Users are required to configure separate privacy settings across different platforms, often leading to inconsistent configurations, privacy fatigue, and unintended data disclosure\. Existing privacy mechanisms remain largely platform\-specific and do not provide unified or user\-centred control across platforms\. This study aims to design, implement, and evaluate an integrated architecture for protecting user privacy across social media platforms\. The proposed system employs a Rule\-Based Privacy Enforcement Algorithm \(RBPEA\) to translate user\-defined privacy preferences into platform\-specific enforcement actions\. Using the Design Science Research methodology, a modular, web\-based prototype was developed, incorporating secure user authentication, centralized privacy rule management, and cross\-platform enforcement through platform adapters\. The system was evaluated based on enforcement consistency, usability, and system performance\. Results indicate improved consistency of privacy settings across platforms, reduced user effort in privacy configuration, and enhanced transparency through audit and feedback mechanisms\. The findings demonstrate that algorithm\-driven, integrated privacy management is both feasible and effective for improving user privacy control in multiplatform environments\. This study contributes an empirically evaluated architectural model and algorithmic approach for cross\-platform privacy protection, addressing a significant gap in existing privacy management solutions\.
