# Inteligencia artificial, gestión aduanera y competitividad del comercio exterior mexicano \(2019\-2026\)

**Autores:** Rubén Rodríguez Carrizal

**Data:** 2026-10-14 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Mexican customs services currently face persistent challenges in terms of time and efficiency: the Time Release Study conducted by Mexico&\#x27;s Tax Administration Service \(2019\) documented that, when goods are subject to physical\-documentary customs inspection, clearance time multiplies up to 14\-fold compared to an operation without inspection, reaching up to 218 hours at the port of Manzanillo\. This article aims to analyze, through a theoretical and documentary review, the role that artificial intelligence \(AI\) plays in the modernization of customs management and its potential impact on the competitiveness of Mexican foreign trade, given that such trade amounted to more than 369 billion dollars in exports during the first half of 2025\. The study draws on administrative modernization theory applied to customs management, which explains how the adoption of information technologies affects inspection, tax collection, and trade facilitation\. Methodologically, it is conducted through a qualitative, documentary, and descriptive study that draws on international organization reports, peer\-reviewed academic literature on the Mexican case, and official data from the Tax Administration Service\. Findings suggest that Mexico has a solid track record of customs technology modernization, but no public measurement currently isolates the specific effect of artificial intelligence on clearance times, which constitutes both a research gap and an opportunity to strengthen national customs competitiveness, as well as evidence of the need to create regulations to govern the use of AI in customs processes\.
