# Applications of Quadruple Neutrosophic Soft Topological Spaces in Multi\-Criteria Decision Making and Artificial Intelligence

**Autores:** Mohammad Ali Khalid; Dadas Dadas

**Data:** 2026-10-12 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

AbstractThis paper introduces a novel framework based on quadruple neutrosophic soft topological spaces\(NSQTS\) for multi\-criteria decision\-making \(MCDM\) and artificial intelligence applications\. Unlikeclassical fuzzy and intuitionistic models, the proposed structure integrates four parallelneutrosophic soft topologies representing expert opinion, historical data, risk assessment, anduncertainty tolerance\. Extended numerical examples and practical applications demonstrate themodel’s ability to handle contradictory, incomplete, and indeterminate information, providing moreaccurate and reliable decision\-making outcomes\. Keywords: Neutrosophy; Soft Set; Neutrosophic Soft Topology; Multi\-Criteria Decision Making;Artificial Intelligence; Expert Systems\.
