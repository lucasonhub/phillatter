# Inclusione e Accessibilità

**Autores:** a cura di Gabriella Baptist, Arianna Careddu, Francesca Ervas, Lucienne Huby, Massimiliano Mameli, Donatella Petretto

**Data:** 2026-10-12 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Inclusion and accessibility are now central terms in public, academic, and institutional discourse\. But what does it mean, in practical terms, to create truly inclusive and accessible environments? What conditions make participation possible, and what barriers—visible or otherwise—continue to limit it? Inclusion and Accessibility\. Challenges and Perspectives brings together interdisciplinary contributions that address these questions from various fields of research\. Through philosophical, communicative, psychological, and pedagogical perspectives, the volume explores the multiple dimensions through which inclusion and accessibility take shape in individual and collective life: from communication to empathy, from social participation to educational processes, and on to the opportunities and new challenges introduced by digital environments\. The contributions demonstrate how accessibility concerns not only the removal of barriers but also the ways in which we design spaces and practices, build relationships, produce meaning, and define the conditions for participation\. Inclusion thus emerges as a dynamic and shared process that involves individuals, communities, and institutions and requires us to continually question practices, languages, and environments\.
