# A lean statistical cell\-to\-pack framework for sustainable battery pack capacity estimation

**Autores:** Chen Ma; Qinghao Zhang; Bin Duan; Chenghui Zhang; Changlong Li; Long Chang; Benben Jiang

**Data:** 2026-10-17 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Accurate battery pack capacity estimation is vital for electric vehicle and energy storage safety, but conventional pack\-level aging tests are costly, energy intensive, and wasteful\. This study proposes a lean statistical cell\-to\-pack \(LS\-CTP\) framework that eliminates pack\-level tests\. Using aging data from only a few independent test cells, it combines lightweight machine learning and decision algorithms to achieve multi\-feature statistical transformation and fusion from routine fragmented charge data\. It requires few hyperparameters and low computational overhead and applies to parallel, series, and mixed configurations\. Monte Carlo analyses show a mean absolute percentage error below 1\.24% for parallel packs and 1\.92% for series packs using as few as two test cells\. A sustainability assessment indicates over 90% reduction in battery waste and testing energy\. By decoupling algorithm development from pack\-level cycling, LS\-CTP advances cost\-effective, energy\-efficient, and sustainable battery health diagnostics, supporting the clean energy transition\.
