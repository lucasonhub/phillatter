# Trepp, Tenzin C\.: Everyday Reality Under Construction: Neuroconstructivism and the Hidden Labor of Everyday Experience

**Autores:** 

**Data de descoberta:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** unknown

## Descrição do feed

Ordinary experience appears effortless, immediate, and self\-evident\. This paper argues that this appearance conceals continuous cognitive work\. Drawing on neuroconstructivism, predictive processing, and cognitive constructivism, it proposes that mundane situations depend on the ongoing construction, updating, synchronization, and maintenance of a multi\-level world\-model\. The claim is not that mind\-independent reality is cognitively constructed; rather, organisms construct usable models of that reality under persistent bodily, environmental, and social constraints\. Analyses of a familiar café, street crossing, and casual conversation show how perceptual stabilization, action control, memory, affect, narrative, and social expectations jointly sustain normal experience\. The resulting realistic constructivism treats experienced stability, meaning, and epistemic objectivity as achievements of constrained model\-building\. Ordinary cognition is therefore better understood not as intermittent processing but as sustained world\-maintenance\. \(direct link \)
