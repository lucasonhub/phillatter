# CONTINUOUS MULTIDISCIPLINARY HEALTH MONITORING OF HIGH\-RISK CHILDREN IN A REGIONAL FOLLOW\-UP CENTER

**Autores:** Olena Vasylivna Vlasova; S\.V\. Kapitula; M\.P\. Perepichka; H\.S\. Horokhova; K\.I\. Zhar

**Data:** 2026-10-14 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Despite significant advances in modern neonatology in the survival and care of infants with critical conditions and extremely low birth weight, the number of children at high risk of developing long\-term neurological, cognitive, and behavioral impairments continues to increase\. Under these circumstances, the development of an effective system of continuous follow\-up care has become particularly relevant\. Such a system should be based on a multidisciplinary approach, standardized assessment tools, and the early implementation of personalized rehabilitation interventions\. Objective\. To improve the effectiveness of follow\-up care for children during the first four years of life who belong to high perinatal risk groups through the development and implementation of a clinical patient pathway in a follow\-up clinic\. Materials and methods\. The performance of the follow\-up clinic of the Chernivtsi Regional Children&\#x27;s Clinical Hospital was analyzed during 2025 and the beginning of 2026\. The study included 202 children at high perinatal risk\. The multidisciplinary assessment was performed using standardized international tools, including WHO growth charts, the Hammersmith Infant Neurological Examination \(HINE\), Alberta Infant Motor Scale \(AIMS\), and Ages and Stages Questionnaire \(ASQ\-3\)\. Demographic characteristics, nosological structure, gender\-related features, and the effectiveness of the implemented clinical pathway were evaluated\. Results\. During the first 11 months of operation, 155 children were enrolled, and an additional 47 patients were registered during the first quarter of 2026, resulting in an increase in the average monthly admission rate from 14\.1 to 15\.7 patients\. Boys predominated \(55\.9%\)\. The most common conditions were hypoxic\-ischemic encephalopathy, feeding disorders, and neonatal sepsis\. The prevalence of hypoxic\-ischemic encephalopathy increased from 30\.3% in 2025 to 46\.8% in the first quarter of 2026 \(AR – 17\.41%; RR – 1\.4; 95% CI: 0\.9\-2\.0; OR – 2\.0; 95% CI: 1\.1\-3\.6\)\. Severe congenital malformations were first identified in 12\.8% of children during the first quarter of 2026\. Several gender\-related trends in the distribution of pathological conditions were observed and require further investigation\. The implemented clinical pathway ensured standardized assessment, early detection of developmental disorders, and optimization of multidisciplinary follow\-up care\. Conclusions\. The establishment of a clinical follow\-up center with an implemented standardized patient pathway and digital technologies provides effective monitoring of children at high perinatal risk, facilitates early identification of pathological conditions, and creates prerequisites for personalized rehabilitation and long\-term medical support programs\.
