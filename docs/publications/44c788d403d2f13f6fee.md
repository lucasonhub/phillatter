# Metacognitive training and AI\-based Socratic scaffolding in pre\-service teachers’ reflective practice: a dual\-study experimental design

**Autores:** Wen Li Zhang; Sun Sijie; Lifan Hu; Li Liu; Wenwuyu Gao; Shuangqi Li

**Data:** 2026-10-13 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

This study examined whether metacognitive strategy training strengthens the contribution of an AI\-based Socratic reflection system to pre\-service teachers’ reflective practice\. Study 1 used a 2 \(metacognitive training: present vs\. absent\) × 2 \(tool condition: AI\-based Socratic scaffolding vs\. traditional digital tools\) randomized between\-subjects design \( N = 60; n = 15 per cell\)\. Written reflection quality at post\-test was analyzed by ANCOVA with pre\-test performance as the covariate\. The metacognitive\-training main effect was significant, F \(1, 55\) = 33\.89, p &lt; 0\.001, η 2 p = 0\.381, as was the tool\-condition main effect, F \(1, 55\) = 9\.63, p = 0\.003, η 2 p = 0\.149\. More importantly, the metacognition × tool interaction was significant, F \(1, 55\) = 10\.91, p = 0\.002, η 2 p = 0\.165\. Under metacognitive training, AI\-based Socratic scaffolding produced a significant model\-estimated advantage over traditional digital tools \(Δ M = 0\.56, p &lt; 0\.001\), whereas the tool difference was not significant without metacognitive training \(Δ M = −0\.03, p = 0\.821\)\. Study 2 examined dialogue logs from the two AI conditions\. Participants who received metacognitive training generated a substantially larger proportion of higher\-order prompts than participants who received placebo training \(44\.1% vs\. 12\.2%\), t\(28\) = 7\.14, p &lt; 0\.001, d = 2\.61\. These process data are consistent with the proposed account that metacognitive preparation changes how learners engage with AI, although they do not constitute a formal mediation test\. The findings suggest that, within this configured Socratic AI environment, metacognitive preparation is an important condition for realizing higher\-quality reflective practice\.
