# SUZISH MASHG‘ULOTLARIDA QATNASHUVCHI BOLALARDA DIQQAT VA ISHCHI XOTIRANING KLINIK\-PSIXOLOGIK XUSUSIYATLARI

**Autores:** Ostanaqulov Alijon Dadajon o&\#x27;g&\#x27;li

**Data:** 2026-10-14 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

This article theoretically examines the clinical and psychological characteristics of attention and working memory in children participating in swimming training\. It discusses the development of cognitive processes during childhood, including sustained attention, concentration, attentional shifting, divided attention, and the mechanisms through which working memory temporarily maintains, processes, and uses information during ongoing activity\. Particular emphasis is placed on the sensorimotor demands of swimming, remembering movement sequences, understanding coaches’ instructions, and coordinating breathing with motor actions\. The potential relationships between cognitive performance and individual developmental characteristics, emotional states, physical fatigue, and training experience are also considered\. Neuropsychological, clinical\-psychological, and psychodiagnostic approaches are discussed as possible methodological foundations for assessing attention and working memory in young swimmers\. Two analytical tables present assessment criteria and a proposed framework for empirical data analysis\. The article emphasizes that the relationship between swimming participation and cognitive development should be examined through appropriately designed research and that causal conclusions require sufficient empirical evidence\.
