# Optimal Online Regret for Context\-Lumpable Bandits

**Autores:** Chenyu Lu; Zihan Zhang

**Data:** 2026-10-13 · **Tipo:** preprint

## Resumo

We study stochastic contextual bandits with an unknown context\-lumpable structure\. The $S$ contexts are partitioned into $r$ blocks, and every arm has the same mean reward across contexts in the same block\. Under arbitrary context distributions and without separation assumptions, we design an algorithm with expected regret $\\widetilde\{O\}\(\\sqrt\{r\(S\+K\)T\}\)$, matching the lower bound of Lee et al\. \(2023\) up to logarithmic factors and improving their previous upper bound by a factor of $r$\. The algorithm operates over dyadic accuracy levels, using a baseline policy and confidence\-based early stopping to control exploration\. It then performs cross\-context elimination: since rewards are identical within a block, one selected arm eliminates all surviving records from that block, requiring at most $r$ selections at each level\. Multi\-scale exploration assigns finer accuracies to frequent blocks and coarser accuracies to rare blocks, yielding a near\-optimal arm for every block while keeping the weighted policy error small\.
