# Isolation, Phenotypic Screening, and Extracellular Serotonin\-Like Immunoreactivity of Presumptive Lactic Acid Bacteria from Butterfly Pea Flower \(Clitoria ternatea L\.\) Kombucha

**Autores:** Rafeyfa Alya Mukhbita; Desi Purwaningsih; Isniani Ramdhani; Dara Juliana

**Data:** 2026-10-17 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Background: Major depressive disorder is a leading cause of disability, and interest in the microbiota\-gut\-brain axis has stimulated bioprospecting of food\-derived lactic acid bacteria \(LAB\) for neuroactive metabolite production\. Objective: To isolate and phenotypically screen acidogenic bacteria from butterfly pea flower \(Clitoria ternatea L\.\) kombucha and to evaluate their cell\-free supernatants for extracellular serotonin\-like immunoreactivity\. Methods: Colonies were isolated from day\-14 kombucha on MRS agar supplemented with 1\.0% CaCO3 and characterised by Gram staining, catalase testing and broth acidification in MRS broth \(24 h, 37 °C\)\. Serotonin\-like immunoreactivity in cell\-free supernatants was quantified by a competitive enzyme\-linked immunosorbent assay \(ELISA\) calibrated with a four\-parameter logistic \(4\-PL\) model\. Results: Three isolates \(Isolates 3, 6 and 8\) were presumptive LAB \(Gram\-positive, catalase\-negative bacilli\), dissolved CaCO3 \(clear zones 14\.8–18\.4 mm\) and reduced broth pH from 7\.00 to 3\.94–4\.15\. The 4\-PL model returned R2 = 0\.9993 and back\-calculated the standards to within 0\.1% of nominal\. Cell\-free supernatants gave serotonin\-like immunoreactivity averaging 18\.11 ± 4\.54 ng/mL \(Isolate 3 highest, 23\.13 ng/mL\) versus 1\.28 ± 0\.67 ng/mL in sterile media \(exploratory Welch&\#x27;s t = 6\.34, df = 2\.09, p = 0\.022\)\. Conclusion: Presumptive lactic acid bacteria from butterfly pea flower kombucha yielded a measurable serotonin\-like immunoreactivity signal in culture broth\. These preliminary in vitro findings warrant 16S rRNA gene sequencing, chromatographic confirmation of analyte identity \(HPLC/LC\-MS\) and in vivo evaluation\.
