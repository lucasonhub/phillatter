# Current controversies and challenges in unresectable stage III NSCLC

**Autores:** Yuzhen Cui; Xueying Zhai; Xinrui Yu; Jiangao Ren; Zhixuan Xu; Xiangjiao Meng; Yu Chen

**Data:** 2026-10-12 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Unresectable stage III NSCLC is clinically heterogeneous and therapeutically challenging\. Although the PACIFIC regimen defines the current standard of care, more than half of patients fail to achieve long\-term control\. Concurrent immunotherapy with chemoradiotherapy has not proven superior to standard consolidation, while induction, surgical conversion, and de\-escalation strategies remain insufficiently validated\. This review critically appraises these controversies, molecular stratification, and the key questions future trials must address\.
