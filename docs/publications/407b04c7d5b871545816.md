# Comparative Evaluation of Conventional RCC and BIM\-Integrated Precast Construction Systems in India: A Cost, Time, Sustainability, and Stakeholder Perspective

**Autores:** 

**Data:** 2026-10-14 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

The construction industry in India is undergoing a significant transformation, with urbanisation, infrastructure development, and the increasing focus on sustainable construction practices shaping the landscape\.While the traditional Reinforced Cement Concrete \(RCC\) techniques still dominate the industry, the industry is seeking to adopt the Modern Methods of Construction \(MMC\) including precast concrete systems due to project delays, material wastage, labour dependency and environmental issues\.Meanwhile, Building Information Modelling \(BIM\) has risen as a virtual means of enhancing the planning, coordination, costing control and life\-cycle management of a project\.This paper provides a comparative analysis between traditional RCC and the BIM integrated precast building system with a controlled G\+4 residential building case study in the context of India\.The adopted approach was mixed methods research, which comprised BIM based quantity take\-off analysis, 4D schedule simulation, life\-cycle carbon assessment, and structured questionnaire survey \(n = 52\) and SmartPLS Structural Equation Modelling \(SEM\)\.The study shows that BIM facilitated precast construction shortened the project timelines by 189 days \(65\.8%\), thereby saving financial costs of ?48\.4 lakhs due to finances savings, avoiding RERA penalties and quicker revenue realisation from the project\.The material efficiency analysis showed that the use of concrete was reduced by 18\.2% and the use of steel was reduced by 13\.4%\.The results of the lifecycle assessment showed that although there were higher emissions at the production stage, the net carbon benefits were marginal\.The findings from SEM revealed that the use of BIM has a positive impact on the perceptions of stakeholders and intention to adopt BIM\.The study finds that the integration of BIM in precast construction has the potential to significant enhance productivity, sustainability, and project delivery performance in the Indian construction industry\.
