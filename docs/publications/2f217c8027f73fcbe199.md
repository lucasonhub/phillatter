# Data Science: from stochastics to machine learning with Python

**Autores:** Konrad Voelkel

**Data:** 2026-10-18 · **Tipo:** book

## Resumo

An undergraduate introduction to data science that begins with stochastics and ends with machine learning without leaving a gap in between\. The estimators and learning algorithms — k\-means, principal component analysis, regression, expectation maximization — are reimplemented rather than called\. Thirty chapters covering probability spaces, random variables and distributions; expected value, variance and the central limit theorem; vectorised computation with Numpy and Scipy; exploratory data analysis with Pandas and Matplotlib; multivariate statistics, covariance and relative entropy; statistical models, maximum likelihood, tests and model selection; and data protection under the GDPR\. The English edition of the lecture notes used in the computer science bachelor&\#x27;s programme at Heinrich Heine University Düsseldorf\. Published as a website and as a 318\-page PDF; sources, including every notebook, at github\.com/konradvoelkel/ds\-en\.
