# Microglial dynamics associate with the spatial pattern of tau deposition in early Alzheimer’s disease

**Autores:** Marco Öchsner; Matthias Brendel; Nicolai Franzmeier; Lena Trappmann; Mirlind Zaganjori; Ersin Ersoezlue; Carolin Kurz; Jan Haeckert; Julia Utecht; Philine Marie Schirge; Boris Papazov; Oliver Pogarell; Daniel Janowitz; Katharina Büerger; Michael Ewers; Carla Palleis; Endy Weidinger; Gloria Biechele; Sebastian Schuster; Anika Finze; Florian Eckenweber; Rainer Rupprecht; Axel Rominger; Oliver Goldhardt; Timo Grimmer; Daniel Keeser; Sophia Stoecklein; Olaf Dietrich; Peter Bartenstein; Johannes Levin; Günter Höglinger; Robert Perneczky; Boris‐Stephan Rauchmann

**Data:** 2026-10-12 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

BACKGROUND: Neuroinflammation is implicated in Alzheimer&\#x27;s disease \(AD\) pathogenesis, yet how changes in TSPO PET signal are associated with tau deposition, and how brain network architecture shapes this relationship remain poorly understood\. METHODS: We conducted a prospective, multimodal neuroimaging study in 56 participants: 35 amyloid\-positive individuals across the AD spectrum \(ADS\) and 21 amyloid\-negative healthy controls \(HC\)\. Amyloid status was classified primarily by CSF Aβ42/Aβ40 and, when eligible CSF was unavailable, by baseline amyloid PET\. Participants underwent TSPO PET using \[18 F\]GE\-180 and resting\-state fMRI at baseline\. A longitudinal subgroup additionally underwent follow\-up tau PET using \[18 F\]PI\-2620 \(n = 24\) and repeat TSPO PET \(n = 18\) approximately 18 months later\. RESULTS: Baseline TSPO did not differ significantly between groups\. Baseline TSPO was associated with regional tau in both groups; the reciprocal association between tau and TSPO change was numerically weaker in AD, but this group difference was not statistically significant\. Connectivity to baseline\-TSPO epicenters was associated with higher tau in both groups, whereas connectivity to TSPO\-change epicenters was associated with lower tau in AD but not controls \(group\-by\-connectivity interaction p &lt; 0\.001, participant\-level slope difference p = 0\.012\), a dissociation replicated in independent participants\. CONCLUSION: These findings suggest that connectivity to established versus emerging TSPO epicenters carries distinct spatial information about tau in the AD spectrum, a pattern shaped by functional brain connectivity and absent in healthy controls\. \(ClinicalTrials\.gov: NCT06224920\)\.
