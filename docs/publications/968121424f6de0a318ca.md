# Adaptive Education Platforms for Development of Algorithmic Thinking in Corporate Environment: Criteria for Selection of Experimental Environment

**Autores:** Tsvetelina Rashkova; Gencho Stoitsov

**Data:** 2026-10-18 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

This article develops a methodological framework for screening and subsequently verifying adaptive learning platforms intended to support algorithmic and critical thinking among adults in corporate settings\. It combines a conceptual analysis of selected literature, document analysis, and multi\-criteria modelling\. Six criteria are operationalized through five\-level descriptors, provisional normative weights, and non\-compensatory thresholds\. Documentation for five platforms is examined to distinguish vendor claims from demonstrated functionality and secured access rights\. A separate numerical example using five hypothetical profiles illustrates weighted aggregation and sensitivity to weights and input assumptions; it is not an empirical ranking of the products\. A schematic corporate case provides separate indicators for algorithmic thinking, critical thinking, and transfer\. The main contribution is a two\-stage procedure linking documentary screening to technical and expert verification\. The framework requires validation\. No intervention study is reported, and no platform\-specific educational effect is established\.
