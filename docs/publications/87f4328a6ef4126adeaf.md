# Closed Source: The God that Failed

**Autores:** Matheus Hoffmann Fernandes Santos

**Data:** 2026-10-13 · **Tipo:** book

## Resumo

A position paper arguing that closed source has failed as a protection, for science first\. Large language models now decompile binaries into re\-executable source; black\-box queries extract parts of production models; distillation copies capabilities at industrial scale; and the largest 2026 trade\-secret dispute in artificial intelligence, Apple&\#x27;s suit against OpenAI, turns on people, laptops and recruiting rather than on code that stayed secret\. Against this background the paper revisits Linus Torvalds&\#x27; Linux and Git as evidence that openness scales, and the author&\#x27;s closed undergraduate thesis as evidence of what secrecy costs a researcher\. It argues that the ethical answer to universal reverse engineering is professional citation, proposes three rules for AI agents \(borrow, cite, propagate\), and describes Paper Fabric, the open pipeline in the Ouroboric style that produced it\. v0 is a preprint and has not been peer reviewed\.
