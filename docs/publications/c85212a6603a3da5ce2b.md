# Technology Integration as A Predictor of Utilization of Computer\-Aided Mathematics Tools in Teaching

**Autores:** Neil Raymond K\. Factor; Lynette P\. Rue

**Data:** 2026-10-17 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

This study examined Technology Integration as a Predictor of Utilization of Computer\-Aided Mathematics Tools in Teaching among mathematics teachers in the Schools Division Office of Valenzuela City\. Specifically, it determined the overall level of ICT Integration based on the SAMR Model; assessed the Extent of utilization of computer\-aided mathematics tools in terms of visualization and exploration, computation and problem\-solving, and practice and assessment; determined the significant relationship between ICT Integration and the utilization of computer\-aided mathematics tools; examined whether ICT Integration significantly predicted the identified dimensions of utilization; and proposed a Capacity Enhancement Plan based on the findings\. The findings showed that the respondents demonstrated a high overall level of ICT Integration, while the utilization of computer\-aided mathematics tools was moderate\. Among the three dimensions, visualization and exploration was the most utilized, followed by computation and problem\-solving, while practice and assessment was the least utilized\. The findings further showed that ICT Integration had significant positive relationships with all three dimensions of computer\-aided mathematics tool utilization and significantly predicted each dimension\. These findings indicated that stronger ICT Integration was associated with greater utilization of computer\-aided mathematics tools in mathematics instruction\. The study concluded that strengthening teachers’ ICT Integration competencies could support more purposeful and effective use of computer\-aided mathematics tools\. Accordingly, a Capacity Enhancement Plan was proposed to enhance teachers’ ICT competencies and promote more purposeful integration and utilization of computer\-aided mathematics tools in mathematics instruction\.
