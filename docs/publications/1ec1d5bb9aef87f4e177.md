# Johnson, Jamison: On Witnessing \- On Recording, and Being the One Who Records

**Autores:** 

**Data de descoberta:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** unknown

## Descrição do feed

Have you ever worked at a complicated problem, only to have the answer arrive after you stopped working on it? This lyric\-philosophical essay takes Andrew Wiles&\#x27;s proof of Fermat&\#x27;s Last Theorem as its model of a truth met sideways: through Frey&\#x27;s curve, Ribet&\#x27;s theorem and the modularity of semistable elliptic curves, with the gap Katz found, the repair with Taylor and the community that checked it\. It reads witnessing as both recording and undergoing, with Levi&\#x27;s drowned as the case where the two come apart\. Against the combination problem for panpsychism, it borrows Zurek&\#x27;s quantum Darwinism, in which objectivity is redundant recording in the environment, and adds the author&\#x27;s own posit, marked as such: to be a unified witness is to be redundantly written\. The posit proves nothing\. It yields a question, whether the writing thins where the self comes apart, which the essay places beside split\-brain and depersonalization research\. The essay closes on the posture of leaving the margin showing\. \(direct link \)
