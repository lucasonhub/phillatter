# Research on Enterprise Financial Risk Intelligent Early\-Warning Model Based on XGBoost

**Autores:** Enxi Zhou

**Data:** 2026-10-13 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Against the background that the demand for risk prevention and control in the capital market is increasingly prominent, financial crises of listed companies occur frequently\. Traditional linear early warning models find it difficult to depict the nonlinear associations of financial indicators, and also face practical problems such as sample imbalance and model black box\. This paper selects A\-share non\-financial listed companies, constructs a multi\-dimensional financial early warning indicator system, uses period t indicators to predict period t\+1 financial risk, constructs a financial risk early warning model based on XGBoost, introduces SHAP to realize the interpretable analysis of the model, and conducts comparative empirical research with logistic regression and random forest\. The results show that the XGBoost model has better overall accuracy and early warning precision, and profitability, cash flow, and solvency indicators are the core factors for identifying financial risk; SHAP can effectively solve the model black box problem, and realize global risk factor identification and single\-enterprise risk tracing\. The research can provide technical support and decision\-making reference for enterprise management, investors, creditors, and regulatory agencies to carry out financial risk identification and risk control\.
