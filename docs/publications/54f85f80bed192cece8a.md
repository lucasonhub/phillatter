# Prenatal Diagnosis of Pulmonary Arteriovenous Malformation With 3D / 4D STIC Fetal Echocardiography

**Autores:** Amit Kumar Pandey; Balu Vaidyanathan

**Data:** 2026-10-14 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Pulmonary arteriovenous malformation \(PAVM\) is a rare congenital vascular anomaly characterized by direct communication between the pulmonary artery and vein, resulting in a right\-to\-left shunt\. Prenatal diagnosis remains uncommon\. We report a fetal PAVM diagnosed at 21 weeks&\#x27; gestation on fetal echocardiography\. Color Doppler with graphic flow imaging demonstrated turbulent swirling flow within the right pulmonary vein, while spectral Doppler showed an arterialized pulmonary venous waveform\. High\-definition color Doppler and 3D/4D spatiotemporal image correlation \(STIC\) rendering confirmed a fistulous communication between the right pulmonary artery and vein\. Prenatal recognition of PAVM is essential for serial surveillance, parental counseling, multidisciplinary perinatal planning, and early neonatal intervention\.
