# When Machines Apply for Your Job: Human Employment and Displacement Under Productive AI and AGI

**Autores:** Pascal Stiefenhofer

**Data:** 2026-10-10 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Productive artificial intelligence \(AI\) creates the possibility that an accumulable capital stock supplies services that can substitute for human labour in production\. This paper develops a recursive search\-and\-matching general\-equilibrium model in which human labour, AI\-generated productive services, job vacancies, matching, and investment in productive AI capital are jointly determined\. Artificial General Intelligence \(AGI\) is treated as a limiting high\-capability case in which AI systems can perform a sufficiently broad range of economically valuable tasks\. Three results follow\. First, the model characterises the surplus generated by a match between a worker and a firm and shows how productive AI capital affects this surplus through productivity, job\-finding probabilities, outside options, and continuation values\. Second, it identifies a threshold at which the surplus from human employment falls to zero and a distinct threshold at which firms stop posting job vacancies for human workers\. Firms can stop recruiting human workers while an employment match would still generate positive surplus\. Third, a Production–Distribution Separation result gives transparent sufficient institutional conditions under which changes in the ownership distribution of productive AI capital leave the productive\-equilibrium correspondence unchanged while altering the distribution of AI\-generated income\. A complementary local Jacobian characterisation shows that, on a regular equilibrium branch, ownership neutrality holds if and only if ownership has zero net effect on the productive residual\. The model thus separates technological displacement, labour\-market adjustment, and the distribution of AI income\. Within this framework, representing productive AI solely as a productivity shock would omit the service\-supply channel through which AI capital creates productive services that can substitute for human labour\.
