# A Novel Indicator of Cytomegalovirus Infection After Allogeneic Hematopoietic Cell Transplantation: Current CMV Reactivation

**Autores:** Shunto Kawamura; Hideki Nakasone; Yuki Honjo; Junko Takeshita; Yukiko Misaki; Kosuke Takano; Kazuki Yoshimura; Koki Hashino; Kaisuke Yamamoto; Yuya Nakata; Takuto Ishikawa; Akari Matsuoka; Tomohiro Meno; Yuhei Nakamura; Masakatsu Kawamura; Nozomu Yoshino; Ayumi Gomyo; Masaharu Tamaki; Machiko Kusuda; Shun‐ichi Kimura; Shinichi Kako; Yoshinobu Kanda

**Data:** 2026-10-12 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

BACKGROUND: Cytomegalovirus reactivation \(CMVR\) after allogeneic hematopoietic cell transplantation \(allo\-HCT\) is a reversible event, and conventional indicators such as the simple cumulative incidence cannot reflect transitional CMVR states\. We propose a novel indicator, current CMVR \(cCMVR\), which visualizes both the occurrence and remission of CMVR over time\. METHODS: To evaluate the utility of cCMVR, we analyzed 389 CMV seropositive patients who had received their first allo\-HCT\. cCMVR was defined as the probability of the state where anti\-CMV drugs were administered for CMVR or CMV disease without relapse or death at any time\. RESULTS: We compared two groups with or without letermovir prophylaxis \(LTV and no\-LTV groups\)\. The 1\-, 2\-, 3\-, and 6\-month cCMVR values were 0\.0%, 0\.0%, 2\.9%, and 2\.9% in the LTV group, and 7\.1%, 31\.9%, 11\.0%, and 0\.8% in the no\-LTV group\. In the LTV group, cCMVR never exceeded 10%, while in the no\-LTV group, cCMVR peaked at approximately 2 months after allo\-HCT, and fewer than 5% of patients developed CMVR after 4 months post\-HCT\. Next, in the no\-LTV group, we assessed cCMVR stratified according to the presence of risk factors for the simple cumulative incidence of CMVR, and this revealed that patients grafted from HLA\-mismatched related donors and those who received alemtuzumab for GVHD prophylaxis appeared to experience repeated episodes of CMVR, whereas the early difference in cCMVR according to pretransplant CMV\-IgG titer diminished over time\. CONCLUSION: cCMVR could be a valuable indicator for visualizing recurrent CMV infection and illustrating differences in the time course of CMVR\.
