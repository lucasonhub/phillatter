# Didehvar, Farzad: A Classification of What AI Does \(III\): Second\-, Third\-, and Fourth\-Level Activities

**Autores:** 

**Data de descoberta:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** unknown

## Descrição do feed

In \[1\], the author presents a classification of what artificial intelligence \(AI\) does and what AI does with texts\. In \[2\], an attempt is made to extend and generalize this classification, accompanied by further discussion and analysis by AI\. In the present article, I propose that the activities discussed in \[1\] and \[2\] can be understood as belonging primarily to the first level of AI activity\. I then introduce three additional levels, each representing a more complex form of activity\. The purpose is to extend the classification from AI&\#x27;s direct applications to its capacity to evaluate activities, exhibit apparent intentions, and develop representations of itself as a whole\. These levels are intended as a conceptual framework for classifying AI activities, rather than as claims that present\-day AI systems necessarily possess consciousness, independent desires, or a human\-like personality\. 1\.
