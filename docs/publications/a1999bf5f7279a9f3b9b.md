# A GC\-MS\-based untargeted metabolomics approach for the discrimination of Haymilk and Mountain Product labelled milk

**Autores:** Alexander E\. Lopez; V\.M\. Moretti; Matteo Greco; F\. Bellagamba

**Data:** 2026-10-09 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

The EU market offers a range of milk types, differentiated by origin, production method, processing technology and quality\. In this study, we investigated the distinction between Haymilk, Mountain Product\-labelled milk and milk from conventional production systems by low molecular weight metabolomics using gas chromatography\-mass spectrometry \(GC\-MS\)\. The metabolite profile of the different milk typologies was shaped both by the production specifications underlying each voluntary quality scheme, including feeding regulations for Haymilk and geographical origin restrictions for Mountain Product milk, and by the pasteurization treatment applied to raw milk prior to retail placement \(standard or high\-temperature pasteurization\)\. By coupling partial least squares discriminant analysis \(PLS\-DA\) with metabolic pathway analysis, several metabolic routes and metabolites were identified as significant in characterizing the different retail milk typologies, with myo\-inositol annotated as a putative biomarker for Haymilk\. Overall, these results indicate that GC\-MS\-based metabolomics represents a promising approach for authenticating voluntary milk quality labels at the retail level\.
