# Errantwill, Sonpallax: Consciousness as an Emergent Property of Specific Material Systems

**Autores:** 

**Data de descoberta:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** unknown

## Descrição do feed

Abstract This paper argues that the explanatory gap—the seeming chasm between physical motion and subjective experience—is not a fact to be bridged but a pseudo\-problem manufactured by a faulty conceptual incision\. Illusionism and panpsychism, despite their apparent opposition, share this same false premise: illusionists, facing the gap, declare experience a fiction; panpsychists, facing the same gap, declare experience ubiquitous\. Both are wrong\. The gap dissolves once we recognize that consciousness is not something added to physical motion, but the intrinsic face of a specific material system operating at a specific level of complexity—a holistic property irreducible to its parts, just as wetness is a holistic property of water molecules combined in a specific way\. This position, Substrate\-Specific Emergentism, refutes illusionism by affirming the reality of consciousness, and refutes panpsychism by anchoring consciousness in specific material substrates rather than in every particle\. The paper constructs the concept of reflexive awareness—the self\-manifesting dimension of sensation itself—to establish the genuine metaphysical status of the &quot;I&quot; within a physicalist framework without dualism\. Consciousness is real\. It is the living brain, shaped by billions of years of evolution, bearing witness to its own existence\.
