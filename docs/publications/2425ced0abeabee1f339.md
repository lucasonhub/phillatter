# Digital Biofeedback–Based Nursing Program for Improving Physiological Regulation, Anxiety, Sleep Quality, and SelfManagement among Patients with Heart Failure

**Autores:** Nevin Adel Amer; Hanan Mohamed Safar; Iman Abd\-Elmoaty Mohamed; Rania Reafaat Abdelkader Atia; Bahaa Eldin Abdellatif Ali Ali; Reham AbdElhamed AbdElmawla Elsaid; Samah Zidan; Manal Ahmed Mohamed Ismail; Eman Ali Metwaly Badr; Yasmin Mohamed Ali

**Data:** 2026-10-17 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Background: Heart failure is a complex chronic condition characterized by impaired cardiovascular function and persistent physiological stress, often accompanied by anxiety, poor sleep quality, and difficulties in maintaining effective self\-management\. Digital biofeedback may provide patients with real\-time physiological information that supports relaxation, symptom awareness, behavioral regulation, and active participation in disease management\. Aim: To evaluate the effect of a digital biofeedbackbased nursing program on physiological regulation, anxiety, sleep quality, and self\-management among patients with heart failure\. Subjects and Methods: A quasi\-experimental one\-group pretest–posttest design was used\. A purposive sample of 100 adult patients diagnosed with heart failure was recruited from the cardiac and medical outpatient clinics of Sohag University Hospitals\. Data were collected using a structured patient assessment questionnaire, a physiological regulation assessment, the Generalized Anxiety Disorder Scale \(GAD\-7\), the Pittsburgh Sleep Quality Index \(PSQI\), and a heart failure self\-management assessment scale\. The nursing program incorporated digital biofeedbackassisted breathing and relaxation exercises, physiological monitoring, symptom recognition, medication and dietary adherence, sleep\-management strategies, and individualized self\-management education\. The intervention was implemented through structured sessions with digital biofeedback Digital Biofeedback–Based Nursing Program for Improving Physiological Regulation, Anxiety, Sleep Quality, and Self\-Management among Patients with Heart Failure practice and follow\-up support\. Results: Following implementation of the program, patients demonstrated significant improvement in physiological regulation, with greater ability to recognize and control physiological responses\. Anxiety levels decreased significantly, while sleep quality improved\. A significant improvement was also observed in patients’ self\-management behaviors, including medication adherence, symptom monitoring, dietary and fluid management, physical activity, and appropriate responses to worsening symptoms\. Significant differences were observed between pre\- and post\-intervention measurements \(p &lt; 0\.001\)\. Conclusion: The Digital Biofeedback–Based Nursing Program was effective in improving physiological regulation and reducing anxiety while enhancing sleep quality and self\-management among patients with heart failure\. Recommendations: Digital biofeedback should be incorporated into nursing\-led heart failure management programs as a supportive strategy for physiological regulation, psychological well\-being, sleep improvement, and long\-term self\-management\.
