# Development and external validation of a nomogram for predicting in‐hospital mortality in status epilepticus patients in the intensive care unit: A MIMIC ‐ IV and eICU ‐ CRD study

**Autores:** Fangzhao Yin; Yudan Wang; Cui Deqiu

**Data:** 2026-10-13 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

OBJECTIVE: To develop and externally validate a nomogram for predicting in\-hospital mortality in ICU patients with SE, using readily available clinical data collected within the first 24 h of ICU admission\. METHODS: We identified adult SE patients from MIMIC\-IV \(training cohort, 2008\-2019\) using ICD\-9/ICD\-10 codes\. Twenty\-seven candidate predictors were screened by LASSO logistic regression with 10\-fold cross\-validation\. The final model was constructed with multivariable logistic regression and presented as a nomogram\. Internal validation used 1000 bootstrap resamples with optimism correction\. External validation was performed in an independent SE cohort from the eICU\-CRD database \(2014\-2015\)\. RESULTS: , white blood cell count, glucose, bicarbonate, and the Elixhauser comorbidity index\. The optimism\-corrected AUC was \.807 \(95% CI, \.754\-\.860\) with a Brier score of \.124\. External validation in 597 eICU patients yielded an AUC of \.750 \(95% CI, \.685\-\.815\)\. In sensitivity analyses, a reduced model without the comorbidity score achieved an optimism\-corrected AUC of \.786 and an external AUC of \.730, and a model without the ICU care\-unit type achieved \.817 and \.764, respectively\. SIGNIFICANCE: This nine\-variable nomogram provides a validated, bedside\-applicable tool for mortality risk stratification in ICU SE patients, enabling manual risk calculation without electronic computation\.
