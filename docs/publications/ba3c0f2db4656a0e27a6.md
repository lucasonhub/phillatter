# A Risk Based Digital Food Safety and HACCP Governance Framework for Large Scale Catering Operations in Saudi Arabia

**Autores:** Abdul Rauf

**Data:** 2026-10-13 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Large\-scale catering operations in Saudi Arabia are expanding while major catering, remote\-site catering, energy, logistics and industrial programmes increase the scale and complexity of occupational risk\. Conventional food safety management remains essential, yet lagging indicators, periodic inspections and rule\-based controls often identify deterioration only after exposure has accumulated\. This structured review develops an risk\-based digital food safety and HACCP governance framework suited to Saudi operating conditions\. It integrates leading indicators from records, inspections, sensors, equipment systems, food\-service workforce records, environmental monitoring and food\-safety incident narratives; applies transparent risk models; and routes predictions through human review and proportionate controls\. The synthesis shows that digital food\-safety analytics can improve prioritisation, early\-warning capability and learning across dispersed sites, but only when data quality, privacy, food\-service workforce participation, model drift, cybersecurity and accountability are governed explicitly\. The proposed framework therefore treats digital food\-safety analytics as a decision\-support layer within an ISO 22000\-aligned management system, not as an autonomous replacement for competent safety judgement\. Its principal contribution is a risk\-based operating model linking data governance, prediction, verification, intervention and continuous assurance to Saudi Vision 2030 objectives\.
