# P2\-YOLO12: Small Polyp Detection in Colonoscopy via Endoscopic Image Preprocessing and a High\-resolution Detection Head

**Autores:** Samsul Arifin; Nanik Suciati; Daffa Muhamad Azhar

**Data:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Accurate polyp detection during colonoscopy is crucial yet challenging because polyps are often small, lowcontrast, and irregularly shaped\.This study proposes P2\-YOLO12, a polyp\-detection framework that couples an integrated endoscopic image preprocessing pipeline with an architecture tailored to small objects\.The pipeline sequentially applies Zero\-Reference Deep Curve Estimation \(Zero\-DCE\) to correct low illumination, Specular\-Aware Inpainting \(SAI\) to remove endoscopic light reflections, and Discrete Wavelet Transform \(DWT\) to suppress noise while preserving polyp edges\.For detection, a P2 detection head was added to the standard YOLO12 heads \(P3, P4, P5\); its higher\-resolution feature map \(stride 4\) better captured the fine details and boundaries of small polyps\.The method was evaluated on a leakage\-controlled split of 3,837 in\-distribution images \(3,322 polyp\-containing and 515 polyp\-free frames; 3,681 polyp boxes\) drawn from Kvasir\-SEG, CVC\-ClinicDB, and PolypGen, grouped by sequence for CVC\-ClinicDB and PolypGen and split at the image level for Kvasir\-SEG, with the fully held\-out ETIS\-LaribPolypDB dataset \(196 images, 208 boxes\) reserved for out\-of\-distribution testing\.Across twenty\-eight scenario ablations over four YOLO generations, the proposed P2\-YOLO12 achieved a precision of 95\.80%, recall of 93\.15%, F1\-score of 94\.46%, mAP50 of 97\.25%, and mAP50\-95 of 71\.85%, outperforming the YOLO12 baseline \(mAP50 of 87\.76%\)\.Size\-stratified evaluation showed that the P2 head raised the small\-polyp average precision \(AP\_small\) from 35\.12% to 55\.40% relative to the baseline\.On the fully held\-out, out\-of\-distribution ETIS\-LaribPolypDB dataset, P2\-YOLO12 retained an mAP50 of 85\.25%, a 12\.3% relative drop from in\-distribution performance compared with a 22\.0% drop for the baseline, whereas AP\_small more than doubled from 18\.75% to 43\.75% relative to the baseline\.These results indicate that integrating image preprocessing with the P2 detection head improves detection sensitivity, localization accuracy, and generalization to an unseen dataset for small, low\-contrast polyps that are most prone to being missed in clinical colonoscopy\.
