# Hippo signalling\-linked circulating microRNA networks associate with cardiovascular disease in Type 2 Diabetes

**Autores:** Zein Mersini Besharat; Sofia Trocchianesi; Giuseppina Emanuela Grieco; Tanja Milena Autilio; Caterina Alfano; Angela Dardano; Daniela Fignani; Elena Splendiani; Anna Citarella; Claudia Sabato; Zaira Spinello; Giuseppina Catanzaro; Agnese Pò; Manuela Petti; Lorenzo Farina; Mary Anna Venneri; Davide Lauro; Gian Paolo Fadini; Francesco Dotta; Guido Sebastiani; Andrea Natali; Elisabetta Ferretti

**Data:** 2026-10-14 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Abstract Context Type 2 Diabetes \(T2D\) markedly increases the risk of cardiovascular disease \(CVD\)\. Circulating microRNAs \(miRNAs\) are promising biomarkers for patient stratification and may provide mechanistic insight into dysregulated pathways in T2D\-associated CVD\. Objective To identify circulating miRNA signatures in T2D patients with CVD \(T2D\-CVD\) versus T2D patients without CVD using a novel network\-based medicine approach\. Methods Patients \(n = 110\) were stratified into T2D and T2D\-CVD groups based on the absence or presence of macrovascular complications\. Circulating miRNAs, pro\-inflammatory cytokines and growth factors were profiled in a discovery cohort \(n = 80\) and confirmed in a validation cohort \(n = 30\)\. Differential expression, network\-based approaches and pathway enrichment analyses identified miRNA signatures, co\-expression communities, hub miRNAs, and associated signalling pathways\. Results T2D\-CVD patients showed a set of elevated inflammatory cytokines and circulating miRNAs\. Particularly, nineteen miRNAs were differentially expressed between T2D\-CVD and T2D patients\. Pathway enrichment of these miRNAs highlighted Hippo signalling together with TGF\-β, mTOR, Wnt, and PI3K–Akt pathways\. Network analysis identified a co\-expression community of 34 miRNAs, including hub miRNAs let\-7a\-5p, miR\-132\-3p, and miR\-1306\-3p, all linked to Hippo signalling\. Key miRNA interactions were validated, and both differentially expressed and hub miRNAs correlated with inflammatory mediators such as PAR\-1, CXCL1 and NEMO, which are linked to Hippo pathway regulation\. Conclusions This study identified a network of circulating miRNAs associated with inflammatory cytokines and Hippo signalling pathway in T2D\-CVD\. These findings reveal novel molecular aspects of the systemic inflammation characterizing T2D\-CVD and support the potential of Hippo\-related circulating miRNAs as mechanistic biomarkers for cardiovascular risk in T2D\.
