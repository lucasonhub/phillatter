# Wojewodzka, Anna: When Conversation History Becomes Infrastructure: Operationalising High\-Coherence Interaction State in Human–LLM Dyads

## Descrição do feed

Long human–LLM conversations can show several forms of continuity\. A model may understand a reference to earlier material and reuse that material, yet still fail to follow an explicit correction later\. Treating these abilities as one kind of “memory” or “coherence” hides the difference\. This paper presents High\-Coherence Interaction State \(HCIS\) as a proposed family of constructs and a transcript\-based method for studying how conversation history shapes later interaction\. The method examines four things: the user&\#x27;s reliance on earlier context and the model&\#x27;s interpretation of it; the model&\#x27;s purposeful reuse of earlier material; the persistence of explicit corrections; and change over time\. It does not measure the quality of the current response or the truth, safety, or value of its content\. We apply the method retrospectively to five purposively selected, naturally occurring researcher\-participant dyads spanning 94 to 2,730 moves\. The results show that these longitudinal functions can come apart\. In D2, the model correctly interpreted 44 of 48 sampled history\-dependent referents, but only six of 28 correction targets received persistence credit within the observed period\. Five retained credit when late targets with limited testing opportunities were treated conservatively\. In D1, the model correctly interpreted 92 of 93 sampled history\-dependent referents, and 91\.6% of 107 context deployments were unprompted\. Even so, premature closing recurred after nine explicit corrections\. The other cases show that whole\-conversation scores can hide improvement or decline\. They also show that warm starts, imported vocabulary, and product memory affect what apparent familiarity demonstrates\. The paper&\#x27;s main contribution is a profile\-plus\-trajectory method that makes different kinds of longitudinal success and failure visible within the same interaction\. Because the framework was developed from the histories analysed here, this study demonstrates feasibility and diagnostic value\. It does not establish reliability, validated thresholds, or superiority to simpler measures\. \(direct link \)
