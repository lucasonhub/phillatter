# Artificial Intelligence in L2 Listening Education: A Comparative\-Critical Review of Ethical Challenges and Pedagogical Opportunities

**Autores:** Latifah Alghamdi; Talal Musaed Alghizzi

**Data:** 2026-10-17 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

This article is a systematic review of previously published, peer\-reviewed studies; no participant\-level or confidential data were generated\. All materials underpinning the review are openly available in the Zenodo repository at https://doi\.org/10\.5281/zenodo\.23429519 under a Creative Commons Attribution 4\.0 International \(CC BY 4\.0\) licence\. The repository includes the complete search strategies, the screening and exclusion logs, the data\-extraction and coding materials, the coding frameworks, the Severity, Effectiveness, ESI, and RRW scoring rubrics, and the analytic audit\-trail documentation\. The full texts of the included studies are not redistributed because they are copyrighted publications; they are available from their original publishers\.
