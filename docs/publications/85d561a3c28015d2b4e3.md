# Algorithmic Guardrails for Echocardiography AI

**Autores:** Krish Kabra; Sadeer G Al\-Kindi; Guha Balakrishnan

**Data:** 2026-10-13 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

BACKGROUND: Automated echocardiography analysis using artificial intelligence \(AI\) currently lacks effective guardrails or oversight mechanisms to ensure its safe and reliable deployment\. OBJECTIVES: This study investigated whether deep feature representations can identify echocardiograms at risk of misinterpretation by echocardiography AI\-based left ventricular ejection fraction \(LVEF\) estimation models\. METHODS: We retrospectively validated 2 state\-of\-the\-art models for LVEF estimation, EchoNet \(single\-task model\) and PanEcho \(multitask foundation model\), on 4,010 apical\-4\-chamber videos from 1,353 studies \(n = 1,208\) with biplane LVEF measurements in the MIMIC\-IV\-ECHO database \(2017\-2019\)\. We extracted 4 deep feature representations from a view classifier, a left ventricle segmentation model, and the 2 validated LVEF estimation models\. These features were evaluated for their ability to identify systematic misinterpretations \(error slicing\) and automatically exclude AI estimates for high\-risk inputs \(selective estimation\)\. RESULTS: : \-0\.07 \[\-0\.11, \-0\.03\]\), both models systematically overestimated LVEF at lower values \(proportional bias slope, EchoNet: \-0\.33 \[\-0\.39, \-0\.28\], P &lt; 0\.001, PanEcho: \-0\.31 \[\-0\.36, \-0\.26\], P &lt; 0\.001\)\. All investigated deep feature representations identified error slices for both models\. Shared error slices between the models revealed 2 common failure modes: contrast\-enhanced echocardiograms and poor\-quality apical\-4\-chamber views\. Selective estimation using intermediate features from EchoNet achieved the best performance across both estimation models \(area under risk\-coverage curve, EchoNet: 5\.04 \[4\.45, 5\.66\]; PanEcho: 5\.21 \[4\.54, 5\.92\]\)\. CONCLUSIONS: Deep feature representations can be leveraged to identify and automatically flag misinterpretation\-prone echocardiograms, enabling promising guardrail mechanisms for the reliable deployment of echocardiography AI\.
