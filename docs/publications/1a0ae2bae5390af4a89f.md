# Green, E\. J\.: Molyneux&\#x27;s Question and the Intransitivity of Resemblance

**Autores:** 

**Data de descoberta:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** unknown

## Descrição do feed

\_Locke Studies\_\. forthcomingThis paper considers Locke’s answer to Molyneux’s question and its alleged tension with his broader theory of perception\. Locke maintained that shape is perceived through both sight and touch, and that ideas of primary qualities resemble their worldly causes\. Yet he also denied that a newly sighted perceiver could identify shapes by sight, seemingly saddling him with an inconsistency\. If visual and tactile ideas of shape both resemble their worldly causes, then ostensibly they should also resemble one another, enabling a newly sighted perceiver to identify shapes visually\. I suggest that an analogous threat of inconsistency arises within contemporary philosophy of perception\. It is widely held that many perceptual representations bear a structural resemblance to their causes, and there is overwhelming evidence that common shape properties are seen and felt\. However, there is also evidence that newly sighted perceivers initially fail at cross\-modal recognition\. To dissolve the tension, I argue that we should reject the key application of the transitivity of resemblance principle: Distinct shape representations can structurally resemble the same object without resembling one another\. Finally, I suggest that Locke had resources to anticipate this type of solution\.
