# Los sistemas de geolocalización vehicular y la sostenibilidad del transporte: una revisión sistemática

**Autores:** Leandro González Ramos; Carlos Lázaro Jiménez Puerto; Isis Neisy Ramos Acevedo; Gerardo Yero Morejón

**Data:** 2026-10-15 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

This systematic review examined the evolution, main technological trends, and applications of vehicle geolocation systems\. The study followed the PRISMA guidelines to conduct a comprehensive search in five prestigious scientific databases: Scopus, Web of Science, SciELO, IEEE Xplore, and ACM Digital Library\. Of the 85 studies initially reviewed, 17 were selected, peer\-reviewed, and subjected to a rigorous screening and eligibility process\. All were published between 2021 and 2025\. Data extraction focused on conceptual definitions, fundamental technologies, system architectures, documented applications for use in public transport or logistics, their main challenges, and their contribution to the Sustainable Development Goals\. The findings suggest considering geolocation as more than just a provider of coordinates, but rather for its potential for integration into cyber\-physical systems, even enhanced by artificial intelligence\. It is evident that although numerous prototypes exist for tracking bus fleets, they do not always materialize into robust, scalable, economical, and sustainable solutions\. The feasibility analysis for their use in contexts such as Cuba points to the suitability of employing hybrid GOS\-GSM architectures with centralized data processing\. The results confirm that vehicle geolocation systems contribute to the sustainable management of transport, with an emphasis on SDGs 9 and 11, by contributing to transportation efficiency and reducing environmental impact\.
