# Performance Analysis of a Solar PV System for Industrial Rooftop Applications in the EEC of Thailand: A Case Study of PTTGC 4 Rayong

**Autores:** Ashraf Hussain; Zaigham Ali; Jompob Waewsak; Ruamporn Nikhom; Shahid Ali; Yves Gagnon

**Data:** 2026-10-10 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

This study presents a performance analysis of a large\-scale industrial rooftop solar PV system, using the PTTGC 4 facility in Rayong as a case study in Thailand&\#x27;s Eastern Economic Corridor \(EEC\)\. A total rooftop area of 10,280 m2 was modeled in PVsyst \(version 7\.4\.7\) to evaluate the power generation and performance of a grid\-connected PV system under local climatic conditions \(Meteonorm 8\.1 data for Rayong, 12\.69°N, 101\.15°E\)\. The optimized system arrangement produced an installed capacity of 2\.342 MWp using 3,808 monocrystalline modules with 19 inverters at a tilt angle of 30°\. Simulation results show an annual energy output of 3\.573 GWh, an annual specific yield of 1,526 kWh/kWp, and a performance ratio of 87\.43%, indicating favorable irradiation conditions and an efficient system design\. Thermal losses \(6\.09%\) and inverter losses \(1\.68%\) are the dominant losses in the system for PV deployment in the EEC\. The results show that solar PV rooftop utilization in industrial estates within the EEC is a technically feasible and scalable pathway to reduce greenhouse gas emissions while increasing on\-site renewable energy penetration\. This case study offers a replicable framework for PV\-based decarbonization evaluations in similar industrial settings across Southeast Asia\.
