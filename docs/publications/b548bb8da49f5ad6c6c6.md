# Fujie, Naoto: Operational\-Shape Penetration\-Circulation Theory of Victory and Defeat: How Victory and Defeat Ignite, Reallocate Resources, and Transform Evolution, Persistence, and Extinction—from Capital and AI to Xeno\-Capital and Xenoscience

## Descrição do feed

\_Zenodo\_ 1\. 2026This paper develops an operational\-shape penetration\-circulation theory of victory and defeat by asking when competitive outcomes become non\-redundant and how those outcomes reorganize subsequent possibilities\. Victory and defeat are not treated as terminal labels attached to completed contests, but as configuration\-dependent operational events that alter resource access, institutional position, strategic reachability, evolutionary pressure, persistence, and extinction risk\. The paper distinguishes local success from durable victory: an outcome becomes operationally significant when it penetrates beyond the immediate contest and changes the conditions under which later actors, systems, or populations can act\. Penetration refers to the propagation of victory\- and defeat\-generated differences across economic, technological, institutional, informational, and biological domains, while circulation describes the return of those altered conditions into later competitive configurations\. The analysis applies this framework to capital allocation, artificial intelligence, automated competition, and Xeno\-Capital, where success can recursively reallocate computation, data, infrastructure, labor, access, and institutional authority\. Xenoscience generalizes the argument by treating victory and defeat as cross\-domain transformations in reachability rather than as concepts restricted to war, markets, politics, or biological selection\. The broader claim is that victory and defeat should be analyzed by the downstream reconfiguration they produce: which resources become concentrated or lost, which lineages or systems persist, which alternatives close, and which new competitive conditions are generated in return\. \(direct link \)
