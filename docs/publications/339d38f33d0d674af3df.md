# EL\-RAKHAWI DOCTRINE OF COSMIC CONSCIOUSNESS AND COGNITIVE SOVEREIGNTY THE PHYSICS OF MIND, THE CHEMISTRY OF INTELLIGENCE, AND THE FATE OF HUMANITY IN THE AGE OF SILICON

**Autores:** mohamed kamal arafa el\-rakhawi

**Data:** 2026-10-10 · **Tipo:** book

## Resumo

EL\-RAKHAWI COSMIC CONSCIOUSNESS &amp; COGNITIVE SOVEREIGNTY: SUMMARY This doctrine establishes a unified framework bridging physics, neuroscience, AI, and law to protect human consciousness\. It introduces five pillars: \(1\) The El\-Rakhawi Coherence Threshold \(ECT\), a mathematical model integrating quantum coherence, thermodynamic depth, and dynamic thermal isolation to define conscious emergence; \(2\) Algorithmic Neurochemistry, reconceptualizing neurotransmitters as biological learning variables; \(3\) The Silicon\-Carbon Divide, proving current AI are &quot;Stochastic Mirrors&quot; lacking embodied sentience; \(4\) Neuro\-Capitalism critique, exposing neural surplus extraction and the erosion of compatibilist agency; \(5\) Cognitive Sovereignty Framework, establishing Neuro\-Rights, a Cognitive Firewall, and Market Access Conditionality for geopolitical enforcement\. It features the Unified Cognitive Sovereignty Index \(UCSI\), using a Weighted Geometric Mean with an operational floor and quantifiable proxy metrics to evaluate cognitive protection globally\.
