# THE USE OF GENERATIVE ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE IN CRYPTO\-ASSET\-RELATED CRIMES: EMERGING CRIMINAL THREATS AND CRIMINAL LAW CHALLENGES

**Autores:** Primov Bakhtiyor Olim ugli

**Data:** 2026-10-09 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

This paper examines the impact of generative artificial intelligence \(AI\) technologies on the modus operandi of crypto\-asset\-related crimes\. Drawing on reports issued by international organisations in 2024–2026, it describes such threats as deepfakes, voice cloning, AI\-generated phishing and synthetic identities\. The paper argues that, within the elements of a crime, generative AI should be classified as an instrument of crime\. It assesses the extent to which the criminal legislation of the Republic of Uzbekistan covers such conduct and puts forward proposals for its improvement\.
