# BOSHLANG‘ICH SINF O‘QISH DARSLARIDA XALQ TOPISHMOQLARINI “TABIIY FANLAR \(SCIENCE\)” FANI BILAN INTEGRATSIYALAB O‘QUVCHILARNING KUZATUVCHANLIK VA MANTIQIY FIKRLASH KO‘NIKMALARINI RIVOJLANTIRISH METODIKASI

**Autores:** AZIZA SHIXNAZAROVNA JABBOROVA

**Data:** 2026-10-10 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

This article presents a methodology for developing primary school pupils’ observation and logical thinking skills in reading lessons through integrating folk riddles with the content of the “Natural Sciences \(Science\)” subject\. In the study, riddles about natural objects and phenomena are systematized according to the topics of the primary Science course, criteria for their selection are defined, and three groups of riddles are identified according to the way the attributes of the referent are expressed: metaphorical, descriptive and oppositional riddles\. An integrative methodology has been developed that includes the stages of perceiving the riddle and decoding its image, building an attribute map, testing the guess through observation and experiment, classifying by essential attributes, and composing riddles\. It is substantiated that the methodology helps pupils describe an object by its attributes, distinguish essential from non\- essential attributes, justify their answers with evidence, and consciously acquire science concepts through figurative expression\.
