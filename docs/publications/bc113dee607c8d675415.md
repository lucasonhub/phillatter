# Inteligencia artificial y analítica del aprendizaje: Nuevas fronteras en la evaluación educativa

**Autores:** Erika Elizabeth Llerena Choez; Oscar Anchundia\-Gómez; Edgar Freddy Morales Caguana; Carlos Antonio Barreiro Díaz

**Data:** 2026-10-14 · **Tipo:** book

## Resumo

This book explores the intersection of artificial intelligence and learning analytics in educational assessment\. Four chapters address conceptual foundations, educational data and indicators, AI\-supported assessment and personalized learning, and responsible institutional governance\. The book offers analytical matrices, conceptual distinctions, and practice\-oriented teaching scenarios\. Its central argument is that predictions and automated feedback only become educationally useful when linked to valid evidence, human oversight, equity, privacy, and defensible pedagogical action\. Keywords: artificial intelligence; learning analytics; educational assessment; personalized learning; algorithmic ethics\.
