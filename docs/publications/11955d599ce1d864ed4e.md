# When AI removes the work of thinking

**Autores:** Bryon Larrance

**Data:** 2026-10-17 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

As artificial intelligence increasingly automates cognitive work, organizations face a new challenge: deciding where human judgment continues to create value\. This paper examines how AI changes the relationship between human thought, organizational processes, and decision making\. It explores why preserving human involvement is not always the same as preserving valuable judgment, and how organizations can deliberately determine when to rely on automation, human oversight, or a combination of both\. The central argument is that when human thought is no longer required to perform the work, human judgment must be intentionally designed into the process\.
