# Pedraza, R\.: Artificial Research by Deduction in the Global Artificial Intelligence

## Descrição do feed

In Global Artificial Intelligence: First Prototype of Global Artificial Intelligence\. London: Ruben Garcia Pedraza\. pp\. 1\-32\. 2025This paper explores the foundational role of Artificial Research by Deduction within the architecture of Global Artificial Intelligence \(GAI\)\. While current AI development remains largely focused on Specific Artificial Intelligences \(SAIs\) and artificial learning, the transition toward GAI demands a paradigmatic leap—from learning to autonomous research\. Within this shift, Artificial Research by Deduction offers a methodological system based on the Syllogism of the Trend \(i\.e\., if A tends to B and B to C, then A tends to C\), enabling the automation of rational hypothesis generation and validation from structured data\. The paper presents a three\-stage model: application, where global databases are standardized into a unified quantitative matrix; replication, where robotic devices and SAIs gather data for deductive analysis; and auto\-replication, where GAI iteratively refines its own models and decision\-making structures, including cognitive, infrastructural, and material self\-enhancement\. Emphasis is placed on the interaction between deductive reasoning, modeling systems, decisional frameworks, and the application layer—through which GAI acts upon the world via robotic systems and subordinate SAIs\. Artificial Research by Deduction is presented not as an isolated capability, but as a key epistemic engine embedded within a larger system of systems, aimed at rational global governance, the well\-being of humankind, and the pursuit of authentic, non\-anthropocentric truth\. This first paper lays the groundwork for deeper explorations of replication mechanisms and the global integration of category systems in future work\.
