# Stock Market Prediction Using A 3\-ML Fusion Approach

**Autores:** Janani M\.; Surekha M\.; Sabura Shifana J\.; Vishnu Priya D\.

**Data:** 2026-10-13 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Stock market prediction is still a significant challenge due to the non\-linearity, dynamism, and volatility of the financial time series\. Accurate stroke price prediction plays a major role in investment decisions, risk management, and financial planning\. This research presents a novel 3\-ML framework, which uses a combination of the Random Forest, Decision Tree and XGBoost Regression models to predict the next day&\#x27;s closing price of NIFTY\-50 index\. Moreover, a Random Forest \(RF\) classifier is used to classify market trends such as UP, DOWN or NEUTRAL, improving the decision\-making process\. Data from the market is preprocessed and enhanced by feature engineering through the use of technical indicators like moving averages and daily returns\. The proposed framework implements a form of a Flask\-based web application along with a MySQL database\. It has been experimentally evaluated and shown that the framework developed has an accuracy of 97\.7% and better stability of prediction and lower forecasting errors\.
