# Dynamically perturbed Ruppell Fox optimization with a context aware transformer for coherent document level English to Chinese translation

**Autores:** Weiwei Zhao

**Data:** 2026-10-13 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Document\-level English–Chinese neural machine translation requires consistent handling of lexical choices, entities, anaphora, and discourse relations across sentence boundaries\. Existing context\-aware models partially improve cross\-sentence consistency, but many rely on fixed context windows, manually selected configurations, or single\-path document representations, limiting their adaptability to domains with different discourse structures and terminology densities\. This study proposes a unified framework that integrates a Context\-Aware Transformer \(CAT\) with dynamically perturbed Rüppell’s Fox Optimization \(DPI\-RFO\) to jointly optimize document\-context representation and model configuration\. CAT separates sentence self\-attention, document\-context cross\-attention, and discourse\-aware attention and combines them through token\-wise gated fusion\. DPI\-RFO searches mixed discrete–continuous architecture and training variables using elite\-guided movement, cosine\-decaying perturbation, and stagnation\-triggered Lévy\-flight escape under a joint quality–cost objective\. Experiments were conducted across news, spoken, subtitle, legal, medical, and literary\-style domains using five independent seeds, sentence\- and document\-level metrics, ablation and robustness analyses, statistical testing, professional human evaluation, and computational\-efficiency assessment\. On the News test set, the selected configuration achieved 50\.8 BLEU, 49\.5 document\-level BLEU, and 67\.8 Coherence\-BLEU, exceeding the strongest context\-aware baseline by 2\.2 BLEU points\. DPI\-RFO reached the target fitness with approximately 30\.5% fewer candidate evaluations than baseline RFO\. Ablation results identified CAT as the primary source of translation\-quality improvement, while paired bootstrap testing confirmed significant gains over all evaluated baselines\. These findings demonstrate that coordinated discourse\-aware representation learning and automated configuration search improve document\-level English\-Chinese translation quality and coherence\. To clarify evidence priority, BLEU is the primary automatic translation\-quality metric, Coherence\-BLEU is the primary automatic discourse\-level endpoint, blinded human judgments are the primary subjective evidence, and one\-time search cost is reported separately from deployment\-time inference cost\.
