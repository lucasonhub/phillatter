\_Proceedings of the Aristotelian Society\_:1\-8\. 2026Two celestial bodies stand between you and the sun at the same time\. The smaller object \(Near\) is closer to you and is exactly under the shadow of the larger, more distant object \(Far\)\. What do you see? I argue that you see Far\. However, I claim that you do not see any of Far’s proper parts\.
