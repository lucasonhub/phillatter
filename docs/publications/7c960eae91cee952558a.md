# A Theoretical Framework for Non\-Invasive Neurosurgery Utilizing Biosonic and Bioneurosonic Resonance of the Human Voice: A Technical Monograph on Biowave Mechanics

**Autores:** Abdesselam Abouelfida

**Data:** 2026-10-18 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Background: Traditional neurosurgery and neuromodulation paradigms often overlook the active vibrational and mechanical parameters of the cranial environment, treating brain tissue as a passive substrate\.Methods: This paper introduces a novel biophysical paradigm, establishing that core cognitive and vocal sound units \(\\\(U\_\{s\}\\\)\) are synthesized through a dynamic, non\-linear superposition of two native internal fields: Biosonic Vibrations \(\\\(V\_\{bs\}\\\)\), representing structural mechanical, fluid, and bone\-conducted acoustic oscillations; and Bioneurosonic Vibrations \(\\\(V\_\{bns\}\\\)\), representing synchronized electrochemical neural network activity\.Results: By translating signal processing principles—historically anchored in telecommunications—into computational neuroscience, we mathematically formalize a state\-dependent phase modulation architecture\. This framework provides the quantitative baseline required to utilize focused external human vocal frequencies \(\\\(V\_\{ext\}\\\)\) via phase delay tuning \(\\\(\\Delta t\\\)\)\.Conclusion: The clinical implementation profiles demonstrate that calculated wave interference matrices can systematically yield constructive interference for localized thermal tissue ablation, or destructive interference for the complete neutralization of hyper\-synchronous neurological aberrations, such as epileptic focus discharges\.
