# Research on Business Model Transformation and Performance Evaluation of Internet Healthcare Enterprises from the Perspective of Management Accounting: A Case Study of Ping An Health

**Autores:** Yihan Jiang

**Data:** 2026-10-09 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

This case study focuses on Ping An Healthcare and Technology Company Limited \(hereinafter referred to as &quot;Ping An Health&quot;\)\. Drawing on theories from the \*Guidelines for the Application of Management Accounting\*, the study conducts an in\-depth analysis of the transformation trajectory and economic outcomes of the company&\#x27;s &quot;Managed Care \+ Family Doctor Membership&quot; business model\. The article begins by reviewing the industry landscape, highlighting the monetization challenges faced by internet healthcare firms relying solely on online consultation services—such as Haodafu Online—and the low gross margin characteristics of pharmaceutical e\-commerce models like JD Health\. It then employs marginal analysis tools from operations management to examine how Ping An Health surpassed its break\-even point and achieved its first\-ever profit in the first half of 2024 by balancing high fixed costs \(e\.g\., medical resources\) with high\-contributionmargin revenue streams \(e\.g\., membership fees\)\. Finally, utilizing the Balanced Scorecard \(BSC\) framework from performance management, the study analyzes how the company constructed a non\-financial indicator system centered on paid user counts and customer lifetime value\.
