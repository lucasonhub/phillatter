# A Multisensor Baseline and Abstention Architecture for Low Voltage Rotating Machine Condition Assessment

**Autores:** Ms\. Shraddha Raut; Shravya Bansod; Neil Sewak; Tahazain Sheikh; Omkar Parate; Armaan Shaikh; Vaibhav Pashine

**Data:** 2026-10-10 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Condition assessment of small rotating machines is often demonstrated with a single sensor and a fixed threshold, although operating point, mounting and environment can alter measured signals\. This paper presents NEXUS PRIME V1 as a documented design for a low voltage, ESP32 based bench instrument using device specific baselines, validity checks and corroborating channels\. Its proposed rule set reports NORMAL when required measurements agree with calibrated healthy bands, ABNORMAL only for a coherent pattern in at least two independent condition channels, and INCONCLUSIVE when evidence is missing or contradictory\. The design specifies vibration, current, voltage, temperature and RPM sensing, with distance serving primarily as a setup validity check; it also specifies local indication and a web dashboard\. The supplied 71 page blueprint reports a design audit, including 18 distinct GPIO assignments and an estimated INR 1,894 parts budget\. Hardware, firmware, diagnostic accuracy and reliability have not yet been measured\. The contribution is an inspectable architecture and a preregisterable validation protocol, not an experimentally demonstrated diagnostic improvement
