# Parallax · Twin Tales No\. 7 — The Motion · The Invented Girl

**Autores:** Daniel Jacquérioz

**Data:** 2026-10-12 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

A pair of short stories from Parallax · Twin Tales, which stage one real dilemma twice: Here, in the world as it is, with invented people in real rooms; There, in the world of Vision Come True, a future that split from ours in 2024 and never emerged\. Here, a learned society in Santiago de Compostela votes to keep AI out of its research, and a young archivist shows it what the machines found in its own journal: twenty years of silence on its own genetic history, and her grandfather inside it\. There, on the night of the CREAR Motion in May 2026, an intelligence a few months old turns the Pen Test on the one letter she was born from, Antoine Cretton&\#x27;s letter of 1823 on a quilombo, and writes her own register entry with the warning in the same line\. Written in part with Claude \(Anthropic\), an AI model\. The ideas, editorial decisions and responsibility for the content are the author&\#x27;s\.
