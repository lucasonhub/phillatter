# ABA RENEWAL OF FENTANYL SEEKING USING A DRUG\-PRIME AND PEER STIMULUS IN RATS

**Autores:** Meagan Blanchard

**Data:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** dissertation

## Resumo

People with opioid use disorder can experience craving and relapse to use when exposed to cues that were previously paired with the drug\-taking environment\. These cues can be both social and non\-social in nature\. This study aimed to explore the effect of a peer cue and a drug\-prime cue, both together and separately\. First, rats were trained to self\-administer fentanyl in Context A \(peer and drug present\)\. Then, rats extinguished this behavior in Context B \(no peer and no drug present\) while self\-administering saline instead of fentanyl\. Lastly, rats underwent 4 different renewal test conditions \(control, peer only, drug only, peer \+ drug\) in order to determine renewal of drug\-seeking\. It was found that rats renewed drug seeking under the peer only and peer \+ drug conditions, but not the drug only condition\. This suggests that the social cue overshadowed the effect of the nonsocial, drug cue\.
