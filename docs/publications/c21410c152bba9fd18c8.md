# Outcomes of an Interprofessional Education Event on AAC and Co\-Teaching

**Autores:** Annelise Yost; John McCarthy

**Data:** 2026-10-14 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

This study explored the outcomes of an Interprofessional Education \(IPE\) event between preprofessional speech\-language pathologists \(SLPs\) and special educators aimed at identifying the outcomes of IPE on preprofessional teachers’ and SLPs’ knowledge of co\-teaching and classroom\-based models to support students who use AAC\. Students at a midwestern university completed an IPE session as part of their coursework\. A pre\-post assessment exploring knowledge of co\-teaching and classroom\-based services was utilized\. A focus group with preprofessional SLPs and special educators was run to identify perceptions and practicality of classroom\-based models to support AAC users\. Results demonstrated the ability for a short, focused IPE session to increase knowledge of classroom\-based service delivery for both preprofessional SLPs and special educators in the context of supporting AAC users\. Thematic analysis of the focus group interview identified five themes, views of co\-teaching, necessary supports, IPE session improvements, willingness to collaborate, and current practices\. The results of this study indicate the power of short IPE sessions to improve knowledge of co\-teaching and classroom\-based service delivery to support students who use AAC\. Responses highlight preprofessional educators’ positive perceptions toward co\-teaching\. However, challenges like time restraints and support may impact the perceived feasibility of classroom\-based models in practice\.
