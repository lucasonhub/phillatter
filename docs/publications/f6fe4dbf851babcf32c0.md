# The Mediating Role of Emotional Intelligence Between Investor Behavior and Welfare: Indonesian Cryptocurrency Market Evidence

**Autores:** Akbar Ihza Pratama; Abdul Mukti Soma

**Data:** 2026-10-13 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Background: Indonesia’s cryptocurrency market has expanded rapidly, exposing a growing retail\-investor population to high volatility, overconfidence, herding, and uneven financial capability\. Objective: This study examines the relationships of overconfidence, herding behavior, risk perception, financial literacy, and investment experience with investor welfare, and tests emotional intelligence as the mechanism connecting these factors to welfare\. Its novelty lies in positioning emotional intelligence as a mediator between behavioral and capability\-based antecedents and an ultimate welfare outcome in Indonesia’s cryptocurrency context\. Methods: A cross\-sectional explanatory survey obtained 449 valid responses from Indonesian cryptocurrency investors selected through purposive sampling\. The model was estimated using prediction\-oriented PLS\-SEM in SmartPLS 4\.0\.0\.9 because it contains multiple latent constructs, direct paths, and mediation paths and does not require multivariate normality\. Results: Overconfidence and herding behavior were negatively associated with investor welfare, whereas risk perception, financial literacy, investment experience, and emotional intelligence were positively associated with welfare\. Emotional intelligence partially mediated all five antecedent–welfare relationships\. Financial literacy showed the strongest association with emotional intelligence \(β = 0\.34\), while emotional intelligence was positively associated with investor welfare \(β = 0\.27\)\. Conclusion: Investor welfare in a volatile digital\-asset market depends on both cognitive capability and emotion regulation; therefore, investor education and protection should integrate financial literacy with behavioral and emotional safeguards\.
