# The integration of artificial intelligence in medical imaging practice: A perspective of the Nigerian radiographer

**Autores:** Archibong B\.E; Paulinus S\.O; Abiazie H\.C; Okezie F\.C

**Data:** 2026-10-15 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

ABSTRACT Background: The integration of artificial intelligence \(AI\) in medical imaging represents a transformative advancement in healthcare delivery, particularly within developing healthcare systems like Nigeria\. This study investigated the perspectives of Nigerian radiographers in the South\-South region regarding AI integration in medical imaging practice, focusing on knowledge, attitudes, current practices, and barriers to adoption\. Methods: Using a descriptive cross\-sectional survey design, data were collected from 73 practicing radiographers with at least 3 years of experience via structured questionnaires administered through Google Forms\. The questionnaire assessed their knowledge, attitudes, awareness, and willingness to adopt AI in their clinical practice\. Content validity was established by experts, and reliability was confirmed \(Cronbach’s α = 0\.73\)\. Microsoft Excel and the Statistical Package for the Social Sciences \(SPSS\) version 26\.0 were used to organize the data for descriptive statistics\. Results: Findings revealed high awareness of AI applications, with 78\.1% of respondents familiar with AI technologies\. However, only 21\.9% rated their knowledge as excellent, while 42\.5% considered it fair, indicating a gap between general awareness and in\-depth understanding\. Online resources were the primary source of AI knowledge, highlighting the need for structured educational interventions\. Radiographers demonstrated predominantly positive attitudes, with 73\.9% agreeing that AI would improve imaging accuracy and reduce workload\. Additionally, 64\.4% expressed preparedness for AI adoption, although 41\.1% feared potential replacement by AI technologies\. Current AI utilization was limited, with 31\.5% reporting workplace use, primarily for imaging analysis \(39\.1%\) and reporting assistance \(21\.7%\)\. Despite limited implementation, 89\.0% expressed willingness to undergo professional development, preferring workshops and seminars \(37\.0%\)\. Significant barriers included inadequate infrastructure and high implementation costs\. Conclusion: Successful AI integration requires coordinated efforts from educational institutions, professional bodies, healthcare facilities, and policymakers to address these barriers while building on radiographers&\#x27; positive attitudes toward AI adoption\. Keywords: Artificial Intelligence, Medical Imaging, Nigerian Radiographers, AI Adoption, Barriers, Perspectives
