# I think, therefore I am I am, therefore I think

**Autores:** Zrinka Tomasic

**Data:** 2026-10-10 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

This work is bringing forward questions about animal, plant, inanimate objects \(including AI and molecules, atoms and subatomic particles\) and even post\-mortem consciousness\. It gives examples and researches that are made and still being made\. It connects the real examples with the philosophic thought past and present, Western and Chinese\. It even mentions the research assignment about plant consciousness that is suitable for school children to do\. And, although it has so many examples in biology, philosophy, physics and other fields, in the end it asks a question „Are they really conscious“ to every reader to reflect on\. To come to know animal, plant, inanimate objects and post\-mortem consciousness, and their own\.
