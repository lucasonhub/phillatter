# Adani Enterprises and Jabil Sign MoU for 50:50 AI Hardware Venture in India

**Autores:** Blackridge Research &amp; Consulting

**Data:** 2026-10-14 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Adani Enterprises Limited \(AEL\), the flagship company of the Adani Group, and Jabil Inc\. \(NYSE: JBL\), a global engineering, supply chain, and manufacturing company, have signed a non\-binding Memorandum of Understanding setting out the framework for a proposed 50:50 joint venture to manufacture and assemble artificial intelligence and data center equipment in India\.
