# A Lobulated Cutaneous Nodule on the Abdomen

**Autores:** Akshay Meena; Sanjay Sriram; Sheetanshu Kumar

**Data:** 2026-10-13 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

A 42\-year\-old female presented with painless abdominal swelling for the past 2 months\.Cutaneous examination revealed a solitary, well\-defined, firm, lobulated skin\-coloured to erythematous nodule of size 5 × 3 cm on the right lower abdomen \[Figure 1A\]\.Histopathological examination revealed a dermal\-based spindle\-cell tumour arranged in a storiform pattern within a fibrocollagenous and myxoid stroma \[Figure 1B and C\]\.On immunohistochemistry, spindle cells were diffusely positive for cluster of differentiation 34, negative for SO × 10, S100, diagnostic of dermatofibrosarcoma protuberans \[Figure 1D\]\.Dermoscopy showed a structureless whitishpink background with shiny white streaks and scattered delicate linear vessels \(Dermlite DL4, ×10; polarized mode\) \[Figure 2\]\.e patient subsequently underwent wide local excision of the tumour with adequate surgical margins\.
