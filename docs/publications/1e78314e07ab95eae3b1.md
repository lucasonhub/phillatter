# Bulgaria, Jamel: Structural Ontologies of Bound Possibility: A Unified Framework of Constraint Theory, the Core Emotion Framework, and the Mechanics of Human Flourishing

**Autores:** 

**Data de descoberta:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** unknown

## Descrição do feed

This paper presents the first fully unified structural constructivist ontology integrating Constraint Theory, the Core Emotion Framework \(CEF\), quantum like contextuality, systems neuroscience, and somatic clinical science into a single cross domain architecture\. Building on the foundational claim that constraint is the generative primitive of determinate existence, the analysis extends this ontology into embodied affect by formalizing how CEF’s ten somatic operators instantiate type level openness and token level closure within human cognition\. The paper introduces several new theoretical advances: a mechanistic account of operator fusion as a form of affective non separability; a formal TS 7 structural psychopathology model describing chronic distortions in transition matrices; a TS 10 structural disassembly protocol that operationalizes constraint closure through somatic anchors; and a synthetic affect extension \(TS 9\) specifying how operator architectures generalize to artificial systems\. These developments are grounded in detailed neurobiological mechanisms—including precision weighted interoception, LC–NE attentional gating, and NTS–PAG autonomic integration—and supported by empirical signatures such as the RMSEA/CFI–TLI divergence observed in Phase 1 psychometric validation\. The result is a comprehensive, multi level framework in which physical actualization, cognitive state transitions, emotional regulation, and therapeutic change are shown to be homologous processes of constraint propagation and closure\.
