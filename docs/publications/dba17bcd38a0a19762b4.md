In Real Time: A Reinvention in British Metaphysics 1883\-1928\. Oxford University Press\. 2026In 1880s Britain, philosophers developed what would become a half\-century obsession with time\. Back then, time was widely held to be unreal; by the 1920s, it was widely held to be real\. This chapter provides an overview of that shift, and of the new questions about time developed by realists\. It argues that this philosophy of time era matters for several reasons: scholars are increasingly paying it attention; the historical philosophers themselves really cared about time; and, during this period, time became central to metaphysical debates more widely\. This chapter goes on to give an overview of the book as a whole\.
