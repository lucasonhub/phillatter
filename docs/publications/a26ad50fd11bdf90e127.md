# ЗНАЧИМОСТЬ ИЗУЧЕНИЯ ИНОСТРАННОГО ЯЗЫКА И МЕТОДИКА ЕГО ОСВОЕНИЯ ПОСРЕДСТВОМ МУЛЬТИМЕДИЙНЫХ ТЕХНОЛОГИЙ

**Autores:** Оңласын Колыбай; Onlassyn Kolybai; Ж\.Н\. Бисенбаева; Оңласын Колыбай

**Data:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

AbstractThis article investigates the fundamental pedagogical significance of foreign language acquisition and evaluates the efficacy of multimedia technologies in accelerating this educational process\. As modern educational institutions transition toward SMART\-university ecosystems, traditional instructional paradigms are increasingly being augmented by dynamic, digitally enhanced environments\. This study explores how the systematic integration of multimedia tools—specifically interactive audio\-visual scaffolding and digitized dictionary\-based speech activation platforms—addresses common linguistic barriers such as vocabulary retention and communicative anxiety\. The research employed a mixed\-methods approach, implementing a structured pedagogical intervention where learners utilized varied multimedia resources to practice functional language, receive immediate contextual feedback, and self\-regulate their learning\. Data collected through pre\- and post\-intervention assessments, alongside qualitative learner reflections, indicate that multimedia\-enhanced instruction significantly boosts learner motivation and autonomy\. Furthermore, the findings demonstrate that utilizing targeted digital dictionary work within a SMART\-educational framework effectively activates spontaneous speech and reduces cognitive overload\. The study concludes that systematically embedding multimedia technologies into the curriculum is not merely a supplementary strategy, but a fundamental requirement for developing robust communicative competence in contemporary globalized environments\.
