# From traits to trajectories: overcoming psychometric assessment gap through adaptive developmental potential in organizations

**Autores:** Pravallika Manam; Reeves J Wesley

**Data:** 2026-10-15 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Driven by AI\-integrated and hybrid structures, the organizational ecosystem has shifted from fixed role definitions to the adaptive nature of the workforce\. While existing organizational assessments focus on traditional psychometric tools that prioritize stable personality traits, this perspective article exposes the Assessment Gap and introduces the Adaptive Developmental Potential \(ADP\) Framework, which shifts talent analysis from point\-in\-time snapshots to adaptability\-based growth trajectories\. This framework articulates across four dimensions: Environmental Sensitivity, the degree to which environmental factors amplify individuals&\#x27; development; Learning Adaptability, the capacity to unlearn redundant protocols and update cognitive assumptions; Self\-Regulatory Capacity, the capacity to sustain focus and manage exhaustion under uncertainty; and Developmental Trajectory, the direction of individual growth over time\. Traditional assessments remain useful, but they may be insufficient in predicting behavior in dynamic environments; hence, organizations can implement Precision Development, targeting micro\-interventions over regular corporate training\. Conclusively, this perspective article offers scope for future research to empirically validate this longitudinal model, thereby aligning organizational science with the dynamics of modern work styles\.
