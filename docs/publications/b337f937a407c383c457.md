# Advanced AI/ML Analysis of AQI to Study the Crowd Density Effect

**Autores:** Ashish B\. Jirapure; Komal Mahalle; Swejal Tembhare; Aniket Tayde; Shruti Sarode

**Data:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Public environments can experience rapid changes in person density, particularly during festivals, weekends, and special occasions\. These changes may influence carbon dioxide concentration, particulate matter, temperature, humidity, and the resulting air\-quality condition\. This paper presents an integrated framework that combines camera\-based person counting with environmental sensing and artificial intelligence and machine learning analysis\. A vision module estimates the number of people and crowd density from video frames, while environmental observations are collected with synchronized timestamps\. The combined dataset is preprocessed, aligned, and analyzed to identify relationships between crowd density and air\-quality indicators\. The framework also supports dashboard\-based visualization and alert generation for practical monitoring\. The proposed approach is intended to provide a common analytical view of occupancy and environmental conditions in public premises\. Since experimental measurements are not included in the supplied project material, numerical performance results are not claimed in this paper; the evaluation structure is defined for completion after data collection\.
