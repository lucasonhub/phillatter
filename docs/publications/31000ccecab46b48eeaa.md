# PEDAGOGIK FAOLIYATDA METODIK KOMPETENTLIKNI RIVOJLANTIRISHNING NAZARIY VA AMALIY ASOSLARI

**Autores:** SHAHZODA ZAFARJON QIZI HASANOVA

**Data:** 2026-10-10 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

This article provides a scientific and pedagogical analysis of the theoretical and methodological foundations of developing methodological competence in pedagogical activity, its structural components, and its functional significance in the modern educational process\. Methodological competence is interpreted as an integrative professional quality that enables educators to apply professional knowledge in practice, design educational processes, select effective teaching methods and technologies, consider the individual characteristics of learners, and conduct reflective assessments of pedagogical outcomes\. The study substantiates the role of competence\-based, activity\-oriented, systemic, and reflective approaches in the development of methodological competence\.
