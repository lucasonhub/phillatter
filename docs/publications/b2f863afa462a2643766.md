# The Connection between Flares and Starspots on M Dwarfs Based on TESS Photometry

**Autores:** Chunxiang Wang; Jiaxin Wang; Yanjun He; Heyu Sun; Yu Pan; Xianju Zhou; Shichang Li; Yirong MA

**Data:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Abstract This study uses Transiting Exoplanet Survey Satellite photometry to investigate the relationship between flare characteristics and starspot features on M dwarfs\. From the light curves, we identified 12,661 flare events on 2699 M dwarfs\. For these flares, we extracted parameters such as flare energy, rise time, and decay time\. Additionally, we applied a spot model to light curves exhibiting spot modulation to derive the photometric amplitude and spot distribution complexity\. Our analysis revealed four correlations: \(1\) flare energy is positively correlated with spot size; \(2\) the flare rate initially increases steeply, then declines gradually as spot size increases, a trend whose robustness is confirmed through Monte Carlo uncertainty analysis and independent flare frequency distribution power\-law fits; \(3\) there is no significant correlation between flare energy and spot complexity distribution; and \(4\) most importantly, the flare rate increases with spot distribution complexity before reaching a peak and subsequently showing a slow decline\. These results suggest that spot size is associated with flare energetics, while the spatial distribution of spots, which may reflect the large\-scale magnetic field configuration, could play a role in determining the flare rate\. These correlations provide an observational clue to the influence of large\-scale magnetic field structure on flare triggering\.
