# Polymer\-based metasurfaces for scalable, reconfigurable and multifunctional nanophotonics

**Autores:** Ramin Yazdaanpanah; Sachin Prashant Kulkarni; Yiming Yang; Koray Aydın

**Data:** 2026-10-13 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Abstract Optical metasurfaces have transformed nanoscale light control, but conventional platforms remain predominantly rigid and fabrication intensive\. Polymer\-based metasurfaces offer a complementary design space combining scalable manufacturing, mechanical compliance, chemical tunability, three\-dimensional structuring, and functional molecular and nanoparticle incorporation\. This Review examines polymer materials, fabrication strategies, and emerging applications in sensing, imaging, reconfigurable optics, light emission, and inverse\-designed photonics, highlighting opportunities and challenges for scalable, adaptive, and multifunctional nanophotonic systems\.
