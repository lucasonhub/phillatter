# A rank\-three integral boundary closure hypothesis for electromagnetic and baryonic scalar evaluations

**Autores:** Angus M\. McCoss

**Data:** 2026-10-14 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Dimensionless low\-energy constants are measured with extraordinary precision but remain external inputs to current gauge\-theoretic descriptions\. We formulate a testable rank\-three integral boundary\-closure hypothesis in which the inverse Thomson\-limit fine\-structure constant and the proton\-electron mass ratio are conditionally associated with inequivalent scalar evaluations of one pre\-scalar finite\-boundary structure\. The parent object comprises an integral carrier, projective ray\-covector incidence, one transparent identity quotient, a boundary\-event module, a protected incidence operator, a graded character object and a kinematical sewing quotient\. For the structural choice \( d, q \) = \(3, 3\), exact projective\-incidence identities yield q 2 \+ q \+1 = 13 ray classes, \( q \+1\)\( q 2 \+ q \+1\) = 52 incident flags and the restricted relation D 3 † D 3 = 3 I ; the transparent quotient gives dimension 2\( q 2 \+ q \+1\)−1 = 25\. Typed maps separate inherited parent data, branch representations, fixed evaluator packages and conditional physical identifications\. The electromagnetic Frobenius\-aperture evaluation gives X F A = 137 \+ 2 5 \- 1 3 / \[ 2 S i \( π \) \] , while the baryonic centre\-Schur evaluation gives X C S = 1 , 836 \+ \( 25 / 52 π \) \[ 1 \- \( 425 \- 1 / 9 \) \- 1 \] \. A bounded no\-retuning audit enumerates 47 non\-isomorphic nine\-vertex tree topologies and all 2 8 orientations of each, and compares simple aperture, weighting, phase, reservoir and Schur alternatives\. Minimal support, the Frobenius functional, the first\-zero cutoff, uniform 1/25 averaging, one Schur mode, the inverse Perron coordinate and 1/π conditional on orientation reversal are isolated within the declared classes\. The aperture kernel, the physical necessity of the orientation quotient and the reservoir normalisation remain unresolved\. Both fixed scalars are numerically compatible with the 2022 CODATA recommendations, but neither is derived from QED or QCD\. The hypothesis is falsified by evaluator degeneracy, action\-level non\-realisability, incompatible sewing or prospective metrological exclusion\.
