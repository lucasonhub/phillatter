# Implementation of Latent Dirichlet Allocation for the Analysis of Election Logistics Distribution Reports at Bawaslu RI

**Autores:** Citra Grace Kalangie; Irene Realita Tangkawarouw; Audy Kenap

**Data:** 2026-10-13 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Purpose – This study aims to implement Latent Dirichlet Allocation \(LDA\) for analyzing election logistics distribution monitoring reports at Bawaslu RI and to develop an interactive dashboard to support topic exploration\. The study addresses the difficulty of manually interpreting large collections of institutional reports containing operational, administrative, geographical, procurement, and legal information related to election logistics distribution\. Design/methods/approach – This research applied a quantitative text mining approach\. A total of 254 valid PDF\-based monitoring reports were extracted, validated, cleaned, and transformed into an analyzable corpus\. Text preprocessing involved case folding, text cleaning, tokenization, and stopword removal using Indonesian and domain\-specific stopword lists\. The cleaned corpus was represented using Bag of Words and modeled using LDA with the Gensim library\. Topic configurations from 2 to 15 were evaluated using the C\_V coherence score, and the results were integrated into a web\-based dashboard using Streamlit and PyLDAvis\. Findings – The optimal model produced ten topics with a C\_V coherence score of 0\.4040\. The generated topics represented themes such as regional supervision, logistics completeness, administrative procedures, island distribution, procurement, archipelago distribution, and post\-election legal processes\. The dashboard enabled interactive exploration of topics, coherence evaluation, topic interpretation, and visualization of intertopic relationships\. Research implications/limitations – The study demonstrates the value of topic modeling for improving the analysis of government monitoring reports, although expert validation with Bawaslu officers remains necessary\. Originality/value – This study contributes by applying LDA to official election logistics reports and integrating the results into an accessible analytical dashboard for election supervision stakeholders\.
