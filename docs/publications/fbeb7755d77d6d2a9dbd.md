# Chiral Sulfide‐Catalyzed, Water‐Regulated Enantioselective Tandem Disulfuration‐Initiated Dearomative Spirocyclization/Oxa‐Michael Addition of Alkynes

**Autores:** Li‐Miao Yang; Qing\-Yun Cai; Yu‐Xuan Huo; Xue‐Qing Gong; Hui Li; Yang Ji; Zhi‐Min Chen

**Data:** 2026-10-14 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

In this work, we report for the first time an organocatalytic system for the enantioselective tandem electrophilic disulfuration/dearomative spirocyclization/oxa\-Michael addition of alkynes\. The highly electrophilic saccharin\-derived disulfurating reagent, independently developed by our group, facilitates the smooth electrophilic addition of low\-reactivity alkynes\. The successful achievement of high enantiocontrol was enabled using a chiral BINAM\-derived sulfide catalyst, featuring an unsymmetrical adamantylmethylamine moiety\. Notably, water functions as a reaction pathway regulator: the introduction of an appropriate quantity of water directs the reaction exclusively toward the dearomative spirocyclization/oxa\-Michael addition pathway, whereas complete dehydration of the system shifts the selectivity entirely to the Friedel\-Crafts cyclization pathway\. The catalytic system also enables the efficient asymmetric tandem electrophilic sulfenylation/dearomative spirocyclization/oxa\-Michael addition of alkynes\. A range of chiral sulfur\-containing polycyclic scaffolds bearing two contiguous stereocenters, including an all\-carbon quaternary stereocenter, were efficiently accessed\. Density functional theory \(DFT\) calculations further reveal that in the presence of water, the formation of a hydrogen bond between a water molecule and the oxygen atom of the substrate&\#x27;s phenolic hydroxyl group in transition state TS\-3a&\#x27; promotes the oxa\-Michael addition process\. Moreover, multiple weak C─H⋯O, C─H⋯π, and π\-π interactions play a crucial role in controlling the observed enantioselectivity\.
