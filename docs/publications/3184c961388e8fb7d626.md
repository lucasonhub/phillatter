# Jaksic, Sasa: Realnost bez temelja \(Reality Without Foundation\)

**Autores:** 

**Data de descoberta:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** unknown

## Descrição do feed

Reality Without Foundation examines whether the concept of reality can be sustained once its apparent foundations are removed\. Contemporary physics shows that space and time are not fixed, universal containers but configurations dependent on the position and motion of the observer\. Neuroscience shows that what we experience as direct contact with reality is in fact a construction assembled by the brain from incomplete signals\. Philosophy, in turn, shows that behind apparently solid things there is no underlying substance, only a network of relations that continuously renews itself\. Building on the relational ontology introduced in Reality Without Foundation&\#x27;s predecessor, The Happening of Meaning, this book asks what remains of the concept of reality once substance, absolute space, and absolute time are set aside\. Rather than treating this loss as a form of skepticism or relativism, the book proposes that reality can be coherently understood as a stabilized configuration of relations, one that requires no substantial ground to be real\. The result is an account of reality that is consistent with the findings of physics and neuroscience while remaining a properly ontological, rather than merely epistemological, position\. \(direct link \)
