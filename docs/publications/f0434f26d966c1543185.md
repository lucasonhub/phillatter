# &\#x27;Old Wine, New Wineskins&\#x27;: Prolegomenon to a Socio\-Theology of the Internet

**Autores:** Wjf Keenan

**Data:** 2026-10-15 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

This article draws on a variety of theological, sociological, and anthropological threads of thought from Otto to Berger, from Pascal to Martin, to \(dis\)entangle metaphorical and implicitly religious properties of the Web\. By adding a religio\-spiritual dimension to the unending quest to understand the fuller circle of ideas in which technology is implicated, the thought\-worlds of sociology and theology can be brought into more constructive conversation than has typically been the case in the modern period\. As a contribution to understanding contemporary techno\-religion, this article provides a framework for exploring pre\-modern theo\-spiritual residues implicit in the postmodern habitus of the internet\.
