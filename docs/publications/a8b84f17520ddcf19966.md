# Fujie, Naoto: Position\-Value Evaluation of Research Operations Research Is a Position, Not a Ranking: Counterfactual Reachability from Scientific to Philosophical Intervals

**Autores:** 

**Data de descoberta:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** unknown

## Descrição do feed

\_Zenodo\_ 1\. 2026This paper develops Position\-Value Evaluation of Research Operations \(PVERO\), a framework for evaluating research without assigning context\-independent value to named researchers, institutions, or AI systems\. Research value is instead treated as a configuration\-dependent change in subsequent possibility: an operational difference may ignite under specific conditions, persist or transform, generate observable or intervenable effects, penetrate other media or scales, return through feedback, and reorganize what can subsequently be reached\. The framework introduces counterfactual removal and replacement, operational non\-redundancy, open\-ended research\-state generation, transition\-depth and elapsed\-time separation, environment\-relative revision speed, and an event\-first representation of research evidence\. Human researchers, AI systems, laboratories, collectives, instruments, and hybrid arrangements can therefore be evaluated without assuming in advance that they constitute the same kind of entity\. Controlled synthetic experiments, negative controls, sensitivity analyses, open\-ended state\-generation experiments, and an exploratory real\-world pilot are reported\. A central negative result is that generating more novel research states does not by itself increase research value\. The paper also specifies integration with the Ziran Simulator while explicitly separating current computational validation from still\-unperformed confirmatory blinded coding and native Ziran execution\. The central claim is that research is not adequately represented by a context\-free ranking\. Its value depends on which operational differences become non\-redundant in which configurations, how they alter subsequent reachability, whether they can be replaced, and whether their revision remains fast enough relative to environmental reorganization\. \(direct link \)
