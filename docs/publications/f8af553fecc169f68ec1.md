# Glaucoma neurodegeneration: targeted modulation of intraocular immune pathways – a review of novel therapeutic advances

**Autores:** Yunyun Sun; Keyun Xue; Congqin Wen; Xinyan Ma; Xiaojie Hu

**Data:** 2026-10-15 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Glaucoma is the leading global cause of irreversible blindness, with its pathogenesis extending far beyond elevated intraocular pressure\. Dysregulation of the intraocular immune system plays a central role in the progressive death of retinal ganglion cells\. This review systematically examines the research progress on key intraocular immune pathways in glaucomatous neurodegeneration, with a primary focus on immune\-inflammatory responses mediated by microglia, astrocytes, and T cells\. It delves into the molecular mechanisms of various immunoregulatory networks, including the TLR4/nuclear factor kappa B \(TLR4/NF\-κB\), Janus kinase/signal transducer and activator of transcription \(JAK/STAT\), mitogen\-activated protein kinase \(MAPK\), complement, and T\-cell receptor signaling pathways\. The review analyzes potential therapeutic strategies targeting these pathways\. By integrating the latest evidence from basic research and translational medicine, this article aims to provide a theoretical foundation and directional guidance for developing neuroprotective immunomodulatory therapies that go beyond intraocular pressure reduction\.
