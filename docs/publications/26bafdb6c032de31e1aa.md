# Central Nervous System Vasculitis Mimicking Glioma in a Patient With Ulcerative Colitis: A Case Report and Literature Review

**Autores:** Bowen Tian; Feng Feng; Hongzhi Guan; Yue Li

**Data:** 2026-10-12 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

A patient with ulcerative colitis presented with progressive memory impairment and alexia, with brain imaging showing a mass\-like lesion mimicking glioma\. Histopathology confirmed central nervous system \(CNS\) vasculitis\. The patient&\#x27;s memory and reading ability improved and finally recovered following corticosteroid treatment, with no recurrence during the long\-term follow\-up\. The case report and literature review highlight that clinicians should consider CNS vasculitis in patients with ulcerative colitis and tumor\-like brain lesions\.
