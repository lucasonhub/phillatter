# Comparative Evaluation of Fracture Resistance in Endodontically Treated Teeth Using Four Intraorifice Barrier Materials – An In Vitro Study

**Autores:** Arunachalesh Anbazhagan; Srividhya Srinivasan; Anand Meganathan; Diana Davidson; Dasarathan Duraivel; Farzana Hameed; Manali Ramakrishnan Srinivasan

**Data:** 2026-10-09 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Aim: This study aimed to compare the fracture resistance of endodontically treated mandibular premolars using four different intraorifice barrier materials\.Materials and methods: Fifty extracted single\-rooted mandibular premolars were decoronated, instrumented and obturated\.The coronal 3 mm of gutta\-percha was removed\.Specimens \(N = 50\) were divided into five groups based on intraorifice barriers: Group I: Glass ionomer cement \(GIC\); group II: Polyalkasite cement \(Cention N\); group III: Bonded amalgam; group IV: Nanohybrid bulk\-fill flowable composite; group V: Control \(no barrier\)\.After material placement, fracture resistance was measured using a universal testing machine under a vertical compressive load\.Statistical analysis was performed using one\-way analysis of variance \(ANOVA\) and post hoc Dunnett T3 test \(p &lt; 0\.05\)\. Results:The mean fracture resistance values \(in Newtons\) were: Bonded amalgam \(875\.14 ± 17\.95 N\), composite \(679\.95 ± 15\.27 N\), GIC \(585\.77± 5\.13 N\), polyalkasite cement \(Cention N\) \(461\.86 ± 13\.37 N\) and control \(407\.94 ± 5\.98 N\)\.Statistically significant differences were found among the groups \(p &lt; 0\.0001\)\.Bonded amalgam differed significantly from all groups, and the composite group showed significant differences when compared with polyalkasite cement \(Cention N\) and the control\.Conclusion: Bonded amalgam and nanohybrid bulk\-fill flowable composite resin, when used as an intraorifice barrier, had a significantly higher fracture resistance when compared to teeth without an intraorifice barrier\. Clinical significance:In the present study, the use of bonded intraorifice barriers, whether bonded amalgam or nanohybrid bulk\-fill composite, can enhance the structural durability of endodontically treated teeth, potentially improving clinical outcomes\.
