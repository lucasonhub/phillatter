# Cost Leadership Strategies and Performance of Animal Feeds Manufacturing SMEs in Nakuru County, Kenya\.

**Autores:** John Mutio; Erick Ombati Nyatangi

**Data:** 2026-10-12 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Cost leadership has become a decisive basis of competition for small and medium enterprises \(SMEs\) in agro\-processing, where firms must protect margins while serving highly price\-sensitive customers\. In Kenya, animal feeds manufacturing SMEs operate in an environment in which raw materials account for an estimated 70–80% of production costs, and many firms continue to experience low profitability, unstable sales and weak market positions owing to volatile input prices, production inefficiencies, limited automation and small production volumes\. Therefore, the purpose of this study was to establish the influence of cost leadership strategies on the performance of animal feeds manufacturing SMEs in Nakuru County, Kenya\. It specifically examined the influence of operational efficiency, supplier management, technology adoption and economies of scale strategies on performance\. The study was anchored on Porter’s Generic Strategies Theory and supported by the Resource\-Based View, Transaction Cost Economics and Economies of Scale Theory\. A descriptive and explanatory research design was adopted, targeting the 47 animal feeds manufacturing firms registered in Nakuru County, of which 46 were verified as eligible and studied through a census\. Data were collected from one owner or senior manager per firm using a structured questionnaire and analysed using descriptive statistics, Pearson correlation and multiple regression analysis, and the results were presented in tables and discussed\. The findings revealed that technology adoption had the strongest positive and significant influence on performance \(β = 0\.455, p &lt; 0\.001\), driven by automation and modern equipment\. The study also found that operational efficiency had a strong positive and significant influence on performance \(β = 0\.414, p &lt; 0\.001\), driven mainly by process optimization and inventory control\. The study further found that supplier management had a positive and significant influence on performance \(β = 0\.275, p = 0\.004\), anchored on supplier relationships and timely deliveries\. Finally, the study established that economies of scale had a positive but comparatively weaker significant influence on performance \(β = 0\.164, p = 0\.026\), while the four strategies jointly explained 66\.6% of the variation in performance\. The study, therefore, recommends that animal feeds manufacturing SMEs should invest in automation and modern equipment supported by staff training and maintenance, institutionalize lean production and inventory control, build long\-term collaborative supplier partnerships and diversified sourcing, and pair bulk purchasing and capacity utilization with sound pricing, supported by government financing and technology adoption programmes\.
