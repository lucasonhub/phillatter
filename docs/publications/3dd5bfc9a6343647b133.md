# Comparative Analysis of the Binding Characteristics of Fluorescein and Rhodamine B with Bovine Serum Albumin

**Autores:** Samson Olusegun Afolabi; Viktoria A\. Feoktistova; Dmitrii A\. Sbytov; Oleg Novikov; Anna Botnar; Sviatlana Ulasevich; Екатерина Владимировна Скорб; Sergey Shityakov

**Data:** 2026-10-13 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Abstract Bovine serum albumin \(BSA\) serves as a key model for investigating protein–ligand interactions and is crucial for understanding drug pharmacokinetics and developing biomedical probes\. This study presents a comprehensive comparative analysis of the binding of fluorescein and rhodamine B to BSA, employing UV–vis absorption, fluorescence, molecular docking, and 1000 ns molecular dynamics \(MD\) simulations\. The results revealed competitive binding, with rhodamine B exhibiting a greater affinity \(ΔGMMGBSA = −48\.17 kcal/mol\) than fluorescein \(−23\.16 kcal/mol\), which was supported by fluorescence quenching and binding analyses\. Molecular docking confirmed Sudlow’s site I as the primary binding site, whereas MD simulations highlighted stable complexes driven by van der Waals and electrostatic forces, particularly for rhodamine B\. Unexpectedly, the superior affinity of rhodamine B under physiological pH deviates from lipophilic trends\. These findings enhance the molecular understanding of dye–protein interactions, providing a robust basis for designing advanced fluorescent probes, drug conjugates, and diagnostic platforms\.
