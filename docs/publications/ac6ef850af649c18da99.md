# Role of Artificial Intelligence in Modern Banking Services: Benefits, Customer Experience and Challenges

**Autores:** Sri Krish K B; S\. H\. Varshitha

**Data:** 2026-10-18 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Role of Artificial Intelligence in Modern Banking Services: Benefits, Customer Experience and Challenges This research investigates the transformative role of Artificial Intelligence \(AI\) in modern banking services, with particular emphasis on operational efficiency, customer experience, and emerging challenges\. It examines the application of AI\-driven technologies, including machine learning, intelligent chatbots, predictive analytics, and automated fraud detection, in streamlining banking operations, enhancing service personalisation, improving risk management, and supporting data\-driven decision\-making\. Furthermore, the study evaluates key concerns associated with AI adoption, including data privacy, cybersecurity, algorithmic bias, transparency, and customer trust\. By assessing both the benefits and limitations of AI integration, this research aims to develop a comprehensive understanding of its implications for the banking sector and highlight the importance of responsible, secure, and customer\-centric AI implementation in the evolving financial services landscape\.
