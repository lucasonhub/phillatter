# Rational structure\-guided identification of a Bcl\-2\-targeting peptide with antiproliferative effects on colorectal cancer cells

**Autores:** Xing Jin; Shudan Yang; Qianqian Wang; Shutong Chen; Yinan Wang; Yang Yang

**Data:** 2026-10-14 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Bcl\-2 is critical for tumour survival and serves as a promising anticancer drug target\. Herein, a series of novel Bcl\-2\-targeted peptides were identified through structure\-based virtual screening and systematic biological validation\. Four candidate peptides were selected from the peptide library\. MST results demonstrated that Peptide 1 exhibited the strongest apparent binding affinity towards Bcl\-2 \(Kd = 0\.51 ± 0\.03 μM\), superior to VS\-9 and other screened peptides\. Molecular dynamics simulation, free energy landscape analysis, and MM/GBSA calculation further supported the predicted conformational features of the Bcl\-2–Peptide\-1 complex\. MTT assays demonstrated that Peptide\-1 markedly inhibited the proliferation of HT29 and Colo320HSR colorectal cancer cells, while showing limited effects on normal NCM460 cells\. Bcl\-2 knockdown attenuated the bioactivity of Peptide\-1, and RT\-qPCR and ELISA analyses revealed that Peptide\-1 modulated Bax/Bcl\-2 expression at both transcriptional and protein levels\. Peptide\-1 represents a promising Bcl\-2\-targeting peptide with antiproliferative activity against colorectal cancer cells\.
