# Sistema integrado de trazabilidad y programación inteligente para la clasificación de residuos basado en IoT y blockchain

**Autores:** Li QianKun; Wang Qiming; Zhang Aohan; Shuo Wang; Han Bolin; Alejandro Miguel Güemes Esperón; Martha Dunia Delgado Dapena

**Data:** 2026-10-15 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

In the context of the &quot;Entropy Divide Era — Intelligent Sorting Cloud Chain for Urban Waste Resources&quot; project, the current situation of intelligent waste classification equipment often leads to a phenomenon of &quot;front\-end separation, back\-end mixing\.&quot; To address this issue, this study proposes a full\-process green processing system based on IoT and blockchain technologies\. This system integrates intelligent identification, classified collection, targeted transportation, and professional treatment, forming a closed\-loop waste classification processing system that enables intelligent sorting of urban waste resources\. Through the establishment of a targeted processing network for classified waste, the system ensures that different types of waste receive specialized and resource\-oriented treatment, thereby achieving the goals of waste reduction, resource recovery, and harmless disposal\. This approach resolves the &quot;last mile&quot; problem of waste classification and offers a new strategy for urban solid waste management\.
