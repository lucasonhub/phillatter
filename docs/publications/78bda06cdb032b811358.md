# Nagal, Raphael Kevin ; Trias, Vyndyll &amp; Agustin, Joan: REACH Emergency Rescue Signal Bot: A Patent Based Technical Disclosure and Philosophical Analysis of Emergency Communication

**Autores:** 

**Data de descoberta:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** unknown

## Descrição do feed

\_Bibliographic Information of Patent Applications \(Ipophl\)\_ 28 \(26\)\. 2026REACH, an acronym for Recognize, Extend, Action, Call, Help is on the way, is a portable emergency signaling prototype that combines an Arduino Nano microcontroller, a NEO\-6M Global Positioning System receiver, a GSM communication module, two push buttons, and an external power supply\. One button requests the transmission of geographic coordinates by Short Message Service, while the other initiates a telephone call to a preconfigured contact\. This paper develops a scholarly technical disclosure from Philippine invention application 1/2023/050434, filed on August 12, 2023, and published on March 3, 2025\. It explains the architecture, operating sequence, documented interfaces, and intended applications, and examines the device as a case in the philosophy of technology and social epistemology\. The central argument is that an emergency signal becomes useful evidence only when its location data are current, its transmission is verified, and its recipient can act\. A simple interface may support user agency, but access to a device alone does not establish effective access to assistance\. The disclosure identifies inconsistencies in the GPS wiring descriptions and button logic, together with unresolved questions about serial communication, power, and confirmation of delivery\. A prospective evaluation framework distinguishes prototype construction, technical reliability, usability, and rescue outcomes\. REACH is therefore presented as a documented invention application and prototype whose practical benefits require systematic validation, rather than as a clinically validated rescue system or an established patent grant\. \(direct link \)
