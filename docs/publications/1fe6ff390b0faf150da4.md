# Machine\-learning surrogates enable rapid prediction of 3\-D substrate gradients in industrial bioreactors

**Autores:** Víctor Puig\-I\-Laborda; Jorge Carrasco Muriel; Andrea Castañeda Tena; Teddy Groves; Johan le Nepvou de Carfort; Ulrich Krühne; Krist V\. Gernaey; Cees Haringa; Ignasi Puig‐de‐Dou; Lars Keld Nielsen

**Data:** 2026-10-15 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Abstract High\-fidelity computational fluid dynamics \(CFD\) provide detailed substrate gradient predictions in industrial stirred\-tank bioreactors but are computationally intensive, limiting their routine application\. We developed surrogate models trained on $$760$$ 760 CFD simulations to rapidly predict fully 3\-D substrate concentration fields from global operating conditions \(e\.g\., stirring speed, feeding strategy, feeding point location, fluid density and viscosity, liquid height, bio\-kinetic parameters, biomass concentration\), and local spatial descriptors \(e\.g\., distance to the feeding point\)\. Among various methods, attention\-based neural networks achieved the highest accuracy \(test $$\{\{\{\\rm\{R\}\}\}\}^\{2\}\\approx 0\.91$$ R 2 ≈ 0\.91 \), capturing essential mixing features and enabling millisecond\-scale inference—up to a million\-fold speed\-up compared to CFD\. These surrogates facilitate rapid exploration of operating spaces, supporting real\-time optimization and digital\-twin frameworks in bioprocessing\. Our results demonstrate that data\-driven models can effectively couple hydrodynamics and biokinetics to provide fast, accurate spatial predictions, advancing capabilities for industrial bioreactor design and control\.
