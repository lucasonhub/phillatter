# Electromagnetic transport dynamics of micropolar Casson nanofluids over an inclined surface: Interplay of hall current and ion slip effects

**Autores:** M\. Matangi; M\. Deivanayaki

**Data:** 2026-10-09 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

This study investigates the electromagnetic transport dynamics of a micropolar Casson nanofluid flowing over an inclined surface by incorporating Hall current and ion slip mechanisms\. The developed mathematical model accounts for momentum, microrotation, thermal energy, and nanoparticle concentration transport under boundary conditions, providing a comprehensive description of the underlying EMHD transport phenomena\. Similarity transformations are employed to convert the governing nonlinear partial differential equations into a coupled system of ordinary differential equations, which are solved numerically using the fourth\-order Runge–Kutta shooting algorithm in MATLAB subject to the rigorous adherence to isothermal and isoconcentration wall conditions\. This framework enables the simultaneous consideration of localized nanoparticle spin degrees of freedom and macro\-rheological yield stress boundaries under the combined influence of Hall current and ion\-slip parameters\. Increasing the Casson parameter from B = 1 to B = 5 reduces fluid yield stress and plastic viscosity, according to parametric studies\. This causes the skin\-friction coefficient C fx \( 0 \) to change from 0\.057178 to − 0\.09464 \(a net drop of 0\.1518 \)\. At the same time, the wall couple stress gradient h ′ \( 0 \) decreases from 2\.0077 to 1\.5961 \. The reduced Nusselt number − θ ′ \( 0 \) increases from 1\.5346 to 3\.4204 when the thermal relaxation parameter is raised from 0\.5 to 1\.0, and the mass flux rate − ϕ ′ \( 0 \) rises from 2\.9751 to 4\.0211 when the solutal relaxation parameter is raised from 0\.5 to 0\.7\. Numerical accuracy is established within a maximum relative error of 0\.0447% through dual\-solver validation against the Khan and Pop benchmark\.
