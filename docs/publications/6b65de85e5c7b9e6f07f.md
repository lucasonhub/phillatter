# Synergistic Phase Transition and Defect Engineering Enables High‐Sensitivity Optical Thermometry in Ferroelectric Oxides

**Autores:** Jie Yang; Tao Zhou; Yuyan Deng; Xiahong Lin; Haiqin Sun; Pei Lin Han; Fuchi Liu; Qiwei Zhang

**Data:** 2026-10-14 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

ABSTRACT High‐sensitivity optical thermometry based on non‐thermally coupled levels \(NTCLs\) is vital for advanced remote sensing\. However, achieving robust anti‐thermal luminescent quenching remains a significant challenge by relying solely on negative thermal expansion effect or defect engineering designs, owing to material scarcity and limited carrier compensation\. For this, we propose a novel synergistic strategy that integrates ferroelectric phase transitions with defect engineering in NaNbO 3 :Nd 3\+ /Yb 3\+ \. This approach leverages oxygen vacancies for carrier storage and release, combined with polymorphic phase transitions to modulate local crystal‐field symmetry, effectively offsetting non‐radiative energy relaxation\. Consequently, our results demonstrate a 654‐fold luminescent enhancement at 673 K\. This dramatic enhancement significantly enhances the fluorescence intensity ratio of Nd 3\+ ions \(498 nm/683 nm\), yielding a high relative sensitivity of 4\.3% K −1 and absolute sensitivity of 9\.38 K −1 \. These results not only offer a novel strategy for designing thermally enhanced upconversion luminescent materials, but also reveal the significant potential of ferroelectric oxides as promising candidates for high‐temperature optical thermometry\.
