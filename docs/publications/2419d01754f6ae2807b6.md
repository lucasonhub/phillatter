# Sequential Approach in the Treatment of Extensive Odontogenic Cysts: From Decompression to Excision, a Case Report

**Autores:** Constança Monteiro Lopes; Bibiana Assunção; José Pedro Barbosa; Carolina Carreiro; Pedro Cabeça Santos; Mariana Magalhães Maia

**Data:** 2026-10-09 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Odontogenic inflammatory cysts are benign lesions that often arise from chronic periapical inflammation\. They are asymptomatic and grow without causing immediate symptoms\. These cysts can pose diagnostic challenges due to their potential for significant expansion and the need for differentiation from other maxillary lesions\. Histological examination remains essential for definitive diagnosis and treatment requires a personalized approach, considering cyst size, tooth involvement and patient compliance\. This case report presents the management of an extensive odontogenic inflammatory cyst in a 50\-year\-old male smoker with a history of ulcerative colitis\. The patient presented a small maxillary lesion and an extensive mandibular one, both causing bone deformation, without sensory or infectious symptoms\. The patient underwent decompression and biopsy of the mandibular lesion, followed by endodontic treatments and cystectomy\. The outcome was favorable with no complications or recurrence\. This case highlights the importance of a sequential treatment approach to avoid complications and preserve relevant structures, demonstrating the efficacy of decompression in reducing bone defects and facilitating subsequent surgical interventions\.
