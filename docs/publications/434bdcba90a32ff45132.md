# Mapping the knowledge landscape of artificial intelligence in political communication: a bibliometric and science mapping analysis

**Autores:** Muhammad Ridha Ramli; Gita Juniarti; Hari Eko Purwanto; Irman Puansah; Mamdukh Budiman; Muhammad Wildan Mauludy; Fitriana Sidikah Rachman

**Data:** 2026-10-12 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Artificial Intelligence \(AI\) has fundamentally transformed contemporary political communication by reshaping political campaigns, public opinion formation, information dissemination, and digital democratic engagement\. Despite the rapid expansion of scholarly interest in this interdisciplinary field, the existing literature remains fragmented across diverse themes and academic disciplines\. Therefore, this study aims to map the knowledge landscape of AI in political communication through a comprehensive bibliometric and science mapping analysis\. Data were retrieved from the Scopus database using the query TITLE\-ABS\-KEY \(“artificial intelligence”\) AND TITLE\-ABS\-KEY \(“political communication”\)\. Following the PRISMA 2020 framework, the initial 148 records were systematically screened and refined into 87 publications published between 2015 and 2025\. Bibliometric analysis was conducted using Bibliometrix and Biblioshiny in RStudio to examine scientific production, influential contributors, collaboration networks, and conceptual research trends\. The findings reveal a significant growth in AI\-related political communication research, particularly after 2023, with a remarkable publication surge in 2025, indicating the transition of the field from an emerging domain to a rapidly expanding research area\. The analysis identifies influential authors, publication sources, highly cited documents, leading institutions, and dominant countries contributing to the development of the field\. Furthermore, collaboration network analysis demonstrates the increasing internationalization of research activities, while conceptual structure analysis reveals evolving themes related to algorithmic communication, political misinformation, social media, generative AI, and digital democracy\. Overall, this study provides a comprehensive understanding of the intellectual structure and future research frontiers of AI in political communication and offers valuable insights for researchers, policymakers, and digital communication practitioners\.
