# Allium beesianum \(Amaryllidaceae\): A New Record for the Flora of India

**Autores:** Bipankar Hajong; SAMBIT CHOWDHURY; Anurag Paul; Pankaj Bharali

**Data:** 2026-10-12 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

ABSTRACT Allium beesianum W\.W\.Sm\. \(Amaryllidaceae\), a native of Southern China has been collected from Tawang district of Arunachal, India in 2025 is reported here as an addition to the Flora of India\. Morphological description, distribution map and a photo plate were provided here for its proper recognition\.
