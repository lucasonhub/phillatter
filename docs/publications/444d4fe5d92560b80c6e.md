# Anxiety Levels and Coping Strategies as Predictors of Psychological Well\-Being Among Health Workers in Gboko Local Government Area, Benue State, Nigeria

**Autores:** Aondongu Iulun

**Data:** 2026-10-13 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

This study investigated anxiety levels and coping strategies as predictors of psychological well being among health workers in Gboko Local Government Area of Benue state\. The study adopted a cross\-sectional survey research design where 184 participants were used\. The participants were 34 males \(18\.5%\) and 150 females \(81\.5%\) who were randomly selected for the study through simple random technique\. The Ryff’s Psychological Well\-being Scale, the State Trait Anxiety Inventory Forms X\-1 and X\-2 and the Coping Strategies Inventory were used for data collection\. Two research questions were raised to guide the study while two hypotheses were formulated and tested\. Data analysis involved the use of Pearson Product Moment Correlation and Multiple Linear Regression\. Results revealed that, there was a significant relationship between anxiety levels, coping strategies and psychological well\-being among health workers in Gboko Local Government Area\. Result also showed that, anxiety levels, and coping strategies significantly and jointly influenced psychological well\-being of health workers in Gboko\. It was concluded that, anxiety levels, and coping strategies are strong determinants of psychological well\-being among health workers in Gboko Local Government Area\. The study recommended that, government, non\-governmental organizations, and health institutions should frequently organize symposiums to educate heath workers more on anxiety management approaches as well as coping strategies should be made an integral part of health awareness by health institutions in Gboko Local Government Area to help in designing evidence\-based interventions and updating preventive strategies\.
