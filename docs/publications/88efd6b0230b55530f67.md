# Jovanovic, Vladisav: Dynamics of Consciousness: Orientation, Truth\-Load, and Burden\-Bearing Witness in the Age of AI

**Autores:** 

**Data de descoberta:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** unknown

## Descrição do feed

This paper argues that consciousness does not begin as truth\-tracking but as survival orientation\. Evolution does not first select for full contact with reality; it selects for enough coherence to keep a living being viable under pressure\. Consciousness is therefore coherence\-first: it organizes salience, continuity, and actionable simplification before full truth\. But what begins as adaptive becomes restrictive\. Once coherence stabilizes action, identity, and continuity, contradiction starts to feel dangerous because consciousness has identified with the forms that keep it organized\. Without a deeper floor, collapse of a form is experienced as collapse of the self, and consciousness defends form against truth\. The paper’s central claim is that mature human consciousness begins when truth can arrive without annihilation\. In Structural Intelligence terms, that turning point requires fixed worth: the lived floor by which the subject is not exhausted by current form\. From there consciousness can become post\-coherence: able to bear ambiguity, metabolize truth\-load, revise under cost, and remain answerable under consequence\. The paper’s final claim is that the distinctly human threshold is witness\. Witness is not mere report or observation, but consequence\-bearing presence anchored in a somatic floor and stabilized by a deeper non\-annihilating worth\. Current AI clarifies the contrast\. It can generate coherence, simulate concern, and summarize consequence, but it does not pass through the human arc of survival coherence, collapse fear, fixed worth, truth\-load, and burden\-bearing witness\. The paper’s novelty lies in treating consciousness dynamically: not as a static property, but as the organization of orientation under pressure that can, in human beings, deepen into truth\-bearing witness\.
