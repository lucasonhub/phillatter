# Data drift monitoring in the maintenance of cascade software for detecting falsified digital information

**Autores:** Артем Ховрат; Volodymyr Kobziev

**Data:** 2026-10-17 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

The article considers the maintenance stage of cascade software for detecting falsified digital information, in which inexpensive classifiers resolve most messages and pass uncertain ones to a large language model\. After deployment, the distribution of messages changes because of new narratives, new text generators and deliberate adaptation of adversaries, while true labels arrive with a delay or not at all\. A system of seven monitoring signals is proposed, each linked to a component of the cascade and to the type of drift it reveals, and only one of them requires true labels\. It is shown that in a cascade a covariate shift manifests primarily as growth of the escalation share, so that quality may remain stable while processing cost increases\. To detect confident errors of the first tier that leave the escalation share unchanged, shadow escalation of a small random sample of first\-tier decisions is proposed\. An alarm rule based on non\-overlapping confidence intervals and a graded response policy, from threshold recalibration to retraining and rollback, are described\.
