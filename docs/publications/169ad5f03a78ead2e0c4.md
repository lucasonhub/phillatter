# HIGHER EDUCATION TEACHING PROFESSION: TRAINING, PRACTICES, AND INNOVATION

**Autores:** Alcimar de Lima Marques Filho; Angela Monteiro Simão; Diego Macedo Almeida; Anna Cristhina Barbosa Massulo; Lena Marina Moreira Puga Barbosa; Valdenilson de Souza Ribeiro; Silvya Karen de Carvalho Martins; Aylin Cynthia de Carvalho Lopes

**Data:** 2026-10-14 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

This article analyzes how teacher education, pedagogical practices, and innovation interact in the reconfiguration of the teaching profession in higher education\. It is a qualitative theoretical\-conceptual study supported by structured bibliographic research and an audit of the references used\. The corpus comprises seminal works, reviews, meta\-analyses, empirical studies, and institutional documents addressing teacher professionalism and identity, professional development, pedagogical practices, digital competence, and generative artificial intelligence\. The interpretive synthesis indicates that disciplinary expertise, although indispensable, does not exhaust the competence required for teaching; professional development depends on the interaction among education, experience, reflection, and context; and pedagogical innovation should not be equated with technological novelty\. The analysis also shows that digital competence involves pedagogical judgment and that the diffusion of generative artificial intelligence adds new demands related to verification, contextualization, ethics, and learning mediation\. As a theoretical contribution, the article proposes the Integrative Model for the Reconfiguration of University Teaching \(MIRDU\), which organizes education, practice, reflection, and innovation as recursive components of professional development, conditioned by institutional, pedagogical, and technological factors\. The model is presented as a conceptual proposition to be empirically validated in future studies\.
