# Advances in deep inspiration breath\-hold technique for radiotherapy of right\-sided breast cancer

**Autores:** Wanyi Chen; Zi\-yang Gan; Yuwei Cai; Zhi\-Qiang Hua; Liang Yi

**Data:** 2026-10-09 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Deep inspiration breath\-hold \(DIBH\) has become increasingly common in the postoperative radiotherapy of left\-sided breast cancer, owing to its well\-established role in sparing the heart and ipsilateral lung\.For right\-sided tumors, however, the clinical benefit of this technique is considerably less well defined\.In most studies to date, DIBH has been shown to reduce both V20 and mean lung dose in right\-sided breast cancer, with larger gains observed when regional nodal irradiation is included\.The liver also appears to derive a dosimetric advantage\.Cardiac findings are more mixed: While some reports indicate measurable reductions in whole\-heart and right coronary artery doses, the decreases are typically small and exhibit substantial interpatient variability\.Meanwhile, contralateral breast doses seem largely unaffected\.Regarding breath\-hold monitoring, the vast majority of investigations have utilized real\-time position management or the optical surface monitoring system, whereas active breathing control has seen comparatively limited application in this context\.Taken together, the existing evidence supports the use of right\-sided DIBH for lung and liver protection; however, its value for the heart and contralateral breast remains uncertain, and further prospective studies are warranted to clarify these issues\.
