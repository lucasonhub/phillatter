# Neurograf v1: An End\-to\-End Romanian\-First Language Model Family

**Autores:** Ionut Adrian Rotaru

**Data:** 2026-10-09 · **Tipo:** preprint

## Resumo

We present Neurograf v1, an experimental end\-to\-end Romanian\-first language\-model pipeline developed to investigate the feasibility of training language models from scratch for a lower\-resource European language\. The pipeline comprises RoSfera, a 635 GB Romanian corpus containing 175\.7 million documents; an 80k byte\-level BPE tokenizer; five decoder\-only models ranging from 160M to 6B parameters, trained on 131 billion tokens; three instruction\-tuned assistants; and two contrastively trained embedding models\. Training and evaluation were conducted using computing infrastructure at ELI\-NP/IFIN\-HH\. The model family exhibits consistent scaling of validation loss and increasing Romanian factual and cultural knowledge with model size\. On the 1,000\-question WWTBM benchmark, Neurograf\-6B achieves 69\.89% accuracy on Romanian\-context questions, while Neurograf\-3\.2B reaches 67\.38%\. Compute\-optimal analysis yields a parameter\-scaling exponent of approximately 0\.423\. Neurograf v1 serves as an experimental demonstrator rather than a production system\. The study identifies practical challenges involving diacritic preservation, corpus quality, data composition, and tokenization efficiency, while demonstrating the feasibility of developing Romanian\-first base models, instruction\-following assistants, and semantic embedding models within a unified training pipeline\.
