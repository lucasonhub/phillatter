# Adjoint\-based corrective approach for nodal indoor air quality predictions

**Autores:** Nicolas Carnec; Martin Ferrand; Thierry Duforestel; Bénédicte Wall\-Ribot; Marc Abadie; Karim Limam

**Data:** 2026-10-09 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Indoor Air Quality \(IAQ\) modelling in building simulations often relies on the nodal approach to evaluate exposure to airborne pollutants\. This approach assumes perfect mixing within each zone, which can lead to significant discrepancies compared to CFD predictions, especially under complex airflow configurations\. To address this limitation, this study proposes a correction model that modifies the nodal scalar mass balance using a correction factor derived from CFD results\. These factors are designed to recover CFD\-based spatial average concentrations at steady state while preserving scalar mass conservation through consistency with steady state CFD outlet scalar mass fluxes\. To limit the computational cost, an adjoint scalar transport approach is used, enabling the computation of correction factors for all possible emission positions from a number of CFD simulations only equal to twice the number of outlets, for a given geometry and airflow configuration\. The method is evaluated on a multizone residential case under isothermal conditions\. The results show that the proposed approach significantly improves the prediction of mean concentrations compared to the classical nodal approach, while maintaining a much lower computational cost than forward CFD simulations\. Residual discrepancies are mainly associated with transient behaviour, due to the simplified dynamics of the nodal formulation\. These results highlight the potential of the method for improving scalar concentration predictions in practical isothermal applications, while also emphasizing that the correction factors remain dependent on the airflow configuration\.
