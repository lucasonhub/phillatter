# ВЛИЯНИЕ ЦИФРОВЫХ ТЕХНОЛОГИЙ НА СОВРЕМЕННЫЙ ЯЗЫК И ПОВСЕДНЕВНУЮ ЛЕКСИКУ

**Autores:** Мауленов Райымбек

**Data:** 2026-10-15 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

This article examines the influence of digital technologies on modern language, everyday vocabulary and linguistic research\. It discusses digital communication, digital neologisms, word\-formation processes, Internet slang, semantic change and the role of corpus linguistics, artificial intelligence and translation tools in language studies\. The article concludes that digitalization is a broader transformation of linguistic communication, while human interpretation remains important\.
