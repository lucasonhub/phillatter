# Digital Transformation Capability and Artificial Intelligence Adoption: Investigating Their Effects on Organizational Resilience and Sustainable Business Performance in the Manufacturing Industry

**Autores:** Fitrahtul Saahira Khairunnisa; Khodijah Asshofiah; Maryam Maryam; Humairourrohmah binti Mastor; Maya Ika Trisnawati

**Data:** 2026-10-14 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

This study examines the effects of digital transformation capability and artificial intelligence adoption on organizational resilience and sustainable business performance in the manufacturing industry\. Using a quantitative explanatory design, data were collected through structured questionnaires and analyzed using Partial Least Squares Structural Equation Modeling \(PLS\-SEM\)\. The findings indicate that digital transformation capability positively strengthens organizational resilience by improving process integration, information visibility, organizational flexibility, and data\-driven decision\-making\. Artificial intelligence adoption also contributes significantly to resilience through predictive analytics, automation, risk detection, and faster managerial responses to operational disruptions\. Furthermore, organizational resilience exerts a positive influence on sustainable business performance by supporting operational continuity, innovation, resource efficiency, economic stability, and long\-term competitiveness\. These results highlight organizational resilience as a strategic mechanism through which digital and AI capabilities generate sustainable organizational value\. The study contributes to the literature by integrating technological capability, resilience, and sustainability within a unified manufacturing framework\. Managerially, the findings suggest that manufacturers should align digital infrastructure, AI implementation, employee competencies, and adaptive capabilities to strengthen resilience and achieve sustainable performance under increasingly dynamic and technology\-intensive competitive business environments\.
