# Saba, Faz: The Unconscious Is Everything You Almost Were

**Autores:** 

**Data de descoberta:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** unknown

## Descrição do feed

\- Unconsciousness is not too complicated to use in everyday talk, but not so simple that we can put a bridle on it\. It sits somewhere between mystery and familiarity, between the reachable and the untouchable\. Enough depth to keep pulling you in, enough vagueness to never let you settle\. Philosophy, psychology, neuroscience, sociology — everyone tries to catch it, define it, cage it\. \- We usually picture it as a layer beneath consciousness\. Something underneath\. Something hidden\. Something that navigates our behaviour while we look somewhere else\. The iceberg picture\. The shadow\. The &quot;real you&quot; behind the scenes\. \- This article tries to overturn this picture and, at the same time, redefine it within a new framework: \- Try to overturn: Unconsciousness is not a layer beneath consciousness\. It is not a hidden room/castle behind our vision\. It is not a process that makes/causes/touches all of our consciousness states\. We are not passengers driven by unconsciousness\. \- Instead: Unconsciousness is a collection of mental states that are unmanifested/potential\. Every mental state could be an unconscious state; it doesn&\#x27;t matter how important or how deep it is for us, or whether it is just a simple, everyday mental state\. It changes constantly; every moment, our unconsciousness is different from the moment before\. Distance from what we could be at a given moment is central to defining unconsciousness\. \- From here, two questions arise, both at the heart of this paper: What is the meaning beneath consciousness? Or, where is unconsciousness? And also, what is unconsciousness made of? Is it just a process of mind/brain, or mental states, or just some huge emotional experience, or\.\.\.?
