# Processing Citizen Input for Public Purpose Design: A Framework for Distinguishing Problems, Desired Conditions, and Proposed Solutions

**Autores:** Ruben Junca

**Data:** 2026-10-13 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Public participation can generate large volumes of heterogeneous citizen input, yet its analytical treatment before aggregation remains under\-specified\. This article develops a statement\-level framework that distinguishes expressed problems, desired conditions, and proposed solutions as non\-exclusive forms of participatory content\. The framework is illustrated through 100 challenge–solution pairs from Colombia’s Regional Binding Dialogues for the 2022–2026 National Development Plan\. The application shows that administrative labels do not reliably identify substantive content, desired conditions may remain implicit or indeterminate, some recorded proposals contain no identifiable means, and desired conditions and proposed solutions do not correspond one\-to\-one\. The framework supports conservative aggregation of substantively equivalent desired conditions while retaining proposed solutions as candidate interventions\. It offers governments a transparent and auditable method for disaggregating citizen contributions, preserving situated knowledge, and tracing how problems, desired conditions, and proposed means inform subsequent political authorisation, instrument assessment, and governmental response\.
