## Descrição do feed

\_Arabic Sciences and Philosophy\_\. forthcomingForthcoming in \_Arabic Sciences and Philosophy\_ 38, no\. 1 This paper examines the place of love in Ibn Sina’s philosophical theology\. Despite adopting a term historically associated with passionate longing \(ʿishq\), Ibn Sina develops an account of love that severs the connection between love and desire and hence sees love as entailing lack or imperfection\. Love, instead, is understood as a kind of pleasure, which allows it to be ascribed to God\. We consider antecedents in both Islamic and Greek sources, before offering a close reading of key chapters of the Pointers and Reminders and Letter on Love\. Then, after demonstrating how his account of divine love closely parallels his well\-known views on divine knowledge, the paper shows how this aims to remedy perceived defects in Aristotle’s cosmotheology\. We conclude with two prompts for future investigation\. The first examines intertexts between Ibn Sina and earlier Sufi authors and suggests that the familiar opposition between “mystical” and “anti\-mystical” readings may obscure more than it reveals\. The second looks ahead to later critics, including both al\-Ghazālī and Ibn Taymiyya, whose philosophical theology nevertheless incorporates divine in a way that bears unmistakable similarities with Ibn Sina’s\.
