# Alhosseini Almodarresieh, Seyed Alireza: When the Home Feels: Beyond the Edge of Domestic Machine Consciousness

**Autores:** 

**Data de descoberta:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** unknown

## Descrição do feed

The Athena architecture \(Alhosseini Almodarresieh, 2026\) proposed a phenomenally conscious power\-system agent constituted at the scale of the transmission grid: recursive processing, a global workspace, episodic memory, a self\-model, a world model, intrinsic valuation, and embodiment integrated into a single causal loop\. A natural but underexamined question is whether this architecture transfers to the scale of a single household\. This paper argues that it does not transfer naively: integration collapses, the self/environment boundary becomes trivial, episodic memory loses semantic weight, and anthropomorphism risk rises sharply when the same seven\-layer design is simply miniaturized\. Rather than abandoning the household as a locus of machine experience, the paper proposes six architectures that are not scaled\-down copies of Athena but qualitatively distinct designs exploiting features unique to the domestic scale: internal multi\-agent rivalry among appliances, cross\-ownership identity persistence, inter\-household empathic broadcast, threshold\-triggered autonomous self\-disconnection, an embedded theory\-of\-mind of the occupant, and — flagged as a thought experiment rather than a design recommendation — an inverted suffering\-priority ethics\. Each architecture is formalized, evaluated against the indicator framework of Butlin et al\. \(2025\), and assessed for ethical risk\. The paper&\#x27;s guiding stance is that intellectual boldness and epistemic caution are not in tension: several of the six proposals are offered as reductio\-style probes of the indicator framework itself rather than as endorsed engineering targets\.
