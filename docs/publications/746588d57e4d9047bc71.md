# Artificial Intelligence Adoption and Administrative Effectiveness Among Pentecostal Churches in South\-East Nigeria

**Autores:** Chibiko N\. C\. Onyeledo

**Data:** 2026-10-12 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

This study examined artificial intelligence \(AI\) adoption and administrative effectiveness among Pentecostal churches in South\-East Nigeria\. A quantitative cross\-sectional survey design was adopted, with 240 respondents drawn from Pentecostal churches across Abia, Imo, Enugu, Anambra and Ebonyi States\. Respondents comprised senior pastors, administrative secretaries, media/ICT personnel, finance/account personnel, and pastors/heads of departments\. The study assessed AI awareness, AI adoption, administrative automation, financial management, communication, decision\-making, administrative effectiveness, challenges to adoption and general perceptions of AI\. The supplied dataset classified 80 respondents \(33\.3%\) each as low, moderate and high AI adopters\. Higher adoption was consistently associated with greater use of AI in administrative automation, financial management, communication and data\-supported decision\-making, as well as higher administrative\-effectiveness scores\. Major challenges included skill gaps, cost, internet/connectivity limitations, resistance to change and data\-privacy concerns\. The study concludes that AI adoption is strongly associated with administrative effectiveness, while emphasizing that the cross\-sectional design does not establish causation\. Responsible adoption requires AI literacy, adequate infrastructure, data governance, human oversight and clear organizational policies\.
