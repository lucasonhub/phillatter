# Set in Time — Mathematics for the Able and the Willing

**Autores:** Andrea Tenerini

**Data:** 2026-10-12 · **Tipo:** book

## Resumo

A set comes into being after its elements: from this idea, the cumulative hierarchy built stage by stage, &quot;Set in Time&quot; rebuilds the whole edifice of mathematics on a single foundation\. From just two primitive concepts, set and function, the book travels in six Parts through arithmetic and logic \(up to Gödel\), geometry, abstract algebra \(groups, rings, Galois theory, complex and hypercomplex numbers\), the arc of analysis travelled along two tracks, standard and non\-standard \(Robinson&\#x27;s hyperreals\), topology, functional and harmonic analysis, probability, differential geometry, the calculus of variations and optimal control\. The categorical viewpoint runs throughout: the arrow, of time, of the function, of the morphism, is the thread that binds\. What elsewhere is asserted, here is proved\. The real numbers are not postulated: they arise from the measurement of segments, along the path of Eudoxus and Hölder, rewritten so that no step uses what it is meant to found; the categoricity of the real field is a theorem, and the product of two lengths turns out to be an area\. The non\-Euclidean geometries are constructed: four models, and the independence of the fifth postulate proved\. Number theory reaches the prime number theorem, proved in full, and Dedekind&\#x27;s ideals; algebraic geometry reaches schemes and the Riemann\-Roch theorem\. An entire Part carries the method into mathematical physics: Lagrangian and Hamiltonian mechanics, Sturm\-Liouville theory, spectral theory of operators, the equations of mathematical physics\. The last Part crowns the treatise with theoretical computer science and computation: computability \(Turing machines, the halting problem, the bridge to Gödel\), complexity \(P/NP, Cook\-Levin\), the lambda calculus and the Curry\-Howard correspondence, Lean 4 \(where proofs are programs and the book&\#x27;s theorems are formalized\), Rust, Scala 3 and Python\. Laboratories close the journey with applications: machine learning and neural networks, a Transformer built from scratch, distributed computing on big data, cryptography, verification and error\-correcting codes\. And it trains\. Every exercise is worked out in full, and six gymnasia gather further ones in four levels: checking, manoeuvre, problems, research\. 669 counterexamples, placed next to the hypothesis they measure, show what happens when a hypothesis is dropped; an appendix brings the same discipline to bear on an open problem, the Goldbach conjecture\. The footnotes, with history, alternative proofs, objections and bridges to physics and philosophy, are a second book within the book\. It is a book that takes a stand, promises no shortcuts, and repays attention with understanding\. For university students, teachers and every ambitious self\-learner: mathematics for the able and the willing, for those unafraid of hard work\.
