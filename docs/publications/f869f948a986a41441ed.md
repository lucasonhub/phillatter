# Optimizing electrospun membranes for heavy metal removal from wastewater; a combined machine learning and experimental approach

**Autores:** Fatemeh Sarhadi; Meisam Nouri; Hamid Esfahani; Moslem Noori; Shengming Jin

**Data:** 2026-10-10 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

The presence of heavy metals in wastewater is a serious environmental challenge, and removal of such elements with the help of advanced membrane techniques is of high importance\. In this research, the effective parameters for heavy metal removal from an aqueous solution by means of an electrospun membrane were investigated using machine learning \(ML\) algorithms\. Among the algorithms, decision tree \(DT\), random forest \(RF\), K\-nearest neighbours \(KNN\) and gradient boosting \(GB\) were employed to model the relationship between the electrospinning and ultra\-filtration parameters and removal rate\. The GB algorithm showed the best performance with an R² value of 0\.843 and a mean absolute percentage error of 0\.119\. Shapley additive explanations \(SHAP\) method was used to evaluate the effects of different parameters and properties of the aqueous solution on the heavy metal removal performance and the flux of the aqueous solution was identified as the most effective variable\. To evaluate the prediction capability of the developed model, the ultra\-filtration test was studied with an electrospun Polyamide\-6 \(PA6\) containing 9 wt\. % TiO 2 nanoparticle \(NPs\) membrane suggested by optimized conditions\. Based on the experimental results, 90\.49% of the copper present in the aqueous solution was removed by the fabricated membrane\. To evaluate the prediction capability of the developed model, an electrospun PA6–TiO 2 membrane was fabricated under the model\-guided conditions and tested for Cu 2\+ removal\. The fabricated membrane achieved 90\.49% removal, while the model predicted 88\.39% under the final experimental input conditions\. This comparison was used as a preliminary experimental assessment of the model\. Finally, a graphical user interface \(GUI\) was developed to predict removal ability of heavy metals using variables of electrospinning process, ultra\-filtration test and aqueous solution properties\.
