# Asymptotic mixed graph convolutional with trend\-aware self\-attention networks for traffic flow forecasting

**Autores:** Hongyan Wang

**Data:** 2026-10-15 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Accurate traffic flow forecasting plays a critical role in enhancing the efficiency and safety of transportation systems\. Nevertheless, this task remains challenging due to the complex spatio\-temporal correlations embedded in traffic data\. Existing adaptive\-graph\-based methods usually learn graph structures during training, making them insufficiently responsive to testing\-phase traffic states\. Consequently, their forecasting performance may deteriorate under unexpected fluctuations and irregular traffic variations\. To overcome this limitation, this paper proposes a novel Asymptotic Mixed Graph Convolutional with Trend\-Aware Self\-Attention Network \(AMG\-TSA\)\. By learning trend similarities among traffic nodes, AMG\-TSA constructs an asymptotic adjacency matrix to support both asymptotic structure\-aware graph convolution and asymptotic dynamic graph convolution, enabling synchronized modeling of dynamic spatial dependencies\. Meanwhile, a trend\-aware self\-attention module is introduced to capture local contextual patterns and nonlinear temporal dependencies, while dilated causal convolution enlarges the temporal receptive field and supports long\-range temporal dependency modeling\. Experiments on two real\-world traffic datasets show that AMG\-TSA consistently outperforms representative baselines, demonstrating its effectiveness in traffic flow forecasting\.
