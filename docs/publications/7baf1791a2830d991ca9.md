# Howell, Robert J\.: The Base Problem

**Autores:** 

**Data de descoberta:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** unknown

## Descrição do feed

In Consciousness and the Limits of Objectivity: The Case for Subjective Physicalism\. Oxford: Oxford University Press\. pp\. 13\-40\. 2013Even though the problem of consciousness is often framed as the question of whether consciousness is physical, there is little agreement over what that even means\. Descartes thought that to be physical is to be extended in space, but arguably electrons do not fit that definition\. Physics now posits things like electromagnetic fields, multi\-dimensional strings, and other oddities\. But we can’t say that to be physical is to be posited by current physics, because current physics is incomplete, and talk of future physics is vague\. Some philosophers despair that there is no way to capture what it is to be physical, and some even think this shows that the mind–body problem is ill formed\. This chapter provides a novel account of what it takes to be a physical property according to which physical properties are those which are exhaustively characterized by their spatio\-temporal implications\.
