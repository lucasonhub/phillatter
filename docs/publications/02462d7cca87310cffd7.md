# Prediction of Material Removal Rate for Chemical Mechanical Planarization using Bayesian Additive Regression Trees

**Autores:** Han Bao; Xiyuan Qian

**Data:** 2026-10-13 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Chemical Mechanical Planarization \(CMP\) is a critical surface processing technique in semiconductor manufacturing, where the Material Removal Rate \(MRR\) plays a vital role in determining process quality\. Traditional physics\-driven MRR models based on Preston&\#x27;s Equation have difficulty capturing the complex nonlinear relationships and high\-order interactions present in CMP data\. This paper applies Bayesian Additive Regression Trees \(BART\) to CMP MRR prediction\. BART uses a Bayesian sum\-of\-trees framework to model nonlinear effects and interactions while providing posterior uncertainty estimates and variable\-importance information\. Quantitative comparisons on the validation set show that BART achieves the best predictive performance, outperforming the strongest baseline model by 11% in terms of RMSE\. Model reliability and interpretation are examined through residual diagnostics, MCMC convergence checks, hyperparameter\-sensitivity analysis, omnibus covariate testing, and variable\-importance analysis\. These results indicate that BART is effective for CMP MRR prediction and can help identify process variables that warrant monitoring and diagnostic attention\.
