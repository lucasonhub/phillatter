# ZanIA: aplicación multiplataforma basada en inteligencia artificial para el análisis nutricional de alimentos por imagen conforme a la NOM\-043\-SSA2\-2012

**Autores:** Felipe Cocón; Dámaris Pérez\-Cruz; Fátima Gisell Castellanos Flores; Arthemisa Nicte Ha Nettel Cabello; Víctor Hugo Hernández Hernández

**Data:** 2026-10-14 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Overweight and obesity represent a major public health problem in Mexico: according to the 2023 ENSANUT Continua survey, 37\.3% of adults are overweight and 38\.9% are obese, while combined prevalence among adolescents reaches 40\.4%\. Given the lack of accessible tools that translate official nutritional regulations into practical guidance, ZanIA was developed—a multiplatform application that uses artificial intelligence \(Gemini model\) to analyze photographs of meals and generate nutritional information \(calories, protein, fat, and carbohydrates\) aligned with the NOM\-043\-SSA2\-2012 standard\. Development followed a four\-phase experimental methodology: design and planning, development and implementation, testing, and results analysis\. The prototype, built with Python and Streamlit, integrates a BMI calculator, a recipe generator, and the &quot;Platosaurio,&quot; a dinosaur\-shaped physical device that photographs the meal and, through traffic\-light\-style LED lights, indicates and rates its nutritional balance according to Mexico&\#x27;s &quot;Plato del Bien Comer&quot; scheme\. Usability testing, conducted through direct observation and verbal feedback with a small group of five people, showed an interface perceived as intuitive and a clearly useful value proposition, although no large\-scale external validation or standardized instrument was used\. The project demonstrates technical feasibility and lays the groundwork for future validation with real users\.
