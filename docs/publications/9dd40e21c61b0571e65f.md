# DashGuard: spatio\-temporal deep learning for dashcam\-based road accident anticipation

**Autores:** Lohith Kumar Pullur Somasekar; Ashmi Sinha; Nagasaketh Chintaluri; Bhuvaneswari Madasamy

**Data:** 2026-10-17 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Road traffic accidents claim roughly 1\.35 million lives each year, with delayed hazard perception rather than mechanical failure implicated in most collisions\. Dashcam\-based accident anticipation offers a low\-cost complement to sensor\-heavy ADAS, yet controlled comparisons across architectures remain rare\. We present DashGuard, which trains and evaluates six spatio\-temporal models a 3D CNN, CNN\+LSTM, CNN\+Transformer, and three two\-stream variants \(lightweight CNN, ResNet\-18, and Transformer\) on the Car Crash Dataset \(1500 crash and 2000 normal videos\) under a consistent, leak\-free protocol\. A video\-level 70/15/15 split prevents clip\-level contamination; four\-level soft labels encode temporal distance to the accident frame; and a two\-clip temporal consistency gate substantially cuts false\-positive alerts\. On the held\-out test set, the Two\-Stream Transformer achieves AUC = 0\.981 and F1 = 0\.870, with recall of 0\.991\. The lightweight 3D CNN \(AUC = 0\.949, \\\(\\approx \\\) 180 K parameters\) is a viable option for edge hardware\. A React/FastAPI web interface supports interactive evaluation\. The results show that explicitly separating motion and appearance via two\-stream attention gives the clearest gains over single\-stream designs on this task\.
