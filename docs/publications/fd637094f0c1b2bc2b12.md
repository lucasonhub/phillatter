# Adaptive conformal prediction on the Grassmannian

**Autores:** Dimitrios Loukrezis; Zachary J\. Grey; Dimitris G\. Giovanis

**Data:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** preprint

## Resumo

We introduce a conformal prediction framework for regression with Grassmann\-valued responses\. The proposed method constructs prediction regions in the form of metric balls directly on the Grassmann manifold, using basis\-invariant geometric distances as non\-conformity measures and scaling the corresponding non\-conformity scores through a learned prediction difficulty estimator\. This yields locally adaptive, input\-dependent prediction regions that respect the underlying manifold geometry, while retaining the finite\-sample marginal coverage guarantee of split conformal prediction under exchangeability\. We evaluate several Grassmannian distance metrics and compare their prediction\-region efficiency on synthetic benchmarks and an airfoil inverse shape\-design problem\. The results highlight the advantages of geometry\-aware non\-conformity scores, while local adaptation improves empirical coverage uniformity across prediction difficulty levels and, in strongly heteroscedastic settings, can substantially reduce prediction\-region size while maintaining the target coverage\.
