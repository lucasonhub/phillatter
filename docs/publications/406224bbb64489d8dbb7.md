# From Technology Users to Sustainability\-Oriented Learning Designers: Developing Science Teachers&\#x27; STEM–IoT Competencies through Professional Development

**Autores:** Diana Rochintaniawati; Rika Rafikah; Hanna Nurul Husna; Aay Susilawaty; Joey M\. Suba

**Data:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Purpose: This study examined whether an integrated STEM–Internet of Things \(IoT\)–Education for Sustainable Development \(ESD\) professional\-development program was associated with differences in junior secondary science teachers’ competencies at the group level\. Methods: A quantitative, pre\-experimental repeated cross\-sectional pretest–posttest design analyzed 29 valid pretest and 33 valid posttest responses from the MGMP IPA teacher community in Sumedang, Indonesia\. A researcher\-developed assessment comprised 10 multiple\-choice items and five constructed\-response items \(maximum total score = 100\)\. Descriptive statistics, normalized gain based on group means, Welch’s independent\-samples t\-test, Mann–Whitney U, and Hedges’ g were reported\. Findings: The mean total score was higher at posttest \(83\.24, SD = 11\.07\) than at pretest \(59\.34, SD = 16\.22\); the descriptive normalized gain was 0\.588\. Welch’s t\(48\.49\) = 6\.682 \(p &lt; \.001\), and Hedges’ g = 1\.72 indicated a large group\-level separation\. The largest multiple\-choice differences concerned the Blynk platform and ESP8266/Wi\-Fi modules, whereas constructed responses showed higher scores for sensor\-data interpretation and IoT–ESD project design\. Implications: Integrated technical practice, data interpretation, and pedagogical design may provide a useful structure for science\-teacher professional development\. These cross\-sectional comparisons cannot establish within\-teacher growth, program causality, or transfer to classroom practice\. Originality: The study examines factual, interpretive, and design\-oriented competencies within a combined STEM–IoT–ESD teacher\-learning program\.
