# Fujie, Naoto: What Does a World Model Set in Motion? Qwen\-AgentWorld, Simulated RL, Successor Possibilities, Real Penetration, Feedback, and Xenoscience

## Descrição do feed

\_Zenodo\_ 1\. 2026This paper asks what a world model actually sets in motion once it is embedded in an acting system rather than evaluated solely as an internal representation\. Using Qwen\-AgentWorld and simulated reinforcement\-learning environments as test cases, it analyzes world models in terms of the successor possibilities they generate, constrain, or eliminate through prediction, planning, action selection, and environmental feedback\. The paper distinguishes representational adequacy from operational consequence: two world models may encode similar descriptions while producing different reachable trajectories, interventions, and downstream states\. Real penetration refers to the process by which internally generated model differences propagate into subsequent physical, simulated, informational, or institutional configurations through action\. Feedback then alters the model’s later predictions, policies, and reachable possibilities, producing recursive model\-world coupling\. The analysis therefore treats world models not merely as passive maps of an environment but as components of dynamical loops that help determine which futures become practically available\. Xenoscience generalizes this framework across heterogeneous configurations by tracking when model differences remain non\-redundant under changes in task, resource, embodiment, temporal horizon, and environmental structure\. The broader claim is that a world model should be evaluated by the successor possibilities and real effects it helps generate, not only by the accuracy of its internal representation\. \(direct link \)
