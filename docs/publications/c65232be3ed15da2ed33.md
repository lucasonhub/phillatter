# Error Propagation in AI Models: How Small Mistakes Accumulate, Spread and Grow

**Autores:** Roman Shyrshov; Юрій Григорович Даник; Valery Shestakov

**Data:** 2026-10-09 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

This presentation examines the mechanisms through which errors originate, propagate, accumulate, and amplify in artificial intelligence models\. It distinguishes three interacting sources of error: biases and limitations in training data, hallucinations, and context fading\. Particular attention is paid to causal relationships between these error types and their implications for the reliability of AI\-generated outputs\. The presentation introduces methodological approaches to estimating error probabilities, assessing cumulative risks, and analyzing error propagation across reasoning steps, interconnected AI modules, and successive model generations\. It discusses error reproduction, feedback mechanisms, and strategies for monitoring and mitigating systemic failures\. The findings contribute to understanding AI reliability and risk assessment, particularly in applications involving cybersecurity, critical information infrastructure, and other high\-stakes decision\-making environments\. Presented in connection with the V International Conference “Challenges and Reality of the IT\-Space: Software Engineering and Cybersecurity” \(SECS\-2026\), October 29–30, 2026, Kyiv, Ukraine \(online\)\.
