# BaniRostam, Touraj: From Machine Intelligence to Distributed Representation

**Autores:** 

**Data de descoberta:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** unknown

## Descrição do feed

What is sufficient to attribute intelligence or understanding to a machine? Twentieth\-century debates offered several non\-equivalent answers\. Turing redirected inquiry towards assessable performance; computationalism characterised cognition in terms of organised processing; functionalism identified mental states through their causal roles; Searle challenged the inference from syntactic manipulation to semantic understanding; and connectionism modelled cognition through learned, distributed representations\. These positions are often examined separately or arranged as a linear sequence in which one paradigm supersedes another\. This paper instead reconstructs them historically and conceptually as addressing five distinct questions: what a system can do, how it computes, how its states are functionally organised, how information is represented, and whether its processing amounts to understanding\. Applied to contemporary artificial intelligence, particularly learning\-based systems, the framework distinguishes behavioural success, computational implementation, functional organisation, representation, and semantic understanding\. Evidence for one does not by itself establish the others\. The analysis offers a more disciplined way to state what an AI system has demonstrated, which explanatory claims follow, and where further premises are needed\. \(direct link \)
