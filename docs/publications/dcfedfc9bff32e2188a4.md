# SCIENTIFIC AND METHODOLOGICAL FOUNDATIONS FOR IMPLEMENTING ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE TECHNOLOGIES IN THE CONTEXT OF DIGITAL TRANSFORMATION

**Autores:** Abdullayeva Dilfuzaxon

**Data:** 2026-10-14 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

This thesis examines the scientific and methodological foundations for integrating artificial intelligence \(AI\) technologies into organizational processes under digital transformation\. Drawing on research in information systems, organizational AI readiness, software engineering, and risk management, it identifies the conditions under which AI adoption can generate measurable value\. The analysis distinguishes technological deployment from organizational transformation and considers data governance, model selection, validation, human oversight, and continuous monitoring as interdependent implementation requirements\. A conceptual, stage\-based approach is proposed for connecting organizational objectives with technical design and operational performance\. The contribution is methodological and analytical; no independent experimental outcomes or unverified performance improvements are claimed\.
