# Is AI\-integrated career readiness the new reality? A case study of undergraduates’ perceptions in Pakistan

**Autores:** Maham Mughal; Henna A\. Qureshi

**Data:** 2026-10-11 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

The rapid integration of AI into the labour market is reshaping the world of work and future career pathways\. Higher education must therefore embed AI\-informed career readiness to equip undergraduates with the skills and adaptability needed to thrive in an AI\-driven world of work\. Twenty\-one final\-year STEAM undergraduates took part in semi\-structured interviews through purposive and snowball sampling\. The interviews were recorded, transcribed, and coded concurrently, then analysed using Braun and Clarke’s Reflexive Thematic Analysis\. The findings of the study were: AI as an established academic tool; AI\-integrated career readiness is essential for preparing for emerging careers and building technical and soft skills that match labour market demands; advocating the use of AI to avoid potential risks; and AI integration requires addressing contextual challenges and government policy on AI in education\. Based on these findings, a framework was developed to help institutions, career services, and policymakers align curricula and support services with the AI\-integrated labour market\.
