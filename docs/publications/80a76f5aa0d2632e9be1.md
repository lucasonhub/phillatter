# Vakilo AI \- Legal Assistant Platform

**Autores:** Yogesh Bisane; Roshni Sharma; Samanta Narde; Prachi Pandhurkar; Aumkar Sarode; Mr\. Ashish Trivedi

**Data:** 2026-10-14 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Many people struggle to get legal help at the very start, because they cannot easily put their problem into legal terms or tell which lawyer would suit it\. Vakilo AI is a web platform we built to guide that first stage: it walks a user through case intake and puts them in touch with verified legal professionals\. The user describes the problem and chooses a category and an urgency level\. An intake module powered by Gemini then poses ten follow\-up questions and, from the answers, drafts a structured case summary with suggested next steps\. The platform also proposes lawyers who may fit the case, and each of them can look over the request before accepting or declining it\. Once a request is accepted, the two sides can chat privately, hold video consultations, book appointments, share case documents, and settle payments through Razorpay\. A lawyer must pass administrative verification of their profile before being allowed to accept any case\. The system is built from a Python FastAPI backend, a React front end, PostgreSQL and Supabase for data services, Socket\.IO for messaging, and WebRTC for video\. Vakilo AI is meant to bring order to the early stage of seeking legal help; what the AI generates is there to assist a qualified lawyer&\#x27;s advice, not to stand in for it
