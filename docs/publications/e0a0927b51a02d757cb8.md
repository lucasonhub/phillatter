# Strategic intelligence for emerging and critical technologies

**Autores:** Katherine Richardson; Renda Andrea; Pierre‐Alexandre Balland; Rainer Walz; Paweł Świeboda; Giuliana Battisti; Albert Bravo\-Biosca; Diego Comin; Béatrice Dumont; Andrea Filippetti; Emilija Stojmenova\-Duh

**Data:** 2026-10-12 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Emerging technologies are reshaping global competitiveness at speed, and the gap between early movers and late followers is widening\. For Europe, the cost of inaction \- or of poorly targeted action \- is lost industrial leadership, deepening strategic dependencies, and eroded sovereignty and security\. Yet Europe lacks two things it now urgently needs: the strategic intelligence to know which technologies to back and where it is genuinely well positioned, and the institutional infrastructure to deliver that intelligence systematically and translate it into bold, targeted funding and investment decisions\. This paper makes the case for both\. It argues that the institutionalisation of a strategic intelligence function is necessary in Europe’s current situation\. A dedicated observatory for emerging and critical technologies is one credible \- ambitious but achievable \- form that function can take\.
