# Label\-Free Biochemical Characterization and Subtype Classification of Human Breast Pathologies Using Fingerprint Raman Spectroscopy and Machine Learning

**Autores:** Nguyen Van Hung; Huu Thuan Mai; Ngo Duc Quan

**Data:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Breast cancer remains a major cause of cancer\-related mortality, highlighting the need for improved molecular characterization of breast pathologies\. Raman spectroscopy \(RS\) provides a label\-free, non\-destructive approach for probing tissue biochemical composition\. This study investigated Raman spectral alterations across five human breast histopathological categories and explored their potential for machine learning\-assisted classification\. Formalin\-fixed, paraffin\-embedded \(FFPE\) tissue specimens were obtained from five patients, with one patient representing each category: normal breast tissue, intraductal papilloma, high\-grade ductal carcinoma in situ \(DCIS\), invasive ductal carcinoma \(IDC\), and invasive lobular carcinoma \(ILC\)\. A total of 100 Raman spectra \(20 per patient\) were acquired using 785 nm excitation over the fingerprint region \(500–2000 cm−1\)\. Spectral preprocessing involved Savitzky–Golay smoothing, asymmetrically reweighted penalized least\-squares \(arPLS\) baseline correction, and vector normalization\. The investigated spectra exhibited reduced relative lipid\-associated contributions near 1063, 1300, and 1445 cm−1, accompanied by increased relative protein\- and nucleic\-acid\-associated features near 1003, 1420, and 1600–1650 cm−1\. The phenylalanine\-to\-lipid intensity ratio \(I1003/I1445\) increased from ≤0\.2 in normal tissue to ≥1\.5 in invasive lesions, suggesting a potential spectroscopic descriptor of biochemical remodeling\. Exploratory subtype\-associated differences were also observed, including a prominent aromatic C=C\-associated feature near 1618 cm−1 in IDC and an enhanced carbohydrate\-associated contribution near 890 cm−1 in ILC\. A support vector machine \(SVM\) classifier was evaluated using patient\-independent leave\-one\-patient\-out \(LOPO\) cross\-validation\. Across the five held\-out patients, the model correctly classified 89 of 100 spectra, achieving an overall spectrum\-level accuracy of 89\.0%, sensitivity of 90\.0%, specificity of 87\.5%, and F1\-score of 90\.8%\. Although these findings demonstrate encouraging discrimination within the investigated cohort, the limited number of biologically independent patients and the representation of each histopathological category by a single patient restrict the generalizability of the results\. This study provides preliminary proof\-of\-concept evidence supporting the integration of Raman\-derived biochemical features with machine learning for breast pathology characterization, warranting further validation in larger, multi\-patient and multicenter cohorts\.
