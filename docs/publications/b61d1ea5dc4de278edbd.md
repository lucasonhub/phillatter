# Fostering Student Engagement via a Community Outreach and Engagement Internship Program

**Autores:** Aisha Choudhri; Lucile Lauren Adams\-Campbell; Mary Mills; Mireille Bright; Jennifer Hicks; Jefferny Lin; Rebecca Baab Riggins; Chiranjeev Dash

**Data:** 2026-10-13 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

BACKGROUND: Community Outreach and Engagement \(COE\) is a mandated component of National Cancer Institute \(NCI\)\- designated comprehensive cancer centers\. Community partnerships are necessary to address cancer disparities, yet few examples of training programs exist to guide career development focused on community\-engaged cancer prevention\. We designed and assessed a COE internship to enhance workforce development through experiential learning in cancer prevention and control\. METHODS: We implemented a six\-week, part\-time COE internship that paired didactic training with hands\-on experience in patient navigation, community outreach and engagement, health education, and clinical research\. Undergraduate, graduate, and post\-baccalaureate students engaged in staff\-led learning and participated in outreach at the community\-level\. Intern experiences and self\-perceived competency development were assessed using post\-program surveys\. RESULTS: 35 interns successfully completed the COE internship between inception and Spring 2026\. Twenty\-eight interns responded to the program evaluation survey\. 92% of interns rated their overall experience as either excellent \(64%\) or very good \(28%\)\. The majority of interns reported significant competency development in communication, health education, and knowledge dissemination skills \(46%\)\. Awareness of community health disparities \(36%\), professional skills and interpersonal relations \(29%\), patient navigation and administration \(29%\), and research and analytical skills \(25%\) were also noted\. Themes identified through qualitative responses included appreciation for interdisciplinary mentorship and hands\-on experience\. CONCLUSIONS: Our COE internship provided students an opportunity to gain experience in community\-engaged cancer prevention\. Integration of COE components will continue to develop the necessary workforce skills for those pursuing careers in public health and cancer research\.
