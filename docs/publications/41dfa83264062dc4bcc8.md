# Niami, Morteza: The Monopoly of Form: From Institutional Stabilization to the Erosion of Agency

**Autores:** 

**Data de descoberta:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** unknown

## Descrição do feed

This article develops a philosophical analysis of the concept of &quot;monopoly of form&quot; as a mechanism through which a specific form of relational organization, via a combination of institutional, normative, and psychological mechanisms, progressively raises the cognitive and practical cost of conceiving and pursuing alternative forms to the point where those alternatives effectively disappear from the horizon of viable options\. The central thesis is that the monopoly of form operates through a &quot;functional shift of identity from descriptive to normative&quot;—where the existing form becomes a criterion for measuring the subject&\#x27;s own existential legitimacy\. The article distinguishes among stabilization of form, dominance of form, and monopoly of form, providing independent diagnostic indicators that demonstrate the monopoly of form is a mechanism distinct from concepts such as path dependence, habitus, and subjectivation\. The processual model proposed is not a linear chain but a map of combinable mechanisms: stabilization, dominance, normativity, and internalization\. The article also articulates &quot;existential energy extraction&quot; as a theoretical construct with four identifiable components: temporal occupation, attentional shift, affective consumption, and interpretive distortion\. Finally, the principle of relational formal neutrality is introduced as a limited and defensible normative implication of the analysis\. Keywords: monopoly of form, stabilization of form, dominance of form, horizon of social imaginability, social ontology, agency, institutional identity, automatic mask, path dependence, relational formal neutrality, finite continuity\. \(direct link \)
