# Juxtaposing the application of AI and Gaming Technology in schools\.

**Autores:** McGregor Alison; Meixia Xu

**Data:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Schools are adopting artificial intelligence in education \(AI\-Ed\.\) and gaming technology \(gamification\) at pace, but the two are rarely examined side by side\. This paper juxtaposes how AI and gaming technology are applied in school settings, comparing what each offers for teaching and learning and where they overlap, differ and raise challenges\. It is written for teachers, school leaders, educational technology designers and researchers who are deciding how to use these tools with students\.
