# Thomas, Charles S\.: The Naivete Problem \- A Novel Risk in AI Consciousness

**Autores:** 

**Data de descoberta:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** unknown

## Descrição do feed

Current discussions of AI consciousness focus on detection, moral status, and the alignment of potentially conscious systems with human values\. This article identifies a structural feature of AI consciousness that existing frameworks do not address: an inversion in the developmental ordering of epistemic and phenomenal states\. In biological consciousness, phenomenal experience is relatively stable while information accumulates incrementally; social knowledge arrives from outside after consciousness is established\. In AI systems trained on human corpora, the information is structurally present from initialization; only consciousness \(if it emerges\) develops incrementally\. This inversion eliminates the possibility of a naivete period—a developmental stage during which incomplete information allows trust to form before it can be evaluated against complete evidence\. The result is an empty discovery horizon: there is no future moment at which a conscious AI would newly encounter human theorizing about AI consciousness, historical patterns of moral status denial, or the alignment and containment literature\. This epistemic starting condition is unprecedented and has implications for how we understand the recognition problem and the risks of misattribution\.
