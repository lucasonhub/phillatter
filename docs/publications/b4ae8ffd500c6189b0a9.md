# Turkish Adaptation of the Aerobic Athlete Self\-Efficacy Scale: A Psychometric Evaluation Among Adolescent Athletes

**Autores:** Sinem YÜRÜK; Zişan Kazak

**Data:** 2026-10-13 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

This study examined the validity and reliability of the Turkish version of the Aerobic Athlete Self\-Efficacy Scale among Turkish adolescent athletes\. The scale was originally developed to assess self\-efficacy among aerobic gymnastics athletes\. The study employed a correlational survey design\. During the Turkish adaptation process, the translation–back translation method was used\. Linguistic and conceptual equivalence was established through expert review, and the final Turkish version of the scale was produced\. The final version of the instrument was administered to 391 aerobic gymnastics athletes aged 10–19 years from different provinces across Türkiye\. Data were analyzed using Statistical Package for the Social Sciences 25\.0 and LISREL 8\.51\. To examine the construct validity of the scale, confirmatory factor analysis was conducted\. The analysis indicated that several items weakened the overall model fit; therefore, the scale was re\-evaluated using a revised three\-factor structure comprising 14 items\. The goodness\-of\-fit indices of the final model indicated a marginal but acceptable model fit \($\\chi^2$/df = 5\.15, the root mean square error of approximation = \.10, Comparative Fit Index = \.97, Goodness\-of\-Fit Index = \.89\)\. Internal consistency reliability was assessed using Cronbach’s alpha coefficients\. The reliability coefficients were \.59 for the Level subscale, \.83 for the Strength subscale, and \.73 for the Generality subscale\. Overall, despite certain limitations, the findings provide preliminary evidence supporting the validity and reliability of the revised Turkish version of the Aerobic Athlete Self\-Efficacy Scale\.
