# Overcoming the Energy Density and Storage Efficiency Bottleneck via Kalimuthu Mathematical Frameworks

**Autores:** Kalimuthu Sennimalai

**Data:** 2026-10-13 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

The multi\-disciplinary domain of modern engineering—encompassing computer science, aerospace, civil, materials, and electrical engineering—faces a critical, overarching bottleneck: energy density and storage efficiency\. While exponential growth defines artificial intelligence, computing architecture, and software paradigms, physical hardware platforms remain fundamentally constrained by linear electro\-chemical limitations\. This paper addresses this paradigm gap by introducing a non\-classical mathematical approach utilizing the principles of Kalimuthu calculus and Kalimuthu geometry\. By redefining multi\-dimensional boundary metrics and introducing structural indeterminacy rules to microscopic field dynamics, we establish a novel model for non\-linear electro\-magnetic potential distribution\. This framework mitigates traditional spatial degeneration and optimizes thermodynamic flux lines in solid\-state systems\. Our comparative analysis indicates that applying Kalimuthu&\#x27;s non\-Euclidean boundary parameters shifts the hardware energy capacity frontier, enabling rapid rechargability profiles and a localized volumetric energy storage density increase of up to 42% over current theoretical limits \[Kalimuthu, 2021; Smith et al\., 2024\]
