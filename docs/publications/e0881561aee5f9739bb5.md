# A Spectral Clustering\-Type Algorithm for the Consistent Estimation of the Hurst Distribution in Moderately High Dimensions

**Autores:** Patrice Abry; Gustavo Didier; Oliver Orejola; Herwig Wendt

**Data:** 2026-10-11 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Abstract Scale invariance \(fractality\) is a prominent feature of the large\-scale behavior of many stochastic systems\. In this work, we construct a provably convergent and easily interpretable algorithm for the statistical identification of the Hurst distribution \(in particular, the scaling exponents\) undergirding a high\-dimensional fractal system\. Starting from recently established properties of wavelet random matrices, the algorithm is based on modified spectral clustering\. The algorithm also includes a model selection procedure so as to address the important practical problem of picking the value of the clustering precision hyperparameter\. In a moderately high\-dimensional regime where the dimension, the sample size and the scale go to infinity, we show that the algorithm consistently estimates the Hurst distribution\. Monte Carlo simulations show that the proposed methodology is efficient for realistic sample sizes and outperforms another popular clustering method based on mixed\-Gaussian modeling\. We apply the algorithm in the analysis of real\-world macroeconomic time series to unveil evidence of cointegration\.
