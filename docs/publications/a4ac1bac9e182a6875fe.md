# Precision Oncofertility: Integrating Genomics, Reproductive Biomarkers, Treatment Toxicity Profiles, and Foundation Models for Fertility Preservation in Men and Women with Cancer

**Autores:** 

**Data:** 2026-10-12 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Background: Cancer survivors of reproductive age face substantial risks of treatment\-induced gonadotoxicity, yet clinical decision\-making for fertility preservation remains largely empirical and population\-based\.A precision oncofertility framework that integrates genomic, biomarker, and toxicity data is urgently needed\.Objective: To develop and validate a multimodal foundation model that integrates genomic variants, sex\-specific reproductive biomarkers, treatment toxicity profiles, and clinical variables to predict fertility impairment risk and support individualized fertility\-preservation decisions in cancer patients\.Methods: A retrospective observational cohort of 400 patients \(age 18\-45 years\) diagnosed with breast cancer, lymphoma, leukemia, testicular cancer, ovarian cancer, cervical cancer, or colorectal cancer was assembled\.All patients received fertility\-preservation counselling prior to treatment\.Data included reproductive biomarkers \(anti\-Müllerian hormone \(AMH\), antral follicle count \(AFC\), follicle\-stimulating hormone \(FSH\), estradiol; sperm concentration, motility, testosterone, inhibin\-B\), genomic features \(BRCA1, BRCA2, CHEK2, TP53, FMR1\), treatment toxicity variables \(alkylating agent and platinum exposure, radiation dose, stem cell transplantation\), and standard clinical covariates\.A transformer\-based foundation model with four specialised encoders and cross\-modal attention was developed\.Performance was benchmarked against Random Forest, XGBoost, and a Deep Neural Network using accuracy, precision, recall, F1 score, and area under the receiver\-operating\-characteristic curve \(AUC\)\.Results: The foundation model achieved the highest performance across all metrics: accuracy 0\.883, precision 0\.876, recall 0\.862, F1 0\.869, and AUC 0\.934\.AMH \(importance score 0\.187\), AFC \(0\.171\), and alkylating agent exposure \(0\.152\) were the strongest predictors\.Fertility risk stratification identified 37\.0% \(n=148\) low\-risk, 35\.5% \(n=142\) moderate\-risk, and 27\.5% \(n=110\) high\-risk patients\.BRCA1/2 mutations and radiation dose independently amplified impairment risk\.Conclusion: A precision oncofertility framework incorporating multimodal data and transformer\-based architecture substantially outperforms conventional machine\-learning approaches\.AI\-guided risk stratification can enable individualized, evidence\-based fertility\-preservation counselling and intervention, with direct implications for clinical oncofertility practice\.
