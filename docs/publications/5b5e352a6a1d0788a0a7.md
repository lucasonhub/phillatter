# From Bureaucracy to Human\-Centred Design: Reading Indonesia’s Digital Public Service Reform through Design Thinking

**Autores:** Adi Satrio Nugroho

**Data:** 2026-10-18 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Digital transformation in public services asks governments to do more than adopt new technology as it asks them to reform public services around what citizens actually need and experience\. Indonesia’s M\-Paspor is one such attempt\. The app\-based passport application service was developed to address the limitations of conventional passport services\. This paper analyses the creation of the service through the lens of design thinking \(DT\) combined with the Business Model Canvas \(BMC\), concentrating on three BMC elements: customer segments, value proposition, and channels\. DT is used to understand users’ needs and problems, and how those needs were translated into possible solutions\. Collaboratively, BMC is used to map who is served, what value is created, and how that value is delivered through service channels\. The appraisal indicates that M\-Paspor represents a shift from a service organised around physical attendance to one that is more flexible, better integrated, and oriented towards its users, even though some stages still require face\-to\-face contact\. The analysis also shows that digital service innovation is determined less by technology and significantly more by the fit between user needs, service value, and the channels through which value is delivered\. This paper indicates that the approach may be arguably transferable to other public\-sector cases, particularly services that struggle with access, queues, lengthy administration, and a high volume of public interaction, and it offers a framework for analysing user\-driven service transformation more systematically\.Keywords: business model canvas; m\-paspor, design thinking, digital transformation, public services, human\-centred design
