# Inteligência de Dados Aplicada à Governança das Contratações Públicas: Evidências de Concentração de Mercado e Modelagem Preditiva de Riscos no Estado do Rio de Janeiro

**Autores:** Rafael Cocchiarelli; José Francisco Moreira Pessanha; Nathalia Cordeiro; Antonio Ranha

**Data:** 2026-10-09 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

This article examines the governance of public procurement in the State of Rio de Janeiro through the integration of classical economic indicators and predictive models from data science and artificial intelligence\. Based on real data from public procurement processes conducted between 2022 and 2025, the study investigates patterns of market concentration and inequality in the allocation of public resources using the Herfindahl–Hirschman Index \(HHI\) and the Gini coefficient\. Additionally, linear regression models are employed as a statistical reference, and the XGBoost machine learning algorithm is applied for the predictive classification of procurements with a higher probability of risk\. The results reveal highly concentrated market structures in specific services, as well as significant asymmetries in resource allocation\. The predictive modeling demonstrated a strong capacity to identify outliers and inconsistent procurement processes, reinforcing its potential as a tool for preventive oversight\. The evidence indicates that data intelligence can strengthen public procurement governance by promoting more efficient, transparent, and evidence\-based decision\-making aligned with the principles of Law No\. 14,133/2021\.
