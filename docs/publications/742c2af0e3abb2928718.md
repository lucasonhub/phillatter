# A predefined sulphur\-handling transcriptional programme is associated with colorectal cancer molecular subtypes

**Autores:** Shaheryar Shafi; Arslan Hamid; Jiao Yang; Xiaoxia Cheng; Minxuan Sun

**Data:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Background Colorectal cancer \(CRC\) exhibits substantial metabolic and transcriptomic heterogeneity, but the relationship between sulphur\-handling transcriptional programmes and consensus molecular subtypes \(CMS\) remains incompletely resolved\. Methods We analysed a predefined 14\-gene sulphur\-handling panel across The Cancer Genome Atlas colon and rectal adenocarcinoma cohorts \(TCGA\-COAD/READ\) and six independent HG\-U133 validation cohorts \( n = 1,337 tumours\)\. Analytical workflows integrated differential expression comparing tumours with adjacent\-normal mucosa, CMS association modelling, random\-effects meta\-analysis, adjustment for three orthogonal tumour\-purity estimators \(ESTIMATE, consensus purity estimates, and ABSOLUTE\), reference\-based single\-cell deconvolution with independent cross\-atlas verification, and direction\-aware therapeutic triage\. Results Colorectal tumours demonstrated branch\-specific transcriptional alterations, marked by coordinated downregulation of sulphur oxidation and clearance genes \( SQOR , ETHE1 , TST \) and of the cysteine\-catabolism genes CDO1 and CTH , and selective upregulation of SLC7A11 and AHCY , whereas CBS was not significantly altered\. The composite programme score differed across CMS subtypes and was consistently lower in mesenchymal CMS4 tumours than in non\-CMS4 tumours across all discovery and validation series\. This association remained statistically detectable after adjustment for three global tumour\-purity metrics\. The programme score showed no statistically detectable independent association with overall, recurrence\-free, or disease\-free survival\. Single\-cell deconvolution supported expression across epithelial, stromal, and immune compartments\. Direction\-aware therapeutic triage prioritised SLC7A11/xCT inhibition as an exploratory candidate for preclinical testing\. Conclusion Sulphur\-handling transcriptional architecture is reproducibly linked to CRC molecular taxonomy and is not fully explained by measured tumour purity variation, providing a computational framework for future functional and compartment\-resolved investigation of sulphur metabolism in stratified CRC populations\.
