# Fatigue Mechanisms of Porous Rocks Subjected to Cyclic Pore Pressures: Insights from Hydro\-Mechanical Phase\-Field Simulations

**Autores:** Sijia Liu; Yunteng Wang

**Data:** 2026-10-13 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Abstract In this work, we formulate a hydro\-mechanical phase\-field model to investigate the fatigue failure behavior of porous rocks under cyclic pore\-pressure loading\. Our phase\-field model integrates pore\-pressure diffusion, fatigue\-induced degradation of failure resistance, and breakage constitutive laws into a unified framework for capturing deformation, damage evolution, and failure progression in saturated porous rocks\. The distinctive features of our model include \(i\) a hydro\-mechanical coupling formulation that captures the interaction between fluid diffusion and solid deformation; \(ii\) a fatigue degradation mechanism that describes the progressive reduction of failure resistance under repeated pore\-pressure disturbances; and \(iii\) a breakage constitutive relationship that allows the transition from diffuse compression\-dominated damage to mixed\-mode compression\-shear strain localization\. We first present several numerical benchmarks, including hydro\-mechanical fracture, cyclic fatigue, and triaxial compression tests, for validation\. Then, the underlying fatigue mechanisms of porous rocks are investigated through a series of numerical examples subjected to cyclic pore\-pressure loading\. This work not only helps improve our understanding of the fatigue mechanisms of porous rocks, but also provides a useful numerical tool for predicting hydraulic fatigue degradation in rock engineering\.
