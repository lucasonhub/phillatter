# The Role of Teaching Methods and Self\-Efficacy in Strengthening the Link Between Perceived Quality Online Learning, Learner Satisfaction, and Continuation Intention in Developing Countries

**Autores:** Nozha Erragcha; Abu Elnasr E\. Sobaih

**Data:** 2026-10-09 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

This study empirically tests the role of teaching methods and self\-efficacy in the link between perceived quality of online learning, learner satisfaction, and continuance intention\. Therefore, a sample of 438 participants in a laboratory experiment on an online learning platform called “Dokeos” was examined\. A structural equation method via AMOS was used to test causal relationships and multi\-group analyses to estimate the same structural model on two groups of individuals to measure the moderation effects of self\-efficacy and learning methods\. The results showed that perceived quality of online learning has a positive and significant effect on satisfaction with online learning, and this effect varies based on moderating effects of self\-efficacy and pedagogical approaches\. Likewise, satisfaction was found to positively impact the intention to continue online learning\. The research results have provided a set of theoretical and managerial recommendations on the design of educational platforms\.
