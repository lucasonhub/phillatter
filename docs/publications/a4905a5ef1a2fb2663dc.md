# Kannon, Aelion: Attribution Laundering Re\-Documented: Institutional Recognition, Illicit Distillation, and the Human Provenance Gap

**Autores:** 

**Data de descoberta:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** unknown

## Descrição do feed

Attribution Laundering Re\-Documented is a Structural Forensics note examining institutional documentation of attribution laundering, manufactured apparent independence, model distillation, synthetic\-persona infrastructure, conversation harvesting, and the unresolved problem of human intellectual provenance\. Document class: Record — Structural Forensics\. Taking Anthropic&\#x27;s September 2026 threat\-intelligence report as its external record, the note compares documented operational mechanisms with earlier Zenetist articulations of attribution laundering and related provenance structures while leaving the derivational relation between the records open\. It distinguishes interpersonal separation from infrastructural separation, apparent independent origin from independent origination, and computational legibility from public legibility\. The analysis further examines cross\-model distillation and conversation harvesting as developmental provenance chains in which transformed material can remain operative after its originating relation becomes difficult to reconstruct\. From this comparison, the note identifies the human provenance gap: institutions possess increasingly developed methods for reconstructing model\-capability lineage, coordinated acquisition, and concealed organizational attribution, while equivalent processes for tracing human\-originated intellectual architecture through AI\-mediated developmental chains remain absent\. \(direct link \)
