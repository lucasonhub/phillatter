# Devoured AI benevolence? How and when AI benevolence triggers employees’ taking charge within the zero\-sum jungle

**Autores:** Bin Zhang; Yanlin Ma; Xiaolei Qian

**Data:** 2026-10-14 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Employees increasingly perceive generative artificial intelligence \(GenAI\) as a supportive and benevolent workplace entity\. Yet, despite examining the disruptive challenges and broad benefits of AI, extant organizational literature fails to adequately explicate the higher\-order cognitive mechanisms through which AI benevolence fosters proactive, change\-oriented employee behavior\. Drawing on cognitive appraisal theory \(CAT\), this study examines the mechanisms and boundary conditions through which AI benevolence fosters employees’ taking\-charge behavior\. Results from a three\-wave survey of 313 internet industry employees demonstrate that: \(i\) metacognitive strategies mediated the relationship between AI benevolence and individuals’ taking\-charge behavior; \(ii\) the employee’s mindset attenuated the positive effect of AI benevolence on metacognitive strategies, consequently dampening the overall indirect effect on taking\-charge behavior\. Ultimately, this research contributes to organizational behavior literature by elucidating how GenAI shapes employee proactivity in AI\-augmented work environments\.
