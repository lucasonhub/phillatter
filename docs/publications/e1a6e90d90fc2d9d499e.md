# Mutual Information in High\-Dimensional Neural Coding

**Autores:** Zimo Li; Yuchang Tian; Matthew P\. Leighton; Christopher W\. Lynn

**Data:** 2026-10-15 · **Tipo:** preprint

## Resumo

Estimating mutual information in large neural populations is limited by the exponential growth of possible neural response patterns\. Here we apply a Monte Carlo framework to make this problem tractable by sampling from a conditionally independent population model fit from single\-neuron responses\. The estimator accurately recovers analytical ground truth, corrects finite\-data bias, and scales to empirical recordings containing 100 to 10,000 neurons from visual cortex, hippocampus, and retina\. Across systems, stimulus uncertainty decays exponentially with population size, revealing a simple scaling law for high\-dimensional neural codes\.
