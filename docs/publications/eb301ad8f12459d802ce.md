# Tijorat banklarida raqamli bank xizmatlarini takomillashtirish istiqbollari

**Autores:** Jahongir Ilhomovich Sayitqulov

**Data:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

The article examines the impact of digital technology adoption on the operational efficiency and financial performance of commercial banks\. It addresses the comprehensive assessment of digital banking services, AI\-based customer segmentation, digital lending, and the development of banking ecosystems\. Methodological approaches are proposed for assessing the relationship between digital technologies and banks&\#x27; financial and operational indicators\. Based on the research findings, practical recommendations are developed to improve the efficiency of digital banking services, enhance their strategic management, and advance banks&\#x27; digital transformation\.
