# Bulgaria, Jamel: Systemic Integration of the Core Emotion Framework: A Comprehensive Structural\-Constructivist Analysis of Functional Operator Theory, Mathematical Neuro\-Somatic Architectures, and Applied Affective Engineering

## Descrição do feed

The Core Emotion Framework \(CEF\) presents a unified structural constructivist architecture for modeling human affect, integrating somatic neuroscience, operator functionalism, and quantum semantic state space dynamics into a single computational paradigm\. Departing from the century long dichotomy between discrete emotion theories and dimensional affect models, the CEF reconceptualizes emotions as ten identity preserving regulatory operators distributed across three autonomous somatic hubs—the cephalic, cardiac, and enteric nervous systems\. Each operator is defined by a five facet micro architecture that governs information flow, somatic modulation, and behavioral transformation\. The framework formalizes emotional dynamics through non linear operator coupling matrices, Kuramoto synchronization, and quantum like projection operators that capture context dependent, non commutative sequencing\. Psychopathology is modeled as operator fusion and entanglement, producing rigid, high entropy attractor states that distort somatic cognitive integration\. The CEF further incorporates a tri centric neuroanatomical grounding, a Human Operating System hypothesis, and a holographic QTMM boundary bulk mapping to explain how latent operator eigenmodes project into lived somatic experience\. Empirical components include psychometric validation, biosignal acquisition pipelines, and hardware assisted somatic regulation devices engineered under ISO functional safety constraints\. Collectively, the CEF provides a mathematically explicit, biologically anchored, and clinically actionable model of affective computation capable of resolving longstanding theoretical contradictions while enabling next generation affective engineering, therapeutic modulation, and computational modeling of emotional processes\. \(direct link \)
