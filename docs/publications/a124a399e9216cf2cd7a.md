# Composition determination of biodiesel/ethanol blends using dielectric spectroscopy and tabular prior\-data fitted networks

**Autores:** Julián Corach; Martín González; Lautaro Gómez Ciapponi; P\.A\. Sorichetti

**Data:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Biodiesel/ethanol blends represent a promising alternative to reduce the consumption of fossil diesel\. This work employs frequency\-domain dielectric spectroscopy to determine the permittivity of biodiesel/ethanol blends across the full range of compositions, at temperatures between 293 K and 333 K, and within a frequency range from 1 MHz to 30 MHz\. Within this interval, the real part of the permittivity is frequency\-independent, and electrical losses are negligible\. Leveraging these experimental results, a transformer\-based deep learning model implementing Tabular Prior\-data Fitted Networks \(TabPFN\) was developed to determine the blend composition from temperature and permittivity measurements\. Evaluated via Leave\-One\-Out Cross\-Validation, the proposed TabPFN model predicted the composition with high accuracy, yielding a coefficient of determination of 0\.997, an average absolute deviation of 1\.0 mol%, and a root mean squared error of 1\.6 mol%\. These results outperform conventional machine learning algorithms as well as standard analytical inversion models, operating in a single forward pass without requiring hyperparameter tuning or data scaling\. Overall, using high\-frequency dielectric spectroscopy together with tabular foundation models are the basis of a streamlined, highly efficient strategy for non\-destructive biofuel quality verification\.
