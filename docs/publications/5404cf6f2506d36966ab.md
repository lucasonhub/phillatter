# REAKTUALISASI NILAI\-NILAI PANCASILA DARI AKAR SEJARAH NUSANTARA MENUJU IDEOLOGI TERBUKA DI ERA GLOBALISASI

**Autores:** Martha Putri Grasya Banurea; Solikin; Genysa Arkwayani Pakpahan; Rahel Simamora; Khalishah Maisuuna Rahman; Pebri Andini; Shinta Jouito Samosir; Yohana Tio Syarina Siringoringo; Siti Rahma Fadillah; Virma Dinartha Sinaga; Emelia Reva Lumbannahor; Syifa Nur Aisah; Siti Handayani

**Data:** 2026-10-10 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Pancasila is the philosophical foundation of the nation and state, whose values are applied to the historical experiences, culture, religious life, and social practices of Indonesian society\. Globalization and digitalization present challenges in the form of the influx of foreign cultures, individualism, pragmatism, disinformation, and weakening social solidarity\. This study aims to analyze the relationship between the historical roots of Pancasila values and the need for its reactualization as an open ideology in the era of globalization\. This study uses a qualitative approach with a literature observation method, analyzing journal articles published between 2021 and 2025 and related academic books through identification, classification, interpretation, and synthesis\. The results show that reactualization does not change the basic values of Pancasila, but rather translates the values of divinity, humanity, unity, democracy, and social justice into practices appropriate to changing times\. Reactualization can be strengthened through contextual Pancasila education, local wisdom, digital literacy, ethical use of technology, and social and institutional role models\. As an open ideology, Pancasila can respond to global developments without losing the identity and substance of its fundamental values\.
