# GENERATIVE ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE AS A LEARNING PARTNER: OPPORTUNITIES AND LIMITS FOR DEVELOPING FUTURE TEACHERS&\#x27; CRITICAL THINKING

**Autores:** Fayzieva Dildora Khayotovna

**Data:** 2026-10-10 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Generative artificial intelligence is becoming part of university learning, but its educational value depends on how it is used\. This conceptual article examines ways in which future teachers can use AI to ask questions, compare explanations, and check evidence rather than accept ready\-made answers\. A focused review of work on critical thinking, media literacy, and AI in education was used to build a practical teaching sequence\. The analysis suggests that AI can support discussion and reflection when students first form their own view, check external sources, and explain their final decision\. The article also describes risks such as confident errors and overreliance\. The proposed sequence is a design recommendation, not a tested intervention\.
