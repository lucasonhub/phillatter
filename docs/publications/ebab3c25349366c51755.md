# Thomas, Emily: C230The Rise of Unreal Time and British Idealism 1883–1893

**Autores:** 

**Data de descoberta:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** unknown

## Descrição do feed

In Real Time: A Reinvention in British Metaphysics 1883\-1928\. Oxford University Press\. 2026During the 1880s and 1890s, philosophers widely held time to be unreal\. This chapter contextualises, and explores, that anti\-realism\. The bulk of this chapter is dedicated to British idealism, the movement which accounts for the vast majority of anti\-realists about time\.Section 2 provides background: an overview of British metaphysics of time from the eighteenth to late nineteenth centuries\. Section 3 considers the rise of British idealism and time anti\-realism, illustrating the latter through the 1880s and 1890s work of T\. H\. Green, Edward Caird, and F\. H\. Bradley\. Section 4 speculates that anti\-realism about time snowballed amongst idealists because it offered a religion\-friendly way to accommodate Darwinian evolution\. Section 5 discusses Charles Hinton and Victoria Welby, who approached anti\-realism about time from a novel direction: conceiving it as the fourth dimension of space\.
