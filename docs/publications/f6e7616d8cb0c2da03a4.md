# Puri, Bhakti Madhava: Simple Solution to Hard Problem of Consciousness

**Autores:** 

**Data de descoberta:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** unknown

## Descrição do feed

The hard problem of consciousness, a term coined by philosopher David Chalmers, refers to the difficulty of explaining how and why subjective experiences arise from physical processes in the brain\. In simpler terms: How does matter, which is presumably unconscious, give rise to conscious experience—like the feeling of pain, the taste of mango, or the redness of red? Srila Prabhupada put the reverse challenge to his scientists: Scientifically show how matter comes from life \[consciousness\]\. How shall we begin? This is always an essential consideration, despite the fact that most of science ignores this question\. So, let&\#x27;s begin at the beginning\.
