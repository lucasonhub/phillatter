# Trajectory generation for 3D fly\-by transitions using analytically constrained Bézier curves

**Autores:** Zoe Mbikayi; Agnes Steinert; Florian Holzapfel; Hugh H\. \-T\. Liu

**Data:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Abstract Conventional trajectory generation remains inadequate for high\-accuracy aircraft guidance because Dubins\- and clothoid\-based turn transitions contain discontinuities in curvature or curvature derivative\. As a result, even when high\-order guidance laws are used to reduce tracking error, the reference trajectory itself does not provide suitable higher\-order feedforward commands\. Existing solutions are also unsatisfactory because they either recover higher\-order smoothness only approximately through post\-processing, rely on numerical optimization, which is unsuitable for deterministic airborne systems, or do not actively account for aircraft dynamic constraints\. This paper closes these gaps with a closed\-form method for generating three\-dimensional turn trajectories as ninth\-order Bézier curves\. The curve is constructed analytically from smoothness requirements and aircraft dynamic constraints, yielding a high\-order reference trajectory that is both dynamically consistent and deterministic\. The methods presented are integrated into an operational trajectory generation system and validated analytically and through flight testing on a 700 kg medical evacuation eVTOL aircraft\.
