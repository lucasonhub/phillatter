# Wang, Kaining: A Meta\-Philosophical Analysis of Provisional Construal Advantage

**Autores:** 

**Data de descoberta:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** unknown

## Descrição do feed

This paper investigates a meta\-philosophical puzzle concerning construals in philosophical inquiry\. We consider two mutually exclusive propositions: a target proposition and its negation\. Relative to a fixed set of independently pre\-established admissibility constraints for construals, and relative to a definite body of completed conceptual exploration, at least one construal has been identified that satisfies these constraints and supports the target proposition, while no construal satisfying the same constraints and supporting its negation has yet been identified\. We refer to this resource\-distribution condition as construal\-structural disparity\. This work examines what philosophical and epistemic status ought to be attributed to such disparity\. We introduce a nominal definition to characterize provisional construal advantage held by the target proposition over its negation\. Three competing interpretive stances are developed: a reducible stance, a weakly irreducible stance, and a strong realist stance\. We address the core critical objection that structural features of construal frameworks do not automatically correspond to objective facts about the world\. This paper distinguishes trivial linguistic re\-description from substantive philosophical problem reformulation, and situates the proposed frame\- work within six established research traditions: conceptual analysis, reflective equilibrium, inference to the best explanation, conceptual engineering, interpretive charity, and epistemic justification theory\. Rather than asserting a fixed universal principle, this study systematically unpacks the open normative question of whether construal\-structural dis\- parity carries inherent epistemic weight, and establishes clear boundary conditions for future philosophical assessment\.
