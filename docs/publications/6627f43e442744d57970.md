# A MODEL FOR ASSESSING THE ENERGY EFFICIENCY OF ELECTRIC ROLLING STOCK BASED ON ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE

**Autores:** Izzatillo Rakhmiddinov

**Data:** 2026-10-14 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

The article proposes a hybrid model for assessing the energy efficiency of electric rolling stock that combines a physical traction energy balance with machine learning\. The physical part calculates the reference energy for a given train, track profile and speed from the standard running resistance formulas\. The machine\-learning part learns from on\-board data how actual consumption deviates from this reference\. An energy efficiency index is defined as the ratio of reference to actual energy\. An illustrative calculation for a 3,192 t train on a 50 km section shows that raising the cruising speed from 50 to 80 km/h cuts running time by 37\.5% but increases net energy by 46%\. The model is intended for UzEL electric locomotives operated by Uzbekistan Railways and forms the basis of a driver advisory system\.
