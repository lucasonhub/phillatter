# Inteligencia artificial autónoma y derecho tributario en Ecuador: diseño de un modelo de gobernanza algorítmica para la determinación fiscal, explicabilidad y protección de los derechos del contribuyente

**Autores:** Jonathan Eduardo López Poveda; Nathalie Fernanda Gómez Suárez

**Data:** 2026-10-14 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

The incorporation of artificial intelligence \(AI\) with increasing degrees of autonomy changes how tax administrations detect risk, cross\-check information and prepare decisions with individual legal effects\. This study designs an algorithmic governance model for tax assessment in Ecuador that combines technological efficiency, explainability and protection of taxpayers’ rights\. A qualitative, documentary and comparative legal approach was applied through normative, doctrinal and institutional review of sources published mainly between 2021 and 2026, supplemented by a risk matrix and a layered control design\. The findings show that Ecuador has constitutional, tax and data\-protection safeguards and, since 2026, general rules applicable to AI systems that process personal data; however, no tax\-sector regulation was identified that specifically governs decision\-making autonomy, attribution of responsibility and review of high\-impact tax actions\. The proposed model classifies AI uses by autonomy and risk and requires impact assessment, traceability, individual explainability, verifiable human oversight, auditability and effective remedies\. The study concludes that an AI\-supported tax decision is legally acceptable only when the administration retains competence and responsibility, can reconstruct the relevant reasoning and provides the taxpayer with a real avenue to understand, contest and obtain review of the decision\.
