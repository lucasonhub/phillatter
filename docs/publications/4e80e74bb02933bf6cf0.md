# Quem faz cada etapa? A IA generativa como executora de tarefas e a mudança na rotina de trabalho

**Autores:** Daniela Hayoshi

**Data:** 2026-10-17 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Generative artificial intelligence \(AI\) tools have spread rapidly among professionals, and experiments show substantial gains on isolated tasks\. Yet average effects on earnings and recorded hours have been small, although changes in task organization are already evident\. This conceptual article proposes that an important part of this gap is explained by how people represent the technology: generative AI tends to be perceived as a question\-and\-answer tool rather than as an executor of process steps, let alone of recurring routines that keep running while the person only checks the result\. Drawing on literatures on the task\-based approach in labor economics, levels of automation, trust and overreliance in automated systems, work design and redesign, sociotechnical frames, transfer of training, and habit formation, the article develops a five\-element model \(perception, decomposition, allocation, verification, and routinization\) and argues that the relevant question shifts from “how do I ask?” to “who performs each step?”, with three possible destinations for each step: AI alone, AI with human verification, or the person\. Seven testable propositions are formulated, and implications for research, training design, and organizations are discussed\. The article presents no new data; its contribution is to organize dispersed evidence into an explicit, falsifiable mechanism\.
