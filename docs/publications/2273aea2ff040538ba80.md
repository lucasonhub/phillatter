# Educational innovation: intelligent tutoring systems for learning in the university environment

**Autores:** Olga María Hidalgo Camacho

**Data:** 2026-10-12 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Higher education is undergoing constant transformation driven by information and communication technologies, with platforms such as Moodle and mobile devices fostering collaborative activities in the classroom\. In this context, intelligent tutoring systems are emerging as innovative tools to strengthen educational processes worldwide\. The aim of this research is to understand lecturers’ perceptions of the integration of intelligent tutoring systems in the context of teaching computer engineering at the Ezequiel Zamora National Experimental University of the Western Plains\. To this end, a qualitative methodology was employed under the interpretative paradigm, utilising a phenomenological\-hermeneutic field design of an emergent and negotiated nature, achieving an integrative level of complexity\. The key informants were five lecturers from the university’s Computer Engineering sub\-programme\. The findings show that lecturers perceive ITS as pedagogical mediators; they significantly increase learning outcomes, maintain student motivation, and transform teaching into a collaborative process between lecturer and student\. It is concluded that these tools reduce drop\-out rates and improve academic performance, substantially benefiting the educational process\.
