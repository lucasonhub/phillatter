# Artificial Intelligence in School Leadership

**Autores:** GEORGIOY IOANNIS

**Data:** 2026-10-13 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

This study explores the attitudes, level of familiarity, and use of artificial intelligence \(AI\) tools among school principals in secondary education in Greece\. The sample consists of 63 principals from public schools, with an average age of 53 years and extensive administrative experience\. Using an online questionnaire, the research investigates their perceptions regarding the potential, benefits, and challenges of AI in school management\. The findings indicate a generally positive attitude toward AI utilization; however, practical implementation remains limited due to insufficient training and inadequate infrastructure\. A positive correlation is observed between digital competence and AI use, while a strong need emerges for targeted professional development and the design of integration strategies\. The study contributes to understanding the relationship between leadership competence and technological transformation in contemporary educational administration\.
