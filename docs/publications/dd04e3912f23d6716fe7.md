# Clinical and Stone\-related Factors Influencing Stone\-free Status After Retrograde Intrarenal Surgery

**Autores:** Kadir Böcü; Doğucan Nuri Uğur; Nurullah Altınkaya; Ali Furkan Batur

**Data:** 2026-10-14 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

What&\#x27;s known on the subject? and What does the study add?Stone size and lower\-pole location are commonly linked to residual fragments after RIRS, but the predictive values of Hounsfield unit \(HU\) density, body mass index \(BMI\), American Society of Anesthesiologists \(ASA\) class, and preoperative stenting remain inconsistent\.Using a computed tomography\-based stone\-free definition in a large single\-center cohort \(n=527\) and multivariable logistic regression including discrimination and calibration assessment, we found that stone size, lower\-pole location, stone multiplicity, and age independently predicted stone\-free status, whereas HU density, BMI, ASA class, and preoperative stenting did not \(area under the curve 0\.839\)\.
