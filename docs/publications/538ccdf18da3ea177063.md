# Mcbride Iii, Lee A\.: Leaping into an Abyss \(and Resurfacing\): Radical Empiricism and Chaos

**Autores:** 

**Data de descoberta:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** unknown

## Descrição do feed

\_The Pluralist\_\. forthcomingMcBride revisits the notions of philosophy of struggle and radical empiricism he received from Leonard Harris and Charlene Haddock Seigfried\. McBride focuses on leaping into an abyss, weaving order from chaos, and philosophy’s capacity to help oppressed populations to size up the systems that oppress and imagine radically different worlds\. McBride interprets this “leaping into an abyss” as a returning to immediate experience, a creative/poetic act involving openness, reevaluating, and reexpressing\. Ideally, we resurface from the abyss with new impressions that help us to imagine and work to create a world with less necro\-being and immiseration\.
