# Physics\-Constrained Transfer Learning Uncovers Mineralogical Controls on Shale Caprock Integrity During CO2 Storage

**Autores:** Xiaolong Peng; Chen Zhuoheng; Fanhua Zeng; Wanju Yuan; Hyojong Lee; Chunqing Jiang

**Data:** 2026-10-14 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Quantifying caprock integrity for CO2 storage is often constrained by the limited availability of well\-preserved caprock samples for geomechanical testing and a limited understanding of the localized CO2\-induced mineralogical alterations\. To address this challenge, this study develops a physics\-constrained transfer learning framework to predict static Poisson’s ratio and fracture pressure directly from shale mineralogy\. A fully connected neural network was pre\-trained using a large synthetic siliceous shale dataset generated with a coherent potential approximation model and subsequently fine\-tuned with limited experimental measurements\. The optimized model achieved strong predictive performance \(MAE = 0\.016, RMSE = 0\.024, MAPE = 6\.223%\) on the small experimental dateset\. Application to Clearwater siliceous shale caprocks in Alberta, Canada, indicates limited matrix\-scale mechanical degradation after 84 days of exposure to CO2\-saturated brine, with predicted fracture pressure reductions below \~ 3%\. The proposed framework provides a scalable and non\-destructive approach for early\-stage caprock screening and may be transferable to other subsurface storage systems, including H2 and geothermal applications\.
