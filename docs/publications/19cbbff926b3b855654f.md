# Task Allocation and Improvement of Collective Work Efficiency in Swarm Robotics Using Autonomous Decentralized Algorithms

**Autores:** Taiki Watanabe; Yuki Tanigaki; Tomohiro Hayakawa; Fumitoshi Matsuno; Ryusuke Fujisawa

**Data:** 2026-10-14 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

This study proposes a decentralized, dynamic task allocation algorithm for swarm robotics to enhance efficiency in multi\-foraging tasks\. Inspired by the adaptive foraging behavior of social insects, the proposed method introduces a utility\-based metric called &quot;Umami,&quot; which evaluates food sources based on their quantity and distance from the nest\. Each robot dynamically transitions between three roles—Searcher, Picker, and Carrier—based on local information and threshold\-based switching\. Simulation experiments conducted under various environment sizes and robot densities demonstrated that the proposed integrated model achieved notably higher foraging efficiency, especially in large\-scale environments\. These findings suggest that the proposed approach offers a scalable and adaptable solution for efficient task execution in swarm robotic systems\.
