# Zhang, Guozheng: Theoretical Foundations of Artificial General Intelligence Based on the Logic of Life Evolution

**Autores:** 

**Data de descoberta:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** unknown

## Descrição do feed

Current research on artificial general intelligence \(AGI\) is deeply constrained by the data\-driven paradigm, whose fundamental predicament lies in its forgetting of the biological foundations and evolutionary dimension of intelligence\. By tracing the evolutionary history of consciousness from single\-celled life to the human spiritual world, this paper argues that the essence of consciousness is a “self/non\-self” boundary perception system opened up by living organisms to maintain their own homeostasis, and reveals the operating mechanism of the central nervous system as a predictive coding engine as well as its “bijective prison” predicament \[1–3\]\. On this basis, this paper points out that the birth of language is not merely a revolution in communication, but rather, through a mechanism of symbolic disembedding, enables the mind to break free from the rigid chain of stimulus–response and to ascend from “presence”\-bound consciousness to a reflexive spiritual world \[4–6\]\. Accordingly, this paper offers a systematic critique of the “floating symbol” architecture of current large language models and proposes a three\-layer nested theoretical framework for AGI—an ontological security layer, an embodied prediction layer, and a symbolic deliberation layer—arguing that the construction of AGI must follow the developmental order of “embodiment first, language second,” with “constitutional monarchy” as the adjudicative principle for inter\-layer conflicts\. This paper attempts to provide AGI with an ontological foundation rooted in the philosophy of life and an operable architectural blueprint\.
