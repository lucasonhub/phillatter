# Testosterone and Functional Speed Adaptation to Combined Lower limb Neuromuscular and Motor Coordination Training in Collegiate Badminton players: A Randomized Controlled Trial

**Autores:** Himanshu Himanshu; Amit Saraf; PoojaAnand PoojaAnand

**Data:** 2026-10-17 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Background: Hormone influence the body function which causes biochemical and metabolic changes\. Physical function are to be considered as strong influencer for the endocrinal variability mainly the testosterone and cortisol\. Aim:The study was aimed to examine the impact of combined lower limb neuromuscular training and motor coordination training on the level of testosterone among the male and female collegiate badminton players\. Methods and Material \- Randomized controlled trail was performed with inclusion of Collegiate Badminton players \(1625 years\) of both genders which were recruited from the university and colleges\. Ethical consent and CTRI was done before starting the study\. Statistical analysis:The analysis was done using software SPSS 24 version 2\.0\. Pearson’s test, Independent T test were performed\. P values were taken significant &lt;0\.05\. Results:The findings were signifying an enhancement of training regime in level of testosterone in experimental group whereas control group was also detrimental enhancement but the changes were minimal and no substantial\. Similarly, Speed was demonstrating noticeable improvement in values among the participants of experimental group whereas the control group was not showing any meaningful progress\. Additionally, the progress was more noticeable among males than females\. Conclusion:The exercise programs are effective and strong stimulators for the enhancement in values of testosterone as free marker of performance\. As some sports also shows such findings and remained it as a marker of performance among the athletes\.
