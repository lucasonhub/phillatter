# Secondary School Teachers’ Interaction with Artificial Intelligence Systems: A Qualitative Investigation of Usability, Trust, and the Transformation of the Pedagogical Role

**Autores:** Zotou Evaggelia

**Data:** 2026-10-13 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

This research explores secondary education teachers&quot; perceptions regarding the integration of Generative Artificial Intelligence \(GenAI\) into instructional practice\. Through semi\-structured interviews with a sample of ten \(10\) educators from diverse academic backgrounds, the study examines three core pillars: interaction and usability, issues of trust and ethics, and the transformation of professional identity\. The findings reveal that using AI tools is a complex cognitive process requiring strategic prompt engineering\. Despite the small sample size, participants consistently demonstrated a &quot;cautious acceptance,&quot; acting as critical evaluators of machine\-generated outputs to mitigate scientific inaccuracies and &quot;hallucinations\.&quot; In conclusion, the study highlights a shift in the teaching role from being a source of knowledge to an authority of critical mediation\. It emphasizes the urgent need for professional development that fosters pedagogical agency and ethical action within the modern digital learning environment\.
