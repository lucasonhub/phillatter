# Entre innovación e incertidumbre: prueba digital, inteligencia artificial y debido proceso civil

**Autores:** Michell Stefannya Zurita\-Delgado; Brayan Gabriel Taday\-Guamán; Erick Adrián Vallejo\-Amaguaya; Eddy Stiven Molina\-Arias

**Data:** 2026-10-15 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Messages, audio recordings and videos are now routine evidence in civil proceedings, and artificial intelligence makes it possible to fabricate or alter them with great plausibility\. The paper analysed the legal implications of such manipulation for due process in the Ecuadorian civil sphere\. The research was qualitative and legal\-documentary, analysing the Constitution, the General Organic Code of Procedures and specialised literature\. The results show that the law recognises the validity of digital evidence but lacks procedures to verify its authenticity; that advances in synthetic content make detection difficult, even for experts; and that rational evaluation and judicial reasoning must incorporate technical criteria\. It is concluded that evidentiary certainty can no longer be presumed, and verification protocols, judicial training and reinforced reasoning are proposed\.
