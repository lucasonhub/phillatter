# Truck Platooning—An Overview Study with Time Constraints

**Autores:** Athira Mavoomkuttathil Sivachandran

**Data:** 2026-10-17 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

The truck platoon has a significant impact on modern society\. As fuel costs increase daily,vehicles can be driven in a pattern to reduce fuel consumption\. Vehicle routing problem is discussed in literature with taking only single objective and not considering other objectives\.Multiple objectives are hard to solve\.If the environment changes then vehicle routine problem gets a new route hence problem becomes challenging\.In theory the data about customers and route to reach the customer will be known before but the real case scenario the request from customer changes so the environment is dynamic\.The dynamic vehicle routing problem is discussed in this study with time window where which customer has to visit in what time is included\.Multiple objectives such as the waiting time of the customer and minimizing routing distance to reach the customer are considered here\.Hence there are multiple objectives in the dynamic environment an ensemble learning to obtain an optimization problem is discussed here ie;EL\-DMOEA\.Other algorithms to solve vehicle routing problem along with their comparison additionally different types of platoons, comparison of platoon with other types of the transportation is also discussed in this report\.
