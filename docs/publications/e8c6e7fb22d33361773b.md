# DIRECTIONS FOR IMPROVING ECONOMETRIC MODELING OF THE IMPACT OF WOMEN&\#x27;S ENTREPRENEURSHIP ON ENHANCING THE EFFICIENCY OF HIGHER EDUCATION SERVICES

**Autores:** Kubayiva Sarvinoz

**Data:** 2026-10-15 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

This thesis develops directions for improving econometric modeling mechanisms of the impact of women’s entrepreneurship on enhancing the efficiency of higher education services through modern analytical tools\. It substantiates ways to improve decision\-making efficiency by integrating spatial econometrics, structural equation modeling \(SEM\), threshold regression models, and machine learning algorithms with econometric forecasting\.
