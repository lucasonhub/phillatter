# AI driven health monitoring with indoor localization using geomagnetic sensor fields and WLAN data fusion

**Autores:** Pooja Narasimhan; Anuhya Arun; R\. Parvathi; V\. Pattabiraman

**Data:** 2026-10-15 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Indoor localization is a new technology to monitor of the health condition movements of individual patients that can be accomplished with the assistance of AI driven approach with the multimodal data fusion, i\.e\. geomagnetic sensors fields – accelerometers, gyroscopes, magnetometers and Wi\-Fi signals\. The current methods have three key limitations: \(i\) no robust cross\-modal interaction modelling, \(ii\) low sensor\-noise and environment\-variable adaptability and \(iii\) no integrated health\-conscious mobility tracking\. The concept of finding the location of a person or item in a confined space where GPS signals cannot be used is known as indoor localization\. Wi\-Fi RSSI, inertial measurement units \(IMU\) and geomagnetic sensors have become the multimodal data fusion that has demonstrated great power in enhancing localization accuracy\. This work employs two combinatorial architectures, a hierarchical clustering tree \(HCT\) fusion model model used for fine\-grained hierarchical representation and a RF dominant ensemble used for generalization under sensor noise and variability\. The hierarchical clustering tree \(HCT\) based approach proposed in this study can achieve a classification accuracy of 91\.73%, and its macro F1 score is 0\.9173 in the next\-room prediction task its mean absolute error \(MAE\) in the travel time prediction task is 2\.87 s\.
