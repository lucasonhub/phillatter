# Sample\-Optimal Multi\-Group Learning with an ERM Oracle

**Autores:** Princewill Okoroafor

**Data:** 2026-10-11 · **Tipo:** preprint

## Resumo

In multi\-group learning, a single predictor must perform nearly as well as the best hypothesis in a class on every group in a rich, possibly infinite and overlapping, collection of subpopulations\. The known algorithms with the optimal sample complexity keep track of every pair of a group and a hypothesis, which is impossible for the large classes of interest, and the known oracle\-based algorithms have a worse rate\. We ask whether the optimal rate can be attained by a learner that accesses the classes only through an empirical risk minimization \(ERM\) oracle\. We give an algorithm that uses $\\tilde\{O\}\(\(d\_G\+d\_H\)/\\varepsilon^2\)$ samples and polynomially many calls to a weighted ERM oracle over pairs of a group and a hypothesis, where $d\_G$ and $d\_H$ are the VC dimensions of the group and hypothesis classes and $\\varepsilon$ bounds the excess risk on every group, weighted by its probability\. We also show a matching lower bound of $Ω\(\(d\_G\+d\_H\)/\\varepsilon^2\)$ samples for every learner even with unlimited computation, so oracle efficiency costs no additional samples\. We also consider a natural special case in which every input belongs to at most $k$ groups and these groups can be listed\. In this case, an ERM oracle for the hypothesis class alone suffices, with $\\tilde\{O\}\(\(d\_H\+\\log k\)/\\varepsilon^2\)$ samples in the agnostic setting and $\\tilde\{O\}\(\(d\_H\+\\log k\)/\\varepsilon\)$ samples when every group has a hypothesis that is correct on it\.
