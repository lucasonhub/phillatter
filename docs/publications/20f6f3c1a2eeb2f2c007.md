# Crowd Detection and Overcrowding Alert System

**Autores:** Mrs\.Ayesha Siddiqa; Chaitra P; Varsha H R; Sahana H V; Yashaswini S N

**Data:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

A crowd detection and overcrowding alert system is proposed to monitor public spaces and prevent unsafe crowd conditions\. The system applies computer vision and deep learning techniques to analyze live video feeds from surveillance cameras, detecting the number of people present, tracking movement patterns, and estimating crowd density in specific zones\. When density exceeds a predefined safety threshold, the system automatically generates alerts for authorities, enabling timely intervention and helping to prevent accidents, stampedes, and congestion in locations such as malls, transportation hubs, campuses, and public events\. The proposed solution is designed to integrate with existing CCTV infrastructure as well as IoT devices and mobile notification services for faster response, and to provide continuous monitoring and visual insight through a live dashboard so that authorities can easily identify high\-risk zones\. By reducing dependence on manual supervision, the system is intended to increase surveillance efficiency, support scalable deployment in both small and large environments, and lay the groundwork for future extensions such as behavior analysis and anomaly detection, contributing to public safety and smart\-city crowd management\.
