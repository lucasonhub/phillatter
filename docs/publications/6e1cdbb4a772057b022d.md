# Gramatikoff, Kosi: AI\-Guided Precision Medicine in Europe: Architecture and Action

**Autores:** 

**Data de descoberta:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** unknown

## Descrição do feed

Artificial intelligence \(AI\) now touches genomics, multi\-omics, pharmacogenomics, liquid biopsy and digital biosensing at once, but data infrastructure, governance and evidence standards have not kept pace\. This European Biotechnology Thematic Network Association \(EBTNA\) position paper draws on peer\-reviewed studies, regulatory reviews and official policy surveys published up to June 2026 to assess clinical maturity and set priorities for responsible implementation across Europe\. Rather than treating these domains as parallel technologies, the paper organizes them within a six\-layer conceptual architecture that separates clinical purpose, patient\-state measurement, AI integration, clinical action, evidence and translation, and governance\. Across the WHO European Region, only 8% of Member States have a health\-specific AI strategy, 24% provide in\-service AI training for the health workforce, and fewer than 10% have drafted liability standards for AI in health\. The FDA, meanwhile, had authorized 903 AI\-enabled medical devices by August 2024; 97\.1% were cleared through the 510\(k\) pathway, which typically does not require independent clinical evidence\. On this evidence, EBTNA proposes seven lines of action that link technical performance to clinical utility, equity, transparency and continuous oversight, each mapped to a specific layer of the framework\. \(direct link \)
