# Integrated metabolomic and transcriptomic analyses reveal tissue\-specific distribution and gene\-metabolite associations of quality\-related metabolites in the Tibetan Plateau turnip landrace “Yushuyuangen” \(Brassica rapa ssp\. rapa\)

**Autores:** Caixia Li; Ruxue Qian; Quanhui Li; Yanjing Ren

**Data:** 2026-10-13 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Yushuyuangen \( Brassica rapa ssp\. rapa \) is a characteristic turnip landrace cultivated on the Qinghai–Tibet Plateau and valued for its nutritional and flavor qualities\. However, the tissue\-specific distribution of quality\-related metabolites and the gene\-expression patterns associated with their accumulation remain poorly understood\. Here, metabolomics and transcriptomics were integrated with weighted gene co\-expression network analysis \(WGCNA\) to investigate three root tissues in two varieties, YPW \(purple\-white\) and YGW \(green\-white\): the upper colored peel \(purple in YPW and green in YGW\), lower white peel, and root flesh\. A total of 775 metabolites were identified, showing clear tissue\-dependent metabolic differentiation\. Sugar\-phosphate intermediates and aromatic/aspartate\-family amino acids accumulated mainly in root flesh \(glucose\-6\-phosphate, fructose\-6\-phosphate, phenylalanine, and homoserine\), whereas branched\-chain amino acids were abundant in colored peel \(valine and leucine\)\. Purple peel accumulated higher levels of p\- coumaric acid and ferulic acid, while flavonoids differed markedly between purple and green peel, represented by phloretin and diosmetin in purple peel and trifolin and kaempferol\-7\- O \-glucoside in green peel\. Integrated WGCNA and correlation analyses further identified tissue\-associated relationships among metabolites, structural genes, and transcription factors\. Four candidate transcription factor–structural gene–metabolite associations were highlighted: NAC22 – F16P2\.1 –D\-fructose\-6\-phosphate, bHLH65 / ARFD4 – TRBP2\.2 –L\-tryptophan, MYB62 / AGL63 – PAL1\.2 / C4H\.2 – p\- coumaric acid, and MYB62 – FLS1 / F3′H1\.2 –trifolin/kaempferol\-7\- O \-glucoside\. These associations were inferred from co\-expression and correlation patterns and represent candidate relationships for further functional validation\.
