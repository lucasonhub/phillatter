# A Flexible Cognitive Built Wearable Platform Using Multimodal Therapeutic Sensors and Reinforcement Learning Technique for Upper\-Limb Stroke Rehabilitation

**Autores:** Kalaiarasi Arumugam; Shyma Sasidharan; P\. Prabhakaran; M\. Kavya; Deepa Beeta Thiyam; R\. Shelishiyah

**Data:** 2026-10-14 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Stroke is one of the most common long\-term motor disabilities which requires a personalized rehabilitation strategy capable of delivering reliable therapeutic interventions with continuous assessment\. This paper proposes the integration of the inertial measurement unit, force\-sensing technology, and electromyography \(EMG\), along with an artificial intelligence \(AI\)\-based, cognitive–adaptive control platform and intelligent framework within a closed loop for precision neurorehabilitation\. This research proposes a methodology to continuously monitor patients’ motor functions and compensate for movements, dynamically optimize rehabilitation, and detect fatigue intensity in real time\. This proposed architecture combines deep learning\-based movement analysis with multimodal sensor fusion and reinforcement learning\-based adaptive control\. This is done alongside cloud\-based facility analytics for long\-term longitudinal assessment and clinic medical assistant therapy management, and a lightweight edge computing module for feature extraction and cognitive decision\-making\. A total of 36 post\-stroke patients with upper\-limb hemiplegia were used in an 8\-week clinical study, in which Fugl–Meyer assessment \(FMA\) scores increased by an average of 87\.9%, EMG muscle activation by 42\.8%, and therapy efficiency improved by 31\.6%\. Therapy efficiency improved by 32\.8% and 21\.9% rehabilitation reduction when compared with traditional physiotherapy\. The composite performance index has rapidly increased to 0\.949, indicating overall rehabilitation efficiency, with repeated measures analysis of variance confirming a statistically significant trend\. Thus, the home\-based stroke recovery with substantial potential for research constrains the healthcare environment with smart adaptive and real\-time patient\-specific optimization, which is proposed in this AI framework as an interpretable clinical deployment solution\.
