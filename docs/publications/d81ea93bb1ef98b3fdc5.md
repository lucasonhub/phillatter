# DYNAMIC RECRYSTALLIZATION BEHAVIOR AND GRAIN SIZE CONTROL OF 316LN AUSTENITIC STAINLESS STEEL FOR LIQUID HYDROGEN STORAGE AND TRANSPORTATION

**Autores:** Xin Ouyang; Xuemin Wang; Hongwei Wang; Chenxi Liu; Mengnan Xing; Xinming Hu

**Data:** 2026-10-12 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

In this paper, the effect of hot deformation parameters on dynamic recrystallization behavior of 316LN austenitic stainless steel for liquid hydrogen storage was systematically investigated\. The grain size of the experimental material should be stably controlled at 4–6 levels to optimize the balance of strength and toughness, and improve the hydrogen embrittlement resistance and welding processing performance\. The single\-pass compression deformation experiment \(temperature 900–1150 °C, strain rate 0\.05\-10 s–1, true strain 80 %\) was carried out by Gleeble3800 thermal simulation test machine\. The microstructure was observed by metallography and field\-emission scanning electron microscopy with EBSD, and the grain orientation, size and grain boundary distribution were characterized\. The results show that the content of high\-angle grain boundary \(HAGB\) increases from 62 ± 3% to 78 ± 4% during the heating process at 950–1000 °C, which confirms the recrystallization transition from low\-angle grain boundary to high\-angle grain boundary; When the strain rate increases from 0\.1 s–1 to 1 s–1, the proportion of low angle grain boundary \(LAGB\) increases from 45 % to 68 %, reflecting the inhibitory effect of a high strain rate on the recrystallization process\. The research reveals the dual control mechanism of temperature and strain rate on discontinuous dynamic recrystallization\. The optimized rolling process parameters of 10501100 °C/0\.05–0\.1 s–1 can achieve uniform grain refinement, which provides key theoretical guidance for the manufacture of liquid hydrogen storage and transportation equipment, and is of great significance to ensure the safe operation of the equipment\.
