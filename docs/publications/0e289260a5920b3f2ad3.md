# Emerging Nephrology Nursing: Current &amp; Future roles

**Autores:** 

**Data:** 2026-10-13 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Nephrology nursing has evolved significantly over the past decades, transitioning from task\-oriented care to a comprehensive, patient\-centered specialty that integrates clinical expertise, advanced technology, and chronic disease management\.This review explores the emerging roles of nephrology nurses in the current healthcare landscape and envisions future directions shaped by technological advancements, healthcare reforms, and evolving patient needs\.Current roles encompass not only hemodialysis and peritoneal dialysis care but also kidney transplantation, conservative kidney management, patient education, and multidisciplinary collaboration\.The increasing prevalence of chronic kidney disease and the complexity of renal replacement therapies demand advanced competencies, including leadership, research, and quality improvement\.Future roles are anticipated to expand into telehealth, remote patient monitoring, artificial intelligence\-assisted care, genomics\-informed nursing, and community\-based kidney health promotion\.The integration of advanced practice nursing and nurseled clinics is expected to further strengthen chronic disease management and reduce hospital readmissions\.This review highlights the need for continuous professional development, specialized training programs, and policy support to equip nephrology nurses for these evolving responsibilities\.Emerging nephrology nursing roles are critical to addressing the global burden of kidney disease and enhancing the quality, accessibility, and sustainability of kidney care delivery\.
