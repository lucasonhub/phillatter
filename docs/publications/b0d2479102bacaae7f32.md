# An Analytical Study of Functionalism, Computationalism and Mind

**Autores:** Rajeev Lochan Tripathi

**Data:** 2025-01-01 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

In this article, I make an attempt to focus on the theories of functionalism and computationalism in the philosophy of mind, including materialistic and rationalistic functions of the mind which are dealt with by the Philosophers such as Descartes, Spinoza, Leibniz, Locke, Berkeley and Hume\.The article deals with the fact that functionalism enables the actualization of mental states in a wide range of physical systems, such as robots or alien life forms, which rules out the conventional perceptions of mind\-body correlations\.The paper has critically assessed the shortcomings of functionalism to explain first\-person experiences, subjective life discussed and purported by authors such as Thomas Nagel in his argument on what it is like to be a bat\.It also explores the notion of multiple realizability and how it applies to the artificial intelligence\.Computational models of mind, Turing machines and probabilistic automata are also discussed in the text, with parallels drawn between them and human mind\.All in all, the article gives a concise introduction to major arguments in present\-day philosophy of mind and points out the difficulty of making sense of consciousness and states of mind\.
