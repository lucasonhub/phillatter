# A Survey of AI\-Based Visual Defect Detection Techniques for Automotive Manufacturing

**Autores:** Rutvi Landge; Sakshi Barhate; Rutuja Kashid; Ritesh Kadam; Manisha Darak

**Data:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Quality inspection is an essential process in automotive manufacturing, as defects in vehicle components can affect product quality, reliability, and production efficiency\. Traditional inspection methods often rely on manual examination, which can be time\-consuming and may produce inconsistent results under varying manufacturing conditions\. Recent advancements in Artificial Intelligence \(AI\) and Computer Vision have created opportunities for automated visual defect inspection\. This paper presents a survey of AI\-based visual defect detection techniques applicable to automotive manufacturing\. It examines approaches such as You Only Look Once \(YOLO\), image segmentation, and anomaly detection for identifying defects including scratches, dents, cracks, corrosion, and surface irregularities\. The survey aims to compare these techniques based on their reported capabilities, limitations, computational requirements, and suitability for industrial applications\. It also examines challenges related to dataset availability, class imbalance, changing environmental conditions, and deployment in real\-world manufacturing environments\. Based on the literature review, research gaps are identified to establish a foundation for Defect Lens, a proposed AI\-powered visual quality inspection platform for automotive manufacturing
