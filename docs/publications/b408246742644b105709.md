# Design and Empirical Evaluation of Erhu Teaching Aid System Based on Virtual Reality

**Autores:** Li Han

**Data:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Digital technology facilitates traditional cultural inheritance, and virtual reality \(VR\) provides innovative approaches for ethnic musical instrument teaching\. This paper constructs a three‑module VR‑assisted Erhu teaching system to solve issues of one‑way instruction, delayed feedback, and single learning scenarios in conventional Erhu teaching\. Supported by motion capture and audio analysis, the system delivers real‑time playing correction\. An eight‑week controlled experiment with 120 learners shows that the VR group achieves higher fingering accuracy, lower pitch deviation, and better learning engagement than the traditional‑teaching control group\. Still, the system faces hardware‑related defects, and the study has limitations such as uncertain long‑term skill retention\. VR serves as an auxiliary rather than a replacement for Erhu instruction\. Future optimizations include lightweight Web‑XR adaptation and intelligent teaching resources to advance popularized, personalized VR Erhu education\.
