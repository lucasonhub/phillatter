# &quot;Marketing and Consumer Behaviour in the Digital Era: Innovation, Technology, Consumer Decision\-Making, Challenges and Opportunities &quot;

**Autores:** Preeti Patidar

**Data:** 2026-10-11 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

The digital revolution has transformed the traditional structure of marketing and significantly influenced consumer Behaviour across global markets\. The rapid expansion of internet connectivity, social media platforms, artificial intelligence, e\-commerce systems, and data analytics has reshaped the relationship between businesses and consumers\. Modern consumers are no longer passive recipients of marketing messages; rather, they actively seek information, compare alternatives, evaluate reviews, and make informed purchasing decisions through digital channels\. Consequently, organizations are adopting innovative marketing strategies to engage customers, build brand loyalty, and enhance customer satisfaction\. The present study examines the interaction between digital marketing practices and consumer Behaviour in the contemporary business environment\. It explores the influence of social media advertising, e\-commerce platforms, brand image, customer relationship management, pricing strategies, influencer marketing, green marketing, and artificial intelligence on consumer decision\-making processes\. The study also highlights the differences in consumer Behaviour across rural and urban markets and investigates the opportunities and challenges emerging from technological advancements\. By analyzing existing literature and contemporary market trends, the paper aims to provide a comprehensive understanding of how innovation and technology shape consumer attitudes, preferences, and purchasing Behaviour\. The findings emphasize that organizations must adopt customer\-centric and technology\-driven strategies to remain competitive in the rapidly evolving digital marketplace\.
