# BO‘LAJAK MUSIQA O‘QITUVCHILARINING KASBIY KOMPETENTLIGINI TAKOMILLASHTIRISHNING PEDAGOGIK MEXANIZMLARI

**Autores:** Karimov Inoyat Allamovich

**Data:** 2026-10-13 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

This article analyzes the theoretical and methodological foundations, pedagogical mechanisms for improving the professional competence of future music teachers, and the possibilities for their integration into the higher pedagogical education process\. The professional competence of a future music teacher is interpreted as an integrated system of musical\-theoretical knowledge, vocal and instrumental performance skills, artistic\-aesthetic thinking, pedagogical\-methodological training, communicative abilities, digital competence, and reflexive activity\. The article reveals the possibilities of competency\-based, personality\- oriented, activity\-based, integrative, and reflexive approaches in developing professional competence\.
