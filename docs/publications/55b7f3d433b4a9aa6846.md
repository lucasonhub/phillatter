# Renewable Energy Roadmap for Hospitals: ML\-Enabled Forecasting and Sizing of a Hybrid PV–Wind System in Türkiye

**Autores:** Mehmet Daş; Gül Nihal Güğül; Erhan Arslan

**Data:** 2026-10-15 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Energy use for heating, cooling, and lighting in buildings continues to increase, driving fossil fuel consumption and carbon emissions\. Hospitals face especially high demand\. This study designs and evaluates a hospital\-scale hybrid renewable system \(solar PV plus wind\) for Diyarbakır, Türkiye, and develops real data\-driven performance models to reduce energy consumption\. Pace regression, incorporating weather and building characteristics, is used to predict PV and wind generation\. Three months of data \(Aug–Oct 2022\)—average temperature 26 °C, peak solar irradiance 1012 W/m², average wind speed 1\.18 m/s—and total electricity use of 2895 MWh were analyzed\. Models were validated using RMSE and MAPE\. Results show strong fit \(PV: R² = 0\.9262, RMSE = 0\.037; wind: R² = 0\.986, RMSE = 0\.021\)\. Estimated errors were 14% for solar and 11% for wind\. Scenario analysis indicates the hybrid system could supply approximately 7\.14% of the hospital’s annual demand via PV and 0\.02% via wind\. Even in modest\-wind regimes, machine\-learning\-guided design enhances forecasting and sizing, providing a practical roadmap for public buildings in diverse climates to optimize renewables, reduce grid dependence, and lower emissions while maintaining service reliability\.
