# Prototyping and evaluation of a clinical decision support system in long\-term care in German nursing homes

**Autores:** Maximilian Pilz; Anne Gebert; Matthias Brünett; Laura Steffny; Christina Schubert; Jonas Flechsig; Alexander Scherrer

**Data:** 2026-10-10 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Demographic change in Germany is increasing the demand for professional long\-term care\. Given rising requirements and simultaneous staff shortages, the need for digital systems is growing to effectively support nursing staff and mitigate staffing bottlenecks\. This article introduces a hybrid clinical decision support system \(CDSS\) designed to generate actionable intervention recommendations by combining structured data and narrative nursing documentation\. The CDSS consists of three interacting components: \(1\) knowledge models that encode state\-of\-the\-art nursing knowledge, \(2\) a rule\-based expert system that applies these models to generate individualized nursing interventions within the application fields mobility and pain management, and \(3\) a large language model \(LLM\) that extracts indicators that are necessary for knowledge\-based decision\-making from free\-text entries to fill documentation gaps\. A prototype was implemented and descriptively evaluated in two German nursing homes\. Overall, 72% of the CDSS recommendations were rated as &quot;correct&quot; by nursing professionals, meaning appropriate for the care situation\. In a separate evaluation, the LLM achieved 76% agreement rate in extracting care\-relevant information from narratives\. These results suggest that a hybrid architecture integrating expert knowledge, rule\-based logic, and language models may have the potential to enhance nursing planning in long\-term care settings\.
