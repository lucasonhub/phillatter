# Green nanotechnology for a sustainable future

**Autores:** Mansimzadeh Sama Mugaddas

**Data:** 2026-10-15 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Green nanotechnology is a modern scientific field directed towards using the possibilities of nanotechnology to be safer, resource\-saving, and to reduce the impact on the environment\. In this article, the main principles of green nanotechnology, the synthesis of nanomaterials by ecological methods, and various application areas have been investigated\. The main mechanisms and advantages of obtaining nanomaterials using plant extracts, microorganisms, and biomass\-based raw materials have been reviewed\. At the same time, the application of green nanotechnology in agriculture in the form of nanofertilizers, nanopesticides, and nanosensors, and in the cleaning of water and soil through adsorption, photocatalysis, and nanofiltration processes has been analyzed\. The article has also given attention to problems such as the ecotoxicological effects of nanomaterials, the scaling up of production, and standardization\. It has been shown that in the future, approaches such as artificial intelligence, circular economy, and life cycle assessment can contribute to the more sustainable development of green nanotechnology\.
