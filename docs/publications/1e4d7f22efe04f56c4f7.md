# Lu, Jiapeng: A Realist Reinterpretation of the Interface Problem: An Inquiry Based on the Higher\-Order Representational System

**Autores:** 

**Data de descoberta:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** unknown

## Descrição do feed

In the philosophy of action, the interface problem is usually understood as the difficulty of explaining how intentions in a propositional format are connected with motor representations in a motor format\. Existing solutions diverge between dissolutionism and the bridging approach, a divergence that stems from different understandings of what the problem involves—especially with respect to format, representation, and the boundary between the conscious and the unconscious\. In order to remove this ambiguity, I propose a realist reformulation of the interface problem\. Rather than treating it as a single and abstract problem of cross\-format translation, I recast it as a set of re\-representational problems within a higher\-order representational system jointly realized by brain, body, and environment\. On this basis, I further argue that the crux of the existing debate lies not merely in the question of whether an intermediate layer is needed, but in the failure of existing accounts to explain how intention, bodily feedback, and external effects form continuous functional relations within the representational chains of meaning externalization and meaning internalization\. This reinterpretation does not eliminate the need to explain cross\-format transformations; instead, it relocates that explanatory burden to identifiable boundary sites that can be described phenomenologically, clarified through representational theory, and further investigated empirically\.
