# The groundwater position on the behavior of the fully\-rough strip footings

**Autores:** Kaan Yünkül

**Data:** 2026-10-14 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

In this study, a total of 98 comprehensive 3D parametric finite element \(FE\) analyses were carried out to investigate the effect of normalized water table depth \(dw/B\) and relative density \(Dr\) of the soil beneath the footing on the ultimate bearing capacity \(qult\), pressure\-settlement \(σ\-s\) response, correction coefficient \(cw\), critical failure surface depth ratio \(dc/B\), and failure mechanism of a fully\-rough strip footing on sandy soil\. Seven different Dr values, ranging from 10% to 80% and thirteen different dw/B values, ranging from 0 to 2 as well as fully\-dry condition, were preferred\. The FE analysis results demonstrated that qult and cw values, and stiffness of the σ\-s responses increased with increasing dw/B ratios until reaching the dc/B value\. Beyond this threshold, the obtained values coincided with those corresponding to fully\-dry condition\. It was observed that groundwater position and the Dr of the soil significantly affected the geometry and dimension of the failure surface\. Furthermore, new semi\-empirical equations were proposed for predicting dc/B and cw values considering internal friction angle, dw/B, effective and saturated unit weight values, showing good agreement with the findings of the FE analyses\.
