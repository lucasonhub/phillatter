# Algorithms of agricultural navigation

**Autores:** Anatolii Kaliuhnyi

**Data:** 2026-10-18 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Rationale\. Automation of self\-propelled agricultural machines and machine\-tractor units \(MTU\), which ensures round\-the\-clock performance of field work without tractor driver&\#x27;s participation, can improve the efficiency of crop production and increase food production\. The obstacle is the lack of formed agricultural navigation as an independent scientific direction on instrumental methods and technical means of controlling the movement of unmanned MTU\. Research objective\. Formation of the theory of agricultural navigation of tilled MTU\. Research methods\. The research is exploratory, aimed at using theoretical knowledge to solve the applied problem of determining the trajectory control error of unmanned MTU\. The methods used are analogies, full\-scale and thought experiments, mathematical modeling, intuition, comparisons, etc\. Information from navigation, electrodynamics, mechanization, automation, instrumentation, etc\. were used as initial information\. The methods of analogies, natural and mental experiments, mathematical modeling, intuition, comparisons and were used\. Information from navigation, electrodynamics, electro\-exploration geophysics, agronomy, mechanization of mobile crop production processes, automatics, instrumentation, agricultural engineering, etc\. was used as initial information\. Results\. Programming of MTU trajectories is carried out in coordinate systems rigidly “tied” to the terrain by physical landmarks\. There are only three ways of specifying coordinate systems: two points, one point and a direction, a line along the entire trajectory\. The location of the MTU is defined either in the same coordinate system or in a mobile coordinate system defined by the location of the “leading” point of the tractor and the directions of its axes\. In both cases, the location of the MTU is determined by the distance between the MTU and the landmarks of the stationary coordinate system\. Point landmarks are hundreds of meters and more distant, linear landmarks \- on units of meters\. Therefore, it is easier to determine the MTU location by the distance to linear landmarks than to point landmarks\. It is advisable to use stationary underground current\-carrying wires as linear reference points\. The currents of the wires create navigational magnetic fields that allow to determine the location of MTU with high accuracy\. Programming of trajectories in the magnetic fields of current\-carrying wires is carried out by “guiding surfaces”, which represent the geometric location of points, when the leading point of the tractor is aligned with them, there is no trajectory control error signal formed by the navigation device of location determination\. The trajectories are programmed by setting the planned values of the “electric” and “mechanical” navigation parameters of the positioning device\. The first parameter determines the lateral displacement of the leading point relative to the current carrying wire, the second parameter determines the lateral displacement of the leading point relative to the longitudinal axis of the tractor\. The planned value of one or both of these parameters can be zero\. The maximum accuracy is possessed by positioning devices with zero planned electrical parameter\. The article considers algorithms of formation of trajectory control error signals by less accurate but structurally simpler devices with zero value of mechanical parameter\. Conclusion\. For programming the required and determining the actual trajectories of unmanned MTAs, including tilled ones, it is advisable to use the systems of coordinates set by stationary landmarks in the form of underground current\-carrying wires\. Key words: agricultural navigation; navigation inductor; location algorithm; unmanned agricultural technologies\.
