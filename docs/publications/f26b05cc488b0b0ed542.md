# Spargur, Brian: A Functional Skeleton for Bounded Cognitive Agency: Canonical Protocol Specification

**Autores:** 

**Data de descoberta:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** unknown

## Descrição do feed

Papers I and II define a shared constrained model family for human cognition: a typed, weighted graph with context eligibility, nonredistributing gates, budget\-limited propagation, bounded dynamic regimes, learning, compilation, and separately estimable readouts\. This paper treats that family as a functional specification and declares ten capacities held exogenous in zero closure\. A1\-A17 give a bounded candidate architecture for closing those gaps\. Version 19 preserves every A\-series equation, proposition, CAP/CSC boundary, and CSLP/LPC/CML/BOWB/EBG/BSIC protocol\. It adds only a security\-declaration contract and composition\-level handoff clarifications for the Paper IV mapper, action, temporal\-valuation, and emotion\-observation profiles\. A\-P1 proves exact nesting; A\-P2 proves the activation bound; and A\-P3 bounds the admitted core under finite authorized libraries\. Paper IV v20, not Paper III, owns IAP, FHFS, MP, W1\-W5, and CTT\-1\. Their human\-side interpretations remain prospective\.
