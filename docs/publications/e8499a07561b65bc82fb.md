# Human or Virtual? Comparing How Influencer Credibility Shapes Purchase Intention through Parasocial Relationship and Consumer\-Brand Engagement in East Kalimantan, Indonesia

**Autores:** Purwinahyu Purwinahyu; Asnawati Asnawati; Gusti Noorlitaria Achmad

**Data:** 2026-10-12 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

This study examines whether influencer credibility is associated with purchase intention through parasocial relationship \(PSR\) and consumer\-brand engagement \(CBE\), and whether the proposed sequential association differs between human and virtual influencers\. A cross\-sectional survey of 450 social\-media users in East Kalimantan, Indonesia, was analyzed using partial least squares structural equation modeling, bootstrapped serial mediation, measurement invariance testing, multi\-group analysis, and predictive assessment\. Credibility was positively associated with PSR \(β = 0\.472\), PSR with CBE \(β = 0\.392\), and CBE with purchase intention \(β = 0\.517\)\. The serial indirect association was statistically supported \(β = 0\.096; 95% CI \[0\.064, 0\.131\]\)\. The credibility–PSR association was stronger for human than virtual influencers \(β = 0\.548 vs\. 0\.391; permutation p = 0\.008\), as was the serial indirect effect \(β = 0\.116 vs\. 0\.071; p = 0\.021\)\. By contrast, the CBE–purchase intention association did not differ significantly between groups \(p = 0\.614\)\. These results identify a source\-type difference at the influencer–consumer bonding stage rather than at the brand\-engagement–intention stage\. The study contributes a dual\-stage relational account that distinguishes PSR directed toward an influencer from CBE directed toward the endorsed brand\. For practitioners, the findings suggest that virtual\-influencer strategies may benefit from credible, consistent, and transparent persona narratives that sustain relational engagement, while both influencer types should connect audience attention to the endorsed brand\. The cross\-sectional, non\-randomized design limits causal inference\.
