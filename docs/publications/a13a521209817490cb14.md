# THE EL\-RAKHAWI DISCRETE TIME\-CRYSTAL QUANTUM ARCHITECTURE \(DTC\-QA\): NON\-EQUILIBRIUM PHASE COMPUTING AND ULTRA\-PRECISE SPATIOTEMPORAL METROLOGY VIA BROKEN TIME TRANSLATION SYMMETRY

**Autores:** mohamed kamal arafa el\-rakhawi

**Data:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** book

## Resumo

The El\-Rakhawi Discrete Time\-Crystal Quantum Architecture \(DTC\-QA\) transitions quantum computing from spatial to spatiotemporal paradigms\. It utilizes Discrete Time Crystals \(DTCs\) engineered via Floquet driving and Many\-Body Localization \(MBL\) to perform non\-equilibrium phase computing\. The system spontaneously breaks discrete time\-translation symmetry, exhibiting sub\-harmonic oscillations where the expectation value satisfies O\(t\+T\) \!= O\(t\) but O\(t\+nT\) = O\(t\) for integer n&gt;1\. This topologically protected temporal order enables Chrono\-Reservoir Computing, where classical measurement outcomes of local observables serve as high\-dimensional temporal features without full\-state tomography\. The architecture minimizes the El\-Rakhawi Chrono\-Computational Loss \(L\_CCL\), penalizing subsystem bipartite entanglement entropy growth to prevent Floquet heating\. Performance is quantified by the El\-Rakhawi Chrono\-Computational Fidelity Metric \(ECCFM\), yielding bits per Joule\. Applications include fault\-tolerant quantum AI, ultralight dark matter detection, and precision quantum metrology, bridging quantum physics, materials science, and artificial intelligence\.
