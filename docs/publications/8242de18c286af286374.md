# Fresh at Decision, Stale at Action: Routing New Information to LLM Decisions Already Made

**Autores:** Ayoub Merimi; Souhail Bakkali; Abdeljalil Sakat

**Data:** 2026-10-13 · **Tipo:** preprint

## Resumo

Freshness shapes an LLM decision at the moment it is made; the action it commits to is latent, carried out later, in a changing world\. We therefore introduce a timestamp theory of decision validity and a decision ledger that realises it\. Six timestamps define a decision; a freshness ceiling bounds what up\-to\-date evidence guarantees at action time, placing the remaining risk in information published afterwards\. Accuracy then factorises into two capabilities: routing, which delivers that information to affected decisions before their revision deadlines, and temporal judgment, which reads it for the time of action\. The ledger is persistent state holding each decision&\#x27;s times, evidence and background knowledge, indexed by watch conditions written at decision time\. Controlled decision streams with exact labels confirm four predictions across five LLMs, from 8B to frontier scale: freshness alone yields exactly the accuracy the ceiling predicts; models act on decisive news once it arrives, so accuracy follows routing; the ledger&\#x27;s index sets what routing reaches, and watch conditions reach indirect dependencies at low exposure; and the remaining errors concern when a change takes effect\. Freshness makes a decision right when it is made; routing and temporal judgment keep it right when it is carried out\.
