# Macrophage reprogramming by the ultraviolet filter benzophenone\-1 promotes malignant phenotypes in breast cancer cells: integrated computational and experimental evidence

**Autores:** Binglu He; Longying Ding; Xiaolu Yang; Tianqi Zhang; Li Ma

**Data:** 2026-10-13 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Background Benzophenone\-1 \(BP\-1\) is a benzophenone ultraviolet absorber used in cosmetic and personal care products\. Although BP\-1 exhibits estrogenic activity, its broader effects in breast cancer remain unclear\. This study investigated the molecular and cellular responses associated with BP\-1 exposure\. Methods Integrated computational analyses were used to identify core targets and determine their cellular distribution\. Molecular modeling evaluated BP\-1–target binding\. Macrophage polarization and secretion were assessed by immunofluorescence, RT\-qPCR, enzyme\-linked immunosorbent assay, and western blotting\. The effects of macrophage\-conditioned medium on breast cancer cells were examined using proliferation, migration, invasion, and epithelial\-mesenchymal transition assays\. Results PLAUR was identified as the core BP\-1\-related candidate and was prominently expressed in macrophages\. BP\-1 increased expression of uPAR, the protein encoded by PLAUR , and shifted macrophages toward an M2\-like phenotype\. Conditioned medium from these macrophages enhanced malignant phenotypes in breast cancer cells\. Conclusion This study identifies a macrophage\-associated response to BP\-1 exposure that may contribute to malignant phenotypes in estrogen receptor\-positive breast cancer, with PLAUR /uPAR as a candidate mediator\. These findings provide a basis for further mechanistic and in vivo studies\.
