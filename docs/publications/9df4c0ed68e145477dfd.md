# Parameterization of an inflation swap on the Russian over\-the\-counter derivatives market

**Autores:** A\.V\. Zhdanov

**Data:** 2026-10-14 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Aim\. To develop scientific and practical recommendations for structuring an over\-the\-counter inflation swap on the Russian derivatives market under conditions of persistent uncertainty in inflation dynamics and the heightened importance of hedging inflation risk\. Objectives\. To reveal the economic essence of an inflation swap; to systematize the elements of its structuring mechanism; to determine the sequence of theoretical\-methodological and practical stages in creating the instrument; to substantiate the use of an inflation forecasting model and a methodology for assessing the inflation risk premium when calculating the parameters of the transaction\. Methods \. The research is based on an analysis of scholarly publications on inflation derivatives, inflation forecasting, inflation risk premium assessment, and yield curve modeling\. The methodological foundation is the integration of previously developed elements of a unified inflation swap structuring mechanism: the instrument architecture, the inflation forecasting model, and the inflation risk premium assessment model\. As applied tools, the gradient boosting algorithm, the Svensson model, and a factor approach to assessing the premium component associated with inflation uncertainty are examined\. Results\. A sequential mechanism for structuring an over\-the\-counter inflation swap adapted to the conditions of the Russian financial market is proposed\. The main groups of elements of the mechanism are identified, including parameters of the external market environment, pricing models, the composition of transaction participants, and the key components of the swap structure\. The approach to determining the fixed rate, the floating rate, and the markup in the form of an inflation risk premium is substantiated\. Conclusions\. The developed recommendations form a holistic scientific and practical approach to creating inflation swaps on the Russian over\-the\-counter market\. Their application may be in demand by the Bank of Russia, commercial banks, and institutional and corporate investors when introducing inflation risk hedging instruments, expanding the instrument base of the derivatives market, and enhancing the resilience of participants to inflation shocks\.
