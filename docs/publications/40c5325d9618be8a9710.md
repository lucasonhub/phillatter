# Evaluation of the accuracy of AI\-based pellet size measurement using experimental data and Monte Carlo simulation

**Autores:** Bettina Fazekas; Zoltán Petrovics; Dorián László Galata; Orsolya Péterfi; Éva Pusztai; Nikolett Kállai‐Szabó; István Antal; Edit Hirsch; Zsombor Kristóf Nagy

**Data:** 2026-10-14 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

The purpose of this work is to evaluate the capability of artificial intelligence \(AI\)\-based particle size measurement in the case of pellet coating\. We hypothesize that due to the large number of particles observed per minute, the technique should be able to give statistically significant results even for small diameter differences in the order of several μm\. Experiments were performed with samples taken during a pellet coating process, where each sample was found to be significantly different\. To overcome the limitations of experiments where it is not feasible to produce samples with arbitrarily small size differences, we performed simulations relying on the Monte Carlo method\. Based on the simulations and the observed number of particles per minute during the experiments, we found that in processes where the diameter increase rate of the particles is above 3\.30 μm/min, the current setup of AI\-based endoscopic imaging can be reliably used for process monitoring, because it can provide statistically significant results for the involved particle size changes\.
