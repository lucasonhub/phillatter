# Tech Mining with LLMs: Unveiling Trends from Corporate Websites

**Autores:** Xavier Martínez\-Barbero; Ana Pastor\-Merino; Josep Domènech

**Data:** 2026-10-17 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Abstract Technology mining enables the systematic extraction and analysis of trends regarding scientific and technological developments from diverse Science, Technology, and Innovation \(ST&amp;I\) data sources\. Traditionally, indicators such as patents, surveys, and R&amp;D statistics have been widely used to monitor technological change and innovation\. This study proposes a novel methodology leveraging semantic analysis of corporate website content using large language models \(LLMs\) to facilitate real\-time monitoring of technological developments\. Specifically, we focus on Spanish manufacturing firms that have adopted artificial intelligence \(AI\)\. A pipeline was developed to automatically extract and classify bigrams and unigrams from corporate websites into technological and non\-technological terms, enabling a temporal analysis of technologies for the years 2023 and 2025\. The results indicate an increase in AI\-related mentions, as well as notable patterns of co\-occurrence with Internet of Things \(IoT\) and Industry 4\.0 technologies\. Our study demonstrates the utility of the combination of corporate websites and LLMs as scalable, dynamic, and cost\-effective sources for technology mining, contributing methodologically through advanced text analytics and empirically by providing granular, web\-derived insights into technological dynamics within the manufacturing sector\.
