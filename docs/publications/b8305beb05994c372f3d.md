# Digital Transformation: Enabling Change Management Strategies and Performance for Nonprofit Organizations in Kenya

**Autores:** Duncan Mutulu; Robert M\. Arasa; James Murunga

**Data:** 2026-10-17 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

This study examines whether change management strategies \(leadership capacity building, capability reconfiguration initiatives, stakeholder engagement and communication strategies\) are associated with the performance of nonprofit organizations \(NPOs\) in Kenya, and whether digital transformation moderates their joint influence\. NPOs in resource\-constrained environments face donor dependency, funding volatility and weak institutional capacity, which make adaptability critical\. Anchored on the Resource\-Based View and supported by Dynamic Capabilities Theory and the Technology Infusion Model, the study adopted a pragmatic, convergent mixed\-methods cross\-sectional design\. A sample of 368 of the 4,750 compliant NPOs was determined with Yamane’s formula and drawn by stratified random sampling, and 261 usable questionnaires were returned \(70\.9%\)\. A pilot of 37 NPOs confirmed reliability \(Cronbach’s α = 0\.746–0\.944\) and construct validity\. Hypotheses were tested with ordinal logistic regression using a cumulative logit link, and open\-ended responses were analysed thematically\. Leadership capacity building \(β = 0\.453, p = \.043\) and capability reconfiguration initiatives \(β = 0\.426, p = \.040\) were positively associated with performance, whereas stakeholder engagement \(β = 0\.328, p = \.132\) and communication strategies \(β = −0\.046, p = \.818\) were not significant\. The four strategies had a significant joint association with performance \(β = 0\.960, p = \.009\)\. Digital transformation was positively associated with performance \(β = 0\.495, p = \.017\) and strengthened the joint association of the strategies with performance \(β = 1\.598, p = \.004\)\. The study recommends that NPO boards, managers and regulators strengthen leadership and capability reconfiguration, institutionalize stakeholder engagement and communication, and integrate digital transformation into coordinated change management programmes\. Keywords: Digital Transformation, ICT Adoption, Automation, Data\-Driven Practices, Change Management; Nonprofit Organizations, Strategic Adaptability, Organizational Resilience; Kenya
