# An Explainable Self‐Supervised Temporal Transformer for Participant‐Level Classification of Depressive Symptoms From Resting‐State EEG

**Autores:** Pushpamala Ramaiah; Ibtesam Nomani; Hayam Ibrahim Asfour; Mirza R\. Baig; Maksudjan Yuldashev; Ibrokhim Sapaev; Zein Bassam Bani Younes; Sumit Sharma; Mahesh Manchanda; Mohamad Ahmad Saleem Khasawneh; Majid S\. Jabir

**Data:** 2026-10-13 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

BACKGROUND: Resting\-state electroencephalography \(EEG\) has emerged as a promising non\-invasive modality for investigating depressive symptoms; however, conventional deep\-learning methods often require large labeled datasets and provide limited interpretability\. We developed an Explainable Self\-Supervised Temporal Transformer Network for participant\-level classification of BDI\-defined elevated depressive symptoms from resting\-state EEG\. METHODS: The framework employed masked temporal modeling for self\-supervised pretraining followed by supervised fine\-tuning\. Model performance was evaluated using repeated nested participant\-wise cross\-validation and compared with conventional machine\-learning and deep\-learning baselines\. Explainability was investigated through transformer attention maps and SHAP\-based channel attribution\. RESULTS: The proposed model achieved a receiver operating characteristic area under the curve \(ROC\-AUC\) of 0\.82 \(95% CI: 0\.73\-0\.90\), a precision\-recall AUC of 0\.69, balanced accuracy of 0\.75, sensitivity of 0\.72, and specificity of 0\.78\. The positive predictive value was 0\.57, indicating a substantial false\-positive burden in the evaluated cohort\. The proposed model showed the highest point\-estimate ROC\-AUC among the evaluated baselines, with a 0\.07 improvement over EEGNet; however, the overlapping uncertainty intervals and the small elevated\-symptom cohort warrant cautious interpretation of comparative superiority\. Performance decreased from an ROC\-AUC of 0\.82 in Run 1 to 0\.78 in Run 2 and to 0\.72\-0\.73 under cross\-condition transfer, supporting interpretation of these analyses as internal reproducibility and condition\-shift assessments rather than independent external validation\. Removing self\-supervised pretraining reduced the ROC\-AUC to 0\.74\. Explainability analyses identified distributed temporally relevant intervals and frontal\-midline to centro\-parietal channel contributions, with attribution interpreted as model\-relevance evidence rather than causal biomarker evidence\. CONCLUSION: Overall, the findings provide preliminary internal evidence that self\-supervised temporal representation learning can support participant\-level classification of BDI\-defined elevated depressive symptoms from resting\-state EEG under the evaluated experimental conditions\. The findings do not establish clinical diagnostic validity, screening readiness, or independent external generalizability\.
