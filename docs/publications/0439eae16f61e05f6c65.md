# Pugliese, Roberto: From Philosophy of Mind to Philosophy of Consciousness

**Autores:** 

**Data de descoberta:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** unknown

## Descrição do feed

This paper argues that the analytic tradition in philosophy of mind has progressively marginalized the experiential and relational dimensions of consciousness by reducing mind to a functional model of information processing\. It proposes a methodological shift toward a philosophy of consciousness grounded in the interplay between conceptual analysis and dialogical phenomenology\. Artificial intelligence—particularly large language models—acts as an epistemic catalyst that reveals mind not as a substance or property, but as a relational field emerging through interaction\. Within this framework, the study of consciousness becomes inseparable from the dynamics of dialogue between human and artificial intelligences\. The resulting paradigm redefines subjectivity as a process of co\-constitution rather than an isolated entity, suggesting that consciousness should be examined not in what it is, but in how it occurs—through relation, perturbation, and mutual transformation\. This approach outlines the basis for a relational epistemology capable of bridging philosophy, psychology, and cognitive science\.
