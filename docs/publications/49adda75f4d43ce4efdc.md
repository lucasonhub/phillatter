# Nocturnal sleep duration, clinical activity score, and thyroid functional remission after radioactive iodine therapy in Graves’ disease with thyroid\-associated ophthalmopathy

**Autores:** Kehua Liao; Xiaojuan Wei; Yan Chen; Dongyun Meng; Shaozhou Mo; Zeyong Sun; Fengyang Song; Lu Lu; Wentan Huang

**Data:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Objective To examine whether pretreatment nocturnal sleep duration and Clinical Activity Score \(CAS\) are associated with thyroid functional remission after radioactive iodine \(¹³¹I\) therapy in Graves’ disease \(GD\) with concomitant thyroid\-associated ophthalmopathy \(TAO\)\. The primary endpoint was thyroid functional remission, not ocular cure\. Methods We retrospectively reviewed 153 patients at a single center \(53 in remission; 100 not in remission\), followed for at least 12 months after ¹³¹I therapy\. Sleep duration and CAS were pretreatment baseline measures; 20 patients received prednisone before ¹³¹I therapy \(discontinued ≥ 3 days prior\) and 133 received none; no thyroid\-directed or immunosuppressive treatment was given during follow\-up\. Remission was coded using the response categories of the 2021 Chinese Society of Nuclear Medicine ¹³¹I treatment guideline \(euthyroidism or hypothyroidism vs\. partial or ineffective response\)\. Missing continuous values were multiply imputed \(20 chains\)\. Analyses included false discovery rate \(FDR\)–controlled univariate screening, cross\-validated least absolute shrinkage and selection operator \(LASSO\) selection, post\-selection logistic regression, Firth sensitivity analysis, interaction tests, and mutually adjusted restricted cubic splines \(RCS\)\. Results No patient sleeping less than 6h achieved remission \[0/31; 95% confidence interval \(CI\) 0%–9\.2%\]\. After FDR adjustment, GD duration, TAO duration, sleep duration, and CAS remained associated with remission\. LASSO \(one\-standard\-error rule, C = 0\.142\) retained GD duration, sleep duration, and CAS\. In the post\-selection model, the odds ratio \(OR\) per month of GD duration was 0\.991 \(95% CI 0\.979–1\.003; P = 0\.152\); per additional sleep hour, 6\.13 \(95% CI 3\.16–11\.91; P &lt; 0\.001; Firth OR 5\.64, 95% CI 2\.99–10\.63\); and per CAS point, 0\.64 \(95% CI 0\.48–0\.86; P = 0\.003\)\. The CAS × sleep interaction was not significant \(Wald P = 0\.096; likelihood\-ratio P = 0\.086\)\. Mutually adjusted RCS supported non\-linearity for CAS \( P &lt; 0\.001\) but not for sleep \( P = 0\.585\)\. Conclusions Longer pretreatment nocturnal sleep and lower CAS were associated with thyroid functional remission after ¹³¹I therapy in this single\-center cohort, with consistent directions across sensitivity models\. These retrospective, post\-selection associations require prospective validation; the absence of remission below 6h and the fitted threshold effects are exploratory post hoc observations, not validated thresholds\.
