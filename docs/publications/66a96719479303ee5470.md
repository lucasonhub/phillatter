# IoMT Adoption for Home\-Based COPD Care

**Autores:** Ethar Mansour Alsalbookh; Nouf Khalid Al\-Kahtani

**Data:** 2026-10-13 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Chronic obstructive pulmonary disease burdens Saudi health care, yet Internet of Medical Things \(IoMT\) use among health\-care professionals in home care is only 5\.5%\. This cross\-sectional study examined IoMT adoption intention for chronic obstructive pulmonary disease home care among 421 Saudi health\-care professionals using an integrated technology acceptance model and diffusion of innovations framework\. Behavioral intention was high \(median = 12/15\), revealing a substantial intention–adoption gap\. The model explained 86\.9% of variance \(R2 = 0\.869\)\. Perceived usefulness, ease of use, compatibility, relative advantage, and complexity were significant predictors; younger age and fewer years of experience were associated with higher intention\. Key enablers were usefulness, ease of use, compatibility, and relative advantage; complexity was the main barrier\. Vision 2030&\#x27;s digital health transformation provides a favorable policy context\. Strategies should prioritize intuitive design, workflow integration, and complexity reduction to translate intention into actual IoMT use\.
