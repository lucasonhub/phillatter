# ADABIYOT TA’LIMIDA BADIIY MATNNI KONTEKSTUAL YONDASHUV ASOSIDA TAHLIL QILISH VA O‘QUVCHILARNING TANQIDIY TAFAKKURINI RIVOJLANTIRISH METODIKASI

**Autores:** Azat Ataxanovich Yusupov; Jamila Muxtarbayevna Avezbayeva

**Data:** 2026-10-12 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

This article explores methodological approaches to developing students’ critical thinking through the contextual analysis of literary texts in literature education\. It substantiates the educational value of studying literary works in connection with the author’s life experiences, the historical and social conditions of the period in which a work was created, national and cultural values, and the text’s thematic and artistic features\. Particular attention is given to teaching strategies that encourage students to interpret textual meaning independently, identify the author’s perspective, evaluate characters’ actions using textual evidence, compare alternative interpretations, and formulate well\-reasoned conclusions\. The article also examines the pedagogical potential of integrating contextual analysis with inquiry\-based questions, problem\- solving activities, comparative tasks, classroom discussions, and evidence\-based argumentation\. The proposed approach aims to deepen students’ understanding of literary texts while strengthening independent thinking, logical reasoning, interpretive skills, and the ability to support personal viewpoints with relevant evidence\.
