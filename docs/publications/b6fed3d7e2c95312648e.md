# GaN Semiconductor Manufacturing Factory: AI\-Controlled Fully Autonomous Architecture

**Autores:** Nihat AYDIN

**Data:** 2026-10-09 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Abstract The increasing complexity of gallium nitride \(GaN\) semiconductor manufacturing has exposed the limitations of conventional factory automation, where isolated control systems, centralized decision\-making, and human\-dependent operations restrict production flexibility, scalability, and long\-term optimization\. This study proposes a next\-generation AI\-native architecture for a fully autonomous lights\-out GaN semiconductor manufacturing facility that minimizes routine human involvement while enabling continuous, intelligent, and self\-optimizing production\. In the proposed framework, human participation is intentionally limited to exceptional engineering interventions, major equipment upgrades, regulatory compliance, and strategic decision\-making, whereas all routine manufacturing activities are executed autonomously\. The proposed architecture integrates distributed Edge AI, autonomous robotic systems, a deterministic industrial communication infrastructure, a real\-time Digital Twin, and an AI Factory Operating System \(AI\-FOS\) into a unified operational ecosystem\. Unlike conventional smart factories that primarily automate predefined workflows, the proposed system enables perception, reasoning, adaptive decision\-making, and continuous process optimization throughout the entire semiconductor production lifecycle, including wafer logistics, epitaxial growth, lithography, etching, metrology, testing, packaging, predictive maintenance, and energy orchestration\. A key contribution of this work is the introduction of a distributed twelve\-layer cognitive memory architecture, which provides every autonomous robot and intelligent manufacturing node with a common hierarchical decision framework\. The architecture prioritizes life safety, environmental protection, anomaly recognition, experiential learning, quality optimization, equipment health monitoring, operational context awareness, asset management, maintenance planning, mission support, environmental signature analysis, and low\-priority contextual knowledge\. This shared cognitive framework enables consistent decision\-making across heterogeneous robotic platforms while supporting continuous learning and knowledge reuse throughout the factory\. The proposed architecture represents a shift from automation\-centric manufacturing toward cognitive semiconductor production, where the factory operates as an intelligent, self\-learning ecosystem rather than a collection of independently automated machines\. By combining distributed artificial intelligence, cognitive robotics, Digital Twin technology, and deterministic industrial networking within a unified operating architecture, this work establishes a conceptual foundation for future autonomous semiconductor fabrication facilities designed to achieve high production efficiency, enhanced safety, continuous adaptability, and scalable operation with minimal human intervention\. https://dynisos\.com/
