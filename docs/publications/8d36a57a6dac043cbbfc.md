# Naing, Htet Ko: Beyond Care: The Structure of Particular Personal Love

**Autores:** 

**Data de descoberta:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** unknown

## Descrição do feed

Deep care can be individualized and non\-instrumental without clearly being personal love, while obsession can make another person central to one’s life without caring for that person’s good\. I argue that these failures reveal two directions of mattering\. In paradigmatic particular personal love, B matters for B’s own sake \(FOR\-B\), and B as this particular person matters directly within A’s practical and evaluative life \(TO\-A\)\. The novel claim is not merely that love combines these directions, but that their unity partly consists in a same\-person cross\-directional dependence: across some relevant changes, altered content in one orientation is disposed to make a difference to the other because the person at issue is B\. Role\-Swap distinguishes person\-significance from replaceable role\-significance; Parallel Tracks isolates the dependence from the bare conjunction; and Deep Mentor together with Benevolent Rival tests whether the resulting architecture overgenerates\. Affection, loyalty, identification, attention, appreciation, attachment, and vulnerability remain important possible realizations of love rather than additional universal conditions unless their necessity is independently established\.
