# Exploratory predictive value of novel inflammatory biomarkers for microvascular obstruction after emergency revascularization in STEMI patients

**Autores:** Yang Dai; Longjin Chen; Zhe Zhang; Kaleem Ullah Shahzad; Qing Dai; Ying Tan; Xue Bao; Li&\#x27;na Kang; Biao Xu

**Data:** 2026-10-13 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Purpose To assess the associations of blood cell\-derived inflammatory markers with the formation of microvascular obstruction \(MVO\) and their exploratory predictive performance in STEMI patients after PCI\. Patients and methods A retrospective analysis was conducted based on a prospective cohort, which included the patients with ST\-segment elevation myocardial infarction \(STEMI\) who underwent emergency percutaneous coronary intervention \(PCI\) at Nanjing Drum Tower Hospital\. All patients underwent cardiac magnetic resonance imaging within 7 days post\-surgery, and were classified into MVO and non\-MVO groups based on the examination results\. Emergency and first post\-PCI routine blood test and other clinical data were collected from patients, and various cell ratios were calculated, including the monocyte\-to\-lymphocyte ratio \(MLR\), neutrophil\-to\-lymphocyte ratio \(NLR\), platelet\-to\-lymphocyte ratio \(PLR\), systemic immune\-inflammation index \(SII\), systemic inflammatory response index \(SIRI\), and aggregate index of systemic inflammation \(AISI\)\. The risk factors for MVO were evaluated by univariate and multivariate logistic regression analysis\. Results A total of 311 patients with STEMI were included\. Among them, 198 developed MVO and the other 113 were MVO free\. After adjustment for clinical covariates, higher post\-PCI WBC, NEU, SIRI and NLR and greater peri\-PCI eosinophil decline were associated with MVO\. In the correlation\-reduced joint analysis, post\-PCI SIRI retained the most robust association\. Conclusion Postoperative WBC, NEU, SIRI, NLR, Δ EOS, Δ EOS% showed exploratory predictive performance for the formation of MVO after PCI in STEMI patients, and post\-PCI SIRI showed the most robust association after accounting for biomarker intercorrelation\.
