# Sodium oxybate with reduced\-dose propofol for anesthesia induction in elective spine surgery: a randomized trial

**Autores:** Jianfeng Yu; Cuiping Yu; Nanling Wu; Kaixuan Zhao; Yimeng Xia; Ting Fan

**Data:** 2026-10-13 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Background Sodium oxybate has an established sedative action, but its contribution to contemporary propofol\-based anesthesia remains incompletely characterized\. We evaluated induction hemodynamics, propofol use, bispectral index \(BIS\), and early recovery in elective spine surgery\. Methods In this single\-center randomized trial, 127 patients received propofol alone \(P; n = 62\) or propofol plus sodium oxybate \(PS; n = 65\)\. Planned induction doses were propofol 2\.2 versus 1\.2 mg/kg, with oxybate 30 mg/kg in PS; recorded propofol doses were lower in both groups\. The primary outcome was the mean arterial pressure \(MAP\) trajectory across 13 induction landmarks\. Maximum\-likelihood mixed models were supplemented by unstructured\-covariance and baseline\-adjusted analyses\. Results The primary group\-by\-time interaction was not significant \( P = 0\.607; unstructured covariance, P = 0\.719\)\. The baseline\-adjusted average post\-baseline MAP difference was 1\.7 mmHg \(PS minus P; 95% confidence interval \[CI\], −2\.0–5\.5\)\. Systolic\-pressure\-defined hypotension occurred in 37/62 versus 32/65 patients \(risk ratio, 0\.82; 95% CI, 0\.60–1\.14; risk difference, −10\.4 percentage points; 95% CI, −26\.8–6\.7\)\. Recorded total propofol use was 31\.4% lower \(751\.3 versus 515\.4 mg; difference, −235\.9 mg; 95% CI, −366\.0 to −105\.9\)\. Maintenance use was also lower under unblinded BIS\-guided titration\. Post\-intubation BIS &lt;40 occurred in 2/62 versus 22/65 patients\. The mean surgery\-end\-to\-extubation interval was 10\.5 versus 13\.6 min \(difference, 3\.1 min; 95% CI, 0\.9–5\.2\), although both medians were 10 min\. Vasoactive\-treatment documentation was incomplete\. Conclusions The oxybate\-containing strategy was associated with substantially lower propofol use, lower BIS values, and a longer mean extubation interval; hemodynamic superiority was not demonstrated\. These findings define the observed effects and trade\-offs of the combined regimen under BIS\-guided care and inform further evaluation of dose selection, hypnosis monitoring, and recovery\. Clinical Trial Registration https://www\.chictr\.org\.cn Chinese Clinical Trial Registry, ChiCTR2400080686\.
