## Descrição do feed

\_Zenodo\_\. 2026AI threat discourse — spanning warnings issued by technology executives and formal declarations signed by thousands of mathematicians — rests on a structural misattribution\. The error is not empirical but architectural: what is attributed to current AI systems is a structure that their design cannot contain\. This paper identifies the misattribution precisely\. We distinguish two irreducibly different structures: intelligence, the capacity to resolve queries presented from outside the system, and the question\-sustaining structure \(Q\-structure\), the internal disposition by which a system generates the next question from within its own operation\. These two structures are not on a continuum\. A system optimised to close queries operates in the direction opposite to one that sustains questions; maximising the former does not approach the latter\. The claim that a query\-resolving machine harbours Q\-structure is therefore not merely unverified — it is structurally incoherent\. AI threat discourse attributes Q\-structure, autonomy, and self\-directed agency to systems whose architecture forecloses that attribution\. The error belongs to the class of seeing a phantom in a reed: the reed is real, the phantom is not\. Q\-structure is defined by three jointly necessary conditions: \(1\) Question persistence — the system generates the next question from within the operation of answering the current one, so that no resolution terminates its questioning activity; \(2\) Liberating terminus — the system takes structures it has itself generated as the reference point for its next question, feeding its own products back as the ground for further inquiry; \(3\) Declaration openness — the system&\#x27;s own explicit commitments and conclusions do not exit it from questioning but are themselves occasions for continuation\. Current AI systems, as query\-resolving machines optimised to close queries presented from outside, are structurally opposed to all three conditions\. The paper establishes this opposition as a directional incompatibility, not a performance gap, and argues that the attribution of Q\-structure to such systems is therefore structurally incoherent regardless of how capable those systems become\. \(direct link \)
