# Selection of Electric Vehicles within the Framework of Consumer Preferences: A SWARA\-CODAS Application on the Turkish Market

**Autores:** Murat Kurnuç

**Data:** 2026-10-15 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

The rapid growth of the electric vehicle market in recent years has made it essential, from a marketing perspective, to understand which criteria are decisive in evaluating these vehicles and how the available options are positioned with respect to these criteria\. Accordingly, this study aims to reveal the relative weights of the prominent criteria in the electric vehicle evaluation process and to comparatively rank the competing models in the Turkish market within the framework of these criteria\. In determining the criteria, economic, technical, and usage\-based dimensions were taken into consideration\. A two\-stage approach was adopted in the analysis process; the criteria weights were calculated using the SWARA technique, while the performance ranking of the alternative models was conducted using the CODAS technique\. The findings indicate that the most decisive factor in the evaluation process is driving range, followed by purchase cost, power consumption, and battery warranty period\. In the ranking of alternatives, it was observed that brands offering technical superiority and comprehensive warranty advantages came to the forefront rather than the models with the highest market share\. In addition to contributing to the marketing literature, the study offers various insights and recommendations for manufacturers and distributors\.
