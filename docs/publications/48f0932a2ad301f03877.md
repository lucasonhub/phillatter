# A Quantitative Study on the Role of Emotional Regulation and Coping Strategies in Predicting Smartphone Overuse among Malaysian Young Adults in Klang Valley

**Autores:** Misa Kikuchi; Shalini A/P Munusamy

**Data:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

This paper investigates the role of emotional regulation and coping strategies in predicting smartphone overuse among Malaysian young adults in Klang Valley\. A quantitative study was done among 191 Malaysian young adults between the age 18 to 25\. Multiple linear regression analyses were conducted to explore the relationship and predictive effects of emotion\-oriented coping, task\-oriented coping, avoidance\-oriented coping, cognitive reappraisal and expressive suppression on smartphone overuse\. Smartphone overuse was found to be significantly positively correlated with emotion\-oriented and avoidance\-oriented coping\. However, only emotion\-oriented coping was found to significantly predict smartphone overuse \(beta = \.40, p &lt; \.001\), while task\-oriented coping, avoidance\-oriented coping, cognitive reappraisal and expressive suppression were not significant predictors\. These findings imply that emotion\-oriented coping may be especially applicable to excessive smartphone usage among Malaysian young adults and it underlines the need of encouraging adaptive coping strategies and healthier approaches managing emotional discomfort\.
