# Bulgaria, Jamel &amp; Xǔ, Chénglán: Comparative Analysis of Computational Affective Dynamics: The Core Emotion Framework, the Rubik&\#x27;s Cube of Entrepreneurial Vision, and Clinical Detangling Protocols

**Autores:** 

**Data de descoberta:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** unknown

## Descrição do feed

This paper presents a unified computational model of human affect by synthesizing three domains: the Core Emotion Framework \(CEF\), the entrepreneurial progression of systemic action, and clinical detangling protocols\. Drawing on the CEF’s tri\-centric architecture of Head, Heart, and Gut centers—each containing fixed emotional operators that “rotate, combine, and under healthy conditions, transition dynamically”—the work formalizes emotional processing as a mechanical, rule\-governed system\. The entrepreneurial arc is shown to be a phenomenological instantiation of the ten CEF operators, mapping business decision\-making onto structural affective mechanics\. Pathological emotional fusions, including the Overthinking Loop and the GoodPerson Anxiety Pattern, are analyzed through quantum probability theory, where entangled operator states exhibit non\-separability and require somatic decoherence interventions\. Clinical protocols such as the 7\-Step Detangling Method operationalize these principles through targeted somatic choreography, grounding, and operator isolation\. The paper further extends CEF into synthetic affect, evaluating conversational AI systems through the INTIMA benchmark and modeling agent affective output as a weighted operator vector\. Finally, the psychometric validation program is outlined, highlighting the structural equation modeling paradox that emerges from operator interdependence\. Together, these components demonstrate that emotional, entrepreneurial, clinical, and synthetic systems obey the same computational laws, enabling a unified architecture for affective modeling and intervention\. \(direct link \)
