# Revolutionizing HR Analytics Through Quantum AI and Explainable AI in Modern Organizations

**Autores:** P\. Naresh Kumar; Swetha Pasumala

**Data:** 2026-10-15 · **Tipo:** book

## Resumo

Revolutionizing HR Analytics Through Quantum AI and Explainable AI in Modern Organizations explores the emerging role of advanced Artificial Intelligence techniques in transforming human resource management and organizational decision\-making\. The book examines how Quantum AI and Explainable AI can support workforce analytics, talent acquisition, employee performance evaluation, retention strategies, workforce planning, and predictive HR modeling\. It highlights the potential of intelligent analytical systems to identify workforce patterns, improve decision\-making, and generate transparent, data\-driven insights\. The book also addresses challenges related to data privacy, algorithmic bias, ethical AI, interpretability, and responsible technology adoption\. Combining emerging concepts with organizational applications, it serves as a valuable resource for students, researchers, HR professionals, organizational leaders, and technology practitioners interested in the future of intelligent and explainable human resource analytics\.
