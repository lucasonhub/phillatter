# Pedagogical conditions for developing students&\#x27; academic writing competence through artificial intelligence\.

**Autores:** Mokhlaroyim Khamdamova Oybek qizi

**Data:** 2026-10-14 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

This article examines the pedagogical conditions necessary for effectively developing students&\#x27; academic writing competence by integrating artificial intelligence \(AI\) technologies into higher education\. As global academic standards increasingly demand high\-level research writing, clarity, and adherence to international publication ethics, educators face the challenge of equipping students with advanced text\-production skills\. This study outlines a comprehensive framework of pedagogical, methodological, and technological conditions—such as prompt engineering literacy, critical feedback literacy, scaffolded AI\-assisted drafting, and ethical integrity safeguards\. By establishing these conditions, institutions can transform AI from a mere shortcut into a cognitive partner that fosters critical thinking, structural coherence, and linguistic precision in academic discourse\.
