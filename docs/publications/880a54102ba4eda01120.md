# Teachers’ Understanding and Preparedness towards STEAM Education

**Autores:** Garima Singh; Dinesh Kumar

**Data:** 2026-10-10 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Abstract Education is continuously evolving in response to technological advancements and the changing needs of society\. The emergence of Artificial Intelligence, robotics, big data, and digital technologies has created a need for innovative and interdisciplinary approaches to teaching and learning\. In this context, STEAM \(Science, Technology, Engineering, Arts, and Mathematics\) education provides an integrated framework that promotes creativity, critical thinking, problem\-solving, collaboration, and the application of knowledge to real\-life situations\. The National Education Policy \(NEP\) 2020 also emphasizes holistic, experiential, multidisciplinary, and skill\-oriented education\. The present study examines teachers’ perspectives, understanding, attitudes, and preparedness towards implementing STEAM education in secondary schools\. The study adopted a quantitative research approach using a descriptive survey method\. The sample consisted of 58 teachers from four CBSE schools in Lucknow, selected through a random sampling method\. Data were collected using a self\-constructed structured questionnaire based on a Likert scale\. The questionnaire examined four major domains: teachers’ understanding of STEAM education, their attitudes towards STEAM, preparedness to implement STEAM\-oriented classrooms and laboratories, and professional development requirements\. It also explored teachers’ views regarding interdisciplinary collaboration, digital tools, infrastructure, and innovative pedagogical practices\. The study highlights the potential of STEAM education to enhance teachers’ professional and pedagogical competencies\. STEAM\-oriented approaches can encourage teachers to adopt inquiry\-based, experiential, project\-based, and problem\-solving pedagogies\. Thus, integrating STEAM education in alignment with NEP 2020 can contribute to more innovative, inclusive, engaging, and future\-oriented learning environments in secondary schools\.
