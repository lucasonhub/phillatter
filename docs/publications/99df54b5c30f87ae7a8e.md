# Brusseau, James: The Dignity Matrix of Mimetic AI Digital Twins

**Autores:** 

**Data de descoberta:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** unknown

## Descrição do feed

Mimetic AI digital twins reproduce recognizable features of a particular person\. Their ethical evaluation therefore involves two human parties: the person represented and the person who encounters it\. This paper proposes a two person dignity matrix evaluating each party independently\. Drawing on Kant, dignity has a protective dimension, which prohibits reducing a person to a tool or commodity, and an enabling dimension, which recognizes a person as capable of choice and accountability\. Crossing the dignity of the represented subject with the dignity of the user produces four categories: mutual respect, unauthorized continuation, authorized deception, and exploitative impersonation\. The matrix offers a compact way to operationalize the ethics of dignity by identifying who controls the representation, who understands the encounter, and who is being used for another&\#x27;s purposes\.
