# Функциональная совместимость информационных систем управления и мониторинга электротехнических комплексов интеллектуальных электрических сетей

**Autores:** Антон Валерьевич Иванов; Елена Николаевна Соснина

**Data:** 2026-10-12 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Wide introduction of information and communication technologies into industrial enterprise power supply systems gave rise to the development of cyber\-physical systems\. Specialists who design these systems faced the problem of ensuring functional compatibility of electrical engineering complexes and information systems, which is solved by applying the relevant international standards\. For information systems used in the control and monitoring of electrical engineering complexes, such standards include the IEC 61970, 61968, and 62365 series of the International Electrotechnical Commission \(IEC\), which standardize the Common Information Model \(CIM\)\. In Russia, the IEC CIM standard is supplemented with the GOST R 58651 series\. However, when an updated release of the IEC CIM is issued, modifications to the GOST CIM \(e\.g\., the addition of newly introduced classes etc\.\) are performed manually\. This comes in contradiction with the fundamental goal of digitalization \(which implies the development of artificial intelligence\) and is a source of numerous errors stemming from involvement of the human factor\. The article proposes an approach to extending the IEC CIM that enables updating to be performed in an automated manner\. Examples of adding new classes, attributes, and associations are given\. An example of carrying out automated updating, specifically, the transfer of extensions to a newer IEC CIM version, is presented
