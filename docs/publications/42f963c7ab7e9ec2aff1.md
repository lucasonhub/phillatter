# China, Tourism and Technology

**Autores:** Gary Bowerman

**Data:** 2026-10-13 · **Tipo:** book

## Resumo

China, Tourism and Technology charts China’s rise to becoming the world’s largest travel economy and examines how home\-grown mobile travel ecosystems, AI models, Super Apps, mega\-brands and government\-backed tech strategies continue to shape tourism worldwide\. Readers will discover how China’s closed\-loop travel\-tech networks built on powerful app platforms for booking, payments, social commerce and livestreaming create unique consumer privileges and formidable entry barriers for global competitors\. Through insider interviews, research and case studies, the book explains how these innovations drive Chinese travel behaviours at home and abroad, and how international businesses seeking growth should respond\. This book equips travel industry professionals, policymakers, academics and international brands to anticipate trends and seize opportunities in a rapidly evolving market, as well as offering tourism students practical insights into Chinese digital ecosystems and consumer trends and global market dynamics\.
