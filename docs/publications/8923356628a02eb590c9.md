# Balancing emission reduction and regulatory burden under inspection limits for diesel fleets

**Autores:** Junchao Zhao; Jiaxin Lu; Tingting Zhang; Yunjing Wang; Yunjing Wang; Zhenyu Luo; Xingrui Cai; Xinping Yang; Leqi Zhang; Hongli Wang; Yanjun Wang; Hongli Wang; Weicheng Chen; Hang Yin; Huan Liu; Yan Ding

**Data:** 2026-10-10 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Effectively regulating in\-use vehicle emissions requires methods that can identify high\-impact emitters within heterogeneous vehicle fleets\. This study develops a vehicle\-level NOx accounting method based on inspection data, converting measured NOx concentrations into estimated emissions and aggregating these estimates to the fleet level\. This approach enables fleet\-scale emission accounting that explicitly reflects heterogeneity in both vehicle emission intensity and activity levels\. In the study region, using a full\-sample 2024 dataset, we assess fleet\-level emission heterogeneity and reduction potential under alternative Inspection and Maintenance limits\. We find that China V diesel trucks dominate fleet emissions due to their large population size and substantial vehicle activity\. Furthermore, tightening the inspection limit reveals a trade\-off between emission reduction benefits and compliance costs\. The proposed NOx Reduction Effectiveness Index \(NREI\) identifies an optimal threshold range where emission reduction efficiency peaks, providing a scientific basis for balancing environmental benefits with maintenance burdens in policy optimization\.
