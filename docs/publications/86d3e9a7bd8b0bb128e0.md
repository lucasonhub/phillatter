# The Relationship Between Intellectual Ability And Cognitive Load: Lesson Scenarios Based On The Principles Of Optimal Load

**Autores:** Alisher Ernazarov

**Data:** 2026-10-15 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

This article provides a scientific analysis of the relationship between intellectual ability and cognitive load\. In modern education, the principles of optimal load play an important role in ensuring the effective use of students’ cognitive resources and the full realization of their intellectual potential\. Based on cognitive load theory, the article presents approaches to designing lesson scenarios, distributing instructional materials according to students’ levels of intellectual ability, and implementing interactive methods\. Furthermore, examples are provided to illustrate how excessive or insufficient cognitive load affects educational effectiveness\. Lesson scenarios developed based on the principles of optimal load serve to facilitate students’ learning processes, encourage independent thinking, and develop their professional competencies\.
