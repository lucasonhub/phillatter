# Analysis of Landslide Susceptibility Toward the Presence of Accommodation Areas \(Glamping and Villas\) in Kejajar District, Wonosobo Regency, 2025

**Autores:** Zidan Kurniawan; ‪Kuswaji Dwi Priyono

**Data:** 2026-10-10 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

This study aims to analyze and map the level of landslide susceptibility in accommodation areas in Kejajar District, Wonosobo Regency, using a Geographic Information System \(GIS\) approach\. Scoring, weighting, and overlay methods were applied to integrate rainfall, slope gradient, soil type, geology, and land\-use parameters\. The results indicate that Kejajar District is divided into three landslide susceptibility classes: high susceptibility covering 4,936 ha \(76%\), moderate susceptibility covering 1,515 ha \(23%\), and low susceptibility covering 37 ha \(&lt;1%\)\. Spatial analysis of accommodation areas indicates that most villas and glamping facilities are located in moderate\-to\-high susceptibility zones, particularly in areas with steep slopes, high rainfall, and easily weathered soil materials\. These findings emphasize the importance of using landslide susceptibility maps as a basis for spatial planning and disaster mitigation guidance in the development of highland tourism areas\.
