# TransVJ2\-MCA: power line defect recognition integrating video world model priors and cross\-attention

**Autores:** Li Xu; Xiaoping Wu; Yu Liu; Yasi Peng

**Data:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

To address the dual challenges of scarce defect samples in power line component images and fine\-grained defects being easily overwhelmed in complex backgrounds, this paper proposes a video world model prior\-driven component defect recognition framework, TransVJ2\-MCA\. First, a cross\-modal temporal reconstruction \(CMTR\) mechanism is designed to adapt to the input constraints of the video self\-supervised pre\-trained backbone network \(V\-JEPA v2\)\. By replicating single\-frame power line component images along the temporal dimension and converting them into stationary pseudo\-video sequences, static images are aligned with the V\-JEPA v2 encoder in both input form and feature distribution\. A parameter\-freezing strategy is then adopted to reuse the large\-scale spatio\-temporal structural priors embedded in the model, mitigating the limitation imposed by scarce defect samples on model training\. Finally, a multi\-scale cross\-attention \(MCA\) module is constructed, which uses deep semantic features as guidance to selectively aggregate key discriminative information from shallow features, enabling effective selection and adaptive fusion of cross\-level features, suppressing complex background interference, and enhancing fine\-grained defect recognition capability\. Experimental validation is conducted on five independent power line component classification tasks of the InsPLAD\-Fault dataset\. The results show that TransVJ2\-MCA achieves an average balanced accuracy of 98\.2% and a macro\-average F1 score of 97\.1%, demonstrating the effectiveness of the proposed method under complex backgrounds and scarce defect samples, as well as its cross\-modal transfer capability\. This study provides a referential adaptation paradigm for transferring video pre\-trained large models to static industrial visual inspection\.
