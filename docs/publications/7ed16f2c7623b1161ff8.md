# Preston\-Roedder, Ryan: Love in Personal and Political Life

**Autores:** 

**Data de descoberta:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** unknown

## Descrição do feed

\_Philosophers&\#x27; Imprint\_\. forthcomingThere is a highly influential and widely admired ideal within Black American moral and political philosophy that directs us to respond to America&\#x27;s racial injustice – and to certain other forms of injustice and wrongdoing – with love\. But, despite its considerable influence, this ideal is puzzling on its face, and reflection on some prominent formulations of the ideal raises pressing questions: What kind of love does this ideal refer to? What are the grounds for the view that such love is a reasonable response to relevant forms of injustice and wrongdoing? How can we respond to injustice and wrongdoing in this way without facilitating further, or even more severe, mistreatment? I characterize and defend a version of this ideal that James Baldwin defends in his early fiction and early essays, and I address these questions as they apply to Baldwin&\#x27;s view\.
