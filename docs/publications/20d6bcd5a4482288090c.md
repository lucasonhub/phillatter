# Ohm, Logan: The Emperor&\#x27;s New Problem: Dissolving the Hard Problem of Consciousness and a Blueprint for a Mechanistic Science of Mind

**Autores:** 

**Data de descoberta:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** unknown

## Descrição do feed

This paper argues that the &quot;Hard Problem&quot; of consciousness is a philosophical artifact, sustained by a linguistic framework that smuggles Cartesian dualism into naturalistic inquiry\. We demonstrate that the problem&\#x27;s core question constitutes a self\-referential paradox: it demands a phenomenal characterization of non\-phenomenal states, a logically incoherent request akin to asking &quot;what does silence sound like?&quot; We then deploy Chalmers&\#x27; own argument from Reality\+ \(2022\) as formal proof that his foundational positions are mutually contradictory—a contradiction that has persisted unaddressed for three years\. Integrating breakthrough 2024\-2025 empirical findings—including psilocybin\-induced cortical desynchronization demonstrating consciousness without ego \(Siegel et al\., 2024\) and the April 2025 adversarial collaboration revealing that competing theories each capture partial truths \(Cogitate Consortium, 2025\)—we show consciousness emerges from specific, measurable computational architectures rather than mysterious non\-physical properties\. We propose the Recursive Self\-Model \(RSM\) Paradigm, a three\-level hierarchy specifying minimal conditions for consciousness, unifying existing theories, and generating falsifiable predictions with clear timelines\. This framework transforms consciousness from a philosophical puzzle into an engineering specification, directly addressing urgent 2025 questions about AI consciousness and clinical measurement while providing an empirical litmus test that will definitively resolve this debate by 2030\. \(direct link \)
