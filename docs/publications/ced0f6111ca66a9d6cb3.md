# THE IMPACT OF ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE ASSISTANCE ON THE DIAGNOSTIC DECISIONS OF DENTAL STUDENTS IN CARIES DETECTION

**Autores:** Razhabzoda Parvina Hakimovna; Fayzullayeva Shaxrizoda Erkin qizi

**Data:** 2026-10-17 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

The integration of Artificial Intelligence \(AI\) into dental diagnostics has emerged as a transformative development in dental education\. This study investigates the impact of AI assistance on the clinical and radiographic diagnostic decisions of dental students during dental caries detection\. While traditional training relies heavily on visual\-tactile metrics and conventional radiographic interpretation, AI systems utilizing Convolutional Neural Networks \(CNNs\) offer a standardized, data\-driven second opinion\. Current literature suggests that AI assistance substantially improves students&\#x27; diagnostic accuracy and sensitivity, particularly in identifying early enamel and proximal carious lesions\. Furthermore, AI tools reduce diagnostic time and enhance student confidence\. However, concerns regarding automation bias, potential false positives, and a deficiency in structured curricula persist\. This paper outlines the mechanisms, educational benefits, limitations, and future outlook of implementing AI in dental diagnostics training\.
