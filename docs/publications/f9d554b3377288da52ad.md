# TEACHING GRAPHIC SUBJECTS IN THE CONTEXT OF ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE

**Autores:** T\.V\. Nesterova

**Data:** 2026-10-10 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

The article examines the problem of teaching students using artificial intelligence \(AI\)\. Students find ready\-made papers on subjects and submit them instead of their own\. This process is facilitated by the introduction of graphics programs, which makes it possible to purchase finished work inexpensively\. The experience of conducting classes in mechanical engineering drawing suggests ways to solve this problem\. The article provides recommendations on the use of tests for the operational verification of knowledge and the degree of independence of performing household routine calculation, graphic and term papers\.
