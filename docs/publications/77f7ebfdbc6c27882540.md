# Enhanced precipitable water vapor inversion and rainstorm monitoring based on GNSS PPP regional tropospheric modeling

**Autores:** Ying Liu; Yuze Yang; Kai Wang; Weili Zhou

**Data:** 2026-10-13 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Precipitable water vapor \(PWV\) is critical for severe weather forecasting, yet existing GNSS Precise Point Positioning \(PPP\) retrieval methods are limited by neglecting tropospheric horizontal gradients and lacking regional coverage\. Moreover, PWV alone cannot reliably determine precipitation onset\. To address these issues, we propose an integrated framework combining enhanced multi\-GNSS PPP with tropospheric gradient estimation, a Modified Optimal Fitting Coefficients \(MOFC\) regional model, and a multi\-parameter precipitation judgment method integrating PWV, air temperature, and dew point temperature\. Validated with ∼120 IGS stations and the Zhengzhou 7·20 rainstorm, the gradient\-enhanced PPP improves zenith wet delay accuracy \(bias reduced by 0\.8–1\.1 mm, i\.e\., ∼40%, and RMS by 0\.4–0\.8 mm, i\.e\., up to 8\.8%\), the MOFC model achieves ∼1\.83 cm ZWD and 2\.97 mm PWV regional accuracy while accelerating PPP convergence, and the multi\-parameter method effectively captures precipitation onset when PWV exceeds 60 mm under saturation\. This framework advances GNSS water vapor monitoring by improving accuracy, enabling regional coverage, and providing a reliable precipitation timing criterion\.
