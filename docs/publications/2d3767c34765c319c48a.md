# An Exploration of Approaches to Contextual Teaching in Elementary School Music Education from the Perspective of Authentic Learning

**Autores:** Taotao Jin

**Data:** 2026-10-13 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

To implement the requirement in the Compulsory Education Arts Curriculum Standards \(2022 Edition\) to link contextual teaching with life experiences, integrating the concept of authentic learning into elementary school music classrooms holds significant practical value\. In response to current issues in music classroom situational teaching\-such as fragmented tasks, overly formalized scenarios, and superficial learning outcomes\-this study clarifies the theoretical similarities and differences between authenticity\-based learning and situational teaching\. By addressing practical needs such as classroom scenario creation, task design, and learning experiences, it constructs a teaching pathway driven by real\-world projects, featuring embodied participation, and incorporating authenticity\-based assessment throughout\. The study also proposes optimization strategies in three areas: identifying authentic problems, leveraging digital technology, and expanding resources and learning environments\. This exploration offers insights for conducting more vivid and in\-depth situational teaching in elementary school music classrooms, helping students develop musical literacy through authentic experiences\.
