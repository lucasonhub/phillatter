# Game\-Based Learning Experience and Occupational Health and Safety Learning Achievement: The Mediating Role of Learning Engagement in Vocational Training

**Autores:** Renovan Solala Johannes Nache; Rijanto Purbojo

**Data:** 2026-10-15 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Occupational health and safety is a mandatory component of public vocational training; however, lecture\-based instruction may not sufficiently engage adult learners\. This study examined the effect of the game\-based learning experience on learning achievement, with learning engagement as a mediating variable, among occupational health and safety trainees at the South Jakarta Regional Job Training Center, Indonesia\. A quantitative explanatory survey was conducted with all 174 participants enrolled in the 2026 Batch 1 regular and Mobile Training Unit classes using saturated sampling\. All participants completed a game\-based learning session on the Wayground platform, formerly known as Quizizz\. Data were collected using a five\-point Likert\-scale questionnaire adapted from established instruments and analyzed using partial least squares structural equation modeling \(PLS\-SEM\) with SmartPLS 4\. The game\-based learning experience had a positive and significant effect on learning engagement \(β = 0\.854, p &lt; 0\.001\) and learning achievement \(β = 0\.592, p &lt; 0\.001\), while learning engagement had a positive and significant effect on learning achievement \(β = 0\.343, p = 0\.016\)\. Learning engagement partially mediated the effect of the game\-based learning experience on learning achievement \(indirect effect = 0\.293, p = 0\.017\)\. The model explained 72\.9% of the variance in learning engagement and 81\.6% of the variance in learning achievement\. Approximately one\-fifth of participants reported technical barriers, primarily unstable internet connections, system lag, device limitations, and game time limits perceived as too short\. The findings suggest that game\-based learning activities can enhance learner engagement and perceived learning achievement in occupational health and safety training for adult vocational learners, provided that adequate technical readiness is ensured\.
