# INTERNALISASI MODERASI BERAGAMA DALAM PENGUATAN SIKAP TOLERANSI PESERTA DIDIK DI ERA KECERDASAN BUATAN

**Autores:** Malika Amilia Rahman; Muhammad Rapono; Ranum Aisyah; Nurul Fadilah; Tia Nazriah Harahap; Putri Andini; S\. Maulidatul Husna; Alfikri; Andhika Perangin Angin; Nur Aini Lubis

**Data:** 2026-10-10 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

The development of Artificial Intelligence \(AI\) in education presents opportunities and challenges in fostering tolerance among students in multicultural societies\. Although AI provides access to diverse perspectives, its use may also spread misinformation, stereotypes, and intolerance without proper guidance\. This study aims to analyze the integration of religious moderation and multicultural education into the religious education curriculum to strengthen students’ tolerance in the AI era\. This research employs a qualitative approach using a literature review method\. Data were collected from relevant books, scholarly journal articles, and educational policy documents, then analyzed through content analysis to identify key concepts, educational strategies, and their implications for promoting tolerance\. The findings indicate that integrating religious moderation and multicultural education into the religious education curriculum can foster mutual respect, acceptance of diversity, empathy, religious inclusivity, and peaceful coexistence\. Religious moderation encourages balanced religious attitudes, while multicultural education promotes appreciation of cultural and religious differences\. Their integration can be implemented through contextual learning, interfaith dialogue, collaborative activities, and the responsible use of AI\-based learning media\. Teachers also play an essential role in guiding students to evaluate digital information critically, recognize potential biases, and use AI ethically\. Therefore, integrating religious moderation, multicultural education, and responsible AI use is essential for developing tolerant, critical, and responsible learners who can navigate religious and cultural diversity in the digital era\.
