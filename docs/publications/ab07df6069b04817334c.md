# Procalcitonin\-guided antibiotic discontinuation after ADAPT\-Sepsis: a national survey of current practice and readiness for implementation

**Autores:** Richard O&\#x27;sullivan; Thomas Knight; Daniel Edward Horner; Paul M Wilson; Jo C Dumville; Tim Felton; Paul M Dark

**Data:** 2026-10-13 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Procalcitonin \(PCT\)\-guided antibiotic discontinuation is increasingly supported by evidence, but current UK practice remains uncertain\. We conducted a national survey of acute healthcare staff to describe current PCT use and organisational readiness for implementation of PCT\-guided antibiotic discontinuation\. Eighty\-six responses were analysed\. PCT was mostly measured occasionally in selected patients, while 28% of respondents reported rarely or never measuring PCT\. Nearly half wished to increase their use of PCT\-guided antibiotic discontinuation, while almost a third wanted further information before making practice changes\. Organisational readiness scores were generally favourable\. Ongoing implementation may be supported by national guidance, local protocols and education\.
