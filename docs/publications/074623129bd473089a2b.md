# How emotional financial narratives amplify herd behaviour and create asset bubbles? \(The Blockbuster Economics\)

**Autores:** Alexandra Kim

**Data:** 2026-10-17 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

This article looks at the parallelism of financial market narratives with classical blockbuster emotional story arcs, which is based on a “winner takes it all” perspective, and how the structural similarity of these narratives increases herd behaviour and the risk of forming asset bubbles in global markets\. This article, built on the interdisciplinary concepts of behavioural finance, economic sociology and narrative theory, posits that, in this context \(when financial information is delivered through the vehicle of emotionally powerful storytelling forms\) the rational, analytical process designed for information is inhibited and the affective process designed for feeling is activated, thereby eliciting responses that are not necessarily in line with the fundamental valuation metric\. This study highlights the importance of understanding the euphoria phase of asset bubbles as a narrative manipulation, based on analysis of recent cases such as the Labubu phenomenon, the AI market, and market speculation, during which investors are led towards the &quot;triumphant ending&quot; that is rarely achieved\. Empirical research on informational segregation, homophily of individuals within social networks, and drama marketing methods is used to build a theory to explain why boom\-bust cycles occur in speculative markets despite the fact that they are well\-documented to be harmful\.
