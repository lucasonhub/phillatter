# PENTINGNYA REVISI KURIKULUM UNTUK MENGHADAPI ERA DIGITAL

**Autores:** Sintia Bella

**Data:** 2026-10-09 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Curriculum revision is a crucial step in preparing the younger generation to face the challenges and advancements of technology in the digital era\. With the rapid changes in the world influenced by technological progress, the existing curriculum needs to be updated to meet the demands of the 21st century\. This article discusses the importance of curriculum revision in the context of education, where the integration of digital technology, 21st\-century skills, and character development are key focuses\. Through curriculum revision, it is expected that the learning process will become more relevant to the needs of modern society, producing students who are ready to face the increasingly dynamic and technology\-driven job market\.
