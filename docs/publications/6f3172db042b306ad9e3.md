# A Theoretical Framework for Non\-Invasive Neurosurgery: Bridging Molecular Phase Velocity and Bio\-Nuclear Resonance Mechanics

**Autores:** abdelhadi Abouelfida

**Data:** 2026-10-10 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

This technical monograph introduces an advanced cross\-disciplinary biophysical framework that bridges computational neuroscience with cellular Bio\-Nuclear Physics, authored by Scientist Abouelfida Abdelhadi\. The framework models the human cellular matrix as an actively coherent bio\-nuclear energy engine where nuclear magnetic atomic spins and inherent Larmor frequencies are structurally governed by spatial boundaries\. By utilizing the fundamental physical law \(\\\(v = \\text\{Diameter\} \\times \\text\{Frequency\}\\\)\) as a core scaling tool for biological micro\-resonators, this document outlines the formal mathematical modeling \(Equations 1–7\) governing three novel therapeutic avenues: Bio\-Nuclear Magnetic Coupling, Radiation\-Induced Elastic Permeability Shifts \(\\\(\\Phi \_\{rad\}\\\)\), and the Acoustic Activation of Tracer Elements\. Furthermore, it integrates a complete electro\-acoustic feedback suite including real\-time acoustic sensor microphone capture equations and automated phase\-lock error calibration loops\. This index establishes the mathematical parameters required to synchronize mechanical, electrophysiological, and atomic frequencies for non\-invasive, target\-specific neuro\-therapeutics\.
