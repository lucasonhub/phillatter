# Beyond Distribution Matching: Selective Margin Preservation for Supervised Fine\-Tuning

**Autores:** Yuxuan Wang; Guangxiang Zhao; Xinyu Tan; Yahui Han; Xinxiang Yin; Zhonghai Wu; Xiangzheng Zhang; Lin Sun; Tong Yang

**Data:** 2026-10-14 · **Tipo:** preprint

## Resumo

Supervised fine\-tuning \(SFT\) changes logit margins among non\-target tokens at teacher\-forced prefixes\. Matching a local reference distribution keeps those probabilities and also penalizes margin expansions\. We ask whether a soft auxiliary can preserve selected local non\-target margin relations without freezing that distribution\. We introduce \\textbf\{Selective Rank\-Preserving SFT \(SRP\-SFT\)\}, a soft margin target: a hinge on reference\-scaled lower bounds for pairs from the reference&\#x27;s top\-$K$ non\-target tokens, applied at fixed teacher\-forced prefixes\. The hinge stops once the requested bound is met\. We derive the KL cost of violating a margin, characterize the feasible region and its conditional utility, and give a finite\-step condition for reducing aggregate violations\. In one\-epoch experiments on Qwen3\-4B\-Base and UltraFeedback, this target raises MATH\-500 and code\-generation scores over CE and LP\-SFT at all three training seeds\. At seed 1234, MATH\-500 pass@1 rises from 49\.56\\% to 54\.56\\% and the four\-metric code mean rises from 65\.91\\% to 69\.50\\% relative to LP\-SFT\. Ranking controls, held\-out cache diagnostics, sensitivity sweeps, and six additional backbones show where these score changes appear\.
