# Avaliação do uso de prompt estruturado para auxiliar o diagnóstico pulpar em endodontia

**Autores:** Elielson Melo Queiroz; Jaime Pereira de Jesus Neto; Lucas Henrique Reis Bezerra; Marcílio Oliveira Melo; Sanmyo Martins Oliveira; Matheus Araújo Brito Santos Lopes

**Data:** 2026-10-13 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

This study explores the current developments and applications of emerging technologies in modern information processing, focusing on system optimization, data efficiency, and user experience enhancement\. As digital environments become increasingly complex, the demand for scalable, low\-latency architectures and intelligent processing models has grown exponentially\. Through a multi\-faceted analytical approach, this research evaluates state\-of\-the\-art framework integration, operational benchmarks, and algorithmic performance across diverse data environments\. The findings indicate that tailored technological strategies significantly improve resource utilization, decrease latency, and enhance system adaptability\. Ultimately, this work offers a comprehensive foundational framework for researchers and practitioners aiming to design resilient, next\-generation digital platforms\.
