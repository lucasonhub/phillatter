# Twin\-satellite constraints from GF\-5B and DQ\-1 enable hourly near\-surface NO2 estimation across China

**Autores:** Chengxin Zhang; Zhipeng Ding; Cheng Liu

**Data:** 2026-10-14 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Nitrogen dioxide \(NO 2 \) is a major air pollutant with significant impacts on human health, yet satellite\-based estimates of surface NO 2 with full diurnal coverage remain limited\. Here, we develop a machine learning framework to estimate hourly near\-surface NO 2 concentrations across China by integrating complementary observations from two Chinese polar\-orbiting satellites, GF\-5B \(\~10:30 local time\) and DQ\-1 \(\~13:30 local time\)\. A Light Gradient Boosting Machine \(LightGBM\) model driven by twin\-satellite NO 2 vertical column densities \(VCDs\) and meteorological reanalysis achieves strong predictive performance in 10\-fold cross\-validation, with a coefficient of determination \(R 2 \) of 0\.91 and root mean square error \(RMSE\) of 5\.40 μg/m 3 across all hours \(4\.46 μg/m 3 during daytime; 6\.32 μg/m 3 at night\), and retains reasonable skill under stricter site\-based, time\-based, and independent cross\-year validation\. The reconstructed dataset captures the characteristic diurnal cycle of NO 2 , with morning and evening peaks associated with traffic emissions and a midday minimum driven by photochemical loss\. Compared to the single\-satellite TROPOMI product, the twin\-satellite configuration substantially improves the representation of diurnal variability, increasing R 2 from 0\.81 to 0\.91 and reducing RMSE by 31\.60%, and corrects a distorted urban\-rural NO 2 exposure ratio \(1\.70 for TROPOMI versus a ground\-based 1\.19\), narrowing it to 1\.30\. Feature\-importance analysis further shows that meteorological variables dominate model predictions during both day and night, with satellite VCDs providing a complementary, though indirect, constraint on nighttime concentrations\. The resulting hourly NO 2 dataset demonstrates the value of synergistic multi\-satellite observations for air quality monitoring and provides a robust basis for refined pollution control and population exposure assessment\.
