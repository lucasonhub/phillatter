# Question and Revision to the Computational Theory of Mind

**Autores:** Xiaoli Liu

**Data:** 2008-01-01 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

The computational theory of mind has dominated the two research frameworks namely the cognitivism and connectivism\.Cognitive computationalism not only takes cognition as computation,but constructs cognition by computational ways\.It has met many critics,such as body and situation,intentional contents etc\.\.Contemporary embodied cognition tries to explore the non\-computational cognitive ways such as dynamical system theory and embodied semantics\.It tells us the computational theory of mind is not good enough and can be revised\.
