# Global epidemiology of metabolic dysfunction\-associated steatotic liver disease: 2026 update

**Autores:** Tie Xiao; Nicholas Chew; Giovanni Targher; Christopher D\. Byrne; Gregory YH Lip; Xiao\-Dong Zhou; Ming\-Hua Zheng

**Data:** 2026-10-14 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Metabolic dysfunction\-associated steatotic liver disease \(MASLD\) now affects approximately one\-third of the global adult population and is the most common chronic liver disease worldwide\.An epidemiological update is needed because recent developments have implications for clinical care and treatment of MASLD as a multisystem disease\.The epidemic of MASLD is growing\.Global Burden of Disease 2023 analyses estimate approximately 1\.3 billion prevalent cases, with projections approaching 1\.8 billion by 2050, and a disproportionate rise in metabolic dysfunction\-associated steatohepatitis \(MASH\) and advanced fibrosis\.MASLD is increasingly recognized as a systemic metabolic disorder with hepatic, cardiovascular, renal, and other extrahepatic manifestations\.Recently, the 79 th World Health Assembly adopted a resolution recognizing steatotic liver disease as a major global noncommunicable disease challenge\.Concurrently, cardiovascular\-kidney\-metabolic frameworks and non\-hepatology guidelines have incorporated liver fibrosis assessment into evaluations of selected highrisk populations\.Advances in non\-invasive diagnostics have expanded case\-finding, while the availability of MASH\-directed pharmacotherapies has shifted the clinical and economic rationale for earlier detection\.This narrative review synthesizes the latest epidemiological data on global and regional prevalence and incidence, comorbidities, hepatic and extrahepatic outcomes, advances in screening and prevention strategies, and future directions\.
