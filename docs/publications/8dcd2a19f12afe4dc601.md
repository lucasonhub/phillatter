# APPLICATION OF ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE IN IMPROVING INNOVATIVE ENTREPRENEURSHIP

**Autores:** Faizullo Ubaidullozoda

**Data:** 2026-10-15 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

The article is devoted to the study of artificial intelligence in improving innovative entrepreneurship\. The author notes that artificial intelligence technologies play an important role in the development and establishment of innovative entrepreneurship\. The paper identifies the key advantages of using artificial intelligence, such as speeding up the product development cycle, reducing operating costs, and improving the quality of strategic decisions\. At the same time, the author presented his opinion based on the opinion of scientists in the field of artificial intelligence\. The main functions of AI management, the maturity matrix of artificial intelligence, and the algorithm for implementing the functionality of artificial intelligence in innovative entrepreneurship are also studied\.
