\_Behavioral and Brain Sciences\_ 16 \(1\):85\-86\. 1993A commentary on a target article by Alison Gopnik \(1993\) How we know our minds: the illusion of first\-person knowledge of intentionality\. Focusing on evidence of how children acquire a theory of mind, this commentary argues that there are internal inconsistencies in theories that both argue for the functional role of conscious experiences and the irreducibility of those experiences to third\-person viewable information processing\. \(direct link \)
