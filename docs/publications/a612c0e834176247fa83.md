# Lectotypification of Argyreia laotica Gagnep\. and A\. mekongensis Gagnep\. &amp; Courchet \(Convolvulaceae\)

**Autores:** Tran Thai Vinh; Vu Kim Cong; Đặng Thị Thắm; Quach Van Hoi; Nông Văn Duy

**Data:** 2026-10-12 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

The names Argyreia laotica Gagnep\. and A\. mekongensis Gagnep\. &amp; Courchet were published in 1915, with the original material consisting of syntypes\. Examination of the available original material confirmed seven extant syntype specimens for A\. laotica and seven for A\. mekongensis \. Lectotypes are designated for both names based on examination of their original material\.
