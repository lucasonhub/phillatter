# Extraction, Characterization and Application of Essential Oil from Lemon \(Citrus Limon \(L\.\) Osbeck\) Peel

**Autores:** 

**Data:** 2026-10-15 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Essential oil was extracted from lemon \(Citrus limon \(L\.\) Osbeck\) peel by steam distillation and characterized to evaluate its potential industrial applications\.The oil was recovered in a yield of 0\.38%, within the range reported for citrus peel oils\.It was colourless, soluble in organic solvents and insoluble in water, with a specific gravity of 0\.450\.Chemical characterization gave an acid value of 7\.59 mg/g, an iodine value of 11\.35 mg/g, a saponification value of 100\.70 mg/g and an ester value of 93\.11 mg/g\.The low iodine value classified the oil as non\-drying, while the low saponification value indicated a non\-saponifying oil, consistent with the terpenoid\-dominated composition typical of citrus peel oils\.The extracted oil was subsequently incorporated as a fragrance ingredient in the formulation of liquid soap, demonstrating a practical route for valorizing lemon\-peel waste into a value\-added consumer product\.
