# Horst, Steven: \_The Demise of Reductionism in Philosophy of Science\_

**Autores:**

## Descrição do feed

In Beyond reduction: philosophy of mind and post\-reductionist philosophy of science\. New York: Oxford University Press\. pp\. 47\-64\. 2007This chapter examines the widespread assumptions that intertheoretic reductions are common in the natural sciences and that reducibility serves as a kind of normative constraint upon the legitimacy of the special sciences\. While this was the mainline view in philosophy of science in the mid‐twentieth century, it has received decisive criticism within philosophy of science since the 1970s\. The basic reasons for this rejection of Carnap‐Nagel style reductionism are recounted in this chapter\.
