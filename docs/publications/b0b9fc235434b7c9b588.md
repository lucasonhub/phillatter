# CNT\-network\-engineered V2Se9/CoSe2 heterostructures for thermally robust high\-performance supercapacitors with machine\-learning\-assisted performance prediction

**Autores:** Fouzia Mashkoor; Mohd Shoeb; Sayed Mohammed Adnan; Changyoon Jeong

**Data:** 2026-10-14 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Engineering conductive heterointerfaces in transition\-metal selenides is an effective strategy for improving charge\-transfer kinetics, redox\-site accessibility, and cycling durability in supercapacitor electrodes\. Herein, selenium\-rich V 2 Se 9 , V 2 Se 9 /CoSe 2 heterostructures, and CNT\-integrated V 2 Se 9 /CoSe 2 composites were prepared through a rapid microwave\-assisted strategy\. Cobalt incorporation was used to create redox\-active heterointerfaces, while CNT loading was varied to examine its influence on conductive\-network formation and electrochemical performance\. Structural, microscopic, and surface analyses confirmed the coexistence of V 2 Se 9 and CoSe 2 domains and supported CNT\-assisted interfacial contact\. The optimized CNT 3 \-CoVSe electrode delivered a high specific capacitance of 877\.59 F/g at 1 A/g and retained 267\.14 F/g at 10 A/g\. Electrochemical impedance spectroscopy showed accelerated charge\-transfer kinetics, with the charge\-transfer resistance reduced to 6\.241 Ω\. Temperature\-dependent testing demonstrated thermally adaptive behavior, with capacitance increasing from 825\.90 F/g at 0 °C to 968\.10 F/g at 50 °C before declining slightly at higher temperature\. Machine\-learning\-assisted response\-surface analysis mapped the coupled effects of temperature and current density, with the ANN model providing the most reliable prediction\. The symmetric device delivered 63\.44 Wh/kg at 953\.99 W/kg and retained 80% capacitance after 13,000 cycles, confirming the potential of CNT\-network\-engineered V 2 Se 9 /CoSe 2 electrodes for thermally robust high\-performance supercapacitor applications\.
