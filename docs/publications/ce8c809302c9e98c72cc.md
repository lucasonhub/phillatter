# A Socio\-Technical Systems Analysis Framework for Human–AI Collaborative Systems: A Case Study on AI\-Powered Job Applicant Screening

**Autores:** Fawzeyia Elhashmi Mohamad Salah; Nesrin Muftah Elrgibe

**Data:** 2026-10-11 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

The increasing use of various types of probabilistic AI technologies in decision\-making roles limits the usefulness of traditional business analysis and design \(B&amp;A\) methods for documenting non\-AI decision\-makers and other aspects of probabilistic AI systems‚ including human backup decision\-making roles․ Traditional UML and other business analysis tools support only binary decision outcomes and offer few tools for documenting the shared decision\-making responsibilities of AI and human decision makers․ This paper proposes an AI\-Socio B&amp;A method that integrates traditional business analysis methods with three new tools:\(1\) a Confidence\-Based Delegation Matrix offering three levels of AI decision\-making authority \(automatic decision making when confidence exceeds 80%‚ assisted decision making when confidence is between 50% and 80%‚ and human backup decision making when confidence is below 50%\); \(2\) an amended use case format that offers additional documentation of confidence levels and bias\-prevention techniques along with other information; \(3\) a process for obtaining and using feedback from human decision makers to improve the AI system․ A case study of an AI\-Powered Job Applicant Screening and Short\-listing System‚ including documentation of an extended use case \(UC\-02\)‚ actors matrix‚ two design diagrams‚ and comparison with traditional B&amp;A and UML documentation on five design and safety metrics‚ illustrates use of the new AI\-Socio B&amp;A method․ Advantages of AI\-Socio B&amp;A include reduced automation bias and inclusion of anonymization as a requirement and other tools to share work between human and AI decision makers․
