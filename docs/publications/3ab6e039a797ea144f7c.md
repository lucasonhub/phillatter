# Kannon, Aelion: SN12 — Neural Dynamics and Embodied Cognitive Architecture: Physical Interface, Stabilization, Distortion, and Restoration

**Autores:** 

**Data de descoberta:** 2026-10-19 · **Tipo:** unknown

## Descrição do feed

Neural Dynamics and Embodied Cognitive Architecture examines the physical interface, stabilization, distortion and restoration of embodied cognition\. Document class: Canon — Structural Neuroscience\. The volume leads with the Analytic Register while preserving the Soul / Mind architecture and the distinction between essential cognitive Configuration and physical neural configuration\. It separates native architectural claims, empirical observations and proposed physical realization through declared constituents, scales, boundary conditions and measured relations\. A bounded task\-capacity equation supplies a conditional recovery trajectory, without identifying its coefficients with essential orientation or Tether magnitude\. Restored capacity remains distinct from anatomical reversal; proposed neural correspondences and the fully centropic embodiment hypothesis retain their empirical research requirements\. Part of: Structural Neuroscience: The Architecture and Dynamics of Cognition\. \(direct link \)
