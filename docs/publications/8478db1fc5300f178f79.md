# Text Mining and Machine Learning for New\-MediaPublic\-Opinion Spillovers in Manufacturing Supply\-Chain Digital Collaboration

**Autores:** Chengyan Li

**Data:** 2026-10-12 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Online media coverage may transmit information across supply\-chain relationships, yet converting such signals into evidence requires a clear separation between text measurement, network alignment, and statistical inference\. This study examines whether focal\-firm media attention is associated with connected partners’ annual\-report digital disclosure and develops a manufacturing\-oriented workflow for directed supply\-chain analysis\. We construct a firm–partner–year panel for Chinese non\-financial A\-share firms from 2007 to 2024 by combining aggregated CFND media indicators, annual\-report digital\-term frequencies, CNRDS supplier–customer links, and CSMAR controls\. Fixed\-effects models estimate conditional associations on directed edges\. The baseline specification shows a positive association between focal\-firm coverage and partner digital disclosure \(β = 0\.048, SE = 0\.020\), while alternative aggregate coverage measures yield coefficients with the same sign; mechanism and subgroup results remain observational and descriptive\. These findings do not establish causality or realized interfirm digital collaboration because annual\-report word frequency is an imperfect disclosure proxy\. A Chinese\- BERT–MLP event\-and\-tone module is therefore retained only as a future extension pending article\-level text, human\- reviewed labels, and out\-of\-sample evaluation\.
