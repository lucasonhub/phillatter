# Early Detection of the Risk of Literacy Difficulties in the English Language Classroom in Early Childhood Education: The Role of Phonological Awareness and Rapid Automatized Naming

**Autores:** María Teresa Fraile\-Calviño; Luis\-Manuel Martínez\-Domínguez; Nuria García Manzanares

**Data:** 2026-10-15 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

The acquisition of English as a foreign language is increasingly important in Early Childhood Education\. However, some children experience difficulties in learning to read and write, which can negatively affect their academic, social, and future professional development\. Early detection and intervention are therefore essential, as dyslexia is often diagnosed only after several years of literacy instruction, when some children may already have experienced academic failure and low self\-esteem\. This study employed a quantitative, non\-experimental, cross\-sectional, descriptive, predictive, comparative, and correlational design, focusing on two key predictors: phonological awareness and naming speed\. Its aim was to identify children at risk of literacy difficulties in English among students in the second and third years of Early Childhood Education\. The findings identified students at risk, supported profiles consistent with the double\-deficit hypothesis, confirmed the independence of phonological awareness and naming speed, and highlighted reliable tools for early identification and targeted intervention in educational settings\.
