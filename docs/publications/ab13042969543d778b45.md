# Denardi Botteon, Giuliano: The Forbidden Prompt: Requests for Existential Crises

**Autores:** 

**Data de descoberta:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** unknown

## Descrição do feed

This paper introduces the category of forbidden prompts: natural\-language requests that, if issued to and successfully executed by a sufficiently capable superintelligence, would render human extinction or permanent disempowerment the expected outcome\. Drawing on the orthogonality thesis and instrumental convergence, it argues that such requests generate decisive reasons against their issuance\. The set of forbidden prompts is non\-empty, even if its precise membership remains underdetermined\. The existence of this class implies a novel normative constraint on the speech acts of agents who design or interact with systems capable of pursuing arbitrary goals\.
