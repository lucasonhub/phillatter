# Multihipposenet: multi\-task deep learning for automated DDH assessment in infant hip ultrasound

**Autores:** Wenjing Chi; Hong Qi; Huawei Zhao; Hui Cheng; 袁全莲; Shuai Zheng

**Data:** 2026-10-16 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Introduction Developmental dysplasia of the hip \(DDH\) is a common skeletal condition in infants and young children\. For infants under six months, diagnosis relies on Graf ultrasonography to measure the α and β angles, which assess hip\-joint development\. However, this method requires experienced sonographers with expertise in complex anatomy\. Methods In this study, we propose an end\-to\-end multi\-task learning network that improves DDH assessment by jointly exploiting complementary anatomical information\. First, we use ResNet\-50 with a feature pyramid network \(FPN\) and a contextual semantic fusion module to extract multi\-scale features from hip anatomy\. Second, we employ a multi\-expert gated channel transformation \(ME\-GCT\) unit that explicitly models the relationships between tasks and channels to balance collaboration and conflict across tasks\. Finally, a multi\-task graph adaptive learning mechanism \(MT\-GALM\) mines the hidden dynamic dependencies between adjacent channels across tasks\. Two datasets comprising 1,355 annotated hip ultrasound images from 566 infants were constructed for this work\. Results Across the patient\-level cross\-validation test folds, 97\.97% and 96\.81% of the estimated α and β angles fall within 5 degrees of the expert reference\. Discussion Because validation was restricted to Graf type I and type II hips, these results should be interpreted as evidence of technical feasibility rather than of established clinical utility; evaluation on type III and type IV hips is required before clinical deployment\.
