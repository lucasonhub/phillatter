# Intervención a través de técnicas somáticas desde la pedagogía del loto, para mejorar los procesos de convivencia en estudiantes de un colegio público en una zona de conflicto en Bogotá, Colombia

**Autores:** Alexander Rubio Álvarez

**Data:** 2026-10-15 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

This article presents research conducted with students from the Rodrigo Lara Bonilla School in Bogota, Colombia\. Lotus Pedagogy is an innovative educational methodology that seeks to foster socio\-emotional regulation in youth through practices such as breathing, meditation, mindfulness, hatha yoga, and body percussion within collective group work\. Objective: To evaluate the impact of the somatic techniques of Lotus Pedagogy on reducing violence among eleventh\-grade students at a public school in Ciudad Bolívar \(Bogotá, Colombia\), a vulnerable area with high levels of conflict\. Methods and materials: Quantita\- tive study with a pretest\-posttest experimental design, with a control group \(n = 82\) and an experimental group \(n = 82\), throughout the 2019 school year \(February–November\)\. The experimental group received 20 minutes of somatic techniques three times a week in physical education and arts classes; coexistence was measured using the student behavior record \(“student observer”\)\. Results and discussion: A t\-test showed a significant reduction in conflict situa\- tions in the experimental group compared with the control group \(p= 0\.001\), in line with previous studies on yoga\. Conclusions: Lotus Pedagogy improves school coexistence and is a relevant strategy for peace education and post\-conflict education\.
