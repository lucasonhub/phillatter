# Explore Once, Evaluate Many: Multiple Policy Evaluation for Stochastic Linear Bandits

**Autores:** Spyros Dragazis; Yilei Chen; Aldo Pacchiano

**Data:** 2026-10-15 · **Tipo:** preprint

## Resumo

We study how to evaluate $K$ target policies simultaneously in stochastic contextual bandits with linear reward structure\. Given a known $d$\-dimensional feature map and reward\-free access to policy queries, we propose PRISMA \(Policy evaluation through Regularized Information Sharing and Mixture Allocation\), an algorithm that estimates every target policy&\#x27;s value to accuracy $\\varepsilon$ with probability at least $1\-δ$\. The algorithm uses $\\widetilde\{\\mathcal\{O\}\}\\\!\\left\( \\frac\{\(L^2S^2\+β^2\)\(1\\veeρ^\\star\)\}\{\\varepsilon^2\} \\cdot \\log\(K\) \\right\)$ context draws and reward observations, where $ρ^\\star\\le d$ is an instance\-dependent measure of the difficulty of designing a shared sampling distribution\. Here, $L$, $S$, and $β$ bound the feature norm, reward parameter norm, and reward noise, respectively, and the suppressed factors are logarithmic in $K$, $d$, and $1/δ$\. Our method combines reward\-free planning, a regularized sampling design, and a single ridge regression estimator shared across all target policies\. An adaptive doubling procedure selects the regularization scale without knowledge of $ρ^\\star$\. The guarantees require no numerical lower bound on second\-moment eigenvalues and have no explicit dependence on the cardinalities of the context and action spaces\. Policy\-query costs are accounted for separately, with $K$ queries per shared planning context\.
