# Impact of AI\-Based Personalised Services on Customer Loyalty

**Autores:** Kishor Chauhan

**Data:** 2026-10-13 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

Abstract: Artificial intelligence \(AI\) has fundamentally transformed the capacity of organisations to deliver personalised recommendations, communications, customer service and digital experiences at scale\. This study develops and empirically tests a comprehensive model linking AI\-based personalisation with customer loyalty through the mechanisms of trust and customer satisfaction, while simultaneously examining AI service quality and privacy concern as contextual factors\. Using a quantitative cross\-sectional design, data were collected from 400 digital\-service users through a structured 5\-point Likert\-scale questionnaire\. The analysis encompasses descriptive statistics, reliability testing, KMO and Bartlett&\#x27;s tests, correlation analysis, multiple regression, mediation and moderation analysis, and selected demographic tests\. The results demonstrate acceptable internal consistency for all constructs, with Cronbach&\#x27;s alpha values ranging from \.801 to \.865\. The KMO value was \.916 and Bartlett&\#x27;s test was statistically significant, indicating that the item correlation matrix was suitable for factor\-oriented analysis\. AI\-based personalisation exhibited a positive association with customer loyalty \(β = \.380, p &lt; \.001\), explaining 14\.4% of its variance in the simple regression\. The full regression model explained 42\.4% of the variance in loyalty\. A bootstrap test of the serial indirect pathway through trust and satisfaction was positive \(indirect effect = \.060, 95% CI \[\.040, \.085\]\)\. Privacy concern significantly moderated the AI personalisation–loyalty relationship \(interaction β = \.060, p = \.040\)\. The findings contribute to the growing body of knowledge on AI\-driven personalisation by demonstrating that trust and satisfaction serve as critical mediating mechanisms, while privacy concerns represent an important boundary condition\. Keywords: artificial intelligence; AI personalisation; customer loyalty; trust; customer satisfaction; privacy concern; AI service quality; digital services
