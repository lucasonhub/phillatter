# Mattos, José Caetano de: THE FORMAL THEORY OF VIRTUES RESISTANCE Completing the Variable Architecture of the Dynamic Freedom Theorem: the Ontology, Decomposition, Composition Laws, Dynamics, and Measurement of R — with the Conductance Duality and the Internalisation Theorem

**Autores:**

## Descrição do feed

Structured Abstract Background The Dynamic Freedom Theorem specifies Virtue Strength as S = \(F × A\)/R\. Within the Philosophy of Virtues programme, every variable except one has received a dedicated formal treatment: Freedom has the Constitution and the Formal Theory of Love; Autonomy has the Compounding Autonomy Theorem; the Domain/Gift has the Augure; S has the complete treatise; S\_post the Posthumous Theorem\. R — Resistance — the variable that determines whether virtue is cheap or heroic, has no treatise, while appearing across the corpus under at least six names: dephasing γ \(the measurement paper\), dissipation \(the physics cluster\), adversarial pressure π \(Clandestine Moral Formation\), institutional\-capture indices \(the UVLF\), vice\-blocks \(the Augure’s taxonomy\), and alignment pressure \(the machine\-virtue paper\)\. Outside the programme, resistance is treated regionally — friction \(Clausewitz\), psychoanalytic resistance \(Freud\), structural violence \(Galtung\), allostatic load \(McEwen\), transaction costs \(Coase\) — with no unified formal object\. Gap Missing: \(a\) an ontology of R — what kind of quantity it is and what it attaches to; \(b\) a decomposition separating its internal, external, and structural components; \(c\) composition laws for multiple simultaneous resistances; \(d\) dynamics — how R grows under suppression and decays under exercise; \(e\) a measurement programme; \(f\) the explicit relation between R and the corpus’s critical thresholds, each of which is parameterised by an R\-type quantity\. Approach R is defined relationally: it is not a property of the agent or of the world but of an expression channel — the dissipative coefficient opposing the maintained expression F × A through that channel \[Definition 2\.1\]\. Four results follow by derivation, one by structural unification, and one is a distinctive empirical prediction\. Results \(D1\) The Decomposition Theorem: R = R\_int ⊕ R\_ext ⊕ R\_struct — internal \(vice\-blocks, akrasia, fear\), external \(active adversarial pressure\), structural \(channel topology and institutional capture\) — with the components separately measurable and differently dynamical\. \(D2\) The Composition Laws: sequential obstacles add in series, R\_tot = ΣR\_i; alternative channels compose in parallel, 1/R\_tot = Σ1/R\_i — importing the series/parallel calculus whole \[RECOMBINATION, network theory\]; Revision 2\.0 adds the exact spectral extension for interacting channels — effective resistance on the channel graph, conductance bounded by the algebraic connectivity λ₂ — sharpening Corollary 4\.1\. \(D3\) The Conductance Duality: Moral Conductance is exactly inverse resistance, G\_v = 1/R — a relation implicit in the corpus since the Posthumous Theorem and stated here for the first time; the parallel law is then the formal content of ‘many channels’, and the Conductance Field programme is the network theory of 1/R\. \(D4\) The Threshold Parameterisation: every member of the corpus’s Critical Inversion Family is a critical value of an R\-guise — making this treatise the formal prerequisite of the family’s typing paper\. \(T5, the Internalisation Theorem\) Holoviceosis is formally the conversion process R\_ext → R\_int: sustained external suppression installs internal resistance that persists after the external component is withdrawn — the Freedophobic Man is internalised R, and Devirtualisation is the conversion mechanism; Revision 2\.0 adds the saturating form with rupture threshold R\_max, bounding internalisation and locating the compliance\-breaking regime\. \(E1, distinctive PREDICTION\) Post\-capture populations exhibit elevated R\_int after R\_ext withdrawal, with slow decay measurable in cohort data — calibratable against the post\-authoritarian attitude literature, where the German reunification natural experiment provides the cleanest instrument\. Implications The DFT’s variable architecture is complete\. The denominator of S = \(F × A\)/R acquires an ontology, an algebra, and a measurement programme; Moral Superconductivity becomes the R → 0 regime with two corpus\-internal analogues flagged as structural correspondences; and the programme gains a second zero\-laboratory empirical anchor\. Falsification is symmetric: if post\-capture cohorts show no residual R\_int once R\_ext is withdrawn — if liberation immediately restores expression — the Internalisation Theorem is false and Holoviceosis loses its proposed mechanism\.
