# From the Bazaar to the Algorithm: AI as a Technology Friend, Not a Human Friend

**Autores:** Akbarzadeh Alireza

**Data:** 2026-10-12 · **Tipo:** article

## Resumo

This paper explores the philosophical and architectural implications of artificial intelligence, arguing that AI should be understood as a historical continuation of tools rather than an independent agent\. Focusing on the core concept of the &quot;technology friend,&quot; the study examines the dangers of profit\-driven design choices that simulate human relationships, foster emotional dependency, and obscure responsibility\. It proposes alternative frameworks, such as structural boundaries, non\-deceptive interface design, and bridging mechanisms to human communities, offering a perspective at the intersection of the philosophy of technology, AI ethics, and cognitive science\.
