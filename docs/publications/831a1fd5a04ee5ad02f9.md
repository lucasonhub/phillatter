# Undergraduate Research for a Sustainable Future

**Autores:** Nancy H\. Hensel

**Data:** 2026-10-14 · **Tipo:** book

## Resumo

This timely book presents undergraduate research as a powerful vehicle for engaging students in addressing the United Nations&\#x27; Sustainable Development Goals \(SDGs\)\. Hensel begins with a comprehensive foundation on the SDGs and higher education&\#x27;s evolving role in advancing them and then examines specific goals through detailed case studies and exemplary programs that showcase cross\-disciplinary student research in action\. These narratives illuminate the complex challenges and innovative solutions that emerge when students engage directly with pressing global issues: from climate action to poverty reduction to health equity\. Both a call to action and a playbook for any faculty member involved in undergraduate research, this text also functions as an important resource for administrators and policymakers seeking to support student discovery and innovation\.
